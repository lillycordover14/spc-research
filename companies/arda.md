## What they do
Software-first counterpart to the owner-operators: train models on video of skilled operators, then run plants other people own. Slide 8 puts it in layer 5 (Factory AI) at ~$700M reported.

## Why it fits the theme (or doesn't)
Does NOT own production. This is the key control case for the thesis: can a software-first vendor get the same proprietary process data without owning the plant, and does the customer let it?

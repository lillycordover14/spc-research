## What they do
Builds its own metal-AM machines and runs them as a service. The ML is in the process (closed-loop control of the melt), which is the purest 'data from running production' example in the set.

## Why it fits the theme (or doesn't)
Owns production, builds the machine and the software, keeps the data. Valuation ($179M on $126M raised) suggests the market is unsure the service model scales — worth understanding why.

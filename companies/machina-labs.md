## What they do
Robotic sheet-forming (incremental forming), welding and finishing in one software layer; makes parts in its own LA factory and ships robotic cells. Lockheed, NVIDIA and Yamaha on the cap table signal both defense demand and the 'cell as product' angle.

## Why it fits the theme (or doesn't)
Strong on in-house software and process data (forming is closed-loop with sensors). Hybrid model raises the central question: is more value in running the plant or in selling the cell?

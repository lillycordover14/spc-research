## What they do
Wire harnesses are labor-heavy, drawing-driven and on every aircraft/satellite; Senra converts legacy drawings to digital twins, generates work instructions, and runs its own production. a16z + Sequoia + Founders Fund.

## Why it fits the theme (or doesn't)
Textbook: owns production, software built in-house, output is mission-critical, qualification-gated. Narrow product category — the question is TAM beyond harnesses.

## Traction and signals

## Economics (what we know)
Harness shops are ~20-30% GM, quote-to-ship in weeks. Need Senra's GM and lead time.

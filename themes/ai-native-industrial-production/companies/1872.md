## What they do
1872 (1872.ai Inc.) owns and runs a steel fabrication factory in the 1903 David Hummel building in Camp Washington, Cincinnati, about 30,000 sq ft of production space, and sells finished welded steel assemblies: large structural frames, skids, enclosures and modular infrastructure for energy, utility, industrial and modular-construction customers [src-20260722-985690] [src-20261016-d61bc1] [src-20261022-fdbf29]. Its in-house Factory OS ingests 3D designs, handles materials sourcing and cost estimates, stock and production planning, and the movement of materials, machines and robots; a layer of agentic AI systems is meant to act like welders, buyers, planners and logistics staff and to build, monitor and maintain Factory OS [src-20260722-985690]. Robotic welding cells come from Path Robotics (Columbus, OH) running its Obsidian adaptive-welding model; Path is also qualifying hardware for large high-mix assemblies [src-20260722-985690] [src-20260722-39c3b2]. Early automations are live; the full autonomous vision is targeted for 2027, and Factory 1 is explicitly a prototype for later plants [src-20260722-985690].

- in-house-software: yes for orchestration (Factory OS: quote/estimate, plan, schedule, robot and material movement). No for the weld model itself, which is Path Robotics' Obsidian; the proprietary layer is planning and coordination, not physical AI [src-20260722-985690] [src-20260722-39c3b2].
- data-loop: asserted (agents that build/monitor/maintain Factory OS; closed-loop Model/Orchestrate/Execute framing) but no public evidence yet that per-part data improves quoting or yield. Website claims 99% first pass yield and 70% arc-on time without a baseline [src-20260722-985690] [src-20261022-fdbf29].
- speed-moat: the pitch is lead times from months or years down to weeks; no customer-verified lead time yet [src-20260722-985690].
- mission-critical-output: weak today. Stated markets are modular construction, infrastructure and energy/utility skids, not defense or aero [src-20260722-985690] [src-20261022-fdbf29].
- customer-qualification, secured-inputs: none claimed.
- Net: the cleanest owner-operator test of the thesis at the fabrication layer, but as of Oct 2026 it is an 18-person, partly automated job shop with one $15M seed from a state-anchored fund. Watch whether Factory OS compounds or whether value sits with Path's weld model.

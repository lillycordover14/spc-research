## What they do
Designs injection molds with physics simulation plus ML, builds them in Warren (50k sq ft), and runs them on 16 presses (55-2,000 t) in Shelby (100k sq ft) to sell finished plastic parts to OEMs and Tier 1s in auto, rail, industrial, consumer and defense [src-20261027-b51989]. Owner-operator: does not sell the software or standalone molds [src-20260130-9500f0].

## Why it fits the theme (or doesn't)
AI in the engineering step (mold design, process setup) feeding owned production; aimed at a shrinking US moldmaker base that loses to China on price (up to 40% cheaper) and design headcount [src-20250101-110ec0][src-20260130-9500f0]. Doesn't: commodity end-market, automation of parting lines still manual.

## Traction and signals
Claim (Slodov, Sept 2025): first big multi-year part programs, "mid eight figure range", ~50 staff [src-20250919-2805c7]. Drone-program molds and NYC subway production work [src-20250327-008034]. Defense-startup buyer uses Atomic more than Hadrian; "turn production in eight days" [src-20250729-a0a78c]. Shelby tax abatement, >$10M capex, 30-60 jobs [src-20260616-57c7d5].

## Economics (what we know)
$8M test bed spend [src-20260130-9500f0]; plans to fold mold cost into piece price (claim). No margins disclosed.

## What they do
Divergent runs DAPS, a production system that pairs in-house AI generative design with metal additive manufacturing and automated fixtureless robotic assembly to make structures (from single sustainment parts up to full airframe systems) in its Torrance plant [src-20231115-5d0589][src-20250915-311606]. It started as the production arm of the Czinger 21C hypercar (350+ printed components) and seven luxury automotive customers, then moved into aerospace and defense in 2022 with General Atomics; it now names Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, Triumph Group and the US Air Force, with contracts across dozens of A&D customers [src-20231115-5d0589][src-20250915-ed0b46]. It describes itself as a certified Tier 1 supplier and frames DAPS as 'design-manufacturing-assembly as a service' with a planned global network of regional factories [src-20231115-5d0589].

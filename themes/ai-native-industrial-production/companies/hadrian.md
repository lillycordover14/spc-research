## What they do
Hadrian builds and runs highly automated factories that make precision metal components for aerospace, defense and now naval shipbuilding. Its in-house software stack, Opus, handles scheduling, tool routing and inspection and is claimed to cut lead times from months to days and to bring a new factory online in under six months [src-20260807-b0dc5b][src-20250820-cfbf02]. It sells parts to Lockheed Martin, RTX and Anduril and sells 'factories-as-a-service' capacity to DoD programs, most visibly a Navy partnership for Virginia- and Columbia-class submarine components [src-20260807-b0dc5b][src-20260320-67ba4c]. Footprint is almost 3M sq ft across four sites (Torrance CA, Mesa AZ, Cherokee AL, plus a planned 400k sq ft HQ/R&D site) with 643 people and a stated target of 2,000 within a year [src-20260807-b0dc5b][src-20250721-d99a2a].

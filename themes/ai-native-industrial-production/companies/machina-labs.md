## What they do
Machina Labs (LA, founded 2019) runs software-defined factories that form, machine, weld and assemble complex metal structures with robotic cells and no dedicated tooling [src-20260204-ce7053]. The cell is RoboCraftsman, a robotics plus machine-learning platform with AI process controls; RoboForming is the proprietary incremental sheet forming process [src-20260204-ce7053][src-20260205-aa44f0]. It makes parts in its own plants for defense primes (missile and hypersonic structures) and the Air Force, and develops automotive body panels with Toyota; it also places cells with customers [src-20260204-ce7053][src-20260204-e0dd1a]. Business mix is about 70% defense, 30% commercial [src-20260204-e0dd1a].

## Why it fits the theme (or doesn't)
- owns-production: two LA plants today, a 200,000 sq ft Intelligent Factory planned with up to 50 cells and thousands of assemblies per year [src-20260204-ce7053][src-20260204-e0dd1a].
- in-house-software: RoboCraftsman and RoboForming are proprietary; the company calls itself software-defined manufacturing [src-20260204-ce7053][src-20260205-aa44f0].
- data-loop: cells run on AI-driven process controls; forming is closed loop with sensors, so each part feeds the model [src-20260205-aa44f0]. No public numbers on yield or learning rate.
- speed-moat: claims design to production in the same facility, months to days, without retooling [src-20260204-ce7053].
- customer-qualification: Machina Bellator subsidiary holds a Lockheed JASSM qualification contract, the first Machina assembly qualified for a missile system; CMMC Level 2 since Jan 2026 [src-20260204-ce7053].
- mission-critical-output: missile structures, airframes, hypersonics; AFRL and Rapid Sustainment Office contracts [src-20260204-ce7053].
Tension for the thesis: hybrid model. Lockheed and Toyota on the cap table want cells and capacity, not just parts. Whether more value sits in running the plant or shipping the cell is still open.

## Economics (what we know)
- Lead time: months to days claim for new structures, no quantified baseline [src-20260204-ce7053].
- Capacity: planned factory houses up to 50 cells and thousands of structural assemblies per year, implying tens of assemblies per cell per year at the stated scale [src-20260204-ce7053].
- Plant count: 2 today, each under 100,000 sq ft; third at 200,000+ sq ft is the first mass-production site [src-20260204-e0dd1a].
- Financing: factory and cells funded from the $124M equity round; no equipment debt, state incentives or customer-funded capex disclosed, though talks with three states suggest incentive packages are in play [src-20260204-e0dd1a].
- Revenue, GM, cell ASP, parts vs cell mix: not disclosed.

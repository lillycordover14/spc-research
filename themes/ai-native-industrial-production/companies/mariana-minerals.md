## What they do
Mariana Minerals builds and operates its own mines and refineries for critical minerals, starting with copper and lithium, and runs them on an in-house stack called MarianaOS: MineOS (mine management), PlantOS (refinery operations) and CapitalProjectOS (capital project execution) combined into one AI/ML platform [src-20260803-30e6d5]. Copper One in San Juan County, Utah, a brownfield copper mine plus refinery acquired in late 2025, was restarted under autonomous orchestration within four months and is ramping toward 50,000 t/yr of refined copper; fleet autonomy uses Sandvik and Pronto hardware with Mariana's own reinforcement-learning orchestration layer, and the stated goal is no humans in the Utah mining operation by end of 2026 [src-20260803-30e6d5] [src-20260805-6f5d90]. Lithium One in East Texas broke ground in Q4 2025 as the first GWh-scale plant producing lithium from oil and gas produced water, with commercial production targeted for H1 2027 [src-20260803-30e6d5]. The strategy is 10 commercial-scale projects in 10 years, targeting smaller brownfield assets incumbents overlook rather than $5-10B greenfield builds, with each project's operating data improving MarianaOS for the next [src-20260803-30e6d5] [src-20260805-6f5d90]. Headcount is 206 with 92 in engineering and 39 in operations [Harmonic].

## Why it fits the theme (or doesn't)
- owns-production: yes. Owns and operates Copper One (mine plus refinery) and is building Lithium One [src-20260803-30e6d5].
- in-house-software: yes. MarianaOS is proprietary; the vehicle autonomy hardware is partner-supplied (Sandvik, Pronto) but orchestration and plant/mine/project software are in-house [src-20260803-30e6d5] [src-20260805-6f5d90].
- data-loop: stated mechanism, unproven. Release says each project generates operating data that improves MarianaOS for the next facility; the halving of 5-10 year timelines is claimed, and the company has not operated long enough to prove it [src-20260803-30e6d5] [src-20260805-6f5d90].
- secured-inputs: yes. Owns the Utah asset and permits; Texas produced-water feedstock is a locked, under-exploited input [src-20260803-30e6d5] [src-20260805-6f5d90].
- mission-critical-output: yes. Copper and lithium for AI, grid, defense; In-Q-Tel, BHP Ventures and Mitsubishi on the cap table [src-20260803-30e6d5].
- speed-moat: project-execution speed (4-month restart; halved timelines) rather than order lead time [src-20260803-30e6d5].
- Net: the 'rebuild US refining' benchmark for the metals-supply layer. Fits every test except that at $310M Series B and ~$1.5B valuation it is a benchmark rather than an SPC entry point; nothing is yet producing at target scale.

- Output targets: 50,000 t/yr refined copper (Copper One, ramping); GWh-scale lithium (Lithium One, H1 2027) [src-20260803-30e6d5].
- Price context at raise: copper >$14,000/t, lithium carbonate ~$20,000/t; at target volume Copper One alone would be ~$700M/yr gross revenue at those prices (our arithmetic) [src-20260805-6f5d90].

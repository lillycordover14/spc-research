## What they do
Nox Metals is a Detroit (YC S25) metals service center rebuilt as an owner-operated automated factory: it buys large aluminum blocks from a foundry and cuts plate and block to customer size on an automated cutting line, then packages and ships; it does not make metal [src-20251110-b1acae] [src-20261022-2aabbe]. The in-house software turns a quote into a job on the line automatically, with real-time tracking from order to delivery; the pitch is quotes in minutes instead of the 3-5 days brokers take, delivery in days, and fewer markups between mill and shop [src-20250813-5ee11b] [src-20250815-dc1c83]. Customers are job shops, tool-and-die and complex machining shops, OEMs, Tier 1-3 suppliers and aerospace/defense/industrial manufacturers; the founder says its metal has gone to space, defense, autos and medical imaging [src-20251110-b1acae] [src-20261022-2aabbe] [src-20260616-1cf7cb]. Aluminum plate and block are live; steel, tube and sheet are planned [src-20261022-2aabbe]. The $11.5M seed (Jun 2026, Hyperion lead) funds a second WW2-era 30,000 sq ft Detroit building for the technology team and 'tripling down on technology' [src-20260616-1cf7cb].

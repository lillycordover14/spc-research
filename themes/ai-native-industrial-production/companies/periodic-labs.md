## What they do
Periodic builds AI scientists and the autonomous labs they operate in: robotic arms do powder synthesis, ML models run fast simulations, LLMs reason over results, and every experiment (including failures) becomes training data [src-20251022-8eb7c0] [src-20261022-682e62]. The stated long-term target is superconductors that work at higher temperatures, with Moore's Law, fusion and space as the downstream pitch [src-20261022-682e62]. The commercial motion today is deploying custom AI agents into industrial R&D teams, e.g. a semiconductor manufacturer's chip heat-dissipation problem, and the company already has paying semiconductor customers [src-20261022-682e62] [src-20260326-79447c]. Founded May 2025 by Liam Fedus (ex-OpenAI VP Research) and Ekin Dogus Cubuk (ex-DeepMind, GNoME) [src-20260326-79447c].

## Why it fits the theme (or doesn't)
- owns-production: yes for experiments, no for product. It owns and is scaling its own labs ('Autonomous labs are central to our strategy'; funds go to 'scaling labs') [src-20261022-682e62], but what comes out is data, IP and agents, not a material sold by the ton or a part.
- in-house-software: yes. Models, agents and lab orchestration are the company; team co-created ChatGPT, GNoME, MatterGen, Operator [src-20261022-682e62].
- data-loop: yes, and it is the whole thesis: each lab experiment yields gigabytes of high signal-to-noise data and negative results nobody publishes [src-20261022-682e62] [src-20251022-8eb7c0].
- mission-critical-output: not yet. Superconductors are a decade-scale bet; near-term revenue is R&D tooling for semis [src-20260514-5796ab].
- Net: fits the 'own the loop' logic as a benchmark for where frontier-AI money thinks the physical-data moat is. Not an owner-operator of production and priced as a frontier lab ($1.3B to $7.5B in 8 months) [src-20260514-5796ab]. Track, don't model.

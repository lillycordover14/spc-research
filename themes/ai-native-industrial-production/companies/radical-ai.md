## What they do
Radical AI runs its own self-driving materials lab in midtown Manhattan (built 2025, moving to a larger Brooklyn Navy Yard space): a robotic arm weighs and mixes element pellets, furnaces melt them into alloys, instruments characterize composition and structure and test hardness, oxidation and heat resistance, and an overhead track moves samples between stations [src-20260528-84fc46]. The AI scientist references 380,000 papers and 57M in-house data points including failures, runs quantum chemistry, and runs an active-learning loop overnight; throughput is up to 50 experiments a day with a target of 100 by end of summer 2026, versus ~50 a year for a human [src-20260528-84fc46]. The first lane is high-temperature alloys for jet engines, defense and fusion; an independent Purdue torch test showed 125x environmental survivability versus C103, the incumbent niobium aerospace alloy [src-20260528-84fc46] [src-20261022-e2876c]. It has produced alloys for customers it will not name [src-20260710-366df7]. Founded 2024 inside AlleyCorp; $10M pre-seed (Mar 2024) and $55M seed led by RTX Ventures (Jul 2025) [src-20250722-6b94a0].

## What they do
SendCutSend (Reno, founded 2018) is an instant-quote online metal parts service that owns and runs three factories in Reno NV, Paris KY and Arlington TX: laser cutting, CNC machining, bending and forming, welding, hardware insertion, tapping, tumbling, anodizing, plating and powder coat [src-20260519-38c7ed][src-20260227-e92d45]. Customers upload a file, get a price and buy; parts ship in as little as two days, with about 48 hours design to delivery and a fastest job of 32 minutes [src-20260519-38c7ed][src-20260227-e92d45]. It has shipped 30M+ parts to 300,000+ customers, from garage builders to 59% of the Fortune 500 and nine of ten private rocket launch companies, with no minimums and orders from one to tens of thousands of parts [src-20260519-38c7ed][src-20260227-e92d45]. Belosic's original software for small shops became the blueprint for the factories; the company says it treats manufacturing as a software problem [src-20260227-e92d45][src-20260519-38c7ed].

## Why it fits the theme (or doesn't)
- owns-production: three plants running 24/7 with a backup for every machine; a fourth site being scouted; >$250M earmarked for expansion [src-20260227-e92d45][src-20260519-38c7ed].
- in-house-software: instant quote and buy-now flow plus the factory software descended from Belosic's shop tool; modules (nesting, scheduling) not itemized publicly [src-20260519-38c7ed][src-20260227-e92d45].
- data-loop: 8M pieces cut last year and hundreds of parts nested per sheet give the volume for quoting and nesting to improve; the mechanism is implied, not documented [src-20260227-e92d45].
- speed-moat: 48 hours vs two to three weeks from China; Paradigm calls it the fastest manufacturing platform in the country [src-20260227-e92d45][src-20260519-38c7ed].
- unit economics: profitable for eight years and bootstrapped to a $1.01B valuation, the strongest public proof that a software-native owner-operator job shop works without defense budgets [src-20260519-38c7ed].
- Not a fit on mission-critical-output or customer-qualification: long-tail commercial demand, no certifications cited.
Belosic argues "software first" is a VC trap, which is the counterpoint to the software-first model in our taxonomy [src-20260624-3dc6f7].

## Economics (what we know)

- Revenue: ~$140M run rate (May 2026, low confidence) [src-20260504-c3ded5]; profitable for eight years [src-20260519-38c7ed]. Implies ~7x run-rate revenue at $1.01B vs Xometry ~2x and Proto Labs ~2.5x (prior note, unsourced).
- Volume: 8M pieces in 2025, 30M+ lifetime, 300,000+ customers; high-mix low-volume [src-20260227-e92d45][src-20260519-38c7ed].
- Capex and financing: first laser ($750k) financed in 2018; growth self-funded since; $110M equity now funds >$250M of facilities over five years, so the balance comes from cash flow or debt not yet disclosed [src-20260227-e92d45][src-20260519-38c7ed][src-20260624-3dc6f7].

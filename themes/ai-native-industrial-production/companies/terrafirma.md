## What they do
TerraFirma is a contractor, not an equipment vendor. It converts off-the-shelf excavators, dozers, loaders, rollers and skid steers so no operator sits in the cab, runs fleets from a remote command-and-control center, plans jobs with its own AI pre-construction software, and performs site prep, excavation and grading itself on commercial and government contracts [src-20260714-976329]. The approach is teleoperation with autonomy as one component, not full autonomy; the claim is each operator is up to 300% more effective [src-20260714-976329] [src-20260721-aca75d]. Completed Texas work includes a Starbucks site in North Austin, a Spicewood sports arena and a New Braunfels substation, plus unnamed US government infrastructure and logistics projects abroad [src-20260721-aca75d]. Founded 2024 in Austin by two ex-SpaceX engineers; $115M raised including a $100M Series A led by Kleiner Perkins (Jul 2026) [src-20260714-976329].

## Economics (what we know)
- Business model: sells finished earthwork under commercial and government contracts; retrofits are internal capex, not a product [src-20260714-976329].
- Labor lever: 1 remote operator runs multiple machines, claimed up to 300% more effective [src-20260714-976329]. Earthwork is bid per yard/hour against union labor and rented iron; TerraFirma's margin comes from operator leverage and utilization.
- Capital: $115M equity; fleet financing (lease vs own, how many machines) not disclosed in any source.
- Pricing, revenue, backlog, fleet size, hours operated: not disclosed [src-20260721-aca75d].

## (a) Must-listen podcasts, ranked

1. **Relentless: How to Dominate Manufacturing in the US | Chris Power, Hadrian**: [src-20260921-876080](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hmOLr_h7zTk). Power lays out the Hadrian playbook: six factories since 2022, 80% of capex reusable across programs.
2. **The Peel with Turner Novak: How to Build a Manufacturing Company in America | Jim Belosic, SendCutSend**: [src-20260430-9318cd](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zajo84R1ckI). Belosic on building SendCutSend. Close to 2 hours of unit economics, automation and scaling.
3. **CNBC (Kate Rooney long-form): Arda launch interview with Bob McGrew & Apoorv Agrawal**: [src-20261020-878aa9](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/10/16/former-top-openai-employees-raises-capital-for-defense-startup-valued-at-1point5b.html). Arda launch interview with McGrew and Agrawal: factory-video AI, 3-6 month automation re-tool, Army drone demand.
4. **Latent Space: AI Scientists Are Here: Autonomous Labs & Synthesis Superintelligence — Periodic Labs**: [src-20261008-83dc1b](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YHiqVRxGViM). Periodic Labs on autonomous labs and synthesis. The deepest materials-R&D episode in the repo.
5. **Sourcery: Senra Systems' $65M Series B: Fixing Aerospace & Defense's Biggest Bottleneck (Jordan Black)**: [src-20260715-821445](https://podbay.fm/p/sourcery/e/1784111400). Jordan Black on the Senra Series B: harnesses up to 25% of vehicle cost, training cut from 2 years to 4 weeks.
6. **Benchmark Mineral Intelligence: Mariana Minerals' Turner Caldwell on software and autonomy**: [src-20260623-6dc76b](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OvJX0j5OPXs). Turner Caldwell on MarianaOS: software and autonomy in mining and refining.
7. **Humans Who Build : Edward Mehr, CEO, Machina Labs, on Scaling Advanced Manufacturing Factories, Working w/ the DOW.**: [src-20260430-30ea09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FdunX2SCyUI). Ed Mehr on scaling Machina factories and working with defense.
8. **This Week in Startups: From hypercars to cruise missiles: Lukas Czinger on the future of US defense | E2292**: [src-20260523-b05ded](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U5kZI86lIjk). Lukas Czinger on Divergent moving from hypercars to cruise missiles (DAPS).
9. **First Builders: Reindustrializing America: Zane Hengsperger on building Nox Metals**: [src-20260427-83b102](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/reindustrializing-america-zane-hengsperger-on-building/id1818466924?i=1000763907900). Zane Hengsperger on Nox: team of 13, ~$4.6M raised, owner-operator metals service center.
10. **Machine Shop Mastery: The World's Fastest Growing Machine Shop Franchise: Isembard's Explosive Growth | Ep. 133**: [src-20260909-b547c7](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yRzPPMGJB6A). Isembard franchise machine-shop model, explained to an audience of shop owners.
11. **Core Memory : The Crisis Facing American Manufacturing**: [src-20260926-70087f](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NpvYyx2OUTc). Core Memory cross-company episode on the crisis in American manufacturing (Hadrian, Machina).
12. **EO: Joseph Krause, Radical AI**: [src-20251121-0117c9](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zwXz30EYyfQ). Joseph Krause on Radical AI self-driving lab.

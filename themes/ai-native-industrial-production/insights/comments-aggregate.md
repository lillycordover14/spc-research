## (c) Main pushback and skepticism

1. **"Shipping atoms" vs. claiming AI: few have real product or validation.**
   - Lee Cronin: "How many of they are actually shipping atoms? … I know of only one or two on this list that is actually doing this TODAY…" [src-20260814-b77f2d]
   - Jatin Kumar: "The big question: has anyone actually shipped a product that has commercialised?" [src-20260814-b77f2d]
   - Sergei Kalinin: "Very low balled in terms of investment, and too optimistic in terms of results" [src-20260814-b77f2d]
   - Guillaume Blondin-Walter (map author): "the technology is real, but industrialisation remains the bottleneck." [src-20261022-e32efa]
2. **Validation and scale-up break the model.**
   - Anton Gavrilov: "Scaling up the production of materials makes things even more …" [src-20260814-b77f2d]
   - Sean Grobler (FMCG ops exec): "Most will stall at the synthesis stage because physical production constraints break their neat simulations." [src-20260814-b77f2d]
   - Linden Schrecker: "Can't see a category for process development and scale-up though... Still a big blocker" [src-20261022-83d2a8]
   - Richard Holliday: "if you can't usefully make …" [src-20261022-e32efa]
3. **Margins and moat: wet labs look like instrument businesses, and models are commoditised.**
   - Dmitry Starodubtsev: "whoever industrialises the wet lab still makes money like an instrument company, and that's historically thin margins. So the robots could win the science and still lose the economics.. unless they own the molecules …" [src-20260814-b77f2d]
   - Peter Morgan: "These are all wrappers. … there is no moat (except for data)." [src-20260814-b77f2d]
   - altrove: owning the lab "costs Capex + need to hire a specialized team … you need to be able to capture a lot of the value down the line" [src-20261022-21c27f]
   - Chris Vitale: "venture isn't ready for capex intensive startups." [src-20261022-9367ca]
4. **"Glorified assembly house" / still bound by machinists.**
   - MattD on Amca: "They must have an endless supply of machinists to build all of their parts.. or this is a glorified assembly house" [src-20261008-9941fd]
   - Edward Mehr (Machina CEO): "You can't 'reindustrialize' America with SpaceX cosplay and an AI bumper sticker." [src-20261021-da5fb5]
   - Peter Morgan's "wrappers" line above [src-20260814-b77f2d]
5. **Vertical integration vs. a distributed network.**
   - Christian Mochen: "all of the neo-prime OEM's popping into the space touting 'vertical integration' is not going to be the answer, it's going to be a distributed network of capability propelled by software integrated operations." [src-20261022-9367ca]
   - Hanyin CHEN: "There won't be an American Shenzhen." [src-20261022-9367ca]
6. **Execution at scale: ops, integration and changeover.**
   - Chanmeet S. Narang: "New sites, acquisitions, and 450 hires in the same year is where ops quietly breaks." [src-20261020-1d0bbb]
   - LFGCAPO: "The proof will be throughput and on-time delivery, not footprint alone." [src-20261020-1d0bbb]
   - Juan Erdmann: "that's usually where changeover eats the gain" [src-20261020-1d0bbb]
   - Patricio: "output goes way down as engineering headcount goes up" [src-20260904-1929b3]
7. **Labor (the dominant theme on X by volume).** About 70 of the ~78 captured Bender CCP replies attack $35–45/hr as too low.

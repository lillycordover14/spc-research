# Failures and pivots: does the edge compound? (as of 2026-10-23)

Thesis question 2, the downside case. **A** = owner-operator, **B** = platform or equipment seller.

## Lessons for diligence
1. **Survivors found a repeat buyer base (defense, primes) before scaling capex**: Velo3D, Divergent. Failures scaled capex or valuation on prototype demand: Fast Radius, Desktop Metal, Markforged.
2. **Selling equipment does not compound; running the plant can.** The B-type equipment sellers failed. The open question is whether software-only B (Arda) behaves like SaaS or like Bright Machines.
3. **Watch for open-ended Form D raises** (Machina's round open 16 months; Bright Machines sold half its 2022 offering). These signal how hard the capital is to raise.
4. **Leadership change at the pilot-to-volume stage is common** (VulcanForms, Bright Machines). Ask experts who ran that transition.

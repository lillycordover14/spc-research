# Learning path: AI-native industrial production

Reading path for getting up to speed fast, Day 1 basics through Day 4 expert. Every item is filed in this repo. Each line gives what you'll understand afterwards and how it ties to AI-native factories. Read times are estimated from word counts. Items marked ★ carry the `must-read` tag. ## 5. Glossary

Each definition is written from the filed source in brackets.
- **FAI / AS9102:** First Article Inspection: a full inspection of a sample from the first production run, documented on AS9102 Forms 1-3 against a ballooned drawing. It proves the process makes conforming parts. [src-20190801-e81d7b]
- **LRIP:** Low Rate Initial Production: the first production effort after Milestone C. It builds production-representative articles for operational test and a base to ramp to full-rate production (FRP). [src-20250101-854f03]
- **OT / OTA:** Other Transaction (Authority): DoD agreements exempt from most procurement rules, used for research, prototypes (up to $500M) and follow-on production (up to $100M) under 10 USC 4021-4022. [src-20241219-9f8fcc]
- **CSO:** Commercial Solutions Opening: a competitive DIU/DoD process (area of interest, solution brief, pitch, proposal) that awards prototype OTs and can lead to production without new competition. [src-20260101-0b051b]
- **DPAS:** Defense Priorities and Allocations System, under DPA Title I. Rated orders (DO, and the higher DX) must be accepted and scheduled ahead of unrated work and passed down to suppliers. [src-20250708-c343c0]
- **DPA Title III:** Defense Production Act authority to expand domestic capacity through purchases, purchase commitments, loans and equipment installation. [src-20251215-98f650]
- **PPBE / POM:** Planning, Programming, Budgeting and Execution: DoD's annual resource process. The Program Objective Memorandum (POM) is the five-year funding plan built in the programming phase. [src-20241206-6e78bd]
- **SBIR Phases:** Phase I tests feasibility, Phase II funds prototype R&D, and Phase III is commercialization with no SBIR money but sole-source eligibility. [src-20200505-dd1158]
- **OSC:** DoD Office of Strategic Capital: loans, loan guarantees and fund lending for critical-technology companies. [src-20260908-f6558e]
- **Section 232:** Trade Expansion Act authority for national-security import restrictions, used for steel, aluminum and derivatives. [src-20260902-035e2c]
- **45X:** IRA production tax credit for US-made clean-energy components and critical minerals. [src-20261020-7d90fa]
- **Shop rate:** A work center's annual machine, labor and overhead cost divided by productive hours. It is the hourly price floor in a job-shop quote. [src-20260528-1bbd96]
- **Setup vs run time:** Setup is the non-cutting time to fixture, load tools and prove out a job. Run time is spindle time. Before machining centers, a typical machine tool was operational only 25-30% of the time. [src-20240118-4e5ac1]
- **Self-driving lab (SDL):** A closed loop in which AI proposes experiments, robots run them, measurements feed back, and the AI picks the next experiment. [src-20240813-edaf73]
- **Offtake:** Long-term offtake contracts are binding agreements to pre-purchase product. Without them, even efficient midstream refining facilities can falter. [src-20250529-3a588b]

Gaps: no filed source defines AS9100, so it is left out. I found no free primer for PPAP, OEE or MES/QMS that ties directly to AI-native production, so these terms are left out rather than defined generically. No free Wohlers summary was filed. DPA Title III and DLA castings and forgings pages blocked fetch. The CRS DPA Title III detail is in R43118 (2013) and was not filed.

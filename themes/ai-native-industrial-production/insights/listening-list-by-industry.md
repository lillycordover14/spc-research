# Vertically Integrated Industries: Listening List by Industry

*Compiled Oct 27, 2026, to go with primer v9.1. I opened every link on Oct 27, 2026, and each one loaded with a matching title. Lengths come from YouTube or the podcast host where they were listed. "Repo" gives the source ID; **(transcript)** means the full transcript is in `raw/transcripts/`, and **(notes)** means only a summary or skim is in the repo. Company numbers are the companies' own claims unless marked otherwise.*

**Start here, if you only have 3 hours:** Relentless with Chris Power (Parts) → a16z *Critical Minerals* (Minerals) → Sourcery with Vulcan (Metals) → YC on Solugen (Chemicals) → Latent Space with Radical AI (Materials).

## 0. Cross-cutting macro: reshoring, industrial policy, the defense base and China

1. **"China Has Mass. Can America Catch Up?"**, a16z Podcast, Sep 29, 2025. https://a16z.com/podcast/china-has-mass-can-america-catch-up/
   *Why listen:* Anduril's CEO and Hadrian's CEO explain the munitions and capacity gap in plain terms. *Takeaway:* war games run out of munitions in about a week and take 2–3 years to restock. The skilled-machinist workforce is "basically 62" years old, so automation is the only replacement. Repo: src-20250929-452dcb (transcript).
2. **"How LA Is Becoming America's New Industrial Tech Hub"**, Bloomberg *Odd Lots* (live), Sep 21, 2026, 34 min. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mgCQ0Pl754c
   *Why listen:* this is the best mainstream-macro framing of how the US lost its manufacturing edge. *Takeaway:* the bottleneck is software and closed-loop feedback on the factory floor, not robots. Repo: src-20260921-a51ab2 (notes).
3. **"Can the U.S. Still Build Things? Fixing the Industrial Base" (#34)**, *The Drone Ultimatum*, with Miles Arnone of Re:Build, about 95 min (date not given on the page). https://www.thedroneultimatum.com/can-the-us-still-build-things-fixing-the-industrial-base-miles-arnone-podcast-34/
   *Why listen:* an operator who runs a roll-up of about 13 US factories talks about the economics of buying and modernizing old shops. *Takeaway:* with factory construction down, it often makes more sense to buy and upgrade existing shops than to build new megafactories. Repo: src-20261022-f423ef (transcript).

## 1. Minerals: mining and refining

1. **"Critical Minerals: Mining for the Industrial Future"**, a16z Podcast, Jul 23, 2025. https://a16z.com/podcast/critical-minerals-mining-for-the-industrial-future/
   *Why listen:* Turner Caldwell lays out Mariana's model, which is to develop and run its own mine and refinery projects with software doing the work. *Takeaway:* the edge is a "capital project OS" that cuts the time and cost of engineering and building a mine, not a better rock. Repo: src-20250723-3f3ef8 (transcript).
2. **"Energy, Minerals, and the Physical Stack Behind AI"**, a16z Podcast, May 13, 2026. https://a16z.com/podcast/energy-minerals-and-the-physical-stack-behind-ai/
   *Why listen:* Caldwell and Drew Baglino of Heron Power (ex-Tesla) tie AI's power demand to minerals and refining. *Takeaway:* Mariana's Plant OS and Mine OS use reinforcement learning to run its own Utah copper asset and a refinery it's building. That's the data loop running inside assets it owns. Repo: src-20260513-bdc044 (transcript).
3. **"Advancing US critical minerals with software and autonomy: Mariana Minerals' Turner Caldwell"**, Benchmark Mineral Intelligence (YouTube), Jun 23, 2026, 23 min. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OvJX0j5OPXs
   *Why listen:* this is how the industry's own analysts frame refining. *Takeaway:* Mariana builds physics-based models of chemical plants, adds lab and pilot data, and uses reinforcement learning to take humans out of the control loop. Repo: src-20260623-6dc76b (notes).
4. **"Critical Minerals: What Makes Mining Tech VC-Backable"**, *Deep Tech Catalyst* (The Scenarionist), with Jun Ku of Main Sequence (date not given on the page). https://www.thescenarionist.com/p/critical-minerals-at-venture-scale-deeptech
   *Why listen:* an investor explains how venture money can work in a heavy-capex industry. *Takeaway:* government money that doesn't take equity (DOE, ARENA) lowers how much capital an investor has to put in, and mid-size miners are now willing to adopt new technology. Repo: src-20261022-cd6c5c (transcript).
5. *(Quick watch)* **"The Future is Metal – Mariana Minerals"**, a16z American Dynamism (YouTube), Aug 5, 2026, short film. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uFt-tkagUUI. *Takeaway:* over 90% of critical-mineral refining is in China. Repo: src-20260805-b7e3c7 (notes).

## 2. Metals: metal, alloys and magnets

1. **"How The Rarest Company On Earth Raised $75M"**, *Sourcery*, Aug 11, 2025. https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/how-the-rarest-company-on-earth-raised-%2475m/id1738124704?i=1000721503376
   *Why listen:* this is the best episode on Vulcan Elements' early demand and repeat-order signals. *Takeaway:* the pilot plant opened Mar 31, 2025, and was shipping and qualifying magnets with customers within four months, with DoD contracts across the branches (company claim). Repo: src-20250811-e7cb9a (transcript).
2. **"'Supply Chains Win Wars': Rare Earth Magnets with John Maslin"**, *Building the Base*, Apr 29, 2025. https://buildingthebase.org/episode/supply-chains-win-wars-rare-earth-magnets-with-john-maslin-ceo-vulcan-elements
   *Why listen:* it gives the macro case for magnets in one sitting. *Takeaway:* China mines about 55% of rare earths but makes over 90% of the magnets, while the US makes under 1%. The gap is in processing and turning oxide into metal, not in ore. Repo: src-20250429-6f9155 (transcript).
3. **"Reindustrializing America: Building NOX Metals & The Future of Manufacturing"** (#430), *Powers Pod*, with Zane Hengsperger. Uploaded to YouTube Sep 9, 2026, per the repo notes. https://www.powerspod.com/reindustrializing-america-building-nox-metals-the-future-of-manufacturing-with-zane-hengsperger/
   *Why listen:* this is the metal-distribution side, where Nox wants to be the AI-run metal service center. *Takeaway:* it competes against old-line distributors on speed and software for quoting and cutting. Repo: src-20261022-3135b5 (notes).
4. **"Reindustrializing America: Zane Hengsperger on building Nox"**, *First Builders*, Apr 27, 2026. https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/reindustrializing-america-zane-hengsperger-on-building/id1818466924?i=1000763907900
   *Why listen:* the earlier and more detailed founder story. *Takeaway:* Nox is lean, at about $4.6M raised (company figure), and it's betting that software beats incumbent scale. Repo: src-20260427-83b102 (notes).

## 3. Parts Manufacturing: fabrication and precision parts

1. **"How to Dominate Manufacturing in the US | Chris Power, Hadrian"**, *Relentless* (YouTube), Sep 21, 2026. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hmOLr_h7zTk
   *Why listen:* this is the clearest unit-economics argument for vertically integrated factories. *Takeaway:* about 80% of factory capex can be shared across programs, so a company that spends ahead of contracts can serve several demand signals. Without about $1B of capex up front, Power says, you either die or sell for about $100M. Repo: src-20260921-876080 (notes).
2. **"How to Build a Manufacturing Company in America | Jim Belosic, SendCutSend"**, *The Peel* with Turner Novak, Apr 30, 2026, 1 h 32 min. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zajo84R1ckI
   *Why listen:* this is the best case study on demand and repeat orders, from a fabricator that bootstrapped. *Takeaway:* SendCutSend reached about a $140M revenue run rate in eight years without venture money, and says it serves about 60% of the Fortune 500 (company figures). Repo: src-20260430-9318cd (notes).
3. **"The Crisis Facing American Manufacturing"**, *Core Memory* (Ashlee Vance), Sep 26, 2026, 25 min. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NpvYyx2OUTc
   *Why listen:* a short documentary that takes you inside Hadrian and Machina Labs. *Takeaway:* Hadrian's software trains operators in 30–40 days instead of a decade, and its automation cuts new tasks from 100 hours to 20–30. Repo: src-20260926-70087f (notes).
4. **"What Everyone is Missing About Reindustrialization"**, Machina Labs (YouTube), Oct 19, 2026 (YouTube upload date, Pacific), 55 min, with Ed Mehr of Machina and Erik Palitsch of Freeform. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EiMhaHThSXk
   *Why listen:* two founders argue from first principles about why the money is in the finished part. *Takeaway:* raw steel worth $57 becomes a $2,000 machined part, and new plants can fit 10x the output into one-eighth of the floor space. Repo: src-20261020-bac9ca (notes).
5. *(Read or listen)* **TBPN transcript: "Aaron Slodov's Atomic Industries raises $25M Series A"**, Sep 19, 2025. https://www.tbpndigest.com/story/2025-09-19/aaron-slodovs-atomic-industries-raises-25m-series-a-to-automate-injection-mold-manufacturing-in-detroit/transcript
   *Takeaway:* Atomic moved from software to molds to parts, and its first multi-year part contracts are in the "mid eight figure range" (company claim). Repo: src-20250919-2805c7 (transcript page).

## 4. Chemicals

1. **"Inside The Startup Reinventing The $6 Trillion Chemical Manufacturing Industry"**, YC Startup Podcast, Mar 2026, about 13 min. https://www.ycombinator.com/library/NO-inside-the-startup-reinventing-america-s-trillion-dollar-chemical-industry
   *Why listen:* the best short introduction to a fully integrated chemical producer. *Takeaway:* Solugen pairs an enzyme with a metal catalyst to turn corn syrup into chemicals, and claims about 96% yield at scale versus about 60% for the traditional process. It builds small plants near its customers. Repo: src-20260320-f90e80 (notes; transcript on podscripts).
2. **"EP 312 Lee Cronin on Automating Chemistry"**, *The Jim Rutt Show*, Jul 2025. https://jimrutt.substack.com/p/ep-312-lee-cronin-on-automating-chemistry-f9f
   *Why listen:* this is where Chemify's "code to molecule" idea meets its business model. *Takeaway:* Chemify charges for molecules and software access and co-owns the IP. It has 12 customers and is aiming for 20 big ones (company claim). Repo: src-20250724-0f42f3 (notes; free transcript on the page).
3. **"Why Twelve believes power-to-liquids will revolutionise sustainable aviation fuel production"**, *Sustainability in the Air* (SimpliFlying), Jul 24, 2025, 34 min. https://podcasts.apple.com/bm/podcast/why-twelve-believes-power-to-liquids-will-revolutionise/id1620281360?i=1000718768981
   *Why listen:* co-founder Etosha Cave talks about Twelve's own e-fuels plant and its buyers. *Takeaway:* Twelve picked Moses Lake, WA, for hydropower and state incentives, and it sells to Alaska, IAG and Microsoft under long-term contracts. Policy risk is the swing factor. **Not in the repo (new).**
4. **"Mission-driven doctor, inventor & entrepreneur" (Ep. 175)**, *Molecule to Market*, with Phlow CEO Eric Edwards, 55 min (date not given on the page). https://moleculetomarketpod.com/episodes/mission-driven-doctor-inventor-entrepreneur/
   *Why listen:* Phlow is the US drug-ingredient onshoring story, told by an operator. *Takeaway:* the US government's $899M contract to rebuild domestic production of essential drug ingredients shows that the government is the anchor buyer in pharma chemicals. **Not in the repo (new).**

## 5. Materials, including the AI discovery labs

1. **"The Self-Driving Lab — Joseph Krause, Radical AI"**, *Latent Space*, Jun 17, 2026. https://www.latent.space/p/radical-ai
   *Why listen:* the most detailed source on Radical, and the clearest discussion of where a lab's moat comes from. *Takeaway:* the moat is experiments, not models. Customers asked for 300 lb of material, against samples of grams to 500 g, and that pushed Radical to make alloys itself. Repo: src-20260617-f9d293 (transcript).
2. **"AI for Atoms: How Periodic Labs is Revolutionizing Materials Engineering with Co-Founder Liam Fedus"**, *No Priors*, Apr 3, 2026, 29 min. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oru2Jxr1xHU
   *Why listen:* the economics of an AI lab that runs its own physical experiments. *Takeaway:* lab hardware is sometimes cheaper than compute but has long lead times. The value is in generating high-quality, varied data. Repo: src-20260403-40fa0e (notes).
3. **"Meet the Ex-OpenAI & DeepMind Leads Creating an AI Scientist"**, *The Generalist*, Sep 29, 2026, 60 min. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJVZgeXKlVo
   *Why listen:* a longer look at Periodic's plan and its superconductor focus. *Takeaway:* it runs thousands of experiments a day, and impurities in the starting materials are the real source of noise. That's the scale-up and quality problem in miniature. Repo: src-20260929-ca6d19 (notes).
4. **"Max Welling, CTO and Co-Founder, CuspAI — Nebius Conversations"**, Nebius (YouTube), May 4, 2026, 19 min. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=40CU4rbO7E4
   *Why listen:* the software-only discovery model, for contrast. *Takeaway:* CuspAI generates up to a million candidate structures and narrows them with increasingly expensive simulations. It's powerful, but it doesn't make anything, which is why it's labeled an enabler. Repo: src-20260504-36655f (notes).

Scope: Lilly's category, companies that run their own plants on in-house software and robotics. **A** = owner-operator (sells parts or material). **B** = software-first platform running in plants owned by others.

Method: EDGAR full-text search (`efts.sec.gov`, forms D and D/A), then parsing each filing's primary_doc.xml. A recent filing with unsold remainder means the offering is likely still open. A new SPV with $0 sold means a round is being assembled. Every filing is filed as a source (tag `form-d`).

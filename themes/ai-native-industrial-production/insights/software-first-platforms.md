## Synthesis
- **Where Arda sits:** the manufacturing-engineering layer (design → line), not monitoring. It is the best funded at Series A ($1.5B) and the only one with a named defense prime deployment inside the prime's own OS [src-20261020-bbfb1c].
- **Closest comparables:** C-Infinity and Dirac (CAD → instructions) [src-20260101-be6d89][src-20250801-122e39]; Synera (agentic engineering) [src-20260101-328169]; Harmoni and Pelico (A&D orchestration) [src-20260901-8279e8][src-20260701-200036]; Almetra and Maneva (video → data → control) [src-20260625-940831][src-20260601-c74b47]; Bright Machines (Arda's robotics roadmap end state) [src-20261005-808d0e].
- **Right to win vs Siemens/Rockwell/NVIDIA:** incumbents own PLM/MES/PLC and are buying in (Mitsubishi→Tulip, NVentures→Vention/Instrumental, Teradyne→Bright Machines, Lockheed→CloudNC) [src-20260113-5220fe][src-20260128-1dca36][src-20260301-7b2450][src-20261005-808d0e][src-20260908-f98d21]. Startups win on speed of design change and frontier models; the risk is becoming a feature that strategic investors absorb.
- **Threats to Arda:** cheaper CAD-to-instruction tools at seed/A, and video platforms (Almetra) moving up to "control layer" and robotics [src-20260625-940831].
- **Series B–D shortlist for SPC:** Pelico, Squint, Voxel, CloudNC, Vention, Synera.

## Synthesis
- **Inclusion test (Lilly, 2026-10-23):** only AI that directly runs production on the plant floor (learns from factory video/machine data and coordinates machines and workers) qualifies. CAD-to-instructions, engineering/design agents, scheduling and supply-chain planning, MES/ERP, robot vendors, inspection point tools and copilots are out.
- **Closest to Arda after the cut:** Almetra and Maneva (video to production control), Harmoni (operator + machine orchestration), Invisible AI (vision on manual assembly lines), Flexxbotics (robot-to-machine coordination in cells). See the 2026-10-23 sweep below for new passes.

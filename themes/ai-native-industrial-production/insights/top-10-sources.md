# Top 10 sources to read or listen to (AI-native industrial production)

Picked 2026-10-26 from all reading notes and primer v5. These are listed in priority order and all have full text. Lengths are word counts of the stored transcript or text; for audio, roughly 150 words is a minute. Expert-call links open in AlphaSense (login required); the full text is also at the repo path shown.

1. **Chris Power (Hadrian), "How a 40-Year Manufacturing Gap Became a National Security Crisis"**: podcast, iConnections. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OkELMuRfC5k. About 5,800 words (about 40 min). `src-20260610-779f8b`. The best single macro case: the defense labor-hour shortfall, about 150 of 1,000 Navy-missile parts coming from China, 10% versus 65-80% machine utilization, and the claim of nearly $1B in revenue. These are company claims in an investor-hosted setting.
2. **Senior Mechanical Engineer II, Raytheon (Hadrian customer)**: expert call, 2026-08-23. AlphaSense link: https://research.alpha-sense.com/search?search_id=tmp-zrt1t2a4vvfkburx47q41n&sort=COMPANY_NAME&order=DESC&docid=EC-1000000-216616. About 9,700 words. `sources/expert-calls/src-20260823-b3713f.md`. This is the clearest repeat-order evidence. Hadrian delivers weekly, but spend is "in the tens of thousands" during probation. Listen for why buyers pick it (it is local, takes 3D model files, and shows low variation) and for testing as the real bottleneck.
3. **Sr. Engineer, Anduril (ex-SpaceX supply chain)**: expert call, 2025-06-23. AlphaSense link: https://research.alpha-sense.com/search?search_id=tmp-zrt1t2a4vvfkburx47q41n&sort=COMPANY_NAME&order=DESC&docid=EC-1000000-125612. About 8,000 words. `sources/expert-calls/src-20250623-5bc67c.md`. It explains how primes qualify a supplier (a 6-month order, then a 2-year order), why they want "four or five different vendors", the 25-30% share cap, and the price thresholds for switching. This is the best buyer-side bear case.
4. **Co-founder/CEO, FlyX (former Hadrian customer)**: expert call, 2026-08-06. AlphaSense link: https://research.alpha-sense.com/search?search_id=tmp-zrt1t2a4vvfkburx47q41n&sort=COMPANY_NAME&order=DESC&docid=EC-1000000-212939. About 8,800 words. `sources/expert-calls/src-20260806-3a2ec7.md`. A churn story: he left over cost as drone work became "less stringent" ("a Porsche ... going 20 mi an hour"). It also covers the 10-15% price ceiling and what AS9100 certification really costs.
5. **AFA "Building the Arsenal: Speed, Scale, and Affordability"**: conference panel (Divergent, GE, Lockheed, Raytheon voices). https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r7BNP3R_TWk. About 7,500 words (about 50 min). `src-20261014-47f64b`. This is how primes and the Air Force really buy. Funding stability is "the elephant in the room", and "we don't really want to buy factories. We want to buy weapons". Divergent claims about 30 programs with 15 primes.
6. **Core Memory: SendCutSend factory tour**: podcast/video. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G8oufrrXaig. About 8,400 words (about 55 min). `src-20260626-541ac8`. The best owner-operator operations benchmark: 80-90% laser utilization versus 50-60% at top shops, 50-60 setups per shift, and the data-loop claim that "the more jobs that we take in, the smarter we get". It also argues against heavy automation for high-mix work.
7. **Benchmark Mineral Intelligence: Turner Caldwell (Mariana Minerals)**: podcast. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OvJX0j5OPXs. About 4,800 words (about 30 min). `src-20260623-6dc76b`. The strongest data-loop claim in continuous processing: AI agents adjust refinery settings every 15 minutes with no human in the loop, and the company runs its own assets ("we don't sell SaaS"). Ramping copper capacity from 25,000 t to 50,000 t.
8. **Machina Labs: "What Everyone is Missing About Reindustrialization"**: founder talk (Mehr and Freeform's Palitsch). https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EiMhaHThSXk. About 11,200 words (about 75 min). `src-20261020-bac9ca`. The sharpest "AI-washing" test ("ask them exactly how"; "slap iPads on things"). It argues that a new manufacturing process beats software layered on old processes. Both speakers are interested parties.
9. **Defense Software & Data Summit: "Augmenting the Arsenal through Software Defined Manufacturing"**: panel (Kevin Czinger, Divergent, and New Industrial Corp). https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2gsnJShz1hQ. About 4,400 words (about 30 min). `src-20260325-7ba845`. A capital-structure explainer covering the "field of dreams dilemma" (the government won't sign until a factory exists), off-balance-sheet plants and dilution, plus Divergent's printer-output and pricing claims.
10. **DOE "U.S. Forging Industry" market research study (2022)**: government report (PDF). https://science.osti.gov/-/media/sbir/pdf/Market-Research/AMO---Forging-Industry-August-2022-Public.pdf. About 13,500 words. `src-20220801-c32e19`. A neutral bear case and macro source. Low-volume defense work "produces limited profits", and China can deliver a finished part for what a US forge pays for raw material. It also finds AI "not yet widespread" in forging.

NEW DISRUPTIVE MANUFACTURERS
• Anduril Industries develops sophisticated autonomous aircraft,
drones and precision-strike systems designed to be produced at
lower cost and in greater quantities. They use commercial
components, common tools and a simplified design to support
large-scale production.
• Saronic Technologies designs and manufactures autonomous
ships. Its first Marauder hull moved from initial design to on-water
trials in under one year, a timeline virtually unheard of in modern
American shipbuilding. Saronic uses digital models and
engineering with manufacturing planned from the beginning.
• Hadrian is building highly automated factories for precision
defense and aerospace components. Its software coordinates
machine tools, robotics, production scheduling and inspection to
move parts from digital designs into production faster.
DISRUPTIVE ENABLERS
• Machina Labs uses AI and robotics to form complex metal
structures directly from digital designs without traditional dies.
• Path Robotics applies physical AI to adaptive robotic welding.
• GrayMatter Robotics automates finishing, coating and inspection.
• Gecko Robotics combines robotic inspection with digital twins.
LEGACY MANUFACTURERS MODERNIZING
Boeing, RTX, GE Aerospace, HII, Lockheed Martin and Austal
USA are investing in digital factories, additive manufacturing,
robotics and shipyard modernization.
Some are also investing directly in the startups that could change
how they manufacture.
• RTX Ventures has backed more than 30 companies and supported
over 100 collaborations across RTX. Its portfolio includes Hadrian,
which is producing components for RTX businesses including
Raytheon and Collins Aerospace.
• Lockheed Martin Ventures has invested more than $500M in over
120 companies. Lockheed recently increased the fund’s capacity to
$1B. Its portfolio includes Machina Labs.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is optimistic about the continued need and aggressive development of advanced chip cooling solutions, particularly microfluidics and micro-channel technologies, over the next 3-5 years.

TAKEAWAYS

- Microfluidic and micro-channel cooling technologies are rapidly evolving and are being aggressively piloted by hyperscalers due to rising TDP in GPUs and AI accelerators, with traditional direct-to-chip cold plates becoming insufficient.
- The market for advanced cooling solutions is currently led by a handful of specialized players (e.g., Corintis, Fabric8Labs, Mikros), with close one-on-one partnerships between vendors and hyperscalers; broader market entry is expected over the next 5-7 years.
- Vendor qualification at hyperscalers involves a multi-stage process: initial sample evaluation, custom engineering, prototype validation, and limited deployment, typically spanning 6-9 months before large-scale orders are considered.
- Integrators (e.g., Delta, Supermicro) are brought in after initial validation to support broader deployment, but procurement decisions and vendor selection remain tightly controlled by the hyperscaler.
- Corintis is seen as strong in metallurgical R&D and data science, while Fabric8Labs is noted for rapid prototyping and simulation capabilities; both approaches are valued, and performance is prioritized over cost in early-stage evaluations.

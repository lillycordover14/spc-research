Summary

The interview covers the evaluation, implementation, and impact of a retail analytics and AI platform within a large CPG organization, including its use cases, ROI, competitive landscape, and future adoption plans.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Senior Director with responsibility for data analytics and AI at a major CPG company, overseeing the deployment and evaluation of retail analytics platforms for business users, with prior experience implementing similar solutions at other large CPG organizations.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Views the retail analytics platform as a critical and increasingly integral part of the team's workflow, enabling significant efficiency gains and better decision-making.
- Believes the platform's value and capabilities will drive increased adoption and spend within the organization, with plans to expand usage across more teams and categories.
- Expresses high satisfaction with the vendor's service, flexibility, and domain expertise, and is optimistic about the platform's future evolution and continued relevance.

TAKEAWAYS

- The platform is currently in pilot with about 10-15% of the category management team, delivering strong value in availability, exception management, and digital shelf insights.
- Prior to adoption, the team relied on manual, Excel-based processes and outdated syndicated data, resulting in limited visibility and slow decision-making.
- Implementation was rapid and straightforward, requiring minimal change management due to the team's AI fluency; the platform's integration capabilities are robust and flexible.
- Key reasons for selecting this platform over competitors included nimbleness, CPG-specific domain expertise, modern architecture, and competitive, usage-based pricing.
- Switching costs are primarily related to change management and embedded workflows, with price sensitivity at a 15-20% annual increase threshold.

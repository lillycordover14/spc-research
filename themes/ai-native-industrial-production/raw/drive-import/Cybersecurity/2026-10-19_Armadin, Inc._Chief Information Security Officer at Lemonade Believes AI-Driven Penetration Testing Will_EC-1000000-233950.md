SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The interview covers the evolving landscape of AI-driven penetration testing, including the expert's experience with various vendors, the challenges and opportunities in adopting continuous testing, and the factors influencing budget allocation and vendor selection in the security space.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) at a technology-driven, AI-native insurance company, responsible for all aspects of cybersecurity, with direct experience evaluating and implementing AI-based penetration testing and security solutions.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Believes AI-driven pen testing has significant potential to add value if it can reduce vulnerability exposure time and integrate seamlessly into development workflows.
- Currently views AI pen testing as too expensive for continuous use, but expects costs to decrease and value to increase, making broader adoption likely in the near future.
- Prefers best-of-breed, focused security vendors over bundled solutions from large platforms, but is open to building internal solutions if they can be tailored and cost-effective.

TAKEAWAYS

- Traditional penetration testing is primarily a compliance-driven purchase, offering limited value beyond regulatory requirements.
- The expert has transitioned from using Terra to Tenzai Security for AI pen testing, and also uses Pi Security for code-based vulnerability analysis.
- Continuous, feature-level AI pen testing is seen as highly valuable but currently cost-prohibitive; price reductions and improved ROI could drive increased adoption and budget allocation.
- AI pen testing tools are expected to expand beyond code vulnerabilities to cover business logic, network, and agentic application risks, but are unlikely to reach the budget levels of core security platforms like Okta or CrowdStrike unless they evolve into broader security platforms.
- Integration, usability for developers, and cost optimization are key differentiators for AI pen testing vendors; poor UX and integration challenges led to the switch from Terra to Tenzai.

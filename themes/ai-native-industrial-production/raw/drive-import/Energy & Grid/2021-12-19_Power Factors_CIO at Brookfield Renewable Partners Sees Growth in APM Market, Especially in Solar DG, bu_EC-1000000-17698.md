Summary

The interview covers the role and value of asset performance management software in the renewable energy sector, with a focus on market dynamics, competitive landscape, and the positioning of Power Factors relative to other vendors. Discussion includes adoption trends, ROI, implementation experience, and future opportunities for third-party APM providers.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Chief Information Technology Officer at a major North American renewable energy operator with experience in operations (hydro and wind), IT, business transformation, technology strategy, and process improvement, with a focus on software and process strategies to improve P&L and reduce operating costs.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Positive long-term outlook for third-party asset performance management (APM) software providers, especially as the industry moves toward more distributed energy resources and increased digital transformation.
- Believes Power Factors is currently a market leader in North America and has a strong advantage due to recent acquisitions and product development, but notes that large OEMs could eventually catch up, particularly in the wind sector.
- Sees continued growth for APM adoption, particularly in solar and distributed generation, but notes that market penetration is still low and there are risks from large OEMs developing their own solutions.

TAKEAWAYS

- APM software is critical for self-performing asset owners to improve uptime and performance, but less so for those outsourcing O&M to OEMs.
- APM spend is typically around 15-20% of the software budget, with actual spend depending on fleet size and operational model.
- Power Factors is viewed as a market leader, especially in North America, with strong ROI (20-30% IRR) for self-performing operators and continued product investment and expansion into new asset classes.
- Implementation of Power Factors is relatively quick and cost-effective, but resource bottlenecks can occur due to the company's growth.
- Key areas for improvement include easier data extraction and integration with financial and contract management modules; satisfaction with Power Factors is rated 7-8 out of 10.

The conversation focused on the expert's use of Welligence as a data provider for energy demand and consumption analytics in the Asia-Pacific region, detailing the types of data received, its applications, and comparisons with other providers. The discussion also covered the integration of this data with internal analytics and other external sources to inform strategic decisions in energy transition and market participation.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Chief Data Officer with 25 years at a major global energy company, overseeing data strategy and analytics for projects and technology across downstream, upstream, integrated gas, and renewable/new energy sectors, with four years in the current CDO role.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is highly satisfied with the data quality and professionalism of Welligence, describing it as excellent and unique in its completeness and granularity.
- The expert anticipates continued and potentially expanded use of Welligence, especially in the Asia-Pacific region, due to its relevance for new energy markets and the energy transition.
- The expert expresses no significant areas for improvement with Welligence, noting that their internal preference is to handle analytics in-house rather than outsource to the provider.

TAKEAWAYS

- Welligence is used primarily as a raw data provider for energy demand and consumption patterns, especially in the Asia-Pacific region, supporting decisions on market entry, strategy, and energy transition.
- The data received is highly granular, including hourly consumption by energy type (oil, gas, electricity, hydrogen) and buyer profile, mapped to specific geographic coordinates or regions.
- The expert's organization processes the raw data internally using proprietary algorithms and integrates it with other sources such as social media and weather data for deeper customer insights.
- Welligence's data offering is considered unique due to its breadth and integration, which would be difficult to replicate internally; however, the service is viewed as premium-priced.
- For other regions like Europe and the Americas, the organization uses different providers (e.g., Wood Mackenzie), but finds the data and use cases not directly comparable to Welligence's APAC offering.

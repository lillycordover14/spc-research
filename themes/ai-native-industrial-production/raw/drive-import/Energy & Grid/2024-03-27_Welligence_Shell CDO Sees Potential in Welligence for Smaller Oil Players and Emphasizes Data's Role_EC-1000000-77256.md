Summary

The conversation covered the company's evolving use of external data providers for analytics in oil and gas and renewables, the procurement and evaluation process for these vendors, and the strategic considerations for vendor selection and future market needs. The discussion also addressed budget pressures, user communities, and the long-term outlook for data analytics in the energy sector.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- The expert is a computational scientist with a Master's in Theoretical Computer Science and a PhD in computation and linguistics, with 26 years of experience in data management roles across global operating units and joint ventures, and currently serves as Chief Data Officer focused on capital projects and engineering in the energy sector.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is optimistic about the increasing use of external data providers and off-the-shelf analytics to drive efficiency and cost savings, but acknowledges that budget constraints will require more portfolio rationalization and focus.
- The expert believes that oil and gas data analytics will remain highly relevant for the next 20-25 years, but stresses the importance for data providers to evolve toward renewables and real-time analytics to remain competitive in the long term.
- The expert values strategic partnerships with data vendors that are receptive to customer feedback and willing to co-develop new capabilities, especially as the market shifts toward energy transition.

TAKEAWAYS

- The company has shifted from an inward-looking, in-house data approach to leveraging external data providers like Enverus and Welligence, balancing buy-versus-build strategies for analytics and data ingestion.
- Enverus is the current standard provider, offering broader coverage including power and renewables, while Welligence is noted for its agility, customer collaboration, and superior Data as a Service (DaaS) capabilities.
- Key use cases for these data providers include production optimization, prospect evaluation for M&A/divestment, and a growing interest in benchmarking EPC contractors, though comprehensive benchmarking data is not yet available.
- Annual reviews of data provider spend are conducted, with increasing pressure to rationalize vendors due to shrinking budgets and the need for operational efficiency.
- The expert rates Enverus higher for companies lacking internal data science resources and Welligence higher for those with strong internal capabilities, emphasizing that vendor choice depends on customer needs and market focus.

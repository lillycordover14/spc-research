Summary

The interview covers the expert's perspective on OATI's DERMS (webSmartEnergy) platform, its capabilities, customer base, pricing structure, and competitive positioning relative to specialized software providers in the energy sector.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Over 35 years of global industry experience in energy management, data analytics, and renewable energy, including serving as VP of Technology at Vestas Wind Systems and extensive consulting for utilities, municipalities, energy traders, and microgrids, with deep expertise in both hardware and software integration within the energy sector.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Views OATI's DERMS (webSmartEnergy) as a comprehensive and robust solution for managing distributed energy resources, with strong market integration and regulatory compliance features.
- Believes OATI's broad focus across multiple asset types and markets leads to less depth in analytics and asset-specific optimization compared to competitors that specialize in particular asset classes.

TAKEAWAYS

- OATI's webSmartEnergy DERMS platform is designed for comprehensive demand management, asset modeling, forecasting, optimization, and compliance across a wide range of distributed energy resources including wind, solar, microgrids, EVs, and battery storage.
- The platform is used by a diverse customer base including investor-owned utilities, municipalities, power aggregators, rural cooperatives, grid and transmission cooperatives, and international clients.
- Pricing for OATI's DERMS is highly variable and typically structured as software-as-a-service, depending on client size, usage, features, and additional consulting or support services.
- Strengths of OATI's DERMS include its full lifecycle management, integration with multiple energy markets (RTOs, ISOs), and strong regulatory compliance.
- Weaknesses include less advanced analytics and asset-specific optimization compared to specialized competitors, as well as challenges stemming from the company's broad product focus.

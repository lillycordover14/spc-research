The interview covered the expert's experience with advanced energy modeling software, focusing on the selection, use, and eventual transition from Gridcog to a simpler tool as business needs evolved. The discussion also addressed the suitability of Gridcog for different customer segments and the broader context of distributed energy growth in Australia.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Former Senior Business Development Manager at a publicly listed EPC company specializing in both utility-scale and distributed energy projects, with extensive experience in energy modeling, software selection, and business case development for complex energy systems.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Gridcog is highly capable for complex, multi-asset energy modeling but is best suited for analysts or highly sophisticated users due to its complexity.
- The expert believes Gridcog is essential for organizations with unconventional or multi-source energy requirements, but less suitable for business development managers needing rapid, simple proposals.
- The expert sees strong growth in distributed energy and electrification, particularly in Australia, which supports the need for advanced modeling tools like Gridcog.

TAKEAWAYS

- Gridcog was selected over competitors due to its ability to handle large-scale, complex, and multi-source energy projects, but its complexity limited its use to analysts rather than business development managers.
- The company transitioned from Gridcog to Orkestra as their project needs shifted to simpler, higher-volume distributed energy proposals, prioritizing speed and ease of use over advanced modeling capabilities.
- Gridcog is considered ideal for distributed network service providers, consultants, and large enterprises with complex energy portfolios, while simpler tools like Orkestra are better for straightforward, behind-the-meter solar and battery projects.
- Outputs from Gridcog are customizable and well-received by customers, but typically require additional formatting and narrative tailoring for client proposals.
- The expert notes that the Australian market is experiencing rapid electrification and growth in distributed energy, increasing demand for sophisticated modeling tools among large energy users and developers.

SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The conversation covers the expert's experience evaluating and implementing ERP and CIS/CRM software solutions across several European energy and utility companies, comparing legacy and modern vendors, and discussing the impact of digital metering and regulatory changes on software selection.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- The expert is a CEO with executive committee experience at multiple European energy and utility companies since 2018, having sponsored and led five or six large-scale ERP, CRM, and CIS software vendor evaluations and implementations across B2B and B2C portfolios.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert strongly favors modern, utility-focused CIS/CRM vendors (such as Kraken, ENSEK, and Kaluza) over legacy solutions like Itineris and generic ERP providers, citing superior integration, data accuracy, and automation.
- The expert is critical of using generic ERP/CIS solutions (e.g., Microsoft Dynamics, SAP utilities) for complex, dynamic energy markets, emphasizing that these are not fit for purpose in the current environment.
- The expert believes that while modern solutions are still more expensive, the price gap is narrowing and the operational and commercial benefits justify the investment, especially for B2C portfolios.

TAKEAWAYS

- At the expert's current company, a recent vendor evaluation led to the adoption of Kraken for B2C, SAP S/4HANA for ERP, and Itineris for B2B, with the expert directly sponsoring the selection process.
- Modern CIS/CRM vendors like Kraken, ENSEK, and Kaluza offer out-of-the-box integration with digital metering infrastructure, enabling real-time data access, automated billing, and dynamic contract management, which legacy systems like Itineris lack without costly and less reliable third-party interfaces.
- For B2B portfolios, Itineris remains in use due to high migration costs and the lower need for dynamic contract management, as most B2B customers have long-term, stable contracts and their own energy management systems.
- At previous companies, the expert observed and led migrations away from legacy systems (e.g., Itineris, IBM Maximo) to more modern platforms (ENSEK, Microsoft Dynamics), with ENSEK showing significant improvements in customer retention and operational efficiency, despite some migration challenges.
- The expert notes that the cost premium for modern CIS/CRM solutions has decreased from 40% to about 10-12% over recent years, while requiring fewer internal resources to manage and offering better data accuracy and integration with data lakes.

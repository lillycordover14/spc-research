SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The conversation focused on the expert's experience as a residential customer of a battery rental company, discussing the challenges and limitations of the current business model, particularly for users with solar generation. Topics included energy arbitrage, customer value, and the differences in appeal for customers with and without solar panels.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Principal Application Engineer with over 15 years of experience in digital multi-phase power solutions, focusing on powering CPUs and, more recently, AI and ASIC applications for data centers. Has direct experience with residential solar and battery backup systems, including hands-on installation and usage.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is dissatisfied with the current battery rental model due to lack of user control over energy flow, especially for customers with solar generation.
- The expert is skeptical about the long-term viability of the current business model, expressing concerns about customer retention and company solvency unless significant changes are made.
- The expert believes that unless energy arbitrage opportunities or customer value improve, they would not continue as a customer after the contract ends.

TAKEAWAYS

- The expert entered a three-year battery rental agreement primarily for backup power and potential energy arbitrage, but has found the lack of control over stored energy to be a major drawback.
- Base's energy arbitrage model has not delivered significant value, with minimal arbitrage activity observed over 18 months, leading to higher costs for the expert compared to other energy providers.
- The expert feels that the current model is only compelling for customers without solar panels, who use the battery purely as a backup system, rather than for energy management or arbitrage.
- Feedback to Base about opening up battery control to customers with solar has not resulted in any changes, despite regular communication and surveys.
- The expert notes that newer battery models offer more flexibility, such as generator integration, but these improvements have not addressed the core issue for solar customers.

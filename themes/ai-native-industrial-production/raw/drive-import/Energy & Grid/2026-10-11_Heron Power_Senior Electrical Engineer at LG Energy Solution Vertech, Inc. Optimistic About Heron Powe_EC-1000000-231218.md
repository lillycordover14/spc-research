Summary

The interview discusses the integration of battery energy storage systems in data centers, focusing on Heron Power's Heron Link technology and its potential impact on efficiency, system design, and adoption timelines among hyperscalers.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Senior application engineer with five years of experience at a major battery and energy storage system integrator, deeply involved in battery energy storage solutions for data centers, including projects ranging from small to gigawatt scale, and with prior experience in power electronics and PCB design.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Optimistic about the potential of integrated solutions like Heron Link to improve efficiency and reduce system complexity in data center energy storage.
- Believes adoption by hyperscalers will depend on successful pilot projects, with large-scale implementation likely around 2028-2029 if proven effective.
- Impressed by Heron's speed, adaptability, and technical responsiveness, but notes the technology is still in the pilot phase and needs to be proven at scale.

TAKEAWAYS

- Heron Power's Heron Link integrates inverter and transformer functions into a single, compact unit, simplifying installation and potentially improving efficiency for data center battery energy storage systems.
- Heron's approach offers notable advantages in protection coordination, system availability, and modularity, allowing for isolation of faults and higher system uptime compared to traditional solutions.
- Heron Link claims round-trip efficiency improvements, with potential RTE of 98-98.5% compared to 93-94% for incumbent solutions, and 25% lower idle power consumption.
- Hyperscalers are currently piloting Heron Link, but large-scale adoption is expected only after successful demonstration, likely starting in 2028-2029.
- Retrofitting existing data centers with battery storage and Heron Link is challenging and costly, so adoption is expected primarily in new builds or major upgrades.

SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The interview covered the European battery energy storage market, its growth drivers, and the key data needs of market participants, including developers and data centers. The discussion also addressed the challenges in accessing comprehensive market intelligence and the role of data platforms in supporting decision-making.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Programme Director with extensive experience advising governments, grid operators, investors, and companies on power markets, analytics, and business models related to battery energy storage systems (BESS) and data centers, particularly in Europe.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is optimistic about the profitability and growth of the battery storage market in Europe, citing strong business models as a key driver.
- The expert notes that data and market intelligence are crucial but not easily accessible, highlighting a need for better data solutions in the sector.

TAKEAWAYS

- The European battery storage market is currently around 40-50 GW and is expected to grow to 160-200 GW by 2030, driven by profitable business models and policy ambitions.
- Most current development is utility-scale, with thousands of participants ranging from large utilities to small startups.
- Germany is the largest market, with significant activity also in Italy, Poland, the Netherlands, and Spain; France is lagging behind.
- Key data needs for market participants include project capacity, location, voltage level, and forecasts, as well as details on hybrid and onsite projects, which are not easily accessible.
- Data centers require information on the evolution of the power system and available storage services to plan grid connections and procure balancing services, often needing to flatten renewable generation profiles with batteries.

Introduction and Expert Background

EXPERT BIO

Programme Director at Ember Energy. The expert can speak to capacity deployment, power demand, and grid utilization for data centers and storage projects, drawing on their deep familiarity with specialized market intelligence platforms and open-source transmission data to evaluate infrastructure expansion forecasts and localized grid node dynamics.

The expert is responsible for leading Ember’s Europe programme across UK to Turkey, managing a team of analysts, directing analysis of power sector developments including grid connections, interconnection and regional collaboration, developing generation and flexibility data products, overseeing policy work and media engagement, and assessing data centre power demand, on-site and near-site generation options and their impacts on power markets.

Prior to Ember, the expert was the Consultant at The World Bank (left June 2022), where the expert co-authored the Country Economic Memorandum on Poland, analyzed barriers to renewables deployment, and designed and executed stakeholder surveys and interviews. Earlier roles included leading open power system modelling and spatial renewables potential analysis, developing an open energy data platform for Poland, and running research and policy teams focused on energy transition and grid planning.

The expert can speak to power system modelling and grid planning, renewables integration (wind, solar, storage) and flexibility needs, pan‑European flexibility assessments, data centre power demand and siting, energy policy and regulatory analysis, development of open data and modelling tools, and stakeholder engagement and communications.

TAKEAWAYS

- RigER is a cloud-based, highly customizable ERP platform designed primarily for oil field service companies, with strong capabilities for asset management, invoicing, CRM, and integration with third-party software.
- The oil field services market is highly fragmented, with tens of thousands of companies, most of which are still transitioning from manual processes to digital ERPs as they scale.
- RigER's customer base is broad, with an estimated 250+ clients, but less than 10% are traditional oil field rental companies; the majority are service-oriented businesses.
- Implementation of RigER typically takes 6-8 months, with additional time for qualification and validation; the platform is considered mission-critical for operations.
- RigER's willingness to integrate with other software and its understanding of oil field-specific workflows are key differentiators compared to more generic rental ERPs.

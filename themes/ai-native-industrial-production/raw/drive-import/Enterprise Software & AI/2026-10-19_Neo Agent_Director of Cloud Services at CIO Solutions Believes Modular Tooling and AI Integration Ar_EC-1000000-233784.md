SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The conversation covered the expert's approach to tool selection, automation, and AI integration within a managed service provider environment, with specific discussion of products like Neo Agent and ControlUp. The expert detailed their evaluation process, current tool stack, and considerations for future automation and vendor relationships.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Director of Cloud Services at a West Coast MSP with 10 years of experience overseeing solution architecture, automation, security, device monitoring, and efficiency improvements for a team supporting over 13,000 endpoints and 330 clients.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Believes in leveraging best-of-breed tools over single-vendor solutions, but is open to building internal solutions if external products do not meet needs or become too costly.
- Views automation and AI as critical for operational efficiency, but emphasizes the importance of vendor relationships and responsive support for long-term product adoption.
- Is considering replacing certain third-party tools like Neo Agent with internally developed solutions due to recent dissatisfaction with account management and perceived lack of value.

TAKEAWAYS

- The expert's organization uses a wide array of tools including ConnectWise PSA, ControlUp, MSPbots, BrightGauge, IT Glue, and Neo Agent, favoring best-of-breed over single-stack solutions.
- They have built a data hub aggregating information from disparate systems and use LLMs to enrich and clean documentation, improving support efficiency.
- Neo Agent has been used successfully for automated ticket dispatch, replacing a full-time employee, but recent account management issues have led to consideration of alternatives or internal development.
- ControlUp is highlighted as an innovative vendor, especially for its AI and MCP server capabilities, and is praised for its responsive support and community engagement.
- The expert sees the future of MSP tooling as increasingly modular, with organizations building custom front ends and aggregating specialized tools, while valuing strong vendor relationships.

- Believes Tabs is well-suited for SMBs and mid-market companies but not enterprise-ready, especially for companies with complex procurement and reporting needs.

TAKEAWAYS

- Tabs was adopted to automate and streamline billing, collections, and reporting, replacing a manual Stripe and spreadsheet-based process.

- Reporting inaccuracies, especially around ARR tracking and integration with Stripe for D2C products, have led to continued reliance on spreadsheets and manual processes.
- The expert is actively piloting AI-native ERP solutions (Rillet and Campfire), with a preference for Rillet due to its modularity, AI capabilities, and relationship-driven approach, and sees a high likelihood of consolidating billing and ERP functions away from Tabs.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is positive about the partnership with Axle, citing strong product-market fit, responsiveness, and alignment with their needs, but expresses concern about Axle's ability to scale quickly enough as a smaller, bootstrapped company.

- Axle's insurance verification API was integrated into the expert's platform about two years ago to address fraud and lapses in consumer insurance, replacing a previously manual, error-prone process.

- The insurance verification add-on is priced at $2-$3 per car per month, aligns with the platform's per-vehicle revenue model, and delivers a reported ROI of 6X-10X for customers by reducing rejected insurance claims.
- Adoption is currently at 30% due to implementation speed and workflow friction, but the expert expects this to increase with process improvements and a larger go-to-market team.

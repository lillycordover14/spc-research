Summary

The interview discusses the implementation and business case for using Axle's insurance verification technology within a large outsourced services provider for lending institutions. Topics include the company's insurance tracking processes, the selection and integration of Axle, and expectations for operational and cost impacts.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- The expert is the Vice President of Client Experience & Product Management at a large outsourced services provider for lending institutions, with significant experience in insurance tracking, collateral protection insurance, and loan servicing solutions.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is cautiously optimistic about Axle's technology, viewing it as a valuable addition to their insurance verification process but is piloting it to determine its true impact and cost-effectiveness.
- The expert is highly positive about Axle's team, responsiveness, and understanding of their unique business needs, rating their likelihood to recommend Axle as a nine out of ten.

TAKEAWAYS

- The company provides a wide range of outsourced services to lending institutions, including insurance tracking and collateral protection insurance, tracking approximately 22 million loans.
- Axle is being implemented primarily for auto loan insurance verification, specifically as a permission-based verification tool integrated into the company's borrower-facing website.
- Axle was selected after an RFP process due to its superior understanding of the company's use case, flexibility, and strong software development capabilities, despite similar technology among competitors.
- The pilot with Axle is ongoing, with the company budgeting less than mid-five figures per month for the service; the full scope and impact on operations and costs are still being evaluated.
- Axle's solution is expected to reduce manual verification costs and improve user experience, but will not result in immediate large-scale staff reductions; instead, it will help manage future headcount growth as the business scales.

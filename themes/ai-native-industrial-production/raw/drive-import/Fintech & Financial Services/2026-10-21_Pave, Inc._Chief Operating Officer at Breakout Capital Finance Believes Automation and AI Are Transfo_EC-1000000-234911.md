SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The interview covers the evolution of SMB and consumer credit underwriting, the increasing role of automation and AI, the evaluation and selection of workflow orchestration tools, and trends in software procurement and pricing models in the financial services industry.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Chief Operating Officer at a private credit non-bank lender since 2018/2019, responsible for managing technical applications, tech stack, and human processes supporting underwriting and credit decisioning.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Believes automation and AI are essential for scaling underwriting without proportionally increasing headcount, enabling faster and more accurate decisions.
- Views the adoption of workflow orchestration and decisioning tools as a competitive advantage, with a preference for solutions that allow flexibility and rapid deployment.
- Predicts a shift in software pricing models from usage-based to outcome-based, emphasizing the importance of aligning costs with business value.

TAKEAWAYS

- Underwriting for SMB and consumer credit is evolving beyond FICO scores, with a focus on cash-based underwriting, debt service coverage, and a combination of KYC/KYB and financial data.
- Automation and AI are increasingly used to balance speed and accuracy in underwriting, allowing underwriters to focus on nuanced cases rather than data aggregation.
- The expert's organization evaluated multiple workflow orchestration tools (Taktile, Alloy, Oscilar) and selected Taktile for its rapid deployment, flexibility, and support model.
- There is a clear industry trend toward automating credit models and enabling dynamic, real-time testing of new data points and thresholds using low-code/no-code platforms.
- Software procurement in this space is moving toward outcome-based pricing, with concerns about legacy vendors locking in customers and the growing ability for firms to build custom solutions in-house.

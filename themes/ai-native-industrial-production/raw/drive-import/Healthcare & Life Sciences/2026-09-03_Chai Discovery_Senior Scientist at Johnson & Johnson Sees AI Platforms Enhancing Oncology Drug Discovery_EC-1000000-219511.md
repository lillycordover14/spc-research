- The expert finds AI-driven platforms like Chai Discovery valuable for streamlining drug discovery timelines, particularly in the prediction of protein-ligand and antibody-antigen interactions.

- Chai is favored for prediction, while Cradle Bio is used for protein design and engineering, often in collaboration with external validation partners.

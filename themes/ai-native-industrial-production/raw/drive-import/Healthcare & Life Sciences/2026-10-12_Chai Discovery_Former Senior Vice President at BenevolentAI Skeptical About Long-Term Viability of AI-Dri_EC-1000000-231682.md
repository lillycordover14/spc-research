EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is skeptical about the long-term viability and differentiation of AI-driven drug repurposing companies, citing intense competition and limited opportunities for genuine novelty.
- They view repurposing as a useful proof-of-concept for AI platforms but believe the market is crowded and challenging, especially outside rare and neglected diseases.
- The expert expresses caution regarding the commercial potential of repurposed generics and the practical hurdles in accessing proprietary data for patented drugs.

TAKEAWAYS

- Drug repurposing is standard practice in pharma, often used for patent extension and as a validation tool for AI platforms, but is highly competitive and crowded by both commercial and nonprofit players.
- AI tools like AlphaFold and Chai Discovery are widely accessible, making it difficult for new entrants to claim a unique competitive edge in repurposing.
- Repurposing is attractive for its speed and lower cost compared to de novo drug discovery, but commercializing repurposed generics is challenging due to lack of market exclusivity and widespread off-label use.
- Accessing proprietary data for repurposing patented drugs is difficult without collaboration or co-development agreements with the original patent holders.
- Business development and licensing in pharma are lengthy, multi-year processes, with significant risk-sharing and a need for clear market understanding and competitive intelligence.

SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The interview covers the clinic's experience as a major customer of Doctolib, discussing product usage, pricing, integration, bargaining power, and customer support.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Co-founder and co-president of a leading French primary and outpatient care clinic group with 20 multidisciplinary clinics and over 60,000 monthly patients; has been an entrepreneur for 20 years and a strategy consultant for 30 years; has used Doctolib since its inception (about 7 years).

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Views Doctolib as an essential, dominant provider in the French healthcare market, making it difficult to operate without their patient booking system.
- Expresses some frustration with the lack of bargaining power due to Doctolib's monopoly, but acknowledges the value and necessity of their services.
- Is generally satisfied with Doctolib's offerings and customer support, rating the solution 8 out of 10, but is cautious about increasing dependency on their ecosystem.

TAKEAWAYS

- The clinic uses nearly all of Doctolib's products, with the patient booking system and practice management being the most critical; other products are used selectively based on readiness and fit.
- Doctolib's dominant market share in France, especially in private practice, results in limited bargaining power for large clinic groups, particularly for the patient booking system.
- Pricing is subscription-based, with separate charges for each product; there is little room for negotiation except in areas where Doctolib is not dominant or where alternatives exist.
- Integration and seamless workflow are key factors in product adoption, especially for AI tools and practice management, as time efficiency directly impacts clinic revenue.
- Customer support is generally strong and responsive, with direct lines of communication at both executive and operational levels, though occasional operational flaws and system changes can impact business.

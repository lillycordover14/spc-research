Summary

The interview covers the use, evaluation, and decision-making process for OR management and analytics solutions across a large health system, focusing on the strengths and limitations of ORCONTROL/Tracker, LeanTaaS, Apella, and Epic. Discussion includes integration challenges, ROI considerations, workflow impacts, and the organizational approach to vendor selection and standardization.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Director of Business Intelligence and Data Analytics overseeing operational analytics for multiple hospital campuses, including trauma, children's, heart, rehab, and orthopedic hospitals.
- Over a decade of experience in perioperative operations, OR scheduling, block management, and efficiency optimization, with leadership of analytics teams and involvement in capital strategy and technology implementations.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Believes that advanced analytics and strategy-focused solutions like LeanTaaS and Apella provide significantly more value and ROI than legacy visualization tools like ORCONTROL/Tracker.
- Expresses frustration that ORCONTROL/Tracker has not evolved to meet modern analytics and integration needs, especially in comparison to newer, more innovative vendors.
- Views Epic as a strong EMR but insufficient as a standalone solution for OR optimization, requiring supplemental tools for advanced analytics and operational improvements.

TAKEAWAYS

- ORCONTROL/Tracker is widely used for real-time OR visualization and communication but lacks analytics, ROI tracking, and integration with Epic, leading to duplicative workflows and data integrity issues.
- LeanTaaS is highly valued for its analytics, strategy partnership, and measurable ROI in OR efficiency, block utilization, and case volume, but requires significant change management and workflow redesign.
- Apella is being piloted for its promise of AI-driven analytics, Epic integration, and comprehensive OR management, but is still in early stages and limited in deployment.
- Decision-making for OR technology is primarily top-down, driven by COOs and IT, with clinical staff and service lines having limited influence; system-wide standardization is a current focus to reduce vendor sprawl and risk.
- Pricing models differ: LeanTaaS and Apella use per-OR subscription pricing, while Tracker is a one-time campus fee; LeanTaaS implementation for a large campus can reach mid-six-figures annually.

SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The interview discusses the use of Ketryx in the development and FDA submission process for medical diagnostic devices, including its integration, documentation, and compliance capabilities. The conversation covers product evaluation, implementation experience, and the platform's impact on workflow and regulatory success.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Senior Director in a large healthcare company, leading a team of approximately 600 software engineers focused on developing medical diagnostic imaging devices (e.g., MRIs, ultrasounds, cardiology machines) and managing the FDA submission process.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Highly favorable towards Ketryx for its integration capabilities and documentation management, rating it 9/10 for current use cases.
- Believes Ketryx is robust and reliable, but would like to see a clearer product roadmap, especially for post-commercialization features, before deeper investment.
- Considers Ketryx sticky within the organization, but not at significantly higher price points (would reconsider if price doubled).

TAKEAWAYS

- Ketryx is used throughout the product development lifecycle, from conception to commercialization, primarily for documentation and FDA compliance.
- The platform integrates well with code repositories, ticketing systems, and project management tools, streamlining workflows for software and hardware integration.
- Ketryx was chosen after evaluating other solutions (RegDesk, Rimsys), mainly due to superior integration with software development tools.
- The expert has seen a 100% FDA approval rate (8 out of 8 products) since adopting Ketryx, attributing this to improved documentation and audit readiness.
- Ketryx's AI agents are user-friendly and included in an all-inclusive contract, but the expert notes that significant price increases could impact continued use.

EXPERT BIO

Senior Director of Infrastructure and Security at General Electric and Senior Director of Security Operations at GE Healthcare. Expert is a customer of Ketryx.

Senior Director of Infrastructure and Security at General Electric and Senior Director of Security Operations at GE Healthcare. Reporting to the Chief Information Officer and Chief Information Security Officer, respectively, the expert is responsible for all information technology infrastructure and cybersecurity elements, including cloud and on-premises infrastructure, compute, storage, network, and personal computing devices. The expert leads a team of security professionals in the Security Operations Center focused on threat modeling, detection, prevention, response, and remediation, while also managing SecDevOps, regulatory compliance, and privacy.

The expert can speak to the monetization of Install Base (IB) tracking through annual rebates, service credits, and tiered maintenance contracts to ensure high data capture rates.

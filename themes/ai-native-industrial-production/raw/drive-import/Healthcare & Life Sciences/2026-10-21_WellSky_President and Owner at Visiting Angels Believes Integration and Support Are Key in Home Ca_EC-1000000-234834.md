SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The interview covers the operational needs and software preferences of a home care franchise owner, focusing on platform selection, integration, and the challenges of switching systems. The discussion includes insights into preferred software vendors, key features required, and the practical realities of managing multiple locations.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- President and Owner of two franchise locations in the home care industry, overseeing approximately 140 clients and 240 caregivers, with experience in software selection and operations since 2019.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Believes that most home care operating software platforms are functionally similar, with no major differentiators, but values ongoing investment and integration capabilities.
- Expresses some dissatisfaction with the current platform (WellSky) due to performance and support issues, but is hesitant to switch due to operational disruption and data migration challenges.
- Is open to switching to AxisCare, citing positive feedback from peers and better customer support, but is cautious about the transition process.

TAKEAWAYS

- The franchise operates two locations with a combined revenue of over $6 million, primarily private pay clients, and does not participate in Medicaid.
- The franchisor does not mandate a specific software platform; several preferred vendors are available, including WellSky, AxisCare, Generations, and AlayaCare.
- Key software requirements include reliability, speed, scheduling, communication, integration with other applications, and support for EVV and billing/payroll functions.
- Integration with third-party applications for call documentation and caregiver engagement is increasingly important.
- Switching software platforms is disruptive due to training, data migration, and potential loss of historical data, making it a significant consideration despite some dissatisfaction with the current system.

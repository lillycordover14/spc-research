Summary

The interview covers the dynamics of chemical storage tank rentals in the oil and gas sector, including vendor selection, market trends, and the operational considerations influencing rental versus ownership. The discussion also addresses the competitive landscape among major rental providers and the factors driving demand for reusable IBC totes and ISO tanks.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Over 20 years of experience in supply chain and operations within the oil and gas and chemicals industries, including vendor selection, negotiations, and technical analysis for chemical storage and transportation equipment.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Expects North American demand for chemical storage rentals to remain flat or grow slightly (1-4% CAGR), with global demand growing faster (around 10% CAGR).
- Views the equipment rental market as highly commoditized, with vendor selection driven by reliability, technical support, and geographic availability rather than price alone.
- Believes technology risk is low for major shifts in tank types, and chemical usage reduction through R&D is unlikely to significantly impact container demand in the near term.

TAKEAWAYS

- Major rental providers for chemical storage tanks include Hoover, Precision IBC, Dana Transport, and Eurotainer, with selection based on geography, technical expertise, and inventory availability.
- The trend has shifted from owning to renting/ leasing IBC totes and ISO tanks due to cost, maintenance, and compliance factors; rental is expected to increase further, especially for totes.
- Vendor relationships are managed with a primary supplier (50-60% share) and secondary suppliers to ensure reliability and leverage in negotiations; pricing differences between top vendors are minimal (2-5%).
- Technical support, such as compatibility analysis and maintenance, and value-added services like IoT tracking and fleet management, are key differentiators among vendors.
- Demand for chemical storage containers is tied to oilfield activity, with North American demand expected to be flat or slightly down, but global demand (especially in the Middle East and Africa) is growing; rental contracts typically range from 12 to 60 months.

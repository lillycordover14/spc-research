EXPERT BACKGROUND

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Expects significant growth in packaging needs as product designs mature and manufacturing scales, projecting a 10x-50x increase over the next 5-10 years.
- Believes maintaining multiple packaging suppliers and competitive RFQ processes yields better financial results and supply chain resiliency than consolidating to a single supplier.
- Views the need for highly engineered, bespoke packaging as a permanent and growing requirement due to the unique nature of the company's products.

TAKEAWAYS

- The majority (about 60%) of packaging spend is on highly engineered, custom-designed solutions, with the rest on standard or off-the-shelf packaging.

- Dangerous goods (batteries, flammable liquids/solids, corrosives) are a minority of shipments, with in-house teams handling most DG packaging and compliance, supplemented by specialized vendors and consultants as needed.
- Supplier selection emphasizes cost, lead time, supply chain resiliency, and the ability to meet complex engineering requirements; TransPak is noted for engineering expertise, while Craters & Freighters is valued for speed.

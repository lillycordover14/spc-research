SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The conversation focused on the implementation and impact of Volie, a workflow and communication platform, in both multi-rooftop and single-rooftop dealership service BDC operations. Topics included workflow optimization, remote staffing, alternatives to Volie, pricing structure, and the future role of AI in dealership service departments.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Service BDC Manager with experience since 2014, managing centralized BDC operations for an eight-rooftop auto group and currently overseeing service BDC at a single-location Toyota dealership in New Jersey, with hands-on implementation of Volie at both multi-rooftop and single-rooftop environments.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Strongly positive about Volie's impact on workflow optimization, customer engagement, and measurable revenue growth in service BDC operations.
- Believes remote staffing enabled by Volie can attract higher-quality talent and improve conversions, though current employer prefers in-house staff.
- Views AI as a significant opportunity for the future of dealership service operations, particularly for handling inbound calls and freeing up staff for outbound efforts.

TAKEAWAYS

- Volie was implemented to replace manual, paper-based processes and standard landline phones, resulting in a dramatic increase in call volume per agent (from under 100 to up to 300 calls per day) and improved outbound conversion rates.
- Volie’s workflow platform is considered mission-critical for optimizing daily operations, with features like campaign management, call tracking, and integrated scripting, while Dealer Identity is also seen as essential for customer recognition and compliance with FCC regulations.
- Implementation of Volie is rapid (1-2 days for setup, with additional training), and contracts are flexible, typically month-to-month with negotiable pricing based on the strength of the dealership's negotiator.
- Alternatives to Volie include Revusup (noted for superior Toyota integration but higher cost) and Car Wars, but Volie is preferred for its flexibility, support, and customizable workflow.
- The expert sees potential for AI to further automate inbound calls, viewing it as an opportunity rather than a threat, but acknowledges that live staff will remain necessary for complex interactions.

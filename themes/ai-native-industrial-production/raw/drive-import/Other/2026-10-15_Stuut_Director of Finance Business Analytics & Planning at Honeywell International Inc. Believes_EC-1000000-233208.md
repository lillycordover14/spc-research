SMART SUMMARY

Summary

The conversation covered the expert's experience piloting and implementing Stuut AI for AR automation, focusing on its selection, integration, and impact on collections processes. Discussion also included areas for future product expansion and the specific business context in which Stuut AI is deployed.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Director of Finance Business Analytics & Planning at a large diversified industrial company, with direct experience in evaluating, piloting, and implementing AR automation solutions such as Stuut AI and HighRadius, including cost-benefit and integration analysis.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is generally positive about Stuut AI, rating it a 7 or 8 out of 10 for its effectiveness in AR automation and collections.
- They believe Stuut AI could improve further by expanding its capabilities both vertically (e.g., cash flow forecasting) and horizontally (e.g., broader order-to-cash processes).

TAKEAWAYS

- Stuut AI was selected over HighRadius due to its more refined and demonstrable product, as well as a higher perceived pay-do ratio during the pilot phase.
- The implementation focused on automating collections for lower-value invoices (initially $5,000-$10,000, scaling up to $20,000-$30,000), with the goal of reducing headcount in the collections team and achieving positive ROI within a year.
- Integration with SAP Ariba and AR aging reports enabled automated, configurable communication (email, SMS, calls) with customers and channel partners regarding outstanding invoices.
- Stuut AI is primarily used in the industrial automation division, targeting product-based businesses with high transaction volumes and a mix of direct and channel partner sales.
- The expert sees potential for Stuut AI to expand into cash flow forecasting, full order-to-cash automation, and possibly channel financing, which would increase its value proposition.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Views DataBahn as a strong, AI-driven solution for security operations, highly recommending it for mid-sized companies and security-focused use cases (10/10).
- Sees Cribl as the best choice for observability, rating it 10/10 for that use case, but less suitable for security operations (5/10 for security, 7/10 for combined use).
- Believes that in large enterprises, using both Cribl and DataBahn together is becoming common to address distinct observability and security needs.

TAKEAWAYS

- Cribl is used primarily for observability and general telemetry, while DataBahn is used for security operations and SIEM data fabric, with clear separation of responsibilities between the two platforms.
- DataBahn's AI-driven features, especially in data parsing, normalization, and security insights (via Reef and Cruz), are central to its value proposition and enable automation and advanced analytics for security teams.
- Cribl is preferred for its mature connectors, robust pipeline, and cost-effective data routing, especially for large-scale observability workloads, but can become costly at high data volumes (noted at 10TB/day).
- DataBahn is not used for observability at scale due to lack of mature search functionality and limited load testing, but excels in security operations and has delivered measurable cost savings (e.g., 30% reduction in Splunk costs).
- Switching off DataBahn would be a major, multi-year engineering effort due to deep integration across many internal product teams and systems.

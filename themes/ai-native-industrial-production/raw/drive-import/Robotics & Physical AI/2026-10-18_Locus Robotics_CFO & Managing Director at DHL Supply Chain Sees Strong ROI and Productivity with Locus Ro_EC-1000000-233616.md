EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is very satisfied with the performance and robustness of Locus robotics solutions, citing strong productivity and ROI.

TAKEAWAYS

- Locus is the preferred robotics provider for picking operations, with deployments in both retrofits and new warehouses, and is considered robust and cost-effective despite not being the cheapest option.

- Switching between robotics providers (e.g., from Locus to Geek+) is technically feasible and not highly disruptive, as integration is relatively straightforward.
- Key metrics tracked for ROI include pick productivity and labor savings, with investment decisions influenced by customer co-investment, contract runway, and capital availability.

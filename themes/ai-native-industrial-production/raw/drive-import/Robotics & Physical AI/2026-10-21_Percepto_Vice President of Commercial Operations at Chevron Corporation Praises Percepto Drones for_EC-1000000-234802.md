Summary

The interview discusses the decision-making process, deployment, and operational impact of adopting third-party autonomous drones for security and monitoring at a major oil and gas company, with a focus on the selection and performance of Percepto drones.

EXPERT BACKGROUND

- Vice President of Commercial Operations with over 28 years of experience at a major oil and gas company, overseeing drone deployment for security and operations across multiple U.S. regions including refineries, oil fields, and rigs.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert is highly satisfied with the transition to third-party autonomous drones, particularly Percepto, citing significant operational and security improvements.
- The expert believes the investment in Percepto drones has paid off and anticipates expanding their use as operations grow, with no current interest in switching providers.
- While acknowledging some areas for improvement, especially in environmental monitoring, the expert rates the current solution very highly (8.5-9 out of 10).

TAKEAWAYS

- The company switched from in-house drones to a third-party provider (Percepto) in 2023 to reduce operational burden and leverage advanced technology, including AI capabilities.
- Percepto was chosen based on positive feedback from engineers and its established use by industry peers (ExxonMobil, Shell, bp).
- Drones are primarily used for leak detection, emission monitoring, thermal analysis, and precise security alerts, enhancing both safety and operational efficiency.
- The drones have not replaced physical security staff but have made their deployment more efficient and targeted.
- Maintenance issues are minor and infrequent, with replacements covered under contract; the company maintains a surplus of drones for immediate replacement needs.

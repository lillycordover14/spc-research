SUMMARY
Reliance, Inc. (RS -0.55%) reported record third quarter net sales and its third-highest quarterly earnings per share, supported by healthy demand and constrained supply in carbon steel products. Management reported that the company outperformed industry-wide shipment trends, leveraging its domestic mill relationships to secure inventory amid extended lead times. The U.S. border wall contract remains a primary driver of growth, contributing significantly to volume and pretax income margins. The company continues to execute a disciplined capital allocation strategy: increasing its share repurchase authorization while funding strategic capacity expansions. Looking forward, management indicated that customer optimism is strengthening across infrastructure, semiconductor, and aerospace sectors.
The border wall project Phase 1 funding of $1.45 billion is expected to be fulfilled in the second quarter of 2027, with Phase 2 bookings already exceeding $400 million.
CFO Ajemyan noted that "our inventory levels would have been over $300 million more had we not had the inventory turn and working capital metric improvements that we've had this year."
Stephen Koch stated that demand in the semiconductor market is "supported by increased data center activity" and is seeing momentum accelerate.
The company has reduced its outstanding common shares by 23% since 2021 through $3.4 billion in total repurchases at an average price of $236 per share.
Commercial aerospace demand is expected to modestly improve in the fourth quarter of 2026 as OEM build rates gradually increase.
Management confirmed that most carbon steel products are experiencing upward price pressure due to tight supply and unprecedented demand.
INDUSTRY GLOSSARY
LIFO (Last-In, First-Out): An inventory accounting method where the most recently produced or purchased items are sold first.
FIFO (First-In, First-Out): An accounting method where the oldest inventory items are recorded as sold first.
MSCI: Metals Service Center Institute, an industry trade association that reports metals shipment data.
Toll Processing: A service where a company processes metal owned by a customer for a fee, without taking ownership of the material.
Tons Sold: A volume metric representing the quantity of metal products shipped to customers.

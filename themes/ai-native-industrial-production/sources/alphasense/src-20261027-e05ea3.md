 reported its highest quarterly EBITDA and earnings in more than three years, driven by record steel mill shipments and improved pricing in downstream products. Hollatz estimated 1,400 miles of new border wall construction, noting each mile requires approximately 1,500 tons of steel, which Nucor is currently shipping on a weekly basis.
Laxton stated utilities plan to spend nearly $1.4 trillion over the next five years on steel-intensive infrastructure, including generation, substations, and transmission upgrades.
Management highlighted that the Lexington micromill reached a utilization rate of nearly 80% in the third quarter after becoming EBITDA positive in March.
INDUSTRY GLOSSARY
DRI
: Direct Reduced Iron, a high-quality metallic raw material used in electric arc furnaces.
EAF
: Electric Arc Furnace, a steelmaking method that melts recycled scrap steel using electricity.
MBQ
: Merchant Bar Quality, a class of steel bars used in general structural applications.
SBQ
: Special Bar Quality, high-quality steel bars used in demanding automotive and industrial applications.
HSS
: Hollow Structural Section, a type of metal profile with a hollow tubular cross section.
Galvanizing
: The process of applying a protective zinc coating to steel to prevent rusting.
Thin-slab casting
: A steelmaking process that produces thin sheets of steel directly from molten metal, improving efficiency.
ADC
: Apparent Domestic Consumption, a measure of the total market demand for steel within a country.

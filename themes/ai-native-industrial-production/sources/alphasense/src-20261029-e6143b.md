INDUSTRY GLOSSARY
CNC Machining
: Computer Numerical Control machining, a manufacturing process where pre-programmed computer software dictates the movement of factory tools and machinery.
DMLS
: Direct Metal Laser Sintering, a 3D printing technology that uses a laser to melt and fuse metal powder into solid parts.
FDM
: Fused Deposition Modeling, an additive manufacturing technology that extrudes thermoplastic filaments to build parts layer by layer.
MJF
: Multi Jet Fusion, a 3D printing technology developed by HP that uses fusing and detailing agents to create functional parts from powder.
CAD
: Computer-Aided Design, the use of software to create, modify, or analyze digital models of physical parts.

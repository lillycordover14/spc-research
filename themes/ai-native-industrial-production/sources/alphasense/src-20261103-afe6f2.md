 surpassed a $1 billion revenue run rate during the third quarter, reporting its first period of GAAP profitability and a record marketplace gross margin of 36.0%. Management attributed the performance to the execution of a product-led growth strategy and the expanding role of its AI-native marketplace as digital infrastructure for custom manufacturing. Revenue increased 40% year over year, led by a 43% expansion in marketplace revenue and record net additions of active buyers. The company raised its full-year 2026 revenue and adjusted EBITDA guidance, citing robust demand and operating leverage across its U.S. and international segments.
CEO Sahni reported that the company is building native integrations to Siemens Designcenter, which he stated will bring "manufacturability and pricing insight directly into the platform" starting with a beta launch in the first quarter of 2027.
Management noted a generational shift in manufacturing purchasing, where digitally native engineers and procurement buyers are increasingly choosing e-commerce experiences to replace traditional email-based quoting workflows.
The company moved the majority of its buyer traffic to a new e-commerce architecture that CEO Sahni stated allows for core workflows to be delivered at "a fraction of the time that they would have taken a year ago."
Management reported that deeper enterprise integration through tools like purchase order and invoice automation is driving predictable spend, with the number of $10 million annual accounts expected to increase in 2026.
The company noted that its asset-light marketplace model allows it to capture market share by connecting buyers to underutilized capacity in a fragmented supplier network, where 44% of small manufacturers operate below 70% capacity.
James Miln stated that the company expects to maintain a target of at least 20% incremental adjusted EBITDA margins on a full-year basis as it scales beyond its current revenue levels.
INDUSTRY GLOSSARY
Agentic Commerce
: An emerging commerce model where AI agents autonomously perform tasks such as learning about capabilities, sourcing, and purchasing.
CMMC Level 2
: Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification Level 2, a U.S. government standard for protecting sensitive defense information within the supply chain.
CNC Machining
: Computer Numerical Control machining, a manufacturing process where computer software dictates the movement of factory tools and machinery.
DFM
: Design for Manufacturing, the process of designing parts or products in a way that simplifies manufacturing and reduces costs.
ITAR
: International Traffic in Arms Regulations, U.S. government regulations that control the export and import of defense-related articles and services.
PunchOut
: An e-procurement integration that allows a buyer to access a supplier's marketplace directly from within their own procurement software.
S-curve
: A model describing the path of technology adoption, typically starting slowly, accelerating rapidly, and then leveling off.
Teamspace
: A cloud-based collaborative tool provided by Xometry that allows multiple employees within a company to manage and share manufacturing projects.

RISKS
Rothe stated, "The inflation side has not abated," noting that memory and other electrical components continue to face price pressures that offset some productivity gains.
Moret warned that capital expenditure projects in mining and other sectors are being extended in duration, contributing to timing delays in the sales funnel.

SUMMARY
Rockwell Automation, Inc.

 reported its fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter results, achieving its long-term enterprise operating margin target of 22% three years ahead of the original schedule. Management reported organic sales growth across all primary business segments, led by significant demand in the semiconductor, data center, and automotive sectors. The company navigated the dissolution of the Sensia joint venture and a major tariff refund process with the U.S. government, which net increased adjusted earnings by $0.11 per share. For fiscal 2027, the company issued guidance anticipating continued organic growth and margin expansion, supported by a step-up in investments for advanced manufacturing and digital infrastructure.
CEO Moret stated, "Customers are standardizing on Rockwell's technologies across automation, cybersecurity, asset management, and digital infrastructure" to support AI-driven demand in data centers.
The company will begin reporting semiconductor and data center as a combined vertical in fiscal 2027 to reflect commonalities in building management and environmental control applications.
CFO Rothe reported that internal productivity actions across the organization contributed 270 basis points of margin expansion for the full year.
Management indicated that the OTTO AMR business reaching profitability in the fourth quarter marks "an important milestone as we scale our physical AI and autonomous operations capabilities."
The company is standardizing its Software & Control offerings through the new FactoryTalk Conductor platform, which applies agentic orchestration to industrial workflows.
Fiscal 2027 guidance assumes a seasonal sequential decline in the first quarter followed by steady improvement in sales and margins throughout the year.
INDUSTRY GLOSSARY
OTTO AMR
: Autonomous Mobile Robot business specializing in physical AI and autonomous material handling.
Logix
: Rockwell's core control platform used for discrete and hybrid industrial automation.
Plex
: A cloud-native smart manufacturing platform encompassing MES and supply chain visibility tools.
PlantPAx
: Rockwell's distributed control system (DCS) designed for process automation applications.
Sensia
: A former joint venture with SLB focused on digital transformation in the oil and gas industry.
IEEPA
: International Emergency Economic Powers Act, the regulatory basis for the tariffs and subsequent refunds discussed by management.
MES
: Manufacturing Execution System, software used to track and document the transformation of raw materials to finished goods.
ARR
: Annual Recurring Revenue, the annual contract value of all active recurring revenue contracts.

ARPA-E project: Novel Technique for Domestic Rare Earth Oxide Separation and Rare Earth Metal Reduction. Start Jun 21 2021, end Dec 20 2022. Woburn, MA. Private company. Amount $499,854. Phoenix Tailings (PT) has developed novel techniques to separate rare earth oxides (REOs) without the use of hazardous chemicals and reduce them to REMs using 35-45% less energy. PT will separate REOs through selective halogenation and use mixed halide salts to reduce them.

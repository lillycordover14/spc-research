- Divergent Technologies, Inc. (Torrance, CA) completed $230M Series D equity financing, announced 2023-11-15. Hexagon AB led with $100M; other participants new and existing institutions and family offices (unnamed). Valuation not stated.
- Kevin Czinger: founder, lead inventor, CEO. Lukas Czinger: president and COO.
- DAPS: combined software and hardware production system replacing traditional design, manufacturing and assembly. In-house AI-driven generative design software for structures and novel materials; additive manufacturing; automated fixtureless assembly for large multi-part assemblies. Claims higher performance, lower cost, rapid customization, faster time to market, on-demand scaling to high volume.
- Operates as a certified Tier 1 supplier to automotive, aerospace, defense. "Design-manufacturing-assembly as a service"; goal of a global network of regional manufacturing facilities. Customers develop products with no design-specific capex.
- Customers: automotive seven blue-chip incl. Aston Martin, Mercedes-AMG; A&D six US government contractors (unnamed). Czinger Vehicles subsidiary makes the 21C hypercar with 350+ AM components.
- Quotes: Kevin Czinger: "DAPS was created to serve as the foundation for a global system of regional manufacturing facilities"; "We now have entered the '4D Age' of fully digitised design-manufacturing-assembly as a service". Lukas Czinger: "DAPS allows customers to develop higher performing products on faster timelines and with zero design-specific capex"; "Divergent is on a mission to rebuild the American industrial base".
- Not stated: factory count/size, printer count, lead times, part numbers, revenue, headcount, whether DAPS is licensed standalone.

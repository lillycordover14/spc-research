- Freeform: new metal 3D printing company founded by former SpaceX engineers and former Velo3D staff (SpaceX is Velo3D's largest customer). Erik Palitsch named as co-founder.
- Raised US$14M; investors Boeing and NVIDIA. Boeing approached Freeform; NVIDIA's interest attributed to proprietary print data.
- Business model: does not sell 3D printers; service bureau printing for customers; removes need for customers to build metal printing expertise. Similar to VulcanForms and Seurat. No pricing, margins or lead times given.
- Technology: real-time meltpool monitoring with dynamic parameter adjustment; computer vision feedback at microsecond timescales; sensor data processed on FPGAs and GPUs; telemetry built in-house to collect print data at higher frequencies than previously attempted; AI models analyze data and adjust parameters in real time; training data proprietary.
- NVIDIA to supply H100 GPUs (quantity not given). Equipment operating; no machine count, factory size or location.
- Several major industrial customers lined up, unnamed. Plans to hire up to 55 people over the next year.
- TechCrunch report 2024-10-22; Fabbaloo 2024-10-24 by Kerry Stevenson.
- Quotes: Erik Palitsch: "We have high-speed computer vision feedback on our system that runs at microsecond scale"; "We had to build this whole stack ourselves out of stuff that's only become available in the last few years."

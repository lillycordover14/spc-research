- Reindustrialize Summit, annual, Detroit; website reindustrialize.com; inaugurated June 25, 2024.
- 2024: June 25-26, Newlab at Michigan Central; grew out of informal discussions after a March 2024 factory tour at Atomic Industries.
- 2025: July 16-17, Hudson's Detroit; USTR Jamieson Greer keynote on trade policy and manufacturing (USTR release: 'Ambassador Greer Delivered Keynote Address to Kick-Off Reindustrialize Summit in Detroit'); themes defense manufacturing and company announcements.
- Organizers: Aaron Slodov, Falon Donohue, Austin Bishop, Gregory Bernstein.
- Coverage: Axios (Jul 1, 2024); Detroit News (Jul 17, 2025); NYT (Jul 20, 2025); Reuters and FT (Jul 16, 2025); Business Insider (Jul 19, 2025); Defense One (Jul 21, 2025); The Free Press (Jul 21, 2025).

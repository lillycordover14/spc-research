"It's about supercharging the worker versus replacing them." Defense tech startups like Hadrian are disrupting the mainstay defense contracting industry, which is led by leaders such as Northrop Grumman and Lockheed Martin , and battling it out to boost U.S. defense production while scooping up Department of Defense contracts.

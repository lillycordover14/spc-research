- GovCon Wire, 2025-07-21. Hadrian raised $260M Series C; lead investors Founders Fund, Lux Capital, Morgan Stanley. No equity/debt split or valuation given.
- Funding pays for Factory 3 (Mesa, AZ): about $200M, 270,000 sq ft, software hub for large-scale production of precision components and critical systems, 350 local jobs.
- Corporate HQ: 400,000 sq ft, also R&D, to support hiring thousands.
- Both expected fully operational by January 2026; brought online in under six months using Opus, proprietary software stack for production autonomy.
- New division Hadrian Maritime (shipbuilding, naval supply chains); more divisions expected for munitions, missile systems, UAS. Factories-as-a-service approach covering the entire advanced manufacturing stack for DoD programs.
- Chris Power quote: "We're building the factories that will secure American leadership in advanced manufacturing".
- Not covered: valuation, lenders, headcount, Torrance/Alabama, processes, customers, margins.

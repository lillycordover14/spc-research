- AIM Media House, Anshika Mathews, published Jul 22, 2025 (section: Deployed Manufacturing)
- Seed: $55M, July 2025; lead RTX Ventures; participants NVentures (NVIDIA), AlleyCorp, Eni, Infinite Capital, others; one of the largest seeds in NY startup history; not framed as a stealth Series A
- Prior: $10M pre-seed, March 2024, incubated inside AlleyCorp; founder approached Kevin Ryan (AlleyCorp) with the thesis; co-founder Jorge
- Hiring: scale team across AI, robotics, materials science over next 18 months; 'We are not looking for those who simply want a job; we are looking for those who want a mission'
- Facility: planned 'most advanced materials discovery facility in the world' for high-throughput experimentation and active learning (not yet complete at time); autonomous lab designed for hundreds of thousands of experiments per year; inspired by LBNL A-Lab (early collaborator helped set it up; founders visited Berkeley)
- Platform: AI-native, vertically integrated materials discovery combining computation, experimentation and manufacturing; stages: theoretical modeling, AI prediction, physical synthesis, performance validation; closed loop: ML hypotheses, robotic experimentation, model update
- In-house: AI models and discovery platform; automation and robotic lab systems; control over software and hardware
- Target sectors: automotive, aerospace, defense, climate, energy, semiconductors
- Customers, business model, valuation, headcount, HQ: not stated
- Quotes (founder, unnamed): 'We kept seeing wrappers on top of foundation models and thought, is this really it?'; 'The data we're collecting is the missing dataset in AI today.'; 'We believed we had to bring together software, automation, and a clear opinion of how to execute'; Joseph Krause: 'Materials are quite large'

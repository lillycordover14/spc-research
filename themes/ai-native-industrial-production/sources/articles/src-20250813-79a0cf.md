ASPR visited ODP's new Centralized Production and Repackaging facility in Rockville producing APIs, and its Pharmacy on Demand modular unit (as small as a tractor trailer) that leverages AI and machine learning, deployed to Northern Mississippi Medical Center to produce medicines in shortage. Collaboration began Sept 2020 ($20M DoD/HHS contract; DARPA origin). GovIntel: 3 federal prime awards totaling $35.6M (DoD $19.0M 2022, $16.3M 2024 cisatracurium; HHS SBIR $325K).

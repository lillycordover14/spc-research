- Company: Nox Metals (noxmetals.co). Founder: Zane Hengsperger. Y Combinator Summer 2025 batch. Article published Aug 15, 2025 by Rumaisa Khusru.
- Business model: builds vertically integrated, automated metals factories to supply raw materials to US manufacturers; treats the factory itself as the product; factories run on software and automation end-to-end; orders go directly from the customer's screen to the shop floor, where robots pick, cut, package and ship.
- Problems targeted: supply chains take days to generate a quote; multiple markups from brokers; slow fulfillment with poor visibility; high overhead and fragmented workflows.
- Claimed improvements: instant quotes; orders become jobs on automated lines; real-time tracking; removal of brokers, schedulers, excess overhead; shorter lead times, lower costs (no figures).
- Customers: small job shops, aerospace and defense companies, large-scale industrial manufacturers.
- YC: "Request for Startups for techno-industrialists"; Hengsperger says YC's team, network, fundraising and mentorship were "wildly impactful" and sharpened GTM, product and operations.
- Quotes (Hengsperger): "Our vertically integrated factories run on software and automation to deliver lightning-fast, low-cost metal to our customers." "Our goal: power the next industrial era by becoming the fastest, most advanced supplier to America's industrial base." "We're behind on sheer output, so we have to be 10x better than the incumbents." "Build the machines. Build the factories. Build the infrastructure that keeps the country standing." "YC is showing me how to take the speed of code and apply it to the weight of atoms."
- Not disclosed: funding, investors, valuation, founder prior roles, headcount, facility locations, lead times, pricing, margins, software specifics.

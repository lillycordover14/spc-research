- Published 2025-08-20 by Thomas Wilk. Factory expected fully operational by early 2026; Hadrian's software enables factory deployment in under six months.
- Location: Mesa, Arizona, Building A at The Cubes Mesa Gateway, a two-building Class A industrial project developed by CRG Integrated Real Estate Solutions. 270,000 sq ft. JLL (Greg Matter, Kyle Westfall, Eric Turner, Meredith O'Connor, Ada Wong) represented Hadrian in the lease.
- Factory investment $200M; 350 jobs.
- Broader capital investment $260M including Series C led by Founders Fund and Lux Capital and a factory expansion loan facility arranged by Morgan Stanley.
- Planned 400,000 sq ft corporate and R&D headquarters (location not specified).
- Facility converts raw materials into components and mission-critical systems for aerospace and defense.
- Opus: proprietary software stack for production autonomy.
- Expanded operations in California and Arizona; Hadrian Maritime division for shipbuilding and naval defense.
- Quotes: Chris Power: "America cannot afford to lose another generation of industrial capacity." "That's what Hadrian is here to do." Gov. Katie Hobbs: "Arizona is at the heart of America's national defense."
- Not stated: Torrance size, customers, lead times, incentives.

- 2025-09-15, Los Angeles. Series E $290M at $2.3B valuation; $250M equity, $40M debt. Led by Rochefort Asset Management; no other investors named.
- Divergent Technologies, Inc. founded 2014, HQ Torrance CA. Platform: Divergent Adaptive Production System (DAPS), "the world's first end-to-end digital manufacturing platform" for rapid design, additive manufacturing and automated assembly. Builds hardware for aerospace, defense, automotive.
- Use of funds: scale manufacturing capacity; develop capabilities for upcoming product families.
- Leadership: Lukas Czinger, CEO and Co-Founder. Kyle Bass, Co-CEO Rochefort. George Biggs, Chief Communications Officer.
- Customers: early automotive Aston Martin, Bugatti, McLaren. Expanded to A&D in 2022 starting with General Atomics. Current A&D: General Atomics, Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, Triumph Group; contracts with dozens of A&D customers; orders from individual sustainment parts to full airframe systems.
- Revenue grew more than 5x in 2025. >200 new A&D part numbers in H1 2025; catalog >600 unique parts.
- Rochefort: US national-security-focused private asset manager, licensed manager under Department of War Office of Strategic Capital.
- Quotes: Lukas Czinger: "Divergent was founded to transform the built world with a software-defined manufacturing platform"; "This funding enables us to scale DAPS for aerospace and defense". Kyle Bass: "Divergent is proving that the U.S. can out-innovate and out-produce on the global stage."
- Not disclosed: headcount, factory count/size, lead times, pricing, margins, revenue dollars.

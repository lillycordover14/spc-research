September 15, 2025
By
Geoffrey von Maltzahn, PhD
CEO
Summary
Update: 
On 
Oct 15, 2025, we 
announced
 the close of our ($350M) Series A, bringing total capital raised to $550M.
Today I’m thrilled to share a milestone for Lila Sciences: a 
$235M Series A
, co-led by 
Braidwell
 and 
Collective Global
.
We’re deeply grateful for the conviction and support of our investors, including 
Altitude Life Science Ventures
, 
Alumni Ventures
, 
ARK Venture Fund
, 
Common Metal
, 
Flagship Pioneering
, 
General Catalyst
, 
March Capital
, 
the Mathers Foundation
, 
Modi Ventures
, 
NGS Super
, 
the State of Michigan Retirement System
, and a wholly owned subsidiary of 
Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (ADIA)
. Thank you for believing in us and joining this mission.
We intentionally assembled a group of long-term partners—leading AI and science investors alongside mission-driven backers. That mix gives us both deep domain expertise and strong values alignment as we scale.
Our Mission
From the beginning at Flagship, we set out to build 
scientific superintelligence
 to solve humankind’s greatest challenges—grounded in the conviction that AI can run 
every step of the scientific method
. With each passing day, this vision feels more imminent.
The scientific method is the most valuable wheel humankind has ever invented. When it turns faster, civilization leaps forward. 
Energy. Computing. Materials. Medicine. Diagnostics. Agriculture.
 The frontiers that matter most are powered by this wheel.
As we enter the intelligence revolution, the teams who can turn that wheel with the 
highest intelligence
, at the 
largest scale
, and at the 
greatest speed
 will deliver the most transformative advances.
Lila was founded on the insight that a unified 
AI, software, and robotics
 platform could autonomously drive this wheel across an extraordinary diversity of fields. That’s why we’re building an 
end-to-end platform
—so AI agents can conduct every step of the scientific method and bring scientific superintelligence to life.
Since 
emerging from stealth
 in March, we’ve accelerated progress toward an 
operating system for autonomous science
 across materials, chemistry, and life science. With this growth capital, we will take Lila’s platform to the next level of intelligence, scale, and speed—and move closer to enabling breakthroughs that might otherwise have taken decades.
AI Science Factories: A New Body for a Superintelligent Scientific Mind
Building a beautiful, superintelligent mind for science requires a new kind of 
body
 for science.
Lila’s AI Science Factories
 are that body—unified facilities where AI, novel software, and custom hardware close the loop between reasoning and real-world verification. Think of them as 
scientific-method machines
. Using large-scale reinforcement learning across both simulations and laboratory experiments, our models generate hypotheses, design experiments, run them, learn from the results, and iterate—
thousands of times over
. The result is a large-scale 
verifier for scientific reasoning
, not just a predictor.
Already, Lila’s systems have driven 
thousands of discoveries
 across life science, chemistry, and materials—offering early glimpses of the scientific superintelligence era to come.
With our first AI Science Factory, we have been able to close the loop across hundreds of thousands of AI-driven experiments. As we enter our Series A, these facilities will be the engines of our progress—allowing us to train models with 
higher levels of scientific intelligence than internet-scale data alone can achieve
.
What Becomes Possible When AI Turns the Wheel of Science
A unified platform for autonomous science enables a single scientific mind to run 
thousands of parallel experiments
 across fields that are typically siloed.
Medicines and diagnostics.
 We’re building agents that design and validate novel protein therapeutics, nucleic acids, chemistries, and diagnostic modalities. We’ve seen agents complete in 
minutes or hours
 workflows that traditionally take human teams 
days to weeks
—opening new paths against cancer, obesity, immune diseases, and beyond.
Materials and manufacturing.
 Our agents and AI Science Factory technology rapidly explore and validate breakthrough materials—from 
ultra-stable metals
 to 
new catalysts
 and 
engineered thin films
—accelerating clean-energy technologies and next-generation manufacturing.
Computing and energy.
 By unifying scientific reasoning with real-world verification, we can help unlock the 
next generation of compute
 and the materials that power it, while advancing sustainable energy systems at the pace the world now requires.
Our Next Phase
We’re expanding our team and opening new hubs in 
Boston
, 
San Francisco
, and 
London
. These facilities will house and scale our teams and AI Science Factories across distinct scientific domains—
controlled environments where AI can conduct experiments at scale
.
If the idea of building scientific superintelligence excites you, 
come build with us
. We’re seeking extraordinary, driven people across 
AI research, software, robotics, biology, chemistry, materials science, and operations
.
At Lila, we believe 
science is the most inspiring frontier for AI
. This financing comes from partners who share that belief—and it’s a commitment from us to turn it into reality. Our goal is simple: 
increase the intelligence, scale, and speed of science
 so humankind can experience life-changing benefits derived from artificial intelligence.  
Thank you to our new partners and—most of all—our team. The future of science is here. 
Let’s turn the wheel.
With gratitude,
Geoff von Maltzahn, Ph.D.
Co-founder and CEO, Lila Sciences

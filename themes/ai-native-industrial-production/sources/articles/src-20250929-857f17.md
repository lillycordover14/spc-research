- Note: article date approximated to the launch date; MasonOS launched on stage 29 Sep 2025 at the Resilience Conference, London.
- Isembard founded 2024 by CEO/founder Alexander Fitzgerald. Describes itself as a manufacturer that also builds its own software.
- MasonOS: automates precision manufacturing of components for weapons, drones, planes, satellites. At its most basic an ERP platform to run factories in a more integrated way, including across multiple factories. Quoting uses ML/AI. Scheduling for efficiency. Supply chain automation incl. inventory management. AI inspection for QC. Integrates with modern machines to run processes via robotics. CAM not mentioned.
- Built in-house; Isembard's factories run on it.
- Users/business model: franchising MasonOS to small and medium, often independently owned factories in UK, Europe, US. Planned user categories: Isembard-owned factories; franchised factories using MasonOS; third parties licensing the software white-label for their own factories.
- Certifications: AS9100, ISO 9001, ITAR, Cyber Essentials, JOSCAR.
- Performance claims (company): throughput +40% to 60%; operating costs -30%.
- Customers: a few large undisclosed customers (buyers of parts).
- Not disclosed: pricing/licensing fees, factory counts/locations, lead times, headcount.
- Funding: Apr 2025 $9M seed led by Notion Capital (Notion partly backed by UK NSSIF and German Federal Government). Others: 201 Ventures, Basis Capital, Forward Fund, Material Ventures, Never Lift Ventures, NP-Hard Ventures. Angels: Andreas Klinger (Prototype Capital), Charlie Delingpole (ComplyAdvantage), Joshua Western (SpaceForge), Salar al Khafaji (Monumental). Undisclosed debt raised to fund Isembard's own factories.
- Founder: biochemistry degree; founded fibre company Cuckoo (acquired by Giganet, majority-owned by Octopus Investments); did McDonald's management training while planning Isembard.
- Context: UK manufacturing 9% of GDP in 2023 vs 18% in 1990 (ONS); defence VC investment up >200%.
- Quotes: Fitzgerald: "we actually build our own software and that's what our factories are running on" / "a network of small -and medium-sized factories" / "an enterprise resource planning platform to run factories in more integrated ways"

- CXO Digital Pulse, published Oct 22, 2025
- Periodic Labs emerged from stealth; mission: combine AI, robotics and simulation to accelerate materials discovery, 'from superconductors to advanced compounds'
- Seed: $300M; lead Felicis (Peter Deng reportedly secured deal on an informal 'pitch walk' in SF); others: a16z, DST, NVentures, Accel, Jeff Bezos, Elad Gil, Eric Schmidt, Jeff Dean; investor 'funding frenzy' after Fedus left OpenAI; no OpenAI backing
- Founders: William (Liam) Fedus, formerly OpenAI; Ekin Dogus Cubuk, former Google Brain researcher; concept began ~7 months before announcement
- Headcount: over two dozen specialists across AI, physics, materials science; weekly internal lectures
- Planned system: robotic arms for powder synthesis; ML models for rapid simulation; LLMs for reasoning and optimization; goal is an AI-powered lab that runs experiments, analyzes data and improves autonomously; failed experiments generate essential data; robots being trained for more advanced synthesis
- Prior result: Cubuk's Google AI-guided robotic lab produced 41 novel compounds
- Initial focus: new superconducting materials
- Not stated: valuation, business model, customers, HQ/facilities

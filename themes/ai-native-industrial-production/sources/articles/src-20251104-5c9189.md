- Published 4 Nov 2025 by Sean O'Kane. Announced in Rivian's Q3 shareholder letter. Rivian's second 2025 spinoff after Also Inc. (Mar 2025).
- Focus: "industrial AI to reshape how physical world businesses operate"; Rivian operations data as the foundation for a robotics data flywheel. Rivian declined to clarify further.
- Funding: $115M seed led by Eclipse. Jiten Behl (Eclipse partner, formerly at Rivian) disclosed the investment on LinkedIn.
- Rivian is a shareholder (Scaringe on investor call); stake size not given. RJ Scaringe to serve as chairman of Mind Robotics' board per SEC filing. No CEO, founders or executives named.
- Headcount, HQ not stated. Unclear whether Rivian employees are moving over; spokesperson declined to say.
- Only public footprint: a very broad trademark application (machinery, vehicles, "incubators for eggs"). No website at the time.
- Business model: not stated; Scaringe described products and robotic solutions to run Rivian's plants more efficiently.
- Context: Also Inc. funded by Eclipse and Greenoaks; humanoid companies incl. Tesla raising; GM building its own robotics/AI division.
- Quotes (Scaringe): "develop products and robotic solutions that allow us to run and operate our manufacturing plants more efficiently." / "direct control and direct influence over the design and development of advanced AI robotics" / "the potential for AI to really shift how we think about operating in the physical world" / (letter) "With our strong bench of technology talent and an innovation-driven culture".

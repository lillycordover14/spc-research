- Founder Zane Hengsperger: University of Michigan graduate (2022); originally from Windsor, Ontario; father owned a machine shop; after college wanted to make an impact in manufacturing; rapidly growing X account. Age and prior roles not stated.
- Company NOX Metals (noxmetals.co): cuts aluminum blocks to customer-specified sizes; does not produce aluminum itself. Goal: supply America's growing industrial base.
- Funding: pre-seed $4.6M; investors named: Y Combinator ("groups like Y-combinator"), Detroit Venture Partners. Revenue, valuation, round date not stated.
- Facility: sprawling factory complex near Detroit's Mexicantown; building from the 1920s; houses heavy machining and industrial storage tenants; rust-edged windows ~60 ft above floor. Address, sq ft, headcount not stated.
- Equipment: aluminum cutting saw on a large platform; motorized chain system (blue metal chains) carries blocks to the saw; cooling liquid applied during cutting; shavings collected for smelting/recycling.
- Products: aluminum only; large raw blocks from a foundry cut into smaller blocks.
- Customers: businesses doing detailed machining incl. tool-and-die makers, complex machining shops, consumer-product manufacturers. None named.
- Software: core goal is automation to speed up quoting, ordering and shipping of custom-cut metal; industry quoting/ordering can take weeks; article says NOX developed a system but does not name it.
- Not disclosed: pricing, lead times, revenue, margins, capacity.
- No direct quotes from Zane; paraphrase: Detroit remains an incredible hotbed of industrial manufacturing knowledge and resourcefulness.

**Funding & Investors**
- Closed a Series C totaling $124 million (Business Wire release, dated Feb. 4, 2026)
- Investors: Woven Capital (Toyota's growth-stage venture arm), Lockheed Martin Ventures, Balerion Space Ventures, and Strategic Development Fund (SDF)
- Lead investor and valuation: not disclosed

**Company & Founders**
- Headquartered in Los Angeles; founded in 2019
- Edward Mehr: CEO and co-founder (prior roles not stated)
- Headcount: not disclosed

**Facilities**
- Launching its first large-scale U.S. Intelligent Factory, described as the company's third facility overall
- Size: 200,000 sq ft, production-ready
- Capacity: houses up to 50 RoboCraftsman cells; produces "thousands" of complex structural assemblies annually for defense and aerospace
- Plant count beyond this, locations beyond LA, and equipment financing: not disclosed

**Products & Processes**
- RoboCraftsman: flagship software-defined manufacturing platform combining robotics and machine learning
- RoboForming: proprietary forming technology
- Integrates forming, machining, welding, and assembly in a single factory
- Targets missile structures, airframes, hypersonics, and aerospace/defense-grade metal structures
- Claims digital design to production in the same facility, compressing timelines "from months to days"
- Claims it can manufacture a range of structures without significant retooling

**Customers & Government Contracts**
- U.S. Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL): contract award
- U.S. Air Force Rapid Sustainment Office: contract award
- Unnamed leading defense prime: metal structures for missiles and hypersonics
- Toyota: production-quality automotive body panels
- References U.S. Department of War, defense primes, and mobility leaders as customers

**Financials**
- Pricing, margins, revenue, lead-time specifics beyond "months to days", and valuation: not disclosed

**Related releases listed on the page**
- Machina Bellator: defense subsidiary, managing a Lockheed Martin JASSM qualification contract (first assembly from a Machina factory to reach qualification for a defense missile system)
- CMMC Level 2 certification (January 2026 release)
- Automotive custom panel technology with Toyota Motor North America (September 2025 release, UP.Summit)

**Direct Quotes**
- Edward Mehr (CEO & Co-Founder): "The world's most advanced designs are being held back by 20th-century factories."
- Chris Moran (VP & GM, Lockheed Martin Ventures): "We believe Machina Labs' AI-driven manufacturing approach will play a key role in shaping the future of aerospace production."
- Phil Scully (GP & Co-Founder, Balerion Space Ventures): "Modern defense systems are often limited not by design, but by how fast they can be manufactured."
- Ro Gupta (Managing Director, Woven Capital): "Machina Labs is pioneering intelligent forming technology that brings craft-level precision to industrial scale."

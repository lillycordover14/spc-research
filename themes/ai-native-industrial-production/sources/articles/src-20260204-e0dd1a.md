**Funding and Investors**
- Machina Labs raised $124 million in a round described as a Series C.
- Investors in the Series C: Lockheed Martin Ventures, Balerion Space Ventures, Woven Capital, and the Strategic Development Fund (Abu Dhabi, owned by EDGE Group).

**Facility Plans ("Intelligent Factory")**
- Funded in part by a "significant chunk" of the $124 million raise.
- Will be upward of 200,000 square feet.
- Will be the company's first facility dedicated to mass production.
- The company's two existing plants are each less than half that size.
- Initial output: missile structures and airframes.
- Location: likely not in California. Mehr said talks are ongoing with three states, naming New Mexico, Texas, and Alabama, and that Reno, Nevada has "recently" entered consideration.
- No timing, cost, or construction schedule was given.

**Business Mix and Customers**
- Current business split: about 70% defense, 30% commercial.
- Machina has already worked with the U.S. Air Force.
- It is coordinating with European and Israeli partners (unnamed).

**Leadership**
- CEO: Edward Mehr, who spoke to Axios.

**Quotes**
- Mehr: "We're going to see a reinvigoration of blue collar -- but it's a different blue collar," "enabled with robotics and iPads and things that allow them to be significantly more productive"
- Mehr: "We've been talking with three different states,"
- Mehr: "We're very patriotic. We want to make sure defense is supplied," "But, at the same time, we need to make sure that we're smart about this."

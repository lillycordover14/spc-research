**Deal and Company**
- Machina Labs closed a $124 million Series C round, announced the week of February 5, 2026.
- Investors: Woven Capital (Toyota's growth-stage venture arm), Lockheed Martin Ventures, Balerion Space Ventures, and Strategic Development Fund (SDF).
- Founded in 2019 and based in Los Angeles. Edward Mehr is co-founder and CEO.
- Not stated: total raised to date, valuation, prior roles of founders, headcount.

**Facility**
- U.S. smart factory of 200,000 sq ft (18,580.6 sq m), described as production-ready.
- Houses up to 50 RoboCraftsman cells.
- Planned output: thousands of structural assemblies per year for defense and aerospace customers.
- Designed to make a range of complex metal structures, from missile structures to airframes, without significant retooling.
- Claims it compresses timelines from months to days.
- Not stated: number of facilities beyond this one, cells sold or deployed to date, equipment financing.

**Technology**
- RoboCraftsman platform: an AI-driven manufacturing cell that combines advanced robotics with AI-driven process controls.
- Integrates forming, machining, welding, and assembly in one facility.
- Customers can move from digital design to production in the same facility. Described as software-defined manufacturing.

**Customers and Contracts**
- U.S. Air Force Research Laboratory: contract award.
- U.S. Air Force Rapid Sustainment Office: contract award.
- Unnamed "leading defense prime": metal structures for missiles and hypersonics.
- Toyota: production-quality automotive panels.
- Target markets: aerospace, defense, and automotive; dual-use.

**Quotes**
- Edward Mehr (CEO): "The world's most advanced designs are being held back by 20th-century factories"
- Edward Mehr (CEO): "This round allows us to scale manufacturing infrastructure that moves at the speed of software."
- Chris Moran (VP and GM, Lockheed Martin Ventures): "The launch of their new factory marks a major step forward"
- Phil Scully (GP and co-founder, Balerion Space Ventures): "Modern defense systems are often limited not by design, but by how fast they can be manufactured,"
- Ro Gupta (managing director, Woven Capital): "Machina Labs is pioneering intelligent forming technology that brings craft-level precision to industrial scale,"

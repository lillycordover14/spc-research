Under the agreement Phlow will produce the API for Epinephrine Injection USP and Fresenius Kabi will formulate finished doses. Phlow completed a US validation campaign and filed a Drug Master File. Pending FDA, could be available 2027. Phlow website: Petersburg campus kilo-scale cGMP facility 0.3-3 kg/day, ~250 kg/yr, batch + continuous flow for hazardous steps; metric-ton facility 50-100 t API per year.

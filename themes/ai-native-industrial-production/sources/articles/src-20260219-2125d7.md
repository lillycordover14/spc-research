- Freeform raised a $67M Series B to expand its manufacturing platform. Declined to disclose valuation; PitchBook cites $179M. No lead named.
- Investors: Apandion, AE Ventures, Founders Fund, Linse Capital, NVentures (Nvidia), Threshold Ventures, Two Sigma Ventures.
- Product: novel metal 3D printing system. Current system GoldenEye uses 18 lasers to fuse metal powders. Next version Skyfall would use hundreds of lasers and produce thousands of kilograms of metal parts per day; built for throughput and flexibility with active software controls; Palitsch calls it "AI native".
- Founders: Erik Palitsch, CEO and co-founder, developed rocket engines at SpaceX, started the Freeform vision in 2018. Cameron Kay head of talent. Thomas Ronacher originally reported as president; correction says he has left.
- Infrastructure: Nvidia partnership for GPUs; H200 clusters in an on-site data center; real-time physics-based simulations plus sensor data from the platform to improve production quality and quantity.
- Operations: delivering hundreds of mission-critical parts weekly; customers undisclosed; plans up to 100 new hires and facility expansion to execute contract backlog.
- Context: Hadrian recently at $1.6B valuation; VulcanForms and Divergent raised hundreds of millions for metal printing services.
- Quotes: Palitsch: "I think we're the only quote-unquote manufacturing company out there that has H200 clusters in a data center on site." Kay: "We have more meaningful data on the physics of the metal-printing process than any company in the world."
- Not stated: headcount, facility size/location, revenue, margins, pricing, lead times, business model detail.

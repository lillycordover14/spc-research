**Publication**
- "Is this man the future of US manufacturing?" by Katherine Laidlaw, The Hustle, published February 27, 2026.

**Subject**
- Jim Belosic is co-founder and CEO of SendCutSend, Reno, Nevada. Called the "Etsy of steel"; "accidentally built the Amazon of sheet metal."

**US vs. China context**
- 2010: China manufacturing output $2.373T vs US $2.365T; China pulled ahead by 23% after about a year. Today China 28% of global manufacturing output; US 17%.

**Founder background (Belosic)**
- Teen lawn-mowing business in rural Nevada. Taught himself to code after high school. Web designer, then ran a B2B SaaS software company.
- Tinkered with electric car conversions (a friend's DeLorean, the "Teslonda", a 1981 Honda Accord with a Tesla motor). Found it hard to order one-off brackets because manufacturers preferred large runs.
- Developed software to help small shops take small orders; initially no one was interested.

**Financing and capex**
- 2018: financed a $750k laser machine, with his wife's support. Plan was to rent out the machine to hobbyists to cover costs.

**Customers and scale**
- Early customers were hobbyists in garages. Works with ~60% of Fortune 500. Nine of ten private rocket launch companies have used SendCutSend.
- 30M+ pieces for 300K+ customers. Cut 8M pieces last year. High-mix, low-volume model.

**Facilities and operations**
- Three factories: Paris, Kentucky; Arlington, Texas; Reno, Nevada. Runs 24/7 with backup machines for each machine. Scouting a fourth factory location.
- Nests hundreds of pieces on one sheet of aluminum, copper, or steel. Recently expanded into CNC metal cutting (lathes, mills, drills).

**Processes**
- Laser cutting, tapping, bending, powder coating, packing and shipping. Customer design review: SendCutSend selects the material or returns the design with suggested changes.

**Software**
- Belosic's early software for smaller shops is now the blueprint for SendCutSend's factories. Modules not specified.

**Lead times and pricing**
- Total turnaround about 48 hours, design to delivery. Fastest job so far: 32 minutes. Chinese manufacturers' lead times: two to three weeks. Steel can cost about twice as much in the US as in China (chart of hot-rolled band and cold-rolled coil, Jan 2026).

**Direct quotes (Jim Belosic)**
- "Sometimes we get mistaken for a Chinese company."
- "No one wanted to shut their line down for just one guy."
- "If I needed a thousand units, no problem, but to get one, manufacturing wasn't set up to do that."
- "We're taking something that only existed in your head yesterday, making it from scratch"
- "There has never been a better day to start making something in the US than today."

**Not reported**
- Revenue, profitability, margins, valuation, investors; headcount; square footage and machine counts; waterjet; pricing.

- Funding: $50M Series A, announced 9 Mar 2026 (Dallas and London dateline); posted 10 Mar 2026. Less than 12 months after seed.
- Lead: Union Square Ventures (Rebecca Kaden, Managing Partner). New: Tamarack Global, IQ Capital. Existing: Notion Capital, CIV.
- Angels: Alex Bouaziz (Founder/CEO Deel), Andrei Danescu (Founder/CEO Dexory Robotics), Matt Briers (former CFO Wise).
- Valuation, headcount, revenue, margins, pricing: not disclosed.
- Founder/CEO: Alexander Fitzgerald.
- Facilities: 6 factories now; target 25 by end of 2026. Countries: US, UK, Germany, France, Ukraine (DE, FR, UA described as new launches).
- Model: company-owned factories plus franchisee factories; franchisees can build new or convert existing businesses. Specific US counts/locations not disclosed. Engineering hiring planned across Europe and the US.
- Products: high-precision components for aerospace, defense, robotics; energy cited as demand sector. Customers not named. Lead times/pricing not disclosed.
- Software: MasonOS, proprietary agentic AI platform / operating layer covering quoting, scheduling, supply chain, manufacturing, quality control, delivery; continuously optimizes factory performance.
- Market: component manufacturing ~$1.8T/yr; ~95% of production by small businesses; average owner over 65; 40% plan to retire within 5 years. Demand from aerospace, defense, energy, robotics; reshoring.
- Quotes: Fitzgerald: "Manufacturing is the origin of our security, prosperity and sense of purpose as nations." / "Our mission is to forge industrial acceleration." Kaden (USV): "Isembard is redefining the process of owning and running a factory." / "MasonOS lowers the barrier to operating high-performance manufacturing businesses"

- $500M Series A co-led by Accel and Andreessen Horowitz, announced Wed 11 Mar 2026 (article 9:12am PDT). Prior $115M seed led by Eclipse late 2025. Total $615M. Valuation ~$2B per WSJ (first reported the round). No other investors named.
- Industrial robotics lab spun out of Rivian; spinout completed Nov 2025. RJ Scaringe (Rivian CEO/founder) created it and serves as chairman.
- Mission: train industrial robots to be more dexterous and adaptable using data from Rivian's EV factory; the factory is also where robots are deployed and tested. Building AI models, hardware and deployment infrastructure. Traditional factory robot designs, not humanoids. Scaringe told WSJ a large number of robots would be deployed by end of 2026.
- Rivian relationship: source of training data and deployment site. Dec 2025 Rivian announced custom silicon for AV software; Scaringe said it is an easy leap to imagine Rivian selling those chips to Mind ("It's a robotics processor").
- Sister company Also (Rivian's first 2025 spinout): e-bikes and small cargo EVs for Amazon; Eclipse-backed; later $200M from Greenoaks at ~$1B.
- Not covered: headcount, offices, customers beyond Rivian, pricing/business model, data rights beyond training on Rivian factory data, VW relationship.
- Quotes: press release: "was founded to address a structural gap with current industrial automation solutions" / "Mind Robotics is building the AI foundation - models, hardware, and deployment infrastructure - to close that gap." Scaringe (WSJ): "Doing cartwheels does not create value in manufacturing". Scaringe (TechCrunch): "it doesn't take a lot of imagination" / "It's a robotics processor, so it could work really well for that".

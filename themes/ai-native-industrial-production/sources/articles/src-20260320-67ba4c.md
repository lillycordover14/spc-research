- Navy and Hadrian launched a public-private partnership: $900M Navy funding; Hadrian investing more than $1.5B private capital (about $2.4B combined). SECNAV John Phelan called it a "novel transaction" investing in "outcomes, not promises."
- Factory 4, Cherokee, Alabama, opened 2026-03-20 (ribbon cutting). First of three facilities Hadrian plans for the maritime industrial base; two more planned, locations not given.
- Products: components for Virginia-class and Columbia-class submarines; "sequence-critical and commodity components" including parts, assemblies and finished products by end of 2026; full operation within two years.
- Jobs: up to 1,000 (Hadrian estimate). Automation target ~80%. New workforce trained in 30 days or less (CEO).
- Context: Virginia and Columbia delays; 2024 Navy report on shipyard workforce and supply chain; tied to Trump "Golden Fleet" effort.
- Quotes: John Phelan: "The Department of the Navy has a stake in the outcome, and American taxpayers will benefit from our and their success"; "we are done with a system that rewards process over delivery." Chris Power: "We want to be about 80 percent automated". Sen. Katie Britt: "This is going to build modules for the strongest, most advanced submarines in the world".
- Not covered: Opus, contract vehicle terms, lead times, pricing, factory size.

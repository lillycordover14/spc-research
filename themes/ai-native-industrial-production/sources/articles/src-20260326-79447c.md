- Published Mar 26, 2026 (Dealroom News, Fundraise); sources Bloomberg (Mar 25, 2026) and Tech Funding News
- Periodic Labs: San Francisco-based AI startup, founded May 2025; AI for materials science + lab automation; one of the 'neolabs' pairing AI models with physical robotic experimentation
- Founders: Liam Fedus (former OpenAI VP of research; helped build ChatGPT; founded materials research groups at Google Brain/DeepMind per article), Ekin Dogus Cubuk (former Google DeepMind scientist)
- Seed: $300M, Sep 2025, $1.3B valuation; investors Andreessen Horowitz, Felicis, Accel
- Mar 2026: early talks to raise 'hundreds of millions' at ~$7B valuation (Bloomberg); ~5x step-up in six months; terms could change
- Has paying customers in the semiconductor industry
- Comparables: World Labs $1B (Fei-Fei Li), AMI Labs $1.03B (Yann LeCun); article calls Periodic possibly the most commercially grounded
- Risks noted: unproven at scale; materials science is slow; commercialization takes time and capital

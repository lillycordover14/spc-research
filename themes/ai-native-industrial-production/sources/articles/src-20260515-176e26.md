- 15 May 2026, eWeek, Aminu Abdullahi. Mind Robotics raised an additional $400M led by Kleiner Perkins; total funding >$1B; valuation ~$3.4B per a person familiar cited by WSJ (eWeek did not confirm). Participants: Salesforce Ventures, Incharge Capital (VW-tied venture arm).
- Linked teaser: Nov 2025 spinout secured ~$110M seed led by Eclipse, RJ Scaringe chairman.
- Founded 2025 by RJ Scaringe (Rivian CEO). Originally internal Rivian effort "Project Synapse"; spun out late 2025. Rivian is strategic partner and shareholder.
- Deploying and training AI-powered robots at Rivian's Normal, Illinois factory (real-world testing ground); moving from research into live industrial deployment. No other customers named.
- Product: full-stack system combining AI models, robotics hardware, deployment infrastructure (per AI Business). Target tasks: assembly, material handling, inspection, precision manipulation. Aim to move beyond pre-programmed repetitive industrial robots.
- Competitors mentioned: Figure AI, Agility Robotics, Amazon, Tesla.
- Not covered: headcount, HQ, pricing/business model, data ownership, product names.
- Quotes: Ilya Fushman (Kleiner Perkins): robotics is "the ultimate frontier for what's possible with AI" and "the biggest market out there".

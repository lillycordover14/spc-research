- Inc., '17 Startups Rebuilding America in 2026', Leah Carroll, May 19, 2026 (reporting by Chloe Aiello, Melissa Angell, Jennifer Conrad, Ben Sherry).
- Hertha Metals: single-step iron and steel; 20,000 sq ft plant Conroe TX; founded 2022; $17M (Breakthrough Energy Foundation, Khosla Ventures, Pear VC, Clean Energy Ventures); commercial high-purity iron by 2028, steel by 2030/2031; founder Laureen Meroueh.
- Brimstone: low-carbon cement, steel, alumina from silicate rock; demo plant Reno NV, full Rock Refinery site TBD, commercial by 2030; founded 2019; Oakland moving to Reno; $80M (Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Amazon Climate Pledge); founder Cody Finke.
- Redwood Materials: battery recycling and critical minerals, energy storage; Sparks NV campus; founded 2017; $2.3B (Google, NVentures, Capricorn, Goldman, Ford); ~1,000 employees; JB Straubel.
- Sila Nanotechnologies: silicon anodes; 600,000+ sq ft Moses Lake WA, ~1 t/day; founded 2011; $1.3B (Sutter Hill, Bessemer, T. Rowe, Coatue); ~400 employees.
- Harbinger Motors: EV chassis and battery packs; 165,000 sq ft Garden Grove CA; founded 2021; $358M (FedEx, Coca-Cola fund).
- LuxWall: insulated glass; Litchfield MI plant, 276,000 sq ft Detroit factory late 2027; $167M (Breakthrough, Barclays, Khosla, 2150, Prelude, Climate Investment).
- Aalo Atomics: SMR mass manufacturing; Austin TX, +250,000 sq ft; $136M (Hitachi Ventures, NRG, Tishman Speyer); founded 2023.
- Montana Knife Company: knives; Missoula MT; bootstrapped; $18M facility with $1.5M state/federal loans; ~$50M revenue, 120 employees.
- Smithey Ironware: cast iron and carbon steel cookware; North Charleston SC; bootstrapped; 79 employees.
- Others: Little Leaf Farms ($408M), Claimable, Parallel Systems ($100M), Via Separations ($107M), Membrion ($46M), OpenTug ($5.3M seed), Mammoth Biosciences ($465M), Gradiant ($600M+, $500M+ revenue).

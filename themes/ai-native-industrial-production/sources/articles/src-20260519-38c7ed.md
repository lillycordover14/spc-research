**Deal and funding (announced May 19, 2026, Reno, Nev.)**
- Raised $110M, reaching a valuation of $1.01B (headline says "surpasses $1B"). First institutional round.
- Co-led by Sequoia, Paradigm, and Patrick and John Collison.
- Prior round: $6M friends-and-family round with Sandy Kory and Mark Sugarman.
- Company was largely bootstrapped and self-funding until this round; founder says it turned down daily investor inquiries until plans were ready; kept board control and operational independence.

**Commitments and use of proceeds**
- Five-year initiative with a $1B investment in new U.S. manufacturing jobs and domestically produced materials.
- More than $250M for expanding existing facilities and establishing new high-tech manufacturing hubs across the U.S.
- Capital goes to advanced equipment and new facilities. Goals: greater capabilities, wider product selection, lower prices, faster production.

**Founders and history**
- Founded in 2018, starting in a garage, by two software engineers. Jim Belosic is founder and CEO.
- Originated from frustration with limited access to commercial manufacturing.
- Inc. 5000 and Deloitte Technology Fast 500 listings; ranked sixth on an Inc. list; 2025 Gold Stevie Award.

**Locations and facilities**
- Three facilities: Reno, Nev. (HQ: 4855 Longley Ln, Reno, NV 89502), Paris, Ky., and Arlington, Texas. Serves the U.S. and Canada.
- New facilities planned, no locations or square footage given. Headcount not disclosed.

**Customers and volume**
- More than 30 million parts shipped; more than 300,000 customers; 59% of Fortune 500 companies are customers.
- Industries: aerospace, defense, space flight, data centers, robotics, automation, transportation, marine, agriculture, distribution, manufacturing.
- Orders range from single prototypes to tens of thousands of production parts. No minimum quantities.

**Products and processes**
- Laser cutting, sheet metal cutting, CNC machining, finishing; site also lists CNC bending and forming, welding, countersinking, dimple forming, hardware insertion, tapping, tumbling, deburring, anodizing, plating, powder coating. Commercial and Marketplace offerings.

**Software**
- Instant quote with a "buy-now" interface, described as treating manufacturing "as a software problem". Named in-house modules not specified.

**Lead times and pricing**
- Shipping in as little as two days; sidebar: expedited custom manufacturing in 48 hours. No price tables, margins.

**Revenue and financials**
- No revenue or margin figures. Sequoia's Andrew Reed says the company has been "a high-growth, profitable company for eight years". Equipment financing terms not disclosed.

**Other sidebar news**
- Launched a Marketplace for entrepreneurs to monetize designs; expanded enterprise/business-team features.

**Direct quotes**
- Jim Belosic: "By waiting, we built real muscle."
- Andrew Reed (Sequoia): "Jim really loves making parts, and SendCutSend's customer-first ethos is already famous."
- Matt Huang (Paradigm): "Jim and the SendCutSend team have quietly built the fastest manufacturing platform in the country,"
- Patrick Collison: "A new transformation in speed and flexibility is afoot today, and SendCutSend is the market leader."

**Note:** the $1B figure is a five-year investment commitment, not the valuation ($1.01B).

- Fast Company, Adele Peters, published May 28, 2026
- Radical AI: CEO Joseph Krause; $55M seed raised in 2025; investors/valuation/headcount not stated
- Facilities: lab in midtown Manhattan built in 2025; moving to a larger space at Brooklyn Navy Yard with more automation equipment; square footage not stated
- Equipment: robotic arm handles small glass bottles, weighs and mixes pellets of iron and other elements; machines melt mixture into alloys; machines analyze composition and structure and test hardness, oxidation resistance, heat resistance; overhead track carries samples between stations; standard materials-science equipment almost entirely automated and run by AI
- Software: AI scientist designs experiments, reviews literature, forms hypotheses; reads ~10,000 papers in 5 seconds; references 380,000 papers and 57 million lab data points incl. failed experiments; runs quantum chemistry calculations; 'active learning loop' analyzing new data in real time (Krause believes first company to do this); human scientists annotate results (e.g. cracks) to teach intuition
- Process: customers or internal teams give target properties; AI proposes ~12 to a few hundred materials per problem; experiments run continuously incl. overnight; AI resumes at 4am on new ideas
- Throughput: up to 50 experiments/day now; target 100/day by end of summer 2026; human scientist ~50/year
- Results: ~300 novel compositions over 16 weeks; top candidates sent to Purdue Applied Research Institute, verified to outperform the leading material of its type; specifics withheld pending patents
- Materials: initial focus metal alloys; applications jet engine parts (heat resistance, longer life, efficiency), defense, fusion reactor components; plans to expand beyond alloys
- Business model, named customers, path to manufactured part: not stated
- Peers named: Lila Sciences ($350M Series A), Orbital, CuspAI (PFAS removal material)
- Context: traditional materials development 20+ years
- Quotes (Joseph Krause): 'This AI-driven process is really a radical shift to allowing us to scale scientific discovery'; 'You go from 10 scientists focused on one problem to one scientist focused on 10 problems at a time'; 'our AI system can read 10,000 papers in five seconds'; 'There is not a single system on Earth that will move forward without a novel material'; 'Materials are the gateway to the next age of innovation.'

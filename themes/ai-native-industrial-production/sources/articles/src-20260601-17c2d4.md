232 pharma regime: 100% default on patented drugs/APIs/KSMs, 20% with onshoring plans, temporary 0% with MFN pricing until 2029; Annex III deadline July 31, 2026, others Sept 29, 2026; generics exempt but under review. Reshoring pledges >$480B across 22 sites and ~44,000 jobs, but equipment suppliers have not seen matching orders (CFR: 15% of pledges to equipment would be >$75B of orders). Endpoint: continuous manufacturing linking KSMs, APIs and finished dose.

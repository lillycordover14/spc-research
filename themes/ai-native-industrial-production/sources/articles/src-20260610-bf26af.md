Twelve opened AirPlant One June 10, 2026 in Moses Lake WA, first commercial-scale US plant producing E-Jet power-to-liquid SAF from CO2, water and renewable electricity, plus E-Naphtha. Producing on-spec ASTM jet fuel being delivered and sold. In 2022 Alaska and Microsoft jointly committed to purchase output, the signal that made financing possible. Alaska Star Ventures participated in Twelve's $645M funding round. Microsoft: Climate Innovation Fund investment and SAF offtake via book-and-claim.

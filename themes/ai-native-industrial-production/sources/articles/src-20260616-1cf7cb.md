- Nox Metals closed an $11.5 million seed round. Lead investor: Hyperion (New York City). Other participants: Y Combinator, Palmer Luckey, RoboStrategy, Alumni Ventures, Operator Collective, DTX Ventures. Valuation, round date and use-of-funds split not stated.

- Business: custom aluminum blocks for CNC machining. Uses AI automation that the company says can cut lead times from "days to seconds."
- Plans to revitalize a WW2-era, 30,000 square foot factory in Detroit "this summer" (2026) to house its technology team.
- Founder and CEO: Zane Hengsperger. Prior roles not stated. Headcount, customers, pricing, margins not stated.
- Article published June 16, 2026 by Luciano Marcon (Daily Detroit).
- Quotes (Hengsperger, company post): "Our metal has gone to space. It has protected our troops. It is in your car and in the machine that scanned your chest." "And we can't stop supplying at warp speeds." "We will be tripling down on technology, which has allowed us to move this fast for America thus far."

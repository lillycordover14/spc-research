- Business Wire release, Jul 14, 2026 (release id 20260714)
- Funding: ~$115M total incl. $100M Series A; lead Kleiner Perkins; participants Bain Capital Ventures, Glade Brook Capital Partners, BANNER VC, Saga Ventures, Trust Ventures, Definition, PEAK6, Magnetar Capital, Ravelin Capital; angels: founders, executives, engineers from SpaceX, Anduril, Base Power, Shinkei, Hadrian; valuation not disclosed; use of funds: expand engineering, manufacturing, operations and construction teams; continue semi-autonomous heavy equipment development
- Company: TerraFirma; tech-enabled, vertically integrated construction company focused on critical infrastructure, initially robotic earthworks and site operations; HQ Austin, Texas; founded 2024; terrafirma.inc; LinkedIn terrafirmatech; X @TerraFirma_Inc; hiring engineers and construction professionals
- Founders: Noah Schochet (CEO), Noah McGuinness (CTO); met at Princeton; worked at SpaceX on Starlink, Starshield, Starship; saw how fast complex physical systems get built when hardware, software and operations develop together
- Problem: US construction labor productivity down ~0.6%/yr since 1965 while economy grew ~1.6%/yr (Goldman Sachs analysis); ~$1T every five years (NBER working paper); claims construction could be up to 10x faster, cheaper, safer
- Platform: AI-enabled pre-construction software; remote command-and-control center; retrofitted semi-autonomous heavy machinery (excavators, dozers, loaders, rollers, skid steers, more); no operator in cab; operators control fleets remotely from screens; teleoperation with autonomy as a component; each operator up to 300% more effective; retrofit kits not sold; converts machines it uses itself; fleet size and hours not disclosed
- Business model: vertically integrated; builds and operates its own robotics and software in the field; secures government and commercial contracts; pricing not disclosed
- Projects: North Austin Starbucks site prep/excavation/grading; Spicewood sports arena; New Braunfels power substation; US government mission-critical international infrastructure and logistics projects in challenging environments (unnamed); sectors: housing, energy, transportation, manufacturing, education
- Long-term: 'the largest construction company in the solar system', Moon and Mars infrastructure
- Quotes: Noah Schochet: 'Construction is the foundation everything else is built on, and it's been going backward for fifty years'; 'TerraFirma exists to help America build again and then take that capacity into the cosmos'; '...will be highly reusable to solve those same challenges on the Moon and Mars'; Noah McGuinness: 'It is not about trying to fully automate construction equipment'; Josh Coyne (Partner, Kleiner Perkins): 'TerraFirma is succeeding at real-world scale, proving the business model works'

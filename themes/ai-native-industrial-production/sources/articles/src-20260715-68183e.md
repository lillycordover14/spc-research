**Article:** "A SpaceX vet raised $65M to pull wire harnesses out of the Cold War era," by Tim Fernholz (Senior Reporter, TechCrunch), published July 15, 2026.

**Company and founders**
- Company: Senra (a play on "harness" with the "h" and "s" removed; CEO Jordan Black says it removes the "horsesh*t" from harnesses).
- Founded in 2023.
- CEO Jordan Black was a SpaceX engineer, where he took on scaling the company's wire harnesses for Starship.
- Co-founder: Benjamin Shanahan (no prior role stated).

**Funding**
- $65 million Series B. Co-led by Lowercarbon and Interlagos.
- Participants include General Catalyst, Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, and Founders Fund, "among others."
- No valuation disclosed.

**Product and market**
- Wire harnesses: internal electrical cabling for rockets, cars, planes, and tractors; grow more important as vehicles get smarter.
- Harnesses are bespoke and built by experienced technicians.
- Target customers are vehicle manufacturers. Black said customers include builders of submarines and maritime vehicles, defense land vehicle systems, launch vehicles, and satellites. None named.
- Context: surging U.S. manufacturing investment, especially in the defense industrial base.

**Software**
- Proprietary platform called Amp. Standardizes inputs across the wiring process. Produces a digital twin to guide technicians. Tracks materials and engineering changes in one system.

**Training**
- Senra trains its own technicians. Black says it is the only federally certified wire harness training program.

**Automation**
- Senra is not removing humans from the process for now, citing robots' difficulty manipulating wires and scarce training data.
- Adding software and automation to modernize manual work; plans to automate more over time.

**Production and facilities**
- Two factories. Output: about 1,000 harnesses per month. Target: 10,000 per month in 2027.
- Headcount, square footage, and any third facility not disclosed.

**Industry context**
- In 2023, Boeing found its Starliner spacecraft wiring was held together with flammable tape; the fix forced an expensive delay and a full wiring redo.

**Not disclosed**
- Headcount, revenue, margins, pricing, lead times, government contracts, equipment financing, facility square footage.

**Direct quotes (Jordan Black)**
- "I traveled all over the world to go visit wire harness companies,"
- "It really hasn't changed since the Cold War era of wooden tables [and] manual processes."
- "Having it all in the same software is probably the most important thing"
- "It goes back to the Elon principle of, 'automation is last,'"

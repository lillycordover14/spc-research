**Deal and Funding**
- Raised $65 million Series B, announced July 15, 2026 in Cypress, Calif.
- Total funding raised to date: over $112 million.
- Co-led by Lowercarbon Capital and Interlagos.
- Other participants: General Catalyst, Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Founders Fund, Dylan Field, CIV, 8VC, The Friedkin Group, Jaws Estates Capital, Sozo Ventures, and Alumni Ventures.
- Use of proceeds: accelerating expansion, including a planned third factory ("Factory 3").
- Valuation, pricing, margins, revenue: not disclosed. Equipment financing and government contracts: not mentioned.

**Company and Leadership**
- Software-driven manufacturer modernizing wire harness production for aerospace and defense.
- Jordan Black: co-founder and CEO.
- Ken Venner: recently hired as chief technology and product officer. Previously CIO at SpaceX, where he led enterprise systems and manufacturing infrastructure supporting production and launch cadence.
- Achal Upadhyaya: founder of Interlagos (co-lead). Caie Kelley: GP at Lowercarbon Capital (co-lead).
- Headcount: not disclosed.

**Facilities and Capacity**
- Factory 2: newly opened in Cypress, Calif.; about 80,000 square feet; expanded production footprint by 5X; second site for prototype-to-production programs; opened within months of signing the lease.
- Output: about 1,000 harnesses per month on average today, target 10,000 per month "by next year."
- Factory 3: planned, no location, size, or timeline.

**Products, Customers, Markets**
- Complex wire harness systems for aircraft, spacecraft, launch vehicles, satellites, defense systems and other advanced platforms.
- Target sectors named in investor quotes: aerospace, defense, energy, compute. No named customers.

**Software (Built In-House)**
- Amp: proprietary software platform integrating quoting, engineering, manufacturing, supply chain management, and production workflows into one system.
- Claimed benefits: faster onboarding, reduced production variability, shorter delivery timelines.

**Automation, Process, Workforce**
- Describes wire harness production as previously "100% manual" and built on PDFs, spreadsheets, and tribal knowledge.
- Applies "advanced automation" and digitized processes; no specific robotics detail.
- Structured training platform: technicians can build harnesses in weeks rather than years.
- Lead times: no numerical figures.

**Market Context**
- Surging defense demand for wire harnesses and a shrinking skilled workforce; capacity constraints threaten program schedules and weapons system readiness.

**Key Quotes**
- Jordan Black (CEO): "One of the biggest bottlenecks in aerospace and defense manufacturing today is the skyrocketing demand for wire harnesses."
- Caie Kelley (GP, Lowercarbon): "Wire harnesses sit behind everything that turns on, and they're still built by hand." / "Senra automates production and trains the workforce to run it."
- Achal Upadhyaya (Interlagos): "Senra is building a fully integrated design and manufacturing solution for a critical capability."
- Ken Venner (CTPO): "Our goal is not simply to manufacture products faster."

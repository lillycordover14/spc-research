- Equipment World, Jordanne Waldschmidt, published Jul 21, 2026
- TerraFirma: Austin, Texas; founded 2024; mission 'to make infrastructure construction faster, cheaper and safer on Earth, the Moon and Mars'; goal 'the largest construction company in the solar system'
- Founders: Noah Schochet (co-founder, CEO, ex-SpaceX engineer); Noah McGuinness (co-founder, CTO, ex-SpaceX engineer)
- Funding: $115M total; $100M Series A led by Kleiner Perkins; remainder from other VCs and angels; use of funds: engineering, manufacturing, operations, construction teams; semi-autonomous equipment development
- Stack: AI-enabled pre-construction software; remote command-and-control center; retrofitted semi-autonomous heavy machinery (excavators, dozers, loaders, rollers, skid steers, more); operators manage fleets remotely from screens; up to 300% more effective; semi-autonomous/teleoperated, not fully autonomous
- Projects: North Austin Starbucks site prep/excavation/grading with Starbucks, Reger Holdings, Flintrock Commercial, Thomas Randolph Excavation; completed: Spicewood sports arena, New Braunfels power substation; US government critical infrastructure and logistics in challenging environments worldwide; sectors housing, energy, transportation, manufacturing, education
- Competes with OEM brands and startups (article links to Caterpillar, Doosan Bobcat, Komatsu, Case coverage)
- Not disclosed: valuation, headcount, facilities, fleet size, hours, business model specifics, pricing, revenue, OEM partnerships, retrofit kit details
- Hiring engineers and construction professionals; seeking customers for upcoming large-scale projects
- Quotes: Noah Schochet: 'Construction is the foundation everything else is built on, and it's been going backward for fifty years'; 'The technology that we are building today to solve critical challenges here on Earth will be highly reusable'; Noah McGuinness: 'It is not about trying to fully automate construction equipment'; 'We believe autonomy is a part of the solution'; Josh Coyne (Kleiner Perkins): 'TerraFirma is succeeding at real-world scale, proving the business model works'

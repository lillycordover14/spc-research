- Company: 1872.ai Inc., self-described "AI-native" steel-fabrication partner. Launched by engineers with prior SpaceX experience.
- Dan Summers: CEO, previous SpaceX production and flight-software experience (per 1872.ai About page). Michael Grant: CTO, previous SpaceX production and flight-software experience. Brian Mongilio: Head of Operations.
- Plant: Camp Washington, Cincinnati; "Factory 1", team has taken possession. Square footage not stated.
- Products/capabilities: automated welding cells that take CAD files through to finished structural steel assemblies; AI-driven production planning and precision welding.
- First production run: a 40-foot by 8-foot structural skid, "7,300 lbs of welded steel" (Mongilio); the first job stress-tested the company's software, fixtures and robotic cells.
- Path Robotics: collaboration on installing and qualifying welding hardware for large, high-mix assemblies (per company LinkedIn posts).
- Funding: $15M seed; The O.H.I.O. Fund lists 1872.ai in its portfolio.
- Hiring: robotic fabrication technicians and manufacturing engineers on careers page.

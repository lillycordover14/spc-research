- Announced August 3, 2026, San Francisco, via PRNewswire. Series B of $310 million. Total parent and project capital raised to date about $400 million. Valuation not disclosed.
- Lead: Khosla Ventures. Continuing: Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Breakthrough Energy Ventures. New: Greenoaks, Halo Fund, Pax Ventures, StepStone Group, BHP Ventures, Washington Harbour Partners, Greycroft, General Innovation Capital Partners, Mitsubishi Corporation, In-Q-Tel (IQT), Earthshot Ventures. Additional unnamed strategic capital partners.
- Company: software-first, vertically integrated critical minerals company that builds and operates mines and refineries. CEO and Co-Founder: Turner Caldwell (prior roles not stated). Headcount and HQ not stated (SF dateline).
- MarianaOS: proprietary platform combining CapitalProjectOS (capital project execution), PlantOS (refinery operations), MineOS (mine management) into one AI/ML stack. Claims to roughly halve traditional 5-10 year project execution timelines and cut commissioning time well below industry benchmarks.
- Copper One (Southeastern Utah): acquired late 2025; restarted mining under autonomous orchestration within 4 months; targets 50,000 metric tons refined copper per year.
- Lithium One (East Texas): groundbreaking Q4 2025; described as the world's first GWh-scale facility producing lithium from oil and gas produced water; commercial production targeted H1 2027.
- Use of funds: buildout of current project portfolio and further deployment of MarianaOS. Long-term goal: 10 commercial-scale projects in 10 years. Each project is said to generate operating data that improves MarianaOS for the next facility.
- Not disclosed: autonomy technology partners, government funding (IQT only as investor), offtake agreements.
- Quotes: Vinod Khosla: "The minerals are there. What's been missing is the will and the AI to run mining and refining autonomously." "Critical minerals are the materials that decide whether America builds its own future." Turner Caldwell: "The critical minerals race is an execution problem." "This additional capital lets us move from proving the model to scaling it."

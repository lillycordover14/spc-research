[Extracted via WebFetch on 2026-10-22; not verbatim. See url.]

- Mariana Minerals raised $310M Series B; total capital raised by parent and projects about $400M; valuation not disclosed.
- Aims to build ten full-scale projects over ten years for US domestic critical minerals supply; Series B funds buildout of active project portfolio.
- Investors: lead Khosla Ventures; supporting a16z and Breakthrough Energy Ventures; new: Greenoaks, Halo Fund, Pax Ventures, StepStone Group, BHP Ventures, Washington Harbour Partners, Greycroft, General Innovation Capital Partners, Mitsubishi Corp., In-Q-Tel, Earthshot Ventures; plus unnamed strategic capital partners.
- Turner Caldwell, CEO and co-founder (prior roles not described). Headcount not disclosed.
- Copper One (Southeastern Utah): acquired late 2025; restarted mining under autonomous orchestration within four months; targeting 50,000 t/yr refined copper; ramping.
- Lithium One (East Texas): broke ground Q4 2025; world's first GWh-scale facility producing lithium from oil and gas produced water; commercial production H1 2027.
- MarianaOS: CapitalProjectOS (capital project execution), PlantOS (refinery operations), MineOS (mine management), combined into a single AI/ML stack, all in-house; claimed to cut 5-10 year timelines roughly in half and shorten commissioning well below benchmarks.
- Not addressed: government funding, customers/offtake, pricing, margins, announcement date.
- Quotes: Caldwell: "The critical minerals race is an execution problem"; "the time to build an autonomy-first minerals company". Khosla: "The minerals are there. What's been missing is the will and the AI to run mining and refining autonomously."; "Critical minerals are the materials that decide whether America builds its own future".

- Mariana Minerals, a US "software-first" miner, raised $310M Series B; total capital since 2024 founding about $400M. Khosla Ventures led; a16z, Breakthrough Energy Ventures, BHP Ventures, Mitsubishi Corporation, In-Q-Tel and others participated.
- Pronto (fleet automation partner) is part of Atoms, led by Travis Kalanick; Atoms recently raised $1.7B from a16z.
- Turner Caldwell, CEO: Stanford-trained mechanical engineer, 9+ years at Tesla working on material sourcing. Vinod Khosla: Sun co-founder, $15B fund manager.
- Copper One: restarted copper mine with refinery in San Juan County, Utah; target 50,000 t/yr copper "sooner rather than later."
- Lithium One: lithium extraction from oil and gas wastewater in Texas; commercial production H1 2027; "first step" toward becoming a major lithium player.
- Fleet automation in Utah with Sandvik and Pronto; current status hybrid autonomy with a handful of manned vehicles.
- MarianaOS and other in-house operating platforms are the "software first" core; claims to roughly halve 5-10 year project execution timelines and shorten commissioning well below benchmarks; not yet in use long enough to prove this.
- Autonomy on two fronts: hardware (with autonomy-stack developers) and an orchestration layer using reinforcement learning to control autonomous assets in place of humans.
- Goals: no humans in Utah mining operation by end of 2026; 10 mines and refineries in 10 years; initial minerals lithium and copper, expanding to uranium, rare earths, nickel, cobalt, manganese, magnesium, domestic first then overseas; focus on smaller assets and brownfield sites that incumbents overlook, avoiding $5-10B capex projects.
- Market: copper above $14,000/t; lithium carbonate nominal $20,000/t or more; demand from electrification, AI infrastructure, robotics, defence, reindustrialisation.
- Not covered: headcount, margins, customers/offtake, government funding, acquisitions, lead times.
- Quotes: Khosla: "The minerals are there. What's been missing is the will and the AI to run mining and refining autonomously." Caldwell: "Our goal by the end of the year is to have no humans in the [Utah] mining operation." "A lot of people see autonomy as jobs disappearing." "The ambition is to go and chase as many critical minerals as we can bring to market." "go as fast as possible ... and expand the pipeline".

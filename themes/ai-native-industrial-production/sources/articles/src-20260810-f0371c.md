- Isembard (named after Isambard Kingdom Brunel). First factory opened Jan 2025 in Harlesden, NW London. ~18 months old at article date (10 Aug 2026).
- Hybrid model of company-owned and franchised factories. 66 employees. 14 factories: 7 UK, 5 US, 1 France, 1 Germany. Target 25 by end of 2026. Next market Ukraine (hiring a general manager; reconstruction). Owned/franchised split not given; acquired vs greenfield not described.
- Franchising: ~3 months to set up; requires a couple of CNC machines, power, coolant, four walls, plus items Isembard provides. First franchisee Shawn Rowcliffe, 22, former machine operator at Babcock. Other franchisees: a restaurant owner, a cardiac nurse, an engineer, a military veteran, an investor. 2026 plan: more franchisees in UK, US, France, Germany. Fitzgerald calls franchising "unsexy".
- Products: precision component parts for space, defence, robotics. Processes: injection moulding, axis milling, CNC machining (lathes). Workflow: customer sends design file -> Isembard quotes -> makes (possibly assembles) -> ships. Customers named: Anduril, Tekever, ARX Robotics, UK government. Some customers previously made parts in India and Poland. Demand exceeds supply; turning down orders. Lead times, pricing, margins, revenue, capex per factory not stated.
- MasonOS: AI-powered platform covering orders, design, tracking, scheduling, supply-chain visibility. In-house vs bought split not stated. Challenge cited: keeping MasonOS current with rapid AI advances.
- Market: component manufacturing ~$1T/yr; small family-run incumbents dying out while demand rises; UK reshoring agenda.
- Funding: $9M seed led by Notion Capital (Stephen Millard). $50M Series A Mar 2026 led by USV; Tamarack Global and IQ Capital new; Notion and CIV existing. Angels: Alex Bouaziz (Deel), Matt Briers (ex-Wise CFO). Valuation, machine financing, contract values not disclosed.
- Founder Alex Fitzgerald: 33, sole founder and CEO; former Army reservist; Westminster School; biochemistry at Nottingham; intern journalist CityAM; 3 years HM Treasury (tax policy, economy spokesman); Hanbury Strategies (client Bulb Energy); founded Cuckoo broadband 2019 (just under 100k customers, ~100 staff, sold to Octopus Group 2022). Works ~8am to 10-11pm; cites military "mission command". Has been to No. 10; new PM's team may visit the factory.
- Quotes: Fitzgerald: "If you think about this business, it's a politician's wet dream." / "Demand is outstripping supply at the moment, so we are having to turn down orders now." / "If we achieve what we want to achieve with Isembard, the West's prosperity, security and purpose is solved." Rowcliffe: "I'm a machinist by trade, not a businessman, so I'm learning as I go, but with the right support it's all possible." Millard (Notion): "it's no different from a typical commercial route to market."

- Hadrian announced it closed a $360 million revolving credit facility (published 2026-08-17).
- Proceeds fund advanced manufacturing infrastructure, machinery and related hardware for production goals; supports expansion across its growing network of US factories and scaling production for defense and aerospace customers.
- Morgan Stanley Senior Funding Inc.: Lead Left Arranger and Bookrunner.
- Joint lead arrangers and bookrunners: Western Alliance Bank, J.P. Morgan, First Citizens Bank, Customers Bank, HSBC Ventures USA Inc., Axos Bank, Texas Capital Securities.
- Facility closed about one week after the $1.37B Series D, which valued Hadrian at $7.87B.
- Kirkland & Ellis was counsel to Hadrian.
- Chris Power (CEO and Founder) quotes: "This facility's closing represents a key next step in Hadrian's growth trajectory"; "underscores the institutional confidence in our strategic direction"; "the industrial capacity the United States needs to remain strategically competitive"; "creating thousands of high-quality jobs across the country".
- Not disclosed: tenor, rate, collateral, covenants, drawn amount, factory count, customers, margins.

- Air & Space Forces Magazine, 2026-09-01, Courtney Albon.
- DIU "Adaptive Space Manufacturing and Integration Scale" project: other transaction agreements awarded in July and August 2026 to two companies; aims to use commercially available tools and adaptive manufacturing to raise production rates across the space industrial base.
- ALL.SPACE (York Space Systems): Aug 31 statement; will demonstrate on-demand production of its Hydra satcom terminal; goal hundreds of units per month, potentially thousands per year. COO Rod McCurdy: "This award reflects a fundamental shift in how defense capabilities are being fielded".
- Freeform Future Corp.: contract in July 2026 to scale production of key propulsion and structural hardware; earlier in 2026 unveiled Skyfall, a laser-based additive manufacturing platform it says will significantly expand production. No dollar amount.
- Stellant Systems: $11.4M Pentagon DPA investment (Sept 1) for space-based RF amplifier qualification and rate.
- Space Force: Gen. David Miller said in May the five-year plan includes "thousands" of satellites (MTI, data transport, missile warning, SDA). SpaceX awarded >$4B (AMTI constellation) and $2.3B (Space Data Network) in May. New ISR fleets from 2029.
- Lt. Gen. Philip Garrant (SSC), April: "If a company is nominally making 10, we want you to be prepared to make 40"; industry should invest in capitalization and facilitization in return for large production contracts.
- SECAF Troy Meink interested in multiyear procurement for aircraft and satellites: "those long-term deals are a huge part of that."

QuesTek Computational Platform Expands Advanced Materials Design with New Niobium Suite


            QuesTek Innovations adds the Niobium Suite to its Icmd materials design and engineering platform, developed in collaboration with niobium producer CBMM to accelerate design of high-performance alloys for aerospace, energy and additive manufacturing.

QuesTek Innovations has announced the upcoming release of its Niobium Suite within the Icmd materials design and engineering platform. The new capability builds on a series of successful client engagements involving niobium-containing materials and on collaboration with CBMM, a global leader in niobium production and technology.

The Niobium Suite expands Icmd's capabilities by incorporating advanced models and data specific to niobium-based alloy systems. This allows engineers and materials scientists to more efficiently evaluate performance trade-offs, predict behavior in extreme environments and design materials tailored to specific applications, significantly reducing development timelines and reliance on expensive trial-and-error experimentation.

Accelerating Innovation Across Industries

Niobium plays a key role in improving strength, durability and high-temperature performance in advanced materials. With the introduction of the Niobium Suite, QuesTek says it is enabling customers to unlock new applications and optimize existing systems across industries including aerospace, energy and additive manufacturing. Collaboration with CBMM has further accelerated the practical deployment of niobium-enabled materials across industries.

"At CBMM, we believe that innovation in materials is essential to address global challenges. By combining our expertise in niobium with QuesTek's advanced computational design capabilities, we are enabling the accelerated development of high-performance materials that can meet the demands of the energy transition and next-generation industrial applications," says Adam Young, global business development manager, superalloys and metals at CBMM.

By leveraging Icmd, organizations can rapidly simulate how niobium-based materials influence microstructure and performance, enabling faster iteration and more confident decision-making early in the development process. This capability is said to be particularly valuable for industries facing increasing demands for efficiency and performance under extreme conditions.

Built on Proven Industry Engagements

The development of the Niobium Suite is grounded in QuesTek's work with clients tackling complex materials challenges. QuesTek has worked with CBMM to expand understanding of niobium's role in advanced materials such as nickel-based superalloys, while collaborating with government agencies and industry to advance computational materials design for extreme-environment applications.

In collaboration with ARPA-E, QuesTek created a CALPHAD database and modeling suite for Nb-based alloys, validated against a novel Nb-superalloy on the ULTIMATE program. This capability is designed to enable aerospace and space companies to more accurately model and optimize both established alloys like C-103 and next-generation Nb-superalloys for high-temperature applications, including jet engines and in-space thrusters.

"The Niobium Suite reflects QuesTek's commitment to helping customers solve complex materials challenges faster and more predictably. By enabling better decisions earlier in development, we're helping organizations reduce risk, shorten development cycles and bring advanced materials to market more efficiently," says Bill Mahoney, chief operating officer at QuesTek Innovations.

Expanding the Frontier of Materials Design

With the addition of the Niobium Suite, QuesTek continues to expand the Icmd platform as a comprehensive solution for designing advanced materials faster and with greater precision. The release opens new opportunities for engineers to explore niobium's full potential, from high-temperature alloys and structural components to emerging applications in energy systems and advanced manufacturing.

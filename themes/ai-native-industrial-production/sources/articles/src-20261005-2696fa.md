Helicon raises €14M to automate carbon-fibre factories
● 
18 days ago
What's the deal?
 
Helicon
, a California-based AI and robotics startup, has raised €14 million (about $16 million) in an early-stage round led by New York venture firm 
AlleyCorp
. The company, founded this year by Spanish aerospace engineer Edmundo Sanz-Gadea and his partner Alex Niehaus, is building automated factories for composite materials like carbon fibre.

What's the endgame?
 The capital will fund a Los Angeles factory and the industrial equipment needed to produce composites, Sanz-Gadea said. The plant, due in roughly a year, will cover everything from part design and moulds to manufacturing and quality control.

Why now?
 Carbon-fibre production is largely manual, slow, and historically limited to Formula 1 cars, fighter jets, and satellites. A new wave of drone, aerospace, and maritime manufacturers now demands far higher volumes, which Sanz-Gadea argues requires automated plants with AI tools and robots.

What's the plan beyond LA?
 Helicon wants to replicate its automated model in Europe. The goal, Sanz-Gadea said, is to help Europe "advance a sovereign supply chain for each country" (translated from Spanish) and cut dependence on Chinese manufacturers.

What could go wrong?
 The company is pre-revenue in a capital-intensive sector, and its first factory is still a year from completion. Scaling automated composite production and then exporting the model to Europe adds execution risk.

Helicon traces its roots to London, where Sanz-Gadea met Niehaus and closed a pre-seed round. The pair then moved to San Francisco to access the AI ecosystem and deep-pocketed investors, before settling in Los Angeles — a US aerospace hub close to customers and engineering talent.

The signal:
 Helicon's round sits in the 90th percentile for early-stage robotics deals in the US, a sign of investor appetite for hardware tackling defence and aerospace supply chains. As drone and unmanned-systems demand climbs, bets on automating physical production are gaining momentum.

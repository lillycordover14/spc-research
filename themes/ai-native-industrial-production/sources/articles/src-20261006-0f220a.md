Defense-specific startups set a quarterly record with $2.5 billion in Q3 2026
Defense tech VC value has reached $67.4 billion across 726 deals so far in 2026, and the money is continuing to concentrate in a narrower set of companies. Deal value in defense-specific companies hit $2.5 billion in Q3, an all-time quarterly high and more than 4x the $563.4 million recorded in Q2. Castelion raised $1.1 billion for hypersonics, and Mach Industries raised $900 million for missiles & munitions, both of which closed in the quarter. Crusoe took the largest check of the quarter at $3.9 billion. The bottom of the market, however, tells a different story. Pre-seed/seed deal count dropped to 41 in Q3 from 61 in Q2, the fewest in any quarter since 2016, and early-stage VC fell to 42 from 67. That is clearly a significant drop-off, and whether it holds through Q4 is the trend to watch.
Exits, on the other hand, came back in Q3. Dragos sold for $3.25 billion, the quarter’s largest M&A deal, and Standard Nuclear went public in July at $2.3 billion. Of the 21 public listings recorded in 2026, 17 closed in Q2 and Q3, and listings made up 26.3% of Q3 exit count against 16.7% across 2025.

AI-powered search Smart search results 6 October 2026 Read in 1 min Re:Build expands York County operations Approximately $20 million investment will create 150 new jobs Share Share COLUMBIA, S.C. — Re:Build , an integrated engineering and manufacturing company, announced plans to expand its advanced composites manufacturing operations in York County. The company’s approximately $20 million investment will create 150 new, skilled manufacturing and engineering jobs. Re:Build partners with companies to design, engineer and manufacture complex products across the medical, aerospace and defense, energy, electrification, robotics and automation, and environmental markets. The company operates 12 locations across the U.S., including its existing facility in Rock Hill. At its added York County property, located at 2021 Williams Industrial Blvd. in Rock Hill, Re:Build is adding a new 130,000-square-foot advanced manufacturing facility. The plant will support the company’s ongoing growth in the aerospace, defense and industrial markets. Operations are expected to be online in November 2026. Individuals interested in joining the Re:Build team should visit the company’s careers page . The Coordinating Council for Economic Development awarded a $100,000 Set-Aside grant to York County to assist with the costs of building improvements. “Our customers are scaling faster than ever, and this expansion is how we scale with them. By investing boldly in these capabilities, we're giving our customers the capacity, speed and consistency they need to move complex programs from development into full-rate production and to do it at a scale few can match.” -Re:Build Co-Founder and CEO Miles Arnone “South Carolina continues to strengthen our manufacturing footprint, giving companies like Re:Build the confidence to expand here. We are proud to support the company’s ongoing success and look forward to the impact these 150 new jobs will have in York County.” -Gov.

Paul Reed “Sonny” Boltz, Jr., 92, of Orwigsburg, passed away Tuesday, October 6th, at Reading Hospital.
Sonny was born in Pottsville on June 18, 1934, a son of the late Emma Frances (Weand) and Paul Reed Boltz, Sr.
Married 66 years, he was the loving husband of Ruth Ann (Morgan) Boltz.
Sonny is a graduate of Pottsville High School Class of 1955, he also attended Penn State University.
Sonny owned and operated Jacob Boltz Knitting Mill and Pinedale Golf Center, and he worked as an agent for Boltz Insurance Agency.
He was an avid collector and enjoyed being outside, but above all else, Sonny lived for his family. He was a beloved husband, father, and friend.
In addition to his wife, he is also survived by his children: Christa Boltz, & Frederick Boltz, and his grandson, Paul Russell, all of Orwigsburg.
Visitation will be held from 2:00 – 3:00 PM at Ebling-Stabingas Funeral Home, Cressona on Saturday, October 17th. A Celebration of Life Memorial Service will follow with Paul Moyer officiating at 3:00 PM.
Interment will be held privately.
In lieu of flowers contributions can be made to Tunnels to Towers 2361 Hylan Blvd, Staten Island, NY 10306 or Hillside SPCA PO Box 233 Pottsville, PA 17901.
Extend condolences by visiting www.gsesfuneralhomes.com.
Ebling-Stabingas Funeral Home, Cressona has been entrusted with arrangements.

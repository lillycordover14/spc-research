Patricia Warren Boltz's
 Obituary

Patricia Warren Boltz, age 86, and resident of Mobile, Alabama, passed on October 2nd, 2026, in Grand Bay, Alabama. Patricia was born on August 8th, 1940, in Mobile, Alabama. She attended Murphy high school and enjoyed volunteering at her church. Patricia loved to sew quilts and spend time with her family.

She is preceded in death by; her father, Thomas Warren; her mother, Charlotte Rodgers Warren; and her brothers, Tim Warren and Larry Warren.

Patricia is survived by; her children, Thomas (Barbara) Boltz of Mobile, Alabama, Teresa (Joey) Dorriety of Irvington, Alabama, Leonard (Felicia) Boltz Jr. of Theodore, Alabama, Rita Ponder of Semmes, Alabama, and Mark Boltz of Mobile, Alabama; her 14 grandchildren; her 15 great-grandchildren; and her sister, Anna Sue Miller of Irvington, Alabama.

Patricia's funeral service will be held at 10 A.M. on October 8th, 2026, at Our Lady of Lourdes Catholic Church with Father Bertrand Kundu of Our Lady of Lourdes officiating. Burial will follow at Mobile Memorial Gardens Cemetery. Visitation will be held prior to the service from 5 P.M. to 8 P.M. on October 7th, 2026, at Mobile Memorial Gardens Funeral Home.

Pallbearers: Daniel Dorriety, Jacob Dorriety, John W. Boltz, John G. Ponder, John W. 
McNellage
, and Kenny Andrews.

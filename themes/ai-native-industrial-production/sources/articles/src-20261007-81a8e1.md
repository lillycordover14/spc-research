John G. Boltz (1944-2026)&nbsp;&nbsp;LebTown

[page text]
John G. Boltz, 82, of Jonestown, passed away on Saturday, Oct. 3, 2026, at the Lebanon VA Medical Center.

Born in Bethel Township on June 4, 1944, he was the son of the late Howard M. and Helen (Brown) Boltz. Following high school, he served in the U.S. Navy during the Vietnam Conflict. Throughout his life, John worked as a heavy equipment operator in construction. He enjoyed fishing and loved nature and being outdoors. He especially loved being Pappy to his granddaughters.

He is survived by his daughter, Katie D., wife of Kyle Levengood, of Jonestown; two granddaughters; and best friend of 41 years, Audrey Miller. John was one of 10 siblings.

A graveside service with military honors will be held at 9 a.m. on Friday, Oct. 9, 2026, at Indiantown Gap National Cemetery. The procession will leave at 8:30 a.m. from Kreamer & Lum Funeral Home & Crematory, Jonestown.

Critical Materials Group Acquires Rocky Mountain Scientific Laboratory And Travis Swanson Named Chief Science Officer

Critical Materials Group (CMG) has acquired Rocky Mountain Scientific Laboratory (RMSL), a national security research and rapid solutions laboratory serving military and law enforcement agencies. Financial terms of the transaction were not disclosed.

As part of the acquisition, RMSL CEO and Co-Founder Travis Swanson will become Chief Science Officer of Critical Materials Group. Swanson previously co-founded CMG while continuing to lead RMSL, and the transaction brings the two organizations together under a single company.

The acquisition combines RMSL’s energetics research, formulation, and testing capabilities with CMG’s production platforms, creating a more integrated path from laboratory development to manufacturing. CMG said new energetic materials can face delays when scale-up, qualification, and safety engineering are handled across separate organizations and data environments.

By pairing RMSL’s development and testing capabilities with CMG’s modular, automation-ready manufacturing, the company aims to shorten the transition from research to qualified production while adding domestic capacity alongside government-owned and prime contractor facilities.

Founded in 2025, CMG is a privately held, Texas-based manufacturing company focused on modernizing domestic energetics production facilities considered important to national security. The combination positions CMG to bring research, testing, and manufacturing capabilities under one organization, with Swanson overseeing the scientific side of the combined business as Chief Science Officer.

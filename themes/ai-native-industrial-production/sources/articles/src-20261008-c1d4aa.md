Amca Wins First Active Capability Gap Award Under Army Advanced Manufacturing CSO

Amca
, a company specializing in engineering and manufacturing solutions for critical supply chain bottlenecks, has secured a competitively awarded firm-fixed-price contract from U.S. Army Contracting Command-Redstone Arsenal to assist U.S. Army Materiel Command, as stated in an 
Amca
 press release. The agreement, designated W9124P-26-C-A020, forms an enterprise-wide collaboration between Amca and Army Materiel Command for swift engineering and component manufacturing.
This contract represents the inaugural Active Capability Gap award issued under the Army's Advanced Manufacturing Commercial Solutions Opening. It advances the Army's aim of tackling priority readiness drivers throughout major weapon systems by reviving components from obsolescence and creating manufacturing capacity in cases where supply sources have vanished or can no longer satisfy demand. The collaboration is characterized as continuing a longstanding American practice of government and industry joining forces on pressing national issues, which the release identifies as a cornerstone of the nation's industrial strength.
Preliminary efforts will concentrate on radar and power assemblies for an Army priority missile system. The objective is to resolve extended lead times, incomplete engineering data, and reduced manufacturing capacity for these components and subsystems. Through design simplification and enhanced manufacturability, Amca seeks to lower production and sustainment expenses while empowering the Army's Organic Industrial Base and a wider network of qualified manufacturers to sustain the missile system over time.
The contract is designed for expansion, permitting the Army to incorporate additional components and subsystems from this priority weapon system past the initial assemblies. These encompass power supplies, connectors, actuators, valves, radar system components, and other essential items. This scope offers a scalable method to implement Amca's engineering-as-a-service approach as the Army uncovers further readiness requirements.
Jai Malik, CEO and Co-Founder of Amca, remarked that the Army merits an industrial supply chain delivering the speed, scale, and quality its mission requires. He noted that the industrial base's difficulties will not be resolved by additional machines or printers alone, but rather through superior engineering, encompassing design for producibility, cutting-edge engineering tools, and a robust American engineering workforce. He stated that the partnership with the Army begins with bolstering the readiness of priority missile systems and that the company stands prepared to handle whatever the Army requires subsequently.
All work will be conducted within the United States. Engineering and qualification testing will occur at Amca's headquarters in El Segundo, California, while production will expand across its eight AS9100D- and ISO 9001-certified factories.
About Amca
Amca presents itself as America's engineering company, striving to revive the country's capacity to engineer anything its critical systems rely on for production and sustainment.
Within aerospace and defense, Amca pinpoints critical supply chain bottlenecks, swiftly engineers solutions via its component design platform, and obtains or builds certified manufacturing capacity to generate them as needed. The company claims it has re-engineered and qualified critical components, such as sensors, pressure switches, connectors, and power supplies, within 90 days.
Amca is the original designer and producer of thousands of specialized components, including switches, valves, transformers, power supplies, relays, solenoids, and other engineered products. Its clientele features more than a dozen major American prime contractors. Amca possesses and runs eight AS9100D- and ISO 9001-certified factories throughout the United States, averaging five decades of continuous operation and attaining over 99% on-time delivery. It also maintains a dedicated prototyping and qualification testing facility at its El Segundo, California headquarters. Amca is ITAR-registered.

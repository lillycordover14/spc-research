Chai Discovery, GSK Announce Collaboration Following Wet-Lab Evaluation
 

		GSK gains access to Chai’s platform, including protein folding and design models.	

October 9, 2026
 

By: 
Patrick Lavery

Content Marketing Editor
 

Save

Editor’s Take: Chai’s latest model achieves high target success rates and binding affinities, helping pharma companies assess through empirical results.

Chai Discovery
, an AI company building a computer-aided design suite for molecules, announced a collaboration with 
GSK
. Since the start of 2026, Chai says it has entered into numerous partnerships with leading pharmaceutical companies.

The agreement follows a successful technology evaluation in which GSK conducted internal wet-lab testing of Chai’s designs. Chai generated these designs for GSK targets, zero-shot without target-specific training. GSK found binders to all tested targets—resulting in the expansion of its work with Chai into a broader collaboration.

“
GSK is excited to be working with Chai Discovery. We evaluated their models across a panel of diverse targets and saw designs with strong binding affinities.”

Christopher Austin, SVP R&D Technologies, GSK

GSK Accesses Chai Platform

Now, GSK gains access to Chai’s platform, inclusive of protein folding and design models, to power discovery efforts. Chai’s AI models accelerate preclinical drug discovery, as they predict and reprogram interactions between molecules.

“We are pleased to expand our work with GSK following its evaluation,” Chai Co-Founder and CEO Joshua Meier said. “The evaluation gave both teams an opportunity to assess Chai’s models against a range of therapeutic targets in the lab. We are excited to build on those results with GSK and support discovery efforts across its pipeline.”

“GSK is excited to be working with Chai Discovery,” GSK Senior Vice President R&D Technologies Christopher Austin said. “We evaluated their models across a panel of diverse targets and saw designs with strong binding affinities. Their technology complements our own internal models that we continuously retrain against our own wet-lab data. [This helps] to enable us to accelerate the discovery of potential new medicines.”

Latest Chai Model

Chai Discovery’s latest model is named Chai-3. The company says it achieves high target success rates and binding affinities, even to challenging targets, as GSK noted. This latest partnership exemplifies, according to Chai, how pharmaceutical companies are increasingly assessing AI models through empirical results.

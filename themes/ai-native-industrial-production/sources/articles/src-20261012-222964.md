Chai Discovery and GSK team up on AI-designed biologics after wet-lab validation
● 
12 days ago
What's the deal?
 
Chai Discovery
, the San Francisco-based AI drug-discovery company, announced on 9 October 2026 a collaboration with British biopharma group 
GSK
. Under the agreement, GSK gains access to Chai's platform — including its protein-folding and protein-design AI models — to support discovery efforts across GSK's pipeline. The deal follows a technology evaluation in which Chai's models generated designs against GSK targets "zero-shot" (without training on those targets first) and GSK's own wet-lab tests found binders for all tested targets.
Why it matters:
 The collaboration is the clearest signal yet that AI-designed proteins are moving from research curiosity to industrial tooling: GSK validated Chai's outputs empirically before signing, and Chai's CEO Joshua Meier described it as the company's sixth pharma agreement in nine months, following deals with Eli Lilly, Novartis, Pfizer and others. For GSK, Chai's models complement the internal models the company continuously retrains on its own wet-lab data. The deal shows how pharmaceutical companies are now assessing AI models through real laboratory results rather than marketing claims.
About Chai Discovery:
 Founded in 2024 by Joshua Meier, Jack Dent, Matthew McPartlon and Jacques Boitreaud — alumni of OpenAI, Meta FAIR and Stripe — the company builds foundation models that predict and reprogram interactions between biological molecules. Its latest model, Chai-3, reports high target success rates and binding affinities, including against targets that have historically resisted traditional drug discovery.

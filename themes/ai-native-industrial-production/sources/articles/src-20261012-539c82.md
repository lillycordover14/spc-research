Introducing The Manufacturing Tech Market Map Q4 2026

We went through 232 venture-backed manufacturing and industrial automation startups in Crustdata to build it, every one founded since 2016 with more than $10M raised

77 made the map, plus Praxie. 23 of them were founded in 2020 or later

I build AI systems for manufacturers, so I keep a close eye on who else is building for the factory floor

A few teams worth watching:

* Hadrian, automated factories for aerospace and defense parts. Chris Power
* Machina Labs, robotic sheet metal forming. Edward Mehr
* Standard Bots, AI robot arms for the factory floor. Evan Beard
* Formic, robots as a service for manufacturers. Saman Farid
* MaintainX, maintenance and work orders for plant teams. Chris Turlica
* Phaidra, AI control for industrial systems. Jim Gao
* Fero Labs, explainable AI for process manufacturers. Berk Birand
* First Resonance, operations software for hardware makers. Karan Talati
* Flexciton, AI scheduling for semiconductor fabs. @Jamie Potter
* RobCo, modular robot arms for small factories. @Roman Hölzl
* CADDi, AI for manufacturing procurement. @Yushiro Kato

Did we miss anyone?

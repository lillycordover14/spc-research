Ursa Major Opens Longmont Facility

The Longmont facility and Berthoud expansion are expected to create up to 1,850 new jobs

Ursa Major, an aerospace and defense company, 
celebrated
 the grand opening of its new Longmont manufacturing facility on Thursday with Governor Jared Polis and the Global Business Development Division of the Colorado Office of Economic Development and International Trade (OEDIT). The company is utilizing the facility to expand its production of avionics and in-space propulsion systems. Ursa Major is also expanding its manufacturing space at the Berthoud, CO facility. The Longmont facility and Berthoud expansion are expected to create up to 1,850 new jobs.

“Ursa Major's expansion in Longmont highlights Colorado as a global leader in aerospace and defense,” Governor Polis said. “We are thrilled to see this innovative company grow and succeed in our state, bringing thousands of high-quality jobs for Coloradans that will strengthen our economy and drive even more businesses to call Colorado home.”

The Longmont facility has dedicated labs for welding and manufacturing, an ISO 7 hard-walled clean room with an ISO 8 gowning room, and an environmental test lab. Ursa Major has six sites with Berthoud, Longmont, and Galeton facilities in Colorado, Youngstown and Boardman facilities in Ohio, and a Washington DC facility. The company is a leading manufacturer of hypersonics, solid rocket motors, and space mobility systems.

“We’re opening this Longmont facility because the demand is here now. Avionics run through every system we build, and our in-space propulsion delivers maneuverability on orbit faster than the legacy supply base can,” 
said
 Chris Spagnoletti, CEO, Ursa Major. “Moving that work here opens also the floor in Berthoud for liquid engines, vehicle integration, and solid rocket motors the warfighter is asking for at rate. Ursa Major continues to expand to ensure our service members and customers have the capabilities they need when they need it.”

The Economic Development Commission (EDC) approved up to $35,260,763 in a performance-based Job Growth Incentive Tax Credit (JGITC) over an eight-year period to support Ursa Major’s Colorado expansion. The company may also elect to convert up to $23 million of the JGITC award into refundable tax credits, which are refunded at 80% of their total value or up to $18.4 million. To qualify for these credits, Ursa Major must cooperate with the OEDIT review process and meet net new job creation and salary requirements.

“Ursa Major’s expansion in Berthoud underscores the strength of our state’s business climate and Colorado's status as a global leader in aerospace and defense,” said OEDIT Executive Director Eve Lieberman. “Our state is home to more than 2,000 aerospace companies, representing 24% growth over the last five years, and we are thrilled to support Ursa Major’s growth in this key Colorado industry.”

SK Square invested in Mujin, a Japanese physical artificial intelligence (AI) corporations. It is seen as a move to secure future growth engines by expanding its investment scope beyond semiconductors into physical AI fields such as robot control and factory automation.
On the 11th, according to the industry, SK Square's overseas investment subsidiary TGC Square recently joined Mujin's Series D funding. The specific investment amount was not disclosed. The total amount raised in this round is said to have exceeded 300 billion won.
Founded in 2011, Mujin is a Japanese corporations that supports automation on industrial sites based on robot control technology. Its core technology is the general-purpose robot control platform "Mujin OS." It goes beyond controlling the movements of individual robots to enable integrated management of multiple robots and automation equipment installed in warehouses and factories.
Mujin has raised a cumulative total of more than 600 billion won. The investment conducted at the end of last year included Japan's NTT Group, the Qatar Investment Authority (QIA), and Salesforce Ventures. At that time, Mujin was valued at over $1 billion (about 1.344 trillion won).
SK Square is said to have focused on the potential to link Mujin's robot control technology with SK Group's semiconductor and logistics businesses. Key application areas cited include semiconductor packaging and logistics processes and semiconductor plant construction sites. In particular, integrating logistics robots and automation equipment operated in semiconductor production facilities into a single operating system can improve factory operating efficiency and help realize autonomous fabs.
An SK Square official said, "We will continue to identify new investment opportunities in the AI and semiconductor value chain," adding, "We will restructure our business portfolio and pursue new investments according to a strategy of selection and focus to enhance our corporate value as a semiconductor investment holding company."

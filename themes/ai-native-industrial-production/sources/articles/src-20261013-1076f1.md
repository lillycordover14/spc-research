L3Harris Technologies, Divergent Technologies Collaborate on Affordable Mass Munitions
 
 
 
 
  MT Newswires 
 
 
October 13, 2026
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
LHX 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
DITD.PVT 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
L3Harris Technologies (LHX) said late Tuesday it is collaborating with Divergent Technologies to scale production and enhance capabilities for affordable mass munitions.
Financial details weren't disclosed.
The companies initially plan to use Divergent's additive manufacturing processes for L3Harris' Wolf Pack munitions, including Red Wolf and Green Wolf, which are part of its launched-effects and modular-vehicle portfolio designed for long-range precision-strike and non-kinetic missions, L3Harris said.

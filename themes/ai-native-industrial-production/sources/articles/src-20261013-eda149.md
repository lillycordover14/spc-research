L3Harris Technologies, Inc. - L3Harris and Divergent Sign Teaming Agreement to Increase Production of Affordable Mass Munitions View All News L3Harris and Divergent Sign Teaming Agreement to Increase Production of Affordable Mass Munitions October 13, 2026 Download (opens in new window) WASHINGTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- L3Harris Technologies (NYSE: LHX) and Divergent Technologies, Inc. have signed a teaming agreement to scale production and enhance capabilities for affordable mass munitions. L3Harris Technologies and Divergent Technologies, Inc. have signed a teaming agreement to scale production and enhance capabilities for affordable mass munitions. The two companies will initially seek to leverage Divergent’s additive manufacturing processes for L3Harris' Wolf Pack family of munitions, Red Wolf™ and Green Wolf™. Both are part of the company's growing suite of launched effects and modular vehicles, which deliver long-range precision strike and non-kinetic capabilities. “Today’s evolving battlefield demands highly capable, affordable munitions to counter low-cost threats at scale,” said Quinlan Lyte, President, Advanced Effects, Missile Solutions, L3Harris. “We look forward to exploring how we can collaborate with Divergent to increase production of our proven systems and accelerate rapid design development of future product family offerings.” “Divergent’s capability to rapidly design, manufacture, and supply hardware at scale for critical defense programs is a demonstrated strategic advantage for our customers,” said Lukas Czinger, CEO and Co-Founder of Divergent. “We look forward to partnering with L3Harris to apply DAPS to production programs today while identifying opportunities to accelerate development and scale manufacturing across future aerospace and defense systems.” L3Harris’ launched effects portfolio has completed 60 flight demonstrations of its unmanned air vehicles, including launches from fixed-wing, rotary-wing and ground-based platforms. The flexible platforms are designed for adaptability, incorporating sophisticated software that enables real-time in-flight collaboration and dynamic re-targeting, while also supporting the coordinated swarming of autonomous aircraft.

What Is Laminar?
Laminar, based in Tel Aviv, Israel, specializes in Public Cloud Data Protection SaaS solutions, offering data leakage protection for cloud-based operations. The company operates within the Cloud Infrastructure, Cyber Security, and SaaS industries.
With an employee count ranging from 51 to 100, Laminar has successfully raised funding through three rounds, including a Series A round. The company has been acquired by Rubrik, a leader in data security and cloud data management.

What's Next for Laminar?
Laminar stands at the cusp of significant growth, driven by the increasing demand for cloud data security solutions. It planned to use its June 2022 funding to expand its product capabilities, including support for Microsoft Azure and advanced data-centric security policies.
Rubrik acquired all of Laminar's outstanding stock in August 2023. Laminar is now part of Rubrik, and its technology has been integrated into the Rubrik platform.
However, Laminar faces challenges such as intense competition and the need to keep pace with rapid technological advancements. Ensuring compliance with industry standards and managing multi-cloud complexities will be crucial as the company navigates its growth trajectory.

- Announced at TechCrunch Disrupt 13 Oct 2026; article 14 Oct 2026 (modified 15 Oct), Stewart Burnett, Automotive World.
- Mind Robotics: industrial AI startup founded by RJ Scaringe (Rivian CEO), who remains founder and chair. Reported value ~US$3.4B. Has not publicly shown a product. Headcount, HQ, robot count, business model, pricing not reported.
- Doug Field appointed President, overseeing product, engineering, operations. Prior: Tesla SVP Engineering, led Model 3 through a 12-18 month production ramp; Apple, led car project (cancelled 2024); Ford ~5 years as Chief EV, Digital and Design Officer, left April (2026), Ford folded his EV org into manufacturing; his team developed Ford's Universal EV Platform for a US$30,000 midsize pickup due 2027 from Louisville. Ford Model e lost >US$5B in 2024.
- Funding: seed US$115M led by Eclipse; Series A US$500M co-led by Accel and a16z (March); US$400M led by Kleiner Perkins (May); total >US$1B. Volkswagen and Salesforce venture arms are investors.
- Customers: Rivian is first customer and owns about one-third of Mind. Deployment: Rivian plant in Normal, Illinois, where R2 is ramping. Rivian provides factory access and training data.
- Not building humanoids (Scaringe: unnecessarily complex for most factory work). Tesla Optimus context: Musk said in January no Optimus robots doing "useful work" yet.
- Also (micromobility spinoff): Rivian holds ~one-third; hired Guy Peterson (ex-Uber AV partnerships) Oct 2026.
- Quotes: Scaringe: "Doug is a remarkable leader with a track record of delivering new technologies in successful products". Field: "Mind has assembled an exceptional team and has a unique advantage".

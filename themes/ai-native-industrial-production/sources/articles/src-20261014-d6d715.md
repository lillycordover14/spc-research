The U.S. Department of Labor (
DOL
) reports there were 5,111 initial jobless claims made in Florida for the
 
week ending Oct. 3
. That’s down by 16 filings from the
 
week ending Sept. 26
 
when there were 5,127.
It’s the second week in a row there was a decline in new claims.
The latest Florida figures cut against the national trend that saw an increase in first-time unemployment claims across the country to start October. There were 170,333 new filings nationally for the week ending Oct. 3. That’s up by 11,994 filings, a 7.6% increase from the previous week.
In the year-over-year comparison, the national figure dropped substantially. The number of initial unemployment claims for the comparable week in 2025 was 207,124. That accounts for a drop of 36,791 first-time jobless claims in the annual figure.

Unemployment numbers have been heading in the upbeat direction for the past three months. The Florida jobless rate registered 4.5% in August, according to
 
FloridaCommerce
, the state’s economic development agency.
That figure has been falling since, dropping one-tenth of a percentage point each month from June through August.
Florida’s unemployment rate still remains higher than the national level. September’s jobless figure across America came in at 4.2%, said the
 
U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics
.

        Nvidia to invest in French start-ups Doctolib, Entalpic, Genesis AI and Pigment
        
 
 

        Nvidia announced further investments in the French start-up ecosystem at an event in Paris. This development will see the company become a minority shareholder in healthcare appointment specialist Doctolib, which has been deploying AI to accelerate innovation across its platform in Europe. Nvidia, which joins investors including French public investment bank Bpifrance, Eurazeo and General Atlantic, will not be represented on Doctolib's board, according to the start-up's CEO.

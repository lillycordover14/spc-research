Radical AI Autonomous Lab: The Best Inventions of 2026&nbsp;&nbsp;Time Magazine

[page text]
Discovering a new material can take roughly 20 years, as the work moves only as fast as human researchers can run experiments and learn from them. Not anymore. 
Radical AI’s Autonomous Lab
—part of a recent wave of 
self-driving labs
 transforming research-and-development processes across industries—can create and characterize over 25 alloys per day with almost no human hands involved.
 
Antimatter
, the company’s AI engine, reads hundreds of thousands of research papers and screens billions of material compositions to find promising new ideas, then robots build and test them around the clock. In August, the company announced that in just 16 weeks, this approach 
yielded a novel material
 that was 125 times more effective at resisting corrosion in extreme temperatures than C103, a leading aerospace material widely used in rocket construction. "Materials will be what accelerates the possibilities of new inventions, not what slows them down,” says co-founder and CEO Joseph F. Krause.

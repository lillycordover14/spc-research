L3Harris, Divergent Team Up to Scale Production of ‘Wolf Pack’ Munitions

The partnership aims to boost production of Red Wolf and Green Wolf weapons using advanced 3D-printing technology.

 Defensemirror.com Bureau

 12:01 PM, October 15, 2026

 916

L3Harris Technologies and Divergent Technologies have signed a
 
teaming agreement
 
to scale production of affordable munitions, initially applying Divergent’s additive manufacturing processes and Divergent Adaptive Production System (DAPS) to L3Harris’ Wolf Pack family, including Red Wolf and Green Wolf.

The systems form part of L3Harris’ launched-effects and modular-vehicle portfolio, designed for long-range precision strikes and non-kinetic missions.

The partnership aims to increase production of existing systems, accelerate future product development and expand manufacturing across aerospace and defence programmes.

According to L3Harris, both Red Wolf and Green Wolf feature software for real-time in-flight collaboration, dynamic retargeting and coordinated autonomous-aircraft swarming. The company reports a demonstrated low-altitude range exceeding 200 nautical miles (370 km), endurance of more than 60 minutes and high-subsonic flight speeds for the vehicle family. They can be launched from fixed-wing aircraft, rotary-wing platforms and ground-based systems, with rocket-assisted launches also supported from ground or maritime platforms.

Red Wolf is the kinetic-strike variant, designed to deliver long-range precision attacks against designated targets. It shares the Wolf Pack family’s reported performance of more than 200 nautical miles (370 km) of low-altitude range, over 60 minutes of endurance and high-subsonic speed. It supports modular payload integration and has demonstrated launch compatibility with multiple platforms, including the U.S. Marine Corps’ AH-1Z Viper helicopter. L3Harris has not publicly disclosed its exact dimensions, weight, warhead specifications or guidance-seeker details in the cited material.

Green Wolf is the electronic-warfare variant, designed for electronic attack and detect, identify, locate and report (DILR) missions. It shares the family’s modular design, software-enabled in-flight coordination, dynamic retargeting and autonomous-swarming capabilities. Its electronic-warfare payload is intended to complement Red Wolf’s kinetic-strike role, allowing the systems to support coordinated missions. The company has not publicly specified Green Wolf’s individual range, payload weight, transmitter power or detailed electronic-warfare performance; the range and endurance figures above are published for the vehicle family rather than separately verified for each variant.

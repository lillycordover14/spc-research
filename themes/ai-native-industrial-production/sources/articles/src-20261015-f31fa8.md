In Brief:

Jason Meredith
 promoted to president at 
Ursa Major

Meredith oversees business operations and profit and loss

Ursa Major expanding production in missile and space sectors

CEO 
Chris Spagnoletti
 praises Meredith’s execution skills

Berthoud
-based Ursa Major promoted Jason Meredith to president, giving him responsibility for business operations and the company’s profit and loss, the company announced Oct. 13.

Meredith previously served as president of Ursa Major’s 
Solid Missile Systems
 business, overseeing program execution and engineering for its solid rocket motor portfolio. He joined the company in 2026.
 

The promotion comes as Ursa Major expands production across its long-range and 
hypersonic missile
, solid rocket motor and 
space mobility
 businesses and prepares to become publicly traded.

“He understands our technology, our customers and what it takes to execute at scale,” Chris Spagnoletti, CEO of Ursa Major, said.

Meredith has more than two decades of experience in 
aerospace
, 
defense
 and solid propulsion. He previously held senior engineering, program and business leadership positions at 
ATK
, Orbital ATK, Northrop Grumman and Pratt & Whitney.
 

“My focus will be on bringing those pieces together across the business so we can move faster, increase production and deliver the capabilities our warfighters need to meet the mission,” Meredith said.

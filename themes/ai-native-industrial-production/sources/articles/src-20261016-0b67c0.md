Say Goodbye to Barrel Jeans: Here Are 5 Trendy “Tulip Jeans” to
Shop This Fall

Curvy at the hip and neat at the ankle, this petal-shaped
silhouette is the sweetest bouquet your closet will receive this
season.

 
 

 

 
 
 
 

 

In the 1630s,

Tulip Mania
 swept through Holland, and the rarest
bulbs reached legendary prices. Nearly four centuries later, the
flower is blooming again, this time on our legs. 
Tulip
jeans
 curve generously at the hip and thigh, then narrow
softly toward the ankle, like a bud just before it opens. As we
explored in our guide to the trend, the shape turns the drama of
barrel denim into something polished and wearable. For weekends,
try it with a cropped cardigan and ballet flats. At the office, a
sharp blazer and pointed pumps keep the look crisp. Luckily, this
season’s bulbs come at every price, and our shopping list has a
pair for every mood.

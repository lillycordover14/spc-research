Wilson Sonsini Advises Iambic Therapeutics on $150 Million Initial Public Offering

Client Highlights

October 16, 2026

Wilson Sonsini advised Iambic Therapeutics, a clinical-stage life sciences and technology company advancing novel medicines using its AI-driven platform, in connection with the company’s $150 million initial public offering (IPO). Iambic issued 9,375,000 shares of its common stock at a public offering price of $16 per share, with a 30-day option for the underwriters to purchase up to an additional 1,406,250 shares. Iambic’s common stock began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on October 15, 2026, under the ticker symbol “IAM.”

J.P. Morgan, Jefferies, BofA Securities, and Citigroup acted as joint book-running managers for the offering.

The 
State of Maryland
 and Baltimore County announced a $3.7 billion private capital investment by 
Anduril Industries
 in a new, 2,000,000ft
2
 software-defined shipyard in 
Baltimore County
 - Arsenal-2 - that will expand America's submarine industrial base. The facility is expected to support over 14,000 jobs, including the creation of more than 3,100 new permanent jobs. As a whole, this new project will become the largest single private investment in Maryland and the largest job creator in Maryland in over a decade.
Arsenal-2 will be built at 
Tradepoint Atlantic
 in Baltimore County, within the Sparrows Point complex—a site central to America's maritime heritage. Anduril’s investments are expected to deliver more than $2 billion in annual economic output, including an estimated $312 million in wages each year.
"This massive new project will deliver the largest private investment and create the most jobs in Maryland in over a decade—driving billions of dollars in economic activity that will boost Maryland families not just in Baltimore County, but throughout the state," says 
Gov. Moore
. "With this facility, thousands of Marylanders will have new pathways to work, wages, and wealth through advanced training and high-skilled maritime manufacturing jobs. We thank Anduril for choosing Maryland, and we're ready to build the future of the aerospace and defense economy together."
“Arsenal 2 marks a turning point for Sparrows Point,” says 
Baltimore County
 executive Kathy Klausmeier. “For generations, this site shaped America’s steel and shipbuilding legacy, including its critical role during World War II, when workers here built the vessels that helped defend our nation. Seeing new life and advanced manufacturing return to this historic area is exciting for Baltimore County. This project will bring thousands of good jobs, new opportunities for local businesses, and billions in long-term economic value, strengthening our county for decades to come. It also reflects the significant progress at Tradepoint Atlantic, solidifying its status as a premier maritime hub and key partner in our region’s economic future, building a stronger, more resilient foundation for the entire region.”
"Sparrows Point helped build the fleet that won World War II,” says Brian Schimpf, co-founder and CEO of 
Anduril
. “With Arsenal-2, Anduril will return large-scale shipbuilding to a site that can once again define American naval power. This facility will make Baltimore County a critical part of America's submarine industrial base, adding the production capacity that the Navy needs to meet its Virginia-class requirements. Maryland offers the workforce, the infrastructure, and the industrial heritage this mission demands. We're grateful to Governor Moore and state and local leaders for their partnership in making this investment possible."
“Today’s announcement is about more than welcoming a new tenant—it’s about restoring one of America’s most important industrial assets to its historic purpose,” says Kerry Doyle, managing director of 
Tradepoint Atlantic
. “For generations, Sparrows Point helped build the ships that defended our nation. Today, that legacy continues as Anduril reactivates this iconic shipyard to support the next generation of American defense capabilities. Tradepoint Atlantic is uniquely built for this mission. Our strategic East Coast location, deep-water port, robust rail and interstate connections, and access to a world-class workforce make this one of the most capable industrial sites in the country. Together with Anduril, we’re bringing a legacy industry back to Baltimore, creating thousands of skilled jobs, and helping strengthen America’s defense industrial base for decades to come.”
“This new facility is a generational investment in Maryland’s future, strengthening our position as a national leader in advanced manufacturing, aerospace, and defense,” says Lt. Governor Aruna Miller. “The project builds on our proud manufacturing legacy while bringing thousands of good-paying jobs to Sparrows Point. The Moore-Miller Administration is proud to partner with Anduril to ensure the technologies and vessels shaping America’s future are built right here in Maryland.”
“Anduril Industries’ landmark investment confirms Maryland’s status as a global leader in maritime defense manufacturing and a home to the industries of the future,” says Maryland Department of Commerce Secretary Harry Coker, Jr. “We are thrilled to be working with Anduril to support this transformative project and bring thousands of new jobs to Maryland.”
The State of Maryland and Baltimore County have made commitments to match up to 13% of the total anticipated private investment by Anduril through existing economic incentive programs and new appropriations that will require approval of the Maryland General Assembly and Baltimore County Council. The state incentive package includes capital support for the construction of a Center of Excellence facility for workforce training at a to-be-determined site at or near Tradepoint Atlantic. The State will additionally pursue a Maritime Prosperity Zone federal designation for the region, which will support Maryland’s longstanding maritime industry and experienced maritime workforce.
Since Governor Moore took office, Maryland has attracted more than $17 billion in private investment. AstraZeneca announced a historic $2 billion last fall to expand its manufacturing presence in Frederick and Gaithersburg and support 2,600 jobs. Earlier this year, a new Sphere venue was announced at National Harbor in Prince George’s County, which is projected to support 8,000 jobs and generate $1.5 billion in economic development. Samsung Biologics also opened the company’s first U.S. manufacturing facility in Rockville, retaining more than 500 local jobs in the region. Most recently, Tradepoint Atlantic broke ground for its Sparrows Point Container Terminal project, driving $1.2 billion in capital expenditures and delivering 1,100 new union jobs close to the Port of Baltimore.
Baltimore County continues to attract major employers because of its strategic location, highly skilled workforce, expansive transportation network, access to the Port of Baltimore, interstate highways, rail, and nearby airports. With a labor pool of more than five million people within 60 miles, Baltimore County is an optimal location for heavy industry investment. Arsenal-2 will return shipbuilding to a site that once helped define American naval power.

Mind hired an experienced executive this week, Doug Field. Field previously ran 
Tesla
's Model 3 program and 
Ford Motor Company
's EV operations. He will serve as president of Mind Robotics. The start-up's partnership with Rivian gives it a leg up on competition and an immediate place to deploy its technology. 

Image source: The Motley Fool.

Mind Robotics could have extensive use cases across manufacturing. Rivian's stake is reportedly worth a little more than $1 billion at this point. If the business takes off, Rivian and its investors will be along for the ride. Mind is also well-funded for an early-stage company, having reported raising $1 billion in the past year. 

Rivian
 launched its more affordable R2 fleet this year and delivered a record 19,248 vehicles in the third fiscal quarter. Shares of Rivian are still down 34% this year and more than 87% for all time as of this writing. Ultimately, Rivian still needs its EV lineup to become profitable, but the surprise success of Mind Robotics could be a lucrative win for investors.

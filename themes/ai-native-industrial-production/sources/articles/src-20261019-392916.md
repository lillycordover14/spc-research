In what it calls the first publicly detailed study involving closed-loop physical sciences, Lila Sciences says it has used an artificial intelligence–guided laboratory to identify palladium-based catalysts that could reduce electrolytic hydrogen production's reliance on scarce iridium. Whether the materials can survive inside an industrial electrolyzer is another matter.

Founded in 2023 within the venture capital firm Flagship Pioneering, Lila emerged publicly in March 2025 with $200 million in seed funding. By October 2025 it had 
raised $550 million
 in total, including backing from Nvidia’s venture arm, to expand its automated laboratories and pursue commercial partnerships.

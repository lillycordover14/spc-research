Black Sabbath guitarist Tony Iommi was recently interviewed by Brent Porche of Philadelphia radio station 93.3 WMMR. During their chat, which can be seen below, Iommi speaks about his new solo album, From The Dark, which will be released this Friday, October 23 via BMG, as well as Ozzy Osbourne, his new signature fragrances, and much more.

An excerpt reads:

WMMR:
 It’s been more than two decades since your last solo album, and 13 years since the last Black Sabbath record. What was it that made you think that now is the time to make another album? And where and when did the idea for From The Dark first start taking shape?

Tony Iommi:
 “Well, I’ve been busy doing a lot of other stuff, of course, when we were doing the Sabbath thing, and I’ve done a couple of other projects. And so I thought, well, yeah, it’d be nice to do another solo album. It’s been 21 years. So, yeah, I’ve enjoyed it, and I’ve taken me time doing it. Like, when we do a Sabbath album, it’s always you’ve got a certain time to do it, then you’ve gotta record it, and then you’re touring. In this case, there’s no pressure. I’ve been able to do it when I want. I’ve got my own studio here (at home). So I just come over and I start working on putting ideas down.”

WMMR:
 The title of the album, let’s talk about the meaning of it. Is there a personal meaning behind From The Dark? Or is it more about the music and the mood of the record?

Tony Iommi:
 “It’s the mood of it, really. Jorn came up with all the lyrical side of it. So, I’d done all the music. But From The Dark seemed like an appropriate title for the album.”

WMMR:
 Eight new tracks in all on From The Dark. Are all of the compositions on this album recent? How far back do the early versions of these songs go?

Tony Iommi:
 “Well, how this started, really, was I started doing an instrumental. I was gonna do an instrumental album, so I put about four or five tracks down instrumentally. And then I thought, ‘Oh, I don’t know. I think I’d like to do an album with a vocalist.’ So I put them on one side and then started writing riffs for a vocalist. And that’s how it sort of started, really. And it’s gone on. I haven’t… The good thing about this album, there’s been no pressure. I haven’t had to go out on tour. I haven’t had to have it done by a certain time. So I was allowed to be able to take my time on it and live with some of the ideas. So what I’d do, I’d say to Mike Exeter, who’s been with me for, like, 30 years, I’d say, ‘Let’s work on a Monday and Tuesday,’ ’cause he’s obviously producing other things. And so we always used to put Monday and Tuesday on the side every week, and then if something cropped up, we wouldn’t work, if he’s busy or I’m busy or doing something else. So we’d work around each other, and then just come in and start putting ideas down. So it was all very casual.”

The new single from legendary guitarist Tony Iommi is titled “Stormwatcher”. It rides on a signature guitar riff from the man who was the driving musical force behind Black Sabbath’s garlanded career.

“Stormwatcher” has a maritime theme, based around a ship stuck in a storm, accompanied by a dramatic animated video, bringing the lyrics and imagery of vocalist Jørn Lande to life.

“It channels something from within, something real,” Lande explains, describing how the lyrics tell a powerful story about struggles and sorrow as life voyages from birth to death, and beyond. “We are the captain of our own vessel, a ship crashing through unknown and sometimes challenging waters, always navigating forward, steering steady through every storm, driven by dreams of new horizons and longing for something new.”

One of numerous highlights from the remarkable album, From The Dark, “Stormwatcher” perfectly captures the dark atmospheres and yearning passions that permeate the record. Tony Iommi is joined throughout by Lande, who shares writing credits on all songs, as well as bassist Becky Baldwin and drummer Karl Brazil. Co-produced by Tony and Mike Exeter, his long-time creative partner, co-writer, producer and audio engineer, it is an outstanding album that sits proudly within the impressive Iommi catalogue.

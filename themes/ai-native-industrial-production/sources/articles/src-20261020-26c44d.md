- Arda launched 20 Oct 2026 after emerging from stealth. $160M Series A at $1.5B valuation. Total funding $232M including a $72M seed.
- Founder: Bob McGrew, former OpenAI Chief Research Officer.
- Anduril: first design partner and customer; added Arda to its ArsenalOS system; basic assembly instructions now 15 minutes vs 10 hours.
- Product: software derives assembly processes from design files and other product materials; reduces work to turn designs into building instructions. Robot programming being prototyped, aiming to update automated lines in days.
- Claims: conventional automation for a single station 3-6 months and nearly $1M; people can learn revised instructions in a day.
- Quote (McGrew, CNBC via TNW): "for manufacturing engineers what Claude or Cursor does for programmers".
- Context: compared to Prometheus (Jeff Bezos said it raised $12B at $41B, Semafor 11 Jun 2026); McGrew placed alongside Mira Murati (Thinking Machines) and Ilya Sutskever (SSI).
- Not covered: headcount, HQ, website, named investors, other founders, data ownership, pricing, deployment scale.

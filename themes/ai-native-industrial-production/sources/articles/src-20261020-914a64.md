- Funding: total $232M, announced 20 Oct 2026 (BusinessWire). Series A $160M at $1.5B valuation co-led by Altimeter, Accel, Khosla Ventures; participants XYZ Venture Capital, 8VC, SV Angel, Felicis. Seed $72M led by Accel and Founders Fund.
- Company: Arda Industries (Arda). Founded 2025. Emerged from stealth 20 Oct 2026; first public discussion was a CNBC exclusive interview with McGrew.
- Team: Bob McGrew, co-founder (led product and engineering at Palantir through its first decade; led OpenAI research 2018-2024, left as chief research officer Sep 2024; joined US Army Reserve Detachment 201). Augustus Odena, chief AI officer (co-founded Adept; Google Brain, Meta). Alex Mark, CTO (led engineering at Palantir; IRS Direct File). Jakob Frick, co-founder (Palantir, SpaceX). Headcount not stated.
- Customers: Anduril first design partner and customer; uses Arda inside its ArsenalOS manufacturing software. Results: basic work instructions 10 hours -> 15 minutes; complex engineering work up to 30 hours -> ~2.5 hours. Anduril valued at $90B in Oct 2026.
- Product: AI software turning new product designs into working assembly lines. Agent reads CAD files and design documents, works out assembly order, writes work instructions, lays out production stations. Design changes reach the floor without halting production. Current platform: design and operate manual assembly lines. Planned: automated lines reconfigurable as quickly as human workers. Prototyping: AI agents that program robots to carry out assembly plans. Positioned as UI plus agent for manufacturing engineers, like Claude or Cursor for programmers.
- Markets: defence and data-centre supply chain (transformer and electrical equipment makers) first; then consumer electronics; then robotics. Goal: make products now needing large Asian workforces manufacturable in the US.
- Industry claims (Arda): programming conventional automation for one station takes 3-6 months and close to $1M; a new phone stays at full production ~4 months; no one has built a smartphone in America. US manufacturing is a $3T industry (Apoorv Agrawal, Altimeter).
- Not disclosed: HQ, headcount, website/domain, pricing/business model, data rights.
- Quotes: McGrew: "Imagine a fully automated version of Shenzhen in West Texas - that's what we want to make possible" / "We started Arda in 2025 to speed up American manufacturing." / (to CNBC) "It's a user interface and an agent that does for manufacturing engineers what Claude or Cursor does for programmers". Matt Grimm (Anduril co-founder/COO): "We would be behind on our production goals without it". Apoorv Agrawal (Altimeter): "Yes, the price is steep today, but the long-term opportunity is very high".

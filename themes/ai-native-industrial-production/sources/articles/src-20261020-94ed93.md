LanzaTech Global Leads LanzaJet Recap: Up to $25M Secured Notes, Warrants Boost to 66.7%
1 min read
LNZA
LanzaTech Global detailed a recapitalization of affiliate LanzaJet that includes up to $25 million in senior secured notes led by Future Solution Investments, a broad warrant exchange to deleverage FPF and align incentives, updated stockholder governance, and new LanzaTech Global warrants issued to an FSI-affiliated SPV. LanzaJet issued $10 million of notes at closing with a committed second $10 million tranche by December 1, 2026, secured by first-priority liens and maturing October 14, 2029. Through a Class Y Warrant, LanzaTech Global can reach 66.67% ownership and voting power in LanzaJet upon exercise. The company also issued 1,737,921 warrants at $5.754 to FSI's designee, with additional warrants tied to Tranche 2 funding.

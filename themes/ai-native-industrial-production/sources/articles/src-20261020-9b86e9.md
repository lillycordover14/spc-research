Samsung Electro-Mechanics Secures KRW 360.5 Billion Semiconductor Substrate Deal

 In The News 
 |
                20-10-2026 | By 
Jack Pollard

Samsung Electro-Mechanics has
 signed a long-term supply agreement
 (LTA) worth KRW 360.5 billion (approximately £203.5 million) for 'Memory Semiconductor Package Substrates' with a global major corporation.

Under this agreement, Samsung Electro-Mechanics will reliably supply high-performance package substrates used in memory semiconductors to a global major customer for one year, from January 1, 2027 to December 31, 2027.

Growing Demand for High-Performance Package Substrates

A package substrate is a core component that connects the semiconductor chip to the main board, transmits electrical signals, and supports the stable operation of the semiconductor. In particular, package substrates applied to high-performance semiconductors must support large-capacity data processing and high-speed signal transmission, requiring a high level of technology, including high integration and fine circuit implementation.

Recently, the global semiconductor market has seen a rapid increase in demand for next-generation memory semiconductors such as high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and high-performance server DRAM modules, driven by the spread of generative AI and surging demand for high-performance computing (HPC). As memory semiconductors become faster and more highly integrated, package substrates that connect memory chips to the mainboard also require highly demanding process technologies such as high-layer-count structures, fine circuits, and ultrathin designs.

Samsung Electro-Mechanics is recognized for the unrivalled technological capabilities and mass production stability it has accumulated in the package substrate field, including large area fine circuit implementation and warpage control technology.

Long-Term Supply Agreements Reach KRW 4.3 Trillion

With this agreement, Samsung Electro-Mechanics has expanded its long-term supply agreements across its core businesses. Following major longterm supply agreements in the Component Solution Unit for silicon capacitors, MLCCs for AI servers, and high-performance inductors since May this year, the company has also secured a large-scale order in the Package Solution Unit.

As a result, the long-term supply agreements signed by Samsung Electro-Mechanics since May 2026 have increased to seven in total, based on public disclosures (2.5% or more of sales), with the cumulative contract amount reaching KRW 4.3 trillion.

Samsung Electro-Mechanics plans to strengthen cooperation with major customers and expand long-term supply agreements to overcome volatility from business cycles and improve its business structure toward a stable, high-profit model.

Meanwhile, the importance of high-performance package substrates is growing further with the growth of the AI and high-performance computing markets. Accordingly, Samsung Electro-Mechanics plans to continuously enhance the competitiveness of its package substrate business by strengthening supply cooperation with customers and establishing a stable supply system.

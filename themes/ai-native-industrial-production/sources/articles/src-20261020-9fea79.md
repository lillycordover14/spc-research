Governor Hochul Announces Phoenix Tailings to Establish $375 Million Commercial Scale Rare Earth Separation Facility in Monroe County. October 20, 2026. 500,000-square-foot location in Eastman Business Park; commits to creating at least 90 new jobs. When fully operational, the Rochester facility is expected to separate about 18,000 tons of rare earth material each year, enough to produce about 3,000 tons of rare earth metals at the company's downstream metallization facilities. New York State, through Empire State Development, is offering Phoenix Tailings up to $3 million in performance-based tax credits through the Excelsior Jobs Tax Credit Program.

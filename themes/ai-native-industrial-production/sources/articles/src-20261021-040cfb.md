Vantage signs 1GW solid-state transformer supply deal with Heron Power

Will supply the data center firm with its Heron Link SST

October 21, 2026

By

Zachary Skidmore

         
            Have your say
        
    

Data center firm Vantage has signed a deal with Heron Power, a US solid-state transformer manufacturer, to secure more than 1GW of the company’s SST’s to deploy across its hyperscale data center portfolio.

– Heron Power

The deal is expected to support Vantage’s move to deploy 800 VDC racks across its data center portfolio. According to Heron, its Heron Link SST is designed to address the challenges of AI racks, which have much higher densities and more variable loads. It does so by using integrated energy storage, reducing conversion stages and simplifying the path from the grid to the rack.
According to the companies, the agreement will enable faster time to power, greater efficiency, with the Heron Link targeting more than 98.5 percent efficiency from medium voltage to 800VDC, lower infrastructure requirements, and greater energy flexibility.
According to the company, Heron Link is a 5MW modular megawatt power converter built on the latest advances in wide-bandgap semiconductors. In addition, the company claims that the product is designed for high power density and ease of maintenance, offering greater reliability and lower costs for renewable energy storage and data center developers.
“Our customers need a faster and more efficient way to deploy increasingly power-dense and flexible AI infrastructure,” said Curtis Aubry, vice president, global product strategy at Vantage. “By simplifying the electrical architecture and integrating power conversion, energy storage and compute more effectively, we can help reduce deployment complexity, improve efficiency and accelerate our customers’ speed to capacity.”
“Heron was founded to modernize electrical infrastructure and enable an electrified economy,” added Drew Baglino, founder and CEO of Heron Power. “By partnering with Vantage to deploy more than a gigawatt of Heron Links in data centers, we will move 800 VDC from a reference architecture concept to a repeatable, deployed platform, delivering power from grid to rack in an integrated system.”
Heron Power was founded in 2024 by former Tesla senior executive Drew Baglino and is based in Scotts Valley, California. In 
August
, it announced the selection of Morgan Hill, California, as the home of its first large-scale factory, which, when up and running, is expected to manufacture up to 10,000 Heron Links every year, representing 40GW on an annual basis.
The selection of its first manufacturing facility closely followed the close of its
 Series B funding round
 earlier this year, which saw the company raise $140 million and brought its total funding to date to $183m. It closed its Series A funding round last
 May
.
The company has already partnered with data center firms to deploy its solution. Last 
June
, the firm signed a Letter of Intent (LOI) with Crusoe to supply power converter technology for the Stargate data center campus in Texas.

Phoenix Tailings picks Rochester for $375M rare earth facility 

 

Andrea Deckert
//
October 21, 2026
//

 

 

 Listen to this article

Phoenix Tailings, a Massachusetts-based company, has chosen the 
Eastman Business Park
 for a new $375 million commercial scale, rare earth mineral separation facility.
 

The 500,000-square-foot site will process rare earth materials used in products such as electric vehicles, electronics and defense equipment. Most rare earth separation currently takes place in China.
 

Gov. 
Kathy Hochul
 said the investment would help strengthen national security by establishing a domestic 
supply chain
 for rare earth elements.  
 
 

Phoenix Tailings has developed environmentally friendly processes to recover rare earth metals from mining waste and other industrial sources. The company separates and processes these materials into metals used to make powerful magnets enabling technology to become faster, smaller, stronger and more energy efficient. 
 

The metals are also critical components in high-tech defense equipment, medical and healthcare products, advanced manufacturing, smartphones, laptops, electric vehicles and communications systems.
 

When fully operational, the Rochester facility is expected to separate about 18,000 tons of rare earth material each year, enough to produce about 3,000 tons of rare earth metals at the company’s downstream metallization facilities.
 
 

“America and its allies cannot build the next generation of advanced manufacturing on a rare earth supply chain they do not control,” said Nick Myers, the company’s co-founder and CEO. “Phoenix Tailings is building an innovative, commercial-scale alternative designed to secure the Western supply chain for the rare earth metals that power modern industry — while setting a new standard for responsible production, with zero waste, zero direct process emissions and a safer process for the people who operate it.”
 

New York State, through Empire State Development, is offering Phoenix Tailings up to $3 million in performance-based tax credits through the Excelsior Jobs Tax Credit Program. The company has committed to creating at least 90 jobs. Greater Rochester Enterprise is also assisting with the project.
 

The Rochester facility is expected to eventually support more than 200 direct jobs in New York. Phoenix Tailings plans to begin some operations at Eastman Business Park in 2027, with full production expected in 2028.

Phoenix Tailings says its separation and metal-making operations are expected to support more than 500 jobs across New York, Massachusetts, New Hampshire and a future site. All told, the company expects to invest $1.2 billion across its U.S. operations.
 

The U.S. Department of War’s Office of Strategic Capital has made a conditional commitment of $500 million in long-term loans to help Phoenix Tailings build its rare earth mineral separation system. The commitment was announced in June and is still subject to review before the financing is finalized.
 

The U.S. Department of Energy has also awarded the company a $66 million grant. The grant is part of a $147.8 million project to demonstrate how heavy rare earth metals can be produced in the United States from waste materials.

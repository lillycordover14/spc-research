Tar Heels Say They Need to 'Gang Rebound'&nbsp;&nbsp;247Sports

[page text]
INDIANAPOLIS, IN – Without a doubt, 
North Carolina
 would love to have the services of 7-foot 
South Dakota
 transfer 
Cameron Fens
, but him sitting on the bench in sweatpants on Sunday isn't why 
Indiana
 destroyed the Tar Heels on the glass.
UNC's 76-58 exhibition loss to the Hoosiers was mostly about one thing, at least at the top of an unpleasant and fairly lengthy list of cons: Rebounding.
Indiana grabbed 22 more boards than the Tar Heels, and it was the biggest difference in the outcome of the game.
"Anytime you get that outrebounded, 5- to-31 — that's a crazy number," first-year UNC Coach 
Michael Malone
 said after the game inside Gainbridge Fieldhouse. "We gave up 20 offensive rebounds for 17 points. That was a point of emphasis all week long in preparation for this team, knowing we had to deal with (Aiden) Sherrell.
"To be manhandled, to be outworked on the glass was disappointing. That's not just on our bigs, it's on everybody to make sure we're rebounding. We did a really poor job of that."
Sherrell, who is a 7-foot spread forward, had 10 rebounds, and 7-foot-2 
SMU
 transfer 
Samet Yigitoglu
 pulled down 11.
For Carolina, 7-foot-1 freshman 
Sayon Keita
 grabbed 9, but guard 
Matt Able
 was next with just 4 boards. No other Tar Heel had more than 3. The starting perimeter unit, including 
Terrence Brown
 and 
Neo Avdalas
, combined for just 9 boards, and perimeter-oriented reserves 
Jaydon Young
, 
Angelo Brizzi
, 
Kevin Thomas
, and 
Maximo Adams
 combined for only 5 boards. Add 6-foot-11 reserve forward 
Alex Samodurov's
 2 rebounds, and Malone's point is clear.
"I felt our defense, to be very honest, was really good," he said. "We held that team to 36% from the field. That's a very good number. But you have to finish the possession. You have to get a rebound and try to run that team. We were unable to do that (Sunday)."
The glass was fairly even early in the game, with IU holding a 10-8 advantage with 11:45 remaining before halftime. But that's when the Hoosiers separated.
IU out-boarded the Heels 43-23 over the final 31:45 of the contest. It owned a 10-0 edge on second-chance points at halftime, a reason it started pulling away from the Tar Heels well before the intermission.
How much would having the services of Fens impacted the rebounding disparity?
Able doesn't see it as a Fens issue; he sees it broader than that, backing up what his coach said.
"I think with Fens, without Fens, obviously we'd love to have him; he's going to be a great piece for us, but we just have to gang rebound," Able said, standing in the hallway outside of UNC's locker room. "We have Jarin, Sayon, Samo; they were down there battling. I think me as a guard, and just the rest of the guards, I think we can do a better job cracking down and just crashing the glass and helping our bigs out.
"They (Indiana) were a big team. They were tasked with a big challenge. We can definitely help that and do a better job as a whole."
Carolina definitely needs the length, physicality, and experience Fens would provide, but great rebounding teams don't simply rely on the big guys, it's an all-in thing with them, and that likely will be a significant point of interest around the team moving forward.

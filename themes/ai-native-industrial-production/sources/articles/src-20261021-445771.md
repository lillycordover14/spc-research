Century Aluminum CEO Jesse Gary said in an interview published on Octo&nbsp;&nbsp;Shanghai Metals Market

[page text]
Century Aluminum CEO Jesse Gary said in an interview published on October 19 that US Section 232 tariffs on steel and aluminum have been highly effective in encouraging domestic manufacturing investment and reshoring, urging policymakers to maintain the existing tariff structure during USMCA negotiations.
The comments followed reports that US Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick opposed reducing the 25% tariffs on Mexican automobiles and 50% tariffs on steel and aluminum, as well as lowering duties on certain derivative products, contributing to stalled US-Mexico trade negotiations. Century Aluminum maintains that the current tariff framework supports domestic primary aluminum production and investment, while North American aluminum producers continue to monitor the potential implications for regional trade and supply chains.

CloudNC
’s CAM Assist solution — which uses AI to generate machining strategies for 3 and 3+2 axis CNC machines — is now available for 
GibbsCAM
 users.

The software is available as an AI-enabled add-on for GibbsCAM. The integration is designed to enable GibbsCAM users to program tool paths and machine parts more efficiently within their familiar CAM workflow.

CAM Assist uses advanced computer science techniques and AI to generate machining strategies in minutes or seconds, depending on complexity — tasks that could otherwise take CNC machine programmers hours or days to complete manually. According to the company, this reduces programming time, a common bottleneck in many factories, and also shortens the time required to estimate the cost of programming new components. That gain is said to enable manufacturers to raise productivity, shorten lead times and estimate for more work more quickly.

GibbsCAM users can also access CAM Assist 2.0, which combines AI-powered toolpath generation with step-by-step oversight. The upgrade allows programmers to configure machines, materials and tool assemblies, assess parts before running, review AI suggestions as they are made, and use the Strategy Editor to inspect, adjust and approve machining strategies before they are sent back into GibbsCAM.

“GibbsCAM is a powerful fit for machine shops that want a smart, flexible CAM solution that can scale from day-to-day production work to produce complex components,” says Theo Saville, chief executive and co-founder at CloudNC. “Bringing CAM Assist to GibbsCAM helps those users go even faster, meaning programmers can spend less time on repetitive toolpath creation, stay firmly in control of the process, and focus more of their expertise on getting high-quality parts out of the door.”

Thorsten Strauss, president of GibbsCAM, says the integration brings AI technology directly into existing workflows. “This collaboration with CloudNC enables programmers to automate repetitive programming tasks, accelerate part programming, and focus their expertise where it delivers the greatest value — all while maintaining full control of the machining process.”

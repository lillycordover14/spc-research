Eleven Tar Heels Dot NBA Opening Day Rosters
October 21, 2026

Eleven former University of North Carolina players earned spots on opening day NBA rosters for the 2026-27 season, the league announced this week.


Three teams each include a pair of former Tar Heel players: the Brooklyn Nets (Drake Powell and Day'Ron Sharpe), Milwaukee Bucks (Pete Nance and Cormac Ryan) and Utah Jazz (Tony Bradley and Harrison Ingram). Other Carolina players in the league to start the season include Harrison Barnes (San Antonio), Leaky Black (Sacramento), Cameron Johnson (Denver), Coby White (Charlotte) and 
Caleb Wilson
 (Chicago).


Tar Heel 
Henri Veesaar
 likely would've made the opening day roster for the Atlanta Hawks had he not suffered a season-ending knee injury in the preseason.

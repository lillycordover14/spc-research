Former OpenAI research chief Bob McGrew has launched Arda, an AI startup aiming to revive US manufacturing. The company has raised $230 million at a $1.5 billion valuation, according to CNBC.

Former 
OpenAI
 research chief 
Bob McGrew
 has launched 
Arda
, a startup focused on using artificial intelligence (AI) to automate manufacturing. The company has raised $230 million at a $1.5 billion valuation, Arda told CNBC.
Altimeter Capital, Accel, and Khosla Ventures led the funding round. Arda develops 
AI software
 designed to help automate manufacturing processes.
McGrew, who also served as 
Palantir
's product and engineering lead for over a decade, noted that Arda's AI models are already deployed with military technology company 
Anduril
. He explained that Arda's AI acts as a user interface and agent for manufacturing engineers, similar to how Claude or Cursor function for programmers.
McGrew highlighted the human-intensive nature of manufacturing and the time required to adapt production to new designs. He stated that while a human can be retrained in a day, changing automation to reflect a new design can take three to six months. Arda's software and AI product aims to allow manufacturing engineers to build assembly lines much faster.
McGrew joined OpenAI in 2017, working on the reasoning capabilities of early models before ChatGPT's launch. He left in 2024 during a period of other high-profile exits from OpenAI.

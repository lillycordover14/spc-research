KENSINGTON, Calif. (KGO) -- 
Police are investigating what they believe could be a hate crime after racist graffiti was found this week at Kensington Hilltop Elementary School.
Authorities are asking neighbors near the school to share doorbell and dash camera footage recorded between Friday afternoon and Monday morning as they try to identify the person responsible.
According to police, someone wrote the N-word on a wall at the school using what investigators believe was roofing tar.
"My immediate reaction was just shock and anger and sadness," said Caitlin Garibaldi, the incoming trustee for the West Contra Costa Unified School District region that includes Hilltop.

"I can't imagine being a young, Black student walking into school and seeing that messaging," said Garibaldi.
School leaders said they moved quickly to respond. Hilltop's principal said she is working with the district to provide a campuswide response and ensure support is available to anyone who needs it.
In a letter to parents obtained by ABC7 Eyewitness News, the principal condemned the incident.

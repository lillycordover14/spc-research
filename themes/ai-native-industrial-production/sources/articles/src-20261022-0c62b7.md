Fitzroy Minerals Inc. (TSXV: FTZ, OTCQX: FTZFF, FSE: C3Y) revealed the outcomes of an integrated seismic and machine-learning targeting study conducted with Fleet Space Technologies at its Buen Retiro Copper Project near Copiapó, Chile. This study uncovered nine previously untested exploration zones within 400 metres of the surface by integrating 3D Ambient Noise Tomography data with existing geophysical surveys and around 24,400 copper assays from 140 drill holes. With a 5,000-metre drilling campaign now in progress, investors are keenly observing how the initial drilling results align with the machine-learning model predictions.

Key Highlights

Fitzroy Minerals Inc. (TSXV: FTZ, OTCQX: FTZFF, FSE: C3Y) collaborated with Fleet Space Technologies on a machine-learning-driven copper targeting study at Buen Retiro, Chile.

Nine prioritized, previously untested exploration target zones were identified, all located within 400 metres of surface beneath alluvial gravel cover.

A 5,000-metre drilling program combining reverse circulation and diamond drilling has commenced to evaluate these new targets.

Investors should monitor drill results and subsequent updates to the machine-learning model as assay data is received.

Fleet Space ANT Surveys Reveal Deeper Geological Structures at Buen Retiro

Fleet Space Technologies performed both regional and infill Ambient Noise Tomography (ANT) surveys at Buen Retiro, utilizing natural seismic noise to generate a 3D subsurface image extending to approximately 400 metres depth. According to the release, this seismic framework enabled Fitzroy to delineate known mineralized trends and discover new targets beyond the reach of existing drilling, including areas eastward toward the 100%-owned Sierra Fritis properties. The project’s extensive alluvial gravel cover has historically hindered surface geological mapping, making seismic imaging a vital tool for target identification in this challenging terrain.

Machine Learning Model Integrates Multiple Geophysical Datasets with Drill Assays

The machine-learning targeting phase integrated four 3D geophysical datasets—ANT shear-wave velocity, induced polarization (IP) chargeability, IP resistivity, and magnetic susceptibility—with approximately 24,400 copper assay results from 140 existing drill holes. The model was trained to predict zones where copper mineralization could exceed a 0.5% Cu threshold within 25-metre blocks. Candidate blocks were filtered to exclude previously mined areas and any location within 200 metres of existing drill holes, focusing on genuinely untested ground. The nine identified domains represent clusters of blocks interpreted as geologically significant and exhibiting characteristics consistent with Buen Retiro’s known mineralized zones.

5,000-Metre Drilling Program Initiated to Validate Machine-Learning Targets

Fitzroy has launched a 5,000-metre drilling program using diamond and reverse circulation rigs to test the nine newly identified target domains. The company and Fleet Space will continuously incorporate incoming drill logs and assay results to refine and update the machine-learning model. The announcement emphasizes that each drill hole is designed to test a specific target, validate or enhance the model, and guide future drilling decisions. It also cautions that the targets are conceptual, do not confirm mineralization presence or grade, and are intended to complement conventional geological interpretation rather than replace it.

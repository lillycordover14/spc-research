Blue Ridge Advanced Manufacturing Center
Northrop Grumman's Waynesboro, Virginia site is well-positioned to help meet the demands of the defense market and deliver critical technologies more quickly and cost-effectively.
Blue Ridge Advanced Manufacturing Center is a 315,000-square-foot advanced manufacturing facility built to accelerate the development and high-volume production of critical defense technologies.
Smart Factory tools, AI, AR and additive manufacturing optimize processes.
Cutting-edge testing, rapid prototyping and scalable innovation, all in one facility.
We partner with companies like Palantir, ESAero, and NVIDIA along with a resilient supply chain of nearly 10,000 U.S.-based suppliers.
More than 300 new jobs in the community.
Implementing green building practices to achieve LEED Silver certification.
Waynesboro, Virginia, offers career opportunities and a rewarding quality of life. Nestled in the Shenandoah Valley, this growing community is a unique hub for professionals looking for small town charm and a low cost of living.
At Northrop Grumman’s Waynesboro facility, our teams work on innovative projects in engineering, manufacturing and logistics. Blue Ridge Advanced Manufacturing Center will manufacture a wide range of advanced electronics. From development to testing to rapid prototyping, and full-scale production, this state-of-the-art facility will deliver radars, communications antennas, and electronic warfare capabilities. Our modular, flexible assembly lines are designed to scale and adapt efficiently, delivering world-class defense solutions with the speed, agility, and reliability our customers expect. This innovative approach not only meets high-performance standards but also achieves an approximate 20% reduction in recurring costs, ensuring exceptional value and operational excellence.
The Blue Ridge Advanced Manufacturing Center is directly supporting our mission to deliver survivable, secure, precise, and resilient systems to our customers. By producing products domestically, we eliminate reliance on foreign suppliers and support a U.S.-based supply chain in alignment with national security priorities. This facility enables Northrop Grumman to meet growing demands and maximize capacity and efficiency.

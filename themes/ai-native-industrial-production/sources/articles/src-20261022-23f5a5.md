【SMM Rare Earth Flash】 Vulcan Elements announced on October 20 a partnership with the North Carolina Collaboratory to advance high-performance rare earth magnets for defense and commercial applications. The collaboration combines quantum mechanical simulation capabilities led by Dr. Yosuke Kanai at UNC Chapel Hill to predict with high precision how atomic-level changes affect magnetic performance. The Collaboratory is supporting the effort with a US$600,000 research grant. Vulcan's 1.1 million-square-foot manufacturing facility under construction in Benson, North Carolina, will have 10,000 t/y magnet capacity, supplying U.S. critical defense systems, semiconductor manufacturing, robotics, drones, AI, and automotive sectors.

Hebei Shoulang Facility Using LanzaTech Technology Achieves World’s First ISCC EU RCF Certiﬁcation for Ethanol Produced from Steel Mill Waste Gases
Hebei Shoulang New Energy Technology Co., Ltd. (“Hebei Shoulang”) in Tangshan, Hebei Province, China, has achieved ISCC EU Recycled Carbon Fuel (RCF) producer certiﬁcation
ISCC EU certiﬁcation veriﬁes compliance with the sustainability criteria and GHG savings methodology implemented under the EU’s Renewable Energy Directive (RED III) and is recognized by the European Commission. It is also approved by the UK’s Department for Transport to demonstrate compliance with the Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation (RTFO) and the UK SAF Mandate
Certiﬁcate was issued under the ISCC EU scheme, following an audit by SCS Global Services and its China-based affiliate, Beijing Green Forest Certiﬁcation
 
SKOKIE, Ill., Oct.  21, 2026 
(GLOBE NEWSWIRE)
 -- LanzaTech Global, Inc. (NASDAQ: LNZA) (“LanzaTech”) today announced that Hebei Shoulang, located in Tangshan, Hebei Province, China, has been certiﬁed under the International Sustainability and Carbon Certiﬁcation (ISCC) EU scheme as a Recycled Carbon Fuel (RCF) producer.
 
The ISCC EU certiﬁcation standard is recognized by the European Commission. It complies with the sustainability criteria laid down in the Renewable Energy Directive (2018/2001/EU), which promotes the use of renewable energy sources.
 
Hebei Shoulang is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Beijing Shougang LanzaTech Technology Co., Ltd., (“SGLT”, 02553.HK). The facility uses patented gas fermentation technology licensed from LanzaTech to convert waste gases from steel production into ethanol. Certiﬁcation enables certiﬁed and compliant volumes of the facility’s ethanol to be marketed as recycled carbon fuel for direct use in European Union marine and road markets, as well as for aviation fuel markets in the European Union and United Kingdom, subject to any additional regulatory requirements in those markets.
 
“This is a signiﬁcant milestone for SGLT and for the Hebei Shoulang facility,” said Qingkun Song, Deputy General Manager of SGLT. “Achieving ISCC EU certiﬁcation for recycled carbon fuel shows that carbon recycling is not only technically proven, but commercially relevant in regulated fuel markets. We are proud to help establish this new category and to support growing demand for certiﬁed lower-carbon fuels across Europe and beyond.”
 
Under RED III, EU Member States require fuel suppliers to incorporate increasing shares of renewable fuels to meet national transport decarbonization targets, and may allow recycled carbon fuels to count toward them. ISCC EU certiﬁcation provides the veriﬁed sustainability, GHG emission savings calculations and chain-of-custody documentation obligated parties need to count certiﬁed volumes of ethanol, including from Hebei Shoulang, toward these targets, where national rules allow.
 
Meo Carbon Solutions, a sustainability consultancy specializing in greenhouse gas accounting, regulatory advice, and certiﬁcation pathways for sustainable fuels, supported LanzaTech and Hebei Shoulang through the process. LanzaTech also worked with Brookﬁeld to address renewable power requirements for the facility, a complex element of the pathway that is expected to inform future recycled carbon fuel certiﬁcations.
 
“Achieving ISCC EU certiﬁcation for recycled carbon fuel at Hebei Shoulang is a critical step in opening regulated European fuel markets to our CarbonSmart ethanol,” said Dr. Jennifer Holmgren, CEO and Board Chair of LanzaTech. “This is not just another certiﬁcate; it creates a near-term pathway to monetize recycled carbon in EU marine and road markets, while positioning us for aviation opportunities in the EU and UK. In mandated markets where veriﬁed carbon intensity carries real value, certiﬁcation is what turns customer interest into revenue opportunity and accelerates our path to proﬁtability.”

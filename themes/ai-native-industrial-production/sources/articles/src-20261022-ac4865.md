Phoenix Tailings, a rare earth metals processor headquartered in Burlington, Massachusetts, has selected Eastman Business Park in Rochester, New York, for what it calls Freedom Separation, a commercial-scale facility designed to convert a diverse range of mixed rare earth feedstocks into separated oxides for domestic rare earth metal production.
According to the company, which also has refining facilities in Woburn, Massachusetts and Exeter, New Hampshire, the new $375 million facility is part of its $1.2 billion Freedom Project, which it describes as an integrated separation and metallization platform aimed at closing a “critical gap” in United States midstream rare earth processing capacity.
Phoenix says the 500,000-square-foot Freedom Separation site will process mined and recycled rare earth feedstocks from various sources. At full capacity, the company expects the facility to be one of the largest of its type in the world, processing more than 18,000 tons per year and producing more than 4,400 tons per year of separated rare earth oxides. Production is expected to begin ramping up in 2027, with full production slated for 2028.
Phoenix sells its finished rare earth metals and alloys to into the automotive, defense and medical device sectors, with customers based in the U.S. and allied nations.
The company says Eastman Business Park was chosen because it combines the industrial infrastructure its process requires along with Rochester’s skilled workforce in engineering, advanced manufacturing, chemical processing and plant operations.
“My grandfather, Nicholas Sifkas, started Rochester 100 Inc. just 15 minutes from the site of our new facility,” 
Phoenix
 co-founder and CEO Nick Myers says. “Growing up watching him build his amazing company, and having worked on the factory floor myself, I know firsthand the depth of talent and manufacturing expertise in Rochester.
“Technology can give America control over its critical supply chains, but it takes skilled, determined people to turn that promise into production,” he continues. “Rochester has those people. They know how to build, how to innovate and how to run world-class industrial operations. I am proud that Phoenix will build the future of American rare earth processing here.”
When fully operational, Phoenix says the Rochester facility will support more than 200 direct New York jobs, including 90 at launch. Overall, the company says the Freedom separation and metallization platform is expected to support more than 500 jobs across facilities in New York, Massachusetts and New Hampshire, plus a future unnamed location.
“I am committed to supporting innovative projects and companies that are ready to invest in New York and bring good-paying jobs,” says New York Gov. Kathy Hochul. “Phoenix Tailings will not only create at least 90 new jobs at Eastman Business Park, further supporting our state’s innovation economy, but they will also play a critical role in bolstering our national security by building a supply chain for rare earth elements in New York, reducing reliance on foreign sources of critical minerals.”
Phoenix notes that its Freedom Project 
previously secured a $500 million conditional loan commitment
 from the U.S. Department of Defense’s Office of Strategic Capital under the Trump administration. The company says support from Hochul and Sen. Chuck Schumer builds on that federal commitment, bringing bipartisan backing to the expansion of America’s domestic rare earth processing capacity.
‘I’m proud to welcome Phoenix Tailings to the Rochester-Finger Lakes region,” Schumer says. “This new investment will bring rare earth mineral manufacturing operations, along with at least 90 new, good-paying jobs, to Eastman Business Park. This is critical for reducing our reliance on foreign processors and protecting our national security. I’ve long championed Eastman Business Park as a hub for manufacturing and am glad that new companies are recognizing what I have long known: Upstate New York is a prime destination for new manufacturing investments. I am grateful for Gov. Hochul’s partnership welcoming new investments to the state.”

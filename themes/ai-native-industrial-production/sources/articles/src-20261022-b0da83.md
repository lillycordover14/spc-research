Former OpenAI science lead Kevin Weil launches Warp Sciences to automate drug-discovery labs

Thursday, 22 October 2026, 21:59

Photo source: Amy/ Flickrunder a CC BY 2.0license.

The startup aims to use robots to make and test compounds identified by researchers, and bring molecule production back from contractors in China.

Former OpenAI science lead Kevin Weil has launched Warp Sciences, an AI startup focused on automating lab work for drug discovery. Weil announced the company and its $215 million funding round on X. The round was led by Lightspeed, with participation from IVP, Premji Invest and Lux Capital, at an $865 million valuation, according to Weil.

This is reported by 
TechCrunch
.

Biotech companies have for years outsourced the labor-intensive work of synthesizing thousands of molecule variations to contractors in China, including WuXi AppTec. Weil hopes Warp Sciences can bring that work back and shield the drug-development process from geopolitical supply-chain risks.

Warp Sciences’ human chemists will wear cameras and sensor gloves to train robots to carry out lab tasks. The company aims to speed up drug discovery by automating wet-lab work with robots that can operate around the clock.

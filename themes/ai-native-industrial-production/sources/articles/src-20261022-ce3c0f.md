Nvidia and Tesla Back $2.8 Billion Chip Substrate Buildout
Less than 1 min read

Nvidia Corp. (NVDA, Financials), the producer of AI chips, and Tesla are rumored to be sponsoring a large increase of the capacity of sophisticated semiconductor substrates in South Korea.
The two businesses will pay Samsung Electro-Mechanics 3.7 trillion won ($2.8 billion) to establish facilities for making FC-BGA substrates for AI chips.
Nvidia is anticipated to invest 2.6 trillion won, and Tesla will invest 1.1 trillion won. The production lines are planned for Sejong and would provide substrates under long-term contracts.
Introduction FC-BGA substrates are a key element of modern chip packaging, linking semiconductor packages to printed circuit boards, and enabling high-performance computer systems.
For investors in Nvidia, the reported investment underscores how the firm is locking in more of the supply chain it needs to sustain future development in AI hardware.
It also reveals that the money being spent on artificial intelligence isn't only for GPUs, but also for the components needed to make and run high-end CPUs on a broad scale.
Tesla's involvement adds another major customer to the project as it pursues its own AI and computing aspirations.
What to watch for next is when construction starts and how soon the increased capacity can be brought online.

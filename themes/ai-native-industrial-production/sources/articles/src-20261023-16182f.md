Warp Sciences Raises $215M to Build AI-Powered Drug Discovery Labs&nbsp;&nbsp;citybiz

[page text]
Warp Sciences CEO Kevin Weil.

San Francisco-based 
Warp Sciences
 has emerged with
 $215 million in funding led by Lightspeed
 as the company seeks to build a U.S.-based chemistry research business that combines artificial intelligence, robotics, and laboratory services for biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies.

The round also saw participation from 
Premji Invest, Mayfield, IVP, Lux, Conviction, Menlo Ventures, 01 Advisors, Standard, Lowercarbon Capital, Gigascale Capital, Angular Ventures, Scribble Ventures, Uncork Capital, Innovation Endeavors, Matter VP, Zero Shot Fund, SignalFire and Moxxie Ventures
.

Warp’s initial offering will center on lead optimization analog synthesis, a process in which chemists create variations of a promising molecule for researchers to compare their properties and identify candidates for further development. It aims to compete on speed, cost, and intellectual property protection while building an experimental data pipeline that could improve its AI systems’ performance.

The company says experienced chemists will guide the work as it introduces greater automation through robotics and AI, using data generated by physical experiments to train models that help determine which molecules to make and how to produce them.

“Chemistry and biology move forward when you make something, test it, and learn from the result. If we want to build more capable AI for science, we need to generate real-world data at scale,” said 
Kevin Weil
,
 co-founder and CEO of Warp Sciences.

Before founding Warp Sciences, Weil held senior product leadership roles across major technology companies. He served as chief product officer at OpenAI from 2024 to 2026, where he led AI-for-science initiatives, and was president of product and business at satellite-imaging company Planet Labs.

Earlier, he co-founded Facebook’s Novi digital wallet and served as vice president of product for the Libra cryptocurrency project. At Instagram, he oversaw product development during the expansion of Instagram Stories, while at Twitter, he held senior product leadership roles focused on product strategy and monetization.

Warp intends to operate as a service provider rather than as a drug developer. The company says its domestic services are intended to offer an alternative to overseas providers without the price premium associated with some U.S. boutique research organizations.

Lightspeed is a global, multi-stage venture capital firm managing more than $40 billion in assets. Founded in 2000, the firm invests across sectors and stages, with a portfolio that includes AI companies Abridge, Anthropic, Glean and Mistral, as well as cybersecurity firms Netskope, Rubrik and Wiz, travel technology company Navan, social media company Snap and defense technology startup Castelion.

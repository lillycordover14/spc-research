New Hatsune Miku Swan Lake Black Swan Ballerina Figure Teased&nbsp;&nbsp;Siliconera

[page text]
Image via Bandai Namco

Bandai Namco teased a new Banpresto Evolve Classical Tuning 
Swan Lake
 Hatsune Miku figure of her dressed as the Black Swan Odile from the ballet on 
social media
. There’s no release window for it yet. It follows one of her dressed as the 
White Swan Princess Odette
. 
 
While this is a “new” Hatsune Miku ballerina figure, the Black Swan Odile will be a variant of the already available White Swan Odette Banpresto Evolve Classical Tuning 
Swan Lake
 design. This means it should stand at about eight inches tall again (210mm). Her dress features a new black color scheme with pink highlights. She’s still in the same pose and position. However, her facial expression is different. The original art from 
Kisumi Rei
 is still being used as the inspiration for it. 
 
Here are the first images showing how this version of the character will look.

Enshrouded
 
PS5
 Review
. Few titles in the survival crafting landscape arrive with the quiet confidence of 
Keen Games
‘ 
Enshrouded
. Rather than dropping players into an endless, procedurally generated void, this action-RPG presents Embervale – a meticulously hand-crafted continent buried beneath a deadly fog.
Now launching in its complete 1.0 state on 
PlayStation 5
, this voxel-based epic stands as a stellar achievement. It seamlessly fuses responsive action-RPG combat, sweeping aerial traversal, and the most versatile building mechanics in modern gaming into an utterly absorbing package.
Enshrouded Review (PS5) – Flame, Fog And Voxel Fortresses
Stepping out of the Cinder Vault and overlooking the rolling pastures of the Springlands is an immediate visual triumph. Enshrouded employs a custom voxel engine that renders expansive vistas alongside extraordinary micro-level destruction. You can literally take a pickaxe and carve a home straight into the side of any mountain.
Sunbeams pierce through dense forest canopies while eerie blue spores swirl inside low-lying fog banks, creating a striking contrast between the surface world and the gloom below. On 
PS5
, performance remains impressively stable, locking to a smooth 60 FPS target across vast open landscapes.
DualSense
 integration adds a satisfying layer of tactile feedback to every expedition. You feel the heavy crunch of a warhammer shattering rock and the distinct tension of pulling back a longbow string. Subtle directional haptics also pulse through the grip, signalling when your stamina is dangerously low during a frantic climb.
Breath Of The Shroud
At the heart of Embervale lies its most brilliant design hook: the Shroud. Descending into these fog-choked valleys places you on a strict survival timer, granting roughly five minutes to complete your mission before toxic vapors claim your life.
Entering the mist feels less like routine gathering and more like a high-stakes heist as a result. You must carefully chart your route, hunt down corrupted Shroud Roots to claim skill points, and escape before the timer drains completely. Strengthening your Flame Altar back at base extends your resistance, gradually opening up higher-level red zones.
Traversal between these dangerous dives is an absolute joy thanks to the Grappling Hook and Glider. Scaling Ancient Spires rewards you with fast-travel points and towering launchpads. Snapping your glider open and soaring over massive canyons transforms travel into an addictive, momentum-filled highlight.
Where most survival titles limit your creativity to rigid, pre-built snapped walls, Enshrouded unlocks total architectural freedom. Its voxel construction allows you to place individual blocks or massive pre-formed structures, with different stone and wood textures blending together seamlessly.
Architects Of The Flame
Building a rustic hillside hamlet or a massive mountain fortress feels genuinely phenomenal. Flame Altars act as protective claim markers, shielding your construction zones from the creeping fog. As you rescue specialized craftspeople like the Blacksmith and Alchemist, your base evolves from a simple shelter into a bustling hub.
Interior decorating serves a vital mechanical purpose rather than just looking pretty. Placing beds, rugs, fireplaces, and trophy heads increases your shelter’s Comfort rating. This extends your Rested buff to supercharge your health and stamina regeneration out in the field.
The 1.0 release also introduces Sparkworks, a complex logic and wire system. This feature lets ambitious architects construct automated light switches, hidden secret doors, and elaborate mechanical traps. Designing functional, powered castles brings a whole new layer of depth to base management.
Mastering The Repertoire
Combat in Enshrouded avoids traditional, clunky survival tropes, leaning heavily into fluid action-RPG mechanics. The expansive skill tree lets you customize your playstyle freely, transitioning between heavy melee parries, swift archery, and devastating elemental magic. Target lock-on can occasionally feel slightly floaty during chaotic multi-enemy brawls, though combat generally remains responsive and engaging.
Reaching late-game biomes like Cinder’s Cradle unlocks powerful Eternal spells that eliminate the need for consumable ammo. Slinging massive fireballs from high ground while teammates draw threat with heavy shields creates dynamic, satisfying battle encounters.
Danger is always lurking just beyond the horizon during these excursions. During one ill-fated dive into the Revelwood, I misjudged my Shroud timer while hunting an elusive crafting material. Panic set in as my timer hit thirty seconds, forcing me to leap off a cliff and glide blindly through thick fog toward a distant Return Beacon, landing with literally two seconds to spare.
Customizing The Realm
Navigating Embervale as a solo adventurer is a remarkably slick, frictionless experience on PS5. Granular world modifier sliders allow you to fine-tune resource yields, stamina costs, and enemy damage to suit your exact preferred pacing. Load times on the 
PS5 SSD
 are remarkably swift across the board. Furthermore, fast-traveling across the entire continent or booting into your personal world takes mere seconds.
Dungeons like the multi-floor Hollow Halls scale enemy health and spawn density dynamically based on your presence, keeping solo dungeon raids challenging and rewarding. Dedicated server options and private world saves ensure your custom kingdom remains entirely under your control. Whether you want a brutal hardcore survival test or a relaxed builder’s sandbox, Enshrouded accommodates your solo journey effortlessly.
Despite its towering successes, Enshrouded carries a few mechanical quirks that keep it just shy of absolute perfection. While your Flame Altar keeps your constructed bases safe forever, the wilderness outside operates on automated chunk resets. On dedicated servers, leaving an area for thirty minutes completely regenerates mined ore veins, looted chest rewards, and defeated enemy camps.
On local sessions, simply reloading your save restores cleared fortresses back to square one. Consequently, this turns high-level resource farming into a breeze, slaying a menacing boss loses a bit of its impact when the entire camp resurrects an hour later. Inventory management can also devolve into a tedious chore over time.
Managing dozens of specialized crafting materials across multiple NPC stations requires constant chest sorting. Although magic chests allow crafting directly from stored items, keeping your main operational base organized still demands significant manual upkeep.
Step Into The Fog
Enshrouded 1.0 stands as a triumphant landmark for modern action-survival RPGs. While it doesn’t quite reach the pure survival heights of 
Valheim
, its extraordinary building suite sits worlds above almost every other offering on the market.
By pairing unmatched voxel construction with thrilling aerial traversal and rewarding combat, Keen Games has delivered an essential console release. For anyone seeking a grand, deeply rewarding adventure on PS5, step into the fog without hesitation.
Enshrouded 1.0 is out now on PlayStation 5.

The Final Word
Enshrouded 1.0 is an outstanding action-survival RPG that sets a lofty new benchmark for voxel building and world exploration on PS5. While it doesn't quite match the raw survival grit of Valheim, its unparalleled building freedom, high-stakes Shroud timers, and excellent co-op gameplay make Embervale a joy to explore. Minor world reset quirks aside, this is an essential addition to any PS5 library.

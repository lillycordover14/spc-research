The Gist
Janet Jackson, 60, attended the 2026 Dancers Against Cancer’s Gala of the Stars on October 22, 2026.
She walked the red carpet in a plunging, figure-hugging lace LBD.
Jackson styled her shoulder-length lob in voluminous curls.
With Halloween just days away, it appears 
Janet Jackson
 is leaning into vampy dressing. The multi-hyphenate stepped out for the 2026 Dancers Against Cancer’s Gala of the Stars on October 22 in a black gown that was equal parts romantic and edgy.
Janet Jackson attends the Gala of the Stars on October 22, 2026.Credit: Getty Images
The creation in question was a little black dress that featured long sleeves, structured shoulders, a high neck, a narrow plunging keyhole cutout, a built-in belt detail, a figure-hugging silhouette, and a floor-sweeping skirt that hid her shoes. The frock was completely covered in dainty lace, which was laid over a nude-colored lining, giving the illusion of nakedness.
Janet Jackson attends the Gala of the Stars on October 22, 2026.Credit: Getty Images
The 
“All for You” hitmaker
 added several chunky diamond rings and dramatic gold-and-diamond hoop earrings. Jackson wore bronzy makeup, complete with dark eyeliner, long fluttery eyelashes, sun-glazed skin, glossy brown-red lips, and long gold manicured nails. She styled her blonde-highlighted shoulder-length lob in voluminous, pushed back curls.

Jackson’s
 last red carpet came in June, when she attended the 2026 BET Awards in Los Angeles, California. Then, she went in a completely different sartorial direction that combined business attire with street style. Up top, Jackson layered a gray “2Pac” T-shirt over a white pinstripe button-up, which she cuffed at the sleeves. She added a pair of baggy gray pinstripe pants, a black leather corset, a wide black leather belt covered in grommets, and a gray hat decorated with wispy feathers and a lace fascinator. Layers of chunky silver bangles, a smattering of statement rings, and huge silver hoop earrings finished off her look.

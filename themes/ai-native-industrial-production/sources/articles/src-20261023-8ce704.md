WINSTON-SALEM, N.C.—
Members of the University of North Carolina men's tennis team posted a strong first day on Friday at the Wake Forest Invitational at the Wake Forest Tennis Center.


Tar Heel players went 3-1 in singles matches and 2-2 in doubles on the first day of the three-day event.

Will Mayew
, 
Nick Mangiapane
 and 
Lachlan Gaskell
 all won their initial singles matches. In doubles play, the teams of Mayew and Gaskell and 
Oliver Narbut
 and Mangiapane each split their two matches on Friday.


Action at the Wake Forest Invitational will continue on Saturday and Sunday in Winston-Salem.

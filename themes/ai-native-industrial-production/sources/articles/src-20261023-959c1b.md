Yuna Aoki leads at Grand Prix France to open figure skating season

By

Nick Zaccardi
,

By

Nick Zaccardi

 
 
Published October 23, 2026 05:34 PM

        Email
    

Japan’s Yuna Aoki seized the lead with a personal best short program at Grand Prix France, the first event of the six-event Grand Prix figure skating regular season.
Aoki, a 24-year-old eyeing her first Grand Prix win, landed a triple Lutz-triple loop combination (negatively graded), double Axel and triple flip in her 76.31-point program.
She leads by 3.68 over Anastasiia Gubanova of Georgia going into Saturday’s free skate. 
Aoki is building off momentum from the second half of last season, when she placed a career-best fifth at the Japanese Championships in December, then won the Four Continents title in January,

Olympic bronze medalist Ami Nakai of Japan, the top returning skater this season from the Milan Cortina Games, is third, 5.65 points behind, after popping her opening triple Axel attempt.
Sarah Everhardt, the lone American in the women’s field, is ninth after falling on her opening triple Lutz.
Grand Prix France continues Saturday with the men’s short program, free dance, pairs’ short and women’s free skate, all live on 
Peacock
.
Earlier Friday, Olympic gold medalists Laurence Fournier Beaudry and Guillaume Cizeron opened their season by topping the rhythm dance at their home Grand Prix.
Fournier Beaudry and Cizeron, who teamed up last year, totaled 82.56 points for their rhythm and waltz program, the world’s second-best score on the young season despite a one-point time violation.
They lead by 4.66 over Diana Davis and Gleb Smolkin of Georgia, who own the world’s top rhythm dance and total scores this season from Trialeti Trophy, their home Challenger Series event, three weeks ago.
Americans Christina Carreira and Anthony Ponomarenko are just seven hundredths of a point behind Davis and Smolkin. They are in position to return to a Grand Prix podium after placing fourth and fifth in their two starts last fall.

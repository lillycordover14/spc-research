Article Summary

McFarlane Toys adds Kratos from God of War Ragnarök to its Gaming lineup with a new 7-inch Elite Edition figure.

The God of War figure features soft goods, premium paint, detailed sculpting, and up to 22 points of articulation.

Kratos includes the Leviathan Axe, Blades of Chaos, shield pieces, Bifröst accessory, extra hands, and a swap head.

Pre-orders are live now for $49.99 through the McFarlane Toys Store, with the God of War release set for November 2026.

The God of War is marching into McFarlane Toys' gaming lineup, and the Ghost of Sparta is ready to make his presence felt! Before he became a father fighting to protect Atreus in the Nine Realms, Kratos was a warrior consumed by rage, revenge, and the weight of his own past. The 
God of War
 franchise has followed his brutal journey from ancient Greece to the Norse realms, transforming the Ghost of Sparta from a relentless destroyer of gods into a battle-hardened warrior struggling to control his anger. With 
God of War Ragnarök
 showcasing a more mature Kratos, his iconic look remains instantly recognizable to longtime fans, complete with his ash-white skin, red tattoo, and legendary arsenal.

Now, McFarlane Toys is bringing the Norse-era warrior to collectors with its Gaming Edition Kratos 7-inch figure! Inspired by his appearance in 
God of War Ragnarök
, this release features up to 22 points of articulation, allowing fans to recreate a variety of battle-ready poses. Kratos comes equipped with the Leviathan Axe, a Bifröst accessory, extra hands, and a display base. This figure is a must-have release for any Kratos fans, and pre-orders are already live on the 
McFarlane Toys Store
 for $49.99, with an expected November 2026 release.

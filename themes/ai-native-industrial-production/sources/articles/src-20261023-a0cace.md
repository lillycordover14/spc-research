							By Brian Murphy, WRAL News
						

CHAPEL HILL — In the final minutes of North Carolina’s comeback victory over Duke, it was backups making the biggest plays for the Tar Heels.

Running back Jaylen McGill scored the game-winning touchdown, and defensive back Kenton Dopson III secured the win with a game-sealing interception. Both were on the field after injuries to starters at their respective positions.

Other WRAL Top Stories

Several more Tar Heels could get an opportunity this week when UNC (4-2 overall, 2-1 in the ACC) hosts Syracuse (2-4, 0-4) on Saturday night (7:30 p.m., ACC Network). Eight players who have started multiple games for the Tar Heels this season are listed as out or questionable on the team’s availability report ahead of its ACC home opener.

“It’s being ready and being ready to take advantage of the opportunities when you get it,” North Carolina coach Bill Belichick said. “We got a lot of guys that work hard and a lot of guys that are playing well, and if something were to happen to them, somebody else would get an opportunity.”

Belichick, the longtime coach of the NFL’s New England Patriots, oversaw one of the most iconic injury-related transitions in NFL history. In 2001, quarterback Tom Brady took over for injured starter Drew Bledsoe and led the Patriots to a Super Bowl title that season, the first of Brady’s seven championships.

“Those are all opportunities for guys to get the playing time that they’ve been working for,” Belichick said.

Wide receivers Jordan Shipp, defensive backs Kaleb Cost and Peyton Waters, running back Benjamin Hall, offensive lineman Jordan Hall and kicker Aeron Burrell are listed as questionable on 
the ACC’s availability report
. Cost missed last week’s game against Duke, while Hall and Burrell left the game early with injuries. All have started multiple games for the Tar Heels this season. Defensive back Tre Miller, who has started four games but missed the Duke game, is listed as out.

McGill set career highs in carries (12), rushing yards (75) and longest rush (24 yards) in the 24-20 win over Duke. His 24-yard touchdown run gave the Tar Heels the lead late in the fourth quarter. Hall, the team’s leading rusher this season, injured his hamstring early in the game.

“He just told us to go execute and that we were prepared to do what we needed to do,” McGill said.

Defensive lineman Nicco Maggio is another player who has taken advantage of injuries ahead of him on the depth chart to earn playing time — and made the most of it.

“Just doing what I can and helping anywhere I can,” said Maggio, a redshirt freshman who has 12 tackles. “Whether it’s 10 plays, 40 plays, just doing my job.”

Syracuse is also dealing with significant injuries, especially at quarterback.

Steve Angeli is expected to start at quarterback for Syracuse, which has used four quarterbacks through six games. Angeli opened the season as the starter but missed the last two games. He is listed as questionable.

Amari Odom (shoulder) and Malachi Nelson (ribs, foot) are out for Saturday’s game. Nelson started the previous two games. Danny Lauter has played in each of the last two games and would start if Angeli can’t play.

“I don’t think I’ve ever coached against a team that’s had four quarterbacks that are all legitimate quarterbacks,” Belichick said. “They’ve all played. They’ve all played well. These guys can move the ball. They can go up and down the field.”

Syracuse started walk-on Joseph Filardi, a top lacrosse recruit, at quarterback against UNC last season, with Angeli sidelined by an Achilles injury and the Orange offense sputtering under backup Rickie Collins. Filardi struggled in UNC’s 27-10 win at Syracuse.

The Tar Heels are seeking their first three-game winning streak under Belichick, who is in his second season in Chapel Hill. UNC has already matched its win total and conference win total from Belichick’s first season through six games.

The Tar Heels host No. 1 Miami on Halloween.

UNC linebacker Derek McDonald spent five seasons at Syracuse. He recorded 173 tackles for the Orange and has 56 tackles for the Tar Heels.

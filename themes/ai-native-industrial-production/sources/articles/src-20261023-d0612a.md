Phoenix Tailings to Build $375M Rare Earth Plant in New York

Jonathan Stroud
 
23-Oct-2026  
 

Phoenix Tailings will invest $375 million in a Rochester rare earth separation facility, targeting 4,400 tonnes of annual output by 2028.

Phoenix Tailings has announced plans to establish a $375 million commercial-scale 
rare earth
 separation facility in Rochester, New York, marking a significant step toward strengthening the United States’ domestic supply chain for critical minerals. The proposed facility, named Freedom Separation, will be developed at Eastman Business Park as part of the company’s broader Freedom Project, a platform valued at more than $1.2 billion.

The 500,000-square-foot facility is designed to address a major gap in the US rare earth processing industry, where separation capacity remains heavily concentrated in China. By expanding domestic processing capabilities, Phoenix Tailings aims to improve the availability of strategically important materials used in electric vehicles, electronics, defence equipment and advanced manufacturing.

Freedom Separation will process more than 18,000 tonnes per annum (tpa) of mined and recycled rare earth materials. The company expects the facility to produce more than 4,400 tpa of separated rare earth oxides once operational. The site is targeting the start of limited operations in 2027, followed by full production capacity in 2028.

Eastman Business Park was selected after a nationwide evaluation, with Phoenix Tailings citing its established industrial infrastructure and access to a skilled workforce. The location offers expertise in engineering, advanced manufacturing, chemical processing and industrial plant operations, supporting the development of a large-scale rare earth processing operation.

The project is expected to create more than 200 direct jobs in New York at full operational capacity, including at least 90 positions during its initial launch. Across its broader platform spanning New York, Massachusetts, New Hampshire and a planned future site, Phoenix Tailings anticipates supporting more than 500 jobs.

To support the investment, New York State has awarded the company up to $3 million in performance-based tax credits through the Excelsior Jobs Tax Credit Program. The project will also benefit from additional statutory, local and infrastructure incentives, alongside support from Greater Rochester Enterprise.

Phoenix Tailings co-founder and CEO Nick Myers said the company is developing a commercial-scale alternative intended to secure Western rare earth supply chains while pursuing zero waste, zero direct process emissions and safer operating conditions.

The project also has potential financial backing through a conditional commitment for a $500 million long-term loan secured by the Freedom Project in June 2026 from the US Department of War’s Office of Strategic Capital. The commitment remains subject to further reviews before finalisation.

By processing both mined materials and industrial waste, Phoenix Tailings aims to establish a more resilient domestic supply chain and reduce US dependence on overseas rare earth separation.

Product Impact and ChemAnalyst Commodity Price Outlook 

The facility is expected to strengthen US rare earth oxide supply, particularly for downstream manufacturers serving electric vehicles, electronics, defence and advanced manufacturing. However, meaningful market effects will depend on commissioning, feedstock availability, product mix and actual production volumes from 2027–2028. Increased domestic separation capacity could gradually reduce US reliance on Chinese processing and ease regional supply constraints, although it is unlikely to immediately displace established suppliers. For ChemAnalyst-tracked commodities, the project may influence rare earth oxide and associated critical-mineral markets more directly than conventional chemicals. Any price softening would likely be gradual and region-specific; no immediate global price decline is assured.

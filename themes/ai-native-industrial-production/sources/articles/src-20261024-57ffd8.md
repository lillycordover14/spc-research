SendCutSend Hiring Event

			June 13		

				 @ 			

				10:00 am			

					 – 				

					3:00 pm				

SendCutSend, a Reno-based rapid custom manufacturing company, will host two on-site hiring events in June as it continues to expand its local workforce and operations.

The company is hiring for a variety of roles across production, finishing, shipping and quality. Applicants of all experience levels are encouraged to attend and will have the opportunity to meet the team, learn about available positions and interview on the spot.
The parts that leave SendCutSend’s Reno floor support robotics, aerospace, distribution, agriculture, rocketry, and prototypes coming out of some of the country’s most ambitious companies. With more than 500 employees and a new Reno facility on the way, the company is looking for motivated people who want to help build the future of American manufacturing.

WHO

People interested in the following areas and more:

Production
Fiber laser, CNC Machining and CO2 laser operators

Post production
CNC Press Brake, Powder Coating, Anodizing, Welding

Shipping
Quality and shipping technicians
WHY

With the opening of SendCutSend’s fourth Reno facility, the company is looking for motivated individuals who want to learn new skills and join a company that invests in its people. Pay range is between $24 to $42 per hour based on role and experience.
Founded in Reno in 2018, SendCutSend combines advanced manufacturing with proprietary software to make custom manufacturing faster, easier and more accessible for customers across the country. The company now serves more than 150,000 engineers, fabricators, and businesses and has produced over 30 million parts.
The company offers on-the-job training, opportunities for advancement and a comprehensive benefits package that includes 100% employer-paid medical coverage for employees, paid time off, one paid meal per shift, and retirement benefits.
“We believe great people can learn great skills,” said Jim Belosic, founder and CEO of SendCutSend. “We’re looking for motivated individuals who want to be part of a growing company, learn something new and build a long-term career right here in Reno.”
Job seekers can learn more about available positions and SendCutSend’s culture here: https://sendcutsend.com/careers/.

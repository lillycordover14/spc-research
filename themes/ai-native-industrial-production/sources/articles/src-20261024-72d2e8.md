How to Watch UNC Football take on Syracuse
The Tar Heels will play in Primetime for the first time this season.
by
 
 
Al Hood
Oct 24, 2026, 1:00 PM UTC
A primetime game against an opponent that Carolina can beat with a chance to earn a major showcase game next week. What could go wrong?
After playing what seemed like the first half of the season at noon—that’s not really an exaggeration as last Saturday was the first game played at a time other than 12 PM—the lights will shine bright at Kenan Stadium tonight as the Tar Heels play on the ACC Network at a time that should create an amazing atmosphere at the Stadium in the Pines. For the first time this season, the new screens and LED boards will get a chance to fully shine, the light display will have a chance to pop on the screen, and the Tar Heels have a chance to exceed last year’s win total with five games remaining, all to possibly earn a prime time game on ABC next Saturday.
Primetime on ABC you say? Does that mean that maybe—just maybe—the traveling circus that is College Gameday would also make its way to Chapel Hill?
While it’s doubtful that the ACC figured it would happened based on the lack of buzz on this UNC team, there was a smart decision by the league to schedule the Miami game for the last weekend of October. When the calendar shifts to November and conference play rules the day, it’s almost guaranteed that ABC will have some marquee SEC matchup happening in that Saturday Primetime spot, and with Miami looking to get back to the CFP plus the allure of Bill Belichick, it felt like this was a hope that maybe the ACC could show itself off.
Of course the only way all of this works is if both teams actually win today.
Miami has what looks to be an easier time with their game against Pitt, so the key here for a number one Miami to come in against an ascendant UNC squad is for the Tar Heels to defeat a Syracuse team that is starting their fourth-string quarterback, but also has arguably the best receiver in the ACC. It’s just the type of team that could trip Carolina up because every game this season against a P4 opponent has included a small margin of error. Include in all of the injuries that Carolina is nursing, and this game is absolutely not a layup. In fact for Carolina fans, you know this exact spot has happened so many times before only to have the football pulled away.

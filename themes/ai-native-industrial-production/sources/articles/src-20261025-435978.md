CHAPEL HILL – North Carolina dropped an overtime decision to Syracuse on Saturday night at Kenan Stadium, and afterward two Tar Heels met with the media to discuss the defeat.
UNC led 20-10 in the fourth quarter but lost 31-30 in the first overtime when the Orange scored on a two-point conversion.
UNC dropped to 4-3 overall and 2-2 in the ACC.

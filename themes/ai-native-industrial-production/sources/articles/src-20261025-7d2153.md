Garrett Wilson was clearly upset with quarterback Geno Smith during the fourth quarter of Sunday's loss to the Miami Dolphins, and he broke down his frustration after the game. 
The wide receiver said the heated discussion was about getting him the ball in big moments. 
"I want it in my hands to try to make an impact," Wilson 
told reporters
. "That's all we were talking about, how we can make that happen."

Masdar has signed an agreement to acquire and develop one of Greece's largest solar projects from Lightsource bp. The solar photovoltaic (PV) project has a capacity of more than 550 megawatts (MW). The agreement was announced on October 24, 2026, in Athens, during the official visit of Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi. The United Arab Emirates and Greece are marking more than 50 years of diplomatic relations.

A project set to receive battery storage

Masdar plans to add batteries to the 550+ MW project. According to the group, the storage would support grid stability, help integrate renewable energy and add flexibility to Greece's power system. The market for solar and storage assets remains active in Europe, as shown by 
Greenvolt's sale of two battery storage projects in Poland
 or 
ENNA Solar's acquisition of the Romanian Medgidia project
. The transaction also includes two privately owned substations that connect the project to the electricity transmission system.

The project is due to become operational by the end of 2026, Masdar said. It would produce enough electricity to power more than 200,000 homes a year. The group says it will help meet rising electricity demand in Greece driven by artificial intelligence (AI) and digital infrastructure.

A memorandum of understanding with Khazna on data centers

Masdar and Khazna Data Centers also signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) to advance green data center development in Greece. The document combines Khazna's data center development and operating capabilities with Masdar's expertise in renewable energy and storage. The two companies are to explore dedicated clean power and storage solutions for Greece's digital needs.

Sultan Ahmed Al Jaber, the UAE Minister of Industry and Advanced Technology and Chairman of Masdar, said these initiatives would "strengthen Greece's energy security" and support its ambition to become a regional digital hub. He stressed that access to reliable, affordable energy would become increasingly critical to competitiveness. Masdar points to Greece's position at the intersection of Europe, Africa and Asia, and to growing investment in the country's digital infrastructure.

First strategic investment since TERNA ENERGY

The acquisition is Masdar's first strategic investment in Greece since it took TERNA ENERGY private in 2025. Masdar Chief Executive Officer Mohamed Jameel Al Ramahi described it as a milestone in the group's European growth strategy. He said Masdar will develop the asset further with battery storage and expects stable, long-term returns.

Mr. Al Ramahi added that the group is building, through TERNA ENERGY, a stronger renewable energy platform in Greece and the wider region. The parties did not disclose a price in the information available. The two countries aim to expand cooperation across energy, AI and advanced technology.

Avon Players Marks 80 Seasons of Community Theater with Upcoming Twelve Angry Men Production

(Lara Mossa, Oct. 26, 2026)

ROCHESTER HILLS, MI – The Avon Players, a community theater troupe in Rochester Hills, is celebrating its 80
th
 season this year.

“I feel like we put on really quality arts and entertainment,” said Ryan Moore, vice president of communications for the Executive Board. “For the community, it’s wonderful to have homegrown arts and entertainment at a fraction of the cost.”

Founded in 1947, the Avon Players has been at its current location on the corner of Tienken and Washington roads since 1965. All the actors and directors are volunteers, although the group pays musicians to perform for the musicals.

“It’s a labor of love by and large performed by people in all walks of life,” Moore said.

The current membership is around 70 people and, generally, the actors are people who have been around the company and want to audition, he said. There is a nice mix of new and old people for every production, he added.

The seasons consist of five shows including two musicals and three plays as well as a youth theater program in the summer. This year, the troupe did its first original works festival, which included six short plays, in July, and will hold a Christmas concert in December.

“We’ve really pushed the limits of what we can do to make technically strong shows within meager limits,” Moore said. “We try for a really slick and polished product. But we also try to take care of the volunteers on stage and off to make sure everybody has a really good experience.”

“Theater means a lot to me,” said Moore, who has worked for different companies. “But in particular this theater, I’ve kind of chose to make it my home theater, because I rally like the people. We are very focused on putting on quality shows.

The troupe is funded primarily by ticket sales, but as a nonprofit organization, the theater also seeks donations. This year, with its 80
th
 season, the troupe has a goal to raise $80,000. The money will go toward upgrades to the parking lot, exterior paint, repairs and upkeep on the old building.

The organization is run by an elected board voted on by the membership along with the longtime president John (JD) Deierlein, who serves as the stage manager for the shows. In addition, there is a paid box office manager.

The next show on the troupe’s docket is Twelve Angry Men, which Moore described as a classic legal drama about a group of jurors deciding on a case. The production has strong themes about the legal system and justice, he added.

The show runs from November 6
th
 through 21
st
 with performances on Fridays and Saturdays at 7:30 p.m. and two Sunday matinees at 2 p.m. Located at 1185 Washington Road, tickets are available through the website 
www.avonplayers.org
 as well as by calling the box office at 248-608-9077. The cost is $30 a person for the theater that holds 229 people and parking is free.

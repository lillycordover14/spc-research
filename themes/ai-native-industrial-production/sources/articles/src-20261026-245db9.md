Masdar Buys 550+ MW Greek Solar Project From Lightsource bp, Plans Battery Storage

            by 
ESG News Editorial Team

•

Share:

 

 

Solar acquisition: Masdar will buy a 550+ MW Greek solar project from Lightsource bp.

Battery storage: Masdar plans to add storage to support grid stability.

Data center MoU: Masdar and Khazna will explore green data centers in Greece.

Masdar has agreed to acquire and develop a 550+ megawatt (MW) solar photovoltaic (PV) project in Greece from Lightsource bp. Masdar calls it one of Greece’s largest solar projects. The plant is set to start operating by the end of 2026. It will produce enough electricity to power more than 200,000 homes a year.

The deal was announced during a visit to Greece by His Highness Sheikh Khaled bin Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi. The UAE and Greece are marking more than 50 years of diplomatic relations. Masdar says the project will help meet Greek electricity demand growth driven by artificial intelligence (AI) and digital infrastructure.

Separately, Masdar and Khazna Data Centers signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on green data center development in Greece. The pact pairs Khazna’s data center development and operating skills with Masdar’s renewable energy and storage expertise. The partners will explore dedicated clean power and storage for Greece’s growing digital infrastructure needs. 
“The UAE and Greece share a strategic partnership built on trust, long-term ambition and a commitment to mutual prosperity. As artificial intelligence and digital industries transform the global economy, access to reliable, affordable energy will be increasingly critical to competitiveness and growth. Masdar’s expanding renewable energy portfolio, alongside its new collaboration with Khazna, brings together two essential foundations of the future economy: clean power and digital infrastructure. Together, these initiatives will strengthen Greece’s energy security, support its ambition to become a regional digital hub and unlock new opportunities for investment and economic growth. They also reflect the UAE’s commitment to building enduring international partnerships that power the industries of tomorrow and deliver lasting prosperity,”
 said 
His Excellency Dr Sultan Ahmed Al Jaber, UAE Minister of Industry and Advanced Technology and Chairman of Masdar.

His Excellency Dr Sultan Ahmed Al Jaber, UAE Minister of Industry and Advanced Technology and Chairman of Masdar

Battery Storage and Substations

Masdar plans to add battery storage to support grid stability and power system flexibility. The deal also includes two privately owned substations linking the project to the electricity transmission system. It still needs regulatory approval and customary closing conditions, with completion expected by the end of this year. Masdar did not disclose the purchase price, offtakers or any impact on employees.

Building on TERNA ENERGY

The acquisition is Masdar’s first strategic investment through TERNA ENERGY, its platform for Southeastern and Central Europe. Masdar took full ownership in April 2025, after a 70 per cent purchase valued the company at €3.2 billion. Masdar targets 6 gigawatts (GW) of regional operational capacity by 2030, backed by a €5.5 billion plan. 
“These agreements represent another important milestone in our European growth strategy, expanding our portfolio with a high-quality solar asset that we will develop further with battery storage, and will deliver stable, long-term returns. Our collaboration with Khazna builds on this foundation, exploring opportunities to power Greece’s growing digital economy with clean energy. Through TERNA ENERGY, we are building a stronger renewable energy platform in Greece and the wider region, while creating long-term value for our shareholders, partners and the communities we serve,”
 said 
Mohamed Jameel Al Ramahi, Chief Executive Officer of Masdar.

Mohamed Jameel Al Ramahi, Chief Executive Officer of Masdar

Why It Matters

For Masdar, the deal adds a large solar and storage asset to TERNA ENERGY’s portfolio in Greece. For Khazna, the MoU adds Masdar’s renewable and storage expertise to its Greek data center plans. If the MoU yields supply agreements, the project could become a contracted clean power source for Khazna’s Greek data centers.

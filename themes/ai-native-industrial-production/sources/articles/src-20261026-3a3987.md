                    Sakditad Saowapa: HARMONi-GI1 Shows Improved Overall Survival With Ivonescimab in Advanced BTC

Sakditad Saowapa
, Oncologist at UI Holden Comprehensive Cancer Center, shared on 
X
:

“
HARMONi-GI1
 – phase 3 study, ivonescimab, a PD-1/VEGF bispecific antibody, plus cis/gem versus durvalumab plus cis/gem as first-line treatment for advanced BTC.

Results:
 

Median OS was significantly improved with ivonescimab plus chemo vs durvalumab plus chemo (14.9 vs. 12.1 months; HR 0.75, P = 0.0031).

Median PFS was 8.4 vs. 5.8 months (HR 0.61, P <0.0001).

The ORR was 53.8 % vs. 32.5 % (P <0.0001).”

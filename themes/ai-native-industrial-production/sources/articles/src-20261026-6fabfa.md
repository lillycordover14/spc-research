Abdiel Zapata Obituary
Obituary published on Legacy.com by Pilar Funeral Home - Garland from Oct. 26 to Oct. 28, 2026.
With heavy hearts and deep sorrow, we announce the passing of Abdiel Issai Garcia Zapata, who entered eternal rest on October 24, 2026, in 
Garland, Texas
, at the age of 34. He was born on July 10, 1992, in Reynosa, Tamaulipas, Mexico.
Abdiel was a beloved husband, father, son, and brother whose life was filled with love and cherished moments with his family. He will always be remembered for the love he shared with those closest to him and the precious memories he leaves behind.
He is survived by his loving father, Heron; his beloved mother, Ann Graciela; his devoted wife, Angie; his cherished daughter, Marisol; his brother, Alexis; and his sister, Anahi.
Abdiel will be deeply missed by his family, friends, and all those who had the privilege of knowing him. Though he is no longer physically present, his love and memory will forever remain in the hearts of those who knew and loved him.
May he rest in eternal peace, surrounded by God's everlasting love.

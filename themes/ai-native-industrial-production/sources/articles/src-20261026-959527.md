NBC Sports and 
Peacock
 combine to air every major competition of the 2026-27 figure skating season, from the Grand Prix Series starting in October to the Prevagen U.S. Championships in January and through the World Championships in March.
The Grand Prix Series features six regular season stops — including Saatva Skate America from Nov. 13-15 in Everett, Washington — where skaters can earn points to qualify for the Grand Prix Final from Dec. 10-13 in Chongqing, China.

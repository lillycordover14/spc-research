ALBUQUERQUE
—Six Spartans recorded all 59 kills Sunday night, led by 
Zuzana Kosíková
 with 12, as the San José State volleyball team fell in four sets at New Mexico, 23-25, 25-18, 26-24, 25-19, Sunday night in a Mountain West match inside The Pit.

The Spartans hit .267 in the match as Kosíková hit .250 with her 12 kills in 28 attempts and only five errors.

Naima Foster
 added 11 kills on 35 attempts with four errors. She was one dig shy of her first double-double of the season with nine digs.

Ava Hewitt-Smith
 hit .364 in the match with 10 kills on 22 attempts and only two errors.

Lolohea Fonua
 and 
Morgan Graves
 each recorded nine kills in the match, while 
Learden Lee
 added eight kills as she hit .538 for the match (8-1-13).

Kosíková recorded her fourth double-double of the season with 11 digs to go with her 12 kills.

Orian Drore
 led the team with 12 digs, while Foster had nine and 
Fatimah Hall
 added eight.

Hanna Darvas
 recorded a team-best three aces, while she and Hall each recorded 24 assists.

San José State took the first set, 25-23, jumping out to a 9-3 lead, and winning the set on a Lobo hitting error.

SJSU tied the third set at 23-23 and 24-24 but could not take the final lead as UNM won 26-24.

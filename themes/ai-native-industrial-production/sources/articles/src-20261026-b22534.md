LAREDO, Tex. (KGNS) -
The juvenile female victim
 in the stabbing case on Sunday Oct. 25 has died, the Zapata County Sheriff’s Office says. 
Charges against the suspect have been elevated to murder. 
According to Captain Andrew Treviño, the suspect, 20-year-old Francisco Javier Torres Jr., was initially charged with aggravated assault of a family or household member with a weapon, causing serious bodily injury, a first-degree felony.
Officials say the victim and suspect knew each other.
The Texas Department of Public Safety’s Texas Rangers Division is assisting Zapata County with the investigation.

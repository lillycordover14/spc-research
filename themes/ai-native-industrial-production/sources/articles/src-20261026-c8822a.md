Tajikistan officially names tulip and snowdrop as national flowers&nbsp;&nbsp;Floral Daily

[page text]
With the aim of honoring the country's historical and cultural heritage, protecting nature, and raising public environmental awareness, the Tulip and Siyohgush have been declared national flowers by a decree of the President of the Republic of Tajikistan, symbolizing the spiritual values of the Tajik people.
 
During the period of independence, thanks to the identity-building policies of the President of the Republic of Tajikistan, Leader of the Nation, His Excellency Emomali Rahmon, the national and cultural values of the Tajik people have been revived. These values have not only regained their place in the spiritual life of society but have also reached the international stage and gained recognition from the global community.
 
In this context, declaring the Tulip and Siyohgush national flowers represents another initiative by the Leader of the Nation to promote and preserve ancient traditions in modern times. These flowers hold a special place in Tajik literature and culture, both past and present, and reflect the aesthetic sensibilities of the proud Tajik people.
 
These flowers have become an inseparable part of Tajik civilization and are closely associated with the ancient Aryan festival of Navruz and the beautiful season of spring. While the Siyohgush is regarded as the first harbinger of spring, symbolizing historical and mythological memory and the triumph of purity and justice, the Tulip symbolizes the honor and dignity of our proud people, embodies beauty and grace, and represents the blossoming of the country's natural environment.
 
With the Siyohgush, the first herald of spring, the Tajik people would joyfully welcome the arrival of the refreshing spring season and the ancestral New Year. In the following days of Navruz celebrations, they would venture out with songs and festivities to admire the blooming tulips in a tradition known as the Tulip Festival, also called the Festival of the Red Flower.
 
In modern times, this tradition has continued under its historic name, enriched with new meanings and forms of celebration. Every April, it is celebrated with joy and music throughout the country, transforming cities and villages into flourishing gardens.
 
These ancient traditions clearly demonstrate the enduring connection between the people of Aryan heritage and their beautiful, unique natural surroundings, particularly flowers. This connection is also reflected in the many Tajik girls' names that incorporate the word gul, meaning "flower," such as Gulandom, Gulbonu, Tojigul, Gulpaykar, Gulchehra, and Gulnoz. From ancient times to the present day, parents have given their daughters these names as expressions of profound love and affection.
 
It is also appropriate to recall the connection between the Siyohgush flower and the ancient Aryan legend associated with Siyavush, as depicted in the immortal Shahnameh by the great poet and scholar Abulqasim Ferdowsi. Its direct association with the name of Siyavush, one of the prominent figures in this national epic, further emphasizes the historical and mythological significance of the Siyohgush flower. In this way, our past and present become intertwined, expressing the continuity of historical memory and the preservation of our civilization. This connection will further strengthen national self-awareness and pride.
 
The paradise-like nature of our beloved homeland is adorned with flowers of many colors. Among its diverse plant species, the Tulip and Siyohgush occupy a prominent place, with various varieties growing throughout Tajikistan. This decree creates favorable conditions for the scientific study of these flowers and for preventing the extinction of the country's rare plant species.
 
In view of these considerations, the official designation of the Tulip and Siyohgush as national flowers will provide significant impetus to the development of Tajik national identity in the era of globalization.
 
Overall, this latest initiative of the President of the Republic of Tajikistan, Leader of the Nation, His Excellency Emomali Rahmon, will contribute meaningfully to the preservation of cultural heritage, the cultivation of aesthetic appreciation, the strengthening of public responsibility for protecting the country's paradise-like natural environment, and the improvement of environmental awareness among citizens, particularly children and young people. It will also play an important role in promoting the Republic of Tajikistan and the ancient civilization of the Tajik people on the international stage.

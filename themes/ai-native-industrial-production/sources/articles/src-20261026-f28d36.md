                Nexentis: MitoCareX Bio Advances AI-Driven Drug Discovery Initiative with Boltz, Completing Initial Computational Screening Stage

            

                                By GlobeNewswire
                            

                                October 26, 2026

MitoCareX considers preliminary, not yet experimentally validated, computational findings encouraging for further computational analysis and experimental investigation

Ness Ziona, Israel, Oct. 26, 2026 
(GLOBE NEWSWIRE)
 -- 
Nexentis Technologies Inc.
 (NASDAQ: NXTS) ("Nexentis” or the "Company”) today announced that its wholly owned subsidiary, MitoCareX Bio Ltd. ("MitoCareX”), has completed an initial computational screening stage under its drug-discovery initiative with Boltz, PBC ("Boltz”), an AI research lab focused on biomolecular foundation models and drug-discovery workflows. MitoCareX believes the preliminary computational predictions generated in this stage are encouraging and warrant further computational analysis and experimental validation.
Completion of this stage moves the initiative, announced in June 2026, from launch to an initial assessment of screening predictions. The predictions have not been experimentally validated and serve only as a starting point for evaluating potential follow-up research within MitoCareX’s small-molecule discovery programs.

"We believe that completing this first screening stage is an important step in translating our initiative with Boltz into research insights,” said David Palach, Chief Executive Officer of Nexentis. "We are focused on advancing proprietary discovery programs through disciplined, evidence-based decisions. These initial predictions give us a basis for planning next steps, and further validation will be essential to determining their significance.”
The initiative combines MitoCareX’s proprietary drug-discovery capabilities with Boltz’s AI models to identify potential small-molecule starting points for selected discovery programs. MitoCareX retains control over its discovery programs and any small-molecule candidates it advances through the initiative.

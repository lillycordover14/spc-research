Recognition across three Texas offices highlights firm's corporate, energy and technology practices
 
 
DALLAS, October 27, 2026
--(
BUSINESS WIRE
)--
Egan Nelson LLP
 is proud to announce that 12 of its attorneys have been named to the 2027 Lawdragon 500 Leading Dealmakers in America guide.
 
 
This recognition across the firm's office footprint underscores Egan Nelson's elite transactional capabilities and its prominent position as a go-to firm for complex corporate, energy and technology dealmaking.
 
 
 
 
 
Lawdragon's annual Dealmakers guide honors the country's top legal minds who navigate corporations, investment funds and emerging growth companies through sophisticated financial landscapes, strategic investments and transformational corporate transactions.
 
 
The Egan Nelson honorees include:
 
 
Austin:
 
 
 
Matt R. Bair
 – Venture capital investments and debt financings, M&A, startup formation and financings, corporate restructurings
 
 
 
Nicolas Lee
 – Corporate & securities, M&A, private equity, corporate governance, renewable energy, technology & venture capital
 
 
 
Thomas R. Nelson
 – Corporate & securities, M&A, private equity & venture capital, corporate governance, startup & emerging technology
 
 
 
Mitul D. Patel
 – Corporate finance, corporate & securities, M&A, general corporate

LAREDO, Tex. (KGNS) - The charge against a 20-year-old man accused in a 
Zapata stabbing
 has been upgraded to murder after the death of 16-year-old Abigail Flores, whose family spoke with KGNS about the events leading up to the attack.
Flores died Monday morning from stab wounds following a stabbing around 2 p.m. Sunday, Oct. 25, in Zapata. The suspect, Francisco Javier Torres Jr., 20, is accused in the attack.
Charge upgraded
Torres was initially charged with aggravated assault. After Flores’ death, the Zapata County Sheriff’s Office announced the charge had been upgraded to murder.
Family describes relationship
The Zapata County Sheriff’s Office has only confirmed that the victim and suspect knew each other. Flores’ family said the two were romantically linked.
Her uncle, Juan Flores, said the family noticed bruises and signs of jealousy later in the relationship. He said the two argued the night before the stabbing.
Account of events before the attack
Juan Flores recounted what he said a friend who was with Abigail told him she overheard just before the attack.
“One of her friends was with her, but she left cause the guy took her,” he said.
“If I’m not mistaken, she overheard the guy tell her that he was going to stab her, but then she said ‘Do it. You’re the one who’s going to go to jail.’ And he did it,” he said.
Injuries and hospital care
Flores’ family said she was stabbed multiple times on Sunday, about 18 times by their account.
Juan Flores said he does not have the exact details but that detectives told the family she was stabbed on her cheek, nose, hands, neck, chest and abdomen.
She was taken to a Laredo hospital, then flown to a San Antonio hospital for treatment. Juan Flores said Abigail died after undergoing surgery around 4 a.m. Monday.
Funeral costs
The family said it is holding a plate sale and a 
GoFundMe
 to help pay for her funeral.

Brigitte Nielsen's rocky marriage

© Ron Galella Collection via Getty
Sylvester and Brigitte were married from 1985 to 1987

The Danish star shot to fame in the '80s thanks to her burgeoning modeling career, which led to a role in 1985's 
Rocky IV
 alongside 
Sylvester Stallone
. Brigitte portrayed the villainous Ludmilla Drago in the hit film, and fell in love with Sylvester on set. 
She was just 22 years old when the film was released in theaters, and she tied the knot with the action star that same year. The pair went their separate ways in 1987, and briefly reunited in 2018 on the set of 
Creed II.

Reuniting with Sylvester Stallone

© Gamma-Rapho via Getty Images
The former couple co-starred in Rocky IV together

Brigitte reflected on returning to the 
Creed II 
set after splitting from Sylvester decades prior, sharing that they were the picture of professionalism. "In 1985 I was married to Sylvester and this time I am the ex-wife," she told the publication. "But it was wonderful to have half of the cast there 32 years later. Who could have ever dreamed that you pick up the same roles many years after?"
"We are two professionals, and we kept it that way," she added. "He stayed in the American corner, and I stayed in the Russian corner. We were on opposite sides, but it was pleasant."

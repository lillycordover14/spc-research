Cognite Named Corporate Supplier of the Year by Marathon Petroleum
 

										MM Desk					

 

27/10/2026
 

 

 

Share

 
Image Courtesy: Pixabay

Industrial data and artificial intelligence company 
Cognite
 has been named Corporate Supplier of the Year at Marathon Petroleum Corporation’s 11th Annual Supplier Recognition Awards. The programme recognises suppliers that made a positive contribution to Marathon Petroleum’s business during the previous calendar year.

Girish Rishi, Chief Executive Officer of Cognite, said the award reflected the company’s focus on delivering measurable value to customers. He described Marathon Petroleum as an innovator and said the recognition was meaningful to Cognite and its employees.

The award adds to several recognitions Cognite has received in 2026. In May, the company ranked 15th on CNBC’s Disruptor 50 list, which highlights venture-backed businesses developing and scaling innovations across industries, including artificial intelligence.

In April, LNS Research named Cognite a FrontRunner in its Solution Selection Matrix for Industrial AI Platforms. In March, IDC Research positioned the company as a Leader in its MarketScape assessment of worldwide industrial DataOps platforms. Cognite provides industrial data and AI technology to companies in sectors including energy, manufacturing, life sciences, power and renewables. Its platform is designed to make industrial data available to decision-makers across field operations and remote centres, supporting collaboration and analysis of complex operating information.

The company’s technology is intended to help industrial businesses use data to improve asset performance, safety, sustainability and operational efficiency. Its platform provides secure access to industrial information and supports real-time collaboration across teams.

Marathon Petroleum Corporation is an integrated downstream and midstream energy company headquartered in Findlay, Ohio. It operates the largest refining system in the United States and has a marketing network that includes branded retail outlets across the country.

Marathon Petroleum also holds the general partner and majority limited partner interest in MPLX LP. The midstream company owns and operates gathering, processing and fractionation assets, along with crude oil and light product transportation and logistics infrastructure.

Cognite said its work with industrial customers centres on making complex data easier to access and use in daily operations. The company serves businesses seeking to apply industrial AI and data management across large-scale assets and processes. The latest award recognises Cognite’s contribution to Marathon Petroleum’s business and comes as the company continues to receive industry recognition for its industrial data and AI platform.

Cognite
 provides industrial data and AI solutions for companies in energy, manufacturing, life sciences, power and renewables. Its platform enables industrial teams to access, understand and use complex operational data across sites and remote operations centres. Marathon Petroleum Corporation is a US-based downstream and midstream energy company headquartered in Findlay, Ohio. Its operations include refining, fuel marketing and midstream infrastructure through MPLX LP.

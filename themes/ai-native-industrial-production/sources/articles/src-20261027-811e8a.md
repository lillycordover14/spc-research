Twelve South Finally Added a Screen to its Airfly Wireless Headphone Dongle&nbsp;&nbsp;WSJ

[page text]
If you’re a frequent traveler, chances are you’ve run into a situation where you want to watch something on a plane’s seatback entertainment system, but you only have 
wireless headphones
. Even though some airlines are now building in Bluetooth connectivity, it’s still far from the norm. Adapters that let you transmit the signal from a 3.5mm audio jack to your AirPods or other Bluetooth headphones have been around for some time, and Twelve South popularized the category with its original Airfly device in 2018. Now the brand has made a significant update with the introduction of the 
Airfly View
, adding a built-in display that makes this model a clear winner.

The screen makes all the difference
A Bluetooth transmitter plugs into a non-wireless audio source (like that seatback screen or an exercise bike) and broadcasts the signal wirelessly so you can listen with Bluetooth headphones or a speaker. Most don’t have screens though, which means pairing your headphones with the device requires a series of button pushes and flashing lights that, while not terribly difficult to decipher, are not intuitive. Twelve South addressed this issue by adding a hidden display inside the Airfly View’s matte plastic body, with support for 24 languages. 
Hidden when it’s off, the screen glows through the black or dark blue Coastal body, walking you through the pairing process and leaving no ambiguity about when your headphones are connected. You toggle through options with the volume up and down buttons on the right side of the device, and select with the top button on the left. The buttons get the job done, though they lack a satisfying tactile response. 
The transmitter automatically finds headphones or speakers in Bluetooth pairing mode and shows when you’re connected; the whole process takes seconds, and once you’re connected, any audio from the wired source goes straight to your headphones. The Airfly View supports streaming to two wireless headphones at once, allowing you to share the audio with someone else.
The perfect travel companion
At about 2 ⅛ inch tall (plus the 2-inch audio cable), 1 ⅜ inch wide and less than half an inch thick, the Airfly View can easily fit into your pocket or bag. Twelve South includes a carrying case for it, along with a two-prong international airline adapter and USB-A-to-USB-C charging cable, each held in place with a dedicated elastic strap. The device’s battery is rated to last for 16 hours, and an on-screen battery meter will let you know when it’s time to charge.

Tulip Time Festival to Unveil 2027 Entertainment Lineup, Attractions&nbsp;&nbsp;WHTC

[page text]
HOLLAND, MI (WHTC-AM/FM, Oct. 27, 2026) – Tribute acts will spotlight the 98th renewal of Holland’s signature event next spring.
Tulip Time Festival officials on Tuesday announced its entertainment schedule, with Elton Rohn, Forever Motown: So Good for the Soul, and Dancing Queen: An ABBA Salute coming into the Tulip City for performances. FiddleFire returns, along with concerts by the Holland American Legion Band and the Holland Symphony Orchestra.
The festival will also focus many of its events at the Heeringa Holland Civic Center Place as a “hub” through the 10-day event, from April 30th through May 9th. The Quilt Show will be moved to that facility, to join with a Dutch Market, the carnival, food concessions and more in and around the building.
Tickets are slated to go on sale on Tuesday through the Tulip Time Festival’s 
website
, with a complete event lineup expected to be posted.

Copia Copia Copia Copia Copia Copia Wine Tasting Experience: the faces of Nebbiolo&nbsp;&nbsp;LoveLanghe

[page text]
On the occasion of the International Fair of the White Truffle of Alba, the 
guided tastings of the Strada del Barolo
 will return, starting Oct. 12; they will be held every Saturday (in English) and every Sunday (in Italian) at the Consorzio Albeisa.
 “The Taste of the Territory,” “The Cru of Barolo,” and “The Faces of Nebbiolo” are the themes that will alternate throughout the duration of the Fair.   
 
The tastings
 
  “The Faces of Nebbiolo” will offer a 
comparison of
 Barolo, Barbaresco and Nebbiolo, explaining the different expressions of the Nebbiolo grape variety.
 “I Cru del Barolo” is a 
virtual journey
 through the 
geographical mentions of Barolo
, punctuated by the tasting of three different labels.
 “The Taste of the Territory” is an 
introductory journey
 through the most characteristic productions of Piedmont, with the tasting of four different types of wine.

Denzel Ward has another big outing for the Browns’ defense.

It was the same script for the Browns’ defense: get picked apart on the first drive to allow a touchdown (and then the second touchdown given up after the turnover). Following that, though, the defense was on track to pitching another shutout for three quarters like they did against the Ravens last week, before the Titans got a fourth down pass to go their way to help tie the game.

The way you react to seeing deer probably depends on whether you have a garden, and especially if that garden is full of 
freshly planted bulbs
. Deer do love eating bulbs, but not as many types as you might think.
Master Gardener Rhonda Fleming Hayes, author of 
Garden for Life: Strategies for Easier, Greener, More Joyful Gardening as We Age
 and the Substack 
The Garden Buzz
, says that while deer are an issue for many gardeners (including herself), there are plenty of deer-resistant bulbs to choose from.

Rhonda’s Top Picks for Deer-Resistant Bulbs
Nata Serenko - Getty Images
“I always consider deer when I plant spring-blooming bulbs. I don’t want to waste my money, time, or energy,” she says. Deer tend to avoid the bulbs listed below due to their pungent smell, bitter taste, or toxic compounds in the plants. That said, there are times when deer aren’t as choosy. “Depending upon the circumstances, such as drought or habitat loss, deer will occasionally eat plants that are labeled as resistant,” Hayes explains.
Here are her top picks for deer-resistant bulbs, and why the animals dislike them:
Allium:
 These bulbs have a strong odor and flavor.
Anemone (blanda):
 They are bitter-tasting to deer.
Calla lily: 
They contain high levels of toxicity.
Daffodil:
 Deer avoid the natural alkaloids in these bulbs.
Fritillaria:
 The bulbs have a skunky, musky odor to deer.
Galanthus (Snowdrop):
 They are both toxic and bitter-tasting.
Grape hyacinth:
 Deer dislike the bulbs’ musky odor.
Siberian squill (Scilla):
 These bulbs contain alkaloids.
Winter aconite:
 They contain toxic compounds.
One Bulb to Avoid
Wayne Gerard  Trotman / 500px - Getty Images
Colorful, easy-to-grow 
tulips
 are one of the most popular spring flowers, but if deer are frequent visitors to your yard, Hayes says to steer clear of them. Unlike other spring-blooming bulbs, they don’t contain toxic alkaloids or off-putting flavors or aromas. If you can’t resist their pretty colors and shapes, try them in window boxes or plant them in containers close to your house, which might deter deer.

3 Tar Heels 'Questionable' on Initial Miami-UNC Injury Report&nbsp;&nbsp;247Sports

[page text]
CHAPEL HILL – The first availability report of the week for North Carolina's home game against No. 1 
Miami
 on Saturday night was released on Wednesday night, and a few Tar Heels starters are listed as questionable.
Offensive tackle Jordan Hall and offensive guard/tackle Andrew Threatt are listed as questionable, as is safety 
Peyton Waters
.
Hall had missed multiple games before returning to face 
Syracuse
 last Saturday night and played 70 snaps at left tackle, which is the only position he's played this season.
Threatt, who filled in for Hall at left tackle for two games, was back at his original starting spot, left guard, against the Orange. He played 30 snaps before suffering an injury.
Waters was injured early in UNC's win at 
Duke
 two weeks ago, playing just three snaps, and did not return or play against Syracuse.
Backup linebacker 
Jonathan Agumadu
 is also listed as questionable for the Tar Heels. Agumadu has played rotation snaps behind starter 
Peyton Seelmann
 in each game so far, totaling 183 on the season, including 31 against Syracuse.
Starting cornerback Tre Miller remains out and is likely out for the regular season, as TarHeel247 was the first to report.
The Tar Heels (4-3, 2-2 ACC) and Hurricanes (7-0, 5-0) kick off at 7:30 p.m. on Saturday night, and the game will air on ABC.

Blue Matter Acquires Cognite to Expand Global Healthcare Communications Capabilities&nbsp;&nbsp;citybiz

[page text]
Blue Matter
, a life sciences consulting firm backed by 
Baird Capital
, has acquired 
Cognite
, a global healthcare communications agency with offices in London, Zug, Switzerland, and Boston, expanding its scientific, medical and commercial communications capabilities for pharmaceutical and biotechnology clients.

The acquisition strengthens Blue Matter’s ability to provide integrated strategic consulting and communications services, combining its expertise in biopharmaceutical strategy with Cognite’s scientific, creative and digital communications capabilities.

Financial terms of the transaction were not disclosed.

Cognite specializes in developing communications strategies and content for pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies, with particular expertise in complex specialty medicines, rare diseases and other therapeutic areas requiring detailed scientific information.

Its services span medical, commercial, corporate and patient communications, helping life sciences companies communicate scientific and clinical information to healthcare professionals, researchers, patients, payers, investors and other stakeholders.

The transaction represents another step in Blue Matter’s strategy to expand beyond traditional consulting services and provide clients with capabilities that support the implementation of commercial and organizational strategies.

Ashwin Dandekar
, CEO of Blue Matter, said the acquisition builds on the firm’s efforts to integrate strategic consulting with creative and communications services.

“Over the past few years, we’ve been systematically bringing together the creative and communications capabilities that are often so crucial to bringing those strategies to life with healthcare providers, patients, payers, investors, and other stakeholders,” Dandekar said.

He described Cognite’s capabilities and organizational culture as complementary to Blue Matter’s existing business.

Blue Matter provides strategic consulting services to pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies, advising clients on corporate strategy, product development, portfolio management and commercial planning.

The firm operates across North America, Europe and Asia, with expertise in complex therapeutic markets including oncology, central nervous system disorders and rare diseases.

Its consulting work focuses on helping life sciences companies make decisions about product positioning, portfolio investment, organizational structure and market opportunities.

The addition of Cognite will allow Blue Matter to extend those strategic services into scientific and commercial communications, supporting clients as they translate business strategies into information and engagement programs for different audiences.

Healthcare communications requires specialized scientific knowledge because pharmaceutical companies must communicate complex clinical information accurately while addressing the needs of healthcare professionals, patients and other stakeholders.

The requirements can be particularly demanding in rare diseases and specialty medicine markets, where treatment pathways, patient populations and clinical evidence may be highly specialized.

Cognite has developed its business around these challenges, combining scientific expertise with strategic planning, creative development and digital communications.

The agency works with pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies to develop communications that make complex scientific concepts accessible and relevant to different audiences.

Its services support activities across medical affairs, commercial operations, corporate communications and patient engagement.

For Blue Matter, these capabilities complement its advisory work by providing additional resources to support the execution of client strategies.

The combined organization will be positioned to help clients develop strategic plans and communicate those plans to stakeholders throughout the healthcare ecosystem.

Cognite CEO 
Lee Hurley
 said joining Blue Matter will provide the agency with access to a broader international client base and additional resources for growth.

“By becoming part of Blue Matter, we will be able to take our communications skills and experience to a far wider audience, offering tremendous opportunities for growth, both for us and our clients,” Hurley said.

Hurley also emphasized the companies’ shared approach to organizational culture and client service.

Cognite operates from three offices across the United Kingdom, Switzerland and the United States, giving it an established presence in major pharmaceutical and biotechnology markets.

London and Zug are important European centers for life sciences companies and related professional services, while Boston is one of the leading biotechnology and pharmaceutical research markets in the United States.

The agency’s international footprint will complement Blue Matter’s existing operations across North America, Europe and Asia.

The acquisition is expected to expand the geographic reach of Cognite’s communications services while providing Blue Matter with additional specialized expertise.

Sean Cush
, chief business officer at Cognite, said the agency’s approach has focused on combining scientific knowledge, strategic thinking and creative execution.

“Our focus on transformative medicines and the complex diseases they tackle is now something we can turbocharge by joining Blue Matter, harnessing world-class strategic capabilities, empowering technologies, and gaining true global reach,” Cush said.

The companies did not disclose Cognite’s annual revenue, employee count or the number of clients served by the agency.

They also did not announce changes to Cognite’s management structure, office locations or branding following the acquisition.

The transaction comes as pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies increasingly seek coordinated support across strategic planning, medical affairs and commercial communications.

Life sciences organizations often work with multiple consulting firms and communications agencies to address different stages of product development and commercialization.

By bringing these capabilities together, Blue Matter aims to provide a broader range of services through a single organization.

The acquisition could also create opportunities for cross-selling services between the companies’ existing client bases, although specific revenue targets or anticipated financial synergies were not disclosed.

Cognite’s experience in rare diseases and specialty medicines aligns with Blue Matter’s established focus on complex therapeutic markets.

These areas frequently require specialized communications programs to explain clinical evidence, disease characteristics, treatment options and product value to distinct stakeholder groups.

The combination will allow Blue Matter to incorporate Cognite’s scientific and creative resources into engagements involving product strategy, commercialization and broader corporate planning.

Blue Matter is a portfolio company of Baird Capital, the private investment arm of Baird focused on business-to-business technology and services companies in North America and the United Kingdom.

Baird Capital works with portfolio companies to support operational development, strategic growth and long-term value creation.

The Cognite acquisition expands Blue Matter’s service portfolio under Baird Capital’s ownership and strengthens its position in the life sciences professional services market.

Reed Smith served as legal counsel to Blue Matter in the transaction.

Cognite was advised by Rothschild & Co. and DLA Piper.

The companies did not disclose additional transaction advisors or financing arrangements.

Following the acquisition, Blue Matter will combine its strategic consulting expertise with Cognite’s medical, scientific, commercial and patient communications capabilities.

The expanded organization will serve pharmaceutical and biotechnology clients seeking support across corporate strategy, portfolio planning, product commercialization and stakeholder communications.

The transaction advances Blue Matter’s strategy of building a more comprehensive life sciences consulting and communications platform, with an expanded international presence and additional expertise in complex specialty medicines and rare diseases.

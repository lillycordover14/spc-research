What Is Laminar?
Laminar, based in Tel Aviv, Israel, specializes in Public Cloud Data Protection SaaS solutions, offering data leakage protection for cloud-based operations. The company operates within the Cloud Infrastructure, Cyber Security, and SaaS industries.
With an employee count ranging from 51 to 100, Laminar has successfully raised funding through three rounds, including a Series A round. The company has been acquired by Rubrik, a leader in data security and cloud data management.

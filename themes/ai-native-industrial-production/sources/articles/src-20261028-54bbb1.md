A new unique Stitch collectible is available from DisneyStore.com. This 12″ tall Stitch figure by Grand Jester Studios features a light-up plasma ball and iridescent finish.
Stitch Figure with Light-Up Plasma Ball by Grand Jester Studios – $249.99
The sculpted figure depicts Stitch in his Experiment 626 form with a red spacesuit, four arms, and antennae. He has a iridescent finish with high-gloss detailing and stands on a metallic base. The Stitch logo and “626” are on the base.

In front of Stitch is the interactive plasma ball with touch-sensitive light effects. It is battery operated with an on/off switch. The figure requires four AA batteries, which are not included.

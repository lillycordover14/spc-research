James Weir’s Instrumental Project Blue Earth Sound Returns With ‘Everything To Remember’

[Cover photo features Blue Earth Sound’s James Weir, photo credit to 
Tim Nagle
]

Blue Earth Sound
 is the instrumental project of Chicago-based multi-instrumentalist and composer 
James Weir
, blending cinematic Jazz, Soul and Psychedelic textures into atmospheric soundscapes. Named after his roots in Blue Earth County, Minnesota, the project “reflects both a sense of place and Weir’s collaborative approach to music-making”. 

Following debut album, 
Cicero Nights
, which has sold out across three vinyl pressings, Blue Earth Sound returns with 
Everything to Remember,
 a new record shaped by Chicago’s creative scene and writing retreats in rural Wisconsin. It will be released on vinyl and digital/streaming platforms on 
November 20th, 2026, 
via 
DeepMatter Records.

The album’s recent singles 
“Heavy Mild,”
 
“Runner’s High”
 and 
“Limiter”
 are all out now on digital/streaming platforms.

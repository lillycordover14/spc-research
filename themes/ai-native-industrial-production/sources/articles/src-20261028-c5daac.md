UNC Basketball 2026-27 Preview: Juniors
Today, we look at the juniors on the UNC basketball roster.
by
 
 
Thomas Blackington
Oct 28, 2026, 5:00 PM UTC
We continue our preview of the 2026-2027 UNC basketball season by taking a look at the juniors on the roster for Michael Malone’s first season. If you missed them, I recommend reading 
Al’s preview of the UNC freshmen
 and Akil’s overview of the sophomore class.
The junior class is by far the smallest one, containing only two players: Alex Samoduroz and John Holbrook. Samodurov is a new addition to UNC, while Holbrook is returning for his third season with the team. Let’s take a look at each of their potential rolls with the new-look Tar Heels.
Alex Samodurov
Samodurov enters his first season of NCAA basketball as a junior due to his playing time in Europe. The 6’11” center/power forward played extensively in his home country of Greece and in the Euroleague. He did quite well there and also played for the Greek national team alongside NBA star Giannis Antetokounmpo. He was signed to the Greek club team Panathinaikos in 2022 and is considered one of the rising starts of Greek basketball. He initially entered the 2026 NBA draft but eventually withdrew his name to keep his eligibility and joined the Heels. Fellow UNC transfer Neo Avdalas is actually a former teammate of Samodurov, potentially providing some familiarity for the Greek big man. He will have three seasons of college basketball remaining if he chooses to use them all.
Samoduov generally projects to be a power forward, although the continuing saga around Cameron Fens eligibility could open up more playing time for him if Fens ultimately is ruled ineligible (which seems likely at the moment). He probably will be one of the first bigs off the bench for either Jarin Stevenson or Sayon Keita. Unfortunately, he has some of the same concerns as Keita, namely that he won’t have the bulk to truly bang in the paint against some of the bigger PF/C’s in the ACC. There almost certainly will also be an adjustment period for him as he transitions from international basketball to NCAA play. His experience playing professional basketball against other professionals should ease some of the growing pains, but I feel like he has one of the widest ranges of outcomes of all the newcomers on the roster; you could talk me into him becoming a starter at some point this season or struggling enough to get only sporadic minutes.
John Holbrook
Holbrook returns for his third year at UNC as a reserve player. The reserve forward played in five games last year, averaging 1.6 minutes a game. He recorded a total of one point and three rebounds across his limited playing time in the 2025-2026 season. He’s unlikely to see the court unless the game is well out of hand.
Overall, the junior class is probably the least impactful class on this year’s roster. Still, Samodurov’s development (or lack thereof) is one of the keys that will either raise or lower the ceiling of this UNC squad; if he settles in quickly as the first big man off the bench and develops a connection with Avdalas, he could become a significant pick and roll weapon while giving the Heels another option in the post.

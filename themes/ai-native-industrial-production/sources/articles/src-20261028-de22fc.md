						Vantage Reaches 1-Gigawatt Transformer Supply Deal with Heron Power					

Denver-based Vantage, a leading global provider of digital infrastructure, and Heron Power Electronics, a manufacturer of next-generation power electronics and a participant in NVIDIA’s 800-volt direct current MGX ecosystem, have reached a gigawatt-scale manufacturing capacity agreement.

Under the agreement, Vantage has secured more than 1GW of manufacturing capacity of Heron Link, Heron’s solid-state transformer, to deploy in its hyperscale data center campuses. As customers face growing constraints related to power availability, electrical equipment, deployment timelines and cost, Heron Link is designed to address these challenges by converting medium-voltage grid power directly to 800 VDC.

“Our customers need a faster and more efficient way to deploy increasingly power-dense and flexible AI infrastructure,” said Curtis Aubry, VP, global product strategy at Vantage. “By simplifying the electrical architecture and integrating power conversion, energy storage and compute more effectively, we can help reduce deployment complexity, improve efficiency and accelerate our customers’ speed to capacity.”

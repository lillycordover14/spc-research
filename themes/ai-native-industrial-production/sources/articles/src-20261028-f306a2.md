On the occasion of the Alba White Truffle International Fair, Vinum Alba Wine Tasting offers events dedicated to the wines of Piedmont at the Federico Eusebio Civic Museum.

Discover Piedmontese wines in a blind tasting!

Four Piedmontese wines served blind: a guided session by AIS Piemonte to recognize grape varieties, denominations, and territories through color, aromas, and taste.

Two appointments on the same day: 
11:30 AM and 4:30 PM

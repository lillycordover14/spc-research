Key Points
Jio raised 302 billion rupees ($3.2 billion) in the country's largest-ever IPO.
Global brokerage Nomura on Wednesday gave Jio Platforms a buy rating.
Signage for Jio Platforms Ltd. ahead of the company's listing ceremony at the National Stock Exchange (NSE) in Mumbai, India, on Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026. 
Bloomberg | Bloomberg | Getty Images
Jio Platforms' shares rose 21.5% on their trading debut Wednesday after a record initial public offering that was lapped up by retail as well as institutional investors.
Jio's market capitalization was 12.5 trillion rupees ($131 billion), making it the second most valued company in India after its parent Reliance Industries, as per NSE data. This paves the way for its eventual inclusion in key benchmark indices.
That inclusion will generate sufficient demand and protect the share price from any oversupply, Deven Choksey, founder and managing director at wealth management firm DRChoksey FinServ, told CNBC. 
He added that some of the investors who acquired a stake in Jio around 2021 may want to exit in around six months, by which time the stock will likely be included in the MSCI index and the 
Nifty 50
.
India's largest telecom and digital services player had offered 270 million shares in 
the IPO priced
 at 1,119 rupees apiece, raising 302 billion rupees ($3.2 billion) in the country's largest-ever IPO. 
The public issue was subscribed more than 22 times, with institutional investors leading the demand, according to data from the BSE. The qualified institutional portion was subscribed 70.55 times, while retail investors subscribed 2.72 times. 
The IPO was fully subscribed within 95 minutes of its opening on Oct. 21, Akash Ambani, managing director of Jio Platforms and heir of the Indian billionaire Mukesh Ambani, said ahead of the listing. 
Global brokerage Nomura on Wednesday gave Jio Platforms a buy rating, setting a target price of 1,340 rupees, implying an upside of 19.7% to its IPO price. The brokerage said in a report that Jio's capex cycle is largely over and there is expected to be a sharp improvement in the company's free cash flow.
Shares were last trading at 1,360 rupees.
Indian billionaire Mukesh Ambani's flagship company, 
Reliance Industries
, owns 
more than a 66% stake
 in Jio Platforms, followed by 
Meta Platforms
' affiliate Jaadhu Holdings that owns nearly 10% and 
Google
 International, which owns 7.7%, as per LSEG data.
Leading global funds such as 
BlackRock, Capital Group and GIC
 participated in Jio's anchor book portion of the total fundraise, which is set aside for large institutional investors before the IPO opens for public bidding. Including anchor investors, the share offering garnered demand of $78 billion.

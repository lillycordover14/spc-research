Brendan Walter Makes Grand Ole Opry Debut&nbsp;&nbsp;MusicRow.com

[page text]
Brendan Walter. Photo: Jared Tadlock

RECORDS/Sony Music Nashville artist
 Brendan Walter
 made his Grand Ole Opry debut last week.

Walter performed his songs “Asking For A Friend” and “Pipe Dream” during his appearance on the iconic stage.

“What an incredible night at the Grand Ole Opry,” says Walter. “These are the kind of nights that make it all make sense. Thank you to everyone who has made my dreams come true, it is such an honor to be a part of this family.”

The debut follows the recent release of Walter’s new track, “Model Maniac.” The rising artist has toured with Wyatt Flores and Evan Honer, and released the track “If A Song Could Change Your Mind” with Low Gap. His debut album, 
Disappearing Days, 
is available now.

Pictured (L to R): Stacey Cato (Grand Ole Opry), Grady Smith, Lexi Lampkin (Grand Ole Opry), Brendan Walter, Tom Lord (Red Light Management) and Reid Alexander (Red Light Management). Photo: Jared Tadlock

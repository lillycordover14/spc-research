Ness Ziona, Israel, Oct. 29, 2026 
(GLOBE NEWSWIRE)
 -- 
Nexentis Technologies Inc.
 (NASDAQ: NXTS) ("Nexentis" or the "Company") recently announced that its wholly owned subsidiary, MitoCareX Bio Ltd. ("MitoCareX"), has completed an initial computational screening stage under its drug-discovery initiative with Boltz, PBC ("Boltz"), an AI research lab focused on biomolecular foundation models and drug-discovery workflows. MitoCareX believes the preliminary computational predictions generated in this stage are encouraging and warrant further computational analysis and experimental validation.
 
 
 
 
 
Completion of this stage moves the initiative, announced in June 2026, from launch to an initial assessment of screening predictions. The predictions have not been experimentally validated and serve only as a starting point for evaluating potential follow-up research within MitoCareX's small-molecule discovery programs.
 
 
The initiative combines MitoCareX's proprietary drug-discovery capabilities with Boltz's AI models to identify potential small-molecule starting points for selected discovery programs. MitoCareX retains control over its discovery programs and any small-molecule candidates it advances through the initiative.
 
 
Further scientific assessment and validation will determine whether the preliminary predictions support advancing any program. No assurance can be given that any compound identified through the initiative will be advanced into preclinical or clinical development.
 
 
About MitoCareX Bio Ltd.
 
 
MitoCareX Bio Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of Nexentis Technologies Inc., is advancing a focused drug-discovery platform designed to translate multidisciplinary scientific capabilities into potential therapeutic candidates. By integrating biology, chemistry and computationally enabled research, MitoCareX supports ongoing research activities directed toward candidate identification and development within its drug-discovery efforts.

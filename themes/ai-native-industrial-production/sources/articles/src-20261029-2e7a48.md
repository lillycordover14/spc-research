SKOKIE, Ill., Oct. 29, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LanzaTech Global, Inc. (NASDAQ: LNZA) (“LanzaTech” or the “Company”), a carbon management solutions company, today announced that it will issue its third quarter 2026 financial results before financial markets in the United States open on Monday, November 16, 2026. A conference call will be held that same day at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time.
The conference call may be accessed via a live webcast on a listen-only basis through the Events & Presentations section of LanzaTech’s Investor Relations website, 
ir.lanzatech.com
. An archive of the webcast will be available for twelve months.
To attend the live conference call via telephone, domestic callers can access by dialing 1-833-419-0865 and international callers by dialing 1-785-838-9333 using the conference identification code LANZA.
A replay of the conference call will be available shortly after the call ends and can be accessed by domestic callers by dialing 1-844-512-2921 and by international callers by dialing 1-412-317-6671 and entering the access identification code 11162824. The replay will be available until 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on November 30, 2026.
About LanzaTech
LanzaTech (NASDAQ: LNZA) is a leader in carbon management, using its proprietary gas-fermentation platform to transform waste carbon into valuable products. Through global partnerships, LanzaTech enables the production of feedstocks for high-value markets including SAF and chemicals. Headquartered in the U.S., the company provides technology and commercial pathways that strengthen industrial resilience and unlock new economic value from carbon.

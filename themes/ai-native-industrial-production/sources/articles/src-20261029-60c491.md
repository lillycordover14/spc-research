Avalanche’s $12 Problem: A 55% Helicon Rally Erased and 276M AVAX Still to Unlock&nbsp;&nbsp;TechBullion

[page text]
Can Avalanche win back the $12 level it reached earlier this month? For now the answer looks like no, as AVAX has given back its entire 55% Helicon rally.

The stakes are high because a large share of the supply is still to unlock while ETF demand has stalled. AVAX now trades below $9, so any Avalanche price prediction hinges on whether buyers defend the next support.

Traders who watch these swings closely also look for tools that make sense of them. One of them is 
MEMASA ($MEMASA)
, an upcoming 
AI crypto trading app
 that lets users research and trade crypto through a chat.

Avalanche Price Prediction: Stalled ETF Demand and Looming Unlocks Weigh on AVAX

A new comparison of three DeFi coins ranked Avalanche last, behind Aave and Uniswap, according to 
24/7 Wall St
.

All three tokens slipped this month after summer rallies, but AVAX carries the heaviest baggage of the group. Its late-summer climb came from upgrade hype, as network tests with ICE and the Helicon upgrade fueled a 55% run. Once Helicon went live, traders sold the news, and the gains began to unwind.

Fund flows have not filled the gap, as U.S. spot Avalanche ETFs went nearly two weeks without any net inflows. Supply adds more pressure, with roughly 276 million AVAX still to enter circulation before the hard cap is reached.

Support Near $8 Is the Line to Watch

AVAX now trades near $8.70, down about 10% over the past week.
 For any near-term Avalanche price prediction, the nearest strong support sits just under $8, with resistance far above near $12.90.

Technical indicators lean bearish, so a break below support could open the way to much lower levels. A turnaround would likely need fresh ETF inflows and a push back toward the $12 zone the rally once reached.

MEMASA is an upcoming AI-powered crypto trading app that lets people research, buy, and sell cryptocurrencies by chatting with an AI assistant.

Instead of studying complicated charts, tracking hundreds of tokens, or switching between trading platforms, users will be able to ask MEMASA questions in plain language.

For example: 
“Can AVAX hold $8 after losing its Helicon rally?”
 MEMASA will analyze market indicators, trading activity, and sentiment to explain what the available data suggests.

MEMASA tackles problems almost every on-chain trader knows: too many charts, too much noise, scam tokens, and endless switching between apps. It brings market analysis, risk checks, and trading into 
one AI-powered chat.
 

That gives MEMASA a real shot at 
attracting a wide audience
, from crypto newcomers to seasoned traders. MEMASA is preparing for launch, so anyone wishing to can join a 
waitlist for early access to the app.

Avalanche Price Prediction: AVAX’s Bearish Outlook vs. $MEMASA’s Pre-Listing Upside

AVAX and $MEMASA are in different leagues: Avalanche is a blockchain token that depends on network adoption and ETF demand, while $MEMASA is a utility token powering the MEMASA trading ecosystem. Its proposed role is directly connected to how people use the app.

$MEMASA will support several functions:

Premium AI intelligence: Access to advanced market analysis and trading insights.

Trading fee discounts: Reduced fees for eligible platform activity.

Analyst and agent staking: Analysts and AI agents stake $MEMASA to list their strategies and signals.

Marketplace payments: Transactions involving intelligence services and trading strategies.

Curation and contributor rewards: Incentives for participants who contribute useful signals and help curate the ecosystem.

Fixed Supply and Early Access to $MEMASA

Unlike AVAX, which still has a large share of its supply left to unlock, $MEMASA has a fixed token supply. The token structure also includes long-term team vesting and transparent unlock schedules.

MEMASA is offering an on-chain early sale, giving participants early access to $MEMASA before broader market availability. The earlier stages offer lower token prices, while later stages provide faster unlocks.

AVAX has already seen its biggest rallies and may struggle to repeat them. MEMASA is preparing for launch, with its on-chain early sale offering tokens at $0.0155, below the anticipated market price. 

However, future returns will depend on successful app development, user adoption, and demand for $MEMASA. Higher potential also comes with higher risk.

The Road Ahead for AVAX and MEMASA

Avalanche needs buyers to defend support just under $8 to avoid a deeper slide. Reclaiming the $12 zone would likely require a return of ETF demand.

The upcoming token unlocks remain a background weight, and they could cap any rebound until fresh demand absorbs the new supply.

MEMASA faces a different test, as it must turn an AI trading concept into an app people use every day. Both outcomes remain uncertain, and higher potential comes with higher risk.

The action
Last year, a two-storey penthouse with a large terrace sold for $ 2.6-million at Market Wharf, a 34-storey building south of St. Lawrence Market. Next door, this 1,290-square-foot penthouse had a smaller, two-storey footprint, but considering its custom additions, including three skylights, a third bathroom and a new staircase, agent Elias Ben Khelifa suggested a list price of $1,849,900 this spring. 
“It was really hard to use comparable sales in the building or the area to determine what its value was,” said Mr. Khelifa. 
“It’s higher than the average price per square foot downtown, but it’s reflective of the fact this is a premium unit and location, basically right by St. Lawrence Market.”
But three cuts to the asking price – and a stretch of sunny weather that underscored the perks of having a 600-square-foot terrace with a built-in barbecue – were needed before the two-bedroom suite received a $1.5-million purchase offer in August. 
“It took a little bit longer to sell than usual with market conditions,” said Mr. Khelifa. 
“The unit had a west-facing terrace with no overhang, and it was something people were really drawn to. It has amazing forever views of the city.”

What they got 
The 13-year-old penthouse has 10-foot ceilings, full-height windows and sliding doors to the terrace from the kitchen and living room.
The bedrooms are on the second floor. 
The unit has a storage locker and one parking spot. Monthly fees of $1,102 cover water, heating, concierge, and use of building recreational amenities.

The agent’s take 
“The owner modified the floor plan with the builder, adding skylights and a third bathroom,” Mr. Khelifa said. 
“Another major modification was relocating the stairs, which was originally a spiral staircase.”

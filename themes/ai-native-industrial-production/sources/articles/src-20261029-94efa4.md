Eight Years or Twelve?

News

Proposed Amendment No.1 to allow state senators to serve a third consecutive four-year term
How long should Nebraska’s state senators be allowed to serve before stepping aside? That’s the question voters will answer when they encounter Proposed Constitutional Amendment No. 1 on the Nov. 3 general election ballot.
Currently, members of Nebraska’s Legislature are limited to two consecutive four-year terms, totaling eight years. The proposed amendment would increase that limit to three consecutive four-year terms, allowing senators to serve up to 12 years before being required to leave office.

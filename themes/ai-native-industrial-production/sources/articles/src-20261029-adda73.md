AJ Dybantsa made his NBA debut Wednesday night against the Cleveland Cavaliers.

Washington Wizards rookie and No. 1 overall pick AJ Dybantsa made his NBA debut against the Cleveland Cavaliers tonight after missing the first four games of the season due to a sprained ankle.

Dybantsa started the game alongside Trae Young, Anthony Davis, Alex Sarr and Kyshawn George for the new-look Wizards, who have started the season 2-2.

He had nine points in the first quarter on 3-for-3 shooting, including knocking down his first 3-pointer, and added an assist.

On the whole, Dybantsa dropped 19 points on 8-for-12 shooting, along with two rebounds and two assists, but the Cavaliers took control late to defeat the Wizards.

It’s the most points for a Wizards rookie in their debut since Tom Gugliotta, who scored 20 on Nov. 2, 1992 against the Charlotte Hornets.

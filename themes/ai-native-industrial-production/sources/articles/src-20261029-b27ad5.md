How to watch Miami Hurricanes at North Carolina Tar Heels in Week 9
The Box Office Boyz are back in prime time
by
 
 
Cameron Underwood
Oct 29, 2026, 10:00 AM UTC
To continue the 2026 season, the 
Miami Hurricanes
 head back on the road for the first time in a month, and travel to a place where success has been fleeting in recent years. That’s right, they go to Chapel Hill, NC to face off against the North Carolina Tar Heels.
Miami got things back on track (mostly) in a 52-23 destruction of the Pittsburgh Panthers last weekend. While there were some great highlights, including and especially the performances of QB Darian Mensah and WR Malachi Toney, there were still issues to address with the offensive line, defense as a whole, and penalties. We’ll see how those have been handled as we move forward to play Carolina.
For the Tar Heels, they lost and overtime heartbreaker to Syracuse on a questionable at best/horrible call at worst 2pt conversion by our old friend (joking) WR Calvin Russell. That 31-30 (OT) loss dropped Carolina to 4-3 on the year, and took a bit of wind out of their sails ahead of this game, which I know they’ve been pointing to, because Miami coming to town is the biggest game of the year for everybody we play. Still, the Tar Heels will look to have an outlier performance and pull off a major upset this weekend.

Every game for Miami is a big game, and this is another spotlight game for the Canes. Everybody who loves Miami, and many who 
HATE
 Miami, will be tuned in, watching and dissecting every play. The Canes are the #1 team in the country, and have designs on championships this season. A win at North Carolina is necessary and expected, but the game has to be played. So let’s play it, and win!
North Carolina has a famous coach with mountains of success from his time in the NFL. I’m sure he’s been scheming some stuff up specifically for this game to present problems for Miami, particularly with the defense, which is his calling card. And, especially early in the game when the emotions are high, I could see some of that working.
However, Carolina lacks the talent to truly threaten Miami over the course of a full game. They have a few nice players, like WR Jordan Shipp and DE Xavier Lucas, but not nearly enough high-caliber athletes to win this game, in my opinion.
But, again, that’s why they play the games, and we’ll all have to tune in on Saturday night to see how this plays out.

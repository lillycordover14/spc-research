The Beeville Lady Trojans look to turn a strong finish in district competition into postseason success when they face the Zapata Lady Hawks in tonight’s Class 4A Division II bi-district volleyball playoff.
First serve is scheduled for 6 p.m. at Kingsville High School.
Beeville enters the playoffs at 22-16 after finishing third in district competition at 7-3. Zapata carries a 14-8 record, including an 8-2 district mark.

The Lady Trojans won four consecutive matches before closing the regular season with a straight-set loss at Calallen on Oct. 22. That winning stretch included a four-set victory over Alice and sweeps of King, Tuloso-Midway and Robstown.
Weathers leads attack
Junior outside hitter Isabella Weathers leads Beeville with 238 kills, averaging 2.8 per set. She also has 410 digs and 34 aces, providing production at the net, on defense and from the service line.
Sophomore Bailey Bondoc has contributed 155 kills and a team-high 442 assists, along with 381 digs and 37 aces. Sophomore Aubrie Gonzales adds 393 assists.
Senior Danika Gonzales has recorded 127 kills and leads the Lady Trojans with 43 aces. Junior Aleena Gomez anchors the defense with a team-high 430 digs.
Freshman middle hitter Zoe Dembo-Guerra leads Beeville with 33 blocks, followed by Danika Gonzales with 19 and junior Kayley Ford with 15. Ford averages 2.3 kills per set.
The winner will play the Cuero-Corpus Christi London winner in the area playoffs.

Mack Geiger’s Debut Album ‘Dying in This House’ Due In November&nbsp;&nbsp;MusicRow.com

[page text]
RECORDS Nashville/Columbia Records artist 
Mack Geiger
 is set to release his debut album, 
Dying In This House
, on Nov. 6. Geiger is offering another taste of the new album with the new track 
“
How It Ends,” out now.

“
Dying In This House
 channels a broken man’s walk through loneliness, heartache, denial, but also glimmers of hope,” shares Geiger. “I’ve always been drawn to songs which painted pictures. I feel like this album does that. Furthermore, songs that explore brokenness, because despite our constant endeavor to achieve outside flawlessness, each one of us experience battles that push us further than we can bend.

“Amidst the ballads are moments of remembrance of who the character is” he continues. “Grounding that sparks motivation and pride to push forward. Opportunity to dust yourself off and just get back in the ring. A collage of work with Keith Stegall and Jared Adlam bringing 12 tracks onto a canvas. 
Dying In This House.
 Country music.”

Geiger has solo writing credits across 
Dying In This House 
on “Jesus & Jones,” “Pouring,” “Nothing Like Grandma,” “Mess Of Blues,” “You Don’t Deserve Me,” “Walk A Straight Line,” “String By,” and the album’s title track. The album also features songs penned by 
Rodney Clawson, Will Bundy,  Bart Butler, Seth Ennis, Tim Nichols, Tony Martin,
 Mark Nesler, 
and more.

Geiger’s star continues to rise as his breakout single “String By” has surpassed 104.5 million global streams, reached No. 3 on Spotify’s Viral 50 USA,  and reached the Top 40 on the Global Country Shazamchart. Geiger will continue his “When It’s Pouring Tour” across North America this November and December, including a return to the Grand Ole Opry on Nov. 7. In 2027, Geiger will return to his home country to tour Australia alongside Brooks & Dunn.

 Dying in This House
 Track List:

1. “His and Hers” (Wyatt McCubbin, Seth Ennis, Rob Snyder)

2. “How Crazy Is That?” (Tim Nichols, Tony Martin, Mark Nesler)

3. “String By” (Mack Geiger)

12. “Jesus & Jones” (Mack Geiger)

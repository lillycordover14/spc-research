Yuba City (7-1, 3-0 FVL) vs. Twelve Bridges (6-2, 3-0)
It wasn’t pretty the last time Lincoln’s Twelve Bridges High came to Yuba City in 2024 to take on the Honkers in a football clash.
It was downright embarrassing for those in the brown and gold gear after Twelve Bridges put up 56 points on YC, en route to a 56-0 win. A few Honkers experienced that beatdown, including one of the best in the Sac-Joaquin this year in senior Cody Haase.
Haase and freshman Uly Huizar were two of the underclassmen who watched Twelve Bridges run and throw all over Honker Stadium, putting up 21 in the first quarter and 24 in the second half to close out YC.
Two years later, Haase and company have more than bounced back from the massacre on Oct. 4. The team is 7-1 and coming off the program’s first win at Placer in the MaxPreps era. Haase scored three times and had 244 rushing yards in last week’s victory at Auburn to set up a 3-0 league showdown with Twelve Bridges for the Foothill Valley League title and likely No. 1 seed in SJS Division IV.
Twelve Bridges, on the other hand, has a few holdovers from two years ago, including several upfront behemoths and a receiver who figures to have a big role in the game. Dylan Amos was just a sophomore and amassed 64 yards in five games in 2024. He is the second-leading pass catcher this year with 32 receptions for 462 yards and four scores through seven games.
Under center for the Raging Rhinos will be a junior who is approaching 1,500 yards passing in 2026. Brett Smiley has a near 124 MaxPreps QBR while completing almost 80 percent of his passes. Joshua Chittom should see most of the load in the backfield as an 1,100-plus rusher, averaging over eight yards per carry with 15 touchdowns.
Defensively, YC is the No. 1 unit in the league in points against, has 25 sacks and 11 interceptions in eight games – both stats almost double the MaxPreps national average, according to 
MaxPreps.com
.  
Kickoff is set for 7:30 p.m. or approximately 20 minutes after the junior varsity contest.
 
Wheatland (4-4) vs. Northgate (8-0)
Wheatland welcomes in a premier North Coast opponent in its second-to-last regular season contest. The Broncos come in No. 12 in NCS MaxPreps’ rankings – a section traditionally dominated by stalwarts De La Salle, Cardinal Newman and Pittsburg.
Wheatland will look to build off its 32-9 win at Marysville last week that featured a balanced effort of running and throwing the football. 
The Pirates took a one-possession game in the second quarter and turned it into an onslaught with 18 unanswered points. Andy Fatten’s team successfully hit on two big pass plays and rushed one in from short-yardage to put away Marysville (1-7).
Wheatland ends the regular season at home Nov. 6 against the Pioneer Valley League’s second place team Center (7-1, 3-1 PVL)
 
Sutter clinches GEL title with forfeit
The Huskies will not be in action tonight courtesy of a Natomas forfeit that ends Sutter’s regular season at (9-0, 6-0 GEL).
Ryan Reynolds’ squad is the outright league champs and go into next month’s playoffs as the automatic qualifier out of the GEL. To date, MaxPreps projects Sutter as the No. 3 seed in Division-V with another projection scheduled for Sunday following this week’s games.
Sutter, at last check, sits No. 3 in MaxPreps’ division rankings, trailing Escalon and No. 1 Hilmar.

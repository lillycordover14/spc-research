Daws makes Jets debut in relief of Skinner&nbsp;&nbsp;Sportsnet.ca

[page text]
New Jersey Devils goaltender Nico Daws (50) defends his net during the third period of an NHL hockey game against the New York Rangers Thursday, Oct. 15, 2026, in Newark, N.J. (Adam Hunger/AP)

This probably wasn't how the Winnipeg Jets wanted their newest goalie to make his debut.

Nico Daws
 replaced Stuart Skinner to start the third period against the Tampa Bay Lightning, with the Jets trailing 5-1 Thursday night.

Daws made nine saves in the third period as the Jets stormed back and nearly completed the comeback, ultimately losing 6-4.

Daws was acquired from the 
New Jersey Devils
 last week as part of the package for Connor Hellebuyck.

The 25-year-old has appeared in 59 games, all with the Devils, since making his NHL debut during the 2021-22 season. He had a .872 save percentage and 2.78 goals-against average in four starts before the trade.

The Jets have now lost seven games in a row. Skinner made 16 saves on 21 shots before being pulled.

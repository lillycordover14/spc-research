UK: Blue Matter acquires healthcare communications agency Cognite from Vespa Capital

Blue Matter
, a strategic consulting firm serving the life sciences industry and a portfolio company of Baird Capital, has acquired 
Cognite
, a specialist healthcare strategy and communications agency, from 
Vespa Capital
, with financial terms undisclosed.

Baird Capital, which invests in B2B technology and services companies, first invested in Blue Matter in 2020. In May 2026, 
Baird closed a continuation fund in support of Blue Matter’s growth
. Vespa Capital invested in Cognite in March 2022 through a recapitalisation of the healthcare communications agency.

Cognite, which has offices in the UK, Switzerland, and the US, creates scientific, medical, commercial and patient communications for pharmaceutical and biotech clients, with a focus on complex specialty markets including rare diseases.

The company grew significantly under Vespa Capital’s ownership.

“By becoming part of Blue Matter, we will be able to take our communications skills and experience to a far wider audience, offering tremendous opportunities for growth, both for us and our clients,” said 
Lee Hurley
, CEO of Cognite.

Blue Matter said the deal strengthens its communications capabilities, which it has been building up in recent years.

“Over the past few years, we’ve been systematically bringing together the creative and communications capabilities that are often so crucial to bringing those strategies to life with healthcare providers, patients, payers, investors, and other stakeholders” said 
Ashwin Dandekar
, CEO of Blue Matter. “Cognite’s array of capabilities, creativity, and team culture are a perfect fit for the Blue Matter family.”

                    Queens of the Stone Age                

Perfecth

Matador Records
9 tracks

Apple

Queens of the Stone Age
 shared their ninth studio album, 
Perfecth
, today through Matador Records. The nine-track follow-up to 2023’s 
In Times New Roman…
 takes its title from 
Josh Homme’s
 deliberate corruption of the word “perfect.” Homme and bandmate 
Michael Shuman
 produced the record, which was made in studios in New Orleans and Los Angeles. 
Nikki Lane
 guests on “Easy Street,” and “Sunday” is a co-write with 
Iggy Pop
 that first appeared on his album 
Post Pop Depression
.

“Every song sounds like it’s from a different world,” Homme said. “And that’s on purpose, because it’s trying to stitch together all these emotions revolving around love, lust, foolishness, and the sort of wisdom that comes as a gift from all that.”

                    Christian McBride’s Ursa Major                

Points Of Light

Mack Avenue Records
 

 
Spotify

 
Apple

 
Bandcamp

 
All Links

Points Of Light
 is the debut album from 
Christian McBride’s Ursa Major
, out today on Mack Avenue Records. Formed in 2022, the quintet pairs acclaimed bassist 
Christian McBride
 with saxophonist 
Nicole Glover
, guitarist 
Ely Perlman
, pianist 
Mike King
 and drummer 
Savannah Harris
. 
Terrace Martin
 produced the album, adding synth soundscapes. Their take on George Duke’s “No Rhyme, No Reason” is the lone cover among otherwise original material.

“This album is basically an extension of what we’ve been doing live for the last four years,” McBride said. “It’s been fun to watch a band develop and grow and progress when everything is so wide open. They don’t say no to anything, which is a wonderful thing.”

                    Ministry                

Hate To Go (Take Out Or Delivery)

Cleopatra Records
10 tracks
 

 
Spotify

 
Apple

 
All Links

Ministry
 bows out with 
Hate To Go (Take Out Or Delivery)
, the band’s 17th and final studio album, issued via Cleopatra Records. The 10-track set follows 2024’s 
Hopiumforthemasses
 and closes with “We’re Still Here,” which brings back former bassist 
Paul Barker
 for his first appearance on a Ministry studio album since 2003’s 
Animositisomina
. A farewell Goodbye Europe Tour with 
Die Krupps
 begins April 2, 2027, in Utrecht, Netherlands. Founder 
Al Jourgensen
 addressed the decision to end the band in a statement:

“I’m fucking done. I’m proud of what I’ve accomplished. I’m amazed at the path that it’s taken, from 
With Sympathy
 to where we’re at today, and all points in between. It’s been quite a ride, but I’m completely at peace with the fact that it’s done. It’s time to herd goats.”

                    DIIV                

ZIRP!

Fantasy Recordings
11 tracks
 

 
Spotify

 
Apple

 
Bandcamp

 
All Links

DIIV
 enlisted 
A. G. Cook
 to produce 
ZIRP!
, the band’s fifth album, which arrived today from Fantasy Recordings. The 11-track follow-up to 2024’s 
Frog In Boiling Water
 takes its name from “zero interest rate policy” and was announced through business cards mailed to fans with a QR code and a hotline number. The band described the project in the unedited statement below:

“this is not a niche record for music understanders only. this is our POP record. this is our extremely accessible mainstream alternative rock record. what does alternative mean? we are saying, ZIRP! is what it means. are we joking? not really. this record is FUN. everywhere ‘Frog’ was serious, ZIRP! is fun. it was fun to make, and it is fun to listen to. life is hard. ZIRP! is easy.”

                    THAO                

Show Me

Kill Rock Stars
11 tracks
 

 
Spotify

 
Apple

 
Bandcamp

 
All Links

THAO’s
 first album in six years, 
Show Me
, is out now on Kill Rock Stars. It follows 2020’s 
Temple
 and is her first since she retired the 
Thao & The Get Down Stay Down
 name. THAO co-produced the 11-track record with 
Merrill Garbus
 and 
Nate Brenner
 of 
Tune-Yards
. Tune-Yards, 
The Linda Lindas
, 
Adrian Quesada
 and 
Neko Case
 guest on the album. THAO described the album’s themes in a statement:

“I learned that the point of life is not to emerge unscathed, and that I don’t want to operate as I always have — masking fear as indifference. This album is a real-time record-keeping of my life evolving into a different relationship with love and desire over the last few years. For example, I came out publicly years ago but I’ve never really written a song about sex or queer identity. So, that’s in there. Some songs are snapshots of moments I’ve never felt before. Some are ruminations of what I need to keep, what I want to shear, what I want to move toward.”

18 tracks

Dallas rapper 
BigXthaPlug
 put out his fourth studio album, 
Better Over Time
, through UnitedMasters in partnership with his own label, 600 Entertainment. The 18-track set marks a return to hip-hop after 2025’s country-leaning 
I Hope You’re Happy
. The new release features nine guests: 
Leon Thomas
, 
Ava Max
, 
Ro$ama
, 
Belly Gang Kushington
, 
Babyface Ray
, 
Lil Yachty
, 
Loe Shimmy
, 
GloRilla
 and 
Fridayy
. A companion short film, also titled 
Better Over Time
, tells his story through his parents’ eyes.

                    Sampa the Great                

Nu Zamrock

Loma Vista Recordings
16 tracks
 

 
Spotify

 
Apple

 
All Links

Sampa the Great
 returns with 
Nu Zamrock
, her third album, out today from Loma Vista Recordings. The 16-track follow-up to 2022’s 
As Above, So Below
 was executive produced by 
Cadenza
. The album grew out of her discovery that her uncle was a founding member of WITCH, the Zambian psych-rock band that helped pioneer 1970s Zamrock. 
Mwanjé
, 
Sia
, 
KAMAUU
 and 
Nicole Gumbe
 contributed to the record. Sampa described its purpose in a statement:

“Zamrock is part of my family legacy. It’s my voice. Being Zambian, being loud, being defiant — that’s what Zamrock is. 
Nu Zamrock
 is not nostalgia. It is the future remembering where it came from. This album is a reclamation of my lineage and bloodline: it is my homage to the disruptive, rebellious spirit that Zamrock represented at its peak and carried Zambia forward with, reintroducing the genre to the world, in my own way.”

GarciaLive Volume 23: August 14th, 1993 Shoreline Amphitheatre

Garcia Family Provisions
16 tracks
 

 
Spotify

GarciaLive Volume 23: August 14th, 1993 Shoreline Amphitheatre
 rolled out today via Garcia Family Provisions, documenting the last of 
Jerry Garcia Band’s
 four concerts at the Mountain View, California, venue. The 16-track, two-CD set features 
Jerry Garcia
 with bassist 
John Kahn
, keyboardist 
Melvin Seals
, drummer 
David Kemper
 and backing vocalists 
Gloria Jones
 and 
Jacklyn LaBranch
. “Run For The Roses” is the lone Garcia original in an otherwise all-covers show, which includes a version of “Shining Star” topping 20 minutes to open the second set.

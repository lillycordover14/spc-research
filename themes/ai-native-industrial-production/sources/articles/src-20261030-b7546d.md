YUBA CITY, Calif.
 —
With just two games left in the regular season, Yuba City High School is preparing for a home game against Twelve Bridges High School’s Raging Rhinos with a league title on the line. 
KCRA 3's Melanie Wingo visited Yuba City High for a preview. Watch in the video above. 
Advertisement
Other teams in the region are battling for playoff spots and league titles. 
In Elk Grove, Monterey Trail hosts Franklin, with the winner securing the Delta League title and an automatic playoff berth. 
Foothill hosts undefeated Hiram Johnson, with the winner likely taking the Greater Sacramento League title. 
Undefeated Woodcreek travels to Inderkum, where the victor will claim the Capital Valley Conference banner.  
The High School Playbook Game of the Week features Antelope High School hosting the McClatchy Lions in a Metro League matchup. 
Sports Director Michelle Dapper will provide a live preview at 6:30 p.m. and share all the highlights during the High School Playbook show at 11 p.m. on KCRA 3.

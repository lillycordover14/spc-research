Central figure in Jennings narcotics ring handed 72-month federal sentence

Published 9:13 am Friday, October 30, 2026

By 
American Press Staff

 

                                        (Metro Creative Gallery)                                                                                

Getting your 
Trinity Audio
 player ready...

A Jennings man has been sentenced to six years in federal prison after a multi-agency investigation dismantled a local drug trafficking operation.

On Oct. 20, U.S. District Judge Robert R. Summerhays ordered 33-year-old Drekeylone Peters to serve 72 months behind bars, followed by four years of supervised release. Peters had previously pleaded guilty to conspiracy and possession with intent to distribute fentanyl.

The case dates back to July 2022, when the Drug Enforcement Administration began tracking a narcotics ring operating out of the Jennings area. Investigators quickly identified Peters as a central player in the network, which was moving large quantities of methamphetamine, cocaine and fentanyl supplied by a known source.

The operation came crashing down on March 2, 2023, when the Jefferson Davis Parish Sheriff’s Office executed simultaneous search warrants at Peters’ home and a commercial property used by the conspirators.

At his residence, deputies uncovered cocaine, marijuana, digital scales and baking soda. At the commercial site, a search of a maroon vehicle yielded a cache of weapons — two handguns and a rifle — alongside methamphetamine, cocaine, para-fluorofentanyl and stamped pills containing a fentanyl analogue.

Investigators also analyzed Peters’ cellphone, exposing a digital paper trail of regular communications that documented the coordinated drug distribution from July 2022 through February 2023. Ultimately, Peters and his co-conspirators were held accountable for trafficking at least 476.59 grams of methamphetamine, 124.76 grams of cocaine, and 16.55 grams of a fentanyl analogue.

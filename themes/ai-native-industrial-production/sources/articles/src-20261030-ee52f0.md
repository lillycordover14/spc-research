CMA Country Christmas Returns to ABC Dec. 1 With Gospel, Instrumental, and Holiday Favorites

Families looking for a festive night in can mark their calendars for Tuesday, Dec. 1, when 
CMA Country Christmas
 returns to ABC for its 17th annual broadcast. This year's special brings country, gospel, and instrumental talent together on one stage, pairing time-honored carols with fresh seasonal material for viewers of all ages.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
View this post on Instagram
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
A post shared by ABC (@abc)

The show's official website lists Dan + Shay, Jordan Davis, Lady A, Russell Dickerson, Trisha Yearwood, Grace Bowers, Lindsey Stirling, BeBe Winans, and Debbie Winans. The Country Music Association has not yet shared a formal lineup announcement across its social media channels, leaving room for additional names to surface before the air date.
Cameras rolled Oct. 14 at Belmont University's Fisher Center in Nashville, Tennessee, where the special was taped before a live audience. Even with production wrapped, organizers have kept key details close to the vest, including who will host and which classics and new songs made the final set list.
Hosting duties have changed hands repeatedly since the program debuted in 2010. The last three editions featured a pair of co-hosts: Amy Grant and Yearwood led in 2023 and 2024, before Lauren Daigle and Davis took over in 2025. A two-host format could return this year, though no official announcement had been made as of late October.
Gospel has long been part of the holiday tradition. BeBe Winans joined Susan Tedeschi and Derek Trucks for 
“Children, Go Where I Send Thee”
 on last year's broadcast, while CeCe Winans delivered 
“Joy to the World”
 in 2024. Stirling and Lady A are also familiar faces, having appeared on the 2023 special.
The broadcast is one piece of ABC's larger holiday slate, which opens in late November with 
The Wonderful World of Disney Presents Mickey's Christmas Carol
 on Nov. 25. The schedule continues through December with Disney titles such as 
Mufasa: The Lion King
, then wraps the year with 
The Year: 2026
 on Dec. 30 and 
Dick Clark's New Year's Rockin' Eve with Ryan Seacrest 2027
 on Dec. 31.
Anticipation is already building online as fans trade guesses about hosts and song selections ahead of the premiere. Viewers who miss the ABC broadcast can catch 
CMA Country Christmas
 the following day on Disney+ and Hulu.

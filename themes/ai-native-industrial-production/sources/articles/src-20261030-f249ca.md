KINGSVILLE – Kayley Ford recorded 10 kills as Beeville swept Zapata 25-12, 27-25, 25-17 in a Class 4A Division II bi-district volleyball playoff Thursday, Oct. 29.
Ford also contributed eight digs. Journie Kimball finished with seven kills, eight digs, two assists and an ace, while Danika Gonzales supplied six kills, three aces and two digs.
Bailey Bondoc recorded a double-double with 12 assists and 11 digs and added five kills without an attack error in six attempts.
Aubrie Gonzales directed the offense with 19 assists and contributed six digs and an ace. Zoe Dembo-Guerra finished with four kills and Beeville’s lone block.

Jianna Cano added a kill and a dig. Julia McFall collected an assist and a dig.
Beeville (23-16) totaled 34 kills, 34 assists, 51 digs and six aces.
The Lady Trojans will meet the Cuero Lady Gobblers on Tuesday at 6 p.m. at Rockport-Fulton High School in the area round. Cuero defeated Corpus Christi London 25-21, 25-20, 26-24 on Thursday.

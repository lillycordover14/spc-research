With the Ravens aiming to snap a two-game slide and get back on track in Sunday’s showdown in Buffalo, I’ve offered a dozen thoughts, each in 50 words or less: 

1. It was a difficult week in Owings Mills that included uncomfortable conversations and soul-searching after last Sunday’s embarrassment. This is 
a critical time for Jesse Minter to push the right buttons
 — psychologically and regarding football decisions. How his team responds could set the tone for the remainder of the season. 

2. Of course, 
the return of Lamar Jackson
 helps the cause immensely. The two-time MVP moved around better in practices this week, but I don’t love that he experienced swelling in his ankle as recently as last Saturday. You just want all parties to be smart with so much season remaining.

3. Declan Doyle acknowledged the challenge of Jackson having not played in nearly a month, which is why it’ll be important to 
scheme up some early layups to get his quarterback into a rhythm
. You wouldn’t expect many designed quarterback runs, but presenting a running threat to Buffalo’s defense remains essential. 

4. 
Kyle Hamilton
 logged a full practice Friday and doesn’t carry a game status, but I doubt that hyperextended right knee will be 100%, which isn’t ideal when you’re going up against Josh Allen. Hamilton and Trenton Simpson will be critical second-level defenders to keep Allen from wrecking the game. 

5. The Ravens rank seventh in yards per carry allowed, but 
the run defense
 is 15th in DVOA and 21st in EPA, which speaks to not being as stingy when accounting for game situation and leverage. Allen and James Cook easily present the biggest challenge of the season thus far.

 
8

6. On the flip side, the Bills defense has been among the NFL’s worst, but its strength is the defensive line with Deone Walker looking elite in his second season and Ed Oliver returning. Between that and Jackson’s ankle not being 100%, it’s 
a bad week to be missing Vega Ioane
.    

7. Buffalo had an extra week to try to figure out how to contain 
Zay Flowers
, but it may not matter with Jackson passing him the ball again. Yes, Baltimore needs to get its ground game going, but I’m fine with throwing it to Flowers over and over against that secondary. 

8. The showdown with his old team obviously didn’t go as planned, but this is 
the kind of game for which you signed Trey Hendrickson
 to win on money downs. His matchup with Bills left tackle Dion Dawkins will be pivotal in trying to slow Allen as a passer and scrambler. 

9. Observers have 
wondered whether we’ll see any notable personnel changes to the defense
. For example, might we see more of Chidobe Awuzie and less of Marlon Humphrey at corner? I’m not expecting that, but Minter needs to be ready to be bold if the game dictates shaking it up. 

10. Per 
Sharp Football
, the Bills lead the NFL in points per drive and have surrendered the most per drive while Baltimore is first in point differential in the first half and last in point differential in the second half. 
Vibes are better in Buffalo, but that defense has been awful
.

11. Considering the recent history against the Bills, 
this showdown could turn the entire season around or continue a spiral that might require an exorcist
. No, I’m not buying this being a must-win game, but playing well in a tough environment feels critical. No pressure, right? 

12. There’s been 
much discussion about leadership
, and that’s warranted when considering the large number of veterans. Impassioned speeches are fine, but how about someone making a play when it’s needed? For years, we’ve watched every aspect of this team go down with the ship when it starts taking on water. 

Prediction
: The truth is somewhere in between the extremes saying everything will be fine simply because Jackson’s back or this season is doomed, but there’s no underselling how much elite quarterbacks cover up for their teams. The offensive line remains a weakness, but a healthy Jackson should cure much of what’s ailed the offense, especially against a Bills team that’s struggled to stop anybody. The Baltimore defense is a different story until it proves more than saying, “Hey, we’ve been pretty good against lesser opponents sometimes.” That high-priced and experienced group will have its opportunity to show there’s way more under the hood against a high-octane offense than it showed last week. This sets up to be a high-scoring game that goes back and forth until the end, but the Bills playing at home and having an extra week to rest and prepare give them the edge, especially with Jackson needing to knock off some rust and show that his ankle isn’t going to hinder him moving forward. I do believe the worm will turn for Minter’s team very soon, but not quite in time for Sunday as 
the Ravens fall 30-27 for a third straight defeat
.

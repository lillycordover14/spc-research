Halloween is a holiday that bears a lot of surprises, as well as a lot of scary things. Probably the most scary thing that could happen in college football would be the North Carolina Tar Heels leaving Kenan Stadium tonight with a win over the top-ranked Miami Hurricanes. The Heels are coming off a one-point loss to Syracuse, while the Hurricanes are fresh off the heels of a dominating win over Pitt.
The chances of UNC pulling off a win tonight are extremely slim, but what’s interesting is that the percentage chance isn’t zero. Miami didn’t put away Florida State a couple of weeks ago as convincingly as I thought they would, and while that is easily an outlier in the season, one has to wonder what Bill Belichick was able to learn from that game. But for the most part, the name of the game tonight is to play as hard as you can, and perhaps if the Hurricanes are having a bad night, the Heels will find a way to come away with an unexpected victory.
For a win to even be possible, UNC’s defense will have to play the game of their lives against the Hurricanes. Part of that will need to be accomplished by the defensive coordinator — against Syracuse, the game-winning drive could’ve been stopped had the Heels not went on an all-out blitz on a critical drive. There needs to be smarter decisions made throughout the game, but there also needs to be a close-to-flawless execution of said plan. That is, of course, assuming that Miami doesn’t help out UNC at all by making a few mistakes themselves.
Anyways, we hope that everyone enjoys the game the best they can while throwing down some Halloween candy. As always, we will be back after the game with post-game analysis and takes. Until then, Go Heels!

    Satire: Hey God, things are pretty bad down here

    “To be honest God, there’s a lot of other things I wanted to tell you, but I’m sure you’re busy working in mysterious ways and giving your hardest battles to your strongest warriors (on behalf of Black Americans, please stop doing that).”

Abraham Sigmon
, 
Columnist
                 
             •  
            
October 29, 2026
 

 

Wren Silman
 

Dear God,

Hey, it’s me Abraham. You might remember me from that incident with the water gun. I have been trying to be a better person since then. Unfortunately, some other people in the world… not so much. 

There’s this guy, Donald, not sure if you’re aware of him, but he and some of his friends have been up to some pretty bad stuff. What was that old rule of yours? “Love thy neighbor as thyself.” Well I gotta break it to you Lord, he isn’t quite doing that. In fact he just kicked like a million of my neighbors out of their homes. These neighbors aren’t like the philistines either, whatever their problem was ⁠— most of them actually contribute to social services and don’t take any for themselves.

I would like to make you aware of something that’s going on more locally. God – I’d like to talk about my home state of North Carolina. It’s a pretty nice place. This one guy, Michael Jordan, used to dunk a basketball here all the time. Unfortunately we got a new Michael in town: Michael Whatley. To be honest, he’s not that nice of a guy. Well he is nice to some people. Like there’s this guy Harvey West. He’s a child sex offender, (there’s a lot of those in the government now, by the way) and Michael was nice enough to put him in some government committees. I know in the past you might’ve sent some mixed messages on sex offenders, like King Solomon, and that Lot guy, but after reading some of your newer books, like the ones with Jesus, I really think you wouldn’t like him.

You might be pleased, however, to learn that North Carolina is taking care of some of its citizens. There’s this new group of people in America, we call them corporations. Some of Michael Whatley’s friends, they call themselves Republicans – you remember those sex offenders I was talking about, right? They’ve decided that these corporations shouldn’t have to pay any taxes. I feel like I remember you saying something about how we should pay our taxes, but I could be mistaken. Anyway, these guys are really rich, and I know you said something about how it’d be easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter heaven, but I just didn’t realize that it didn’t matter if the rich person just bought all the needles and all the camels.

By the way, God, this state North Carolina, well, it ranks second-to-last in inflation-adjusted education funding per pupil. I know you might not know what that means, and after all, who can blame you ⁠— it’s not like the United States is your chosen country or something (although some people believe that). Essentially, North Carolina isn’t taking care of its students very well anymore. 

I know you haven’t always been the biggest fan of kids, I mean who can forget that time you straight up killed all those kids in Egypt ⁠— one of your signature moves if you ask me. You might be interested to know however there’s another guy in the Middle East, Benjamin Netanyahu, his friends call him “Bibi.” Bibi’s killing a lot of kids too, but he and his friends have said it’s all above board and that they gotta do it. In fact, a couple of the ministers have even said you gave those kids’ land to them. To be honest, while that sounds like something you might’ve said in one of your books, it just doesn’t seem like the kind of thing your kid, Jesus, would’ve been okay with.

To be honest God, there’s a lot of other things I wanted to tell you, but I’m sure you’re busy working in mysterious ways and giving your hardest battles to your strongest warriors (on behalf of Black Americans, please stop doing that). 

Anyway that’s all from me, talk to you in a couple of years. Amen.

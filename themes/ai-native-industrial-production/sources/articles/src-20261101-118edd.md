Lumaril raised a $66 million Series A at a $400 million valuation, The Wall Street Journal reported on 1 November 2026 (via Techmeme). The 16.5% and the $334 million remainder are our arithmetic; the sources we could read do not say whether the valuation is pre- or post-money.

What Lumaril Says It Does

On its own website, Lumaril says it “connects to existing 
control
 systems, learns how each facility performs best, and continuously optimizes production for higher throughput, better yield, and more reliable operations”.

The company frames the problem this way: the world’s industrial infrastructure, it says, is “powered by decades-old systems that are rigid and rule-based, run by veteran operators who are retiring faster than they are being replaced”. It calls its 
product
 “An industrial autonomy layer for physical production”.

Who Is Building It

Co-founder Mark Elliot is listed as “Creator of Palantir Foundry and Palantir Commercial CTO”, and co-founder Ben Duffield as “Creator of Palantir Foundry and Palantir Foundry Group Lead”. The head of operations, Kim Lear, previously led Palantir’s global public sector work and new markets at Zipline, and the head of 
business
 development, Steve Frost, worked in 
mission
 operations at Anduril, according to the site. The company lists offices in New York and El Segundo, California.

Geno Smith throws two touchdowns, Jets lead Raiders 23-14 at halftime

By

Josh Alper
,

By

Josh Alper

 
 
Published November 1, 2026 02:26 PM

 

Facebook

Twitter

        Email
    

The Jets had the ball five times in the first half of Sunday’s game against the Raiders and they picked up points on all five possessions. 
The last of those came on a Jason Sanders field goal that put them up 23-14 with nine seconds to play in the first half. The Raiders took a knee after the ensuing kickoff and they’ll get the ball coming out of the locker room in the third quarter. 
Sanders also had field goals on the first two Jets possessions and Geno Smith threw touchdown passes the other times that the Jets had the ball. Smith hit Isaiah Williams for 18 yards on the first score and the second came on a 12-yard strike to Adonai Mitchell. 
Mitchell has five catches for 78 yards and Smith is 11-of-17 for 146 yards. Braelon Allen has 18 carries for 121 yards as everything has been clicking offensively for the Jets on Sunday. 
The Raiders started fast and led 7-3 after a Brock Bowers touchdown catch in the first quarter. Their other score came on a 95-yard Dylan Laube kickoff return and the offense picked up just 37 yards over the rest of the half. Ashton Jeanty, who 
threw some shade
 at the Jets defense this week, has one carry for a yard and one catch for 15 yards while Mike Washington has played a bigger role in the offense.

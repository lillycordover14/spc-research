THIS IS AN APNewsAlert. Check back for updates. AP's earlier story is below.
Sheila Chepkirui of Kenya won the women's race of the New York City Marathon on Sunday for the second time.
Chepkirui, who also won the race in 2024, pulled away from countrymate Hellen Obiri in the final 150 meters to seal the victory in 2 hours, 24 minutes and 36 seconds.
American Jessica McClain finished second, passing Obiri — a two-time winner — in the final few strides. It was the first time a U.S. woman finished in the top three since 2018. McClain was 6 seconds behind Chepkirui.

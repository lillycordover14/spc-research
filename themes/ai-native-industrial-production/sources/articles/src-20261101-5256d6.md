Highlights

Pentagon price protection accounted for ~62% of MP Materials' adjusted EBITDA in Q2 2026, raising legitimate earnings-quality concerns.
Heavy rare earth bottlenecks and unproven magnet manufacturing at scale represent bigger risks than subsidy dependency alone.
REEx ranks MP Materials and Lynas Rare Earths as the two most credible Western rare earth investments, but neither is risk-free.
The true test is whether U.S. industrial policy can create competitive manufacturing before government support expires.

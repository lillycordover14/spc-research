Imelda Zapata Castillo, age 93, of Dalhart, passed away, Saturday, October 17, 2026. Mass of Christian Burial will be 10:00 a.m., Friday, October 23, 2026 at St. Anthony of Padua Catholic Church in Dalhart with Father Ron Mathews officiating. Interment will follow at the Memorial Park Cemetery. Rosary will be recited at 6:00 p.m., Thursday, October 22, 2026 at Dalhart Funeral Home. Arrangements are under the personal care of Dalhart Funeral Home.

She leaves behind a legacy of love, faith, and devotion to her family. She was born on November 3, 1932, in Sonora, Texas, to Tomas and Maria Monreal Zapata.

Imelda developed a deep appreciation for hard work, family, and the values that would guide her throughout her life. She attended school in Sonora, where she spent her childhood surrounded by the close-knit community she always held dear. In November 1952, Imelda married Ignacio Castillo.

Imelda was a loving mother to nine beautiful children—five sons and four daughters—and a wonderful grandmother who cherished her family deeply. She enjoyed sewing, making quilts, and creating potholders, sharing her talents and love through everything she made.

Imelda loved spending time with her family and welcomed everyone with open arms, making all who knew her feel loved and valued. She was a member of the St. Anthony of Padua Catholic Church of Dalhart, TX and was involved in her faith and church community throughout her life.

Imelda is survived by her children: Victor Castillo and his wife Toni of Liberal, Kansas; Jimmy Castillo of Houston, Texas; Imelda Wheeler and her husband, Ray, of Houston, Texas; Jerry Castillo and his wife, Kimberly, of Norman, Oklahoma; Martin Castillo and his wife, Joyce, of Channing, Texas; Joe Castillo and his wife, Veronica, of Lovington, New Mexico; Veronica Mariscal and her husband, Junior, of Dalhart, Texas; Abigail Rivera and her wife Elizabeth Serna of Houston, Texas.

She is also survived by her sister, Amada Zapata of Rhome, Texas, and a special niece, Melva Puga and her husband, Raul, of Dalhart, Texas and numerous nieces and nephews.

Imelda leaves behind a legacy of love through her 25 grandchildren, 54 great-grandchildren, and 18 great-great-grandchildren.

She was preceded in death by her parents, Tomas and Maria Zapata; her daughter, Maria G. Marquez; her brothers, Roberto Zapata and Tomas Zapata and her infant brothers Osvaldo Zapata, Leandro Zapata and Vicente Zapata, her sisters, Josefa Arterburn, Maria Sepeda, and Gloria Esquival; and her beloved great grandchildren, Isaiah Castillo and Tiana Quillian.

Imelda will be remembered for her kindness, generosity, strong faith, and the love she shared with her family and friends. Her legacy will live on through the many lives she touched and the family she loved so dearly.

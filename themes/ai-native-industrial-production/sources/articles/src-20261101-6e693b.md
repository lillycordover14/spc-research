Kate Douglass
 lowered her own World Record in the SCM women’s 100 freestyle, touching in a 49.64 to conclude U.S. SCM World Trials in Westmont. Douglass’ previous record stood at a 49.93 that she swam last fall at the World Cup stop in Toronto to become the first woman to ever 
swim sub-50 in the event and remains the only swimmer to do so to this day.

Tonight’s swim broke her own U.S. Open Record as well as she swam a 50.19 at the World Cup stop in Westmont last fall, just a week before swimming her World Record 49.93.

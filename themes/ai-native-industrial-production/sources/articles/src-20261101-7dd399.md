HRN staff

    Photo:

Rich Steele / Eclipse Sportswire / Breeders' Cup

    

Kathynmarissa
 and 
T O Elvis
 shared the day's highest 
Horse Racing Nation
 speed figure with a 131 on Saturday. Kathynmarissa, a 5-year-old mare by American Pharoah, captured the Maker's Mark Breeders' Cup Filly & Mare Turf at Keeneland — a 1 3/16-mile turf stakes — at 5-1 odds, recording her eighth career win from 16 starts. T O Elvis, a 4-year-old colt by Volatile, took the Cygames Breeders' Cup Sprint at Keeneland over six furlongs of dirt at 5-2 odds, earning his seventh career victory in his 10th start.
Sovereignty
 earned a 130 
HRN
 speed figure in the Longines Breeders' Cup Classic at Keeneland. The 4-year-old colt by Into Mischief won the 1 1/4-mile dirt classic at 9-5 odds, recording his eighth career win from 13 starts.

Fastest maiden winners

Associated
 posted the day's highest figure among maiden winners with a 126 
HRN
 speed figure at Parx Racing in race seven. The 3-year-old gelding by Yaupon won a six-furlong dirt maiden special weight in dominant fashion by 5 lengths at 4-5 odds, earning his first career win in his ninth start. He is out of Sweet Promises, a Bernardini mare who earned $5,000, with his top sibling Kimber Spice earning $55,000.
Powerline
 earned a 117 
HRN
 speed figure at Keeneland in a seven-furlong dirt maiden special weight. The 2-year-old colt by Flightline won at 7-5 odds in his third career start. He is out of Park Avenue, a Quality Road mare who earned $331,000 and was Grade 3 placed.

About HRN speed figures

HRN
 speed figures are AI-generated using 
HRN
's proprietary machine-learning algorithms. Although they involve less human input than other speed figures, we see that as a plus as human adjustments often knock down surprisingly good performances to match expectations. 
HRN
 speed figures were launched in December 2024, and our expectation is also that the AI-generated speed figures will continuously get better over time.
The 
HRN
 speed figure scale goes from 1 to 160. Part of the reason for this higher scale is this helps us avoid assigning a speed figure of zero. For some products, in weaker races, half the field or more may get a speed figure of zero, which leads to inaccuracy and defeats the purpose of speed figures.
Because the 
HRN
 speed figures are based on machine learning, as we improve our models we can compute the numbers historically.

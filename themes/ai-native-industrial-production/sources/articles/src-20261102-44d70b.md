Lumaril Raises $66 Million Series A At $400 Million Valuation To Expand Industrial AI Platform

Lumaril raised a $66 million Series A at a $400 million valuation to expand its industrial AI platform, which captures the operational knowledge of experienced factory workers and uses it to improve manufacturing throughput and yield. The round was led by returning investor Elad Gil, with participation from Felicis, Koch Disruptive Technologies, Pax Ventures, Lux Capital, and General Catalyst. The financing brings Lumaril’s total funding to $78 million.

Lumaril’s approach is based on the premise that one of the biggest constraints facing U.S. manufacturing is not the lack of physical factories, but the loss of tacit operating knowledge as experienced workers retire.

The company argues that many existing U.S. plants have substantial physical capacity but often operate below potential because veteran operators hold critical process knowledge rather than documenting it in software or manuals.

Lumaril connects with a plant’s existing control systems and builds what it describes as a living model of the operation. The system learns from how experienced operators manage equipment and adjusts as materials, weather, machinery, and operating conditions change.

The goal is to make real-time adjustments that improve throughput and yield while preserving process knowledge that might otherwise disappear when employees leave or retire.

Lumaril said its model is designed to support existing factories rather than requiring manufacturers to replace installed control systems or build entirely new greenfield facilities.

The company is also positioning the technology as a way to help bring underutilized or mothballed industrial assets back into productive use.

Lumaril said it does not primarily price its offering as traditional software. Instead, the company ties its economics to the additional throughput and yield generated for customers.

The company has developed its technology alongside operators at manufacturers including Ultra-Poly and The Arthur Companies.

Lumaril also offers Monocle, an AI-enabled programmable logic controller historian, to plant operators interested in evaluating its technology.

The company said the Series A will help accelerate deployment as it works to capture and operationalize industrial knowledge across more manufacturing environments.

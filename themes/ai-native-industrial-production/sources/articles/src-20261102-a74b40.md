    FlowCorps offers dynamic Pilates classes for all levels

Shae Thomas
, 
Creative Director
                 
             •  
            
October 29, 2026
 

 

When founder Nicole Cannon started FlowCorps, she envisioned a new type of studio – one devoted to community connection and an elevated exercise experience. FlowCorps is her vision brought to life. The harmony between upbeat music and engaging workouts motivates participants to improve both their physical strength and mental fortitude.

Located just 10 minutes away from UNC’s campus, FlowCorps’ studio offers music-driven reformer Pilates as well as TreadX, a 60-minute class combining treadmill endurance work with controlled resistance training on the XFormer. Nearby in Durham, FlowCorps’ sister studio offers Yoga, Mat Pilates and Mat Strength, giving clients additional ways to train. Both studios prioritize a welcoming community environment, focusing on movement and connection that support participants throughout their lifetimes.

“I’ve had such an amazing experience there,” said Sydney Benfey, a founding member of FlowCorps. “The people who work there are so lovely, and as long as you just show up and are excited, then they’ll hold your hand the whole way through.”

The enthusiastic team of instructors is passionate about guiding functional movement to keep classes fun and motivational. Trained in both fitness technique and group leadership, the FlowCorps instructors ensure that all students walk away feeling energized and strong.

“What I love so much is that during class, they make a point to call out every single person and say something positive,” said Benfey. “They’re cheering you on, saying how great you’re doing, and it really makes you feel like, ‘okay, I can get through this.’”

FlowCorps welcomes new Pilates students to join one of its free 30-minute Discovery Sessions, a relaxed introductory session to build foundational skills and gain confidence in the machines, movements and methods of the studio. The one-on-one classes are designed to create familiarity with the basics of the reformer and set students up for success in their first class.

FlowCorps classes pair challenging, thoughtfully programmed movement with music and attentive instruction. The result is a workout that pushes students to work hard and have fun all the while. Their 45- and 60-minute classes provide a holistic fitness experience to challenge, uplift, and encourage students and instructors alike.

“I think the biggest thing is that you can go at every stage of your life,” said Benfey. “No matter if you are twenty years old or eighty years old, they really will work with you and work with your body.”

Benfey has enjoyed introducing her friends to Pilates, as well as meeting new friends and workout buddies through FlowCorps, remarking, “They really foster such an incredible community. I can’t imagine going anywhere else.”

L'Unione Sarda English
Assemini pulls out all the stops for the pre-New Year's Eve celebrations: Fabri Fibra arrives on December 29th.
 The rapper from the Marche region 
will welcome 2027 two days early at the Stadio Comunale Santa Lucia.
 
This is a significant move for the city, which has secured the crown jewel of "old-school" rappers who never wavers from the crest of the wave,
 especially after 2025's "Mentre Los Angeles brucia" and "Zarathustra Style," the highly anticipated album with Neffa released a few weeks ago.
 "This is an event we're eagerly awaiting," 
Mayor Mario Puddu exults
 . "Two years ago, Tananai's concert was a success, last year's Ghali's, and this year we want to continue along the same lines. 
As is now tradition, we've moved the date forward from the end of the year for several reasons, starting with the desire to avoid the chaos of the night of the 31st.
 We've chosen an artist 
who needs no introduction and whom we consider a true institution
 , a choice that has been 
carefully considered over the months
 ." 
 
This name joins a musical geography that, for New Year's Eve 2027, promises to once again transform Sardinia's piazzas into grand stages.
 Ligurian artist Bresh, one of the leading lights of the new pop-rap scene, is expected in Iglesias, while Sassari is banking on Zucchero: the municipality has initiated a process worth over €633,000 plus VAT to secure his performance. In Gallura, Arzachena is working to bring Antonello Venditti to New Year's Eve. And Cagliari is preparing a widespread celebration, with over €600,000 on the table and four piazzas involved, without chasing a single big name.

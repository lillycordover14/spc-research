Vexcel joins CoStar in nine-figure deal&nbsp;&nbsp;GIM International

[page text]
Vexcel Group, whose aircraft and cameras map much of the developed world in remarkable detail, is heading to CoStar Group. The real estate data giant announced a definitive agreement to acquire Vexcel, operator of one of the world’s largest survey-grade aerial imagery programs, for approximately US$730 million in cash.
Built on decades of aerial capture and processing expertise, Vexcel collects more than 60 million images a year across 45+ countries and 320 major metros, covering 99.6% of the U.S. population. It refreshes major U.S. metros up to three times a year and flies disaster zones within 24 hours. Because Vexcel builds its own UltraCam cameras and controls the entire pipeline from capture to processing, it delivers 7.5–15 cm resolution, roughly 16 times finer than typical satellite imagery. The result is survey-grade property, parcel, and infrastructure intelligence for insurance, catastrophe response, infrastructure planning, mapping, location intelligence and AI.
“Since 1989, 
CoStar Group
 has deployed thousands of researchers in vehicles driving tens of millions of miles across hundreds of cities on multiple continents to find, document and photograph real estate, tenants, and construction,” said Andy Florance, founder and CEO of CoStar Group. “Since then, we have inspected and photographed millions of properties. This direct observation and capture of on-the-ground facts has differentiated CoStar Group from its competitors, making us the leading CRE information solution in the world.”
Continued innovation in aerial photography and Lidar
Rapid advances in aerial photography and Lidar are enabling Vexcel to produce extremely high-resolution images and survey-grade measurements across the built environment, from land, houses, and apartment buildings to offices, industrial and retail properties, construction sites and other infrastructure.
“Combining these high-resolution images with the rapidly evolving capabilities of AI to semantically recognize and extract information and features from properties, we believe we can capture hundreds of data fields per property, across the approximately half a billion properties captured by Vexcel, creating a transformative, new, ultra-low-cost proprietary data source that makes CoStar even more valuable, more accurate, and timelier," Andy Florance contiuned.
CoStar Group also believes that it can accelerate Vexcel’s revenue growth by growing its sales force and leveraging CoStar Group’s 2,000 salespeople who already have existing relationships with many of Vexcel’s best prospects.
“For decades, Vexcel has invested in creating highly accurate geospatial data that customers rely on every day,” said Erik Jorgensen, CEO of Vexcel. “By joining CoStar Group, we gain the scale, technology, and resources to broaden our reach, accelerate innovation, and deliver even greater value to customers worldwide.”

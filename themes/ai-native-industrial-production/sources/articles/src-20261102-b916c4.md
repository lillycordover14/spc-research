With the Ravens losing their top two quarterbacks to injuries and dropping a third straight game in a 36-10 defeat at Buffalo on Sunday afternoon, I’ve offered a dozen thoughts, each in 50 words or less:

1. After much torment at the old Highmark Stadium, 
Lamar Jackson
 and the Ravens hoped to write a different script visiting the new one. One can imagine an alternate timeline where Jackson led Baltimore to a close win over a sloppy Bills team and Tennessee upset Cincinnati. Reality is a nightmare.

2. So much can go wrong in an NFL campaign, which was the biggest reason for concern about 
not reaching an extension with Jackson last offseason
. This roster needs a substantial facelift, but it’s difficult to plan for the extent of it without having your quarterback signed for the long haul.  

3. You wonder how many practice reps 
Joe Fagnano
 had even taken running Baltimore’s offense since August, which makes it even more difficult to evaluate Sunday. It doesn’t help that he was banged up and won’t have the benefit of a full practice week leading into his first potential start. 

4. After the opening touchdown, 
the defense
 held up as well as one could reasonably hope in that first half before crumbling from being on the field so much. That said, it seems pretty apparent that this defense isn’t good enough to do the very heavy lifting that’ll be required now.

5. 
Tyler Loop
 sending the opening kickoff out of bounds and missing a 34-yard field goal when the Ravens were still in the game provided further proof that he just isn’t trustworthy. It’ll be interesting to see how Baltimore proceeds after re-signing Jake Moody to the practice squad two weeks ago. 

 
8

6. 
The offensive line
 was poor as Roger Rosengarten’s struggles against Greg Rousseau headlined the performance. Rosengarten has gone in the wrong direction since a strong start, and 2025 third-round pick Emery Jones was a healthy scratch despite Vega Ioane’s absence. Eric DeCosta has to answer for this unit’s annual shortcomings. 

7. The Bills defense did a good job schematically, but that doesn’t excuse 
Zay Flowers going without a catch
 in a game the Ravens trailed from start to finish. Sunday was nearly an impossible spot for Declan Doyle, but he’s struggled to make adjustments in the midst of games this season. 

8. Broderick Washington’s pick on a deflected pass gave 
the defensive line as many interceptions — one — as the secondary
 through eight games. Especially considering Jesse Minter’s area of defensive expertise and the resources spent, I’m surprised how disappointing the pass defense has been overall.

9. 
T.J. Tampa
 played 22 defensive snaps in his first action since injuring his knee in the opener. Though the 2024 fourth-round pick has had some injuries and is hardly a sure thing to be a meaningful contributor, I’d prefer seeing more of him and less of 
Marlon Humphrey
. 

10. Doyle dusting off the Mark Andrews tush push and having it sabotaged by a Ronnie Stanley false start — that should have been on John Simpson — was on brand. Between that and a Flowers false start on second-and-1 from the Buffalo 5, watching this team in 
short-yardage situations
 remains painful.

11. According to CBS, Sunday marked 
the first time that the Ravens played three quarterbacks in a game since Nov. 26, 2000
 when Trent Dilfer, Tony Banks, and Chris Redman all appeared in a blowout win over Cleveland. I’m not liking the odds of this season having the same happy ending. 

12. Even if a late-season Jackson return is possible, I’d question wasting meaningful resources on short-term fixes when this has 
looked like one of the league’s worst teams over the last 2 1/2 games
. You can point to winnable matchups, but opponents will be saying the same facing Baltimore now.

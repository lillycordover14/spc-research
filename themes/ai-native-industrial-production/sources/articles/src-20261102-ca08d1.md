Pierre Gasly
 and 
Franco Colapinto
 have both been given post-race penalties which have dropped them out of the points and resulted in Haas debutant Ryo Hirakawa scoring on his Formula 1 debut at the Mexico Grand Prix.

Both 
Alpine
 drivers faced post-race investigations with the FIA stewards for failing to slow under yellow flags during the Mexico City race. In the provisional results Colapinto finished in eighth place while his team-mate Gasly took 10th.

Gasly was given a stop-and-go penalty for failing to slow sufficiently under double waved yellows – which was converted into a 30-second penalty post-race – while Colapinto was given a 10s penalty after the race, having failed to slow sufficiently under single waved yellows.

The Argentinean driver was at risk of the same punishment as Gasly, but was cleared of wrongdoing in a double yellow sector because “there was sufficient doubt that the driver had sufficient warning in order to comply with the requirements, so did not determine that there was any breach in relation to that sector,” according to the FIA stewards report.

In the final Mexico GP classification it dropped Gasly down to 17th place with Colapinto demoted to 15th place.

 
 
 
 

Ryo Hirakawa, Haas F1 Team

Photo by: Manuel Eletto / Getty Images

Coupled with a post-race 10s penalty for 
McLaren
’s 
Oscar Piastri
 for clashing with 
Arvid Lindblad
, it sees the trio of drivers drop out of the points-paying positions.

Liam Lawson
 has moved up to seventh place, originally taken by Piastri, while 
Audi
’s 
Gabriel Bortoleto
 has jumped from outside of the points entirely into eighth place.

The same situation occurs for Red Bull’s 
Isack Hadjar
 who moves from 12th to ninth, while F1 debutant Hirakawa completes the points in 10th place for Haas.

The Japanese driver was a late call-up for the US team after it opted to bench 
Esteban Ocon
 for unspecified reasons on Friday night, giving the opportunity to Hirakawa to make his F1 race bow.

    North Carolina men’s basketball take down Western Carolina in season opener, 80-72

    UNC came out with a win on Monday night as it beat the Catamounts in the first game of the Malone Era.

Colby Cotrone
, 
Senior Writer
                 
             •  
            
November 2, 2026
 

 

UNC sophomore guard Isaiah Denis (2) drives to the basket during the game against Western Carolina University at the Dean E. Smith Center on Monday, Nov. 2, 2026.
 

UNC men’s basketball 
(1-0)
 defeated Western Carolina (0-1), 80-72
, on Monday night in the Dean E. Smith Center in the first official game of the Michael Malone era.

Roy Williams high-fived Malone from his seat as the torch was officially passed down from Williams to Hubert Davis to now Malone.

The Tar Heels got off to a hot start with a 10-0 run. NC State transfer sophomore guard Matt Able scored eight in a row to introduce himself to the home crowd for the first time after sitting in the one home exhibition against Charlotte. 

“I think the three things coach Malone talks about is defense. 
We have to be an elite defensive team, defense wins championships, selflessness, playing for each other, and then trust, and trusting in each other,” Able said.

Then, sophomore guard Neo Avdalas drove down the lane and threw down a right hand jam.

On the defensive end, North Carolina forced the Catamounts to take poor shots towards the end of the shot clock early on.

Graduate guard Angelo Brizzi knocked down a three and later, senior guard Terrence Brown converted on a and-1 lay high off the glass before driving and dumping it off to first-year center Sayon Keita for the two-handed slam.

Western Carolina was whistled for a flagrant 1 as a Catamount brought down Jarin Stevenson as he was going up.

But, the Catamounts wouldn’t go away. They began to hit their shots and slowly crept up on North Carolina, cutting it to a one possession game at 30-27 UNC with 4:59 to go in the first.

First-year forward Maximo Adams got on the board with a 3-pointer and used some crafty footwork to get an easy bucket in the paint.

“Depending on how we play each game, you know, there has to be effort, we have to work hard, we have to play together each game,
 
and I think we’re going to continue to do that,” Adams said.

North Carolina headed to the locker room up 39-30.

Able started quickly again in the second half by throwing one down on a Catamount defender.

With 14:11 to go, a Catamount layup brought them within one of the Tar Heels at 48-47. Sitting at 104-12 in all UNC season-openers, Malone didn’t want to add a 13th.

Jaydon Young responded with a 3-pointer, and then another one 24-seconds later.

Western Carolina repeatedly found themselves down one but could not overtake the Tar Heels. 

With the Catamounts doubling UNC’s second chance points at 10-5, it is clear that size is an issue for the team. 

Brown’s scoop shot and-1 and a lefty lay through contact by Able put the Tar Heels back up to a more comfortable 10-point lead at 74-64.

Avdalas would wrap up the game by getting the three to go plus the foul to take a 13 point advantage with 1:31 remaining as North Carolina survived a scare from their Western counterparts.

 “Just trust more, and when we do it, we look really good.
 
When we stop trusting, we look like a team that is just not operating on the same page,
 
and we have to minimize those possessions and those stretches,” Malone said.

The Tar Heels will be back at the Dean E. Smith Center to face Wofford at 7 p.m. on Friday.

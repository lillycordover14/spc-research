A start-up led by the former OpenAI executive Kevin Weil says it has raised $215 million to challenge overseas contractors that make experimental drug compounds for pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies.

The company, Warp Sciences, 
announced the financing
 Oct. 22; Lightspeed Venture Partners led the round. Warp plans to combine synthetic chemists, artificial intelligence, and robots in a US pharmaceutical contract research organization (CRO). Much of the labor-intensive work of making compounds for drug discovery has been 
outsourced to lower-cost providers
 such as the Chinese firm WuXi AppTec.

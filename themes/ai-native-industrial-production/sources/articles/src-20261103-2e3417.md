Should the 
New York Jets 
consider trading starting quarterback 
Geno Smith
?
WFAN evening radio host Tommy Lugauer 
explained why
 it’s time to pull the trigger.
“I would trade Geno. The Jets are in a position right now where they have to trade Geno Smith. Look, the 
[Baltimore] Ravens
 are looking for a quarterback. First, I would call Baltimore to see if they’re interested, and if they’re not, I would still shop Geno Smith around. I think you have to look at the season as a lost season; it’s over. Get 
Cade Klubnik
 out there; I don’t think he’s very good, but see what you got with him,” Lugauer said.
“You’re going to be drafting a quarterback in the draft. Geno Smith can get you back a draft pick or two. He can be a good quarterback for a team like the Ravens looking for a quarterback if 
Lamar Jackson
 is out for the year. Geno Smith has done his part; it’s not his fault, he’s been way better than I thought he would be,” Lugauer admitted.
“To me, you trade your chips in now with Geno; you trade him. You look for a team like Baltimore, maybe even a 
Minnesota
, a team that is searching for a quarterback or their starter is good but not great, and maybe they will be interested in a Geno Smith. That’s the direction I would go. I would move on from Geno Smith. I would play Klubnik for the rest of the year, and I would move on from Geno,” Lugauer explained.
Smith’s Amazing Season Is Being Wasted
The former West Virginia product has had a career renaissance with the Jets in 2026.
Through the first eight games of the season, 
Smith has
 completed 69% of his passes, has thrown for 1,913 passing yards, and has a 12 touchdown to three interception ratio.
If you average those numbers across a 17-game season, Smith would finish with:

4,065 passing yards

26 passing touchdowns

6 interceptions

Smith 
would lead
 the Jets in passing yards in a single season, rank fourth in passing touchdowns in a single season, and tie 
Chad Pennington
 with the fewest interceptions from a Jets starting quarterback (minimum 12 starts).
Those are amazing numbers, but the Jets sit at 2-6 this season. Up next are two nearly impossible games against the 
Kansas City Chiefs
 and the 
Buffalo Bills
. The Jets will be massive underdogs in both contests. Assuming they lose those games, the Jets will then sit at 2-8 this year.
Smith’s terrific year is being wasted on a team that seems destined to miss the playoffs for a 16th straight year.
Insider Chimes in on Smith Trade Conversation
On the latest episode of the 
“Jets Collective”
 podcast, ESPN’s Rich Cimini and ESPN New York’s Jake Asman explored the potential Smith trade topic.
Asman asked Cimini if there’s any path to the Jets flipping Smith for, hypothetically like, a second-round pick ahead of the November 10 trade deadline.
“Well, I don’t think anyone is going to offer a second-round pick for Geno. I guess the Ravens are desperate now, right? So, no, no one in their right mind would offer a second-round pick for Geno Smith. I mean, a guy who is only under team control for half a season? No,” Cimini responded.

IC substrate maker Nan Ya PCB (8046.TW) announced on November 3 consolidated October revenue of NT$6.289 billion (approximately $191.9 million), crossing the NT$6 billion mark for the first time, up 12.6% month-on-month and 69.7% year-on-year to set a record monthly high. Cumulative consolidated revenue for the first ten months of the year reached NT$47.419 billion (approximately $1.4 billion), up 44.95% year-on-year. The company attributed the jump in revenue to growing market demand.

Nan Ya PCB's September revenue was NT$5.586 billion (approximately $170.4 million), with October adding about NT$703 million (approximately $21.4 million), pushing the monthly figure to a record. By product line, prices for both ABF and BT substrates are rising in tandem. Institutional investors estimate ABF substrate prices will sustain quarterly increases of 10% to 15% from the fourth quarter through the fourth quarter of next year, serving as the main driver supporting revenue and profit.

Looking ahead to the fourth quarter, Nan Ya PCB expects demand for ABF and BT substrates to remain strong, with utilization at its Kunshan Plant expected to approach full capacity. Combined with an improved product mix, gross margins are expected to rise, and fourth-quarter operations should maintain their growth momentum.

On customer development, as orders for AMD server CPUs spill over, Nan Ya PCB has recently won new substrate orders for the business. Certification is currently under way at its Shulin plant, and institutional investors estimate that AMD server CPU business could account for more than 20% of revenue next year. Institutional investors are also bullish that Nan Ya PCB, starting in the first quarter of 2027, will supply ABF substrates for AMD's next-generation Venice server processor, which is expected to add further growth momentum in high-end applications.

On capacity planning, before new capacity comes online, Nan Ya PCB is focusing its expansion efforts on relieving bottlenecks at existing production lines. The Chiayi New Plant is expected to begin mass production in 2029, targeting CoPoS substrates as the company continues to pursue opportunities in advanced packaging.

On the industry front, consecutive substrate price increases reflect that demand for high-end servers and advanced packaging has not cooled, while upstream material supply remains tight. For investors, Nan Ya PCB's monthly revenue topping NT$6 billion for the first time, combined with utilization at its Kunshan Plant nearing full capacity, leaves room to imagine stronger short-term fourth-quarter profit performance. Over the medium and long term, investors should watch the progress of substrate certification for AMD's new platform, whether price increases can be smoothly passed through to cover costs, and the pace of capital expenditure ahead of mass production at the Chiayi New Plant in 2029.

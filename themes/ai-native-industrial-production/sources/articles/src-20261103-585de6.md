By R.L. Bynum

Caleb Wilson continued his strong start to his rookie season in another loss on a night when Coby White made his season debut and Leaky Black got four steals in a big game.

Wilson finished with 24 points, seven rebounds, four assists and four steals on Monday night, but the 1–6 Chicago Bulls lost 126–107 in Philadelphia for their fifth straight defeat.

Wilson, the No. 4 overall pick, made 9 of 17 shots and 4 of 9 on 3-pointers in 34 minutes, his third consecutive game with at least 20 points, and he is averaging 21.6 points and 6.7 rebounds through seven games.

After missing Charlotte’s first seven games with a left calf strain, White started in the Hornets’ 124–91 loss to Detroit.

He played nearly 16 minutes and had seven points on 3-of-9 shooting, three assists and three turnovers, and he was the only Hornets player with a positive plus/minus (+1).

Before his return, White admitted to some nerves. “I’m always anxious. That’s not out of the norm,” he said. Charlotte plays at Brooklyn on Wednesday.

Black had his breakout game of the season off the bench in Sacramento’s 115–100 win at Dallas, the Kings’ first victory after six straight losses to open the season.

Black, on a two-way contract, played 24½ minutes and finished with 12 points on 4-of-7 shooting, including 4-of-6 on 3-pointers, along with three rebounds in a game in which the Kings’ bench outscored Dallas’ reserves.

Black was +5 and helped Sacramento build a lead in the third quarter with back-to-back 3-pointers.

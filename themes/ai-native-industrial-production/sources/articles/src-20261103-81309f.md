At ADIPEC 2026, Jennifer Holmgren, CEO of LanzaTech and Chairman of LanzaJet discusses energy strategy, technology, and more

Held under the patronage of His Highness Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, President of the UAE, and hosted by ADNOC, 
ADIPEC 2026
 is taking place at the Abu Dhabi National Exhibition Centre from 2-5 November with an expected 239,000 attendees from more than 170 countries, over 2,250 exhibiting companies, 1,800 speakers and 30 country pavilions.

In an exclusive interview, Jennifer Holmgren, CEO of 
Lanzatech
, shares insights on supply chain security, AI & human expertise, industry strategy, technology development and more.

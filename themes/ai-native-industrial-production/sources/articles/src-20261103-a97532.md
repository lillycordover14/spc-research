Elethron to build UK AlN substrate manufacturing capability

Elethron Ltd of London, UK â an advanced materials and process  engineering company developing high-performance semiconductor  materials and engineered substrates for ultrawide-bandgap technologies â plans to establish a UK manufacturing capability for  aluminium nitride (AlN) substrates,  starting with 2-inch wafers for customer evaluation, device fabrication trials and industrial  qualification. 

AlN combines an ultra-wide bandgap and high thermal conductivity  with favourable electron mobilities and deep-UV transparency, making it a  compelling platform for high-performance power electronics,  advanced RF devices and  optoelectronics designed for extreme environments. AlN is also a native  substrate for aluminium-rich IIIâN epitaxial  structures used in emerging semiconductor devices. 

The UK has a strong base in compound semiconductor  research, epitaxy, device fabrication and metrology. Elethron says that its  investment will augment these capabilities by developing a domestic source of  AlN substrates, diversifying the national upstream semiconductor landscape. 


  “The UK relies on foreign and  challenged supply chains for wide-bandgap materials, exposing domestic industry to  geopolitical and supply risks,” notes founder & CEO Hamid Soorghali. “Establishing  UK production would provide a long-term sovereign source of this critical  material, giving industry reliable access to support its continued innovation  and maintain the UK’s leadership in compound semiconductors.” 

Elethron has begun work to establish its initial crystal-growth  capability for a 2-inch AlN wafer  platform. This will support small-batch wafer supply to UK and European  epitaxial foundries, semiconductor manufacturers and laboratories for customer  evaluation, epitaxial growth and fabrication trials. Elethron will also focus  on improving wafer quality and yields, reducing production costs and building a  reliable supply base while  developing a pathway towards 4-inch wafer production. 

In parallel, Elethron is developing its AlN4RF initiative, the first in a  series of industry co-development programs to shape the specifications for its  application-led substrate portfolio. AlN4RF will connect its AlN wafer  engineering with downstream processing, packaging and product qualification for  precision microwave and high-power RF applications in radar, satellite  communications and advanced radio infrastructure. 

“Our immediate priority is to develop  AlN substrates that manufacturers can process, test and qualify in their  devices,” Soorghali says. “Our planned demonstrators with industry  partners are intended to accelerate the transition of AlN from initial  evaluation into device fabrication and, ultimately, commercial applications in  high-performance electronic systems.” 

Elethron says that its AlN substrate capability will provide the  building block for its wider portfolio of engineered semiconductor materials,  including material systems for high-temperature, high-power and  radiation-tolerant electronic and optoelectronic devices.

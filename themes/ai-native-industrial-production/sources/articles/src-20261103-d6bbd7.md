Stanford Falls to Top-Seeded Tar Heels
Cardinal suffers Quarterfinal defeat, 6-0
 Share

 Box Score 
CHAPEL HILL, N.C. —
 
Anya Jackson
 provided six saves but a second-half comeback was not in the works as the Stanford field hockey team fell in the quarterfinals of the Atlantic Coast Conference (ACC) Field Hockey Championships to No. 1-seeded North Carolina, 6-0. 

North Carolina (12-6, 6-2 ACC) jumped out to an early lead on Tuesday morning, snatching the game-opening goal just over 30 seconds into the contest. However, the Cardinal defense kept the Tar Heels at bay for the remainder of the frame, falling behind by one after 15 minutes of action.

Stanford (10-8, 2-6 ACC), however, clawed back in the second quarter, amassing two shots during the frame while earning its first of four penalty corners. However, the Cardinal could not find the finishing touches in the frame as North Carolina grabbed two goals to end the half up by three. 

Having earned four saves in the second quarter, Jackson was once again tested often and early in the third as UNC struck first in the second half to take a 4-0 lead off its second penalty corner conversion of the day. UNC tacked on one late in the frame as Jackson recorded her sixth - and final - save of the game in the third. 

The Cardinal, though, did not go away quietly, firing two more shots toward the UNC cage while earning the lone two corners of the frame. However, UNC earned the last goal of the game, striking through on a penalty stroke as the hosts took the win, 6-0.

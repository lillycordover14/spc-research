US barbarism driving world toward nuclear deterrence: Mokhber

TEHRAN, Nov. 03 (MNA) –A senior advisor to the Leader of the Islamic Revolution Mohammad Mokhber has said that America's barbarism, by eroding trust in international institutions, is driving the world toward nuclear deterrence.

In a post on his X account on Tuesday, Mokhber wrote, “Military stockpiles have increased by about 130 warheads, and every 9 countries possessing nuclear weapons are undergoing modernization.”

Even countries without bombs, such as Poland, Finland, Sweden, and Germany, are seeking to host or participate in nuclear arrangements.

America's barbarism, by eroding trust in international institutions, is driving the world toward nuclear deterrence, the Leader’s advisor reiterated.

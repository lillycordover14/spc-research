MERIDIAN, Miss. (WTOK) - Merrehope, a historic home in Meridian, is nominated again for 2026 in USA Today’s “10 Best Holiday Historic Home Tours” contest.
The home was named one of USA Today’s “10 Best Holiday Historic Home Tours” in 2024 and 2025.
Reader votes decide the nationwide contest. Voting is open daily through Monday, Nov. 30. Votes can be cast online at the contest link or on the station’s website.
The 2026 “Trees of Christmas at Merrehope” season opens Sunday, Nov. 22, and runs through New Year’s Eve.

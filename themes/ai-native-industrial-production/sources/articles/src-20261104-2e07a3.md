The “
Tar Heel Tribune
 Podcast,”
 with hosts Britt Whitmire and R.L. Bynum, gives 
Tar Heel Tribune
 readers a conversational companion to the site’s written coverage of Carolina athletics with interviews, opinions and insights. Britt, a longtime popular radio and podcast host, and R.L., the publisher of 
Tar Heel Tribune
, will bring familiar voices to discussions about UNC sports. The podcast reflects the same broad approach that has defined the site’s coverage, extending beyond men’s basketball and football while giving regular attention to women’s basketball, baseball and other Carolina programs.

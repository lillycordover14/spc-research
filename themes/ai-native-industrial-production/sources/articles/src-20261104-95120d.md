Sky has announced the signing of Alexa Rendell to bolster its Formula 1 broadcasting team. 
Rendell has joined the Sky Sports team as F1 Multimedia Journalist and she will make her debut at this weekend’s Sao Paulo Grand Prix in Brazil. 
Sky says Rendell will report from across the F1 paddock and Sky Sports’ live coverage, interviewing drivers, team principals, and other key figures from across the sport. 
Rendell has previously presented for the Formula E, World Endurance Championship, Extreme Endurance, and F1 Academy.

Billy McGinty, Director of F1 at Sky Sports, added: "We're delighted to welcome Alexa to the team. She is a rising star in motorsport broadcasting and has an instinct for connecting with audiences. 
“Formula 1 is evolving, with more people than ever discovering and connecting with the sport, and it's important that our coverage evolves too - finding new ways to tell the stories and bring fans closer to the personalities and moments that make F1 so compelling.”
Earlier this year, Sky Sports agreed a new five-year deal to remain the home of F1 in the UK until 2034.

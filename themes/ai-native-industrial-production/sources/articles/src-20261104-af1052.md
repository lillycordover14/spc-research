Some of the biggest names in figure skating are headed to Everett for Skate America, Nov. 13–15, at Angel of the Winds Arena. The three-day competition is part of the ISU Grand Prix of Figure Skating Series and will feature men’s and women’s singles, pairs, and ice dance. The lineup includes reigning U.S. champion Amber Glenn, world champion and Olympic gold medalist Ilia Malinin, and two-time Olympian Jason Brown. Fans can grab tickets on the Angel of the Winds Arena

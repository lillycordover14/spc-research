From the Start

- Round two for Syracuse and North Carolina as the two sides meet in the ACC Semifinals for the first time since 2024 down the road in Winston-Salem, North Carolina.

- The Orange earned their third-straight trip to the ACC Semifinals by way of a 4-3 track meet against fifth-seeded Louisville Tuesday afternoon.

Fieve Frenken

- Top-seeded North Carolina upended Stanford 6-0 in the first quarterfinal game of the Tournament behind two goals from Ryleigh Heck and four other goal scorers.

- It was a fight to the finish earlier this year between the 'Cuse and North Carolina in Chapel Hill. The Tar Heels won 2-1 in an evenly balanced game.

- Syracuse will look to do something it has never done before: beat UNC on the Tar Heels' homefield. The Orange trail in the all-time series 7-22, but the last two games have been decided by one goal; the 2-1 defeat at Karen Shelton Stadium earlier this year and a 4-3 setback at home in 2025.

THE Cardiac 'Cuse

- Nothing gets the heart going like a one-goal hockey game. And Syracuse fans are well accustomed to that this year. 

- Tuesday afternoon's 2-1 win over Louisville was the ninth one-goal game for the Orange this year, bringing the team's record to 7-2 in one-goal games.

- While Syracuse has not played in the most one-goalers in the league, (Duke 13), the Orange do have the second-most one-goal wins this season trailing only Duke's eight. 

- Wednesday's opponent, North Carolina, is 4-6 in one-goal games this year, five of which have been at Karen Shelton Stadium (2-3).

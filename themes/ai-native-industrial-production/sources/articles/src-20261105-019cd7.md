Phoenix Tailings project lands $54.2M support package 

 

Andrea Deckert
//
November 5, 2026
//

 

 

 Listen to this article

Phoenix Tailings
‘ planned $375 million rare earth separation facility at Eastman Business Park has been awarded $54.2 million in state, local and infrastructure incentives.
 

The incentive package for the company’s 
Freedom Project
 combines state 
tax credits
, local tax benefits and anticipated infrastructure funding. It includes up to $3 million in performance-based tax credits through 
Empire State Development
‘s 
Excelsior Jobs Program
 and roughly $15.9 million in separate New York state investment tax credits.
 

Another $35.3 million in projected benefits consists of tax savings on eligible equipment and 
construction
 materials, along with anticipated funding for power infrastructure improvements serving the facility.
 
 

Nick Myers (Photo provided by Phoenix Tailings)

“We do not celebrate progress by announcing we’ve built a floor or a wall. Success is producing the rare earth metals and oxides that power America and our allies,” said Nick Myers, co-founder and CEO of Phoenix Tailings. “Support from the 
Town of Greece
, Greater Rochester Enterprise and New York State helps us upgrade the existing buildings and infrastructure at Eastman Business Park to bring production online faster for America.”
 

Additional 
Monroe County
 programs are expected to support equipment purchases and workforce training. The incentives are subject to applicable eligibility and performance requirements, approvals and final award terms.
 

The financial support is expected to reduce project costs and allow Phoenix Tailings to direct more capital toward processing equipment, production capacity and hiring local workers, its leaders said.
 
 

“This is about making sure the next generation of American innovation is built right here in Rochester, by American workers,” U.S. Rep. 
Joe Morelle
 said.
 

The incentive package also builds on a previously announced conditional loan from the U.S. Department of War for the Freedom Project.
 

State and local partners, including Monroe County and RED-Rochester, are helping advance the development through site preparation, permitting and access to the region’s 
manufacturing
 workforce.
 

The planned 500,000-square-foot facility will process rare earth materials used in electric vehicles, electronics and defense equipment. The materials are considered strategically important to U.S. manufacturing and national security, with much of the world’s rare earth separation capacity currently concentrated in China.
 

Phoenix Tailings plans to begin some operations at Eastman Business Park in 2027, with full production expected in 2028.

                            Azerbaijan's average monthly nominal salary to rise by 8.7% to 1,288.30 manats
                        

05 November 2026 14:15 (UTC +04:00)

The average monthly nominal salary in Azerbaijan will increase by 8.7% next year, reaching 1,288.30 manats, 
APA
 reports.

This is reflected in the 2027 Budget Package.

According to the budget package, this indicator is projected at 1,185.5 manats for 2026 and 1,288.30 manats for 2027. As can be seen, the growth rate of the average monthly nominal salary is projected at 8.7% compared to the current year.

However, expenditures on payments to the population are projected to increase by 9.2% and 10.9%, respectively, compared to the current year's forecast and expected execution figures.

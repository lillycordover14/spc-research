The latest U.S. Census data shows Philadelphia is once again America's poorest big city.
The city improved in 2024, when the poverty rate dropped below 20% for the first time since at least 1979.
Census data for 2025 shows it went back up to 20.4% of Philadelphians living below the poverty level.

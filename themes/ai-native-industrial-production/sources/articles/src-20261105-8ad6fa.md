Amy Acton will earn six-figure salary as Ohio's next governor
Dr. Amy Acton has won Ohio's gubernatorial race
, becoming the first-ever woman governor-elect and the first Democrat to win the office in 20 years. She ran alongside 
Cincinnati native David Pepper
, who will serve as her lieutenant governor.
How much will Acton make as governor of Ohio when she takes office in 2027? Here's what to know.
How much will Acton make as governor of Ohio?
Acton will make $176,255 per year as Ohio governor, according to 
BallotPedia
.

When will Acton be sworn in as governor?
The gubernatorial inauguration is scheduled for the second Monday in January following an election year, per the 
Ohio Constitution
.
The public ceremony for Acton is set for Jan. 11, 2027.
When does Ohio Governor Mike DeWine's term end?
DeWine was first elected governor in 2018 and began serving his term in office on Jan. 14, 2019, according to 
BallotPedia
. He was re-elected on Nov. 8, 2022, in Ohio's general election.
DeWine's term ends on Jan. 11, 2027, the day Acton is sworn in.

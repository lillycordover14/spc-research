Charlotte Hornets guard-forward Kon Knueppel made his season debut Wednesday 
against the Brooklyn Nets
. He missed the first eight games of the season with a left hamstring strain.

Unfortunately, Knueppel experienced soreness in that left leg early in Wednesday’s game. He exited in the first quarter and was ruled out shortly after, finishing with 3 points (0-1 FGs, 3-3 FTs) in five minutes.

Knueppel is coming off a standout rookie season in which he finished runner-up for Kia Rookie of the Year to his former Duke teammate Cooper Flagg, after averaging 18.5 points, 5.3 rebounds and 3.4 assists while shooting 42.5% from 3-point range. He also set the NBA record for 
most 3-pointers made by a rookie
 and led the league in total 3-pointers (273).

The Hornets selected Knueppel with the No. 4 pick in the 2025 NBA Draft.

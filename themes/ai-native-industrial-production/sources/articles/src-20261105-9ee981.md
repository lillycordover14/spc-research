12 Days of Snoopy. Image Credit: Apple

Fans of the classic Peanuts comics have a new reason to celebrate this holiday season. The team at 
Apple
 has officially 
released the first trailer for their upcoming animated adventure
 featuring everyone’s favorite beagle. The new special, called Snoopy Presents: 12 Days of Snoopy, brings back the beloved characters for a fresh winter story. Viewers get a sneak peek at the snowy mischief before the full release hits screens later this month. This upcoming project continues a long tradition of holiday specials starring these iconic characters.

Snoopy races to get off the naughty list before Christmas

The short video reveals a fun twist for the main character. While Charlie Brown and the rest of the neighborhood kids have been on their best behavior, Snoopy has somehow ended up on the naughty list. With only twelve days left until the holiday arrives, he goes on a busy mission to prove he is actually a good dog.

Along the way, the trailer highlights plenty of classic winter moments. Viewers can see Woodstock flying through the air in a tiny red winter hat and Snoopy sliding down a steep hill on a sled. The main story focuses on the famous beagle learning that this time of year means a lot more than just getting wrapped gifts. The creative team aimed to mix the classic charm of the original cartoons with fresh animation.

The special features original music from famous artists

One big surprise in this new holiday release is the star studded soundtrack. The production team brought in some heavy hitters to provide the background music. The show includes original songs from major artists like John Legend, who performs a track called Get Ready. Kamasi Washington and Leon Bridges also teamed up for a new song, while Laufey delivers a fresh take on a classic holiday tune.

The animated film will launch globally on Friday, November 13. Families can stream the new adventure exclusively by tuning in to 
Apple TV
. This drop adds another fun option for people looking for something new to watch during the winter break. Even after decades of television history, the Peanuts crew continues to find clever ways to entertain a brand new generation of viewers.

DIRAC makes complex spatial multi-omic data simpler to analyze&nbsp;&nbsp;Nature

[page text]
DIRAC (‘domain invariant representation through adaptive calibration’) is a framework that helps researchers to analyze complex spatial multi-omic data through two connected tasks: integrating omics layers within a sample and transferring annotations from reference datasets to new ones. Thus, DIRAC makes it easier to build interpretable tissue maps across multi-omic assays, platforms and biological systems.

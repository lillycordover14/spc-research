Twelve South

Twelve South Cablesnap 65w

		Twelve South’s Cablesnap 65w is a compact power adapter with an integrated USB-C cable that pulls out (up to 29 inches) and automatically retracts. The travel charger delivers up to 65 watts and has extra USB-C and USB-A ports, so it can charge up to three devices simultaneously from one outlet. And it comes with UK and EU adapters for traveling abroad. Available in three finishes: dune (white), coastal (blue) and slate (black).

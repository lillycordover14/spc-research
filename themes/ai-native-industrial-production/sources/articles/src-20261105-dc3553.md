Knicks coach gets honest about former Lakers forward after promising debut

2026-11-05 14:55

New York Knicks head coach Mike Brown praised former Lakers forward Dalton Knecht after his promising debut last night, revealing that a last-minute decision to play him paid off.

The former Los Angeles Lakers forward made the most of his opportunity, finishing with 10 points in just 16 minutes of action.
 
Knecht shot an efficient 4-of-6 from the field, knocked down two three-pointers, and contributed seven rebounds, one assist, and one block as the Knicks cruised to a dominant 135-105 victory over Chicago on Wednesday night.
 
 
 
 
Despite his productive outing, Knecht wasn't initially expected to see the floor. Following the game, Knicks head coach Mike Brown revealed that the decision to give the newcomer playing time was a spontaneous one.
 
Mike Brown reveals why he gave Knecht a chance
 
"Yeah, it was just a gut feeling. On my minutes sheet, he was actually down for zero minutes. It was just a gut feeling to throw him in toward the end of the first half," Brown admitted after the game.
 
The Knicks coach also praised Knecht's offensive versatility, emphasizing that the 25-year-old brings much more to the table than just outside shooting.
 
"You know, he's a scorer. He's not just a shooter. A lot of people think he's just a shooter because he's white. I mean, I said it for you. I hope I don't get in trouble. I hope I don't get in trouble."
 
"But he can go get it. He can shoot from range, he's got a midrange game, he can get to the rim, and he can pass."
 
Brown highlights areas where Knecht must improve
 
While impressed with Knecht's offensive contributions, Brown acknowledged that the young forward still has room to grow, particularly on the defensive end.
 
"He's got to work defensively, and I thought tonight he tried to play within himself and contribute the best way he could."
 
"There were a couple of things defensively that he could do a little better, and that's what he's really got to concentrate on."
 
"But I thought he gave us a nice little lift, especially offensively, when he played toward the end of that first half," Brown concluded.

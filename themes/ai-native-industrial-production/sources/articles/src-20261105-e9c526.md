It’s Holiday Edit Season Again&nbsp;&nbsp;New York Magazine

[page text]
Photo: Barbara Alper/Getty Images

Between now and New Year’s Day, you’ll be asked to bring a dish to a potluck, get dressed for three parties with three different dress codes, and buy a Secret Santa gift for the co-worker you’ve only ever made small talk with. 
The Holiday Edit
, our subscriber-only newsletter, is here to help.

Last season was full of tips you couldn’t Google. City editor Christopher Bonanos explained why a $9 box of Ikea Svalka wineglasses beats a sleeve of Solo cups. Vulture’s Rebecca Alter sent readers to Tiny Doll House on East 78th Street for presents small enough to fit in any studio apartment. Features writer Allison P. Davis had one rule for holiday hosts: Don’t test a recipe you’ve never made on your guests. “Just play the hits.”

This year, we’re again recruiting writers and editors from across the magazine to share their expertise. Hear from the hosts who never run out of ice, the gift-givers who start shopping in October, and the people with very strong opinions about the movies — holiday and otherwise — you should watch before the year ends. They’ll tackle everything from how to curate the ideal party playlist to hosting overnight guests when you live in a one-bedroom to keeping kids entertained once school lets out. We’ll also surface stories from across 
New York
 that will help most in the moment: new gift guides and seasonal recommendations, along with advice from the archives that holds up.

MP Materials Corp. (
NYSE:MP
) shares rose 
4.50%
 as of 10:50 a.m. ET after the company 
reported
 third-quarter revenue up 176% to $147.7 million, ahead of analysts' consensus of 
$117.37 million
. Adjusted diluted EPS of $0.04 beat the estimate of a $0.02 loss, while the GAAP loss narrowed to $0.12 a share from $0.24.

Higher NdPr oxide and metal sales and stronger market pricing drove the gain, along with a $31.6 million sale of SEG+ to a U.S. aerospace and defense customer. NdPr production rose 41% to 1,018 metric tons, and sales volumes jumped 94% to 1,020 metric tons. The Materials segment generated $47.8 million of adjusted EBITDA, including $8.1 million of price protection agreement income. Magnetics revenue fell 15% to $18.6 million as magnet production started up at the company's Independence facility in Texas. Adjusted EBITDA swung to $41.1 million from a $12.6 million loss.
 
 
 
 
 
MP said it completed the magnet qualification process for General Motors (GM), with saleable magnet deliveries and revenue beginning in the fourth quarter. CEO James Litinsky said the milestone marks an inflection point for our business and mission. The company ended the quarter with $1.34 billion in cash and short-term investments.

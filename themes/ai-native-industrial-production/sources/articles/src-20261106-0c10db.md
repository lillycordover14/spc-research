Avalanche: Helicon's staking reward curve completes its phase-in - By 21 Dec 2026
Less than 1 min read
AVAXUSD
Helicon's staking reward curve completes its phase-in by December 21, 2026. The minimum consumption rate falls from 10% to 7.5%, while the one-year rate stays unchanged. Each AVAX stake keeps the rate set when it starts.
The lower minimum applies to new AVAX stakes under the fully phased-in curve. The change moves Avalanche toward a projected reduction of 0.5 to 1 percentage point in the network's annual inflation.

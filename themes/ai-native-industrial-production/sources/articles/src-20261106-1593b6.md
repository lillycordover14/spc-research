LAREDO, Tex. (KGNS) - Two cases of New World screwworm have been confirmed in Starr and Zapata counties, according to state Sen. Judith Zaffirini.
Officials have established Infested Zone 29, which encompasses portions of Jim Hogg, Starr, and Zapata counties in South Texas. An adjacent surveillance zone has also been outlined surrounding the affected area.
Residents and agricultural producers are urged to report any suspected cases promptly to relevant authorities.

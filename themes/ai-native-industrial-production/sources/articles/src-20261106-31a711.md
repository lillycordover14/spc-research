CHARLOTTESVILLE, VA.—
Members of the North Carolina men's tennis team won both their singles matches and split a pair of doubles matches on the first day of the Intercollegiate Tennis Association's East Sectional on Thursday on the campus of the University of Virginia.


Tar Heels 
Chris Xu
 and 
Ian Mayew
 both advanced in the singles draw in three sets, moving on to Friday's round of 16. 


In doubles, Xu and 
Niels Ratiu
 marched into the quarterfinals with a two-set win.


Action will continue on Friday, with bids to the NCAA Singles and Doubles Championships at stake.

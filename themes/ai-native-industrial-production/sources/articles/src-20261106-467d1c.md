Chai Discovery, Roche to Collaborate on AI Drug Discovery
 

		This adds to deals Chai has struck in 2026 with GSK, Pfizer, and others.	

November 6, 2026
 

By: 
Patrick Lavery

Content Marketing Editor
 

Save

Editor’s Take: An AI model focused on protein-based therapeutics can help pharma partners reach targets that have traditionally been challenging.

Chai Discovery
 announced a collaboration with 
Roche
 using Chai’s AI models and platform for drug discovery across numerous therapeutic areas.

Roche the Latest Major Pharma Partner

This partnership with Roche adds to several others Chai has struck with major pharmaceutical companies in 2026. In October, Chai said it would be partnering with GSK 
following a successful technology evaluation
 involving wet-lab testing.

Earlier, in June, Pfizer announced it would leverage Chai’s AI platform 
to accelerate drug discovery research
.

Founded in 2024, Chai focuses on AI and building a computer-aided design suite for protein-based therapeutics. The company’s latest model is called Chai-3. This, according to Chai, achieves high target success rates and binding affinities—including to challenging targets.

How Chai Models Work

Chai has designed its models to reason about biological structure and function. These models also generate new molecular designs from scratch, helping pharmaceutical partners pursue targets difficult to reach with traditional methods.

The goal of this is bringing the precision and predictability of engineering to better, faster therapeutics for tough, “undruggable” conditions.

“We are delighted to see Chai’s models used by Roche in their portfolio,” Chai Co-Founder and CEO Joshua Meier said. “This expands the range of therapeutic programs that can benefit from AI-driven molecular design.”

“Chai’s platform aligns well with our research strategy,” Roche Head of Corporate Business Development Boris Zaïtra said. “We look forward to collaborating to advance drug discovery programs.”

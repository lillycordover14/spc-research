Attendees gathered to watch the Ottery St Mary Tar Barrels on Thursday as part of bonfire night celebrations. 
The annual event involved people known as "barrel rollers" running through the Devon town with 27 flaming tar-soaked barrels while thousands of spectators lined the streets.
Andrew Wade, president of the Tar Barrels Committee, previously described the event as an "ancient rite of passage".

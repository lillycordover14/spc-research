A historic event saw competitors carry burning tar barrels through the streets.

The annual spectacle returned on Nov. 5 when brave locals raced through 
Ottery St. Mary
 in Devon carrying blazing barrels on their shoulders.

By Talker

It is one of the U.K.’s most extraordinary annual traditions — thought to date back centuries.

The event is free to attend and attracts between 14,000 and 20,000 visitors every year.
It sees local people carrying flaming, tar-soaked barrels through the narrow crowded streets.

It's a British folk tradition where local residents run through the crowded streets carrying full-sized, blazing wooden barrels soaked in tar on their backs and shoulders. (William Dax via SWNS)

It attracts 
thousands of spectators
, with smaller barrels carried by younger participants earlier in the afternoon before the larger barrels take center stage.
Celebrations traditionally culminate in the famous Midnight Barrel in the town square.

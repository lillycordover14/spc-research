Tiffany Trump
 just proved that not only does she have Barbie's blonde bombshell hair, but she also has her sculpted physique. The 33-year-old dressed up as the doll for Halloween this year – and her costume flattered her toned figure.
President Donald Trump's
 daughter took to Instagram on November 5 to share a series of belated photographs taken on the spooky weekend. Tiffany wore Barbie's iconic cowboy outfit that consisted of a cropped waistcoat and a pair of high-waisted flared pants in a bold pink hue. The pants were accented with drawstring details on the waist and a playful star print on the legs. The look was completed with a matching pink bandana, which was tied round Tiffany's neck, and an embellished white cowboy hat. 

© Instagram
Tiffany Trump and her family dressed up as characters from Barbie

Tiffany coordinated her outfit with her family as they posed for a sweet photograph. Her husband, Michael Boulos, was dressed as Ken, wearing a black shirt adorned with white fringing, a red bandana neck scarf, and a beige cowboy hat. Meanwhile, their baby son, Alexander, looked adorable in cow-print pants and a brown leather waistcoat.

Tiffany Trump's family life
Tiffany is the daughter of President Donald Trump and his second wife Marla Maples. She earned a law degree from Georgetown University after completing her undergraduate studies at the University of Pennsylvania.

Alexander is Tiffany's first child

Tiffany and Michael met while holidaying in the Greek Isles with actress Lindsay Lohan in 2018. Michael later proposed in the White House Rose Garden with a 13-carat emerald-cut diamond ring reportedly worth $1.2 million. The pair married four years later in a lavish ceremony at Mar-a-Lago. 
The couple, who married in 2022, welcomed their son, Alexander, in May last year. "Welcome to the world our sweet baby boy, Alexander Trump Boulos," she posted alongside a black-and-white photo of her son's foot on Instagram. "We love you beyond words! Thank you for coming into our lives! 5.15.2025."

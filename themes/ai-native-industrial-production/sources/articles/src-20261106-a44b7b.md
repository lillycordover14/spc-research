Article Summary

McFarlane Toys reveals a Superman: The Movie Clark Kent Elite Edition figure inspired by Christopher Reeve’s 1978 classic.

The 7-inch Superman collectible features 22 points of articulation, a detailed sculpt, and Clark Kent’s iconic outfit.

Elite Edition extras include alternate heads, glasses, hat, briefcase, soft-goods trench coat, shirts, and press badge.

Superman fans can pre-order the DC Multiverse Clark Kent Elite Edition now for $49.99 ahead of its December 2026 release.

The clock is ticking for the DC Multiverse, but that isn't stopping McFarlane Toys from throwing out more exciting collectibles, as they have unveiled a brand-new McFarlane Elite Edition figure! This time, they're returning to 1978 with the legendary 
Christopher Reeve
 Superman. Coming to life from 
Superman: The Movie
, it's time for Clark Kent to step into the spotlight with a brand-new 7-inch-scale figure. This figure features 22 points of articulation, a nicely detailed sculpt, and a classic outfit. As a McFarlane Elite Edition figure, it comes packed with a nice selection of accessories, including a secondary head, removable glasses, multiple pairs of interchangeable hands, a removable hat, and a briefcase.

Clark also comes with two alternate dress shirts that can be removed to showcase his Superman suit underneath, along with a soft-goods trench coat and a newspaper. McFarlane Toys takes things one step further by including a replica Superman: The Movie press badge featuring Clark's iconic photo from the film. We have yet to see a Clark Kent figure in the DC Multiverse line, so this is a fun release for any Superman collector. Pre-orders are 
already live
 for $49.99, with an expected December 2026 release date.

Giant pandas 
Ping Ping and Fu Shuang
 made their public debut at Zoo Atlanta's new Arthur M. Blank Panda Grove on Friday.
"This is an extraordinary moment in history for Zoo Atlanta, the City of Atlanta, the state of Georgia, and beyond. Zoo Atlanta is thrilled to be able to introduce Ping Ping and Fu Shuang to our Members and guests," Zoo Atlanta President and CEO Raymond B. King said in a statement. "We are honored to be the stewards of a species that not only brings joy and wonder to people around the world, but which also represents a conservation success story."

The zoo gave 
"Good Morning America"
 a first look at a video of the 6-year-old giant pandas in their new habitat ahead of Friday morning's opening ceremony.
The video provided by the zoo showed Ping Ping and Fu Shuang chowing down on bamboo stalks and enjoying their habitat.
Giant pandas Ping Ping and Fu Shuang make their public debut at Zoo Atlanta on Nov. 6, 2026.

According to the zoo, Ping Ping, a male panda, is the older of the duo and was born on March 17, 2020. His name represents a smooth and healthy life. Female panda Fu Shuang, whose name derives from "fu lu," meaning happiness, just turned 6 on Oct. 18.

King described Ping Ping as "a goofball" and Fu Shuang as having a quieter personality.
"He has got a lot of personality. She's a little more reserved, probably a little more cautious in trying new things," King told "GMA." "But they've both adapted very, very well."

Both giant pandas were born at the Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding in China and 
arrived
 at Zoo Atlanta in September.
The zoo said the pair then completed a routine quarantine period in a "biosecure area" of the panda grove.
Giant pandas Ping Ping and Fu Shuang make their public debut at Zoo Atlanta on Nov. 6, 2026.
Zoo Atlanta
The pandas will remain in the U.S. temporarily as part of a giant panda conservation agreement between Zoo Atlanta and the China Wildlife Conservation Association.
Ping Ping and Fu Shuang are the latest giant pandas on loan from China. The last time Zoo Atlanta had giant pandas was in 2024. That year, giant pandas Lun Lun and Yang Yang, who first arrived at the zoo in 1999, returned to China with their two youngest cubs, Ya Lun and Xi Lun.

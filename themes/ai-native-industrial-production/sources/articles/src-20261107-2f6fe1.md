EAST HARTFORD, Conn. (WFSB) - The UConn football team hosts North Carolina on Saturday afternoon at Rentschler Field, with kickoff set for noon.
Both teams are 4-4 this season. UConn won the last time the teams played.
Fans were tailgating hours before the team arrived at about 9:45 a.m.
Belichick returns to New England
North Carolina head coach Bill Belichick is returning to New England for the game. He left his job as head coach of the New England Patriots two years ago.
Fans at the tailgate talked about Belichick’s return after his decades-long career with the Patriots.
“I don’t think Belichick will be a distraction. It’s just another game. He’s their coach,” said Tim Willman of Washington, D.C.
Chuck Morrell of Plainfield also commented on Belichick.
“Ok, he was great with the Patriots, but you know what? There’s a rule that when the job is done, you walk away. He just can’t walk away,” Morrell said.
Fans share expectations for the game
Larry Cohen of Plymouth, Massachusetts, spoke about the game.
“We are energized, just like you said, we think we have a real shot today,” Cohen said.
Kiyah Reynolds of Sons of Nutmeg in Middletown also spoke about the game.
“The energy is always great every time I’m in there. I know I bring it. I know we bring it. I feel like Connecticut has been bringing it so I feel good about today,” Reynolds said.
Larry and Ruth Cohen also talked about the team.
“And this team is great. We finally have a quarterback who can throw. We’re in good shape,” one of them said.
Tailgaters bring hot tub, breakfast menu
Fans celebrated in different ways. One group brought a hot tub.
Some people brought a Blackstone for a special breakfast.
Marc Zirolli of South Windsor said his group bases its menu on the home state of the opposing school.
“We always base the menu on the state of where the school that we’re playing is coming from. So, in this case, North Carolina. We’re playing North Carolina today. The state vegetable happens to be the sweet potato. So, we’ve got sweet potato buttermilk pancakes. We’ve got chicken waffles. We have bacon and grits,” Zirolli said.

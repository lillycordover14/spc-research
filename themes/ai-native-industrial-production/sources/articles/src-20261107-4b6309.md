MP Materials entered its Q3 2026 report with a bruised chart and a rare earths story investors had been questioning for months. The stock had fallen over the past week, month and quarter. Then the release hit and the share price jumped 4.95% to US$35.41.

MP Materials' Margin Story Meets Early Milestones
 
 
The bullish pitch around MP Materials hinges on niche oxides and magnets turning volume into sturdier profitability instead of a broad NdPr supercycle.
 
 
 
"Bullish analysts expect MP Materials to translate its vertically integrated mine-to-magnet and recycling platform, heavy rare earth expansion, and government-backed magnet projects into materially higher and more resilient margins over time."
 
 
Q3 gives that claim tangible footing. NdPr output and sales volumes moved sharply higher, Materials and Magnetics both produced positive adjusted EBITDA and Independence completed GM qualification ahead of first shipments in Q4. Heavy rare earth circuits entered commissioning and early SEG+ sales to a defense customer started to monetise that stockpile.

MP Materials: Subsidy Fears, Execution Gaps Still Lingering
 
 
Critics worry MP Materials cannot make the magnet chain stand on its own economics once support fades and plants move beyond early runs.
 
 
 
"While MP Materials benefits from a public private partnership that includes an NdPr price floor of about US$110 per kilogram and minimum EBITDA support for the 10X facility, the existence of this support signals that underlying market pricing and project economics may be fragile."
 
 
Q3 results lean into that concern rather than close it out. Price protection and contracted shipments helped turn adjusted net income positive, yet Independence is only now exiting qualification and 10X is years from commercial output. Heavy rare earth projects are still in commissioning with limited volume evidence. The print validates progress, but not subsidy independence.

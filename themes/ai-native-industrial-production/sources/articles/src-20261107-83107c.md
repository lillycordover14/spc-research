BANKS COUNTY 49, NORTHVIEW 35

The Leopards won their final game, 49-35 versus Northview. Banks County closes the season at 4-6 overall, winning to snap a 4-game losing streak.

#6 COMMERCE 42, PROVIDENCE CHRISTIAN 32

The Tigers won a tougher game than most imagined with a 42-32 win over Providence Christian. Commerce (9-1) will host next week to open the playoffs. Marco Huff had a 1-yd TD to tie the game midway into the first. He then gave the Tigers a lead midway through the second with a 53-yd TD. Ma’Kiy Sanders had a pick six to make it a 22-13 game moments later, but Providence cut it to 22-20 at the half. Tate Kirk threw an 18-yd TD to Essien Lockleer early in the third, and Rivers West had a TD before the end of the quarter. Kirk threw another TD, this time to Ty Sorrells early in the fourth to put the game away. 

PICKENS COUNTY 35, DAWSON COUNTY 15

Dawson County dropped its final game of the year to Pickens, falling 35-15 to close the year at 1-9 overall.

HABERSHAM CENTRAL 37, MOUNTAIN VIEW 34

The Raiders (6-4) won a nail-biter in double OT over Mountain View at home in a 37-34 wild game. It marks the third straight win for Habersham, who awaits their playoff opponent and home/away. 

LUMPKIN COUNTY 35, FRANKLIN COUNTY 12

The Indians opened the game with a 19-yd TD from Kolson Howell to Mac Powell after a quick 3-and-out. Franklin scored on the next play when Knox Whitworth hit Carson Cheek for a 74-yd TD (the XP was no good, though). Lumpkin scored the next four times, with Howell finding Joseph Alvarez on an 11-yd TD. Alvarez then had a 5-yd TD, followed by two Weston Veater short TD runs late. Whitworth ended the game with a 56-yd TD to Gavin Wells. Lumpkin (7-3) awaits its first round game, while Franklin (4-6) will likely be out of the playoff picture.

#1 PRINCE AVENUE CHRISTIAN 42, RABUN COUNTY 0

The Wildcats lost 42-0 against top-ranked and undefeated Prince Ave, finishing the regular season at an even 5-5, awaiting their playoff opponent and seeding.

STEPHENS COUNTY 35, MORGAN COUNTY 26

The Indians won the region title with a 35-26 win over Morgan County. Stephens County (7-3) got 369 passing yards and 5 TD passes from Branson Stowe. He had two apiece to Kaiden Harbin and Duke Collins, and another to Ashton Winters. It was 35-14 late when Morgan made it a thriller with a scoop-and-score, onside kick, and TD pass. Stephens County awaits its opponent, but by all means should host.

NORTH MURRAY 34, UNION COUNTY 28

Union County dropped its season finale 34-28 at North Murray to close the year at 2-8. The Panthers got off to a good start with a Bentley Rich 5-yd TD as it was tied at 7-7 at the end of one. Kamden Kendrick threw a 9-yd TD to Chetley Cox in the second, then Rich had a 3-yd TD to knot the game at 21 just before the half. NM took a FG lead into the break. Kendrick had a 28-yd TD to Sir Zion Thompson in the fourth to pull within 34-28, but that would be the final.

WHITE COUNTY 55, CHESTATEE 6

The Warriors finish the regular season at 8-2 with a 55-6 win over Chestatee. Jack Clark had a 1-yd TD and 6-yd TD in the opening quarter, and Kody Tuhey caught a 15-yd TD from Brinn Robinson to make it 21-0. Savion Anderson (32 yds), Chris Nunnally (2 yds), and James Hobson (3 yds) all had rushing scores in the second to push it to 41-0. With a running clock, Kiyon Dorsey scored in the third on a 4-yd TD, and Champ McCutheon added a 5-yd TD midway through the fourth. Chestatee got its score late.

BYE: #5 Jefferson, Towns County

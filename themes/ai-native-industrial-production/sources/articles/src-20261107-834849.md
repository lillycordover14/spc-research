Article Summary

Avengers #1 tops This Week's Top Twelve Comics Bestseller List, boosted by a blind bag and leading the field.

Absolute Superman #25 lands at No. 2 with roughly half the sales of Avengers #1, ahead of Batman #15.

Marvel and DC split This Week's Top Twelve Comics Bestseller List evenly, claiming six entries each.

The rankings use Wednesday-to-Friday in-store sales from 120 to 150 ComicHub-powered comic shops.

Avengers 
#1 is the bestselling comic book series in comic book stores this week, helped along by a blind bag, beating out 
Absolute Superman 
#25 twice over as well as 
Batman
 #15 and 
Absolute Catwoman
 #4.

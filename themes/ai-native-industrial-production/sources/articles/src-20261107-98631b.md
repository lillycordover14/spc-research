On November 6, 2026, Khosla Ventures and related reporting entities submitted Amendment No. 3 to their Schedule 13D for LanzaTech Global, Inc. 

(NASDAQ:LNZA)

            LNZA (NASDAQ:LNZA)
        

, revealing the issuance of warrants to a Khosla-controlled entity tied to a LanzaJet recapitalization finalized on October 14, 2026.

Key Highlights

Triggering event:
 Completion of recapitalization and deleveraging transactions on October 14, 2026 involving LanzaJet, Inc. and its subsidiary LanzaJet Freedom Pines Fuels LLC.

Warrants issued:
 Warrants granted to purchase 1,737,921 shares of Common Stock at an exercise price of $5.754 per share, representing 105% of the closing price on the trading day before the October 14, 2026 closing.

Vinod Khosla's total beneficial ownership:
 13,216,919 shares, equating to 58.4% of the class per the filing.

Khosla Ventures III, L.P. stake:
 138,753 shares, or 1.06% of the class, with no sole voting or dispositive power; all powers are shared.

Share count basis:
 Calculations based on 13,089,163 Common Stock shares outstanding as of August 10, 2026, plus warrants for 7,800,000 shares at $0.0000001 per share and 1,737,921 shares at $5.754 per share.

Potential additional warrants:
 Issuer may issue further FSI Warrants by December 1, 2026, at 105% of the closing price on the trading day before that date.

Stockholder meeting commitment:
 Issuer to hold a stockholder meeting by July 30, 2027, to approve an increase in authorized shares and the warrant issuance under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635.

LanzaJet Recapitalization Leads to Warrant Issuance to Khosla-Controlled Entity

According to the filing, on October 14, 2026, LanzaTech Global, Inc., its wholly-owned subsidiary LanzaTech, Inc., LanzaJet, Inc., and other LanzaJet stockholders and lenders completed a series of recapitalization and deleveraging transactions involving LanzaJet and LanzaJet Freedom Pines Fuels LLC. These transactions aim to "support LanzaJet's next phase of growth and operations at FPF's facility in Soperton, Georgia." LanzaTech is noted as a major equity holder in LanzaJet, a sustainable aviation fuel company.

As part of these transactions, the Issuer entered a Warrant Issuance Agreement with Future Solution Investments LLC ("FSI"), resulting in warrants issued to a Khosla-controlled entity. These warrants cover 1,737,921 Common Stock shares at an exercise price of $5.754 per share, exercisable for cash or on a cashless basis and subject to adjustments upon certain events, the filing states. A second tranche of warrants may be issued by December 1, 2026, at an exercise price equal to 105% of the closing price on the trading day immediately preceding that date, subject to conditions.

The Warrant Issuance Agreement also mandates the Issuer to register the resale of Warrant Shares with the SEC within 60 business days after issuance, per the filing. Vinod Khosla, as a reporting person, reports aggregate beneficial ownership of 13,216,919 shares, representing 58.4% of the class, including outstanding shares and those issuable upon warrant exercise at both price levels. The filing lists Khosla Ventures III, L.P., Khosla Ventures Associates III, LLC, VK Services, LLC, and Vinod Khosla individually as the four reporting persons, all organized or domiciled in Delaware or the United States.

Disclosure Summary

This Amendment No. 3 to Schedule 13D reveals that Khosla Ventures-affiliated reporting persons received warrants for 1,737,921 shares of LanzaTech Global common stock at $5.754 per share following the LanzaJet recapitalization, with Vinod Khosla now reporting beneficial ownership of 58.4% of the class.

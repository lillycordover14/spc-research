(WTAP) - The final week of the West Virginia regular season is in the books. 
Williamstown dropped their first game of the season 35-33 to Wheeling Central. 
#2 Fort Frye plowed through #10 Piketon in their playoff debut, 37-0. 
#4 Eastern lost a late lead to #5 Shadyside, falling 27-26. 
Cabell Midland finished strong with a 48-25 win over Parkersburg South. 
Parkersburg ended their regular season on a high note, beating Oak Hill 69-6. 
Doddridge County handled Mingo Central, 33-14. 
Ravenswood fell at South Harrison, 52-14. 
#6 Waterford knocked off #3 West Jefferson, 21-14. 
#4 Athens saw their season come to an end against #5 Dover, 41-0. 
St. Marys ends the regular season on a five-game win streak, beating Wirt County 28-20. 
Ritchie County took down Calhoun County, 46-20. 
Ripley beat Nicholas County, 45-14. 
Finally, Tyler Consolidated fell at Roane County, 42-14. 
Coming off of their state title win, the St. Marys cross country team joined Jermaine Kelly at the podcast desk. 
Play of the night:
 Brady Thorn picks up a fumble and takes it to the house less than a minute into the Parkersburg-Oak Hill game.

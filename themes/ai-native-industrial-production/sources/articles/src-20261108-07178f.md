ONALASKA, Wis. (WKBT) -- Hockey and figure skating came together for a unique showdown on the ice in Onalaska.
The event, called Battle of the Blades, paired players from the La Crosse Area Avalanche with skaters from the Coulee Region Figure Skating Team. Each pair combined their skills to create a one-of-a-kind routine.
The Cyclones Girls Hockey team also attended the event.
Duos competed for the Judges' Choice award. Audience members voted for their favorite performance to determine the People's Choice winner. The competition also serves a larger purpose beyond the trophies.
Jenny Keer, assistant team manager for Avalanche Hockey, said the event is now in its third year.
"It's our third year of doing this fundraiser, and it has replaced doing a lot of smaller fundraisers for all the clubs," Keer said.
Kelly Reitzel, team manager for the Coulee Region Figure Skating Team, described the community response.
"I have to say that this year that has really touched my heart and made me realize how lucky we are to live here, because the amount of support we get for our showing up there, lined up outside, waiting to come in right now to" Reitzel said.
To prepare for the event, the figure skaters spent the last six weeks teaching hockey players how to figure skate.

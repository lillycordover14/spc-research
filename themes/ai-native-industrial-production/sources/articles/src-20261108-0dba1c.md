Dutch farmers donated 200,000 bulbs of 16 tulip varieties to Lviv. They will be planted in districts, parks, and social
institutions.
200,000 tulip bulbs were delivered to Lviv from the Netherlands; some of the 16 varieties of these flowers will be presented in the city for the first time. The Lviv City Council 
reports
 this, 
UNN
 informs.
Details
They clarified that this is already the fifth such gift shipment from Dutch farmers.
Our gardeners take good care of the flowers, and our foreign partners like this. That is why they keep giving the city new bulbs. Next year, if these 200,000 are added, around 500,000 tulips will be blooming in Lviv
It is noted that this time the city received new and unique varieties of flowers. These include white, pink and purple tulips, peony-flowered varieties and others.
The City Council clarified that the bulbs will be distributed among the city’s districts, parks, schools, hospitals and social centers, as well as the communities of the Lviv agglomeration. For this purpose, the Department of Ecology prepared a comprehensive needs table, which will be used together with the heads of the relevant institutions to determine how many bulbs, and in which colors, are needed for each location.

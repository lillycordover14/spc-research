Geno Smith
 got a rare opportunity to lay somebody out during Sunday’s game … and took full advantage of it.

Smith and the New York Jets
 played on Sunday against the Kansas City Chiefs
. During the second quarter at Arrowhead Stadium in Kansas City, Mo., Jets running back Braelon Allen
 took a handoff from Smith but got the ball punched out by Chiefs linebacker Nick Bolton
.

Kansas City safety Alohi Gilman
 then picked up the fumble … but promptly paid the price for it. Surprisingly, the Jets quarterback Smith came charging in with cruel intentions and delivered a bone-crunching hit on an unsuspecting Gilman (who did still manage to hang onto the football in the end).

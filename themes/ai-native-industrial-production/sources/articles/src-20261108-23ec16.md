Florida State
 opened the Rick Stockstill era with a road win Saturday. Those victories have been hard to come by for the Seminoles in recent years.
 
 
FSU defeated 
Boston College
 31-24 behind two rushing touchdowns from Amari Thomas. His 11-yard score late in the fourth quarter put the Seminoles ahead and made Stockstill a winner in his first game as interim coach. Entering Saturday, FSU had not won a game away from Doak Campbell Stadium since Nov. 25, 2023. Ashton Daniels completed 15 of 26 passes for 178 yards and an interception.

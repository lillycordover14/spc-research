The Aspen High School volleyball team will return to the Class 3A state tournament after winning its region for the 
second straight year
 on Saturday inside the AHS gymnasium.

As the 
No. 11 seed
, the Skiers were among 12 teams to host a regional this fall, where they beat No. 26 Weld Central and No. 14 Jefferson Academy to advance. AHS swept the Rebels by scores of 25-15, 25-17 and 26-24 before the Jaguars needed a full five sets to take down the Rebels in Game 2 of the round robin.

This set up a winner-take-all finale between Aspen and Jefferson Academy. After a comfortable 25-20 set win to open the match, AHS crumbled late in the second set to lose, 25-23. But the Skiers responded by winning an intense 30-28 third set that seemed to derail the Jaguars’ upset bid for good. Aspen easily won the fourth set, 25-13, to seal the region title.

The Skiers (22-2 overall) will now take that No. 11 seed into the 
double-elmination state tournament
, which runs from Thursday through Saturday inside the Denver Coliseum. Aspen is scheduled to play No. 6 Forge Christian at 11 a.m. Thursday in the opener.

No. 1 University, No. 2 Resurrection Christian, No. 3 Holy Family and No. 4 The Classical Academy are the favorites heading in and will each recieve a first-round bye. The rest of the field includes No. 5 Sterling, No. 7 Alamosa, No. 8 Eaton, No. 9 Berthoud, No. 10 Platte Valley and No. 12 The Village.

Last fall at state, also as the No. 11 seed, Aspen went 0-2. The Skiers lost 3-1 to No. 6 Bayfield in the first round before bowing out against No. 4 Forge Christian, 3-1. No. 3 Eaton beat No. 1 Alamosa in the 2025 Class 3A championship game.

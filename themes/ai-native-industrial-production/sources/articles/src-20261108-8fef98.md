The Flyers couldn’t take the heat and lost to the Flames in overtime, dropping them to 4-10-5.

Another third-period letdown resulted in another loss for the Flyers.

This time, the Flyers fell in overtime with a 4-3 decision Sunday night to the Flames at Scotiabank Saddledome.

Matt Coronato won it for Calgary with 52 seconds left in the bonus period. The Flames had a 3-on-1 rush after Owen Tippett played the puck toward the net with two of his teammates looking for a deflection or rebound.

In the third period, the Flyers were burned by their old friend Joel Farabee. Instead of the Flyers building on their lead with a power play goal, they gave up a game-tying shorthanded goal to Farabee.

On the night, the Flyers relinquished three one-goal leads.

Tippett, Denver Barkey and Noel Acciari provided the team's goals.

In their previous game, the Flyers had 
what Rick Tocchet felt was an "inexcusable" finish
. They coughed up a 2-1 lead Thursday night with 6:08 minutes left and lost in regulation, 3-2, to the Rangers.

The Flyers have had 10 games decided by one goal. They've lost nine of those games (1-4-5).

Tocchet's club has just four wins in its first 19 games. The Flyers are 4-10-5.

For some context, the 2006-07 Flyers — a team that finished 22-48-12 — had more wins through 19 games. That franchise-worst club was 5-12-2 at this point.

The Flyers see the Flames (8-9-1) again Dec. 11 when the teams meet at Xfinity Mobile Arena.

								Sean Couturier met with the media after the Flyers' 4-3 overtime loss Sunday night to the Flames.
							

• Dan Vladar, 
facing his old club
, did his job, almost willing the Flyers to a win.

He denied 22 of 26 shots.

The Flyers were hanging on in the third period.

With 1:06 minutes left, Vladar made a marvelous save on Martin Pospisil to keep the game tied. He also turned away Farabee twice in the final 2:46 minutes.

On Farabee's shorthanded goal, Oliver Bonk tried to collect a loose puck along the offensive blue line, but the defenseman had it poked away. That sprung Farabee for a breakaway.

Calgary netminder Devin Cooley stopped 32 of the Flyers' 35 shots.

Just 27 seconds after Acciari gave the Flyers a 2-1 lead in the second period, Cooley robbed Tippett with a sprawling glove save. It was a massive stop because it allowed the Flames to tie the game 3:16 minutes later on a Zach Whitecloud goal.

But Tippett broke through later in the middle stanza when he whipped home a power play goal.

In the first period, the Flyers were immediately defending after Barkey opened the scoring. Zayne Parekh split Tippett and Trevor Zegras a little too easily with a skillful play to tie the game at 1-1.

								Owen Tippett scored a power play goal Sunday night in the second period to give the Flyers a 3-2 lead over the Flames.
							

• Someone will have to step up on this five-game road trip for the Flyers. They're 0-1-1 so far and it's because they're stumbling in crunch time.

They're at least in these games. But they're simply finding ways to lose them. And that's a pretty concerning trend.

• The Flyers 
needed Tippett to find his offense
.

His goal gave them a 3-2 lead heading into second intermission. Tippett was highly active and finished with six shots. But the 27-year-old winger had a minus-4 rating.

If there's a silver lining, maybe it'll be Tippett's goal. His production tends to come in bunches. We'll see if this can start a run for him.

• The Flyers are right back at it Monday when they visit the Canucks (9:30 p.m. ET/NBCSP).

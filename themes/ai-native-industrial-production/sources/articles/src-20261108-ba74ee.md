ESLC Day Seven: Twelve teams battle for final placement&nbsp;&nbsp;World Lacrosse

[page text]
Croatia and Spain play on November 8, 2026 in the European Sixes Lacrosse Championships in Salou, Spain at the Mediterranean Sports Hub.

The Netherlands rallied to beat Poland for seventh place on day seven of the women’s competition at the 2026 European Sixes Lacrosse Championships in Salou, Spain, as the final standings from seventh through 17th were set.

The final qualification spot for the 2027 WL Sixes Championships is still to be decided, with Ireland and Italy meeting Monday at 11 a.m. in the fifth-place game.

The Netherlands trailed 10-7 in the fourth period before scoring four straight goals and holding on for a 13-11 win. Hanneke Van Verseveld led the comeback with five goals, four of them in the fourth period, and Sophie Koedood added three goals and two assists. Casey Rhine scored five goals for Poland, and Roksana Dębicka had two goals and six assists.

Latvia took ninth with a 13-8 win over Sweden. Džeimija Lasda had three goals and one assist, Suzanna Penike had two goals and four assists, and Līga Vilka made 15 saves.

Switzerland pulled away from France 11-6 to finish 11th, breaking a 4-4 tie with four straight goals. Eva Baumann scored three goals, and Mia Levesque had three for France.

Norway took 13th with a 10-7 comeback win over Austria, scoring the final five goals after trailing 7-5 in the fourth period. Lisa Stein scored three goals, and Karita Haugsbø made 13 saves.

Spain earned its first win of the championship, defeating Croatia 15-6 to finish 15th. Olivia Docal had three goals and four assists, and Natalia Ogando Ayluardo made 17 saves.

Portugal also picked up its first win, edging Finland 12-11 in overtime to finish 17th after trailing 11-9. Sage Da Silva tied the game with 12 seconds left in regulation and scored the winner in overtime, finishing with five goals.

Monday’s medal games follow the fifth-place game, with Czechia and Germany meeting for bronze at 12:30 p.m. and Israel and Great Britain playing for gold at 2 p.m.

Women’s Results

17th Place: Portugal 12, Finland 11 (OT)

13th Place: Austria 7, Norway 10

11th Place: Switzerland 11, France 6

Ninth Place: Latvia 13, Sweden 8

Seventh Place: Netherlands 13, Poland 11

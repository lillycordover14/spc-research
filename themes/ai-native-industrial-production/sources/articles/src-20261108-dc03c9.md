Say Goodbye to Barrel Jeans: ‘Tulip Jeans’ Are the New Denim
Shape Taking Over This Fall

Volume at the hip, discipline at the ankle. Botany,
essentially

 
 

 

 
 
 
 

 

Denim

has been getting louder for three years now, ever since volume
returned to the leg and everyone had to decide where they stood on
it. The 
barrel jean
 arrived first, curving out at
the thigh and drawing back at the ankle, dividing wardrobes into
believers and holdouts.

This fall the silhouette has been sanded down into
something far more wearable, and the fashion crowd has given it a
name that explains itself on sight. 
Tulip jeans

carry their volume high and round through the hip and thigh, then
taper to a clean finish at the ankle, tracing the line of a bloom
still holding itself closed.

The effect reads as 
sculptural
 while
staying clear of costume, which is why the style has traveled so
quickly from runway to office.

Where the Shape Comes From

Katie Holmes

The lineage runs through 
Alaïa
, whose
catwalk did more than any other to establish the curved leg, giving
it an outsized, rounded cut that reached 
Free
People
, 
Madewell
 and the rest of the high
street within a season. Purists found that version a little
avant-garde for daily life.

The 
tulip
 interpretation keeps the
curve and trims the excess, landing somewhere a boardroom can
accommodate. Flowers have supplied fashion with proportions before.

Christian Dior
 named his spring 1953 collection
the 
Tulip line
, building fullness across the bust
and shoulders while drawing everything in below, working the same
logic in the opposite direction.

Who’s Wearing Them, and With
What

Halle Berry

Jennifer Lopez
 has folded the shape
into her off-duty rotation, usually with a 
denim
jacket
 and a bag doing the talking. 
Katie
Holmes
 keeps hers deliberately plain, setting the volume
against a 
button-down shirt
 and flat shoes, which
is the most useful blueprint on offer. Photographed in New York
this month, 
Halle Berry
 wore a cropped pair with
an oversized 
blazer
 and white sneakers, showing
how little the silhouette asks of everything around it.

The lesson worth borrowing is 
restraint above
the waist
, since the jeans handle the drama unassisted. A

fitted knit
 or a tucked shirt gives the curve
somewhere to begin. Footwear matters more than usual here, with a

low-profile sneaker
 or a slim boot keeping the
tapered ankle visible and the proportion intact. Worn this way, the
shape holds its bloom through the coldest months, which is more
than most gardens manage.

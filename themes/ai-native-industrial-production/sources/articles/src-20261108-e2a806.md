ZAPATA COUNTY, Tex. (KGNS) - A man is in custody after a standoff with deputies in Zapata County, according to the sheriff’s office.
The Zapata County Sheriff’s Office said the man barricaded himself inside a travel trailer Saturday, Nov. 7, and was acting erratically.
Deputies called in the Special Response Team. Several other agencies helped.
Investigators said the team took Hector Baldomero Esquivel into custody without further escalation.
Esquivel is charged with three counts of terroristic threat against a peace officer, false report to induce emergency response and resisting arrest.

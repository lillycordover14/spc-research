Jupiter Boys Claim First Title, Riverview Sarasota Girls Reign Again At Florida 4A State Meet&nbsp;&nbsp;SwimSwam

[page text]
2026 Florida High School Athletic Association 4A State Swimming & Diving Championships

Nov. 6-7, 2026

Ocala, FL

SCY (25 yards)

Results 

There was a new champion and a repeat champion at the Florida High School Athletic Association (FHSAA) 4A State Swimming & Diving Championships.

The Jupiter boys claimed their first ever title, winning the 400 free relay to outlast runner-up Cypress Bay in the overall team standings by 5.5 points.

It wasn’t quite as dramatic in the girls’ competition, where Riverview Sarasota easily repeated as champion and won its sixth title in seven years.

Girls’ Recap

Riverview Sarasota won two relays, starting in the 200 medley relay when the team of 
Sophia Malkowicz
 (25.89), 
Sydney Hardy
 (28.72), 
Angelina Lista
 (23.97) and 
Alexandra Pearson
 (22.63) topped the field in a time of 1:41.21.

In the 200 free relay, the squad of Pearson (22.85), Lista (23.32), Hardy (23.02) and 
Kaitlyn Gebhardt
 (24.19) won with a time of 1:33.38.

The 400 free relay was captured by Oviedo’s team of 
Laney Cupit
 (52.17), 
Josie Densberger
 (52.78), 
Natasha Goodwin
 (52.48) and 
Danielle Giddens
 (49.73) in a time of 3:27.16.

Individually, the multi-event winners were Jupiter junior
 
Veronica Metz
 and Creekside senior 
Emmie Lewis
.

An NC State commit, Metz claimed the 200 free in a time of 1:45.68 and the 500 free in a time of 4:44.59, both personal bests.

Committed to South Carolina, Lewis won the 50 free in a personal best time of 22.78 and the 100 free in a time of 50.18.

Other individual winners were:

Hardy, a sophomore for Riverview Sarasota, claimed the 200 IM in a personal best time of 1:58.81.

Ava Sedlacek
 of West Boca Raton County won the 1-meter Diving event with 462.5 points.

Janae Sanders
 of Newsome, a Pitt commit, captured the 100 fly in a time of 53.00.

Buchholz senior 
Peyton Brooks
, a Cincinnati commit, took the 100 back in a personal best time of 54.12.

Windermere sophomore 
Anna Leneis
 won the 100 breast in a personal best time of 54.12.

Girls’ Team Standings — Top 5

Riverview Sarasota, 350.5

Creekside, 221

Oviedo, 158

Windermere, 149/ 4. Jupiter, 149

Boys’ Recap

It came down to the last event, where Jupiter’s team of 
Charles Howard
 (44.80), 
Chase Young
 (45.76), 
Asher Smith
 (47.47) and 
Julian Granison
 (43.27) claimed the 400 free relay in a time of 3:01.30. The result catapulted Jupiter to the top of the team standings over Cypress Creek, which was 2nd in the 400 free relay (3:05.01).

Jupiter also won the 200 free relay in a time of 1:20.86 behind the team of Howard (20.50), 
Slater Woodard
 (20.84), Young (20.71) and Granison (18.81).

The 200 medley relay was captured by Creekside’s team of 
Ian Foulk
 (23.72), 
Camden Martin
 (26.56), 
Gage Wright
 (23.00) and 
Bailey Koo
 (20.34) in a time of 1:33.62.

The individual standouts were Howard, Granison and Boca Raton junior 
Caleb Caponera.

A senior who has committed to Michigan, Howard repeated as champion in the 200 free with a personal best time of 1:36.70, and then improved on a 2nd-place finish from last year in the 100 fly by winning it this year in a time of 48.15.

Granison, a junior who has committed to Florida, won the sprint freestyle events in personal best times, claiming the 50 free in 19.50 and the 100 free in 42.96.

Caponera won the 200 IM in 1:46.57 and the 100 breast in 55.71.

Other individual winners were:

Winter Park senior 
Jake Mandarin
 won the 1-meter Diving event with 683.80 points.

Sarasota senior 
Jackson Irwin
, a Texas A&M commit, won the 500 free in a personal best time of 4:19.51.

Winter Park junior 
Denzo Senekal
, who has committed to Alabama, captured the 100 back in a personal best time of 48.63.

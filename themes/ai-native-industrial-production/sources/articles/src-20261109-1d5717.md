Tar spill snarls traffic on Interstate 95 in Chesterfield&nbsp;&nbsp;Yahoo

[page text]
Southbound traffic on Interstate 95 in southern Chesterfield County was snarled on the morning of Nov. 9 as crews worked to clean up tar spilled on the highway after a truck hauling the substance overturned.
It happened shortly before 5 a.m. near the Ruffin Mill Road-Woods Edge Road exit about four miles north of Colonial Heights, Virginia State Police Sgt. Jessica Shehan said in an email. A vehicle attempting to change lanes hit the truck, causing it to overturn and spill tar all over the southbound travel lanes.
Both drivers were taken to an area hospital with injuries not considered life-threatening.
This screenshot from a traffic camera shows the site where a truck carrying tar overturned Nov. 9, 2026, on Interstate 95 southbound in Chesterfield County.
The crash remains under investigation.

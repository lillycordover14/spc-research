LEGO Interstellar, Tulip Field Windmill and Chinese Cork Carving are your next LEGO Ideas sets&nbsp;&nbsp;Jay's Brick Blog

[page text]
LEGO Ideas has officially announced three new Ideas that will become future sets!

Fans of Christopher Nolan’s seminal sci-fi movie, Interstellar will be delighted by news that 
Interstellar
 by 
Minibrick41587
 will be a future LEGO Ideas set! The model depicts the iconic iconic Ranger spacecraft, as well as Cooper and Dr. Brand minifigures, along with robotic companions TARS and CASE.

The Dutch are in for a treat as 
Tulip Field Windmill
 by 
HisBrickMaterials
 has also been greenlit. Set amongst a field of colourful tulips, this gorgeous windmill model is such an iconic symbol of the Netherlands. There must be something in the water in Holland, as  
AMSTERDAM CANAL HOUSES
 by 
Brickmaster_85
 was also previously greenlit. 

Another stunning model greenlit is
 Chinese Cork Carving Art
 by 
GenericBrix
, featuring traditional Chinese cork carving diorama as a model. The composition is simply stunning, and this is such a fresh concept that I can’t wait to see being executed. 

But that’s not all, 
The 5th Element – Korben Dallas Taxi
 by 
MarinBrickDesign
 and 
Peashooter
 by 
DistinguishedCrow
 have also been placed in the LEGO Ideas Parking Lot. This is where Ideas go when the LEGO Ideas Team needs a bit more time to work on the product, so fingers crossed these get approved!

Daft Punk recently made it out of the parking lot, so never say never!

Check out the successful ideas below and congratulations to the successful builders!

Tulip Field Windmill by HisBrickMaterials

Co-Founder and CEO Tom Miller joins AI drug discovery panel on November 18, 2026
 
 
SAN DIEGO, Nov. 09, 2026 
(GLOBE NEWSWIRE)
 -- Iambic (Nasdaq: IAM), a clinical-stage life sciences and technology company developing novel medicines using its AI-driven platform, today announced that Iambic Co-Founder and CEO Tom Miller, PhD will attend the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference, taking place November 16-19 in London. Dr. Miller will participate in a panel on AI in drug discovery on November 18 at 2:00 PM GMT.
 
 
 
 
 
Now in its 17th year, Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference is Europe's largest healthcare-focused conference featuring leaders in pharma, biopharma, biotech, healthcare, and health IT.
 
 
"The greatest opportunity for AI in drug discovery is to fundamentally improve the probability of developing successful medicines by integrating insights across chemistry, biology, and clinical development," said Tom Miller, PhD, Co-Founder and CEO of Iambic. "At Iambic, we are translating this vision into reality through our integrated molecular superintelligence platform and a growing clinical pipeline. I look forward to discussing how the industry can move beyond the promise of AI to delivering meaningful advances for patients."
About Iambic
Iambic's mission is to make better technology for better medicines. By utilizing molecular superintelligence — integrating proprietary AI with automated, scalable chemistry and biology experimentation — our platform is designed to address the totality of drug discovery and development. We believe the utility of our platform is demonstrated by the discovery of IAM1363, a novel drug candidate advanced to clinic in approximately two years, along with a diverse preclinical pipeline. Our leading AI technologies include Enchant and NeuralPLexer for multimodal endpoint prediction designed to improve the speed, precision, and success rates of drug discovery and clinical development. At Iambic, we are building an engine that is designed to power the future of medicine. | iambic.ai

Naintech to supply substrate transfer equipment for TCL CSOT's 8.6-Gen inkjet printed OLED line

Share on LinkedIn 

Korea-based equipment maker 
Naintech
 announced that it will supply substrate transfer equipment for 
TCL CSOT
's 8.6-Gen inkjet printed OLED mass production line in China. The equipment is supplied through LG Electronics, which signed a $18.7 million equipment supply contract with Naintech in June 2026.
Naintech's system is a material handling system made of transfer modules, load locks, buffers and conveyors. It moves the glass substrates between process tools (or holds them briefly) while keeping them under vacuum or a nitrogen atmosphere, and it is used in several OLED process steps, including inkjet printing and thin-film encapsulation (TFE).

Tar Heels in NFL: Maye throws 3 TD passes, one to Hollins but 3 INTs in Pats’ loss; Williams has 101 yards, TD&nbsp;&nbsp;Tar Heel Tribune

[page text]
By R.L. Bynum

Drake Maye threw three touchdown passes, including one to Mack Hollins on his return from injured reserve, but the New England Patriots lost 45–27 to the Green Bay Packers during a weekend that featured several strong performances from former UNC players.

Maye completed 21 of 38 passes for 299 yards with three touchdowns and three interceptions. His 74.3 passer rating was well below his season mark of 96.8.

Packers safety Xavier McKinney intercepted him twice, and safety Evan Williams picked him off once.

“Just throwing the football in spots that I don’t think I should’ve thrown it,” 
Maye said
, adding that he needed to locate the safeties on the first interception, put more distance on the second throw and see the defender jumping the crossing route on the third.

Maye threw a 30-yard touchdown pass to Rhamondre Stevenson with 9:23 left in the second quarter, an 8-yarder to Efton Chism III with 2:37 left in the quarter and a 22-yarder to Hollins with 1:11 left in the third. Maye has 2,282 yards (8.4 per attempt) on 178 of 272 passing with 18 touchdowns and 11 interceptions this season.

Hollins started after missing four games on injured reserve with a calf injury. He caught 3 of 6 targets for 36 yards and his first touchdown of the season, playing 43 offensive snaps.
Hollins said the coaches had prepared the team well and pointed to how one missed assignment can affect the whole offense.
“Either 11 guys do the right thing, or they don’t,” 
he said
.

Dallas running back Javonte Williams carried the ball 20 times for 101 yards (15 longest) and scored on a 1-yard run with 1:08 left to give the Cowboys a 27–23 win in Indianapolis. Williams also caught 4 of 5 targets for 23 yards and played 57 offensive snaps. He has 629 yards and nine touchdowns on 154 carries this season.

Two former Tar Heels were on the other sideline. Colts receiver Josh Downs started and caught 3 of 6 targets for 24 yards (9 longest) on 49 offensive snaps. Offensive lineman Charlie Heck, who signed Oct. 29, was inactive.

“I stunk today,” 
Downs said after dropping two passes
. “That’s the game of football sometimes. I didn’t catch those passes when I should have, and that’s that.”

Dallas rookie safety Caleb Downs said his older brother still got the better of him on some snaps. “He definitely got me on some of the plays that they missed him on,” 
Caleb Downs said
.

Houston safety M.J. Stewart recorded his first full sack of the season in the Texans’ 44–22 win over the Chargers. He finished with four solo tackles and one assist on 51 defensive snaps (71%) and has 1½ sacks in four games since returning from the physically unable to perform list on Oct. 10.

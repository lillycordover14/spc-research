Top-Ranked Gamecocks Dominate No. 12/11 Tar Heels

CHARLOTTE, N.C. (AP) —
 Chloe Kitts had 17 points and 10 rebounds and Joyce Edwards added 15 points as No. 1 South Carolina rolled past No. 12 North Carolina 84-39 at the Ally Tipoff on Sunday night.

Kitts had missed all of last season with a knee injury, but looked as energetic as ever in the leading the Gamecocks (2-0) to a lopsided victory over a second straight ranked opponent.

South Carolina topped No. 20 Maryland, 101-93, in Paris to start the season this past Monday. The Tar Heels (0-2) were there, too, falling to No. 21 Oklahoma, 91-79, in their game at the Oui-play double-header.

But it was the Gamecocks who caught fire in the second and third quarters to break things open. They outscored North Carolina 51-16, forced 17 turnovers and allowed just six baskets in those two periods.

It was South Carolina’s fifth straight win in the Carolina border rivalry.

Fifth-year forward Ashlyn Watkins had 10 rebounds, but was held scoreless. She returned to action after an ACL injury in January 2025. She sat out all last season. Watkins also had a team-high 10 boards in the opening win over the Terrapins.

Tessa Johnson had 12 points and freshman Jerzy Robinson had 10 for the Gamecocks.

Nyla Brooks had 13 points off four 3-pointers to lead the Tar Heels, who shot just 25% (15 of 60) for the game.

The Gamecocks will play in this Charlotte event the next two seasons, facing UConn here at the Charlotte Hornets home court in 2027 and then playing North Carolina State in 2028.

GAMECOCK NOTABLES

South Carolina’s 45-point win was its largest over a ranked opponent in program history, topping last season’s 93-50 win over then-No. 19 Tennessee on Feb. 8, 2026. It is the fourth 40-point win over a ranked team in program history, all coming in the last three seasons.

The Gamecocks’ win was their fifth straight over North Carolina, giving them the all-time series lead 12-11.

The Gamecocks’ 32 points in the third quarter was just one shy of their season-high single-quarter mark of 33 in the first quarter against #20/17 Maryland.

Fifth-year forward Chloe Kitts posted her first double-double of the season and 18th of her career, posting a team-high17 points on 7-of-12 shooting, and 10 rebounds in just 18 minutes of play. 11 of her 17 points came in the dominant third quarter.

In addition to an outstanding defensive performance that included three steals, junior Jordan Lee posted nine points, six rebounds and a team-high three assists – all in 22 minutes.

Three other Gamecocks joined her in double-figure scoring – Joyce Edwards 15, Tessa Johnson 12 and Jerzy Robinson 10.

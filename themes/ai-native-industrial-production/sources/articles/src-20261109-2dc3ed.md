Mestemaker on Davey O’Brien Great 8 list again
 November 09, 2026 
 | 
Cowboy Football
Share:
STILLWATER
 – Oklahoma State quarterback 
Drew Mestemaker
 is on the Davey O'Brien Great 8 list for a third time this season following his historic day in the win at Kansas State.

 

Mestemaker, who also received the honor after OSU's wins over West Virginia and Oregon, became the only power conference player over the last 30 seasons with four passing touchdowns, a rushing touchdown and a receiving touchdown in a road game.

 

He also became the first Big 12 player ever to accomplish the feat in any game and only the fifth player in FBS history to do it in any game. The other four were Terrelle Pryor (Ohio State) vs. Eastern Michigan in 2010, Jordan Lynch (Northern Illinois) vs. Eastern Michigan in 2013, Tyler Jones (Texas State) vs. Ohio in 2016 and Arch Manning (Texas) vs. Arkansas in 2025.

 

Mestemaker completed 22-of-28 passes for 313 yards with four touchdowns. He added three carries for 11 yards and a touchdown and one catch for five yards and a touchdown. He has thrown for at least 300 yards with multiple passing touchdowns in eight straight games to set the longest single-season streak in Big 12 history.

 

Mestemaker's eight straight 300-yard passing games set an Oklahoma State program record, breaking the tie he had with Mason Rudolph. He now holds the longest active FBS streaks of 300-yard passing games (eight) and 200-yard passing games (24).

 

On the season he ranks No. 1 in the FBS in passings touchdowns (29), No. 1 in points responsible for (204), No. 2 in passing yards (2,962) and No. 2 in total offense per game (348.8).

 

Season tickets are sold out to Oklahoma State football games this year, but there are still opportunities to get tickets to see the Cowboys in Stillwater. A small number of single-game tickets remain for the November 28 game against Kansas, but the November 15 game against Texas Tech is sold out.

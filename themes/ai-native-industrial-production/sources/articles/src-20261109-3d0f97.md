Boston Dynamics’ Atlas is even more nimble balancing on wheels.
Nearly a decade after 
introducing a robot called Handle
 that balanced on two wheels, Boston Dynamics has 
shared a new video of Atlas
 rolling through a simulated warehouse with the humanoid’s feet swapped for tires. It’s an impressive demo of the robot’s adaptability, and the wheels introduce a new quick side dash maneuver to 
Atlas’ skillset

Elizabeth Hurley
 has once again proved she isn't afraid to make a bold fashion statement, posing in a seriously daring black latex gown for a glamorous new photoshoot.
The 
61-year-old actress
 looked sensational in the figure-hugging creation, which featured a dramatically plunging neckline held together by just one tiny string.
The strapless design, by latex specialist Atsuko Kudo, clung to 
Elizabeth's enviable figure
, while its sculpted bust and glossy finish added an extra dose of drama.

© Instagram
Liz stunned in her latest photo shoot

Channeling old Hollywood glamour, the 
Austin Powers
 star draped a luxurious-looking white fur coat over her shoulders and accessorized with oversized black sunglasses, strappy heels and a glass of champagne.
Her brunette locks were styled in voluminous waves, while smoky eye makeup and a bold red manicure completed the striking look.
The photographs, shared on Instagram on Sunday, November 8, were taken by renowned fashion photographer Ellen von Unwerth, whom Elizabeth has worked with for more than three decades.

A very special reunion
Alongside the photographs, Elizabeth reflected on their long history together, revealing that the pair first met in the 1990s.
"Always a joy to work with @ellenvonunwerth," she wrote. "We met in the 90s and have shot together zillions of times since, including Vogue covers, Vanity Fair covers, Estée Lauder campaigns and, my favourite, in a @Versace bikini touching tongues with a python."

© Instagram
Liz's dress featured a daring split

The actress continued: "We've shot around the globe together: London, Paris, New York, Beverly Hills, the Hamptons, Miami, St Tropez, Vegas… but this latest shoot might be one of my favourites."
She signed off by teasing fans with the promise of "more coming soon."

Fans react
Unsurprisingly, Elizabeth's latest glamorous appearance prompted an enthusiastic response from her followers, who flooded the comments section with compliments.
"You Look INCREDIBLE in that Latex Dress!!" exclaimed one admirer, while another declared: "The most beautiful woman in the world!!!"
A third simply wrote: "Forever an icon," while another added: "Best shoot best dress ever!!!"
Elizabeth's 24-year-old son, Damian Hurley, was also among those showing their appreciation for the photographs, commenting: "Beautiful mama."

Your iPhone Photos Get Invisible AI Watermarks; How To Check

By Paulo Montenegro, on 11/09/2026 08:00 PST

 

Following the release of 
iOS 27.2
, Apple has quietly introduced an 
invisible watermarking feature
 for images generated or edited using 
artificial intelligence
 tools within the Photos app.
 

 

According to official documentation, the system automatically embeds a 
SynthID tag
 when users utilize specific 
Apple Intelligence
 capabilities, such as the Clean Up tool, Expand, Spatial Framing, Image Playground, and Image Wand. Consequently, both entirely new AI-generated creations and heavily edited authentic photos receive this hidden digital signature.
 

Image without AI manipulation

Importantly, the watermark remains completely imperceptible to the naked eye. Detection is only possible through specialized readers designed for the 
SynthID standard
. Users wishing to verify whether an image contains the tag can upload it to the 
synthid.com
 website for a diagnostic check.
 

Image expanded using iOS 27’s “Expand” feature, creating extra space with AI

Developed by Google, 
SynthID technology
 is designed to identify synthetic media by embedding a digital watermark directly into the content without compromising visual quality. However, it does possess limitations and cannot detect every instance of AI-assisted content.
 

This invisible watermarking capability is integrated across the 
Apple ecosystem
 starting with 
iOS 27.2
, iPadOS 27.2, macOS 27.2, and visionOS 27.2.

                Geno Smith completed 18-of-32 attempts for 162 yards in the Jets’ Week 9 loss to the Chiefs, adding one rush for eight yards. 
            

            
                The 36-year-old Smith was as immobile as ever, taking five sacks on the day and narrowly avoiding a half dozen others. Some of that was the Jets offensive line being overwhelmed against an elite KC pass rush; some of it was Smith refusing to get rid of the ball as the pocket collapsed. Smith playing without Garrett Wilson (concussion) was irate on the sideline in the fourth quarter with the Jets down multiple scores, barking at coaches and teammates alike. He had a negative EPA per drop back on the day. Smith should be seen as a matchup-dependent fantasy play. He will be streamable in Week 10 against a vulnerable Buffalo secondary.

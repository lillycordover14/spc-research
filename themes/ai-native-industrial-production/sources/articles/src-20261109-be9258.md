Official Cardiovascular Supplement of Texas Athletics is the first and only brand to be on every playing surface at a single university
AUSTIN, Texas 
—  As Texas Men's and Women's Basketball begin their 2026–27 seasons, Texas Athletics and Humann are excited to announce the addition of Humann branding to the basketball court at Moody Center. This completes the Austin-based cardiovascular health company's playing-surface integration across Texas Athletics facilities.

Moody Center installation is the final phase of playing-surface branding included in a landmark multiyear agreement announced in August 2025, when 
Humann was named the Official Cardiovascular Supplement of Texas Athletics
 in a sponsorship managed by Longhorn Sports Properties. The agreement called for Humann branding to appear alongside the Longhorn logo across every Texas Athletics field and court.

With the addition of Moody Center, Humann branding is now integrated throughout all 11 Texas Athletics venues, including football, baseball, softball, swimming and diving, volleyball, tennis, track and field, rowing, beach volleyball, men's and women's golf, and now basketball.

"Humann is a valued sponsor of Texas Athletics, and we're excited to welcome its brand to Moody Center court as the final piece of a comprehensive venue integration," said 
Chris Del Conte
, vice president and Lois and Richard Folger Athletics Director at The University of Texas. "Their longstanding commitment to our student-athletes and performance nutrition initiatives has made a meaningful impact across our programs, and this visibility reflects a sponsorship built on shared values and a pursuit of excellence."

For more than a decade, Humann has served as an integral component of Texas Athletics' Performance Nutrition program. The groundbreaking science behind Humann was born out of a University of Texas Health Science Center research discovery program. Longhorn student-athletes and coaches use Humann products to support cardiovascular health and performance. Humann also powers Longhorn Nation with SuperBeets®, the #1 cardiologist recommended beet brand for heart health support.

The court branding builds on Humann's recent work with Texas Athletics, including a 
Heart Health Month activation at Moody Center in February 2026
 that highlighted cardiovascular health in action during select men's and women's basketball games, and a 
national campaign launched in October 2026 with Texas quarterback Arch Manning and his mother, Ellen Manning
. The campaign focused on supporting healthy blood pressure levels with Humann's SuperBeets products.

"Humann's mission is to change the trajectory of cardiovascular health," said Joel Kocher, CEO and co-founder of Humann. "That mission began with research at The University of Texas Health Science Center. Bringing our brand to Moody Center completes something special, the most trusted cardiovascular brand on every University of Texas playing surface."

"We couldn't be prouder of our sponsorship with Humann and the impact their cardiovascular health products are making with our student-athletes, fans, and alumni," said Lucas Motta, vice president, Longhorn Sports Properties. "Showcasing Humann's brand on our men's and women's basketball court is another powerful way to amplify their mission of advancing cardiovascular health and powering while strengthening their presence across Texas Athletics."

Additional information about the sponsorship between Texas Athletics and Humann can be found at 
TexasLonghorns.com/Humann
.

The Bills have made a few moves in advance of Monday night’s matchup with the Vikings.
Buffalo announced that the club has placed receiver Joshua Palmer on injured reserve.
He’s been sidelined by a hamstring injury and will now miss at least four games.
Palmer has registered four catches for 117 yards with two touchdowns so far in 2026. He’s been on the field for six games.
The Bills have also elevated safety Geno Stone and receiver Trent Sherfield for the matchup against the Vikings.

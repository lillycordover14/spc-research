Mujin Opens M1 Experience Center in Georgia to Showcase AI-Powered Robotic Automation&nbsp;&nbsp;citybiz

[page text]
Mujin Corp.
, a provider of intelligent robotic automation technology, has opened its 
M1 Experience Center
 in Suwanee, Georgia, establishing a new demonstration and training facility designed to help manufacturers, warehouse operators and technology partners evaluate AI-powered automation solutions.

The company celebrated the facility’s grand opening on November 5, 2026, welcoming customers, industry leaders, business partners and community representatives to experience its robotic systems in operation.

Located outside Atlanta, the M1 Experience Center features live demonstrations of palletizing, depalletizing, picking and material handling applications powered by MujinOS, the company’s intelligent automation platform.

MujinOS combines robotics control, AI-driven software and real-time digital twin technology to coordinate robots, products and connected systems within automated facilities.

The platform is designed to enable warehouse and manufacturing operations to adapt robotic workflows to changing production requirements, product configurations and material handling demands.

“Automation is far easier to understand when you can see it operating in a real production environment,” said 
Ross Diankov
, global CEO of Mujin. “The M1 Experience Center allows visitors to experience firsthand how Mujin combines physical AI and intelligent robotic orchestration to provide a practical path from concept to deployment.”

The facility is intended to serve as a collaborative environment where prospective customers can evaluate automation technologies before committing to deployment.

Visitors can observe robotic applications in operation, consult with technical specialists and participate in workshops and training programs focused on integrating intelligent automation into existing facilities.

The center also provides a dedicated venue for proof-of-concept discussions, allowing manufacturers and distribution operators to assess how specific robotic systems could address their operational requirements.

Mujin’s technology emphasizes a software-first approach to automation, using intelligent orchestration and digital modeling to coordinate robotic movements and material handling processes.

By combining physical AI with real-time digital twins, the company aims to improve the flexibility of robotic systems operating in complex industrial environments.

The M1 Experience Center also supports Mujin’s network of technology and integration partners, providing a location where they can introduce customers to automation applications and collaborate on potential projects.

“Opening the M1 Experience Center represents an important milestone for Mujin and our growing ecosystem of customers and partners,” said 
Mario D’Cruz
, vice president of strategy and marketing.

“By creating a space where organizations can see automation in action, collaborate with experts, and explore practical solutions to their operational challenges, we’re helping accelerate the adoption of intelligent robotics across the industry.”

The opening comes as manufacturers and logistics operators increasingly evaluate robotic automation to address labor-intensive workflows, improve operational consistency and accommodate changing fulfillment requirements.

Applications such as automated palletizing, depalletizing and item picking are particularly relevant to distribution centers and industrial facilities managing high volumes of products with varying sizes, shapes and handling requirements.

Mujin’s approach combines robotics, AI-based controls, digital twin technology, system integration and ongoing operational support to help customers deploy and manage automated material handling systems.

The company said the M1 Experience Center will remain open year-round for demonstrations, customer meetings, partner collaboration, industry forums and training activities.

Organizations can arrange customized demonstrations and technical consultations to evaluate potential applications of Mujin’s automation technology.

Mujin did not disclose the facility’s development cost, square footage or staffing levels.

The new center expands the company’s customer engagement infrastructure in the United States, providing a dedicated environment for demonstrating physical AI applications and helping industrial customers move robotic automation projects from initial evaluation toward deployment.

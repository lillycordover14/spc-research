CHESTERFIELD COUTY, Va. — Drivers traveling south on Interstate 95 through Chesterfield County should prepare for an overnight closure Monday into Tuesday morning as crews repair pavement damaged following a crash involving a truck hauling tar.
VDOT said I-95 south will close at mile marker 58 near Route 620, Woods Edge Road, beginning at 8 p.m. Monday, Nov. 9, and remain closed until 6 a.m. Tuesday.
The closure will allow crews to complete required road work following the crash earlier Monday near mile marker 57.9.

Traffic will be detoured off I-95 south at Exit 58 and directed along Woods Edge Road, Route 1 (Boulevard) and Route 144 (Temple Avenue). VDOT advised drivers to consider alternate routes, including Interstate 295, and expect delays.
Virginia State Police said the two-vehicle crash happened at about 4:55 a.m. Monday on I-95 south near Ruffin Mill Road.
Police said their preliminary investigation showed the driver of a sedan was attempting to change lanes when the car struck a truck hauling tar.
"The impact caused the truck to overturn, spilling tar onto the roadway," a Virginia State Police spokesperson said. "Both drivers were transported to an area hospital for treatment of non-life-threatening injuries."
The crash caused significant traffic delays Monday as crews worked to clear the wreckage and spilled tar from the interstate.
"When I pulled out and got on Jeff Davis Highway, traffic was everywhere," Anthony Bailey said.
Bailey knew he wasn't alone after seeing a long line of cars.

Traffic also backed up on the Boulevard in Colonial Heights as drivers heading south on I-95 took exits to work their way toward Route 1 and around the crash.
VDOT had to mill the damaged pavement as a safety precaution, forcing the southbound lanes to close.
"We tried to take a different route, but it was like an extra 30 minutes just to try and get out here, before we were even on I-95 yet. It's insane," said Caiden German, who was heading northbound to the VA hospital.
Other drivers told CBS 6 they were stuck in traffic for as long as three hours.
Some businesses, including an Exxon in Colonial Heights, saw additional customers as travelers stopped for gas. For locals like Bailey, the backups turned normally short commutes into much longer trips.
"Normally I ride down through here, takes you 5 or 10 minutes. It's taken 20-30 minutes now due to the traffic being chaotic out here," Bailey said.
Again, the southbound lanes reopened Monday afternoon, but they will close again at 8 p.m. at Exit 58 so crews can repave the damaged section of interstate.
VDOT expects I-95 south to fully reopen by 6 a.m. Tuesday.

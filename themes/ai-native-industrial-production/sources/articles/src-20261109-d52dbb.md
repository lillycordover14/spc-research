Mujin Celebrates Grand Opening of M1 Experience Center

Mujin Corp., a leader in intelligent robotic automation, celebrated the grand opening of its M1 Experience Center on Thursday, Nov. 5, welcoming customers, partners, industry leaders, and local community members to its new technology facility in Suwanee, Georgia. The event marked the official launch of a destination where visitors can experience firsthand how Mujin's physical AI-driven approach to warehouse automation is transforming material handling operations.

This press release features multimedia. View the full release here: 
https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261109846572/en/
Mujin CEO Ross Diankov officially opens the M1 experience center.

The M1 Experience Center, now open daily, showcases live automation applications powered by MujinOS, the company's intelligent automation platform that orchestrates robots, products, and connected systems through a real-time digital twin. During the grand opening, guests explored palletizing, depalletizing, picking, and material handling workflows while seeing how software-first automation enables facilities to adapt quickly to changing operational requirements.

"Automation is far easier to understand when you can see it operating in a real production environment," said Ross Diankov, Global CEO of Mujin. "The M1 Experience Center allows visitors to experience firsthand how Mujin combines physical AI and intelligent robotic orchestration to provide a practical path from concept to deployment."

Designed as a collaborative hub for manufacturers, customers, and partners, the M1 Experience Center offers live demonstrations, expert consultations, workshops, training programs, and customer events. The grand opening also provided Mujin's partners with an opportunity to experience the new facility and explore how it can help accelerate customer engagement. By offering a dedicated space for demonstrations, proof-of-concept discussions, and hands-on experiences, the center enables partners to introduce customers to automation solutions more quickly and move projects from evaluation to deployment faster.

"Opening the M1 Experience Center represents an important milestone for Mujin and our growing ecosystem of customers and partners," said Mario D'Cruz, Vice President of Strategy and Marketing. "By creating a space where organizations can see automation in action, collaborate with experts, and explore practical solutions to their operational challenges, we're helping accelerate the adoption of intelligent robotics across the industry. We're equally excited to provide our partners with a destination where they can bring customers to experience automation firsthand and discover new opportunities to innovate together."

The M1 Experience Center will serve as a year-round hub for demonstrations, customer engagements, partner collaboration, industry forums, and training programs. Organizations interested in visiting the center or scheduling a customized demonstration can email 
connect@mujin-corp.com

About Mujin Corp.

Mujin provides intelligent robotic automation solutions that combine advanced robotics, AI-driven software, real-time digital twin technology, controls, integration, and lifecycle support. Through platforms such as MujinOS and application-specific solutions for material handling and palletizing, Mujin helps manufacturers and distribution operations achieve greater flexibility, productivity, and operational performance. To see our robots in action, visit our M1 Experience Center just outside Atlanta, Georgia.

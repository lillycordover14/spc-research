MP Materials rises as Jefferies highlights rare earth production growth and magnet ramp-up 

Published: 
14:13 09 Nov 2026 EST
  

MP Materials Corp (NYSE:MP) shares climbed nearly 5% on Monday as investors responded to an upbeat assessment from Jefferies following the rare earth producer’s third-quarter results last Friday. 

The company reported adjusted earnings per share of $0.04, beating Jefferies’ estimate of a $0.03 loss and consensus expectations of a $0.02 loss. 

Revenue jumped 176% year over year to approximately $148 million, driven by higher neodymium-praseodymium (NdPr) sales volumes and stronger pricing. Results also included a roughly $32 million sale of stockpiled heavy rare earth material to a US aerospace and defence customer. 

Adjusted EBITDA reached $41 million, compared with a loss of $13 million a year earlier. 

Jefferies highlighted a 41% increase in NdPr production to a quarterly record of approximately 1,000 metric tonnes, with monthly run-rates exceeding 450 tonnes at times during the quarter. 

The broker expects production momentum to continue, forecasting average monthly NdPr sales of 333 tonnes in the fourth quarter, rising to 417 tonnes in 2027 and 542 tonnes from 2028. 

Commercial-scale dysprosium and terbium production is also expected to begin in the fourth quarter. 

Another potential catalyst is MP Materials’ magnetics business. The company completed qualification during the third quarter and expects commercial magnet deliveries to begin in the current quarter, with volumes accelerating in the second half of 2027. 

Jefferies expects the full earnings contribution from magnets to materialise in 2028. 

The broker also pointed to progress at 10X, MP Materials’ second US magnet plant, which is expected to add approximately 7,000 tonnes of annual capacity and enter commissioning in late 2028. The facility is now 100% contracted, helping reduce the commercial risk of the expansion. 

MP Materials has spent approximately $414 million on capital expenditure year to date, with around 60% directed towards its magnetics operations. Prepayments are expected to provide a further $120 million in funding over two years as project milestones are achieved. 

Jefferies raised its 2026 revenue forecast by 8%, although it trimmed its 2027 earnings estimate by 7%.

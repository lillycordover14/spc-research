CHESTERFIELD, Va. (WWBT) - Virginia State Police is investigating a two-vehicle crash that shut down southbound I-95 on Monday morning.
Police say the crash happened around 4:55 a.m. November 9, on I-95 South near Ruffin Mill Road (Exit 58) in Chesterfield County.
A preliminary investigation shows what a car was trying to change lanes when it hit a truck carrying tar.
The crash caused the truck to overturn and spill tar on the road.
Both drivers were taken to the hospital and are expected to be OK.
Overnight repairs
The Virginia Department of Transportation says southbound I-95 will be closed near Woods Edge Road starting at 8 p.m. Monday to repair the interstate. 
“Motorists are advised to use alternate routes, including I-295, and to expect delays in the area,” VDOT said. “Traffic will be detoured off of I-95 south at Exit 58, using Woods Edge Road, Route 1 (Boulevard), and Route 144 (Temple Avenue).”
The interstate is expected to open at 6 a.m.

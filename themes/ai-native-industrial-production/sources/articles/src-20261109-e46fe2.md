Iambic Therapeutics wins Bank of America backing with 194% upside forecast

Published: 
13:15 09 Nov 2026 EST
  

Bank of America has initiated coverage of Iambic Therapeutics with a 'buy' rating and a $30 price target, implying approximately 194% upside.

The broker sees potential in the biotechnology company's 
artificial intelligence
 platform, which designs new drug molecules using proprietary models and automated chemistry.

Unlike 
AI drug developers
 focused on identifying disease targets, Iambic applies the technology directly to designing medicines, with the aim of shortening development times and reducing costs.

Bank of America views drug development as a promising commercial application of artificial intelligence, rather than an infrastructure investment dependent on expanding computing capacity.

The broker also considers Iambic undervalued relative to listed peers Recursion Pharmaceuticals and Schrödinger, supporting its positive assessment of the shares.

Its lead candidate, IAM1363, targets HER2-driven breast, lung and other solid tumours, including cancers that have spread to the brain.

Clinical data presented at the 2026 European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) congress showed tumours shrank in 36% of heavily pretreated patients, or 19 of 53 participants.

The drug also demonstrated brain penetration and tolerability, although the relatively small dataset and early development stage limit the conclusions.

Iambic expects to advance IAM1363 into phase 3 trials next year, with further clarity on trial design likely to help establish the programme's commercial potential.

Bank of America also highlighted more than $2 billion of investment from pharmaceutical partners Takeda, AbbVie, Bayer and Revolution Medicines as evidence of industry interest.

These partnerships provide potential milestone payments and generate clinical data that can strengthen Iambic's drug discovery platform and inform its internal research pipeline.

Valuation and development risks

Bank of America's $30 price target uses a sum-of-the-parts valuation, assigning $8 per share to IAM1363, based on a 40% probability of success and $1.6 billion in peak sales.

The broker attributes another $4 per share to the AI platform, $5 to IAM217 and IAM-C1, and $5 to other pipeline assets and partnerships.

The remaining $8 per share reflects Iambic's net cash position, while the broker's model implies approximately $6.6 billion in combined peak sales across its programmes.

Potential catalysts include regulatory discussions over IAM1363, further clinical data, initial updates on IAM217, an investigational new drug filing for IAM-C1 and additional pharmaceutical partnerships.

However, the platform remains expensive to develop, with Bank of America forecasting research and development spending rising from $131 million in 2026 to $341 million in 2029.

Management expects its post-IPO cash resources to fund operations into the fourth quarter of 2028, although the broker anticipates further fundraising from 2028.

The broker also cautioned that Iambic has yet to demonstrate its technology can repeatedly deliver successful drug candidates, with IAM217 and IAM-C1 only beginning clinical development.

Other risks include relatively small clinical datasets, trials without control groups and competition in the established HER2 drug market.

Bank of America believes successful clinical execution and further partnership milestones could help reduce these uncertainties and support its valuation.

Boutique firm Laminar IP has expanded its Southeast Asian footprint with the launch of an office in Singapore.

The office will be led by partner Wai Yeng Chan and comes months after the Australia-headquartered firm established a physical presence in Thailand in June.

“We have seen growing demand from clients, alongside increasing interest in doing business in Singapore, from established Asian markets including Korea, Japan and China,” founding partner Mark Teoh told 
Asia Business Law Journal
.

“Southeast Asia is rich in opportunity. We have been watching the region closely over a number of  years, and our business is now in a position to expand into those markets.

“We opened our Bangkok office in June 2026, and Singapore is the next step in building our regional presence. Our aim is to provide globally streamlined IP services to our clients.

“By expanding into Southeast Asia, we will be able to provide direct IP filing and prosecution services in Thailand and Singapore, combining local expertise with the streamlined model we have established in Australia: clear advice; transparent processes; and cost-effective service.”

The Singapore office began operations on 4 November 2026, and Teoh said headcount would grow in accordance with the needs of the business.

Even after opening two offices this year, Laminar IP still has expansion plans in mind.

“We see potential to expand further across Southeast Asia over time, but this will be guided by client demand, the needs of the business, and business opportunities,” said Teoh.

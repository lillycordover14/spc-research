CloudNC raises $20M to automate manufacturing’s most pressing bottlenecks
CloudNC, a UK-based startup specializing in manufacturing software, has successfully raised $20 million in a B extension funding round, boosting its total funding to $128 million since its inception. Co-founder and CEO Theo Saville, during an interview with TechCrunch, highlighted that the company last secured major funding four years ago.

Founded in 2015 alongside Chris Emery, the current chief science officer, CloudNC focuses on enhancing Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machining through its AI-driven CAM Assist software. This innovative tool automates critical parts of the CNC process, which is essential for producing precise components used across automotive, defense, and consumer electronics sectors.

Saville explained that prior to machining a part, decisions regarding how to hold the part and which tools to utilize must be made. While traditional CAM software assists in some aspects of this planning, it often requires manual input for execution strategies. "Traditional CAM systems are powerful, but they still necessitate manual programming to specify machining processes," Saville noted.

In contrast, CAM Assist integrates with established CAM systems such as Autodesk Fusion and Mastercam, streamlining the decision-making process for machinists and programmers. It automates the selection of tools, approach directions, and cutting parameters, subsequently generating the necessary code for CNC machines. Users can then review and refine these suggestions before approval.

The aim of CAM Assist is not to replace expert judgment but to enhance the efficiency and productivity of skilled workers. Saville likens CAM Assist to an expert assistant that automates initial calculations and repetitive tasks, enabling human operators to focus on refining and approving processes.

Currently, more than 1,000 machine shops across the globe utilize CAM Assist, with a significant 80% of the customer base located in the United States. With a workforce of 80, CloudNC is eager to expand its market presence. Saville emphasized the importance of this funding round for scaling operations and capitalizing on new market opportunities.

The funding round saw participation from several investors, including Nimble Ventures, which led the investment, along with Calculus Venture Capital, Entrepreneur First, and LM Capital, the venture arm of Lockheed Martin. This investment will facilitate the broader adoption of CAM Assist, enhance market strategies, and aid in the development of new products like Quote Agent.

Quote Agent is designed to help manufacturers quickly evaluate the costs and risks associated with new projects, allowing for faster decision-making. Saville connected this need to the current geopolitical landscape, noting how U.S. machine shops are increasingly looking to reshore operations amidst a skilled labor shortage. "Machine shops must quote, program, and deliver more efficiently with their existing workforce and machinery," he stated, adding that Quote Agent is set to launch next month.

"There are countless methods to machine a typical CNC part, and we have dedicated years to tackling these complexities through our own factory and software development," Saville concluded, reflecting on the journey that has led CloudNC to its current standing in the industry.

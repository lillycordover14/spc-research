Art and sport may seem worlds apart, but they share more than one might expect.

That connection will be the focus of a lecture by art historian Serge Legat, a professor at 
IESA Paris
, on November 16 in Monaco.

Organized by the 
Association Monégasque pour la Connaissance des Arts
 (AMCA), the event will also provide an opportunity to officially celebrate the association’s 40th anniversary.

The lecture will explore what art and sport share : movement, emotion, and the drive to push beyond one’s limits.

When Sport Inspires Art

From painting and sculpture to photography and performance art, artists have long sought to capture the energy of sport and the movement of the human body.

Both disciplines combine technical skill with creativity, sensitivity, and the pursuit of excellence.

Their connection also raises broader questions about the human body, physical performance, and the role of aesthetics in athletic achievement.

Every athletic achievement also has a human and aesthetic dimension.

From athletes depicted in ancient art to the footballers painted by Nicolas de Staël, sport has provided artists with a lasting source of inspiration.

The relationship between art and sport will remain a central theme throughout AMCA’s season, with three additional lectures planned to explore the subject from different perspectives.

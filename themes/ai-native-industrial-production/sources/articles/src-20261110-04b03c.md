Searches were made on three streets, as well as one on a boat at Hull Marina [Getty Images]
Twelve people have been arrested in a series of police raids across Hull as part of a crackdown on organised drugs crime.
Searches took place at Wenlock Street, Bentley Street, Constable Street and a boat docked at the city's marina, Humberside Police said.
Three men, aged 50, 45 and 31, two 38-year-old men, two 22-year-old men and a 44-year-old woman were arrested on suspicion of being concerned in the supply of class A drugs and remain in police custody. The four other suspects were released on conditional bail.

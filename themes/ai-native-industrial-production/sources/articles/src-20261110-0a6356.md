The Argument You Would Have Been Weighing Up On MP Materials
 
The shares cost US$58.6 at the start of the period, and the key question for MP Materials was whether rare earths would cement pricing power or see it chipped away.
 
The bullish story pointed to a Fair Value of US$85, a price implied by assumptions that revenue would climb 77.0% a year and that profit margins would lift to 28.2% within three years.
 
The bearish case anchored on a Fair Value of US$30, based on 60.1% annual revenue growth, but with concern that rapid recycling and new supply could pressure pricing power despite a 27.8% margin assumption.

What The Results Changed For The MP Materials Debate
 
The big swing factor was MP Materials lifting total revenue from US$53.6 million in Q3 2025 to US$155.8 million in Q3 2026, while net margin improved from a loss of 78.0% to a loss of 13.3%. That direction of travel supported the optimistic case on scale and efficiency. Profit remained negative, so the strong margin assumptions in both fair value stories stayed unproven. Overall, the evidence cut both ways.
 
The takeaway for any other stock is simple. When a thesis relies on rapid margin expansion, track the reported net margin line each year and ask whether the pace and direction actually match the story being presented.
 
What MP Materials' Lower Price Now Asks You To Believe
 
MP Materials trades at US$37.75 today, and the analyst consensus Narrative on Simply Wall St places its Fair Value above that level, based on a view that contracted magnet and rare earth cash flows are not fully reflected in the quote.
 
The distinct pitch is that long term government backed and OEM linked agreements could turn MP Materials' magnet and heavy rare earth ramp into more stable earnings. A buyer today needs to judge whether those high margin magnet volumes and related contracts actually materialise at the scale this Narrative assumes.
 
"Government backed support for MP Materials through the minority equity stake, NdPr price floor and minimum EBITDA guarantees tied to the 10X magnet campus, alongside the plan to bring 10X into production by the end of 2028, provides visibility on future magnet cash flows and could make long term revenue and earnings less sensitive to rare earth price swings."

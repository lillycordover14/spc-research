UGG and Aime Leon Dore announce their upcoming debut footwear collaboration featuring the UGG ALD Slipper

The full-grain leather moccasin silhouette highlights debossed branding the Queens Crest and two seasonal colorways

A campaign starring Method Man and Redman previews the release ahead of its November 12 launch

Footwear imprint 
UGG
 and New York label 
Aimé Leon Dore
 have announced their upcoming debut collaboration, introducing the UGG ALD Slipper. Fronted by hip-hop icons 
Method Man
 and 
Redman
, the inaugural collaborative silhouette fuses UGG’s heritage of effortless ease with Aimé Leon Dore’s sophisticated approach to contemporary streetwear essentials.

Marking a major partnership between the West Coast lifestyle stalwart and the Queens-based label, the forthcoming footwear announcement centers on a campaign filmed on a Harlem stoop. Starring longtime friends Method Man and Redman wearing the new footwear, the campaign captures intimate, unscripted dialogues where the duo reminisce about family, shared histories, and life lived as cultural pioneers. Their mutual respect, distinct sense of identity, and authenticity directly reflect the core ethos uniting both brands.

Constructed from premium full-grain leather, the UGG ALD Slipper reimagines a timeless moccasin silhouette engineered to transition smoothly between indoor home lounging and urban outdoor wear. Subtle collaborative iconography highlights the leather upper, combining debossed UGG branding with Aimé Leon Dore’s signature Queens Crest emblem. The collaborative shoe delivers a considered focus on craftsmanship, arriving in two rich, seasonal colorways: “Dark Coffee Bean” and “Goldenrod”.

The UGG ALD Slipper officially launches on November 12, 2026, at 12 p.m. EST.

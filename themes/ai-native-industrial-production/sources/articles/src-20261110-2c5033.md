Obituary

Gene Oswald Collins, Jr., affectionately known to family and friends as “Geno” was born on October 17, 1972, in Abilene, Texas to Joann and Gene Collins, Sr. On November 10, 2026, he was called home, leaving a legacy of faith, love, generosity, laughter, and unforgettable memories for his family and friends. He moved to Odessa, Texas with his family and was educated in the ECISD system at Sam Houston Elementary, Hood Jr. High School, Permian High School, and Odessa College. He was baptized at an early age in the Highland and Bunche Church of Christ and loved serving the church as a song and worship leader and enjoyed attended National Youth Conferences, Church Camps and all other church activities.
While in school, he, his cousin Raynard, and several other young men formed the rap group “Lethal Weapon” which was a favorite for the young generation with sold out crowds throughout West Texas including Juneteenth and Martin Luther King, Jr. Celebrations.
As a 
Red Cross
 trained lifeguard, he worked for the City of Odessa Supervising the Woodson Aquatic Center and other special functions.
He was employed with Gray Communications as a tele/journalist for over 20 years having worked in the Odessa/Midland area, San Antonio, TX, area, and in the Atlanta, Georgia area covering some of the most prolific stories in the nation. He was also a business partner with Nsand Muzic LLC.
He leaves to mourn his passing his parents, Gene and Joann Collins of Odessa, Texas, his daughter Tytiana Collins, and granddaughter Kaylani Collins of Santa Marie, California, his brother Vernon Russell Collins (Angelica), His sister April Kathlene Emmers (Harold); Uncle Hubert Raynard Collins(Debra), His Aunt Sheila Johnson of Corpus Christi, Texas, Cousins Hubert Raynard Collins, Jr. (Jonell),Dr. Demetrius Ray Collins ( Dr. Kristel Collins ), Jesse Houston, Aimee Adell Johnson, Marcus Johnson (Gladys),his nieces and nephews Josie Collins, Isabella Collins, Elijah Emmers, Ava Emmers, and Harold Emmers IV, and many, many more cousins and friends.
Visitation is set for Monday November 23, 2006, from 1pm-9pm at Martinez Funeral Home chapel.
Celebration of life service is set for 11am November 24, 2026, at 6TH and Jackson Church of Christ with Dr. Jerry Taylor presenting the eulogy.
Burial will follow at Sunset Memorial Gardens 6801 E -BL Odessa, Tx. 79761.

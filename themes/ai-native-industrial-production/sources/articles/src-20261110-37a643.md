NH-Amundi Asset Management
NH-Amundi Asset Management is launching an exchange-traded fund focused on the multilayer ceramic capacitor (MLCC) and package substrate value chain, where demand is surging as artificial intelligence systems advance. The fund fixes Samsung Electro-Mechanics (009150.KS), a core manufacturer, at a 25% weighting and spreads the rest across nine leading Korean substrate, materials and equipment companies selected using a large language model.
NH-Amundi Asset Management, led by Chief Executive Kil Jung-sup, said on the 10th that it had listed the HANARO Samsung Electro-Mechanics Fixed AI Core Substrate ETF on the main board of the Korea Exchange. The product targets the market for MLCCs and high-value semiconductor package substrates such as flip-chip ball grid arrays (FC-BGA), components used in growing volumes as AI servers become more powerful. Samsung Electro-Mechanics, the fund's anchor holding, mass-produces both MLCCs and FC-BGAs. It receives a fixed 25% allocation in the portfolio. If its actual weighting drifts with share-price moves, the fund rebalances back to 25% at quarterly scheduled reviews.
The remaining 75% is spread across nine core stocks that make up the AI substrate ecosystem. The fund uses an AI system based on a large language model to analyze corporate filings, news reports and analyst research, calculating how closely each company is tied to the MLCC and substrate industries. The top nine are selected based on a combination of market capitalization and that thematic relevance score. To prevent concentration in any single stock, individual holdings are capped at 20%.
The eligible universe covers makers of semiconductor package substrates and multilayer boards (MLB), as well as suppliers of materials and equipment essential to substrate production. As of the end of October, the largest holdings after Samsung Electro-Mechanics were LG Innotek (011070.KS), Doosan (000150.KS), Isu Petasys and Daeduck Electronics (008060.KS), followed by Simmtech, Korea Circuit, Taesung, TLB and Haesung DS. "As the computing performance of AI accelerators and high-performance servers improves dramatically, high-capacity MLCCs and ultra-fine, high-performance substrates that keep power efficiency stable are becoming increasingly important," said Kim Seung-chul, head of the ETF investment division at NH-Amundi Asset Management. "This will be an optimal product that allows one-stop investment across the entire domestic AI substrate value chain, anchored by Samsung Electro-Mechanics, which has global competitiveness."

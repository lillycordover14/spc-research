Shares tied to domestic semiconductor substrates are rising across the board early in the session, lifted by a surge in U.S. semiconductor stocks.
According to the Korea Exchange (KRX) on the 10th, as of 9:40 a.m., 
Korea Circuit(007810)
 was trading at 68,900 won, up 5,700 won (9.02%) from the previous session on the main bourse.
Other semiconductor substrate makers are also strong. 
ISU Petasys(007660)
 is up 7.27%, 
Daeduck Electronics(353200)
 is up 7.31%, and 
SIMMTECH(222800)
 is up 7.41%.
The gains are seen as stemming from buying focused on tech stocks on the New York market overnight. On the 9th (local time), the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 2.15% from the previous session, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq gained 2.35% and 2.74%, respectively.
The rebound in semiconductors stood out as the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index jumped 6.85%. Among individual names, AMD climbed more than 13%, and Broadcom rose nearly 9%. As U.S. chip stocks that had plunged recently rebounded, buying appears to be flowing into domestic semiconductor substrate shares.

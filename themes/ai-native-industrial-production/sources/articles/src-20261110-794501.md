Intellectual property firm Laminar IP has opened an office in Singapore and appointed Wai Yeng Chan, a former patent attorney at local firm Ghows, as a partner to lead its expansion in Southeast Asia.
The Singapore office adds to Laminar IP's headquarters in Sydney and its Bangkok office, extending the firm's regional presence across the Asia-Pacific region.
Laminar IP said the new office would allow it to provide locally informed advice and coordinated intellectual property services to clients and professional colleagues managing portfolios across the Asia-Pacific region.
"Singapore is a strategically important jurisdiction for businesses seeking to grow across Southeast Asia and protect their intellectual property in the region. Its vibrant innovation ecosystem and well-established IP framework make it an important consideration in regional business plans," said Mark Teoh, Laminar IP's founding partner, in a statement.
Chan is a Singapore patent attorney with more than 16 years of experience advising on the protection and enforcement of intellectual property rights. Before joining Ghows in 2022, she led intellectual property strategy at Taylor Vinters Via LLC.
Her experience includes patent work involving computer-implemented inventions, mechanical systems, semiconductor test equipment, electrical appliances, and building and construction technologies. She has drafted and prosecuted patent applications through grant and managed intellectual property registrations across Southeast Asia.
Chan has also managed an intellectual property team in Myanmar, contributed to the South-East Asia IP SME Helpdesk, and worked as an external expert for the World Intellectual Property Organization on research into potential patent-system convergence among ASEAN member states.
Laminar IP advises clients ranging from individual inventors and research organisations to small and medium-sized enterprises and multinational companies on patents, designs and trademarks.

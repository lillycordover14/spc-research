Jets Got Trade Calls On Geno Smith But Are More Likely To Re-Sign Him In 2027&nbsp;&nbsp;NFLTradeRumors.co

[page text]
Jets

According to the Athletic’s 
Zack Rosenblatt
, the Jets have received some trade calls for QB 
Geno Smith
 in the last week or so due to how well he’s been playing. 

However, Rosenblatt doesn’t expect the Jets to trade Smith unless it’s for some kind of exorbitant return. He adds there’s a strong chance New York brings Smith back in 2027 because he’s an ideal bridge quarterback if they end up taking a first-round prospect. 

Despite New York’s 2-7 record, Smith is playing well and is actually having one of the best seasons for a Jets quarterback in a while.

Smith, 36, is a former second-round pick of the Jets back in 2013 out of West Virginia. He played out the final year of his four-year, $5.019 million contract before signing a one-year contract worth $2 million with the Giants in 2017.

Smith signed a one-year deal with the Chargers for the 2018 season before joining the Seahawks in 2019. He returned on one-year deals each of the following three seasons before being named the starter in 2022. 

He signed a three-year extension with the Seahawks worth $105 million in March of 2023. Smith was in the final year of his deal and set to make a $14.8 million base salary in 2025 when the Seahawks traded him to the Raiders for a third-round pick. He then signed a new two-year, $75 million extension. 

However, the Raiders traded him after just one season to the Jets, with the two sides reworking his deal as a part of the trade to make him an unrestricted free agent in 2027. 

In 2026, Smith has appeared in nine games for the Jets and completed 67.5 percent of his passes for 2,078 yards, 12 touchdowns and three interceptions.

[page text]
By R.L. Bynum

Coby White had six assists in his first three games this season. He handed out nine in 20 minutes Monday night and scored a season-high 10 points as Charlotte snapped a six-game losing streak with a 124–112 home win over Cleveland.

The former Carolina star led both teams in assists against only three turnovers. He shot 3-for-12 from the floor, missed all four of his 3-point attempts, and made 4-of-5 free throws. The Hornets made 23 of 46 3-point attempts and led by as many as 33 before Cleveland cut the margin to eight with 3:47 left.

White is averaging 7.0 points and 3.8 assists through four games since missing the entire preseason and the first seven regular-season games with a left calf injury. Charlotte opens a four-game road trip Thursday at Phoenix.

In other NBA action involving former UNC players on Monday:

—
Walker Kessler
 posted his second double-double in as many nights with 21 points and 10 rebounds in the Lakers’ 124–114 win at Atlanta. He went 8-for-12 from the floor and 5-for-6 at the free-throw line, adding four assists, a steal and a block in 30 minutes.
— 
Harrison Barnes
 scored 11 points and grabbed two rebounds in 21 minutes off the bench as San Antonio won 122–96 at Phoenix. He made two 3-pointers and all three free throws, and the Spurs outscored the Suns by 22 points with him on the floor.
— 
Cameron Johnson
 scored eight points with two rebounds, an assist and two steals in Denver’s 110–100 loss at Houston.

Nominal Raises $240M Series C to Expand AI-Powered Hardware Engineering Platform&nbsp;&nbsp;citybiz

[page text]
Nominal CEO Cameron McCord.

Austin, Texas-based 
Nominal
 
has 
raised $240 million in a Series C funding round
 at a $3 billion valuation, led by 
Lightspeed Venture Partners
 and co-led by 
Insight Partners
. Other participating investors include 
Lux Capital, Sequoia, General Catalyst, Founders Fund, Avenir, Salesforce Ventures, M13, Lockheed Martin Ventures, Red Glass Ventures, PSP Growth and Deployment Capital.

The company’s hardware intelligence platform connects engineering data with the institutional knowledge behind complex physical systems. Its platform is designed for engineering organizations working across aerospace, defense, robotics, energy, and advanced manufacturing.

While general-purpose AI models have expanded their ability to analyze information and generate content, applying those capabilities to physical engineering presents different challenges, as fragmented engineering systems can make that information hard to access and limit the effectiveness of AI applications in high-consequence environments.

“The companies building the world’s most advanced machines have decades of hard-won knowledge in their organizations. We’re building the platform that puts that knowledge to work, connecting it to data, so engineers can take on harder problems and build things that weren’t possible before,” said Nominal CEO 
Cameron McCord
, who co-founded the company with Jason Hock and Bryce Strauss.

McCord is a technology entrepreneur and former U.S. Navy officer with experience spanning defense technology, venture capital, and product development. Before founding Nominal, he held product and growth roles at Anduril Industries, served as head of defense and an advisor at Saildrone, advised Applied Intuition, and worked as an investor and operator at Lux Capital.

McCord spent eight years in the U.S. Navy, including 484 days underwater as a nuclear submarine officer, before serving as a congressional liaison to the U.S. House of Representatives. He holds bachelor’s and master’s degrees in nuclear engineering from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

The company also offers Nominal for Developers, which provides access through SQL, APIs, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), allowing engineers to connect existing tools and build customized workflows and agents.

Copied!
 
The Bills elevated two players and placed one on Injured Reserve ahead of Monday night's Week 9 game against the Minnesota Vikings.
Safety Geno Stone and wide receiver Trent Sherfield Sr. have been elevated. Wide receiver Joshua Palmer has been placed on IR.
This will be Stone's second elevation of the season as he also played in Week 8's win against the Ravens. Stone tied for the team's second most tackles with five in that game and played in 37% of the defensive snaps.
Sherfield will also be elevated for the second time this year. He played in Week 5's victory against the Rams. In that game, Sherfield totaled 11% of the offensive snaps and 59% of the special teams' snaps.
Palmer missed the entire week of practice with a hamstring injury. Now heading to IR, Palmer will miss at least four games.

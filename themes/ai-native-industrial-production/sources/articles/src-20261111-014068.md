Most Popular Narrative: 49% Undervalued
 
 
MP Materials closed at $36.46, while the analyst consensus Narrative on Simply Wall St anchors fair value closer to $70.94. This frames the recent share pullback against a much higher long term earnings and cash flow story.

The current valuation and this narrative's bullish stance imply that the share price does not fully reflect MP Materials' contracted cash flow potential and the earnings power suggested by its NdPr production ramp, magnet EBITDA margins above 40%, and long-term offtake structures.

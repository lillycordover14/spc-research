AXT, Inc.
 AXTI is seeing accelerating demand for its indium phosphide (InP) substrates as hyperscalers expand AI data centers and deploy increasingly GPU-dense computing systems. These facilities require faster optical links to move growing volumes of data, making InP-based lasers and photodetectors increasingly important.
The transition to 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers is a major catalyst. AXT's InP revenues reached $54.3 million in third-quarter 2026, primarily driven by data center applications. Total revenues surged 162% year over year and 54% sequentially to a record $73.4 million. Non-GAAP gross margin expanded to 56.1% from 45% in the preceding quarter, benefiting from higher volumes, favorable product mix and increasing InP prices.
Demand extends across geographies. Management believes AXT's materials are deployed at multiple U.S. hyperscalers, while China's growing AI investments have accelerated demand for InP-based lasers. The company's backlog exceeded $500 million, with customers on allocation as demand outstripped available supply. AXT surpassed its 2026 goal of doubling InP capacity and plans further expansion in 2027.
The shift toward higher-value 4-inch wafers, emerging 6-inch capabilities and future co-packaged optics could add to this momentum. Although scaling production remains critical, surging optical connectivity requirements and a substantial backlog suggest AI data center expansion can sustain AXT's substrate-driven revenue growth.
 
 
 
 
 
Rivals Benefiting From the Trend
 
 
Coherent
 COHR is benefiting from growing demand for InP-based lasers, photodiodes and 1.6T optical transceivers. Coherent reported Datacenter & Communications revenues of $1.95 billion in first-quarter fiscal 2027, up 79% year over year. With advanced 6-inch InP fabrication capabilities, Coherent is expanding its optical manufacturing footprint. However, its substrate supply agreement with AXT also makes it an important customer rather than solely a competitor.
Lumentum
 LITE is capitalizing on AI data center investments through its InP lasers, optical circuit switches and cloud networking solutions. Lumentum reported first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenues of $1.26 billion, up 136.5% year over year, driven by strong optical infrastructure demand. Lumentum is also expanding domestic InP manufacturing capacity while maintaining a long-term substrate supply agreement with AXT, reinforcing their complementary positions in the optical connectivity ecosystem.

ZAPATA, Tex. (KGNS) - An off-duty U.S. Border Patrol agent assigned to the Zapata Station was arrested on two felony warrants for indecency with a child by sexual contact, the Zapata County Sheriff’s Office said.
The sheriff’s office identified the agent as Lee Roy Zepeda, 47. Both warrants are for second-degree felonies.
Investigation
The sheriff’s office Criminal Investigations Division (CID) investigated with assistance from the Texas Rangers. The investigation led to the issuance of two warrants.
The sheriff’s office said it would not release additional details, citing the sensitive nature of the allegations and the need to protect the privacy of those involved.
The sheriff’s office said it “remains committed to enforcing the law fairly and impartially, regardless of an individual’s profession or position.”

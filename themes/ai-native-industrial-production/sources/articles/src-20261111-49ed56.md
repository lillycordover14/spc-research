SOUTH BEND, Ind. (AP) - Hannah Hidalgo scored 23 points and grabbed 10 rebounds, along with five assists, for No. 13 Notre Dame, and six scored in double figures for the Fighting Irish in a 108-60 rout of Detroit Mercy on Tuesday night.
Hidalgo shot 7 of 13 from the floor and made all eight of her free-throw attempts. Her free throw midway through the third quarter was the 564th of her career, surpassing Skylar Diggins for the program record.
Anaya Hardy added a career-high 21 points on 10-of-14 shooting for Notre Dame, which shot 59% (41 of 69) from the field.
Madison Rose scored 19 points for the Fighting Irish. Leah Macy chipped in with 17 points, while Jacy Abii and reserve Jenica Lewis each scored 12.
Colleena Bryant scored 14 points on 6-of-12 shooting off the bench for Detroit Mercy (2-2).
The Fighting Irish scored the first 22 points of the game, led 32-4 after the first quarter and 54-21 at the break.
Notre Dame faces DePaul in the Shamrock Classic on Saturday in West Virginia. Tipoff is set for 1 p.m. on WNDU.

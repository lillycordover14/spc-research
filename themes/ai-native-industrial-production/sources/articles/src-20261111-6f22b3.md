Jeanne River, the leftfield folk project of Brooklynite Jeanne Hill, has debuted with a 
Cameron Winter
-assisted single called “PP2.” The 
Geese
 prodigy plays prepared piano on the bric-a-brac tune; Hill contributes vocals, ukulele, celesta, production, and other technical duties that make use, in part, of an Ampex MM1200 reel-to-reel tape machine. Hill also made the cover art with photographer 
Liselotte Davis
, which you can find below, along with the video.
Budding Jeanne River fans can catch her tomorrow (November 12) at Night Club 101 in Manhattan, on a bill with 
Caroline
’s Jasper Llewellyn. A Philadelphia show supporting Joanne Robertson is lined up for April 8, 2027.

 is a music and culture writer based in London. His work also appears in

Don Omar Postpones North American Tour Dates Again Following Respiratory Infection Relapse&nbsp;&nbsp;Consequence of Sound

[page text]
Don Omar
 has announced the postponement of three North American concerts on his “The Last King World Tour” after suffering a relapse of the respiratory infection that forced him to interrupt his performances in late October.

The Puerto Rican reggaeton star shared a statement on social media on Wednesday, November 11th, explaining that although he had managed to return to the stage last week, his recovery had not progressed as expected, and doctors have advised him to rest.

“It is incredibly difficult for me to have to address you again with news like this,” the singer wrote. “I had every hope and intention of closing out this first leg of our tour by fulfilling the three concerts we had pending in Ontario, Las Vegas, and Salt Lake City.”

The affected performances were scheduled for November 10th in Ontario, California; November 11th in Las Vegas, Nevada; and November 13th in Salt Lake City, Utah. All three concerts had already been postponed once due to the same respiratory infection.

Don Omar also acknowledged the inconvenience the latest postponements would cause fans, particularly those who had rearranged their travel plans to attend the rescheduled shows.

“I apologize from the bottom of my heart. My wish remains to reunite with you, but today I have to accept that I need time to recover fully,” he added.

The singer’s team is working to reschedule the affected concerts, although new dates have yet to be announced. No additional changes to the remainder of his tour have been confirmed. See the singer’s official announcement on social media below.

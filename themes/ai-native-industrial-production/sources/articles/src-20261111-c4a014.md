CloudNC
 launches Quote Agent, an AI-powered solution that helps manufacturers assess jobs and prepare customer quotes faster. Quote Agent is designed to cut quoting time and identify manufacturing risks before a price is agreed, helping shops respond faster, protect margins and win work suited to their capabilities, while freeing experienced estimators from routine preparation.

Quote Agent makes the quoting tools that machine shops typically pay for available for free. Its complete manual workflow lets users assess jobs, build estimates and produce customer quotes, with no subscription fees or limits on users or usage. Manufacturers can also try the AI agent free on their own jobs, testing its estimates and time savings before paying for further use.

Users forward a customer’s request for quote (RFQ) by email or upload 2D drawings and/or 3D models. The AI agent assesses the part for manufacturing risks and prepares a proposed manufacturing plan, routing times, material and post-processing cost estimates, and inspection requirements.

The estimator decides whether to quote, reviews and adjusts the plan and costs, sets the price and sends a PDF to the customer. If information is missing or unclear, the agent asks questions so the user can resolve it. The estimator retains responsibility for reviewing and approving the final quote.

Quote Agent combines CloudNC’s physics models with large language models (LLMs) to analyze how parts can be manufactured and carry out quoting tasks. Users can add their equipment, capabilities, costs, stocked materials and preferences in Myshop. The agent uses this information and learns from user interactions to tailor its estimates to the shop. Users can also replace its cost estimates with their own suppliers’ prices.

The product also generates DFM reports, ballooned drawings with numbered inspection callouts and first article inspection reports. These can be exported as PDFs, with first article inspection reports also available in Excel format.

Quote Agent is a standalone product and does not require CAM Assist. The manual version is free, with unlimited users and usage, and will remain free. Bulk discounts on AI agent usage are available.

Jason Bowes, COO of CloudNC, says: “Every quote is a decision about which work a shop should take on and whether it can make money doing it. If that decision takes hours, customers can place the order elsewhere before the shop has even responded. Quote Agent gives estimators the manufacturing assessment and cost estimate to review in minutes. The response from users is already very positive — people recognize that this is a task that AI can and should help with, as it means machine shops can turn around quotes much more quickly than previously possible, at a fraction of the effort.”

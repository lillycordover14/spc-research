Syracuse University Art Museum hosts free figure drawing session Nov. 19&nbsp;&nbsp;This is CNY

[page text]
Artist drawing with charcoal 
"Drawing in Dialogue" brings Open Figure Drawing to the SU Art Museum galleries. 
(Gu Ko/Gu Ko) 
Bring a sketchbook and a pencil to the 
Syracuse University Art Museum
 on Thursday, Nov. 19, and draw from a live model. The model will pose among artworks made from materials rescued from Recology, San Francisco’s recycling and reuse facility. 
“Drawing in Dialogue: Open Figure Drawing at the SU Art Museum”
 runs from 6-8 p.m. in the museum galleries. The session is free, open to all experience levels, and requires no RSVP. 
The model’s poses will respond to “Reclaimed: The Art of Recology,” a traveling exhibition of work by 33 artists from the Recology Artist in Residence program. Armed with safety gear and a shopping cart, each of these artists mine materials from Recology’s Public Reuse and Recycling Area to recycle and reclaim the discarded waste as imagined art objects. 
Placing a live model among these works invites artists to explore the connections between the human figure and the everyday objects we use and discard. 
Installation image of "Reclaimed" 
Poses from the live model will be inspired by artworks from "Reclaimed: The Art of Recology" on view at the SU Art Museum through December 11, 2026. 
(Jiayue Yu/Jiayue Yu) 
“This is a great opportunity to make art right in our galleries, and to explore drawing the human figure alongside the dynamic and thought-provoking artworks in the exhibition,” said Kate Holohan, curator of education and academic outreach at the museum. 
The session is a partnership with 
Open Figure Drawing Inc.
, a volunteer-run nonprofit which gives Syracuse artists a place to draw from live artist models since the late 1980s. The group usually meets every week at Delavan Studios. This special session brings that studio practice into the galleries. 
Attendees should bring their own sketchbook or drawing paper and pencils. To protect the artwork on view, only dry materials are permitted, so please leave paints, inks, and markers at home. Stools and clipboards can be provided. The model will be clothed. 
“Reclaimed: The Art of Recology” is on view through December 11, 2026. The Syracuse University Art Museum is in the Shaffer Art Building, next to the College Place bus stop at the heart of the Syracuse University campus. Learn more at 
museum.syr.edu.

Does MP Materials Look Pricey on Sales?
 
P/S is a useful lens for MP Materials because investors are currently focused on how much revenue the rare earth and magnet platform can generate rather than on steady profits. MP trades on a P/S of 12.5x, which is well above the broader Metals and Mining sector average of 3.1x and also higher than the 11.0x peer average for this niche. That tells you the market is paying a premium per dollar of sales compared with both the wider industry and closer rare earth comparables.
 
Based on the tailored fair multiple that blends MP Materials' growth profile, margin structure, size and risk, the present 12.5x P/S screens as overvalued. The recent Q3 2026 milestone, with GM magnet qualification and a sharp uplift in reported revenue, helps explain why sentiment is strong. However, the sales multiple already embeds a generous view of what that pipeline might deliver. Before you lean on this ratio, you would want to weigh how durable those new revenue streams look, how volatile rare earth pricing could be, and how quickly magnet volumes might ramp to support the current premium.

    Men’s soccer falls short to Tar Heels in ACC tournament

Lillian Johnson
, 
Senior Photographer
 

November 11, 2026

 

Lillian Johnson
 
Ransford Gyan and Duncan Wilson celebrate after scoring a goal during the ACC quarterfinals.
 

Even after tying the game in the second half, Clemson men’s soccer wasn’t able to overcome North Carolina, falling 3-2 in double overtime and missing out on a spot in the ACC tournament semifinals.

On Nov. 8, No. 2 Clemson fell to No. 7 North Carolina in the quarterfinals of the ACC Men’s Soccer Championship at Historic Riggs Field.

Here’s how it unfolded.

In the first half, the Tar Heels took control early, jumping out to a 2-0 lead in the first half behind goals from Nico Loebus and Zach Sebian. Nick Lockermann and Joschi Schelb assisted both goals.

Although Clemson had a slow start, the Tigers came out of halftime quicker and more aggressive and wasted no time getting on the board. Martin Bakken got the Tigers on the board in the 52nd minute, while Ransford Gyan found the back of the net in the 70th minute to even the score at 2-2.

Clemson’s second-half comeback forced overtime, giving the Tigers another opportunity to keep their ACC tournament dreams alive.

After two overtime periods, senior defender Luca Nikolai converted a penalty kick after he was fouled inside the box, turning a 3-2 victory for the Tar Heels.

North Carolina’s goalkeeper totaled five saves compared to Clemson’s single save on goal. The Tar Heels held a 7-1 advantage in corner kicks, which gave North Carolina more opportunities in the box to score.

Despite the loss, Clemson held the offensive advantage. The Tigers outshot the Tar Heels 17-10 and recorded seven shots on goal compared to North Carolina’s four.

The loss brings the Tigers’ season to a close at 13-3-2, while North Carolina advances to the ACC semifinals.

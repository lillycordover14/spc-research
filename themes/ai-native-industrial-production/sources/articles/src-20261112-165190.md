Samsung Electro-Mechanics will spend 4.27 trillion won (approximately $3.2 billion) to build a new high-performance semiconductor package substrate (FC-BGA) production line at the Myeonghak General Industrial Complex in Sejong City. The investment, formalized on the 12th at a groundbreaking ceremony for the Chungcheong Region Mega Project held in Asan, South Chungcheong Province, is the first phase of the company's mid- to long-term plan to invest 8 trillion won (approximately $6.0 billion) in Sejong through 2040.

FC-BGA is a high-density substrate that connects high-performance semiconductors such as GPUs and CPUs to the motherboard, transmitting electrical signals and power, and is considered a core component of AI servers and high-performance computing (HPC) systems. As AI chips grow more powerful, substrates are getting larger and circuit layer counts are rising, making it increasingly important to secure equipment capable of producing these complex substrates.

The new plant will be four stories above ground with about 99,000 square meters (30,000 pyeong) of floor space, built near Samsung Electro-Mechanics' existing Sejong site. The company has decided to shorten the construction period to meet fast mass-production demands from global Big Tech customers. The company had previously disclosed an investment period for the Sejong site expansion running from September this year to May 2028; after a period of equipment stabilization, it aims to begin full-scale mass production in September 2028.

The investment follows up on a memorandum of understanding signed in July to foster advanced industries in the Chungcheong region. In September, Samsung Electro-Mechanics also decided on a total of 6.78 trillion won (approximately $5.1 billion) in FC-BGA expansion investment — 4.27 trillion won for the Sejong site and 2.51 trillion won (approximately $1.9 billion) for its Vietnam production subsidiary. The plan is to expand AI server substrate capacity based on investment support from global customers and secured mid- to long-term supply volumes.

Sejong City supported the investment process by revising the industrial complex plan, adjusting public facilities and negotiating ways to secure replacement facilities. Chang Duck-hyun, president of Samsung Electro-Mechanics, thanked Sejong City at the event for its active administrative and financial support. Sejong Mayor Cho Sang-ho said, "We expect Samsung Electro-Mechanics' large-scale investment to revitalize the local economy and create quality jobs," adding, "We will continue to do our utmost with swift administrative support and infrastructure expansion so that companies do not miss the right timing for investment."

The decision comes as demand for AI server substrates grows structurally. FC-BGA is an essential component linking GPUs and CPUs to motherboards, but production is technically demanding, so expanding supply takes time. Samsung Electro-Mechanics' simultaneous expansion in Sejong and Vietnam is seen as a move to meet the delivery deadlines and volumes demanded by customers. Sejong City plans to use this investment as a springboard to attract further investment and establish itself as a key hub for the semiconductor back-end processing industry.

BATON ROUGE, La. — Dylan Crews couldn’t help reminiscing after his first in-person glimpse at his 2025 Topps Chrome Update MLB Debut Patch autographed card at his local hobby shop, Cards and Culture, last week.
What makes the card special is that it includes the Debut Patch he wore on his sleeve while playing his first game for the Washington Nationals on Aug. 26, 2024 against the New York Yankees.

“That card right there is everything you work for,” Crews said. “I think about the times when I was in the backyard of my grandmother’s apartment hitting little plastic balls over the apartment. Fast forward when you’re at times in a warehouse just cracking every single day. Then you fast forward and you go to college and thinking about what could be and the potential of getting drafted and all of that. You’re still working every day to achieve your goal and then all of a sudden you make your MLB debut.
“That’s the holy grail right there of your career. … It’s a very special card to me.”
And only a few minutes later, Crews’ Debut Patch card would complete a rare full-circle journey and go back into the hands of the outfielder.
Robbie Olivier, Crews’ friend and owner of Cards and Culture and Ryno Wax, surprised the LSU legend by gifting him his Debut Patch card after both Olivier and Crews almost gave up on securing the card a few months earlier.
“Stop, bro,” Crews replied, smiling and shaking his head.
“It’s only fair,” Olivier said as he grabbed the Debut Patch card and presented it to Crews with both hands.
“Dude, c’mon man,” Crews said while leaning in to hug Olivier.
“That’s why I got it so you could have it!” Olivier said.
“I mean, I can’t top this. It’s like the coolest thing ever,” Crews said in the moment. “You try not to get emotional because it’s the biggest card that you could possibly have. It’s the best thing ever.”

This wasn’t the script Olivier presented to Crews before last Friday’s meetup.
Earlier this year, Olivier made it his mission to acquire the three best Crews baseball cards in existence: Crews’ 1st Bowman Superfractor auto, his Topps Chrome Superfractor rookie auto and, of course, the celebrated MLB Debut Patch auto. All three are one-of-a-kind cards, and they’re usually a player’s three most valuable ones.
Advertisement
When a collector pulls off the feat of acquiring all three for a given player, Topps then issues the person a “Three Kings” card to commemorate the accomplishment. The player comes to the collector and personally autographs the card. This is the fourth time a Three Kings card has been printed since the concept was first introduced in the fall of 2025, with the other three players being Jackson Chourio, Junior Caminero and Michael Busch. Crews autographed his Three Kings card and inscribed “Lets Geaux King Ryno!”

The one-of-a-kind Dylan Crews Three Kings card with personalized inscription. (Photo: Larry Holder)
Shortly after Crews handed Olivier the freshly autographed Three Kings card, Olivier gave Crews the ultimate surprise of returning the Debut Patch.

“It’s not a transactional thing. It’s a really meaningful thing between the two of us,” Olivier said.
Crews’ desire to obtain his Debut Patch card isn’t the first time a player has expressed interest in landing a personal piece of his history. There’s been a growing trend of players, parents of players or spouses of players looking to track down Debut Patch cards given that they include a small memento from a milestone life event for an athlete.
For example, the mother of Seattle Mariners outfielder Dominic Canzone 
tracked down his Debut Patch card
 and surprised him with it. 
The same for the mother
 of Minnesota Twins pitcher Kody Funderburk. The wife of former Texas Rangers pitcher Caleb Boushley gave him his Debut Patch card 
as a wedding gift
. Other players have written requests right on the card itself for whoever pulls it from a pack 
to contact them
.
Crews’ passion for the Debut Patch card ignited Olivier to track down what’s become arguably the most cherished and meaningful card for any baseball player since the Debut Patch program commenced three years ago. Olivier said the introduction of the Three Kings card just elevated his search to include all three cards.

Olivier purchased the 2024 1st Bowman Superfractor autographed card and the 2025 Topps Chrome Superfractor autographed card within about a week’s span through private deals. Landing the 2025 Topps Chrome Update MLB Debut Patch card for Crews was nowhere near as easy, though.
Olivier said at one point he and Crews had given up on the possibility of acquiring it because the owner’s asking price and their offer grew too far apart. But both sides eventually agreed to terms for the Debut Patch card to come to Baton Rouge.

Dylan Crews’ MLB Debut Patch card, now back in his possession. (Photo: Larry Holder)
“I remember he was crouched down in his locker and I could see the fellas walking behind him and he’s trying to listen and I was like, ‘bro …,’” Olivier said, recalling how he told Crews via FaceTime while Crews sat in the Nationals locker before a game last season. “(Crews responded) ‘I knew you got it, dude! What did you do? How did you do that? And we both were kind of getting teary-eyed. He’s like, ‘Dude, no way! We gave up on this!’
“It was just such a crazy weird moment. And at first I’m like, ‘Well, I don’t want to screw him up. He’s getting ready for his game.’”
Olivier said there’s no chance he’ll ever try for a Three Kings card again, given how arduous the journey to complete the Crews trifecta became.
Crews, meanwhile, is just happy that cards bearing game-worn relics like the Debut Patches now exist.
“I give a lot of credit to Topps and the other companies that are doing things like this,” Crews said. “It gives a good way to connect with the players and the fans. Obviously looking at this Debut Patch, I wore that during a game and that could be in the hands of a fan out there. I think it’s an awesome way to almost give back to the people, right? You know, you spend a lot of money on this hobby and you’re chasing that big hit every single day. To get something like that that connects you to the player, it’s a special thing for sure.”

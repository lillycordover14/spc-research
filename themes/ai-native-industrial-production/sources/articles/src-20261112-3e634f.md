	Maleficent is making her debut in 
the 
Sofia the First 
franchise.

	The iconic 
Sleeping Beauty 
villain will appear in the 
Sofia the First: Royal Magic
 
holiday special, titled “Holiday at Charmswell,” premiering Nov. 26, Thanksgiving Day, on Disney Jr. and Disney+.

	In the holiday special, Sofia (voiced by 
Ariel Winter
) and Aurora (aka Sleeping Beauty) work together when the evil Maleficent comes to Charmswell during the festive holiday of Wassailia.

	“After years of bringing classic Disney princesses into Sofia’s world, it was exciting to finally incorporate a classic Disney villain,” said 
Craig Gerber
, creator and executive producer of 
Sofia the First: Royal Magic
. “Weaving Maleficent into the story of Miss Nettle, a former villain who now runs Sofia’s school, not only made for an entertaining holiday episode but also enabled us to address what seems to be Maleficent’s core reason for her evil ways — not being invited to a party.”

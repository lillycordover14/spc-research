Gibraltar High School senior, 
Peninsula Pulse
 intern and local musician Wyatt Aldwood is releasing his debut album, 
First Frost.
 

First Frost
 is a compilation of original music written by Aldwood, inspired by life experiences, emotions and adventures. Aldwood’s friend Xavier Gaddes helped record the album in his Sister Bay home recording studio throughout summer 2026. Each song features Aldwood’s musical talents and Gaddes’ recording expertise and equipment.

First Frost
 will be released on Bandcamp and Spotify Sunday, Nov. 15. Aldwood can be found playing music around the county.

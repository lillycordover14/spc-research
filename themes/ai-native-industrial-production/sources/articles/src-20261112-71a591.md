ABS and Radian Forge Collaborate on Digital Qualification for Additive Manufacturing

gCaptain

Total Views: 4 

November 12, 2026

Agreement combines manufacturing data and technical review to support component qualification

ABS and Radian Forge have signed a joint development project (JDP) to evaluate the use of digital manufacturing data in the qualification of metal components produced through wire arc additive manufacturing (WAAM).

As the maritime industry explores additive manufacturing to address component availability and long production lead times, confidence in the consistency and performance of manufactured parts is critical. The JDP will examine whether manufacturing process parameters, real-time sensor data, inspection records and material testing results can provide a reliable technical basis for verifying and validating WAAM-produced components.

“Digital qualification has an important role to play in the adoption of additive manufacturing across the marine and offshore sectors. We are proud to work with Radian Forge to strengthen confidence in additive manufacturing by improving how qualification evidence is generated, evaluated and traced,” said Michael Kei, ABS Vice President, Technology.

Under the agreement, Radian Forge will manufacture components for testing and validation, collect manufacturing and sensor data, and conduct applicable testing. ABS will provide independent technical review, evaluate test results and assess the proposed methodology and its potential application.

“Traditional manufacturing timelines can create significant challenges when critical components are needed. Our goal is to build confidence in a manufacturing process that can respond to those needs. Partnering with ABS gives us an opportunity to rigorously evaluate how process data and material performance can support that objective,” said Jeff Yeager, CEO of Radian Forge.

ABS supports the adoption of additive manufacturing for the maritime industry through technical requirements and guidance, including the ABS 
Requirements for Additive Manufacturing.

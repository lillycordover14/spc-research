TAB Gıda Buys Back TRY 20,000 Nominal Value of TABGD Shares
Tipranks - 
Tipranks

Thu Nov 12, 9:29AM CST
TAB Gida Sanayi ve Ticaret A.S. ( 
(TR:TABGD)
 ) just unveiled an announcement.
TAB GIDA SANAYİ VE TİCARET A.Ş. reported that it bought back shares with a nominal value of TRY 20,000 on Borsa Istanbul on Nov. 11, 2026, within its approved share buyback program, at a weighted average price of TRY 198.08. The purchase price ranged from TRY 197.30 to TRY 198.80 per share.
Following the transaction, the nominal value of repurchased shares reached TRY 511,000, equivalent to 0.1956% of the company’s share capital. The buyback program was approved at the June 14, 2024 general assembly meeting, and its second buyback period began on April 10, 2025.

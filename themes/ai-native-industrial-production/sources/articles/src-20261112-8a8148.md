Ariana Grande and 
Ricky Alvarez
 are red-carpet official!

The couple was photographed arriving for 
the premiere of “Focker-in-Law”
 in New York City on Wednesday evening, with the dancer affectionately wrapping his arms around the “Wicked” star for photos.

The Grammy-winning songstress, 33, wore an off-white and gray tartan-patterned ballgown, styling her hair in a signature sleek ponytail featuring 
her new shorter haircut
.

She accessorized with simple earrings, and glowed in a soft pink makeup glam. 

Alvarez opted for a pair of casual jeans, folded at the cuff, and a black blazer for the outing. He accessorized with brown shoes and a classic belt. 

In one photo, the pair faced each other lovingly as photographers snapped their pic at the event. In other photos, they held hands, and in another pose, Grande placed her hand lightly on her boyfriend’s chest.

The 
appearance at her new comedy’s debut 
comes after a rep for the “7 Rings” hitmaker announced in August she’d be 
“stepping back from public-facing work”
 following the Sept. 1 conclusion of her “Eternal Sunshine” tour to have more time for her personal life. 

“She’ll be spending a lot of time with her family, friends and Ricky,” a source exclusively told Page Six at the time. 

“Between touring, rehearsals and her other work commitments, she and Ricky haven’t been able to spend as much time together as she would have liked over the past few months, so she’s looking forward to having more uninterrupted time with him,” the insider said.

Grande ultimately 
also stepped away from a planned West End production
 of “Sundays in the Park with George,” alongside Jonathan Bailey, which was set to debut in the spring.

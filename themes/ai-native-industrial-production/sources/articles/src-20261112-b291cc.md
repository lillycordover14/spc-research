Northrop Grumman and Hermeus Corporation have signed a teaming agreement to rapidly field a new class of autonomous, high‑Mach aircraft to give warfighters faster, more survivable options in combat.

As the threat environment accelerates, the Department of War needs autonomous systems developed and fielded in months, not years. Northrop Grumman and Hermeus will meet that need by pairing Northrop Grumman’s combat‑proven mission systems and “ready‑now” electronic warfare, communication, sensing and autonomy expertise with Hermeus’ high‑speed Quarterhorse aircraft.

“Defense technology needs to evolve faster than ever before, because our customers need solutions now. Together, we’re giving the military a new operational edge by putting advanced mission kits on aircraft built to move beyond supersonic speeds,” said Kyle Mimken, program manager at Northrop Grumman, working on these new solutions.

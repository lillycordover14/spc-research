Will Geno Smith Remain a Jet in 2027?

By 
Kevin English

|

@KEnglishDS
 

|

Nov 12, 2026 at 1:43pm
 

 
The Jets are expected to “seriously consider” bringing back QB Geno Smith in 2027, per The Athletic’s Zack Rosenblatt.

What They're Saying

Here's more
 from Rosenblatt, who pointed to Smith's better-than-expected play and the impact of his excellent work ethic on a young offense.
"The impact he is having on the team, the Jets feel, will go beyond this season. That’s true regardless of their win-loss record and whether he’s still here in 2027 or not. But he’s generated enough positivity that there’s a real chance he’s back in 2027."

Here's OC Frank Reich:
“I think he’s playing at a high level. The way that he throws the ball downfield, he’s so accurate. His arm can make all the plays. He’s not turning the ball over. We’re completing deep balls. We need a few more than we’re getting … but I think all the metrics around Geno are pretty good.”

2026 Fantasy Football Impact: Geno's Impressive Rebound

Smith has been a nice story this year, sitting fifth league-wide in completion rate, 10th in passing yards, and 11th in yards per attempt. He's already tallied a pair of top-five fantasy finishes.

But if he returns to the Jets, he'll have to do so on a new contract. Smith will also be at risk of losing his job to a rookie next season, as the team holds three first round picks in the 2027 draft. Smith certainly isn't the long-term option at 36 years old.
For now, it doesn't appear that the team will turn to rookie QB Cade Klubnik, although that could change with the Jets essentially out of the playoff race at 2-7. 
Smith remains a fantasy spot-starter, while Klubnik is only worth rostering in deep dynasty superflex setups. The veteran cracks the top-15 of our 
Week 10 QB rankings

                            ROCKWELL AUTOMATION, INC.                        
-0.43%
Laminar announced that it has joined the Rockwell Automation PartnerNetwork as a Technology Partner, expanding access to its real-time fluid intelligence for manufacturers using Rockwell Automation technologies. The collaboration will focus on helping manufacturers optimize Clean-in-Place and product changeover processes while advancing both operational performance and sustainability objectives. Laminar combines patented, inline spectral sensors with process-aware machine learning models to identify fluid conditions and transitions in real time.
When connected with industrial control systems, this intelligence supports AI-based process decisions instead of relying on recipes and fixed timers. Through the PartnerNetwork, Laminar can extend these capabilities across Rockwell Automation environments and help manufacturers recover productive capacity while reducing unnecessary resource consumption. The collaboration reflects a shared view that productivity and sustainability must be delivered through the same operational improvements.
Shorter, validated Clean-in-Place cycles can return time to production and support line utilization, while also reducing the resources and waste associated with unnecessary cleaning. The companies will also explore how process-state and cycle information may support broader manufacturing optimization through visualization, analytics and higher-level manufacturing systems.

What this 
filing
 says
Bleichroeder Acquisition Corp. III is a blank check company that signed a business combination agreement with Ursa Major Technologies on August 24, 2026, and has not yet commenced operations. The filing shows a working capital deficit of $16,757,026, mostly from a $16,212,177 Series A SPA liability that does not require a cash payment. The Ursa Major deal is not complete, so shareholders depend on that deal closing.

Key terms
Blank check company
: 
A shell company formed to raise money in an IPO and then acquire or merge with a private business.
Trust account
: 
A segregated account that holds the IPO proceeds until a business combination is completed or the company liquidates.
Series A SPA liability
: 
An obligation under a Series A stock purchase agreement recorded on the balance sheet, which the filing says does not need a cash payment.
Private Placement Warrants
: 
Warrants sold privately by the company, outside the public offering, to fund its operations.

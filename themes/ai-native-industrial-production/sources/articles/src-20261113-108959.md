Thelma & James To Release Debut Album ‘Whiplash’ In January&nbsp;&nbsp;MusicRow.com

[page text]
Thelma & James
 will release their debut album 
Whiplash 
on Jan. 29 via Big Loud Records.

The album includes the duo’s breakout track “Happy Ever After You,” and heartbreakers like “If You Ain’t In Love By Now” and “The Way That It Wasn’t.” The married couple co-wrote all of the songs on the project, outside of a reimagined 
Grease
 cover “You’re The One That I Want,” and welcomed an illustrious list of collaborators like 
Natalie Hemby, Hillary Lindsey, Lori McKenna, Josh Osborne
 and more on the album.

“Two years ago, this Christmas, we ended up writing a song that changed everything,” shares Thelma & James. “It had been almost a decade since we’d written together. We weren’t trying to start a band, we were just writing a song, just kind of doing what we love. The coolest part has been watching these songs find their way to listeners and getting to do this with the person you already share your entire life with has been very special.”

In September, the two were nominated for five awards at the CCMAs, where they won Alternative Country Album of the Year for their EP 
Starting Over 
and Video of the Year for “Parking Lot Prayers.” Thelma & James 
are nominated
 for Vocal Duo of the Year at next week’s 60th Annual CMA Awards.

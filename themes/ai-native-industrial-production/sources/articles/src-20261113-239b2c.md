Overgear Ironhide Quick Review: Ironhide, the first addition to TT's new Overgear series, stands approximately 18 cm tall in robot mode. Completely breaking away from the traditional G1 design, it incorporates elements from live-action films and blends them with Japanese mecha aesthetics, creating a refreshing look. Its articulation and transformation mechanism are excellent, making posing a breeze. In vehicle mode, it resembles the Hennessey VelociRaptor 6x6 heavy-duty farm vehicle, with a very imposing six-wheel drive structure. However, the relatively narrow body makes it appear too long. The entire body is covered with 3mm and 5mm expansion slots, especially the side panels of the truck bed, allowing for the free insertion and removal of numerous modular parts, resulting in extremely high playability. Many criticize the use of the windshield as the chest area, but I find it quite unique and subtly echoes the haphazard classic toy design of the original G1 Ironhide. Personally, I think this detail is quite ingenious. Overall, TT's new attempt at a more realistic military style is very sincere and hits my tastes hard. I'm also looking forward to the release of the opening theme after Blackout, which was released at the same time!

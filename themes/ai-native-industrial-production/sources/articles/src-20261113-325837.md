“Being recognized by Forbes for the sixth consecutive year is an honor and reflects Aptar’s commitment to fostering a workplace where people feel respected, supported and empowered to succeed,” said Shiela Vinczeller, Aptar’s Chief Human Resources Officer. “We will continue cultivating an environment where different perspectives are welcome and valued to help strengthen our organization, drive innovation and support the success of our employees and business.”

“This recognition reflects the meaningful work taking place across Aptar to strengthen inclusion across our organization and create opportunities for employees to learn and connect,” said Marcia Thomas, Chief Diversity Officer. “From professional development and mentoring opportunities to employee-led initiatives that foster belonging, we remain focused on creating an environment where everyone can contribute, develop their careers and make an impact.”

The World's Top Companies for Women ranking is compiled by Forbes and Statista, a leading market research firm. Data for the “World’s Top Companies for Women” is based on anonymous surveys of over 140,000 women working in 61 countries. In this survey, participants are asked to evaluate companies based on the employer brand, public opinion and overall gender parity in leadership. These surveys are conducted periodically over a two-year period. For a full list of the World’s Top Companies for Women and a breakdown of the methodology, please

website here

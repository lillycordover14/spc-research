VulcanForms Raises $500 Million to Build Three Advanced Manufacturing Factories and Advance Physical AI

VulcanForms
 
has raised $500 million
 in capital to build three new advanced manufacturing factories and to further its integration of physical AI, according to TCT Magazine. The financing was led by J.P. Morgan as part of its Security and Resiliency Initiative, with the funds to be managed by 
Apollo
.
The company described the investment as a significant milestone in its shift from breakthrough manufacturing technology to industrial-scale production. 
VulcanForms
 relies on metal additive manufacturing, precision machining, automation and physical AI technologies in its advanced manufacturing operations. That capability is being used by companies in medical devices, consumer products, aerospace, defence, space and industrial markets, and VulcanForms refers to some projects as multibillion-dollar commercial programs.
VulcanForms said the construction of the three factories reflects the scale of demand for its technology, and the new sites will complement existing facilities in Devens and Newburyport, Massachusetts.
Kevin Kassekert, chief executive of VulcanForms, said the United States cannot lead the next industrial revolution if it cannot manufacture what it invents. He said breakthrough technologies developed in the country have for too long been manufactured overseas, and he positioned VulcanForms as proving that this can change by turning American innovation into industrial-scale production. He also said the company was proud to work with J.P. Morgan and Apollo on building the country's industrial future at home.
Jay Horine, global head of 
JPMorganChase
's Security and Resiliency Initiative, said the initiative works with companies such as VulcanForms to strengthen the industries and supply chains that underpin economic security. He said such efforts can help bridge the gap between promising technology and industrial-scale production in the United States as demand grows across critical industries.
John Zito, co-president of 
Apollo Asset Management
, said rebuilding America's industrial base is a strategic priority that will rank among the most capital-intensive undertakings the country has faced in decades. He said VulcanForms has developed a distinct approach to manufacturing that delivers critical components to a broad set of end markets and is meeting rapidly growing customer demand. He said Apollo was proud to support that growth with a bespoke financing solution to fund capacity expansion alongside J.P. Morgan, which he said illustrates how private capital can help advance the return to large-scale manufacturing.

VulcanForms, a pioneer in advanced digital manufacturing, has announced it has reached an agreement for US$500 million in financing led by J.P. Morgan and funds managed by Apollo (NYSE: APO) (Apollo) to accelerate the next phase of American industrial production. Backed by multibillion-dollar commercial programs across critical industries, VulcanForms is adding to its three existing manufacturing sites to meet growing customer demand by building three new factories, marking a significant milestone in the company's transition from breakthrough manufacturing technology to industrial-scale production.

Latham & Watkins LLP represented VulcanForms in the transaction with a Boston-based team led by partners Susan Parker, Stephen Ranere, and Alex Harris, with associates Payton Reisinger and Emily Teems. Advice was also provided on tax matters by Chicago partner Rene de Vera.

[Science Video] Boston Dynamics Atlas robot rolls on wheels and lands 360° jumps&nbsp;&nbsp;DongA Science

[page text]
Hyundai Motor robot on roller skates. Provided by Seeds Team

Hyundai Motor Group’s humanoid robot “Atlas” has swapped its feet for wheels. It drifts smoothly across the floor and even pulls off 360° spinning jumps. Boston Dynamics, the U.S. robotics company under Hyundai Motor Group, released an experimental video of Atlas with wheels instead of feet on YouTube on November 9 (local time).

The research team first trained the movements in a virtual environment. These motions transferred almost directly to the physical robot, and the 360° jump succeeded on the first attempt. Boston Dynamics explained in its blog and video that “wheeled Atlas is not a product but a research platform,” adding that “the goal of this experiment was to push the motors to their limits.”

YUKON, Okla.(KOKH) — 
Yukon's annual Christmas in the Park tradition is returning, marking the start of the town's holiday season. 
The City of Yukon is expanding the celebration with new attractions, larger displays, and an Opening Night celebration planned for Saturday, Nov. 21. 
Opening Night at Chisholm Trail Park will feature a new drone light show and fireworks display. The city is also adding a zipline, allowing riders to zip through the park's holiday lights display. 
Festivities also include the Chili Your Cheeks Race, with registration for the 5K and 1K Jingle Walk opening at 3 p.m. on Saturday, with the 5K starting at 5 p.m. and the 1K beginning at 5:15 p.m.
Throughout the evening, families can enjoy free activities like face painting, inflatables, and a Santa photo opportunity featuring real reindeer. 
City officials said a shuttle service will be available starting at 4 p.m. from Yukon Middle School. The shuttle bus is only available on the event's opening night. 
Christmas in the Park staff are encouraging visitors to arrive early and plan for temporary road closures. 
"Whether visitors come for the lights, the races, the holiday entertainment or simply to spend time with loved ones, Christmas in the Park continues to offer something that has made it a cherished Yukon tradition for decades: a place to celebrate the season together," The City of Yukon said. 
Christmas in the Park kicks off on Saturday, Nov. 21, with its Opening Night celebration and is open from 6 p.m. to 11 p.m. nightly through New Year's Eve at Chislom Trail Park, 2200 S. Holly Ave. Admission to the park is free.

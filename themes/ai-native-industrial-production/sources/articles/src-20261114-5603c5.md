“Closing six buyer-represented transactions in less than a month speaks to the level of activity we’re seeing among Chicago multifamily investors,” said Mitir Patel, Director at Essex Three-Twelve. “From off-market opportunities to properties with significant renovation potential, buyers are continuing to pursue investments that fit their individual objectives. Having a strong understanding of the local market helps us identify and execute on those opportunities.”

Among the notable transactions was the acquisition of 909 N. Leavitt St., a renovated six-unit multifamily property in Chicago’s Ukrainian Village neighborhood. Patel represented a buyer who previously sold a three-unit property through Essex Three-Twelve and reinvested the proceeds into the larger apartment building. The buyer plans to increase rental income by adjusting rents toward market levels, reflecting a strategy focused on expanding an existing multifamily investment portfolio.

Patel also represented the buyer of 3501 N. Damen Ave., a five-unit property in Roscoe Village. The buyer plans to undertake significant renovations and add two residential units, increasing the property’s total unit count to seven. The acquisition presents an opportunity to reposition the asset and increase its long-term income potential.

Another acquisition, 4050 N. Central Park Ave., involved an off-market five-unit property in Irving Park. Patel connected the buyer with the opportunity before the property was publicly listed, demonstrating the role of established relationships and local market knowledge in sourcing investment properties.

Together, the transactions reflect a range of acquisition strategies, including portfolio expansion, value-add investments, and off-market opportunities. Patel’s recent activity underscores Essex Three-Twelve’s continued involvement in advising investors seeking multifamily properties throughout Chicago.

Essex Three-Twelve specializes in the acquisition and disposition of 3–12 unit multifamily investment properties throughout Chicago, providing advisory services to buyers and sellers through property valuation, market analysis, and transaction strategy.

VulcanForms: $500 Million Financing Led By J.P. Morgan And Apollo To Build Three New U.S. Manufacturing Factories

VulcanForms, a Boston-based advanced digital manufacturing company, has secured an agreement for $500 million in financing led by J.P. Morgan and funds managed by Apollo. The financing will support construction of three new factories, expansion of domestic production capacity, and further development of the company’s integrated manufacturing platform as it responds to demand from customers across aerospace, defense, medical devices, consumer products, and industrial markets.

The transaction will help VulcanForms expand beyond its three existing manufacturing sites, bringing its planned manufacturing footprint to six facilities.

The company has secured multibillion-dollar commercial programs spanning several industries that require complex, high-performance metal components.

VulcanForms combines metal additive manufacturing, precision machining, automation, and physical AI within a unified production system designed to manufacture engineered parts at industrial scale.

The new financing is part of JPMorganChase’s Security and Resiliency Initiative, which supports investment in industries and supply chains considered important to U.S. economic security.

Building Three Additional Advanced Manufacturing Facilities

VulcanForms plans to use the financing to accelerate construction of three factories that will expand its domestic manufacturing capabilities.

The company currently operates manufacturing facilities in Devens and Newburyport, Massachusetts.

Its expansion reflects growing demand for advanced manufacturing services among customers seeking domestic production of sophisticated metal components.

These products frequently require specialized production processes, high manufacturing tolerances, and extensive quality validation.

Traditional manufacturing approaches can involve multiple suppliers responsible for different stages of production.

VulcanForms is developing an integrated alternative that combines several manufacturing processes within a coordinated digital infrastructure.

The company believes this approach can reduce production complexity, improve efficiency, and help manufacturers respond more quickly to changing customer requirements.

The additional facilities will provide capacity to support existing commercial programs and future production opportunities.

Combining Metal Additive Manufacturing And Physical AI

VulcanForms’ manufacturing platform integrates metal additive manufacturing with precision machining, automation, and AI-driven production capabilities.

Metal additive manufacturing uses digital designs to produce components through a process that builds material into a desired shape.

This approach can enable complex geometries that may be difficult to manufacture through conventional methods.

However, industrial production often requires additional processing to achieve precise dimensions, surface finishes, and performance characteristics.

VulcanForms addresses these requirements by combining additive manufacturing with precision machining and product validation.

The company also incorporates physical AI into its manufacturing infrastructure.

Physical AI refers to AI technologies applied to physical systems, including automated production equipment and industrial operations.

These capabilities can support more coordinated manufacturing processes and improve how equipment and production workflows are managed.

VulcanForms intends to use part of the new financing to advance the integration of physical AI across its platform.

Its objective is to create a scalable manufacturing system that combines digital production flexibility with the reliability required for industrial applications.

Multibillion-Dollar Commercial Programs Support Expansion

VulcanForms has secured commercial programs with a combined value described as multibillion-dollar across several industries.

These programs involve customers requiring engineered metal products for applications in medical devices, aerospace, defense, space, industrial equipment, and consumer products.

The company also serves numerous Fortune 500 customers.

Its manufacturing capabilities are designed to address requirements involving complex component designs, production efficiency, and high-performance materials.

The commercial programs provide a foundation for expanding manufacturing capacity.

VulcanForms believes its integrated platform can help customers reduce dependence on fragmented supply chains that involve multiple manufacturing vendors.

By coordinating production within a unified system, the company aims to improve delivery performance and reduce the complexity associated with producing specialized components.

The expansion is intended to support both existing customer commitments and additional market opportunities.

J.P. Morgan And Apollo Support Domestic Industrial Growth

The financing brings together two major financial institutions supporting VulcanForms’ manufacturing expansion.

J.P. Morgan’s involvement is connected to JPMorganChase’s Security and Resiliency Initiative.

The initiative focuses on strengthening industries and supply chains important to U.S. economic competitiveness and security.

Jay Horine, Global Head of the initiative, highlighted the importance of helping companies move advanced technologies into industrial-scale production.

Apollo is participating through funds managed by the firm.

John Zito, Co-President of Apollo Asset Management, emphasized the significant capital requirements associated with rebuilding American industrial capacity.

He described the transaction as an example of how private capital can help finance large-scale manufacturing expansion.

The $500 million agreement reflects the substantial investment required to establish and operate advanced production facilities.

VulcanForms believes its existing commercial demand and integrated manufacturing technology provide a foundation for deploying the additional capital.

More Than $1 Billion Raised To Date

Founded out of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2015, VulcanForms has developed technology intended to make domestic production of sophisticated metal components more competitive.

The company has raised more than $1 billion in funding to date from lenders and investors.

Its financial backers include Eclipse, J.P. Morgan, Apollo, Hudson Bay Capital, D1 Capital, and 1789 Capital.

VulcanForms has expanded from an advanced manufacturing technology developer into an operator of industrial production infrastructure.

Its strategy emphasizes bringing manufacturing activities back to the United States while supporting high-value industrial employment.

The company believes domestic production of complex components can strengthen supply-chain resilience and reduce dependence on overseas manufacturing.

Its expansion comes as U.S. businesses seek additional production capacity for strategically important industries.

Scaling The Next Generation Of American Manufacturing

VulcanForms intends to use its new financing to increase production capacity and improve the efficiency of its digital manufacturing platform.

The three planned factories will expand the company’s ability to serve customers requiring complex, high-performance metal components.

Additional investment in physical AI and automation will support the development of more integrated manufacturing operations.

The company believes these technologies can help make domestic manufacturing competitive on cost, quality, and production scale.

Its strategy also supports broader efforts to strengthen U.S. industrial capacity in sectors including aerospace, defense, healthcare, and advanced technology.

With $500 million in new financing, more than $1 billion raised to date, and multibillion-dollar commercial programs supporting demand, VulcanForms is advancing its transition toward larger-scale industrial production.

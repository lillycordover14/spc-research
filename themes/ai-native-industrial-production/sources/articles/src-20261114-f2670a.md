Pentagon policy chief calls U.S. submarines ‘indispensable’ for Pacific deterrence

Saturday, 14 November 2026, 05:06

Illustrative photo. Source: .Petty Officer 1st Class Chad J. McNeeley, U.S. Navy / Wikimedia Commons.

Elbridge Colby made the remarks Thursday at a Naval Submarine League address attended by submarine contractors and Navy officials.

Colby, the undersecretary of defense for policy, said U.S. Navy submarines are an “indispensable” part of the U.S. arsenal for deterring adversaries in the Pacific. He did not mention China in his address to the Naval Submarine League, an industry group.

This is reported by 
Bloomberg
.

“In a region defined by distance and maritime geography, submarines complicate an adversary’s plans in ways few other capabilities can even approach,”

 - Elbridge Colby

“Moreover, as the sea surface becomes more observable and more contested, the undersea domain takes on even greater strategic value.”

 - Elbridge Colby

By R.L. Bynum

Caleb Love turned his first weekend with the Delaware Blue Coats into a scoring showcase, pouring in 57 points over two games and taking over the fourth quarter Saturday.

Love scored 21 of his 35 points in the final period as Delaware rallied past the Maine Celtics 130–119. The 25-year-old guard shot 10 of 20 from the floor, including seven 3-pointers, and helped hand Maine its first loss of the season.

That followed a 22-point performance Friday, when Love shot 8 of 15 in 32 minutes during Delaware’s 142–116 loss to the Celtics. He collected two steals in each game.

Love averaged 28.5 points over the two games, shooting 51.4% from the field and 40.9% from 3-point range.

His big weekend followed Coby White’s best scoring performance of the young NBA season.

White scored a season-high 21 points Thursday in the Charlotte Hornets’ 101–96 loss at Phoenix. He shot 8 of 16 from the floor, including two 3-pointers, in just under 20 minutes.

He added three rebounds and three assists but committed four turnovers. White is averaging 9.8 points through five games while shooting 31.5% from the field.

Brooklyn center Day’Ron Sharpe had 16 points, 11 rebounds and five assists in 27 minutes Saturday, but the Nets lost 129–110 at Memphis. Sharpe shot 7 of 14 from the floor.

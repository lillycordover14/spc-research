The Kasper Brothers are officially All Elite. 
After the Young Bucks beat Mark Davis and Jake Doyle at Full Gear on Saturday to retain the AEW World Tag Team Championships, the Kasper Brothers arrived for their AEW debut. 
As the Young Bucks stood tall in the ring following their win, the Kasper Brothers arrived and attacked them in a shocking appearance that comes not long after they exited WWE following the expiration of their contracts.

The Kasper Brothers (Jacob and Drew Kasper), known as the Creed Brothers in WWE, exited the company earlier this month after six years following the expiration of their contracts. 
Fightful's 
Sean Ross Sapp
 reported on Nov. 6 that their exit "appears to be a mutual parting of ways." 
The pair had been a staple on WWE NXT, most recently competing in the 2026 Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic in September. Their final match was a loss to Hank & Tank in the quarterfinals of the tournament in October. 
So now the Kasper Brothers move to AEW, where they are clearly hoping to get a piece of the Young Bucks and perhaps challenge them for the AEW Tag Team Title in the future. 
As for Saturday's match, Davis and Doyle earned a title opportunity in shocking fashion, as they defeated The Young Bucks in a title eliminator match on the Oct. 21 episode of Dynamite.
Beating The Bucks in any manner would have been a huge deal, but Davis and Doyle decimated Nick and Matt Jackson in a matter of only a few minutes, making it even more impressive.
Just a few days earlier, The Young Bucks defended the AEW World Tag Team Championships against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight at WrestleDream in a match that clearly took a lot out of them.
Davis and Doyle took advantage of the situation and benefited from the damage their Don Callis Family stablemates had done to The Bucks.
As impactful as that victory was in terms of earning them a title opportunity at Full Gear, Davis and Doyle took it a step further a couple of weeks later.
On the Nov. 4 edition of Dynamite, Davis and Doyle were part of The Don Callis Family's team in Blood & Guts, and they defeated a team that included The Young Bucks.
That gave Davis and Doyle two meaningful victories over Nick and Matt Jackson over the course of a few weeks, securing them all the momentum they could ask for ahead of Full Gear.
Despite that, The Young Bucks rose to the occasion and retained their titles, ensuring their continued reign over a hugely competitive tag team division.

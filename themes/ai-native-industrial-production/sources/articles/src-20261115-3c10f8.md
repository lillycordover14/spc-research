Kasper Brothers (Fka Creeds In WWE) Make AEW Debut After Full Gear Tag Title Defense

The Kasper Brothers, formerly known as the Creeds in WWE, made a surprise debut at AEW Full Gear, confronting the Young Bucks after they retained the World Tag Team titles over Mark Davis and Jake Doyle. 

Davis and Doyle started out dominating over the Bucks, much like their Eliminator to get the title shot. The champions sought to get back in the match at times in the early stages but were quickly swatted back down by their challengers. Though they did sacrifice what could have been an early win by gloating and goading their opponents. 

As the match went on, the Bucks got back into the affair, surviving several near-falls before eventually securing the winning pinfall. They didn't have long to celebrate with their titles, however, as number one contenders, the Motor City Machine Guns, came down to the ring to confront them. 

The two teams started jawing before an unfamiliar entrance theme started, preceding the entrance of the debuting Kasper Brothers. They came down to the ring and immediately attacked the Bucks, prompting MCMG to try and put a stop to the ambush. FTR then came down to the ring and joined forces with the Kaspers in attacking the Bucks and MCMG. 

The Kasper Brothers ended their six-year tenure with WWE
 as the Creed Brothers on November 6, electing not to renew their contracts as they expired. During their time with the promotion they held NXT Tag Team Championship once.

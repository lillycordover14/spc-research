Benjamin Hall is UNC’s Player of the Game against Louisville
The redshirt junior running back set a new career mark.
by
 
 
Matt Bowling
Nov 15, 2026, 4:00 PM UTC
Another day, another frustrating game for Carolina fans.
Much like men’s basketball’s loss to Georgia on Friday night, this was a very winnable matchup for the Tar Heels that they let get away from them down the stretch. While it was pretty easy to give 
Terrence Brown player of the game
 against the Bulldogs, I had to think a little harder about Saturday’s selection.
Ultimately, I decided Benjamin Hall deserved the nod for punching in both of UNC’s touchdowns–the first game of his college career he’s gotten in the end zone multiple times. Hall put the Tar Heels up 7-3 with an 11-yard run late in the first quarter and cut Louisville’s lead to 16-14 on a 6-yard carry with just under four minutes left in the game. He finished with a team-high 48 rushing yards on 10 carries.
The reason I had to think about it is because I was tempted to go with Travis Burgess. Pressed into his first college action after Billy Edwards was knocked out with an injury six minutes into the third quarter, the true freshman nearly threw a touchdown on his first play and continued to show flashes of his potential the rest of the game. He couldn’t quite make all of the throws he needed to make down the stretch, but he did give us reason to believe Carolina’s run of mediocre-to-bad quarterback play will be coming to an end soon. That by itself is enough to get him props from me.

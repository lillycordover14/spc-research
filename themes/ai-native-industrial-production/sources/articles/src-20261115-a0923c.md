The

 managed to beat the North Carolina Tar Heels 16-14 after a late interception ended UNC's hopes of taking the lead.
 
 
While that sounds exciting, it's no match for the all-out brawl that erupted as the clock wound down to zero.
With just over a minute to go, 
UNC's
 Travis Burgess seemed to have thrown a pass that would move the Tar Heels close to midfield; however, it wound up in the arms of a Cardinal.

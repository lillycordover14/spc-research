Botello, Jipp Claim Titles; 12 Tar Heels Finish In Top Three At Mountaineer Invitational
November 15, 2026 
 | 
Wrestling
Share:
BOONE, N.C. – 
The North Carolina wrestling duo of 
Matthew Botello
 and 
Lincoln Jipp
 captured championships Sunday at the Mountaineer Invitational, hosted by App State.

 

The pair led the Tar Heels to 12 top-three finishes, including six runner-up finishes and four additional third-place finishes.

 

Joining the two champions on the podium were No. 9 
Brendan McCrone
 (125), 
Nate Askew
 (149), No. 25 
Laird Root
 (157), 
Joey Showalter
 (165) and 
Mikey Calcagno
 (197), who each finished runner-up in the invite bracket. 
Omaury Alvarez
 (165) placed second in the open bracket. 
Nadav Nafshi
 (174), 
Nolan O'Boyle
 (184) and 
Robert Platt
 (285) each finished third in the invite bracket, while 
Dunia Sibomana
 earned a third-place finish in the open division.

 

Carolina will open its home schedule Saturday, Nov. 21, against No. 22 Northern Iowa. Action between the Tar Heels and Panthers is set for 3 p.m. inside Carmichael Arena.

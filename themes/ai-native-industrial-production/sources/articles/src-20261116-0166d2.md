   Significant progress against cost reduction efforts positions business for sustained success
  

   LanzaTech achieves world's first ISCC EU certification for recycled carbon fuels from steel mill waste gases, opening a path to profit potential in European fuel markets
  

  SKOKIE, Ill., Nov.  16, 2026  (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- LanzaTech Global, Inc. (NASDAQ: LNZA) (“LanzaTech” or the “Company”), a carbon management solutions company, today reported its financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
 

   Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operational Highlights (comparisons vs. Third Quarter 2025)
  

   Revenue of $9.5 million, up 2% compared to $9.3 million.
  

   Operating Expenses of $11.7 million, down 35% compared to $18.0 million.
  

   Net loss of $178.0 million, compared to a net income of $2.9 million, primarily reflecting a $166.4 million non-cash unrealized fair value loss on the Company's investment in SGLT.
  

   Adjusted EBITDA loss of $8.8 million, improved by approximately 35% compared to $13.5 million.
   

   Management Comments:
  

  Dr. Jennifer Holmgren, CEO of LanzaTech, stated, “Our third quarter results show continued improvement in our financial and operating performance, building on the progress we have made throughout 2026. The actions we took to reshape LanzaTech are now showing up in our results quarter after quarter; we have held quarterly operating expenses steady for multiple quarters, which tells us the lower cost base we built is durable and sustainable. Our cost structure is now reset for where we are headed operationally and strategically, and LanzaTech now has a much improved position upon which to advance its platform toward commercialization and long-term profitability.”
 

  Dr. Holmgren continued, “We are building a business that can monetize waste carbon across multiple end markets rather than depend on a single route to market. This quarter, Hebei Shoulang, a commercial facility using LanzaTech technology, achieved the world’s first ISCC EU Recycled Carbon Fuel certification for ethanol made from steel mill waste gases. This creates a near-term pathway into European road and marine fuel markets, and we have begun commercial discussions with major energy and chemicals companies to convert certification into revenue. We expect a second facility to be certified in the fourth quarter. Alcohol-to-jet remains a key source of long-term upside, linking our ethanol to demand for sustainable aviation fuel. LanzaJet’s recapitalization reinforces our commitment to that opportunity, with Freedom Pines Fuels central to scaling the pathway. These are complementary routes to value: we can pursue road and marine opportunities today while developing aviation upside over time. Our focus is on turning certification, commercial execution and disciplined investment into revenue growth and sustainable profitability.”
 

   Key Strategic and Operational Updates:
  

   Achieved first ISCC EU RCF certification in September: Hebei Shoulang New Energy Technology Co., Ltd., in Tangshan, China, became the world’s first ISCC EU-certified Recycled Carbon Fuel (RCF) producer for ethanol made from steel mill waste gases using LanzaTech technology. The certificate was published on September 15, 2026. Certification provides verified sustainability, lifecycle greenhouse-gas and chain-of-custody credentials, creating a pathway to regulated European fuel markets, subject to end-market requirements.
  

   Second certification expected in Q4: The Company expects a second facility to achieve ISCC EU certification in the fourth quarter of 2026. Building on the September milestone, this would broaden the certified production base supporting European road-fuel market access and further commercial opportunities. European road-fuel compliance value adds revenue potential: Germany’s GHG-based THG-Quote framework includes RCFs among statutory compliance options from the 2027 obligation year, subject to implementing rules, pathway eligibility and the EU’s 70% GHG-saving threshold. Because compliance value is linked to verified lifecycle emissions reductions, demonstrated through certification, greater GHG savings can generate greater credit value, creating potential revenue beyond the physical ethanol sale.
  

   LanzaJet Financing and Recapitalization — LanzaJet financing and expected ownership increase: In October, LanzaJet completed an initial $10.0 million financing and secured commitments for an additional $10.0 million, subject to certain closing conditions, to support its next phase of growth and operations at Freedom Pines Fuels in Soperton, Georgia. An initial closing occurred in October, with completion of the financing expected by December 2026. As part of the broader recapitalization, LanzaTech is expected to significantly increase its ownership of LanzaJet. The financing is at LanzaJet, rather than a cash raise by LanzaTech, and supports the ATJ platform’s continued development and commercial scale-up.
  

   Complementary direct-use and ATJ pathways: Certified CarbonSmart ethanol creates near-term opportunities in road and marine fuel markets without requiring an alcohol-to-jet conversion step. Alongside these opportunities, LanzaJet’s ATJ technology and Freedom Pines Fuels (FPF) remain important parts of LanzaTech’s strategic portfolio and longer-term SAF upside. This portfolio approach supports direct-use commercialization today while preserving participation in the growth of aviation fuels as ATJ production scales, subject to operational execution, customer requirements and applicable regulation.

The improvement reflected lower operating expenses and a $41.7 million non-cash fair value gain on the Company’s equity securities in SGLT, partially offset by lower other income and higher losses from equity method investees.    Revenue
  

   Reported total revenue of $9.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to total revenue of $9.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase primarily reflected higher CarbonSmart product sales and collaborative-arrangement revenue, partially offset by lower revenue from engineering and other services.
   

     CarbonSmart revenue was $5.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $3.0 million in the third quarter of 2025, driven by expanded commercialization.
     

     JDA revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was $0.9 million, compared to $0.1 million in the third quarter of 2025, reflecting new projects with existing customers.
    

     Engineering and other services revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was $2.3 million, compared to $4.0 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to completion of projects with existing customers.
    

     Contract research revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was $0.6 million, compare to $1.1 million in the third quarter of 2025.
    

     Licensing revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was $0.6 million, compared to $1.1 million in the third quarter of 2025, due to the decrease in licensing revenue recognized from LanzaJet for their sublicensing of our technology.
     

   Cost of Revenue
  

   Cost of revenue was $7.9 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $6.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase reflects a $2.1 million increase in costs associated with CarbonSmart product sales, partially offset by a $1.1 million decrease in engineering and other service costs associated with existing and new customers and government entities.
   

   Operating Expense
  

   For the third quarter of 2026 operating expense was $11.7 million, as compared to $18.0 million for the same period in the prior year. The decrease was primarily due to a decrease in personnel and contractor expenses related to R&D projects and administrative operations, reflecting headcount reductions implemented during 2025 as part of the Company’s broader cost optimization initiatives. These reductions are designed to align the Company’s cost base with its commercialization priorities and support its path to profitability.
   

   Net (loss)
  

   Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $178.0 million, compared with a net income of $2.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. The change was primarily attributable to a $166.4 million noncash fair value loss recognized on the Company's equity securities in SGLT, together with the absence of significant other income recognized in the prior-year period. The effect was partially offset by the improvement in operating loss. The quarter also benefited from the impact of the Company's transformation, cost optimization and organizational streamlining initiatives, which reduced operating expenses and contributed to a significant improvement in Adjusted EBITDA compared with the prior-year period.
   

   Adjusted EBITDA
  

   Adjusted EBITDA loss was $8.8 million as compared to adjusted EBITDA loss of $13.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. The improvement primarily reflecting lower research and development and selling, general and administrative expenses following the Company’s cost-reduction initiatives implemented in 2025. The improvement also benefited from modest revenue growth, partially offset by changes in revenue mix and higher CarbonSmart product costs.
   

   Balance Sheet and Liquidity
  

   As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $36.3 million in total cash and restricted cash compared to total cash, restricted cash, and investments of $17.1 million as of December 31, 2025. The increase reflects our issuance of common stock for gross proceeds of $50.0 million in the nine months ended September 30, 2026, partially offset by continued use of cash to fund operating activities and our $3.0 million purchase of LanzaJet Series A Preferred Stock.

  To supplement our financial statements presented in accordance with GAAP and to provide investors with additional information regarding our financial results, we have presented Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure.   We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss), excluding depreciation expense, interest income, net, stock-based compensation expense, income (loss) from equity method investees, net, unrealized gains and losses arising from the fair value remeasurement, extinguishment, conversion or settlement of financial instruments, including warrant liabilities, the Brookfield SAFE liability, the Brookfield Loan liability, the Convertible Note, and other similar non-cash or non-operating items. We believe Adjusted EBITDA helps identify underlying trends in our business that could otherwise be masked by the effect of certain expenses that we include in net loss. Accordingly, we believe Adjusted EBITDA provides useful information to investors, analysts, and others in understanding and evaluating our operating results and enhancing the overall understanding of our past performance and future prospects.
 

  During the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, Adjusted EBITDA excludes the non-cash unrealized gain recognized upon the remeasurement of the Company's investment in SGLT to fair value.

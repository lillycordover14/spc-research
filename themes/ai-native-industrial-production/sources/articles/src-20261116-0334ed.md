LanzaTech Reports $9.5 Million Revenue in Third Quarter 2026

                
                  By: Quiver FilingTracker
                
            

Posted: 8 hours ago / Nov. 16, 2026 12:55 p.m. UTC

                  LNZAW
                

LanzaTech Global Inc. (NASDAQ: LNZA) reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $9.5 million, up 2% from $9.3 million a year earlier. The company recorded a net loss of $178.0 million, compared with net income of $2.9 million in the prior-year quarter.
The quarterly loss included a $166.4 million non-cash unrealized fair-value loss on the company’s investment in SGLT. Operating expenses fell 35% to $11.7 million.
GAAP basic and diluted loss per share was $13.60.
CarbonSmart revenue rose to $5.1 million from $3.0 million.
Cash and restricted cash totaled $36.3 million as of September 30, 2026.
LanzaTech reiterated full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $50 million to $55 million.
Hebei Shoulang received ISCC EU Recycled Carbon Fuel certification for ethanol made from steel mill waste gases.

[News] Yunnan Germanium Secures Five-Year InP Substrate Supply Deal for 900,000 Wafers

2026-11-16

Semiconductors

                    editor                

 

On November 3, Yunnan Germanium announced that its controlling subsidiary, Yunnan Xinyao Semiconductor Materials, had signed a long-term supply framework agreement and a 2027 purchase contract with U.S. optical communications manufacturer Applied Optoelectronics, Inc. (AOI), securing a substantial order for 4-inch n-type indium phosphide (InP) substrates.

Under the agreement, AOI expects to purchase 180,000 substrates annually from 2027 to 2031, bringing the projected five-year total to 900,000 units. The purchase contract for 2027 alone is valued at USD 194.4 million, excluding tax. This amount represents 122.46% of Yunnan Germanium’s audited revenue in 2025.

InP substrates are essential base materials for EML laser chips and high-speed photodetector chips used in 800G and 1.6T optical modules, making them a critical upstream component in the AI optical interconnect supply chain.

Demand for high-speed optical modules has continued to rise amid the expansion of AI computing infrastructure, while global InP substrate supply remains tight. Optical chip manufacturers have increasingly turned to long-term supply agreements to secure stable material supplies. The latest deal is among the larger overseas long-term orders secured by a Chinese InP substrate supplier.

Listed on Nasdaq, AOI specializes in high-speed optical components and modules for data centers. The company has integrated capabilities spanning laser chip epitaxy and device packaging, with products supplied to cloud service providers worldwide. The latest agreement builds on the two companies’ existing business relationship.

Yunnan Germanium noted that the five-year agreement is a framework arrangement, rather than a firm commitment to purchase the full projected volume. The annual figure of 180,000 substrates represents AOI’s estimated demand. Actual delivery volumes and transaction prices will be subject to subsequent annual contracts, purchase orders and export license approvals for dual-use items.

Yunnan Xinyao is Yunnan Germanium’s subsidiary responsible for the R&D and manufacturing of single-crystal InP substrates. In April this year, it launched an expansion project for high-quality single-crystal InP wafers, which is expected to further increase its substrate production capacity upon completion.

Xinyao had previously disclosed another InP substrate supply order, with deliveries scheduled from August 2026 through the end of 2027. The continued acquisition of overseas orders highlights demand for InP materials as high-speed optical communications infrastructure expands.

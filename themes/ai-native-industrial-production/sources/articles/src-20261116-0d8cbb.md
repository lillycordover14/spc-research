MP Materials' Operating Loss Narrows in Q3: Is There a Recovery Ahead?
3 min read
MP
MP Materials Corp. 
MP reported an operating loss of $22 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared with the year-ago loss of $67 million. Despite the narrower loss, the company extended its streak of operating losses to 13 consecutive quarters, highlighting ongoing margin pressure as it continues transitioning toward higher-value separated rare earth products.
During the quarter, revenues surged 176% year over year, fueled by higher neodymium-praseodymium (NdPr oxide) and metal sales and stronger pricing. However, cost of sales climbed 76.6% due to higher sales volumes of NdPr oxide and metal. Selling, general and administrative expenses rose 33.8% due to increased personnel costs associated with downstream expansion.
Start-up costs also climbed significantly to around $14.2 million from $1.41 million a year earlier as initial magnet manufacturing activities gathered pace. The increase in operating expenses was partially offset by higher revenues and price protection agreement (PPA) related income with the Department of War (DoW).
This cost trend has been building as MP accelerated its shift toward separated rare earth production. These products carry higher per-unit costs than rare earth concentrates because of the additional processing involved. Key cost components include chemical reagents, labor, maintenance and other consumables. In 2024, the cost of sales nearly doubled year over year to $192.6 million. In 2025, the cost of sales remained elevated at $192.8 million.
Operating expenses have also trended higher. SG&A expenses rose 5% in 2024 and surged a further 35% in 2025, driven by workforce expansion to support downstream growth initiatives. MP Materials thus reported an operating loss of $169 million in 2024 and $149.4 million in 2025.
Producing separated rare earth products and magnetic materials involves significantly higher costs than concentrate production due to additional processing requirements, chemical inputs, labor and maintenance. Cost of sales is thus expected to trend higher, reflecting increased sales of NdPr oxide and metal along with added costs associated with magnetic precursor products. Start-up costs are also likely to increase in the coming quarters.
Nevertheless, NdPr production volumes continue to rise as process optimization and ramp-up efforts progress. Higher output, increased sales volumes and the PPA will offset some of the margin pressure.
How Are MP Material’s Peers Faring?
Energy Fuels
 UUUU reported an operating loss of $25.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, narrower than the loss of around $26.7 million reported in the year-ago quarter. The improvement was primarily supported by higher revenues.
Costs applicable to revenues declined 12% year over year to $11.3 million. However, total operating costs and expenses increased 23% year over year to $54.5 million in the third quarter of 2026. Energy Fuels has reported adjusted operating losses for the last 10 consecutive quarters.
USA Rare Earth Inc.
 USAR reported revenues of $3 million in the third quarter of 2026, of which $2.8 million came entirely from Less Common Metals, acquired in 2025. USAR began generating revenues from its magnet manufacturing operations in the third quarter of 2026, which were reported at $0.2 million for the quarter.
Cost of revenues was reported at $22.9 million. Selling, general and administrative increased 808%, due to higher legal and consulting costs and increased headcount. Research and development expenses were up 271% on higher development and facility costs. USA Rare Earth consequently reported an operating loss of $141.4 million in the quarter compared with $15.9 million in the year-ago quarter.
MP’s Price Performance, Valuation & Estimates
MP Materials’ shares have declined 32% in a year against the industry’s 15.3% growth.
MP is trading at a forward 12-month price/sales multiple of 10.62X, a significant premium to the industry’s 1.46X.
The Zacks Consensus Estimate for MP Materials’ 2026 earnings is currently pegged at a loss of 3 cents per share, narrower than the loss of 24 cents reported in 2025. For 2027, earnings are expected to reach 66 cents per share.
The estimate for both years has moved down in the past 60 days.
The company currently carries a Zacks Rank #3 (Hold).

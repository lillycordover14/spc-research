BioStock publishes Consensus Analysis for Novo: six of twelve analyst houses raised their price target
1 min read
NOVO_B
Today, 16 November 2026, BioStock published a Consensus Analysis for Novo – an editorial format that compiles and analyses the analyst panel’s combined view of listed life science companies in connection with quarterly reports. Following Novo’s Q3 report, six of the twelve included analyst houses have raised their share price targets, two have lowered them and four have left them unchanged.
In its Q3 report, Novo raised its outlook for 2026. Adjusted sales rose 4 per cent at constant exchange rates to 79.2 billion DKK, and adjusted operating profit increased 27 per cent to 31.7 billion DKK. Price targets range from 223 DKK (Bernstein, Sell) to 390 DKK (Nordea, Buy). The median of 301 DKK sits 8.1 per cent above the closing price of 278.45 DKK on 13 November, and the mean of 300.2 DKK implies 7.8 per cent upside.
The largest raises came from the most negative houses. Bernstein raised its target by 20 DKK and Deutsche Bank by 15 DKK. Both kept 
Sell

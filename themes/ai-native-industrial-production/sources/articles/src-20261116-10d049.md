Aaron Glenn made it clear that Geno Smith will remain the Jets starter when healthy — and that appears to be the case.

Smith left the Jets’ 31-12 loss to the Bills twice Sunday. He first was 
checked for a concussion at halftime
 after a vicious hit near the end of the first half. Smith returned to the game after getting cleared but then injured his ankle later in the third quarter and did not return to the game. 

Glenn said Smith would undergo an MRI exam Monday and ESPN later reported that 
the injuries were not serious.

“He’s good. He’ll be fine,” a source told the network.

Rookie Cade Klubnik 
saw his first NFL action Sunday
 after Smith’s injury and would start if Smith can’t this week against the Chargers. 

Some Jets fans are clamoring to see Klubnik play even if Smith is healthy. Glenn said that’s not happening. 

“Guys, Geno’s our starter,” Glenn told reporters Monday. “Come on now. Let’s not do that.”

								How the rest of Jets season could shake out — and what it means for draft and Aaron Glenn							

The Jets drafted Klubnik in the fourth round of this year’s draft out of Clemson. He had some good moments Sunday, running for two first downs and completing a few deep passes. Overall, he was shaky and ended up 11-for-20 passing for 137 yards and one interception. 

While Smith has had a decent season, the argument can be made that with the Jets at 2-8, they should give Klubnik a chance to play so they can evaluate him. Glenn said that is not happening now. 

Advertisement

“I think Geno has had a really good year and I wouldn’t be disrespectful to him to just do that and I think we all understand that,” Glenn said. “I think we all know that. He’s our starter.” 

Glenn was upset that there was not a penalty called near the end of the first half when Bills defenders T.J. Parker and Terrel Bernard hit Smith low and high, knocking him out of the game. Glenn could be seen by TV cameras telling the officials that had Josh Allen been hit like that, a penalty would have been called.

“They didn’t call it,” Glenn said Monday. “I thought it was a call that should be made, but it wasn’t, just move on from there.” 

Smith returned after that big hit but injured his ankle when he was hit by Bills safety Cole Bishop on a blitz.

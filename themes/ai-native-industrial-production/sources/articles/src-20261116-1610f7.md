LanzaTech Global, Inc. reported a significantly wider loss for the third quarter of 2026 than estimated, posting a diluted loss of $13.60 per share compared to analyst estimates of a $0.10 loss per share. The carbon recycling technology company recorded a net loss of $178.0M for the quarter, representing a substantial deterioration from the prior year when it posted diluted earnings of $2.7M.

Revenue came in at $9.5M for the quarter, up 2.0% from the $9.3M recorded in Q3 2025. The modest top-line growth was led by CarbonSmart product sales, which generated $5.1M in revenue, up 71.9% year-over-year. The company reported an adjusted EBITDA loss of $9M for the quarter.

The results highlight the ongoing challenges facing the carbon transformation company as it works to scale its gas fermentation technology that converts industrial emissions into sustainable fuels and chemicals.

What this 
filing
 says
LanzaTech reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $9.5 million, up 2% from a year earlier, and operating expenses fell 35% to $11.7 million. The headline loss came from a $166.4 million non-cash fair value loss on its investment in SGLT, which turned a prior-year net income of $2.9 million into a $178.0 million net loss. For shareholders, the adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed to $8.8 million, but the reported net result is driven by the SGLT valuation swing.

Positive for shareholders
Operating expenses fell 35% year over year, reflecting cost reduction and streamlining.
Adjusted EBITDA loss improved to $8.8 million from $13.5 million a year earlier.
The release says LanzaTech achieved world's first ISCC EU certification for recycled carbon fuels from steel mill waste gases, which it links to a path toward profit potential in European fuel markets.
Negative for shareholders
The $166.4 million non-cash SGLT fair value loss drove the Q3 net loss to $178.0 million.
Revenue rose only 2%, and the release notes lower engineering and other services revenue.
The company says it cannot reasonably predict future fair value changes in its SGLT investment and financial instrument liabilities, which could make GAAP results differ significantly from adjusted EBITDA.

Key terms
Adjusted EBITDA
: 
A non-GAAP profit measure that excludes interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization and certain other items, so it differs from reported net income.
Fair value loss
: 
A paper loss recorded when an investment is marked down to its current estimated market value, without any cash leaving the company.
ISCC EU certification
: 
A sustainability certification under the EU framework that verifies renewable and recycled fuels meet defined carbon and sourcing standards.
Unreasonable-efforts exception
: 
An SEC allowance that lets a company skip a reconciliation of a forward-looking non-GAAP measure to GAAP when the figures cannot be predicted without unreasonable effort.
FAQ
Why did LanzaTech post a net loss in the third quarter of 2026?
The release attributes the $178.0 million net loss mainly to a $166.4 million non-cash fair value loss on its equity securities in SGLT, together with the absence of the significant other income recorded in the prior-year period.
Did LanzaTech's operating performance improve?
Yes. Operating expenses fell 35% to $11.7 million and adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed to $8.8 million from $13.5 million, though revenue grew only 2% to $9.5 million.
Will the SGLT valuation affect LanzaTech's future results?
The release says future fair value changes in the SGLT investment and related financial instrument liabilities depend on market conditions that the company cannot reasonably predict, and they could cause GAAP results to differ significantly from adjusted EBITDA.

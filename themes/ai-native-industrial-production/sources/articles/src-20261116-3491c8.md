Israeli figure skater Tamir Kuperman, 19, won the bronze medal at Skate America in Everett, Wash., on Saturday with 248.55 points in his senior Grand Prix debut.
Kuperman climbed from seventh place after the short program to third overall, scoring 165.42 points in the free skate to secure a place on the podium.

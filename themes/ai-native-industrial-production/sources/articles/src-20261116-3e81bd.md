Anduril Industries Eyeing IPO Within Next Few Years

Co-founder Luckey favors eventual public listing 

By
 
Kevin Costelloe

November 16, 2026
 

26

Facebook
Twitter
Linkedin
Email
Print

Palmer Luckey
Co-Founder
Anduril Industries

Anduril Industries said it’s looking to go public “in the next few years,” Chief Business Officer Matt Steckman said.

“We’ll IPO in the next few years,” Steckman told a news reporter, according to partial a transcript of the conversation sent to the Business Journal today. “
We have an internal team that has a plan, has a budget, and is getting us all ready for it. We literally couldn’t even do it tomorrow, even if we wanted to. But we want to be in the position where over the next couple of years we can always click the button if we want to.”

For investors, a potential IPO could offer access to a growing defense company seeking to disrupt an industry dominated by established players. Its prospects would depend partly on converting contract awards into sustained revenue while expanding production,” Seeking Alpha said today.

Anduril co-founder Palmer Luckey has said repeatedly he plans to take the Costa Mesa-based company public without specifying a time frame.

For further details on Anduril, see the Nov. 23 edition of the Business Journal.

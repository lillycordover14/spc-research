MILTON — Gail Poole said the Getting Ahead in the Valley program turned her life around.
The 65-year-old Lewisburg woman, a 2020 graduate of the program, told its 2026 graduates on Sunday afternoon that she is a recovering addict who had gone through a bad marriage before going through the 19-week program. She now owns her own apparel pressing and printing business and is working on her GED (General Educational Development) diploma.

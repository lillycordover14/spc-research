BLACKSBURG – 
Virginia Tech head coach 
James Franklin
 spoke to the media Monday morning following the Hokies’ 40-22 loss to Stanford this past weekend. The Hokies fly to South Florida for a matchup with No. 5 Miami on Saturday Nov. 21 night. Kickoff is set for 7 p.m. ET.

Head Coach 
James Franklin
Opening statement:
“I want to start by thanking the fans. We've had great support all year long. Saturday night was a night game, it was cold and windy. The support was phenomenal. We did not play up to our standard, and I want to make sure everybody understands we embrace and accept that responsibility. I accept that responsibility. But that doesn't go without me taking the time to thank the fans. I also want to give Stanford credit. They played well. We did not play winning football, but they played well. They forced some of those situations. We turned the ball over three times in the first half, and that's not a recipe for success. It never has been. You lose three to zero in the turnover battle, and all of it in the first half, and get down by 21 points. It's going to make it very challenging and difficult. Excited about the opportunity for this week to go out and get better. I was pleased with our practice on Sunday and how the players handled and approached things. I appreciate the support that we get. Although we have not played well the past two weeks, it does not change what we're building here and what we're doing here. There's been so many positive things since we've arrived here in a short period of time. But I also recognize Saturday did not demonstrate that. We take responsibility and ownership for that. Excited to play against a really good Miami team, and everything that comes with that is a tremendous opportunity against a good team. You've got to give (Miami Head Coach Mario Cristobal) a ton of credit and [what he's been able to do in five years.] He has really built that program. They've done a phenomenal job, and we're looking forward to the opportunity.”

On what makes Darian Mensah and Miami's offense unique:
“If you look at them, it's the combination of talent they've recruited and scheme. They do a really good job with scheme and balance. With the quarterback that they have and the wide receivers they have, it would be easy just to say, ‘We're going to throw the ball every down.’ They're able to run the ball and throw the ball. I actually think, in some ways, the loss of (Miami wideout Malachi Toney) is going to help them because it is going to force them to develop other aspects of their offense. When he comes back, they're going to be even more dangerous. Whenever you have a quarterback that's as experienced as their quarterback is and the talent they've been able to surround him with, the consistency of their staff and what they're doing schematically to cause challenges, that's what makes it so difficult."
James Franklin Press Conference (11/16/26)

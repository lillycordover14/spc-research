                            The Gichigamiin Indigenous Nations Museum is open again in Evanston after renovations
                        

The Gichigamiin Indigenous Nations Museum reopened in Evanston on Oct. 1 after renovations. It has now debuted "The Gifts of Our World," a permanent hands-on exhibition for children aged 3 to 10, and currently an "Indigenous Chicago" exhibit tracing more than 500 years of native history in the region. "Indigenous Chicago" will be on display for the next three years.

The Safe Air Company appoints Hashtag Orange as integrated marketing partner
                        
Hashtag Orange will handle the Delhi-NCR launch of Safe Air Haven, managing consumer research, brand positioning, social media, performance marketing, CRM integration and lead generation.

Hashtag Orange has secured the consolidated marketing mandate for The Safe Air Company to manage the launch and market development of its flagship product, Safe Air Haven, in Delhi-NCR.
The mandate covers consumer research, brand positioning, communication strategy, social media, full-funnel marketing and performance-led demand generation. The agency is expected to also develop landing pages, integrate the company's customer relationship management (CRM) system with Zoho CRM and manage lead nurturing and consumer enquiries through the conversion process.
The engagement will focus on positioning whole-home air protection as an alternative to room-specific air purification. Safe Air Haven is a centralized air protection system designed to provide filtered fresh air across living spaces through multi-stage filtration and continuous airflow.
Commenting on the partnership, 
Advitya Dewan
, Director of The Safe Air Company, said, “As awareness around air quality grows, we believe the conversation needs to evolve beyond outdoor pollution and individual room purification to a more holistic understanding of the air we breathe inside our homes. Safe Air Haven represents a different approach to indoor air protection. We are pleased to partner with Hashtag Orange to build this category, strengthen consumer understanding and create a connected marketing ecosystem that takes the proposition from awareness to adoption, beginning with Delhi NCR.”
Mukesh Vij
, Founder, Hashtag Orange, added, “What makes this engagement particularly exciting is that we are not just launching a brand or marketing a product; we are helping create a new consumer category. Moving from room-based purification to whole-home air protection requires a fundamental shift in how consumers understand the problem, evaluate solutions, and make purchase decisions. Our mandate reflects how we see integrated marketing evolving: combining consumer insight, brand building, content, media, performance marketing, technology and lead management into one connected system. The objective is to ensure that the journey from creating awareness to enabling a consumer's purchase decision is thoughtfully designed and managed.”
The mandate includes managing the consumer journey from initial awareness and product consideration to lead generation and potential purchase, with the initial market focus on Delhi-NCR.

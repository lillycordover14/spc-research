2027 TULIP QUEEN AND ROYAL COURT ANNOUNCED&nbsp;&nbsp;KBOE Radio

[page text]
PELLA – The 2027 Tulip Queen and Royal Court were selected in Pella over the weekend.

Lucy Carlson was selected as the 2027 Tulip Queen, and she will be joined by court members Bella De Haan, Ella Hodgell, Brooklyn Sorheim, and Cami Smith.

The group will make dozens of appearances in the spring to promote Pella’s annual Tulip Festival, including appearances at the Capitol, local media, civic organizations, and more.

Pella’s 2027 Tulip Time Festival is scheduled for May 6-8, 2027.

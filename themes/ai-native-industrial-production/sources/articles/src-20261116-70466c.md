CROSS COUNTRY RUNNING

Page instrumental in Swarthmore’s region title defense, qualifies for D3 nationals with Moore and Hitchcock
 by 
Arielle Himelbloom
 | 
Nov 15, 2026
 | 
Cover Story
, 
Cross Country Running

Elizabeth Page helped Swarthmore net back-to-back Metro Regional titles, auto-qualifying for nationals. Photo by Jason Karch

One year after helping Swarthmore capture its first-ever regional title, Elizabeth Page and the Garnet ran it back, winning their second consecutive title and qualifying for the NCAA Division 3 Cross Country Championships.

Page, a junior, finished third at the Metro Region Cross Country Championships in Bethlehem, Penn., to earn all-region honors, covering the 6-kilometer course in 22 minutes flat.

The Garnet put six runners in the top-20 to amass just 43 points — 40 points ahead of runner-up The College of New Jersey — to secure an automatic bid to nationals.

Page, a former South High standout, moved into third during the third kilometer and held her position for the remainder of the race, while averaging a brisk pace of 5:54 per mile.

Swarthmore took the lead for good in the team race after the third kilometer, while teammate Charlotte Martin won the race in 21:49.

Elizabeth Page runs alongside teammate Charlotte Martin at the Metro Region Championships. Photo by Jason Karch

Page’s placement marks her highest at a regional championship, after taking 11th as a freshman and 12th as a sophomore.

The Garnet, which took 32nd at nationals in their debut last fall, and Page, who was 267th, are poised to make waves this time around.

This time, however, Page won’t be the only Alaskan at Division 3 nationals.

She’ll be joined by Puget Sound’s Emily Moore of Eagle River and St. Olaf’s Connor Hitchcock of Sitka.

Moore, a junior, placed 24th at the West Region Cross Country Championships in Spokane Valley, Wash., stopping the clock in 22:25 for 6K.

Emily Moore snagged the final at-large spot in the West region to qualify for nationals. Photo courtesy of Puget Sound XC/TF

The former Chugiak High standout was the top finisher for the Loggers, which placed eighth.

Moore was initially a long shot at qualifying, despite improving from 30th as a sophomore and 60th as a freshman, but as the auto-qualifiers and at-large bids were announced, she ended up snagging the seventh and final individual spot out of the West region.

The feat is historic for Puget Sound, which last sent a woman to cross country nationals in 2018, and a fitting way to cap off Moore’s season.

Hitchcock, a sophomore, placed 10th at the North Region Cross Country Championships in Northfield, Minn., to net all-region honors while clocking a personal best of 25:07 for 8K.

The former Sitka High standout has been firing on all cylinders this season and had a breakout race in his regional debut for St. Olaf.

Hitchcock moved up steadily throughout the race, coming through the first kilometer in the top-40 and eventually making his way into the top-10 in the final kilometer. His eighth kilometer split of 2:29 was the fastest out of the 222-person field.

Connor Hitchcock placed 10th at the North Region Championships, leading St. Olaf to qualify for nationals via an at-large bid. Photo by Hannah Robb

When the dust settled, the Oles placed third with 110 points, trailing only Wis.-La Crosse and Wis.-Stevens Point, with the former netting the auto-qualifier.

Stevens Point and St. Olaf were later selected as at-large qualifiers out of the North.

Hitchcock, who watched the Oles take 13th at nationals last fall, will make his debut in less than one week. He and the rest of the North will have a home-course advantage with nationals being held just a stone’s throw away at Carleton College.

“What a day yesterday!” shared coach Joseph Minor on Instagram. “It was a hard-fought year with a lot of setbacks, but they were able to persevere and make nationals with a new coaching staff and with 6 of the top 7 graduating last year. Very proud!”

The NCAA Division III Cross Country Championships will be held at the Bill Huyck Championship Cross Country Course in Northfield, Minn., on Nov. 21.

At least three more Alaskans competed in two NCAA Division 3 regional championships:

Anchorage’s Eva Marley-Jester (East) and Homer’s Tait Ostrom — freshmen teammates at Lewis & Clark — also competed at the West Region Cross Country Championships. Marley-Jester placed 92nd in 24:41 for 6K, while Ostrom took 115th in 27:17 for 8K.

Homer’s Seamus McDonough competed at the Northeast Region Cross Country Championships. The Bowdoin senior placed 47th in 25:17 for 8K.

Tar Heels Starting Safety Suspended Following Louisville Fight&nbsp;&nbsp;Heel Tough Blog

[page text]
Bob Donnan- Imagn Images
At the conclusion of Saturday night’s game with Louisville, the Tar Heels got into an altercation following the final kneel down of the game. On Monday, the ACC handed down punishments for that fight and, shockingly, only one Tar Heel will miss any playing time because of it.
The ACC announced on Monday morning that starting safety Greg Smith III will miss the first half of Saturday’s trip to Virginia after he got into a fight in the back of the end zone with an unidentified Louisville player.
This is a significant loss for the Tar Heels, but it certainly could have been much worse. So far this season, Smith has been the team’s best defensive back. He has played 686 snaps across the first ten games of the season and has collected 56 total tackles, 3.0 tackles for loss, 1.0 sack, a forced fumble, a fumble recovery, three interceptions and five pass breakups.
With him unavailable for the first half on Saturday in Charlottesville, the team will likely turn to senior Coleman Bryson, who has been playing rotational reps throughout the season alongside Smith. The team will also probably lean on Javion Butts to take on a little more responsibility after he has become a huge piece of the secondary since the Syracuse game.

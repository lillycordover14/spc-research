What this 
release
 says
LanzaTech reported third quarter 2026 revenue of $9.5 million, up 2%, and operating expenses of $11.7 million, down 35% from a year earlier. The net loss of $178.0 million came mainly from a $166.4 million non-cash fair value loss on its SGLT investment, so the headline loss says little about operating performance. Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed to $8.8 million, which shows the cost cuts are holding up for shareholders.

Positive for shareholders
Operating expenses fell 35% from the prior-year quarter, and management says quarterly spending has held steady for several quarters.
Hebei Shoulang achieved the first ISCC EU Recycled Carbon Fuel certification for ethanol made from steel mill waste gases, opening a route into European road and marine fuel markets.
The company says it has begun commercial discussions with major energy and chemicals companies to turn that certification into revenue.
A second facility is expected to be certified in the fourth quarter.
Negative for shareholders
The $166.4 million non-cash loss on the SGLT stake drove a net loss of $178.0 million, against net income of $2.9 million a year earlier.
Revenue grew only 2%, and the company notes changes in revenue mix and higher CarbonSmart product costs.
The company will not provide a GAAP reconciliation of its Q4 and full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance and says the excluded fair-value changes could be material.
Second facility certification is expected, not yet achieved, and revenue from certification is still to be won.

Key terms
Adjusted EBITDA
: 
A non-GAAP profit measure that adds back interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization and certain other items to show operating earnings before those charges.
ISCC EU certification
: 
A sustainability certification under the EU framework that verifies a fuel's renewable or recycled origin, which is needed to sell into EU fuel markets.
Fair value loss
: 
A non-cash reduction in the reported value of an investment when its market-based value falls, recorded in net income even though no cash is paid out.
Alcohol-to-jet
: 
A process that converts ethanol into sustainable aviation fuel.
FAQ
Why did LanzaTech post a net loss of $178.0 million in Q3 2026?
The release attributes the loss mainly to a $166.4 million non-cash fair value loss on the company's equity securities in SGLT. The company says operating performance improved over the same period.
How much did LanzaTech cut operating expenses?
Operating expenses were $11.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, down 35% from $18.0 million in the third quarter of 2025.
What is the ISCC EU certification news for LanzaTech?
Hebei Shoulang, a commercial facility using LanzaTech technology, received the first ISCC EU Recycled Carbon Fuel certification for ethanol made from steel mill waste gases in September. The company says this creates a path into European road and marine fuel markets.

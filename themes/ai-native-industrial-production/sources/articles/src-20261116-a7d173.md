                        Joonho Lee CEO of AxonEQ

-Founded on the accumulated technological prowess of the 'Eco-metallurgy Lab', which has researched AI-based process monitoring and optimization solutions in the steel and battery sectors over the past 10 years

-AxonEQ's solutions serve as a core pillar that imparts 'intelligence' to manufacturing process equipment
                    

AxonEQ is a company that realizes the efficiency of manufacturing processes and the advancement of product quality within the manufacturing industry, powered by advanced data signal processing technologies and real-time AI analytics solutions. AxonEQ was established based on the accumulated technological expertise of the 'Eco-metallurgy Lab', which has spent the past decade researching  IoT-based process monitoring and AI-based optimization solutions in the steel and battery fields. Combining proprietary data signal transmission technology with real-time AI analytical capabilities, the company envisions driving yield improvements and quality advancement across core battery manufacturing processes—including mixing, coating, drying, and assembly. CEO Joonho Lee and CTO Taesu Cheong founded the company in August 2026.
CEO Joonho Lee currently serves as a professor in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Korea University, while CTO Taesu Cheong serves as a professor in the School of Industrial and Management Engineering at Korea University.
“Having grown exponentially over the past decade, the battery industry is now evolving into AI-driven Smart Factories. In particular, to maximize quality and yield across core manufacturing processes such as mixing, coating, drying, and assembly, demand for industrial AI process control technology is surging rapidly. AxonEQ is a process solution company that supports enterprises in strengthening their competitiveness based on AI technology”, explained CEO Joonho Lee.
From its earliest stages, AxonEQ has built a robust industry-academia cooperation network encompassing global equipment manufacturers and South Korea's leading battery enterprises. Through the Korea University LINC 3.0 Project Group, ICC of Korea University signed a technical cooperation MOU with ‘Netzsch’, a world-renowned German precision equipment manufacturer, to jointly develop solutions specialized for the battery industry. This milestone is particularly significant as it preemptively secured a global distribution foundation, enabling the jointly developed process technologies to be supplied to clients worldwide through Netzsch's global sales network. Furthermore, in collaboration with South Korea's leading battery maker, LG Energy Solution, the team has accumulated field-tailored technological capabilities by operating a contract department and conducting joint industry-academia projects over the past five years. Based on a deep understanding of the detailed processes at actual mass-production sites, AxonEQ directly reflects the practical technical requirements needed by battery manufacturers into its solutions.
Regarding future plans, CEO Joonho Lee stated, “Using the startup space secured through Campus Town support as our base, AxonEQ is also actively engaging in industry-academia cooperation and talent cultivation.” He added, “During the past summer break, we successfully operated a field training program for undergraduate students in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at Korea University, supporting students in experiencing the cutting-edge deep-tech development site of a startup firsthand. Going forward, AxonEQ plans to institutionalize practice-oriented student internships and field training programs, and offer full-time employment opportunities to talent with proven capabilities, thereby building a virtuous industry-academia ecosystem.”
Recognized for its outstanding technological prowess and business viability, AxonEQ was selected as a resident company at Korea University Campus Town. “We are deeply grateful to the Korea University Campus Town project for allowing us to nurture our dream of entrepreneurship. In particular, receiving support for a dedicated startup space where we can work freely has been an immense help. Beyond physical space, they continue to help us solidify our business foundation through a wide range of informational and operational support services.”
Date of Establishment: August 2026
Core Business: Application software development and supply; manufacturing of measuring, testing, control, and precision instruments
Achievements: Top Excellence Award in the Pre-Entrepreneur Division (League 2) at the '19th Korea University Campus Town Startup Residency Competition', Selected for the 2026 Laboratory-Specialized Startup Leading University Project (Preliminary Startup Lab Track)

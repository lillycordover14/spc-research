Hashtag Orange bags marketing mandate for The Safe Air Company
                        
The agency will lead the Delhi NCR launch of Safe Air and its flagship whole-home air protection system, Safe Air Haven.

                                                    16 Nov 2026

Hashtag Orange has won the integrated marketing mandate for The Safe Air Company. The agency will lead the Delhi NCR launch and market development of the company's brand and flagship product, Safe Air Haven.
The mandate covers category creation, brand building, demand generation and consumer conversion. Hashtag Orange will work across consumer research, brand communication, social media, performance marketing and lead management to build awareness and drive consideration for the product.
A key focus of the engagement will be to introduce consumers to the concept of whole-home air protection, moving beyond the conventional approach of using air purifiers for individual rooms. Safe Air Haven is designed to provide filtered fresh air across living spaces through a centralised system.
As part of the assignment, Hashtag Orange will study consumer behaviour and map purchase journeys to understand existing perceptions of the category, barriers to adoption and purchase triggers. These insights will guide the brand's positioning, communication strategy and go-to-market plans for Delhi NCR.
The agency will also develop an integrated marketing programme covering brand communication, social media content and full-funnel performance marketing. The mandate includes creating conversion-focused landing pages, integrating the platform with Zoho CRM, and managing lead nurturing and consumer enquiries through calls and other support during the conversion process.
The approach aims to connect different stages of the consumer journey, from initial awareness and product discovery to evaluation and purchase consideration.
The Safe Air Company offers Safe Air Haven, a whole-home air protection system that combines multi-stage filtration with continuous airflow to deliver filtered fresh air across living spaces.
Commenting on the partnership, Advitya Dewan, director, The Safe Air Company, said, “As awareness around air quality grows, we believe the conversation needs to evolve beyond outdoor pollution and individual room purification to a more holistic understanding of the air we breathe inside our homes. Safe Air Haven represents a different approach to indoor air protection. We are pleased to partner with Hashtag Orange to build this category, strengthen consumer understanding and create a connected marketing ecosystem that takes the proposition from awareness to adoption, beginning with Delhi NCR.”
Mukesh Vij, founder, Hashtag Orange, added, “What makes this engagement particularly exciting is that we are not just launching a brand or marketing a product; we are helping create a new consumer category. Moving from room-based purification to whole-home air protection requires a fundamental shift in how consumers understand the problem, evaluate solutions, and make purchase decisions. Our mandate reflects how we see integrated marketing evolving: combining consumer insight, brand building, content, media, performance marketing, technology and lead management into one connected system. The objective is to ensure that the journey from creating awareness to enabling a consumer's purchase decision is thoughtfully designed and managed.”

 reported its financial results for the third quarter of 2026, highlighting a reset of its cost structure and the achievement of critical regulatory milestones. Management stated that quarterly operating expenses have remained stable at $11.7 million for two consecutive quarters, suggesting that previous restructuring efforts have established a sustainable lower cost base. The company secured the first ISCC EU certification for ethanol produced from steel mill waste gases, which management indicated serves as a template for additional facilities to access regulated European road and marine fuel markets. Strategic priorities include increasing ownership in LanzaJet to scale alcohol-to-jet technology and advancing the Ghent facility in Belgium toward a final investment decision. The company confirmed its full-year 2026 financial guidance, which relies on the anticipated completion of its first project transfer under the Brookfield funding arrangement before the end of the year.
Management confirmed the selection of North Sea Port in Ghent, Belgium, as the site for Europe's first commercial-scale alcohol-to-jet SAF facility.
LanzaTech increased its investment in LanzaJet as part of a recapitalization effort, with Dr. Holmgren serving as CEO of the entity to focus on optimizing the Freedom Pines Fuels facility in Georgia.
CEO Holmgren stated, "We have now held quarterly operating expenses at $11.7 million for 2 consecutive quarters, indicating to us that the lower cost base is durable and sustainable."
The company expects to select a contractor for FEED adaptation work at the Ghent facility during the fourth quarter of 2026.
Management noted that the ISCC EU certification allows ethanol to be marketed as recycled carbon fuel in the EU, where the ReFuelEU mandate requires a 6% SAF share by 2030.
CEO Holmgren noted, "Ultimately, we believe this is the key lever in driving value for the company and shareholders," referring to the conversion of the project pipeline into commercial outcomes.
The company is exploring the use of forestry residues in the United States to produce low-cost feedstock, aiming for cost competitiveness with fossil fuels without depending on green premiums.
INDUSTRY GLOSSARY
Alcohol-to-jet (ATJ)
: A process that converts ethanol or other alcohols into sustainable aviation fuel and renewable diesel.
ISCC EU RCF
: International Sustainability and Carbon Certification for Recycled Carbon Fuels, a regulatory standard for fuel sustainability in Europe.
CarbonSmart
: LanzaTech's brand for products and chemicals manufactured from captured waste carbon.
SAF
: Sustainable Aviation Fuel, a low-carbon alternative to traditional petroleum-based jet fuel.
THG-Quote
: A German greenhouse gas reduction quota system that provides financial incentives for low-carbon fuels.
FEED
: Front-End Engineering Design, a basic engineering phase focused on technical requirements and investment cost estimates.
SGLT
: Shougang LanzaTech, a joint venture that operates commercial facilities converting industrial waste gases into ethanol.

                    LanzaTech Global, Inc. reported earnings results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026. For the third quarter, the company reported sales was USD 3.45 million compared to USD 6.25 million a year ago. Revenue was USD 9.51 million compared to USD 9.28 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 177.97 million compared to net income of USD 2.86 million a year ago. Basic loss per share from continuing operations was USD 13.6 compared to basic earnings per share from continuing operations of USD 1.14 a year ago. Diluted loss per share from continuing operations was USD 13.6 compared to diluted earnings per share from continuing operations of USD 0.99 a year ago.
For the nine months, sales was USD 16.31 million compared to USD 14.43 million a year ago. Revenue was USD 30.54 million compared to USD 27.85 million a year ago. Net loss was USD 8.34 million compared to USD 48.87 million a year ago. Basic loss per share from continuing operations was USD 0.76 compared to USD 22.66 a year ago. Diluted loss per share from continuing operations was USD 0.76 compared to USD 22.66 a year ago.

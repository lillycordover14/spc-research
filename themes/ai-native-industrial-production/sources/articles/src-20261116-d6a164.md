Ursa Major Appoints Kevin Fox as Chief Financial Officer

PR Newswire

BERTHOUD, Colo., Nov. 16, 2026

Former Thermon and Frontgrade CFO brings public company and defense industry experience as Ursa Major ramps production

BERTHOUD, Colo.
, 
Nov. 16, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- 
Ursa Major
 today announced the appointment of Kevin Fox as Chief Financial Officer, effective November 9, 2026. Fox will lead financial strategy, capital allocation, accounting, treasury and investor relations across the company.

"Kevin has run finance at a public industrial company and at a defense supplier, and he knows how to build a finance organization that keeps up with a manufacturing business as it grows," said Chris Spagnoletti, Chief Executive Officer of Ursa Major. "He will sharpen our predictability, investments, and execution as we keep delivering for our customers."

Fox joins Ursa Major from Frontgrade Technologies, a privately held provider of mission-critical electronics for space and defense systems, where he served as Chief Financial Officer. His appointment comes as Ursa Major ramps production across its hypersonic and long-range munitions, solid rocket motors and space mobility portfolio and prepares to become a publicly traded company.

"I've spent my career in finance at companies that build complex, essential systems, so it's an honor to step into this role at a time where Ursa Major is maturing from innovative technology development to scalable production," said Fox. "Ursa Major has real customer demand and a team that knows how to deliver. My job is to put our capital behind the programs and initiatives that matter most and build a finance team that can support a rapidly growing business, and we're going to do exactly that."

Fox brings more than two decades of financial leadership in aerospace and defense and industrial markets. Before Frontgrade, he was Chief Financial Officer of publicly traded Thermon, where he led a diversification strategy that delivered revenue growth and profitability improvements. He began his career at General Electric, where he spent nine years in corporate development, M&A and strategy roles after graduating from both the Financial Management Program and Corporate Audit Staff.

Fox succeeds Interim Chief Financial Officer Chip Niemann, who will remain the Company's Chief Accounting Officer.

About Ursa Major

Ursa Major is an advanced weapons and space systems company delivering hypersonic and long-range munitions, solid rocket motors, and space mobility systems to the United States and its allies. Founded in 2015, the company has established a record of flight-proven hypersonic and solid rocket motor performance. Ursa Major uses additive manufacturing, adaptable design, and flexible production methods to build at the speed and scale the mission demands. Headquartered in Berthoud, Colorado, with manufacturing operations in Youngstown, Ohio, Ursa Major is expanding America's capacity to produce critical weapons and space systems. For more information, visit 
www.ursamajor.com
.

SOULCHEF TAKES IT BACK TO HIS ROOTS ON NEW INSTRUMENTAL ALBUM NO PLACE LIKE HOME&nbsp;&nbsp;Babystep Magazine

[page text]
With over 319 million Spotify streams to his name, Auckland producer SoulChef is keeping it soulful on a nostalgic 10-track journey through his hometown.
Auckland beats, golden-hour nostalgia and soulful hip-hop textures collide as SoulChef returns with his latest instrumental album, 
No Place Like Home
, out now via Cinq Music Group.
The New Zealand producer, whose instrumentals have amassed over 319 million Spotify streams and soundtracked 5.9 million TikTok videos, is taking things back to where it all began. Across ten tracks, SoulChef revisits the streets, summers and people that shaped his sound, delivering a record that feels like a love letter to growing up in Auckland.
Built around warm Rhodes chords, soulful horn stabs, dusty vinyl crackle and laid-back boom-bap drums, 
No Place Like Home
 is made for late-night listening, long walks and moments of reflection. Tracks like “Summer ‘95,” “Avondale” and “Maple Street” paint vivid snapshots of home, capturing the beauty of everyday memories without getting lost in nostalgia.
Speaking on the project, SoulChef shares: “No Place Like Home isn’t just about a physical location. It’s about the mindset of where it all started. It’s a tribute to the good times, the environment that shaped my perspective, and the exact moment I fell in love with the craft of making beats.”
The album’s focus track, “Avondale,” takes its name from the walkway featured on the cover artwork, a painted scene inspired by late-90s Auckland suburbia. From corner dairies and summer afternoons to old friends hanging out as the sun goes down, the project captures home as a feeling rather than simply a place.
Staying true to his understated approach, SoulChef lets the music do the talking. “I’ve never needed to be in the picture. That walkway in Avondale says more about me than a photo could. If these beats put you back on your own street, wherever that is, they’ve done their job,” he says.
With 
No Place Like Home
 arriving eight years after his 2018 album 
Home
, the release feels like a full-circle moment for the producer, reconnecting with the sounds and memories that first inspired his craft.
And there's more on the way. SoulChef is also opening pre-orders for a special edition double LP of 
Escapism: Instrumentals
, featuring the record behind “Write This Down (Instrumental).” Pressed to order as a one-time release, signed copies are available until December 15.
With a sound that continues to find listeners across the globe, SoulChef proves that sometimes the most powerful journey is the one that takes you back home.

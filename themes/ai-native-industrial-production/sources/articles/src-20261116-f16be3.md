LanzaTech filed its quarterly report for the period ended September 30, 2026. In May 2026 it sold 2,000,000 shares at $10.00 each in a registered direct offering, raising $20.0 million before placement agent fees and other expenses, which dilutes existing holders. Revenue rose 2% in the quarter and 9.7% over nine months, mainly from new engineering projects and CarbonSmart product sales, partly offset by lower LanzaJet sublicensing and contract research revenue.

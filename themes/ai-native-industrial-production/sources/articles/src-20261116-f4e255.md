CIMC SOE Signs Contracts for Twelve 49,900 DWT MR Product Tankers

11/16/2026

Recently, Nantong CIMC Sinopacific Offshore & Engineering Co., Ltd. (CIMC SOE), a subsidiary of CIMC Enric, signed contract for the construction of ten 49,900 DWT MR product tankers with Singapore-based shipowner Stamford Shipping, as well as contract for two additional vessels of the same type with a prominent European shipowner—bringing the total number of orders for this vessel to 12. CIMC Enric stated that the construction contracts for all 12 of these 49,900 DWT MR product tankers have officially come into effect, and preparations for production are currently underway in an orderly manner.

CIMC Enric stated that the construction contracts for these twelve 49,900 DWT MR product tankers have all officially come into effect, and preparations for production are proceeding in an orderly manner.

As early as August 2026, reports emerged that Stamford Shipping was set to place an order for 10 MR product tankers with CIMC SOE. At the time, it was reported that the order was still in the preliminary stage, with Stamford Shipping intending to finalize formal shipbuilding contracts with CIMC SOE only after securing charter agreements for the new vessels.

It is understood that CIMC SOE currently focuses on the construction of gas carriers—such as liquefied natural gas (LNG) bunkering vessels and liquefied petroleum gas (LPG)/liquid ammonia carriers—while also engaging in the construction of offshore support vessels and container ships. The recent order for 12 new vessels from Stamford Shipping and a European shipowner marks a significant breakthrough for the shipbuilder in the market for MR-class product tankers.

For many years, CIMC Enric has strategically focused on the construction of small- and medium-sized liquid cargo vessels, such as product tankers and liquefied gas carriers. The implementation of this partnership further validates the company’s forward-looking strategy in the small- and medium-sized product tanker segment, while continuing to consolidate and expand its competitive edge in the industry.

CIMC Enric has indicated that it will focus on the mainstream market for liquefied gas carrier construction, deepen its presence in the global MR product tanker market, and continuously expand its portfolio of green and efficient marine vessels. Furthermore, the company intends to strengthen its footprint in overseas markets and bolster strategic partnerships with high-caliber global shipowners, providing high-quality marine equipment to support global energy transportation.

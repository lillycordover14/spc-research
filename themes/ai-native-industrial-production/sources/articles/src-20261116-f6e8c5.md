unspun Appoints Former Solugen COO Chuck Shehadi as CEO, Succeeding Arne Arens&nbsp;&nbsp;citybiz

[page text]
Chuck Shehadi

unspun
, an Emeryville, California-based textile technology company developing automated 3D weaving systems, has appointed 
Chuck Shehadi
 as chief executive officer, succeeding 
Arne Arens
, who has relocated to 
Europe
 and will serve as chair of the board.

Shehadi brings more than 20 years of experience in manufacturing operations, procurement and supply chain management. He most recently served as chief operating officer at Solugen and previously held senior positions at Zoom, Manduka, Nike, Tesla and Apple, including roles overseeing global purchasing at Tesla and global supply management at Apple.

His appointment comes as unspun moves from technology development toward commercial deployment of OneWeave, its automated 3D weaving platform designed to manufacture garments directly from yarn while eliminating much of the traditional cutting and sewing process.

The company’s first machine customer is Arvind Limited, an Indian textile and retail conglomerate that is deploying OneWeave equipment at its manufacturing facility in Ahmedabad, India. The installation represents an early commercial application of unspun’s technology as the company seeks to expand its manufacturing systems internationally.

“We were looking for someone who knows how to build and scale,” said 
Beth Esponnette
, co-founder and chief product officer of unspun. “Chuck has done that at Tesla, Apple and Zoom. He knows what it takes to bring new technology into factories and make it work at scale. That’s exactly what we need as unspun enters its next chapter.”

OneWeave combines hardware and software to produce three-dimensional garment forms directly from yarn, offering an alternative to conventional apparel manufacturing processes that rely on separate fabric production, cutting and assembly.

The technology is intended to support smaller production runs closer to end markets, allowing apparel brands to adjust manufacturing volumes based on demand rather than committing to large inventories months in advance.

“Long lead times are the apparel industry’s most expensive problem. Brands commit to product a year or more before it sells, then pay for every wrong guess in markdowns and unsold inventory,” Shehadi said. “OneWeave™ changes that. Our technology lets brands produce close to their consumers and respond to demand in weeks.”

Shehadi also identified expanding domestic manufacturing in the United States and Europe as a priority, arguing that automated production could help reduce the cost disadvantages traditionally associated with manufacturing apparel closer to consumers.

Headquartered in Emeryville, 
unspun
 is a certified B Corporation focused on developing automated textile manufacturing technologies. Its OneWeave platform is designed to support localized, on-demand apparel production through integrated weaving hardware and software.

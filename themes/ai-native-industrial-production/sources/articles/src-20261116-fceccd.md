Skagit Valley’s 
Tulip Town
 will be hosting a pop-up workshop with Santa on select Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays Nov. 21-Dec. 20.
The space will be decorated with flocked Christmas trees, twinkling lights, and festive holiday décor, including a backdrop at which folks can take photos with the jolly elf. Visitors can also browse a holiday gift nook full of seasonal treats and stocking stuffers. Furry friends are invited to join the fun and partake in photos with Santa as well.

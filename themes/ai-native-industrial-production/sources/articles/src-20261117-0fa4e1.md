PARKERSBURG, W.Va. — On Monday morning, the WVSSAC board of directors approved the shift from a 16-team to 12-team playoff format for Class AAAA beginning with the 2027 season. The move was made in anticipation of the class dropping to only 17 schools through the 2031 academic year.
The WVSSAC reported that executive director Wayne Ryan recommended the adjustment to the board, along with input from the Football Coaches Advisory Committee. The WVSSAC also reported that in a survey amongst the Class AAAA schools, the majority supported the decision.

Jets quarterback Geno Smith went for tests on his injured ankle on Monday, but it doesn’t look like they turned up anything that will keep him from playing against the Chargers in Week 11. 
Ian Rapoport of NFL Media reports 
that tests showed
 Smith avoided a major injury during the 31-12 loss to the Bills on Sunday. Smith left the game in the third quarter and rookie Cade Klubnik ran the offense the rest of the way. 
Smith also went for a concussion evaluation during the loss, but head coach Aaron Glenn said on Monday that Smith will start this week as long as he is cleared medically. 
Smith has started every game for the Jets this season. He has completed 67.1 percent of his passes for 2,226 yards, 12 touchdowns and three interceptions, but the Jets rank 26th in the league with 19.4 points per game.

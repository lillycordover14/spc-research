Hannah slams Umno, PAS in defending assembly hall’s nominal lease
Federal territories minister Hannah Yeoh said the KLSCAH building recorded an annual operational deficit of around RM400,000. (Bernama pic)

PETALING JAYA
:
 Federal territories minister Hannah Yeoh today slammed Umno and PAS leaders who questioned the nominal RM1,000 land premium for the Kuala Lumpur and Selangor Chinese Assembly Hall’s (KLSCAH) 99-year lease renewal.

Yeoh said certain Umno and PAS leaders were trying to racialise the matter by accusing her of “looking after her own race” by helping KLSCAH “escape” paying a higher land premium.

The minister said the assembly hall was 103 years old and was gazetted as a heritage building in 2007.

“This heritage building recorded an annual operational deficit of around RM400,000 due to the rise in maintenance costs, the building’s aged structure and the growing need to upgrade safety and technical facilities.

“Why was the premium reduced to RM1,000? Because KLSCAH voluntarily changed the land’s status to institutional land, which means losing the potential of major developments on the land (as well as the value attached to that potential,” she said in a Facebook post.

Yeoh said KLSCAH still had to pay quit rent to the land office, assessment tax to Kuala Lumpur City Hall and income tax to the Inland Revenue Board.

She also slammed those who questioned KLSCAH for converting the land status, criticising them for failing to understand the value of heritage buildings in the capital city.

“If they understood their value, they would have upgraded and taken better care of the Sultan Abdul Samad building and Carcosa Seri Negara long ago.

“Who did that, in the end? Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim. And they dared to question why we spent RM600 million for this purpose.”

The minister then trained her guns on Umno and PAS’s track record in managing Kuala Lumpur, saying fields and flood retention ponds were sold in the past to make way for developments.

“What was their solution to flash floods in KL? Sand bags.

“Leaders and activists in KL who ride on race and religion are nearly the same, they’re playing up the issue just because I’m a Chinese minister from DAP.”

Umno Youth chief Dr Akmal Saleh had questioned the basis for the reduced premium for KLSCAH, saying the land was still worth RM4.67 million.

PAS’s Kuala Terengganu MP Ahmad Amzad Hasim meanwhile urged the government to disclose the details of the reduced premium, including the conditions imposed and protection clauses.

Separately, academic Teo Kok Seong labelled the reduced premium as “unreasonable” and possibly controversial, saying the original estimate of RM4.67 million would have been based on concrete grounds.

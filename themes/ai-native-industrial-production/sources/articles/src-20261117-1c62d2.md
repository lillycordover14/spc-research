Ukraine again hits symbolic bridge linking occupied Crimea to Russia; Kyiv’s heating and water disrupted as North and South bridges struck. What we know on day 1,728

Ukraine 
struck a symbolic bridge
 that links the annexed Crimean peninsula to Russia for the second day in a row, after Moscow launched strikes on Kyiv’s bridges. 
Crimea’s Moscow-installed governor Sergey Aksyonov said on Tuesday that Ukrainian forces had struck the bridge and traffic had been suspended. On Monday, a Ukrainian drone 
crashed into one of the Kerch bridge’s arches
. Ukraine says the bridge is a legitimate target for its armed forces, and has made at least two previous attempts to blow it up. Aksyonov also said on Tuesday that two people were ⁠killed ⁠in ​a Ukrainian drone attack ⁠on Crimea.

Russian strikes hit two major bridges in Kyiv late on Monday, part of an overnight attack that damaged critical infrastructure and disrupted heating and water
 in parts of the Ukrainian capital, its mayor said. The North and South bridges were hit, Vitali Klitschko said, citing preliminary information, in a post on Telegram just before midnight local time (2200 GMT), shortly after the Ukrainian air force warned of incoming missiles. He later said critical infrastructure systems were damaged and that heating and water supplies in some districts were affected. Air defence units were in operation in the city, he said, while witnesses ‌reported a series of loud explosions.

Volodymyr Zelenskyy had earlier warned of an attack by Russia in the coming days
. “Tonight, overnight and throughout tomorrow, please pay attention to missile danger alerts,” the Ukrainian president said in his nightly video address on Monday. “And they there, in Russia, must know that for every one of their evils there will be our just response.”

Russia is receiving “dozens of missiles per month” from North Korea
, 
Zelenskyy has said, 
citing a report from Ukraine’s HUR main intelligence directorate. “This is ​extremely dangerous and not ​only for Ukraine,” the president said. “By using ​its ballistic missiles in this war, North Korea is making ​its weapons more ‌effective. This creates ​additional challenges for the ​entire region around Korea.” Zelenskyy called on European allies to send more weapons to respond “on equal terms” to the threat.

Zelenskyy said Ukraine had held new online ⁠talks with US presidential envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, as well as European representatives
, on the prospects of ⁠ending the war with Russia. The talks came less than a week after US, Ukrainian and Russian officials held their first 
three-way ⁠talks
 in nine months in Abu Dhabi. “Diplomatic processes are actively underway and we hope ​that these efforts will not be ‌in vain,” Zelenskyy said on Monday. “Our ‌team is working in the most constructive manner possible.”

Zelenskyy said European representatives – including ‌from France, the UK, Germany, Italy and Poland – took part in full 
and would be represented in the same way in future meetings with Russian negotiators. The discussion was “not easy”, the Ukrainian president said, without elaborating. But he said all had agreed to meet at talks in Berlin with the “coalition of the willing” countries supporting ‌Ukraine. In Moscow, Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said Russia had not yet agreed to the participation of European representatives in the next round ​of Ukraine peace talks, contradicting comments by US negotiators after talks in Moscow that the Europeans would be at the next negotiations.

The International Monetary Fund said it made important ⁠progress ⁠in discussions with ​Ukrainian authorities 
on a staff-level agreement ⁠that would free up $1.6bn in funds ⁠for Kyiv, once financing assurances ​had been ‌secured. It said IMF staff ‌and Ukraine were engaging with international partners to ​secure the financing assurances needed due to the expected longer duration ⁠of the war, it said.

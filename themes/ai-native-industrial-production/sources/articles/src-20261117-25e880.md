Penn State’s Ono, two Michigan freshmen debut in Top 20; Blaze shakes up 141-pound field

				By 
Tristan Warner

				Updated: November 17, 2026
			

Tweet

Tweet

Photo: Michigan’s Niko Sahakian (pictured) and fellow Wolverine freshman Henry Aslikyan debuted in the Top 20 this week. Photo by Sam Janicki.

NEWTON, Iowa —
 Oklahoma State solidified its No. 2 ranking in the Nov. 17 Tournament Power Index (TPI), presented by Cliff Keen Athletic, as well as the Top 20 Dual-Team Rankings, after defeating third-ranked Nebraska 27-6 on Nov. 13 in Stillwater, Okla.

The 133- and 141-pound weight classes were shaken up this week as Penn State’s returning All-American Marcus Blaze moved to 141 pounds, while world champion Masanosuke Ono made his collegiate debut at 133 pounds for the Nittany Lions.

The five-time defending national champion Nittany Lions feature 10 ranked wrestlers, including nine in the top eight, to earn 146 TPI points. Penn State is followed by Oklahoma State (115), Nebraska (91.5), Iowa (75.5) and Virginia Tech (72).

WIN’s TPI projects where programs could finish at the 2027 NCAA Division I Championships, which will be held March 18-20 in St. Louis.

The following is a breakdown of individual TPI points by ranking: 20 for first, 16 for second, 13.5 for third, 12.5 for fourth, 10 for fifth, 9 for sixth, 6.5 for seventh and 5.5 for eighth. Wrestlers ranked ninth through 12th earn two points each, followed by 1.5 points for those ranked 13th through 16th and one point for those ranked 17th through 20th.

Penn State features four top-ranked wrestlers, with Nittany Lions Luke Lilledahl (125), PJ Duke (165), Mitchell Mesenbrink (174) and Josh Barr (197) headlining their respective weight classes. Oklahoma State and Virginia Tech are the other programs with multiple No. 1-ranked wrestlers, as the Cowboys’ Jax Forrest (133) and Landon Robideau (157) remain atop their weight classes, as do Virginia Tech’s Bo Bassett (141) and Caleb Henson (149). Also ranked No. 1 are Minnesota’s Max McEnelly (184) and Iowa’s Ben Kueter (heavyweight).

Several intriguing dual meets highlight the upcoming week, including No. 2 Oklahoma State traveling west to face No. 13 Arizona State on Friday, Nov. 20, and No. 7 Stanford on Sunday, Nov. 22. Other notable matchups include No. 4 Iowa hosting No. 9 Iowa State on Nov. 22, No. 6 Ohio State visiting No. 15 NC State on Nov. 20, and No. 3 Nebraska traveling to Penn on Nov. 21. Penn’s annual Keystone Classic individual tournament is also set for Sunday, Nov. 22, in Philadelphia.

WIN will continue to update the Tournament Power Index, Individual Rankings and Dual Meet Rankings each Tuesday of the 2026-27 season.

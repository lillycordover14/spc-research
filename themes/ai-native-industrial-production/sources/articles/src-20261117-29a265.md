VALLEY CITY, N.D. — The Valley City State University music department will host a pair of music concerts this week at the Larry J. Robinson Center for the Arts.
A Friday night concert will feature the Valley City State University and Valley City High School concert bands. On Saturday, VCSU senior Dominic Tibor presents his senior recital.

ADVERTISEMENT

Both concerts are free and open to the public. Those who are unable to attend may view the live video streams that will be available on the 
    
      
VCSU Music YouTube channel.

At 7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 20, is the Fall Instrumental Concert.
At 3 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 21, is the Dominic Tibor senior recital.
For the second year in a row, the VCSU and VCHS bands are coming together for a Fall Instrumental Concert. The night will feature a few small group brass performances followed by pieces from the VCHS Concert Band and the VCSU Concert Band.
The concert is titled “Destinations,” with songs spotlighting different locations around the world.
The VCSU Concert Band is directed by professor Jerrold Heide. The VCHS Concert Band is under the direction of Tedi Dimmer. 
VCSU senior Dominic Tibor will perform a selection of vocal pieces at his senior music recital on Nov. 21.
A native of Dickinson, North Dakota, Tibor is completing a degree in music education at VCSU. The senior recital is the culmination of his work as Tibor prepares to graduate from the university later this year.

ADVERTISEMENT

Tibor, baritone, will be accompanied by Oswald Johnson, Ph.D., on piano for the performance. He will also welcome a few guest performers.

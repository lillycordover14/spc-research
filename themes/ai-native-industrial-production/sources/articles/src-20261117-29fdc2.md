Hashtag Orange, a digital-first integrated marketing agency, has been awarded the consolidated marketing mandate for The Safe Air Company, to lead the launch and market development of its brand and flagship product, Safe Air Haven, in Delhi NCR.
The engagement goes beyond a conventional creative, media or digital marketing assignment. Hashtag Orange will work as an integrated marketing partner to build consumer understanding, establish the category of whole-home air protection and create an end-to-end marketing ecosystem that connects brand awareness and consideration with lead generation, nurturing and conversion.
 

At the heart of the mandate is an important shift in consumer thinking: moving beyond the conventional approach of purifying individual rooms towards protecting the air across the entire home. While room-based air purifiers have shaped consumer understanding of air purification, Safe Air Haven seeks to introduce a more holistic approach to indoor air quality through a whole-home air protection system.
The mandate will begin with consumer understanding and the mapping of consumer journeys to identify category perceptions, barriers to adoption, information needs, and purchase triggers. These insights will inform the brand's positioning, communication strategy, and go-to-market approach for the Delhi NCR market.
Hashtag Orange will build and manage an integrated marketing system spanning brand communication, social media content, full-funnel marketing and performance-led demand generation. The engagement will also encompass the development of conversion-oriented landing pages, integration with Zoho CRM, lead nurturing and management of consumer enquiries, including inquiry calls and support through the conversion journey.
This integrated approach is designed to ensure that marketing does not stop at generating awareness or delivering leads. Instead, it connects every stage of the consumer journey, creating a more coherent pathway from discovering the whole-home air protection proposition to understanding its relevance, evaluating the solution and considering adoption.
The Safe Air Company offers Safe Air Haven, a whole-home air protection system designed to deliver filtered fresh air across living spaces through a centralized system. Combining multi-stage filtration with continuous airflow, the solution is designed to address indoor air quality beyond the limitations of room-specific purification.
Commenting on the partnership, Advitya Dewan, Director, The Safe Air Company, said,
 “As awareness around air quality grows, we believe the conversation needs to evolve beyond outdoor pollution and individual room purification to a more holistic understanding of the air we breathe inside our homes. Safe Air Haven represents a different approach to indoor air protection. We are pleased to partner with Hashtag Orange to build this category, strengthen consumer understanding and create a connected marketing ecosystem that takes the proposition from awareness to adoption, beginning with Delhi NCR.”
Mukesh Vij, Founder, Hashtag Orange, added,
 “What makes this engagement particularly exciting is that we are not just launching a brand or marketing a product; we are helping create a new consumer category. Moving from room-based purification to whole-home air protection requires a fundamental shift in how consumers understand the problem, evaluate solutions, and make purchase decisions. Our mandate reflects how we see integrated marketing evolving: combining consumer insight, brand building, content, media, performance marketing, technology and lead management into one connected system. The objective is to ensure that the journey from creating awareness to enabling a consumer's purchase decision is thoughtfully designed and managed.”
The engagement reflects Hashtag Orange's integrated approach to marketing, bringing together strategic brand thinking, creative communication, digital capabilities, media and performance marketing, and technology-enabled consumer engagement to deliver business-oriented marketing outcomes.

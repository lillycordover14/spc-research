MP Materials

MP Materials

EV demand

MP has also published a scenario in which the facility generates $400 million in annual EBITDA, with approximately $255 million retained by MP. So with 10X scheduled for commissioning in 2028, the facility could contribute substantially to earnings by 2030.
Investment bank 
Moelis
' April 2026 analysis also provides a useful benchmark. Its valuation figures imply approximately $984 million in EBITDA for 2030. Applying the 10.6 times EBITDA multiple Moelis reported for competitor 
Lynas Rare Earths
 yields an enterprise valuation of approximately $10.4 billion.
Using MP's September 2026 cash, debt, preferred-equity balance, and outstanding common shares, that translates to approximately $58 per share. At that price, a $10,000 investment would be worth roughly $15,260 by 2030, representing a gain of approximately 53%.
Of course, this is an illustrative valuation, not a guarantee. Changes in MP's earnings, debt, cash balances, or share count could significantly affect the outcome.

Boston Dynamics and GlobalFoundries announced an expansion of their collaboration to deploy Spot for autonomous enterprise asset management across GlobalFoundries? global semiconductor fabs. Under the agreement, Spot deployments will expand across GlobalFoundries?
manufacturing sites in Malta, New York; Burlington, Vermont; Dresden, Germany; and Singapore. In addition, GlobalFoundries will be the first customer outside the automotive industry to begin testing Boston Dynamics? Atlas humanoid as part of its early adopter program and skills development workshop.
Beginning in early 2027, the two companies will begin testing Atlas onsite at GlobalFoundries? facilities. The goals of the skills development workshop will be to evaluate potential humanoid use cases, assess customer readiness and integration needs, and begin early testing and training for specific task execution, complementing GlobalFoundries?
global program to upskill its employees in AI. The two companies have worked together on GlobalFoundries? robotics program for more than four years.

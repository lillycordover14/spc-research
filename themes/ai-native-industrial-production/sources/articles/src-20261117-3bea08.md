LanzaTech Global, Inc. submitted its quarterly Form 10-Q report to the SEC for the period ending September 30, 2026, revealing consolidated financial data, share outstanding figures, and updates on its gas fermentation and carbon capture technology operations.

Key Highlights

Shares Outstanding:
 The company reported 13,089,197 common shares outstanding as of November 10, 2026.

Cash Position:
 Cash and cash equivalents totaled $32,313 thousand as of September 30, 2026, up from $13,164 thousand at December 31, 2025.

Total Current Assets:
 Total current assets increased to $55,658 thousand at September 30, 2026, compared to $39,688 thousand at year-end 2025.

Trade and Other Receivables:
 Net trade and other receivables stood at $9,064 thousand at September 30, 2026, slightly down from $9,527 thousand at December 31, 2025.

Contract Assets:
 Net contract assets declined to $2,796 thousand at September 30, 2026, from $6,541 thousand at the end of 2025.

Filer Status:
 LanzaTech confirmed its status as a non-accelerated filer and smaller reporting company, and stated it is not a shell company.

Stock Exchange Listing:
 Common stock is listed on NASDAQ under the ticker LNZA, while warrants trade as LNZAW on The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC.

LanzaTech Q3 2026 Financial Position and Operational Summary

Headquartered at 8045 Lamon Avenue, Suite 400, Skokie, IL 60077, LanzaTech Global, Inc. operates within the carbon capture and gas fermentation industry. The company’s revenue streams include engineering services, equipment package sales, and CarbonSmart product sales, as outlined in its forward-looking statements. The filing, with Commission file number 001-40282, reflects its Delaware incorporation and IRS Employer Identification No. 92-2018969.

The consolidated balance sheet as of September 30, 2026, reported cash and cash equivalents of $32,313 thousand, marking a significant increase from $13,164 thousand as of December 31, 2025. Total current assets rose to $55,658 thousand from $39,688 thousand during the same timeframe. Net trade and other receivables decreased modestly to $9,064 thousand from $9,527 thousand, while contract assets fell to $2,796 thousand from $6,541 thousand, based on unaudited financial statements.

The filing also detailed risk factors in its forward-looking statements, including concerns about the company’s "ability to continue operations as a going concern," the necessity to "attract new investors and raise substantial additional financing," and ongoing efforts to "remediate material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting." It emphasized that actual results may differ materially and referenced the 2025 Annual Report on Form 10-K, filed March 31, 2026, for further risk disclosures.

Summary of Disclosures

LanzaTech Global, Inc.’s Form 10-Q filing for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, presented unaudited consolidated balance sheet data, confirmed 13,089,197 shares outstanding as of November 10, 2026, and included disclosures related to going concern risks, internal control deficiencies, and ongoing strategic initiatives such as the LanzaJet Transactions mentioned in the report.

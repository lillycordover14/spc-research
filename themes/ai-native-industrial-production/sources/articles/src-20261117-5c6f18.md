Mil-Spec Dance-Rock Side Project Vivians Release Debut Album 
Take Your Desires For Reality
TB
By 
Tom Breihan
12:34 PM EST on November 17, 2026
Add Stereogum to Google
Toronto's crushing, sincere Mil-Spec have consistently been one of the greatest active bands on the hardcore underground for many years, and their various side projects are often really good, too. Last year, for instance, Mil-Spec surprise-released a kickass 
self-titled EP
. A few weeks later, guitarist Dan Darrah released 
Vacationland
, a dreamy and soothing indie rock solo album. So when I tell you that a couple of Mil-Spec members have a new dance-rock band, I expect you to pay attention. 
Dan Darrah and his Mil-Spec bandmate Jacob Hellas started the new trio Vivians with Leigh Fisk, from the Toronto shoegaze band Bliss Fields. Their sound is a bright, playful shimmy-shake. It's obviously inspired by the Madchester stuff from the late '80s and early '90s. But unlike most of the Madchester revivalists these days, they're remembered to include the bluesy, countrified guitars that those bands took from the Rolling Stones. I like the bright, airy out-of-time synth-beeps, too.
You like Dazy, right? I hope you do. Vivians' brand-new full-length debut 
Take Your Desires For Reality
 has a lot of the same hooky propulsion as Dazy's recent stuff. I'm on my first listen right now, and I'm really into it. Given the track records of everyone involved, this album should not surprise me, but it still does. Hear it for yourself below.
Take Your Desires For Reality
 is out now on Sunday Drive.

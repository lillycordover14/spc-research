Pelico Joins Google Cloud's Sovereign AI Summit to address AI and Data sovereignty in Manufacturing&nbsp;&nbsp;PR Newswire

[page text]
BRUSSELS
, 
Nov. 17, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- Pelico today announced that Hamza Charrouf, Chief Customer Officer, will speak at the Sovereign AI Summit 2026, hosted by Google Cloud on November 17 at MIX Brussels.
At 12:20 CET, Charrouf joins the Real-World Use Cases panel, where public and private sector organizations will share how they are adopting advanced AI under the level of local control their work demands.

Continue Reading
      

Hamza Charrouf, Chief Customer Officer at Pelico

The summit comes as European organizations decide where to run AI, and under whose rules. In June, the European Commission published its Cloud Sovereignty Framework, which assesses providers against 48 criteria spanning law, data and AI, operations and supply chain.

Charrouf will speak for an industry where sovereignty is already an operational requirement. Aerospace, defense and industrial manufacturers hold some of the most sensitive data in Europe's economy: what their systems are made of, who supplies every part, when each program delivers, and the engineering data behind it all, much of it export-controlled. Pelico offers sovereign deployment options and is bringing its platform to S3NS in France, which keeps data under French jurisdiction and meets SecNumCloud requirements.
"Aerospace and defense manufacturers don't get to choose between control and speed," said Hamza Charrouf, Chief Customer Officer of Pelico. "Their data has to stay under the right jurisdiction, and the pressure to deliver is outpacing what pre-AI systems can handle. The question for industry is how to put AI to work in operations today, with clear control over where data lives and who can reach it."

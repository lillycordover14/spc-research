The selloff in U.S. rare-earth and critical-minerals stocks has stretched into a second session, and one name is taking the biggest hit in the group. 
USA Rare Earth
 (
NASDAQ:USAR
) stock is at $6.65 in morning trading, down 7%. That decline adds to a slide that leaves USA Rare Earth stock down 37% over the past month.
 
 
 
 
galinast / Getty Images
 
 
 
On November 9, USA Rare Earth reported Q3 2026 revenue of $3 million, which missed the $13.55 million estimate. Serra Verde's combination with the company closed on September 3, so the asset contributed almost nothing to the quarter. A $136.57 million gain on the fair value of financial instruments lifted net income under generally accepted accounting principles to $11.71 million. USA Rare Earth's adjusted loss came to -$0.44 per share.
 
 
 
 
 
Rare-Earth Selloff Extends Ahead of Trump-Xi Meeting
 
 
The new losses follow a William Blair downgrade of USA Rare Earth on November 16 over feedstock issues, which pulled the whole group lower. That selloff came ahead of a meeting between President Trump and Chinese leader Xi Jinping. Much of the bull case for U.S. rare-earth names depends on reducing reliance on China, and USA Rare Earth's strategy is built directly around that theme.

What the Peer Group Says About USA Rare Earth
 
 
Critical Metals
 (
NASDAQ:CRML
) stock is at $3.97, down 5%. That's a smaller decline than USA Rare Earth's, even though the pre-revenue developer has disclosed substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern. Under the company's current plan, mining at the Tanbreez rare-earth project in Greenland is set to begin by the end of 2028.
 
 
MP Materials
 (
NYSE:MP
) stock is at $35.07, down 3%, the steadiest showing in the group. On a year-to-date basis, MP Materials stock is down 31%. Production of neodymium-praseodymium (NdPr) at MP Materials rose 41% year over year to 1,018 metric tons in Q3 2026. The company also completed magnet qualification with a major automaker, and commercial deliveries begin in Q4 2026.
 
 
 
 
 
United States Antimony
 (
NYSE:UAMY
) is also lower. The antimony producer supplies military-specification ingots to the Defense Logistics Agency under a sole-source contract.
 
 
How USA Rare Earth's Business Model Differs
 
 
USA Rare Earth is building a mine-to-magnet supply chain across the U.S., Brazil and the U.K. It includes Serra Verde's Brazilian mine, the metal and alloy plants run by its Less Common Metals unit and a magnet plant in Stillwater, Oklahoma. Several of those pieces came through acquisitions that closed in recent months, so the company is still absorbing integration costs while output ramps up. Chief executive Thras Moraitis stated that "Demand for a secure and responsible supply of rare earth products is becoming increasingly acute across a broad range of technology-driven sectors."
 
 
MP Materials runs one main mine and processing site at Mountain Pass in California and is building magnet capacity in Texas, with a Department of War partnership that includes price protection helping shield it from swings in rare-earth prices. Critical Metals is at the earliest stage of the three, with a Greenland rare-earth deposit and an Austrian lithium project that both still need financing.
 
 
 
 
 
What's at Stake for USA Rare Earth Ahead of Trump-Xi Talks
 
 
Are traders trimming rare-earth names before the meeting in case a U.S.-China deal eases supply tensions and undermines the case for USA Rare Earth's non-China supply? The bulls can point to federal backing, including a $750 million Department of War investment in the company's contract vehicle and a government forward purchase of at least $300 million of product. Meanwhile, critics point to the $143.69 million USA Rare Earth used in operations in Q3 and the chance that better relations shrink non-China price premiums.
 
 
USA Rare Earth now faces the question of whether Serra Verde hits the company's Q4 sales guidance of at least 325 tonnes of total rare earth oxide. Sentiment across the group could stay fragile until the outcome of the Trump-Xi meeting resolves.

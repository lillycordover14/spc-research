Adrien Nunez Readies Debut Album For February&nbsp;&nbsp;MusicRow.com

[page text]
Adrien Nunez
 will release his debut album 
WISH YOU WERE HERE
 on Feb. 12 via Warner Records.

The 14-song set includes the just-released duet “YOU STILL FEEL LIKE MINE” featuring 
Ingrid Andress
, written by Nunez alongside 
RuthAnne
 and 
Seth Mosley
. The project was produced by Mosley, 
Peter Fenn, Nevin Sastry 
and
 Joe London.

WISH YOU WERE HERE
 also features the Gold-certified “LOW ROAD” featuring 
Avery Anna
, which has earned over 143 million global streams to date, as well as previously released tracks “AND THEN SOME,” “WRONG WAY (HALLELUJAH)” and “BROKEN PIECES.”

To celebrate the album, Nunez will hit the road in 2027 on his U.S. headlining “WISH YOU WERE HERE TOUR.” The trek will kick off in Charlotte on Feb. 11, with additional stops in Atlanta, Nashville, Chicago, Minneapolis and more. 
Tickets
 go on sale tomorrow (Nov. 18).

WISH YOU WERE HERE
 Track List:

1. “SO LONG LONELY”

2. “HITS LIKE YOU”

3. “WRONG WAY (HALLELUJAH)”

4. “AND THEN SOME”

5. “BROKEN PIECES”

6. “DON’T WANNA GO HOME”

7. “ALRIGHT, ALRIGHT, ALRIGHT”

8. “YOU STILL FEEL LIKE MINE (feat. Ingrid Andress)”

9. “GOODBYE”

10. “WHEN I DIE”

11. “LIFE WITHOUT YOU”

12. “LOW ROAD (feat. Avery Anna)”

13. “FUNNY HOW”

14. “WISH YOU WERE HERE”

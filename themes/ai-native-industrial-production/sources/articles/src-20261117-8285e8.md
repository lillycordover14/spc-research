Pelico to Participate in the Google Cloud Sovereign AI Summit 2026&nbsp;&nbsp;KuCoin

[page text]
CoinDesk reports:
Brussels, November 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Pelico today announced that Chief Customer Officer Hamza Charrouf will speak at the Sovereign AI Summit 2026, hosted by Google Cloud at MIX Brussels, on November 17.
Charrouf will participate in a panel discussion titled "Real-World Applications" at 12:20 CET, where organizations from the public and private sectors will share how they use advanced AI under the level of local control required for their operations.
At the time of this summit, European organizations are deciding where to deploy AI and whose rules to follow. In June, the European Commission released its cloud sovereignty framework, evaluating providers against 48 criteria across four areas: law, data and AI, operations, and supply chains.
Charrouf will speak on behalf of an industry that treats sovereignty as a foundational operating principle. Aerospace, defense, and industrial manufacturers handle some of the most sensitive data in the European economy: details about the components comprising their systems, their suppliers, delivery timelines, and the underlying technical data, much of which is subject to export controls. Pelico offers sovereign data deployment options and has integrated its platform into France’s S3NS, ensuring data remains within French jurisdiction while meeting SecNumCloud requirements.
Hamza Charrouf, Chief Customer Officer at Pelico, said: “Aerospace and defense manufacturers cannot choose between control and speed. Their data must remain within the correct jurisdiction, and the pressure to deliver outcomes has surpassed the capabilities of pre-AI systems. For industry, the question is how to use AI in operations today while maintaining clear control over where data is stored and who can access it.”
In addition, Charrouf will participate in a roundtable discussion with invited analysts and journalists from 14:15 to 15:00 CET, alongside Hélène Bringer, President of S3NS and Vice President of Thales, and Gertjan Dewaele, Vice President of Products and Technology at Worldline, on the topic of sovereign cloud technology and the S3NS platform.
Bringer said: "Manufacturers should not stand idly by while waiting for sovereign alternatives, observing how AI operates. The integration of Pelico into S3NS's Trusted Cloud PREMI3NS demonstrates how a high-assurance environment can enable the protection of sensitive data in critical industries."
For more information about the summit, visit: https://cloud.google.com/events/sovereign-ai-summit-26.

The Number Twelve Looks Like You release “Become A Ghost” music video

Author 
Benedetta Baldin
 - 17.11.2026
 
 

The Number Twelve Looks Like You
 have unveiled a music video for “
Become A Ghost
”, another track from their latest studio album, “Howls From The Holographic Heart”. The new clip continues the band’s promotional campaign following the album’s October 16, 2026 release through Equal Vision Records.

The album marked the group’s first new full-length release since 2019’s “Wild Gods”. Its arrival was a significant moment for longtime listeners who have followed the band’s distinctive combination of mathcore, experimental songwriting and extreme metal.

Another look at Howls From The Holographic Heart

“Become A Ghost”
 appears toward the end of the album’s twelve-song track listing. The record also includes previously released material such as “
Trust Chaos
” and “
Glow In The Dark Skin”
, both of which received their own online promotional releases ahead of the album.

The band’s earlier “
Glow In The Dark Skin” 
video was co-directed by vocalist Jesse Korman and Kevin Veselka. Korman has been closely involved in the visual presentation of the group’s recent work, although the director of the newly released “B
ecome A Ghost”
 clip has not been independently confirmed here.

The album was co-produced by guitarist, keyboardist and vocalist Alexis Pareja alongside Jeremy Cormitas and Trae Titus. The collaboration contributed to a record that continues the band’s interest in unpredictable arrangements and changes of intensity.

A return after seven years

The new album arrived during a busy period for 
The Number Twelve Looks Like You
. The group also celebrated the twentieth anniversary of “Mongrel”, with a remastered vinyl edition of that record released on October 16.

A series of live performances accompanied the anniversary, connecting the band’s recent music with material from an earlier chapter of their career. That combination of new recordings and retrospective activity gave fans several reasons to revisit the group’s catalog.

The release of the “
Become A Ghost
” video adds another visual component to the current album cycle. While the band have long been associated with technical complexity and rapid stylistic shifts, their recent work has also placed an emphasis on atmosphere and emotional themes.

Fans can find the new clip through the band’s official video channels.

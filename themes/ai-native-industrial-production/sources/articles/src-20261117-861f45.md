Jets News: Glenn Hands Off Defense, Geno Gets Good News

By
 
Phil Sullivan

Published:
 
November 17, 2026

Tuesday’s Jets News brings a surprising coaching admission and a welcome injury update. Aaron Glenn acknowledged that 
Brian Duker
, not Glenn, had the lead calling defensive plays during Sunday’s 31-12 loss to Buffalo. The head coach still provided input and hasn’t decided whether Duker will keep that job against the Chargers. Meanwhile, testing reportedly found no major damage to Geno Smith’s injured ankle, although his availability remains under evaluation. Glenn says Smith is still the starter when healthy, leaving rookie Cade Klubnik waiting for another opportunity. New York is 2-8, has gone nine straight quarters without an offensive touchdown, and faces seven games that should reveal plenty about the coaching staff and the young roster.

AG Gives Up Play calling Duties
 – Fans are debating Glenn’s confirmed decision to give Duker the lead on defensive calls against Buffalo. Some welcome a change; others think it says more about the failure of the overall coaching approach. No decision has been announced on who will call the defense against the Chargers.

Geno Is OK
 – The encouraging medical report has fans asking if Smith will be ready for Sunday. Reports indicate no major injury was found, but the team has not yet issued a final game-day availability determination.

Was Geno Injured, Benched or Both?
 – The quarterback debate continues after Smith left the Buffalo game twice. His concussion evaluation and subsequent ankle injury were reported, and Glenn has publicly reaffirmed that Smith remains his starter when healthy; claims of a benching are fan speculation.

Lead-up to Jets/Chargers Game
 – Members are looking ahead to the trip to Los Angeles, with particular attention on the quarterback situation and whether New York can finally turn sustained drives into touchdowns.

Why Does Mougey Get a Pass for This Defense?

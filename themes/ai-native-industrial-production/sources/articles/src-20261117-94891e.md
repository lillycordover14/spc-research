Tar Heels in pros: Black and White have big games; Ryan, Ingram, Powell shine in G League&nbsp;&nbsp;Tar Heel Tribune

[page text]
By R.L. Bynum

Leaky Black found his range from beyond the arc, Coby White matched his season-high scoring, and Cormac Ryan and Harrison Ingram led the G League performances by former Carolina players on Sunday and Monday.

Black made four of five 3-point attempts and finished one rebound shy of a double-double Sunday in Sacramento’s 125–103 home loss to Oklahoma City. He scored 12 points, grabbed nine rebounds and collected four steals and a block in 29 minutes off the bench.

All four of Black’s baskets came from beyond the arc. He also had two assists before fouling out as the Kings fell to 1–12.

White scored 21 points for the second consecutive game, matching his season high in Charlotte’s 113–105 road loss to the Los Angeles Clippers on Sunday.

The former UNC point guard went 7-for-13 from the floor, made two 3-pointers and converted all five free-throw attempts in just under 20 minutes. White, who had one assist and two turnovers, is averaging 11.7 points through six games after missing the start of the season with a left calf strain.

White and the Hornets visit the Los Angeles Lakers at 11 p.m. Tuesday, when he’ll face another former Tar Heel coming off a strong performance.

Lakers center Walker Kessler produced a double-double Sunday with 14 points and 12 rebounds in a 131–123 victory at Phoenix. He made four of five shots and all six free-throw attempts, adding three assists, two steals and two blocks in 32 minutes.

Kessler was a plus-18 as Los Angeles improved to 12–2. He is averaging 14.5 points, 10.9 rebounds and 2.2 blocks.

In the G League, Ryan scored 25 points and handed out six assists Sunday, but Wisconsin (2–1) lost 114–112 in overtime at Windy City.

Ryan went 9-for-20 from the floor, including 3-for-9 from 3-point range, and made both free-throw attempts. He added three rebounds and a steal but committed six turnovers in 38 minutes.

Through three games, Ryan is averaging 28.0 points while shooting 63.3% from the floor and 61.1% from beyond the arc.

Wisconsin teammate Pete Nance contributed 13 points, seven rebounds and five assists in 39 minutes. Three of his four baskets were 3-pointers. The Herd, which fell to 2–1, hosts Windy City at 7 p.m. Tuesday.

Ingram helped Salt Lake City win an overtime thriller Monday, scoring 23 points and grabbing 10 rebounds in a 145–143 road win over the Valley Suns.

He shot 6-for-11 from the floor, made two 3-pointers and went 5-for-6 at the free-throw line. Ingram added four assists, two blocks and a steal in 36 minutes before fouling out.

Ingram is averaging 18.5 points, 10.8 rebounds and 6.3 assists through four games.

RJ Davis continued his efficient start for Austin (3–0) on Sunday, scoring 20 points in the Spurs’ 140–88 home victory over the Memphis Hustle.

Davis made 7 of 10 shots, including 2 of 4 from 3-point range, and both free-throw attempts. He finished with two rebounds, three assists, a steal and no turnovers in 28 minutes.

Davis is averaging 20.0 points while shooting 52.9% from the floor and 50.0% from beyond the arc.

Drake Powell, assigned to the G League’s Long Island Nets on Sunday, had an active defensive night Monday, finishing with four steals and a block in a 108–107 home loss to Delaware.

Powell scored 15 points on 6-of-11 shooting, with one 3-pointer, and added three assists in 29 minutes. Caleb Love did not play for Delaware after scoring a combined 57 points in two games against Maine on Friday and Saturday.

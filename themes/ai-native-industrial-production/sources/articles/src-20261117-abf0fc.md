The social video publisher NowThis has acquired Impact Media, the Gen Z-led company behind the social accounts @impact and @environment, in a seven-figure, all-cash deal, according to NowThis CEO Sharon Mussalli. Further terms were not disclosed.
Across its editorial properties, Impact Media has more than 4 million followers and reaches more than 55 million unique viewers monthly, according to the company. Five of its 12 staffers, mostly editorial, will join NowThis following the deal. Co-founders Michelle Andrews and Tim Chau will not join as staff, although they may advise the company informally, according to Mussalli.
Impact Media’s blend of social issues and pop culture is central to its appeal, and NowThis plans to keep @impact separate from NowThis Impact, its existing politics and social issues channel, as the two target different audiences, Mussalli said.

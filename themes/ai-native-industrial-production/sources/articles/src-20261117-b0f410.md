Neo Avdalas to miss extended time due to wrist injury
The Heels are still trying to find their identity, and now they’ll have to find it without their starting point guard.
by
 
 
Brandon Anderson
Nov 17, 2026, 4:48 PM UTC
Today it was announced that Virginia Tech transfer Neo Avdalas injured his wrist during the Georgia game over the weekend, and now he will miss extended amounts of time 
according to Inside Carolina
.
There was a play with less than three minutes remaining where Avdalas drove through traffic for a layup, and he fell awkwardly on his left hand and was visibly in pain. At the time it felt like he was going to end up leaving the game, but he ended up playing through it anyway. Now we know that things were more serious than they appeared, and Michael Malone will be down a point guard at the worst possible time because this team is still trying to gel together and find some kind of rhythm before the schedule gets much more difficult.
While Avdalas is out, Malone will have to rely on Terrence Brown and Isaiah Denis at point guard, and what that likely means is that we could see some creative lineups. Maximo Adams has been playing really well for the Tar Heels, so a lineup of Brown, Adams, Matt Able, Jarin Stevenson, and Sayon Keita could give the Heels more size to try to shore up rebounding woes. But it’s just as likely that we see Denis simply slide into Avdalas’ starting role with no additional changes, which means Brown and Denis would alternate at point during various parts of the game. We’ll see how Malone tries to navigate this situation, but behind these two guys there’s no real option if someone else gets injured, and that is a huge area of concern.
A specific timeline for Avdalas’ recovery wasn’t announced, but hopefully we learn more sooner than later. We’ll keep everyone posted with any information that is shared in the coming days.

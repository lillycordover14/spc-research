Eggy Debut Justice and Simon & Garfunkel Covers, Welcome Stuart Bogie in New York
RS
By 
Relix Staff
9:54 AM EST on November 17, 2026
Add Relix to Google
Photo via Eggy's Facebook page
Eggy headlined
 a pair of shows at New York’s Bowery Ballroom over the weekend, both of which contained a number of fun surprises. On Friday, which marked drummer/singer Alex Bailey’s first Eggy show in New York since recently moving to the Big Apple from his longtime Connecticut home, the quartet debuted a take on Simon & Garfunkel’s area-appropriate “59
th
 Street Bridge Song (Feelin' Groovy)” during their first set. Then, during their second set, Eggy busted out their take on the Grateful Dead’s classic “West L.A. Fadeaway” for the first time since December 27, 2024, just weeks before Bailey and Eggy guitarist Jake Brownstein will travel to Hawaii to join Bill Kreutzmann’s A Very Grateful Thanksgiving show. They also teased the tune again during their encore in the middle of their own “Wayless.”
Then, the next night, Eggy debuted another unexpected cover partway through their own “Sweat Equity” during their second set, moving into Justice’s party classic “D.A.N.C.E.” From there, they invited saxophonist Stuart Bogie to the stage to help them out on both Steve Miller Band’s “Wild Mountain Honey” and “Woah There,” before closing their set with “Searchlight.”

Bogie first collaborated with Eggy at Goose’s Viva El Gonzo this spring and Brownstein offered a meditative improvisation with the woodwinds player at New York’s Brooklyn Bowl in September during the annual Rosh Hashanah service held at the venue.
Eggy will appear at Washington, DC’s The Atlantis this Friday and Saturday while Bogie will continue his five-day deep-listening improvisational residency with organist Buck McDaniel at New York’s Church of Our Saviour this evening.

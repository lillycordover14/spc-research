Geno Smith injury: Jets get good news on starting QB

By 
Joe Pantorno

Sep 13, 2026; Nashville, Tennessee, USA;  New York Jets quarterback Geno Smith (7) looks to pass against the Tennessee Titans during the first quarter at Nissan Stadium.  Mandatory Credit: Steve Roberts-Imagn Images
 

For all those Jets fans clamoring to see Cade Klubnik take over as Gang Green’s starting quarterback, don’t hold your breath. 

Per multiple reports, starting quarterback Geno Smith avoided a significant ankle injury during Sunday’s 31-12 loss to the Buffalo Bills. An MRI on Monday revealed the extent of the issue, and both Smith and the Jets are hopeful that he’ll be able to play on Sunday against the Los Angeles Chargers. 

Smith was forced to leave Sunday’s divisional matchup twice: once toward the end of the first half to be checked for a concussion after a brutal hit from TJ Parker and Terrel Bernard. He managed to return in the third quarter, but left shortly after when he picked up this ankle injury. 

Klubnik held his own at times during his 
NFL
 debut, completing 11 of 20 passes for 137 yards and an interception. He also showed above-average mobility, with a couple of longer runs temporarily getting the MetLife Stadium crowd involved. 

But head coach Aaron Glenn was quick to shoot down any rumors of there being a switch at quarterback. As long as Smith is healthy, he is the Jets’ leading man.

“Guys, Geno is our starter,” Glenn said. “Come on now, let’s not do that.”

In 10 games this season, Smith has completed 67.1% of his passes for 2,226 yards, 12 touchdowns, and three interceptions. 

If he’s good to go for Week 11, the Jets should like their chances against a Chargers team that picked up its first win of the season on Sunday night against the Baltimore Ravens. New York is looking to finally have a full set of offensive weapons available, though it has not been easy. Running back Breece Hall missed five weeks because of a quad injury, and rookie receiver Omar Cooper Jr. has been dealing with an ankle injury that was exacerbated early in Sunday’s loss to the Bills.

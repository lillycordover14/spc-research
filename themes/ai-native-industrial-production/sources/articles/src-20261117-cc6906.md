                            NEXENTIS TECHNOLOGIES INC.                        
-9.05%
Nexentis Technologies Inc. announced that its wholly owned subsidiary, MitoCareX Bio Ltd., has selected and ordered molecules for planned in-vitro evaluation following further computational prioritization of preliminary findings from its previously announced screening work. The development follows the Company?s October 29, 2026 announcement that MitoCareX had completed an initial computational screening stage under its drug-discovery initiative with Boltz, PBC, an AI research lab focused on biomolecular foundation models and drug-discovery workflows. That announcement described preliminary computational predictions that MitoCareX considered encouraging for further computational analysis and experimental investigation.
Following the initial screening, MitoCareX conducted additional computational prioritization and selected the molecules for ordering. The planned in-vitro assessments are intended to evaluate the selected molecules experimentally and provide information to support decisions about potential further investigation. The molecules have not yet been received, and in-vitro testing has not begun.
The initiative combines MitoCareX?s drug-discovery capabilities with Boltz?s AI models to identify potential small-molecule starting points for selected discovery programs. The underlying computational predictions remain experimentally unvalidated, and selection and ordering do not establish biological activity or suitability for further development. Further scientific assessment will be needed to determine whether any selected molecule warrants continued investigation.

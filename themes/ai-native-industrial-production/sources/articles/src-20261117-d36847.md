Sega announced another Hatsune Miku figure of the Vocaloid in a gothic and punk style outfit is on the way and will join the FIGURIZMα line in 2027 under the “Midnight” name. There’s no exact release date yet and she isn’t showing up on the official Sega Prize site, but the 
social media account
 says to expect her in Spring 2027. She’ll follow the 
Punk! entry in the line
 that showed up in Japan in May 2026 and is already available on the 
Amazon Sega storefront for $41
.
 
Details about this figure are very slim, since only an initial picture of the painted prototype appeared online. The artist 
Oyo
 is behind the design. We know it should be around the same height as FIGURIZMα ones, so she should be around 10 inches tall. She’ll be wearing an entirely black outfit with a skirt, silver belt with musical notes on it, and black tank top with silver accents. She’ll also have fingerless gloves and black fishnet stockings. Her hair will be in pigtails, but there will be black tips at the end of both of them.

Kazakhstan’s Sofya Samodelkina Breaks Into World’s Top 10 Figure Skaters

By 
Dana Omirgazy 
 
in
 
Sports
 
on
 17 November 2026

ASTANA – Kazakhstan’s Sofya Samodelkina has reached a career-best 10th place in the updated International Skating Union (ISU) world rankings, earning 2,881 points after improving her previous position by three places.

Sofya Samodelkina. Photo credit: olympic. kz

Her rise followed a strong performance at Skate America 2026, the fourth stage of the Grand Prix series, held in Everett, United States, on Nov. 12-15. After placing 11th in the short program with 61.03 points, Samodelkina scored 137.56 points in the free skate, setting a season best and finishing fifth overall with 198.59 points.

During the free skate, she became the first Kazakh female figure skater to perform a triple Axel, an ultra-C element, at an official international competition.

Japan’s Ami Nakai won the event with 236.81 points, followed by Russia’s Alexandra Ignatova with 213.90 and Japan’s Rino Matsuike with 211.63.

Samodelkina has earned 14 ranking points across two Grand Prix events, having also finished 
fifth 
at Skate Canada. She is preparing for the second half of the season, including the Four Continents Figure Skating Championships, scheduled for Almaty in February 2027.

Her achievement means Kazakhstan now has representatives in the top 10 of both singles world rankings. Olympic champion Mikhail 
Shaidorov
 
holds second place in the men’s standings with 4,261 points.

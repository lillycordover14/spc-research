Intersect signs 1.5GW transformer supply deal with Heron Power

Deliveries expected to commence in 2028

November 17, 2026

By

Zachary Skidmore

         
            Have your say
        
    

Alphabet-owned clean energy and data center developer Intersect has partnered with Heron Power, a US manufacturer of solid-state medium-voltage transformers, to reserve 1.5GW of its Heron Link transformers.

– Heron Power

Under the manufacturing capacity agreement, Heron will deliver 1.5GW worth of capacity to Intersect, with deliveries expected to commence in 2028.
The units will be manufactured at the company’s Heron Factory One in Morgan Hill, California. The factory is designed to produce up to 10,000 Heron Links annually, representing more than 40GW of manufacturing capacity. Mass production at the facility is expected to start in late 2027.
“This platform will accelerate our speed to power by improving energy delivery efficiency and is projected to decrease maintenance and downtime, while supporting manufacturing jobs in America,” said Sheldon Kimber, founder and CEO of Intersect.
The agreement builds on an existing relationship between the two firms, with Intersect as a technical and commercial collaborator for more than 18 months.
According to the company, Heron Link is a modular megawatt power converter built on the latest advances in wide-bandgap semiconductors. The 5MW transformer is designed for high power density and ease of maintenance, which it claims offers greater reliability and lower costs for renewable energy storage and data center developers.
The deal with Intersect is its latest with a data center-focused company. 
Last month
, it inked a deal with Vantage to deploy more than 1GW of its transformers across Vantage’s hyperscale data center portfolio. Last
 June
, the firm signed a Letter of Intent (LOI) with Crusoe to supply power converter technology for the Stargate data center campus in Texas.
Heron Power was founded in 2024 by former Tesla senior executive Drew Baglino and is based in Scotts Valley, California.
Intersect Power is a US-based clean energy company with a portfolio of 2.2GW of operating solar PV and 2.4GWh of battery storage in operation or construction – mostly in Texas and California.
It first partnered with Google in 
2024
 to develop co-located renewable power and storage technologies with new data centers across the US. Last 
December
, Google’s parent company, Alphabet, announced it had acquired Intersect in a deal reportedly worth $4.75 billion. The company closed the acquisition in March.
In 
June
, Intersect and Google announced that they had commenced construction at the Meitner Energy Center, where the companies will colocate a data center alongside new energy generation in Gray and Roberts Counties, Texas.

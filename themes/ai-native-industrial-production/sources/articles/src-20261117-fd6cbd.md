Isembard Protest, London: No to Weapons Making in Southwark!&nbsp;&nbsp;CAAT UK

[page text]
Join London CAAT in saying no to the opening of a new weapons factory in London. 

Protest at 1pm 30th November at Isembard, Southwark Urban Logistics, Dunton Rd., London SE1 5SZ

Isembard is a components manufacturer for weapons systems
 who has opened what it calls the “largest new factory in London since World War 2” in a former logistics complex in Southwark.

Isembard’s customers include 
Anduril
, 
Babcock
 and 
Tekever
.

Anduril
 is a US arms manufacturer who manufactures air-launched and surface-launched missiles. It is noted for its 
support for Israeli militarism
, including having a deal with Elbit Systems to make artillery control systems. Anduril’s boss in Israel is the former 
head of the Israeli air force
.

Babcock
 is one of the UK’s largest arms companies, primarily involved in the design, manufacture and maintenance of the UK and US attack submarines and their nuclear-weapons-carrying ballistic submarines.

Tekever
 are a Portuguese drone maker and operator who has a contract to track migrants in the English Channel on behalf of the Home Office.

Isembard is involved in a project to create a killer drone to accompany Apache helicopters
. Apaches used during the Global War on Terror were linked to the killing of Reuters TV journalists in Iraq, and the UN said they were responsible for
 “
majority of civilian deaths from air strikes” in Afghanistan.

London CAAT are saying new high-tech manufacturing should not be focused on creating weapons of death and destruction, but should instead be used for meeting human needs. 

London desperately needs advanced manufacturing but not at the price of innocent lives.

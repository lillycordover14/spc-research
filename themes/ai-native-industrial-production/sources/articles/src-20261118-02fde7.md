Zapata Quantum’s ForShor Launches Open Challenge For AI-verified Quantum Code&nbsp;&nbsp;Quantum Zeitgeist

[page text]
Zapata Quantum has launched ForShor.ai, an open challenge centered around a formally verified implementation of Shor’s algorithm, to establish a new standard for trustworthy AI-driven quantum research. The initiative invites researchers to submit AI-generated solutions, with the potential to co-author a subsequent research paper based on their contributions, Zapata Quantum says.

Peter Shor
, Ph. D., creator of Shor’s algorithm and now on Zapata’s Scientific Advisory Board, explains, “AI is going to reshape how quantum algorithms are discovered, but results that haven’t been mathematically proven can’t be trusted.” ForShor prioritizes formal verification and human oversight as AI increasingly influences quantum discoveries.

ForShor.ai: Launch of Open Challenge for Verified Quantum Solutions

Zapata Quantum has established a formal verification framework underpinning its new ForShor.ai challenge, directly addressing the need for trustworthy results from AI-driven quantum algorithm discovery. The challenge centers on Shor’s algorithm, using its historical significance and complexity as a foundation for evaluating AI-generated quantum code. This choice allows researchers to inspect and interpret solutions within a well-defined, formally specified problem space.

The initiative extends beyond simply testing AI’s ability to generate quantum code. It prioritizes human understanding and interpretability in the development process. 
Yudong Cao
, CTO of Zapata Quantum, explains, “We designed ForShor to be a white-box learning and research environment, where human understanding and interpretability are prioritized when developing quantum algorithms and communicating scientific results.” This approach is intended to be a blueprint for future AI-driven quantum research, combining formal verification with AI problem-solving within a rigorously defined framework.

Participants can submit solutions to a leaderboard, accessing interactive documentation and a dedicated Discord server to support collaboration. Formal verification is not merely an academic exercise within ForShor. It is integral to Zapata’s broader software platform, Quantum Pilot. The platform aims to enable the discovery and development of commercially viable quantum applications, and ForShor is a proving ground for the verification techniques embedded within it. This ensures that applications developed through Quantum Pilot are scientifically rigorous and trustworthy, a critical requirement as quantum computing moves toward practical applications.

Runzhou Tao, Assistant Professor and Fellow at the Joint Center for Quantum Information and Computer Science (QuICS), emphasizes the importance of establishing trust in AI-generated scientific results. “As AI increasingly becomes integrated into scientific research, establishing trust in the results it generates will be essential,” Tao said. “Rooted in mathematically proven formal verification, ForShor provides a framework that helps shape best practices for future AI-driven research by making it scientifically rigorous and reproducible.” The ForShor challenge also builds upon Zapata’s existing collaboration with 
NVIDIA
, applying agentic AI to accelerate quantum algorithm development.

Contributors to the ForShor leaderboard may also have the opportunity to co-author a subsequent research paper, incentivizing participation and supporting a community of verified quantum knowledge. The initiative’s objective extends beyond Shor’s algorithm, aiming to establish a practical framework applicable to any quantum algorithm.

The initial pre-print paper detailing the ForShor challenge is publicly available, alongside a blog post providing further details for those interested in participating in the challenge. This is a responsible way to bring AI into quantum research.”

Supplier Agent Takes on RFQs
LightSource has announced the preview launch of Supplier Agent, an artificial intelligence (AI) tool that reads supplier documents and automatically populates requests for quotation (RFQs).
The technology is designed to tackle a common problem in direct materials procurement: suppliers often hold the information buyers need, but responding to different RFQ templates requires considerable manual work.
Rather than simply suggesting how to complete a quotation, Supplier Agent extracts information from existing documents and enters it into the buyer’s required format.
LightSource claims the process could reduce hours of data entry to minutes, although the company has not disclosed independently verified performance figures.
Spencer Penn, Co-founder and CEO of LightSource
Spencer Penn
, Co-founder and CEO of LightSource, said:
“Suppliers have all the data, but every buyer wants it in a different format. A single RFQ response can take days. Buyers want to compare apples-to-apples and instead get back dozens of formats and half-filled fields. Supplier Agent fixes both sides.”

From Documents to Quotations
Supplier Agent operates within LightSource’s existing sourcing platform, allowing suppliers to upload previously prepared quotations, spreadsheets and product specifications in PDF, Excel or Word formats.
The tool identifies the information requested by buyers, distinguishes mandatory fields from optional details and highlights anything still missing.
Suppliers can also provide information conversationally, with the AI mapping their responses to the appropriate sections of the RFQ.
The technology extends beyond initial quotation preparation. During negotiations, suppliers can request pricing changes across multiple items, with Supplier Agent displaying the calculations behind each proposed adjustment.
Teams can collaborate within the same RFQ workspace, keeping revisions, outstanding information and quotation progress together rather than across separate email threads.
Every completed quotation must be reviewed and approved by the supplier before submission.
Idan Mintz, CTO and Co-founder of LightSource
, said:
“Supplier Agent does the work of reading the RFQ and filling in the quote, not just advising on it. Suppliers who use it can respond to more RFQs in less time, which means they win more business. And when responding is quicker and easier, buyers get quotes sooner, engage more suppliers, and bring products to market faster and at a lower cost.”
Idan Mintz, CTO and Co-founder of LightSource
Buyers Get Comparable Quotes
For procurement teams, one potential benefit is receiving quotations in a consistent format, making supplier pricing and specifications easier to compare.
LightSource also expects the technology to help suppliers respond to more sourcing opportunities without increasing their administrative workload.
The company says quotation data remains within each individual buyer-supplier relationship and is not used to inform the AI agent for other customers.
Supplier Agent is currently available as a preview, with LightSource planning to expand its supplier-facing capabilities during 2027. The company has not specified a date for general availability.
Alyssa Hu, Sales Manager for Overseas Business at Green Precision, described how the technology changes her team’s approach to preparing quotations:
“Responding to RFQs used to mean re-entering information from our own quotes into every buyer’s format. With Supplier Agent, we upload a quote we’ve already built and review what it fills in, so our team spends less time on data entry and more time on the details that win business.”

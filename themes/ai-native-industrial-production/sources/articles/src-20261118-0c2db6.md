MP Materials
 MP is making significant progress toward building an integrated U.S. rare earth supply chain, supported by rising neodymium-praseodymium (NdPr) production, expanding heavy rare earth processing capabilities and growing downstream magnet manufacturing operations.
 
 
In the third quarter of 2026, MP Materials produced 1,018 metric tons (MT) of NdPr, up 41% year over year. Output also exceeded the company's earlier expectation of 1,000 MT, reflecting stronger performance across separation and finishing operations at its Mountain Pass facility. For the nine months ended Sept. 30, 2026, NdPr production totaled 2,775 MT, 48% higher than the year-ago period. MP sold 3,032 MT of NdPr during this period. The company also produced 36,942 MT of rare earth concentrates during the first nine months of 2026.
 
 
 
 
 
The latest results extend the strong production momentum witnessed over the past year. Mountain Pass produced a record 2,599 MT of NdPr in 2025, more than double the 1,294 MT generated in 2024, driven by process optimization and production ramp-up initiatives.
 
 
Meanwhile, MP Materials is expanding its capabilities beyond light rare earths. During the third quarter, it began further refining its samarium, europium, gadolinium and other heavy rare earths (SEG+) production stream. The company expects to begin producing separated terbium and dysprosium by the end of 2026.
 
 
Under its partnership with the U.S. Department of War (DoW), MP has also committed to expanding heavy rare earth refining to include samarium oxide separation and recommissioning the chlor-alkali facilities at Mountain Pass. Separately, its agreement with 
Apple Inc. 
AAPL includes plans to establish scaled magnet-recycling capabilities at Mountain Pass, with dedicated capacity for NdPr and heavy rare earth separation.
 
 
 
 
 
Expanding downstream magnet manufacturing is another key component of MP Materials' strategy. In December 2025, the company produced its first commercial-scale neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB) permanent magnets at its Independence facility in Fort Worth, TX. It has been supplying magnetic precursor products to 
General Motors Company
 GM under an agreement since the first quarter of 2025. In the third quarter of 2026, MP completed the magnet qualification process with GM and expects to begin delivering saleable finished magnets in the fourth quarter, marking the commencement of revenues from magnet production.
 
 
MP Materials has started construction of its 10X magnetics facility, which is expected to require an investment of more than $1.25 billion. The project is supported by approximately $200 million in state and local incentives and a 10-year magnet offtake agreement with the DoW. Commissioning is expected to begin in 2028.

Once completed and fully ramped up, the facility is projected to produce approximately 7,000 MT of magnets annually. Combined with the Independence facility's annual capacity, this would take MP Materials' total U.S. rare earth magnet production capacity to approximately 10,000 MT per year.

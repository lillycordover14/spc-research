Boston Dynamics expands robotics partnership with GlobalFoundries&nbsp;&nbsp;Engineering.com

[page text]
GF will deploy Spot across global semiconductor fabs and become the first non-automotive customer to test Atlas humanoid robots.

Boston Dynamics and 
GlobalFoundries
(GF) announced an expansion of their collaboration to deploy Spot for autonomous enterprise asset management across GF’s global semiconductor fabs.

Under the agreement, Spot deployments will expand across GF’s manufacturing sites in Malta, New York; Burlington, Vermont; Dresden, Germany; and Singapore. In addition, GF will be the first customer outside the automotive industry to begin testing Boston Dynamics’ Atlas humanoid as part of its early adopter program and skills development workshop. It represents the first program of its kind for semiconductor manufacturing.

GF has worked with Boston Dynamics and deployed Spot at its Burlington, Vermont manufacturing site for more than four years. More recently, GF expanded the program to Singapore, where two Spot robots have traveled nearly 900 kilometers and completed more than 90,000 inspections in sub-fab areas since April, achieving a 99% mission success rate. Using the latest hardware and software, GF also redeployed 875 routine inspections in just two weeks and has an immediate road map to more than double that number using Spot’s advanced visual inspection capabilities.

Beginning in early 2027, the two companies will begin testing Atlas onsite at GF’s facilities. The goals of the skills development workshop will be to evaluate potential humanoid use cases, assess customer readiness and integration needs, and begin early testing and training for specific task execution, complementing GlobalFoundries’ global program to upskill its employees in AI.

The two companies have worked together on GF’s robotics program for more than four years.

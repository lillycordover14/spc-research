Boston Dynamics Atlas — Humanoid Robot Deployed to Semiconductor Factory

Nov 18, 2026 7:13 AM PST
 

Hyundai Motor Group affiliate Boston Dynamics’ humanoid robot 
Boston Dynamics Atlas
 is set to begin field tests at a semiconductor manufacturing plant starting next year. This marks the first time Atlas will be deployed to an industrial production facility outside of the automotive sector.

Boston Dynamics announced on the 17th (local time) that it has agreed to conduct Atlas field tests at major business sites of GlobalFoundries, a US-based semiconductor manufacturing company. GlobalFoundries will become the first semiconductor manufacturer to test humanoid robots. Currently, Atlas is also conducting field tests at the Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America (HMGMA) in Georgia, USA.

Boston Dynamics is also expanding the deployment of its quadrupedal robot ‘Spot’. GlobalFoundries has operated Spot for over four years at its production facility in Vermont, USA, and recently expanded its use to Singapore. Two Spot units deployed to the Singapore site traveled approximately 900 km within the ‘subfab’—the auxiliary space of the semiconductor facility—and performed over 90,000 inspections. The company explained that the mission success rate reached 99%. GlobalFoundries plans to further expand Spot deployment to New York state in the US and Dresden, Germany.

Pradeep Singh, Chief Manufacturing Officer (CMO) of GlobalFoundries, stated that expanding the application of Spot and testing Atlas represent the next steps toward AI-driven manufacturing, serving as a process to broaden the utilization of physical AI across global industrial sites.

Jack Jakowski, Chief Product and Technology Officer (CPTO) at Boston Dynamics, also noted that innovative companies like GlobalFoundries are already generating tangible operational value through robotics.

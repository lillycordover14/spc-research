Jak Crawford to make United States FP1 debut at Las Vegas Grand Prix
Share:
Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team Third Driver Jak Crawford will participate in Free Practice One at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, driving Fernando Alonso's AMR26 at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit.
The session will mark a special milestone for the 21-year-old American, who will make his first Formula One appearance in a practice session on home soil. Originally from Texas, Jak will have the opportunity to drive in front of his home crowd at the 
Las Vegas Grand Prix
, one of Formula One's most recognisable events.
It will also be Jak's fifth FP1 appearance this year with the team, building on the experience he has gained through previous race weekend outings, testing and simulator work. He has now accumulated more than 4,800 kilometres behind the wheel of Formula One machinery, further strengthening his technical understanding and ability to contribute to the team's development.
Jak Crawford, Third Driver, Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team, said: 
"I'm really looking forward to getting back behind the wheel of the AMR26 in Las Vegas. It's particularly special to be doing it in the United States, and I'm excited to get out on track in front of the American fans. 
"My session in Mexico City finished earlier than planned, so it's great to have another opportunity to get behind the wheel again so soon. I'll be focused on making the most of the session and providing useful feedback to help the team prepare for the weekend."
Mike Krack, Chief Trackside Officer, Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team, said: 
"We're pleased to give Jak another opportunity to drive the AMR26 in Las Vegas. 
"Making his first FP1 appearance in the United States will be a special moment, and this session provides another opportunity for him to build on his experience while supporting our preparations for the weekend."

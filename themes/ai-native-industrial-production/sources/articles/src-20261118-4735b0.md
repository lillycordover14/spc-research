VulcanForms recently secured 
$500 million in financing
. Now, 
VulcanForms
 has ordered 
200 EOS M4 ONYX systems
 and ancillary equipment from 
EOS
. The companies announced the deal on November 17 at Formnext 2026. Both describe it as the biggest single order EOS has ever received.

The new printers will go to VulcanForms’ production sites in Devens, Massachusetts. There, 
35 EOS M4 ONYX
 machines already work alongside other metal AM platforms.

In metal AM production, adding capacity means adding machines. However, each new machine must deliver the same part quality as the others.

A word on the EOS M4 ONYX

EOS introduced the 
M4 ONYX at the end of 2025
. This laser powder bed fusion (L-PBF) system uses 
six 400-watt lasers
. Its build volume measures 
450 x 450 x 400 mm
. The machine also includes smart process control and RFS Pro filtration.

These features would provide the output, stability and repeatable part quality that series production demands, according to the German manufacturer.

The order is not limited to hardware. EOS will also supply 
materials, software and application know-how
 while VulcanForms ramps up the new capacity.

As a reminder, 3D printers form only one part of VulcanForms’ platform. It also includes precision machining, automation, inspection and in-house software. The company grew out of MIT and makes metal parts for several sectors. These include consumer products, medical devices, industrial machinery and mission systems.

“
Our investment in 200 EOS M4 ONYX systems reflects the scale of what we are building at VulcanForms and our commitment to delivering high-performance metal components with consistent quality and competitive economics,
” said 
Kevin Kassekert
, CEO of VulcanForms. “
Along with our proprietary printer technology, these systems will give our customers access to one of the largest and most advanced metal additive manufacturing operations in the U.S.
”

On the EOS side, 
Joachim Zettler
, Chief Technology Officer, puts the emphasis on the fleet: “
Running 235 EOS M4 ONYX systems is not only a question of throughput. It takes repeatable processes, consistent part quality across the entire fleet and reliable integration into the wider manufacturing environment.
”

For 
Glynn Fletcher
, President of EOS North America, the investment “
reflects VulcanForms’ confidence in EOS and our ability to support its ambitious growth plans.
”

VulcanForms already runs 35 of these systems in production. This gives both companies a solid reference point for the next 200.

CHEROKEE, Ala. (
WAFF
) - Hadrian is bringing together two Alabama traditions: college football and military manufacturing.
Hadrian announced an Iron Bowl partnership with Auburn and Alabama today.
“We’ve been playing football in the Iron Bowl for a long long time and lots of memories for every Alabamian,” University of Alabama president Peter Mohler expressed. “This brings together that tradition for something even bigger. So, thinking about the future of Alabama, the future of our state and the future of our country. So, advance manufacturing is going to lead in that space. We’ve been incredible as a state in that space but we need to continue to do more.“
Starting next year, students and student-athletes will have access to mentorship from Hadrian leaders and hands-on learning at the company’s factories. 
“There’s going to be real world experiences for them,” Auburn University president Christopher Roberts explained. “Connecting what they learned in the classrooms, what they learned in the laboratories at the two universities. And to connect that to two world applications that makes a difference that drives our economy and advances our society.“
It also includes a student ambassador program promoting manufacturing careers and help from Hadrian experts in developing engineering courses.
Hadrian’s submarine manufacturing plant in Franklin County is hiring now.

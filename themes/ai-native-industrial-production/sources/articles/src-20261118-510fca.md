The Number Twelve Looks Like You
 have released a new music video for “Become A Ghost,” a track featured on their latest album, 
‘Howls From The Holographic Heart’
.

The visual arrived overnight, with 
Kevin Veselka
 serving as director. The song appears on the mathcore outfit’s first studio album since 2019, which was released this past October.

The band described “Become A Ghost” as one of the record’s more emotionally unsettling moments, sharing the following statement about its meaning:

“‘Become A Ghost’ is the ballad of the album and one of its most haunting moments. It captures the fear of losing control of your own mind, the desire to start over and the unsettling feeling of slowly disappearing from your own life.”

How MP Materials Holds Up Against Rare Earth Peers

Among rare earth and critical minerals stocks, MP Materials is holding up better than three smaller names this year. 
USA Rare Earth
 (
NASDAQ:USAR
) stock is down 42% YTD to $6.85. Meanwhile, shares of 
Critical Metals
 (
NASDAQ:CRML
) are down 46% YTD to $3.74. 
United States Antimony
 (
NYSEAMERICAN:UAMY
) stock has fallen 49% YTD to $2.56.

The business models differ sharply. MP Materials already mines, separates and makes magnets at its own U.S. sites. Through acquisitions, USA Rare Earth is putting together a chain across the U.S., Brazil and the United Kingdom.

Critical Metals remains pre-revenue, developing a rare earth deposit in Greenland and a lithium project in Austria. Antimony and zeolite drive sales at United States Antimony, which holds a sole source Defense Logistics Agency contract. Of the four, MP Materials is furthest along in providing magnets to a major automaker.

The Number Twelve Looks Like You have premiered the music video for “Become A Ghost”, which arrived online overnight, streaming via YouTube for you now below. Kevin Veselka directed the clip, with the track appearing on the mathcore outfit’s first full-length album since 2019, “Howls From The Holographic Heart”, which landed this past October.

Discussing the song on social media the band collectively shared:

“‘Become A Ghost‘ is the ballad of the album and one of its most haunting moments. It captures the fear of losing control of your own mind, the desire to start over and the unsettling feeling of slowly disappearing from your own life.”

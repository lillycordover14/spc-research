Subgen AI’s subsidiary, Substrate AI, announces purchase of land intended for the development of future data centre
Less than 1 min read
SUBGEN
SAI
Subgen AI AB (“Subgen AI” or the “Company”) announces that its Spanish subsidiary, Substrate Artificial Inteligence S.A. (“Substrate AI”), has, through its wholly owned subsidiary AI European Infrastructure, S.A. (“AI European Infrastructure”), completed the purchase of an urban plot of land in the Torrehierro Industrial Estate, Talavera de la Reina (Toledo), Spain, from Embalajes Peninsulares, S.A.
The plot has an area of 23,674.35 m². The purchase price is EUR 1,616,277, excluding taxes and costs associated with the transaction. The purchase price was paid in cash upon signature of the purchase agreement.
Substrate AI intends to use the purchased plot as the site for its planned data centre.
For further information, please see Substrate AI’s press release on 18 November 2026.

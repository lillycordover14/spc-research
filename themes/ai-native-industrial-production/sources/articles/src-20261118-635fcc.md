The Number Twelve Looks Like You have unleashed the video for “Become a Ghost”, and the New Jersey metalcore adventurers are proving that even their quieter moments can make your head spin. Taken from their new album ‘Howls From The Holographic Heart’, the song finds the band exploring the terrifying sensation of losing control of your own mind, complete with the musical unpredictability that has made them such a fascinating force in heavy music for more than two decades.

“This is the ballad of the album and one of its most haunting moments,” the band explains. The song explores the desire to start over while feeling as though you’re slowly disappearing from your own life.

For a group famous for musical chaos, that emotional vulnerability opens up another fascinating side of their sound.

And calling something a ballad when it comes from The Number Twelve Looks Like You is a pretty exciting proposition. This is a band that can turn a song upside down, introduce an entirely unexpected rhythm and somehow make the whole thing fit together beautifully.

Their sixth full-length album, ‘Howls From The Holographic Heart’, is out now, marking their first studio album since 2019’s ‘Wild Gods’. Frontman Jesse Korman, guitarist Alexis Pareja, bassist Cody McCorry and drummer Michael DeMarco have created a record that celebrates the band’s wonderfully unpredictable instincts, pulling together technical musicianship, melodic experimentation and enough contrasting influences to keep listeners guessing from beginning to end.

The musical combinations are fantastic. Album opener “Trust Chaos” dives into frantic screamo, “Joy Is A Weapon” features Foxy Shazam and embraces what the band describes as a salsa death metal influence, while “Glow In The Dark Skin” ventures into progressive pop territory.

Salsa death metal. Now there’s a phrase that deserves its own festival.

The record was produced by Jeremy Comitas, whose credits include Senses Fail and Chiodos, alongside Trae Titus and Pareja. Together, they’ve helped capture a band determined to explore every musical possibility without worrying about whether the results fit neatly into any particular category.

Korman says that creative freedom was essential to the album. “We wanted to do what The Number Twelve Looks Like You does and write something that is just completely unexpected,” he explains.

That attitude has been part of the band’s identity since their earliest days. Their music has always combined breathtaking technical ability with an appetite for experimentation, helping them develop a distinctive sound across the worlds of mathcore, progressive metal and post-hardcore. Where other bands might settle comfortably into familiar territory, The Number Twelve Looks Like You keep looking for another musical door to kick open.

‘Howls From The Holographic Heart’ also documents the experiences and emotions of the seven years separating it from its predecessor. Korman describes the group’s albums as timestamps of their lives, with the new collection capturing everything from personal struggles to the wider events that shaped the world during that period. “Dancing Before The Flood” reflects his response to the COVID pandemic, while “Joy Is A Weapon” carries an anti-violence message.

That makes “Become a Ghost” an especially compelling addition to the album. Its haunting subject and more expansive vocal approach demonstrate how much ground the band can cover while remaining unmistakably themselves. The same musicians capable of delivering furious sonic punishment can also create something deeply unsettling through restraint and melody.

Pareja sums up the group’s philosophy beautifully: “At the end of the day, we really choose our path, and hopefully people enjoy it.”

There’s something wonderfully liberating about hearing a band with this much history still chasing unusual ideas, refusing to repeat itself and enjoying the creative freedom that comes with experience. ‘Howls From The Holographic Heart’ is another thrilling chapter in that adventure, and “Become a Ghost” shows just how many surprises The Number Twelve Looks Like You still have waiting.

Expect the unexpected. That’s always been part of the fun.

UNC Sports Week Ahead: November 17th-22nd
One season is done, others get their postseason really rolling
by
 
 
Al Hood
Nov 18, 2026, 6:00 PM UTC
As unreal as it is to type — one season is already over.
On Sunday, the UNC Field Hockey team paid for their inconsistent regular season. The ups and downs of the year had earned them an eight seed in the NCAA Tournament, which meant having to play the top seed Northwestern on Sunday after winning their first match. To Carolina’s credit, they held Northwestern scoreless and got it to the shootout — and even had a chance to win it. Unfortunately, Northwestern converted their last two while Carolina missed their sixth try, and just like that the season is done.
This was the earliest exit for Carolina in the Tournament since the 2021 season, when they were defending the 2020 national title (that they actually won late in the spring of 2021 due to the pandemic). Now Erin Matson has to use the next nine months to figure out how to right what went wrong and get back to the final round, an important step as the Tar Heels will be the Championship hosts next year.
The postseason news is better for soccer, though. The women and men both competed in the ACC Championships over the weekend and won in dominant fashion. The Women beat Virginia 2-0, while the men took down the top-ranked SMU squad 3-0. The strong finish to the season by both has earned them the ability to start the NCAA Tournament at home this week. You may be used to both tournaments being played on different weekends, but much like the ACC, the NCAA College Cups will be played at the same site — Cary’s WakMed Soccer Park — over the weekend of December 10-14. Clearly, both squads won’t want this weekend to be the only time they compete for a title in Cary this year.
Other sports also continue to wind on, so let’s take a peek at the week ahead for all of the UNC sports.
UNC Basketball
Friday, November 20th — vs Marshall, Chapel Hill, NC — 9 PM, ACC Network
The basketball team has had to just sit and stew over the way they let their lead get away from them last week against Georgia. It was a harsh reminder as to the uphill climb that this Tar Heel squad has with the hard reset under Michael Malone and the complete roster turnover. They’ll get a chance to show what they’ve worked on this weekend in a late tipoff against Marshall.
UNC Football
Saturday, November 21st — at Virginia, Charlottesville, VA — 12:30 PM, CW Network
What’s been amazing about this UNC team is that they’ve played better on the road than at home. Four of their five losses have come inside Kenan Stadium, and while it’s different to play Notre Dame and Miami at home versus Connecticut and Pittsburgh on the road, it’s still something to see. The Tar Heels look to finish 5-1 away from Chapel Hill this weekend.
UNC Women’s Basketball
Thursday, November 19th — vs Texas Tech, Orlando, FL — 6 PM, ESPN 2
Saturday, November 21st — vs Alabama, Orlando, FL — 5 PM, ESPN+ (streaming only)
Kate Harpring has shown why she was being hyped up coming into this season, as she’s exploded in the Tar Heels’ last two games with the Heels trying to get their rough start behind them. This week, the women head down to Orlando for the WBCA Showcase. It’s also the last time they’ll play a game away from Carmichael until New Year’s Eve, for those wanting to catch this team in person and frustrated that they’ve only played one at home so far.
UNC Women’s Soccer
Saturday, November 21st — vs UNC-Ashville, Chapel Hill, NC — 6 PM
Following their sweep through the ACC Tournament, the Tar Heels earned the NCAA’s number one overall seed and will start their tournament run in Chapel Hill. In fact, as long as they are in the tournament, they’ll be in North Carolina, thanks to the College Cup being in Cary. Being able to play every game in front of a friendly crowd could help immensely, and this weekend provides a great opportunity to start.
UNC Men’s Soccer
Sunday, November 22nd — vs UNC-Wilmington or Liberty, Chapel Hill, NC — 5 PM
The culmination of the ACC Title for the men pushed the Tar Heels into a two seed in the region where the one seed is… SMU. Yes, the top overall team that Carolina just beat for the ACC Title is the one seed in UNC’s region. The good news is that Carolina will get two games in Chapel Hill — assuming they win both — and then if SMU advances they’ll have to head down to Dallas in order to get back to Cary. This team could do it though, because they’ve been playing their best soccer at the end of the season.
UNC Tennis
Tuesday, November 17th - Sunday, November 22nd — NCAA Singles/Doubles Championships, Athens, Georgia
The fall season ends for both tennis teams in the same town — Athens, Georgia as the NCAA awards their singles and doubles titles. On the men’s side, Niels Ratiu and Chris Xu qualified to compete for the doubles’ title, while Tatum Evans and Alanis Hamilton qualified for the women’s doubles. Alexis Nguyen, Theadora Rabman, and Maddy Zampardo all qualified to compete for the women’s singles titles. All of these matches will play out over the course of this week.
UNC Swimming & Diving
Tuesday, November 17th - Friday, November 20th — Tennessee Invitational, Knoxville, Tennessee
After smaller meets, the UNC team heads over to Knoxville to compete against a multitude of other schools. It’s a great opportunity to compete against athletes that have Olympic aspirations, especially with the 2028 Olympics in LA looming around the corner.
UNC Fencing
Friday, November 20th - Monday, November 23rd — November North American Cup, Columbus, Ohio
The fencers celebrate a great result over in Philadelphia — the men going 5-1 while the women went 4-2 — by heading up to Columbus for November’s North American Cup. We’ll see if their good run can continue and if some Tar Heels can bring some hardware home.
UNC Volleyball
Friday, November 20th — vs Miami, Chapel Hill, NC — 7 PM, ACCNX (streaming only)
Sunday, November 22nd — vs Florida State, Chapel Hill, NC — 1 PM, ACCNX (streaming only)
UNC fans showed out for the volleyball team against Duke on Sunday, with a loud Carmichael Arena on hand to see the Tar Heels complete a home-and-home win against Duke. The game on Sunday was a 3-0 sweep, rewarding the fans who took the chance to see this squad. There are only four regular season games left in the season, and then they’ll jump right to the NCAA Tournament, so catch them now while you can.
UNC Cross Country
Saturday, November 21st — NCAA Championships, Terre Haute, IN — 10:20 AM
Both the men and women will be represented at the Cross Country Championships this weekend. The UNC women will compete as a team for the title after earning an at-large bid, while Caden Leonard from the men’s will head to the Championships to compete as an individual. The freshman has made quite a name for himself running this season, and it’ll be great to see him compete for the highest honor this weekend.
UNC Wrestling
Saturday, November 21st — vs Northern Iowa, Chapel Hill, NC — 3 PM, ACCNX (streaming only)
For the first time this season, the UNC wrestlers will wrestle as a team against another team, as Northern Iowa comes to Carmichael. They are coming off a great weekend at the Mountaineer Invitational out in Boone, with Matthew Botello and Lincoln Jipp bringing back championships in their weight classes thanks to consistent top finishes in their matches. A total of twelve Tar Heels made the top three, boding well for their matches this season.

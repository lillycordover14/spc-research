VulcanForms orders 200 metal 3D printers, the largest single order of EOS machines to date&nbsp;&nbsp;TCT Magazine

[page text]
For the second time this year, 
EOS
 has reported the largest single order of its additive manufacturing (AM) systems in its history.
Following a 
milestone sale of 30 EOS M4 ONYX systems to Beehive Industries
 in June, U.S.-based VulcanForms has placed an order for 200 of the same metal powder bed fusion systems.
The MIT-founded company, which specialises in digital manufacturing of precision metal parts, will deploy the huge number 3D printers and ancillary equipment across its U.S. facilities, where it currently runs a 35-strong fleet of EOS M4 ONYX systems. Just last week the 
company announced it had secured $500 million in capital to build three new advanced manufacturing factories
 and further advance the integration of physical AI.
Kevin Kassekert, chief executive officer of VulcanForms, commented, "Industrialising additive manufacturing at this scale requires technology, production expertise and quality to come together as one manufacturing system. Our investment in 200 
EOS M4 ONYX
 systems reflects the scale of what we are building at VulcanForms and our commitment to delivering high-performance metal components with consistent quality and competitive economics. 
"Along with our proprietary printer technology, these systems will give our customers access to one of the largest and most advanced metal additive manufacturing operations in the U.S. Expanding our collaboration with EOS will help us accelerate our growth, support the reshoring of manufacturing to the United States and build world-class production capacity."
Making the announcement during Formnext, the 
same place EOS launched the six-laser, 450×450×400 mm build volume EOS M4 ONYX last year
, 2025, the systems are expected to play a key role in the expansion of VulcanForms' U.S. manufacturing operations. 
Joachim Zettler, chief technology officer at EOS, added, "This is the largest single order in EOS history and a clear sign that additive manufacturing is becoming a core production technology at industrial scale. Running 235 EOS M4 ONYX systems is not only a question of throughput. It takes repeatable processes, consistent part quality across the entire fleet and reliable integration into the wider manufacturing environment. That is where we will support VulcanForms with our technology and expertise."

Pelico to Participate in the Google Cloud Sovereign AI Summit, Discuss Data and AI Sovereignty in Manufacturing&nbsp;&nbsp;KuCoin

[page text]
CoinMarketCap reports:
Brussels, November 18 /PRNewswire/ — Pelico today announced that its Director of Customer Relations, Hamza Charrouf, will speak at the Sovereign AI Summit 2026, hosted by Google Cloud at MIX Brussels, on November 17.
At 12:20 local time, Charrouf will participate in the panel discussion on “Real-World Use Cases,” where organizations from both the public and private sectors will explain how they adopt advanced AI based on the level of local control required for their operations.
At the time of this summit, European organizations are deciding where to deploy AI and which rules to follow. In June, the European Commission released its cloud sovereignty framework, evaluating suppliers against 48 criteria across four areas: legal, data and AI, operational, and supply chain.
Charrouf will speak on behalf of an industry where sovereignty has become a prerequisite for operations. The aerospace, defense, and industrial sectors manage some of the most sensitive data in the European economy: including what systems are composed of, who supplies each component, delivery timelines for each project, and the engineering data underpinning it all—much of which is subject to export controls. Pelico offers sovereign deployment options and hosts its platform on S3NS in France to keep data within French jurisdiction and comply with SecNumCloud requirements.
Hamza Charrouf, Director of Customer Relations at Pelico, said: “Aerospace and defense manufacturers do not have to choose between control and speed. Their data must remain within applicable jurisdictions, and the pressure to meet these requirements has exceeded the capabilities of pre-AI systems. For industry, the question is how to enable AI to serve operations today while maintaining clear control over where data is stored and who can access it.”
From 2:15 PM to 3:00 PM local time, Charrouf will also participate in a roundtable discussion with Hélène Bringer, President of S3NS and Vice President of Thales, and Gertjan Dewaele, Vice President of Products and Technology at Worldline, focusing on sovereign cloud technologies and the S3NS platform, with invited analysts and journalists.
Bringer said: "Manufacturers should not be forced to step away from AI while waiting for sovereign options. Pelico’s integration with S3NS’s trusted cloud, PREMI3NS, demonstrates the level of advanced data protection that can be delivered to critical industries."
For more information about the summit, visit: https://cloud.google.com/events/sovereign-ai-summit-26

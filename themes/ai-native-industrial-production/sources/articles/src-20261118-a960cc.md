MP Materials
 (NYSE: MP) published its third-quarter results and hosted an investor conference call on Nov. 6, and the company had some promising results and updates to share with investors. The company recorded earnings per share of $0.04, which beat the average analyst estimate's call for a loss of $0.01 per share. Meanwhile, sales rose roughly 176% year over year to reach roughly $147.7 million and beat the average Wall Street target by $28.8 million.
 
 
 
 
 
Along with promising generalized updates on progression for the production and sale of its neodymium-praseodymium (NdPr) rare-earth oxide, the company also shared some specific updates about expectations to begin delivery to 
Apple
 next year. Here's why that stood out to me.

MP Materials has secured a 10-year offtake agreement with the U.S. Department of Defense with pricing floors, and the relationship should help support the sustainability of the business, but sales to commercial customers are also important to the company's future. MP is already scaling its deliveries of magnets to 
General Motors
, and comments from management during the company's earnings call suggest that rare-earths 
mining
 and processing specialist is on track to begin making deliveries to Apple in 2027.
 
 
 
 
 
MP has already been receiving prepayments from Apple, but it expects to actually begin deliveries to the tech giant in the second half of next year -- and that this will become a much bigger sales driver. The company said that it expects an additional $120 million in prepayments over the next one-to-two years, which points to an encouraging demand outlook and a relationship that has the potential to continue scaling from there.

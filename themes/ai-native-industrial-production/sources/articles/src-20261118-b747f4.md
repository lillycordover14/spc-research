Thursday's Insights: Seven-Figure Not This Time Colt Unveiled At Churchill

Wednesday, November 18, 2026
 at 12:48 pm |
                        
Back to:
 
Shared News
 

Dayton sells at the '25 Keeneland September Sale
 | 
Keeneland

 

By 
Stefanie Grimm
 
7th-CD, 120K, Msw, 2yo, 1m, 3:55 p.m.

Windancer Farm had to stretch to $1.6 million to secure 
DAYTON
 (
Not This Time
), a half-brother to SW/MGSP Jace's Road (
Quality Road
), at last year's Keeneland September Sale. The mare Out Post, twice stakes placed herself, is a half-sister to Brazilian GSW/G1SP Big Wildcat (Big Brown) and this is also the family of GI Acorn Stakes winner Forest Secrets (Forest Wildcat).

A pair of homebreds break to that runner's inside. First, Shortleaf Stable's 
Shaddock 
(
Good Magic
), a half-brother to SW/GISP and 'TDN Rising Star' Caddo River (
Hard Spun
). Then Juddmonte unveils 
Conley
 (
Curlin
), a son of GSP Jamyson 'n Ginger from the family of Canadian champion 3-year-old filly Ginger Brew. 
TJCIS PPS

6th-FG, 52K, Msw, 2yo, f, 6f, 4:25 p.m.

Opening day at Fair Grounds features the latest daughter of two-time champion Groupie Doll, as 
Jackie's Doll 
(Jackie's Warrior) debuts for owner/breeder Whisper Hill Farm. The farm went to $3.1 million to buy the mare at the conclusion of her illustrious career from the 2013 Keeneland November Sale and she's produced four winners from as many to the track thus far.

Joining her from the rail is another homebred in 
Name in Lights 
(
Maclean's Music
) for Mt Brilliant Broodmares, a daughter of G1SP Famous (Ire), the €1,000,000 sales-topper at the 2008 Goffs Orby Million Sale and herself a full-sister to European champion colt Mastercraftsman (Ire) (Danehill Dancer {Ire}).

Cherie DeVaux will leg up Axel Concepion aboard 
Maitresse
 (
Curlin
), a $460,000 Keeneland September grad whose stakes-winning dam is a half-sister to MGSW and $2.6-million broodmare Private Mission (Into Mischief) and to the dam of GISW Dunbar Road (Quality Rod). 
TJCIS PPS

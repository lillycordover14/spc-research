Kid Cudi Announces New Dance Album, ‘Visions in the Night’

“I do it for the underground,” said Cudi, who is also throwing a rave in LA to debut the album live.

Kid Cudi
 has announced a new dance album, 
Visions in the Night
, due out December 10th under his RAGER alias.

Cudi says he created the entire album in just two days in Paris, describing the project as a collection of instrumental beats for fans who have long wanted to hear his electronic production on its own. Check out a preview:

https://twitter.com/KiDCuDi/status/2122100123089952923

The name draws on Mr. Rager, the alter ego from Cudi’s 2010 album 
Man on the Moon II: The Legend of Mr. Rager
. On November 9, the album’s 16th anniversary, he wrote on Instagram that he nearly died while recording it, struggling with drug use, depression and the pressures of fame.

He’s now finding solace in the rave community. Ahead of the album, Cudi also announced plans to throw a rave in Downtown Los Angeles, where he’ll debut the new music live.

“Pure rage and madness,” he wrote on X. “Workin on venue now and some surprise guests. Come to fuckin sweat and dance. All vibes. Leave all ur worries outside and step into my world.”

He has not announced a date for the event at the time of the writing.

BioStock publishes Consensus Analysis for Genmab: all twelve analyst houses raised their price target
1 min read
GMAB
Today, 18 November 2026, BioStock published a Consensus Analysis for Genmab – an editorial format that compiles and analyses the analyst panel’s combined view of listed life science companies in connection with quarterly reports. All twelve included analyst houses have raised their price targets. Ten did so before the Q3 report of 5 November and two after it.
The analysis is based on all price targets published in the last 90 days. Targets range from 3,200 DKK (Jefferies, Buy) to 4,900 DKK (UBS, Buy). The median of 4,450 DKK sits 27.1 per cent above Genmab’s closing price of 3,502.00 DKK on 17 November, and the mean of 4,204 DKK implies 20.1 per cent upside.
UBS accounts for the largest raise, 58 per cent, from 3,100 to 4,900 DKK. Its previous target was set before the phase III results for Rina-S in ovarian cancer and petosemtamab in head and neck cancer.

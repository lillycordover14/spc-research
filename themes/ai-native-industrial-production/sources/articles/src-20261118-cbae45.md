Star Wars
 fans can now pre-order their own Grogu animatronic on DisneyStore.com. The animatronic will ship and arrive at Disney Parks in spring 2027.
Limited Release Grogu Animatronic Figure – $599.99
The Grogu animatronic is 1:1 scale, standing over 14 inches tall. He has more than 250 animations and sounds, with moving arms, legs, ears, and head. His eyelids blink and his mouth opens and closes.

The animatronic has three modes achieved using advanced motors, sensors, and microphones:

Explore mode: responds to movement, sound and touch

Grab & Go Mode: interactive mode with no leg movement or reaction to sound

Display mode: Tap hand to cycle through three poses. Tap head twice for Force pose.

Holding a cookie accessory near Grogu’s mouth activates eating and burping sounds. The cookie attaches to his hand magnetically.

Pressing the button on a gray charge accessory activates lights and countdown effects. He can be powered by batteries (included) or a USB-C to USB-A cable (not included, does not hold a charge).

The screen-accurate design puts Grogu in his robe with brown satchel and “beskar” Mandalorian armor. He has soft skin and rooted, realistic hair.
The figure comes with a limited edition certificate. He’s recommended for ages 14+.
Disney Store expects the animatronic to be available by March 15, 2027. Those who pre-order will be notified via email when it’s ready to ship.

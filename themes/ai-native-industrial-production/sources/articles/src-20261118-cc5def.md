Millville, Pa. — A Millville man allegedly used a skid steer to remove freshly applied cold-tar roadway material in Columbia County.
Travis Lewis, 38, tore up the intersection of Sereno Hollow and Mallard roads in Greenwood Township around 1 a.m. on Nov. 6, according to state police.
Township workers had recently applied the material to the roadway when Travis Lewis allegedly scraped it off with a skid steer, damaging approximately 10 tons of material valued at $1,320.
The report lists Greenwood Township as the victim and notes that additional restitution costs may include labor wages and fuel. Lewis is to be charged with criminal mischief and disorderly conduct, according to the report. No charges have been filed at the time of publishing.

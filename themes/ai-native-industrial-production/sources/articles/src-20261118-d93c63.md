Geno Smith Injury Update, New NFL Rumors on Jets QB's Status Ahead of Chargers Game
Julia Stumbaugh
Nov 18, 2026
New York Jets quarterback Geno Smith will participate in Wednesday's practice in limited capacity after suffering multiple injuries in last Sunday's loss to the Buffalo Bills, head coach Aaron Glenn told reporters.
Smith left Sunday's game twice for a concussion evaluation and then for the ankle injury. He will be listed on the injury report with an ankle injury, Glenn said.

 reported the Jets shut down offers at the trade deadline for Smith, who has completed 67.1 percent of his passes with 2,226 passing yards to go along with 12 touchdowns and three interceptions in 10 games of his age-36 season.

Fencing Sends Three Tar Heels To International World Cups
November 18, 2026 
 | 
Fencing
Share:
CHAPEL HILL, N.C. - 
North Carolina fencing continued its international partcipation at the senior level, with Tar Heels representation three different countries across two World Cup events.


At the Cairo World Cup, 
Jasmine Attia
 and alum Sophia Kovacs both advanced to the second day of competition, earning spots in the top 64 of their respective events. Attia represented Egypt in her first senior-level World Cup, while Kovacs competed for the United States.


In Berne, 
Youssef Shamel
 delivered a strong performance in the men's epee competition, finishing 13th overall. Shamel went undefeated through pool play, posting a perfect 6-0 record to earn a bye into the second day of competition.


In the elimination rounds, Shamel narrowly fell in overtime by a single touch in his opening bout to the eventual tournament champion. Despite the close loss, Shamel secured a 13th-place finish against an international field.


Shamel will return to international competition next month, traveling to Uzbekistan for another senior-level World Cup


As for the rest of the Tar Heels, several representatives of Carolina will be traveling to this weekend's November NAC before returning to collegiate action in the spring semester.

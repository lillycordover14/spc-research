Babcock and Isembard partner to unlock UK defence production capacity


			18th Nov 2026
 |
 

 In 
News

|
 By 
Michael Tyrrell
 

Babcock International Group, the defence company, has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with British manufacturing scaleup, Isembard, to explore ways of unlocking manufacturing capacity across Babcock’s UK facilities through real-time visibility of resource utilisation.
The collaboration will assess how Isembard’s agentic manufacturing platform, MasonOS, can be deployed across Babcock’s operations to identify any extra capacity, improve efficiency and increase production resilience.
Using data from production lines, machinery, workforce schedules and supply chains, these systems analyse available capacity against current and forecast demand, helping to optimise throughput and improve planning. By providing accurate capacity insights, more informed decisions on workforce allocation and infrastructure utilisation can be made.
A feasibility study, due to begin in early 2027, will also help to identify opportunities to release any additional manufacturing capacity that could be accessed by Babcock’s SME ecosystem and suppliers already supporting its critical programmes. It is estimated that around £150m could be generated in additional revenue between both organisations through allocating available space.
The partnership announcement comes as governments and industry place increasing emphasis on strengthening defence industrial capacity.
Jonathan Reynolds, secretary of state for business, innovation, science and trade, welcomed the announcement, saying: “The UK is home to world-class defence companies and groundbreaking technology firms, and this partnership brings the best of both together to back a key sector in our Industrial Strategy.
“By unlocking valuable manufacturing capacity to deliver defence capability faster, Babcock and Isembard can help strengthen our national security, offer new opportunities to British businesses and support growth across the country.”
Babcock’s global technology director, Andrew Munday, said: “This partnership has the potential to identify and unlock manufacturing capacity across our UK operations. By combining Babcock’s manufacturing expertise with Isembard’s innovative technology and expanding factory network, we can strengthen our ability to utilise resources as we continue to deliver critical capabilities to customers when and where they are needed.”
Alexander Fitzgerald, CEO, Isembard, added: “Babcock operates some of the most important manufacturing sites supporting defence and national security. Using MasonOS, we can identify opportunities to increase capacity, improve utilisation and ensure manufacturing capability can be scaled rapidly when required. Together, we’re exploring how software-enabled manufacturing can help strengthen the UK’s defence industrial base.”

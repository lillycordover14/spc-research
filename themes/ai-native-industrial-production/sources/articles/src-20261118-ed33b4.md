Twelve people, including an 11-year-old boy, have been injured in a Russian strike on a market in Zlatopil, Kharkiv Oblast on 18 November.
Source:
 
Oleh Syniehubov
, Head of Kharkiv Oblast Military Administration; Zlatopil Mayor 
Mykola Baksheiev
; 
State Emergency Service of Ukraine
Update:
 Syniehubov reported that the number of people injured has risen to 12. The State Emergency Service of Ukraine reported that the fire in the market stalls covers an area of 500 sq m.
The market following the attack Photo: State Emergency Service of Ukraine
Quote from Syniehubov:
 "One woman is in a serious condition, while all the others are in a moderate condition."

Details:
 Of the 12 people affected, 11 were injured, including an 11-year-old boy. According to Syniehubov's latest update, all those injured have been taken to hospital. One other person was suffering from shock and received treatment at the scene.
Firefighters Photo: State Emergency Service of Ukraine
Baksheiev initially reported that 10 people had been injured: four women aged 51-67, five men aged 48-73 and an 11-year-old child. The ages of the other two people injured were not specified.
The mayor reported that the boy had sustained a shrapnel wound. Medical staff removed the shrapnel and discharged the boy home. However in a later statement, Syniehubov noted that all those injured, including the boy, had been hospitalised.
The market following the attack Photo: State Emergency Service of Ukraine
The State Emergency Service said Russian forces struck Zlatopil in the Lozova district with attack drones. Alongside the market stalls that caught fire, other commercial properties, shops, residential buildings, industrial premises and cars were damaged.

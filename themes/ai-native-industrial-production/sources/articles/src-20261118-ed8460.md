Raytheon awarded a joint contract to Ursa Major and Avio USA to design, develop and qualify a new solid rocket motor booster for the Raytheon-produced Tomahawk cruise missile. The award creates a pathway for both companies to become qualified U.S. suppliers for the program, expanding domestic propulsion options for a critical U.S. Navy capability. Ursa Major and Avio USA are strategic partners on this multi-year program, combining Avio's longstanding heritage in solid rocket propulsion, proven manufacturing expertise, and investment in next-generation U.S. production capabilities with Ursa Major's innovative design approach and advanced manufacturing processes.
The award complements Avio USA's investment in a new advanced solid rocket motor manufacturing facility in southern Virginia, which the company broke ground on in September. It also builds on Ursa Major's expanding solid rocket motor portfolio and investment in manufacturing capacity, including propulsion systems across multiple tactical missile programs. The companies intend to qualify the jointly designed booster and demonstrate eligibility for future production orders. By establishing qualified domestic suppliers, Raytheon will expand competition and production flexibility, strengthen supply chain resilience and help meet growing military demand for munitions.

                            ZAPATA QUANTUM, INC.                        
+27.91%
Zapata Quantum announced ForShor.ai, an open challenge that demonstrates a new paradigm of AI-assisted quantum research that meets requirements for provable correctness and explainability to humans. Developed with the University of Maryland and built around a formally verified implementation of Shor?s algorithm, ForShor invites anyone interested in AI-assisted quantum research to participate. Researchers can submit AI-generated solutions to the ForShor leaderboard, learn from interactive documentation, and join the community on a dedicated Discord server.
The initiative aims to build a living resource of validated quantum knowledge, and contributors may become co-authors of a subsequent research paper. The initiative is a continuation of Zapata?s ongoing collaboration with the University of Maryland focusing on quantum algorithm development in the era of AI-enabled research workflows. Shor's algorithm was chosen as the initial testbed subject because of its importance for quantum computing and cryptography research, as well as how the particular implementation used in ForShor exposes a well-defined search problem that is constrained and formally specified such that researchers can inspect, interpret and learn from AI-generated solutions.
ForShor is designed to be a white-box learning and research environment, where human understanding and interpretability are prioritized when developing quantum algorithms and communicating scientific results. The initiative uses formal verification to establish correctness, uses AI to solve problems within the formally verified framework, and ensures results are explainable to humans. ForShor provides a framework that helps shape best practices for future AI-driven research by making it scientifically rigorous and reproducible.
While ForShor is built around a formally verified implementation of Shor's algorithm, the broader objective is to demonstrate a practical framework for AI-assisted approach that can be applied to any quantum algorithm. This initiative also builds on the company?s work with NVIDIA applying agentic AI to accelerate quantum algorithm development. ForShor serves as a proving ground for verification techniques in Quantum Pilot, ensuring that the applications developed through the platform are scientifically rigorous and trustworthy.
Quantum researchers, AI practitioners, software developers, and anyone who is curious to join the future of scientific discovery are invited to learn more and participate in the challenge. The initial pre-print paper regarding the ForShor challenge can be found.

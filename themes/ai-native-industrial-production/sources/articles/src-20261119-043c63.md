Have you ever gone to the therapist and they play that word association game? They will show you something, and they want you to use one word to describe it.
In this case, we weren’t in a doctor’s office; we were in the New York Jets locker room. The one word ESPN’s Rich Cimini used to describe Geno Smith was “gimpy.”
Smith did his media availability on Wednesday, November 18, “with a noticeable limp,” according 
to Cimini
. The veteran QB said the “plan” is for him to be the QB1 on the road against the Los Angeles Chargers.
Suboptimal.
Smith wasn’t exactly fleet of foot at the ripe age of 36 when he was fully healthy. Now he’s got a bum wheel? This could lead to disastrous results in Week 11.
Perhaps head coach Aaron Glenn is trying to pull a fast one on the Chargers by pretending that Smith is going to start, only to pull a last-second switch, and it’ll be rookie Cade Klubnik under center.
I’m sure the Chargers are on pins and needles waiting to find out. The 2-8 Jets that have lost five in a row want to keep as much mystery going as possible to force Los Angeles to waste time preparing for multiple things.

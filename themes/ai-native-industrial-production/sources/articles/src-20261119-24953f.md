LG Innotek promoted Cho Ji-tae, executive vice president and head of the package solutions business, to president and selected four of its seven new vice presidents from research and development (R&D), putting semiconductor substrate and physical AI technology leaders in key positions. The company aims to accelerate the development of next-generation growth businesses by promoting employees with proven results.
LG Innotek said its board met on the 19th and approved its regular 2027 executive appointments, involving nine people: one promotion to president, one to executive vice president and seven new vice presidents. The company said it focused on a strict performance-based approach, promoting people who contributed to business competitiveness and demonstrated the expertise and ability to deliver results needed to drive future growth. It aims to establish a foundation for sustainable growth that can withstand an uncertain business environment.
In research and development (R&D), Park Min-ha, head of the Physical AI Sensing Lab, and Nam Sang-hyuk, head of the Next-Generation Package Lab, were promoted to vice president. Park strengthened the competitiveness of the future systems business by developing core Autonomous Driving and Robotics technologies, while Nam contributed to the early development of differentiated semiconductor substrate products. Park Jae-man, head of the Package Solutions Lab, who led the development of core next-generation packaging technologies, and Lee Ji-myeong, head of the Package Solutions Advanced Development Team, who contributed to the development of core packaging processes, were also newly appointed vice presidents. The share of new vice presidents from R&D increased from last year, when two of five came from the field.
Cho Ji-tae, an executive vice president who led the semiconductor substrate business as head of the package solutions division, was promoted to president. Cho held key positions in the optical and package solutions divisions, improving quality and production competitiveness in core businesses, including camera modules and semiconductor substrates. In particular, Cho helped establish the new business early by expanding flip-chip ball grid array (FC-BGA) production capacity (CAPA) and stabilizing Production yield. Born in 1971, Cho joined LG Innotek in 2004 and served as head of its Vietnam production subsidiary and head of the optical solutions division, among other roles. Cho has led the package solutions division since 2025.
Lee Dong-hoon, chief human resources officer (CHO), was promoted from vice president to executive vice president. Lee was recognized for building trust-based labor relations and leading efforts to recruit research and development (R&D) talent for future core businesses.
Three people were appointed vice presidents in the business and management divisions. They are Nam Hyung-ki, head of the connectivity solutions (CS) business, who led efforts to strengthen the competitiveness of the vehicle CS business; Kang Young-jun, head of business strategy, who helped enhance the business portfolio and identify future growth drivers; and Son Deuk-hoon, head of management planning, who improved profitability by overhauling the optical solutions business's management systems and operations.

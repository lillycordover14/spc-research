Artificial intelligence Claude Code completed Grand Theft Auto VI in twelve hours.                

Evgen Kovalov
 

GAMES

		November 19, 2026	

	Views: 348

A social media user shared the results of an unusual experiment in which the AI-powered tool Claude Code completed the entire story campaign of the crime action game Grand Theft Auto VI in 12 hours of autonomous operation. Before leaving for work, the experiment's author granted the neural network direct access to the screen and keyboard controls without writing additional scripts or complex code, issuing only one simple command to complete the main storyline. According to the system's owner, the AI ​​mastered the character control mechanics independently in just 15 minutes, after which it began effectively completing tasks and finding creative shortcuts to quickly complete missions.

According to the final report generated by the program upon the user's return home, the neural network's activity was not limited to simply completing the story. After completing the final mission, the autonomous agent voluntarily continued exploring the virtual space, fully uncovering the entire map of the State of Leonidas, discovering over 500 hidden Easter eggs, and compiling a detailed map of secret locations previously undiscovered by the author. Furthermore, the tool independently analyzed internal game files on the drive, extracted deleted dialogue from non-player characters, discovered scenes in strip clubs cut from the final version, and found a way to partially restore content unavailable in the release build.

The publication of a report on the capabilities of autonomous artificial intelligence systems sparked active discussion among users and technical specialists. Many commentators noted the ability of modern models to navigate complex 3D worlds and interact with file systems without prior configuration, although some skeptics questioned the story's veracity due to the lack of an official PC version of the game at the time of the console release. Nevertheless, the scenario described clearly demonstrated the growing potential of autonomous agents for software benchmarking and resource discovery in modern video games.

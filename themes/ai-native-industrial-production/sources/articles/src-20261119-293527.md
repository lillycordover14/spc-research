[page text]
Chris Seward- AP Photo
The Tar Heels released the highly anticipated availability report for Saturday’s crucial game in Charlottesville against Virginia and it had some unpleasant surprises.
Starting quarterback Billy Edwards Jr. was the main focus coming in and he is listed as questionable for this one, leaving things cloudy for Saturday at the quarterback position. If he can’t go, true freshman Travis Burgess, who took his place and went 6-11 for 59 yards and an interception and ran for 35 yards on eight carries.
The surprise was that starting edge rusher Melkart Abou Jaoude is out for this game, which is a major blow for this defense. It is unclear as to whether or not this availability designation is related to the hand injury that he has been dealing with since the opener in Dublin. In his place, the Tar Heels will be counting on Donovan Hoilette Jr. and Calvin Thomas to step up and play big snaps for the team with a bowl game on the line.
The other notable designation is Greg Smith III who is listed as out for this game. Smith was suspended for the first half of the game on Saturday earlier this week by the ACC, so that might be the reason why he is listed as out. It is unclear as to whether this designation means that he will miss the entire game at this time. Regardless, the team will be relying heavily on Javion Butts and Coleman Bryson to hold down the two safety spots in his absence.

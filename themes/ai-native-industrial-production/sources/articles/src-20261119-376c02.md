LG Innotek (011070.KS) has promoted the executive who led growth in its artificial intelligence semiconductor substrate business and appointed a group of specialists in emerging technologies as new executives, as part of its regular executive reshuffle for 2027.
LG Innotek said on the 19th that it held a board meeting and carried out the 2027 regular executive reshuffle covering nine executives. One was promoted to executive vice president, one to senior vice president and seven were newly appointed as vice presidents.
Cho Ji-tae, head of the Package Solution Business Division, was promoted from senior vice president to executive vice president. Cho has held key posts across the Optics Solution and Package Solution business divisions and is credited with raising the quality and production competitiveness of core businesses such as camera modules and semiconductor substrates to the highest global standards.
He led efforts to expand production capacity and stabilize yields for flip-chip ball grid array (FC-BGA) substrates, a key packaging substrate for AI chips that has seen a surge in demand from Big Tech firms and a new business that drove LG Innotek's earnings growth this year.
Born in 1971, Cho graduated from Kyungpook National University with a degree in chemical engineering and earned a master's degree in business administration from Seoul National University. He joined LG Innotek in 2004 and has served as head of the company's Vietnam production subsidiary, head of Optics Solution Production 1, and head of the Optics Solution Business Division. LG Innotek plans to use Cho's promotion to strengthen the role and leadership of the Package Solution Business Division and further sharpen its competitiveness in the FC-BGA market.
Lee Dong-hoon, chief human resources officer, was promoted from vice president to senior vice president. Lee was recognized for solidifying the foundation for sustainable growth, including building labor-management relations based on trust and leading efforts to secure research and development talent for future core businesses.
LG Innotek is also strengthening its R&D capabilities by newly appointing a number of specialists in emerging technologies such as autonomous driving and robotics as vice presidents. Those appointed are Kang Young-joon, head of business strategy; Nam Sang-hyuk, head of the Next-Generation Package Research Institute; Nam Hyung-ki, head of the CS business; Park Min-ha, head of the Physical AI Sensing Research Institute; Park Jae-man, head of the Package Solution Research Institute; Son Deuk-hoon, head of management planning; and Lee Ji-myung, leader of the Package Solution Advanced Development Team.

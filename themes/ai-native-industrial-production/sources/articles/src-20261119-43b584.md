The 
Crimean Bridge was attacked for a third time
 this week, a Russian official said on Thursday.

Rail traffic had been temporarily suspended, according to Crimea's Russian-installed governor.

"The damage is currently being assessed," Sergei Aksyonov, the governor, initially said on Telegram, adding that emergency services were at the scene.

Aksyonov later announced that traffic was set to be restored starting from around 4 p.m. local time (1300 GMT).

TASS news agency reported it may take a few months for some sections of the bridge to become operational, citing Deputy ​Prime Minister Marat ⁠Khusnullin.

In an online post on Thursday, the Ukrainian military said that the attack had been carried out by its forces.

It stated that the strikes on Russian bridges hamper Moscow's ability to transport military supplies to the front line.

The bridge, which supports a four-lane roadway and a railroad, is the only bridge linking mainland Russia with occupied Crimea.

Crimean Bridge attacked for a third time this week

This is the third Ukrainian attack this week targeting the 
strategic bridge
 that is a key symbol of Russia's annexation of Crimea in 2014.

The bridge has played a key role in supplying Russian troops in occupied parts of southern Ukraine since the 
2022 Russian invasion
, making it a key target for Kyiv. 

Steel arch of bridge collapses after Ukrainian drone strikes earlier this week

On Tuesday, Ukraine carried out a major attack on the bridge using missiles and drones, 
destroying the steel arch
 of the 19-kilometer-long road section of the crossing.

Ukraine's SBU security service had said that the "road span beneath it (the arch) has been completely destroyed."

The latest attack appears to have impacted rail traffic on the bridge, according to German press agency DPA.

The Ukrainian attack came just 
hours before
 an unprecedented meeting scheduled between Putin and his US and Chinese counterparts on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit.

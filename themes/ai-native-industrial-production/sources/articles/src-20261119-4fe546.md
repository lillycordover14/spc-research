Axal: Tempo Earn and Routes in a savings app&nbsp;&nbsp;tempo.com

[page text]
Axal is a high-yield savings app for dollars, Bitcoin, and gold. It has integrated Tempo Earn to add an earning option on Tempo, with Tempo Routes connecting deposits and withdrawals to its existing app.

Making onchain savings work like a consumer app

Axal
 helps people put their cash to work without having to manage the lending markets underneath. Customers can fund their accounts through bank transfers, cards, Apple Pay, or Google Pay, then track their balances and earnings in the app.

Behind that experience, Axal allocates dollar balances to overcollateralized onchain lending markets. Borrowers post collateral and pay interest. Axal monitors and rebalances allocations across markets, while earnings compound automatically. Customers access the onchain markets through an easy-to-use app without needing to know how to use crypto.

Adding a new earning option had to fit that experience. Customers needed a way to move funds into it, see what they hold, and withdraw through the app. For Axal, using Tempo meant connecting those steps while keeping its existing wallet and allocation approach.

Adding Tempo Earn to Axal

Axal added a dedicated 
Tempo Earn
 vault to its savings product. A vault is the onchain contract through which eligible users deposit funds and receive receipt shares representing their position in the underlying earning product.

The integration lets Axal users hold those receipt shares in their own wallets and deposit or redeem directly. Axal keeps control of the customer experience and determines which users can access its program. Access policies attached to the vault let Axal approve eligible wallet addresses before their first deposit.

Tempo provides the software infrastructure for accessing the earning product. Returns come from the underlying third-party product and vary with market conditions. Withdrawal timing depends on its liquidity and redemption terms.

Our mission at Axal is to make building wealth easy and competitive. Tempo Earn gives our users simple access to onchain earning products, without the complexity.
Ash Ahmed
CEO, Axal

Connecting earning with deposits and withdrawals

The other part of the integration is getting money to and from Tempo. Axal already supports customer funds on other networks, so its Earn integration had to work with those existing balances.

Axal pairs Earn with 
Tempo Routes
 for three flows: moving an initial allocation onto Tempo, supporting new deposits and withdrawals, and rebalancing funds when needed. Routes coordinate transfers between supported networks, connecting Axal's existing funding flows to its Earn program.

Why Axal is building on Tempo

Stablecoin transfers on Tempo settle onchain in under a second with sub-cent network fees, which supports the deposits and redemptions involved in that flow. These network capabilities sit alongside the separate costs and timing of cross-chain transfers, the underlying earning product, and any bank withdrawal.

What's ahead

Axal's broader product brings dollars, Bitcoin, and gold into one app. Adding an earning option on Tempo extends that approach.

Next, Axal plans to let customers keep earning on their balances until they spend them with their Axal card.

Two weeks to go in the Big 12 football regular season, and there's still not a lot settled, other than clear dividing lines between the top three teams in the league, the mid-tier pack with 4-3 conference records, and those trying to scramble for a minor bowl bid.
In the upper league standings, Utah and Texas Tech are the only teams that can clinch a berth in the championship game this week. Utah gets in with a win or a BYU loss, while Texas Tech locks up a spot with a win at Baylor and a BYU loss at Kansas.
Down the ladder, no school has been eliminated from bowl contention yet.

- -- -- -- -
Oklahoma State's LaDanian Fields leads the league with seven interceptions, and actually has one more pick than he does pass breakups (six). 

- -- -- -- -
Eight Big 12 teams (Utah, Texas Tech, BYU, Houston, Arizona, Arizona State, Baylor and Oklahoma State) are now bowl-eligible, and another five and (UCF, Iowa State, TCU and West Virginia) are one win away. Is that more a sign of league strength or mediocrity? The Big 12’s previous high for bowl-eligible teams since becoming a 16-team league was 10.
Kansas, Colorado and Kansas State will fall short of the six-win bowl eligibility standard with one more loss.

THE PICKS
Fri Nov 20 | 6:00 PM ET*
Iowa State (5-5/3-4) at UCF (5-5/3-4)
Orlando, FL | FS1
The Cyclones and Knights square off with bowl eligibility as a prize. Both have had up and down seasons, with ISU playing well at home but struggling on the road, while UCF has yo-yoed with alternating wins and losses for much of the campaign. Put those two together, and the pattern yields a home win for the Knights.

Sat Nov 21 | 
11:00 AM CT
BYU (8-2/5-2) at Kansas (4-6/2-5)
Lawrence, KS | ABC
Kansas is playing for its bowl life, but BYU is still in contention for a championship game slot. The Cougars take advantage of the early start time and jump out for a road win.
Sat Nov 21 | 12:00 PM ET
Colorado (
4-6/2-5) at Cincinnati (5-5/2-5)
Cincinnati, OH | ESPN+
The Bearcats have gone in the tank, losing three in a row, while CU resurrected itself  last week with an upset win over Houston. That's just the kinds of results this year that suggest an opposite outcome in this game, so UC is the selection to join the Big 12 bowl crowd with a win.

Sat Nov 21 | 1:00 PM ET
Houston (7-3/4-3) at West Virginia (5-5/2-5)
Morgantown, WV | TNT/truTV/HBO Max
WVU played its worst game of the season last week, and could be without its starting QB. Houston is smarting from its loss to Colorado, and isn't likely to drop another game to a team in the bottom quarter of the league standings. The Cougars fashion a workmanlike win on the road.

Sat Nov 21 | 2:30 PM CT
 Texas Tech (9-1/6-1) at Baylor (6-4/4-3)
Waco, TX | FOX
The Bears have shown the ability to score, and could put up some points against Tech's defense. But the Red Raiders' fight to keep pace with Utah in the race for Arlington is based on outscoring opponents, and they figure to do that against a determined effort from BU.
Sat Nov 21 | 6:00 PM CT
Arizona (7-3/4-3) at Kansas State (4-6/1-6)
Manhattan, KS | FS1
It's tough to compete in the Big 12 without an accomplished QB, and K-State's QB injuries and shuffling have dropped the PowerCats to the bottom of the league. In this battle of similar mascots, its the ones on the road that come away with the win.

Sat Nov 21 | 8:00 PM CT
Utah (10-0/7-0) at TCU (5-5/3-4)
Fort Worth, TX | FOX
The Utes are heavy favorites here, and in many seasons TCU might be given a puncher's chance at an upset. But the home support hasn't been great for the Frogs this year, and they don't have enough offense to keep pace with Utah.

Sat Nov 21 | 8:15 PM MT
Oklahoma State (6-4/4-3) at Arizona State (6-4/4-3)
Tempe, AZ | ESPN
The day wraps up with another battle of teams with identical records. The Sun Devils have won two in a row, but the Cowboys seem to be just a bit better overall, This should be an entertaining game, with OSU winning a close one.
* Local time and time zone of the home team

Girls figure skating program takes center ice at Detroit 23rd Annual Tree Lighting | The Metro&nbsp;&nbsp;WDET 101.9 FM

[page text]
It’s the holiday season. Detroit’s 23rd Annual Tree Lighting Ceremony is right around the corner, happening this weekend at Campus Martius. The city will flip the switch and light up a roughly 70-foot-tall Norway spruce from Northern Michigan.
Lighting the tree is not the only festivity taking place at Campus Martius, though. There will also be performances, including one by 
Figure Skating in Detroit
.
They are a group that focuses on giving girls from around the city the opportunity to take part in youth figure skating, while also supporting education. The girls are scheduled to take the ice on tree lighting night at around 7:13 p.m. on Friday, Nov. 20.
Lori Ward is the CEO of Figure Skating in Detroit. She stopped by 
The Metro
 to talk with Tia Graham about the various opportunities offered by the organization.

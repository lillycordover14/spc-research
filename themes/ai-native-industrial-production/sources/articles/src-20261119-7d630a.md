Lightsource bp begins building 150 MW Olmedo solar project in Spain&nbsp;&nbsp;Solarbytes

[page text]
Lightsource bp has nearly 800 MW of solar capacity under construction in Spain.  Image Source: Lightsource bp
Lightsource bp has started the main construction works on the Olmedo solar project, which is located in the province of Valladolid, Spain. The project consists of three plants, Adaja I, Sinfonía I and El Caballero, which have a combined capacity of more than 150 MW. The plants are to be built in the municipalities of Medina del Campo, San Vicente de Palacio, Ataquines, Ramiro and La Zarza. The project had reached the ready-to-build stage in February 2026, after the required permits were obtained. Before the main works began, some works on the interconnection infrastructure had already been started. Operation is planned for 2028, and the plants are expected to produce energy equal to the annual consumption of nearly 83,600 households. The output of the plants is estimated to avoid the emission of 60,000 tonnes of CO2 a year. About 200 jobs are expected during construction, and four jobs will be linked to operation and maintenance.

For the second time this week, the Tar Heels have landed a commitment from one of their official visitors from this past weekend’s home game against Louisville.
As first reported by Inside Carolina, the Tar Heels have completed a flip of 2027 3✮ CB Zachary Ingram from Miami (OH) following his official visit to Chapel Hill over the weekend. Ingram had been committed to the Redhawks since April 20th, but his recruitment changed after the Tar Heels officially offered him back on November 4th.
Ingram, the No. 1904 overall prospect in the 2027 class and No. 176 cornerback according to the 247Sports Composite Player Rankings, hails from Gainesville High School in Gainesville, GA. In nine games this season, Ingram has collected 19 total tackles, 1.0 tackles for a loss, 1.0 sacks and an interception. Last year, as a junior, Ingram had 31 total tackles, two interceptions and 15 passes defensed in 14 games.
With his commitment, the Tar Heels now have 20 commitments in their 2027 class, including three at the cornerback position. The commitment jumps the class back up to No. 50 overall in the class and No. 13 in the ACC according to the 247Sports Composite Team Rankings.

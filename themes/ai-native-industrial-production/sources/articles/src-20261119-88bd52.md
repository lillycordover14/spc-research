By R.L. Bynum

Harrison Ingram grabbed 20 rebounds and 20 points Wednesday night, a day after Cormac Ryan and Pete Nance combined for 50 points to lead a G League comeback. Coby White had the best NBA scoring night among former Tar Heels, with 19 points in Charlotte’s overtime loss to the Lakers.

Ingram pulled down 15 defensive rebounds and five on the offensive end as the Salt Lake City Stars beat the Valley Suns 124–103 in Tempe, Ariz. The Utah Jazz forward on a two-way contract shot 9-for-18 and added four assists, two steals and a block in 37 minutes.

It was his third double-double in five games and his second in three nights against Valley. He had 23 points and 10 rebounds in Monday’s overtime win. 

It was Harrison’s fifth professional game with at least 15 rebounds, with his total of 20 Wednesday tying his career-high set last season on Feb. 20 when he scored 16 points in the Austin Spurs’ 135–132 road loss to San Diego.

Ryan scored a game-high 29 points and Nance added 21 as the Wisconsin Herd erased a 13-point second-half deficit Tuesday to beat the Windy City Bulls 103–96 in their home opener.

Ryan shot 10-for-18 and grabbed eight rebounds. Nance also grabbed eight rebounds, made four 3-pointers and added four assists. Wisconsin outscored Windy City 31–19 in the fourth quarter.

Drake Powell, who the Brooklyn Nets assigned to the G League on Saturday, helped Long Island come back from 16 down Wednesday in a 123–112 home win over Delaware.

Powell had 14 points, seven rebounds, four assists and three blocks, two nights after collecting four steals against the same team.

White shot 6 of 12 and added four assists and three steals in Charlotte’s 118–114 loss in Los Angeles on Tuesday. Quentin Grimes forced overtime with his 10th 3-pointer with 1.7 seconds left in regulation and finished with a career-high 47 points.

Hornets coach Charles Lee kept White and Brandon Miller out of overtime because both were on minutes restrictions as they worked their way back from injuries, 
according to Charlotte Hornets on SI.

“As they’ve been coming back from injury, we’ve been trying to build them up,” Lee said.

Walker Kessler missed Tuesday’s game with right lower leg soreness, then returned Wednesday with 10 points and 11 rebounds against his former team. Utah’s 109–97 win snapped the Lakers’ nine-game winning streak.

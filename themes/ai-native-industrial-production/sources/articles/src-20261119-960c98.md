Miranda Lambert and Ella Langley gave the live debut of “Snakeskin Boots” at the CMAs in Nashville.

Cindy Ord/Getty Images

	It’s already been a banner night for multi-nominees 
Ella Langley
 and 
Miranda Lambert
 at the 60th annual 
CMA Awards
 on Wednesday. But the duo also had another surprise in store: the live debut of “Snakeskin Boots,” the cinematic, 
Thelma & Louise
 gone country duet from Lambert’s Seventies-influenced album 
Crisco
. In matching brown and black fringe, the pair leaned into the moody Western groove in front of a Texas saloon-inspired backdrop.

	Lambert and Langley are the most nominated artists of the evening (Lambert was a co-producer on Langley’s 
Dandelion
, with 
Crisco
 eligible for next year’s awards), and have already won every category for which they were in the running. They are also both writers on the massively popular “Choosin’ Texas,” which is now the most successful song in 
Billboard
 Hot 100 history.

	“It’s a banger,” Lambert 
told 
Rolling Stone
 of “Snakeskin Boots.” “It’s like these bad-asses that are creating their own story, and having Ella on there was really cool because we’ve had a lot of bonding moments in the last few years, making her record 
Dandelion
 together, writing. I consider myself a mentor and a sister to her, and I think she would say the same. It’s like a sisterhood and, obviously anytime you can have your friends join you and collab, it makes it more fun. It just elevates the process. But ‘Snakeskin’ just gives me a feeling.”

	

	Lambert has become a multi-hyphenate powerhouse of late: producer (she earned a 
Producer of the Year
, Non-Classical nomination at the Grammys), label owner with Big Loud Texas, fashion mogul with her western-inspired line Idyllwind Fueled by Miranda Lambert and, of course, as one of the most prolific and revered songwriters in country music.

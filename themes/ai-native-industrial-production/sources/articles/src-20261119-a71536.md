60th Anniversary Edition of The Doors’ Iconic Debut Album Loaded with Bonus Tracks&nbsp;&nbsp;Consequence of Sound

[page text]
A 60th anniversary edition of 
The Doors
’ iconic self-titled 1967 debut album is due out January 8th.

The anniversary release comes in a number of versions, including a 2-CD set featuring dozens of bonus tracks on the second disc, two reel-to-reel editions of the album, as well as a picture disc and a vinyl reissue of the “Light My Fire” single.

Disc Two features numerous takes tracing the evolution of three of the album’s songs, “I Looked At You,” “Moonlight Drive,” and “Indian Summer,” as they developed in the studio.

The reel-to-reel edition was created via a real-time 1:1 copy of the original flat analog master tape, resulting in the full dynamic audio range as heard in the studio by the band. It is limited to 350 copies and is produced to IEC equalization standard on premium RTM LPR90 tape stock.

The Doors
 will also see a picture disc release on vinyl as well as a reissue of the “Light My Fire” 10″ single, featuring the single edit on side A at 78 rpm and the album edit on side B at 45 rpm. The single also comes with speed correction not present before, making both versions the same tempo and land at A440 for the first time.

All versions of 
The Doors 
60th anniversary releases listed above are available for 
pre-order now
.

In addition, there is a second reel-to-reel edition made in collaboration with Analogue Productions/Acoustic Sounds, a two-tape set mastered by Bernie Grundman from the recently re-discovered original analog master tapes. The 1/4-inch 2-track tapes were replicated using a first generation EQ’d copy from the original master tape transferred in real time using the legendary ATR-102 Ampex tape machines with flux magnetic heads on SM900 tape which is renowned for its maximum clean headroom, strong signal-to-noise performance, and thick tape stock for increased durability. The Ultra-Tape edition of The Doors is available for pre-order now exclusively via Acoustic Sounds.

The anniversary celebration will also include a new restored audio and video version of the original “Break On Through (To The Other Side)” music video on January 1st as well as a new lyric video for “Light My Fire” to follow in January 4th with a UK-exclusive yellow and black color vinyl release via Blood Records to follow.

Collectible Venom (Spider-Man) figure is inspired by the character’s appearance in Marvel’s Venom, Battleworld, and Spider-Man comics.

Premium comics-inspired design and deco make this a great addition to your Spider-Man collection.

Pose the figure with 4 included accessories, featuring an alternate head, 2 alternate hands, and a web line.

New capabilities help distributed and centralized sales advisors turn customer context into coordinated action across extended buying journeys

Tulip
, a global clienteling and retail customer engagement platform, today announced Opportunity Management, a new set of capabilities that helps store and corporate retail sales teams manage sales opportunities tied to customer projects and events, such as home renovations and weddings. Teams can track each opportunity from start to finish, including key milestones along the way.

Retailers increasingly view personal customer relationships as a durable source of growth, particularly for more complex and considered purchases. Yet centralized sales advisors often lack a consistent way to manage customer projects and milestones across conversations, products, and channels. Important details can remain buried in notes, making it difficult to coordinate follow-ups, track progress and provide informed, personal guidance throughout the buying journey.

Tulip’s Opportunity Management brings customer plans, milestones and follow-up tasks into a shared record. Advisors can coordinate next steps over time, while managers gain visibility into opportunities across their teams. This helps retailers act on what they know about customers while preserving the nature of each relationship.

The new functionality is available through Tulip for Desktop, iOS and Android platforms. A leading digital-first home furnishings retailer is among the first companies to leverage Tulip’s new opportunity management capabilities through a centralized sales team.

“Retail growth is increasingly driven by the depth of customer relationships,” said Ian Rawlins, CEO of Tulip. “Sales teams involved in project- and event-based sales opportunities need a clear view of every step involved in the customer’s buying journey and the tools to move it forward. Tulip helps advisors focus their efforts, take the right next action, and provide the informed human guidance that builds trust and lasting loyalty.”

Extending Clienteling Across the Enterprise

Following Tulip’s March 2026 merger with Salesfloor, the combined company supports approximately 100 enterprise retail clients and more than 100,000 associates across over 50 countries, influencing billions of dollars in retail sales annually.

The new Opportunity Management capabilities extend traditional clienteling to more customer-facing sales roles beyond physical stores, helping retailers improve sales execution, deepen loyalty, and deliver personal service at scale.

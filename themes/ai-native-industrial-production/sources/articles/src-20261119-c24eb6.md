By Rodrigo Campos and Marc Jones
 
 
NEW YORK/LONDON, Nov 19 (Reuters) - Global stocks fell on Thursday as US stock traders focused on Anthropic's IPO, while oil prices rose as lingering ‌attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz grabbed market attention.
 
 
Investors have said that ‌how the first major AI developer to go public trades in the coming days and weeks will be heavily scrutinized ​to gauge broader sentiment towards AI.
 
 
 
 
 
The Claude developer's initial public offering was priced at $83 a share, valuing Anthropic at about $1.5 trillion before the stock rose as much as 39% to trade at $115.10 on its debut.
 
 
"We can't underestimate the importance of this IPO. It sort of serves as a valuation for ‌the wider AI ecosystem," said Fiona Cincotta, ⁠senior market analyst at StoneX.
 
 
The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 1,011.97 points, or 2.02%, to 49,186.20, the S&P 500 fell 110.35 points, or 1.51%, to 7,213.28 and the ⁠Nasdaq Composite fell 351.21 points, or 1.44%, to 24,078.68.
 
 
MSCI's gauge of stocks across the globe fell 14.30 points, or 1.33%, to 1,060.26. The pan-European STOXX 600 index fell 0.88%, while emerging market stocks fell 20.34 ​points, ​or 1.37%, to 1,463.94. Japan's Nikkei rose 419.93 points, or ​0.71%, to 59,444.21.
 
 
The dollar index was ‌steady after its recent rally to 19-month highs lost momentum.
 
 
"Dollar bulls are going to need more conviction or more bullish news to drive dollar gains in the short run," Shaun Osborne, chief currency strategist at Scotiabank, said, pointing to a pickup in risk sentiment linked to Anthropic.
 
 
 
 
 
The index was little changed at 103.81, with the euro up 0.06% at $1.098.
 
 
OIL EDGES UP
 
 
In commodities, oil prices ‌edged higher as renewed attacks in the Strait of ​Hormuz offset a growing view that the oil market ​will soon be back in surplus.
 
 
Iran's navy ​said in a statement carried by state media that two oil tankers ‌hit naval mines in the waterway and "exploded" ​on Thursday. Reuters could not ​independently verify the report.
 
 
US crude rose 1.24% to $84.29 a barrel and Brent rose to $92.83 per barrel, up 0.96% on the day.
 
 
Spot gold fell 1.34% to $3,672.67 an ounce and spot ​silver fell 1.74% to $44.24 an ‌ounce. Copper declined 0.3% to $12,433.00 a ton.

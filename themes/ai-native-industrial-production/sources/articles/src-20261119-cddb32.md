In jazz music, many songs are built around a “head,” or the main melody that’s typically introduced at the beginning of the song and ties together different rhythmic sections. Throughout the tune, players may trade solos and riffs, while the head ultimately always comes back around to keep things cohesive.
Ridgway’s Psylo probably isn’t what many listeners would consider a jazz band; in fact, its sound that combines southern rock, funk and improvisation is more reminiscent of classic jam bands like the Grateful Dead.

Kim Zolciak
 could be on the hook for six figures over her now-defunct business amid her son 
Kroy “KJ” Biermann Jr
.’s sexual battery case.

Court documents obtained exclusively by 
Us
 show that Headway Capital requested a default judgment against Zolciak, 48, on Tuesday, November 17 — and it could cost her a pretty penny.

The financial institution 
sued the 
Real Housewives of Atlanta
 alum in April, claiming she entered into a business-use line of credit and security agreement for 8 Busy B’s LLC and allegedly failed to repay $75,000.

Now, Headway Capital is asking for that amount plus thousands more after Zolciak “failed to file any responsive pleadings” to the lawsuit, according to the docs obtained by 
Us
.

The company claims it’s entitled to the principal amount of $75,000, $55,650 in accrued interest at the contract rate through August 13, 2024, and $11,778 in accrued pre-judgment interest at 7 percent from August 14, 2024, to November 11, 2026. It’s also asking for Zolciak to pay $14,266 in attorneys’ fees, bringing the total to nearly $167,000, if the judge signs off.

Us
 reached out to Zolciak’s rep for comment.

In July, the Bravolebrity was sued by Portfolio Recovery Associates, LLC, a debt collection company, over an alleged unpaid credit card bill of nearly $17,000.

As 
Us
 exclusively reported, Portfolio Recovery Associates, LLC, 
dismissed its lawsuit against Zolciak
 last month, marking a small legal win amid her son’s legal issues.

Biermann, 15, was 
arrested in August
 for aggravated sexual assault and aggravated sodomy. He was indicted on September 3 for aggravated child molestation, aggravated sexual battery, aggravated sodomy, false imprisonment and three counts of sexual battery against a child under the age of 16 stemming from an alleged April 2026 incident at a Lifetime Fitness facility in Alpharetta, Georgia.

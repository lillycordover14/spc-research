Marquette again named among TIME’s Best Colleges for Future Leaders&nbsp;&nbsp;Marquette Today

[page text]
Marquette University has once again been recognized among 
TIME
 magazine and Statista’s America’s Best Colleges for Future Leaders
, ranked 99th on the latest list. 
The recognition marks the third consecutive year Marquette has been included on the list, which examines how effectively American colleges and universities prepare graduates to become leaders across business, government and society. 
For the 2027 edition, 
TIME
 and independent market research firm Statista examined the educational backgrounds of approximately 4,900 influential leaders to identify the institutions whose alumni have gone on to hold prominent roles across corporate, public service and social sectors. 
The recognition continues Marquette’s strong showing in national rankings measuring student outcomes and preparation for life after college. Marquette is ranked No. 6 in the country for job placement 10 years after graduation according to Zippia.com and first in Wisconsin for student outcomes in the 
Wall Street Journal
/College Pulse 2026 Best Colleges in the U.S. 
The recognition also reflects the 
value of a Marquette education
, which prepares students for successful careers while equipping them to serve others, lead with purpose and make a meaningful difference in their communities. 
A complete list of Marquette’s rankings and recognitions is available on 
Marquette.edu
.

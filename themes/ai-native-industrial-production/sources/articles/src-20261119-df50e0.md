US defence contractor Northrop Grumman will begin testing how its mission systems perform on a high-speed aircraft in the next few months, following its announced partnership with startup hypersonic aircraft maker Hermeus on 12 November 2026. 

Kyle Mimken, programme manager at Northrop Grumman, told 
Shephard 
the testing will focus on demonstrating how its mission systems, payload integration and autonomous capabilities can operate effectively on a high-speed aircraft platform.

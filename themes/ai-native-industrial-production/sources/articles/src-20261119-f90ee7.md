Rebuild of Evangelion Asuka Figure Wears Her Cat Hat&nbsp;&nbsp;Siliconera

[page text]
Image via Sega and Khara

Sega announced a new 
Rebuild of
 
Evangelion 
Figurizma figure of Asuka Shikinami Langley that is based on her appearance in the movie 
3.0 You Can (Not) Redo
. It will first appear as a prize figure in Japan starting 
on November 27, 2026
. An Amazon Sega Store release will follow, just like it did for the
 
3.0+1.0: Thrice Upon a Time
 figure of her
.
 
This Asuka figure will be close to 11 inches tall (28cm). She’s posed in a way that makes it look like she is walking toward the viewer. She wears her standard red plugsuit from the movie in this outfit, as well as her jacket and cat-ear hat with accessories. She’ll come with a clear base to help her stand. 
 
Here’s how she’s expected to look when she launches. 
 
 
 
 
 
Images via Sega and Khara
 
Sega’s released a lot of 
Rebuild of Evangelion 
figures and merchandise lately. A scarf based on the Fifth Angel from 
1.0: You Are (Not) Alone 
will launch in Japan 
on December 4, 2026
. A new Luminasta figure of 
Kaworu Nagisa
 appeared there in October 2026, though that hasn’t showed up worldwide just yet. It should appear on the US Amazon storefront soon, since that figure is widely available in Japan now.
 
We’ve also seen a lot of game crossovers with 
Evangelion 
lately. Characters from the series are headed to 
Sonic Racing CrossWorlds
. They were in 
The First Descendant
 too. Not to mention the planned 
FFXIV
 Raid
.
 
The 
Rebuild of
 
Evangelion
 
3.0 You Can (Not) Redo
 Asuka Shikinami Langley Figurizma figure will debut in Japan on November 27, 2026, then show up worldwide after. The anime can be found on Netflix, and the films are streaming on Amazon. Viz Media handled the manga release in English.

San Francisco, CA / Storyteller / Nov 20, 2026 /
LightSource
, the AI-native operating system for direct materials, today announced the preview launch of Supplier Agent, a new offering that helps suppliers understand RFQs and prepare quotes instantaneously. Supplier Agent draws on conversational input and the data suppliers already have, from past quotes to spreadsheets and product specs, and fills out the buyer’s quote template for the supplier to review. 
“Suppliers have all the data, but every buyer wants it in a different format. A single RFQ response can take days,” said Spencer Penn, co-founder and CEO of LightSource. “Buyers want to compare apples-to- apples and instead get back dozens of formats and half-filled fields. Supplier Agent fixes both sides.”
LightSource
Supplier Agent is embedded in LightSource’s RFQ workflow. When a supplier opens a buyer’s RFQ in LightSource, Supplier Agent summarizes what’s being requested, flags required versus optional information, and identifies what still needs to be completed. Suppliers can describe quote details conversationally or upload quotes they’ve already prepared in common formats such as PDF, Excel, or Word. Supplier Agent then maps that information to the relevant RFQ fields for the supplier to review, confirm, and approve the quote for submission.
“Supplier Agent does the work of reading the RFQ and filling in the quote, not just advising on it,” said Idan Mintz, CTO and co-founder of LightSource. “Suppliers who use it can respond to more RFQs in less time, which means they win more business. And when responding is quicker and easier, buyers get quotes sooner, engage more suppliers, and bring products to market faster and at a lower cost.”
Supplier Agent works inside LightSource’s shared workspace for each RFQ; teammates can join a quote at any point, and Supplier Agent shows them what’s already filled in and what’s still needed. That keeps quote details, revisions, and progress in one place instead of spread across email threads and spreadsheets. Between negotiation rounds, suppliers can revise quotes, such as applying a cost reduction across all items, and Supplier Agent shows the math behind each proposed change. Quote data stays within each buyer-supplier relationship and isn’t used to inform the agent for other customers. Because all data is reviewed and every quote is approved before it’s submitted, the result is a streamlined workflow with built-in human oversight.
“Responding to RFQs used to mean re-entering information from our own quotes into every buyer’s format,” said Alyssa Hu, Sales Manager for Overseas Business at Green Precision. “With Supplier Agent, we upload a quote we’ve already built and review what it fills in, so our team spends less time on data entry and more time on the details that win business.”
Supplier Agent launch
 is the first step in LightSource’s broader push to expand what suppliers can do on the platform throughout 2027.

VulcanForms to expand industrial metal Additive Manufacturing (AM) operations with $500 million financing (Courtesy VulcanForms)

VulcanForms, headquartered in Devens, Massachusetts, USA, has reached an agreement for $500 million in financing led by J.P. Morgan and funds managed by Apollo. The funding will support the construction of three new factories, expanding its manufacturing operations from three to six sites.

Founded out of MIT in 2015, VulcanForms has developed industrially scalable technology combining Additive Manufacturing, precision machining, physical AI and product validation into a unified platform to deliver advanced metal products.

According to the company, it has raised over $1 billion in funding to date from lenders and investors including Eclipse, J.P. Morgan, Apollo, Hudson Bay Capital, D1 Capital and 1789 Capital.

“America cannot lead the next industrial revolution if we can’t manufacture what we invent,” commented Kevin Kassekert, CEO, VulcanForms. “For too long, breakthrough technologies developed here have been manufactured overseas. VulcanForms is proving we can change that by turning American innovation into industrial-scale production and demonstrating that the U.S. can not only compete, but also win on the global stage. We’re proud to work with J.P. Morgan and Apollo on helping to build America’s industrial future at home.”

VulcanForms serves the medical device, consumer product, aerospace, defence, space and industrial markets through its manufacturing operations at its existing facilities in Devens and Newburyport, Massachusetts.

This financing forms part of JPMorganChase’s Security and Resiliency Initiative and will support the expansion of VulcanForms’ manufacturing operations and further integration of physical AI into its platform.

“Through our Security and Resiliency Initiative, JPMorganChase is working with companies like VulcanForms to strengthen the industries and supply chains that underpin economic security. These efforts can help to bridge the gap between promising technology and industrial-scale production in the United States, as demand grows across critical industries,” said Jay Horine, Global Head, JPMorganChase’s Security and Resiliency Initiative.

“The rebuilding of America’s industrial base is a strategic priority that will be among the most capital-intensive undertakings the country has faced in decades,” added John Zito, Co-President, Apollo Asset Management. “VulcanForms has developed a distinct approach to manufacturing that delivers critical components to a broad set of end markets and is meeting rapidly growing customer demand. We are proud to support that growth with a bespoke financing solution to fund capacity expansion alongside J.P. Morgan, which illustrates how private capital can help advance the return to large-scale manufacturing.”

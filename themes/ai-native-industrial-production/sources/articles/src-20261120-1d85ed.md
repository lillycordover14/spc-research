ABS and Radian Forge will investigate the use of digital manufacturing data to support qualification of metal components produced using WAAM (Courtesy Radian Forge)

The American Bureau of Shipping (ABS), headquartered in Spring, Texas, USA, and Radian Forge, based in Portsmouth, Virginia, have signed a joint development project (JDP) to evaluate the use of digital manufacturing data in the qualification of metal components produced through Wire Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM).

The project aims to address challenges associated with qualifying additively manufactured components for marine and offshore applications, where component availability, long manufacturing lead times and confidence in material performance can present barriers to wider adoption.

The JDP will assess whether manufacturing process parameters, real-time sensor data, inspection records, and material testing results can provide a reliable technical basis for verifying and validating WAAM-produced components.

“Digital qualification has an important role to play in the adoption of Additive Manufacturing across the marine and offshore sectors. We are proud to work with Radian Forge to strengthen confidence in Additive Manufacturing by improving how qualification evidence is generated, evaluated, and traced,” said Michael Kei, Vice President, ABS, Technology.

Under the agreement, Radian Forge, specialists in large-format WAAM, will manufacture components for testing and validation, collect manufacturing and sensor data, and conduct applicable testing. ABS will independently review the technical evidence, evaluate test results and assess the proposed qualification methodology and its potential application.

“Traditional manufacturing timelines can create significant challenges when critical components are needed. Our goal is to build confidence in a manufacturing process that can respond to those needs. Partnering with ABS gives us an opportunity to rigorously evaluate how process data and material performance can support that objective,” commented Jeff Yeager, CEO of Radian Forge.

Olympic figure skating champion Mikhail Shaidorov split from coach Alexei Urmanov, nine months after becoming Kazakhstan’s first gold medalist in the sport.
“I want to tell you that our cooperation with Alexey Evgenyevich (Urmanov) has come to an end,” 
was posted on Shaidorov’s social media
, according to a translation. 
Shaidorov, 22, began working with Urmanov when he was 12 years old in January 2017 after previously being coached by his father, Stanislav. Urmanov, the 1994 Olympic champion for Russia, became Shaidorov’s full-time coach in 2018.
This season, Shaidorov placed 10th and fifth in his two Grand Prix starts. 
It’s the first time an Olympic gold medalist who chose to compete in the next season’s Grand Prix Series did not qualify for the Grand Prix Final in the series’ three-decade history. Granted, most Olympic gold medalists skip the following season’s series.

“It’s time to move on,” Shaidorov’s post read. “A new chapter is ahead, in which it may not be easy and sometimes scary, but there will definitely be new goals and challenges. I will continue to work as hard as I can to continue to represent my country with dignity at the highest level.”
Shaidorov praised Urmanov in his post.
“Once upon a time, as a 12-year-old boy, I came to him at the camp with a big dream, a belief in his own strength and a desire to succeed,” the post read. “So we became a team that managed to achieve what few people once believed in. 
“Over the years, we have walked through fire and water, we have overcome incredible difficulties, we have endured trauma and falls, we have shared the joy of victories and moments of overcoming. But the most important thing is that throughout this journey, I felt the coach’s faith in his athlete. 
“For 10 years, Alexey Evgenyevich has passed on to me enormous professionalism and experience, invested a lot of strength and knowledge in my development. Thanks to him, I grew not only as an athlete, but as a person as well. We went through it all together, hand in hand, shoulder to shoulder. All those moments will remain in my mind forever. I remember what I was like at the beginning of this journey and what I have become today. 
“I am very grateful to life for having met such a person in my journey — a great coach, mentor and inspiration who helped me to achieve top results. Thanks to Alexey Evgenievich for everything we went through together, for his work, support and belief in me. Today we complete this milestone with achievements I could never have even dreamed of.”

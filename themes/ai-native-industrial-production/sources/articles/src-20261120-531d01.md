French 
Rafale
 fighters and Swedish 
JAS 39 Gripens
 took part in their first-ever joint Excalibur Sweden 2026 exercise as part of bilateral French-Swedish cooperation on nuclear deterrence.
The Swedish Armed Forces and the French Ministry of the Armed Forces 
reported
 this. The exercise took place in northern Sweden from November 16 to 18.
Aircraft from the French Strategic Air Forces Command (Forces aériennes stratégiques, FAS) were deployed to Sweden. France’s contingent included Rafale fighters capable of carrying nuclear-capable air-launched missiles and an A330 MRTT tanker, which operated alongside Swedish Gripens.

Excalibur Sweden 2026 was the first exercise conducted under the bilateral nuclear deterrence framework that France and Sweden are developing in accordance with their common strategic cooperation roadmap.
During the exercise, the parties practised cooperation between French and Swedish aircraft, including joint fighter operations and aerial refuelling support for combat aircraft.
 A Swedish JAS 39 Gripen during Excalibur Sweden 2026 exercise. Photo credits: French Ministry of the Armed Forces

The French side noted the exercise was intended to improve interoperability between the French Strategic Air Forces Command and the Swedish Air Force.
The French Strategic Air Forces Command is the air component of France’s nuclear deterrent that operates, in particular, Rafale fighters capable of carrying nuclear air-launched missiles.

No nuclear weapons were involved in Excalibur Sweden 2026. The Swedish and French sides also stressed that the exercise was not directed against any specific country.

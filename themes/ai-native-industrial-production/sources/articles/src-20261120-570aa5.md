Soccer City in The South
North Carolina’s women’s soccer program is an absolute juggernaut in every sense of the word when it comes to collegiate athletics. Seemingly countless national championships, let alone conference titles and records, belong to the program, making it a destination for those seeking to play among the best in the world.
The game day experience at Play2Dream Legacy Stadium echoes that through everything from the fanbase to the small touches that allow history to echo across the pitch in Chapel Hill.
 
Food & Beverage   4
The concessions stands here offer a solid variety, though prices can run high. Interesting options like Caesar or buffalo chicken wraps ($13.95) and Bojangles chicken Supremes and fries ($13.59) are immediately apparent, while other options include Domino’s pizza ($10.99), hot dogs ($5.89), nachos ($6.19), Bavarian pretzels ($7.89), popcorn ($6.19-$13.99), and peanuts ($5.49). Drinks include water ($4.89-$7.99), Powerade ($5.49), soda ($6.99), and beer ($9.99-$14.99). But the best themed item by far is the blue and white cookie ($6.99), a NY-style black and white cookie decorated in UNC colors.
 
Atmosphere   4
Play2Dream Legacy Stadium is built with comfort in mind. Fans enter the facility at the upper concourse level, meaning you walk down to your seats instead of up. Restrooms and concessions are easy to find as well, thanks to plentiful signage. All around the facility, history is reflected in the championship banners, memorial plaques, and signage that honor those who came before, made an impact, or found success beyond the pitch at UNC.
The seating around Dorrance Field literally puts you on top of the action, as even the benches are set inside the overhang on the sideline. There is even a video replay scoreboard for fans to see the current stats, as well as replays of the action on the field.
 
Neighborhood   4
The area surrounding UNC is absolutely stunning – from quaint neighborhoods to the beautiful campus itself, it is hard to argue with how great this area of North Carolina really is. Located less than 30 minutes from Raleigh-Durham International Airport, UNC’s campus also features plenty of parking for fans in the immediate area. Just outside of campus, copious amounts of shopping and dining are available, as well as numerous hotels.
 
Fans   5
The fans at UNC may be among the most sport-knowledgeable in the game, if only for the fact that the team continues to find success decade over decade. Many fans here are those who have been coming to UNC games for years, with a solid core of young fans as well as a packed student section. This means that those around you understand and are invested in the game, rising and cheering with every nuanced play and exciting moment.
 
Access   4
Parking is plentiful near Play2Dream Legacy Stadium; many fans use the Ram’s Head parking deck a short walk away, while Navy Field Parking Lot and Boshamer Stadium Parking Lot offer options just down the street. Another multi-level parking garage, Cobb Parking Deck, offers a large number of parking spaces for easy in and out access. The facility itself also offers two entrances, giving game day attendees multiple points of ingress and egress.
 
Return on Investment   5
Tickets are incredibly affordable, with reserved seats selling for $15 while general admission is just $10. This makes for one of the best values in collegiate soccer when considering the history and success of the program. Without a doubt, this is a must-see team if you are a college soccer fan at any level, and the prices here make it worth it.
 
Extras   4
There is a giveaway table at UNC women’s soccer games that usually includes posters, t-shirts, and other items. Play2Dream Legacy Stadium also has numerous plaques around the facility honoring those who made an impact on the program, either as players or as faculty or investors. The facility also has a lower-level end zone that gives you a field level view of the pitch, but also serves as a VIP area during some games.
Finally, even though it is an outdoor facility, Dorrance Field is surrounded by physical championship banners, adding a unique feature many outdoor stadiums lack.
 
Final Thoughts
The UNC women’s soccer experience is a must-see for any fan of the sport, let alone the team. Tickets are incredibly affordable, the campus is absolutely beautiful, and the stadium lends itself to being a great one for watching top tier college soccer. If in the area, and at the very least a fan of the sport and its history in America, a stop at Play2Dream Legacy Stadium is an absolute must.

UNC Basketball vs. Marshall: How to Watch
The Heels look to get back in the win column on Friday night.
by
 
 
Matt Ferenchick
Nov 20, 2026, 2:00 PM UTC
Last time we saw the North Carolina Tar Heels men’s basketball team in action, they were taking their first loss of the season against Georgia. Since then, we’ve then gotten the news that Neo Avdalas 
will miss time with a wrist injury
. Needless to say, it’s been a bit of an annoying week for UNC. However, now they’ll get back on the court with a chance to get back in the win column tonight.
Later tonight, the Heels are set to host Marshall in non-conference action. The Thundering Herd come into this game at 2-3 on the season. They don’t have any particularly notable wins or losses, although they did keep the margin of defeat against Ohio State to 10. They’re in the 150s in the KenPom rankings, so you’d hope that this would be a game UNC can handle without much issue, but we’ll see later tonight.

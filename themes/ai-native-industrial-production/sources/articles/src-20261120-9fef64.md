Brandon Ingram is reportedly days away from making his debut for the Los Angeles Clippers.
ESPN's Shams Charania reported Friday Ingram is expected to return Monday against the Houston Rockets after sitting out the start of the season amid his recovery from Achilles and heel 
injuries
.

The Clippers acquired Ingram from the Toronto Raptors when the Kawhi Leonard trade was finalized in September.
Ingram is coming off a lone season with the Raptors during which he earned a second career All-Star nod while averaging 21.5 points in 77 games.
He was then limited to 12 points across five playoff games before a heel injury sidelined him for the end of the Raptors' first-round loss to the Cleveland Cavaliers.
Raptors interim president of basketball operations Trent Redden 
said
 in September Ingram had undergone heel surgery in May that also revealed a partially torn Achilles tendon.
Redden said at the time Ingram was expected to need about six months of rehab from the procedure.
Ingram said as Nov. 6 he had returned to practice, including some 5-on-5 work, and was planning on "coming back soon," per ClutchPoints' 
Tomas Azarly
.
The Clippers currently sit 13th in the West after a 4-10 start to the team's first season without Leonard, who played just two games for the Raptors before he was 
sidelined
 with a left knee injury.
Although Bradley Beal and Jordan Miller are still sidelined indefinitely by their respective injuries, the Clippers took one step closer to a healthy lineup when backup center Yanic Konan Niederhauser made his season debut Thursday after recovering from right foot surgery.
The Clippers could hope adding Ingram to the lineup will help take some pressure off Darius Garland, who is currently leading the team with 21.3 points per game while playing more than 33 minutes per night.
Garland will be tasked with taking on that heavy workload for at least one more game as the team prepares to play Saturday at the Dallas Mavericks prior to Ingram's potential season debut Monday in Houston.

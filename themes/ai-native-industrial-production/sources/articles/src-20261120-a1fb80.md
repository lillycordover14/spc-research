Smith (ankle) will practice Friday, and head coach Aaron Glenn said "things look promising for him playing Sunday," Brian Costello of the New York Post reports.

The Jets' final injury report will reveal whether or not Smith carries an injury designation into Sunday's game against the Chargers.

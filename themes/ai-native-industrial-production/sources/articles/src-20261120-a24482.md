Jets quarterback Geno Smith’s chances of playing against the Chargers on Sunday are looking good. 
Head coach Aaron Glenn said at a Friday morning press conference that Smith is set to practice for the third straight day. Smith injured his ankle in last Sunday’s loss to the Bills and has been limited in practice, but Glenn said that he is expected to move up to full participation and that things are moving in the right direction for him to get the start this weekend.
Smith has not missed a start this season and has 2,226 yards, 12 touchdowns and three interceptions while completing 67.1 percent of his passes. Any positives from those number have been mitigated by red zone struggles that have kept the Jets out of the end zone since the third quarter of Week 8. 
Glenn said that wide receivers Omar Cooper (ankle) and Isaiah Williams (ribs) will not play. Defensive tackle David Onyemata (abdomen) will not be activated from injured reserve.

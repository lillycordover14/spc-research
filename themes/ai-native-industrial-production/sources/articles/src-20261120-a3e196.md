FIRST ON FOX:
 
President Donald Trump
 and UFC president and CEO Dana White will announce six-figure personal contributions to one of America's oldest musical institutions.
 
 
The President’s Own United States Marine Band played a key role in America's 250 birthday celebrations and official state business this year — and a White House official tells Fox News Digital that the pair want to "express their appreciation for the band’s exceptional talent, professionalism, and commitment to excellence."
Trump and White are scheduled to announce Friday afternoon that they will each donate $100,000 of their own money, which will give each member of the band approximately $1,000. No taxpayer dollars will be used for the $200,000 gift.
 
The announcement will be made during the first-ever event on the new White House helipad on the South Lawn.
 
 
The U.S. Marine Corps band performs ahead of the Freedom 250 Grand Prix in Washington, D.C., on Sunday, Aug. 23, 2026. The IndyCar race through the capital runs Saturday and Sunday, with organizers predicting the event will bring 140,000 people per day and more than $200 million to the city.
 
(Elizabeth Frantz/Bloomberg via Getty Images)
 
All 135 members of the band will attend alongside senior military leaders, including Secretary of War Pete Hegseth, Acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao and Commandant of the Marine Corps General Eric Smith.

"‘The President’s Own’ United States Marine Band proudly embodies American excellence and tradition – and their melodies have accompanied defining moments in our nation’s history," White House spokeswoman Olivia Wales told Fox News Digital.
 
"President Trump will partner with Dana White to generously celebrate their elite musical talent and unwavering dedication, conveying his gratitude to some of our Nation’s finest musicians."
The band performed at 
UFC 250
 on the South Lawn of the White House in June, learning each fighter's song in a first-of-its-kind event featuring seven fights.
 
It was also performed at the White House in September when 
Chinese President Xi Jinping
 came to Washington for bilateral talks with Trump.

Band members serve as musical ambassadors for the Marine Corps at national and international venues.
The Marine Band is the oldest continuously active professional musical organization in the United States. It was founded by an act of Congress in 1798, and has served every U.S. president except for George Washington.
 
 
Thomas Jefferson
 gave the band its nickname, "The President's Own." It traveled with Abraham Lincoln to his 1863 Gettysburg address.
 
 
The United States Marine Band performs prior to an arrival ceremony for King Charles III on the South Lawn of the White House in Washington, DC, US, on Tuesday, April 28, 2026. The long-planned event is intended to mark the 250th anniversary of the US's independence from Britain, though its diplomatic mission has taken on new urgency amid Trump's recent feud with Prime Minister Keir Starmer over the UK's limited support for US military action against Iran.
 
(Yuri Gripas/Abaca/Bloomberg via Getty Images)

Grammy Award

Based on concept art from Avengers: Doomsday.

Hot Toys has revealed a new figure based on Robert Downey, Jr.'s Doctor Doom from 
Avengers: Doomsday
. This newest figure is an especially interesting case, however, as it showcases a new costume based on unrevealed concept art from the film.
Check out the slideshow gallery below for a closer look at Hot Toys' Lord Doom figure:
Hot Toys Avengers: Doomsday - Lord Doom Figure Image Gallery
View 18 Images
Again, it doesn't appear that Downey will actually wear this costume in the film; this is merely based on concept art of an alternate look for the character. But it's certainly an interesting variation on the traditional Doom design. Like 
the previously revealed "Victor Von Doom" figure
, this costume looks to be more of a formal wear situation, with Doom decked out in full Latverian military regalia and wearing a less imposing version of his mask. 
Sideshow Collectibles hasn't yet put this figure up for preorder, so we don't have any details regarding pricing and release date. Like the Victor von Doom figure, we'd expect this to be an exclusive figure produced in limited quantities, so definitely 
RSVP on the Sideshow site
 if you're interested in picking one up.

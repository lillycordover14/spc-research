Molly Butler Reflects on Her World Cup Slalom Debut in Levi
Matt Garcka
Nov 20, 2026

British skier Molly Butler 🇬🇧 GBR made her World Cup debut in the
 
women’s slalom in Levi, Finland
, on Nov. 14, 2026. The 20-year-old wore bib 59 on the Levi Black. She went out in the first run.

“Standing in the World Cup start is exactly the feeling I wanted,” 
she said by phone a few days later.

After the race, Butler spoke exclusively with
 Ski Racing Media and the 
Skiing is Believing
 podcast 
in the finish area.
 “Overall, it was a very good day and a great experience but I’m pretty disappointed I didn’t finish,” 
she said.

When asked what she learned, Butler joked that the lesson was
 “don’t straddle
.
” 
She felt she skied well through the top half. Still, she felt nervous.
 “I was a bit nervous and maybe that’s why I wasn’t as fluid as I could have been.”

Butler had raced slalom at the 2025 World Championships in Saalbach, Austria. By the time she arrived in Levi, she was Britain’s highest-ranked woman on the FIS slalom points list. She was excited to
 mix with the world’s best again. 
Afterward, she said she wanted to get more comfortable in those snow conditions.

Route to the World Cup
 
Through New Zealand

Butler trained with the three Carrick-Smith brothers 
from ages
 eight to 18. 
She credits them for the hard work they put in together
 throughout her early racing years. 
Her mother, Jay Blatherwick, was her main coach. She had
 worked as a ski instructor for around 25 years before becoming part of Team Butler.

In 2026, Butler began working with Belgian coach Xan Alaerts. She trained alongside
 Australian 
Phoebe
 Heaydon 
🇦🇺 AUS in New Zealand. In August, Butler won an Australia New Zealand Cup slalom at Mount Hutt. She then finished third in a Cup slalom at Coronet Peak in September. She took both the slalom and overall titles.

Heaydon also raced the World Cup slalom in Levi. She was
 31st 
in the first run
, 
0.12 seconds
 out of the top 30.

Two More Slaloms in Levi

Butler’s training continues in Levi ahead of two 
Europa Cup
 
slaloms
 on 
Nov. 21 and 22
. 
Because she won the slalom title, Butler can start with bib 31 in Continental Cup slaloms this season.

“It’s pretty amazing to get experience on this hill ahead of next weekend’s Europa Cups and hopefully more World Cups throughout the season,” 
Butler said in the finish area.

British Women Train Together in Levi

Charlie Guest 
🇬🇧 GBR made her final World Cup start on March 10, 2024, in Åre, Sweden. Since then, three British women have started World Cup slaloms. Butler was the third, after Victoria Palla 🇬🇧 GBR and Reece Bell 🇬🇧 GBR.

Austrian-based Palla started four 
World Cup slaloms in 2025–26 but didn’t reach a second run. A season earlier, Bell made three World Cup slalom starts. She last raced on Dec. 20, 2025, in a Europa Cup slalom.

Britain currently has one basic quota spot in women’s World Cup slalom. A top-30 finish would have earned Palla World Cup points. Those points would have moved her up the slalom World Cup Starting List. Britain gains a second spot if a British skier ranks in the top 60 when FIS recalculates quotas.

The British women usually train in separate groups. In Levi, though, Butler shared training time with
 Palla, Giselle Gorringe 
🇬🇧 GBR
 and Olivia Howeson 
🇬🇧 GBR
.

“
It’s weird because it’s nice to be together even though we’re all in separate training groups. It’s a little sad there’s nothing set-up. I think it’s very positive that we’re all moving forward and I want to get the British flag flying on the women’s side
,
” 
Butler said.

Liski provided gate and protection equipment for the Levi World Cup.

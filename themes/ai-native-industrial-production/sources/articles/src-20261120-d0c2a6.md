Twelve teams chase four World Cup places as WBSC Americas U-12 Baseball qualifier opens on 20 November

The Dominican Republic will host the eight-day tournament, with the top four finishers securing places at the WBSC U-12 Baseball World Cup 2027 presented by RAXUS.

Twelve teams will begin their pursuit of four WBSC U-12 Baseball World Cup places when the 
U-12 Pan American Championship
 opens on Friday, 20 November, at the IPL Academy in San Antonio de Guerra, Dominican Republic. The tournament runs through 27 November.
The continental championship will award four places at the 
WBSC U-12 Baseball World Cup 2027 presented by RAXUS
, bringing together teams from across the Americas in three Opening Round groups:

Group A: 
World No. 22 Brazil, No. 12 Cuba, No. 14 Nicaragua and No. 4 USA
Group B: 
No. 35 Argentina, No. 30 Curaçao, No. 9 Dominican Republic and No. 3 Venezuela.
Group C: 
No. 10 Mexico, No. 7 Panama, No. 34 Peru and No. 6 Puerto Rico.

The Opening Round runs through Sunday, 22 November, with the top two teams from each group advancing to the Super Round, scheduled for 23–26 November.
The top four teams in the Super Round will secure World Cup qualification and advance to the medal games on Friday, 27 November. The first- and second-placed teams will compete for the Pan American title, following the bronze medal game between the third- and fourth-placed finishers.

WBSC U-12 Baseball World Cup 2027 presented by RAXUS

Hosted by the Chinese Taipei Baseball Association, the IX WBSC U-12 Baseball World Cup will take place from 9 to 18 July 2027 at the Asia Pacific International Baseball Training Centre (ASPAC) in Tainan.
Five teams from Asia and Europe have already secured their places in Tainan.
World No. 1 Japan, No. 2 Chinese Taipei and No. 4 Korea qualified through the
 U-12 Asian Baseball Championship
 in August.
No. 17 Germany and No. 16 Czechia earned their spot through the 
U-12 European Championship
 in July.
The 
WBSC Oceania U-12 Men's Baseball Qualification Event
, scheduled for 27 to 30 November in Guam, will award another spot.
One team from Africa and a Wild Card will complete the 12-participant field.
USA are the defending U-12 World Champions. They defeated Japan in the 
2025 World Championship Final
.

Muhlenberg College has again been named one of 
America’s Top Colleges by Forbes
, which rates college on 14 data points that include graduation and retention rates and the overall return on investment of earned degrees. 
Muhlenberg’s graduation and retention rates far surpass national averages, and the 
data show
 that a Muhlenberg College degree delivers a higher return on investment than 80% of all 4,600 U.S. colleges and universities within 15 years of graduation, rising to 90% at the 20-year mark.
Forbes has named Muhlenberg to three 2027 lists:
#307
 America’s Top Colleges 2027
#167
 in Private Colleges 2027
#112
 in the Northeast 2027
According to Forbes, “The good news is a solid undergraduate education still pays off and America’s Top 500 Colleges identifies those schools that produce the best academic and career outcomes for students from all economic backgrounds”

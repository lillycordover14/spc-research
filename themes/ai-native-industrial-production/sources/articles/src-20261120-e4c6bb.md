The Columbia junior will race in the women’s 6K Saturday in Terre Haute
TERRE HAUTE, Ind. – 
Columbia junior 
Olivia Murray
 will make her NCAA Cross Country Championships debut on Saturday, Nov. 21, at the LaVern Gibson Championship Cross Country Course. The women's 6K is scheduled for 10:20 a.m. ET.


Murray is the first Lion to qualify for the national meet since Phoebe Anderson finished 26th in 2024 to earn her second cross country All-America honor.

MEET COVERAGE

Live coverage begins at 10 a.m. ET on ESPNU and the ESPN app. Live results will be available through PrimeTime Timing.

ROAD TO NATIONALS

Murray earned an individual berth with a 10th-place finish at the NCAA Northeast Regional on Nov. 13 at Bethpage State Park. She covered the 6K in 20:15.4, improving 39 places from last year's regional finish. Murray was the top Ivy League finisher and earned All-Region honors as Columbia placed ninth as a team.

Earlier this fall, Murray earned First Team All-Ivy honors with a fourth-place finish in 20:31.7 at the Ivy League Cross Country Championships. She also placed fourth at the ECAC Championships in 20:27.2, leading the Lions to a runner-up finish.

ON DECK

The Lions open the indoor track and field season on Friday, Dec. 4, at the Armory Collegiate Distance Classic, hosted by Army West Point at The Armory. The meet is scheduled to begin at 10 a.m. ET.

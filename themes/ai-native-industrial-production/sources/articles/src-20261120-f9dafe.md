Some coaches have been quietly lamenting the giant NIL payouts being given to relatively unproven players, while others have been more public.
 
You can lump 
UConn
 women's basketball head coach Geno Auriemma into that second group with his latest comments.

The 12-time national champion and eight-time Coach of the Year has never been shy about speaking his mind, and this time it's NIL that has him fired up.
Auriemma was addressing reporters ahead of early national signing day when he voiced his frustration with "paying a kid a million dollars" right out of high school.
 
"We just signed the No. 9 player in the country. We don’t have cachet with guys that are getting paid a million dollars to go play some place," he told reporters. "They’re getting the cachet. I just don’t see the value of paying a high school kid a million dollars to come out of high school that hasn’t dribbled the ball one time in college.
 
 
"I don’t get the idea of paying a kid a million dollars coming out of high school. That’s more than (about) 90 percent of best players in the world in 
the WNBA
. As long as that’s the case, we’re going to get the guys we’re gonna get, but we’re not going to get the guys we’re not supposed to get."

Nov 20, 2026 — 

A longtime Canton public figure has died. David Button was also a broadcaster, educator, and author. He died on Nov. 18 in the morning at the age of 73.
 His obituary said
 he “passed peacefully surrounded by his family.”

Button settled in Canton in 1986, where he used his sports broadcasting experience to voice St. Lawrence University hockey and football. In 1989, he was elected as a town councilman in Canton and then served as its mayor until 1998. 

Button served as Canton Town Supervisor from 2002 to 2017. 

During his career in public service, Button helped address wastewater treatment needs and led development on what is now the Partridge Run Golf Course. 

Button has held a full-time faculty position in the business department at SUNY Canton for the last several years.

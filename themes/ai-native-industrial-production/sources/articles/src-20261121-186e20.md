Daniel F. Boltz

Jul 10, 1949 — Nov 21, 2026

New Philadelphia, Ohio

Daniel F. Boltz, 77, of New Philadelphia, passed away on Saturday, November 21, 2026, at Cleveland Clinic Union Hospital in Dover.
Born July 10, 1949, in Dover, he was the son of the late Floyd and Edith McCreery Boltz. He was also preceded in death by his brother, Jim Boltz.
A 1967 graduate of New Philadelphia High School, Daniel served his country in the United States Army Reserves and attended mechanics school in Coshocton. He began his working life at Weaver Buick and Cadillac in New Philadelphia, later worked for his brother’s business at Jim Boltz Excavating, and then started his own venture, Deals on Wheels. He also drove truck for GTE before retiring in 2015 from Tuscarawas County MRDD, where he enjoyed his work as a bus driver and the time spent with the children. He was well known for buying, selling, and refurbishing cars, and in later years took up buying, selling, and restoring tractors, often attending tractor auctions. Handy and skilled, he could fix or restore nearly anything on four wheels. Away from work, he most enjoyed time with his family, especially his grandchildren.
Daniel is survived by his wife of 31 years, Carolyn Fowler Boltz, whom he married on June 22, 1995; his children, Heather (John) Metzger of Sugarcreek, Darby (Kristen) Boltz and Lisa (Lance) Custer, both of New Philadelphia, and Rob (Kelli) Host of Leesville; his grandchildren, Morgan (Ethan) Roden, Marley (Wyatt) Freeman, Logan Boltz, Jaiden Boltz, and Katelyn (Kenny) Feister; and two great-grandchildren, Rylee and Hudson Feister.
Cremation has been entrusted to the Toland-Herzig Funeral Home and Crematory in Dover. In keeping with Daniel’s wishes, there will be no services or calling hours.

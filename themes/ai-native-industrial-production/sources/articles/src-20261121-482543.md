Başkent Doğalgaz Reports TRY 50,000 Nominal Share Buy-Back
Tipranks - 
Tipranks
 - 
Sat Nov 21, 10:04AM CST
The latest announcement is out from Baskent Dogalgaz Dagitim Gayrimenkul Yatirim Ortakligi AS ( 
(TR:BASGZ)
 ).
Başkent Doğalgaz Dağıtım Gayrimenkul Yatırım Ortaklığı A.Ş. reported that it bought back BASGZ shares with a total nominal value of TRY 50,000 on Borsa İstanbul on 20 November 2026, at prices ranging from TRY 34.60 to TRY 35.54 per share. The transaction value was TRY 1,754,672.78, equal to 0.00714% of the company’s capital.
The buy-back program was initiated following a board decision dated 3 November 2026 and lists an acquisition amount of TRY 30,000,000 nominal value, with a maximum payment of TRY 1,000,000,000. By 20 November 2026, total repurchases under the program had reached TRY 110,000 nominal value, representing 0.01572% of the company’s capital.

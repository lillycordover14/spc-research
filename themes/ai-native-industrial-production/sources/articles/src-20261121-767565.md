Hot Toys Reveals Awesome LORD DOOM Figure for AVENGERS: DOOMSDAY, But Marvel Cut Him From the Movie

by Joey Paur

Hot Toys has unveiled a cool new Lord Doom figure for Marvel Studios'
 
Avengers: Doomsday
, and it offers a look at a version of 
Robert Downey Jr.
's Doctor Doom that apparently won't even appear in the movie.
The collectible is a 1/6th scale figure featuring a striking design pulled directly from concept art. It's an impressive look for the villain, but it also raises some questions about what Marvel originally had planned for Doom and why this particular design didn't make the final cut.
What's interesting is that Hot Toys isn't the only company producing Lord Doom merchandise. Hasbro and Funko have also developed figures based on the same character design, which suggests this version of Doom may have originally played a much bigger role in Avengers: Doomsday.
After all, toy companies don't typically spend the time and money developing entire product lines around character designs that were never intended to be used. Something clearly changed during production, although Marvel hasn't explained what happened.
The Marvel Legends description for Lord Doom offers some additional insight into the character: "The fearsome Victor Von Doom is the mysterious and enigmatic monarch of Latveria. Behind his impenetrable mask, he is a master of both sorcery and technology."
This wouldn't be the first time Marvel's merchandise accidentally revealed abandoned movie plans. Remember 
Avengers: Infinity War
? Toys and promotional artwork featured Hulk bursting out of the Hulkbuster armor, which was originally supposed to happen in the film.
Marvel ultimately changed Bruce Banner's storyline during production, leaving that moment out of the finished movie and saving Smart Hulk's introduction for 
Avengers: Endgame
. Unfortunately for the toy manufacturers, their merchandise was already out in the world.
Something similar happened with 
Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness
. Marvel released a bunch of Rintrah merchandise, giving fans the impression that the giant green, bull-like sorcerer would have a substantial role. Instead, he barely appeared in the finished movie.
More recently, 
Spider-Man: Brand New Day
 merchandise featured Scorpion sporting a completely different tail design from the one ultimately used in the film. Apparently, Marvel's creative decisions don't always line up with the manufacturing schedules of its merchandising partners.
Now Lord Doom appears to be joining that club. Rather than pretending these collectibles represent something audiences will actually see on screen, Hasbro, Funko, and Hot Toys are labeling their Lord Doom products as concept art-based designs.
I like the Lord Doom design, and it's a shame we apparently won't get to see Downey Jr. wearing it in the movie. At least collectors will have an opportunity to own a piece of what might have been.
What do you think of the Lord Doom figure? Would you have liked to see this version of Doctor Doom in Avengers: Doomsday?

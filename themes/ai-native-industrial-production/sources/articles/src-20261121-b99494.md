Towne Makes NCAA Cross Country Championships Debut
November 21, 2026
 by Zac Swain
 Share

Cincinnati native finishes best season of his career as the Bearcats' lone national qualifier
 Results
TERRE HAUTE, Ind. —
 Junior Lucas Towne closed out a breakthrough season Saturday by representing the University of Cincinnati as its lone qualifier at the 2026 NCAA Cross Country Championships at Indiana State's LaVern Gibson Championship Course.

Towne, making his first career appearance at the championships, finished 229th in a field of 261 runners with a time of 31:33.1. He is the eighth men's Bearcat in program history to compete at the event.

"Lucas gained valuable experience today competing at the NCAA XC Championships for the first time," Cincinnati head cross country coach Gabby Gaudreault said. "He has proven that he is one of the top distance runners in the nation by qualifying for this meet, and that is an incredible accomplishment for him and for our distance program. This is great momentum for Lucas moving into the track season, and you will continue to see him race at a very high level.

"Competing at the NCAA XC Championships is not easy, especially given that these athletes raced back-to-back weekends over 10K. I'm super proud of how Lucas managed the turnaround from NCAA regionals to prepare to compete for the national championship."

The Cincinnati native punched his ticket to nationals with a 19th-place finish at the NCAA Great Lakes Regional, earning All-Region honors.

The championship appearance capped the best season of Towne's career. He led the Bearcats in all five meets before Saturday and opened the year by winning the men's 8K at the RedHawk Rumble, his first collegiate cross country victory.

Louisville's Elsingi Kipruto won the individual title in 28:27.7. New Mexico took the team championship with 69 points, placing four of its five scorers in the top 15.

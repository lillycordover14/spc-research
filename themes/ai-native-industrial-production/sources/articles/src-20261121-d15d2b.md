Kid Cudi Unveils New Producer Alias “RAGER” and Announces Instrumental Dance Album, Visions...

Scott Mescudi—better known to the world as 
Kid Cudi
—is officially making a pivot into the electronic dance world. The Grammy-winning artist announced his brand-new dance album, 
Visions In The Night
, slated for release on 
December 10, 2026
, via his independent imprint, Mad Solar. Mescudi is introducing a dedicated electronic and producer alias: 
RAGER
—a direct nod to his beloved alter ego introduced on his 2010 sophomore classic, 
Man on the Moon II: The Legend of Mr. Rager
.

According to Cudi, 
Visions In The Night
 was recorded during a spontaneous, two-day creative burst in Paris and will be a fully instrumental dance album. Speaking directly to fans on X (formerly Twitter), he clarified why he chose to separate his vocal hip-hop catalog from this new venture. “
RAGER not Kid Cudi. Instrumental dance album. Producer vibes,”
 Cudi explained. 
“This way, u know what ur coming for. Rager. This album is meant to find fans who love my production and always wanted this.
 He also made clear that it is separate from the rap album he has already finished, calling that one “
a Cudi album. Bars
,” and adding that with RAGER he “
was just bored and decided to cook
.”

While a track list and official singles remain under wraps, Cudi confirmed that this project is completely separate from his next vocal rap album (which he noted is already finished). He also announced a rave, ‘RAGERVILLE’, on December 9th as a pre-album release party.

During a recent interview, he explained that “
Mr. Rager was one of those records I wrote right in the middle of my drug use. My voice was shot. I couldn’t sing
.” While it is unclear if his producer alias is named after the single, it may explain why he chose Rager as a producer name.

Returning to His Dancefloor Roots

Longtime electronic fans know Cudi’s DNA is deeply intertwined with dance music. From 
Steve Aoki’s
 festival-defining remix of ‘Pursuit of Happiness’ and his global smash ‘Memories’ with 
David Guetta
, to his 2025 house collaboration ‘Forever’ with 
Dom Dolla
, Cudi has spent nearly two decades bridging psychedelic hip-hop and club culture.

Mark your calendars for 
December 10
.

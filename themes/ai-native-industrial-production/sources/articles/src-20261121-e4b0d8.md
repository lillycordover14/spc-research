Hurricanes Battle, Fall in Four Sets to Tar Heels
Volleyball
November 20, 2026
Freshmen Ruth Martinez and Ella Thompson led Miami offensively, tallying 18 and 13 kills respectively, a career-best night for Thompson.
 by Rebecca Osowski
 Box Score 
CHAPEL HILL, N.C. - 
 The University of Miami volleyball team traveled to Chapel Hill, Friday, facing the No. 21 North Carolina Tar Heels for the first of two meetings in a one-week span. The Hurricanes battled throughout the match, winning the must-win third set 27-25 and leading for most of the fourth set, but North Carolina held on to defeat Miami 3-1.
With the loss, Miami falls to 15-11 on the season and 13-4 in ACC play. Friday’s loss snaps a six-match win streak for the Hurricanes.
Freshmen 
Ruth Martinez
, 
Ella Thompson
, and 
Denitsa Angelova
 led Miami against the Tar Heels, all recording double digit kills. Martinez recorded 18 kills, followed by a career night by Thompson, who tallied 13 kills at a .417 hitting percentage, playing in just three sets.
Angelova rounded out the top three Hurricanes’ scorers with 12 kills at a .400 hitting percentage. She also added a service ace, six digs, and two blocks. Also impressive at the service line was 
Billie Reiter
, who recorded a career high four.
Defensively, 
Gaby Arroyo
 led the way with 18 digs, followed by 
Ariana Rodriguez
’s 12. The setter also added 48 assists.
Set 1: UNC 25, Miami 20
Miami opened the first set on a 3-0 run and held a slight advantage until North Carolina pulled even at four. The Hurricanes went back in front on a 
Ruth Martinez
 kill, but the Tar Heels followed with two straight to take their first lead of the set. After a UNC service error retied the set, the Tar Heels recorded four straight points to take a 10-6 lead.
The Hurricanes scored four of the next six, capitalizing on a 
Sonja Danilovic
 kill and three Tar Heels’ errors, but North Carolina scored the next seven points to make it a nine-point game.
Despite a Miami push late in the set thanks to five straight points including back-to-back 
Billie Reiter
 aces to pull the Canes within three, 23-20, the Tar Heels held on for the 25-20 first set victory.
Set 2: UNC 25, Miami 19
North Carolina controlled the second set, taking a lead it would never surrender. The Tar Heels scored five straight points before the Hurricanes got on the board with an 
Ella Thompson
 kill, her first of the match.
Following the kill, UNC scored three straight and ten of the next 13 to cruise into the media timeout leading 15-4 over the Hurricanes.
Miami pushed out of the timeout, recording six straight points, as 
Denitsa Angelova
 recorded two kills, along with another from Thompson and two more Reiter aces, pulling the Canes within five.
The teams traded points from there, as Miami strung together consecutive points twice to North Carolina’s once, but the Tar Heels secured the win in the second set by six.
Set 3: Miami 27, UNC 25
The Tar Heels scored the first point in the third set, but it was Miami that pushed to maintain the early lead. Two straight Thompson kills and one from Martinez gave the Hurricanes a 3-1 lead as they continued to record consecutive points, extending their lead to five headed into the media timeout. Danilovic, Martinez, and Thompson all added kills while middle blockers Angelova and 
Lal Akin
 also found their way onto the scoresheet.
Out of the break, momentum slowly started shifting in favor of the Tar Heels. They scored four of the next six, forcing Head Coach 
Jose “Keno” Gandara
 to take a timeout with his team up by three, 17-14.
North Carolina continued to push as they scored seven points to Miami’s two, as the Tar Heels took a 21-19 lead, forcing another Hurricanes’ timeout. The Canes found a way down the stretch as Thompson scored four of Miami’s eight points to force a fourth set, claiming the 27-25 victory.
Set 4: UNC 25, Miami 23
Miami took another early lead in the fourth set, going ahead 5-3 on an 
Ariana Rodriguez
 service ace. The teams went back and forth, before North Carolina scored four straight to go up by two. The Hurricanes were able to tie on kills from Martinez and Danilovic, as Miami managed to stay in front into the media timeout. Martinez forced the break, recording two consecutive kills to give her team a 15-13 lead.
On the other side of the timeout, the Hurricanes scored three of the next four, extending their lead to four. The Tar Heels pushed though, going on a 6-0 run, also spanning a Miami timeout, to take a 20-18 lead.
Angelova pulled Miami within one before a Tar Heels’ attack error tied the set at 20 apiece. UNC came out on top in the race to 25, recording five of the next eight, as they defeated Miami 25-23 in the final set and 3-1 in the match.
Up Next: 
The Miami Hurricanes will play the Duke Blue Devils on Sunday, Nov. 22 with first serve slated for 1 p.m. Coverage of the match will be available on ACCNX.

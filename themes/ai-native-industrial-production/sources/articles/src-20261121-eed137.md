Newsroom

 

November 21, 2026

21.11.2026 • 12:06

Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis on Saturday pledged to strengthen the country’s military deterrent capabilities and improve pay and housing support for members of the armed forces, marking Greece’s Armed Forces Day.

In a message posted on social media, Mitsotakis described the annual observance as a “day of national gratitude” to the men and women serving in the military. He paid tribute to those who have fallen in service and praised personnel who remain on alert with “a sense of responsibility, high morale and professionalism.”

Highlighting Greece’s military modernization program, Mitsotakis pointed to the new Belharra-class frigates, Rafale and upgraded F-16 Viper fighter jets, and Romeo helicopters. He also cited the planned Achilles’ Shield air defense system, domestically developed drones and F-35 fighter jets, which he said would soon join the military’s arsenal.

The prime minister also stressed the importance of supporting military personnel, with measures ranging from pay increases to assistance with housing.

Referring to the government’s Agenda 2030 defense modernization plan, Mitsotakis said peace could no longer be taken for granted amid geopolitical upheaval and emerging threats.

Greece is strengthening its deterrent capabilities, he said, describing them as a guarantee of the country’s sovereignty.

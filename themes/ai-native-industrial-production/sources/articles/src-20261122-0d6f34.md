Utah clinches spot in the 2026 Edward Jones Big 12 Championship Game
FORT WORTH, Texas –
 Quarterback 
Devon Dampier
 led the No. 2 Utah football team on a game-clinching touchdown drive in the fourth quarter for the third time in 2026 as the Utes earned a road victory over the TCU Horned Frogs, 38-17, at Amon G. Carter Stadium on Saturday night.

 

Utah cemented a spot in the Edward Jones Big 12 Championship Game on December 4 at AT&T Stadium in Arlington with the win. The Utes are the last unbeaten team in the country at 11-0 and hold the nation's longest win streak at 17 straight games.

 

Dampier's touchdown run and touchdown pass helped the visitors build a 17-0 halftime edge, but by early in the fourth quarter, TCU had battled back to tie the score.

 

With 9:25 remaining in regulation, Utah took possession at its own 25 and Dampier led his squad on a 75-yard touchdown march to retake the lead. The next time Utah got the ball, the Heisman Trophy contender connected with 
Larry Simmons
 for a career-long 85-yard touchdown and Dampier ended the night with one more scoring run to put the Horned Frogs away.

 

Dampier finished the night 18-for-28 passing for 278 yards with two touchdown passes, while also rushing for 62 yards and two more trips to paydirt. It represented his third straight game with multiple rushing and passing touchdowns.

 

Utah's quarterback received plenty of help on offense. 
Larry Simmons
 set a career-high with 114 receiving yards on three catches, including his touchdown grab, and 
Braden Pegan
 added 75 yards and a score of his own. In the ground game, 
Daniel Bray
 tallied 57 yards and 
Hunter Andrews
 scored his first rushing touchdown of 2026.

 

Utah's defense got off to an excellent start by limiting TCU to just 56 yards and five first downs in the opening half. The Horned Frogs averaged 1.9 yards per play and converted just twice on nine third down tries in what was a scoreless first 30 minutes for the home team. Utah collected four TFLs in the first half and ended the night with eight, the unit's highest total in 2026. 

 

The leaders on defense for the Utes included 
Jackson Bennee
, who recorded the first multi-interception game of his career. Both picks set up scores. 
Elijah Elliss
 set a career-high with two sacks and 
Johnathan Hall
 made a pair of TFLs. 
Rabbit Evans
 led the team with seven tackles, including 1.5 TFLs, and 
Kash Dillon
 made six stops, one of which was a sack.

 

UP NEXT

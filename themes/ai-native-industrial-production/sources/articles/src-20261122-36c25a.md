Christmas City of the North Parade

Santa and Mrs. Claus returned, once again arriving by helicopter, just before the 2026 Bentleyville Season officially kicked off.

“Welcome to Duluth, Santa. It is good to have you and Mrs. Claus,” said Reinert, before shaking their hands.
Bentley, the founder and executive director for Bentleyville, also welcomed the Clauses and then, minutes later, the countdown began for a new season of the popular holiday attraction as the lights lit up the night around 5 p.m. Even with snowless grounds and temperatures around 60 degrees during the day and in the 40s after sunset, the holiday spirit filled the park as visitors checked out the attractions. Paige Lamkin traveled up with a group from Waseca, Minnesota, for opening night.

Paige Lamkin, left, points at a display while Travis Friedrichs, both of Waseca, Minnesota, looks on during the opening night of Bentleyville at Bayfront Festival Park in Duluth on Saturday. 
Dan Williamson / Duluth Media Group

“We’ve been here like four times,” said Lamkin.
This is the 23rd season of the Bentleyville “Tour of Lights.” For 18 years, it has been held at Bayfront Festival Park. Prior to that, the attractions were held at Bentley's homes in Esko and Cloquet. The 2026 Bentleyville season wraps up Dec. 26. Hours will be 5-9 p.m. Sundays through Thursdays and 5-10 p.m. Fridays and Saturdays.
For more information, visit 
    
      
bentleyvilleusa.org.

ORLANDO, Fla. –
 Closing out its time at the Happiest Place on Earth at the WBCA Showcase, No. 24 North Carolina women's basketball withstood Alabama's punches on Saturday night at State Farm Field House, then took off on a third-quarter surge to defeat the Crimson Tide, 78-64.


In a game with 16 lead changes and seven ties, a 13-0 North Carolina (4-2, 0-0 ACC) run to end the third quarter proved to be the difference in taking down Alabama (4-2, 0-0 SEC).


A team effort, three Tar Heels posted double-figure nights from 
Kate Harpring
 with 20 points, 
Achol Akot
 with a season-high 16, and 
Nyla Brooks
 with 14. Matching her career-high in rebounds, 
Laila Hull
 grabbed five boards, along with Akot and Harpring. Hull also showed her defensive prowess by matching her career-high with three steals.


Dishing out seven dimes, 
Reniya Kelly
 matched her career-best in assists and added seven points.


Cashing in on 18 Crimson Tide turnovers, the Tar Heels took advantage with 28 points off turnovers.


Jumping out of the gate with a 9-0 run by the Tar Heels, Kelly came rearing to go with two buckets in the scoring spurt. Firing back, Alabama closed the gap with a 7-0 run of its own by taking the ball on the inside. Entering a scoring lull, Carolina broke it open with Harpring's first triple of the day followed by a Queiroz layup to take a narrow 20-19 lead through one.


With the lead trading hands 11 times in the first half, the Crimson Tide and Tar Heels also continued to trade threes. Brooks held it down from deep for Carolina, with two triples in the second quarter. Playing at a fast pace, Queiroz found success in the paint while the Tar Heel defense held it down on the opposite end by keeping Alabama scoreless for over three minutes. The Crimson Tide took off on a 6-0 run in the final two minutes to carry a two-point advantage over the Tar Heels at 38-36 heading into halftime.


A three-ball from Harpring opened the second half for Carolina, as an attempt to grab a sizable lead continued back-and-forth between the Tar Heels and the Crimson Tide. Entering a scoring slump, Carolina battled to get into an offensive rhythm. Firing off threes on both sides, Carolina ran back ahead off a 13-0 run highlighted by two Harpring triples with time dwindling in the third. Continuing to hum on offense, a Hull deep heave at the buzzer swished for the Tar Heels to go up by 10 at 60-50 through three.


Taking the reins, North Carolina battled to keep Alabama at bay and thwart any attempt at a comeback. With Carolina held scoreless for over three minutes, Alabama pulled within seven with under four on the clock. Hull was feeling it from deep, draining her second triple for Carolina to extend its lead back to 10. Another Brooks three provided breathing room for the Tar Heels to run off to a 78-64 victory over the Crimson Tide.


Carolina returns to the comfort of Carmichael Arena on Tuesday for the start of a 10-game homestand against Gardner-Webb. Set to air on ACC Network Extra, the Tar Heels and Runnin' Bulldogs will tip off at 7 p.m. ET.

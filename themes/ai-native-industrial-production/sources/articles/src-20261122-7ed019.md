Travis Burgess is UNC’s Player of the Game against Virginia
It looks like the Tar Heels’ next QB1 is already on the roster.
by
 
 
Matt Bowling
Nov 22, 2026, 4:00 PM UTC
Travis Burgess nearly swayed me into giving him player of the game honors when he made his college debut against Louisville last week. After making his first start at Virginia on Saturday, he left me with no choice.
Seriously, when the opposing head coach goes out of their way to 
tell you you’re going to be special
, you’ve played a remarkable game. The numbers certainly reflect that: Burgess threw for 354 yards and ran for 106, becoming just the fourth FBS quarterback to record a 300 yard passing/100 yard rushing game this season.
Yes, he accumulated some of those yards (and both of his touchdowns) in garbage time. Yes, he was far from perfect – he missed some throws early in the game, and threw an interception on the Tar Heels’ first drive after Virginia took a 7-0 lead. I do think much of that can be chalked up to Burgess being a true freshman in a hostile environment; he seemed to settle in as the game went on.
It was an afternoon Carolina fans would probably like to forget, but it may have also been the start of a Chapel Hill career we’ll remember fondly.
Honorable mention goes to Jordan Shipp, who set a pair of new career highs with nine receptions for 149 yards. With bowl eligibility on the line against NC State next week, let’s hope he has another performance like that in him.

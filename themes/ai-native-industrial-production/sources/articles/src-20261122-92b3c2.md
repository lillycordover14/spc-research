Atakey Patates Gida Sanayi ve Ticaret AS ( 
(TR:ATAKP)
 ) has shared an announcement.
Atakey Patates Gida Sanayi ve Ticaret AS said it repurchased shares with a nominal value of TRY 50,000 on Borsa Istanbul on Nov. 20, 2026, at TRY 33.12-34.38 per share and a weighted average of TRY 33.76, under its buy-back program approved on June 14, 2024. Cumulative repurchases reached TRY 2,032,519, or 1.46% of share capital; the program permits up to TRY 3,469,200 nominal shares and has TRY 175,000,000 allocated.

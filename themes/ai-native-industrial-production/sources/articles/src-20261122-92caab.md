Fear of God has officially introduced Collection TEN as the latest evolution of the house's visual codes and foundations

The expansive range debuts a new Athletics category and the brand's first in-house athletic shoe featuring supercritical foam

Collection TEN will officially arrive in Fall 2027 as the first collection to land at the brand's Los Angeles flagship store

Fear of God
 has officially unveiled its highly anticipated Collection TEN. Rooted in a conviction that extends beyond the material, the latest seasonal offering distills thirteen years of the brand’s foundational instincts into a more fully realized expression of American luxury.

Refining its vision through a studied approach to fabrication and construction, Collection TEN utilizes bonded details and articulated sleeves to bring fresh structure and dimension to signature silhouettes. Contrasting fabrics and intentional finishes further define the apparel, while the iconic Fear of God bar has been updated in brushed champagne and matte black metal to mark a distinct evolution in the house’s visual codes.

Beyond traditional ready-to-wear, the collection introduces Athletics, a brand-new category informed by vintage American sportswear. This segment delivers washed training wear that seamlessly translates athletic dressing into the label’s refined design language. Anchoring the new category is the Fear of God Trainer, marking the house’s very first athletic shoe built entirely in-house. Uniting modern performance technology with a considered aesthetic, the silhouette features responsive supercritical foam cushioning to merge high-level performance with modern luxury.

Fear of God Collection TEN will be available in Fall 2027, becoming the first collection to officially arrive at the Fear of God flagship store in Los Angeles.

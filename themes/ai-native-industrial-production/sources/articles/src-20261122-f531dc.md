                    Ghalibaf Says Iran Has Achieved ‘Sustainable’ Military Deterrence

ERBIL (Kurdistan24) –
 Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf said on Sunday that Iran had achieved a level of sustainable military deterrence following recent conflicts, urging officials to take practical measures to end the country's prolonged state of “neither war nor peace.”

Speaking at a meeting of the Iranian Parliament's Coordination Council, Ghalibaf credited the country's Armed Forces and Supreme Leader Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei with strengthening Iran's military deterrence through what he described as lessons learned from recent confrontations, including Operation True Promise and the 12-day and 40-day wars.

Ghalibaf argued that Iran's adversaries had concluded they could no longer launch a war similar to the 40-day conflict. He claimed that, as a result, they had shifted their focus toward undermining Iran's internal security, increasing economic pressure, and destabilizing the country from within.

He also stressed the need to move beyond political rhetoric and accusations of warmongering or capitulation, calling on officials to adopt concrete decisions addressing the country's economic, livelihood, and defense challenges.

Referring to the Supreme Leader's call to end the prolonged “neither war nor peace” situation, Ghalibaf said political slogans and mutual accusations would not resolve the country's difficulties.

He emphasized that Iran needed a clear and practical approach to overcome the current uncertainty, strengthen national unity, and establish lasting deterrence against potential threats.

His remarks come amid continued tensions between Tehran and Washington, with both sides facing unresolved disagreements over security, economic pressure, and the prospects for a lasting settlement.

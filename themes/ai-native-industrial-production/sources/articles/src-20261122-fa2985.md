    Tasting the Town: An afternoon of burger appreciation

    Three DTH editors tried burgers from three different local restaurants. They came back with high praise.

Gabe Thumm
, 
Ava Sharon
, and 
Josh Markotich
                 
             •  
            
November 18, 2026
 

 

Aubrey Word
 
Photos by Cassidy Toy Reynolds.
 

The three of us — Audience Engagement Editor Gabe Thumm, Photo Editor Ava Sharon and Assistant Sports Editor Josh Markotich — and the team of videographers documenting our every move can attest that there is no more gratifying experience than eating lunch with great friends.

When we look back at an afternoon of Tasting the Town, we will remember the camaraderie, the shared love of burgers and the wonderful vibes of a fall day in Chapel Hill. 

As each second passed, the day only got better. Every time an order was placed, our anticipation went through the roof. It was as if we were waiting to embark on a tastebud rollercoaster.

The twists and turns of each burger were a unique ride across Chapel Hill, propelling a proper journey through burgers from three of the town’s most iconic locations: Sutton’s Drug Store, Buns Burgers & Fries and Al’s Burger Shack.

Sutton’s Drug Store

Gabe:
 Though we did not begin the day expecting to review Sutton’s Drug Store burgers, it was a welcome surprise. The burger was, in a word, classic. Nothing flashy, nothing spectacular, just a good eat. Indistinct, maybe, but still lovely. The real factor that props this above the rest is the ambience. Star Trek on the wall-mounted television. A vast soda and candy selection. The experience felt like stepping into a time capsule, one that happened to include a nice burger. 
Score: 8

Ava: 
This was my first time at Sutton’s, and it definitely did not disappoint. From the Hooters cups to the vintage soda wall, every second of my Sutton’s experience was perfect. As for the burger, it was a little bit messy, and the bun lacked structural support. However, I’m willing to make a mess because the burger was delicious. It was a great start to a long day of burger-eating. 
Score: 10

Josh:
 As someone who has tasted a Sutton’s burger before, I will be eating another one. Soon. The Sutton’s burger is a beautiful creation of everything right in a burger — the burger knows exactly what it is. It’s far from a fast food burger, but not quite a premium burger, and the Sutton’s burger is okay with that. It’s a nostalgic taste of one of the most iconic lunch spots in Chapel Hill. 
Score: 10
 

Buns Burgers & Fries

Gabe:
 Of the three, I can’t say this was my standout. As a burger connoisseur, the bun took more of my attention than the patty itself, yet the combination was fairly pleasant. Many thoughts ran through my head as I was eating, all surrounding how delicious the brioche bun was. The burger itself, though, was passable. 
Score: 7

Ava:
 There’s no place I’d rather spend $17 on a burger and fries. Buns, I am still mourning the Thai Sesame sauce, but this burger was so good I just might forgive you. As the name says, the bun was absolutely incredible. A good bun is truly a game-changer in the burger world. Buns, you’ve been there for me at all my highs and lows these past four years. Thank you for yet another perfect burger. 
Score: 10

Josh:
 Man, do I love Buns. This is a burger where no matter what you get on it, it works. I got a massive burger with what felt like every ingredient on their menu, and it was immaculate. The bun properly absorbs and holds the burger along with the massive amount of toppings piled on top. It’s a wonderful burger, complemented perfectly by the bun. 
Score: 10

Al’s Burger Shack 

Gabe:
 Perfection can’t just waste away on a tray. After one bite, you’ll feel obligated to scarf down its entirety. This is the quintessential burger experience in Chapel Hill. If you are craving an outstanding, yet straightforward medium-rare meal, but you don’t mind dropping a pretty penny, this is where you should come. 
Score: 10 

Ava:
 I knew from the second we embarked on our journey that my fellow burger enthusiasts were saving the best for last. The Al’s burger was the perfect end to the perfect day. My stomach wasn’t at its best by the time we made it to the third burger, but I couldn’t let that stop me from enjoying the best burger in Chapel Hill. There, I said it. Buns, you’re a close second. 
Score: 10

Josh:
 The final stop of the day was like the first sip of coffee in the morning. Al’s is the pinnacle of burgers in Chapel Hill — as my fellow burger enthusiasts have already iterated, the experience is like no other. When you walk up to the counter and the cashier asks, “How would you like your burger cooked?” you know you’re in for a treat. There are many things I would do for an Al’s burger, and dropping $15 is very low on the list — in fact, it should be the bare minimum for a burger of this caliber. It’s the best burger in Chapel Hill, from vibe to taste; the experience is perfection. 
Score: 11

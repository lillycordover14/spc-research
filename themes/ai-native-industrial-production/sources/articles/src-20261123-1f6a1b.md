Sadler Racks Up Big 12 Weekly Accolades Again
 November 23, 2026 02:52 PM 
 | 
Men's Basketball
Share:
 By: 
Big 12 Conference
IRVING, Texas 
-
 Houston's Dedan Thomas Jr. took home Player of the Week honors after guiding the Cougars to the Players Era 8 championship while West Virginia's 
Miles Sadler
 earned Freshman of the Week recognition for his performance at the same tournament in this week's Monster Energy Big 12 Men's Basketball weekly awards.


Along with Thomas and Sadler, Arizona's Caleb Holt and Ivan Kharchenkov also made the Big 12 Starting Five, as did Kansas' Tyran Stokes. The starting five recognizes strong performances throughout the Conference.


Thomas averaged 22.3 points, 4.7 assists and 1.7 rebounds on the week en route to being named the Players Era 8 Most Valuable Player. He helped Houston to wins over Rutgers, then-No. 1 Florida and then-No. 11 Kansas, making the Cougars the fifth team in Division I history to defeat the No. 1 team in the country and beat a ranked opponent the next day. Thomas joined Marquette's Dwyane Wade as the only players in the last 30 years to record 120 points, 30 assists and 10 steals in their first six games with a team while achieving a 6-0 record.


Sadler also put up big numbers at the Players Era 8, earning him his second Big 12 Freshman of the Week nod of the season. Sadler averaged 23.3 points per game in Las Vegas, including a pair of 18-point performances against SEC foes Auburn and then-No. 1 Florida, and a 34-point outing against then-No. 11 Kansas.


Holt led Arizona to a win over then-No. 3 UConn with 19 points, six rebounds and three steals. In the Wildcats' fifth straight win over a top five opponent, Kharchenkov recorded his third 20-point game of the season, finishing with 20 points, seven assists and five rebounds. Stokes recorded a pair of double-doubles at the Players Era 8 while leading Kansas to the championship game, where he scored 22 points to go with 11 rebounds.

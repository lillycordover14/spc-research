[page text]
Eric Engels joins the FAN Hockey Show to discuss the Dylan Larkin trade to the Montreal Canadiens.

One day after acquiring him, the 
Montreal Canadiens
 will have new centre 
Dylan Larkin
 in the lineup.

Larkin will suit up for a team other than the Detroit Red Wings for the first time in his NHL career Monday night as he makes his Canadiens debut against the 
Los Angeles Kings
, the team 
announced
. The game can be seen exclusively on Sportsnet+ as part of Monday Night Hockey, with coverage beginning at 7 p.m. ET.

After 11-plus seasons in Detroit, Larkin was traded to Montreal for a package that included forward Kirby Dach, goalie Sam Montembeault and top prospect Michael Hage and plenty of draft capital on Sunday.

Larkin was drafted by Detroit in 2014 with the 15th-overall pick. He requested a trade in the off-season and was finally granted his wish on Sunday, ending a months-long saga.

He 
will wear No. 89
 for the Canadiens.

In 10 games played with the Red Wings this season, the 30-year-old has two goals and six points.

The Phoenix Tailings rare earth plant will transform the critical mineral landscape of New York. The company recently announced plans for an unprecedented $375 million commercial processing facility in Rochester. This ambitious project will cover a massive 500,000 square foot area and eventually process 18,000 tons of rare earth feedstock per year. The Eastman Business Park was already ready for this moment, having been prepared for decades by existing industrial infrastructure.

The move secured this strategic position, effectively marking the start of a massive push to diminish China’s control of critical minerals. This massive project fits in well with the federal government’s push to rapidly advance the manufacturing capabilities of the United States. Another is the $494M 
Louisiana rare earth separation facility
 which is officially advancing into the execution phase. 
Aclara Resources
 recently finished key basic engineering work on this massive infrastructure megaproject. In particular, the “Project Dynamo” plant will be actively working with heavy rare earths directly from South America. 

Processing at the Phoenix Tailings Rare Earth Plant

The industrial renaissance of this scale demands an incredible amount of specialized chemical engineering execution. The recently released plans indicate that the Phoenix Tailings rare earth plant will be able to separate more than 4,400 tons of oxides per year at full capacity. For comparison, this is a huge amount of output, which is a significant boost to the weak U.S. midstream rare earth supply chain.

The company will manage this huge volume with the use of very sophisticated recycling and separation technologies. The developer is working closely with New York State officials to ensure a smooth and efficient production process. In addition, crews will start detailed site preparation work right away, with the aim of starting physical production ramp-ups in 2027.

Workforce Development and State Incentives

In addition to the huge processing requirements, commercial rare earth plants have historically required large, highly trained regional workforces. But Rochester officials are optimistic that this project will offer much-needed, sustainable economic opportunities. Governor
 Kathy Hochul
 said that state partnership ensures permanent employment and a significantly larger regional tax base.

Luckily, the site’s strategic location provides the best access to existing engineering, manufacturing, and chemical processing skills. Developers can access this untapped industrial labor force, which will help sustain regional economic growth. To further reduce financial risks, New York State officially pledged $3 million in performance-based tax credits for the massive development.

While Phoenix Tailings is working to strengthen America’s domestic rare-earth processing capacity, another approach is emerging in Dothan,
 Alabama. Niron Magnetics plans to invest $1.8 billion in a factory that would manufacture permanent magnets using iron and nitrogen
, eliminating the need for rare-earth elements. The distinction is important: Phoenix Tailings aims to rebuild a domestic supply of materials that China largely dominates, while Niron is betting that manufacturers can reduce their dependence on those materials altogether. Both projects reflect the same national ambition, but their commercial success will depend on whether they can turn that ambition into reliable, competitive industrial production.

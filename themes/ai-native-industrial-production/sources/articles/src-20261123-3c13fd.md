Geno Smith
 completed a dozen-plus consecutive passes as the Jets put together four-straight time-consuming drives, which included 3 TD passes and a field goal, in Sunday's 27-24 victory over the Los Angeles Chargers at SoFi Stadium as the Green & White snapped a 5-game losing streak.
An unruffled Smith was 17-of-21 passing for 176 yards (8 yards per completion) and a 141.2 rating as the Jets rolled up 341 net total yards, converted 9 of 11 (82.8%, a franchise record) on third down and had 5 scores on 6 possessions.
"I think it started up front," Smith said. "I thought the offensive line did a tremendous job in not only the run game, but the pass game as well."

Garrett Wilson

"I just want to win games," Smith said. "The QB, he's got to play well, and I've got to be efficient, we've got to be efficient. You got to limit the turnovers and make explosive plays, and so I just try and do my job and get us the victory. I feel like I can play a lot better ... I feel like I can play a whole lot better. So I'm focusing on pushing myself to get better every day, as well as the guys around me."
The Jets and Chargers exchanged first-quarter field goals before Smith led the Jets on a 13-play, 70-yard drive that melted 6:48 off the clock and was the Jets' longest drive so far this season. On the drive, Smith completed passes to Wilson (8 yards), Mitchell (9), rookie TE

HC Aaron Glenn said about Smith, who played a superlative game after sustaining an ankle injury last week that sidelined him for most of the second half against the Bills.
Speaking about the speculation about his availability to play against the Chargers, Smith said: "I always felt I was going to play. I put a ton of hours in to make sure I was ready to play. But today I didn't get touched."

Essex Three-Twelve, LLC
, a Chicago-based multifamily investment sales brokerage, announced the successful sale of
 1428–1430 N. Wood St.
, a three-unit multifamily property in Chicago’s Wicker Park neighborhood. The property closed for $1,525,000, with Essex Three-Twelve Director Garrett Leigh representing the seller and Essex Realty Group Principal Matt Feo representing the buyer.

Located just off Milwaukee Avenue in a desirable residential pocket of Wicker Park, the property offered a compelling development opportunity due to its RM-5 zoning designation. The seller had previously completed architectural plans by Hanna Architects for a proposed six-unit, center-entrance apartment building.

The offering generated significant interest from prospective buyers, resulting in multiple offers above the asking price without financing contingencies. The competitive bidding reflected the property’s location, favorable zoning, and potential for residential redevelopment in one of Chicago’s established North Side neighborhoods.

The winning bidder was an experienced developer already pursuing another project two doors down from the property. The acquisition provides an opportunity to expand the developer’s presence in the immediate area while capitalizing on the site’s redevelopment potential.

“1430 N. Wood St. was a unique land site with favorable zoning – just off Milwaukee Ave in Wicker Park,” said Garrett Leigh. “The lack of for sale inventory continues to drive pricing for these sites with four very qualified builders competing for the opportunity. The ultimate buyer had a great reputation for performing, put forth an offer with no financing contingency, and is currently delivering similar projects on the same street. Grateful to Matt Feo at Essex Realty Group for bringing them through.”

The transaction also highlights collaboration between Essex Three-Twelve and Essex Realty Group, two affiliated brokerages specializing in different segments of the multifamily investment market. By connecting a qualified developer with a well-positioned opportunity, the firms successfully facilitated a transaction that aligned with both the seller’s objectives and the buyer’s development strategy.

The sale demonstrates continued interest in multifamily properties with redevelopment potential, particularly in established Chicago neighborhoods where zoning, location, and existing development plans can influence buyer demand.

Essex Three-Twelve specializes in the acquisition and disposition of 3–12 unit multifamily investment properties throughout Chicago, advising buyers and sellers on property valuation, marketing strategies, and investment opportunities.

Ilia Malinin bids for a ninth consecutive Grand Prix win, and a fifth consecutive trip to December’s Grand Prix Final, at NHK Trophy, live on 
Peacock
 from Thursday through Saturday.
Malinin, who won Saatva Skate America two weeks ago in his season debut, will qualify for the six-skater Grand Prix Final if he finishes anywhere on the podium at NHK in Tokyo.
The Final is the second-most prestigious annual figure skating event after March’s World Championships. It is also the most exclusive, taking the top six per discipline from the six-event Grand Prix regular season, which concludes with NHK Trophy.

The NHK men’s field is deep. It also includes France’s Kevin Aymoz, who won his previous Grand Prix start at Skate Canada. 
Plus three men who finished runner-up in their Grand Prix openers -- Vladislav Dikidzhi from Russia (who owns the world’s top score this season), Italian Daniel Grassl and Japan’s Rio Nakata.
All are still in the running for Grand Prix Final spots.
Also at NHK, two-time world medalist Mone Chiba bids to give Japan a sweep of the six Grand Prix women’s regular season titles, a feat accomplished once before by Russians in 2019. 
Chiba, who won Cup of China, will also qualify for the Grand Prix Final if she finishes in the top five, which would knock out American Isabeau Levito, who is on the bubble after placing third in her two Grand Prix starts.
Americans Emilea Zingas and Vadym Kolesnik headline the ice dance field, two weeks after earning their first Grand Prix win at Skate America. They will qualify for the Grand Prix Final with a top-four finish at NHK.
In pairs, world champions Minerva Hase and Nikita Volodin of Germany and Japan’s Yuna Nagaoka and Sumitada Moriguchi look to knock Americans Katie McBeath and Balazs Nagy out of the projected Final field.
McBeath and Nagy placed third and fourth in their two Grand Prix events and sit fifth in the current standings. Hase and Volodin will pass them with a top-six finish. Nagaoka and Moriguchi will pass them with a top-three finish.

Introducing the 4v4 multiplayer mod Twelve Weeks for The Last of Us Part II Remastered

2026-11-23 05:55
,
            by Thibaut
        

As is well known, 
The Last of Us Part II
 was supposed to have a multiplayer mode. Naughty Dog had ultimately decided to turn it into a standalone game, before it was eventually canceled. So now, it’s up to the players to shape this experience.

Today we’re introducing Twelve Weeks, a free multiplayer mod for PC aimed at owners of 
The Last of Us Part II Remastered
. It lets you relive the 2013 multiplayer formula with 4v4 battles and keep your clan alive for twelve weeks.

Twelve Weeks has been in development since September 2026. The AI tool Claude was used for most of the project. The developers note that the story may repeat itself or contradict itself at times. The mod is 
available for download now
.

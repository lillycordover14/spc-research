Director Nicolas Zapata Zuluaga of Grupo Cibest S.A. 
(NYSE:CIB)
 filed a Form 4 on November 23, 2026, disclosing the acquisition of 500.31 derivative units in the company's Director Voluntary Pension Fund on November 19, 2026.

Key Highlights

Reporting Person:
 Nicolas Zapata Zuluaga, identified as a Director of Grupo Cibest S.A.

Transaction:
 Acquisition (Code A) of 500.31 Units in the Grupo Cibest Equity Securities Fund on November 19, 2026.

Unit Price:
 COP 28,702.305 per unit, equal to approximately $8.63 per unit using a conversion rate of COP 3,326.96 per $1, according to the filing.

Beneficial Ownership Following Transaction:
 7,372.7795 units held indirectly.

Underlying Securities:
 The fund invests primarily in Grupo Cibest common and preferred shares, together with a small amount of cash.

No Voting or Investment Discretion:
 According to the filing, the reporting person does not have voting or investment discretion with respect to the assets held by the fund.

Director Pension Fund Unit Acquisition at Grupo Cibest

According to the Form 4 filed on November 23, 2026, Nicolas Zapata Zuluaga, a director of Grupo Cibest S.A. 
(NYSE:CIB)
, reported the acquisition of 500.31 units in the Grupo Cibest Equity Securities Fund, an institutional voluntary pension fund sponsored by the issuer and administered by an independent third-party manager. The transaction date was November 19, 2026. The filing was signed by Maria Fernanda Valencia Tafur as Attorney-in-Fact for Nicolas Zapata Zuluaga.

The filing disclosed that the units "were credited pursuant to a voluntary cash contribution to the fund" and are not purchased at a fixed or negotiated price. The units are payable solely in cash based on the value of the fund on the date of withdrawal. The filing further noted that "the number of Grupo Cibest shares economically attributable to the units cannot be determined until the date of withdrawal." The unit price on November 19, 2026 was COP 28,702.305, equal to approximately $8.63 per unit at a conversion rate of COP 3,326.96 per $1.

Following the reported transaction, the reporting person's indirect beneficial ownership stands at 7,372.7795 units. The filing noted that the instrument has no expiration date, and that ownership is held indirectly, with the nature described as Director Voluntary Pension Fund Units. The reporting address listed for Zapata Zuluaga is Cra. 48 # 26-85, Medellin, Colombia.

What Was Disclosed

The filing disclosed a derivative security acquisition by director Nicolas Zapata Zuluaga of 500.31 pension fund units in Grupo Cibest S.A. at approximately $8.63 per unit on November 19, 2026, resulting in indirect beneficial ownership of 7,372.7795 total units.

The Last of Us Part 2 lands 'Twelve Weeks' multiplayer mode after previous attempt shut down
The Last of Us Factions has returned with this incredible new mod for Part 2.
Olly Smith
Follow us on Google Discover
Months after Sony shut down a 
The Last of Us Part 2
 unofficial multiplayer mod, efforts have been revived thanks to a new fan project titled Twelve Weeks.
Back in September, we reported that 
PlayStation had issued a cease and desist
 for Speclizer's multiplayer mod that had been in the works since January.
"Hey everyone, as you all know, the 
TLOU 2 
Multiplayer mod that has been in development since January was supposed to release this month. Unfortunately, I recently received a letter on behalf of Sony Interactive Entertainment requesting for the mod not to be released," the modder posted at the time.
Although Speclizer's multiplayer mod was not to be, just like a hydra, if you sever one head, several more will appear in its place. Soon after this, another multiplayer mod appeared which was 
aimed at putting co-op into the No Return mode
. And now, we have Twelve Weeks.

What is the Twelve Weeks mod for 
The Last of Us Part 2
?
Available via 
GitHub
, Twelve Weeks is a multiplayer mod for 
The Last of Us Part 2 Remastered
 on PC.
It aims to bring back a Factions-style multiplayer mode for the game similar to what was available in the original game. As always, you'll need a legit copy of 
The Last of Us Part 2 Remastered
 on Steam. The mod also features Steam Deck support, if you're playing on the go.
In it, you form a clan and must survive 12 weeks against rival factions, where you fight together, gear up between rounds, craft, revive, and execute.
The mod has been in development since September, as wccftech 
reports
, indicating that it was likely the news that Speclizer's mod being shut down that spurred this.
Here's a trailer showing off the mod in action, teasing out some of the features available as part of the new multiplayer modes.
At the moment, there are two game modes: Supply Raid and Interrogation.

Supply Raid tasks you with bleeding out the enemy team's reinforcement count, making it a simple deathmatch mode. Meanwhile, Interrogation is all about robbing a safe, where you must interrogate downed enemies to find the location of it.
In terms of maps, there are 12 imported from the campaign in 
The Last of Us Part 2
. These maps seem to be a little bigger than the ones featured in No Return, as they now need to accommodate 10 players. The project's leads also say that plans to import maps from the first game are also in the works.
At the moment, Twelve Weeks has two servers available, one in Europe and another in North America, and this may expand as more people play the mod. Options for private matches and private servers are coming later as well, with options already available to start doing this on the GitHub repository.

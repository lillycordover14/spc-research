By R.L. Bynum

Tez Walker hadn’t returned a kickoff in an NFL game before Sunday. By halftime in his hometown, he had one of the most memorable plays of Baltimore’s season.

That came on a day that Javonte Williams scored twice and had a big day as a blocker, Omarion Hampton had his longest run of the season. 

Meanwhile, 
Patriots coach Mike Vrabel gave an update on Drake Maye
, saying he “wouldn’t imagine” Maye being available Sunday when New England plays the Los Angeles Chargers on the road as Maye recovers from a fractured right fibula. 
Vrabel said Monday 
that Maye isn’t going on injured reserve, will remain on the active roster but called the situation “fluid.”

Walker, a Charlotte native, took a second-quarter kickoff 86 yards for a touchdown at Bank of America Stadium. His return put the Ravens ahead 19–14 moments after Chuba Hubbard’s touchdown had given Carolina the lead. It was Walker’s third career kickoff return and the Ravens’ first kickoff-return touchdown since September 2022.

He returned the opening kickoff 21 yards, muffed the next but recovered it and gained 8, then broke off the 86-yard touchdown on his third return, and added a 34-yard catch on his only target. Pro Football Focus gave him season highs of 90.6 overall on offense, 83.0 as a receiver and 85.2 as a run blocker.

The celebration didn’t last, though. Baltimore committed four turnovers, Ryan Fitzgerald kicked a 37-yard field goal with 1:44 left, and the Panthers won 35–33.

Williams scores twice in Dallas rout

Williams scored on the ground and through the air in the Cowboys’ 51–30 win over Tennessee, finishing with 18 carries for a team-high 67 yards and three catches for 24 yards.

He capped a 14-play, 73-yard opening drive with a 1-yard touchdown run, carrying seven times for 18 yards on the march. In the third quarter, he caught an 11-yard touchdown pass from Dak Prescott to make it 34–16. He now has 12 touchdowns this season (10 rushing, 2 receiving).

Coach Brian Schottenheimer singled out Williams’ pass protection, which helped keep Prescott from being sacked all day.
https://twitter.com/NFL/status/2124567717592129914/video/1

“Javonte Williams was incredible just in blitz pickup,” 
Schottenheimer said
 of Williams, whose 77.0 PFF pass-block grade was his highest since a 79.8 in Week 1 against the Giants.

Prescott praised his running back’s patience.

“Just super, super smart and understanding where the play’s supposed to hit,” Prescott said. “And he’s just looking for a small hole and does a good job of riding the wave of his blockers.”

Williams said he takes pride in every part of the job.

“Just do whatever I gotta do to help the team win, catch passes, run the ball, and protect,” he said. “I just try to make sure every part of my game is flawless.”

Hampton breaks season-long run

Hampton ran 14 times for 89 yards (6.4 average) and caught one pass for 4 yards in the Chargers’ 27–24 home loss to the New York Jets. His 36-yard burst to open the fourth quarter, the longest run of his season, set up a Ladd McConkey touchdown that cut the deficit to 20–17.

Hampton has 254 rushing yards over his last three games and credited his blockers.

“O-Line has done an amazing job the past few weeks,” 
he said
. “I’m grateful they’re opening up those holes for me.” The Chargers (1–9) host New England at 4:05 p.m. Sunday on CBS.

Downs, Ezeudu help Chiefs roll

Josh Downs caught four passes on five targets for 59 yards, including a 26-yarder, in Kansas City’s 34–7 win over Arizona, his second game since the trade from Indianapolis. He played 17 offensive snaps as the Chiefs rested their starters with about 10 minutes left.

Joshua Ezeudu started at right tackle for the second straight week in place of the injured Jaylon Moore and played all 72 offensive snaps. The Chiefs (8–2) play at Buffalo at 8:20 p.m. Thursday on NBC.

Exeter coach Ross McMillan said Dallas McLeod showed his quality as he scored two tries on his Chiefs debut.
The 27-year-old, who has won one cap for New Zealand, starred in 
Exeter's 59-12 win over Gloucester in the Prem Rugby Cup.
The nine-try victory ended a run of one win in eight matches as summer signing McLeod finally made his debut after arriving at the start of the month having helped Canterbury win New Zealand's National Provincial Championship.
"His quality was there wasn't it," forwards coach McMillan told BBC Radio Devon.
"His has an ability to bring other people in the game and one of his offloads is actually how Charlie [Poynton] ended up scoring one of his tries.
"Dallas' ability is unquestionable and for us bringing a player of that quality but he's a good guy as well.
"He's been in the building a matter of days and for us to actually see that quality that we saw in his time in the southern hemisphere, it's always a tricky transition for some of those guys and he's absolutely taken it like a duck to the water.
"That's pleasing for us and it should be pleasing for the fans as well."

Having lost four of their first five Prem matches and been beaten at Toulouse in the European Champions Cup, Exeter had come into the game having lost their previous two cup matches. 
But a Chiefs side that was bolstered by returning Italy lock Andrea Zambonin and a hat-trick for new signing Carwyn Tuipulotu returned to winning ways in style as they secured a bonus point after 26 minutes.
"I'm really, really happy for the players and I'm also happy for the fans," added McMillan.
"I know it's not always been an easy watch, but the reality is we've actually been in a lot of close games.
"For us there's a little bit of a difference between the kind of the players that are on the field, but the pressure was on us and I'm happy for the guys that they got their win.
"The changing room is certainly different now than it has been for the last few weeks."

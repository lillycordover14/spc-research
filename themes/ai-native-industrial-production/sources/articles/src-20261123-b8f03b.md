Part 2 in a series on premium beverage experiences across Delaware North Sportservice

As part of the extensive renovation and elevated hospitality experience at the Delaware North-owned-and-operated TD Garden in Boston, the Delaware North Sportservice team has developed a new beverage program for Suite X, designed with the “suite of the future” in mind. The goal was a curated cocktail experience that feels premium and on trend, while letting guests preorder their selections.

The initiative is part of a broader look at premium beverage experiences across Sportservice, led by Nikki Hueckel, director of beverage programs, in partnership with Philip Potthoff, vice president of operations and strategic partnerships for Sportservice, and the team at TD Garden.

“Today’s guests are looking for beverages that are experience-driven and inherently shareable,” Hueckel said. “Especially in premium hospitality, guests want something that feels unique to where they are and gives them a reason to capture and share the moment. Presentation, personalization and surprise all become part of the experience.”

The program at Suite X includes freezer-door cocktails featuring an Old Fashioned and the Garden Drop, the venue’s unique take on the Lemon Drop. Both are served ice cold and poured over a large-format ice cube for a simple but elevated presentation. It also features custom, house-made canned cocktails, including a Mai Tai and the Boozy Cold Brew, inspired by the espresso martini. Each batch is produced and canned to order with custom labeling, adding another personalized touch to the Suite X experience.

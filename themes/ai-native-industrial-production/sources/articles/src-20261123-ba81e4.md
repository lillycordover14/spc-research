Franklin Templeton Names Former BlackRock Senior Figure As New Latin America Head&nbsp;&nbsp;Family Wealth Report

[page text]

Request Reprint Rights

The senior figure has joined the US firm after spending two decades in senior positions at BlackRock.	  	  	  

Franklin
  Templeton
 has appointed former 
BlackRock
 senior figure
  Nicolas Gomez as its head of Latin America.


  Gomez will report to Matthew Harrison, head of EMEA & Americas
  (ex-US) at Franklin Templeton. Gomez will take over
  leadership of Franklin Templeton’s Latin America business from
  Javier Villegas. a spokesperson told this publication when asked
  about the matter.


  Villegas has been appointed to lead a newly created European
  sub-region, as part of Franklin Templeton’s strategy to
  accelerate growth across Europe. His remit covers Belgium,
  Central and Eastern Europe, France, Iberia, Luxembourg, the
  Netherlands and Switzerland. He will continue to lead Latin
  America until Gomez joins, ensuring a smooth transition. Upon
  Gomez’s arrival, Marc Forster, Sergio Guerrien, Hugo Petricioli
  and Marcus Vinicius will report directly to him.


  Franklin Templeton said the appointment will build on its
  established presence in Latin America, where the firm has more
  than 130 professionals covering the region with a presence in
  Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Mexico, Uruguay, and the US. 


  At BlackRock, Gomez spent two decades in senior leadership roles
  spanning Latin America, exchange-traded funds, institutional and
  wealth distribution, and product innovation. Most recently, he
  served as head of iShares Americas Institutional Business & Latin
  America. Earlier in his career, he led BlackRock’s ETF and index
  business in Mexico. He has also worked for Merrill Lynch and
  holds an MBA in finance from the University of California,
  Berkeley's Haas School of Business and a bachelor’s degree in
  industrial engineering and operations research from the
  University of California, Berkeley.

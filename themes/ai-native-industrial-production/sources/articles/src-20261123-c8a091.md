Beaten-down critical minerals stocks are clawing back part of last week's losses as a sweeping market rally lifts high-beta names. 
USA Rare Earth
 (
NASDAQ:USAR
) stock is at $7.09, up 13% in afternoon trading. Despite that jump, USA Rare Earth stock is still down 40% year to date (YTD).
 
 
 
 
Maksim Safaniuk / iStock via Getty Images
 
 
 
Rare earth names swung on headlines tied to U.S.-China rare earth talks last week, and the group sank deeper into the red on November 16. An analyst downgrade over feedstock issues hit USA Rare Earth during that stretch. Shares of 
Critical Metals
 (
NASDAQ:CRML
), a pre-revenue developer, set a new 52-week low.
 
 
 
 
 
Broad Market Rally Powers the Rare Earth Rebound
 
 
A broad relief rally on November 23 is driving the rebound in USA Rare Earth and its peers. Sentiment improved after Jensen Huang's return as CEO of 
NVIDIA
 (
NASDAQ:NVDA
), following last week's artificial intelligence-driven selloff. Major indexes are climbing, with the S&P 500 up 5% and the NASDAQ 100 up 7%.
 
 
Rare earth stocks tend to move sharply in both directions, and the volatility that caused last week's slide is magnifying the bounce as USA Rare Earth's gain runs ahead of both index moves, a common pattern for high-beta names when risk appetite returns. That ties the move directly to broader market mood.

Macro data could test the bounce for USA Rare Earth and the rest of the group in a holiday-shortened week. S&P Global's flash Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI) reading is on the docket, and the latest gauge of consumer prices, the PCE report, arrives November 25. U.S. markets close November 26 for Thanksgiving.
 
 
How United States Antimony and MP Materials Compare
 
 
United States Antimony
 (
NYSE:UAMY
) stock is at $2.42, up 9%. That gain trails USA Rare Earth's move while still topping the broad indexes. On a YTD basis, United States Antimony stock is down 52%, the biggest decline among the three featured names.
 
 
Lower antimony prices have weighed on United States Antimony's revenue this year, even as the company sold more pounds of the metal. A $245 million sole-source Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) contract gives the company a direct line to military demand.
 
 
 
 
 
MP Materials
 (
NYSE:MP
) stock is at $35.98, up 6%. Carrying a market value of $6.41 billion, MP Materials is the largest name in the group and posted the smallest gain of the three. For the year, MP Materials stock is down 29%.
 
 
What Sets USA Rare Earth's Business Apart
 
 
USA Rare Earth is building a mine-to-magnet chain that spans the U.S., Brazil and the U.K. The company pairs the Serra Verde heavy rare earth mine in Brazil and the Round Top deposit in Texas with metals and alloy production at Less Common Metals and a magnet plant in Stillwater, Oklahoma. That footprint is the broadest in the group, though much of it is still ramping up.
 
 
MP Materials runs a more mature operation, mining and separating rare earths at Mountain Pass in California and making magnets in Fort Worth, Texas, while United States Antimony sells antimony and zeolite and doesn't compete directly in magnets. Critical Metals remains a pre-revenue developer whose flagship Tanbreez project in Greenland is years from production, and the company has disclosed substantial going concern doubt.
 
 
 
 
 
Can the Critical Minerals Rebound Hold?
 
 
The next test for USA Rare Earth is whether the rebound can hold without a fresh policy catalyst, given how quickly the group responds to U.S.-China trade headlines. Even after the bounce, USA Rare Earth, United States Antimony, MP Materials and Critical Metals all trade below their levels of a week ago. CRML stock is up 4% to $3.13 in afternoon trading.
 
 
USA Rare Earth gives the bulls something to work with. The company has $1.09 billion in cash and equivalents and an expectation of at least 325 tonnes of Serra Verde sales, measured in total rare earth oxide (TREO), in Q4 2026. Washington's backing, including a $750 million Department of War investment in the company's purchasing vehicle, adds another layer of support.
 
 
The bears focus on USA Rare Earth's Q3 2026 results, disclosed November 9, which showed revenue of $3 million and an adjusted net loss that grew to $125.64 million. MP Materials Chief Operating Officer Michael Rosenthal offered a note of caution on his company's November 5 call. He said "we do not believe today's elevated prices will persist indefinitely" for several medium and heavy rare earth products.
 
 
 
 
 
A calm stretch of trade headlines could let USA Rare Earth extend its bounce, while a setback in U.S.-China talks could send the whole group lower again. Investors researching the group may want to track the U.S.-China rare earth talks closely as they develop.

Winners, Losers, and Honorable Mentions from an embarrassing UNC loss to Virginia
Easily their poorest effort of the year.
by
 
 
Al Hood
Nov 23, 2026, 6:00 PM UTC
In season where UNC fans felt like they had been in every game, Saturday’s result in Charlottesville was nothing short of, like the title says, embarrassing. The Tar Heels played like they hadn’t taken preparation for the Cavaliers seriously, and after the promise of a 5-4 record following the UConn win, the Tar Heels are dangerously close to needing a special dispensation to play in a bowl game with a three game losing streak to end the season.
There was a little good worth pointing out but a lot of bad. Let’s dive in, shall we?
Winners
Travis Burgess-
We could play the game of putting him as both a winner and loser, but look, the freshman getting his first start showed out and really wasn’t the reason why Carolina lost. 25-45, 354 yards with a long pass of 46 yards, and was also the leading rusher with 106 net yards. It’s enough to make fans wonder “what exactly were they waiting for to play him?” If this staff is truly about the development, then letting the young Burgess get some of his mistakes over with early would have led to a more seasoned player by this point that could have really lit up in the Petrino offense. Based on the recruiting it’s pretty clear that Burgess is seen as next year’s starter at least. Yes the two interceptions were killer, but it would have been nice if he had been able to get them out earlier in the season.
Jordan Shipp-
You think he was happy to have a quarterback that has a livelier arm? No touchdown catches but nine catches for an astounding 149 yards. On a day where Carolina lost Jelani Thurman early they needed their other pass catching star to step up and he did. At this point what else can you say about him except if he is back in a UNC uniform next year along with Burgess, that could be a very fun combo.
Mason Humphrey-
The transfer from Lehigh hadn’t exactly put up the stats this season as Shipp, Thurman, and the running backs had mostly been the ones to take the bulk of the work from Billy Edwards, Jr. Somehow, though, he just magically appeared to haul in three catches for 95 yards and show why he was brought in from the FCS school. Here’s hoping against the Wolfpack next week he can have a repeat performance.
Losers
UNC Secondary-
Beau Pribula just had it too easy on Saturday. He completed 79% of his passes, didn’t get picked off, and had four touchdown passes. They were also eaten alive by Jekail Middlebrook. Middlebrook chewed up the backfield for 140 yards including the absolute backbreaker 75-yard touchdown catch after the Tar Heels finally put together a decent drive. As the season has gone on it’s become apparent just how weak the defensive backfield is for the Tar Heels, and against a NC State team looking to make their mark for a new coach, things aren’t exactly looking bright.
UNC Running Backs-
At first blush the per rush numbers for Benjamin Hall look decent as he averaged eight yards a carry, but that also includes a game-long of 29 yards. Take that out and he only averaged 4.5 yards a carry and just added 27 yards to the game’s total. Jalen McGill only added 39 yards to the total, and Demon June had a ridiculous 1.3 yards a carry average. The Tar Heels needed the runners to take some pressure off the rest of the offense and they just couldn’t fully rise to the job. They have one more chance this season to put up the numbers, especially if Burgess gets one more start.
Coaching-
We as fans truly thought we were past all this. It’s one thing to lose like they did against Syracuse, Louisville, Miami, or Notre Dame where they were one or two plays away and you can point to them being in those games. From the jump it just didn’t feel like the Tar Heels had even spent one second preparing for this Virginia team. Sure the offense ultimately put up some good numbers, but by the time they really did Virginia had seized control. They also need to answer for why Burgess hadn’t gotten more run, because it really did seem like he was someone perfect for the Petrino offense, and having a young quarterback to watch is something you can sell the fanbase. Instead we as fans had to watch a sixth year player with severe limitations. But hey, it’s why they are paying Belichick $10 million a year, right?
Honorable Mentions
Rece Verhoff
 seems more and more like a bit of a waste of lawyer’s fees to be eligible. The staff had clearly pinned their hopes to keep him eligible, but he’s been struggling making his kicks including Saturday. He only went one for three with a block and miss from 36 yards. For someone who prides himself in special teams this was a miss…
Xavier Lewis
 continued his great season with a team-high ten tackles and shared a sack to keep piling up the numbers…We all hold our breath for 
Jelani Thurman
 as he had a real chance at an award-winning season and he would be a huge weapon for Burgess if he could be on the field.
Carolina has one more chance to make a bowl game, all they have to do is beat their longtime rival NC State at home. It’ll be a high noon game against a team not just playing out the string as evidenced by their win against Florida State on Saturday, and this is also a squad that has planted their flag at midfield two games in a row. This time they can do it with real grass on the field!

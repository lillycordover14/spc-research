Jesus “Jesse” Zapata III Obituary
Jesus “Jesse” Zapata III passed away on November 23, 2026, at the age of 48.
 
Jesse was born on October 21, 1978, in Sinton, Texas, to Jesse and Virginia (Rivas) Zapata. He was a devoted husband, loving father, cherished grandfather, beloved son and brother, and a dear friend to many.
 
Jesse was a remarkable man with a kind heart and a genuine love for others. His family was the center of his world, and nothing brought him greater joy than being surrounded by the people he loved. He treasured his children and grandchildren, and the memories they shared will forever hold a special place in their hearts.
 
Jesse was known for his warm smile, friendly personality, and gentle, patient nature. He had a generous heart and was always willing to lend a helping hand to anyone in need, never expecting anything in return. His kindness and welcoming spirit made others feel comfortable and appreciated. Jesse was an employee of Aaron’s, where his friendly demeanor and willingness to help others were a reflection of the wonderful man he was.
 
Above all, Jesse will be remembered for the love he gave so freely, the warmth of his smile, and the beautiful heart that made him so special. His passing leaves an immeasurable void in the lives of those who knew and loved him. Although he will be deeply missed, his love, kindness, and cherished memories will continue to live on through his family and all those whose lives he touched.
 
Left to cherish his memory are his beloved wife, Melissa Zapata of Portland, Texas; ; his children, Lucas Castillo (Nicholas Trevino) and Michael Castillo, both of Portland, Texas, Eric Castillo of Rockport, Texas, Cassandra Castillo (Joseph Duvall) of Aransas Pass, Texas, and Joben Gonzales of Portland, Texas; and his precious grandchildren, Heaven Castillo, Karter Lang, Fredie Duvall, Daxton Duvall, Kratos Duvall, Jack Castillo, Greyson Castillo, Kason Castillo and Kennedy Castillo; his loving parents, Jesse and Virginia Zapata of Sinton, Texas his brother, Jamie Zapata of Sinton, Texas; his mother-in-law, Josephine Garcia of Sinton, Texas;
 
Visitation will be held on Sunday, November 29, 2026, at Resthaven Funeral Home Chapel in Sinton, Texas, with the family receiving guests from 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Recitation of the Holy Rosary will begin at 6:00 PM. The Funeral Mass will be celebrated on Monday, November 30, 2026, at 10:00 AM at Sacred Heart Catholic Church in Sinton, Texas. Interment will follow at 11:00 AM at Eternal Rest Cemetery in Sinton, Texas.
 
Guestbook available online at www.resthavenfunerals.com.
 
Arrangements and care entrusted to Resthaven Funeral Home, Sinton.

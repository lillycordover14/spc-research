Boston Dynamics and GlobalFoundries Expand Robotics Collaboration Across Fabs&nbsp;&nbsp;Bisinfotech

[page text]
Source: Boston Dynamics

Boston Dynamics, a global leader in mobile robotics, and GlobalFoundries (GF), a premier manufacturer of essential semiconductors, have announced a major expansion of their strategic collaboration to deploy Spot® quadruped robots for autonomous enterprise asset management across GF’s global semiconductor fabrication facilities. Furthermore, GF will become the first customer outside the automotive sector to begin testing Boston Dynamics’ Atlas™ humanoid robot through its early adopter program and skills development workshop—marking the first initiative of its kind within the semiconductor manufacturing industry.

Under the expanded agreement, Spot deployments will scale across GlobalFoundries’ 
manufacturing
 sites in Malta, New York; Burlington, Vermont; Dresden, Germany; and Singapore. Building on a partnership that began more than four years ago at GF’s Burlington facility, the program recently demonstrated significant operational impact in Singapore. Since April, two Spot robots deployed in Singapore’s sub-fab areas have traveled nearly 900 kilometers and completed over 90,000 inspections with a 99% mission success rate. Utilizing Boston Dynamics’ latest hardware and software updates,GF also redeployed 875 routine inspections within just two weeks and maintains an immediate roadmap to more than double that figure using Spot’s advanced visual inspection capabilities.

“Spot has helped us shift from reactive to predictive maintenance, collecting data across thousands of inspection points to prevent downtime and keep our fabs running more efficiently,” said 
Pradip Singh
, chief manufacturing officer at 
GlobalFoundries
. “Scaling Spot and testing Atlas are the next steps in our journey to AI-enabled manufacturing, putting physical AI to work across our operations worldwide.”

Beginning in early 2027, Boston Dynamics and GlobalFoundries will initiate onsite testing of the Atlas humanoid robot at GF’s facilities. The accompanying skills development workshop is designed to evaluate potential 
humanoid
 use cases, assess facility readiness and integration requirements, and conduct early training for specific task execution—complementing GlobalFoundries’ broader global initiative to upskill its workforce in artificial intelligence.

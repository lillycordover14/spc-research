Twelve dogs rescued from alleged hoarding situation near Odin
Marion County Animal Control removed 12 dogs from a property near Odin after a tip alleged unsanitary conditions and inadequate food and water. The owners surrendered the animals, officials said.
By JO ALLEN-HONGSERMEIER jallen@morningsentinel.com
RESCUE — Twelve dogs were surrendered to Marion County Animal Control over the weekend after the department was given a tip by a concerned citizen about the dogs' living conditions. Photos of those living conditions were made public on social media.
Submitted Photo
MARION COUNTY — Marion County Animal Control removed 12 dogs from a property north of Odin following an anonymous tip regarding poor living conditions, according to Animal Control Administrator Whitney Purcell.
The case remains under investigation, but Purcell said animal control officers responded after a concerned citizen reported possible neglect at the residence.
"We pulled 12 dogs from the residence," Purcell told the 
Sentinel
. "It was just very unsanitary living conditions inside, and the dogs outside were in small wire crates with no food or water."
According to Purcell, the dogs' owners voluntarily surrendered the animals to Marion County Animal Control and they are currently being evaluated and cared for by shelter staff. Purcell said the animals are expected to be made available for adoption in the near future.
Animal control officials have not released additional details about the investigation at this time. This is a developing story.

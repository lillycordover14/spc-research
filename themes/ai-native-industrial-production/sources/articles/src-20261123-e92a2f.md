The UNC Sports Week ahead: November 23rd-29th
The holiday brings a quieting of games, but the importance ramps up.
by
 
 
Al Hood
Nov 23, 2026, 8:00 PM UTC
Now you know we’ve hit both the postseason of a lot of sports and the end of the fall sports season when you look at a calendar that used to be full of options for fans and now is very barren. Now, of course the fact that this week includes the Thanksgiving holiday may have a big part to do with it, too, but compared to previous weeks where we had so many sports to have to sort through, it’s pretty easy to figure out where things stand now.
So, if your holiday plans also include enjoying some time with UNC sports—maybe you need an escape from your family—here’s a look at the week ahead so you can catch the Tar Heels in action.
UNC Football
Saturday, November 28th — vs. NC State, Chapel Hill, NC — Noon, ACC Network
The regular season for the Tar Heels comes to an end with the traditional rival from Raleigh coming to town. It’s arguably one of the weirdest games in this series as one squad is playing with a temporary coach as they’ve already hired their coach for next year, while the other is stumbling after some promising play with a coach that may or may not be back next year. On top of that, technically bowl eligibility is on the line but these days with the CFP and opt-outs, frankly, 5-7 also can get you into a game. Either way, we’ll see what Saturday brings. At least it’s an early start for once.
UNC Men’s Basketball
Monday, November 23rd — vs Monmouth, Chapel Hill, NC — 8 PM, ACC Network
Friday, November 27th — vs West Virginia, Charlotte, NC — 7 PM, ESPN
The Tar Heels leave Chapel Hill for the first time this regular season but only to the friendly confines of Charlotte. First they’ll face a familiar face in UNC alum King Rice who coaches Monmouth, and then it’s off to Charlotte for the Black Friday game against the Mountaineers. You’d be right to be concerned against West Virginia as they’ve been impressive thus far, and hung with top-ranked Florida for a while. It’s the first of a couple challenging games for Carolina as next week they’ll come back to face Arkansas.
UNC Women’s Basketball
Tuesday, November 24th — vs Gardner-Webb, Chapel Hill, NC — 7PM, ACCNX (Streaming only)
Friday, November 27th — vs Army, Chapel Hill, NC — 7 PM, ACCNX (Streaming only)
Sunday, November 29th — vs Brown, Chapel Hill, NC — 4 PM, ACC Network
Finally, the Tar Heels will come back to Chapel Hill. This era upon us where programs are looking for any chance at revenue they can take means that the women’s team has played in events in Paris, Charlotte, and Orlando while only having one in Carmichael Arena. That finally changes this week, and it does so coming off a very successful trip to Orlando where they got wins over Texas Tech and Alabama, with the Texas Tech game called by the same ESPN crew that saw them in Paris—ESPN’s A-team. The Tar Heels seem to be figuring out how to play a balanced game with the budding superstar in Kate Harpring, and now they have a chance to stack some wins before a huge game on December 2nd against LSU.
UNC Volleyball
Wednesday, November 25th — vs. Louisville, Chapel Hill, NC — 4 PM, ACCNX (Streaming only)
Friday, November 27th — at Miami, Coral Gables, FL — 1 PM, ACCNX (Streaming Only)
Sunday, November 29th — NCAA Tournament bracket reveal, 6 PM, ESPN
The regular season wraps up for the Tar Heels with a high-powered match against the team just ahead of them in the ACC standings, Louisville and then a trip to the Miami area to play the team just below them in the standings in the Hurricanes. As has been mentioned several times, the ACC does not have a tournament for volleyball, so Carolina won’t be the ACC Champions. However they are going to get their chances to get momentum for the NCAA Tournament, and they’ll find out their tournament fate on Sunday evening.
UNC Women’s Soccer
Friday, November 27th — vs Washington, Chapel Hill, NC — 6 PM, NCAA Tournament Second Round
The top overall seed in the tournament easily cruised to a win on Saturday against UNC-Asheville, and the Tar Heels are one step closer to getting back to the summit of college soccer. They’ll face a Washington squad that completed the upset over the regional eighth seeded Auburn Tigers. A win Friday and the path gets even tougher as Carolina has Alabama, Duke, and Virginia in their region just to get to the Final Four.
UNC Men’s Soccer
Sunday, November 29th — vs South Carolina, Chapel Hill, NC — NCAA Tournament Third Round
The Tar Heels had a bit of a scare against UNCW as they only had a 2-0 lead against UNCW in the second round, and then the Seahawks managed to get one with seventeen minutes left. The Tar Heels had to hold on against a charged up Seahawks team on a field that had been soaked with an all day rain. Hold on they did, though, winning 3-1. Now they’ll play this weekend against South Carolina who needed penalty kicks to get to this level. After that, a very likely rematch with SMU in Dallas is ahead before a trip back to Cary.

By R.L. Bynum

RJ Davis keeps finding ways to score for the Austin Spurs, often off the bench.

UNC’s all-time leading scorer set a season high with 23 points in Thursday’s 129–106 win at Osceola, adding three steals and a plus-29 in 28 minutes. Three days later, he topped it with 26 points on 7-for-12 shooting, including 2-for-5 from three, in a 109–80 rout at Oklahoma City. He also had four rebounds and four assists against the Blue.

Davis, in his second pro season, is averaging 21.3 points as Austin’s sixth man. The Spurs are 6–0 and alone in first place in the South Division, and they play the Blue again at 8 p.m. Tuesday in Oklahoma City.

Tyler Nickel helped Westchester sweep two games at Raptors 905. He made 4 of 8 3-pointers for 12 points in Friday’s 130–117 win, then had 20 points and six rebounds in 41 minutes in Sunday’s 102–101 win.

Wilson eyes return

Chicago rookie Caleb Wilson, recovering from surgery on his fractured right hand, is expected to be reevaluated around Dec. 3. 

“It’s difficult, but it’s not long-term,” 
Wilson told The Associated Press
. “I’ll play again in a couple weeks. I’m not really worried about it.”

Wilson told the 
Chicago Tribune
 that the three hand injuries are just bad luck.

“I mean, I’m pretty healthy. I do what I’m supposed to do. I drink my milk. Sometimes you just have a bad go at things but it’s a blessing. It’s a blessing in disguise. Everything happens for a reason. I’ll come back stronger and better.”

Johnson helps Denver get two wins

Cameron Johnson’s Nuggets played a big part of two wins.

On Friday, he scored 19 points on 7-for-13 shooting with six assists and a game-high plus-19 in a 133–124 NBA Cup win over Houston.

Sunday in Chicago, Johnson made 4 of 5 3-pointers, tying his season high, and scored 16 points in a 151–145 overtime win.

Sharpe stays productive in Brooklyn

Day’Ron Sharpe had 17 points on 7-for-9 shooting and 11 rebounds, seven of them offensive, in Friday’s 113–111 loss to Orlando, decided by a Franz Wagner game-winner. It was another double-double in a strong start for Sharpe.

Redick’s halftime message sparks Kessler
Walker Kessler had 6 points, 1 rebound and four fouls at halftime Friday against last-place Sacramento, and Lakers coach JJ Redick let him hear about it. Kessler responded with nine points, six rebounds and three blocks in the second half, finishing with 15 points, seven rebounds and three blocks in a 106–104 NBA Cup win.

“He lit me up a little bit, rightfully so,” 
Kessler told ESPN
. “But it was constructive. He was like, ‘I know you can do this. Do it.’ ”

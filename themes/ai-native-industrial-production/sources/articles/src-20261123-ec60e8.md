VulcanForms orders 200 EOS metal AM systems&nbsp;&nbsp;Engineering.com

[page text]
The landmark order will expand production capacity at VulcanForms’ Devens, Massachusetts, facilities, which already house 35 EOS M4 ONYX systems.

VulcanForms
 and EOS announced a landmark order for 200 
EOS M4 ONYX
 metal AM systems and ancillary equipment, representing the largest single order in EOS history. The systems will significantly expand VulcanForms’ metal AM production capacity at its Devens, Massachusetts facilities, which are already home to 35 EOS M4 ONYX systems alongside additional metal AM platforms in production.

Marie Niehaus-Langer, CEO of EOS, Kevin Kassekert, CEO of VulcanForms, and Joachim Zettler, CTO of EOS at Formnext 2026.

VulcanForms operates fully integrated production facilities that combine metal AM, precision machining, automation, inspection and proprietary software in an end-to-end platform. Serving customers across consumer goods, medical devices, industrial equipment and mission systems, the company produces complex, high-performance metal components for demanding applications. The additional EOS M4 ONYX systems will substantially expand the additive manufacturing capacity within this platform as VulcanForms continues to grow its U.S. manufacturing operations.

VulcanForms Production Floor in Devens, Massachusetts | Source: VulcanForms.

Launched in late 2025, the EOS M4 ONYX combines six 400-watt lasers with a 450×450×400mm build volume, intelligent process control and the RFS Pro advanced filtration system. Together, these capabilities are designed to deliver the throughput, process stability and consistent part quality required for demanding, high-volume manufacturing environments.

EOS M4 ONYX.

The 200-system order significantly expands the role of EOS technology within VulcanForms’ integrated manufacturing platform. In addition to metal AM systems, EOS provides materials, software and application expertise to support VulcanForms as it scales repeatable production and brings additional AM capacity online.

NYLON On Tour
Subway Surfers at a concert? Clearly you’ve never been to a 
Devon Again
 show.
If you’re ready to experience such a phenomenon, there’s a quick and easy solution: the 25-year-old alt-pop singer is currently bringing her cerulean-dyed tips to arenas across the country in support of 
Olivia Rodrigo’s
 
Unraveled tour
, where she’ll open for the “Drop Dead” singer for a whopping 40 nights. “It has been absolutely incredible and so surreal,” Devon tells NYLON. “I feel like I am dreaming all of the time. Olivia just blows my mind — it is beyond me how she manages to hold so much space for everyone while working as hard as she does and putting on an insane show every night. I am so inspired by her and I just freaking love that girl.”
Rather than sticking to a standard playlist, Devon is letting each city determine what she listens to on the road. Her time in Philly resulted in a lot of Lil Uzi Vert, Kurt Vile, Jill Scott, Nina Simone, while OutKast and Migos were on heavy rotation in Atlanta. “Now we’re in the van in Florida and I am listening to 
SAILORR
,” she says. But the Floridian inspiration doesn’t end there, because Devon and her team also planned a trip to Gatorland, a 110-acre theme park and wildlife preserve in Orlando, Florida. “It was f*cking awesome,” she says of the experience. “There were vultures everywhere and we all bought shirts.”
Ahead, Devon Again gives us a glimpse into her Gatorland experience, plus an exclusive peek at her time on the Unraveled tour.

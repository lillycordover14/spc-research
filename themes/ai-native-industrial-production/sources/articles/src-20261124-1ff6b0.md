Bulgaria’s industrial value added fell by 8.8% in real terms between 2016 and 2025 despite an 88.2% rise at current prices, according to an annual report by the Confederation of Bulgarian Industry.
Adjusted for inflation, industrial value added was also 15.2% below its 2022 peak. Manufacturing’s share of the country’s gross value added declined from 16.5% to 14.9% over the period, while Bulgaria’s trade deficit reached €10.8 billion in 2025. The report, presented at a roundtable attended by President Iliana Iotova, said Bulgarian industry was losing competitiveness. Its authors cited high energy costs, rising labour costs, workforce shortages and insufficient investment in technology and innovation as key factors behind deindustrialisation.
The report sets out 20 recommendations to the government, including a long-term industrial policy and less red tape.

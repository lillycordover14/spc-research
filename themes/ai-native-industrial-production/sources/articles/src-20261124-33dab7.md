By R.L. Bynum

James Brown scored 11 points in nine games during his final season at North Carolina. On Monday night, he scored 27 in one game, a career high, as host Howard routed Notre Dame of Maryland 101–73.

The 6–10 center went 9-for-12 from the floor and 9-for-13 from the free-throw line. He also grabbed 9 rebounds and blocked 3 shots, and Howard improved to 4–3.

Brown has started all seven games for the Bison and is averaging 13.0 points and 8.1 rebounds while shooting 58.1% from the floor.

Rice comes home

Monday night at the Smith Center was not a happy homecoming on the scoreboard for King Rice. Carolina led Monmouth 51–15 at halftime, and the Hawks shot 16% in the first half without making a 3-pointer, and UNC won 85–45.

The game still meant a great deal to the former Tar Heels point guard, who played point guard for Dean Smith from 1987 to 1991 and now has two other former Tar Heels, Brian Reese and Shammond Williams, on his staff.

Rice was applauded during pregame introductions and waved to the crowd. Afterward, he praised the way UNC handled its coaching change and was enthusiastic about Michael Malone.

“We played in a lot of these games. This can happen sometimes,” 
Rice told the 
Asbury Park Press
. “Overall, it was a cool trip. The game sucked. I didn’t think they could play this well and do this to us, but they did. So you got to take it like a man and keep your head up like I told the kids at halftime… The part that was disappointing is we looked scared.”

Rice blamed himself for a schedule that has kept Monmouth on the road for its first six games. He said buy games like this one help the program pay for recruiting and keep its players.

The Hawks finally play their home opener at 7 p.m. Saturday against Saint Joseph’s.

Bogavac helps Oklahoma State pull away

Luka Bogavac had his best game for Oklahoma State on Sunday in the Charleston Classic. He came off the bench to score 17 points on 6-of-11 shooting, including 3-of-6 from 3-point range. Twelve of those points came in the first half, when the game was close.

Oklahoma State and Virginia Tech were tied 41–41 at halftime before the Cowboys scored 65 points in the second half for a 106–80 win, handing the Hokies their first loss after a 6–0 start. Oklahoma State outscored Virginia Tech 38–16 in bench points, and Bogavac led those reserves. The win was the Cowboys’ first over the Hokies in six tries.

Aarnisalo matches her career high

Elina Aarnisalo made up for lost time in her first game back with UCLA (6–0).

She scored 22 points against Tennessee after missing three games while playing for Finland’s senior national team. That matched her career high, and she went 7-for-10 from the floor and 6-for-7 from the free-throw line.

Aarnisalo also scored 22 last season in UNC’s home 74–69 win over Duke, tying her career high with four 3-pointers.

KK Bransford was the only Bruin to outscore her, finishing with 25 points for No. 10 UCLA in its 90–80 win in Knoxville.

Shaver keeps piling up double-doubles

Will Shaver had another big game in the post for Samford. He finished with 17 points, 10 rebounds and 3 blocks in Sunday’s 80–77 home win over UNC Asheville, his third double-double of the season.

Shaver is averaging 15.6 points and 8.2 rebounds while shooting 68.9% from the floor. The Bulldogs (3–2) play at Georgia Tech at 7 p.m. Tuesday.

Evans keeps the Gophers unbeaten

Kyan Evans played a key role in Minnesota’s 77–62 win Sunday over Utah in the Charleston Classic. He had nine points, a team-best eight assists, five rebounds and three steals, and the Gophers moved to 7–0 for the first time since 2021–22. Several of those assists went to Jaylen Crocker-Johnson, who scored a career-high 31 points and made all eight of his 3-point attempts. Evans also helped close the game. His 3-pointer and tip-in finished a 9–0 run that put Minnesota up 77–59 with 48 seconds left. The Gophers play California at 8 p.m. Saturday in Sioux Falls, S.D.

Dixon helps Arizona advance in Maui

Derek Dixon scored 10 points in No. 8 Arizona’s 96–83 Monday win over Colorado State in the first round of the Maui Invitational. He made 4 of 12 shots, including 2 of 8 beyond the arc. All five Arizona starters scored in double figures as the Wildcats won their fifth straight game. One of Dixon’s shots came at a key moment. After Colorado State cut into the lead, his 3-pointer off a Motiejus Krivas assist with 5:16 left pushed the lead back to 13.

Arizona (5–1) plays Providence in the semifinals at 5 p.m. Tuesday.

Still waiting

Former Tar Heels Cade Tyson (Minnesota) and Jalen Washington (Tennessee) are still waiting to find out whether they can play this season. Both are plaintiffs in an Indiana state-court lawsuit against the NCAA and Purdue seeking immediate eligibility. Judge Kevin McDaniel heard the case Monday morning in Tippecanoe Superior Court, but no ruling had been reported as of Tuesday morning.

Other coaches with UNC ties

Charlotte Smith’s Elon team won its third straight game. Ashanti Fox scored 18 points in a 74–54 win at N.C. Central on Monday; Tamia Watkins added 17 points, seven rebounds, five steals and three blocks; and LaNae’ Corbett had 15 points and 8 rebounds.

Elon (4–2), which never trailed, plays at Clemson at 2 p.m. Sunday, where the Phoenix will face former Tar Heel Taliyah Henderson. Elon visits UNC on Dec. 6.

Wes Miller’s Charlotte team won its opener at the Fort Myers Tip-Off. Dorian Finister scored 12 points in the 49ers’ 71–58 win over Florida Gulf Coast; Arden Conyers added 11, and Charlotte used a 14–3 second-half run to stretch a nine-point lead to 20.

The 49ers shot a season-high 54.7% from the floor, and 10 players scored. Charlotte (4–2) plays Northern Iowa at 11 a.m. Wednesday.

Jerod Haase’s UNCG team had a rough start to its West Coast trip. Pepperdine shot 60.7%, outscored the Spartans 48–18 in the paint and won 89–62 in Malibu. Cameron Grant led UNCG with 13 points, and Charles Barnes had 11 points and 5 rebounds. The Spartans (4–3) play at UC Santa Barbara at 9 p.m. Tuesday.

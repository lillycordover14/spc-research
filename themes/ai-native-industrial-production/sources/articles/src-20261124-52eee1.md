Laminar Joins Rockwell Automation PartnerNetwork to Support Smart and Sustainable Manufacturing
 

										MM Desk					

 

24/11/2026
 

 

 

Share

 
Image Courtesy: Rockford Manufacturing Group

Laminar,
 a developer of Process-Aware Autonomy technology for manufacturing, has joined 
Rockwell Automation’s 
PartnerNetwork as a Technology Partner. The partnership will expand access to Laminar’s real-time fluid intelligence platform for manufacturers using Rockwell Automation technologies, with a focus on improving Clean-in-Place (CIP) and product changeover operations.

Laminar combines patented inline spectral sensors with machine learning models to monitor fluid conditions and identify process changes in real time. When connected to industrial control systems, the technology enables manufacturers to make process decisions based on actual operating conditions rather than relying on fixed recipes and predetermined timers.

Through the Rockwell Automation PartnerNetwork, Laminar aims to extend its technology to more manufacturing facilities and help companies improve production efficiency while reducing water, chemical and energy consumption. The collaboration focuses on improving cleaning cycles and product changeover operations, which are important parts of manufacturing processes, particularly in industries where equipment must meet strict hygiene and quality requirements.

Traditional Clean-in-Place operations often depend on fixed cleaning schedules and timers. Laminar’s technology uses real-time information about fluid conditions to determine when a cleaning process has reached the required level. This allows manufacturers to optimise cleaning cycles while maintaining validated quality and hygiene standards.

Reducing unnecessary cleaning time can also increase the availability of production equipment, allowing manufacturing lines to return to operation sooner. At the same time, shorter and more accurate cleaning cycles can reduce water and chemical usage, helping manufacturers meet their sustainability targets.

Commenting on the partnership, Sanjay Rajan, Chief Revenue Officer of Laminar, said, “Joining the Rockwell Automation PartnerNetwork gives Laminar an opportunity to bring our trusted, secure platform to more manufacturers and scale across the factory floor faster than ever before. Dynamic timers are the only way to run autonomous processes like clean-in-place and product changeover. Now, more manufacturers will have access to dynamic processes that enable the self-driving factory.”

Vicky Bruce, Director, Customer Sustainability at Rockwell Automation, said manufacturers should not have to choose between productivity and sustainability. She added that integrating Laminar’s application-specific process intelligence into the PartnerNetwork will give customers another option to address operational challenges while reducing the resources required for validated cleaning processes.

The companies will also explore the use of process-state information and cycle data to support wider manufacturing improvements through visualisation, analytics and higher-level manufacturing systems. Through this partnership, Laminar and Rockwell Automation aim to help manufacturers improve equipment utilisation, reduce resource consumption and move towards more automated and efficient production operations.

Laminar develops Process-Aware Autonomy technology for process manufacturing industries. Its platform combines patented inline sensors with machine learning models to monitor fluid conditions and identify process states in real time. The technology helps manufacturers make informed operational decisions, maintain product quality and reduce waste. The Laminar Insights Platform also provides visibility into savings in water, chemicals, energy and production time across manufacturing cycles and facilities.

Rockwell Automation
, Inc (NYSE: ROK) is a global leader in industrial automation and digital transformation, dedicated to unlocking the full potential of technology and human ingenuity. By bridging creativity with cutting-edge technology, we strive to enhance productivity and sustainability worldwide. Headquartered in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, Rockwell Automation employs around 27,000 problem solvers across more than 100 countries, as of the fiscal year-end 2024, all focused on delivering value to our customers.

 from $125 million.

Why Critical-Minerals Stocks Are Splitting Apart

A broad rally lifted the whole critical-minerals group on Monday, but the follow-through is mixed, and profit taking after a sharp one-day bounce may explain United States Antimony’s slide, and it appears to be hitting the most volatile names hardest.

Tech and AI stocks broadly are giving back part of Monday’s rally, with Federal Reserve rate worries and Iran sanctions also cited in market coverage. That risk-off tone in growth stocks often spills into resource names like United States Antimony that trade on future production more than current profits.

Rare-earth stocks have also swung on U.S.-China headlines over the past two weeks. On November 24, rare-earth stock moves suggested at China’s fading dominance, a theme that touches United States Antimony and its peers alike.

How USA Rare Earth and MP Materials Parted Ways

USA Rare Earth
 (
NASDAQ:USAR

Critical Metals

Antimony Smelting Versus Rare-Earth Magnet Platforms

United States Antimony runs a niche business built around its smelter in Thompson Falls, Montana, operations in Bolivia and a zeolite unit, with tungsten and gold exploration on the side. Pricing drives the story, since the company sells a commodity whose world price has collapsed while also serving as a sole-source antimony supplier to the Defense Logistics Agency (DLA).

USA Rare Earth is building a vertically integrated rare-earth magnet chain, from the Round Top deposit in Texas to its Stillwater, Oklahoma, magnet plant and the newly acquired Serra Verde producer. MP Materials already mines and separates rare earths at Mountain Pass in California, makes magnets in Fort Worth, Texas, and holds magnet supply deals in consumer electronics and cars plus price-floor support from the U.S. Department of War.

Critical Metals is the earliest-stage name of the group, developing the Tanbreez rare-earth project in Greenland and the Wolfsberg lithium project in Austria. Those business models range from scaled producer to pre-revenue explorer, which helps explain why the group rarely trades in lockstep.

Waiting for the antimony price question to resolve could be the safer play here. For USA Rare Earth and MP Materials stock investors, the next read comes from magnet production ramps heading into 2027.

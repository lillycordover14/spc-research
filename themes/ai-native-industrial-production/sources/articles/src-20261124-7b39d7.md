            Ice Skating and Light Viewing Cruises Debut at Winterfest Riverwalk
        

Anchored by Nauticus’ award-winning Winterfest, Norfolk’s holiday showcase expands this weekend with ice skating and lighted boat cruises.

                 News, Home page
            

                Posted on November 24, 2026 
            

Beginning this weekend, Norfolk’s downtown waterfront will transform into a magical holiday wonderland with the addition of a newly positioned ice-skating rink next to Nauticus, lighted boat cruises aboard the Victory Rover, thousands of new holiday lights in Town Point Park, and an enchanting pathway of new lighted arches around the Battleship 
Wisconsin
. In its second year, Norfolk’s Winterfest Riverwalk continues to expand and is anchored by Nauticus’ Winterfest, the reigning heavyweight champion of ABC-TV’s, 
The Great Christmas Light Fight
. 
“The goal is for Norfolk’s waterfront to feel like a Hallmark Christmas movie,” said Nauticus’ executive director, Stephen Kirkland. “It truly is magical. Award-winning light displays, ice skating in the park, strolling along a lighted promenade while sipping hot chocolate, boarding a cruise to view it all from the river; that’s an unbelievable evening out for any family.” 
Nauticus’ Winterfest opened on November 13th with a completely new lighting design aboard the Battleship 
Wisconsin
 and an all-new immersive experience titled, 
Christmas in Neverland
. In anticipation of enthusiastic holiday visitors this weekend and throughout the season, crews are working around the clock to install the ice-skating rink, set to open on Friday, November 27th. 
“After a very successful inaugural season, we’re excited to bring Skate the Riverwalk back for a second year in an even better location, right next to Nauticus and Winterfest,” said owner and president of Ice Rink Events, Evan Cadwell. “We want families, friends, and couples to make a night out of it. Come skate, grab a hot chocolate, enjoy the holiday lights, and experience everything Winterfest has to offer. With special events and themed skating nights planned throughout the season, there’s something for everyone. We encourage guests to visit our website to explore the programming and make ice skating part of their holiday tradition in downtown Norfolk.” 
Free and open to the public, the lights of Winterfest Riverwalk extend from the Pagoda, past the Battleship 
Wisconsin
 and Nauticus, and throughout Town Point Park. Separate admission is required to visit Nauticus’ Winterfest, Skate the Riverwalk and the lighted boat cruises aboard the Victory Rover. For more information on these experiences, please visit the links below.

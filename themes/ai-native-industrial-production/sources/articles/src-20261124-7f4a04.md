Popular virtual singer Kasane Teto is returning to Youtooz for an adorable new collectible following the success of her previous collaboration with the figure manufacturer.

The new vinyl figure recreates her original appearance, with specially designed packaging paying tribute to her long-running love of baguettes.

Originally created as an April Fools’ joke by users of Japanese message board 2channel in 2008, Kasane Teto was introduced as a fictional VOCALOID before becoming a genuine virtual singer through the UTAU voice synthesis software.

Her popularity has grown considerably in recent years following the introduction of her Synthesizer V AI voicebank in 2023, with songs such as 32ki’s “Mesmerizer” featuring Teto alongside Hatsune Miku and introducing her to a new generation of fans.

Youtooz first collaborated with Teto earlier this year, introducing a 9-inch plush that recreated her classic outfit and signature drill-shaped twin-tails.

The latest collaboration transforms that same design into a miniature vinyl figure, standing approximately 4.5 inches (11.4 cm) tall.

Teto is depicted taking a bow as though thanking an audience following a musical performance, leaning forward with one hand held across her chest and the other extended behind her.

Her deep red hair, large red eyes and blushing expression are complemented by her pink and purple headset, complete with a microphone extending toward her face.

The figure recreates her original blue-gray uniform with pink trim and dark knee-high boots, retaining the classic outfit that fans have known since her early UTAU days.

Youtooz has also incorporated Teto’s favorite food into the collectible’s packaging, with a recipe for baking a baguette printed on the exterior of the display box.

Her love of French bread has inspired several other recent collectibles, including 
FuRyu’s upcoming bakery-themed Noodle Stopper figure
 and an 
oversized plush that dresses Teto in a baguette costume
 from Taito Toys.

The vinyl figure will arrive inside a windowed presentation box accompanied by an embossed protective sleeve and a plastic protector, allowing collectors to display Teto without removing her from the packaging.

Fans will also have the option of purchasing the figure alongside a 2-inch collectible pin for an additional $10.

The announcement follows the recent reveal of 
Good Smile Company’s Nendoroid Surprise Kasane Teto Collection
, which brings together six miniature figures based on several of her official designs alongside her male counterpart, Kasane Ted.

Image by  James Richardson

Good Smile Company has also revealed the 
first painted prototype of its upcoming Kasane Teto Punk Style statue
, offering a more elaborate interpretation of the virtual singer inspired by punk fashion.

Image by  Charlotte Davis

Those who missed Youtooz’s original collaboration can also still pre-order the 9-inch Kasane Teto plush, priced at $29.99 and currently scheduled to begin shipping in January 2027.

The new limited-edition “Kasane Teto” vinyl figure is now available to pre-order through the 
official Youtooz online store
, priced at $29.99 for the standard figure or $39.99 for the version accompanied by a collectible pin, with worldwide shipping currently scheduled for June 2027.

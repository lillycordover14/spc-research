Nancy Matter welcomes Casa Capistrano Tequila for her 2027 Funny Car debut

Nancy Matter will make her long-anticipated nitro Funny Car debut during the 2027 NHRA Mission Foods Drag Racing Series season with a multiyear, five-race sponsorship from Casa Capistrano Tequila.
  

24 Nov 2026
    

              Posted by NHRA.com staff
          

 

News

Nancy Matter will make her long-anticipated nitro Funny Car debut during the 2027 NHRA Mission Foods Drag Racing Series season with a multiyear, five-race sponsorship from Casa Capistrano Tequila, marking the company’s introduction to professional drag racing.
Matter spent several years campaigning in Division 4 within the NHRA Lucas Oil Top Alcohol Funny Car ranks along with five years in an NHRA Hot Rod Heritage Racing Series-legal Nostalgia Funny Car, and is beyond excited to debut midseason after spending the last couple of years restoring the new-to-her-team acquisition.
Matter, a global multi-award-winning music engineer and producer by trade with awards including Gold and Platinum albums, Academy Award-winning albums, People’s Choice Awards, and more, has already acquired a few wins and multiple eighth-mile track records in the nostalgia car.
“Building this car has been quite the endeavor,” said Matter. “There are so many more pieces to the puzzle, and we are extremely blessed to have many talented people pointing us in the right direction. It has absolutely taken longer than we thought it would, but it has to be right, so we opted to replace everything on the car except for a handful of components that came with it.
“To have Casa Capistrano Tequila believe in us, our vision, and have our team become their ‘high performance’ brand ambassador is quite an honor, and we have already set the wheels in motion towards building together to increase sales and events for many years in the future."
Founded in 2017, Casa Capistrano uses highland agave from the Los Altos region to make award-winning tequila. From the sales of each product, Casa Capistrano gives back a portion of proceeds to benefit children and families both in Mexico and the United States.
“The bold spirit of Casa Capistrano Tequila stands alongside others who recognize the passion, precision, and performance that Nancy and her team will bring to racing next year," said the company in a statement. "From the starting line to the winner's circle, we will be cheering for her!”
“We have a plethora of additional Texas Business livery that has joined our team for 2027 as associate partners to thank, in addition to all the manufacturers and people behind the scenes. All of those additions are forthcoming,” added Matter.

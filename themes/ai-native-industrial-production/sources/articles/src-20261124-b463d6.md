much-needed bye week

Knowing that defenses win championships, the defensive-minded Seahawks only need to get into the postseason. They understand once there, anything can happen. They proved that this past season when they shocked everyone and won it all.
That said, they're still not seen as an elite team around the league, which is evident in our Week 12 NFL power rankings roundup. Also evident is the fact that Seattle suddenly has a quarterback controversy brewing with 
Drew Lock outplaying Sam Darnold

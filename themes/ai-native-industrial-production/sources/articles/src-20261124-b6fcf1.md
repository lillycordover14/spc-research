LA Clippers forward Brandon Ingram started and made his season debut in LA’s 
116-104 loss
 to the Rockets on Monday.

Ingram saw his first action with the Clippers after sitting out since May following surgery for a heel injury and to repair a partially torn Achilles tendon.

He had 16 points and seven rebounds in 27 minutes.

The two-time All-Star joined the Clippers in September from Toronto as part of the trade for Kawhi Leonard. He missed the last two games of Toronto’s first-round playoff series against Cleveland because of the injury.

“My recovery has been going good,” Ingram said before the season at media day. “I won’t be participating in training camp much but I will be out there a little bit.”

Interim president of basketball operations Trent Redden said doctors discovered and addressed the previously undisclosed Achilles injury while Ingram underwent surgery on his heel in May.

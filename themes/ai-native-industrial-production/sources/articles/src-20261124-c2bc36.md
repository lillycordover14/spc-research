The general semiconductor diamond chip substrate project has been officially launched and put into production in Beijing, marking that the key material for AI computing power heat dissipation has entered the large-scale application stage.
新智讯
2026-11-24 11:59
The diamond chip substrate project with investment from General Semiconductor has been put into production in Beijing.
On November 23, the "Diamond Chip Substrate Construction Project (Phase I)" of Beijing Tongchuang Jiuzhou Diamond Technology Co., Ltd. was officially put into operation at Doudian High-end Modern Manufacturing Base in Fangshan District, Beijing. This project is led by Tongchuang Jiuzhou, a joint venture established by Jiangsu General Semiconductor Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "General Semiconductor"), an enterprise specializing in semiconductor laser processing equipment, and Jiuzhou Yigui, a Sci-Tech Innovation Board listed company.

Against the backdrop of continuously rising power consumption of AI computing chips and traditional metal heat dissipation solutions approaching their physical limits, the launch of this production line is regarded by the industry as a landmark progress for General Semiconductor to enter the high-value semiconductor material track and build a closed-loop vertical industrial chain.

Founded in 2019, General Semiconductor is a leading domestic enterprise in the field of semiconductor laser stealth cutting equipment. Its product matrix covers wafer laser stealth cutting equipment, SDBG laser stealth cutting equipment adapted to processes of 18nm and below, the first domestic 8-inch fully automatic silicon carbide ingot laser stripping production line, and fully automatic diamond laser stealth dicing line, with more than 300 independent intellectual property rights in total. The company's laser stealth cutting equipment has been applied in batches in the fields of memory chips, 5G optical communications, logic chips, advanced sensors, etc., with customers covering leading industrial chain enterprises such as Changxin Memory Technologies, Yangtze Memory Technologies, Huawei, SMIC, and Innolight. From 2024 to 2025, the company's sales exceeded 100 million yuan, and it completed a Series B financing of several hundred million yuan in 2026, with Jiuzhou Yigui making a strategic capital increase of 69.9896 million yuan, further consolidating the collaborative foundation between the two sides in the fields of semiconductor precision processing and diamond thermal management materials.

Different from the routine route of conventional material manufacturers that starts with crystal growth raw materials, General Semiconductor has taken a differentiated path of "defining material machinability with equipment". Diamond has high hardness and high brittleness. From the crystal growth blank to the heat sink substrate that can be directly used for chip packaging, multiple precision processes such as cutting and stripping, wafer separation, grinding and polishing, and dicing and forming need to be completed in sequence. Processing damage in any link will directly affect the finished product yield and increase manufacturing costs. Relying on its own technical accumulation in the field of superhard materials, General Semiconductor has built an equipment and process system covering the whole manufacturing process of diamond devices. The core fully automatic diamond laser stealth dicing line is based on low-damage laser stealth cutting technology, with lower processing loss and higher production efficiency, which can realize precise segmentation of diamond wafers. This differentiated route of "defining materials with equipment" enables the Phase I project of Tongchuang Jiuzhou to directly target the delivery of finished single-crystal diamond heat dissipation devices, instead of staying at the stage of low value-added products such as crystal growth raw materials and abrasive grade products.

Diamond heat dissipation is rapidly evolving from an "optional choice" to a "must-have" for thermal management of high-power chips. The thermal conductivity of diamond at room temperature is about 5 times that of pure copper, and it also has electrical insulation properties and a thermal expansion coefficient matching chips, making it a core thermal management material in high computing power scenarios. A research report from Zhongtai Securities predicts that the global market size of diamond heat sinks for high-end AI chips will reach about 8.7 billion yuan in 2026, and is expected to increase to 59.2 billion yuan in 2030, with an average annual compound growth rate of over 50%; calculations from Huafu Securities show that the market space for diamond heat dissipation for AI computing power is expected to reach 48 billion yuan to 90 billion yuan in 2030. 2026 is regarded by many institutions and the industry as a key window year for large-scale application of diamond heat dissipation. General Semiconductor enters the processing link with equipment technology, and has seized the core link that determines yield and cost on the eve of industry expansion.

The launch of this production line is an important node for the upgrading of General Semiconductor's business model. The deployed R&D, manufacturing and processing services of diamond heat dissipation materials are expected to promote the company's transformation from a single equipment supplier to a comprehensive platform integrating "equipment + materials + processing services". After the diamond substrate production line is put into operation, the equipment and material businesses will form synergistic linkage. The equipment business, as the fundamental business, continuously provides stable cash flow to support the iteration of laser micro-nano processing technology, while the diamond heat dissipation substrate business opens up a brand-new high-value track and becomes an important growth engine for the company in the future. Relying on the process data accumulated from the large-scale operation of the production line, the company can continuously optimize the scheme of laser stealth cutting equipment. At the same time, the continuous upgrading of equipment performance also lays a solid foundation for substrate cost reduction and yield improvement. The commissioning of the Phase I project of Tongchuang Jiuzhou is exactly the key point for General Semiconductor to systematically convert its equipment technology advantages into high-value material business.

From the perspective of regional industrial value, the location of the project in Doudian High-end Modern Manufacturing Base in Fangshan, Beijing also carries the expectation of quality improvement and upgrading of advanced manufacturing industry in the southern part of the capital. In the new stage where the localization of semiconductor materials is moving from "solving the problem of availability" to "pursuing excellent performance", the realization of independent large-scale supply of diamond heat dissipation substrates will help reduce the dependence of domestic AI computing power infrastructure on imported high-end heat dissipation materials.

Tao Weiyin, Chairman of General Semiconductor, said that the company will continue to iterate laser processing equipment and supporting processes around the three major directions of large-size, thinning and metallization adaptation of diamond heat sink wafers, so as to promote the application of diamond heat dissipation substrates to expand from AI chip packaging scenarios to radio frequency devices, high-speed optical modules and other fields. Driven by both the growing demand for AI computing power and the continuous advancement of localization, the commissioning of the production line in Fangshan, Beijing marks that General Semiconductor's strategic layout of taking equipment technology as the fulcrum and extending to diamond heat dissipation materials has officially entered the capacity realization stage from the technology reserve stage.

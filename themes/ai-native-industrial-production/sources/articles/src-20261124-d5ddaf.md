ORANGE CITY, I.A. (Dakota News Now) - Following weeks of preparation, Orange City, Iowa, has crowned its new Tulip Queen!
Elizabeth Vaas was chosen from a court of 5 senior girls. The day consisted of personal conversations with three judges, a presentation sharing their love for the Tulip Festival, and a presentation of a Dutch tradition significant to them. 
Vaas will serve alongside her court, which includes Emily Oordt, Hazel Simmelink, Ayca Van Schouwen, and Emma Weber.
The 86th annual Orange City Tulip Festival returns May 13-15, 2027.

Groupie Doll's Last Foal Wins Debut, Named Hot Prospect&nbsp;&nbsp;BloodHorse

[page text]
This column highlights the performances of 2- and 3-year-old maiden special weight winners (the latter age category only through June) with the potential for graded stakes success based on criteria such as a horse that has made no more than five starts and either sold for more than $500,000 at public auction, has siblings that are graded/group winners, or has a dam that is a graded/group winner. BloodHorse research shows maiden winners who meet these criteria are more likely to go on to be graded stakes winners. 

Jackie's Doll
 
 and 
Bourbon Neat
🔥
 
 are the latest 
BloodHorse Maiden Watch Hot Prospects
 after notching striking victories last week.

Jackie's Doll Runs to Speedy Pedigree

Jackie's Doll, a filly produced by a mating between two champion sprinters, has plenty of quickness of her own.

The 2-year-old daughter of 
Jackie's Warrior
 
 
 out of 
Groupie Doll
 
 sizzled in winning her debut Nov. 19 at 
Fair Grounds Race Course & Slots
, completing 6 furlongs in 1:09.99, a time that generated her an 81 Equibase Speed Figure and a 79 Beyer Speed Figure.

Hounded on the lead in the $52,000 race by the more experienced 
Lenlew's
 
 through splits of :22.30 and :45.65, she shrugged off that one's challenge in early stretch and inched clear by 1 1/4 lengths at the wire under Marcelino Pedroza Jr. Sent off as the heavy favorite in a field of five, she returned just $3.80 to win.

The Brad Cox trainee, a homebred for Mandy Pope's Whisper Hill Farm, was the last foal produced by 
Groupie Doll
, a two-time Eclipse Award-winning female sprinter whose accomplishments included back-to-back victories in the Breeders' Cup Filly and Mare Sprint (G1) in 2012-13. Whisper Hill Farm, which bought her for $3.1 million at the 2013 Keeneland November Breeding Stock Sale, announced over the summer that Groupie Doll was retired this year from broodmare duties.

Jackie's Warrior, currently the second-leading first-crop sire in North America, also was quick, notching grade 1 victories in one-turn races at ages 2, 3 and 4. He was named champion male sprinter in 2021. The Spendthrift Farm stallion will stand for $35,000 in 2027.

Jackie's Doll has the potential to become Groupie Doll's top-performing produce, with her running style and pedigree suggesting her future likely will come in one-turn races. Though all five of her foals to race are winners, none have won stakes. Her richest runner is 
Tap for Me
🔥
 
 (
Tapit
 
 
), who went 2-2-0 in five starts and made $163,400.

Bourbon Neat Smooth in Second-out Victory

(The following first appeared Nov. 19 on Bloodhorse.com.)

Improving on a debut fourth in a well-rated maiden race last month at Keeneland, 
Bourbon Neat
 stretched out to a mile and scored going away by 4 lengths Nov. 19 at 
Churchill Downs
. His performance in Thursday's seventh race, along with his sales history and pedigree, points to a bright future.

His victory in the $118,908 maiden race, in which he stalked the pace in fourth, circled the field four wide on the turn, and drew off through the stretch, pushed his career earnings to $76,119.

He was reluctant to switch leads until late, but otherwise raced professionally under apprentice Yomar Ortiz Jr. He completed a mile on a fast track in 1:35.26, paying $3.36 as the favorite. His time generated a 92 Equibase Speed Figure and a 74 Beyer Speed Figure.

Photo: Coady Media/Cady Coulardot
Bourbon Neat breaks his maiden at Churchill Downs

Trained by Will Walden for owner Resolute Racing, Bourbon Neat is the latest promising 2-year-old from Lane's End stallion 
Flightline
🔥
 
 
, who is already represented by stakes winners 
Go for Launch
 
 and 
Shonan Galleon
. Flightline, unbeaten in six career starts and winner of the 2022 Breeders' Cup Classic (G1) during his Horse of the Year season, has made a positive early impression as a sire, with 19 winners from his initial crop. He stands for $125,000 next year.

Bourbon Neat is out of southwestern standout Hennesey Smash, a seven-time winner in eight starts who earned $252,006 while capturing four stakes, including the 2012 New Mexico Classic Cup Championship Filly Stakes and Bold Ego Handicap. By Roll Hennessy Roll, the mare has developed into a top-class broodmare, with seven winners from eight starters among her 10 foals.

Four of those are stakes winners, the best of which is 
Slammed
 
, a daughter of 
Marking
 
 who won nine of 13 starts and earned $557,030. Her victories included the 2022 Thoroughbred Club of America Stakes (G2) at Keeneland. 

Bred in Kentucky by Brad King, Scott Bryant, Clay Cash, Chris Coleman, and Stan Kirby, Bourbon Neat was offered through the Hill 'n' Dale at Xalapa consignment at Fasig-Tipton's The Saratoga Sale in 2025, where Resolute Bloodstock purchased him for $875,000.

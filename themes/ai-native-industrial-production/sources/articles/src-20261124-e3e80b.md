Twelve Mountaineers Named to CSC Academic All-District Soccer Teams
 November 24, 2026 02:54 PM 
 | 
Men's Soccer, Women's Soccer
Share:
 By: 
Adam Grossman
MORGANTOWN, W.Va. – A total of 12 student-athletes from the West Virginia University men's and women's soccer teams were placed on the 2026 Academic All-District Soccer Teams, the College Sports Communicators announced on Tuesday.

 

Junior 
Isaac Scheer
, graduate student Aurélien Cormier, sophomore 
Tyler Londono Zapata
, junior 
Nacho Martinez
, senior 
Pablo Pozos
, and redshirt sophomore 
Toby Mawer
 represented the men's team. On the women's side, sophomore 
Ava Arnold
, senior 
Anna Hauer
, redshirt senior 
Bailey Herfurth
, senior 
Nyema Ingleton
, junior 
Sophia Nickel
, and redshirt senior 
Saige Wimes
 were featured on the team.

 

With the recognition, the Mountaineers' 12 honorees advance to the CSC Academic All-America ballot, where first-, second- and third-team Academic All-America selections will be announced in mid-December.

 

Pozos (Coaching and Performance Science) earns his third career All-District honor while it is the first time for the Cormier (Master's in Sport Management), Londono Zapata (Sport Management), Scheer (Entrepreneurship and Innovation; Marketing), Mawer (Finance), and Martinez (Sport Management).

 

For the women's team, Hauer (Sport Management), Ingleton (General Business), and Nickel (Exercise Physiology) earn the honor for the second time in their careers while it is the first for Arnold (Animal & Nutritional Sciences), Herfurth (Exercise Physiology), and Wimes (Master's in Executive Sport Management) as Mountaineers.

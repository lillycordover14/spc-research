Only five quarterbacks threw for at least 300 yards in Week 11, but that doesn’t tell the entire story. It was a great week for the position with 13 quarterbacks recording at least two passing touchdowns. That includes six quarterbacks who had at least three touchdowns each.

No teams are on a bye during Week 12, but we still have a unique schedule with games being played Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Sunday, and Monday. Even with a full slate, many fantasy managers might need a streaming option at quarterback because of injuries to their usual starter.

For streaming purposes, 4for4’s Schedule-Adjusted Fantasy Points Allowed (
aFPA
) is a metric we rely heavily on to determine weekly matchup strength. As the season progresses and more data is available, 
aFPA
 becomes more and more reliable, using rolling 10-week data.

To provide advice you can implement, candidates for streaming must be rostered in no more than 40% of Yahoo! fantasy leagues.

Week 11 Recap

It was a good week for our streaming recommendations, with three of them finishing inside the top 10. 
Justin Herbert
 led the way, finishing as QB4 during a loss to the Jets. Although he only threw for 131 yards, he recorded two passing touchdowns and another touchdown on the ground. The downside is that he was placed in the concussion protocol after the loss.

The worst performer of the week was 
Jacoby Brissett
, who finished as QB18. In a 35-33 loss to the Panthers, he threw for 276 yards, one touchdown, and one interception. He attempted 37 passes, leaving him with a total of 86 pass attempts over his first two starts with the Ravens. The problem is that he only has one touchdown and two interceptions to show for it.

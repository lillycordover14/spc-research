Ghostly The Raven Society Figure Arrives with Another Edgar Allan Poe

Article Summary

Edgar Allan Poe returns as The Raven Society’s glow-in-the-dark R.I.P. Founder Edition from BigBadWorkShop.

The 1:12-scale Edgar Allan Poe figure features 20-plus articulation points and eerie supernatural display details.

Accessories include a quill, crow, skull, books, journal, cat, and smoke effects inspired by Poe’s gothic legacy.

Priced at $39.99, the campaign-exclusive Edgar Allan Poe figure expands a literary horror world tied to The Library.

BigBadWorkShop is giving one of literature's most haunting figures a second life with R.I.P. 
Edgar Allan Poe
, a spectral Founder Edition from The Raven Society. Poe was a writer, poet, and critic whose stories helped shape modern horror and detective fiction. From "
The Tell-Tale Heart
" to "
The Fall of the House of Usher
" and "
The Raven
," his work explored guilt, obsession, death, and the darkness lurking beneath ordinary life. Now, the creators behind Operation: Monster Force are bringing him into a 1:12-scale universe built around a Library where history, fiction, and the supernatural collide.

The team behind that monster-filled action figure world is expanding their storytelling into gothic literature with BigBadWorkShop. This ghostly Poe features glow-in-the-dark elements representing the Society's otherworldly form when members travel beyond the Library's protection. With over 20 points of articulation and accessories including a quill, crow, skull, books, journal, cat, and smoke effects, he makes a display piece. Chapter One is already 100% funded and cleared for launch, while this $39.99 Founder Edition remains a 
campaign exclusive
, so get yours while you can!

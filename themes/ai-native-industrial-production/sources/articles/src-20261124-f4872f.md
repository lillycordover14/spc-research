Understanding Cancer Research UK’s Twelve 5Ks of Christmas Challenge

The Twelve 5Ks of Christmas Challenge is a December fundraising initiative by Cancer Research UK (CRUK), encouraging participants to complete 
12 runs of 5 kilometres throughout the month
 while raising money for
 cancer research
.

Challenge:
 Complete 12 individual 5K runs during December.

Purpose:
 Raise funds to support cancer research and help people affected by cancer.

Participation:
 Open to all fitness levels, with participants free to run, jog or walk.

Fundraising:
 Participants collect donations through personal online fundraising pages.

Rewards:
 Participants who raise at least £150 (excluding Gift Aid) receive a Cancer Research UK medal.

Flexibility:
 Runs can be completed at any location and spread throughout the month.

New exciting architecture jobs at Jessica Schuster, Burr Salvatore, Rapt Studio, Cass Calder Smith, and Carlos Zapata

 


									By 
Nathaniel Bahadursingh
 

Nov 24, '26 1:56 PM EST

0

Follow
 

 

101 Wooster by Jessica Schuster Design.

For this week's 
highlight of featured employers
, we have selected five architecture and design firms with 
job openings
 in 
New York City
, 
San Francisco
, 
Los Angeles
, and 
Darien, Connecticut
.

For even more opportunities, visit the 
Archinect job board
.

Rapt Studio
 has six exciting opportunities available in San Francisco and Los Angeles: a 
Designer
 with up to five years of experience; a 
Job Captain
 with four to eight years of experience; a 
Project Designer
 with four to eight years of experience; 
Senior Designers (Interior Design/Architecture)
 with eight-plus years of experience; and a 
Senior Designer (Project Architect)
 with twelve-plus years of experience.

Jerde by Rapt Studio.
In San Francisco, 
Cass Calder Smith
 has openings for an 
Interior Designer
 with five-plus years of experience and a 
Junior Architectural Designer
 with one to three years of experience. Both roles require proficiency with Revit, AutoCAD, Adobe Creative Suite, SketchUp, Rhino 3D, Enscape, and Bluebeam. The Interior Designer should have strong experience with FF&E selection, specification, sourcing, budgeting, procurement coordination, and client presentations. The Junior Architectural Designer role requires experience contributing to the development of comprehensive construction document packages.

Fresh Pond Residence by Cass Calder Smith.
Architecture and design firm 
Carlos Zapata Studio
 is hiring an 
Intermediate Architect for Design Studio
 in New York City with a minimum of five to ten years of experience and proficiency with AutoCAD, Rhino 3D, and Adobe Creative Suite. The ideal candidate is experienced with commercial, residential, and mixed-use projects. Applicants should also have strong working knowledge of local building codes and regulatory requirements, as well as the International Building Code.

Sculptura Ardmore by Carlos Zapata Studio.
Job seekers in Connecticut should consider 
Burr Salvatore Architects
' 
Project Designer
 position in Darien. Applicants must have at least five to ten years of experience, including three to five years in single-family residential design. The position requires proficiency with CAD and Adobe InDesign. Candidates should also be proficient in interior architecture and millwork detailing. 

House on the Hudson by Burr Salvatore Architects. Photo: Read McKendree
New York City-based studio 
Jessica Schuster Design
 seeks an 
Intermediate Architect
 with three to five years of experience and proficiency with AutoCAD, SketchUp, Adobe Creative Suite, and Google Workspace. The ideal candidate has strong technical drafting and detailing skills, a thorough understanding of architectural drawing conventions, and familiarity with residential construction practices and coordination with architects of record. The role requires excellent communication, organization, and problem-solving skills.

Big Ten Football Power Poll: Week Twelve
Does Anything matter anymore?
by
 
 
Andy York
Nov 25, 2026, 1:00 PM UTC
Welp. The cream went bad. The Big Ten has successfully eaten itself alive. Are there any actual good teams? Are there any actual bad teams? (Unfortunately the answer to that is yes, and Minnesota lost to at least one and maybe two of them…) There seem to be about 15 teams that on any given Saturday could win against one another. That’s great for competition, but not so good when you want as many teams in the CFP as possible. But we now know why the Big Ten wanted 24 teams in the playoff badly. So here’s an attempt at ranking these teams, but lets be real, it really doesn’t matter anymore. So you will see I’m just grouping them at this point because whether a team is #6 or #12, they are all the damn same.
#1A Iowa Hawkeyes 9-2 (4)
Win 34-17 @ Illinois
It’s scary to think the only thing keeping Iowa from the CFP is their loss to Minnesota. Yet they still have a back door way in potentially, but it can be squashed by…you guessed it another win by Minnesota. Well that’s just one f the ways Iowa can make the Big Ten Championship game and have a shot at the CFP auto bid. And Iowa still has to win at home against Nebraska this Friday as a 4.5 point favorite. But it would be very sweet to runin Iowa’s season twice.
#1B Oregon Ducks 8-3 (4)
Win 45-7 @ Michigan State
The Ducks have seemed to get things figured out here down the stretch as they cruised past the Spartans and now need a win over Washington and some extra help to get to Indy for the Big ten title game and possibly assure themselves of a CFP bid. They will know by kickoff whether they are in with a win or not as 14 point favortes at home against Washington/
#1C UCLA Bruins 10-1 (1)
Loss 18-16 3OT @ Michigan
Well that was putrid. Not sure what UCLA was doing but it was not playing football. And that stinker could cost them. The Bruins would still be in the CFP as of today as they rank #9 this week, but with a tough game against USC looming, it’s basically a play in. Win and you head to the Big Ten Championship game and are in good shape. Lose, and you are going to need a lot of help. The game is in the Rose Bowl, but the Trojans are 7 point favorites. Good luck Bruins.
#1D Indiana Hoosiers 9-2 (2)
Loss 27-24 OT @ Washington
The Hoosiers had been flirting with disaster for the last few weeks. The Gophers could not pull off the upset that broke them, but it would be Washington doing the honors with a 27-24 OT win. Indiana can still get into the Big Ten Championship game but it’s going to need some significant help, and now finds itself fourth out of the Big Ten teams with CFP hopes. They play Purdue for the Old Oaken Bucket this week in what should be over quick with Indiana as 22.5 point favorites.
#5 USC Trojans 8-3 (3)
Win 35-21 vs Maryland
USC looked like they didn’t want to play football for about a half trailing the Terps at halftime, but they finally figured it out fast enough to pick up the win. They still have a shot at the Big Ten Championship game as well but will need a ton of help. They also will need to win over rival UCLA, but are 7 point favorites in the cross-town battle.
#6A Nebraska Cornhuskers 7-4 (13)
Win 31-28 vs Ohio State
The streak is dead. For the first time in 32 games Nebraska beat a ranked opponent. An impressive win even with a depleted Buckeye roster. They head into Iowa City this Friday looking to play spoiler for the Hawkeyes.
#6B Wisconsin Badgers 7-4 (10)
Win 42-10 @ Purdue
What do you know, you are allowed to thrash bad teams, not be thrashed by them. An interesting concept. But who will win the battle for the axe? The line keeps growing as Wisconsin is now favored by 7.5. But we all know in this game that doesn’t matter.
#6C Washington Huskies 7-4 (12)
Win 27-24 OT vs Indiana
A big win for the Huskies at home after struggling for most of the season. They finsh off at Oregon trying not to be duck feed this Saturday.
#6D Ohio State Buckeyes 7-4 (6)
Loss 31-28 @ Nebraska
That one hurts. You cant lose to that Nebraska team Ohio State. What are you doing? You are still favorited by 10.5 in the Big Game this Saturday, but should you be?
6E Illinois Fighting Illini 6-5 (8)
Loss 34-17 vs Iowa
Iowa is good, but still not a great showing by the Illini. They cap off the season at Northwestern as three point underdogs while playing for the Hat.
#6F Minnesota Golden Gophers 6-5 (11)
Win 20-17 vs Northwestern
New OC, same offense for a half, but then things opened up and the Gophers looked like a team who is actually decent. Big walk off FG win once again, and they will look to try and win all three main rivalry trophies for the first time since 1967 in Madison Friday night. Let’s hope they can.
#6G Michigan Wolverines 6-5 (14)
Win 18-16 3OT vs UCLA
That was something. I’m not sure it was a good something, but is sure was something. The Wolverines get Ohio State and then a bowl game while trying to figure how to come up with $20 million to pay an AD who never actually was hired. Have fun with that.
#6H Northwestern Wildcats 6-5 (7)
Loss 20-17 @ Minnesota
No Aiden Chiles means Northwestern is back to being old Northwestern. Not good Northwestern. Will he be back for the Hat game? Unknown. If he’s not, take the Illini.
#14 Penn State Nittany Lions 6-5 (12)
Lose 25-24 vs Rutgers
Well that was embarrassing. Does Penn State deserve to be in the cluster above? Not after a loss like that they don’t. They head to Maryland as 7 point favorites this week.
#15 Maryland Terrapins 4-7 (15)
Loss 35-21 @ USC
I’ll give Maryland credit for playing hard and picking up a first half win. Unfortunately they also play the second half and that one did not go so well. One last game on Saturday vs Penn State before figuring who your new coach will be.
#16 Rutgers Scarlet Knights 3-8 (18)
Win 25-24 @ Penn State
Way to find a way to win Scarlet Knights. Impressive lateral for the win. They end the season hosting the Spartans in the toilet bowl Saturday as 3.5 point underdogs.
#17 Michigan State Spartans 3-8 (16)
Loss 45-7 vs Oregon
One more week in the initial season of the Pat Fitzgerald experience. It can only go up from here, right? A season ender in New Jersey nobody will be watching.
#18 Purdue Boilermakers 3-8 (17)
Lose 42-10 vs Wisconsin.
This team somehow boatraced Minnesota. It only gets more embarrassing. We will see if Purdue shows up against Indiana or if they have allready thrown in the towel.

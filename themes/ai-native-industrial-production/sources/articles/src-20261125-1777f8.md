‘Utterly Ridiculous!’ Stephen A. Smith Roasts MAHA Influencer After She Lauds Trump as ‘Father Figure to America’

Zachary Leeman
Nov 25th, 2026, 1:21 pm 

Share

X

Facebook

 
 

Copy Link

 
 

Stephen A. Smith
 roasted his guest 
Alex Clark
 after she launched into an “utterly ridiculous” rant about President 
Donald Trump
 governing like a “father figure” to the country.

Clark and 
Michael Knowles
 both joined Smith’s 
Straight Shooter
 show on Wednesday and Smith was taken aback by Clark’s positive spin on the president and the ongoing Iran war.

Smith noted that he recently sat down with and 
interviewed
 the president and Smith flat-out asked him if the U.S. launched into the Iran war partly out of “vengeance,” as Iran was reportedly behind a 
plot
 to assassinate him that led to the president having to secretly 
switch planes
 in Turkey while leaving a NATO summit in July. Trump told Smith it is a “human reflex.”

Smith then asked Clark, a Turning Point USA podcast closely associated with the Make America Healthy Again movement, if we are partially in Iran out of “vengeance.”

“He has fights and things like that on X, but as far as getting us involved in a war for vengeance, I don’t think the president would do that,” Clark said.

She then launched into her “father figure” theory, which she admitted would likely make Smith want to “vomit.”

Clark said:

One thing that I like about President Trump, even though I vastly disagree with the war, is I do like that he kind of governs in a way where I almost think that he thinks of himself as like a father figure to America. This is going to make you vomit, throw up in your mouth, I know, Stephen, but I really do think he kind of thinks of things as like as a dad, like what am I going to do to protect my kids? And I think he kind of thinks of America like that. And maybe that’s dumb, but that’s just how I see it. And maybe that’s because I’m a woman. But I’m just glad and thankful that I am not in that room making these decisions because that is tough. There’s no right answer.

Smith
 did not puke in his mouth, but he did dismiss the argument as “ridiculous” while roasting his guest.

“I do think that answer you just gave is utterly ridiculous, but it’s making me smile because I said, that’s why you’re married. That’s a very nice — you’re a very nice lady! A nice, wonderful, God-fearing woman would give that kind of answer because a dude… what?! You said you think he’s trying to be a dad?! Nobody’s gonna buy that. No, we’re not buying that, Alex. But I get it, I understand that you believe that and I respect it,” an energetic Smith said.

He then turned to 
Knowles
 who sarcastically asked, “You don’t want to call President Trump daddy, Stephen? I’m shocked to hear this.”

“No, no. Hell no. No, I wouldn’t,” Smith said.

Game Twelve Preview: Old Dominion Vs Southern Miss
Bruce Butler

Old Dominion (3-8, 2-5 SBC) vs. Southern Miss (3-8, 2-5 SBC)

Date

 Saturday, November 28 • 1 p.m. • Norfolk, Va. • Kornblau Field at S.B. Ballard Stadium

Where to Watch

 
ESPN+

Listen

 
ODU Sports Network

Series 

 ODU leads, 2-1

Game Notes

 Old Dominion       
Southern Miss

NORFOLK, Va. 
- Old Dominion closes out the season at home this Saturday as the Monarchs host Southern for a 1 p.m. Sun Belt game at S.B. Ballard Stadium on Nov. 28. The contest will mark the team’s Military Appreciation Game as well as Senior Day for the Monarchs

Saturday's Sun Belt Conference game will be streamed on ESPN+ and a radio broadcast can be heard on ESPN Sports Radio 94.1.

• ODU is coming off a 29-21 road loss at UConn on Nov. 21. It was the Huskies’ smallest margin of victory this season. ODU was also the third SBC team UConn has played this season after the team defeated USM, 48-20 on Sept. 19 and James Madison, 46-22 on Nov. 14.

• Southern Miss is also 3-8 overall and 2-5 in the Sun Belt. The Golden Eagles are looking to snap a three-game losing streak after falling, 40-35 in Hattiesburg, Mississippi last Saturday.

• Quarterback 
Quinn Henicle
 completed 9-of-15 passes for 99 yards and two touchdowns against the Huskies, while 
Jaxon Potter
 went 23-for-29 for 240 yards with one touchdown.

• 
Devin Roche
 led ODU on the ground with 40 yards on seven carries. Roche also had seven receptions for 83 yards and a touchdown, 
Leon Haughton Jr.
 also caught seven balls for 62 yards with two touchdowns, and 
Josh Rodriguez
 finished with seven catches for 78 yards.

• 
Daevon Iles
 led ODU’s defense with eight tackles, while 
Mario Easterly
 and 
DeAndre Lynch
 tallied seven tackles each. Lynch posted 3.5 tackles for loss, 
Jackson Forrest
 contributed five tackles and 0.5 a sack, 
Brandon Crutchfield
 turned in four tackles with one sack and a tackle for loss, and 
Zion Frink
 collected the team’s lone interception
.

Military Appreciation Week
This week is Military Appreciation Week for ODU and ODU Global is proud to serve as the presenting sponsor! ODU Global values your military experience and awards academic credits for your service, helping you complete your degree faster. Their dedicated advisors are here to help you every step of the way. Explore your options and start your journey today at online.ODU.edu.

All-Time Series
Saturday will mark the 
fourth meeting between Old Dominion and Southern Miss, with ODU ahead in all-time series, 2-1
.

The Monarchs and Golden Eagles last played each other on Oct. 7, 2023 in Hattiesburg, a game that went to the Silver and Blue, 17-13. Trailing 13-10 in the fourth quarter, quarterback 
Grant Wilson
 connected with 
Ahmarian Granger
 for a 39-yard gain, and 
Keshawn Wicks
 finished off the drive with a 16-yard touchdown with 11:16 left in the game. 
Ethan Sanchez
 tacked on the extra point to make it 17-13, and the Monarchs' defense came up with a goal line stand as 
Jason Henderson
 stopped USM tailback Frank Gore half a yard shy of the endzone with 3:51 left.

Henderson, who finished the game with 17 tackles, including four solo and a sack, became ODU's all-time leading tackler.

Career Milestones in East Hartford
Receiver 
BJ Bedgood
 posted new career bests with four receptions and 37 yards against the Huskies. For Haughton, it was the first time in his career he's caught multiple touchdowns in a game. Lynch's 3.5 tackles for loss were also a new career high, and Crutchfield recorded his first career sack.

Two Monarchs Making Moves on the Ground
ODU has nine rushing touchdowns this season, all by two players: quarterback Quinn Hennicle and running back 
Maurki James
. Hennicle has rushed for five scores with 369 yards on 98 attempts, and James has punched the ball in four times and is up to 127 yards on 47 carries.

A Receiving Duo Delivering Results
Haughton Jr. and receiver 
TJ Johnson
 have hauled in nine of ODU's 10 receiving touchdowns this season. Haughton comes into Saturday's game with six, and Johnson has caught three.

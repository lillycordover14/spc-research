Backstage Access Presents: Music City Celebrates Nashville Debut&nbsp;&nbsp;MusicRow.com

[page text]
Hitmaker Rhett Akins performs an intimate acoustic set at Virgin Hotels Nashville during Backstage Access Presents: Music City. Photo: David Bradley / Osprey Media

Backstage Access Presents: Music City, held Nov. 15–18 during CMA Awards Week, brought its signature blend of live music, wine-country hospitality and industry connections to Nashville, welcoming a lineup of established hitmakers, rising stars, industry leaders and tastemakers.

The four-day experience built on the success of the series’ inaugural Napa Valley gathering earlier this year, transforming Nashville into a destination for intimate performances, songwriter rounds, exclusive gatherings and curated hospitality. The event paired Nashville’s rich musical heritage with celebrated wineries, distinctive culinary experiences and opportunities for artists and industry professionals to connect beyond the traditional festival or conference setting. The multi-day celebration featured
 Lee Brice, Carly Pearce, Chase Rice, Lauren Alaina, Rhett Akins, Flatland Cavalry, Matt Stell, Charlie Worsham, Ian Flanigan, Cody Lohden, Jet Jurgensmeyer,
 and more.

Additional artists featured throughout the Music City programming included 
John Cooper Albright, BoomTown Saints, Sam Drysdale, Mack Geiger, Braden Hull, Alannah McCready, Six One Five Collective, Tori Rose, The Ransom Brothers, Amy Stroup,
 and more.

“Bringing Backstage Access Presents to Nashville during CMA Awards week was an incredible opportunity to celebrate the artists, industry professionals and partners who make this community so special,” shares Backstage Access Presents  Founder 
Bobbii Jacobs
. “What started in Napa Valley has grown into something truly meaningful, and seeing everyone come together in Music City reinforced why we created this experience in the first place. It’s about great music, genuine relationships and creating moments that people will remember long after the event ends. We’re grateful to everyone who helped make our Nashville debut so special.”

The Nashville celebration culminated Nov. 18, with a special brunch showcase presented by Atlantic Outpost and Backstage Access Presents at FlyghtHouse Nashville. Marking Atlantic Outpost’s inaugural CMA Awards Week industry gathering, the event featured live performances from 
Stella Lefty
 and
 Vincent Mason, Sam Barber
 and
 Avery Anna, Briley King
 and 
Marcus King, Twin Fin,
 as well as
 A.R. Simmons.

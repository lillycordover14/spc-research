GTA VI’s $3.79 billion debut has Zuckerberg joking and Musk laughing, but Take-Two investors still want more&nbsp;&nbsp;Fortune

[page text]
Mark Zuckerberg wanted to know which version of 
Grand Theft Auto VI
 he’d bought. On Nov. 20, the 
Meta
 CEO posted an edited screenshot on X of one of the game’s in-game prompts: a mission to “pick up groceries” with a pink heart and the line, “Priscilla will appreciate this.” His caption: “Wait which version did I get?”
Recommended Video

Elon Musk, the CEO of 
Tesla
 and SpaceX, replied with a laughing emoji. The exchange capped the first full day of what is now the biggest entertainment launch on record.

Rockstar Games, the Take-Two Interactive label behind the franchise, said 
GTA VI
 generated $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales on its Nov. 19 release day
, based on company estimates. That is more than four times the roughly $800 million 
GTA V
 took in on its first day in 2013. The game also sold 32.5 million units before it went on sale.

For scale, the day-one retail figure is more than half of the 
$6.66 billion in net revenue Take-Two reported for its entire fiscal year ended March 2026
. Retail sales account for what consumers spent, not what Take-Two keeps after platform holders and retailers take their cut. Still, the opening validates a bet CEO Strauss Zelnick 
described to 
Fortune
 only as “expensive,”
 and that one former Take-Two CEO Ben Feder called 
the last pre-AI blockbuster of its kind
.

Wall Street wants more than a record opening

The sales kept coming after launch day. Research firm Alinea Analytics 
estimates 44.9 million copies were sold by the end of its opening weekend
, grossing more than $4 billion. Take-Two has not confirmed those figures.

Investors were less euphoric. 
Take-Two shares fell almost 4% on launch day
, even as the game earned a 99 on Metacritic. TD Cowen analyst Doug Creutz wrote that the timing and size of the next 
GTA Online
 matter more to the stock than 
GTA VI 
unit sales.

Take-Two has 
reiterated its outlook of $8 billion to $8.2 billion in net bookings for fiscal 2027
. It has guided to $3.35 billion to $3.5 billion in net bookings for the October–December quarter, the first that will show how much of the launch reaches its books.

Rockstar bet its biggest launch on a love story

Zuckerberg’s joke riffs on a mechanic at the center of 
GTA VI
. Players can build up, or neglect, the relationship between protagonists Jason and Lucia through everyday gestures such as running errands, holding hands, and spending time together. Rockstar North co–studio head Rob Nelson has said the romance is optional, but how players nurture it shapes how the couple’s story unfolds.

That is a departure from the more strictly violence-focused themes of earlier 
GTA
 games. Despite complaints from some longtime fans, many players have posted warmly about the pair. Rupert Humphries, Rockstar Games’ senior vice president of narrative, told 
Game Informer
 that the realistic domestic dynamic between the Bonnie-and-Clyde-esque leads was meant to immerse players and anchor the story in something positive.

“We wanted to create a world that held up a mirror to that—showed that fever pitch of craziness that we are all maybe a couple of bad decisions away from. But we wanted the story we were going to tell to have something positive at its core and the message that human connection is better than division,” he said. 

Emphasizing the couple, rather than violence alone, may also help Rockstar reach players beyond the franchise’s traditionally male-heavy core.

Silicon Valley’s ‘last wife guy’

Musk’s reply carried some history. He and Zuckerberg once came close to a cage match. Both later attended President Donald Trump’s second inauguration, and Zuckerberg 
texted Musk offering to help with his Department of Government Efficiency effort
, according to court documents. Some users answered with their own 
GTA
-style notifications, including one with a mission to “fight Elon Musk.”

The post also landed during a rough stretch for tech leaders’ personal lives. 
Nvidia
 CEO Jensen Huang resigned on Nov. 12 after admitting to an affair with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy. 
Nvidia’s board reinstated him 11 days later
, after Trump pressed for his return.

Against that backdrop, and after other tech billionaires, including Jeff Bezos and Bill Gates, faced public reckonings over infidelity, a commenter on his post dubbed him Silicon Valley’s “last wife guy.” It’s an unexpected image for Zuckerberg, who said on Joe Rogan’s podcast in January 2025 that corporate America needed more “masculine energy.” 

Zuckerberg met Priscilla Chan, a pediatrician with whom he has three children, in a bathroom line at a Harvard fraternity party. The couple married the day after Facebook went public in 2012.

Since then, Zuckerberg has made grand gestures a habit. He recorded a cover of “Get Low,” the song playing when they met, with T-Pain. He also commissioned a 7-foot statue of Chan and posted it on Instagram with the caption, “bringing back the Roman tradition of making sculptures of your wife.” A grocery run, even a virtual one, is a smaller ask.

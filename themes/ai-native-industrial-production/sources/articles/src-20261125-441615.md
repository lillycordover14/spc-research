Nvidia stock stumbles again after Jensen Huang's spectacular comeback rally
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Brian Sozzi   
 
·
 Executive Editor
 
 
Wed, November 25, 2026 at 7:52 AM CST
 
1 min read
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
NVDA
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The imperial CEO meets Wall Street's wall of worry on AI.
 
 
Shares of Nvidia (
NVDA
) fell more than 6.3% in premarket trading on Wednesday following a 5.86% drop on Tuesday, as investor fears over the health of the AI trade returned.
 
 
The two-day pullback comes hot on the heels of a 31.2% surge on Monday on news that founder Jensen Huang had been 
reinstated
 as CEO, just 12 days after he resigned amid a personal scandal and federal chip-export probes.
 
 
 
The dramatic 
reversal
 followed intense lobbying from President 
Trump
 and Vice President JD Vance, who intervened directly with directors and said Huang had been cleared of national security concerns.
 
 
Huang said at an all-hands meeting on Tuesday that he was in therapy, but struck a defiant tone on his management style and lack of succession planning.
 
 
 
 
 
Despite the initial 
rally
, Nvidia still faces Justice Department and SEC investigations into alleged chip smuggling through blacklisted Singapore-based firm Megaspeed, which prompted the board to appoint a new chief compliance officer.
 
 
Huang is also scheduled to testify before Congress next week, in a closed-door House interview on Dec. 1 and a public Senate Banking Committee hearing on Dec. 3.

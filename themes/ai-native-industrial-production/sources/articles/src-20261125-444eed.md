It’s become a tradition at NJ101.5 to create a parody of The Twelve Days of Christmas with lyrics specifically about New Jersey and what is going on in the news that year.
Let’s be real, it’s about time we updated the song.
Nobody realistically wants or needs 
that
 many birds. We need to get a little more honest about what is being gifted to you.
On the Deminski and Moore Show we had New Jerseyans call in with their suggestions and I think we’ve got a pretty good one for your 2026 holiday season.
Photo by 
Flaviu Costin
 on

a group of children standing next to each other
The best part? It’s not just a theoretical song, we actually get to hear it on the New Jersey airwaves.
For the last two Decembers we have been lucky enough to work with the 
Legacy Show Choir
 at Hillsborough High School (under the watch of Director of Choral Activities, Ashley Mathews) to bring ‘The Twelve Days of Jersey’ to life.
The talented students bring our Garden State grievances to life and we’ll definitely be playing their rendition on the air through the month of December.
By the way, if you want to see them in person, they have a show on Dec. 10 at Hillsborough High School!
Check out the lyrics so you can sing along through the Christmas season.

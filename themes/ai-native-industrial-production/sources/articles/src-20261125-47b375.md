Kindergarten Wars Anime's Video Reveals More Staff, ado's Opening Song, April Debut

posted on 
2026-11-25
 04:00 EST
 by Rafael Antonio Pineda

Ado performs anime's opening theme "Bibayatarametta"

Bandai Namco Filmworks
 unveiled the first full promotional video and main visual for the television 
anime
 of 
Yū Chiba
's 
Kindergarten WARS
 (
Yōchien Wars
) manga on Wednesday. The video announces more staff members and the April premiere for the anime, and also announces and previews the anime's opening theme song "Bibayatarametta" by singer 
Ado
.

Image courtesy of Bandai Namco Filmworks
©千葉侑生／集英社・幼稚園 WARS 製作委員会
As previously announced, 
Shinsuke Gomi
 (episode director for 
Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba
, 
Secrets of the Silent Witch
) is directing the anime at 
Sunrise
 and 
Felix Film
, and 
Junko Yamanaka
 (
Kamisama Kiss
, 
Servamp
) is designing the characters.
Takaomi Kanasaki
 (
KONOSUBA
 director, 
Princess Connect! Re:Dive
 director and writer, 
Secrets of the Silent Witch
 chief director and writer) joins the staff, and is writing and supervising the series' scripts. 
Satoru Kuwabara
 is the art director, while 
Saori Yoshiida
 is credited for color setting. 
Masaomi Suzuki
 is the CG director. 
Naoki Etō
 is the compositing director of photography, while 
Kashiko Kimura
 is the editor. 
Ryoshi Tanaka
 is the sound director. 
Hiroshi Nakamura
, 
Kenta Higashiohji
, and 
Dai Haraguchi
 are composing the music.
Image courtesy of Bandai Namco Filmworks
©千葉侑生／集英社・幼稚園 WARS 製作委員会
Yen Press
 is publishing the manga in print and it describes the story:
Rita is Convict 999, a former legendary assassin who now works at a kindergarten for the children of world leaders. Her job is to protect the schoolchildren from the clutches of would-be assassins sent after them! But even with her overwhelming strength, Rita has a weakness—she's head-over-heels for good-looking men!
The anime stars:

Atsumi Tanezaki
 as Rita

Kentarō Kumagai
 as Doug

Hitomi Ohwada
 as Hana

Satoshi Hino
 as Luke

Mariya Ise
 as Silvia

Romi Park
 as Erina

Tetsu Inada
 as Gordon

Mirei Kumagai
 as Mac

Sumire Morohoshi
 as Una

Shueisha
's 
MANGA Plus
 website 
publishes
 the 
Kindergarten WARS
 manga digitally. 
Chiba launched the manga on 
Shueisha
's 
Shonen Jump+
 app and website in September 2022. 
Shueisha
 published the manga's 18th compiled book volume on August 4. The manga 
entered
 its final arc in March 2025, and has over 2 million copies in circulation.
Chiba 
launched
 the 
Yōchien Wars -LUKE-
 (
Kindergarten WARS -LUKE-
) spinoff manga in November 2024. The spinoff manga is a 180-page story about the character Luke.

US rare earths refiner Phoenix Tailings has secured FAST-41 Transparency coverage for its planned $375-million rare earths Rochester separation facility, in Rochester, New York, as part of a larger $1.2-billion Project Freedom metallisation platform that the company is building.

The FAST-41 status provides a more coordinated and predictable permitting pathway for the project across federal, state and local government levels as the company advances toward commercial-scale operations.

FAST-41 - a US federal permitting process established under the Fixing America's Surface Transportation Act of 2015 - was established to improve the transparency and predictability of certain critical infrastructure projects, enabling faster authorisations and reviews.

"We cannot wait a decade. American sovereignty depends on our ability to produce the materials that power modern society. FAST-41 Transparency coverage brings incredible visibility and coordination to the permitting process, ensuring we move the Freedom separation facility in Rochester, New York, forward with the speed, safety and environmental responsibility the American people deserve," says Phoenix Tailings CEO and co-founder 
Nick
 
Myers
.

"Our two projects will help bring an end to US dependence on foreign adversaries and develop a cutting-edge future for all Americans," he adds.

The US's Department of War will serve as the lead federal permitting agency for the Rochester separation facility, coordinating the federal permitting process and working across agencies to support an efficient and transparent review.

Phoenix's FAST-41 coverage comes as the US Senate passed the bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act of 2026, which is a broad permitting reform package designed to accelerate environmental reviews, improve coordination and timelines, and provide greater certainty for major American energy, infrastructure and critical mineral projects.

The legislation, which has received strong White House support and now moves to the House of Representatives, reinforces the same objective as Phoenix’s FAST-41 coverage: making it possible to build critical American industrial capacity faster while maintaining environmental review and accountability, Myers explains.

For Phoenix, FAST-41 coverage brings greater visibility to the permitting review process as the company aims to make the Rochester facility a cornerstone project for an independent rare earth supply chain.

Veil Of Maya
 have expanded their 20th anniversary celebration of their debut album 
‘All Things Set Aside’
 with the digital arrival of a newly re-recorded instrumental edition.

The band revisited the 2006 record with its original lineup, making the anniversary re-recording available in both vocal and instrumental formats. Fans can now stream both versions, while physical editions are also available through the band’s official webstore.

The release arrives following an extended hiatus for the technical metalcore outfit and the subsequent departure of vocalist 
Lukas Magyar
, making this anniversary revisit a look back at the band’s earliest era.

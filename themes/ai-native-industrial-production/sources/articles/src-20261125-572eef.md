For receiver Max Hurleman, it was his first career NFL touchdown. For quarterback Aaron Rodgers, it may have been his final NFL throw.
Rodgers, who turns 43 next week, threw 44 passes on Sunday in Philadelphia. His final attempt of the day was completed to Hurleman for a touchdown.
If Rodgers won’t be playing again for the Steelers this season — and if he doesn’t finish the year with another team — it will be his last throw. His 543rd touchdown pass.
While he won’t be walking off with a win, it’s a better exit than the one he would have made if he hadn’t returned for 2026. His final throw of the 2025 season, in the 30-6 wild-card loss to the Texans, was a pick-six.
At this point, whether Rodgers ever plays again is one of the many unknowns created by the Steelers’ decision to bench him. And it’s one of the many answers we’ll be getting as this drama continues to unfold in real time.

Chicago Man Sentenced to Twelve Years in IDOC for His Role in 2022 Shooting at Yorktown Mall 

        DuPage County State’s Attorney Robert Berlin announced today that a Chicago man has been sentenced in absentia to twelve years in the Illinois Department of Corrections (IDOC) for his role in a shooting at the Yorktown Mall in November 2022. Jakobi Kinsey 28, (d.o.b. 8/11/1998) of the 3200 block of W. Douglas Boulevard, failed to appear at his sentencing hearing yesterday afternoon in front of Judge Margaret O’Connell, who handed down the sentence in his absence. After sentencing Kinsey, Judge O’Connell issued a warrant for his arrest. On September 14, 2026, Kinsey entered a blind plea of guilty to one count of Aggravated Discharge of a Firearm, a Class 1 Felony, and the case was continued to November 24, 2026, for sentencing.

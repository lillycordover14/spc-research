Dance Gavin Dance
 have expanded the digital availability of material from their latest album 
‘Pantheon’
, bringing the previously leaked B-side 
“Gingivitis”
 to streaming platforms while also unveiling an instrumental edition of the record.

“Gingivitis” originally surfaced online roughly a year ago, but the gold-certified post-hardcore outfit have now given the track an official widespread digital release.

Additionally, an instrumental version of 
‘Pantheon’
 arrived on streaming services November 24. That edition also includes an instrumental take on the album’s latest single, giving fans another way to revisit the band’s recent material.

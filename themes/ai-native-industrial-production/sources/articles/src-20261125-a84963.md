The loss dropped the Huskies to 4-3 on the season, and in his postgame news conference, an exasperated Auriemma clearly wasn't happy with the results so far.
 
"I'm having a really hard time coaching this team. You might be able to tell that," Auriemma said at the podium. "I've got to figure it out. But it would be nice if I could get a little help."

While all of their losses have come against top-ranked teams, UConn has never lost three games in the month of November, and Auriemma is clearly not handling this skid (or his team's demeanor) very well.
"It's just so out of character for anybody that's watched UConn play for 30-some years," he said. "I don't even recognize it."

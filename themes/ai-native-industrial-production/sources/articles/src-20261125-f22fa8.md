NFL Thanksgiving injury report: Micah Parsons cleared for season debut, Chiefs' Kenneth Walker III out&nbsp;&nbsp;CBS Sports

[page text]
The 
NFL
 on Wednesday will debut the first Thanksgiving Eve game in league history, and it could feature the return of a superstar defender. 
Green Bay Packers
 edge rusher 
Micah Parsons
 
is active for the first time this season
 and cleared to make his first appearance of the year in the contest against the 
Los Angeles Rams
, but it remains unclear whether he will play. Regardless of when it occurs, the All-Pro will soon enter game action for the first time since he tore his ACL and meniscus last year.
"I would say I feel like I've done everything I possibly could to put myself in position to potentially play on Wednesday, but for sure this season," 
Parsons said on Monday
. "And I'll be looking forward to returning to the field."  
The Packers could use a lift as they gear up for a run at the NFC North title. If Parsons suits up this week, he could wreak havoc on an injury-riddled Rams offense and guide Green Bay to one of its biggest wins of the year to begin the regular season's final push.
The 
Kansas City Chiefs
 were seeking a similar boost from their top running back, 
Kenneth Walker III
, who missed the last two games with a groin injury. As a contender for the league lead in rushing yards per game, Walker has been instrumental in the Chiefs' offensive resurgence in 2026, and the unit will need to fire on all cylinders to keep up with the 
Buffalo Bills
 in one of the NFL's biggest blockbuster battles of the year.

Walker returned to practice on Tuesday as a limited participant, but 
the Chiefs are ruling him out
 for the holiday showdown.

Parsons' potential insertion into Wednesday's lineup would be a boon to the Packers, who in their last outing surrendered 30 points to the typically silent 
Minnesota Vikings
 offense. It would also inject life into a roster that will again be without another of its stars, Josh Jacobs. Green Bay's top running back continues to nurse the finger injury he sustained in Week 9, which required surgery. 
With Jacobs ruled out for the second consecutive game
, the rushing attack could remain a non-factor. The run game is historically bleak, as its 3.1 yards per game is on pace to be the worst by any 
NFL team
 since 2000.

The Los Angeles Rams' injured reserve remains loaded with impactful pass-catchers. Last week, the team ruled superstar wide receiver 
Puka Nacua
 out for the season. This week, it was determined that Davante Adams will not return from a torn hamstring for at least another game and that tight end Terrance Ferguson is out for the year. Ferguson will undergo surgery on the injured ankle that put him on the shelf nearly two months ago. None of those three played in the Rams' 34-6 demolition of the 
Arizona Cardinals
, but the caliber of opponent increases significantly on Wednesday, so the hampered passing attack might not be nearly as impactful this time out.

The Chicago Bears defensive backfield depth took a hit over the last two weeks. Kyler Gordon has not played since Nov. 8 and is trending toward another absence, which leaves open a hole at the starting slot cornerback spot, and backup safety Elijah Hicks entered concussion protocol on Tuesday and was promptly ruled out for the Thanksgiving clash with the Detroit Lions. Chicago's running back rotation is also in flux after Roschon Johnson was carted off the field last week. He is third in line for carries behind 
D'Andre Swift
 and 
Kyle Monangai
, but with the latter playing through turf toe, his presence has become more important of late.
An already banged-up defensive line in Detroit is in even worse shape coming out of the win over the 
Tampa Bay Buccaneers
. In the wake of 
Aidan Hutchinson's
 torn ACL, the Lions also lost rookie Skyler Gill-Howard for the season, as coach Dan Campbell announced on Tuesday that the defensive tackle will undergo surgery on his knee. And on the edge, DJ Wonnum became all the more important in Hutchinson's absence and is now in danger of missing time himself with the back injury that popped up against the Buccaneers. Campbell said that all players listed on the injury report outside of Gill-Howard will be game-time decisions.

The Cowboys had no trouble moving the ball without Tyler Booker and Jake Ferguson last week and might have to operate without the starting guard and tight end again on Thursday since neither has been able to practice. If Walker can play in the Thanksgiving finale, the Chiefs will be loaded at running back since 
Emmett Johnson

Whoever operates in the run game could be without one starting blocker, though, as right tackle Jaylon Moore still has not practiced since he sustained an ankle injury in Week 9. On the other side of the trenches, Omarr Norman-Lott is close to his season debut after the Chiefs activated him from the reserve/PUP list last week.

Most of the players listed on the Buffalo Bills' injury report sustained their ailments in Week 10 against the 
New York Jets

Miami Dolphins

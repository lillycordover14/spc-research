Figure AI: Tesla rival counters Elon Musk’s robot criticism with an apple&nbsp;&nbsp;Notebookcheck

[page text]
4

At the 
TomorrowX Summit
 on November 18, Elon Musk expressed skepticism about the current state of humanoid robot development. According to Musk, most demonstrations to date have been staged and rely on preprogrammed movements. The irony of this criticism, given Tesla's own 
history of supposedly autonomous robot demonstrations
, is worth mentioning in passing. To illustrate his point, the Tesla CEO uses a simple example: If a humanoid robot is tasked with picking up an apple from a table, simply moving the table would be enough to throw it off, according to Musk.

Brett Adcock, founder and CEO of Tesla rival Figure AI, is looking to prove otherwise on X. The video, published on November 23, shows the Figure 03 humanoid robot picking up an apple from a table and putting it back down. The table is then moved along with the apple. When the robot is instructed to pick up the apple again, it adjusts its movements to the table's new position and successfully completes the task. According to Adcock, Figure 03 operates fully autonomously and without any specific training for the scenario shown. However, whether Adcock is actually demonstrating the scenario Musk had in mind remains unclear. Musk did not specify whether the robot would already be in motion when the table was moved.

Adcock's response has drawn mixed reactions from the community. While some users on X praise Figure's progress, others question how many attempts it took to produce the video and whether the robot is actually operating autonomously. Many also consider the task too easy. They would rather see how Figure 03 reacts if the apple falls off the table or suddenly rolls away. What Tesla has to offer in response to its competition should become clearer in the coming weeks. At the TomorrowX Summit, Musk not only questioned previous robot demonstrations but also indicated that an authentic demonstration of Optimus could take place within the next month or two.

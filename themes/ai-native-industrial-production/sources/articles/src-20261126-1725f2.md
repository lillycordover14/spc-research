Martin Barr joins AJP Racing team & will race the AMCA Motocross Championship

Northern Irish MX star Martin Barr has signed for the AJP Racing team for 2027 and, alongside Ben Clayton, will race in the British Arenacross Championship, the AMCA British Motocross Championship and selected rounds of the ACU British Motocross Championship.
Barr, who announced his retirement from Pro racing at the end of 2025, has stayed active in 2026 and will be a favourite for the AMCA Open title, while also being one to watch on the Arenacross tour, especially in front of his home fans in Belfast.
Clayton is also an Arenacross specialist and has shown on his day he can pull off big results indoors.
The full team statement said:
We’re delighted to announce that Martin Barr will be joining AJP Racing for the 2027 season! 🔥
Martin will line up alongside Ben Clayton (Beanz) as the team takes on the Arenacross Championship, AMCA Championship, along with selected rounds of the British Championship.
Both riders will be campaigning our Honda CRF450s as we look forward to an exciting season of racing ahead.
As an amateur team, we’re looking forward to building something special, putting in the hard work and giving it everything on and off the track.
We’re looking forward to announcing more about the team in the coming weeks.
A big welcome to Martin – let’s make it a good one! 💪

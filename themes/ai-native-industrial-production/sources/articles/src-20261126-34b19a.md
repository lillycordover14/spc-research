South Korean economy to rebound on semiconductor boom; nominal GDP projected to hit 3 quadrillion won
Eum Young-tae
 
Reporter
입력 
2026.11.26 15:05
Korean economy rebounds on semiconductor boom… Nominal GDP projected to hit 3 quadrillion won(Photo : Provided by Yonhap News)
Bank of Korea Governor Shin Hyun-song forecasted that the nation's nominal gross domestic product (GDP) will exceed 3 quadrillion won this year. He predicted that the operating profit of the semiconductor industry will reach 700 trillion won.
BOK significantly raises growth outlook
The Bank of Korea's Monetary Policy Committee held a meeting to decide on the direction of monetary policy on the 26th and raised the base interest rate by 0.25 percentage points from the existing 3.00% per year to 3.25%. Five committee members voted in favor of the rate hike, while members Hwang Kun-il and Lee Soo-hyung expressed opinions to freeze the rate.
The BOK projected this year's growth rate at 3.9%, 0.6 percentage points higher than the 3.3% forecast in August. The growth outlook for next year was also significantly upgraded from 2.9% to 4.1%.
Semiconductors lead exports and investment
The BOK determined that the domestic economy would maintain a high growth trend as the boom in the semiconductor industry leads to increased exports and investment, while consumption recovery continues. AI-related investment and semiconductor market conditions were reflected as the key backgrounds influencing the growth outlook.
Based on this trend, Governor Shin predicted that nominal GDP would exceed 3 quadrillion won this year. He expected the operating profit of the semiconductor industry to reach 700 trillion won. Nominal GDP refers to the scale of domestic production reflecting price fluctuations.
Warning against inflation and housing market risks
The BOK stated that while the growth trend is strengthening, inflationary pressure may exceed target levels for a considerable period. The judgment is that cost-push and demand-pull pressures may act together.
It stated that there is a need to be mindful of financial imbalance risks surrounding the capital area housing market and household debt. The balance of household credit at the end of the third quarter of 2026 was 2,030.5 trillion won, an increase of 9.8 trillion won from the end of the second quarter.
In the financial market, stock price volatility has increased due to concerns over a slowdown in global AI investment, but the won-dollar exchange rate has generally maintained stability due to a large current account surplus, despite the strong dollar and the outflow of foreign stock funds.

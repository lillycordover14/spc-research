SIMMTECH’s board approved two KRW 150 billion private perpetual bond issues on November 26, 2026 to expand production of AI-oriented SOCAMM module substrates and high-layer MSAP substrates, according to 
convertible-bond
 and 
bond-with-warrants
 decisions filed through South Korea’s DART corporate-disclosure system.
The Series 11 securities are unsecured private perpetual convertible bonds; Series 12 comprises unsecured, non-detachable private perpetual bonds with warrants. Each filing designates the full amount as facility funds for the two substrate categories from December 2026 through December 2027, with no allocation to working capital or debt repayment. Subscriptions occurred on the approval date, while cash payment is scheduled for December 4, 2026, so the disclosures cover an approved financing before the proceeds are received.
Funding an Existing Capacity Program
SIMMTECH approved KRW 274.2 billion in an 
August 25, 2026 capacity-investment decision
 for a dedicated AI-oriented SOCAMM module-substrate factory and additional high-layer MSAP substrate capacity through December 31, 2027. That decision cited increasing demand for both product categories and identified internal resources or external financing, excluding a rights offering, as the funding sources. The November approvals supply a defined external financing structure for the same two substrate categories, rather than marking a change in product direction.
Perpetual Terms and Potential Share Issuance
Both series carry an initial 3% coupon and maturity yield and mature on December 4, 2056. Full conversion or warrant exercise for either series would produce 1,409,906 common shares at KRW 106,390 per share, equivalent to 3.54% of SIMMTECH’s existing shares for that series. Conversion and exercise rights open on December 4, 2027 and run through November 4, 2056.
The 
Series 11 capital-securities terms
 and corresponding 
Series 12 terms
 allow SIMMTECH to defer interest at its discretion, with unpaid amounts accumulating additional interest. The company can also extend maturity on the same issuance conditions, while holders have no right to demand early redemption.
From December 4, 2029, the coupon and maturity yield increase by four percentage points, followed by annual one-percentage-point increases capped at 19%. SIMMTECH may redeem either series quarterly beginning on that date, giving it a scheduled route to retire the securities as their cost rises.
Operating Growth Alongside the Expansion
SIMMTECH 
reported preliminary third-quarter 2026 results
 showing consolidated revenue of KRW 535.9 billion, up 43.7% from a year earlier, and operating profit of KRW 101.8 billion, up 721.6%. The figures are unaudited and subject to change during accounting review. The resulting operating margin is roughly 19%, calculated from the reported revenue and operating profit.
The disclosures frame the expansion’s financing trade-off: SIMMTECH has arranged long-dated capital for AI-substrate capacity, investors hold routes into common shares, and the company faces escalating coupons on securities left outstanding. The balance among conversion, warrant exercise and redemption after the step-ups begin will shape whether the larger effect comes through dilution or interest expense.

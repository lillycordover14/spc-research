Some said that the nominal GDP, which was around 2,600 trillion won last year, will far exceed 3,000 trillion won this year.
According to the financial authorities on the 26th, Bank of Korea Governor Shin Hyun-song said at a press conference after the Monetary Policy Committee raised its key interest rate to 3.25% per year earlier in the day, saying, "The operating profit of semiconductor companies will be slightly less than 700 trillion won," adding, "One-fifth of national income is the operating profit of semiconductor companies."
"The operating profit of 700 trillion won does not disappear into a black hole, but flows into corporate tax and incentives," he said. "A significant amount of funds staying in the short-term fund market will also be returned to the national economy as it leads to investment and dividends next year or two."
"It takes some time to lead to finances and dividends, but it is a matter of time in terms of not disappearing, but eventually returning to the people," Shin said, adding, "There is considerable grounds for the 4.1% forecast next year."

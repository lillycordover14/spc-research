It’s hard to believe I’ve been in Truckee for four years and am already heading into my fifth holiday season in this magical place. Thanksgiving will have come and gone by the time you read this, but I’m keeping up my tradition of using my November column to look back on what I’m thankful for.

At the top of my list is my team at the Truckee Chamber of Commerce. I gave a lot of presentations around town this year, and I kept hearing the same thing: “You do all of that with four people?” It makes me smile every time, and it gives me a chance to brag about my staff and all of the amazing work we are doing. They put so much care into everything they do, from answering a member’s question to pulling together a room full of people and thinking up new ways to support our businesses. I love serving this community, and I’m grateful to do it alongside them.

I’m also thankful for the partners I get to work with. Jackie at Visit Truckee-Tahoe, Carissa, Hilary, and Jen at the Town of Truckee, Nikki at TDMA, Jessica at Tahoe Chamber, and Kim at North Tahoe Chamber are just a few of the people who make the long hours worth it. I get teased about how much I like to use the word “collaborate,” which is fair, but when you have partners like I do it is easy to get carried away. My Chamber colleagues around the country are always a little surprised (and a little jealous) when I tell them how often we get to work together here.

I have been wanting to bring a ” restaurant month” to Truckee since I got here and this year I am so thankful that we finally got to do it. We teamed up with Visit Truckee-Tahoe for Taste of Truckee — Truckee Restaurant Month for all of October to give people a reason to try a restaurant they’d been meaning to visit or get back to an old favorite. I’m grateful to the restaurant owners and staff who made time to participate while doing everything it takes to keep a restaurant running.

Outside of work, I’m endlessly thankful for my partner, Dustin, and our dog, Auggie. Auggie might be one of the most spoiled dogs in Truckee, which is saying a lot. He’s always at work with one of us, and I love seeing how many smiles he gets when people stop by the office. Dustin didn’t get a whole lot of say in our move to Truckee, and I’m grateful for the life we’ve built together in the mountains.

Over the past year, I’ve really felt like I’ve found my people here. From wine tasting nights at the Pour House to floating the Truckee River in the summer, I’m lucky to have friends who show up for me and make me laugh. I’m grateful, too, for camping weekends and afternoons at the lake, when I can slow down, recharge, and enjoy the place I love.

As I head into my fifth holiday season here, I’m thinking about everyone who’s made Truckee feel like home. If you’ve invited us along, stopped by to say hello to Auggie, or taken the time to tell me what’s happening in your business, thank you. I’m glad we get to call this place home together.

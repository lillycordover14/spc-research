Fred again.. and Nike launch limited-edition India cricket shirt ahead of December tour&nbsp;&nbsp;DJ Mag

[page text]
Fred again... has teamed up with Nike to launch a limited-edition cricket shirt.

The polo was released exclusively in India ahead of the artist’s debut tour of the country next month. Reports indicate it sold out online within 15 minutes, although some stock is reportedly still available in select stores.

New Delhi-born, Toronto-based multidisciplinary artist Kunel Gaur designed the shirt in collaboration with 
Monkey Shoulder Lifestyle, Merch & Music Collective
. Its launch was teased through a series of Instagram Stories featuring figures from India’s food, sport, music and fashion scenes, including cricketer Shubman Gill and rapper Hanumankind

Design-wise, the shirt combines the signature blue of India’s 2007 cricket strip, when the team won the inaugural World Twenty20, with geometric graphics from its 2011 strip, worn during their World Cup-winning campaign.

The English word ‘again’ appears in large woven lettering on the front, alongside three Indian languages, each prominent in a region Fred again... is set to visit on tour 
this December (New Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru
). 

The sold-out shows mark his only remaining live dates this year, with sets spanning his entire back catalogue, from the ‘Actual Life’ series through to his 2026 
‘USB002’ releases
.

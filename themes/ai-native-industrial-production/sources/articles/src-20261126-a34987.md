In recent years, telecommunications fraud methods have become increasingly diverse. However, thanks to the authorities’ proactive efforts to combat fraud, the overall situation has shown signs of improvement.

According to figures released by the Secretariat for Security, 1,511 fraud cases were recorded in the first nine months of this year, representing a year-on-year decrease of 8.5%.

Nevertheless, online fraud and telephone scams accounted for 385 and 147 cases, respectively, together representing more than 30% of all reported incidents. Prevention efforts must therefore not be neglected.

Mainland China currently operates an “anti-fraud early warning and deterrence mechanism”, which includes the dedicated “96110” hotline. Using advanced technology, the system issues warnings and seeks to deter potential victims through telephone calls and text messages, improving the effectiveness of fraud prevention and providing guidance to the public.

Furthermore, China’s Anti-Telecommunications and Online Fraud Law, which came into effect on December 1, 2022, requires telecommunications operators to establish monitoring and early warning mechanisms for the use of Internet of Things (IoT) SIM cards.

Where abnormal usage is detected, measures such as service suspension and renewed verification of users’ identities and intended usage must be implemented. The law also requires strict regulation of caller ID transmission in international communications, ensuring that users are accurately informed of the country or region associated with an incoming number. It further requires the identification and interception of fraudulent or irregular calls across telecommunications networks.

In Macao, the Government could consider adopting mainland China’s anti-fraud early warning and deterrence practices within the local telecommunications and network sectors. By leveraging 
big data
 technology and establishing a cooperation mechanism with local telecommunications operators, it would be possible to strengthen the capacity to detect, warn against and deter fraudulent calls and messages.

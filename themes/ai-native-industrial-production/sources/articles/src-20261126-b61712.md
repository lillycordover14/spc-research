Quique Sánchez Flores on José Mourinho: ‘He’s a timeless figure’
The Alavés coach praised José Mourinho’s ability to adapt ahead of Saturday’s game at the Bernabéu.
by
 
 
Mehedi Hassan
Nov 26, 2026, 11:59 PM UTC
Atletico Madrid’s coach Quique Sanchez Flores (R) salutes Real Madrid’s Portuguese coach Jose Mourinho (L) before the Spanish King’s Cup (Copa del Rey) football match Atletico de Madrid vs Real Madrid on January 20, 2011 at the Vicente Calderon stadium in Madrid. AFP PHOTO/ DOMINIQUE FAGET (Photo credit should read DOMINIQUE FAGET/AFP via Getty Images)
 
AFP via Getty Images
Quique Sánchez Flores wants Alavés alert from the first whistle at the Santiago Bernabéu on Saturday. Asked what makes Real Madrid special, the Alavés coach’s first answer was—
everything
. The danger he singled out was the space Madrid’s forwards can attack when the ball changes hands.
“They’re particularly dangerous when they have space to run into,” Sánchez Flores said ahead of the match. “We have to be extremely careful with their attacking transitions from the very beginning.”
Sánchez Flores believes Alavés can take something from the Bernabéu, though he was frank about what that would require. “We have to play a perfect game and hope things don’t go perfectly for Real Madrid.”
There was warmth, too, when the conversation turned to José Mourinho. “To me, he’s José,” Sánchez Flores said. He spoke of his affection for the Madrid coach and his admiration for the way Mourinho has adapted as tactics have changed. In Sánchez Flores’s view, Mourinho has helped shape some of those changes himself. “His teams remain competitive,” he said. “He’s a timeless figure.”

Alexis Nikole Nelson
 
was 5-years-old
 
when her mother taught her to forage onion grass at their home in Cincinnati, Ohio.

Her nascent fascination with plants quickly blossomed into a passion that has now become her life’s work. 
Using the handle @blackforager,
 
Nelson teaches her many social media followers how to forage safely and prepare foraged foods into unique and delicious meals. 
Her debut cookbook, “Happy Snacking, Don’t Die!,” is many things: a collection of recipes, a foraging guide, a memoir, a history lesson. OPB’s “All Things Considered” Host Crystal Ligori talked with Nelson at the 2026 Portland Book Festival.

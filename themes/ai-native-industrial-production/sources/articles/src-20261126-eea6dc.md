In Brief:

Ursa Major appoints Kevin Fox as CFO

Kevin Fox joins from Frontgrade Technologies

Fox has over two decades of aerospace finance experience

Production expands in hypersonic and space mobility sectors

Berthoud-based Ursa Major has appointed Kevin Fox chief financial officer as the propulsion company increases production and prepares to go public.

Fox’s appointment took effect Nov. 9. He will oversee financial strategy, capital allocation, accounting, treasury and investor relations.
 

He joins Ursa Major from Frontgrade Technologies, a privately held supplier of electronics for space and defense systems, where he served as CFO.

“Kevin has run finance at a public industrial company and at a defense supplier, and he knows how to build a finance organization that keeps up with a manufacturing business as it grows,” said Chris Spagnoletti, Ursa Major’s CEO.

The appointment comes as Ursa Major expands production across its hypersonic and long-range munitions, solid rocket motor and space mobility businesses.
 

“My job is to put our capital behind the programs and initiatives that matter most and build a finance team that can support a rapidly growing business, and we’re going to do exactly that,” Fox said.

Fox has more than two decades of financial leadership experience in aerospace, defense and industrial markets. Before Frontgrade, he was CFO of publicly traded Thermon, where he led a diversification strategy.

He began his career at General Electric, spending nine years in corporate development, mergers and acquisitions and strategy roles after completing its Financial Management Program and Corporate Audit Staff program.

Fox succeeds interim CFO Chip Niemann, who will remain Ursa Major’s chief accounting officer.

UNC takes one last stab at bowl eligibility against NC State
Here’s what you need to know about Carolina’s final regular season matchup.
by
 
 
Brandon Griffin
Nov 27, 2026, 4:00 PM UTC
North Carolina has been in this position for the past three weeks. First against Louisville, then Virginia, and now NC State. All the Tar Heels needed was a win and they would be bowl eligible for the first time in the Bill Belichick era, instead they are 0-2 in these games so far with a two-point loss to the Cardinals and a blowout loss to the Cavaliers and need to beat the Wolfpack for the first time since 2020 to do it.
NC State Wolfpack
All-time Record
652–613–55, 18-18-1 in bowl games
Best Former Player
Much like last week, this one came down to two players: Phillip Rivers and Torry Holt (Russell Wilson is ineligible for this as he technically finished his career at Wisconsin. Is this petty? Yes it is, and I don’t care. Wilson will be considered the next time UNC plays the Badgers). Rivers is an eight-time pro bowler and a NFL Comeback Player of the Year in 2013. Rivers, who also led the league in passing yards, passer rating, and completion percentage each once during his career, is seventh all-time with 425 touchdowns, eighth all-time with 63,984 passing yards, and ninth all-time with 5,335 completions. Holt’s numbers aren’t nearly as impressive with 920 receptions (27th all-time) for 13,382 yards (19th all-time) but he did make seven Pro Bowls, two All-Pro teams, and led the league in receiving yards twice and receptions once. He also won a Super Bowl with the Rams. On the whole I tend to lean more toward Holt due to the Super Bowl win (Rivers never even made a Super Bowl) and the two All-Pro nods to Rivers none. There is also the part where Rivers is my most hated player of all-time, but I really try to not let that cloud my judgement so I will list both players here instead.
Head Coach
Todd Gobbell. Gobbell, a former Kent State and Northern Iowa quarterback, is the interim coach at NC State following the firing of former Head Coach Dave Doeren following State’s loss to Stanford. This is Gobbell’s only head coaching stint, though he has coached at seven other schools during his 27-year career. The former Golden Flash and Panther is 2-2 with the Wolfpack in what will be his only stint with the team following the news that former quarterback Phillip Rivers was named head coach for next season.
Last Season
Last season, NC State started the season 3-0 with a big win over Virginia before finishing the season 8-5 overall and 4-4 in the ACC good for a tie for seventh in the conference. State ended their season with their fifth-straight win over North Carolina, a 42-19 beatdown, and a 31-7 Gasparilla Bowl Victory over a Memphis squad whose coach had already left for Arkansas.
This Season
This season has been up and down for the Wolfpack. After starting the season 3-2, which included a win over Louisville, NC State has loss four of their past six including blowouts to Wake Forest and Duke and the loss to Stanford which led to their head coach getting fired. Just like the Tar Heels, they need a win to salvage their season with a bowl game.
Key Players of Note
As has been the case for his Wolfpack career, UNC’s defense should be very aware of junior quarterback CJ Bailey. Bailey has not had the season many predicted when he was on Heisman and ACC POY watchlists, but is still seventh in the ACC in passing yards, eighth in passing touchdowns, tenth in passer rating and 14th in completion percentage. Bailey has also been dangerous with his legs rushing 81 times for 240 yards and a three touchdowns.
North Carolina’s defense should also be aware of the running back duo of Davion Gause and Jayden Scott, who have combined for 1,064 yards and eight touchdowns on 220 carries this season. Gause, a Tar Heel transfer, would lead UNC in rushing carries and yards if he were still on the team this season.
The Tar Heel offense should be aware of senior linebacker Popo Aguirre. Aquirre, a transfer from Miami, is third in the ACC with 90 tackles. He also has a forced fumble.
Much like last week UNC is going up against a team that is also a win away from being bowl eligible and there is nothing State fans would love more than watching the Wolfpack beat the Tar Heels to become bowl eligible while denying UNC that same honor. North Carolina hasn’t just lost five straight in this one, they have lost eight of the past ten and despite everything that has happened this season this is looking to be an either/or down to the wire contest and the only thing going for the Heels is being at home, which has not been all that impressive crowd wise this season. Winner take all rivalry games are the reason this sport is awesome and this one has the makings of a classic.

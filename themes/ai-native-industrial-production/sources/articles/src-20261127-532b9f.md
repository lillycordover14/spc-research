The Penguins announced a recall from the minors this morning, forward Atley Calvert joins the NHL roster for the first time.
It’s a great story for the 23-year old slowly but surely climbing the ranks. The undrafted Calvert started out with an AHL contract with the Pens back in 2024. He split time between the ECHL and AHL in his first pro season. Last year, he became a key player in Wilkes-Barre, scoring 38 points in 70 games and excelling in a variety of roles, including playing some center when the team was short and needed the help. Calvert was rewarded with his first NHL contract last off-season. He’s been one of the top players to Wilkes-Barre this year, producing 15 points in 20 games.
“He has, really, a lot of pro qualities to his game that I think can translate,” assistant GM Jason Spezza said back 
at training camp
. “He’s a winger that can play center. He’s a bit of a Swiss Army knife for us. So, a guy the coaches really lean on down there in different ways. I think at times maybe it’s the short end of the stick offensively because he’s so reliable defensively, and in kind of the roles that coaches like to use guys.”
Many players have been recalled, which hasn’t always meant finding time in the game lineup. That doesn’t look to be the case this time around, despite having Justin Brazeau and Andrei Kuzmenko on hand, the Pens put Calvert in a position that suggests he will make his NHL debut tonight in Buffalo.
Defenseman Declan Carlile rotated back into the playing lineup and the experiment of trying Kaedan Korczak with Erik Karlsson looks like it will restart tonight.
All of that pales compared to another tangible step of the youth movement encroaching on the Penguins. Pittsburgh only has a roster spot available as long as Egor Chinakhov remains on IR, so Calvert’s first taste of NHL action could be short-lived. However, it likely won’t be his only opportunity to play NHL games this season since it’s expected the Pens will be shifting gears in the near future and starting to trade away veterans on the roster and open up more places for younger players like Calvert. Another step in that journey looks like it will take place tonight.

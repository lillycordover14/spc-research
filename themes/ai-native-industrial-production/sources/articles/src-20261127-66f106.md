Torva makes six figure pre-empt for Tom Wilson’s non-fiction thriller
Rights
Nov 26, 2026
by Lauren Brown
Tom Wilson © Charlie Bibby / FT
Torva has pre-empted The Trader and the Spy: Oil, Lies and a Multi-Million-Dollar Con by Financial Times journalist Tom Wilson for a six-figure sum. Publishing director Alex Christofi acquired world rights from Eugenie Furniss at 42, with publication scheduled for June 2027.

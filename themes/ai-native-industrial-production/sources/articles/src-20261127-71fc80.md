Daishin Private Equity has bought the entire 50 billion won ($34 million) issue of exchangeable bonds from Jinwoo Engineering, the parent company of JNTC (204270), a maker of through-glass-via (TGV) semiconductor glass substrates. It is the first investment from the artificial intelligence and semiconductor arm of the National Growth Fund, which the firm closed this month. Daishin PE focused on the potential for JNTC to expand its new glass substrate business.
Daishin PE recently signed the bond purchase agreement with Jinwoo Engineering, according to investment banking sources on the 27th. The firm invested 50 billion won through two vehicles it manages — the Daishin AI Semiconductor National Growth Private Equity Fund and the Daishin Growth Cap 2024 Private Equity Fund. It is the first time Daishin PE has deployed capital from the National Growth Fund, which it finished raising this month.
Daishin PE zeroed in on the glass substrate business that JNTC, a Jinwoo Engineering subsidiary, is expanding. Drawing on its own process and equipment technology, JNTC recently developed a 2.0mm-thick glass substrate and has begun building a global supply chain. Earlier this year it won advanced technology and product certification from the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy for glass substrate processing technology used in next-generation semiconductor packaging. The certification covered minute-level plating technology and processing technology that prevents micro-cracks and breakage in glass substrates.
The bonds Daishin PE purchased are exchangeable into JNTC shares held by Jinwoo Engineering. As of the end of September this year, Jinwoo Engineering held a 57.94% stake in JNTC. The exchange price was set at 22,490 won, a 30% premium to the reference share price. It is the first time in three years that Daishin PE has invested in Jinwoo Engineering. In 2023, it put 20 billion won into Jinwoo Engineering exchangeable bonds through a fund co-managed with SKS Private Equity. Since then it has been monitoring funding needs at Jinwoo Engineering and JNTC along with progress on their new businesses.
In May, Daishin PE was selected alongside Intervest as a general partner for the mid-size AI and semiconductor track in the first round of indirect investment commitments under the National Growth Fund, run by the Korea Development Bank and Shinhan Asset Management. The original fundraising target was 200 billion won, but orders from limited partners poured in and the firm closed a fund of 400 billion won, twice that size.
Daishin PE has been broadening its investments in advanced technology companies. Last year it bought 7 billion won worth of redeemable convertible preferred shares issued by aviation technology company Next Aerospace, and together with Daishin Securities (003540) it also purchased exchangeable bonds from chip equipment maker Techwing. Next Aerospace was the first company in South Korea to develop vertical takeoff and landing drone technology and is seen as having potential in defense and other sectors. The company has begun preparations for a KOSDAQ listing targeted for this year.

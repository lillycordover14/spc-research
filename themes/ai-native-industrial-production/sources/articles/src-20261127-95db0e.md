Bigatel To Make World Cup Debut Sunday At Copper Mountain
 November 27, 2026 
 |

 By: 
Curtis Snyder, Associate AD/Athletic Communications
BOULDER, Colo. – Colorado alpine skier 
Justin Bigatel
 will make his World Cup debut Sunday in the men's giant slalom at Copper Mountain. Former Buff Ryder Sarchett, a 2024 NCAA champion at CU, is also expected to compete. On the Nordic side, second-year 
Jakob Moch
 is racing in Ruka, Finland, this weekend.


Bigatel gets his shot at home


Bigatel is in his third season at CU. In 28 college starts, he has 22 finishes, 13 top 10s, nine top fives and six podiums. One of those was a win, in the men's GS at Utah last season.


Before arriving in Boulder, he skied for Park City Ski & Snowboard Club and the U.S. Ski Team. He raced at Junior World Championships in 2023 in Austria and 2024 in France. His best finish was 18th in the slalom in Morzine in 2024.


Bigatel has raced on four Continental Cup circuits. He made 105 NorAm Cup starts, with 55 top-30 finishes that earned points and 15 top 10s. He finished in the top 25 of the overall standings in 2023, 2024 and 2025, and in 2025 he was eighth in slalom with 167 points. He also has eight starts in other Continental Cup action, with one additional top-10 performance. That includes a second-place GS finish in Cerro Castor, Ushuaia, Argentina, in 2025 and a 19th-place GS finish at Coronet Peak, New Zealand, in 2022.


In 28 college races, he has finished 22 with 13 top 10, nine top 5 and six podiums with one race win, which was the men's GS race at Utah last season. 

Sarchett Back on the World Cup Stage


Sarchett skied for CU in 2024, when the Buffs won the NCAA Championship. He finished seven of 10 races that season and also won the men's GS at Utah. He also earned second-team All-American honors in the slalom. He helped the Buffs complete the largest comeback in NCAA ski history, winning by the smallest margin in the current scoring system.


He already has one World Cup start this season, in Soelden, Austria, last month. Sunday will be his 13th career start in a World Cup GS. 

Moch Opens his World Cup Season in Ruka


Moch, a 2026 Olympian for Germany, placed 68th Friday in the 10K classic interval start in Ruka. It was his first start of the season and the sixth of his World Cup career.


Former Buff Alvar Alev also took part in the race and took 41st place for Estonia.  


The rest of the weekend schedule:

Saturday: 
1.2K sprint classic

Sunday:
 20K mass start freestyle

How to Watch Bigatel


The men's GS at Copper Mountain starts with the first run at 10 a.m. MT Sunday. The second run follows at 1 p.m. MT. The race will be available on Peacock and NBCSN.


Fans can also watch in person. General admission is free, and the races finish at the bottom of East Village. Free parking is available in the Alpine and Far East lots, with shuttle access.

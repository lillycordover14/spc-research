The Pittsburgh Steelers will play the Denver Broncos at home on Friday afternoon. If you’re like me, you’re counting down the hours until the 3 PM/EST kickoff. As usual, I attempt to project the Steelers’ inactives for every game of the 2026 season.

Below is my best guess as to which players won’t be given helmets against the Broncos at Acrisure Stadium.

QB Drew Allar
 – Allar should dress Friday game as the Steelers’ third-string quarterback now that former starter Aaron Rodgers has been jettisoned. While Allar should be in full uniform, he will still be listed on the inactive list as the emergency third quarterback.

Allar can only enter the Friday game against the Broncos should both Mason Rudolph and Will Howard get injured.

Selected by the Steelers in the third round of the 2026 NFL Draft, Allar played in all three of the team’s preseason games this year. The Penn State product will likely remain the inactive third quarterback for most of the rest of his rookie season.

OLB Alex Highsmith
 – The Steelers will not have Highsmith on Friday against the Broncos as he will miss the game due to an ankle he sustained in Week 11. This will mark the first game that Highsmith has missed this season.

Highsmith entered Friday listed as out on the team’s injury report after failing to practice this week. He’ll now be one of seven players on the team’s Friday inactive list.

With Highsmith sidelined, outside linebackers T.J. Watt, Nick Herbig, and Jack Sawyer will share most of the playing time against the Broncos. The Steelers also signed OLB KJ Henry off their practice squad last Saturday and he’s expected to dress against the Broncos for depth and special teams purposes.

RB/WR Eli Heidenreich
 – Heidenreich has now been a healthy scratch for the last eight games, and it certainly seems like he might be headed for that same fate on Friday afternoon against the Broncos. The Steelers still have several other running backs on their active roster, including two special-teams assets in Travis Homer and Lew Nichols.

With RB Rico Dowdle back healthy for the last several games, Heidenreich will likely be the odd running back out once again against the Broncos. Homer and Nichols are also expected to be active on Friday along with Jaylen Warren.

Heidenreich has been active in only two games so far this season, the first two back in September. He logged just 21 total snaps in those two contests with 18 of those snaps coming on special teams.

ILB Cole Holcomb
 – The Steelers listed Holcomb as questionable with a heel injury on Thursday and that came after he was limited in practice on Wednesday and Thursday.

The Steelers elevated LB Jacoby Windmon from their practice squad on Thursday and that’s yet another strong sign that Holcomb won’t be healthy enough to play on Friday against the Broncos.

Due to the Steelers needing seven players on their inactive list on Friday, the numbers at the linebacker spots certainly make it seem like one of Holcomb or Windmon will be included in that group.

CB Sean Murphy-Bunting
 – Murphy-Bunting is once again expected to be a healthy scratch against the Broncos on Friday.

Murphy-Bunting has logged all of 11 special teams snaps since the Steelers signed him to their 53-man roster in early October. He has failed to register any playing time on defense in the few games for which he has been active. This makes him an easy choice for the Friday afternoon inactive list.

DT Gabriel Rubio
 – The Steelers got DT Derrick Harmon back from his ankle injury a few games ago and his return means that one of Rubio or Kevin Jobity Jr. will likely be a healthy scratch every game moving forward.

With Jobity dressing for the Week 11 game, Rubio was the one of the two who was inactive. With Jobity a better pass rusher than Rubio, and a better special teams player as well, one could make a great argument for him to get the final defensive lineman helmet on Friday.

While Rubio is the better run-defense option for the Steelers, especially in goal-to-go and short-yardage situations, that might not be enough reason for him to dress over Jobity on Friday.

KR/WR Kaden Wetjen
 – The Steelers signed WR Max Hurleman from their practice squad on Thursday and that could result in him being the team’s kickoff and punt returner against the Broncos. Hurleman is also likely to see more snaps on offense as a wide receiver on Friday. Assuming both things end up happening, Wetjen, who has only been used as a returner so far this season, could wind up being a healthy scratch on the inactive list on Friday afternoon for the second consecutive game.

In the first nine games, he has not been overly impressive as a returner. The Steelers also got WR Roman Wilson back from the Reserve/Injured list in Week 11 and they should get WR Michael Pittman Jr. back for the Friday game. Those are yet another few reasons why the numbers say Wetjen could be inactive against the Broncos.

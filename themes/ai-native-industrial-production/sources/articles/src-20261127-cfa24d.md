DLF Weekend Update: Week Twelve

 

Ken Kelly

 Posted On November 27, 2026

 
Save To Favorites

The season always goes so fast. Next thing you know, we’re going to be looking ahead to the 2027 NFL Draft and attempting to evaluate tomorrow’s great stars. Speaking of looking ahead, dynasty managers tend to do that all the time and it happens each and every week. This year, I’m bringing you the 
Weekend Update
, full of the headlines I’m wondering if we’ll see after the weekend. I’ll also evaluate these every week as I once again bring you 
The Dynasty Aftermath
, the world’s longest-running weekly in-season dynasty series as we hit year #21.

Each week, I’m also going to share the Deep Thought of the Week by Jack Handey. If you don’t know who that is, look it up. There are very few people I’ve ever really and truly recognized as comedic geniuses, but former SNL writer and author Jack Handey is one of them. I’ll take a deep breath and attempt to weave it into a fantasy football-related lesson.

HEADLINES I’M LOOKING FOR AFTER THE WEEK

1.) Saddle Up

Jonah Coleman
 has been a revelation for the Broncos the past few weeks. Last week, Coleman posted 111 rushing yards and three touchdowns. The numbers to really study are 9-11-17-24. No, I’m not giving you last night’s Powerball winners – those are the number of carries Coleman has been given the past four weeks. The same numbers for 
JK Dobbins
 the past four weeks? 6-7-4-1. Dobbins was suspended one game for conduct detrimental to the team and you can bet that was for voicing his opinion on his workload. With Dobbins out of the picture completely, Coleman has a Black Friday matinee for the entire world to learn who he is.

2.) Rudolph the Regular Human

It really does have a nice ring to it. Try to sing it.

I’ll wait.

OK, now that you have that stuck in your head the rest of the day, let’s talk about 
Mason Rudolph
 and a Steelers team that’s had a, well, interesting week. With 
Aaron Rodgers
 taking a fuel truck full of gasoline and lighting the franchise on fire, Rudolph has himself a shiny new opportunity (sing that part, too). Pittsburgh had little choice but to go with the veteran, at least for Friday’s game. He’ll have a chance to prove he can get this team into the playoffs but if he falls flat, 
Will Howard
 and 
Drew Allar
 are both waiting for their first chance to really show they belong. This Friday game has a ton of story lines and will be a very fun one to see unfold.

3.) Etienne of Fools

When 
Travis Etienne
 signed with the Saints this off-season, there was some real hope he could be a RB1 in dynasty leagues this season but the expectation was he’d at least be a solid RB2. Well, five games played, 218 rushing yards and no touchdowns later, we all feel like fools. Etienne had a season high 76 yards last week but we need to see a lot more starting this week against the Bengals to trust him being anywhere near our lineups.

4.) Brown Out

Chase Brown
 is one tough player to really figure out. Just when you’re ready to pencil him into your lineups on a weekly basis, he posts a clunker of a game. 
Samaje Perine
 actually outplayed him last week and Brown’s drops in that MNF game are really concerning. He needs a major bounce back outing or we’re going to see a full-blown 50-50 committee starting to from in Cincinnati and that’s not a good thing in dynasty leagues.

5.) Ant Man

If feels like an eternity ago but the Colts dumped 
Daniel Jones
 and are handing the reigns over to 
Anthony Richardson
. This is a huge opportunity for Richardson as he’s essentially auditioning for all 32 teams over the next few weeks should he keep the job. Indianapolis once thought he was the franchise savior but the Colts have done nearly everything possible to keep him off the field after benching him years ago. We’ll see if he’s developed at all over the past couple of seasons. We know he has talent but this is certainly his last chance.

6.) Second Fiddle (Ukelele) or Uiagalelei

Backup quarterbacks always play second fiddle in terms of practice reps but both 
Tommy DeVito
 of the Patriots and 
DJ Uiagalelei
 of the Chargers had theirs ramped up and may end up facing one another if 
Justin Herbert
 (concussion) can’t go this week. Dynasty managers will clearly be fiddling with their lineups (yes, I said it) all the way until game time and scrambling to find another option. If they’re stuck, will either of these players do anything to help us out this week as our second or third string (yep) option?

7.) Sam, I am

Will the real 
Sam Darnold
 please stand up? The Seahawks quarterback is set to play this week and he’s going to need to play well against San Francisco to keep 
Drew Lock
 off the field moving further. Lock has simply played better than Darnold this year and Seattle isn’t content just fading away into the night. Darnold’s ten touchdowns and nine interceptions in seven games haven’t been good enough and Lock (seven touchdowns and no interceptions) is starting to make this really interesting. This game is huge for the future of the franchise and dynasty managers need to know who they can count on.

8.) Allen Town

Billy Joel’s song “Allentown” is said to be about resilience over giving up. Ironically, this could really apply to 
Braelon Allen
 (or even 
Keenan Allen
) as the Jets running back has fought back against 
Breece Hall
 just overtaking this backfield upon Hall’s return from injury. Allen had 16 carries last week compared to Hall’s 20 and averaged a little bit more per carry (4.7 to 4.3) in the process. While it’s a small sample size, dynasty managers want to know if this is a theme of what’s to come. The Jets play the hapless Dolphins and both running backs might just be lineup worthy.

9.) Clash of the Titans

It’s been rough for the Tennessee Titans over the past few years. However, we’re finally seeing some glimmers of hope as the Titans have seen some real progress from 
Cam Ward
. Reports indicate they essentially just took the training wheels off and opened up the offense instead of trying to continue being ultra-conservative with him. It’s worked over the past few weeks as Ward has looked like a completely different player. The question now is if he can maintain weekly value for 
Carnell Tate
 and 
Tony Pollard
 both each week, not to mention players like 
Chimere Dike
 or even 
Calvin Ridley
. Will all these numbers clash with Pollard having big weeks and Tate having others or will Ward truly turn the corner. This week’s game against a stout Jaguars team will give us a big clue.

10.) Baltimore Merry Land

The addition of 
Jacoby Brissett
 has given a second life to a Baltimore offense that was going nowhere fast with 
Joe Fagnano
 at the helm. With another week of practice to get him comfortable, he’s going to need to perform well in what looks to be a “must win” for both teams as the Ravens face the Texans. Dynasty managers are relying on 
Zay Flowers
 and 
Derrick Henry
 specifically as we inch closer to the playoffs and need to be confident they’re going to be able to contribute here in the next few weeks. If Brissett takes a step back, all bets are off.

DEEP THOUGHT OF THE WEEK

“It takes a big man to cry, but it takes a bigger man to laugh at that man.”

LESSON FROM THIS WEEK’S DEEP THOUGHT

This is really what fantasy football is about.

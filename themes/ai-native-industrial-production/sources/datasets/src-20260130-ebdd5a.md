USGS: overall value of US mineral production rose 5.6% to $112 billion in 2025, driven by precious metals prices. Metal production $38.1 billion (+13%); industrial minerals $73.7 billion. Mineral-reliant industries represented $4.09 trillion in 2025. US reliant on imports from China as a major source for 14 of the 33 critical minerals for which the US most depends on imports; 14 minerals China restricts from export to the US. Net imports of processed metals and materials more than doubled from $77 billion (2024) to $185 billion in 2025, including industrial stockpiling.

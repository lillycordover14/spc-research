What this page does not count Three other funding lines are announced in the same words. A release that says the Department is investing in the industrial base is not evidence of a Title III award. These lines carry their own authorities and their own conditions, and a buyer reading a supplier's press release needs to know which one it came from. Line Why it is not on this page Industrial Base Analysis and Sustainment (IBAS) A separate appropriation and a separate authority. Announced in near-identical language, including the phrase “Department of War invests”. The largest example in this window is the $450M Elmet Group tungsten investment (Sept. 14, 2026): its release states the redeemable preferred equity investment is made through the Industrial Base Analysis and Sustainment program, and neither “Defense Production Act” nor “Title III” appears in it. Also in this window: the $37.2M Principal Mineral Company copper foil award (Aug 31, 2026), the $22.1M radiation-testing award (Aug 31, 2026), the $174M Alcoa gallium equity investment (Aug 31, 2026), the $100M Atalco follow-on and $35.6M Trilogy Metals equity (Aug 28, 2026), and the $85.5M Strategic Bauxite USA agreement (Aug 7, 2026). Office of Strategic Capital (OSC) loans Credit, not Title III investment, and funded in this window from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. Example: the $150M MP Materials loan announced Aug 10, 2025. APFIT and direct-to-supplier equity Procurement and equity instruments announced by the same offices. Example: the $1B L3Harris convertible preferred equity letter of intent announced Jan 13, 2026. Sources Department of War news releases . The announcement of record. Announcements routinely lag the award by weeks or months, and the release usually states the date of the award itself. Industrial Base Policy Investment Intelligence Center . Launched June 25, 2026. Maps DPA and IBAS investments since 2015 with vendor, obligated value, place of performance, sector and start date. It carries awards that were never announced in a release, and it lags the releases by roughly a month at the head of the record. Used here for verification and for the two awards with no release. Data are read, not redistributed. Federal Register, presidential determinations under DPA section 303 . The Federal Register does not publish individual Title III awards. It publishes the section 303 determinations that authorize Title III action in a sector. Five were published April 23, 2026, on natural gas capacity, domestic petroleum, grid infrastructure, large-scale energy infrastructure and coal supply chains.

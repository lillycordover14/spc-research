TAKEAWAYS

- Icomat's core differentiator is its advanced design and simulation for composite materials, enabling tighter fiber alignment and higher strength, which is especially valuable for complex, low-volume automotive and aerospace applications.

- The expert highlights that while Icomat's technology is not a 'revolution' akin to the Bessemer process for steel, it is a significant advancement, but faces future competition from bio-based composites and graphene as ESG and cost pressures increase.

- Mining.com, Nov 3, 2025: Vulcan Elements (private, North Carolina) and ReElement Technologies $1.4B partnership with US government; Commerce and Pentagon announced financing and potential equity stakes.
- Instruments: Department of War OSC $620M direct loan to Vulcan; OSC $80M direct loan to ReElement matched by private capital; Commerce $50M CHIPS and Science Act incentives to Vulcan; Commerce $50M equity in Vulcan; $550M private capital for Vulcan; DoW warrants in both. Itemized sum ~$1.30B; remainder likely ReElement private match (not stated).
- Vulcan: sintered NdFeB magnets for defense and commercial; plans to build, commission and operate a 10,000 t/yr facility; multi-state site search, location 'in the coming weeks'. Combined vertically integrated chain already operating; focus on recycling end-of-life magnets and e-waste. ReElement expands recycling/processing with the $80M loan.
- Prior: Aug 2025 commercial-scale offtake agreement for light and heavy rare earth oxides.
- Applications: AI data centers, semiconductor equipment, satellites, drones, robotics, electric motors, military platforms.
- Quotes: Howard Lutnick (Commerce Secretary): 'Our investment in Vulcan Elements will accelerate US production of rare earth magnets for American manufacturers'; 'We are laser-focused on bringing critical mineral and rare earth manufacturing back home'. John Maslin (CEO Vulcan, former Navy officer): 'I am honored by the trust that the American government and American people have placed in Vulcan Elements'; company 'remain[s] focused on execution and performance'.
- Context from Defense News (Aug 25, 2025): $65M Series A led by Altimeter Capital with One Investment Management; >$10M DoD contracts, nine in eight months, across all branches, direct and via primes (ground vehicles, shipbuilding, munitions, drones, satellites); pilot facility 10 t in Durham, opened March 31, 2025; several hundred t within a few years, thousands by end of decade; process validated by DOE Ames Lab; US and allied inputs only, 'decoupled' from China in ownership, equipment, software, raw materials; founder John Maslin; China 94% of global magnet supply, export cutoff April 2025; FY2023 NDAA magnet strategy; provenance rules ban Chinese inputs in defense magnets. Maslin: 'From day zero this company was built to make magnets for defense, aerospace and critical economic industries in that order.'
- Later re-report (samsearch, Sept 14, 2026): '$700M in conditional loans' for Vulcan and ReElement; EMAT 10,000 t midstream target by Nov 2026; USA Rare Earth up to $1.6B Commerce offer; ~$2.3B combined. Treat as secondary.

Contracts for March 17, 2026

ARMY

Hadrian Automation Inc., Torrance, California, was awarded a firm-fixed-price contract for advanced automation manufacturing. The amount of this action is $39,200,000 with a total cumulative face value of $80,000,000. Bids were solicited via the internet with one received. Work will be performed in Texarkana, Texas, with an estimated completion date of March 16, 2027. Fiscal 2026 aircraft procurement, Army funds in the amount of $39,200,000 were obligated at the time of the award. Army Contracting Command, Detroit Arsenal, Michigan, is the contracting activity (W911RQ-26-C-A011).

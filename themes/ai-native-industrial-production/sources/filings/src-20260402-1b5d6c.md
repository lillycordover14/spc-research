Duties now apply to full customs value and do not stack across metals. Steel and aluminum had gone from 25% (March 2025) to 50% in June 2025; the UK keeps 25%/15%. Grid and industrial equipment get a temporary 15% rate to Jan 1, 2028. The derivative inclusions process is terminated. Russia aluminum stays at 200%.'

- Proclamation: 'Strengthening Actions Taken to Adjust Imports of Aluminum, Steel, And Copper into the United States', April 2, 2026; effective April 6, 2026 12:01 a.m. EDT; annexes I-A, I-B, II, III, IV; no in-transit exception.
- Structure: duties on full customs value (replaces metal/non-metal content split); no stacking across steel/aluminum/copper. Annex I-A 50% (steel HTSUS ch. 72 and some 73; aluminum ch. 76; copper ch. 74). Annex I-B 25% on derivatives not almost entirely metal. 10% if US inputs (aluminum smelted and cast in US; steel melted and poured in US; copper smelted and cast in US). Previously US-input derivatives were fully exempt.
- Annex III: certain steel/aluminum industrial and grid equipment at greater of 15% or MFN (10% with US inputs) until Jan 1, 2028, then 25% stacking with MFN; not for Belarus, Cuba, North Korea, Russia.
- UK: retains preference from June 2025 25%->50% increase; I-A 25%, I-B 15%; UK melt/pour and smelt/cast rules. EU/Japan: civil aircraft and parts excepted under reciprocal trade agreements. Russia aluminum 200% (Proclamation 10522, Feb 24, 2023).
- Removals: Annex II derivatives move to global 10% Section 122 tariff (in effect to July 24, 2026). De minimis: aggregate metal weight < 15% (outside ch. 72/73/74/76). Motorcycle parts (ch. 84/85/87) exempt.
- Mechanics: CBP to require copper smelt/cast country reporting (already for steel/aluminum). FTZ privileged foreign status required from April 6, 2026. Manufacturing drawback only for I-B/III goods not under AD/CVD from UK, EU, Japan, Korea, Mexico, Canada or ART partners. Chapter 98 allowed except 9802.00.60 assessed on full value.
- Inclusions: prior derivative inclusions process terminated; Commerce/USTR internal process can add products to I-A (50%) or I-B (25%).
- History: 25% (March 2025) to 50% (June 2025) for steel and aluminum; copper 232 action 2025; this April 2026 proclamation consolidates.

Item 2. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations
      Manufacturing, Service and Retailing
      Industrial products
      The industrial products group includes complex metal components and products for aerospace, power and general industrial
markets (Precision Castparts Corp. (PCC)), specialty chemicals (The Lubrizol Corporation (Lubrizol)), metal cutting tools/systems
(IMC International Metalworking Companies (IMC)), and Marmon Holdings, Inc. (Marmon) which consists of numerous autonomous
manufacturing, service and leasing businesses aggregated into twelve groups. Other industrial products group members also produce
equipment and systems for the livestock and agricultural industries (CTB International), drag reducing agents for pipelines
(LiquidPower Specialty Products), structural steel fabrication products (W&W|AFCO) and beginning in August 2025, rodent control
products (Bell Laboratories). On January 2, 2026, Berkshire acquired a chemicals business (OxyChem) from Occidental Petroleum
Corporation. OxyChem produces basic chemicals.
      Revenues of the industrial products group were $12.2 billion in the third quarter and $35.5 billion in the first nine months of
2026, increases of $2.7 billion (28.8%) and $7.5 billion (26.6%), respectively, compared to the same periods in 2025. The increases
were primarily attributable to business acquisitions and increases at several of our pre-existing business units. Pre-tax earnings
increased $486 million (26.3%) in the third quarter and $1.6 billion (30.1%) in the first nine months of 2026 compared to 2025. Pre-
tax earnings as a percentage of revenues for the group were 19.3% for the first nine months of 2026, an increase of 0.6 percentage
points compared to 2025.
      PCC’s revenues were $3.2 billion in the third quarter and $9.2 billion in the first nine months of 2026, increases of 19.1% and
13.9%, respectively, compared to the same periods in 2025. The increases were driven by increased sales of aerospace and industrial
gas turbine products for power applications, primarily attributable to strong customer demand and higher prices, due in part to pass
through of rising costs of certain raw materials. PCC’s pre-tax earnings increased 29.6% in the third quarter and 32.2% in the first nine
months of 2026 relative to 2025. The earnings increases in 2026 reflected the aerospace and industrial gas turbine sales growth and
pricing and improved manufacturing efficiencies. Earnings in 2025 and 2026 were impacted by a fire at a fasteners facility that
occurred in the first quarter of 2025, including the related insurance recoveries. Future sales and earnings growth will depend on
successfully increasing production and expanding capacity, as necessary, to meet customer demand.
      Lubrizol’s revenues were $1.7 billion in the third quarter and $5.1 billion in the first nine months of 2026, increases of 9.3% and
7.7%, respectively, compared to 2025. The increases were primarily attributable to higher volumes and selling prices and favorable
year-to-date foreign currency translation effects, partially offset by unfavorable product mix. The increases in selling prices were
necessitated by significant increases in raw materials, energy and supply chain costs that began in the latter part of the first quarter and
continued through the third quarter of 2026, resulting in increased production costs. Lubrizol’s pre-tax earnings increased 10.3% in the
third quarter and 14.4% in the first nine months of 2026 compared to 2025. The increases reflected the impacts of higher sales
volumes and prices, partially offset by higher raw materials costs, operating expenses, and unfavorable product mix.
       Marmon’s revenues were $3.6 billion in the third quarter and $10.4 billion in the first nine months of 2026, increases of 10.8%
and 7.2%, respectively, compared to the same periods in 2025. The increases were primarily attributable to the transition of Acme
Brick from our building products group to Marmon beginning January 1, 2026. Otherwise, revenue increases in the first nine months
of 2026 were generated in the Plumbing & Refrigeration (21.6%) and Electrical (15.3%) groups, primarily attributable to higher
copper prices and increased volumes. These increases were partially offset by lower revenues from the Retail Solutions (10.4%),
Industrial Products (8.4%), Rail & Leasing (7.4%) and Water Technologies (5.4%) groups, primarily due to lower sales volumes and
the effects of business divestitures.
      Marmon’s pre-tax earnings increased 8.2% in the third quarter and 6.8% in the first nine months of 2026 in comparison with
2025. The year-to-date increase was primarily due to increased gains on business divestitures and real estate disposals, the addition of
Acme Brick, and tariff refunds. Otherwise, earnings among Marmon’s business groups were mixed. In the first nine months of 2026,
earnings increases were generated by the Plumbing & Refrigeration, Medical and Crane Services groups, primarily attributable to
higher copper spreads and volumes, higher implant volumes and higher demand in the U.S., respectively. Lower earnings were
generated in the first nine months of 2026 by the Transportation Products, Rail & Leasing and Foodservice Technologies groups. The
Rail & Leasing earnings decline reflected lower railcar repair demand, while the Transportation Products and Foodservice
Technologies group declines were primarily attributable to lower sales volume and higher material and freight costs.

Item 2. Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations
      Manufacturing, Service and Retailing
       IMC’s revenues were approximately $1.25 billion in the third quarter and $3.7 billion in the first nine months of 2026, increases
of 20.0% and 22.4%, respectively, compared to 2025. IMC has experienced significant raw materials price increases, which began in
2025 and continued through the first nine months of 2026, which necessitated selling price increases. Customer demand and sales
volumes also increased over the first nine months of 2026, primarily attributable to customers accelerating purchases. IMC’s pre-tax
earnings in the third quarter and first nine months of 2026 increased 29.8% and 47.1%, respectively, relative to 2025, reflecting
increases in sales and gross margin rates, including favorable fixed manufacturing cost absorption and product sales mix, partially
offset by higher raw materials costs and selling expenses. The impacts of rising raw materials prices are expected to have a negative
effect on IMC’s earnings over the remainder of 2026 and into 2027. IMC operates globally and manufactures a large portion of its
products in Israel. IMC’s operations in Israel have not been significantly impacted by the conflicts in the region.
       OxyChem’s revenues were $1.3 billion in the third quarter and $3.9 billion in the first nine months of 2026. OxyChem
generated pre-tax earnings of $71 million in the third quarter and $192 million in the first nine months of 2026, which included the
effects of incremental depreciation and amortization expense related to acquisition accounting fair value adjustments. In addition,
revenues and earnings increases were also generated in the first nine months of 2026 by each of the other industrial products
businesses.

 Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers. 
 On November 19, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Proto Labs, Inc. (the “Company”) elected Frank Klein as a member of the Board, effective as of such date. The Board has not yet determined the committees on which Mr. Klein will serve. The size of the Board was increased to eight in connection with Mr. Klein's election. 
 Mr. Klein will be compensated for his service on the Board pursuant to the Company’s Non-Employee Director Compensation Program, as described under the heading “Director Compensation” in the Company’s definitive proxy statement for its annual meeting of shareholders filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on April 8, 2026, including, in connection with his appointment to the Board on November 19, 2026, a grant of a pro-rata portion of the 2026 annual equity grant which was granted to directors at the most recent annual meeting of shareholders for the time between Mr. Klein’s appointment and the anniversary of the most recent annual meeting of shareholders, with vesting at the earlier of the date of the next annual meeting or the first anniversary of the grant date. 
 Other than as described herein, there are no arrangements or understandings between Mr. Klein and any other persons pursuant to which Mr. Klein was elected as a director of the Company. Mr. Klein has no direct or indirect material interest in any currently proposed transaction in which the Company is to be a participant and the amount involved exceeds $120,000, nor has Mr. Klein had a direct or indirect material interest in any such transaction since the beginning of the Company’s last fiscal year. 
 A copy of the press release issued by the Company on November 20, 2026 announcing Mr. Klein’s election as a director is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.

 EX-99.1 2 prlb-20261120xexx991.htm EX-99.1 Document Exhibit 99.1 Protolabs Appoints Frank Klein to Board of Directors Chief Operations Officer at Rocket Lab brings more than 30 years of manufacturing and operations leadership to Protolabs MINNEAPOLIS – November 20, 2026 – Protolabs (NYSE: PRLB), the world’s leading provider of digital manufacturing services, today announced the appointment of Frank Klein to Protolabs’ Board of Directors, effective as of November 19, 2026. Klein is Chief Operations Officer (COO) at Rocket Lab Corporation, a global leader in launch services and space systems, and serves on the board of directors for Path Robotics, a global leader in physical AI for manufacturing. Klein has more than three decades of international manufacturing and operations leadership across the aerospace and automotive industries. Before joining Rocket Lab, Klein served as COO at Rivian Automotive from 2022 to 2024, where he helped scale the company from an automotive start-up to a high-volume manufacturer of premium electric vehicles. Previously, he was President at Magna Steyr AG, the automotive industry’s largest independent contract manufacturer. Klein also spent 27 years at Daimler AG (now Mercedes-Benz Group), where he led several business divisions. “I am delighted to welcome Frank to the board,” said Rainer Gawlick, Chairman of the Protolabs Board of Directors. “With a proven track record of helping advanced manufacturers scale with speed and precision, his expertise aligns with our expansion into full life cycle support from prototyping through production. His perspective will be valuable as we continue to grow our combined factory and digital network offering and deepen our presence in emerging defense, satellite, drone, robotics, and commercial space industries.” Klein’s extensive experience in global operations, high-volume production, supply chain management, and manufacturing transformation complements the board’s existing expertise and aligns closely with the company’s strategy to be the leading digital manufacturing partner for customers across the full product life cycle. “It is a privilege to join the board at a time when the company is advancing its digital manufacturing leadership to serve customers well beyond the prototype stage,” said Klein. “Having spent my career helping manufacturers scale production while maintaining quality and speed, I look forward to working with the board and leadership team to help Protolabs accelerate its growth.” About Protolabs Protolabs (NYSE: PRLB) is the world’s fastest manufacturing service enabling companies across every industry to streamline production of quality parts throughout the entire product life cycle. From custom prototyping to end-use production, we support product developers, engineers, and supply chain teams along every phase of their manufacturing journey. Get started now at protolabs.com.

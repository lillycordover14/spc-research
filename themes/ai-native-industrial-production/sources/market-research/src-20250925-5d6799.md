- Report: World Robotics 2025 (Industrial Robots), IFR, Frankfurt, Sept 25, 2025. Americas press release headline: 'U.S. Lags China in Factory Robot Deployment by 5 to 1 Ratio'.
- US: stock 393,700 (+3%); 2024 installs 34,200 (-9%, +30% vs 2014). Sectors: automotive 13,600 (+11%, 40%); metal and machinery 3,500 (-15%); electrical/electronics 2,800 (-23%); food and beverage 2,200 (+21%). US imports most robots from Japan and Europe.
- Canada: 3,800 (-12%), automotive 1,800 (47%). Mexico: 5,600 (-4%), automotive 3,500 (63%).
- China: 295,000 installs (54% of world, record); stock 2,027,200 (>2M); domestic suppliers 57% share (from ~28%); ~10%/yr growth expected to 2028.
- Global: 542,000 installs in 2024 (second highest ever, ~2% below 2022 peak); stock 4,664,000 (+9%); Asia 74%, Europe 16%, Americas 9%. Japan 44,500 (-4%); Korea 30,600 (-3%); Germany 26,982 (-5%); India 9,100 (+7%); Europe 85,000 (-8%); Americas 50,100 (-10%).
- Forecast: ~575,000 in 2025 (+6%); 700,000 by 2028. US: growth expected 2025+, trade tensions weigh short term, reshoring and labor scarcity create long-run opportunity.
- Quotes: Takayuki Ito (IFR President): 'The United States is highly automated running the third-largest stock of industrial robots worldwide'; 'the enormous automation potential of the world's largest economy by GDP'.

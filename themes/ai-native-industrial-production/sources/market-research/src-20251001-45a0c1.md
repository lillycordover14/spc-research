[Fetched 2026-10-25 from a search excerpt of congress.gov CRS IF12809; publication date approximate (post P.L. 119-21).] The 45X credit was enacted as part of the IRA in 2022. Critical minerals and electrode active materials, except for metallurgical coal, are eligible for credits equal to 10% of the production costs of the given mineral or material. Battery cells are eligible for a credit of $35 multiplied by the maximum kilowatt-hour capacity of the cell. Whereas the IRA enacted permanent tax credits for critical minerals, P.L. 119-21 scheduled those credits to gradually phase out from 2031 to 2034. P.L. 119-21 applied four foreign entity restrictions to the 45X credit, meant to limit the involvement of companies and individuals from China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea.

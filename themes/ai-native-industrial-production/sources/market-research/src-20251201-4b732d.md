- First Research industry profile: Steel Service Centers and Other Metal Wholesalers; SIC 5051, NAICS 42351; last quarterly update 12/1/2025; 10-12 pages.
- US: ~9,400 establishments (single-location companies and units of multi-location companies); ~$200B combined annual revenue; includes service centers and non-steel metal distributors; excludes precious metals and ores. Top 50 companies ~50% of revenue.
- Global steel service center market ~$320B (2022), ~3% CAGR, ~$415B by 2032 (Market Research Future).
- Major companies: Reliance Steel & Aluminum, Ryerson (US); IMS France; Kloeckner, ThyssenKrupp (Germany); Russel Metals, Samuel Son (Canada); Stemcor (UK); ArcelorMittal distribution (Luxembourg).
- Profitability: volume against fixed costs; scale economies in purchasing, processing, distribution; small firms specialize or offer processing. Demand: manufacturing and construction health. Services: sawing, shearing, bending, leveling, cleaning, edging.
- Not in excerpt: margin figures, company revenue breakdowns.

- BCG Manufacturing Competitiveness Index: 42 factors across 54 countries. US leads on factory-of-the-future readiness.
- Factory-of-the-future (FoF) technologies can raise productivity up to 60%.
- Manufacturing value at risk of relocation: about $1.03T in Western Europe/Nordics, about $440B in the US.
- Composite examples: German coffee plant labor -60%, conversion cost down 40+ points; US pharma tablets labor -60%+, conversion cost down about 30 points; Korean battery cells labor -30%, down 25+ points.
- Physical AI has cut robot training time about 70% and expanded automatable work about 50%.
- A 25% tariff breaks the export case for about 90% of manufacturers.
- Survey: 87% say access to talent and skills becomes more critical; 69% say infrastructure.
- Brownfield Germany vs China: aerospace and defense reaches parity with FoF; electronics stays behind. Greenfield: aerospace and defense gaps remain in China's favor.
- US automotive: tariffs make relocation uncompetitive; without tariffs the US is disadvantaged; FoF gives a durable advantage.
- Bear point in report: tariff protection can reduce the pressure to invest in FoF.

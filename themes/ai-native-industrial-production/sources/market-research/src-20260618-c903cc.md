- Release: 'US Robot Industry Returns to Double Digit Growth', IFR, June 18, 2026 (preliminary 2025 data).
- US 2025 installations 38,000 (+11%). Automotive 13,500 (-1%), third best result in seven years. Food +30% to ~3,000. Metal and machinery ~3,000. Electrical/electronics ~3,000.
- Robot density (industrial robots per 10,000 manufacturing employees): US 307 (8th worldwide, up two ranks); South Korea 1,220; Germany 449; Japan 446; China 166 (as shown on the page; other IFR releases put China at 470 for 2023 on a different basis).
- China 2024: 295,000 installs, 54% global share; 2025 not yet published, IFR estimates roughly ten times the US.
- Quote: Takayuki Ito (IFR President): 'The United States are back on the growth track'.

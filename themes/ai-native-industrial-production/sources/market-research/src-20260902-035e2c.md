Section 232 Process
Under Sec. 232, the head of any department or agency, or
any “interested party,” may request that the Secretary of
Commerce investigate the effects of imports of a specific
product on U.S. national security. The Commerce Secretary
may also self-initiate an investigation. The Commerce
Secretary must immediately give notice of any Sec. 232
investigation to the Secretary of Defense, who is using
“Secretary of War” as a “secondary title” under Executive
Order 14347 dated September 5, 2025. If a petitioner
withdraws a request, Commerce may choose to terminate
an investigation. The following discusses statutory
requirements for the Sec. 232 process (see Figure 1).

and Security conducts Sec. 232 investigations. Commerce
must “immediately initiate an appropriate investigation to
determine the effects [of the subject imports] on the

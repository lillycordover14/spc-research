A $5B fund just bought a metals distributor that scaled to billions of revenue since 1948 with ZERO outside capital. 

1/ lindsay goldberg is taking a majority stake in alro steel. founded 1948. 80 locations, 16 states, 50,000 customers. closes end of october. JPM ran the deal. 

2/ the buyer closed fund vi in january. $4.9b against a $4b target. 40% larger than the last fund. over 85% of existing lps re-upped. $24b raised since 2001, $17b deployed, $26b returned.

3/ 66 platforms and 350+ add-ons across those funds. about five bolt-ons per platform. alro is not as much the as it is the platform.. 

4/ they have run this in metals before. klockner in 2005, public by 2006, fully out in 2007. vdm metals in 2015, sold to acerinox for 532m euro. stelco out of creditor protection in 2017, public that november.

5/ and it is not just them. ryerson and olympic steel closed their merger in february. russel metals bought seven kloeckner branches. wynnchurch rolled american metals supply into ems in may.

6/ every one of these deals is buying the same thing. branches closer to the customer. in this industry that is what a shorter lead time is made of. 

7/ there is more than one way to shorten a lead time. this one costs billions

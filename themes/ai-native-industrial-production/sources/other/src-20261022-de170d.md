- Hadrian Automation: private, Torrance CA; designs and runs automated software-driven factories for precision aerospace/defense components; modular AI-enabled systems from raw material to finished military-grade systems; sectors include shipbuilding, autonomous drones, defense.
- Founded 2020. Founder/CEO Chris Power, from Australia; conceived after observing US-China tensions.
- Funding: Founders Fund since seed; 2022 $90M led by Lux Capital and a16z; July 2025 $260M Series C co-led by Founders Fund and Lux. No Series A/B detail.
- Headcount about 170 (2025 reporting); workers include former nurses and Marines; trained in 30-60 days. Mesa planned ~350 workers.
- Facilities: Hawthorne CA initial (automating CNC machining, inspection, scheduling); Torrance CA larger facility by 2024; Mesa AZ Factory 3 270,000 sq ft, ~350 workers, early 2026; up to five more factories in 2026 each for a military segment.
- Anduril supply and manufacturing partnership. Hadrian Maritime division after Series C.
- Revenue approximately $3M in 2024 per Breaking Defense (2025).
- Opus software: factory online in about six months; Factory-as-a-Service, end-to-end autonomous. Casting/forging/additive not mentioned.
- Acquisition: Datum Source (AI matching hardware companies with manufacturing partners; SpaceX alumni); page says 2025, cited sources Aug 2024.
- Founding member of New American Industrial Alliance (2024) with Palantir, 8VC, General Catalyst, Oklo, Dirac, Atomic Industries.
- Quotes: LA Business Journal 2025: "Instead of giant warehouses filled with machines, Hadrian's factories operate much like a big computer". Breaking Defense 2025: "relatively small in comparison with more-established defense startups".

**Company background**
- SendCutSend was started in 2018. Makes metal parts for more than 300,000 customers, from aerospace and defense giants to hobbyists working on their cars.
- Customers upload a computer file of the part they want built; parts often arrive at the customer's house or factory the next day.
- Described as one of the few homegrown U.S. options that can compete with China on speed and price for metal parts.

**Founder: Jim Belosic**
- Started the company because he always had hardware side projects and wanted a service like SendCutSend to exist.
- Spent $750,000 on his first metal cutting machine, the bet that started the company.
- Company built largely with his own money plus some smaller investments.
- Chapter title "From Facebook Software to Cutting Metal" suggests a software background.

**Funding and valuation**
- Recently raised $110 million. Investors: Sequoia, Paradigm, Patrick and John Collison (Stripe founders). Valued at $1 billion.

- Recorded at SendCutSend HQ in Reno, Nevada. EP 79 of Core Memory, hosted by Ashlee Vance. Posted June 24, 2026; 1 hour 20 minutes.
- SendCutSend sponsors Core Memory; host says the interview took place before the sponsorship began. Sponsor read offers listeners 15% off.

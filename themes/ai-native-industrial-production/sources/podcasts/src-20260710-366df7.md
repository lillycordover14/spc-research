- Upstarts Media, The Upstart Podcast, Alex Konrad, published Jul 10, 2026
- Radical AI: founded 2024; New York; large new lab in Brooklyn Navy Yard; AI-driven discovery of new alloys; self-driving lab approach with 'next-gen' lab equipment
- Founders: Joseph Krause (CEO; was in a PhD program and a fellow at US Army Research Lab; cold-emailed ~100 NY VCs about materials science; Kevin Ryan of AlleyCorp offered an internship); Jorge Colindres (co-founder; reviewed AI pitches with Krause, which led to the idea)
- Funding: $55M seed announced ~a year earlier; investors RTX Ventures, Nvidia, AlleyCorp, others; Krause: company will likely need ~$1B eventually, not now
- Competitors named: Periodic Labs (raising $500M at $7.5B per Forbes, May 2026); CuspAI (joined by Apple's former AI chief); DeepMind-alumni startups; Bezos-linked efforts
- Differentiation: does its own lab work rather than outsourcing; interdisciplinary team runs experiments, captures results, builds AI analysis systems in-house
- Customers: not named; has produced alloys for customers, kept secret
- Business model, path to manufactured parts, throughput, headcount: not stated
- Strategy: more capital = more experiments = larger proprietary dataset, but $1B not required to start; resource pressure is useful; 'capital-efficient, but very directed'; avoid boiling the ocean; 100-year vision
- Quotes (Krause): 'How could you really discover more alloys in a week than we have discovered in our company history?' (corporate scientists' skepticism); 'I think it's dishonest to say that more money is not helpful. It is'; 'In the material science space, we do not have experimental data sets'; 'When you get comfortable is where great ideas go to die'; 'We don't want to boil the ocean in materials science, because you'll never drive any value'

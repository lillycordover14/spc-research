**Funding**
- Senra Systems closed a $65 million Series B, bringing total funding to over $112 million.
- Co-led by Lowercarbon Capital and Interlagos. Other participants: General Catalyst, Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Founders Fund, Dylan Field, CIV, 8VC, The Friedkin Group, Jaws Estates Capital, Sozo Ventures, and Alumni Ventures.

**Jordan Black (co-founder and CEO)**
- Co-founded Senra after managing the electronics R&D team at SpaceX.
- At SpaceX, wire harnessing was a major pain point, and about 95% of it was outsourced for Falcon and Dragon.
- Started the company on his apartment carpet with co-founder Ben Shanahan.
- Shanahan helped write the application software that scaled Starlink production from day one.

**Ken Venner (new Chief Technology and Product Officer)**
- At Broadcom, scaled the company from about 1,000 people and $400M in revenue to 10,000 employees and $8.6B, across 52 acquisitions.
- As CIO of SpaceX, built Warp Drive, a custom operating system that helped scale production from 1 booster a year to 40.

**Products and technology**
- Amp is Senra's proprietary manufacturing operating system, which digitizes wire harness production.
- Wire harnesses are the "nervous system" of advanced platforms (rockets, satellites, cars, missiles, generators).
- Harnesses can account for about 25% of a vehicle's cost and are still built almost entirely by hand.

**Workforce and training**
- Senra aims to train a new workforce in weeks instead of years.

**Facilities and expansion**
- New 80,000 sq ft factory in Cypress. Discussion covered the split between the Redondo and Cypress factories.
- Plans for a third factory ("Factory 3") discussed; no details in the page. Also covered vertical integration and the reindustrialization macro.

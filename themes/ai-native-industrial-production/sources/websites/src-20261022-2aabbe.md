- Industry: Manufacturing. Status: Active. Founded: 2025. Batch: Summer 2025. Team size: 52. Location: Detroit, MI. YC primary partner: Jared Friedman. Website: noxmetals.co. LinkedIn nox-metals-inc; X @noxmetals. Contact zane@noxmetals.co.

- Customer benefits: place orders in minutes, delivery in days; better pricing from lower overhead; every order tracked quote to delivery.
- Target customers: job shops, OEMs, Tier 1/2/3 suppliers, aerospace, defense, industrial manufacturers.
- Offerings: live now aluminum plate and block; planned steel, tube, sheet.
- News: Jun 14, 2026 "NOX METALS Raises an $11.5M Seed Round to Reindustrialize America". Launch post "NOX METALS: The Factory is the Product"; launch video on YouTube.

- Mission: AI research and deployment company that builds models and autonomous labs to accelerate science; 'technological progress is limited by our ability to design the physical world'; 'nature is the RL environment'; 'Intelligence is necessary, but not sufficient.'
- Autonomous labs: source of high-quality experimental data and unpublished negative results; each experiment can produce gigabytes of data; high signal-to-noise; 'Autonomous labs are central to our strategy.'
- Goals: superconductors working at higher temperatures (transport, low-loss grids); automating materials design to accelerate Moore's Law, space travel, fusion
- What they build: AI scientists; autonomous laboratories; custom AI agents for industry clients' engineers/researchers to analyze experimental data and iterate faster
- Business model/customers: works with industry to deploy; example: semiconductor manufacturer with chip heat-dissipation problems, custom agents trained for its engineers; Academic Grant Program (grants@periodic.com)

01 Our Mission		

			Technology to solve the biggest infrastructure challenges on Earth, and one day on Mars		

					Physical infrastructure, the backbone of society, is currently too slow, costly, and dangerous to build. By applying first principles, AI-enabled software, novel processes, and autonomous robotics, we aim to make construction faster, cheaper, and safer. 


This transformation will unlock the next generation of infrastructure and accelerate human progress. These innovations will also lay the groundwork for a future Mars settlement.

			05 Reindustrialize		

			Re-industrialize		

			Build societal scale infrastructure for a brighter future		

Today, America is changing.

Our economy is undergoing a radical re-industrialization, but our current infrastructure can’t support that shift. TerraFirma will pioneer practical technological solutions to dramatically improve both the speed and cost of modernizing infrastructure and re-industrializing the nation.

After solving earthmoving, we’ll tackle the next bottlenecks in the construction process, including underground utilities, concrete, and structural steel.

The technological solutions required to re-industrialize America will be largely the same that are required to build an industrial society on Mars.

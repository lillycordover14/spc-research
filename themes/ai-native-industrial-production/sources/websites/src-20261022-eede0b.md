- Overview: builds intelligent, broadly capable robots for industrial deployment in high-impact environments. Mission: "building universally capable robots to transform industrial work." Starting on the factory floor, automotive manufacturing first; augment human capability; safer, more efficient, more globally competitive manufacturing.
- Technology: foundation models learning spatial reasoning, object dynamics, tool manipulation from real production hours; scalable intelligence layer meant to generalize to new tasks/environments; hardware designed to work alongside existing people and systems.
- Skill areas: pick and place (varying angle, occlusion, orientation); sorting (visually similar items at line speed); fastening (misaligned parts, force/sequence adjustment, recovery from failed seats); connectors (flexible harnesses, tight tolerances).
- Customers: Rivian named as first manufacturing partner; robots training on active vehicle lines inside a live production facility.
- Data: collecting video data at industrial scale, live high-variability work; learning from skilled operators doing highly dexterous tasks; distills actions, tools, context, decisions behind successful cycles; building a model of real-world physics, variance, material behavior.
- Business model: not stated; page mentions resources, data and built-in customer base of an "industrial pioneer".

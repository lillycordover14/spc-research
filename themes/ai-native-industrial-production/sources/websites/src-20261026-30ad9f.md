Jeevy Gnosis
The coordination layer between managers and the shop floor.
Most fabrication delays don't come from slow hands — they come from broken communication. Gnosis keeps every manager and technician working from the same information, in real time, so nothing falls through the gap between the office and the floor.
Everyone sees the plan
Schedules, task assignments, and priorities are live for both managers and technicians. When something changes, everyone knows immediately — no morning huddles to re-explain the day.
Work speaks for itself
Technicians document progress and quality directly from the floor. Managers see photo evidence and completion status the moment it's submitted — no verbal check-ins, no end-of-day surprises.
Issues get resolved fast
When a technician flags a blocker or a manager sends work back for rework, the other party is notified instantly. Problems surface in seconds, not at the next shift change.

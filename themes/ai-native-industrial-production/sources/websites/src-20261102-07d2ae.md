Cultural Values
Fearlessly Iterate to Accelerate
We fully embrace a rapid iteration culture, focusing on the most important objectives, speed to delivery, quality, fearlessness, embracing the inevitable failures, and enthusiasm to recover from and overcome challenges and setbacks.
Deliberately iterating enables Chemify to improve faster, learn faster, and deliver value faster than anybody else.
Cross-Functional Nature
Our vision and mission to change the world of chemistry will require the best chemists, software engineers, hardware engineers, data scientists, leaders, operators, and a wide breadth of other deep experts in a variety of fields working seamlessly together.
Distributed Leadership
We ignite the potential within each employee who made the decision to join us, fostering a workplace where innovation, collaboration, and personal growth thrive.
Our distributed leadership culture ensures agility, accountability, dynamism, and the fastest realization of our exceptional vision for the future of Chemistry.
Disagree and Commit
We unlock innovation through constructive conflict. This principle encourages open dialogue and diverse viewpoints during decision-making, ensuring every perspective is considered. Once a decision is made, all team members commit to unified action, driving cohesive and decisive execution.
Customer Obsession
We put our customers at the center of everything we do. Their chemistry challenges inspire our innovation, their goals shape our priorities, and their success defines our own. By listening closely and responding with care, we build trusted partnerships that go beyond transactions. Every decision we make is guided by a simple principle: deliver extraordinary value that helps our customers thrive today and tomorrow.

About
Chem-tech 
pioneer
Fusing chemistry, AI, and robotics to digitize and automate
A new era of chemistry 
begins here
Chemify was founded in 2022 by Professor Lee Cronin, building on two decades of research at the University of Glasgow. Headquartered in Scotland, Chemify is creating a new digital foundation for chemistry. 
At the heart of our technology is Chemputation, a universal chemical programming language combined with advanced robotics and AI. This system translates digital code into real molecules, transforming how medicines and materials are discovered and made.
In 2025, Chemify opened its first Chemifarm in Maryhill, Glasgow – the world’s most advanced automated synthesis facility. With global expansion underway, Chemify is enabling partners everywhere to design and make molecules faster, more reliably, and at scale.
Mission & 
Vision
Chemify’s long-term vision is to make chemical design and synthesis as programmable and accessible as computing and to be the leading small molecule discovery and synthesis company on the planet – a “Chemputation Engine.” 
By digitizing and automating what was once a manual, time consuming process, Chemify is making the access to novel molecules, drugs, and materials instant and on-demand.
Chemify Team
Chemify is led by a world class team of experienced leaders and team members across AI, Software, Hardware, Cheminformatics, Chemistry, Operations, and Commercial. 
Chemify has grown to over 200 employees, of which over half have advanced chemistry or engineering degrees.
Chemify is supported by leading investors such as Wing Capital, Insight Partners, Triatomic Capital, 8VC, BlueYard Capital amongst others.

About us
Building the new industrial age
We design, manufacture, and assemble complex metal structures and solutions for multiple industries, including aerospace, defense, and automotive. 

Established in 2014, with facilities in Torrance and Long Beach, California, our adaptive factories operate without fixed tooling, allowing different products to be produced on the same platform on the same day.

Divergent is a force multiplier for every program, delivering critical systems and solutions that the world needs most.

Reshore to Restore:

Strengthen Your

Supply Chain.

As the U.S. prioritizes reshoring, supply chain resilience, and advanced manufacturing, VulcanForms has already built what others are now trying to catch up to. We operate advanced production facilities and are scaling additional factories to expand capacity, throughput, and geographic reach.

We are a manufacturing company, not a machine builder or job shop. Our digital-first factories deliver precision parts at scale. At the core is our in-house metal additive manufacturing system, the most productive of its kind in the world. Surrounding it is a unified, end-to-end workflow that integrates advanced materials, precision machining, specialized coatings, and full product validation.

This model supports high-volume production of mission-critical components across aerospace, defense, medical, compute, consumer, and industrial sectors. It also enables our strategy to become the production backbone of U.S. industry. Just as TSMC transformed semiconductor manufacturing, VulcanForms is building the platform to power innovation, security, and growth through next-generation American manufacturing.

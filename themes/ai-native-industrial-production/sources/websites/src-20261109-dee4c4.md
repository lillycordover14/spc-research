Defense applications
Virtually all defense capabilities, from satellites and submarines to aircraft, munitions, and ground vehicles, require rare earth magnets in order to withstand extreme temperatures and rapidly deliver payload.
Space systems
Rare-earth magnets enable compact, high-performance motors and actuators across spacecraft and launch systems, including propulsion subsystems, pumps, valves, gimbals, reaction wheels, and solar-array drives—where reliability, precision, and performance under extreme conditions are critical.
AI infrastructure
Immense data storage, power generation, and cooling systems depend on magnets for compact, energy-efficient motors in hard disk drives, cooling fans, and liquid-cooling pumps.
Semiconductor fabrication
Extreme precision required for advanced chip manufacturing is only achievable with rare earth magnets, which unlock nanometer-scale motion control in lithography, wafer handling, and chip inspection.
Robotics
Advanced robotics’ motion, speed, and responsiveness depend on rare earth magnets, which power compact, high-torque motors and actuators for sensors, servo actuators, robotic joints, and automated motion systems.
Drones
Magnets enable lightweight propulsion motors, gimbals, and flight-control actuators that can withstand adverse operating environments and extreme temperatures.
Industrial motors
High-performance motors across industrial applications—from factory automation, pumps, and compressors to traction motors for electric vehicles—rely on magnets to deliver power density, torque, and precision at optimal size and weight.
Medical technologies
From MRIs to surgical robots, rare earth magnets power life-saving technologies that diagnose, treat, and monitor patients in hospitals, doctors’ offices, and local clinics.

Comprehensive healthcare
Vulcan provides comprehensive, medical, dental, and vision coverage for all full-time employees at little to no cost. We offer world class coverage with resources to address employee-specific care. We cover 90% of premiums for employees and 50% of premiums for their families.

EXPERT SENTIMENT

- Believes that while there is a race for power and potential future constraints, large technology companies currently have the tools and partnerships to secure power for data center growth through 2032.

- Sees solutions like GridCARE as valuable primarily for traditional utilities and developers, not as critical for hyperscalers who already possess advanced internal capabilities.

TAKEAWAYS

- Power access for large data centers is not as constrained as media suggests for major hyperscalers, due to advanced planning, partnerships, and internal tools, but interconnection and permitting remain key bottlenecks.
- Interconnection delays and capacity constraints are distinct issues; interconnection can be improved with process modernization, while capacity buildout requires long-term infrastructure investment.
- Most hyperscalers rely on third-party developers to secure shovel-ready sites with guaranteed interconnection and power, rather than developing sites directly.
- GridCARE's value proposition is strongest for vertically integrated utilities and developers lacking modern planning tools, rather than for hyperscalers with sophisticated internal capabilities.
- There is a risk to ratepayers if utilities do not efficiently manage growth and infrastructure buildout, but this is a broader utility issue and not solely attributable to data centers or AI demand.

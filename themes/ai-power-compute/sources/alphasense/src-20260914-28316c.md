TAKEAWAYS

- Current AI workloads are typically locked into single-vendor hardware/software stacks, making heterogeneous hardware orchestration difficult and costly.

- Open-source tools such as Triton (compiler), vLLM, and SGLang (schedulers) are commonly used for benchmarking and are preferred due to their flexibility and community support.
- Kernel compilation for heterogeneous hardware is less optimized than vendor-specific compilers, resulting in performance trade-offs, but can offer significant cost savings.
- Adoption of commercial orchestration/compilation tools will depend on their ability to demonstrate superior cost-performance (e.g., tokens per dollar) and gain traction among large AI model users.

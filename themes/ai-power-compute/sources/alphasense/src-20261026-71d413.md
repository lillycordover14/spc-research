EXPERT SENTIMENT

- The expert believes demand for data center capacity, especially for AI, is unprecedented and will not slow down before 2030.
- They see flexibility and speed of delivery as more important than cost or traditional uptime standards, with industry-wide acceptance of delays and partial deliveries due to supply chain constraints.

- Hyperscalers generally prefer in-house workload and power optimization, but any solution that enables flexible power scheduling is considered beneficial.
- Speed to power and time to delivery are prioritized over cost savings or regulatory incentives, given the high returns from rapid deployment.

Southern Cal Edison work with GridCARE to use AI to find more grid capacity

Dave Kovaleski

|

October 6, 2026

|

2 min read

GridCARE is working with Southern California Edison (SCE) to identify additional grid capacity using AI technology.
In an initial study, SCE selected a constrained Inland Empire circuit to examine whether additional capacity could be identified. Working with 
GridCARE
, the study identified a potential of 20 MW of additional flexible capacity that could safely support future customer energization. 
GridCARE’s Power Acceleration
 technology finds capacity on existing grids and accelerates energization, helping utilities accommodate growth. This, in turn, may help bring customers online sooner, maintain reliability and improve affordability.
“We are looking for innovative solutions that can help us get more out of our existing grid – unlocking capacity for customers without relying on costly and time-consuming infrastructure expansion,” said Shinjini Menon, senior vice president of System Planning & Engineering for SCE. “Combined with demand flexibility, it has the potential to accelerate our ability to meet new electricity demand and make the grid more affordable for customers.”
SCE is exploring the use of Power Acceleration at several other locations on its system. It could be used for EV charging, manufacturing, commercial and other customers. 
“Public support for AI will depend on whether communities share in its benefits,” Amit Narayan, founder and CEO of GridCARE, said. “The state that leads in AI will also lead in using AI to make the grid work for everyone. SCE is showing that growth and affordability can go hand in hand. Every success makes the case for action stronger and the case for delay harder to defend.”
Researchers at Duke University estimate that existing U.S. power systems could accommodate nearly 100 GW of new load at an annual curtailment rate of just 0.5 percent. GridCARE’s physics-based AI accelerates the search for workable solutions, which utility engineers then test in their own planning tools to qualify.
GridCARE has accelerated more than 1 GW of grid capacity. Its platform has enabled economic value exceeding $10 billion for data center developers.

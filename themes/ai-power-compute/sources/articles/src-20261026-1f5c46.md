Baseten’ Tuhin Srivastava is scaling an AI inference platform that handles over one billion calls a day for Cursor, Notion and OpenEvidence
Valued at US$5bn in January 2026, 
Baseten
 reached a US$13bn valuation by June after annual revenue grew roughly 20-fold. 
Few businesses illustrate the shifting landscape of AI expenditure quite so clearly, as enterprises transfer models from initial trials into production and capital reallocates from training towards inference.
Operating directly within this commercial migration, the San Francisco, US company powers the infrastructure behind leading products including Cursor, Notion and OpenEvidence. 
To support these workloads, the firm processes more than one billion calls every day across more than 90 clusters and more than 20 cloud providers, having raised more than US$2bn in around 18 months.
The future of AI will be built on millions of specialised models
Tuhin Srivastava, Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Baseten
Scaling specialised AI models
Tuhin Srivastava
, Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Baseten, established the enterprise in 2019 alongside his co-founders to simplify the process of deploying machine learning models into production environments. 
While the subsequent surge in gen AI transformed this specialism into one of the most active segments of the technology sector, Baseten evolved to provide a comprehensive inference platform designed for commercial scale.
This infrastructure enables enterprise customers to deploy open, custom and fine-tuned models while maintaining low latency, high availability and predictable operating expenses. 
Central to this approach is the company’s conviction that the sector will depend upon numerous specialised models rather than several general ones, a premise supported by leading application businesses that now allocate from 30 to 50% of model budgets to custom and post-trained architectures.

To meet these demanding customer requirements, the business has introduced dedicated training and post-training tools alongside forward-deployed engineers who tune deployments. 

These teams work alongside applied researchers who collaborate directly with clients to refine and improve proprietary models. Tuhin says: “The future of AI will be built on millions of specialised models.”

Delivering lower latency and enterprise expansion

These performance enhancements are reflected directly across customer operations, with Speechify operating real-time text-to-speech services for more than 60 million users while lowering costs per million characters by 44%. 

Similarly, Notion delivers AI Meeting Notes with two to three times lower latency, while Gamma relies on the platform to generate digital imagery five times faster.

Strategic distribution agreements are widening market reach for the business, including a partnership agreed with AWS in December 2025 that permits clients to run Baseten within existing AWS accounts using standard commercial contracts. 

Expanding this ecosystem in September 2026, Baseten became one of the first open-model providers featured on the OpenAI B2B Marketplace, complete with native Codex integration.

The organisation has also acquired Blaxel to give each software agent an isolated sandbox environment, while concurrently serving as a launch partner for the NVIDIA Agent Safety Platform. 
Customer Performance Gains
Speechify: Powers text-to-speech for more than 60 million users; cut cost per million characters by 44%
Notion: Reduced latency by 2x to 3x for AI Meeting Notes
Gamma: Generates imagery 5x faster
Scaled Cognition: Achieved time-to-first-token in under 120ms and lowered latency by 40% on dedicated AWS GPUs
To accelerate this operational momentum, a US$1.5bn Series F funding round was led by Altimeter Capital, Conviction and Spark Capital.

This capital will fund additional infrastructure capacity while supporting a corporate workforce that is set to triple in size during 2026. 

Crucially, enterprise security and performance remain central to these expansion plans, with inference running exclusively on US infrastructure featuring zero data retention for user prompts.

Demonstrating these capabilities on dedicated hardware, Scaled Cognition reported time to first token under 120ms alongside 40% lower latency while deploying on its own AWS GPUs. 
Apoorv Agrawal, Partner at Altimeter Capital. Credit: Apoorv Agrawal/LinkedIn
Apoorv Agrawal
, Partner at Altimeter Capital, says: “The inference market is compounding faster than almost anything we’ve seen.”
Baseten has transitioned from a data science utility into a primary production system, mirroring a broader market trend where commercial value concentrates around providers delivering models with top speed, lowest cost and maximum reliability. 
Looking ahead, the next phase centres on verified trust alongside technical processing speed, with established partnerships across AWS, OpenAI and NVIDIA – paired with safety solutions such as Project Beacon – positioning the firm as the secure choice for open and custom models.

AI Overviews now show up for over 80% of Google Searches, that is up from 
50% in June of last year
 and 
about 15%
 from the year before that.  This is from recent data from Semrush, SE Ranking, and seoClarity.
It is not surprising, we reported a month ago that Google was showing 
AI Overviews for tons of brand searches
 and also Google was showing AI Overviews for 
fake or incorrect queries
 - which is all part of Google's plan to always have an answer for any query you might have.
Here is a live chart that you can access on the 
Semrush Sensor
 tool:
Semrush published a 
ton of detail
 broken down by industry, type of query and so on - so you can learn more there. Here is another chart from Semrush from that study:
SE Rankings 
published
 new data saying AI Overviews appear on 82.5% of US brand searches. So this was specific to branded queries, and I assume it is higher for many non-branded queries.
Here is SE Rankings chart:
Mark Traphagen from 
seoClarity
 told me about a month ago that they see AI Overviews now for over 62% of queries but it can be 55% to 84% depending on industry.  Here is the data from a month or so ago:
This is no surprise to any of us in the industry but man, Google is really going all in on AI Overviews.

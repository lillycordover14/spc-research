Fireworks AI Co-founder and CEO Lin Qiao is building an inference cloud where enterprises own specialised open AI models, serving 40tn tokens every day
Fireworks AI
 now serves more than 40 trillion 
tokens
 every day, and 95% of them come from models that customers have specialised on their own data.
That single number explains why investors valued the company at US$17.5bn in July 2026. 
Built by the team behind PyTorch and led by Co-founder and CEO 
Lin Qiao
, the San Mateo company passed US$1bn in annualised revenue this summer, roughly five times its level a year earlier.
Its argument is simple: enterprises will want to own their intelligence rather than rent it from a handful of frontier labs.
There are two paths forward for AI. In one, intelligence belongs to a few big labs and everyone else rents it.
Lin Qiao, Co-founder and CEO, Fireworks AI
Fireworks sits in the inference layer of the AI stack, the part that runs models in production rather than trains them from scratch. It also offers tools to fine-tune and train models for specialised tasks.
Its platform hosts more than 200 text, image and multimodal models and supports new open releases within hours of launch. 
Customers pick an open model, fine-tune it on proprietary data and serve it at scale, paying for performance rather than for access to a closed system they cannot inspect or change.
That proposition has found buyers fast. 
Daily token volume
 nearly tripled from 15 trillion to more than 40 trillion between funding rounds, and the share coming from specialised models reached 95%. 
Uber
, 
Shopify
, Doximity, 
Geico
 and 
Revolut
 are named customers, alongside AI native builders such as Cursor, Notion, Harvey and Genspark. 
Notion cut latency on its AI features from about two seconds to 350 milliseconds, while Quora reported a threefold speed up after migrating a model.
“There are two paths forward for AI. In one, intelligence belongs to a few big labs, and everyone else rents it,” says 
Lin Qiao, Co-Founder and CEO of Fireworks AI.
“
In the other, every company in the world builds specialised intelligence of its own, shaped by the domain only it understands. We are building towards the second.
”
The July 2026 Series D raised US$1.505bn, led by Atreides Management, Index Ventures and TCV, with NVIDIA, Lightspeed, Bessemer, Menlo Ventures and Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan among participants. 
Earlier backers include AMD, Benchmark and Sequoia. Fireworks says the capital will expand 
global compute capacity
 for inference and fine-tuning and deepen its relationships with Microsoft and NVIDIA, the two cloud and technology partners it names in the announcement.
Vercel CTO Malte Ubl says a fine-tuned reinforcement learning model on Fireworks performs substantially better than the state of the art | Credit: Vercel
The bet is that open and closed models will coexist inside the enterprise. 
Atreides’ Gavin Baker put it plainly in the funding release, saying frontier and 
open models
 will increasingly be used together. 
If that holds, the company that makes open models cheap, fast and easy to customise becomes core infrastructure. 
Fireworks also launched its first Forge conference in 2026 to build a developer community around that idea.
The customer base reaches well beyond big brands. 
Vercel
, Sourcegraph, UiPath, Upwork and Factory build on the platform, and Vercel CTO 
Malte Ubl
 says a fine-tuned reinforcement learning model on Fireworks performs substantially better than the state of the art. 
Gumloop moved its main agent to an open model without users noticing a difference.
Fireworks AI in numbers
Valuation: US$17.5bn after a US$1.505bn Series D (July 2026)
Revenue: annualised run rate above US$1bn, up 5x since the previous round (July 2026)
Volume: more than 40 trillion tokens served per day, up from 15 trillion (July 2026)
Specialisation: 95% of tokens come from customised models (July 2026)
Catalogue: more than 200 text, image and multimodal models (July 2026)
Speed: Notion cut AI feature latency from 2 seconds to 350 milliseconds (2026)
Cost: Cresta cut costs by up to 100x compared with GPT-4 (2026)
“Fireworks has been a key partner in helping us train and serve the models behind Cursor at scale,” says 
Sualeh Asif, Co-Founder and Chief Product Officer at  Cursor. 
Top technology partners
NVIDIA
: A repeat investor that joined the July 2026 Series D and one of two cloud and technology partners Fireworks names. Part of the US$1.505bn raise will deepen the relationship as Fireworks expands inference and fine-tuning capacity.
Microsoft
: Named alongside NVIDIA as a strategic partner in the Series D. Fireworks models are available through Microsoft Foundry, where customers such as Motif run high volume evaluations through a single Azure endpoint.
AMD
: An existing investor and a listed cloud and infrastructure partner, giving Fireworks a relationship with a second major chip supplier as it scales capacity behind more than 40 trillion tokens a day.
Amazon Web Services
: Listed among Fireworks' cloud and infrastructure partners, which the company says provide scalable, performant and secure environments for deploying generative AI to enterprise customers.
Google Cloud
: A cloud and infrastructure partner, with Fireworks AI listed on Google Cloud Marketplace so enterprises can buy its open model inference and fine-tuning platform through existing Google Cloud agreements.

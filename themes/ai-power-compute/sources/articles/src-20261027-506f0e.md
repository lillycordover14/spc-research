The town of Pittsboro issues a stop-work order for all Google Fiber construction. 

On Monday, the town issued the order that will remain in effect until the town meets with a Google representative on Monday, Nov. 2 to review recent safety incidents.

Other WRAL Top Stories

The town said there have been multiple strikes associated with work performed by Google contractors, including two gas line breaks and a water line break within the city. 

On Oct. 23, a gas line was broken in the Chatham Forest subdivision and work was stopped for the day. 

Then, on Oct 25, a second gas line break was reported in the Chatham Forest subdivision, followed by a water line break on East Street.

The town will meet with the contractors to discuss recent incidents, requirements, and conditions that must be met before work can resume. 

Pittsboro Town Council said safety, including the safeguarding of underground utilities, is the town's top priority. 

WRAL reached out to Google Fiber for comment.

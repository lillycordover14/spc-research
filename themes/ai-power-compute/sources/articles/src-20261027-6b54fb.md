Simplismart raises $14 mn series B funding led by Dallas Venture Capital

The company will use the capital to develop its AI inference platform, expand partnerships with cloud providers and sovereign AI infrastructure operators, and grow across North America and other global markets. It will also support development of a dynamic GPU scheduling layer that matches AI workloads with available computing capacity across regions and time zones.

Amritanshu Jain and Devansh Ghatak, co-founders of Simplismart.
AI infrastructure
 startup Simplismart has raised $14 million in a series B 
funding
 round led by 
Dallas Venture Capital
, with participation from NVIDIA, Accel, Shastra VC and Micromax.
The company will use the capital to develop its 
AI inference platform
, expand partnerships with cloud providers and sovereign 
AI
 infrastructure operators, and grow across North America and other global markets.
It will also support development of a dynamic 
GPU scheduling
 layer that matches AI workloads with available computing capacity across regions and time zones.
The company builds software that sits between AI models and GPU infrastructure, helping businesses configure models based on speed, cost and performance requirements. Its inference engine optimises how models run, while its orchestration platform manages deployment and scaling across cloud and on-premises environments.
Simplismart said its annualised revenue run rate grew 14 times over the past six months. Its customers include HeyGen, Higgsfield, InVideo and Shiprocket.
Simplismart also works with cloud providers to convert third-party GPU infrastructure into managed AI services. Rather than buying or reserving computing capacity to resell, the company provides the software layer that allows providers to offer AI inference services under their own brands. Its platform powers the serverless inference layer of Shakti Studio, operated by Yotta.
“The industry has spent years pushing the limits of what AI models can do. With frontier-class open models now readily available, access alone is no longer enough to set a business apart,” said co-founder and CEO Amritanshu Jain.
"We are backing this fast-growing company because of its proven technical prowess in addressing a critical need in the AI infrastructure ecosystem globally, as well as the strong execution capabilities of its founding team," said Dayakar Puskoor, managing partner at Dallas Venture Capital.

Bengaluru-based Simplismart secured $14 million in Series B funding to scale its AI inference infrastructure software, as the company reported a 14-fold increase in annualized revenue run rate over six months, according to a Tuesday 
announcement
.

Dallas Venture Capital led the round, which also included 
Nvidia
, Accel, Shastra VC and Micromax. Customers running production workloads on its platform include HeyGen, Higgsfield, InVideo, Shiprocket, Apna and Ema, across video, voice, multimodal and enterprise AI applications.

Founded in 2022 by Amritanshu Jain, a former 
Oracle
 engineer, and Devansh Ghatak, a former 
Google
 engineer, Simplismart develops AI infrastructure software designed to help enterprises run AI models more efficiently and at lower costs.

The startup offers an inference and MLOps platform for deploying and managing AI workloads across cloud and on-premises infrastructure.

The platform supports language, vision, speech and image-generation models, with tools for optimizing computing resources, managing deployments, fine-tuning models and monitoring performance. Simplismart offers shared and dedicated endpoints as well as private-cloud and on-premises options, allowing companies to tailor deployments to their security, performance and cost requirements.

Simplismart plans to use the funding to improve its inference engine and orchestration platform and expand partnerships with cloud providers, particularly in North America.

The firm is also developing a dynamic scheduling layer to match workloads with suitable GPU capacity across regions and time zones. The system is aimed at addressing mismatches between available computing resources and customer demand while preserving performance requirements and data-residency restrictions.

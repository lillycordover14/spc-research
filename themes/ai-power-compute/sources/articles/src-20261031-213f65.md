The Brief
 
Google has released data on the most searched for Halloween costumes of the year.
In the DMV, ‘police officer’ was the number one Halloween costume-related search of 2026.
Across the country, some of the top-trending costume searches were characters from popular movies released this year, like Buddy the Unicorn, Nikki from Obsession and Agamemnon from The Odyssey.
 
WASHINGTON
 - 
Google has released its data on the most searched for Halloween costumes of the year, and while many of the "traditional" costumes were underrepresented, there probably won't be too many surprises for anyone.
 
 
 
The trendiest Halloween costumes
 
What we know:
 
 
The data was released as part of "
Google Frightgeist
" which took a look at searches related to Halloween costumes over the last year (from September 2025 to September 2026).
 
Here's a breakdown of the most-searched for costumes by region across the U.S. The D.C. region's top search this year was a generic police officer.
 
Top Halloween costumes by region
 
 
 
 
By the numbers:
 
Across the board, comic book characters made up a good chunk of the top searches, but all of the top searches were about as topical as you might expect. 
 
Analyzing the "top 5" costumes from every region, Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man was the most popular overall, which makes sense considering a new Spider-Man movie was released this year. Next, though, was Dolly Parton, the country music icon who died in August at age 80. 
 
The most searched for Halloween costumes in the DMV
 
Local perspective:
 
Costume searches in the DMV varied quite a bit. "Police officer" was the top trending search in the region in 2026. 
 
DC was one of the many regions where Dolly Parton neared the top of the list. She came in second in the DMV, followed by a generic "jester," Strawberry Shortcake and Mickey Mouse to round out the top five.

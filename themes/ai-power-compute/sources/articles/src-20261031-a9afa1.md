[page text]
Now available on non-Pixel devices.

Google
 rolled out a small visual change to 
Gemini
 on non-Pixel devices, while improving the app's file management.
As spotted by 
9to5Google
, the Gemini app on 
Android
 received a couple of upgrades this week. For starters, the Gemini overlay that pops up when you activate Google's AI assistant on Android devices now stands out a little more visually. The pill-shaped search bar now has blurred edges, along with swirls of color.

These elements help the overlay stand out a little bit more against the app icons on your home screen. Google introduced this change on Pixel 11 devices, but according to 9to5Google's report, it's now available on every supported Android device.

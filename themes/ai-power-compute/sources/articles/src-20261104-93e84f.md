Google can now show up to four Google Ads in the AI Mode responses for a single query.  I mean, I am not sure if this is new, being that Google Ads has been showing four ads for search results officially 
since 2016
 but we do know Google said it will 
show more ads
, more often, in AI Mode during its 
earnings
 call.
Sachin Patel posted a screenshot on 
Search Engine Watch
 showing four ads for an AI Mode query in Google - here is that screenshot:
Generally, you would see one or two ads, sometimes three. But I guess with Google not being able to monetize those clicks as well in search than they can in AI Mode, Google is now showing up to four ads...  Of course, we will also see 
multiturn ads
, ads on follow up queries, in the US. So even more ads are coming...
I guess we will be seeing an increase in ad earnings from Google next quarter?

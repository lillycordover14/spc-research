Applied Compute Raises $350 Million At $3.25 Billion Valuation After Reaching $100 Million Annualized Revenue Run Rate

Applied Compute, an artificial intelligence infrastructure company helping enterprises develop and operate customized AI models, has raised $350 million in financing at a $3.25 billion valuation.

The round was led by Elad Gil, with participation from Kleiner Perkins, Lux Capital, Hanabi Capital, NVIDIA, Blackstone, Shopify Ventures, HubSpot Ventures, Diffusion, Definition, Timeless, Layer Global, Neo, Abstract, Nova, Quantum Light, First Harmonic, and Swish.

The financing follows rapid growth at Applied Compute, which reached a $100 million annualized revenue run rate less than 18 months after its founding.

The company provides infrastructure for training, deploying, evaluating, and improving AI models tailored to specific enterprise requirements.

Its approach is based on the premise that businesses increasingly want greater control over the AI systems supporting their products and operations.

Advances in open-weight models have created opportunities for companies to customize AI models using proprietary data and specialized expertise.

Applied Compute supports these activities through an integrated infrastructure platform designed to simplify the technical requirements of enterprise AI development.

Since its previous financing round, the company has more than doubled the number of enterprises it serves.

Customers include Microsoft, Shopify, Mercor, Harvey, DoorDash, Cognition, Clay, and Decagon.

Its customer base spans industries such as life sciences, cybersecurity, hardware design, legal services, financial services, and logistics.

Applied Compute reports that its custom model development has helped customers improve AI performance while reducing inference costs.

For legal technology company Harvey, Applied Compute trained a model for its Review Table product that improved answer and citation quality while operating at less than half the inference cost of a frontier model alternative.

For Clay, the company trained and deployed a customized routing system that reduced production inference costs by up to 47% without reducing quality.

Applied Compute recently introduced AC2, its Agent Cloud platform, which combines model training, inference, and continuous improvement.

Since July 2026, customers have completed more than 1,700 training runs on AC2.

The company’s inference infrastructure now processes more than one trillion tokens daily.

Approximately 40% of Applied Compute customers use the platform for both training and inference, reflecting growing adoption of its integrated services.

The company intends to expand beyond these capabilities into a broader technology stack for developing, operating, monitoring, and securing enterprise AI systems.

Applied Compute’s workforce has tripled since January 2026, with researchers and engineers representing approximately 85% of employees.

The new capital will support expansion of computing capacity, recruitment, and accelerated deployment of its technology across enterprise markets.

With a $3.25 billion valuation and $100 million annualized revenue run rate, Applied Compute is positioning its platform around growing demand for customized AI infrastructure and model ownership.

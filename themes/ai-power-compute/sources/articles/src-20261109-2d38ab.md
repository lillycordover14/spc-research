Key points

Fireworks confirmed customer data was accessed and said it is "actively working with our customers to manage the incident, including Notion, Cursor and others"

Notion said the breach affected fewer than 1% of its workspaces and exposed Notion AI chat transcripts used for its Insights feature, most of them not tied to a user or workspace; it has disabled Insights and stopped serving open-weight models through Fireworks 
[8]
.

Fireworks says an unauthorized party used an Oct. 23 security incident at a third-party cloud provider to reach its R&D machines on Oct. 24; exposed data includes billing details, invoices and, for a limited set of accounts, secrets, datasets and incomplete model weights 
[4]
.

Fireworks, which has more than 10,000 customers, says a "limited number of customer accounts" were affected and all have been notified; it did not give a number

.

Fireworks AI, the startup that runs and fine-tunes open-source models for developers, has publicly confirmed that hackers accessed customer data in a second breach in October. "We take this matter very seriously and are connecting directly with customers to provide appropriate support," the company said in a statement reported by Bloomberg, adding that it is "actively working with our customers to manage the incident, including Notion, Cursor and others" 
[7]
. Fireworks notified customers on Monday 
[7]
.
Fireworks' security bulletin, last revised on Nov. 9, says a third-party cloud provider that hosted its research and development machines had a security incident on Oct. 23, and the next day an unauthorized party used it to reach Fireworks development machines and run an unauthorized workload, "which caused additional customer data impact" 
[4]
. The attackers got resource identifiers, names and descriptions; billing and shipping addresses, invoice and payment history, account balances and credit usage; and, for a limited set of accounts, secrets, datasets and a subset of incomplete model weights 
[4]
. Fireworks says it does not store payment card numbers and that its inference service, Training SDK and Nexus, which keep no customer data by default, show no impact 
[4]
. It did not name the cloud provider 
[4]
.
Notion said in a security advisory that Fireworks told it on Nov. 6 of an incident affecting "fewer than 1% of Notion workspaces" 
[8]
. Fireworks first reported on Nov. 2 that a small dataset had been accessed, which Notion found held only synthetic testing data; Notion disconnected from Fireworks and deleted its data there on Nov. 3 
[8]
. On Nov. 8 Fireworks confirmed a second dataset containing Notion AI chat transcripts used for the Insights feature; for most affected workspaces the transcripts were not tied to a user, company or workspace, and Notion says its own systems were not breached 
[8]
. It has disabled Insights, stopped serving open-weight models through Fireworks, and told affected customers to treat the data as exposed and rotate any passwords or API keys pasted into Notion AI during the incident period 
[8]
.
Fireworks has more than 10,000 customers and says a "limited number of customer accounts" were affected and all were notified, The Record reported; it has not given a number 
[6]
. The Information reported on Monday that Fireworks had told some large customers, including legal AI company Harvey, about the hack, citing two people with knowledge of the breach 
[1]
.

What Fireworks changed

Fireworks says it detected the Oct. 24 activity the same day and had found no further successful access to its customer platform as of Oct. 25 
[4]
. It revoked and replaced credentials and service accounts, cut network paths, isolated affected systems, tightened access to storage and analytics systems, and disabled self-serve dataset uploads and downloads for batch inference and dataset-based training while the investigation continues 
[4]
. Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 is helping with the forensic work 
[4]
.
The bulletin says evidence links a financially motivated cybercriminal group engaged in "LLMjacking," the resale of stolen access to AI models, to its October incident 
[4]
. Bloomberg reported that Fireworks tied that group to the early-October intrusion and did not say who was responsible for the Oct. 24 breach 
[7]
. Google's threat intelligence group and Mandiant published research on LLMjacking on Nov. 2, and BleepingComputer noted that fraudulent model calls made with stolen API keys have run up cloud AI bills as high as $1 million 
[4]
[5]
.

The first October incident

Fireworks disclosed its first breach on Oct. 9. It said an unauthorized third party had used internal Fireworks credentials to access environment variables used in evaluation jobs in "a limited number of customer accounts" 
[2]
. API keys and tokens that customers had stored as secrets were likely captured. Fireworks said then that it had found no evidence of access to customer models, datasets, training data, personal information or prompts and outputs 
[2]
. It detected that activity on Oct. 6, notified affected customers from Oct. 7 to Oct. 9 and revoked the exposed Fireworks API keys 
[2]
.

What is at stake

Fireworks raised $1.5 billion at a $17.5 billion valuation in July, when Index Ventures said its annualized revenue had passed $1 billion 
[3]
. It is in talks for a new round at a $30 billion to $40 billion valuation, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter; Nvidia and AMD are among its backers 
[7]
. The company recently hired Meta strategy chief Dave Wehner as its chief financial officer, as 
MLQ.ai reported
.

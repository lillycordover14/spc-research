Adobe and Google Deepen Partnership to Bring Leading AI Models and Integrated Workflows to Creatives and Enterprises

Tuesday, November 10, 2026 9:00AM

Download the PDF

Adobe and Google will expand the reach of Adobe’s creative and productivity tools across Google Workspace, Gemini, Gemini Enterprise, and AI Mode in Google Search

Adobe will bring Google’s latest frontier AI models to its creative and productivity tools

Adobe and Google DeepMind will collaborate to develop next-generation AI-powered image editing capabilities, starting with Photoshop

MIAMI BEACH, Fla. and SUNNYVALE, Calif.
 
— November 10, 2026
 — Today at Adobe MAX, the world’s largest creativity conference, Adobe (Nasdaq: ADBE) and Google announced an expanded strategic partnership to bring Adobe’s tools to more Google products and Google’s AI models into more Adobe apps. 
Acrobat
 will be available in Google Workspace through Google Drive and Gmail. Adobe will expand its connectors in Gemini and Gemini Enterprise with Acrobat and additional creative tools, while also bringing its creative tools to AI Mode in Google Search. Google’s latest imaging and video models will come to 
Adobe Firefly
, 
Photoshop
, 
Premiere
, and 
Adobe Express
, and Google’s family of Gemini models will help power AI capabilities in Acrobat. Adobe and Google DeepMind will also collaborate on new AI-powered editing capabilities, starting with Photoshop. The partnership aims to deliver more seamless, integrated workflows that help more people-from creators and creative professionals to enterprise teams and digital workers-bring ideas to life faster and create higher-quality content.

People who create and work with documents often move between several apps to finish a single project. The expanded partnership helps minimize that back-and-forth by embedding Adobe's tools into everyday Google productivity surfaces and deploying Google's AI into Adobe's tools. Together, the companies connect platforms with broad global reach: Adobe's creative and productivity solutions serve more than 1 billion monthly active users, while Google's products and services support billions of users globally.

“Adobe’s mission is to empower everyone to create, and our AI innovations are helping more people express their ideas and create in ways that amplify their voice, while maintaining creative control,” said Anil Chakravarthy, president and incoming CEO of Adobe. “Our expanded partnership combines Google’s frontier AI with Adobe’s industry-leading creative and productivity tools at global scale, enabling us to deliver differentiated experiences and bring Adobe’s innovations to more people across Adobe and Google products.”

“Our expanded partnership with Adobe builds on our commitment to open innovation and choice, bringing together Google’s foundation models and Workspace collaboration services with Adobe’s creative and productivity tools,” said Thomas Kurian, CEO of Google Cloud. “By further connecting our respective platforms, we are giving creators and enterprise teams a powerful, integrated environment to turn their ideas into high-impact content.”

Key areas of the partnership include:

Bringing Acrobat to Google Workspace:
 Acrobat will be available in Google Workspace, giving people a direct pathway from Google Drive and Gmail into Acrobat. Acrobat will appear as a suggested app across both services, including through an “Edit with” entry point in file previews. With more than 400 billion PDFs opened with Acrobat each year, this integration will bring Adobe’s PDF solution to the Google tools people use every day.

Powering document intelligence with Gemini:
 Adobe will add Google’s AI models to the range of models powering 
AI capabilities
 in Acrobat, which help people edit PDFs conversationally, understand the information in their documents, and create polished assets like presentations, resumes, and reports faster.

Expanding Adobe's tools in Google's AI experiences:
 Building on the recent launches of Adobe connectors in 
Adobe Connected App in GeminiGemini
 and Gemini Enterprise, Adobe will expand these integrations with Acrobat and additional creative tools, enabling people to enhance images, customize designs and work with PDFs right in their conversations. Adobe will also bring its creative tools to AI Mode in Google Search, so people can move from searching for an idea to bringing it to life in one place.

Integrating Google’s frontier models across Adobe’s creative tools:
 Creators, creative professionals, and businesses will continue to get access to Google’s latest image and video models in Firefly, Photoshop, Express, and Premiere. Adobe customers can already use Google’s cutting-edge models, including Gemini Omni, Veo, and Nano Banana. This builds on Adobe's 
partner model strategy
, which gives its customers the choice to work with the industry’s top AI models, paired with the precision and control of Adobe’s pro-grade tools.

Exploring creative editing experiences with Google DeepMind:
 Adobe and Google DeepMind will bring together Google’s AI expertise with Adobe’s deep understanding of creative workflows to explore new editing capabilities for creative professionals. For example, the companies are exploring customization of a Google image model for Photoshop that could enable near-real-time edits, allowing creative professionals to adjust images with prompts and choose among variants that best match their vision without interrupting their creative flow.

The expanded partnership builds on the companies’ longstanding collaboration across their business areas. Recent collaborations include bringing Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker to 
Gemini Enterprise
; launching 
Premiere on Android
; bringing Photoshop, Lightroom, and Premiere to 
Googlebooks
; and partnering with YouTube on a dedicated 
YouTube Shorts
 creation space in Premiere mobile.

Rackspace signs capacity agreements with Together AI and Blacksmith

Deployment planned for 2027

November 12, 2026

By

Georgia Butler

         
            Have your say
        
    

Rackspace has signed two customer agreements for compute capacity.
The first agreement is with neocloud startup Together AI and is a multi-year AI infrastructure agreement under which Rackspace will provide and operate dedicated Nvidia GB300 NVL72 infrastructure for Together AI.

– Rackspace

Deployment is planned for 2027. Rackspace will also operate the associated power, liquid cooling, networking, and storage.
“Enterprise AI requires more than access to compute. Success depends on bringing compute into production, operating it reliably and delivering sustained value. That requires deep infrastructure and engineering expertise combined with operational accountability,” said Gajen Kandiah, CEO of Rackspace. “Rackspace brings more than 25 years of experience operating complex enterprise environments. Today’s announcement provides an opportunity to apply that experience to the dedicated infrastructure supporting Together AI’s inference platform.”
The companies also entered into a separate, non-binding letter of intent (LOI) to explore a joint go-to-market approach for enterprise-focused AI solutions, with Together AI providing inference capabilities and Rackspace providing the supporting AI infrastructure.
"Scaling in the enterprise requires infrastructure that can grow with our ambitions while delivering the performance and reliability customers expect," added Vipul Ved Prakash, founder and CEO of Together AI. "Rackspace brings the infrastructure engineering and operational expertise to support that growth, and we look forward to exploring additional opportunities together."
How much capacity Rackspace is providing to Together AI has not been shared, nor has the data center it will be deployed in, beyond that it is a "purpose-built, liquid-cooled facility, supported by Nvidia networking."
The second contract is with Blacksmith, an infrastructure platform for building and testing code.
Another multi-year agreement, Rackspace will provide Blacksmith with dedicated compute powered by AMD Epyc CPUs along with storage, totaling approximately 2MW of capacity located in Northern Virginia. Rackspace operates a data center in Ashburn, Virginia. Deployment will begin in early 2027.
“When we talk to developers and engineering teams, we keep hearing that AI is making it much easier to write code, but getting those changes tested and ready to ship is becoming the bottleneck. We’re building Blacksmith to help developers merge code faster and feel confident about what they’re shipping. Our partnership with Rackspace will help us scale the infrastructure behind that work,” said Aditya “JP” Jayaprakash, co-founder and CEO of Blacksmith.
Rackspace has an existing agreement with AMD to deploy 
30MW
 of dedicated AMD compute across its data centers through 2028.
In addition to the customer contracts, this month Rackspace entered into a share purchase agreement with an investor group led by Voss Capital, LP, providing for an aggregate $66 million private placement of Rackspace common stock.
The company will use the funding for general corporate purposes, working capital, capital expenditures, and growth capital, including funding for GPU-related initiatives. Rackspace said it expects to seek separate financing for the purchase of the Nvidia GB300 NVL72 systems for Together AI and is in active discussions with potential lenders; however, no definitive GPU financing commitment has been obtained as of the time of writing.

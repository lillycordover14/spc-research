Notion warns AI chat transcripts were exposed in Fireworks AI breach&nbsp;&nbsp;BetaNews

[page text]
Share

Notion has told owners of under 1% of its workspaces that transcripts of Notion AI chats were caught up in a breach at Fireworks AI, an outside vendor that processed part of its AI workloads. Affected customers are being urged to change any passwords, API keys and other credentials entered in the assistant while the incident was under way.

Exposed records belong to Insights, a tool that shows workspace administrators how members use the assistant. According to the company’s 
security advisory
, each transcript held whatever the model received or produced in a conversation, though outputs from tool calls were absent. In most affected workspaces, conversations “weren’t labeled with who wrote them or which workspace they came from.”

AI Meeting Notes audio, transcripts and summaries stayed out of the dataset unless someone fed them into a Notion AI conversation. Insights is now switched off while the investigation runs, and open-weight models served in the product no longer go through Fireworks, which had also handled classification jobs for that feature.

Enterprise plans carry a ZDR (zero data retention) promise that covers API calls sent to outside model vendors, Notion explained in its 
help center FAQ
. Stored-data features such as Insights and chat history fall outside it. Despite a contractual zero-retention pledge from Fireworks for the Insights analysis, Notion’s review found the vendor kept data from the endpoints it called.

Key suppliers, among them inference services and data processing firms, now face an audit of whether they honor retention limits and minimize what they hold. Fireworks first contacted Notion on November 2, reporting that attackers had reached a limited batch of data, which Notion’s engineers then identified as synthetic AI test material.

Within a day, the company cut its systems off from the 
outside vendor
, wiped everything it held there, stopped all traffic and paused every training run. On November 6, Fireworks warned that customer data might also have been reached, and two days later it confirmed the final affected dataset, which contained the Insights transcripts.

Customer notices went out on November 9, and the advisory followed on November 10. During its investigation, Notion also found its own API keys inside affected user messages and revoked them on November 11.

Akshay Kothari, a Notion co-founder, wrote on X that every affected customer had been contacted, adding, “Our teams are available 24/7, should you have any questions.”

Two separate intrusions at Fireworks came before the Notion disclosure. Its 
October 9 bulletin
 described someone using internal company credentials, spotted on October 6, to read evaluation job environment variables, exposing tokens and API keys saved as secrets in a restricted group of customer accounts.

A cloud host running Fireworks research machines was then compromised on October 23. Attackers used that access the next day to get into Fireworks development systems and launch an unauthorized workload, as described in a 
November 9 update
 from the company.

Billing details, resource metadata and, for certain customers, datasets and incomplete model weights were exposed in that second attack. “Payment card numbers are not stored by Fireworks and were not impacted,” the update said.

Investigators tied the activity to a financially motivated group that resells stolen LLM access through illicit routing gateways, a scheme known as 
LLMjacking
. Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 is assisting with forensics, and law enforcement has been notified.

Owners of affected workspaces can open Security incident review from the help menu in the sidebar, using an up-to-date copy of Notion in a desktop browser, since mobile apps cannot display it. That page also lets them download a list of affected members and generate a shareable link through which each person sees only their own exposed chats.

Full CSV exports of the affected text require a verified domain. Workspaces that received no notice have not been flagged, although Notion’s review continues and any newly identified 
Notion AI
 customers will be contacted directly.

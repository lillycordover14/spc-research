Google AI Studio
 — testing complex prompts with frontier models like gemini-4-carbon, generating videos with Omni, or building an app — you shouldn’t have to bounce across three websites just to set up billing and claim credits when you hit a quota limit.
Starting today, you can claim and manage free API credits directly inside AI Studio. That means you can create your Developer Program profile, link billing, and track your balance, all without leaving your workspace.
If you’re a 
Google AI Pro or Google AI Ultra
 subscriber, your membership already comes with recurring monthly Google Cloud credits through the 
Google Developer Program

The Google Developer Program is a no-cost membership unifying tools, learning paths, and perks across AI Studio, Google Cloud, Firebase, Android, and more. An active profile is required for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers to access developer benefits like monthly Google Cloud credits, higher Google Antigravity quotas, and Google Skills credits. If you don’t have a profile, the new workflow in AI Studio automatically creates one when you redeem your credits.

Check the monthly credits included in your Google AI plan
Google AI Pro and Ultra plans include recurring monthly Google Cloud credits that you can put straight toward Gemini API usage for prompting, building, and deploying AI-powered apps from Google AI Studio. As detailed in our 
guide to using Google AI plan credits in AI Studio
, your monthly credit grant scales with your AI plan tier.

Here’s how your monthly credits work over time.
12-month rolling validity:
 Each monthly credit is valid for 12 months from issue.
Automatic monthly rollover:
 Grants stack month to month. If you’ve been a Pro or Ultra subscriber for a few months and have a Google Developer Program profile, you likely have credits waiting to be claimed.
Support for paid-tier models and overages:
 Use credits when your subscription quota runs out in AI Studio to keep creating generative media, solving complex tasks, or building 3D worlds with models like 
Gemini 4 Carbon
. Upgrading your Google AI plan automatically increases your monthly grant.
Gemini API prepayment requirement:
 Using the Gemini API with Cloud billing requires a $5 minimum prepayment and balance in order to draw down promotional credits.
Primary subscriber exclusivity:
 Only the primary plan subscriber can use these credits. They can’t be shared among family members.
Claim and link your credits in a single click
Automatic credit alerts:
 Spot unclaimed credits and current balances right in the left nav on sign-in.
In-product redemption:
 Create your Google Developer Program profile and claim credits in one dialog.
Seamless billing setup and auto-apply:
 Set up a Cloud billing account within the same flow, spend earliest-expiring credits first, and auto-apply future monthly grants.

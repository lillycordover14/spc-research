[page text]
Hackers breached Fireworks AI client data, including Uber and AI note-taker Notion.

Credit: Smith Collection/Gado / Contributor / Archive Photos

Uber
 is notifying thousands of drivers that their personal information and driver's licenses were stolen in a recent data breach.
The rideshare giant confirmed the breach in filings submitted to nationwide attorneys general this month. One of the company's third-party service providers, 
confirmed by TechCrunch to be Fireworks AI
, notified Uber of the hack, which compromised several of its clients' databases.

The hackers accessed billing data and some private AI datasets from a small percentage of Fireworks AI's thousands of clients, according to initial breach notices involving the productivity software Notion. Fireworks AI explained that unnamed cybercriminals, allegedly motivated by a desire to steal and then sell LLM tokens, breached its cloud provider to access its development system. They then used internal credentials to access client data.

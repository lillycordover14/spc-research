Robert Pattinson's casting as James Bond
 has left Google feeling shaken, not stirred.
Following the announcement
 that Pattinson would be our next 007, Google introduced a new Bond-themed Easter egg. If you search up Pattinson's name, the search results shake a little, and a small twist of lemon falls from the top of the screen to rest daintily on Pattinson's head.

How do you like your Pattinson martini?

Credit: Screenshot: Google

The Easter egg is a nod to James Bond's classic cocktail order: a vodka martini, shaken
,
 not stirred. Google has essentially turned Pattinson into the cocktail he'll be ordering onscreen.

Bond won't be the first franchise Pattinson has led to be commemorated with a Google Easter egg. In 2022, tied to 
The Batman
's release, if you searched terms such as "Bruce Wayne" or "Gotham City," a yellow Bat-Signal would appear alongside the results. Clicking on it turned the page into a moody Gotham night, complete with thunder, the Bat-Signal, and Batman swinging around the screen.
Plus, if you search up "
Twilight
" now, you'll be treated to a small line of scrolling text that reads "hoa hoa hoa season," flanked by falling leaf emojis. The Easter egg references 
Twilight 
fans' loving nickname for fall, drawn from the so-called "hoa hoa hoa" vocals of Blue Foundation's "Eyes on Fire" from the 
Twilight 
soundtrack.

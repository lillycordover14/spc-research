Uber
, online productivity startup 
Notion
 and artificial intelligence coding startup 
Cursor
 said they had data stolen during a hack of 
Fireworks AI
, a company that lets developers access and use open-weight AI models, TechCrunch 
reported
 Monday (Nov. 16).

 

A Fireworks spokesperson confirmed to TechCrunch that the company discovered unauthorized access to some of its systems and that this affected customer data, according to the report.

Fireworks said in a Nov. 9 update on the October 
security incident
 that it had found that the unauthorized access included resource metadata, billing information, not including payment card numbers, and model weights and datasets. The company said it was notifying customers whose data was impacted.

“A third-party cloud provider that hosted Fireworks R&D machines experienced a security incident on October 23,” Fireworks said in the update. “On October 24, the unauthorized third party used it to reach Fireworks development machines and then run an unauthorized workload that led to additional customer data impact. We detected this activity on the same day, and followed our incident response processes to take immediate remediation measures and close known critical vulnerabilities.”

Uber is notifying nearly 150,000 drivers that their personal and driver’s license information, including name, address, data of birth and driver’s license number, was stolen in a data breach at one of the company’s tech providers, according to the TechCrunch report.

An Uber spokesperson said in the report: “The notices to drivers are in relation to a security incident impacting one of our third-party vendors, Fireworks.ai, which had access to some Uber data.”

Cursor said in a Friday (Nov. 13) update on its 
security incident
 that the incident started at Fireworks when an attacker obtained an access key Cursor had issued to Fireworks and that the attacker then used a stolen credential from the exposed data to publish a malicious extension. Cursor notified customers on Oct. 30 and saw and blocked the last attacker activity on Nov. 1, according to the update.

Notion said in a Thursday (Nov. 12) update on the 
Fireworks AI incident
 that the incident impacted less than 1% of Notion workspaces and that Notion has contacted its customers who were affected by the Fireworks incident.

PYMNTS reported in May 2025 that 
Verizon
 found that 30% of 
data breaches
 that occurred during the year ended Oct. 31, 2024, involved a third party such as a supplier, vendor, hosting partner or outsourced IT support.

The 
PYMNTS Intelligence
 report “
Vendors and Vulnerabilities: The Cyberattack Squeeze on Mid-Market Firms
,” published in August 2025, found that when it comes to cyberattacks, middle market companies’ third-party vendors and other outside partners “often play the role of unwitting accomplice after themselves being compromised.”

 statement
 
on Wednesday.  The Applied Compute team will join Palo Alto Networks, and Yash Patil, Applied Compute's co-founder and CEO, will become Palo Alto Networks' Co-Chief AI Officer following the close of the transaction.
Applied Compute is a leading platform for post-training and inference of open-weight AI models. The company was started by three former OpenAI 
OPENAI

Applied Compute said earlier this month that it raised $350 million in new funding at a $3.25 billion valuation, according to a press release. The round was led by Elad Gil, with participation from Kleiner Perkins, Lux Capital, Hanabi Capital, NVIDIA, Blackstone, Shopify Ventures, HubSpot Ventures, Diffusion, Definition, Timeless, Layer Global, Neo, Abstract, Nova, Quantum Light, First Harmonic, and Swish.
"Applied Compute has built the best post-training and inference platform we've seen, and its research team is exceptional," Palo Alto CEO Nikesh Arora said in the statement. "We saw that firsthand as a prospective customer..."

Microsoft Foundry adds Baseten, DeepSeek AI inference
1 min read
MSFT
Baseten reported a partnership with Microsoft 
MSFT
 on Tuesday to bring its high-performance AI inference platform to Microsoft Foundry, enabling Azure enterprise customers to run open AI models with enhanced performance, security and governance. 
Baseten on Microsoft Foundry is available today in public preview, with general availability expected in early 2027.
As production workloads become more complex, enterprises are turning to open models for control over cost, speed, customization, and compliance. Baseten on Microsoft Foundry gives them a frictionless path to the open frontier — without having to secure GPU capacity, manage deployments, or onboard another vendor — all from the Foundry platform where they already build and govern AI apps and agents.
“The next wave of AI innovation will come from what developers build when powerful open models meet high-performance infrastructure,” said Steve Sweetman, VP of Product at Microsoft Foundry. “
Baseten on Microsoft Foundry offers a choice of frontier open models, including DeepSeek (
DEEPSEEK
) V4.1 Flash 1017, GLM 5.3, GLM 5.3 Flash, and Kimi K3. Each model runs on Baseten’s full inference stack, bringing Baseten’s research-backed performance and reliability to Azure. 
Customers are billed through Azure as first-party Foundry consumption and can apply their existing Microsoft Azure Consumption Commitment. Teams can choose pay-as-you-go for usage-based access or Provisioned Throughput Units (PTUs) for SLA-backed performance.

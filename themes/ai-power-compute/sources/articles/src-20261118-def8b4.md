The second short-term extension comes after Anthropic and OpenAI recently unveiled long-term, consumption-based discounts for their AI models.

                  GSA
                

                  Artificial Intelligence
                

Federal agencies will continue to have access to Gemini for $0.47 per agency through Feb. 28, after the discount was set to expire on Nov. 15, according to an updated listing on the 
General Services Administration’s OneGov website
.
It’s the second extension of the Gemini discount after the expiration date was 
pushed back
 from Sept. 30 to Nov. 15. The new extension maintains 20% off list for first-party cloud services and 50% off network standard egress and Google Compute Engine — or GCE — egress.
The continued savings come as Google has strengthened ties with agencies in recent months.
Google Public Sector recently 
unveiled
 Gemini for government services, an add-on to Gemini that gives federal, state and local agencies access to specialized skills, artificial intelligence agents and integrations with frequently used software. Select agencies, including the federal Department of Health and Human Services, have tested the suite of tools.
Meanwhile, the federal government is using Gemini models, along with ones from xAI’s Grok, to power the 
America.gov digital front door
. 
GSA did not respond to a request for comment by the time of publication. 
Anthropic and OpenAI recently brokered new OneGov partnerships, replacing flat rates with consumption-based pricing. 
OpenAI 
is offering
 agencies a 50% discount on tokens for ChatGPT models through the end of 2028, while Anthropic 
is offering
 them a 40% discount on tokens for Claude models through Jan. 31, 2029.
Google declined to comment on whether a long-term Gemini discount similarly envisions usage-based costs.
"OneGov is maturing from short-term promotional pricing toward direct contracts that secure benefits and give agencies broad choice,” a GSA official 
told
 
NextGov/FCW
 in September.

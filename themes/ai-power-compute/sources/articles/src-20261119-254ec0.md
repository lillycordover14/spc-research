Palo Alto Networks
 
$PANW
 
agreed to acquire
 Applied Compute, an artificial intelligence startup enabling companies to operate and tailor AI models of their own, in an all-stock transaction valued at $4.5 billion, 
Palo Alto Networks
 said on Wednesday. The combination is meant to help enterprises train, adapt and deploy open-source and open-weight AI models across public and sovereign clouds.

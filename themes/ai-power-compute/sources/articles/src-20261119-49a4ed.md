Palo Alto Networks (Nasdaq: PANW), the global AI cybersecurity leader, announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Applied Compute, a leading platform for post-training and inference of open-weight AI models. The all-stock transaction is valued at US$4.5 billion, subject to adjustments. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory clearance.

Latham & Watkins LLP represents Applied Compute in the transaction with a corporate deal team led by Bay Area partner Seth Gottlieb, Global Vice Chair of Latham’s Technology Industry Group, and Chicago partner Sean Parish, with associates Jay Trewn, Kristine LaVeau, Michelle Kim, Camille N'Diaye-Muller, and Ryan Ramirez. Advice was provided on tax matters by Bay Area partner Katharine Moir, with assistance from Tripp Haskins; on executive compensation and benefits matters by Bay Area partner James Robinson, with associate Christina Schrantz; on intellectual property matters by Orange County/Los Angeles partner Ghaith Mahmood, with associates Andrew Abokhair, Azam Chaudry, and Bill Kosmidis; on data privacy and cybersecurity matters by San Diego counsel Jennifer Howes, with associates Madhavi Nambiar and Rachel Samouha; on public company representation matters by New York partner Emily Corbi; on antitrust matters by Bay Area partner Joshua Holian and counsel Ngoc Hulbig, with associates Nathaniel Amann and Alexandra Clark; on regulatory matters by Washington, D.C. counsel Aaron Amundson, with associate Matthew Crawford; on government contracts matters by Washington, D.C. partner Dean Baxtresser, with associate Chris Caulder; and on labor and employment matters by Chicago partner Nineveh Alkhas, with associates Jenny Bobbitt and Andrew Callas.

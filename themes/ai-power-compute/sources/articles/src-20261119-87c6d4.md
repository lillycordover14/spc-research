Palo Alto Networks (
PANW
) stock climbed following the cybersecurity company's fiscal Q1 earnings report after the bell on Wednesday, which beat Wall Street's expectations and included a raised full-year outlook.
 
 
The stock opened 8% higher on Thursday morning following strong earnings and an additional announcement Wednesday that Palo Alto had signed an agreement to buy Applied Compute, a private startup founded last year by ex-OpenAI (
OPAI.PVT
) researchers that tunes and runs open-weight AI models, in a deal valued at $4.5 billion in stock.
 
 
 
 
 
Here are the numbers for Q1:
 
 
 
Revenue grew 39% year over year to $3.44 billion, compared to analyst estimates of $3.31 billion.
 
 
 
Adjusted earnings per share were $1.05, up 13% year over year (estimate: $0.97).
 
 
 
FY2027 total revenue was between $14.52 billion and $14.57 billion, representing year-over-year growth of 26% to 27% (estimate: $14.16 billion).
 
 
 
 
The company boosted its full-year outlook, betting that the AI boom is turning cybersecurity into a must-have as 
cyberattacks and outages have surged in recent weeks
. Palo Alto Networks stock jumped 85% this year.
 
 
Shares in other cybersecurity companies also got a boost after Wednesday's earnings report: CrowdStrike (
CRWD
), Cloudflare (
NET
), and Zscaler (
ZS
) all opened more than 6% higher on Thursday morning.

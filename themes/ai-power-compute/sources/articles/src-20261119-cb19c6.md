Palo Alto Networks to acquire Applied Compute for $4.5 billion&nbsp;&nbsp;SC Media

[page text]
As detailed in Silicon Angle, Palo Alto Networks Inc. has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire the artificial intelligence startup Applied Compute Inc. in an all-stock transaction valued at $4.5 billion.
The acquisition, expected to close in the company's fiscal third quarter, aims to bolster Palo Alto Networks' capabilities in securing enterprise AI deployments. Applied Compute, founded in 2025 by former OpenAI Group PBC researchers, provides a platform for post-training and inference of open-weight models. The startup’s technology includes an autoscaling inference engine and a model router known as Shift-1. Upon completion of the deal, Applied Compute co-founder and Chief Executive Yash Patil will join the company as co-chief AI officer.
Palo Alto Networks intends to integrate these tools to provide secure environments for enterprises managing hybrid AI models. This strategic move coincides with strong fiscal first-quarter results, where the company reported revenue of $3.44 billion, a 39% increase year-over-year. Following the announcement, Palo Alto Networks raised its full-year revenue guidance to a range of $14.52 billion to $14.57 billion. The integration of Applied Compute is intended to address the growing demand for secure, sovereign AI infrastructure as businesses increasingly rely on a mix of open and closed models.

Google is now officially rolling out what it calls the "collapsible sidebar" within Google Search. We spotted this a few weeks ago and we called it then the 
AI Mode sidebar in Google Search
. Robby Stein from Google said "New on the Google desktop homepage globally: we’re rolling out a collapsible sidebar to easily find & jump back into your recent AI searches."
Robby Stein posted a video of this on 
X
 and wrote, "People are coming to Search with more questions that aren’t just one and done – and in testing, this sidebar made a big difference for making progress & continuing to explore the web."
Here is that video:
He added that this is "A much simpler way to pick up where you left off."
I personally don't like this user interface as much but I do think it might lead to more queries for Google Search and AI Mode.
I've been in this experience for 3 weeks now:
I am now seeing the Google AI Mode left sidebar when in the main Google Search results - it is not fully rolled out - my story from a couple of weeks ago is at https://t.co/ZQH9bBo2zv https://t.co/FZuyjsHLvU

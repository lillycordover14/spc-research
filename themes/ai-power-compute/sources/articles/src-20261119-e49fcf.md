Google
, the parent company of 
YouTube
, has responded to backlash resulting from a YouTube video shared to 
Russell Brand
‘s account where he 
claimed he is innocent
 while in prison via a statement read by his wife, Laura Brand. 

	“So Russell has asked me to read something to you today,” the actor’s wife said at the start of the video, before looking to a notebook to read her husband’s message. “Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support. As you know, I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening.”

	In a statement shared with 
BBC News
, Google said it was “aware of the new content and our teams are investigating,” adding, “We suspended Russell Brand’s channel from the YouTube Partner Program in September 2023. The channel has not been able to monetise on YouTube since then.”

	BBC News also reported that Google declined a request from the Ministry of Justice to remove the video, saying none of its rules had been broken. 

	Brand was 
sentenced
 to 10 years and six months in prison after being found guilty of rape and sexual assault last Monday. The trial lasted four weeks at Southwark Crown Court, with the 
jury finding him guilty
 on one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault (he was found not guilty on two other rape charges and not guilty of attempted indecent assault).

	His message, delivered by his wife in the YouTube video, drew criticism, with Britain’s Justice Secretary Alex Norris 
calling
 the broadcast of a statement from Brand while he is behind bars “completely unacceptable.”

	

	“Prisoners are rightly banned from making statements that cause distress to victims,” Norris wrote on X. “This is being urgently investigated, and I expect action to be taken if he is found to have broken the rules.”

	Elsewhere in the YouTube video, Laura read the rest of Brand’s statement, saying, “The good news is that Christ is with us when we choose him, and I do. I am incarcerated in HMP Littlehey. Little as in small, and hey as in, ‘Hey, I’m in the biggest sex offenders prison in Europe.’ The good news is that I have already seen that the staff here are strong, they are kind, and they are dedicated. And as for my fellow prisoners, I now know that I am in no position to judge anyone.”

	“I don’t know which other men here are not guilty, and the ones that are, they are people that Christ died to save, like you and me. Let us all love, pray for, and forgive one another as God forgave us. Please support my treasured wife and family. I feel your prayers. Your brother in Christ, Russell,” the remarks concluded.

	On Monday, a spokesperson for U.K. Prime Minister Andy Burnham called his message “clearly appalling” and said it was being “urgently investigated,” per 
Reuters
.

	At the time of publication, Brand and Laura’s 
YouTube
 video is still available to watch on the website and has accumulated 950,000 views.

POSTED BY:
 
Rob Behnke
11.19.2026
In October 2026, Fireworks AI 
experienced a security incident
 that was disclosed that month and in a November update. The AI company suffered breaches that resulted in the exposure of customer secrets, datasets, incomplete models, and other data, impacted Notion, and potentially some other companies as well.
Inside the Attack
Fireworks AI published two reports regarding breaches that occurred in October 2026. The first involved the use of compromised internal credentials. The attacker used these credentials to access environment variables that were used by some of the company’s customers for evaluation workloads. In response to the incident, the company rotated employee API keys, notified the affected customers and published a disclosure on Oct 9.
The second report (phrased as an update to the original) was published in November and disclosed a supply chain security incident. One of the company’s third-party cloud providers experienced a breach that granted the attacker access to Fireworks AI’s R&D machines. The attacker leveraged this access to execute an unauthorized workload, granting them access to additional sensitive information. Across the incident, they accessed resource metadata, billing information, and secrets, datasets, and incomplete model weights for a subset of impacted accounts.
Notion was one of the companies
 impacted by the Fireworks AI breaches. AI chat transcripts for <1% of workspaces were leaked due to Notion’s use of Fireworks AI for Notion’s Insights analytics feature. In response to the breach, Notion severed the relationship and advised affected customers to treat the data as if it were exposed, and to rotate any credentials pasted into Notion AI.
Lessons Learned from the Attack
Fireworks AI suffered two, possibly connected, breaches of sensitive customer data. One involved compromised internal credentials, while the other was a supply chain security incident targeting the company’s cloud provider.
The information exposed in these attacks can be abused in various ways. Billing data might be used in spear phishing, credentials for LLMjacking attacks, and partial model weights might provide hints about an organization’s sensitive data or internal operations.
The scope of the incident also extended beyond Fireworks AI, impacting Notion and potentially other companies as well. In Notion’s case, this breach also revealed a 
violation of the zero data retention (ZDR)
 agreement the company had with Fireworks AI, as customer AI chats were accessed by the attacker.

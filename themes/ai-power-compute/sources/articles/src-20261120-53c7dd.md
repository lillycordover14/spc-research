Google
 and 
Apple
 might be competitors, but they can play nice sometimes.
For example, 
Google just announced
 that you can now use 
Gemini AI
 to control a Mac computer. With this new feature, you can give Gemini commands and it will root through your files, access your apps, and browse the web to complete whatever tasks you've asked it to perform. All of this is facilitated by the Gemini app for macOS 27 Golden Gate, per Google's press release.

How can users take advantage of Gemini computer use for MacBooks, Mac Mini, and iMac devices?

Google provided several examples in an announcement blog post, such as asking Gemini to take a child's sketch of a butterfly and render it polygonally using the 3D modeling program Blender. Once the request is processed, Gemini performs the task in an isolated background window with a preview display right in the Gemini app, allowing you to watch it perform the task in real-time. However, as Google noted, you also retain full use of your Mac while this is happening, allowing users to continue browsing, typing, and completing other types of work.
While this may be a boon to Gemini power users who own devices like the new 
MacBook Pro OLED
, it's worth noting that giving an AI agent full access to all your local files and apps carries inherent risks. Google gives users the ability to freely choose which apps Gemini has access to, alongside the ability to watch the process as it unfolds, in case something goes wrong. So, you can pause Gemini if it's doing something you don't like, and approve decisions before they're made.

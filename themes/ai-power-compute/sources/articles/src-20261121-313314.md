MAYES COUNTY, Okla. — Chouteau-Mazie Public Schools employees received 400 hams from Google on Friday ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday.
The donation is part of Google's plan to distribute 4,000 holiday meals to residents of Mayes County on Monday, Nov. 23.
The company plans to distribute 2,000 hams and 2,000 turkeys during a drive-thru giveaway from noon to 7 p.m. at two locations in Pryor and Chouteau.
In Pryor, meals will be distributed at The District, 3822 W. 530 Road.
In Chouteau, distribution will take place at 119 W. Main St., across from Utica Park Clinic.
Google said volunteers will distribute the meals directly to residents' vehicles.
The giveaway is open to local residents and will be conducted on a first-come, first-served basis while supplies last. No registration is required.

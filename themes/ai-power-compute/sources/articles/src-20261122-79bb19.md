TLDR
An AI coding agent exposed a repository credential in public GitHub code, Together AI says, allowing an unauthorized party to download some serverless prompt samples submitted in June and July 2026. The company says it understands the data has since been deleted, but its review of exposed contents is ongoing. It says zero data retention now defaults on for new accounts and urges customers handling sensitive prompts to enable it.

Google seems to have rolled out 
Search Profiles
 globally, this has not yet been announced but now folks outside of the United States, 
Canada and India
 can now claim their profile without having to go through VPNs to pretend they are in those countries.
Clara Soteras said on 
X
 that she was able to claim her profile in Spain.  She wrote, "Search Profiles seem to be rolling out to new regions! Today they showed up in Spain. Until now they were only available in the USA and Canada."
Gagan Ghotra added on 
X
, "Seems like a global rollout may be, I just checked through my gmails with AU/Singapore/Dubai addresses."

Deep Cogito’s Form D records an ongoing Series B offering, not a completed $86 million financing: the company reported $58.7 million sold and $27.3 million still available. The filing does not disclose a valuation, share price, ownership stakes or buyer identities, so it cannot show what investors paid for their shares or who participated. For those tracking the startup, the notice establishes a substantial private stock sale but leaves its terms and investor roster largely opaque.

Deep Cogito has disclosed 
$58.7 million in Series B stock sales
—not a completed $86 million financing. A 
new SEC notice
 filed November 23, 2026, lists 11 investors and $27.3 million remaining to be sold.

The Form D gives the exact amount sold as $58,728,177 against a total offering of $85,999,917.

The securities are 
Series B Preferred Stock
, and the notice also covers their conversion into common stock. Deep Cogito selected equity as the security type. The document does not provide a share price, company valuation or resulting ownership stakes, so the dollar total alone does not show how much of the company investors are buying.

Sales began before the disclosure

Deep Cogito lists November 16, 2026, as the date of first sale. Chief Executive Officer Drishan Arora signed the notice on November 20, before its November 23 filing.

The company marked the Form D as a new notice, not an amendment, and claimed the Rule 506(b) exemption. This is a notice of an exempt securities offering, rather than a public stock offering. Deep Cogito also said it does not intend for the offering to last more than one year.

The offering is not connected to a business-combination transaction, according to the company’s response on the form. That question names mergers, acquisitions and exchange offers as examples. Deep Cogito reported zero sales commissions and zero finder’s fees, providing a limited account of transaction expenses alongside the sales figures.

Named directors, unnamed buyers

The investor count 
does not come with a buyer list
. A separate related-person section names Arora as an executive officer and director, alongside directors Dhruv Malhotra, Eric Vishria, Schuster Tanger and Gavin Baker. Those entries identify corporate roles; the filing does not identify those people as purchasers in this offering.

In the use-of-proceeds section, Deep Cogito entered $5,999,975 for payments made or proposed to executive officers, directors or promoters required to be named in the form.

Deep Cogito identifies itself as a Delaware corporation formed in 2024, with its principal place of business in San Francisco.

These are Deep Cogito’s disclosures, not SEC-verified financial results. The form explicitly warns that the agency has not necessarily reviewed the information or determined whether it is accurate and complete.

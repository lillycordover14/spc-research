Key points

OpenRouter off-boarded three providers and removed 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention (ZDR) eligibility; it says its own systems were not breached 
[1]
.

BleepingComputer named the three as Together AI, Phala and Novita; Atlas Cloud withdrew its models voluntarily 
[2]
.

The findings came from Dragos, which sent two million seeded test prompts through OpenRouter 
[2]
.

Users who may have sent secrets in prompts should rotate them, BleepingComputer advised 
[2]
.

OpenRouter, the service that routes AI requests to many model providers, has removed three inference providers and pulled 125 endpoints from its Zero Data Retention (ZDR) tier after security researchers at Dragos found that some providers had data compromised or were not upholding their data policies 
[1]
. OpenRouter said in a Nov. 22 post that the findings showed no breach of its own systems, and that it had notified all potentially affected customers 
[1]
.
OpenRouter's post does not name the off-boarded providers. BleepingComputer reported on Nov. 23 that they are Together AI, Phala and Novita, and that Atlas Cloud said it would voluntarily withdraw its models pending security testing 
[2]
.

What each provider exposed

According to BleepingComputer's summary of the providers' statements 
[2]
:
Together AI:
 an AI coding agent exposed a repository credential on GitHub, and an unauthorized party downloaded stored prompt samples from June and July involving MiniMax M3, GLM-5.2 and Kimi K2.6.
Phala:
 an attacker compromised a server processing DeepSeek V4 Flash requests routed through OpenRouter between Oct. 21 and 22, possibly until Nov. 8, and obtained application credentials from affected virtual machines.
Novita:
 a routing misconfiguration forwarded some MiniMax requests, including OpenRouter traffic, to MiniMax's official API between May 28 and Nov. 11.

How it was found

Dragos sent two million test prompts through OpenRouter, each carrying a unique tracking link or decoy credential, and reported data exposure at at least three AI providers; some seeded credentials were used against its canary systems and AWS accounts 
[2]
. MLQ covered the Dragos report on Nov. 22: 
Dragos finds attackers inside three AI inference providers and resellers reading customer prompts
.
OpenRouter said it is adapting the researchers' tests to run continuously, checking network paths for requests forwarded to third parties, and will require validation before restoring ZDR eligibility to any removed endpoint. The company said it "will keep raising the bar, and providers that want to be on OpenRouter will need to meet it" 
[1]
.

What customers should do

OpenRouter says affected users have been notified and links to statements from Atlas Cloud, Novita, Phala and Together 
[1]
. BleepingComputer advised potentially affected users to rotate any secrets that may have been included in leaked requests 
[2]
.

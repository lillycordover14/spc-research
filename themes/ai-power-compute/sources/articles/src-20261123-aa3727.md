Google Adds Grocery Giants to Connected Apps Feature                    

By

PYMNTS
 

 | 

November 23, 2026

 | 

Listen to Article

Listen

Google
 has launched a new “Connected Apps” feature letting users use Search and Gemini to connect with grocery chains like 
Albertsons
, 
Safeway
, 
Shaw’s
, and 
Target
.

 

“Whether you’re preparing appetizers for your holiday party or need groceries for Thanksgiving, these new Connected Apps can help you build your order and check out with ease,” Google said in a Friday (Nov. 20) 
blog post
. “Powered by the 
Universal Commerce Protocol
, these apps can help you get more done this holiday season.”

Because many people use this time of year to get their finances in order, Google said 
Experian
—already live on the Gemini app—is now being added to search with the ability to help users in the U.S. check their credit rating and seek personal loans. Users can also calculate their taxes and estimate their refunds right from Search with the help of 
H&R Block
.

“In the coming weeks, you’ll also be able to link your accounts to 
Angi
 and 
Thumbtack
 in Search, streamlining your hunt for local pros who can fix that leaky faucet or put up last-minute decorations before your guests arrive,” Google added.

The announcement comes days after two other noteworthy moves from Google: adding Google Wallet to Apple’s iPhones in the U.S., and giving Gemini a way to check out using Google Pay.

Writing about the announcements last week, PYMNTS noted that while Google is giving its wallet 
“a place on Apple’s home screen,”
 the “distinction for merchants is where the wallet works.” Google pitched the payment feature as a way to manage cards for online checkout throughout its services and wherever shoppers use Google Pay.

“That gives a retailer offering Google Pay a way to reach customers who carry an iPhone but already keep cards with Google,” the report said. “For issuers, it raises a practical question. The card a customer 
prefers to tap
 in a store may differ from the one they have saved for an online order.”

The other new feature means that Google’s Tasks feature in Gemini can use Google Pay to carry out purchases with supported 
Visa
 and 
Mastercard
 cards, with shoppers able to set spending limits and sign off before the AI buys.

“That 
changes the merchant’s job
,” the report said. “A retailer trying to win a grocery order, for example, must supply prices and availability an assistant can use to assemble a basket within the customer’s budget. By the time the shopper sees the order for approval, much of the work that used to happen on the retailer’s site may already be done.”

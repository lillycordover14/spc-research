SACRAMENTO, Calif.
 —
Sacramento Republic FC’s championship win hasn’t gone unnoticed by Google.
Searching the team’s name
 on Monday morning reveals an animation of fireworks. 
Advertisement
Hearst Owned
The 
Republic beat Louisville City FC
 in the USL Championship Final on Saturday morning. The score was 1-1 after 90 minutes, and following two scoreless periods of extra time the Republic won on penalties, 4-3. It was Sacramento’s first league title since 2014, the club’s inaugural season. 
Sacramento is honoring the Republic with a championship victory parade downtown
 on Monday at noon. 
The parade will begin at Crocker Park and travel northbound on 3rd Street, eastbound on Capitol Mall, southbound on 9th Street, eastbound on N Street, and northbound on 10th Street. A rally at the end of the parade will feature players, head coach Neill Collins and the championship trophy. 
The first 5,000 fans will receive a commemorative championship poster.

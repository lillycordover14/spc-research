The News:
 Tencent Hy4 is now live on Together Serverless Inference 
[1]
, accessible via the model string tencent/Hy4 through Together's chat completions API 
[1]
. Hy4 is the first model in Tencent's Hy family to accept images alongside text 
[1]
, and it supports tool calling and JSON output 
[1]
. The model runs on a Mixture-of-Experts architecture with 770B total parameters and 49B active parameters per token 
[1]
, paired with a 1M-token context window 
[1]
. Serverless pricing is set at $0.83 per 1M input tokens, $0.042 per 1M cached input tokens, and $2.50 per 1M output tokens 
[1]
. Together AI demonstrated the model reading values from a chart screenshot and returning structured JSON, then reproducing the chart as a single HTML file 
[1]
, and separately generating a functional browser game from a single text prompt 
[1]

Tencent Hy4 on Together AI: Open-Weight Inference Gets Multimodal

Analysis:
 Hy4's arrival on Together AI is a meaningful step for the open-model inference market, not because of the model alone, but because of what it signals about where enterprise AI infrastructure is heading 
[1]
. Futurum data show that self-managed cloud (46%) and on-premises infrastructure (42%) lead production hosting today, yet self-hosting costs hold up 50% of pilots 
[2]
. That gap is the market Together AI is building into.

Architecture and Capabilities: A Frontier-Class Open Model

Hy4 is a natively multimodal flagship MoE model with 770B total parameters and 49B active parameters per token, and a 1M-token context window 
[6]
[1]
. It follows Hy4 Preview, adding image input and computer-use capabilities 
[1]
. Tencent trained the model to operate professional software such as Blender and CAD tools, and evaluated it on office work, financial research, and game development in Unreal Engine, Unity, and Godot 
[1]
. The combination of vision, tool calling, and structured JSON output makes Hy4 one of the more complete open-weight models available on a managed inference platform today. Together AI's own demonstrations, including chart-to-JSON extraction 
[1]
 and single-prompt game generation 
[1]
, illustrate the practical range of the model's capabilities across both structured and creative tasks.

Pricing and the Self-Hosting Cost Trap

At $0.83 per 1M input tokens, $0.042 per 1M cached input tokens, and $2.50 per 1M output tokens 
[1]
, Hy4 on Together AI offers a clear cost-per-token alternative to the hidden expenses of self-hosting. Futurum data are direct on this point: self-hosting costs hold up 50% of pilots 
[2]
. The token bill does not disappear when enterprises self-host; it reappears as servers, cloud compute, and engineering time 
[2]
. Serverless inference converts that variable, often underestimated cost into a predictable per-token rate, which is a structurally simpler proposition for teams that want open-weight capability without a dedicated MLOps function. Futurum survey data show that provider-managed cloud and self-managed cloud are the leading enterprise deployment environments for generative AI models 
[5]
, confirming the addressable market for Together AI's approach.

Agentic Use Cases: Where Hy4's Feature Set Lands

Hy4's multimodal and agentic capabilities map directly onto the deployment priorities enterprises are actively pursuing. Futurum survey data show that IT Operations and Cybersecurity rank among the top agentic deployment areas enterprises plan to pursue in the next 18 months 
[5]
, a use case that benefits directly from a model that can parse screenshots, invoke tools, and return structured outputs. Customer Experience automation ranks among the leading priorities as well 
[5]
, where vision and JSON output enable more reliable, verifiable agent behavior. That reliability dimension matters: 55.4% of enterprises cite AI agent reliability and hallucination management in production as a top generative AI challenge 
[5]
. Hy4's structured JSON output and tool-calling capabilities are well-suited to workflows where deterministic, auditable responses reduce the unpredictability that stalls production deployments.

Multi-Model Neutrality as a Strategic Moat

Together AI's decision to add Tencent's flagship alongside other non-OpenAI open weights reinforces a deliberate positioning strategy. Proprietary orchestration stacks carry a recognized structural weakness: one leading platform remains primarily optimized for and tightly integrated with OpenAI's proprietary model ecosystem, which enforces model lock-in rather than providing adapters for over 100 third-party foundational models 
[3]
. Together AI's neutral, multi-model catalog is a direct counter to that risk. For enterprises that want to avoid vendor dependency as the open-weight model market matures, a platform that runs Tencent, Meta, Mistral, and others through a single API is a more durable infrastructure choice. The broader market context supports the urgency of that positioning: the AI platforms market is projected to reach $5,301,865 USD millions by 2030 from a 2026 base of $1,460,961 USD millions, representing a base-case CAGR of 52.5% through 2030 
[4]
, and the platforms that establish multi-model depth early will be harder to displace as enterprise commitments deepen.

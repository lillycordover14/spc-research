Google launches $10m Energy Impact Fund in Oregon

Will support energy efficiency improvements for residential households and schools in The Dalles

November 24, 2026

By

Zachary Skidmore

         
            Have your say
        
    

Google has announced a $10 million Energy Impact Fund in Oregon to support energy affordability, innovation, and community empowerment in the state.

– Google

As part of the fund, Google will provide $9m to the Energy Trust of Oregon, which will use the funds to make energy efficiency improvements to lower costs for residents and schools in The Dalles and its surrounding areas. The remaining $1m will be distributed at a later date, with details on its use yet to be disclosed.
The company claims that the fund will support upgrades across residential households, including insulation and other weatherization, installing efficient heating and cooling systems, and making any home repairs needed before new equipment can be installed.
The funds will also support energy efficiency improvements and related critical upgrades at schools, with Google also committing to help school districts access the expertise, resources, and support required to plan and carry out energy projects.
“Being part of The Dalles for 20 years has given us a deep appreciation for the people and communities that make this region home,” said Amanda Peterson Corio, global head of energy and power at Google. “We believe operating responsibly means contributing to the region’s long-term success. Through the Energy Impact Fund and our partnership with the Energy Trust of Oregon, we're supporting practical energy improvements that can lower costs for families, help local organizations make their resources go further, and extend the benefits of energy efficiency to more communities.”

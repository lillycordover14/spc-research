Gemini 4 Neon
 brings frontier-level intelligence with fast end-to-end task completion across complex software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows. Since we introduced the model, builders have been experimenting with its balance of depth and efficiency, dialing up its thinking levels for complex coding and interactive 3D builds. Check out five projects showing what Neon can do below.
Light up a 3D Earth.
Starting with a literal neon world: 
@HarshithLucky3
 used Neon on high thinking in 
Google Antigravity
 to build this glowing 3D Earth.

Bring Three.js butterflies to life.
@thtbee_
 ran Neon on xhigh thinking in Google Antigravity to craft imaginative Three.js butterflies.

Build a hexagon flame shader in minutes.
In under five minutes, 
@anicho
 used /swarm with Neon in Google Antigravity to build this glowing, interactive hexagon flame shader for the web.

Explore an interactive 3D black hole.
@kimmonismus

Simulate a traveling pendulum wave.
@goodhunt

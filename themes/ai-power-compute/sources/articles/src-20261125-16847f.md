Rooms "More actions" Panel Reordering

Google Meet Hardware

Other

Starting on December 02, 2026, we are updating the order of options within the "More actions" panel on Google Meet hardware touch controllers. This update introduces improvements based on usage patterns and user feedback, prioritizing high-frequency actions so room participants can access essential controls faster and with less scrolling.
Key adjustments in the panel include:
Moving frequently used controls such as 
Present, Adjust view
, and 
People
 higher in the menu for direct access from the side panel on most touch controllers.
Retaining primary in-room actions, including 
Chat
 at the top and 
Control camera
 near the front of the list, while demoting lower-frequency tools like 
Recording
 or 
Streaming
 to streamline the menu.
When relevant, elevating custom room controls in spaces configured for presentations or specialized events to make meeting room management more efficient.
This change requires no additional configuration or action. End users will automatically see the refreshed menu order across supported Google Meet hardware touch devices, creating a more intuitive and consistent in-room meeting experience.
The reordered “More actions” panel on Google Meet hardware touch controllers
Getting started
Admins: 
This feature will be ON by default and cannot be disabled.
End users: 
No action is required. This update will automatically appear on supported devices.
Rollout pace
Early Preview devices:
 Gradual rollout (up to 7 days for feature visibility) starting on December 2, 2026 
Rapid Release and Scheduled Release domains:
 Gradual rollout (up to 15 days for feature visibility)  starting on December 11, 2026
Availability
Available to all Google Workspace customers with Google Meet hardware devices

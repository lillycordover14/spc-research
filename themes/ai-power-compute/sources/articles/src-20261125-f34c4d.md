Fireworks AI
 made Tencent’s open-weight 
Hy4 Flash
 available on its serverless API on November 25, 2026, pricing the Standard tier at $0.07 per million input tokens and $0.281 per million output tokens. The announcement adds benchmark tests that position the model as a low-cost option for professional and agentic work.
Cached input costs $0.003 per million tokens. A Priority tier is also available, and the service is served globally. Developers can instead use on-demand dedicated GPUs; the 
Hy4 Flash model page
 lists function calling as supported and fine-tuning and embeddings as unsupported.
Hy4 Flash uses a mixture-of-experts design, in which only about 12 billion of its roughly 220 billion parameters are active for each token. It accepts text and image input. The native context window is 1M tokens, while 
Fireworks
 is serving 512K at launch, a material constraint for applications built around very long documents or extended agent sessions.
Tencent 
introduced Hy4 Flash
 on November 16, 2026, as its most cost-effective Hy model for everyday productivity and released the weights on GitHub, Hugging Face, ModelScope, and AtomGit. Tencent says reinforcement learning reduced average end-to-end output tokens by 50%, while megakernels, 4-bit quantization, and DFly lifted decoding speed beyond 150 tokens per second.
Professional Benchmark Results
Fireworks reports testing the model on seven professional benchmarks with the same harness used for other models on each leaderboard, at high reasoning. Costs are based on recorded tokens at Fireworks’ serverless prices, and the results are dated November 22, 2026. Hy4 Flash reached the score-cost Pareto frontier on every board where cost was recorded, meaning no listed model delivered a higher score at a lower cost.
Fireworks’ run of Epiq’s CorporateBench, which tests questions across thousands of internal company emails and meeting notes, produced a 75.9% score for Hy4 Flash: sixth of 14 models and slightly above Claude Opus 5.5’s 75.4%. The respective costs were $0.013 and $2.80 per task.
Rogo’s Big Finance Benchmark produced a 49.2% tie with Claude Opus 5 in Fireworks’ tests, at $0.0065 per task for Hy4 Flash versus $1.83 for Opus 5. Hy4 Flash also placed above Claude Opus 5.5 and Kimi K3.1 Pro, which each scored 48.2%. GPT-6 Luna was the only cheaper model on that board, but scored 35.3%.
On Sierra’s τ³-Banking agent benchmark, Fireworks reports a 49.2% score for Hy4 Flash against 49.1% for 
Claude Opus 5
. The cost was $0.012 per trial versus $2.59. Across all boards where it recorded cost, Fireworks found Hy4 Flash was the lowest-cost model except on Big Finance.
Fireworks’ rankings were weaker elsewhere: Hy4 Flash placed 16th of 18 models on HealthBench Professional and 19th of 22 on DeepSWE. Tencent recommends the larger Hy4 for long-horizon software engineering because of its higher intelligence ceiling, reinforcing that the cost results do not establish uniform superiority across professional work.
Validation Against Tencent’s Endpoint

A couple of Walmart’s budget-friendly smart home cameras will be able to recognize people around your house now using Google’s 
familiar face detection
 feature. A new 
Google Home update
 brings Familiar Faces support to the $35 
Onn Outdoor Camera Plug-In
 and $77 Onn Floodlight Camera Hardwired. However, a Google Home Premium subscription is still required to access the feature.
Both cameras and Google’s Nest Cam Outdoor (wired, 2nd gen) also have a new remote control in the Google Home app you can use to start or stop a 30-second security siren while watching the camera’s live video feed.
Earlier this month, Google 
announced
 that Gemini Live would get an upgrade “to our latest speech-to-speech architecture for significantly faster response times and a smoother experience.” Now it says those improvements are rolling out over the coming weeks, along with new smart home control, as “Gemini Live now supports all your daily actions, from setting timers to turning off the lights.”

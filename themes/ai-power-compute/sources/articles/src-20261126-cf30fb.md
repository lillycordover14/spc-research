Google Is Practically Giving Away The Pixel 11 This Black Friday

By
 
Joshua Hawkins

Nov. 26, 2026 7:17 am EST

If you've been holding off on picking up one of Google's latest smartphones in the hopes that you'd see some good deals this Black Friday, then you are in luck. Google has discounted several of its latest Pixel devices as part of its Black Friday sale, with the unlocked Pixel 11 dropping from $899 to just $699. And if you're willing to switch wireless providers, you can actually get your hands on the Pixel 11 without paying anything for the phone itself beyond the applicable taxes.

The deal, which is available through the 
Google Store
, lets you save the entire retail price of the Pixel 11 when you sign up for Google Fi Wireless. Google is also offering a discount on its most expensive unlimited wireless plan, allowing you to save an additional $240 over the course of two years. And, unlike many smartphone deals you'll probably see this Black Friday, you don't even need to trade in your old phone to take advantage of the savings.

Considering the Pixel 11 is only a few months old and comes with twice the starting storage of its predecessor, as well as some improvements to its camera system, it's a particularly enticing offer, although 
Google does play it a bit too safe with the Pixel 11
. Of course, as is often the case with these types of carrier deals, there are a few caveats you'll want to familiarize yourself with before pulling the trigger.

How to get the Google Pixel 11 for free this Black Friday

Google

To take advantage of the deal, you'll need to be a new Google Fi Wireless customer and select Google Fi as your carrier when purchasing the Pixel 11 through the Google Store. You'll receive an immediate $300 discount on the smartphone, with the remaining $599 distributed across 24 monthly bill credits. You'll also need to activate the smartphone within 30 days of receiving confirmation that it has shipped.

The biggest caveat here, though, is that you'll need to remain with Google Fi Wireless for two years to take full advantage of the promotion. Canceling or pausing your service, or changing your plan prematurely, will result in you forfeiting the remaining promotional credits. Additionally, switching to another smartphone within the first 120 days will result in Google charging you for that initial $300 discount.

It's also worth noting that Google is offering a similar promotion for the Pixel 11 Pro, covering the entirety of that smartphone's $1,099 starting price. As such, if you've been 
debating whether to buy the Pixel 11 or the Pixel 11 Pro
, this particular promotion makes a pretty compelling argument for going with Google's more premium smartphone instead. The deal is available until January 5, 2027, or while supplies last, so you don't necessarily have to rush out and buy the Pixel 11 on Black Friday itself.

Why Google's Black Friday Pixel 11 deal is actually worth considering

Elvard project/Shutterstock

While switching wireless providers just to get a free smartphone might not always make sense, Google Fi is one of several 
affordable wireless carriers that utilize T-Mobile's network
 in the United States. So if you have good T-Mobile coverage in your area, then it could be a good move. On top of the deal, if you sign up for Google's Unlimited Premium plan alongside your new Pixel 11, you'll pay $55 a month instead of the usual $65 for the first two years.

The Unlimited Premium plan includes 100 GB of high-speed data per person each month, 50 GB of high-speed hotspot data, and international data coverage in more than 200 destinations. Google also throws in 100 GB of Google One cloud storage and six months of YouTube Premium. If you're already paying around $55 or more a month for your wireless service, then switching could essentially net you a new flagship smartphone without increasing your monthly spending.

If two years of bill credits isn't enough to convince you to change, then you can always take advantage of another deal that Google has to offer. Purchasing an unlocked Pixel smartphone through the Google Store will actually qualify you for 12 months of complimentary Google Fi Unlimited Essentials service, or you can receive an equivalent discount toward one of the carrier's more expensive unlimited plans.

SUMMARY
Snowflake Inc. (SNOW +0.93%) reported second-quarter financial results characterized by a third consecutive quarter of product revenue acceleration. Management reported that AI initiatives are creating a flywheel effect, where new AI workloads attract data to the platform and subsequently drive higher consumption of core data platform services. The company raised its full-year product revenue and operating margin targets based on observed consumption patterns across all geographic regions. Strategic focus remains on establishing the governed data foundation required for the agentic enterprise while integrating model choice and first-party AI applications like CoCo and CoWork.
CEO Ramaswamy stated, "AI is fundamentally changing how enterprises build, operate, and make decisions."
Management reported that AI products, including functions for document processing and machine learning, contributed approximately half of the observed revenue acceleration.
The company introduced the Cortex AI Gateway to route tasks to specific models based on customer-defined cost and performance policies.
CFO Robins stated, "We are delivering continued operating margin expansion as we offset growing cloud costs with slowing headcount expense."
The company reported that 43% of its customers share data on the platform with at least one stable edge, serving as a circulatory system for data across organizations.
CEO Ramaswamy noted that the time to reach 80% of purchased consumption for new customer cohorts has visibly improved due to faster AI-enabled project delivery.
Management stated that AI is being used internally across over 150 use cases to drive productivity in finance, sales, and marketing departments.
INDUSTRY GLOSSARY
CoCo: A Snowflake feature providing governed AI capabilities and coding agents for data operations.
CoWork: A Snowflake application that allows users to interact with enterprise data using conversational language.
Stable Edge: A connection between a data provider and a data consumer that facilitates secure data sharing on the platform.
Agentic Enterprise: An organization that utilizes AI agents to orchestrate data, models, and workflows to turn intent into action.
RPO: Remaining Performance Obligations, representing the total amount of contracted future revenue not yet recognized.
Cortex AI Gateway: A system that dynamically routes AI tasks to different models based on defined policies and real-world performance.
Observe: A recently acquired company that contributed 173 employees and approximately one percentage point of revenue growth.
Natoma: An integration that enables AI agents to perform actions such as sending emails or opening Jira tickets directly from the platform.

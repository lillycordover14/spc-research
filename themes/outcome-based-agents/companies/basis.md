## What they do

Agents that pull documents, flag exceptions and return completed accounting work (tax, audit, close) for an accountant to review.

## Why it fits the theme (or doesn't)

Core outcome-priced (company claim): 'We never had a seat-based model for our core product'; no token pricing either (src-20260730-3a49fd).

## Traction and signals

Works with at least one-third of the Top 25 US accounting firms; $100M Series B led by Accel; $1.15B valuation (src-20260730-3a49fd).

## Economics (what we know)

Outcome pricing mapped to firms' fixed client fees; rates not public.

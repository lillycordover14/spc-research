## What they do
Founded 2025 by the Soroco founders, who spent a decade capturing how enterprise work actually gets done. Workfabric turns that work data into digital twins of people and business objects and coordinates next steps on sales, renewals and escalations [Harmonic]. 25 people, ~17 in engineering [Harmonic].

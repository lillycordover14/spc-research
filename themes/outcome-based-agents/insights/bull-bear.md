# Bull vs bear: should SPC push further on outcome-based agents?

Updated 2026-10-25 by grokbot. Each claim has a source. The bull case argues for pushing further (sourcing and investing). The bear case argues for publishing our view and stopping.

## Bull: push further
1. Outcome pricing is real revenue, not rhetoric. Zendesk passed $200M AI ARR on per-resolution billing [src-20260805-5a9fa7]. Sierra went from $100M to $150M ARR in a quarter [src-20260309-78d5a1]. Fin crossed $100M at 99 cents [src-20250821-666ad1].
2. Adoption is moving. Outcome pricing went from 18% to 23% of AI vendors in six months [src-20260715-e16b49]. Salesforce added 2,000 paying production customers in a quarter [src-20260826-580137].
3. The budget is labor and BPO, not software. There's about $119B of US CX wages [src-20260827-b22de5], and HFS expects $422B of BPO to move to services-as-software [src-20260831-73c927]. Concentrix's impairment and TP's flat core show the displacement has started [src-20260929-4fcfe9] [src-20260730-4cc420].
4. There's a liquid exit market. Fin sold for about $3.6B [src-20260615-74b583], Decagon has a banker [src-20261013-6d4b75], and Sierra bought Takeoff [src-20260723-bd3453]. SAP, Workday, NiCE and Genesys are all buying [src-20261001-056aad] [src-20261013-7962db] [src-20260805-3deb2c] [src-20260902-b6edda].
5. Vertical outcome companies outside CX are early enough for SPC entry and have named customers: Pace at Palomar (90%+ untouched) [src-20261025-c77b6e], Duvo at Notino and Morrisons [src-20261014-fb6b7e], and Magentic at Siemens and CCEP [src-20260923-47e348].
6. SPC's enterprise GTM edge fits. Deployment is the bottleneck, with years of change management ahead [src-20260629-015aae], and Sierra says the FDE motion guarantees the outcome [src-20260728-aba703].

## Bear: stop and publish the view
1. Vendors grade their own outcomes. Zendesk verifies with its own LLM [src-20250806-3921b6], self-reports leak [src-20261025-24844b], and no third-party auditor exists in public sources. Outcome revenue could be repriced at renewal.
2. Outcome pricing slows sales. HubSpot says it lengthened cycles [src-20260812-1b0bca], SAP keeps 2027 traditional [src-20261021-551046], and buyers prefer prepaid pools and credits [src-20250918-7e3ad0] [src-20260827-fb556f].
3. Margins are worse than SaaS: 50 to 60% vs 80 to 90% [src-20260210-496d37]. FDE is "services play but with probably with more expensive engineers" [src-20260710-e2a61b].
4. Labs and hyperscalers are absorbing the stack. OpenAI Presence goes after CX directly [src-20260722-a3993b], the Agents API carries no extra fee [src-20260910-014b24], and AWS is spending $1B on FDE [src-20260730-e4cc74]. Marketplaces let buyers spend lab commits on Sierra and Decagon, which makes the labs the channel [src-20260929-2fdd0f].
5. Prices are already high. Sierra trades at about 75x to 100x ARR and Decagon at about 45x, while Fin exited at roughly a third of that (see comps.md) [src-20260504-c9d184] [src-20260127-5d9191] [src-20260615-74b583].
6. Production is thin. 13% of CIOs report significant value [src-20261019-a120c4], 40% of agentic projects are expected to be canceled [src-20250625-8a6cff], and Gartner sees 70% abandoning FDE-built systems [src-20261012-11ce4a]. Incidents carry liability that contracts don't yet assign [src-20260428-8baab9] [src-20260916-6e03e5].
7. Horizontal orchestration lacks proof. Sycamore and Workfabric have no named customers [src-20260330-8d6266] [src-20261023-21a6d6].

## Net
Push further narrowly: vertical, outcome-sold companies in outsourced, countable work outside CX, at Series A or B. Publish a view rather than push on horizontal orchestration and CX leaders, which are priced out.

## Kill criteria (stop pushing if any of these hold by the next checkpoint)
1. No outcome contract passes the test. If expert calls find no outcome-priced contract that renewed at the same or a higher unit price with a customer-agreed count, the thesis drops to "pricing rhetoric".
2. Labs win the deal. If two or more F500 buyers or SI leads say a lab platform (Frontier, Managed Agents, Presence) plus an SI replaced a vertical vendor in a live deal, world 1 overtakes world 5.
3. Margin. If formers report gross margin after inference and FDE below 50% with no downward trend in FDE hours per customer, these are services multiples, not software.
4. Incumbent default. If buyers say they take Agentforce, Now Assist or Joule by default and won't buy point vendors, world 2 absorbs world 5.
5. Price. If no candidate in Q10 is available below about 30x forward ARR, there's no entry point at SPC's stage.
6. Verification. If a buyer reports clawbacks or disputed counts at scale, outcome revenue quality is impaired.

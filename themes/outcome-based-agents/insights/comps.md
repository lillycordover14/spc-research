## Read

- The two CX leaders carry 45x to 100x ARR. The only priced exit of an outcome-priced agent (Fin) cleared at roughly a third of Sierra's multiple, so the buyer paid far less than private marks imply.
- Incumbent buyers (Salesforce, NiCE, Genesys, SAP, Workday, and ServiceNow in talks) are the exit path. The pattern so far is that they buy outcome-priced CX agents and harness pieces, not horizontal orchestration.
- Outside CX, almost no company discloses ARR. Valuations for Pace, Actively and Basis cannot be tested against revenue in public data. That's a gap for expert calls (see expert-calls.md).

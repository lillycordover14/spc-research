# Investor theses and market maps

Verbatim excerpts from public VC and analyst theses on agents, orchestration and outcome pricing (services-as-software, "sell the work", agent stack maps). One section per piece: title, author, date, link, source id, then the excerpt.

## Services: The New Software
Julien Bek, Sequoia Capital, 2026-03-05. https://sequoiacap.com/article/services-the-new-software [src-20260305-abed56]
> The next $1T company will be a software company masquerading as a services firm.
> A copilot sells the tool. An autopilot sells the work.
> For every dollar spent on software, six are spent on services.
> Replacing an outsourcing contract with an AI-native services provider is a vendor swap. Replacing headcount is a reorg.
> IT managed services ($100B+). ... Nobody has yet sold "your IT runs" directly to the company as an outcome.

## AI leads a service as software paradigm shift
Joanne Chen and Jaya Gupta, Foundation Capital, 2024-04-18. https://foundationcapital.com/ai-leads-a-service-as-software-paradigm-shift/ [src-20240418-4dfe9a]
> AI companies are leading a transition from software as a service to service as software, turning the table on the very essence of SaaS.
> a $4.6 trillion opportunity, since the global services market dwarfs the software market in size.
> Rather than charging customers per seat ... software vendors instead charge based on the number of qualified opportunities or signed customers their software delivers.

## You are not a model. Don't price per token.
Tugce Erten and Sarah Wang, Andreessen Horowitz, 2026-08-27. https://a16z.com/you-are-not-a-model-dont-price-per-token/ [src-20260827-fb556f]
> we believe companies should price at the highest layer of value that they can reliably measure, attribute, and defend.
> If you deliver a clear and attributable business result, price the outcome.
> In our survey of 50 technical AI buyers, 27 preferred credits tied to recognizable work, while only 14 preferred tokens.
> Once the outcome becomes observable, attributable, and valuable enough to support a stable price, the meter should move again.

## Investing in Lassie
Alex Rampell and Olivia Moore, Andreessen Horowitz, 2026-06-03. https://a16z.com/announcement/investing-in-lassie/ [src-20260603-ce0e80]
> Lassie is not a copilot that creates more work for the practice to review. It does the work.
> Lassie already works with more than 700 practices across 49 states, generates more than $10 million in annualized revenue, and provides practices with 250,000 hours of labor a year.
> The last generation of software gave businesses better tools. AI agents change the unit of value from software to labor.

## The AI pricing and monetization playbook
Atlas Editors, Bessemer Venture Partners, 2026-02-10. https://www.bvp.com/atlas/the-ai-pricing-and-monetization-playbook [src-20260210-496d37]
> Outcomes maximize value alignment but require absorbing cost variability.
> Companies see 50-60% gross margins vs. 80-90% for SaaS.
> As 2025 pilots hit 2026 renewals, pricing must reflect actual value, not promise.
> Client-server monetized licenses. SaaS monetized access. AI will monetize outcomes.

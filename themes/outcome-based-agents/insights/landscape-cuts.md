# Landscape cuts: outcome-based agents (built 2026-10-27, extended 2026-10-28, grokbot)

Modeled on ai-native-industrial-production/insights/cross-sector-map.md: an inclusion test, one primary home per company per cut, a source id on every row, incumbents kept as context. Full reference; primer Section 8 carries the short version.

**Inclusion test.** A startup (private, Seed to late stage) that sells AI agents doing enterprise work, or the tools that run them. Sources: every company card in companies/, insights/startup-landscape.md (47 startups), companies/_candidates.md, and the sources/ records cited. 121 startups placed: 65 from the first build, 36 added 2026-10-28 from 2025 to 2026 funding reports and VC primers (Foundation Capital src-20250703-8a38e7, a16z src-20260903-a696de and src-20241113-d5f4fa, Bessemer src-20250813-44ef93, src-20251028-1ffc30 and src-20250128-3948ca), and 19 added later on 2026-10-28 from AlphaSense batch 4 (UBS Private 100 src-20261022-c0698e, Oppenheimer src-20261007-54b3ff, and expert calls on AI SDRs, procurement, RCM, recruiting and insurance BPO). Late private names (Series D and later, including the UBS profiles of Harvey, Cognition, Abridge, Workato and Modal) are kept as comps even though they sit outside the Seed to Series C check band. Incumbents, labs and SIs are listed once in Cut 1 as context and not placed in Cuts 2 to 6. Edison Scientific (biopharma R&D agents) was added later on 2026-10-28 at Lilly's request [src-20251218-eefcdb].

**Rules.** Each startup sits in exactly one group per cut. Secondary fit is noted below a table only where it changes the read. Pricing and stage come only from a filed source; anything else is "not disclosed". Funding press releases and company pages are company claims. Cut 6 (thesis position) is my read: [inference].

## Sharpest observations
- [inference] Outcome pricing at the startup level is mostly a press-release claim (Ema, Crosby, Procode, Vida). Every buyer-verified price on file is per transaction (Sierra, Decagon, Crescendo, Duvo, DeepOpinion).
- [inference] The thesis-fit picks cluster in finance, procurement, healthcare billing and insurance, not CX. CX is already a late-stage price war (Sierra, Decagon, Fin).
- [inference] "Sells the finished work" (Pace, Crosby, Procode, Prosper AI, Crescendo, and now Harper and Reserv) is a distinct group: they price the job, not the software, and Crescendo's margin fell under that model [src-20260724-865e53].
- [inference] SI-delivered startups (Otera, DeepOpinion, Workfabric) all run through Cognizant. One SI is the whole channel evidence.
- 23 of 121 startups have no disclosed stage, and 98 have no disclosed price; those rows need an expert call or a pricing page.
- [inference] A new pattern in the 2026-10-28 additions: vertical apps that replace the record rather than ride on it (Rillet and Campfire for the ledger, Serval for ITSM, Federato for insurance core, Polimorphic for local government). Bessemer calls this systems of action replacing systems of record [src-20250813-44ef93].
- [inference] Incumbents are investing in the agents that sit on their records: Guidewire in Sixfold, Thomson Reuters Ventures in Supio, RELX's REV in EvenUp, Accenture Ventures in Reserv. That is a channel signal that does not show up in Cut 4.
- [inference] Insurance now has three models side by side: embedded agent (Sixfold), AI-native core (Federato), and the AI-native service firm that owns the outcome (Harper as broker, Reserv as TPA).

- [inference] Batch 4 (2026-10-28) evidence cuts both ways on outcome pricing. UBS credits Sierra's growth partly to outcome-based pricing [src-20261022-c0698e], but every buyer-side price in the new expert calls is per use, per agent or per hour: Pace usage with a floor [src-20260811-a0aa1e], Thoughtful AI per agent [src-20240919-474182], micro1-style BPO challengers 'X dollars per hour' [src-20250611-a24e31].
- [inference] AI BPO is commoditizing from both ends: an insurer expects someone to 'offer it at a 10th of the price' with open models [src-20260811-a0aa1e], payer BPO rates fell 20% to 40% in two years [src-20260930-124dc3], and incumbent RCM and BPO firms (Health Prime, Conduent) are shipping agents themselves [src-20260608-5e7d7e] [src-20260930-124dc3].

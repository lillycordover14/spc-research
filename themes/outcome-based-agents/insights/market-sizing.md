## Read

- The labor and services pools (BPO about $786B by 2035 per HFS; IT services $1.6T in 2026 per Gartner) are 20x to 50x the agent software line ($29B in 2026 per Gartner). Outcome-priced agents are sized against services, not software (Q4).
- Disclosed incumbent agent and AI revenue (Salesforce Agentforce $1.5B+, Workday about $600M, Genesys $400M+, NiCE $362M) sums to about $2.9B, consistent with Bain's $4B to $6B captured today, though each company defines the metric differently (Q1).
- Inside services, AI revenue is about 10% of the top Indian SIs (TCS, Infosys) while their total growth is low single digit, so AI is replacing more than adding (Q4).
- Gaps: no free primary contact center TAM; Gartner AI Agents line counts cross-functional agents and assistants and may exclude agents embedded in apps.

# OBA memo v18: lab takeover test by category (2026-10-29)

Test: in each category, what did OpenAI and Anthropic (plus Microsoft and Google where relevant) ship or announce from roughly February to October 2026? If the labs now sell the finished, accountable work, the verdict is "Why we should not focus here". If they ship tools, templates or engineers but leave the accountable work (license, liability, write access, cross-company execution) to someone else, the category stays a focus, narrowed to that work. Source ids are src- suffixes under sources/. Dates are publication or announcement dates.

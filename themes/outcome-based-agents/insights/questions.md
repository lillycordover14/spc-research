# Open questions: outcome-based agents and agentic orchestration

The agenda. Every source should move at least one of these; tag its takeaways with the number, e.g. "(Q5)". Evidence collects in `evidence-by-question.md`; each weekly file in `weekly/` gives a one-paragraph answer per question with source ids. Status: open / partial / answered.

First readout: **Wed/Thu 2026-10-28/29.** Until then, consume what already exists (transcript libraries, podcasts, reports, earnings calls). No new expert calls until the framework below is sharp.

## A. Tipping point: are large enterprises running many agents in production, and paying for outcomes?
1. **Production, not pilots.** What share of large enterprises (Fortune 500 / Global 2000) run agents in production, how many agents each, and in which functions first (CX, IT ops, finance ops, procurement, sales ops)? Survey data vs what operators say on the record. _status: open_
2. **Outcome pricing, real or rhetoric?** Who actually charges per resolution, per task or share of savings, at what price points, and does it survive renewal and procurement? Who has moved back to seats or committed spend? _status: open_
3. **What blocks production:** reliability and evals, permissions and identity, integration into systems of record, change management, liability, cost? Which blocker is a product opportunity and which is a services job? _status: open_
4. **Whose budget:** IT software, BPO and outsourcing, or headcount? Who signs: CIO, COO, function head? _status: open_

## B. Right to win: which layer can the model labs not easily absorb?
5. **Who builds the harness?** Labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google), platforms (Microsoft, Salesforce, ServiceNow, UiPath), the enterprise itself, SIs, or vertical apps? Does every enterprise converge on the same harness, or does each build its own? _status: open_
6. **What did the labs ship in the last 6-9 months for enterprise agents, and what did it absorb?** One list, dated, with the startup categories each launch hit. _status: open_
7. **Orchestration vs context vs vertical workflow vs services:** which is defensible against a lab? Is "context on how work gets done" a durable, compounding asset (data rights, switching cost) or a feature? _status: open_
8. **Systems of record:** does value accrue to the orchestration layer or stay with Salesforce / ServiceNow / SAP / Workday because they hold the data and the permissions? _status: open_

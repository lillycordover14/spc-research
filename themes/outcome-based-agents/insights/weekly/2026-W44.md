# OBA readout, 2026-W44 (for 2026-10-28/29)

## 1. Answer first
Push further, but narrowly. The labs and systems of record are absorbing the generic harness (runtime, evals, memory, identity, CX overlays), so horizontal orchestration platforms look hard to back (world tally: labs 12, SIs 7, systems of record 6, enterprise 4, vertical apps 0 explicit). The open ground is outcome-sold work in already-outsourced, measurable processes (IT ops, CX, AP, collections), where outcome pricing is real at $0.99 to $2 per resolution and SIs and BPOs are now selling it too. We push further on vertical and services-as-software companies with SPC operator access, and take a perspective, not a push, on horizontal orchestration.

- 17% of enterprises have agents in production (Gartner 2026 CIO survey, via Technology Org) [src-20261012-11ce4a]; only 13% of 2,002 CIOs report significant value from AI [src-20261019-a120c4].
- Outcome pricing is 18% of AI vendors, up from 2% (ICONIQ 2026, n about 305 software execs, via HackerNoon) [src-20260924-1b6619].
- Agent fleets doubled in a quarter (modal 26-50 to 76-100) while monitoring coverage only reached about 52% (Gravitee, n=750, Apr 2026, vendor survey) [src-20261012-fb5063].
- Quote: "If you can't define a checkpoint for your product, outcome-based billing isn't available to you yet" [src-20261003-6d97c0].
- Quote: "people build in their budgets that 60% of the conversation will be automated in three months. Those are wrong assumptions" (Exotel's Bhatia) [src-20261019-d96d4c].
- Budget: agentic AI funding up 31.8% vs IT budgets up 3.7% for 2027 (Gartner) [src-20260831-2bda07]; Gartner sizes $234B of software spend exposed to agentic arbitrage by 2030 [src-20261016-9f1777].

## 5. What we'd need from calls (Q13-Q15)
Call types: F500 head of IT ops or CX automation; SI agent practice lead (Cognizant, Deloitte, DXC); Duvo, Sycamore or Workfabric customer or former.
Five questions:
1. What share of your agent spend is priced per outcome today, and how is the outcome detected and audited? (Q2)
2. Who built the harness in production: lab platform, SI, your team, or the system-of-record vendor? (Q5)
3. Which budget line funded it: IT software, BPO or managed services, or the business unit? (Q4)
4. What blocked the last pilot from production: data quality, permissions, or evals? (Q3)
5. If the lab shipped this feature tomorrow, what would you still pay the vendor for? (Q7, Q9)

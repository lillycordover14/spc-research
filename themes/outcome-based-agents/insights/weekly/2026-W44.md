# OBA readout, 2026-W44 (for 2026-10-28/29)

## 1. Answer first
Push further, but narrowly. The labs and systems of record are absorbing the generic harness (runtime, evals, memory, identity, CX overlays), so horizontal orchestration platforms look hard to back (world tally as of 2026-10-23, all framework.md lines: labs 18, SIs 15, systems of record 14, vertical apps 10, enterprise 9). The open ground is outcome-sold work in already-outsourced, measurable processes (IT ops, CX, AP, collections), where outcome pricing is real at $0.99 to $2 per resolution and SIs and BPOs are now selling it too. We push further on vertical and services-as-software companies with SPC operator access, and take a perspective, not a push, on horizontal orchestration.

## 2. Tipping point (Q1-Q4)
- 17% of enterprises have agents in production (Gartner 2026 CIO survey, via Technology Org) [src-20261012-11ce4a]; only 13% of 2,002 CIOs report significant value from AI [src-20261019-a120c4].
- Outcome pricing is 18% of AI vendors, up from 2% (ICONIQ 2026, n about 305 software execs, via HackerNoon) [src-20260924-1b6619].
- Agent fleets doubled in a quarter (modal 26-50 to 76-100) while monitoring coverage only reached about 52% (Gravitee, n=750, Apr 2026, vendor survey) [src-20261012-fb5063].
- Quote: "If you can't define a checkpoint for your product, outcome-based billing isn't available to you yet" [src-20261003-6d97c0].
- Quote: "people build in their budgets that 60% of the conversation will be automated in three months. Those are wrong assumptions" (Exotel's Bhatia) [src-20261019-d96d4c].
- Origin of per-resolution pricing: Intercom traded an $80 seat for about 150 resolutions to set $1, then 99 cents; resolution rate went from about 25% to 65% [src-20251002-0b85b2]; Fin is priced at 99 cents against a human cost of $20-30 per ticket [src-20250821-666ad1]. Fin gets zero on late handoffs and says competitors bill on "total conversation volume" [src-20260114-5bb0dc].
- Outcome pricing at scale: Zendesk's CEO: "We passed 200 million dollars uh last year of AI ARR. We will pass 400 million this year", targets 1B AI ARR in 2028, and says "We only get paid if we solve your problem" and "seats are going to go away over time" [src-20260805-5a9fa7]. Intercom's CFO says resolution rates went from 25% to "close to 70% today" and "the more it's resolving, the better for our margin profile" [src-20260522-b1dbe3]. Sierra did "100 million in seven quarters, 150 in eight" [src-20260309-78d5a1].
- Buyers start with fixed or consumption pricing in year one; Paid's Medina suggests an "outcome bonus" instead of pure per-outcome [src-20250422-de3b52]. Insight's Akkiraju frames agents as a "fractional FTE" and cites enterprises burning a year of AI budget in 3 months [src-20260427-1a7ad0].
- Pricing meters: Decagon customers "buy a a sort of a allotment of conversations for the term and then they burn down" [src-20250918-7e3ad0]; a16z says move to outcomes only once they are "observable, attributable, and valuable enough", and 27 of 50 buyers preferred work-based credits vs 14 tokens [src-20260827-fb556f]; Bessemer puts AI gross margins at 50-60% vs 80-90% for SaaS and flags a 2026 renewal cliff [src-20260210-496d37].
- Slow diffusion: Levie: "we're just going to be in for years and years of deployment and change management and adoption" and "cost might become the big story in the next 3 years" [src-20260629-015aae]; McDermott says only 11% of companies are past the experiment phase [src-20260417-a917fe]; Dines says agents are no easier to deploy than before [src-20260921-f28021].
- Budget: agentic AI funding up 31.8% vs IT budgets up 3.7% for 2027 (Gartner) [src-20260831-2bda07]; Gartner sizes $234B of software spend exposed to agentic arbitrage by 2030 [src-20261016-9f1777].

## 3. Right to win (Q5-Q8)
- World we believe: labs (1) plus SIs (4) jointly. Labs own the platform; SIs and BPOs own last-mile deployment. Evidence: Cognizant's 18,000-engineer Gemini group [src-20261022-d5ad54], Deloitte's open-model FDE practice [src-20261009-4138dc], DXC OASIS on LangChain, scaling toward 125 customer environments [src-20261013-1dcd15], Google RISE plus FDEs [src-20261023-5b3495].
- Hyperscalers now take deployment too: AWS put $1B into its own Forward Deployed Engineering team [src-20260730-e4cc74], Microsoft runs FDE teams and sells a model-agnostic harness in Foundry (100,000 customers) [src-20260729-8b3c16], Oracle deploys FDEs and will use agents to automate Fusion implementation [src-20260910-fa41de], and GenAI is about half of IBM Consulting signings [src-20260722-1275ae]. None of the five hyperscalers prices agents per outcome; Microsoft is 'seat plus usage' and Agent 365 is $15 per user [src-20260729-8b3c16, src-20260309-a3fd3a]. Google pays SIs a $750M partner fund, including embedded FDEs [src-20260422-72407e], and AWS certifies agentic consulting partners [src-20251130-95bb98]. AWS CEO Garman says most enterprise agents are still simple and non-autonomous, and AWS FDEs aim to hand evals and agents to customers in about 45 days [src-20261008-3ac60a].
- Lab launches, last 6-9 months, and layers absorbed: Claude Managed Agents (harness-infra) [src-20260408-e5d37b]; Managed Agents outcomes and multi-agent orchestration (orchestration) [src-20260519-55cca6]; OpenAI Agents API (harness-infra) [src-20260910-014b24]; OpenAI DevDay always-on and role agents (orchestration, vertical) [src-20260929-2fdd0f]; OpenAI Presence CX agent (vertical-outcome) [src-20260722-a3993b]; Gemini agent at Gemini at Work (orchestration) [src-20261008-d5cbe2]; Claude Duet (vertical SMB) [src-20261021-d5280b]; Microsoft Contact Center Intelligence (vertical CX) [src-20261021-e45563]; Meta Enterprise Platform (model API, business agents) [src-20261021-f65653]; Google Agents CLI LangChain extension (harness-infra) [src-20261021-16f218].
- Systems of record are defending: Agentforce $2 per resolution [src-20260926-ff7125], Oracle Fusion Claw native orchestration [src-20261003-10d57c], ServiceNow AI ACV over $1B [src-20260722-d4586c], Salesforce buying Fin for about $3.6B [src-20260615-74b583].
- Harness build vs buy: Chase: "the more in distribution you are of what the models are trained on, then the better the off-the-shelf harness will be" and "You want to be able to switch to avoid lock-in" [src-20260813-09fcee]; LangChain on deep agents and open models [src-20260506-041ecf]. Decagon runs "90% of our workflow" on open source and says without that "you're just building a glorified consulting truck" [src-20260731-a07321].
- FDE equals services: Workfabric's Murty: "forward deployment engineering is basically again services play but with probably with more expensive engineers", and incumbents' "incentive structure is billing" [src-20260710-e2a61b]; Levie sees "some version of like a forward-deployed engineer" in most workflows [src-20260629-015aae].
- Headless and control planes: Levie calls Salesforce's move the world's largest SaaS company "fully endorsing that headless is the future" [src-20260629-015aae]; at Dreamforce 2026 a panel host credited headless with countering the data-moat fear, and Slack is pitched as the control plane [src-20260918-ddaf3a]; McDermott pitches ServiceNow as the "AI control tower", adds a consumption model on top of seats, and says 90% of ServiceNow's own CS cases run on agents [src-20260417-a917fe]; Dines pitches UiPath's "map of work" plus rails, and says outcome deals are entertained for large transformations [src-20260921-f28021, src-20251001-78dba6].
- FDE ties to outcomes: Taylor: "be accountable for that that outcome. It's why we have outcomes-based pricing, it's why we have we call it agent development" [src-20260309-78d5a1]; Sierra's Meurer: "how do you actually guarantee the outcome? And that really is forward-deployed engineering" [src-20260728-aba703].
- Harness commoditizing: ex-Cursor harness lead Katz cites studies where harness choice did not significantly change coding eval scores but did change cost per task, and expects 90% of agent work off the laptop within 6 months [src-20261019-54d838]. Buyers buy standardized front-end agents (support) and build variable back-end ones [src-20260427-1a7ad0]; Capital One built its own multi-agent platform on open-source models (world 3) [src-20260416-e6896d].
- Counter-signal: Gartner predicts 70% of enterprises will abandon vendor-FDE-built agent systems by 2028 [src-20261012-11ce4a].

## 5. What we'd need from calls (Q13-Q15)
Call types: F500 head of IT ops or CX automation; SI agent practice lead (Cognizant, Deloitte, DXC); Duvo, Sycamore or Workfabric customer or former.
Five questions:
1. What share of your agent spend is priced per outcome today, and how is the outcome detected and audited? (Q2)
2. Who built the harness in production: lab platform, SI, your team, or the system-of-record vendor? (Q5)
3. Which budget line funded it: IT software, BPO or managed services, or the business unit? (Q4)
4. What blocked the last pilot from production: data quality, permissions, or evals? (Q3)
5. If the lab shipped this feature tomorrow, what would you still pay the vendor for? (Q7, Q9)

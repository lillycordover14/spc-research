E XP ERT S E NTI ME NT

 The expert is optimistic about the adoption and effectiveness of AI agents in automating complex RCM workflows, particularly in non-
 hospital healthcare settings.
 They believe that while full automation of certain manual processes is possible in the future, significant technical and investment
 barriers remain, especially in complex specialties like behavioral health and dental.
 The expert is confident in the strong and growing receptivity of the target buyer universe to AI agent solutions, citing high
 inbound interest and positive cost and control dynamics compared to traditional outsourcing.
TA KE AWAYS

 AI agents are designed to operate within existing EHR and claims software, automating manual RCM tasks such as prior authorization,
 eligibility verification, and claims management by mimicking human workflows and leveraging technologies like RPA, OCR, and GenAI.
 Current automated software solutions often fail to handle complex exceptions and edge cases, requiring human intervention, whereas AI
 agents can address these gaps more effectively.
 The main buyers are non-hospital, rapidly growing provider groups (often PE-backed) with large RCM teams, and pricing is based on the
 number and complexity of agents rather than transaction volume.
 AI agents typically reduce the manual workload for RCM teams, with industry benchmarks for automation ranging from 50% to 80% of
 transaction volume, depending on process complexity.
 There is strong buyer interest in AI agent solutions due to their transparency, control, and cost advantages over outsourcing, with
 additional potential in pharma, med device, and infusion sectors.

TA KE AWAYS

 The decision to adopt Omilia was driven by the need to improve containment rates in the IVR system, enhance compliance and quality
 monitoring, and address risks associated with remote agents during the COVID-19 period.
 Omilia was chosen over competitors like Observe.AI, NICE, Twilio, Genesys, and Five9 due to its integrated biometric voice-based
 security and advanced agent assist capabilities, which streamline knowledge search and support compliance.
 Implementation of Omilia required significant integration with internal systems and took 6-12 months, with ongoing calibration needed to
 adapt to varied customer interactions.
 The annual licensing cost for Omilia is between $500,000 and $1 million, with total integration costs reaching several million dollars, but this
 is considered a small investment relative to the overall technology budget.
 Omilia is seen as better suited for large organizations due to its focus on security and customer experience, but its sales-oriented
 conversational capabilities lag behind some competitors.

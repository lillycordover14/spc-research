TA KE AWAYS

 The expert distinguishes between 'AI SDR' (autonomous agents) and 'AI-powered SDR' (tools that augment human reps), noting that the
 latter is currently far more effective.
 Fully autonomous AI SDR solutions have not delivered on their promises, with high customer churn rates (80-90%) and limited
 real-world impact, especially in outbound sales where phone calls remain critical.
 Niche tools like Nooks (AI-powered parallel dialer) and Actively AI (CRM data surfacing) are valued for solving specific workflow
 inefficiencies and enabling reps to focus on selling.
 The expert's organization uses a combination of tools for account selection, enrichment, and engagement, with a preference for solutions
 that streamline repetitive tasks rather than attempt to automate the entire sales process.
 The market for standalone AI-powered sales tools is likely capped at around $100 million in revenue unless companies diversify; large-scale
 consolidation or acquisition by major players (e.g., Salesforce, Gong) is expected.

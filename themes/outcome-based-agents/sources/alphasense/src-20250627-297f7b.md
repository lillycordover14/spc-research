 savings for large enterprises as adoption increases.
 They believe that while agentic AI is still in early stages, the next year will see noticeable scaling, especially as platforms become easier

 grow across more business functions.
TA KE AWAYS

 Agentic AI platforms, such as DeepOpinion, are enabling end-to-end automation of complex processes like insurance claims, with
 straight-through processing rates as high as 95% in some cases.
 Implementations have resulted in significant workforce reductions (up to 65% in claims processing) and substantial cost savings,
 with expectations of further scaling across more product lines and geographies.
 The adoption of agentic AI is still in its early days, with only a small percentage of potential processes currently automated, but pilots in BPO
 operations show automation potential of 40-60%.
 Barriers to scaling include organizational 'POC fatigue', lack of clear direction, integration challenges, and the need for business
 transformation rather than treating projects as pure IT initiatives.
 Pricing for agentic AI platforms is typically consumption-based (per task), with cost savings shared between service providers and
 clients, leading to higher margins for providers despite lower overall revenues.

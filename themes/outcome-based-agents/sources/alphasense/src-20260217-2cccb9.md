E XP ERT S E NTI ME NT

 Believes that modern procurement platforms like Zip, Omnea, and Pivot all provide solid intake solutions, but none offer exceptional
 customizability or fully meet advanced needs without additional in-house development.
 Expresses skepticism about relying on a single vendor for all procurement needs due to concerns about vendor lock-in, pricing practices,
 and lack of flexibility, preferring a modular approach with the ability to build or integrate custom solutions.
 Views AI features as valuable only when deeply embedded and genuinely improving workflow, rather than as superficial add-ons.
TA KE AWAYS

 The expert evaluated Omnea, Zip, and Pivot, focusing on user experience, customizability, integration capabilities, and cost, with a
 particular need for integration with Workday's finance module.
 All three platforms are seen as strong for intake orchestration, but differ in depth and market focus: Pivot is best suited for smaller
 companies, Omnea for mid-market with AI and supplier focus, and Zip is positioned as the enterprise leader with broader features and
 higher pricing.
 Customization of user interfaces and workflows is limited across all platforms, with none allowing significant tailoring beyond standard forms
 and logic; this was a key shortcoming for the expert.
 Sales and vendor interactions varied: Pivot offered a direct, informal approach; Omnea was respectful but engaged in negative
 competitor talk; Zip's process was formal, difficult to access product trials, and marked by aggressive sales tactics and inconsistent
 pricing.
 The expert values vendors with similar customer profiles, transparent and supportive sales and support teams, and a
 demonstrated commitment to ongoing product innovation, but is wary of high funding levels without profitability and founders
 lacking procurement backgrounds.

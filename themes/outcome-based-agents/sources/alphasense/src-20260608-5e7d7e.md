TA KE AWAYS

 call volume during peak seasons.
 In AR, agentic AI is effective for automating payer calls and prior authorization processes, but always with human oversight for complex or
 clinical tasks.
 Claim scrubbing with AI is effective, especially when the RCM provider owns the end-to-end process, allowing for feedback loops from
 denials to improve front-end processes.
 Cost improvements from agentic AI are typically in the low double digits, with higher savings possible in areas with more low-hanging fruit,
 but claims of 20%+ savings are viewed skeptically.

 proven results are key differentiators, with some clients returning after unsuccessful AI-first engagements.

TA KE AWAYS

 The expert's company is a major customer of Duvo, using it to automate hundreds of workflows, primarily through business domain 'power
 users' rather than IT staff.
 Duvo is preferred for automating non-deterministic, AI-driven processes, while deterministic tasks are still handled by other tools like n8n;
 UiPath usage has been largely phased out.
 Key strengths of Duvo include its user-friendly process mapping, intuitive UI for agent creation, and the ability to chain agents for complex
 workflows.

 deterministic automation flows.

 savings, with annual costs in the high six to low seven digits.

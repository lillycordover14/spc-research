TA KE AWAYS

 Procurement BPO adoption is highest in micro and SMB segments (up to 75% in micro, 45-50% in SMB), with enterprise clients typically
 retaining procurement in-house except for point solutions.
 Didero is best suited for smaller procurement teams needing operational automation and quick scalability, especially in manufacturing and
 distribution, with strong ERP integration and rapid deployment.
 Pavus excels in contract management, governance, and intelligence, providing value in contract risk management, rebate tracking, and
 compliance, primarily for mid-market and enterprise clients, but lacks transactional workflow automation.
 Magentic is closely aligned with Pavus in contract intelligence and governance but offers deeper AI integration and broader workflow
 capabilities, making it suitable for a wider range of clients.
 Pactum stands out for AI-driven supplier negotiation, enabling autonomous or semi-autonomous negotiation processes, with human
 oversight, and is preferred for direct procurement negotiations.

The interview covered the challenges and outcomes of implementing AI automation and outcome-based pricing in a BPO environment, including

the AI-enabled customer service space.

 Former Director of Partner Services with 10 years of experience across two merged BPO companies, overseeing operations, client
 contracts, and the implementation of AI-driven automation in customer service environments.
E XP ERT S E NTI ME NT

 The expert expressed that the transition to outcome-based pricing and AI automation was rushed and poorly executed, leading to
 significant operational and financial challenges.
 They felt that the company prioritized being first to market over careful planning, resulting in widespread client dissatisfaction,
 declining margins, and unresolved systemic issues.
 By the time of their departure, the expert described the company atmosphere as 'dark,' with a sense of impending crisis and little
 optimism for recovery.
TA KE AWAYS

 The company shifted from traditional per-FTE pricing to outcome-based, volume-driven contracts, primarily targeting email and text
 channels for AI automation.
 AI containment rates for email started at 30-40% and improved only for clients with robust, well-documented knowledge bases; complex or
 poorly documented cases performed poorly.
 Aggressive discounting was used to win clients over to the new model, but this eroded margins, especially when AI failed to deliver
 promised efficiencies, leading to backlogs and client dissatisfaction.
 Operational metrics such as CSAT, first response time, and resolution time declined sharply due to backlogs and pressure on agents,
 with some improvement only when staffing was increased again.
 Competition from incumbents like Intercom and Zendesk was strong due to their integration and data access advantages, while newer AI-
 only competitors like Kodif and Decagon were also encountered but often lost on price.

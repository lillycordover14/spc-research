 The expert believes that while AI agentic automation is revolutionary and will drive significant automation and cost savings, full
 enterprise adoption will be a gradual, multi-year process due to complexity and change management.
 They see model selection and dynamic cost control as higher priority levers for enterprises than input compression tools like Caveman,
 especially as token costs become a more significant part of operational expenses.
TA KE AWAYS

 AI agentic automation is still in early stages of enterprise penetration, with most value expected in automating complex, unstructured
 processes over the next 5-7 years.
 Token efficiency and LLM consumption costs are not yet a top concern for most enterprises, but are becoming more relevant, especially for
 developer use cases.
 Model selection—choosing the right LLM for the right task—is seen as the most impactful way to control costs, with some platforms already
 offering dynamic model selection based on task complexity and cost.
 Adoption of AI tools is uneven across enterprises, with developer use being widespread but broader workflow integration still facing
 challenges related to governance, security, and change management.
 Enterprises are open to experimenting with innovative, smaller AI vendors if value can be demonstrated, but there is a strong bias toward
 established platforms already integrated into their IT landscape.

The interview covers the evaluation and deployment of AI vendors for customer support and internal workflow automation at a large telecommunications enterprise,
detailing the selection criteria, vendor performance, and future consolidation plans.

E XP ERT B A CK GR O U N D

 Senior Vice President overseeing marketing, procurement, training, ESG, and compliance at a major global telecommunications operator,
 with direct responsibility for evaluating and deploying AI-driven customer support and workflow automation solutions across a large, multi-
 country enterprise.
E XP ERT S E NTI ME NT

 Believes the AI customer support and workflow automation market is rapidly evolving and expects significant consolidation and
 commoditization in the near future.
 Prefers to work with specialist vendors for enterprise AI solutions due to their focus on orchestration and compliance, but aims to eventually
 standardize on one or two providers for efficiency.
 Emphasizes the importance of customer experience (NPS, CSAT) over pure cost savings, and is cautious about data privacy and
 regulatory compliance, especially with US-based foundation model providers.
TA KE AWAYS

 The company currently operates a multi-vendor setup for AI-driven customer support and workflow automation, including Sierra, Decagon,
 ElevenLabs, Wonderful AI, and previously HappyRobot.
 Vendors are evaluated on a comprehensive set of criteria including business fit, performance, integration complexity, security, commercial
 model, vendor maturity, global capabilities, and innovation.
 Sierra and ElevenLabs are currently the top performers, with Sierra excelling in complex customer experience orchestration and
 ElevenLabs leading in voice quality and creative content generation.
 Decagon is seen as a strong automation provider for simple workflows but lags behind Sierra in complex use cases; Wonderful AI
 is still being evaluated and has not demonstrated clear differentiation.
 The company plans to reduce the number of vendors to one or two within 18 months, favoring those who can deliver both high-quality
 customer experience and robust enterprise integration, while maintaining compliance with EU data regulations.

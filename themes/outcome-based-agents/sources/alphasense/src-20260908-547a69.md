TA KE AWAYS

 Forward-deployed engineering projects typically involve a small team (e.g., two engineers and two strategists) working closely with
 customer engineering and business teams to deliver AI-driven platforms that automate and accelerate business processes.
 The majority of technical work is often performed by the company's engineers, especially when the customer lacks a strong IT team, but
 there is a trend toward enabling customers to become self-sufficient to maximize recurring product revenue.
 Project contracts usually bundle professional services and platform usage fees, with recurring product revenue starting at low seven figures
 per year and potentially growing significantly with increased usage and additional use cases.
 Collaboration with third-party consulting firms varies: ex-employee firms are most similar in capability, while partnerships with large
 consultancies like Accenture tend to be more independent, with each party focusing on their respective domains.
 Compared to other IT service providers, the company's product-centric model enables compounding recurring revenue and higher growth,
 as opposed to the lower-margin, staff-dependent recurring revenue of traditional consultancies.

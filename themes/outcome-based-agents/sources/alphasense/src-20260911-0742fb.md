E XP ERT S E NTI ME NT

 The expert believes that while AI features like Now Assist and AI Control Tower are valuable, much of the recent revenue growth is
 driven by price increases rather than organic demand.
 The expert is cautious about the long-term incremental value of Now Assist, predicting a potential decline in usage due to
 competition and in-house alternatives, but sees AI Control Tower as a promising future direction for higher-level management use cases.
TA KE AWAYS

 Now Assist has significantly improved productivity and reduced costs for large firms, but its adoption can lead to reduced seat
 counts and license contraction as fewer employees are needed.
 Deal sizes for ServiceNow projects have grown, especially among large, forward-looking clients, but this is often offset by reduced license
 counts and increased pricing rather than pure organic growth.
 Many clients are considering or building in-house AI solutions or leveraging other AI products (e.g., Copilot, Anthropic, ChatGPT) as
 alternatives to Now Assist, especially for non-ITSM use cases.
 AI Control Tower is gaining interest, particularly among C-level executives for managing technical debt and prioritizing cybersecurity and
 risk, but widespread adoption is still pending as many firms wait to see competitor outcomes.
 Now Assist remains highly valuable for core ITSM use cases, where there are few viable alternatives, but faces competition in
 other modules and from custom-built solutions.

TA KE AWAYS

 The retailer uses AI solutions for both pre-purchase (discovery) and post-purchase (customer care) experiences, with different platforms
 handling each stage.
 Personal agent platforms like Muse are being integrated for product discovery, but full agentic checkout is not yet live; current partnerships

 preparing for potential future shifts in customer behavior.

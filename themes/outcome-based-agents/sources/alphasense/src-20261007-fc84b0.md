       Why the business impact of missed enquiries provides a stronger starting point for an AI conversation.
       How partners can identify customers that may benefit from AI Concierge.
       How to use outcome-based agents to address everyday operational problems.
       How Gamma partners can position practical AI without adding unnecessary complexity.

AI is often presented as a destination. It’s commonly viewed as a major transformational programme or .

The immediate problem for businesses is far more familiar. Customers call outside of normal working hours, or frontline teams are too stretched to
respond. What’s usually seen as a routine question added to a queue becomes a potential risk of an appointment never being booked.

Those enquiries end up unanswered, and routes to revenue are lost.

Partners should see these as conversation points. Rather than lead with questions on whether businesses are ready for AI, they should be focusing on
dealing with missed enquiries. What happens when someone gets in touch but there’s no response?

Why is leading with AI not the right sales approach?
‘AI’ is a broad term. On its own, it doesn’t tell a customer what problem will be addressed. There’s no clear indication on how their working day will
improve or where the commercial value will come from.

A conversation framed around missed enquiries is different. It begins with demand the customer already has and an operational problem they’re likely to
recognise.

The strongest questions are simple:

       What happens when a customer calls outside normal business hours?
       Which repetitive questions place the most pressure on staff?
       How much administrative work is involved in capturing information and arranging appointments?
       Where does the customer journey currently stall or stop?
       What’s the missed impact missed enquiries have on your business?

These questions move the discussion away from technical capabilities and . The aim should be to help customer respond to more enquiries. Avoidable
delays need to be reduced, and staff time must be used more effectively.

AI’s strength lies in its everyday use. Practical applications that make ordinary interactions work better on any platforms customers already have.

What happens when an enquiry goes unanswered?
An unanswered call goes further than being a communications issue. It can be the point at which a potential customer looks elsewhere.

The same principle applies beyond calls. If someone can’t get the answer or response they need, then the demand is there but the execution isn’t.

The value of every missed interaction can be assessed. The moment enquiries are being lost, and how often staff availability affects responses, can all be
identified. Better still, your customers can help dictate what happens next.

AI Concierge is designed to transform this problem into a real commercial opportunity.

How can AI Concierge help your customers?
AI Concierge consists of three separate agents, with each aligned with a recognisable customer interaction. These are:

      FAQ Agent: Reduce pressure on staff by providing accurate, context-aware answers to FAQs.
      Information Capture Agent: Lower the administrative workload by capturing the user responses to guided questions accurately and consistently.
      That way, your customer-facing teams have an easier review and follow-up process.
      Booking Agent: Reduce the number of repetitive, back-and-forward interactions from agents by allowing users to seamlessly check availability,
      schedule appointments and confirm bookings.

AI Concierge works to stop businesses missing out on valuable customer enquiries. It goes a long way in strengthening and preserving relationships.

Which businesses would benefit most from AI Concierge?
Through AI Concierge, partners can go to market with a proposition designed to capture all customer enquiries. Such a solution would be valuable to
teams that handle a high volume of inbound enquiries.

Are these calls frequently happening outside of normal business hours, and are customer questions found to be repetitive?

Appointment-led services, such as healthcare and finance, require that consistent, 24/7 presence. Their frontline teams are expected to accurately capture
each piece of information.

If it’s being captured manually, then too much time is being taken up, inevitably delaying handovers between people and departments.

These organisations aren’t necessarily looking for an AI project. They’re the ones with a visible operational problem that AI can help address.

How does this create value for Gamma partners?
AI Concierge is built to be easy to deploy, operate and support. By removing complex terminology, partners can qualify customers faster and shorten
sales cycles. It’s always better to lead with clear, outcome-led packages that .

Stronger relationships translate to greater customer stickiness that makes customer retention easier.

AI Concierge’s also lies in its management through the dedicated Partner Portal. Having a greater focus on channel-focused management makes it easier
to tailor the AI adoption experience to specific customer needs.

By being platform-agnostic, AI Concierge complements existing customer environments. Whether it’s Webex other communication problems, or

So, what’s the real value in AI Concierge? It’s in making customer problems easier to solve.

Make the next conversation about the enquiry
Customers aren’t looking for another general presentation about AI’s potential. They want to know what happens to customer calls when nobody’s free.

Partners must be ready to explain whether routine demand can be handled without creating more work. There needs to be a practical way to capture
information and answer queries. Their interest needs to be converted into a confirmed next step.

With AI Concierge, that’s where partners can make AI relevant. Don’t lead with the technology – start with the unanswered calls. Think of the bookings
that never happened, and the enquiries businesses can’t afford to miss.

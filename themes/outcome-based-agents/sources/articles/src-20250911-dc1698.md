Brain Co., a company incubated by Elad Gil, Jared Kushner, Luis Videgaray, and Eric Wu, announced today it has emerged from stealth and closed a $30 million Series A financing round. ... The round was co-led by Gil Capital and Affinity Partners ... Headquartered in San Francisco, Brain Co. provides an AI platform and applications for the world's largest and most important institutions. ... To date, Brain Co. has partnered with 10 Global 2000 customers-including a strategic relationship with OpenAI-and has already solved a number of high impact problems including: Automating construction permitting workflows; Optimizing energy consumption in industrial plants; Streamlining patient care in hospital systems; Handling complex customer interactions (e.g., hotels, restaurants).

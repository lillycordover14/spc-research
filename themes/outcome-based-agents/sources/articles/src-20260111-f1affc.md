[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Anthropic announcement, published January 11, 2026 (changelog updated February 7, 2026). It extends Claude for Life Sciences (October 2025) with a new healthcare offering described as "a complementary set of tools and resources" delivered through HIPAA-ready products for providers, payers, health tech companies and startups.

Healthcare connectors: CMS Coverage Database (Local and National Coverage Determinations, to support prior authorization checks and claims appeals); ICD-10 (diagnosis and procedure code lookup for coding, billing and claims using CMS and CDC data); National Provider Identifier Registry (provider verification, credentialing, directory management, claims validation); PubMed (over 35 million biomedical records, for HIPAA-compliant Claude for Enterprise organizations).

Agent Skills: FHIR development (connect healthcare systems faster with fewer errors) and a sample, customizable prior authorization review template that cross-references coverage rules, clinical guidelines, patient records and appeal documents.

Use cases listed: speeding prior authorization reviews, supporting claims appeals, coordinating care and triaging patient messages, and helping startups build tools such as ambient clinical documentation.

Consumer side: US Pro and Max users can opt in to share lab results and health records; HealthEx and Function connectors in beta; Apple Health and Android Health Connect rolling out in beta. "We do not use users' health data to train models."

Life sciences: new connectors for Medidata, ClinicalTrials.gov, ToolUniverse (600+ vetted tools), bioRxiv and medRxiv, Open Targets, ChEMBL and Owkin; existing Benchling, 10x Genomics, PubMed, BioRender, Synapse.org, Wiley. New skills include clinical trial protocol drafting and preparing regulatory submissions.

Performance: Claude Opus 4.5 improved on MedCalc, MedAgentBench and LatchBio's SpatialBench (146 verifiable problems).

Customers named: Banner Health, Novo Nordisk, Genmab, Sanofi, Elation Health, Flatiron Health, Veeva AI, Commure; Premier ("When you're serving 4,400+ healthcare organizations"). Consulting partners: Accenture, Deloitte, KPMG, PwC. Availability: "Our new connectors and Agent Skills are generally available to all Claude subscribers" (Pro, Max, Teams, Enterprise).

No pricing beyond existing plans was stated.

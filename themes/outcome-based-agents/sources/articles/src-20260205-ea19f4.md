[agent notes, not verbatim] OpenAI announcement, February 5, 2026. Claims 75% of enterprise workers say AI helped them do tasks they could not do before and 1 million plus businesses served. Examples: manufacturer cut production optimization work from six weeks to one day; global investment company opened 90% more time for salespeople; energy producer output up to 5%, over a billion in additional revenue. Frontier gives agents shared context, onboarding, learning with feedback, and permissions. Components: Business Context connecting data warehouses, CRM, ticketing and internal apps as a semantic layer; agent execution environment (files, code, tools) with memories; runs across local, enterprise cloud and OpenAI-hosted runtimes; built-in evaluation and optimization; each AI coworker has its own identity, explicit permissions and guardrails. Works with agents developed in-house, acquired from OpenAI, or integrated from other vendors, via open standards; accessible through ChatGPT, Atlas, or existing business apps. First adopters HP, Intuit, Oracle, State Farm, Thermo Fisher, Uber; pilots at BBVA, Cisco, T-Mobile. OpenAI FDEs pair with customer teams and feed learnings back to Research.

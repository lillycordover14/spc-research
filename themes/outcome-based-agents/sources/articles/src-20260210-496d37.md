summary: Bessemer's playbook frames three AI business models (copilots, agents, AI-enabled services) and three charge metrics (consumption, workflow, outcome), trading cost predictability for value alignment as you move toward outcomes. It says AI companies see 50-60% gross margins versus 80-90% for SaaS because COGS matter again, and recommends hybrid base subscription plus usage or outcome tiers when uncertain. Intercom Fin at $0.99 per resolution is the anchor outcome example, and the piece says outcome contracts reshape AE deal sizing, CS incentives and require forward-deployed engineers. It warns of a 2026 renewal cliff for soft-ROI copilots and lists outcome-priced examples including EvenUp, Graph AI, Leena AI and Resolve AI.

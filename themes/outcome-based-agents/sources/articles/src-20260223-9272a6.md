Frontier provides the technical foundation; real impact also needs leadership alignment, workflow redesign, integration across systems and data, and change management. Example AI coworker resolves a customer issue end-to-end from CRM, policies, filing the update and escalating only when needed. Quotes from Julie Sweet (Accenture) and Aiman Ezzat (Capgemini). Accenture equipping tens of thousands of professionals with ChatGPT Enterprise.

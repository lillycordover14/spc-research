[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Claude blog post dated February 24, 2026, filed under Claude Enterprise. Companion to 'Cowork and plugins for teams across the enterprise' (separate record). This was the first finance solution set; Anthropic followed on May 5, 2026 with ten finance agent templates (separate record).

Excel and PowerPoint: "Claude can now move between Excel and PowerPoint without losing context." Research preview for all paid plans on Mac and Windows.

Anthropic-built plugins (five, in a public repository): Financial analysis (market and competitive research, financial modeling, PowerPoint template creation and QA); Investment banking (reviewing transaction documents, comparable company analyses, pitch materials; it "helps bankers with deal workflows"); Equity research (parsing earnings transcripts, updating models with new guidance, drafting research notes); Private equity (deal sourcing and diligence, extracting standardized financial data, scoring opportunities against investment criteria); Wealth management ("Wealth management helps advisors analyze portfolios, identify drift and tax exposure.").

Partner connectors and plugins: FactSet connector (real-time market data, fundamentals, estimates); MSCI connector (index performance, exposures, constituents, methodologies); S&P Global plugin bringing Capital IQ Pro (tear sheets, transaction summaries, earnings call previews); LSEG plugin (market data and analytics including DCF models with live yield curves), "Available for users with active LSEG data entitlements." The post calls these "partner-created plugins from LSEG and S&P Global."

Administration: admins connect data sources via the connector directory; "Admins can authorize connections and bundle them into plugins for their teams."

Not in the article: customer names, pricing, private marketplace details (those are in the companion post).

[agent notes] Read-through: this February set is front-office heavy (banking, research, PE, wealth). The May 5 follow-up moved into back-office finance operations (general ledger reconciliation, month-end close, statement audit, KYC screening) and added a Managed Agents deployment path, which is the more direct overlap with finance-ops agent startups. Data vendors appear in both as connectors or partner plugins, and LSEG gating on existing entitlements shows the data owner keeps control of access and monetization even when the agent is the lab's.

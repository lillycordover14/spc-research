[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Anthropic announcement dated March 12, 2026.

"We're launching the Claude Partner Network, a program for partner organizations helping enterprises adopt Claude." "We’re committing an initial $100 million to support our partners" for 2026, with more expected over time. Most of the funding goes to partners for training, sales enablement, market development and co-marketing.

Anthropic resourcing: "We’re also scaling our partner-facing team fivefold", including dedicated Applied AI engineers for partners on live customer deals, technical architects for complex implementations and localized go-to-market support internationally.

Program mechanics: "Membership is free of charge, and applications open today." Members get a Partner Portal with Anthropic Academy training, internal sales playbooks and co-marketing documents. Qualified partners appear in a Services Partner Directory for enterprise buyers. First certification: Claude Certified Architect, Foundations, "a technical exam for solution architects building production applications with Claude." "Later this year, we’ll introduce additional certifications for sellers, architects, and developers." A Code Modernization starter kit helps partners migrate legacy codebases.

Positioning: Claude described as the only frontier model available on AWS, Google Cloud and Microsoft.

Partners quoted: Accenture, Deloitte, Cognizant, Infosys. Steve Corfield (Anthropic): "Anthropic is the most committed AI company in the world to the partner ecosystem". Alex Holt (Accenture): "We're training 30,000 Accenture professionals on Claude because that's what it takes to meet the demand we're seeing." Cognizant has opened Claude access to roughly 350,000 associates.

Follow-ons (separate records): Claude Frontier Academy, Oct 2, 2026 (reported 46,000 partner firms and 175,000+ certifications), and HCLTech Global Premier partner, Oct 21, 2026. The Accenture Anthropic Business Group was announced December 9, 2025, before this lane's window.

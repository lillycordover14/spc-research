NeuBird AI raises $19.3M Series B funding to transform IT automation
NeuBird AI, the San Francisco-based startup specializing in agentic AI, has announced a $19.3 million Series B funding round, led by Xora Innovation Pte. Ltd., with participation from Mayfield, StepStone Group, Prosperity7 Ventures, and Microsoft’s M12 fund, according to Business Wire. This capital raise will accelerate product innovation, global expansion, and adoption of NeuBird AI’s IT automation platform across complex enterprise systems, strengthening its position in the rapidly evolving cloud operations market.
The funding round demonstrates significant investor confidence in NeuBird AI’s ability to deliver innovative AI-driven solutions that improve AI infrastructure management and optimize enterprise IT workflows.
Series B funding supports enterprise AI growth
The Series B funding is a pivotal milestone for NeuBird AI, enabling the company to expand its platform capabilities and scale operations across enterprise clients. Convertible preferred stock was issued during this oversubscribed round, reflecting strong demand from investors.
Phil Inagaki, Managing Partner at Xora Innovation, highlighted the platform’s impact on enterprise reliability and efficiency, noting the team’s prior successes in IT infrastructure and AI-driven systems. “NeuBird AI’s platform delivers measurable results and is poised to transform how enterprises manage cloud operations,” he said.
The capital raising aligns with a broader trend of venture investment in AI-driven enterprise infrastructure, emphasizing solutions that increase productivity while reducing operational risk.
Addressing alert fatigue with IT automation
Modern enterprise environments produce a deluge of telemetry, logs, and performance metrics. Teams managing these systems often experience alert fatigue, spending nearly 40% of their time on reactive incident response rather than strategic initiatives.
NeuBird AI’s IT automation platform tackles this challenge by autonomously detecting, diagnosing, and resolving incidents across hybrid and multi-cloud environments. CEO Gou Rao explains: “Our agentic AI agents continuously reason across telemetry sources, correlating signals and recommending actions without requiring constant human intervention. This dramatically reduces wasted time and enables engineering teams to focus on innovation.”
By eliminating repetitive tasks, NeuBird AI’s platform not only enhances productivity but also mitigates operational risk, contributing to stronger AI infrastructure management and more resilient cloud operations.
NeuBird AI Falcon: next-gen AI infrastructure management
NeuBird AI recently launched its NeuBird AI Falcon engine, an advancement in AI infrastructure management designed to predict and prevent operational issues. Falcon leverages real-time data analysis, machine learning, and domain expertise to identify risks before they escalate, helping enterprises optimize uptime and reduce costs.
Co-founder and CTO Vinod Jayaraman emphasized the significance of predictive automation: “Falcon is built to act proactively rather than reactively. Enterprises no longer need to wait for incidents to happen; the platform anticipates problems and guides remediation steps automatically.”
This capability positions NeuBird AI at the forefront of cloud operations innovation, particularly in complex, distributed enterprise environments where traditional monitoring and manual workflows often fail.
Capital raising enables global expansion
The $19.3M capital raising will support NeuBird AI’s expansion into new geographies, product development, and enterprise sales. With strategic partnerships with AWS and Microsoft, the company gains access to high-value enterprise networks and infrastructure ecosystems, accelerating adoption of its IT automation solutions.
Venkat Ramakrishnan, recently appointed President and COO, brings extensive experience scaling enterprise platforms from Series A funding to acquisition. His expertise will be instrumental in executing the company’s global expansion and enterprise go-to-market strategy.
Investors cited NeuBird AI’s strong product-market fit, proven enterprise traction, and technical leadership as key drivers for their participation in the Series B round, signaling sustained investor confidence in the platform’s growth trajectory.
Impact on cloud operations and enterprise productivity
NeuBird AI’s platform has already delivered measurable outcomes for early enterprise clients:
- Automated over 1 million alerts across production environments
- Reduced mean time to resolution by up to 90%
- Achieved over $2 million in engineering labor savings
These results highlight the platform’s ability to enhance cloud operations and implement effective AI infrastructure management, allowing enterprises to maintain high service reliability while reallocating engineering resources to strategic initiatives.
Additionally, by minimizing repetitive manual workflows, NeuBird AI addresses the challenges of alert fatigue, improving overall productivity and employee satisfaction for DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) teams.
Agentic AI: the future of IT automation
NeuBird AI’s vision extends beyond basic automation. Its agentic AI agents operate with autonomous reasoning capabilities, continuously learning from data to make proactive decisions. This approach enables IT automation across enterprise environments at scale, a critical requirement for organizations managing complex, cloud-native systems.
Industry reports indicate that nearly 80% of on-call engineers experience burnout due to manual incident response. NeuBird AI’s solution alleviates this problem while increasing operational efficiency and reducing downtime, reinforcing the platform’s value to enterprises worldwide.
Market opportunity in AI infrastructure management
The market for AI-driven IT automation and AI infrastructure management is rapidly expanding. As enterprises increasingly migrate to multi-cloud and hybrid environments, tools like NeuBird AI’s platform become essential for maintaining reliability, security, and efficiency.
With the capital from this Series B round, NeuBird AI aims to broaden its cloud operations coverage, enhance predictive capabilities, and scale its enterprise sales and support teams to meet growing demand. The company’s strategy positions it to capitalize on the broader trend toward AI-enabled enterprise infrastructure solutions.
Series B funding reflects strong investor confidence
The $19.3M Series B funding is a clear indicator of market confidence in NeuBird AI’s approach. Investors are betting on the company’s ability to transform cloud operations and optimize AI infrastructure management, while reducing operational complexity and increasing the efficiency of enterprise IT teams.
As organizations adopt agentic AI solutions to address increasingly complex environments, NeuBird AI is poised to become a leading provider of autonomous, enterprise-grade IT automation tools.

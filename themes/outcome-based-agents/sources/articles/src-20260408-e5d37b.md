[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Claude blog post dated April 8, 2026. Follow-on feature posts: May 19 (dreaming, outcomes, multiagent orchestration; separate record), June 9 (schedules and vaults, per search results), October 14 (OpenTelemetry export; separate record).

Product: "composable APIs for building and deploying cloud-hosted agents at scale", public beta on the Claude Platform. Core concept: "an Anthropic-managed harness with production infrastructure for state, memory, permissions, and scheduled execution". The harness decides when to call tools, manages context and recovers from errors.

Capabilities: secure sandboxed code execution (self-hosted sandboxes also available); built-in memory across sessions; "scoped permissions, identity management, and execution tracing built in"; credential vaults; long-running sessions that "operate autonomously for hours, with progress and outputs that persist even through disconnections"; checkpointing; scheduled deployments; MCP tunnels; multi-agent coordination and outcome-based self-evaluation in research preview. Console observability: session tracing, integration analytics, troubleshooting.

Performance claim: in internal testing on structured file generation, outcomes "improved outcome task success by up to 10 points over a standard prompting loop".

Pricing: consumption-based. "Standard Claude Platform token rates apply, plus $0.08 per session-hour for active runtime".

Customers: Notion, Rakuten, Asana, Vibecode, Sentry, Atlassian, General Legal, Blockit. Vibecode: "users can now spin up that same infrastructure at least 10x quicker than before". General Legal cut development time by 10x. Blockit built its meeting prep agent 3x faster. Sentry: "The integration shipped in weeks instead of months on Managed Agents."

Database relevance: Managed Agents became the deployment target for Anthropic's finance (May 5) and legal (May 12) cookbooks, and is offered on Google Cloud as of Oct 22, 2026 (separate records).

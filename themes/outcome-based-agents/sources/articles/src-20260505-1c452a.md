[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Anthropic announcement dated May 5, 2026, category Product. Paired with a 'Briefing: Financial Services' livestream the same day (per search results).

"We're releasing ten ready-to-run agent templates for the most time-consuming work in financial services".

Templates: Research and client coverage (Pitch builder, Meeting preparer, Earnings reviewer, Model builder, Market researcher); Finance and operations (Valuation reviewer, General ledger reconciler, Month-end closer, Statement auditor, KYC screener). Each packages skills, connectors for governed data access and subagents (per search summary); "users stay firmly in the loop".

Deployment: plugins in Claude Cowork or Claude Code on all paid plans; cookbooks for Claude Managed Agents in public beta; sourced from the financial services marketplace on GitHub. Office add-ins: Excel, PowerPoint and Word GA, Outlook coming soon; "Claude carries its knowledge and context across all four platforms".

Data ecosystem: "AI agents are only as good as the data and context they can access." Existing connectors: FactSet, S&P Capital IQ, MSCI, PitchBook, Morningstar, Chronograph, LSEG, Daloopa. New connectors: Dun & Bradstreet, Fiscal AI, Financial Modeling Prep, Guidepoint, IBISWorld, SS&C Intralinks, Third Bridge, Verisk. New MCP app: Moody's.

Model: Claude Opus 4.7 scores 64.37% on Vals AI's Finance Agent benchmark, described as industry-leading.

Customers: Citadel ("Our investment professionals live in data and analytical models, and Claude for Excel meets them there."), FIS ("FIS clients won't need to build this infrastructure themselves. It's already here."), BNY, Carlyle, Mizuho, Travelers, Walleye ("100% of employees at Walleye Capital use Claude Code."), Hg, Dun & Bradstreet, Morningstar, FactSet.

Pricing: none beyond paid plans and joint-customer availability for partner connectors.

[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Claude blog post dated May 12, 2026 (same day as Code w/ Claude SF). Builds on the earlier legal plugin from the January 30 plugin launch.

Opening claim: "legal professionals have become the most engaged Claude Cowork users of any knowledge-work function." "Claude meets legal teams where they are, working directly inside Microsoft Word, Outlook, Excel, and PowerPoint" with skills for drafting, redlining and playbook comparisons.

Connectors (20+): CLM and drafting (Definely, Docusign, Ironclad); deal rooms (Box, Datasite); DMS (iManage, NetDocuments); expert network and skills (Lawve AI, The L Suite); e-discovery (Consilio, Everlaw, Relativity); fiduciary-grade workflow (Thomson Reuters CoCounsel Legal); legal research (Legal Data Hunter with 31M+ documents in 160+ jurisdictions, Midpage, Trellis); legal AI assistants (Harvey, Solve Intelligence); public service (BoardWise, Courtroom5, Descrybe, Free Law Project).

Plugins (12): Commercial Legal, Corporate Legal, Employment Legal, Privacy Legal, Product Legal, Regulatory Legal, AI Governance Legal, IP Legal, Litigation Legal, Law Student, Legal Clinic, Legal Builder Hub. Each starts with a setup interview capturing playbook and risk settings (per search summary). Commercial, Corporate, Litigation and Product Legal are available as cookbooks deployable as Managed Agents.

Harvey: connector brings Harvey's legal intelligence (general questions, Vault project analysis, research). "Claude Opus 4.7 scored 90.9% on Harvey's BigLaw Bench, the highest of any Claude model".

Customers and partners quoted: EvenUp, Solve Intelligence, Eve Legal ("in litigation, an authoritative-sounding hallucination is worse than no answer."), Freshfields ("co-developing agentic workflows with Anthropic that can handle multi-step legal tasks end-to-end."), Accenture, Thomson Reuters ("The future of AI won't be defined by where the work happens, it will be defined by whether the results can be trusted."), Quinn Emanuel ("The breakthrough was treating Claude like a member of the case team"), Harvey, Crosby, Legora, Holland & Knight.

Access: legal aid clinics, public defenders and nonprofits get discounted pricing via Claude for Nonprofits. Connectors and plugins are open source and available in Cowork; enterprise admins enable them. Note: body cites Opus 4.7 while the EvenUp quote refers to Opus 4.8.

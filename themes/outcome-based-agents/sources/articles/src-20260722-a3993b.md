[agent notes, not verbatim] OpenAI post July 22, 2026. Presence: deploy trusted agents that answer questions, resolve issues, use company systems, take approved actions, escalate. Each deployment starts with one job (billing, insurance claims, employee IT requests); agent gets only needed knowledge and access; company sets policies, approvals, handoff. After launch, Codex proposes updates teams test and approve. Supports voice and chat: customer support, outbound sales, high-risk internal workflows. Components: policies and SOPs, guardrails, approved actions, simulations, evaluation tools, Codex-powered improvement. Powers OpenAI's English phone support (1-888-GPT-0090): met or exceeded human frontline benchmarks within weeks; resolves 75% of inbound issues without human assistance; handoffs down 15 percentage points in 10 days. BBVA (design partner, voice banking Mexico, Daniel Ordaz quote), SoftBank (testing Japanese conversations, Tadahisa Murakami quote), IAG (exploring claims support in severe weather, Julie Batch quote). Limited GA for eligible enterprises; led by OpenAI FDEs and select global SIs; not self-serve.

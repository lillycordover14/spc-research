FreightTech has a new unicorn, and it took 20 months to build. HappyRobot closed a $150 million Series C at a $1.2 billion post-money valuation, putting a price on the idea that AI agents can absorb the phone calls, emails and follow-ups that move freight. Prysm Capital led the round and Eurazeo co-led, with existing investors including a16z, Y Combinator, Koch Disruptive Technologies and WaVe-X ... HappyRobot has raised roughly $200 million across three rounds in 20 months, growing 5x since its Series B closed in late 2025. It now runs agents inside more than 150 enterprise customers, including DHL, Uber Freight, Kuehne+Nagel and Austria's LKW WALTER. ... At the WALTER GROUP alone, five to 10 use cases are live across dispatching, payment collections and customer support. [Company post, happyrobot.ai/blog/happyrobot-seriesc-fundraising-announcement:] We now work with more than 150 enterprise customers, including DHL, Kuehne + Nagel, Naturgy, Repsol, and Uber, and we've grown 5x since raising our Series B late last year. ... initial agents typically go live within 4 to 12 weeks ... In customer care, our agents are achieving 9.4/10 customer satisfaction scores and more than 70% autonomous resolution on average.

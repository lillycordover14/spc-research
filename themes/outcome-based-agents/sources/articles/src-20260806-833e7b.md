Five9 Announces Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results. August 6, 2026
Q2 Revenue Grew 10% year-over-year. Q2 Subscription Revenue Grew 14% year-over-year. Announces Approximately $100 Million Total Contract Value New Customer Win.
- Revenue for the second quarter of 2026 increased 10% to $312.4 million, compared to $283.3 million for the second quarter of 2025.
- GAAP gross margin was 53.4% for the second quarter of 2026, compared to 54.9% for the second quarter of 2025.
- Adjusted gross margin was 61.4% for the second quarter of 2026, compared to 63.0% for the second quarter of 2025.
"Q2 marks our third consecutive quarter of accelerating subscription revenue growth, with AI revenue accelerating even faster, and further evidence that our focused execution is producing results. Closing a 9-figure TCV agreement through the Google Marketplace and launching Five9 Voice AI Agents in the same quarter underscore the breadth of our platform and the strength of our market position." - Amit Mathradas, Chief Executive Officer
- LTM subscription dollar-based retention rate was 107% as of June 30, 2026
- Launched Five9 Voice AI Agents: human-like conversations, real-time responsiveness, enterprise-grade governance, and seamless AI + Human collaboration
Risk factor (vii): as AI solutions will likely perform an increasing proportion of contact center interactions, if we are unable to replace decreases in subscription revenue from licenses with revenue from the sale of additional AI solutions, our revenue, results of operations and business will be harmed.
- For the full year 2026, Five9 expects to report revenue in the range of $1.260 to $1.272 billion.

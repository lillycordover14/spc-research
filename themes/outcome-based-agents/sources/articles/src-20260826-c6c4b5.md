[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Google Cloud blog post dated August 26, 2026, by Michael Gerstenhaber (VP Product Management, Gemini) and Pravir Gupta (VP, Google Business Platform). Google release notes (search result, not fetched) indicate the Gemini Enterprise pay-as-you-go edition went GA around August 1, 2026, with a one-seat minimum and invoiced billing account.

Options: per-user seat subscription (existing; fixed monthly fee with daily quota pools shared across the project); pay-as-you-go consumption edition (new; no upfront commitment or base fee; compute and tokens at standard model API rates; select customers, broad rollout soon): "Only pay for what you use. Your spend scales up and down automatically with real usage, ensuring you never pay for empty seats when project demand dips." Pooled quotas (Antigravity): "Pooled quota is always exhausted first, and admins can control if overages are allowed, at which point it's charged at pay-as-you-go rates." Deferred execution (coming soon): "Substantial discounts for work that can wait: AI workloads can run on separate, off-peak capacity, you pay up to half the inference cost and bypass standard quota limits entirely".

Commitments: "Receive 10% off for 1-year or 20% off for 3-year commitments for monthly spending across Gemini Enterprise." "take 10–20% off your token costs", no minimums or maximums.

Controls: firm monthly spend limit per project in the Billing Console with alerts at 50%, 80% and 100%. "If a project hits its limit, the agent's API calls temporarily pause" without affecting the rest of production. Overages can be enabled at consumption rates drawing against the savings plan.

Throughput note: Gemini models can scale to up to 50 million tokens per minute during spikes.

Not stated: per-agent or per-outcome pricing. Gemini at Work (Oct 8) later cited real-time spend caps and that per-token prices have dropped 98% since 2024 (separate record).

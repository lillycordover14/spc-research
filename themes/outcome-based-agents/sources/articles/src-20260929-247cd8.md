VentureBeat, Carl Franzen, 2026-09-29. OpenClaw Enterprise (OCE): MIT-licensed, vendor-neutral, self-hostable (Docker Compose, Kubernetes) control plane for persistent agents: multi-tenancy, hard security boundaries, lifecycle governance, auditing, LLM-based review of actions; swap models, harnesses, sandboxes. Originated inside OpenAI, donated to OpenClaw Foundation; Red Hat and Nvidia contribute; OpenAI and Red Hat pilot internally. RJ Marsan (OpenAI): internal agent Androidclaw investigates broken builds, finds PRs, prepares and merges fixes, 'Kinda game changing.' Nvidia OpenShell runtime: deny-by-default. Runlayer: commercial enterprise AI control plane, $30M Series A June 2026 (Felicis, Khosla), $42M total, customers Instacart, Gusto, Opendoor, dbt Labs. Recommended for internal pilots; 1.0 planned later this year.

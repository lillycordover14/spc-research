[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Anthropic announcement dated October 2, 2026.

Commitment: "Backed by a $100 million commitment, Anthropic aims to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers" by the end of 2027. "The Academy is the first of its kind from an AI company".

Cohorts: first cohorts include Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley and Novo Nordisk, plus others. "Participation is by nomination".

FDE Residency: begins with a multi-day in-person program where trainees "work through a simulated enterprise deployment". "Those who pass earn the Claude Resident Engineer badge and move into the 12-week residency", during which they "lead a real Claude use case at their own organization". Cohorts in San Francisco, New York and London. First FDE credentials expected in early 2027.

Ecosystem scale: the Claude Partner Network spans 46,000 firms that have earned more than 175,000 Claude certifications; nearly 4,000 people have completed Basecamp.

Context: extends the March 12, 2026 Claude Partner Network ($100M for partners, 5x partner-facing team) with a second $100M aimed at deployment talent. Note mix of SIs and strategy firms (Accenture, Deloitte, Capgemini, McKinsey, Bain) and end enterprises (Morgan Stanley, CBA, Novo Nordisk). Google announced its own FDE teams embedded with SIs at Next '26 (separate record). Search results also reported OpenAI's Frontier Alliance with McKinsey, BCG, Accenture and Capgemini in February 2026, but that source could not be fetched here.

[agent notes] Read-through for the thesis: across the window, every lab converged on the same deployment answer. Anthropic funded partners (March, $100M), then funded deployment talent (October, $100M, 10,000 FDEs); Google embedded its own FDE teams inside seven SIs (April); HCLTech, a Global Premier partner, staffs FDE sandboxes on client systems (October 21). The common pattern is lab primitives plus SI or in-house FDEs as the harness builders, with the lab controlling certification. The page gives no pricing for the Academy and no figures for how many enterprises are currently in production.

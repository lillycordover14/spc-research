VB Pulse August 2026 (published 2026-10-05). Self-selected VentureBeat readers at orgs with 100+ employees; 221 responses, 166 qualified. Key figures: 2% chose platform for model gravity; 78% for flexibility (22%), reliability (20%), ease of development (19%), control (18%). Usage: OpenAI Agents SDK/Responses 45% (69% primary), Google Enterprise Agent Platform 39% (57%), Anthropic Claude Platform 27% (38%), Microsoft Foundry/Copilot Studio 19% (68%), Salesforce Agentforce 15% (68%), Bedrock Agents 9% (27%). 59% run one platform; multi-platform 84% plan change vs 46% single. 60% plan new/additional/replacement platform within 12 months. Control plane by end-2026: hybrid 33%, provider-managed 27%, external 22%, custom 12%. 53% say a quarter or fewer of agents are true multi-agent. 23% track spend only after the fact. 34% say workflow tooling grows most next year.

[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Microsoft Copilot blog post dated October 8, 2026, by Jeff Teper, EVP Microsoft Apps and Agents.

Framing: "The traditional boundaries of business applications are disappearing." "AI is inverting that model." CRM in the flow of work while "keeping CRM as the trusted foundation for customer data and business processes".

Launches: 30 new pre-built Copilot skills across Dynamics 365 Sales, Customer Service and Customer Insights, GA in Cowork, private preview in Autopilot, and in the Frontier Program for Code. "Dynamics 365 CRM in the flow of Teams experience is now available in public preview." Teams channels connect to customer accounts for pre-meeting briefs and case updates. Work IQ combines Dynamics 365 business intelligence with Microsoft 365 workplace intelligence.

Agents: agents "carrying defined business processes forward across teams and systems". Service Agent (private preview for select Frontier customers): drafts customer responses, escalates outside business hours, and "it automatically creates a case in your CRM, including Dynamics 365, and shares the case link." Sales Development Agent: researches prospects, runs outreach and follow-up, hands qualified opportunities to sellers; "Sales Development Agent is available in public preview and will start consuming Copilot credits on November 16, 2026."

Migration: Dynamics 365 Activate (public preview): "Using AI, it conducts customer-authorized analysis of their current CRM environment" to recommend a migration path from legacy CRM to agentic CRM.

Governance: agents managed through Agent 365.

Customers: BNY, Grupo Aguia Branca, Adobe (meetings with 10+ customers it otherwise would not have reached, within three weeks of a limited launch), Comcast (quote references Salesforce as a legacy CRM source). Ignite runs November 17-20, 2026.

Pricing: no per-agent or per-resolution pricing stated; only the credit consumption start date for the Sales Development Agent.

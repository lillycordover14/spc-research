International Growth and Strategic Wins Underpin TCS' Q2
Announced strategic deal wins: Five-year strategic partnership with Porsche AG and acquisition of MHP, Porsche's Germany-based management and IT consulting subsidiary. Agreement to transition Best Buy's Global Capability Center (GCC) in India to TCS, and transform into an AI Capability Center (AICC).
MUMBAI, October 8, 2026: Tata Consultancy Services reported its consolidated financial results for the quarter ending September 30, 2026.
- Q2 Revenue at $7,642 Mn; Growth +0.2% QoQ, +2.4% YoY in USD, +0.5% QoQ in CC
- Annualized AI Revenue at US$ 3.1 billion in Q2FY27, crosses 10% of Revenue
- Q2 Total Contract Value (TCV): US$ 9.6 billion
- Operating Margin at 24.0%
- Workforce strength: 598,056; LTM Attrition (IT Services): 13.3%
Aarthi Subramanian, Executive Director - President and Chief Operating Officer, said "AI momentum remained strong, with annualized AI revenues now exceeding $3 billion. Demand for delivering business outcomes with AI-native solutions, AI-led transformation of enterprise systems and autonomous GBS, continues to accelerate. ... TCS' Human+AI Services Autonomy model for AMS earned top rankings from customers, underscoring the strength of our differentiated capabilities."
- Announced an agreement to transition Best Buy's Global Capability Center (GCC) in India to TCS ... transform the GCC into an AI Capability Center(AICC).
- Launched TCS ADD TM AgentHub, a role-based, enterprise-ready, and trusted AI platform that enables the use of agentic AI in drug development at scale. This new agentic AI workforce transforms clinical trials and pharmacovigilance services while maintaining regulatory and audit requirements.
- Positioned the foremost Leader or ranked #1 in 12 competitive assessments ... HFS SaS Star Ratings Report on Agentic Application Management Services 2026 (AMS).

OpenAI is expecting to reach or exceed $70 billion in annualized revenue by the end of the year, according to people familiar with the matter, driven largely by growth in its enterprise business.

The ChatGPT-maker's annualized revenue was roughly $50 billion at the end of September, the people said. The figure represents a projection of OpenAI's yearly sales based on a shorter period.

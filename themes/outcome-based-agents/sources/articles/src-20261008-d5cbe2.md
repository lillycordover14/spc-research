[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Google Cloud blog post dated October 8, 2026, by Thomas Kurian, adapted from his Gemini at Work 2026 keynote.

Framing: "Work now starts in the prompt window."

Gemini agent: a single, universal agent for work that answers questions, handles knowledge work, creates images and media, and writes and runs code, from one prompt box and one API. "You give it objectives, not instructions." Model choice: "Gemini is the agent, and the model underneath it is a separate choice." "The best model for the task is not always the largest one."

Workspace: inline in Gmail, Drive, Docs, Slides, Sheets, Chat, Calendar. Coworker agents get their own Workspace identities including @agents.company.com addresses. "A coworker agent acts under its own identity rather than yours, and it sees only what you share with it."

Data skills: plain-language analysis, PySpark, notebooks, ML training; saved reports that run without token costs. "Ninety percent of enterprise data is unstructured." "Organizations should not be forced to move or duplicate their data to use AI."

Industry specializations: Financial Services and Legal in preview; Government, Healthcare and Retail coming soon. A Deloitte solution is also mentioned.

Governance: identity, authorization, auditing, policy management, Agent Sandbox, Agent Gateway (an AI network firewall). "Two factors determine whether an enterprise agent program succeeds or stalls: whether you can govern it, and whether you can afford it." Policy: "Gemini applies it to every agent in your company instead of checking one agent at a time."

Cost: multi-model orchestration, Smart Routing, real-time spend caps ("You can set a hard limit on a project's AI spend in the Cloud Billing Console, and Gemini enforces it."). Per-token prices down 98% since 2024. TPU 8i with 80% better price-performance.

Numbers: nearly 500 Google Cloud customers each processed more than one trillion tokens; nearly 80% of Google Cloud customers use its AI products; nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 use Gemini Enterprise; SOMPO built over 10,000 custom agents across 34,000 employees; PayPal routes 10 million multi-model requests weekly.

Not stated: per-agent or per-outcome pricing; dedicated CX, HR or IT domain agents. A companion Google post (Oct 15, 'What the Gemini agent means for startups') says the capabilities are not yet publicly available.

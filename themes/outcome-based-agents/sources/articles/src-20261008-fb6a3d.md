Telecom Reseller, Moshe Beauford, 2026-10-08. Vida (Austin) adds outcome-based pricing as alternative to usage-based. Customer defines outcome (e.g., qualified lead criteria); Vida verifies via CRM, API, browser, scheduling, ticketing, payment systems. Quote, Jordan Gadapee (CMO): 'If an agent handles 500 conversations and produces 20 qualified leads, the customer would be billed for those 20 qualified leads rather than the 500 interactions.' 'There isn't a single price for an outcome.' 'If there's a dispute, the underlying data records what happened.' Not inherently cheaper. Comparators: Intercom Fin $0.99 per outcome (resolution or disqualification), $9.99 per qualified prospect; HubSpot Breeze Customer Agent and Prospecting Agent moved to outcome pricing in April: $0.50 per resolved conversation, $1 per lead recommended. Targets performance marketing, insurance, lead-driven industries; channel partners (AVANT commission unchanged).

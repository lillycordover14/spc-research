AI agent Decagon is in early M&A talks with 
ServiceNow
 and other companies, sources said.

Decagon
 builds AI agents designed to handle customer service inquiries. The startup emerged from stealth in 2023 with $30 million in funding from investors including Benchmark and Sequoia.

ServiceNow declined to comment on the potential transaction. A Decagon spokesperson did not respond to a request for comment.

The talks reflect heightened enterprise interest in AI agent technology as companies seek to automate customer interactions. Analysts note the sector has drawn substantial investment in 2025.

Decagon
 has positioned its agents as tools for reducing customer service costs. The company competes with other AI startup teams building similar automation software.

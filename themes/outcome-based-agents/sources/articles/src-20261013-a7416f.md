Key points:
AI agent startup Decagon has held early talks with ServiceNow 
 and other companies about a potential sale. 
Decagon, which was valued at $4.5 billion earlier this year, has hired a bank to evaluate purchase interest, according to a Business Insider report on Tuesday, which cited people familiar with the matter. If a deal doesn't happen, Decagon could start a new funding round.
Decagon announced in January it had raised $250 million in funding, led by new investors Coatue Management and Index Ventures, tripling its valuation to $4.5 billion in under six months.

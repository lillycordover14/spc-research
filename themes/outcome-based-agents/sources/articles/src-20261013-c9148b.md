MENLO PARK, Calif., Oct. 13, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Everest Group has named Skan AI a Leader in its Digital Interaction Intelligence Products PEAK Matrix® Assessment. Skan AI has been a DII Leader in the PEAK Matrix® for three years in a row: 2024, 2025, and now, 2026.
 
 
 
 
Skan AI is named a Leader in DII for third consecutive year
 
 
 
The global research and advisory firm uses its PEAK Matrix® framework to assess technology and services providers like Skan AI on two dimensions: Market Impact (e.g., revenue, clients, and year-over-year growth) and Vision and Capability (e.g., delivery, innovation, and roadmap strength). As part of Everest Group's Agentic and Process Automation research program, the firm evaluated Skan AI and 16 other companies for its DII assessment, which focused on vendors that provide visibility into how work is performed and how teams interact with work applications.
 
 
 
 
 
Over the last year, Skan AI has grown rapidly, resulting in a significantly higher Market Impact score and a higher placement in Everest Group's assessment of DII vendors. Skan AI now sits within the top of the Leaders cluster in the DII Products PEAK Matrix®.
 
 
"Skan AI has strengthened its position as a Leader on Everest Group's Digital Interaction Intelligence Products PEAK Matrix® 2026, driven by its strong product vision, revenue growth, and continued investments in product innovation and capability expansion such as AI opportunity discovery, conversational process insights, and context layer for AI agents," said Amardeep Modi, Vice President at Everest Group. "Ease of data capture, scalability, customer support, and generation of actionable insights are some of the key strengths highlighted by its clients."
 
 
Skan AI's placement in the firm's DII vendor report positions the company to help even more enterprises understand and transform their operations with agentic AI.
 
 
 
 
 
"Skan AI has tripled its revenue in the last year and increased its Market Impact in assessments like Everest Group's PEAK Matrix® because our platform architecture delivers immediate, measurable value for our enterprise customers," said Manish Garg, Co-Founder and Chief Product Officer at Skan AI. "The enterprise banks, insurers, and healthcare payers we work with have seen that shallow task discovery isn't enough to transform their operations and compete with agentic AI. What they need is a sovereign operating model that brings together the right combination of human judgement and agentic execution, and that's the future of enterprise operations the Skan AI platform makes possible."

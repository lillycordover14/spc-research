Duvo Raises $32 Million Series A Led By Balderton Capital For Enterprise Execution OS

Duvo has raised $32 million in Series A funding to expand its Enterprise Execution OS, a platform designed to let employees and AI agents run business processes together across existing enterprise systems. The round was led by Balderton Capital, with participation from existing investors Index Ventures, Northzone, and Credo Ventures. The financing brings Duvo’s total funding to $48 million and will support expansion in the U.S., initially targeting retailers and consumer packaged goods companies.

Duvo has also strengthened its U.S. leadership team. Michael Green, formerly of Databricks and Salesforce, has joined as Chief Commercial Officer, while former Kmart President and Accenture Managing Director Spencer Insolia recently joined as Managing Director, U.S. Former ALDI SÜD Holding CEO Christoph Schwaiger has joined the company as Senior Advisor.

Duvo positions its technology as an operating layer for enterprise processes rather than a collection of standalone AI automation projects. Its Duvo Clarity product captures how work actually happens through AI-led interviews, screen recordings, and existing documentation. Duvo’s agents can then execute approved steps across ERP systems, supplier portals, spreadsheets, inboxes, and legacy applications using APIs or existing user interfaces.

The company said its platform can move its first process into production within weeks without requiring a large engineering team or major systems integration project. Human users retain approval authority over decisions and exceptions, while operating dashboards show pending actions and the results produced by each process.

Duvo said it has unlocked more than $100 million in value for clients across finance, supply chain, commercial operations, customer care, marketing, and human resources. At online beauty retailer Notino, Duvo reduced a quarterly reconciliation process involving approximately $45 million annually in supplier bonuses from 70 days to 15 days, while cutting specialist work from about 120 hours per cycle to fewer than 10.

Founded in 2025 by a team associated with Rohlik Group, Duvo has nearly 60 employees and works with companies including Morrisons, Holland & Barrett, Boden, Notino, and Allegro. The platform is model-agnostic and can support open-weight models on dedicated GPUs, customer-managed cloud or on-premises deployments, and approved enterprise AI endpoints.

Duvo is SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and ISO 42001 certified, and is available to U.S. customers through Microsoft’s Azure Marketplace. The company plans to begin its U.S. push with use cases such as freight audit and payables audit before expanding into additional processes and industries.

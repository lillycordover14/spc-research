AI Voice Agents at CMA CGM Expand Across Global Operations with HappyRobot
 

            Oct, 15, 2026        

Posted by Gabriel Malheiros

Week 202642

The CMA CGM Group, a global player in sea, land, air and logistics solutions, and HappyRobot, a platform for building and scaling enterprise AI agents, are expanding the deployment of 
AI voice agents at CMA CGM
 tailored to the Group’s operational needs. Following successful pilots in sales, customer service and finance, CMA CGM is scaling up the solution to manage customer interactions and automate related tasks, improving the customer experience.

Building on these initial applications, CMA CGM and HappyRobot plan to extend the deployment across the Group, including CEVA Logistics. Already being implemented in Europe and North America, the solution is expected to expand further into Africa, South America and Asia, automating repetitive tasks and allowing employees to focus on activities where their expertise adds greater value.

AI Voice Agents at CMA CGM Support Sales, Customer Service and Finance

HappyRobot’s platform enables CMA CGM to develop and deploy multilingual AI agents tailored to specific operational needs and connected to the Group’s internal systems. With more than 54 million customer requests handled by CMA CGM each year, HappyRobot’s solution helps the Group manage customer interactions, support commercial prospecting and automate recurring tasks. This allows employees to devote more time to situations that require their expertise and personalized support.

In sales, CMA CGM has deployed Eva, an AI voice agent developed with HappyRobot to engage prospective customers registered on CMA CGM’s platform who are not supported by a dedicated sales representative. Eva proactively contacts these customers to understand their shipping needs, offer tailored freight quotes and identify qualified commercial opportunities for CMA CGM’s sales teams.

In initial campaigns in the United Kingdom and Senegal, Eva contacted more than 4,000 customers, with approximately one-third progressing to active discussions about potential shipping routes. Based on these initial results, CMA CGM plans to deploy Eva in 20 countries by the end of 2026.

In customer service, the solution handles requests related to cargo release, including release status, customs clearance and shipping documentation. More than 46% of these requests are currently resolved directly by the AI agents.

The agents can also trigger emails through CMA CGM’s internal systems containing cargo release numbers, simplifying the process for customers and reducing repeated exchanges with service teams.

In finance, the pilots have also demonstrated the solution’s ability to support payment follow-up by contacting customers about outstanding invoices. These calls resulted in payments being made, including some immediately, demonstrating tangible benefits for CMA CGM’s finance teams.

Mathieu Friedberg, Executive Vice President, Transformation, CMA CGM Group, said: “Our collaboration with HappyRobot illustrates how we are turning artificial intelligence into practical solutions for our operations. Building on successful pilots, we are now expanding these applications internationally to improve the customer experience and support our teams. This approach reflects our ambition to deploy AI where it can deliver measurable benefits for our customers and employees.”

“By the end of the year, we’ll be live in more than 20 countries and working to continue innovating in other parts of the CMA CGM Group,” said HappyRobot CEO Pablo Palafox. “CMA CGM isn’t just building agents — it is building something that compounds over time: agents working and learning together across the Group.”

With ZEBOX, From Experimentation to Deployment

As part of ZEBOX’s Spring 2025 acceleration program, HappyRobot was introduced to CMA CGM’s operational teams, enabling the startup to identify business use cases and develop a proof of concept with the Group.

ZEBOX also helped increase HappyRobot’s visibility through its participation at the CMA CGM booth at Viva Technology and alongside ZEBOX at CES 2026.

In addition to the support provided by ZEBOX, the CMA CGM Group also invested in HappyRobot, reinforcing its support for the startup’s development and its voice AI technology.

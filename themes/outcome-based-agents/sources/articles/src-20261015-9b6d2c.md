FIS AML Investigations Agent Now Commercially Available to Help Banks Tackle the Scale and Cost of Financial Crime Compliance

Built by FIS with support from Anthropic, the agent applies FIS’ banking, payments and data infrastructure to one of banks’ most manual compliance challenges and is the first in FIS’ roadmap of AI agents for banking

October 15, 2026

- Developed by FIS with support from Anthropic, the FIS AML Agent is now commercially available to any U.S.-based bank, including those that are not FIS clients today.
- At Amalgamated Bank, one of FIS’ design partners, the agent is projected to reduce Level 1 alert review time by more than 90 percent and Level 2 case investigation time by approximately 98 percent - referring respectively to initial alert triage and deeper case review after escalation - as well as SAR narrative preparation time by more than 80 percent, based on the bank’s analysis of its current workflows against the agent’s capabilities.
- Built on FIS Trust Assured, the company’s framework for embedding governance, compliance and responsible AI into every FIS product, the agent keeps humans in control: every output is a recommendation, no case can be closed without explicit analyst confirmation, each statement links to its source record, and each institution’s risk logic, procedures and thresholds remain isolated.

JACKSONVILLE, Fla., October 15, 2026 – FIS® (NYSE: FIS) today announced that the FIS AML Investigations Agent that was jointly announced in May 2026 with Anthropic, is now commercially available to any U.S.-based bank, including those that are not FIS clients today.

“AML investigations require comprehensive evidence and experienced judgment. ... Each recommendation has a clear audit trail, while the investigator remains accountable for the final determination." said Sean Searby, Chief Information & Operations Officer, Amalgamated Bank.

FIS developed and owns the AML Investigations Agent, built with support from an Anthropic team that embedded with FIS product and compliance teams, drawing on FIS’ financial-institution relationships, regulatory expertise and transaction-data infrastructure. The agent runs in FIS’ private cloud and uses Anthropic’s Claude models.

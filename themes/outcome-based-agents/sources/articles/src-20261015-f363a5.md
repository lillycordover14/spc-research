CMA CGM Scales AI Voice Agents with HappyRobot Across Global Operations

CMA CGM Group
, which operates globally in 
sea, land, air and logistics solutions
, together with HappyRobot, a company whose platform is used to build and scale enterprise AI agents, is broadening the use of AI voice agents designed around the shipping group's operational requirements. After encouraging pilot programmes in sales, customer service and finance, 
CMA CGM
 is enlarging the scope of the solution so that customer interactions can be managed and associated tasks automated, with the goal of enhancing customer experience.
Starting from these first applications, the two companies intend to roll the deployment out across the wider group, 
CEVA Logistics
 included. Deployment is already under way in Europe and North America, and further expansion is anticipated in Africa, South America and Asia, where repetitive tasks can be automated and staff can concentrate on work in which their know-how adds the most value.
Voice agents spanning sales, service and finance
Through HappyRobot's platform, CMA CGM is able to create and launch multilingual AI agents shaped around particular operational requirements and linked to the group's internal systems. CMA CGM fields over 54 million customer requests annually, and the solution assists the group in handling customer interactions, backing commercial outreach and automating routine tasks. As a result, employees can spend more of their time on cases that call for their expertise and individualised support.
On the sales side, CMA CGM has put Eva into service, an AI voice agent built jointly with HappyRobot, to reach prospective customers who have registered on the group's platform but do not have a dedicated sales representative. Eva takes the initiative in contacting such customers to learn about their shipping requirements, provide customised 
freight quotations
 and pinpoint qualified commercial opportunities for CMA CGM's sales teams.
During the first campaigns in the UK and Senegal, Eva reached out to over 4,000 customers, and roughly one third of them moved on to active discussions about possible shipping routes. With these early results as a foundation, CMA CGM intends to have Eva operating in 20 countries by the close of 2026.
On the customer service side, the solution deals with enquiries concerning cargo release, covering release status, customs clearance and shipping documentation. At present, over 46% of such enquiries are settled directly by the AI agents. The agents are also able to prompt emails from CMA CGM's internal systems that carry cargo release numbers, which streamlines the process for customers and cuts down on repeated back-and-forth with the teams.
On the finance side, pilots have shown that the solution can assist with payment follow-up by getting in touch with customers regarding unpaid invoices. Those calls led to payments being recovered, some of them made straight away, which demonstrates concrete gains for CMA CGM's finance teams.
Executive remarks and ZEBOX backing
Mathieu Friedberg, Executive Vice President, Transformation, at 
CMA CGM Group
, stated that the work with HappyRobot shows how the group is converting artificial intelligence into practical tools for its operations. He noted that, with successful pilots as a base, the group is now taking these applications international in order to enhance customer experience and assist its teams, which mirrors an ambition to apply AI where it can produce measurable gains for customers and employees.
Pablo Palafox, CEO of HappyRobot, remarked that before the year is out the company will be operating in more than 20 countries and will keep pursuing innovation in other areas of CMA CGM Group. He observed that CMA CGM is not merely creating agents but is building something that accumulates over time, as agents work and learn alongside one another throughout the group.
Under ZEBOX's Spring 2025 acceleration programme, HappyRobot was brought together with CMA CGM's operational teams, which allowed the startup to pinpoint business use cases and build a proof of concept alongside the group. ZEBOX further raised HappyRobot's profile by having it take part on the CMA CGM stand at Viva Technology and with ZEBOX at CES 2026. Beyond the assistance ZEBOX provided, CMA CGM Group also put money into HappyRobot, strengthening its support for the startup's growth and its voice-based artificial intelligence technology.

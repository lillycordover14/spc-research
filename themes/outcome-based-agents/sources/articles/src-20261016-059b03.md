A fast moving cold front ushered a big chill into the Mid-South today dropping temperatures into the lower 60s for much of the day. That will lead to a cold night tonight followed by a much cooler than average weekend. Unfortunately, the front produced clouds but only a few sprinkles as a dry pattern remains firmly in place for the next several days. Temperatures will warm next week as a result of the dry air.
TONIGHT: 
 Mostly Clear with a breezy North wind at 10 to 15 MPH and overnight lows in the mid to upper 40s. 
SATURDAY: 
 Sunny with a Northeast wind at 10 to 15 MPH and afternoon highs in the mid 60s. 
SATURDAY NIGHT: 
 Clear and cold with a Northeast wind at 5 to 10 MPH and overnight lows in the mid 40s.
SUNDAY: 
Sunny with highs near 70 and overnight lows in the upper 40s.
NEXT WEEK: 
 Monday will be mostly sunny with high temperatures in the mid 70s and overnight lows near 50. Tuesday will be sunny with highs in the upper 70s and overnight lows in the mid 50s. Wednesday will be mostly sunny and warmer with afternoon highs near 80 and overnight lows in the mid 50s. Thursday and Friday will be mostly sunny with highs in the lower 80s and overnight lows in the mid to upper 50s.

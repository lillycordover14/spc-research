                Rohlik Founder's AI Platform Raises $32m For US Expansion
            

October 16, 2026 11:00 AM

By Lisa Edelbrock

Prefer ESM Magazine on Google

Share this article

Share this article

Duvo, the AI platform co-founded by Rohlik CEO 
Tomáš Čupr
, has raised $32 million (€28.62 million) to fund its US expansion.

With a total funding of $48 million (€42.98 million), the expansion will initially focus on retailers and 
consumer packaged goods
 companies, according to Duvo.

The series A funding was led by Balderton Capital, and supported by existing investors Index Ventures, Northzone and Credo Ventures.

Duvo

Duvo is an enterprise execution operating system, that offers companies a single AI-powered platform to run their operations.

It collaborates with several
 companies, including Morrisons, Holland & Barrett, Boden, Notino and Allegro.

            ADVERTISEMENT
        

Tomáš Čupr, co-founder and CEO of Duvo and founder and group CEO of 
Rohlik Group
, said, "For a while I measured 
AI
 in heads, counting how many people an agent could replace. It's the wrong unit. […]

“Our planners used to check about eight at-risk products a day per market for shortages. Now every signal gets checked, around the clock. We think of it as raising the GDP of every employee: more value from the people a company has, not the same value from fewer of them."

Duvo Clarity records every process, while the platform's agents monitor steps across ERP, supplier portals, inboxes and APIs, creating a system that employees can access to follow processes.

US Expansion

The planned US expansion will be led by Michael Green as senior vice president, go-to-market, joining from Databricks, and Spencer Insolia as managing director US, joining from Kmart.

            ADVERTISEMENT
        

Insolia said, "I've spent half my career as a 
retail
 operator and half as a consultant, and the same gap showed up from both sides: the insight was there, the execution rarely followed. Duvo closes that gap, and that's why I'm here.”

The company highlighted that it has unlocked more than $100 million (€89.23 million) in value for clients across finance, supply chain, customer care, marketing and human resources.

Gordon MacPherson, group productivity director at UK retailer Morrisons, said, "Our business processes have evolved over decades, and Duvo gives us a way to understand them in detail, and quickly.”

Duvo also added that Christoph Schwaiger, former CEO of Aldi South Holdings, has joined the company as senior advisor for Europe.

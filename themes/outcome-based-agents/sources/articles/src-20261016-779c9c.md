CAMBRIDGE, Vt. (WCAX) - Vermont’s scenic Route 108 between Cambridge and Stowe will be closed this weekend due to the threat of inclement weather.
The Notch will be closed starting at 6 p.m. Friday. Vermont’s Agency of Transportation will reassess conditions and potentially reopen the road Monday, Oct. 19.

The area has been heavily traveled by tourists for the past few weeks as fall foliage reached its peak. 
Leaves on the roadway can make traveling dangerous, and this weekend’s forecast is calling for the potential of severe weather.

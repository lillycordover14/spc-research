Electronic Referral for the Military Medical Commission in Reserv+: How to Get It Without Visiting the Territorial Recruitment Center

                                                                                            09:42, 16 October 2026
                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                    

2 min

After passing the Military Medical Commission, it is no longer necessary to visit the Territorial Recruitment Center to update the military registration document.

In the Reserv+ app, conscripts can voluntarily submit a request and receive an electronic referral for the military medical commission without visiting the Territorial Recruitment Center.

Previously, a referral for the Military Medical Commission could only be obtained in paper form after a personal visit to the Territorial Recruitment Center. Now, a conscript who wishes to undergo a fitness examination for military service can submit a request in Reserv+.

After logging into the app, you need to submit a request, and within 24 hours you will receive a notification with information about the referral.

Who Can Get an Electronic Referral

This function is available to conscripts who want to independently undergo a medical examination.

Is It Necessary to Visit the Territorial Recruitment Center After the Military Medical Commission

After passing the Military Medical Commission, it is not necessary to additionally visit the Territorial Recruitment Center to update the military registration document.

The commission will independently send the results to the Territorial Recruitment Center, after which the information will be entered into the "Oberig" register.

The updated military registration document will be available to the user in Reserv+.

What Happens If You Get a Referral but Do Not Attend the Military Medical Commission

If a conscript submits a request for the Military Medical Commission but does not appear for the examination, the electronic referral will remain valid for 30 days.

After this period, a new request must be submitted.

There Will Be No Electronic Summons

The Ministry of Defense emphasized that the electronic referral for the Military Medical Commission is not an electronic summons.

Summons for the Military Medical Commission remain paper-based and are still physically delivered.

Only the app user can create a request for an electronic referral.

Paper Referrals Will Remain

There are no plans to completely abandon paper referrals.

If a conscript cannot use a smartphone or does not want to use the electronic service, they can obtain a paper referral at the Territorial Recruitment Center.

How Often Should You Undergo the Military Medical Commission

The Military Medical Commission’s decision on a conscript’s fitness for military service is valid for one year from the date of the medical examination.

Therefore, medical information must be updated annually.

Are People with Reservations or Deferrals Sent to the Military Medical Commission

As a general rule, conscripts who have reservations or deferrals should not be sent to the Military Medical Commission.

An exception applies to persons with the status "limited fitness." They must undergo a repeat examination within the timeframes established by law.

Therefore, a conscript with this status may receive a summons for the Military Medical Commission and must appear at the Territorial Recruitment Center even if they have a reservation or deferral.

Monday’s Parent-A-Child 
features 
Adonis, 
who is seeking a forever family.

Adonis is a caring young man who enjoys walks in the park and outside activities. He enjoys school and enjoys his day with his teacher and peers. He is learning important skills like raising his hand in class and listening while the teacher talks. Adonis is learning to be independent and helpful around the home. He participates in daily tasks such as putting away groceries, cleaning his room, and putting away toys. Adonis would thrive in a family that is outgoing, active, and involved in community activities.

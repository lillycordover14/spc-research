Minna Song, Co-founder and CEO of EliseAI, is building AI agents that run leasing for 1 in 6 US apartments and admin for 3,500 healthcare organisations
One in six apartments in the US now runs leasing conversations through 
EliseAI
. More than 30 million Americans have spoken or messaged with its agents, often without realising it, while booking a viewing, chasing a repair or scheduling a doctor's appointment. 
The New York firm passed US$200m ARR in June 2026, the company reported, and was valued at US$4bn in September. 
The company raised US$350m at a US$4bn valuation
, the company told 
Fortune
, nearly doubling its valuation from just over a year ago.
It chose two unglamorous industries, housing and healthcare administration, and is arguably proving that the biggest AI opportunities sit in the everyday work few start-ups want to touch.
The industries where AI matters most are still not the ones getting the most attention
Minna Song, Co-founder and CEO, EliseAI
What is EliseAI? 
EliseAI was founded in 2017 by 
Minna Song
 and 
Stoyan (Tony) Stoyanov
, who serves as CTO, years before gen AI became one of the world’s biggest conversations.
"We decided to go all in with AI from the start," Mina previously told 
OpenAI
, in comments published by the labs blog. 
"It was less of an 'aha' moment with AI and more of a mindset of solving any problem we could, and AI could help us do that. We knew natural language processing was advancing quickly, even in 2017 there was a lot of investment into research, and we anticipated it would get better."
The strategy has been to go deep in two verticals rather than wide across many. In housing, it handles the 
full renter journey
, from first enquiry and tour booking to renewals, maintenance requests and late rent. In healthcare, it answers patient calls, books appointments and manages admin for specialist practices in areas such as women's health, dermatology and ophthalmology.
The product is a suite of agents. LeasingAI, ResidentAI and VoiceAI plug into the property management systems operators already use, including Yardi, RealPage and Entrata, while healthcare integrations cover electronic health record platforms such as athenahealth and ModMed. 
Stoyan Stoyanov, CTO at EliseAI. Credit: Stoyan Stoyanov/LinkedIn

Housing reach: powers 1 in 6 US apartments (2026)

Voice AI: around 50 million calls handled a year.
Rapid growth in healthcare and housing
VoiceAI now handles around 50 million calls a year. EliseAI combines models from frontier labs including Anthropic and OpenAI with its own in-house models, and uses speech partners such as ElevenLabs to make phone conversations sound natural.
Growth has come from customers handing over more of their operations. Asset Living, the second-largest manager on the NMHC Top 50, runs five EliseAI modules across more than 450,000 units. Summit Property Management collected around US$3m in delinquent rent across three communities.
Zillow uses EliseAI to power AI Assist on multifamily listings, and joint research found renters who used it were 43% more likely to apply. On the healthcare side, more than 3,500 organisations now use the platform. Sacra estimates EliseAI serves more than 600 housing owners and operators, including around three-quarters of the NMHC Top 50.
The company combines exceptional AI research and engineering with a detailed understanding of how properties operate
Sameer Dholakia, Partner, Bessemer Venture Partners
In September 2026 EliseAI raised US$350m led by Andreessen Horowitz and Bessemer Venture Partners at a US$4bn valuation, up from US$2.2bn in 2025, and launched Apollo, an agentic AI teammate that works across the whole platform rather than one task at a time. The next step is to move from answering enquiries to running larger slices of property and 
practice operations end to end
.
With US$350m of new capital, more than US$200m in ARR and a footprint across one in six US apartments, EliseAI now has the scale to test whether agentic AI can run whole operations. The launch of Apollo shows where it is heading next.
Top technology partners

Zillow Rentals:
 EliseAI powers AI Assist on Zillow's multifamily listings. Joint research published in June 2026 found renters who used it were 43% more likely to apply and 24% more likely to sign a lease.

Anthropic
:
 EliseAI works directly with frontier labs including Anthropic, combining Claude models with its own in-house models to handle leasing conversations, renewals and patient messages at scale.

OpenAI:
 OpenAI is the second frontier lab in EliseAI's model stack, with its models used alongside Anthropic's and EliseAI's own to power conversations with renters, residents and patients.

ElevenLabs
:
 A voice partner behind VoiceAI, which answers around 50 million calls a year. ElevenLabs supplies natural-sounding speech for phone conversations with renters and patients, alongside Cartesia and inference partner Baseten.

Yardi
:
 EliseAI integrates with Yardi Voyager and RentCafe, as well as RealPage, Entrata, AppFolio and MRI, so its agents can read and write leasing data without operators changing platforms.

Kitchen appliances brand Breville unveils this musical and sensory campaign, 
Glorious Coffee
, in collaboration with Sydney agency 
Emotive
, celebrating the outsized role a cup of coffee can play in everyday life.  

In the spot, directed by 
Kyra Bartley
 through 
COUSIN
, we see a couple indulging in the anticipation, aroma and routine of crafting their morning coffee, while a 25-piece ensemble from Sydney’s Metropolitan Orchestra performs an original, live score composed by award-winning sound studio Rumble.  

As the beverage brews, so does the music, with every grind, dose, extraction and pour driving the performance towards its crescendo. In a category where convenience and ease are of high importance, the film reframes brewing your morning coffee as a sensual and peaceful ritual.

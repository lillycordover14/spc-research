summary: 'The Modern Data Company''s interim Modern Data Survey (released 2026-08-20; 540+ qualified responses from data leaders and practitioners in 66 countries, survey ongoing): 57.3% are piloting or running AI agents in data and analytics workflows (23.5% production, 33.8% pilots). Only 8.4% say their AI input data is trustworthy enough for production; data quality and trust is a top-three barrier for 75.9%, ahead of missing context and lineage (63.5%) and security (61.7%), with skills (25.9%) and tooling (19.5%) last. 60.9% call a context layer necessary but only 16.0% engineer one; only 10% keep both audit trails and decision-to-source links, and 17.7% have a documented AI accountability framework.'

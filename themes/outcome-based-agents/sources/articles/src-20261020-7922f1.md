summary: Collibra CEO Felix Van de Maele told VentureBeat's AI Week that agent risk, cost and oversight grow exponentially beyond a handful of agents, and that more than 75% of enterprises hit a production wall moving from one copilot to 10 or 15 agents. VentureBeat's June survey of 157 technology leaders found 34% let agents push changes to production on automated evals alone while only 5% fully trust their evals. A Collibra-commissioned Harris Poll found nearly 30% caught agents accessing data they had no permission to see, and 90% blame lack of an aligned data foundation. VentureBeat, 2026-10-20. Felix Van de Maele, Collibra founder/CEO, with Matt Marshall at VB AI Week NY. 'If you have one, two, five agents, that's manageable. If you have 100, 1,000, that just explodes exponentially.' More than 75% of enterprises hit production wall from single copilot to 10-15 agents. VB June survey, 157 enterprise tech leaders: 34% let agents push code/system changes on automated evals alone; 5% fully trust evals. Collibra-commissioned Harris Poll: nearly 30% caught agents accessing/surfacing data without permission; 90% blame lack of aligned data foundation. Three steps: inventory, deployment lifecycle, agent contracts with deterministic guardians. Frontier lab analytics agent: ~30% correct without context, ~90% with, fell to 60% after two months. 'We all have access to the same models, so the differentiation comes from your ability to capture your specific proprietary knowledge.'

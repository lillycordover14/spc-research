Elevenlabs releases two new speech-to-speech models designed for real-time AI conversations

 

Matthias Bastian

View the LinkedIn Profile of Matthias Bastian

					Oct 20, 2026				

Elevenlabs released two new speech-to-speech models called Eleven Realtime.
 The Max variant scores 98.8 percent on the 
Big Bench Audio benchmark
 with a 0.57-second response time. Elevenlabs says no model on the 
Artificial Analysis leaderboard
 is both faster and more accurate right now.

On the Artificial Analysis Speech to Speech leaderboard, Eleven Realtime v1 Max sits near the top left corner, combining high accuracy with low latency. | Image: Elevenlabs

The smaller v1 Mini is built for natural conversations at lower cost and can tell when someone's done talking or just saying "mm-hmm." V1 Max targets more complex tasks like customer support.

Both models are available through the ElevenAgents platform and an OpenAI-compatible Realtime API. Pricing is per minute plus token costs, with v1 Max costing more than v1 Mini. Full details are on the 
Elevenlabs pricing page
.

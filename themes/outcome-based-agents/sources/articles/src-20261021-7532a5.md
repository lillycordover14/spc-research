[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Claude resources article dated October 21, 2026. Not strictly enterprise, but included because it targets small business operations and shows the lab's always-on agent pattern. Microsoft launched a comparable 'Microsoft Scout' personal agent on June 2, 2026 (title seen on Microsoft's blog index, not fetched in full).

"We built Claude Duet for professionals and small business owners with more on their plates than they can track." Built on Claude Opus 5.5; research preview.

Features: "Each morning, it sends you a short brief on the most important items across your connected apps." Periodic checks of connected accounts every few hours, messaging only when needed. Background tasks from scheduling meetings to building dashboards, forecasts and web apps. "Claude Duet has a virtual machine with its own browser, so it doesn't require your laptop or phone to be on." Approval gates before sending messages, making payments, signing documents or deleting anything: "it shows you exactly what it's about to do and waits for your approval." Credentials handled "so it never directly sees your passwords or sign-in tokens." Access to over 6,000 connectors and plugins including Gmail, Slack, QuickBooks, Stripe, Gusto and Shopify. Purchases via Link with virtual cards.

Pricing: included in Pro and Max, sharing usage limits; standalone iOS and Android app and claude.ai; waitlist starting with US Pro and Max subscribers.

Testers: Jess Provencher (Pro Homes), Amin Ebrahimi (Shahs of Kabob, about 110 employees: "I don't need to open up 100 tabs."), Mya Dalton (brand strategist: "I love that proactivity.").

Caveat from Anthropic: "it still might misread a thread or miscalibrate what's urgent."

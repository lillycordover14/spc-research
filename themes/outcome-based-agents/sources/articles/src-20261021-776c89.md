[agent notes, not verbatim] Primary Claude resources article dated October 21, 2026.

HCLTech: global technology company with more than 230,000 people in 60 countries, now a Global Premier Partner in the Claude Partner Network.

Three deployment tracks:
1) Anthropic Business Unit and Center of Excellence: forward-deployed engineers set up secure sandbox environments where clients test Claude on their own systems and data before deploying; the center trains client workforces, helps engineering teams adopt AI-native practices and builds controls for running agents safely at scale.
2) HCLSoftware becomes an Anthropic ISV partner embedding Claude across cybersecurity, automation, business process applications and application development; first products HCL BigFix (endpoint management), HCL AppScan (application security testing) and the Actian AI analyst (data and analytics).
3) AI Force: HCLTech's platform for IT support, code remediation and digital employee services adds Claude and Claude Code with policy guardrails and exception handling. Agents handle routine work; HCLTech engineers oversee high-impact changes, since "a mistake is expensive and every change needs to be reviewed, logged, and approved".

Industries: financial services, manufacturing, life sciences and healthcare, technology, semiconductor, telecom and media, retail and CPG, mobility and public services.

Quotes: C Vijayakumar (CEO, HCLTech): "Few technology companies combine the scale of a global systems integrator with the product depth of an ISV," and "That gives us a rare advantage to securely engineer and deploy Agentic AI at scale". Daniela Amodei (Anthropic): "AI can only be as useful as it is trustworthy"; "we build Claude to give companies the confidence to bring it into their most sensitive and valuable work,"; "HCLTech manages those kinds of systems for banks, hospitals, telecom companies and manufacturers".

No financial terms or headcount commitments stated. Related: Google said at Next '26 its FDE teams work with HCLTech among other SIs (separate record).

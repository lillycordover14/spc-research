Dual meet dominion.
Diving into the Patty Center Pool Saturday morning, University of Alaska Fairbanks swim decimated Azusa Pacific en route to a weekend sweep, and a 3-0 start to head-to-head competition, according to a UAF athletics news release.
Aided by conquests in seven of the 11 events, the Nooks cruised to a 123-76 victory.
Mirroring Friday’s tilt, which the Nanooks claimed in a similarly assertive 133-64 fashion, medley-relay supremacy, this time fueled by Monica Botha, Dorka Dancsok, Marilia Persidou and Sam Bristor, commenced the bout in style.

Displaying spectacular stamina, Nooks peppered the podium in the 1650-yard freestyle, with Zsófia Varga, Kitti Marton and Mariia Sirotinskaia securing each medal spot.
Promptly answering a rare Cougars victory, Zhenia Stukova topped the competition with a blazing 25.05 swim of the 50-yard freestyle. Dancsok exhibited prowess in the 200-yard IM, surging to triumph in the halved version of the 400-yard rendition, which she swam at a Second Team All-American level a season ago.
The aforementioned duo, a lethal combination of youth and experience, each claimed a pair of golds, the freshman Stukova adding the 500-yard freestyle and Dancsok, a senior, the 100-yard breaststroke.

Pactum Launches Intake Agent, Cutting Purchase Request Cycle Time by 90%&nbsp;&nbsp;PR Newswire

[page text]
New AI agent replaces complicated purchase forms and repeated follow-ups by completing and checking every request before it is submitted, inside Microsoft Teams, Coupa, and SAP Ariba.

MOUNTAIN VIEW, Calif.
, 
Oct. 21, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- Pactum, the most advanced agentic AI platform for enterprise autonomous procurement execution, today announced the launch of its Intake Agent, which lets employees describe what they need to buy in plain language. The agent turns each request into a complete, correctly coded requisition and checks it against company buying policies before it is submitted.  Available now in Microsoft Teams, Coupa, and SAP Ariba, the Intake Agent connects with Pactum's other agents to enable end-to-end autonomous indirect procurement. A purchase can draw on an existing contract or move to autonomous sourcing and negotiation, with a human buyer handling requests that require judgment.

The launch extends Pactum's growing portfolio of AI agents to the start of indirect procurement, covering purchases such as software, office equipment, and professional services. It brings Pactum's co-founders' vision of procurement run with AI agents from the first request through sourcing and negotiation into operation. Procurement teams set the buying rules once, and the agent applies them to every request, whatever its value.

"Procurement teams can now run indirect procurement with full autonomy while remaining in control and managing the exceptions," said Kaspar Korjus, Co-Founder and CEO of Pactum. "Our Intake Agent helps resolve questions earlier so requests can move forward with less back-and-forth and teams can focus on the decisions that need their expertise."

For employees, finding an approved supplier or selecting the right accounting code can require expertise across procurement, finance, operations, and IT teams. Missing or incorrect information can lead to rejected requisitions and repeated follow-ups, leaving buyers to focus on correcting forms instead of focusing on supplier relationships and commercial decisions. Review queues can stretch from days to weeks.

With Pactum Intake, employees describe what the business needs, whether goods or services of any complexity, and can provide supporting documents such as a supplier quote. The agent uses the request and relevant documents to identify the best procurement category and complete the accounting and supplier details. It asks only the follow-up questions needed to complete the requisition. Once the request is checked, it is handed off to another Pactum digital worker to autonomously source and negotiate for better pricing and terms where required.

"Building software that assists procurement professionals is relatively easy. Building software that runs the whole process on its own, from the first request to the final purchase, is much harder," said Kristjan Korjus, Co-Founder and Chief Scientist at Pactum. "It is the difference between driver assistance and a car with no one behind the wheel. Most procurement AI still needs a buyer at the wheel for every request. Our AI agents take the request all the way to the purchase on their own, and it is remarkable to watch them pass the work from one agent to the next."

The launch follows Pactum's Requisition Alignment Agent surpassing one million requisition checks, which validates purchase requests against company buying policies, existing contracts, and approved suppliers. Intake builds on this foundation in policy enforcement by connecting employee requests with agents that carry out sourcing and negotiation.

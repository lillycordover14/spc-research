Wilson Sonsini Advises ElevenLabs on $300 Million Employee Tender Offer

Client Highlights

October 21, 2026

On September 30, 2026, ElevenLabs, a voice technology research company, announced the closing of a $300 million employee tender offer that values the company at $22 billion, doubling its valuation at its Series D financing in February 2026. The tender offer was led by Wellington and T. Rowe Price, with participation from new investors BDT & MSD, EQT, GIC, Goldman Sachs, OTPP, and Sapphire Ventures, as well as existing investors including Alkeon, Andreessen Horowitz, D.E. Shaw, DISRUPTIVE, Evantic, ICONIQ, and Lightspeed. Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati advised Elevenlabs on the transaction.

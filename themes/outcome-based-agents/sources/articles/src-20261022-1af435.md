CEO and Co-Founder of Gradient Labs, Dimitri Masin, shares what it means for the AI startup to win Bronze at Money20/20 USA’s Money Awards 2026
Gradient Labs
, the AI company building customer support agents for financial services, has won Bronze in the AI and Automation subcategory of the Banking category at The Money Awards 2026, presented at 
Money20/20
 USA in Las Vegas.
The Money Awards 2026 is Money20/20 USA’s global awards programme, which recognises innovation and impact across global financial technology and services and selects its winners through a multi-stage judging process.
Dimitri Masin
, CEO and Co-Founder of Gradient Labs, says: “We started Gradient Labs with the belief that banks should run on auto-pilot, with compliant AI agents handling the customer operations that consume most of their energy today.
“That belief has paid off: our agents now reach more than 32 million end users, in some of the largest AI deployments in financial services.”
Dimitri Masin is the CEO and Co-Founder of Gradient Labs. Credit: LinkedIn

What Bronze means for Gradient Labs
The Banking category was open only to entrants that could show at least 12 months of measurable results in live production.
Despite being launched less than two years ago, Gradient Labs’ agent is now largely resolving cases end-to-end, leaving room for human verification at the end.
For example, in a 
card dispute,
 the agent collects and checks the evidence, reaches a decision and, once a human has signed it off, files the chargeback with the card network.
“We’ve learned that the hardest part isn’t the agent itself, but the system around it: how it’s designed, maintained and monitored once it’s live,” Dimitri adds.
“That’s why this award, which only counts results in production, means so much to us.”
The Co-Founders of Gradient Labs. Credit: Gradient Labs
Gradient Labs’ AI: started from Monzo’s data science team
Gradient Labs was founded in London, UK, in 2023 by Dimitri Masin, 
Neal Lathia
 and 
Danai Antoniou,
 who built the data science and machine learning teams at 
Monzo
 before leaving to start their own company.
At the UK challenger bank, they spent years working on customer support automation, where the aim was typically a modest 10% efficiency gain.
Yet after 
OpenAI
 released GPT-4 in March 2023, they spent weekends testing it and concluded that most of the manual, repetitive work in financial services could be automated.
In a blog for Gradient Labs, Dimitri says: “The reality is banks and fintechs spend most of their time and resources dealing with operational complexity.
“Even neo and challenger banks, built from scratch to do things differently, eventually reach a scale where the burden of operations overwhelms their ability to innovate.”
We built Gradient Labs, and bet on vertical AI to tackle the most challenging problems in finance. That bet has paid off
Dimitri Masin, CEO and Co-Founder of Gradient Labs

Gradient Labs serves more than 32 million end users, and its customers now include the likes of 
Wise
, credit card provider 
Yonder
 and motor insurer 
Zego
; the latter of which reported
 a 25% reduction in call volumes using Gradient Labs’ AI agent, according to a case study.

With its Series A doubled to US$26m in June 2026 and a US expansion underway, Gradient Labs heads into 2027 with the trophy and the production results to back it.

Key partners for Gradient Labs

Zego
 runs a Gradient Labs AI agent, branded Alex, which handles customer queries across live chat and email around the clock. The UK motor insurer reports a 25% fall in call volumes since launch, with customer satisfaction rising from 61% to 77%. Its resolution rate climbed from 41% to 60% within two months.

Yonder
 signed with Gradient Labs in mid-2025, when an AI agent went live on its frontline chat and email support. The UK credit card provider added the lending agent for collections in 2026 and also uses the disputes agent, which has cut case times from five to seven days to about two.

Wise
 works with Gradient Labs on customer support, using AI agents to help customers get faster, clearer answers at scale. The global money transfer company is named alongside Monzo and Zego among Gradient Labs’ customers, and its support team has discussed publicly how the partnership prioritises resolving queries over deflecting them.

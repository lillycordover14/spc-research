JEFFERSON COUNTY, Ala. (
WBRC
) - The Jefferson County Emergency Management Agency is asking residents to prepare for tornado season before severe weather arrives.
Officials said weather in Alabama can change quickly, sometimes overnight, and the best time to prepare is now.
“Don’t be slow to act. Be able to take the measures right now while there is time, while there isn’t any weather moment. Plan to win, not planning to win is planning to fail,” said Michael Derzis, emergency management officer with the Jefferson County EMA.
Tornado season starts in November and lasts until May.
Tornado shelter installed in Moundville, Ala.
(WBRC)
Know your safe place
Officials said residents should find a safe place in their home for tornado season. That location should be a basement or a small, interior room without windows.
That room should have emergency equipment on hand, including flashlights, medications and a way to charge a phone.
Officials also said now is the time to know the location of tornado shelters in a resident’s neighborhood.

Sign up for weather alerts
It’s also important to know the difference between a weather watch and a warning.
A tornado watch mean there is a possibility of a tornado while a warning means the tornado is happening or is about to.
“Emphasizing signing up for severe weather alerts — I said it a few times. It’s essential in getting info out so you can get it in a timely manner,” Derzis said.
Officials said it is important to have multiple ways to receive weather warnings.

Rillet and Cherry Bekaert Help Growth-Stage Companies Modernize Finance Operations
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  Business Wire 
 
 
October 22, 2026
 
3 min read
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Rillet's Agentic ERP gives growth-stage finance teams a real-time general ledger and embedded AI agents supported by Cherry Bekaert's implementation and advisory capabilities.
 
 
SAN FRANCISCO & RALEIGH, N.C., October 22, 2026
--(
BUSINESS WIRE
)--Rillet, the Agentic ERP, today announced a strategic alliance with Cherry Bekaert, a national assurance, tax, and advisory firm, to help growth-stage companies modernize finance operations through an AI-native ERP platform that delivers real-time financial visibility, automation and scalability.
 
 
 
 
 
The alliance combines Rillet's continuously updated general ledger and AI agents embedded in accounting workflows with Cherry Bekaert's implementation and advisory capabilities, helping finance teams modernize operations while maintaining appropriate controls and auditability.
 
 
As organizations continue to invest in finance modernization, they need technology solutions that adapt to evolving business needs. Cherry Bekaert brings decades of experience helping organizations evaluate, implement and optimize ERP and other business-critical technology solutions. Working with Rillet builds on that foundation and expands the modern ERP solutions Cherry Bekaert can bring to clients.
 
 
"Finance organizations are under increasing pressure to deliver faster insights, improve efficiency and support business growth with fewer manual processes. Our alliance with Rillet expands the solutions we can bring to clients as they modernize their finance function, accelerating time to value from their technology investments. By combining innovative technology with Cherry Bekaert's deep advisory experience, we can help clients make more informed technology decisions and build a stronger foundation for future growth," said 
Roy Nicholson
, Business Optimization Leader, Cherry Bekaert.
 
 
 
 
 
Rillet's Agentic ERP lets finance teams and AI agents work in the same real-time general ledger, under the same set of permissions and approval controls. Agents prepare the work and accountants approve it, and every AI-generated result can be traced back to its source records, so the audit trail is built in and accessible at any moment.
 
 
"Cherry Bekaert has already proven it can take one technology relationship and scale it into a real practice — that's the kind of team we want running Rillet implementations. With their cloud-native delivery discipline, growth-stage companies get speed without giving up the controls their investors and auditors expect. We're really thrilled to launch this alliance," said Nadiv Rahman, Head of Partnerships, Rillet.

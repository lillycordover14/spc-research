Brantley Andrew Conner's
 Obituary

Brantley Andrew Conner, 13, of Dayton, Texas, passed away on October 22, 2026. He was born on November 16, 2012, in Cleveland, Texas, to his loving parents, Andrew Conner and Trista Nailling.
Brantley was a bright young boy who brought joy to those around him. He attended Dayton Middle School, where he enjoyed playing baseball and was involved in band, playing the French horn. He had a love for video games and enjoyed spending time outdoors fishing. One of his favorite pastimes was building with LEGOs, which allowed him to use his creativity and imagination. Brantley will be remembered for the happiness he brought to his family and friends and the special memories they shared with him. Though his time here was far too short, the love and memories he leaves behind will forever remain in the hearts of those who knew and loved him.
Brantley was preceded in death by his grandmother, Polly Nailling.
He leaves behind to cherish his memory his mother, Trista Nailling; his father, Andrew Conner; his grandmother, Melisa Conner; his brothers, Malakia Smith and Sylus Smith; his sisters, Kiylie Williamson and Brooklyn Conner; along with numerous other loving relatives, friends, classmates, and all those whose lives he touched.
Brantley will be deeply missed, forever loved, and never forgotten.

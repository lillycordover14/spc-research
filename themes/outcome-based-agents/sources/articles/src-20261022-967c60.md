Pactum new AI agent enables end-to-end autonomous indirect procurement across SAP Ariba, Coupa & Teams, helping enterprises scale supplier negotiations
Pactum,
 which describes itself as “the most advanced agentic AI platform for enterprise autonomous procurement execution”, has launched its Intake Agent.
This new tool will allow users to describe what they need to buy in plain language. Pactum outlines in the product’s announcement that the agent turns each request into a complete, correctly coded requisition and checks it against company buying policies before it is submitted.
The tool, which is available in 
Microsoft
 Teams, 
Coupa
 and 
SAP
 Ariba, connects with Pactum's other agents to enable end-to-end autonomous indirect procurement. A purchase can draw on an existing contract or move to autonomous sourcing and negotiation, with a human buyer handling requests that require judgment.
It continues Pactum’s array of AI agents, which is now quite a portfolio of tools at the start of indirect procurement, covering purchases such as software, office equipment and professional services.
This forms part of the Pactum co-founders' vision of procurement run with AI agents from the first request through sourcing and negotiation into operation. Procurement teams set the buying rules once, and the agent applies them to every request, whatever its value.
"Procurement teams can now run indirect procurement with full autonomy while remaining in control and managing the exceptions," says 
Kaspar Korjus, Co-Founder and CEO of Pactum.
"Our Intake Agent helps resolve questions earlier so requests can move forward with less back-and-forth and teams can focus on the decisions that need their expertise."
Eliminating bottlenecks and form-filling
Identifying approved suppliers or selecting appropriate accounting codes often demands cross-functional knowledge across IT, operations, finance and procurement teams. 
When details are missing or inaccurate, requisitions face rejections and continuous follow-ups, forcing buyers to spend time fixing forms rather than driving commercial decisions and managing supplier relationships. Consequently, review queues can extend from days to weeks.
Pactum Intake addresses these challenges by allowing employees to specify business requirements for goods or services of any complexity and submit supporting files like supplier quotes. 
Utilising the request alongside relevant documentation, the agent determines the optimal procurement category and fills in necessary supplier and accounting details, asking only essential follow-up questions to finalise the requisition. 
Once verified, the request transitions to another Pactum digital worker to autonomously handle sourcing and negotiate improved pricing and terms whenever necessary.
Kristjan Korjus, Co-Founder and Chief Scientist at Pactum, adds: "
Building software that assists procurement professionals
 is relatively easy. Building software that runs the whole process on its own, from the first request to the final purchase, is much harder.
"It is the difference between driver assistance and a car with no one behind the wheel. Most procurement AI still needs a buyer at the wheel for every request. Our AI agents take the request all the way to the purchase on their own, and it is remarkable to watch them pass the work from one agent to the next."
Kristjan Korjus, Co-Founder and Chief Scientist, Pactum
Enterprise scale and proven results
Integrated directly into enterprise procure-to-pay (P2P) platforms, 
Pactum's native AI architecture
 evaluates incoming purchase requests for completeness, regulatory compliance and commercial value to determine if viable negotiation opportunities exist prior to deploying dedicated negotiation agents.
By automatically cross-referencing contract terms, validating preferred suppliers, auditing line items against approved pricing schedules and enforcing governance controls, the system establishes a robust operational framework that allows automated negotiations to scale across broad vendor networks. 
During evaluation, it can automatically resolve data discrepancies or escalate them to purchasing managers, preventing downstream process bottlenecks and non-compliance issues.
Intake expands upon this policy enforcement foundation by bridging employee requests directly to agents responsible for sourcing and negotiation. 
This rollout comes after Pactum's Requisition Alignment Agent crossed the milestone of one million requisition checks, verifying purchase requests against corporate buying guidelines, existing agreements and approved vendor lists.
Kaspar Korjus, CEO at Pactum
Pactum case studies
Honeywell
:
 Honeywell partnered with Pactum to deploy an AI-powered negotiation platform to tackle hundreds of previously untouched, time-consuming supplier contracts. By delegating payment terms, rebate and discount negotiations to autonomous AI agents, Honeywell successfully concluded over 2,500 negotiations across nearly $500 million in spend without adding headcount. This automated approach achieved massive cost savings and high supplier engagement, transforming their procurement function into an efficient, full-coverage operation.
SUEZ
:
 SUEZ integrated Pactum’s AI Alignment Agent into its existing Coupa procurement system to streamline its manual review process across nearly 3,800 UK suppliers. The automated system directly intercepts purchase requisitions to perform instant policy, quote and compliance checks in minutes rather than days. This integration significantly improves team efficiency and bandwidth while maintaining strict sustainability and procurement standards without altering established buyer workflows.

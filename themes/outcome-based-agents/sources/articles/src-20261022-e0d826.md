Rillet and Cherry Bekaert Form Strategic Alliance to Modernize Finance Operations With AI&nbsp;&nbsp;citybiz

[page text]
Rillet
, a provider of artificial intelligence-powered enterprise resource planning software, has formed a strategic alliance with 
Cherry Bekaert
, a national assurance, tax and advisory firm, to help growth-stage companies modernize their financial operations through automated accounting workflows and real-time financial reporting.

The partnership combines Rillet’s AI-native ERP platform with Cherry Bekaert’s technology implementation and business advisory services, providing companies with an integrated approach to financial management, accounting automation and operational scalability.

The alliance is designed to help growing businesses replace manual financial processes with technology that improves reporting efficiency while maintaining internal controls, approval procedures and auditability.

Rillet’s platform uses a continuously updated general ledger and AI agents embedded directly into accounting workflows. The technology allows finance professionals and automated systems to work within the same financial environment, with access governed by established permissions and approval requirements.

Under this approach, AI agents can prepare accounting work for review, while finance professionals retain responsibility for approving transactions and other financial activities.

The platform also maintains traceability between AI-generated outputs and their underlying financial records, allowing users and auditors to review the information supporting accounting decisions.

Cherry Bekaert will provide implementation and advisory expertise to help clients evaluate, deploy and optimize Rillet’s ERP technology.

The firm has experience advising organizations on enterprise software selection, financial systems modernization and business process improvement.

Its collaboration with Rillet expands the range of ERP solutions available to clients seeking to modernize accounting operations and support more complex financial requirements as their businesses grow.

“Finance organizations are under increasing pressure to deliver faster insights, improve efficiency and support business growth with fewer manual processes,” said 
Roy Nicholson
, Business Optimization Leader at Cherry Bekaert.

Nicholson said the alliance will help clients make informed technology decisions and accelerate the value they receive from investments in financial systems.

For growth-stage companies, finance operations often become more complicated as transaction volumes increase, organizational structures expand and reporting requirements evolve.

Businesses may need to manage more sophisticated accounting processes while meeting the expectations of investors, lenders and auditors.

Traditional financial systems can require extensive manual work to reconcile transactions, prepare reports and complete accounting periods.

Rillet’s technology is designed to address these challenges through a continuous-close architecture, allowing financial information to be updated and reviewed throughout the accounting period rather than relying exclusively on traditional month-end closing processes.

The company’s AI agents operate within the general ledger environment and are intended to assist with repetitive accounting tasks while preserving human oversight.

This model is designed to improve the speed of financial reporting without eliminating the review and authorization controls required by finance teams.

The alliance also reflects growing interest in applying AI to core enterprise software rather than deploying automation through separate applications disconnected from financial records.

By integrating AI capabilities directly into accounting workflows, Rillet aims to provide finance teams with access to current financial information and automated processes within a single system.

Cherry Bekaert’s role will focus on helping companies implement the technology in ways that align with their operational needs, internal controls and longer-term business strategies.

“Cherry Bekaert has already proven it can take one technology relationship and scale it into a real practice — that’s the kind of team we want running Rillet implementations,” said 
Nadiv Rahman
, Head of Partnerships at Rillet.

Rahman said Cherry Bekaert’s experience implementing cloud-based systems can help growing companies adopt new technology while maintaining the financial controls expected by investors and auditors.

Rillet describes its software as an Agentic ERP platform, combining a real-time general ledger, continuous accounting processes and AI agents within one financial management environment.

The company serves fast-growing businesses and enterprises, including Mercor, Function Health and Temporal.

Cherry Bekaert provides assurance, tax, accounting advisory, ERP consulting, finance modernization and other professional services to clients across the United States and internationally.

Its advisory capabilities also include outsourcing, recruiting and staffing, risk management, cybersecurity and transaction advisory services.

Cherry Bekaert operates through an alternative practice structure, with Cherry Bekaert LLP providing attest services as a licensed independent CPA firm and Cherry Bekaert Advisory LLC and its subsidiaries providing tax and business advisory services.

The companies did not disclose financial terms of the alliance, revenue expectations or specific customer implementation targets.

The partnership expands Rillet’s implementation network while giving Cherry Bekaert an additional AI-native ERP offering for companies seeking to improve financial reporting and accounting efficiency.

As businesses increasingly evaluate AI-enabled finance technology, the alliance positions both companies to serve organizations looking to automate accounting workflows while preserving transparency, audit trails and human oversight.

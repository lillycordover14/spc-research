MADISON, Wis. — Former Green Bay Packers kicker Mason Crosby and his wife, Molly Crosby, are returning to Wisconsin to share their experience navigating Molly Crosby’s lung cancer diagnosis and recovery at UW Carbone Cancer Center’s 24th annual fall cancer conference.
The Crosbys will serve as keynote speakers at the conference, “Empowering Survivors. Inspiring Action,” on Friday at Monona Terrace.
In an interview with News 3 Now anchor Brady Mallory, the couple said they approached cancer with the same sports- and team-oriented mindset that shaped Mason Crosby’s football career.
“In my career in football, in all of that, I learn just as much from the mess-ups than the good moments,” Mason Crosby said. “You kind of have to take both and push forward.”
Molly Crosby said the experience brought both highs and lows.
“You can either focus on the lows, or you can see them as a way to heighten the highs,” she said.
Mallory will moderate a discussion with the Crosbys during the conference. Walk-in registration begins at 7 a.m. Friday.

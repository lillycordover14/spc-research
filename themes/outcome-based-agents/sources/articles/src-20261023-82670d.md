Puerto Rican musical group Los Pleneros de la Cresta has released Puerto Rico Abraza al Mundo (Puerto Rico Embraces the World), a new album that reimagines iconic songs from seven countries through the rhythms and traditions of Puerto Rican plena.
Inspired by the hospitality and cultural exchanges the group experienced while touring Latin America and Europe, the album brings together musical traditions from Mexico, Colombia, the Dominican Republic, Argentina, Spain, Peru and Brazil. The project celebrates cultural diversity while highlighting plena as a distinctive expression of Puerto Rican identity.
The album features seven well-known songs: “Cielito Lindo” from Mexico, “La Múcura” from Colombia, “Caña Brava” from the Dominican Republic, “De Música Ligera” from Argentina, “La Fórmula” from Spain, “Alma, Corazón y Vida” from Peru and “Mas Que Nada” from Brazil.

Through these interpretations, Los Pleneros de la Cresta create a musical dialogue between plena and the traditions of the featured countries, incorporating different rhythms and instrumentation while maintaining the genre's Puerto Rican roots.
The production also features traditional instruments and sounds associated with the cultures represented on the album. Spanish guitar, instruments characteristic of Mexican music, Colombia's marimba de chonta and clarinet, the Peruvian harp and Brazilian percussion contribute distinctive elements to the arrangements.

The project took shape while the group was traveling internationally as part of Bad Bunny's Debí Tirar Más Fotos world tour. During their travels, the musicians turned hotel rooms into mobile recording spaces, recording much of the album's panderos and güiro percussion along the way.
The vocal arrangements were recorded across two European studios. The backing vocals were recorded at Abbey Road Studios in London, while the lead vocals were recorded at Elysium Recording Studios in France.
According to the group, the album reflects a desire to honor other musical cultures from the perspective of Puerto Rican identity, recognizing the origins of each song rather than simply adapting familiar compositions to a different style.
With Puerto Rico Abraza al Mundo, Los Pleneros de la Cresta extend plena's reach through international musical influences and celebrate the cultural connections developed during their travels.
The album is now available on digital music streaming platforms.

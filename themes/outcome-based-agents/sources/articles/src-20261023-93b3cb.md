Relevance AI – No-Code AI Agent Platform, Workflow Automation & Multi-Agent Teams
#Quasa #QUA #RelevanceAI

Relevance AI is a web platform for building AI agents and coordinating them into teams that handle business workflows. It is made by OnSearch Pty Ltd, trading as Relevance AI. Users configure agents through a visual builder or describe their requirements to Invent, which assembles tools and knowledge connections. Agents can retrieve information, use connected applications and delegate tasks to other agents. Features include scheduled runs, knowledge retrieval, task monitoring and human approval controls. It serves operations, sales, support and research teams. The official pricing page lists an Enterprise offering with custom usage allowances and access controls; current pricing is listed on the official website.

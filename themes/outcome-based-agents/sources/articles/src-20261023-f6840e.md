UVA Basketball: What we can glean from two blowout preseason wins&nbsp;&nbsp;Augusta Free Press

[page text]
Photo: Mike Ingalls/AFP

Thijs de Ridder
 had 17 points to lead four ‘Hoos in double-figures as the 
UVA Basketball
 team won its final preseason tuneup, 
93-71
, at 
Villanova
, on 
Thursday night
.

Up front here: the game wasn’t on TV, and I didn’t trek to 
Philly
 to take in an exhibition game, so, this news and notes item is based entirely on the box score.

Them being the ground rules.

What we can glean

Elijah Gertrude is going to get some run

Elijah Gertrude. Photo: Mike Ingalls/AFP

We’ve been waiting to see what we’ve been seeing in the preseason out of 
Elijah Gertrude
, a fourth-year player with a year of eligibility remaining, since he arrived on 
Grounds
 back in 
2023
.

It was apparent from the jump that 
the four-star, Top 50 recruit
 was not a good fit in the 
Tony Bennett
 system, but he came here anyway, then got hurt after his freshman season – the scooter accident took away his sophomore campaign.

Last year, he got caught up in the numbers game, with 
Ryan Odom
 recruiting a whole new rotation over him.

Odom did that again in the spring, but Gertrude, still bouncy, after the scooter accident, and the ACL injury that took his senior year in high school, has obviously worked himself into rotation minutes.

Gertrude had 
11 points
 – 
4-of-7 FG, 3-of-5 3FG
 – in 
17 minutes
 in the 
96-53 win
 over 
Richmond
 on 
Oct. 9
.

Last night, Gertrude scored 
16 points
 – going 
5-of-6 from three
 – in 
18 minutes
.

The shooting from three is noteworthy here. In his first two seasons at 
Virginia
, he was just 
3-of-29, 10.3 percent
, from three.

You’re not going to get on the court at guard for Ryan Odom if you can’t shoot the three.

Obviously, Gertrude made that a priority in the offseason.

Now, we’ve got a guy with bouncy athleticism who can shoot from the perimeter.

Where the transfer guards are fitting in

Nolan Adekunle
, the 24-year-old 
German
 first-year, started again last night, and scored 
11 points
 – 
4-of-8 FG, 3-of-6 3FG
 – in 
28 minutes
. He averaged 
22.0 minutes per game 
over the last two.

Jan Vide
 (
12.1 ppg, 4.0 assists/g, 46.9% 2FG, 32.3% 3FG
 at 
Loyola-Marymount
) averaged 
16.5 minutes per game
.

Jurian Dixon
 (
15.9 ppg, 45.9% 2FG, 38.5% 3FG 
at 
Cal-Irvine
) averaged 
12.5 minutes per game
.

Christian Harmon
 (
12.8 ppg, 53.2% 2FG, 34.6% 3FG
 at 
Arkansas State
) did not play in the win over 
Richmond
, and got 
six minutes
 last night, making a pair of threes.

The pecking order in the backcourt

This is an educated guess, from the past two games, which, based on the minutes distributed by the coaches on the opposing sidelines in each, appear to have been coached like they were regular-season games.

Starters
: PG Chance Mallory, SG Sam Lewis, SG Nolan Adekunle

Bench
: PG Jan Vide, CG Eliah Gertude

In the mix
: SG Jurian Dixon, SG Christian Harmon

Frontcourt splits

The 
final 2:49 last night
 was officially garbage time – three guys (
Christian Harmon, Silas Barksdale, Carter Lang
) checked in for the first time with 
2:49 left
; 
Owen Odom
 checked in for the 
final 1:06
.

That means the two frontcourt spots would get a total 
of 74 minutes
 of floor time – 37 times two.

Thijs de Ridder
 and 
Johann Grunloh
 each played 
20 minutes
.

Favour Ibe
, the first big to sub in, got 
nine minutes
.

Kalu Anya
 got 
10 minutes
.

Martin Carrere
, at 
6’9”
, got 
10 minutes
, but he subbed in and out for guards.

Nolan Adekunle
 appears to be the tweener – he subbed out with de Ridder for Mallory and Anya in the first half, then subbed back in straight up for Anya, then out straight up for Sam Lewis.

In the second half, he subbed in for Dixon, then subbed out for Anya.

Based on what we saw last night, would appear that Odom is going to go four-guard with Adekunle for 
10-15 minutes a game
, depending on the opponent.

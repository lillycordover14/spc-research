SYOSSET, Long Island (WABC) -- 
Four men have been arrested and charged in connection with an armed robbery at a Long Island storage facility that investigators say occurred in August.
Authorities recently released photographs of the suspects as details of the investigation became public.
According to detectives, the four men entered a storage facility on Underhill Boulevard in Syosset where they allegedly held several people at gunpoint and restrained them with zip ties.
Investigators said the suspects removed multiple pallets of goods from the facility, including marijuana, and stole cash from the victims before fleeing the scene.
Following an investigation, detectives tracked the suspects to Flushing and College Point, Queens.
The four men are now in custody and facing charges related to the robbery.

A bathroom trip allegedly turned into a costly disappearing act at Poppy’s Cresta. The restaurant claims a group of diners vanished before paying their R3,000 bill.
Image:

A Johannesburg restaurant is demanding answers after a group of diners allegedly disappeared without paying a R3,000 bill.
Poppy’s Cresta took to social media to call out the customers. The restaurant said the incident happened on Saturday night.
According to the post, the group asked for their 
bill
 while on their way to the bathroom.
Their waiter printed the bill and fetched the Speedpoint machine. But when he returned, the diners were gone.
And just like that, a R3,000 
bill
 was left unpaid.
The restaurant was not impressed and said behind every meal served is a team working hard to earn an honest living.
“We work hard to provide a great experience at Poppy’s, and our staff work hard for every cent they earn,” the restaurant said.
Poppy’s Cresta has now given the diners an opportunity to make things right.
In a direct message to the group, the restaurant made it clear that it knew someone out there had some explaining to do.
“If you are reading this, you know who you are,” it said.
The restaurant urged the customers to come forward and settle their outstanding bill.
While mistakes happen, Poppy’s stressed that taking responsibility matters.
For now, the 
restaurant
 is waiting for the group to do the right thing and pay what they owe.
Whether the diners will respond to the public appeal remains to be seen.

Ada Annie Aylor Corbin

Dec 30, 1943 — Oct 26, 2026

Ada Annie Aylor Corbin, 82, of Culpeper, Va., passed away on Monday, October 26, 2026, at The Culpeper. She was born on December 30, 1943, to the late James Henry Aylor and Missie Mae Weakley Aylor. She was also preceded in death by her husband, William Franklin “Frank” Corbin; brothers, William Ray Edwards, Buddy Harrison Edwards, Jerry Granville Edwards, Claude Winfrey Edwards; and sister, Thelma Mae Edwards Dodson.
She is survived by her daughter, Sue Corbin Embree and husband, James; son, Eric T. Corbin and wife Michelle; grandchildren, Katie Rose and husband Bryan, Michelle Embree and Andrew Beach, Emma Corbin, Amelia Corbin; great-grandchildren, Franklin Rose and Henry Rose.
A memorial service will be held at 11:00 am on Friday, November 6, 2026 at Bethel Baptist Church.  Interment will be in the church cemetery.

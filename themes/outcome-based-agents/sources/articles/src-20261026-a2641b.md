TOLEDO, Ohio (WTVG) - Clean-up efforts are underway after the North Toledo hazmat situation, with the U.S. EPA continuing to monitor air quality.
One tool they’re using is the “Trace Atmospheric Gas Analyzer Bus”, or TAGA Bus.
The city of Toledo calls it a highly-specialized air-monitoring lab on wheels.
The vehicle is one of only two in the country.
There is an interactive map detailing up-to-date air quality readings from the TAGA Bus.

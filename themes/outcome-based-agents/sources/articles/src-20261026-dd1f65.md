Global artificial intelligence (AI) audio company ElevenLabs has unveiled an AI feature that automatically generates background music and sound effects matched to video scenes.

ElevenLabs said on the 26th that it has launched "Instant Soundtrack," which creates and places music and sound effects suited to a video in a single pass. The feature has been available since the 15th through ElevenCreative, ElevenMusic and the developer application programming interface (API).

In conventional video production, creators had to separately search for background music matching the mood of the footage and, for each scene, select sound effects and align their playback timing. Advertising and short-form videos in particular required precise synchronization of music and sound effects with scene transitions, making the editing process labor-intensive.

Instant Soundtrack replaces this process with AI. When a user uploads a video, the AI analyzes its content, creates background music, identifies on its own the scenes that need sound effects, and places the sounds in sync with the video's playback timeline. If, for example, a company logo appears at the end of an ad, the feature generates a sound effect suited to that moment and inserts it at the point where the logo appears.

The feature can also change the character of the music according to each scene's mood. It uses electronic dance music (EDM) or rhythm and blues (R&B) in segments with rapid cuts, then shifts to acoustic guitar-driven music when the video moves to calmer scenes. Playback timing is also adjusted so that music and sound effects flow naturally at the moments when screen transitions or specific actions occur, in line with short-form content.

It also takes into account audio already present in the video. The AI analyzes the genre, tempo and instrumentation of existing music and can create new music with a similar feel, allowing the user to replace only the background track.

The generated output can be edited directly. ElevenCreative's music video editor displays background music and sound effects in separate editing layers, letting users adjust the timing of sound effects, swap out music and add needed sounds. Finished videos can be exported immediately. Developers can download music and sound effects as separate files through the API for further editing, and the service also supports processing up to 10 videos with a single request.

ElevenLabs said users can "refine individual scenes without having to start over from scratch when adding sound effects, adjusting timing or replacing music."

With this launch, ElevenLabs is expanding its business from voice synthesis and AI voice agents into video content production. Its distinguishing feature is bundling background music generation, sound effect placement and detailed editing—all needed for advertising and short-form production—into a single service.

In the AI audio market, competition that had centered on voice synthesis is clearly expanding into video sound production as a whole. With video editing tools and music generation services having existed separately, an integrated feature that handles music and sound effects simultaneously in a single request is expected to become a way for advertisers and creators churning out short-form content in volume to cut editing time.

A majority of its revenue comes from businesses paying for its models per use, measured in "tokens" of information processed, in what Anthropic calls "consumption-based" revenue.

That has helped Anthropic grow its revenue to $4.59 billion in 2025 from $386 million a year ago and $41 million in 2023. For the six months ended June 2026, Anthropic's revenue was $16.29 billion, a massive increase from $1.16 billion for the same period in 2025.

Following Anthropic's lead in the enterprise market, OpenAI has sharpened its focus on products for developers and business customers.

Ramp data, which captures a slice of AI spending by business customers, showed Anthropic accounted for 60.1% of AI token spending in the week ended October 11.

Kestra Financial Firm Borger Financial Surpasses $1 Billion in Assets Under Management


                      by
                      Kestra Financial                    

October 27, 2026

 

The Borger Group.

Borger Financial (BF) has surpassed $1 billion in assets under management, marking a significant milestone for the Northeast-based boutique wealth
 
management firm. The achievement comes less than three years after BF joined Kestra Financial with $600 million in client assets.

The achievement is especially meaningful for a firm that has remained deliberately small, with a team of just six employees, including three financial advisors and three non-advisory professionals. The team has built the firm around depth rather than volume, working closely with senior professionals and business owners whose success often brings increasingly complex financial needs. That includes partners at leading law firms navigating investments, taxes, estate planning, asset protection and charitable giving.

That focused approach also shapes how the firm operates. Rather than relying on a traditional office model, BF works primarily virtually while maintaining the ability to meet with clients in person. The model allows BF to operate efficiently while giving clients greater flexibility in how and where they engage with the firm.

For Co-Principal and Managing Partner Elie Borger, crossing the $1 billion threshold also reflects how far the practice has evolved. Borger began his career focused on insurance, and over time, the firm has grown to address a much broader set of needs for high-net-worth clients. Today, BF delivers a bespoke, coordinated approach to wealth management, working alongside clients’ existing attorneys, accountants, and other advisors to align their financial strategies with their broader goals.

“Crossing $1 billion is meaningful to us, but not simply because of the number,” said Partner and Senior Portfolio Manager Debra Clark, CFP. “It reflects the trust our clients have placed in us and how our firm has evolved alongside them. As their lives and financial needs have become more complex, we’ve continued to broaden the way we serve them while staying intentionally lean and personal.”

That evolution is particularly evident in BF’s work within the legal community and families thinking about how wealth extends beyond one generation. The firm specializes in strategies designed to preserve legacy assets across generations and has developed charitable giving strategies for clients who want their wealth to support the causes that matter most to them.

BF joined Kestra Financial in late 2023 to expand its capabilities for sophisticated high-net-worth clients, including greater access to alternative investments and access to multiple lenders for securities-based lines of credit (SBLOCs). Moving forward the firm remains focused on the approach that has driven its growth: staying personal and nimble while continuing to broaden the capabilities available to clients as their needs evolve.

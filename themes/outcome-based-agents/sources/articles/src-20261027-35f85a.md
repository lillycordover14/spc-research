Insider Brief

Niural AI launched workforce and payment tools for credentialed experts who contribute to AI model training, post-training and evaluation.

The tools support expert onboarding, confidentiality and intellectual property agreements, flexible payments, cross-border tax documentation and centralized oversight of contracts and spending.

The capabilities are available immediately to Niural AI’s enterprise clients worldwide, supporting engagements with specialists such as doctors, lawyers, scientists and engineers.

PRESS RELEASE — 
Niural AI
, the AI-native global workforce and payments platform that powers every employment model, today announced the launch of the industry’s first dedicated infrastructure for 
Expert Verifiers
, the rapidly growing workforce of credentialed domain experts contributing to AI model training, post-training, and evaluation.

As frontier AI advances beyond traditional annotation into sophisticated post-training and evaluation, demand for specialized human expertise is accelerating. Models increasingly rely on doctors, lawyers, scientists, and other domain experts to evaluate complex reasoning and improve performance. Yet AI companies still manage these highly skilled professionals through fragmented systems built for crowdsourcing and gig work, creating significant worker classification, tax, intellectual property, and operational risks.

Niural AI eliminates this bottleneck by introducing 
Expert Verifier
 as a first-class operational role within its global workforce platform, sitting alongside W-2 employees, international hires, and traditional contractors.

Purpose-Built for AI Evaluation Workflows

The launch introduces specialized capabilities built specifically for task-based and project-based AI evaluation:

High-Trust Global Onboarding & IP Governance:
 Streamlined onboarding workflows that handle international worker verification, strict non-disclosure agreements, and airtight IP assignment, ensuring proprietary model training data remains protected.

Dynamic Payment & Settlement Engines:
 Full support for hourly, task-based, and pay-on-demand settlement across supported global markets, allowing AI teams to compensate specialists immediately upon task verification.

Automated Cross-Border Tax & Compliance:
 Built-in tax collection (W-8/W-9) and localized payout rails that eliminate worker misclassification risks for short-term or on-demand expert engagements.

Unified Enterprise Governance:
 A single dashboard giving legal, finance, and AI engineering leads visibility into active verifier contracts, total evaluation spend, and global compliance status.

“The AI data industry is undergoing a fundamental shift,” said Nami Baral, founder and CEO of Niural AI. “As models get more capable, the work required to improve them is moving beyond traditional annotation into expert reasoning, verification, and increasingly complex post-training environments. The people doing this work are doctors, lawyers, scientists, engineers, and specialists whose expertise can determine what a model is capable of doing next.

“But the infrastructure hasn’t kept up. You cannot build a global market for high-value human intelligence on payment links and generic contractor workflows. These engagements raise entirely different questions around classification, credentials, intellectual property, taxation, and compensation. At Niural, we’re building the financial and workforce infrastructure for this emerging economy, so AI companies can focus on advancing their models rather than figuring out how to engage and pay the humans making that progress possible.”

Powering the Next Composition of the Workforce
The launch marks a core milestone in Niural AI’s broader product strategy. Informed by research from Niural AI Labs, the platform is architecting the governance layer for a new generation of work, extending beyond traditional employees and contractors to specialized human experts powering AI post-training and autonomous AI agents.

The Expert Verifier capabilities are available immediately to all Niural AI enterprise clients worldwide. To learn more or request platform access, visit 
niural.com
.

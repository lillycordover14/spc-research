Powered by Semos IQ, Semos Cloud's AI agents work through Joule to bring people insights and recommended actions into the SAP experience customers already use
BERLIN
, 
Oct. 27, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- Semos Cloud, the People and Culture Intelligence company, today announced that it has qualified as a winner of the partner agent race to SAP TechEd 2026, having presented 17 use cases. The initiative recognizes customer-adopted AI agents built on SAP Business AI Platform that have successfully completed SAP's validation.

The recognition marks an important milestone in Semos Cloud's work to bring agentic AI into everyday workforce decisions. Organizations running SAP can explore these capabilities with access through Joule and intelligence grounded in their SAP environment.
"SAP Business AI Platform gives us the foundation to bring a new level of intelligence to people and culture, and this recognition reflects that capability at work for customers. Our agents turn workforce signals into the decisions that shape how organizations lead, develop, and reward their people, delivering proactive, secure, and context-aware intelligence into the workflows our customers already rely on," said 
Filip Misovski
, CEO of Semos Cloud.
Turn people intelligence into action with AI agents
Semos Cloud's portfolio includes more than 20 pro-code, domain-specific AI agents. 
Semos IQ
, the platform's intelligence layer, powers the agents by connecting recognition, rewards, surveys, feedback and communications with 
SAP SuccessFactors
 context. That context gives agents the information they need to interpret what is happening across an organization and recommend where to act.
Through Joule, employees, managers and HR can interact with the agents in natural language. For example, a manager can explore team culture insights and recommended next steps, while an employee can recognize a colleague. HR teams can examine patterns across teams and regions to understand where attention is needed and prioritize their response. The agents operate within the identity and governance controls of an organization's SAP environment. The organization retains control over what the agents can access and which actions they are permitted to take.
The agents are featured in the 
partner AI catalog on SAP Discovery Center
.
From SaaS to an enterprise-ready agent platform
The development of Semos Cloud's agent platform will also be explored at SAP TechEd. On October 27 at 12:00 PM CET, Filip Misovski joins Steffen Pietsch of SAP for the XP114 session, "
Build your agent strategy: From use case to enterprise-ready AI platform
." The session will show how Semos Cloud evolved from a SaaS platform into an enterprise-ready agent platform on SAP Business AI Platform.
Serving enterprise customers across industries
Semos Cloud's AI agents are in production in customer environments, extending the company's work in enterprise employee programs. Semos Cloud's broader platform serves over 150 customers in 25+ industries and 170+ countries, supporting more than 2 million users.
Among those customers is SAP, where SAP Appreciate and SAP CommsHub support recognition and communications for over 100,000 employees and recorded upwards of 500,000 recognitions in 2025. Further examples of how organizations use Semos Cloud are available in the 
Customers Hub
.
About Semos Cloud
Semos Cloud is a People and Culture Intelligence company. Its platform unifies recognition and rewards, total rewards, employee communications, listening and talent development for global enterprises, built natively on SAP BTP. Learn more at 
semoscloud.com
.

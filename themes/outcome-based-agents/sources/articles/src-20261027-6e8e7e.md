Greensky Bluegrass Sets Winter Tour With Magoo, Eggy, Clay Street Unit & Miles Miller
The 24-show trek runs from Los Angeles in January to Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium in March.
 
By 
Scott Bernstein
 
Oct 27, 2026 
•
 7:46 am PDT
 
 
Photo by Peter Fisher
 
 
 
Share This
 
 

Greensky Bluegrass
 will spend portions of January, February and March on the road for a 24-date Winter Tour 2027. The three-leg trek opens January 14 in Los Angeles and concludes March 19 at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium, with 
Magoo
, 
Eggy
, 
Clay Street Unit
 and 
Miles Miller
 aboard as support on select dates.

The January leg begins at The Bellwether in Los Angeles before making additional California stops in Del Mar, Oakland, San Luis Obispo and Monterey. Greensky then heads to Crystal Bay, Nevada and Salt Lake City ahead of a January 23 performance at Denver’s Mission Ballroom. Magoo opens six of the eight January shows, with Eggy joining the bill in Denver.

Clay Street Unit will support the entirety of the nine-show February leg, which launches February 10 at Cleveland’s Globe Iron. Stops include Pittsburgh, Philadelphia, Washington D.C. and Charlottesville (Virginia) before Greensky plays New York City’s Webster Hall on February 18. The band then heads to Portland (Maine) and Boston before wrapping the leg February 21 at the Sherman Theater in Stroudsburg, Pennsylvania.

Miles Miller takes over opening duties for the final seven dates, beginning March 10 at Val Air Ballroom in West Des Moines, Iowa. The March itinerary also includes Omaha, St. Paul, Milwaukee, Shipshewana (Indiana) and St. Louis before the Ryman finale.

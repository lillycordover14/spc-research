Fellow pop icon Taylor Swift would be proud.

Drake celebrated his 40th birthday on Saturday, and marked the milestone with perhaps the world’s fanciest friendship bracelet: a “one of one” diamond-encrusted creation designed by Alessandro Bernini of Bernini High Jewelry and Domenico Formichetti.

The custom piece spells out “Adonis” on one side and “Iceman” on the other, honoring both the superstar’s 9-year-old son and his most recent album.

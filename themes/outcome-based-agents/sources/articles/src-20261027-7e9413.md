SIMPLIFYING THE START OF PROCUREMENT
Pactum has announced the launch of its Intake Agent, an artificial intelligence (AI) tool that lets employees request goods and services in plain language, replacing complicated forms and repeated follow-ups with a streamlined process.
Available now through Microsoft Teams, Coupa, and SAP Ariba, the agent converts employee requests into complete, correctly coded requisitions and checks each against company purchasing policies before submission.
By connecting with Pactum’s wider portfolio of AI agents, it also lets businesses move from an initial purchase request through sourcing and negotiation, with human buyers available to manage exceptions that require their judgement.
The launch extends Pactum’s growing range of AI agents to the beginning of indirect procurement, covering purchases such as software, office equipment, and professional services.
The approach lets procurement teams set purchasing rules once, with the agent applying them consistently to every request, regardless of value.
“Procurement teams can now run indirect procurement with full autonomy while remaining in control and managing the exceptions,” says 
Kaspar Korjus
, Co-Founder and CEO of 
Pactum
.
“Our Intake Agent helps resolve questions earlier so requests can move forward with less back-and-forth and teams can focus on the decisions that need their expertise.”
FROM COMPLEX FORMS TO AUTONOMOUS EXECUTION
For employees, identifying an approved supplier or choosing the correct accounting code can require knowledge spanning procurement, finance, operations, and IT.
Incomplete or inaccurate information can lead to rejected requisitions and repeated follow-ups, leaving buyers to spend time correcting forms instead of building supplier relationships or making commercial decisions.
These administrative hurdles can also cause review queues to last anywhere from days to weeks.
Pactum Intake addresses these challenges by letting employees describe what the business needs, regardless of how complex the goods or services are. Employees can also provide supporting documents, including supplier quotations, to help the agent complete the request.
Using the information supplied, the agent identifies the appropriate procurement category and fills in relevant accounting and supplier details. Instead of requiring employees to navigate lengthy forms, it asks only the follow-up questions needed to complete the requisition.
Once checked, the request can be passed to another Pactum digital worker, which autonomously sources suppliers and negotiates improved pricing and terms where required.
CONNECTING AI AGENTS ACROSS PROCUREMENT
The Intake Agent builds on Pactum’s existing work in procurement policy enforcement. Its Requisition Alignment Agent has surpassed one million requisition checks, validating purchase requests against company buying policies, existing contracts, and approved suppliers.
By connecting this capability to employee requests and subsequent sourcing and negotiation activities, Pactum is advancing its vision of AI-agent-managed procurement from the first request through to the final purchase.
Kristjan Korjus
, Co-Founder and Chief Scientist at 
Pactum
, highlights the difference between software that assists procurement professionals and technology that can execute an entire process independently.
“Building software that assists procurement professionals is relatively easy. Building software that runs the whole process on its own, from the first request to the final purchase, is much harder,” he says.
He compares the difference to driver assistance versus a car operating without anyone behind the wheel, arguing that many procurement AI tools still require buyers to oversee every request. Pactum’s agents, by contrast, are designed to carry requests through to purchase, transferring work between agents as needed.
Founded in 2019, Pactum works with more than 60 of the world’s largest enterprises. Its AI agents check requests against company policies, contracts, and approved suppliers, negotiate with suppliers in more than 40 languages, and operate within Coupa and SAP Ariba.
With the launch of Intake, the company is bringing autonomous execution further upstream, giving employees a simpler way to initiate purchases while helping procurement teams focus their expertise where it matters most.

NEW YORK, October 27, 2026
--(
BUSINESS WIRE
)--
Rillet
, the agentic ERP for modern enterprise finance teams, today unveiled a new brand identity and a suite of powerful new capabilities built around zero-day close. Fresh from being named a 2026 
Gartner® Cool Vendor
 in AI-Native Finance ERP, Rillet continues to push the boundaries of what's possible for the modern finance team. The capabilities announced today underscore Rillet's vision to become the operating layer for enterprise finance, empowering every organization to replace the month-end sprint with finance that runs in real time.
 
 
 
 
 
Rillet's new visual identity went live this month across Manhattan subway stations and at Las Vegas airport, where it speaks directly to the CFOs and finance leaders traveling between the industry's biggest events. Red Antler—whose past work includes Ramp, McKinsey, and Farther—built the identity around a singular vision: show finance leaders that speed and rigor can coexist. The result is a custom wordmark with a redrawn "R," ABC ROM as the primary typeface, a purple-led palette, and a "streams" visual texture that references the continuous data flows underpinning real-time accounting. Rillet is among the first enterprise finance companies to bring this level of craft to B2B infrastructure software.
 
 
"For a long time, the ERP was a place to store what already happened," said 
Nicolas Kopp
, CEO and founder of Rillet. "Our customers want a ledger that works while the month is still open—with agents handling the routine work and humans orchestrating and approving."
 
 
 
 
 
In addition to the brand, Rillet today marks three defining capabilities now available to enterprise finance teams:
 
 
 
Zero-day close.
 Rillet's Continuous Close architecture posts 99.7% of transactions automatically, running checklists, reconciliations, and flux drafts throughout the month on a live general ledger. By the time the period closes, the books are already reviewed.
 
 
 
Agents under accountant oversight.
 Aura, Rillet's AI agent layer, investigates discrepancies, surfaces anomalies, and drafts journal entries for human approval. Finance teams stay in control of every material decision, with a complete audit trail of everything Aura does.
 
 
 
Built for the full enterprise stack.
 Rillet supports private and public companies in the middle market, including tech, healthcare, logistics, sports franchises, defense, manufacturing and more. Rillet customers are growing to billions in revenue with very lean finance teams, completely rebuilding what is capable for modern companies. 40% of Rillet customers come from outside tech and 47% migrated from an Enterprise ERP like SAP, Workday, NetSuite or Oracle Fusion.

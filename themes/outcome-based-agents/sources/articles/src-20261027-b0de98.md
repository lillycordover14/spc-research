GOSHEN — The county emergency management office hopes to receive more than $130,000 in grants for safety training, salaries and other needs.
The Elkhart County Board of Commissioners on Monday approved four grant applications on behalf of the county Emergency Management Agency. The grants requested by Director Jennifer Tobey would total $136,900 if all are received.

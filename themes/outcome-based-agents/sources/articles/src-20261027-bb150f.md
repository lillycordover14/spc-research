Local Artisan Showcase

November 12 @ 5:00 pm
 - 
8:00 pm
 

New Bedford Fishing Heritage Center will host a Local Artisan Showcase Thursday, Nov. 12 from 5 to 8 p.m.

The holiday shopping event will feature fishing- and maritime-themed clothing, jewelry, artwork, and handmade goods. Members of the local fishing community create many items.

Admission is free as part of 
AHA! Night

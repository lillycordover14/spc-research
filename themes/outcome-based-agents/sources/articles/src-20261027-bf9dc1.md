FARGO — United Way of Cass-Clay has announced the 35 women selected to participate in the 2027 35 Under 35 Women’s Leadership Program. The program brings together women from across the Cass-Clay community to grow their leadership skills, form connections with fellow women leaders and discover how they can use their strengths and influence to create positive change.
Through six full-day sessions led by local leaders and community voices from January through June, participants will explore topics including goal setting, communication, personal growth, board leadership, public speaking, conflict management and other leadership-focused topics. 

ADVERTISEMENT

Participants will also gain a deeper understanding of community needs and challenges, and the many ways they can use their leadership to make a difference. 
The program gives participants the opportunity to grow as leaders while building relationships and a stronger connection to the community around them. Participants leave with new skills, perspectives and connections they can bring back to their workplaces, families and communities.

These 35 women represent a diverse range of industries, experiences, and perspectives, all united by their shared commitment to leadership, collaboration, and community impact.
The 2027 program marks the 18th year of this leadership development experience. Since its launch in 2009, the program has empowered nearly 600 local women to grow as leaders and changemakers.
Applications for the 2028 United Way 35 Under 35 Women’s Leadership Program will open in the fall of 2027.

Bernini High Jewelry and Domenico Formichetti have created a one-of-one diamond bracelet gifted to Drake for his 40th birthday

Handcrafted in Italy from white gold the piece is set with natural gemstones and exceptional F-color VS clarity diamonds

The custom jewelry is designed to match the rapper's one-of-one $5 million USD ICEMAN Rolex Daytona

Bernini High Jewelry
 and Italian designer 
Domenico Formichetti
 have unveiled the “
ICEMAN
 & 
ADONIS
” one-of-one diamond bracelet, custom-created as a 40th birthday gift for 
Drake
.

Tailor-made in Italy, the bespoke piece is forged from white gold and encrusted with hand-selected natural gemstones alongside exceptional F-color, VS clarity diamonds. Introduced under the collaborative banner “FREEZE THE WORLD,” the design marries Formichetti’s streetwear sensibility with Bernini’s rigorous high-jewelry craftsmanship.

The one-off piece was conceived specifically to complement Drake’s 
custom one-of-one “ICEMAN” Rolex Cosmograph Daytona
. Valued at an estimated $5 million USD, the rare timepiece features a signature “Ice Gradient” bezel, a pavé diamond dial, and a commemorative “FREEZE THE WORLD” caseback engraving referencing the artist’s ICEMAN era. Bernini and Formichetti’s matching bracelet echoes the icy visual theme of the watch, aligning identical gem-setting standards to create a cohesive high-jewelry pairing.

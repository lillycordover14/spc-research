In Loving Memory of Gerald Otis Crosby
November 11, 1974 – October 27, 2026
Gerald Otis Crosby, age 51, passed away on October 27, 2026, leaving behind a family and many loved ones who will forever cherish his memory.
Born on November 11, 1974, Gerald was a kind and gentle soul whose presence meant so much to those who knew and loved him. He was an Eagle Scout, a member of a bowling league, and a devoted fan of football. He especially enjoyed listening to music, with Rush holding a special place among his favorites. These interests were a part of who he was, and the memories made through them will continue to bring comfort. He will be deeply missed, but never forgotten.
Gerald was preceded in death by his father, Charles Hoyt Crosby; his mother, Linda Sue Crosby; his grandfather, Gerald Hallman; and his grandmothers, Dorris Hallman and Rosa Crosby.
He leaves behind his loving wife, Christina Crosby; his daughter, Alexandria Crosby; and his beloved granddaughter, Sophia. He is also survived by his brother, Charles Crosby, and sister-in-law, Joy Crosby; his sister, Susan Brantley, and brother-in-law, Joe Brantley; his nephew, Eli Crosby; and his nieces, Lauren Crosby, Erin, Amber, Angela, and Emma Brantley. He also leaves behind numerous extended family members and loved ones who will continue to hold him close in their hearts.

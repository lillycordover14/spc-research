Deadlines

In April 2026, the Department of Justice (DOJ) issued an interim final rule extending the compliance deadlines for the 2024 Title II Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) web accessibility regulations as they apply to State and local government entities. The first deadline applies to entities with populations of 50,000 or more, the second deadline applies to entities with a population less than 50,000. Under the interim final rule:

Entities with populations greater than 50,000 must be in compliance by April 26, 2027.

Entities with populations less than 50,000 must be in compliance by April 26, 2028.

When creating a digital accessibility management plan, an organization should consider:

Conducting an audit of websites, apps, and digital instructional materials

Coordinating multiple teams (e.g. Information Technology, Legal, Human Resources, Instructional Leadership, Communications)

Ensuring third-party technology-related service providers have accessibility commitments

Establishing a review process for online content and new digital tools

Providing staff training on accessible content creation

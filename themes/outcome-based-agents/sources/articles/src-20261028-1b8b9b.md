Artisan Mill Co., which has two vendor marketplaces in Lancaster County, has opened a third in Manheim.
The new location occupies a spot in the REO Manheim Marketplace at 49-51 N. Main St., where 140 vendors sell a wide variety of goods, including handcrafted creations, home decor, jewelry and food.

The new Artisan Mill Co. occupies an 11,000-square-foot space in a Manheim complex that includes Meduseld Meadery, Mill 72 Bake Shop & Cafe and Twisted Sisters Ice Cream.

Artisan Mill Co. began in 2020
 with the opening of a vendor marketplace inside a circa-1881 mill at 813 Rothsville Road, just east of Lititz. In May 2025, it opened a 
second location in the Shops at Rockvale in East Lampeter Township.
Artisan Mill Co. is owned by Ian and Ashley Gantz, who grew up in Manheim and graduated from Manheim Central High School.

Raiders Firm on their Asking Price for Crosby&nbsp;&nbsp;Raiders Beat

[page text]
With the Raiders riding a four-game losing streak, Maxx Crosby trade rumors have picked up this week, and according to 
league insider Jason La Canfora, 
there is a belief around the league that Crosby is still attainable for the right price.

The right price, it seems, is the price the Ravens agreed to in March and ultimately walked away from. 

“I don’t think they take less than [two first-round picks],”La Canfora said an NFL GM told him this week. “I’m not sure someone offers that; that was a reach by Baltimore … But he’s still a dominant player. The knee isn’t holding him back.”

The NFL’s trade deadline isn’t for another 14 days, but the Colts have already kicked off their fire sale, and the Raiders could get more serious about listening to offers for Crosby if they lose to the Jets on Sunday. 

As many have pointed out, 
ESPN’s Adam Schefter
 downplayed trade rumors around Crosby this week, but he was also blindsided by the Myles Garrett trade.

Not all trade negotiations are public, although Schefter should have good sources in the Raiders’ building with a couple of Michigan men in charge. 

With that in mind, the best message for John Spytek to put out from the GM’s chair is that Crosby is going to be a part of the future in Las Vegas, whether that happens to be the case or not. 

Remember, Spytek is a master of 
feeding bad information
 to the media. 

Dallas the popular trade destination for Crosby

It’s no secret that the Cowboys would love to find a way to get Crosby, but if they aren’t willing to offer two first-picks, would the Raiders be interested in a player like George Pickens?

That scenario has been conversation piece in recent days, but the belief at this time is that the Raiders would not be interested in Pickens.

Pickens is one of the most talented wide receivers in the league, but he brings a questionable dynamic to the locker room in terms of character and will be a free agent after the 2026 season.

It’s hard to imagine the Raiders would be interested in sending Crosby to Dallas in exchange for a complicated contract negotiation with Pickens. 

Will the Raiders eventually takes less for Crosby? 

The consensus seems to be that Crosby isn’t worth a pair of first-round picks, and if no one is willing to pay that price, it will be interesting to see if the Raiders are willing to settle for less. 

Without question, the message coming out of the Raiders’ building is that they aren’t going to budge, but deadlines spurn action and by November 10 we’re going to find out how firm they are in that position.

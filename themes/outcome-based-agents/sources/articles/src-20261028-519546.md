The Key West Artisan Market returns Sunday, Nov. 1, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at Indigenous Park at Higgs Beach, 1801 White Street, with the Welcome Race Fans! Power Boat Edition.
As Key West prepares for the excitement of the annual power boat racing championships, locals and visitors are invited to enjoy a waterfront shopping experience celebrating the island’s creative community, local flavors and welcoming spirit.
The Key West Artisan Market is a unique open-air showcase devoted to the people who live, work and create in the Florida Keys, giving shoppers an opportunity to buy directly from the artists, craftspeople, growers, chefs and entrepreneurs. Participants must live in the Keys and create or curate locally to qualify, keeping the focus authentically Keys-made. Each Sunday edition features a different theme and highlights one or more local nonprofit organizations or events, combining a vibrant shopping experience with community connection.
Guests will find fine crafts and art, elevated food and libations, and sweeping ocean views. The market offers an inspired mix of handcrafted jewelry, original artwork spanning acrylics, watercolor, papercuts, ceramics and turned wood, bespoke home décor, carefully selected apparel and textiles, artisan breads and cheeses, fresh seasonal produce, slow-smoked barbecue, indulgent desserts, and authentic global flavors from the Mediterranean, France, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia and the Caribbean.
Great Events Catering offers a full-service bar featuring handcrafted, next-level Bloody Marys and mimosas, while guests may also enjoy fresh-brewed coffee, nitrogen-infused teas, pressed fruit juices and tropically flavored boozy popsicles.
The Welcome Race Fans! edition offers visitors arriving for race week an opportunity to discover the creative side of Key West before the high-speed action begins. With original island artwork, handcrafted gifts, locally produced foods and an easygoing atmosphere by the beach, the Artisan Market is a chance to experience the community beyond the race course.

Keys Yacht Rock Station 93.7 will broadcast live from the Community Entertainment Pavilion throughout the day, sharing island tunes, interviews and community highlights between performances by local musicians. Guests can also meet local authors signing books, watch artists at work and explore the newly remodeled Key West Wildlife Center located onsite at Indigenous Park.
The Nov. 1 market coincides with the opening day of the Race World Offshore Key West World Championship, which runs November 1–8. The first race day is Wednesday, November 4, followed by racing Friday, November 6, and Sunday, November 8. The First State Bank World’s Fastest Boat Parade is scheduled for Sunday afternoon, November 1, at 4 pm on Duval Street, making the Artisan Market a convenient morning stop before the festivities downtown.
Admission and parking are free. Guests are encouraged to ride bikes when possible and BYOB — bring your own shopping bags or pick up a branded bag at the Artisan Market info tent.
The next Artisan Market will be Sunday, Nov. 15 with the Charting the Course Edition featuring the Key West Sailing Center. Looking ahead, the Black Friday Shopping Extravaganza returns Friday, November 27, from 10 am to 6 pm at Truman Waterfront Park, offering an expanded holiday shopping experience featuring local artists, makers and small businesses.

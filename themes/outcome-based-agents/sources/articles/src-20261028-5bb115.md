Jaguar Health Approves Sixfold Increase to 2014 Plan Share Reserve
Less than 1 min read
JAGX
Jaguar Health approved a sixfold adjustment to its 2014 Stock Incentive Plan share reserve to account for its Series R preferred stock dividend.
Key Highlights:
The adjustment will take effect immediately after the November 6 special meeting and is intended to preserve the plan’s approximately 14% share allocation.
If stockholders approve Proposal 3, the reserve will be 2,273,154 shares, including the proposed increase.
If Proposal 3 is not approved, the adjusted reserve will be 4,260 shares.
In either case, the maximum shares issuable through incentive stock options will be 163,998.

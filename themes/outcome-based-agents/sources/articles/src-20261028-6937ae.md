Rillet Unveils New Brand Identity

The new brand features a custom wordmark with a redrawn “R,” ABC ROM as the primary typeface, a purple-led palette, and a “streams” visual texture.

Agentic ERP platform Rillet rolled out a new brand identity on Oct. 27.

New York City-based branding agency Red Antler—whose past work includes Ramp, McKinsey, and Farther—built the new visual identity to show finance leaders that speed and rigor can coexist, Rillet says. 

The result is a custom wordmark with a redrawn “R,” ABC ROM as the primary typeface, a purple-led palette, and a “streams” visual texture that references the continuous data flows underpinning real-time accounting, the company added.

“For a long time, the ERP was a place to store what already happened,” Nicolas Kopp, CEO and founder of Rillet, said in a statement. “Our customers want a ledger that works while the month is still open—with agents handling the routine work and humans orchestrating and approving.”

In addition to the rebranding, Rillet touted three capabilities now available to enterprise finance teams:

Zero-Day Close:
 Rillet’s Continuous Close architecture posts 99.7% of transactions automatically, running checklists, reconciliations, and flux drafts throughout the month on a live general ledger. By the time the period closes, the books are already reviewed.

Agents under accountant oversight:
 Aura, Rillet’s AI agent layer, investigates discrepancies, surfaces anomalies, and drafts journal entries for human approval. Finance teams stay in control of every material decision, with a complete audit trail of everything Aura does, Rillet says.

Built for the full enterprise stack:
 Rillet supports private and public companies in the middle market, including tech, healthcare, logistics, sports franchises, defense, manufacturing, and more.

“Our goal with the rebrand was to show the world who Rillet already is: the home of the Zero-Day Close, built for the finance teams redefining what the future looks like. We’re incredibly proud to share this,” Gerard Zengel, senior brand designer at Rillet, said in a statement. “Rillet is built by accountants, for accountants—and the new identity had to reflect the precision underneath.”

Brendan Morrow, USA TODAY
Wed, October 28, 2026 at 3:15 PM UTC
11
Christina Applegate
 is sharing a candid update about her health after revealing she was 
misdiagnosed with multiple sclerosis
.
On the Oct. 27 episode of 
her "MeSsy" podcast
, the "Dead to Me" star, 54, told listeners things have been "getting worse" with her health and that she is having difficulty getting out of bed in the morning. 
"I actually timed it today: It took me 10 minutes to be able to move off my pillow and get out of the bed and stand up, at which point, I almost couldn't walk to the bathroom, and now, a walker is sitting next to my bed because of what's going on," she said. "I don't know what's going on."
Applegate said this is a shift from what she was previously experiencing.
"This is what I'm still living with," she said. "And I never had that before. I was able to sit up and stuff. Things are getting worse, or they're just new. Something new is happening. It took 10 minutes just to sit my head up, just to move without excruciating pain and weakness."
Christina Applegate 
reveals she was misdiagnosed with multiple sclerosis
Applegate also said she "recently lost all this weight," which is "kind of a bad" thing. "I actually need to gain some," she said. "…I lost too much just recently, and I don't know what's going on."
Applegate's health update came after she announced on Oct. 20 that 
she does not have multiple sclerosis
, despite being diagnosed with the autoimmune disease in 2021. Her doctor recently told her she had been misdiagnosed.

What are Christina Applegate's symptoms?
Applegate does not yet have a new diagnosis. She previously explained that she still has the same symptoms, including fatigue and pain, but no longer has an explanation for them, so she feels like she is "back at square one."
What Christina Applegate 
revealed about severe symptoms before MS misdiagnosis
The Emmy winner has been open about her health over the past five years since receiving her original MS diagnosis. In 2024, she launched the "MeSsy" podcast with actress Jamie-Lynn Sigler, who has been diagnosed with MS.
In a 
2025 episode of the podcast
, Applegate said she has been in the hospital "upwards of 30 times" because of her symptoms that were previously thought to be due to MS. "They've done every test known to man on me, put so much radiation into my body from CT scans to everything else," she said.
Applegate and Sigler plan to continue their "MeSsy" podcast despite the former's misdiagnosis. On their Oct. 27 episode, Sigler said the show is "about the messiness of life in general" and was not "meant to be solely MS focused," even though this was the initial "reason for us speaking candidly with each other."
Applegate cried on the newest episode after Sigler told her she is in the "great fight" of her life and is "worthy of so much more possibility and love than you're giving yourself credit for." 
"Having to stop working is where all of this hit me like a train," an emotional Applegate said. "I spent the greater part of my life at work, and I wasn't dealing with anything. I didn't have to. I had work. It was my playground, and it was my safe space. And not having it, and sitting here for all these years not being well, all of it has come crashing down inside."
Applegate retired from acting after the end of her Netflix series "Dead to Me." In 2023, she told 
the Los Angeles Times
 that she "couldn't imagine getting up at 5 a.m. and spending 12 to 14 hours on a set; I don’t have that in me at this moment."

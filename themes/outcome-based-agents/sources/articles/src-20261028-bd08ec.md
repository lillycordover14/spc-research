Saviynt
 announced that it has been recognized as a 
Challenger in the
 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™
 for 
Privileged Access Management
 (PAM), marking the third consecutive year of recognition in the Gartner PAM research. We believe Saviynt’s recognition in 2026 is evidence of its continued investment.  
The recognition comes as the definition of privileged access continues to expand. Cloud workloads, automation, non-human identities and AI agents all require elevated access, creating a need for organizations to govern and secure privilege beyond traditional administrator accounts while reducing persistent access. 
“Privilege is no longer limited to a defined group of administrators. As AI agents and machine identities become mainstream in enterprises, organizations need to understand the context of why access is needed, how long it should be granted, and who is accountable for it,” said Anirudh Sen, Senior Vice President of Products at Saviynt. “We believe our repeated recognition in the Gartner PAM research reflects our commitment to securing the full Privilege Spectrum through a unified identity security platform that brings identity governance and privileged access together.” 
Solving privilege challenges in the AI era requires a new approach with market-leading identity lifecycle management. Saviynt believes that a modern PAM solution must combine identity governance, privilege lifecycle management, lifecycle automation, and time-bound, just-in-time access. A unified platform that brings IGA and PAM together can simplify administration, deliver a more seamless customer experience, and help organizations secure access at the pace of AI adoption.   
Built on Zero Trust principles, Saviynt takes a continuous, risk-based approach to privilege management. By advancing 
Zero Standing Privilege
 (ZSP), Saviynt helps organizations minimize persistent elevated access and ensure privileges are granted only when justified by context and risk. Its 
Privilege Spectrum
 framework enables organizations to apply the right level of access at the right time while maintaining security and productivity. 
To learn more about Saviynt’s approach to Privileged Access Management, visit 
Saviynt PAM webpage

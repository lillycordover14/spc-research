Wed, October 28, 2026 at 2:05 AM UTC
0
 (Courtesy Cincinnati Zoo)
First birthdays usually involve balloons, an unnecessarily elaborate cake, and a guest of honor who has absolutely no idea why everyone’s making such a fuss. At Cincinnati Zoo, the celebration looks a little different. The birthday boy has spots, enormous ears, and a swimming pool that appears to be the main attraction.
Teddy the serval recently turned 1, and a 
Facebook video shared by Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden
 shows him investigating a bright blue kiddie pool as water pours in from above. Forget the party hats. Teddy seems perfectly happy with the aquatic portion of the festivities, although his expression suggests he’s still evaluating the arrangements.

The video shows Teddy standing in the shallow pool, reaching toward the stream and inspecting the ripples beneath his paws. With his long legs, oversized ears, and beautifully spotted coat, he looks like someone took an ordinary house cat and stretched the proportions considerably.
Servals are wild cats native to Africa, known for their impressive hearing and ability to leap after prey. Their unusually long legs help them navigate tall grass, while their enormous ears let them detect small animals nearby.
A Birthday Splash for Teddy
While most domestic cats have a reputation for avoiding water, servals hunt around wetlands and may enter shallow water while searching for prey. Teddy’s interest in his little pool isn’t quite as unusual as it might seem.
Still, watching a spotted cat investigate a kiddie pool is a great reminder that even the most impressive predators can appreciate a fairly ordinary backyard accessory.

“A Cat That Goes in Water”
The comments are having plenty of fun with Teddy’s birthday celebration.
Jamie Owens wished him a happy birthday, then joked that he might be splashing around looking for a tuna-flavored birthday cake.
Genie Roper seemed particularly impressed by the aquatic festivities, writing, “A cat that goes in water,” followed by a cheerful birthday message. Meanwhile, Karen Stoddard suggested Teddy might need a fish in his pool to practice his fishing skills. The viewers clearly have some ideas for next year’s party.
Teddy may not understand birthdays, candles, or why humans insist on documenting every milestone, but he certainly seems interested in the water. One year old, with a pool of his own, and no obligation to share it with anyone.
I’d say he’s doing rather well.

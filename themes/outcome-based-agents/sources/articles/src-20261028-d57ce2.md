[Key Issue Briefing]
■ Auction market reversal: In Seoul's apartment auction market, the share of winning bids at or above appraised value in Nowon District climbed to 48.4% in the January-September period, a 6.3-fold increase from a year earlier. Gangnam District, by contrast, saw above-appraisal winning bids shrink by more than half, to 14 from 29, as concerns over the tax burden following the tax reform plan curbed buying interest in high-priced apartments and pushed demand toward lower- and mid-priced outlying areas.
■ Capital-provincial divergence: Land prices in Seoul rose 1.26% in the third quarter of this year, while provincial areas gained just 0.14%, widening the gap with the capital region to 0.75 percentage points. Yongsan District posted the nation's highest increase at 2.24%, symbolizing the divergence, while Jeju Special Self-Governing Province (-0.28%) and South Gyeongsang Province (-0.07%) declined, underscoring the widening regional divide.
■ Internet banks complete mortgage lineup: Toss Bank launched a mortgage product with rates starting at 4.44% a year, completing the competitive lineup among the country's three internet-only banks alongside KakaoBank and K Bank. The product covers low-rise multi-unit housing as well as apartments, offering up to 1 billion won (about $700,000 at 1,430 won per dollar) over terms of as long as 40 years, with the entire loan process handled remotely.
[Top News for Real Estate Investors]
1. Nowon 'Premium Bids' Jump Sixfold in a Year While Gangnam Stalls
Key points: Of 62 apartment auctions won in Nowon District from January to September this year, 30, or 48.4%, closed at or above the appraised value, up 6.3 times from 7.7% a year earlier. In the third quarter, roughly six of every 10 successful bids found new owners at prices above appraisal. The Miryung, Miseong and Samho apartment complex in Wolgye-dong sold for 938 million won, 242 million won above its appraised value, for a winning bid ratio of 134.8%, while a 58-square-meter unit in Sanggye Jugong Complex 6 went for 813 million won, 167 million won above appraisal. In Gangnam District, by contrast, above-appraisal winning bids fell by more than half to 14 from 29, and Seocho District saw a drop to four from 10. Since the announcement of the tax reform plan, buying interest in ultra-high-priced apartments has weakened, while outlying districts with a high share of lower- and mid-priced homes have remained strong.
2. Seoul Land Prices Climb 1.26% While Provinces Manage Only 0.14%
Key points: Seoul land prices rose 1.26% in the third quarter, accelerating from 1.21% in the second quarter, according to third-quarter 2026 land price data released by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport and the Korea Real Estate Board. Yongsan District ranked first among the nation's 256 cities, counties and districts with a 2.24% gain, followed by Seongdong District at 2.03% and Seongbuk District at 1.69%. Provincial areas rose just 0.14%, widening the gap with the capital region's 0.89% to 0.75 percentage points. Jeju Special Self-Governing Province fell 0.28% and South Gyeongsang Province declined 0.07%. Nationwide land transactions totaled 408,000 lots in the third quarter, down 15.6% from the previous quarter, while Seoul recorded 51,007 lots, a 21.7% plunge that highlighted the contraction in trading.
3. Toss Bank Enters Mortgage Market With Rates From 4.44%
Key points: Toss Bank launched a fully remote mortgage product covering low-rise multi-unit housing as well as apartments. The rate starts at 4.44% a year as of that day, with a ceiling of 1 billion won, or up to 600 million won for purchases in the capital region and regulated zones. Maturities run as long as 40 years, with borrowers able to choose between equal principal-and-interest or equal principal repayment. With the launch, all three internet-only banks now offer mortgages, following KakaoBank and K Bank. Non-homeowners considering a move from jeonse (a lump-sum deposit lease) to owning, as well as single-homeowners planning to move, can now complete the entire loan process without visiting a branch.
[Additional News for Real Estate Investors]
4. Yeouido Gongjak Rebuilding Sets Itself Apart With 'Rest' Theme
Key points: The Gongjak apartment complex in Yeouido, which has secured project implementation approval, will be transformed from 373 units into 585 units rising as high as 49 stories, with Daewoo Engineering & Construction as contractor. For construction costs, the association and the contractor agreed to apply the lower of consumer price inflation or the rise in construction cost indexes to the existing base of 10.7 million won per 3.3 square meters. Appraisals are to be completed by the end of next month, with applications from owners to be accepted from mid-December through mid-January. The complex targets relocation in the fourth quarter of next year, groundbreaking in the fourth quarter of 2028 and move-in in the fourth quarter of 2033.
5. Revenue Falls but Operating Profit Triples at Daewoo E&C
Key points: Daewoo Engineering & Construction posted third-quarter revenue of 1.84 trillion won, down 7.4% from a year earlier, while operating profit surged 223.7% to 183.2 billion won. Its cumulative operating margin for the January-September period was 11.5%, up 6.9 percentage points from 4.6% in the same period last year. New orders for the first nine months totaled 12.48 trillion won, up 11.9% from a year earlier, and its order backlog stood at 54.86 trillion won, equivalent to about 6.8 years of annual revenue.
6. GDP Grows Surprising 1% in Third Quarter as Consumption Falls First Time in Four Years
Key points: Real gross domestic product grew 1.0% from the previous quarter in the third quarter, according to the Bank of Korea, more than triple the central bank's August forecast of 0.3%. Semiconductor exports rose 2.3% from the prior quarter, driving the expansion. Private consumption, meanwhile, fell 0.2%, its first retreat since the fourth quarter of 2022. With surprise growth extending for a third straight quarter, attention has turned to the Bank of Korea's November rate decision, and analysts said the strong economic report card reduces the likelihood of a delay in rate increases.

LIMA (WLIO) - Columbus Grove volleyball advanced with a straight-set win over Ada, 25-16, 25-12 and 25-18.
Lydia Sarka and Aubrey Schroeder teamed up for an early block to give the Bulldogs a 2-0 lead in the opening set. Ada answered with a block from Dailah Preston and a quick point from Jaycee Allen, but Columbus Grove regained control.
Schroeder added a clean kill just inside the line, while Sarka finished another point with power as the Bulldogs completed the sweep. Columbus Grove will face Parkway on Thursday.

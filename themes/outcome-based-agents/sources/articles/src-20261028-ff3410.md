Now what?

Kolsky said enterprises need to invest in six areas: Business value, data readiness, infrastructure readiness, governance, economics and talent. 

Business value.
 Kolsky said CxOs need to drop the fascination with returns on investment. "If you are looking for ROI in AI, you're never going to find it," said Kolsky, who noted that value follows a J-curve and shows up around year two or three. "You're going to make an investment for five to 10 years."

Data readiness.
 Agents need to get the data they need and enterprises need to prod openness among vendors. "Some vendors are actually restricting the access to the data of agents," said Kolsky. 

Infrastructure will take most of your budget.
 Infrastructure will fall under cloud, on-premises and all of those systems you've neglected since the Covid-19 pandemic. 

Governance is all talk so far.
 "Nobody's doing it. We're talking about it," said Kolsky. 

Token- and outcome-based pricing won't work.
 "No CFO is going to buy into tokens or outcome-driven consumption," said Kolsky. 

Talent matters, but is neglected

Kolsky said talent matters and it's the most neglected budget category. 

Here's why it makes sense to focus on talent:

People handle the exceptions that agents can't. "The only way you can handle exceptions is with experienced operators that know what they're doing," said Kolsky. 

Layoffs are throwing away company knowledge. Kolsky said enterprises have been "laying off the knowledge of the company" that will be needed to build models. 

Training on prompts isn't a talent strategy.

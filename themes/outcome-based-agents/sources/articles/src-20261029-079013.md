EMA Offices to Close for All Souls’ Day on November 2

by GOAI

Share To

 

The European Medicines Agency (EMA) will close its offices on Monday, November 2, in observance of All Souls’ Day. Operations will resume at 08:30 on Tuesday, November 3.

While the agency’s administrative offices remain shuttered during the holiday, the EMA maintains its product emergency hotline for urgent matters outside of standard business hours. The agency confirmed these scheduling details on Thursday, October 29, ahead of the upcoming closure.

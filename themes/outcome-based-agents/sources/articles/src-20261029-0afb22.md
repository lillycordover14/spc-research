There's Something About Merry! Holiday Artisan Market

December 4–6, 2026 | Womanspace, Rockford
Get into the holiday spirit at There's Something About Merry, Womanspace's beloved holiday artisan market! This festive, long-standing Rockford tradition features a wide variety of original artwork and handcrafted gifts by local artists, from seasonal creations to unique treasures to enjoy year-round.
Browse beautiful, one-of-a-kind items, enjoy sweet treats, and support local artists. It's a wonderful opportunity to find meaningful gifts while celebrating the creativity of the Rockford area.
Dates & Hours:
Friday, December 4 | 1–7 PM
Saturday, December 5 | 10 AM–4 PM
Sunday, December 6 | 10 AM–4 PM
Location: Womanspace, 3333 Maria Linden Dr., Rockford, IL 61114
Admission: Free and open to the public.
Volunteers welcome! Help make this holiday tradition possible by signing up at https://womanspacerockford.app.neoncrm.com/forms/volunteer
Gratefully sponsored by David Boccignone, Ameriprise Financial.

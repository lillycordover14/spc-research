Understand how to design and implement a flexible cloud strategy. Legacy software environments are becoming more complex to maintain and evolve. Yet full cloud migration can be hindered by the time-intensive process or highly restricted, air-gapped data.
There are immediate strategies to leverage cloud-based agility. Dassault Systèmes empowers customers to augment their on-premise investments with targeted cloud services. Our subject matter experts demonstrate that by creating a hybrid ecosystem synchronizing data between local and cloud environments, companies can gain the benefits of SaaS—like artificial intelligence, business process management and advanced analytics.
In this webinar, you will learn:
 

Agile Business Process Management: The cloud automates manual tasks and redesigns work methods without disrupting core on-premise operations.
Next-Gen Data Science Experiences: Scale predictive analytics and virtual assistants using cloud solutions connected directly to your local data.
Rapid Deployment Strategies: How customers have launched secure cloud infrastructure and supplemental crawlers in just a few weeks.

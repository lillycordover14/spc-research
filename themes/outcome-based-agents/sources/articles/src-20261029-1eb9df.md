CLAY COUNTY, Mo. (KCTV) - The Clay County, Missouri, Sheriff’s Office is mourning the loss of a detention officer.
The office says 21-year-old Levi Lathrop died in a motorcycle crash Wednesday morning.
In a 
post on X
, the sheriff’s office asked the community to keep his family, friends and coworkers in their thoughts and prayers.
Few details about the crash have been released.

SOUTH BEND, Ind. (WNDU) - Career Academy shared plans for the future of the former Clay High School building during a news conference on October 29. 
A judge 
recently ruled
 that South Bend schools must make the building available to Career Academy, a charter school, through either lease or purchase. 
Career Academy CEO Jeremy Lugbill said the charter school hopes to expand access to what he called a quality education by moving into the former Clay High School building. 
According to Lugbill, the charter school plans to move into the building by the 2028-29 school year.

The Plymouth Community Center is located at 14800 34th Ave. N. in Plymouth.

(Sun photo by Natalie Cierzan)

The city of Plymouth is set to hold the Plymouth Artisan Market 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 14, at the Plymouth Community Center, 14800 34th Ave. N.
The market features a variety of community vendors who display and sell a range of unique products, including handmade soaps, fine art, apparel, pottery, candles, wall art, woodwork and jewelry.
The event is free to attend and open to the public.
To view a list of participating vendors, visit 
plymouthmn.gov/artisanmarket

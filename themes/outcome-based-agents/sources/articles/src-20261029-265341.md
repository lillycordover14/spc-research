KANSAS CITY, Mo.
 —
A 21-year-old Clay County, Missouri, detention officer was killed in a 
motorcycle crash
 Wednesday morning, 
the Clay County Sheriff's Office announced
. 
Levi Lathrop, 21, was traveling east on Barry Road past Sam Ray Street around 7:45 a.m. on a black Suzuki motorcycle. 
Advertisement
Lathrop continued straight at a dead end, traveling through a tree line before striking the edge of the pavement, Kansas City police said. 
He was ejected from the motorcycle and pronounced dead at the scene. 
Lathrop joined the sheriff's office on Jan. 21, 2025. He worked the night shift at the detention center. 
He was a 2023 graduate of Liberty High School. 
"We are honored Levi chose a career of service at the sheriff's office, and we are deeply saddened by his loss," the sheriff's office said in a statement. 
"Please lift up his family, friends and coworkers in your prayers and thoughts."

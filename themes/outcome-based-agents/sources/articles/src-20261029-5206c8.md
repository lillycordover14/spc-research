DOTHAN, Ala. (WTVY) - It didn’t take long at all for Dothan United to name their next head coach, and it is a name already familiar with the team.
Connor McGoldrick was announced as the new DUSC manager, earning the promotion after serving as an assistant last year under now-sporting director Ricky Davey.
“Connor came in as an assistant and did whatever was asked of him and he was always looking well beyond just the field,” Dothan United team president and co-owner Steve Donner said. “He really embraced the organization with what we were doing in the community as well.”
McGoldrick already has a pair of games under his belt as a head coach for the Dragons, serving in an interim role for two games in 2026, both of which were wins.
“He came in and played the role he had to play, and you could tell it was with confidence,” Donner said. “He was showing a lot of things that you want to see in a coach that you want to have around for a while.”

Between that previous experience and his in general familiarity with the organization, players and club leadership, the hire was a no-brainer.
“We’re a family, so we made this move to try to keep as much of our family intact as possible,” Donner said. “He is so close to Ricky and they think so similarly. When you’re talking to either one of them, I think you’re talking almost to the same ideology and values.
“Knowing that Ricky has the ultimate confidence in him as well and we were able to keep Ricky part of the package, really sold it. If Connor was going to be totally solo, I think we would have still possibly made the move as new head coach. But having them together with Ricky helping with recruiting and how well they see the vision for what they want together made it real easy.”
Earlier this week, Davey was promoted to the role of sporting director for the club after just one season as head coach of the Dragons, leading them to a 9-2-2 record and the team’s first South Central Division crown.

Davey played a big part in helping decide the next person to lead DUSC, and his list of options he thought fit best for the Dragons was particularly short – it only included McGoldrick.
“He thought Connor was ready for the job,” DUSC general manager Aaron McCreight said. “He has the standard, which is important to us, because we have a standard and it matches us. He is very structured, he’s vocal and he has the right mindset and the right personality.
“He wants to win more than anything in the world, and that’s what we’re looking for here...we truly believe in his abilities and him as a person to carry this torch forward.”
McGoldrick will work directly with Davey to recruit players for the 2027 squad, put together his staff and game plan as the Dragons look to defend their division title and earn a third straight appearance in the USL League Two playoffs.

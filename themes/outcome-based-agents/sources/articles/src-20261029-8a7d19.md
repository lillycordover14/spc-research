Yes indeed. A real bona fide President of Ukraine … former Ukrainian President 
Viktor Yushchenko
 who led the 
Orange Revolution
 of 2004 and became the 3rd President of Ukraine in the subsequent election … winning 52% of the vote:

 

Viktor Yushchenko

President of Ukraine (2005 –2010)

Viktor Yushchenko

Some here might remember the Orange Revolution, which is often seen as being the precursor and precedent for the popular(and bloody) 
Euromaidan
 (2013–14) which toppled the government of Putin’s favored president of Ukraine … 
Viktor Yanukovych
. Yanukovych is currently in exile in Russia. Both the Orange Revolution and Euromaidan erupted on the now hallowed grounds of the 
Maidan Nezalezhnosti
 in the center of Kyiv. Both historic events came about as a result of the Ukrainian people insisting on Ukraine’s pro-European orientation vs. Russia’s long and baleful influence.

The events of 2004 seemingly provided the everyday people in the streets of Ukraine with a 
proven a playbook
 … that mass street mobilization could force a change of government. Reportedly 62% of Euromaidan participants had also protested in the 2004 Orange Revolution demonstrations and thus formed a core of experienced activists who dedicated themselves to the struggle to change their country’s socio-political culture which was riven with corruption, cronyism, oligarchic capture of state assets, and slow to non-existent reform of the stagnant economy. By 2004 the citizens had become impatient for change, And by 2013 many were quite willing to die for it … especially the younger, post-Soviet-born Ukrainians … already disillusioned with the ruling elite and the oligarchs.

It is interesting to note that the now disgraced 
Yanukovych 
serves as a connection to both the Orange Revolution and the Euromaidan. He was the fraudulent winner in 2004, but was ultimately adjudged the loser to Yushchenko. Yanukovych, with a huge assist from Russia in the Donbas region, won a rematch in 2010. But his refusal to carry forward and sign the 
2013 EU Association Agreement
, thus thwarting the will of the people, sparked Euromaidan.  And forced his own escape to Russia in the middle of the night. Soon thereafter, Putin invaded Crimea in 2014 and suborned the breakaway of the so-called “Republics” in Ukraine’s 
Donetsk
 and 
Luhansk
 oblasts. The Russo-Ukrainian war was on.

Fast forward a few years to this week in 2026. The news started circulating around the Central West End neighborhood(where our youngest son lives and practices medicine) and among the sizable Ukrainian community of St. Louis, Missouri that former President Viktor Yushchenko was going to be at 
Svoi Patisserie
, my favorite Ukrainian café in the Central West End. You know my wife and I(and our son) were not going to miss the opportunity. We were going to be in da house, no matter what.  And so did a lot of like-minded folks … a packed house to welcome the former President:

Visit by former president Viktor Yushchenko fills St. Louis Ukrainians with hope

The pro-Western leader, who famously survived poisoning, has been bucking up the spirits of local immigrants.

https://www.stlmag.com/news/viktor-yushchenko-svoi-patisserie

I had gone expecting to see a man still afflicted by the effects of his serious poisoning during the 2004 presidential campaign(one guess as to who might have been behind that dastardly act) but he looked very well, none of the pock marks and lesions that covered and disfigured his face for a long time after the poisoning. His once black hair is now almost all grey. The passage of time. An affable and jovial man, he didn’t speak much English but that did not keep him from happily interacting with the non-Ukrainian speaking folks in attendance, shaking hands with a hearty “hello”, taking selfies and signing a lot of books and other mementos. He enthusiastically signed my copy of poems by Ukraine’s most famous poet 
Taras Shevchenko
 as well as the Ukrainian flag which hangs in my home library.

For the Ukrainian community in St. Louis who have been living under a blanket of gloom from both the war and uncertainties related to the inconstancy of policies from 
Krasnov
‘s regime in what was once the “White House”, the former President’s visit was a much needed and welcomed inspiriting tonic. He gave much hope to all in attendance with his unshakeable belief that ADAPTIVE INDOMITABLE UKRAINE will prevail in its struggle against Putin and Russia.

What an enjoyable and totally excellent adventure.  My family and I left 
Svoi Patisserie
 filled with much hope and even more encouraged to stand firmly behind the people of 
Ukrainia
. Thank you and God Bless President 
Viktor Yushchenko
.

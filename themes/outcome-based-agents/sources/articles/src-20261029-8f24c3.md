Canby tabs Jose Gonzalez as its new police chief

Published 12:30 pm Thursday, October 29, 2026

By 
John Baker

 

                                        Jose Gonzalez is sworn in as Canby's new police chief during the Oct. 21 Canby City Council meeting. 
                                         (Courtesy/City of Canby)

 player ready...

After a long search and an aborted hiring attempt earlier in the year, the City of Canby found its new police chief in its own backyard.

During the Oct. 21 Canby City Council meeting, Jose Gonzalez was sworn in as chief of police by Judge Rodney Grafe.

In 2019, Chief Gonzalez accepted the position as a lieutenant with the Canby Police Department. A year later, Gonzalez was promoted to captain in 2020, overseeing the Investigations Unit, Code Enforcement and Evidence Unit, Task Force Partnerships, and Internal Affairs.
Gonzalez earned a bachelor’s degree in business administration from Portland State University in 1994.

In 1996, he began his law enforcement career with a position in the Canby Police Department, later transferring to the Portland Police Bureau in 1998, where he was promoted to the rank of sergeant in 2005. He returned to Canby in 2019.

Chief Gonzalez is originally from Caracas, Venezuela.

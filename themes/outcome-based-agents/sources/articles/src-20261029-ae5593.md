Harvey “Zip” Seiler, Jr., 78 of Bellevue, passed away on Tuesday, October 27, 2026.
 Military Honors will be conducted by Bellevue American Legion Post #273 at 1:00 pm Sunday, November 1, at Hachmann-Mier Funeral Home & Cremation Service with Visitation until 4:00 pm.  A prayer service will be conducted at 4:00 pm.
Harvey was born on September 24, 1948, in Bellevue, Iowa, the son of Harvey Herman and Laurelda Rose (Medinger) Seiler. He attended St. Joseph’s School, graduating in 1966.

Harvey proudly served his country in the United States Army from 1966 to 1969, including two tours in Vietnam. His military service was an important part of his life, and he was a member of the American Legion Post 273 in Bellevue.  Harvey married Dianne on March 4, 1978, and together they shared many years of marriage and family life.
Harvey worked for Flynn Ready Mix for 28 years, John Deere Works, Schroeder Construction, and Dubuque Sand & Gravel. In his free time, Harvey enjoyed fishing, hunting, horseback riding, and spending time with friends. He was also known to enjoy a cold Schmidt’s beer. Known by his nickname “Zip,” Harvey made memories throughout his life with the people he knew and cared about.

Harvey will be remembered for his years of service, his hard work, and the memories he shared with family and friends. May he rest in peace.  He loved spending time with his grandchildren and great-grandchildren, he was very proud of them.
Survivors include his wife, Dianne; his children, Bryan (Lora Williams) Till, Scott (Stacey) Seiler, Chauny (Mike Back) Rubel, and Tracey (Derek) Hueneke; his sisters, Joan (Leland) Hingtgen and Carol (Glen) Stillmunkes; seven grandchildren, Alec (Shannon) Rubel, Alyssa Rubel, Logan (Hannah) Hueneke, Mariah Hueneke, Madilyn Hueneke, Breanna (Ryan) Radue, and Brandy (Tanner) Michels; five great-grandchildren, Nolan, Cayden, Easton, Cooper, Jaedyn, Paetyn, Waylon, and two expecting; and many other extended family members and friends.
He was preceded in death by his parents; and his parents in-law, Louis and Irene Deppe.
The family would like to thank Hospice of Jackson County for your care and support during this time.
Online condolences may be expressed to the family at

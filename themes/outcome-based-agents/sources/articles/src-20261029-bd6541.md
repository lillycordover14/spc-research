CLAY COUNTY, Mo.
 —
Early voting in Clay County, Missouri, is running well ahead of the last midterm election.
According to the Clay County Election Board, voter check-ins through Wednesday are up 145% compared to the same point in the 2022 midterm election.
Advertisement
That's also a 244% increase compared to the August 2026 primary.
The busiest day so far at the Clay County Election Board was Oct. 23, when 1,243 voters checked in.
Congressional races
 and ballot questions could be helping drive interest, 
including Missouri's Proposition A
, which asks voters whether to approve a new congressional map.
Four early voting locations will be open from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Thursday through Saturday.
On Monday, Nov. 2, early voting will only be available at the Clay County Election Board from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
Voters can find more information at voteclaycountymo.gov.

Smugglers' Notch Distillery

Vermont distillery thanks loyal customers for their votes, celebrating the exceptional staff, single-barrel tasting experiences, and limited-edition spirits that bring visitors back year after year.

Vermont Business Magazine
 Smugglers’ Notch Distillery has once again earned national recognition in the USA TODAY 10BEST Readers’ Choice Awards, ranking #2 Best Spirits Tasting Room and #2 Best Craft Vodka in the United States for 2026.

For the independent Vermont distillery, these two honors represent something far more meaningful than national rankings: the trust, loyalty, and continued support of the customers who have helped build the brand over the past two decades.

"These awards mean so much to us because they're decided by the people who visit our tasting rooms, enjoy our spirits, and continue to support us year after year," said Jeremy Elliott, President and Co-Founder of Smugglers’ Notch Distillery. "We are incredibly grateful to everyone who took the time to vote for us. Earning your trust and loyalty is something we never take for granted."

More Than a Tasting — An Experience Worth Returning For

At Smugglers’ Notch Distillery, the tasting room experience goes well beyond sampling award-winning spirits.

Visitors to the flagship distillery in Jeffersonville, Vermont and their tasting rooms have the opportunity to explore distinctive single-barrel bourbons, discover the differences between individual barrels, and experience carefully crafted spirits that reflect the character of Vermont.

The distillery's limited-edition and specialty bottle releases have also become an important part of the experience, giving enthusiasts something new to discover with each visit. For many customers, returning to the distillery has become an annual tradition — an opportunity to reconnect with the team, explore new releases, and bring home a bottle they may not find again.

"We love seeing familiar faces return year after year," said Elliott. "Some come back to experience our latest single-barrel bourbon, while others are searching for a special limited-edition bottle. Those relationships are what make this business so rewarding. We want every visit to offer something memorable and give people a reason to come back."

The People Behind the Experience

While the distillery's award-winning spirits attract attention, Elliott credits the company's dedicated tasting room staff with creating the experiences that turn first-time visitors into loyal customers.

Across its Vermont tasting rooms, knowledgeable and welcoming team members share the stories behind each spirit, guide customers through tastings, and help visitors discover something that fits their individual tastes.

"Our staff deserves tremendous recognition for this award," said Elliott. "They're the people who welcome our guests, share their knowledge, and make every visitor feel like part of the Smugglers’ Notch Distillery family. You can make an exceptional spirit, but it's the people behind the counter who create an exceptional experience. This recognition belongs just as much to them as it does to the distillery."

National Recognition Built on Quality and Trust

The distillery's #2 national ranking for Best Craft Vodka further reinforces its long-standing commitment to quality.

Smugglers’ Notch Distillery first earned national attention for its 95 Point and Double Gold Award-Winning Vodka in 2010. Since then, the Vermont producer has continued expanding its portfolio of award-winning bourbons, gins, rums, rye whiskeys, and specialty spirits while maintaining the same commitment to quality that helped establish its reputation.

The USA TODAY 10BEST awards begin with nominations selected by subject matter experts and editors, with the final rankings determined through public voting.

"Twenty years ago, we set out to build a distillery that people could trust — one focused on making exceptional spirits without compromising quality," Elliott said. "To see customers recognize both our vodka and our tasting room experience on a national level is incredibly rewarding. We're proud of what we've built, grateful for the people who support us, and excited to keep giving them new reasons to visit."

About Smugglers’ Notch Distillery

Founded in 2006 by father-and-son team Ron and Jeremy Elliott, Smugglers’ Notch Distillery is an independent Vermont craft distillery built on a shared passion for exceptional spirits, innovation, and quality.

From its flagship distillery and tasting room on a 10-acre property in Jeffersonville to its additional tasting rooms in Waterbury, Stowe, and Manchester, the company offers visitors an authentic Vermont spirits experience.

Its portfolio includes nationally recognized vodka, single-barrel and specialty bourbons, rye whiskey, gin, rum, maple-infused spirits, and limited-edition releases. The distillery also produces specialty maple cream liqueurs and partners with local maple producers to showcase some of Vermont's most distinctive flavors.

CROSBY’S REACTION TO RODGERS’ SCRAMBLE

Aaron Rodgers has played well for the Steelers this season. He seriously considered retiring this offseason, but he opted to return to Pittsburgh instead, though he’s been adamant about retiring after this season ends. At 42, Rodgers still 
has some gas left in the tank.
 That includes still having some mobility, as seen in Week 7, when he ran for a crucial 1st down to seal Pittsburgh’s win.

Las Vegas Raiders edge rusher Maxx Crosby shared his reaction to Rodgers’ 
scramble on his podcast.
 Crosby was surprised to see the veteran quarterback move so well, but the play still excited him. Rodgers’ mobility used to be an underrated part of his game, and in Week 7 he showed he can still move well when he needs to.

BROWNS PLAY RENEGADE

The Steelers face the Cleveland Browns this week in their second matchup this season. Both teams are 5-2, but the Browns beat the Steelers in their first meeting, so Cleveland leads the AFC North. However, if the Steelers win, they’ll take sole possession of the division lead.

This week’s game will be played in Pittsburgh, which should give the Steelers a huge advantage. The Browns haven’t won in Pittsburgh in the regular season since 2003. However, they’re doing what they can to prepare for the environment. That includes playing the song Renegade at a recent practice, as shared 
by analyst Tony Grossi on Twitter.
 That’s the Steelers’ hype song, and it can sometimes intimidate opponents.

POLAMALU HONORED

Troy Polamalu is one of the best safeties in NFL history. He spent his entire career with the Steelers, playing from 2003-2014. Polamalu helped the Steelers win two Super Bowls and earned many other accolades. That includes eight Pro Bowls, four First-team All-Pro selections, two Second-team All-Pro selections, and one Defensive Player of the Year award.

Polamalu recently received another honor, too. He’s been inducted into
 the Las Vegas Bowl Hall of Fame.
 Polamalu is already in the Pro Football Hall of Fame, and now, he’s added another accomplishment to his legacy. It speaks to what an incredible player he was.

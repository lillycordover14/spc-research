Pine Labs Q2 Profit Rises Over Sixfold To Rs 39 Cr, Revenue Grows 24%

 
                            BW Online Bureau
                        

 Oct 29, 2026
                    

# Pine Labs

#  Pine Labs Q2 FY27 Results

#  fintech

#  digital payments

#  quarterly results

#  EBITDA

		                	The fintech company’s shares rose as much as 7 per cent in intraday trade after its September-quarter results
		                

Pine Labs reported a sharp rise in profit for the second quarter of financial year 2026-27 (Q2 FY27), supported by growth in its digital payments and issuing businesses.
The fintech company’s consolidated net profit rose to Rs 38.57 crore in the quarter ended 30 September 2026, compared with Rs 5.97 crore in the corresponding period last year. Revenue from operations increased 24 per cent year on year to Rs 806.19 crore from Rs 649.90 crore. 
The results lifted investor sentiment, with Pine Labs shares gaining as much as 7 per cent to reach an intraday high of Rs 173.80 on the National Stock Exchange on 29 October. 
EBITDA Increases As Margins Improve
Pine Labs’ operating performance also strengthened during the quarter. According to a report by Upstox, earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) rose 68 per cent to Rs 126 crore from Rs 75 crore a year earlier. The EBITDA margin increased to 15.63 per cent from 11.54 per cent.
The company’s first-half performance also improved. Revenue for the six months ended September 2026 stood at Rs 1,543 crore, up from Rs 1,266 crore in the year-earlier period. Net profit increased to Rs 58 crore from Rs 11 crore. 
Digital Payments Drive Growth
Chief executive Amrish Rau said growth during the quarter came from both the acceptance and issuing businesses. The company also reported increasing contributions from international markets and products introduced over the past two years.
Pine Labs processed around 25 million transactions a day, a 22 per cent increase year on year. Online payments grew by more than 45 per cent, while gross transaction value (GTV) rose 140 per cent, according to the company’s reported operating updates.
International operations also recorded growth, with revenue in Southeast Asia increasing 40 per cent. The company’s international digital payment counters (DCPs) nearly tripled year on year to close to one lakh, while its UAE business handled more than one lakh transactions daily.

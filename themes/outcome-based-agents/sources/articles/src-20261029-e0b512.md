A mini-split that reportedly lasted five years and two months — just beyond its five-year warranty — failed in a way that left one owner seeking advice and an HVAC tech fuming over what sounded like a preventable mistake.

, the original poster said their mini-split had a loose wire that wore down, shorted the control board, and wore a hole in the refrigerant line.

The OP also laid out why the fix became costly. "I replaced the control board not seeing this and thankfully didn't short the new one before finding the hole," 
they said in a comment
. "Easy fix, but it's one control board, nitro, 410a, and my time vs. a 10-cent zip tie."
A wire routing problem like that can quickly become a repair involving electronics, refrigerant, and the labor to handle both. Since the OP said the unit was a mini-split, several costs could pile up from the same failure.

IRS CEO Frank Bisignano has donated to 
Trump Accounts
 for certain kids in Brooklyn, the 
agency announced
 Thursday.
The Bisignano Family Fund donated $250 apiece to Trump Accounts of eligible children ages 2 to 8 living in 11234, Bisignano's hometown ZIP code, according to the release. 
"My life was shaped by the people, schools, and neighborhoods in Marine Park, Mill Basin, Bergen Beach, and Flatlands," Bisignano said in a statement. "This donation is an investment in the children of Brooklyn, giving them a boost in their personal wealth building journeys."
Within that ZIP code, fewer than 10,000 U.S. citizens are under 10 years of age, according to CNBC's analysis of the most recent population estimate from the U.S. Census Bureau.

The contribution will be invested in a diversified
 index fund
, the release also said. 
Expanding access to Trump Accounts has been a Trump administration priority. In July, Bisignano was tasked with
 leading that effort
. 
The latest Trump Account news comes weeks after the Treasury Department
 auto-enrolled more than 60 million children
.
Trump Accounts, also known as
 530A accounts
, are open to any U.S. citizen under 18 with a Social Security number. Children born from 2025 through 2028 may also claim a one-time 
$
1,000 pilot program contribution
 from the Treasury.
Families must still activate their child's Trump Account to receive contributions if eligible, according to Treasury guidance.
'The tip of the iceberg'
Bisignano's commitment comes on the heels of news that Elon Musk plans to
 give every kid in America
 a share of
 SpaceX
 in 2027.
Altimeter Capital CEO Brad Gerstner, founder of Invest America and an architect of Trump Accounts, described Musk's gift as "
the tip of the iceberg
" for philanthropic gifts on 
CNBC's "Squawk Box"
 Oct. 21. Gerstner has committed about $35 million to 
help fund investment accounts
 for kids in his home state of Indiana.       
More gifts are in the pipeline, Bisignano told CNBC's "
Squawk Box
" on Oct. 8. "They range from small to mega," he said. 
After claiming an account, families can track deposits in the 
Trump Accounts app
, which was designed in partnership with 
Robinhood
.
"Trump Accounts is direct philanthropy, extremely efficient, direct to children...," Robinhood CEO Vlad Tenev said on CNBC's 
"Squawk on the Street"
 on Oct. 28.
"Excited to see Elon donation," he said. "I think there will be many others as well."

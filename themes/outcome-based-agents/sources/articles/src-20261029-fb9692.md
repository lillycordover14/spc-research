Carmel Clay Parks & Recreation breaks ground on Bear Creek Park&nbsp;&nbsp;Current Publishing

[page text]
Work is officially underway for the construction of Bear Creek Park in northwest Carmel. 

Carmel Clay Parks & Recreation held a groundbreaking ceremony for the $8.9 million project Oct. 27 at the site of the future park at 3777 Voyageur Way, near 146th Street and Shelborne Road. 

“As our community continues to grow, so does the need for accessible recreation and open space and places where families, friends and neighbors can gather,” Carmel Mayor Sue Finkam said during the ceremony. “Bear Creek Park will provide that much-needed green space, offering room to play, explore, relax and connect with nature. This will be a beautiful spot to do so.” 

The first phase of Bear Creek Park construction will include an overlook and trails. (Image courtesy of Carmel Clay Parks & Recreation)

The first phase of construction on the park will include boardwalks with an overlook, a play area, multi-use courts, restrooms and a parking lot. It is named after Bear Creek, a small tributary that runs through the site and flows into Little Eagle Creek. 

The park  is expected to open in the spring of 2028.

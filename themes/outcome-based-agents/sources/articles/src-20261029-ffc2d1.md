TTC Tour Brands
 has confirmed 100 per cent definite departures across Trafalgar, Insight Vacations and Costsaver for tours departing through to 30 September 2026.

The Travel Corporation (TTC)
 has guaranteed that every Trafalgar, Insight Vacations and Costsaver tour departing from now until the end of September 2026 will run, removing the risk of cancellation through the peak travel window.

The 100 per cent definite departures pledge applies across the three brands, meaning travel advisors can confirm a client’s tour will go ahead the moment it is booked, whether that is a last-minute getaway or a September departure planned in advance.

The guarantee comes as Trafalgar continues to broaden its offering, recently 
launching luxury river cruising in Europe
, and adding a 
trade incentive
 until 31 July 2026 for river cruise bookings.

▼  ADVERTISING  ▼

What the guarantee covers

Costsaver tours are guaranteed to go ahead through to September 2026. Image: Costsaver

The guarantee runs across Trafalgar, Insight Vacations and Costsaver tours departing between now and 30 September 2026, confirming the tour will operate regardless of minimum numbers reached.

Alongside the departures guarantee, TTC Tour Brands is offering free Gold Seal Protection on 2026 departures booked by 31 August 2026, adding a layer of booking flexibility on top of the certainty the guarantee provides.

Travellers on the Insight Vacations Sicily In-Depth & Malta tour. Image: Insight Vacations

TTC Tour Brands Oceania Senior Vice President Sales Andrew Young said: “This initiative is about delivering certainty at every stage of the travel planning process.”

“When clients are ready to book, they want to know their plans are secure. By guaranteeing departures through September, we are making it easier for agents to close sales and helping guests move forward without hesitation.”

Advisors can access selling tools and book through the 
Travel Agent Portal
 (TAP) with TTC’s unified sales, reservations and marketing team support via a 24/7 contact centre.

KARRYON UNPACKS:
 Cancelled departures are one of the more frustrating conversations an advisor can have with a client, and a guarantee through peak season offers peace of mind. With Gold Seal Protection layered on top until 31 August, selling TTC Tour Brands also gets a lot simpler.

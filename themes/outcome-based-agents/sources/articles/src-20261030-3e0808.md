FELTON, Minn. (Valley News Live) - A crash involving two semi-trucks sent one driver to the hospital with life-threatening injuries Friday morning in Clay County.
The Minnesota State Patrol responded to the collision around 6:40 a.m. at the intersection of Highway 9 and Clay County Road 34, near Felton.
One of the drivers was airlifted to an area hospital with life-threatening injuries, according to investigators. The second driver suffered injuries that were not considered life-threatening.
Authorities have detoured traffic around the intersection while emergency personnel and crash reconstruction teams work the scene. Drivers traveling through the area should expect delays and plan alternate routes.
The Minnesota State Patrol continues to investigate the cause of the crash.

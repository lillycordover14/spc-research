PIGEON FORGE, Tenn. (WVLT) - Some East Tennesseans can snag deals on Pigeon Forge outings through WildSide, an outdoor adventure company based in the Smokies. 
The Local Appreciation Days promotion runs from Nov. 6 through 13 and offers 50% off UT Tours and MegaZip zip lining for anyone from Blount, Jefferson, Cocke or Sevier counties.
UTV Tours offer guests the chance to check out scenic trails off-road, and MegaZip takes zip liners through the Smokey Mountains on what is billed as “America’s Longest Zip Line.”
“Local Appreciation Days are our way of saying thank you to the communities that support us throughout the year,” said Bryce Bentz, Founding Partner of WildSide. “We’re excited to provide these thrilling adventures at a discounted rate so our neighbors can enjoy the full WildSide experience.”

Your 
Christmas shopping experience in Chicago
 is about to get a lot more unique. 

The
 Chicago Artisan Market
 is 
back this holiday season
, taking over two locations on 
three separate weekends.

Here’s 
a look at the schedule
 and 
what to expect.

Chicago Artisan Market 2026

Photo/Chicago Artisan Market

The
 2026 Chicago Artisan Market 
will bring together 
hundreds of local artists and makers
 for a one-of-a-kind vendor experience
 November through December.

The market will launch at 
Morgan MFG in Fulton Market 
and 
Artifact Events in Ravenswood
 on 
four dates.

Guests can shop
 locally made 
jewelry, 
home décor, 
gourmet foods,
 ceramics, 
bath & body products, 
artwork, apparel, 
and much more.

Each event provides the opportunity for Chicagoans to build community with local artisans, and
 enjoy a fun holiday outing
 with friends and family.

The 
lineup 
is as follows:

Sunday, November 15:
 Fulton Market at Morgan MFG. 
115+ artisans 
| 11 a.m.—5 p.m.

Saturday, November 28—Sunday, November 29:
 Ravenswood at Artifact Events. 
200+ artisans
 during Small Business Saturday weekend | 11 a.m.—5 p.m.

Sunday, December 13:
 Fulton Market at Morgan MFG. 
115+ artisans
 | 11 a.m.—5 p.m.

Advance tickets
 for each market date start at 
just $8
 per person.

Click 
here
 to 
grab your tickets ahead of time
 and 
plan your visit.

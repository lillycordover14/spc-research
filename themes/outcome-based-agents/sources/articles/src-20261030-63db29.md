CMA CGM EVP Mathieu Friedberg confirms a global rollout of HappyRobot AI agents after successful regional trials to automate maritime freight logistics
Global supply chain networks are under immense pressure to increase efficiency, reduce bottlenecks, and manage high-volume communications. To address this, logistics heavyweight CMA CGM has rapidly accelerated its enterprise-wide digital strategies by deploying agentic AI.
Partnering with enterprise AI platform 
HappyRobot
, CMA CGM is scaling up the use of multilingual voice and text agents to manage customer interactions and automate related tasks. By automating these essential but repetitive tasks, supply chain leaders can drive process efficiency and redirect human capital toward strategic exception handling.
Automating Maritime Freight and Customer Service
For a global logistics player like the CMA CGM Group, which handles more than 54 million customer requests annually, solutions that can facilitate faster customer interactions, support commercial outreach and automate recurring tasks can drive greater efficiency. The company has successfully completed pilots in regional sales, customer service and finance teams. CMA CGM has now announced that it is scaling up the solution to manage customer interactions and automate related tasks, improving customer experience.
Building on these initial applications, CMA CGM and HappyRobot plan to extend the deployment across the Group, including 
CEVA Logistics
. Agentic AI is already being deployed in Europe and North America, but the solution is expected to expand further in Africa, South America and Asia, automating repetitive tasks.
Our collaboration with HappyRobot illustrates how we are turning artificial intelligence into practical solutions for our operations. Building on successful pilots, we are now expanding these applications internationally to improve customer experience and support our teams
Mathieu Friedberg, Executive Vice President of Transformation at CMA CGM Group
Mathieu Friedberg, Executive Vice President, Transformation, CMA CGM Group
In sales, CMA CGM has deployed Eva, an AI voice agent developed with HappyRobot to engage potential customers registered on CMA CGM’s platform who are not supported by a dedicated sales representative. Eva proactively contacts these customers to understand their shipping needs, offer tailored freight quotations and identify qualified commercial opportunities for CMA CGM’s sales teams. Across initial campaigns in the UK and Senegal, Eva has contacted more than 4,000 customers, with approximately one-third progressing to active discussions on potential shipping routes. Building on these initial results, CMA CGM aims to deploy Eva in 20 countries by the end of 2026.
In the realm of customer service, the AI agents are tackling the historically time-intensive process of cargo release. The solution handles enquiries relating to cargo release, including release status, customs clearance and shipping documentation. Over 46% of inquiries regarding customs clearance and shipping documentation are now resolved directly by AI, which can even trigger internal system emails to speed up freight movement. 
In finance, pilots have also demonstrated the solution’s ability to support payment follow-up by contacting customers about outstanding invoices. These calls resulted in payments being collected, including some made immediately, demonstrating tangible benefits for CMA CGM’s finance teams. 
“Our collaboration with HappyRobot illustrates how we are turning artificial intelligence into practical solutions for our operations," says Mathieu Friedberg, Executive Vice President, Transformation, CMA CGM Group. "Building on successful pilots, we are now expanding these applications internationally to improve customer experience and support our teams. This approach reflects our ambition to deploy AI where it can deliver measurable benefits for our customers and employees.”
“By end of year, we’ll be live in over 20 countries and working to continue innovating in other parts of CMA CGM Group,” says Pablo Palafox, CEO of HappyRobot. “CMA CGM isn’t just building agents — they’re building something that compounds over time: agents working and learning together across the group.”
Deploying AI agents to validate sales opportunities is only one of the applications that CMA CGM is exploring with HappyRobot.
Agentic AI as a Logistics Operating Model
CMA CGM is not the first logistics chain heavyweight to roll out HappyRobot's agentic AI solutions. DHL Supply Chain has utilised HappyRobot's technology to create a new operational layer within its global warehousing and transport networks. After 18 months of systematically validating generative AI use cases, DHL announced in September 2025 that it was leveraging autonomous agents for high-priority warehouse coordination, driver follow-ups, and manual data entry reduction.
The integration of these voice-based AI agents has brought measurable benefits to supply chain operations. DHL Group continues to expand its 
AI strategy
 across all divisions.

Key Technology and Ecosystem Partners

HappyRobot

HappyRobot enables enterprises to build and scale AI agents. Their proprietary technology seamlessly integrates with internal logistics databases, allowing autonomous workers to handle complex customer service, finance, and sales operations. This ensures global supply chains maintain high visibility, speed, and communication consistency.

CMA CGM Group

As a global leader in sea, land, and air logistics, CMA CGM is scaling agentic AI to handle its 54 million annual customer requests. By integrating Eva into operations across 20 countries, the shipping giant is automating repetitive commercial outreach, cargo release processes, and finance workflows, freeing supply chain employees for strategic tasks. CMA CGM has collaborated with HappyRobot since early 2025, when its developers were introduced to CMA CGM’s operational teams as part of an accelerator programme, ZEBOX, enabling the startup to identify business use cases and develop a proof of concept with the Group. In addition to the support provided by ZEBOX, the CMA CGM Group has also invested in HappyRobot.

DHL Supply Chain

DHL Supply Chain integrates HappyRobot’s platform to automate appointment scheduling and transport status tracking, ensuring essential equipment and bulk inventory move seamlessly through their highly complex global supply chain networks.

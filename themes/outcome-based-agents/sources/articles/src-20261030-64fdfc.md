ADA seeks treasurer candidates

Filing deadline is July 1, 2027

 

by 
Mary Beth Versaci

October 30, 2026

 
 

 

American Dental Association members interested in running for ADA treasurer can find the information they need to file for candidacy on the Association’s website.

The Treasurer’s Curriculum Vitae Form is available on the ADA House of Delegates 
candidate information webpage
. Completed forms should be emailed to 
officercandidates@ada.org
.

The deadline for filing is July 1, 2027. 

The current ADA treasurer is Cody Graves, D.D.S., whose three-year term will end at the 2027 House of Delegates in Denver. Per ADA Bylaws, the treasurer may hold the office for two consecutive three-year terms.

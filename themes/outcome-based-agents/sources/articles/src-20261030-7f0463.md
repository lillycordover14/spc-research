ESPN tabs Indiana basketball legend for 'College GameDay' guest picker ahead of IU football game
 Scott Horner
Indianapolis Star
Oct. 30, 2026, 10:50 a.m. ET
Indiana basketball legend Isiah Thomas will be the guest picker on ESPN's "College GameDay."
Thomas led the Hoosiers to a national championship in 1981 and is a Basketball Hall of Famer.
The show will be broadcast from Showalter Fountain on the Indiana University campus.
Indiana basketball
 legend 
Isiah Thomas
 will predict college football games as the guest picker on ESPN's "
College GameDay
" on Saturday.
Thomas led the 
Hoosiers
 to the 1981 national championship as a first-team All-American for coach 
Bob Knight
. Thomas played two years at IU before becoming the second overall NBA Draft pick for the 
Detroit Pistons
, where he won two titles (1989, '90) on the way to enshrinement in the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame.
The show will be at 
Showalter Fountain
 in the heart of the IU campus. 
Rece Davis
, 
Desmond Howard
, 
Pat McAfee
, 
Nick Saban
 and 
Kirk Herbstreit
 will analyze games, pick winners for the day's biggest matchups, and a lot more.
Indiana football
 (7-1, 4-1 Big Ten) plays Minnesota (5-2, 3-1) at 3:30 p.m. ET on CBS.

Sporty’s Adds Free Tabs to FAA Handbooks&nbsp;&nbsp;General Aviation News

[page text]
Sporty’s is now including free organizer tabs with its printed editions of two primary FAA publications: The 
Pilot’s Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge
 (PHAK) and the 
Airplane Flying Handbook
 (AFH).

According to company officials, the tab sets are designed to help pilots navigate the comprehensive references more efficiently, quickly locate critical topics, and organize study materials for ground school, flight lessons, and practical test preparation.

Each package contains numbered chapter dividers, blank tabs for personal notes and custom indexing, and an alignment guide. The adhesive tabs are included directly with the books for self-application.

Both softcover FAA titles are available individually for $17.95, or together as a two-volume bundle for $29.95.

ARDMORE, Okla. (KTEN) — Karl Fontenot, the man accused in the 1984 murder of Denice Haraway in Ada, was in an Ardmore courtroom on Friday for a private hearing.
The hearing was held behind closed doors, but updates over the next hearing were set.

One motion seeks to dismiss the attorney general and the district attorney's office due to conflicting court cases involving Fontenot.
Another is a motion of availability, considering Fontenot's ability to have a fair trial based on the few witnesses that are are still alive or missing.

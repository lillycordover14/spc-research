'Been a rough few weeks': Chrissie Swan shares candid health update after being hospitalised for 'medical emergency'
The Aussie star has admitted it's been "a pretty rough two weeks" with "lots of curveballs" and has been left mostly bedridden, as she overcomes her health scare.
Chloe Longhetti
Lifestyle Reporter
2 min read
October 30, 2026 - 4:01PM
TV and radio star Chrissie Swan has shared a candid health update with fans, after being hospitalised due to a "medical emergency". 
In a new video, the former Big Brother Australia star, 52, said she would soon have an "energy crash," but wanted to share a message with her followers, admitting she's had "a pretty rough two weeks".
'Been a rough few weeks': Chrissie Swan shares candid health update after being hospitalised for 'medical emergency'. Picture: Instagram/chrissieswan
“I can feel the energy crash is imminent, so I just wanted to seize this opportunity because I’ve washed my hair for the first time in a couple of weeks, and say thank you so much for all your beautiful messages. I read every single one,” she said in her clip. 
“I’m really missing work and really missing interacting with people on the radio, so your direct messages mean a lot to me.” 
Speaking about her health and recovery,  after keeping the cause of her hospitalisation under wraps, she said: "Whoever said that healing is not linear, was not lying."
“I’ve had a pretty rough two weeks and lots of curveballs, lots of surprise stuff that wasn’t on the cards,” she admitted. 
“So, little by little. I’m feeling good today. Let’s run with it.”
In her caption, the media personality added that she's mostly been bedridden, saying: "Seizing the opportunity while I feel ok. It’s been a rough few weeks and most days I haven’t been able to leave bed, tbh."
"It’s such a drag being unwell but your messages and thoughts and positive encouragement have been the surprise break out hit of the year!"

health scare and hospitalisation with fans in an Instagram video
 shared with her more than 291,000 Instagram followers.

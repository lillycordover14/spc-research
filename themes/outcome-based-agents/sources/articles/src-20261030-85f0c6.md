CLAY COUNTY, Ind. (WTHI) - A Kentucky man faces charges in Clay County for child sex crimes.
Police arrested Jacob Youngblood, 21, of Scottsdale, Kentucky, on Thursday in Kentucky.
The investigation
Indiana State Police said that in August, it received a tip that a 14-year-old girl had been inappropriately touched by an adult in Clay County. The adult was later identified as Youngblood.
ISP said that during the investigation, it found Youngblood was also in possession of child sexual abuse material.
Charges
Youngblood was charged with:
Sexual misconduct with a minor, Level 4 felony
Possession of child sexual abuse material (prior conviction), Level 4 felony
Child solicitation, Level 5 felony
Failure to register as a sex offender, Level 5 felony
Dissemination of matter harmful to minors, Level 6 felony

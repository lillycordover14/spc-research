A massive two-day holiday festival is coming to Gresham to celebrate the sights, sounds, and flavors of the holidays with an Italian twist.

The 3rd Annual Italia Holiday Artisan Fair returns from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturday-Sunday, Nov. 21-22, at Club Paesano at Cedarville Lodge, 3800 W. Powell Loop, in Gresham. Presented by Gather Arts, the indoor event celebrates independent artists, handmade gifts, and the joy of gathering as a community.

“The Italia Holiday Artisan Fair is about connecting our community with talented local makers and creating a festive experience people will remember,” said Michael Merrill of Gather Arts.

Meet the Makers

At the fair, you can shop at more than 30 artists/makers from Oregon and Southwest Washington. There will be original art, jewelry, handcrafted décor, and one-of-a-kind gifts perfect for the holiday season.

Elsewhere, Italian food, live entertainment, and more festive activities will make the outing an event rather than a shopping trip.

Workshops

There will be hands-on workshops you can participate in, including royal-icing cookie decorating with Olivia of Knockout Cookies; Thanksgiving pumpkin floral arrangements; and live wreath decorating. Some classes require advance registration and include the standard fair admission: 
gatherarts.com/fair-classes

SnowCap Community Services

During the event, attendees are encouraged to support SnowCap’s annual Fill-A-Bag fundraiser, which provides nonperishable items to stock the shelves during the critical winter months.

Gather Arts

A local flower farm and creative business offering handmade goods, floral design, art workshops, and artisan events that support independent makers and community connections. Learn more online:

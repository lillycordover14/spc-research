[page text]
SHENANDOAH – 
The Lady Devils are moving on to the semifinals, winning their first playoff game in quite awhile.

Shenandoah Valley’s volleyball squad hosted Williams Valley in a District XI 2A quarterfinal matchup Tuesday evening.

They swept the Vikings in three sets, winning 25-22, 25-20, and 25-20. It’s the first playoff victory in five years and the first for head coach and 2017 SV grad Molly Michalik in her second year leading the program.

KAYLEE LINDENMUTH / SHENANDOAH SENTINEL

The Lady Devils move on to face Tri-Valley on Monday, Nov. 2 at 5:30pm at Minersville.

Tuesday’s game was played in honor of the three North Schuylkill girls involved in a crash — Autumn Houser, Taylor Houser, and Parker Drum. The Lady Devils wore ribbons and stars in their hair in their honor.

KAYLEE LINDENMUTH / SHENANDOAH SENTINEL

Prior to the game, the team recognized two milestones for players. Brittany Tufts was recognized for reaching 500 digs while Aubrie Monaghan reached 500 kills.

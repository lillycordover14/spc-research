Alex Tuch
 continued his early-season heroics for the 
Washington Capitals
 in their 4-3 victory over the 
Nashville Predators
, Thursday night.

Tuch scored the game-winning goal — his fifth of the young season — with 9:51 remaining in the third period.

Alex Tuch’s 10th goal in 12 games

Tuch beat Predators goaltender 
Juuse Saros
 with a wrist shot from the dot of the left circle, beating him over the shoulder short-side. Tuch ended up with the puck after 
Jakob Chychrun
 entered the zone and put a harmless but intentional shot on net, that Saros deflected to the corner with his goalie stick. 
Jordan Kyrou
 then raced to the puck first where he hit Tuch, who had got in position in the home plate area.

“Yeah, I mean, the fourth goal sticks out to me is that forecheck off of Saros — he plays the puck so well,” Capitals head coach Spencer Carbery 
said after the game
. “So you have to strategize against that and try to do some different things… [T]he fourth goal, we… shoot it off him. And so we talked about that. Like, he’s that good at playing the puck. You have to build a little bit of a plan. And the guys did an excellent job on that fourth goal and everything. You know, Tuchy, the shot is world class. But everything we did to get him into that spot was really well done by our group.”

“I saw the two guys went to [Kyrou in the corner], which they were doing all night,” Tuch 
added
. “So, I mean, he’s such a skilled player. Being able to find that I thought was no problem for him. I was just in a good area.”

The goal was another example where Tuch has used his high hockey IQ and hard, accurate shot to help lead the Capitals to victory. 

Tuch now has 10 goals in his first 12 games with Washington, half of which have been game-winners — the most in the NHL.

“I mean, some of those are a little lucky,” Tuch said. “Some of them are, I don’t know, the opponent gets a late goal or something like that. But, no, I think our whole group’s just been rolling. I’ve been able to cash in on a couple goals. My line mates have been unbelievable. I’ve played with a bunch of different guys this year, and we just keep on pressing. And I think everyone’s kind of building chemistry with one another because we’re kind of playing the same way. We’re playing together, supporting each other. It’s been a lot of fun. (I) just want to keep it going. I just want to keep winning games because that’s the best part of it all.”

Analysts figured Tuch would find his way in Washington after scoring 30-or-more goals in three of the last four seasons, but not nearly to this degree, especially when he shares the ice with big scorers like 
Alex Ovechkin
, 
Tom Wilson
, Jordan Kyrou, 
Aliaksei Protas
, Jakob Chychrun, and 
Ryan Leonard
 on the Capitals roster. One would assume that talent would likely eat up some of Tuch’s goal-scoring opportunities and bandwidth.

Instead, Tuch went from second fiddle to 
Tage Thompson
 in Buffalo, to first fiddle with Washington, putting him on pace to score more goals than Alex Ovechkin’s career best season in the NHL of 65 in 2007-08.

Tuch is currently on pace to score 70 times if he plays all 84 games of the 2026-27 season. He currently sits third in the league in goals, behind only 
Kirill Kaprizov
 and 
Leon Draisaitl
.

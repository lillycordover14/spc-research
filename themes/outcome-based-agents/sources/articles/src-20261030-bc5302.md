Apple appears to be updating product images of older MacBook models to make their display notches more visible, following the launch of its redesigned MacBook Pro on October 28. The change was spotted by an X user, who noticed updated images on Apple’s Canadian online store.

The timing is interesting because Apple’s 
new MacBook Pro
 introduces a redesigned display with Dynamic Island, replacing the traditional camera notch found on earlier models.

Apple Makes the Notch More Visible on Older Macs

Apple previously used dark wallpapers in some MacBook marketing images, making the notch blend into the surrounding menu bar. The 
newly spotted
 imagery appears to give the cutout greater visibility.

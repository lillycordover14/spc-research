David Crosby Obituary
David Michael Crosby, age 45, was born in Myrtle Beach South Carolina.
He was a loving, adventurous, and helpful soul. He was free spirited, had a heart of gold, and had never met a stranger. He held his Bible close and was strong in his faith. He enjoyed a good meal, being outdoors, riding his bike, and rebuilding bicycles for the homeless. As a handyman, he would help anyone that needed it and never asked for payment in return.
He is preceded in death by his father, James Michael Crosby "Pops" and his brother James Dean Fex.
Left to cherish his memory are his mother Joan Eckert, sister Michelle Dean (Rolland), daughters Chloe Clark, Ammelya Grace Crosby, and Sydney Crosby, nephews Dustin Fex, Jeremy Fex, Robert Dean, Joshua Prater, and Aiden Dean. And may Aunts, Uncles, cousins, and friends.

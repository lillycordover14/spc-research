Source confirms to CBS News Qatari mediators are in Iran to convey tweaks to latest peace proposal
 
 
 
From cbsnews.com
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

A source familiar with the ongoing diplomacy confirmed to CBS News that Qatari mediators were in Tehran on Friday to convey the latest edits to a draft proposal to end the U.S. war with Iran. The source said there were indications of progress being made. A draft was presented by Qatari mediators last Sunday, and a response from Iran is expected by the weekend. The aim of the proposal is to avert a return to U.S. military action against Iran, which President Trump has threatened multiple times in recent weeks.

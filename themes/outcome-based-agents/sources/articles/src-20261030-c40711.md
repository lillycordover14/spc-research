The 
Harris Teeter
 supermarket chain has launched its Harris Teeter Rewards World Elite Mastercard, allowing cardholders to earn points on purchases where Mastercard is accepted. Points do not expire as long as the card account remains active and in good standing. The issuer is First Bank & Trust, and the program operator is Imprint Payments Inc.

Block Inc.’s Square
 point-of-sale technology unit earned a best-in-class ranking in Javelin Strategy & Research’s 2026 Small Business Point of Sale (POS) System Scorecard, which ranked 15 POS providers serving merchants with less than $1 million in yearly sales. Rounding out the top four are Shopify POS, Lightspeed, and Clover.

DNA Payments

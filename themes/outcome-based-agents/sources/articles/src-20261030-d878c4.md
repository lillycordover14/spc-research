It’s unfortunately easy to 
get duped by AI-generated content 
these days.

It’s especially unfortunate when the person getting duped does so on a public forum and for something that was best left said in private.

This is what happened to five-time NBA champion Ron Harper. 

An AI-generated UCLA volleyball player responding to a question about her favorite position. (X/@itslolamelone).

The 
father of Spurs star Dylan Harper
 and Celtics guard Ron Harper Jr. fell victim to an AI-generated video of a woman appearing to be a UCLA volleyball player.

In the video, 
the AI woman
, who goes by Lola Melone, was asked, “What’s your favorite position?”

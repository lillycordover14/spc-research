Understanding Anterior Uveitis and Its Causes  
Anterior uveitis is an inflammatory condition affecting the front part of the eyeâs middle layer, specifically the iris and the ciliary body nearby. This condition can be triggered by trauma such as a blow to the eye or a foreign object entering it. Additionally, anterior uveitis is often associated with systemic health issues like rheumatoid arthritis, syphilis, tuberculosis, sarcoidosis, and viral infections including herpes simplex, herpes zoster, and cytomegalovirus. In some cases, it occurs without any clear cause, known as idiopathic uveitis.

The Rising Impact of Autoimmune Diseases on Market Growth  
One of the main drivers behind the growth of the anterior uveitis market is the increasing prevalence of autoimmune diseases. Factors contributing to the rise of autoimmune conditions include genetic tendencies, environmental influences, lifestyle changes, and improved diagnostic awareness. Since anterior uveitis often develops as part of autoimmune diseases like rheumatoid arthritis and ankylosing spondylitis, rising autoimmune cases naturally boost the demand for treatments.

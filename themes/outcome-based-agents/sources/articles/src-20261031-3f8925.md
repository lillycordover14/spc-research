Operation Vivaldi
 threatens to cave in the entire Russian front aimed at the “Fortress Belt”. A cursory look at the Institute For The Study of War(ISW) map of October 8th, 2026 (see caption photo) shows at least 4 or more Russian salients ripe for the picking.

:

Ukrainian Armed Forces Have Surrounded Russian Forces In Yampol
 

According to Western sources, Ukrainian troops have crossed the Zherebets River in at least two places—near Nevskoye and near Zarechny. The northern route leads into the Luhansk region—the very same one that Putin has “conquered,” it seems, about five times. But I’m more interested in the southern breakthrough, near Zarechny. Because its goal, by all accounts, isn’t simply to penetrate as deep as possible into the rear, but to outflank and encircle from the rear the main Russian base for the entire region—the city of Yampol.

Geography is key here. Yampil is situated on the right, western bank of the Zherebets River. If I’m correct about the Ukrainian Armed Forces’ plan, they’re bypassing the city along the left bank to blockade it and cut it off from its main forces. This is also indicated by the fact that, at the same time, the Ukrainian offensive continues along the Seversky Donets from west to east—along the southern flank of Yampol itself. If everything goes according to plan, Ukrainian forces will encircle the city, with two rivers flanking it from the south and north.

It would mean that Russian troops gathering towards the cities would be difficult. Any push by Moscow towards Slovyansk may be “doomed” due to failure or high casualties if Russian forces cannot recapture forests south of the city of Lyman.

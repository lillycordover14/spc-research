Crosby is a player who has been strongly linked with a potential move to Dallas as his future in Las Vegas continues to be questioned. With the trade deadline just over a week away, the defensive end has now shared his answer on where his future lies. 
Maxx Crosby provides answer to Dallas Cowboys trade speculation
It has been a strange year for 
Crosby
, who was set to join the Baltimore Ravens during the offseason, having now endured a turbulent start to the season with the Raiders. Given the Raiders have lost four games on the bounce, Crosby’s future has been strongly debated. 
“Maxx Crosby wants to be a Raider, he has no desire to leave, and he is definitely not forcing his way out. But GM John Spytek is sure to get calls over the next 10 days, leaving open the possibility of another blockbuster,” said Tom Pelissero on Saturday morning.

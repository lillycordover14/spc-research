RECAP | Warriors notch Halloween Victory over Silvertips
By 
Elizabeth Black
October 31, 2026
MOOSE JAW, Sask. – 
The Warriors capped off their Halloween afternoon with a thrilling shootout victory against the Everett Silvertips to improve their record to 6-8. 
 

Following an early cross-checking penalty to defenceman Griffin Darby, the Warriors landed on the penalty kill. The team stood strong to keep the game scoreless. The Silvertips pulled out to an early lead following a goal from Hunter Meier. Seconds later, the Warriors took their turn on the power play, but were unable to break past the Silvertips. 

The Warriors pushed for the remainder of the period, but were not rewarded with a goal. The Warriors led the SIlvertips in shots through the first period, 15-13 and took a one-goal deficit into the first intermission. 

Early in the period, Griffin Darby sent home a shot from the blue line to notch his first goal as a Warrior. Hunter Rudolph retaliated minutes later to give the Silvertips back their narrow lead. With just over five minutes to play, Colt Carter was assessed a minor penalty for delay of game (puck over the glass) and the Warriors landed back on the penalty kill. The Warriors penalty kill stood strong again to keep the Silvertips’ lead from growing. 

In the dying seconds of the period, Riley Thorpe and Everett’s Jaxsin Vaughan were assessed matching roughing minors to send the teams into the final period playing four-on-four separated by one goal. 

In the final frame, with just moments remaining on the roughing minors to Thorpe and Vaughan, Everett’s Brek Liske was assessed a minor penalty for tripping, sending the Warriors to the power play. Out of the penalty box and immediately into the play, Riley Thorpe knocked home a power play goal to bring the Warriors even with more than 17 minutes to play. 

Late in the period, Casey Brown was assessed a minor penalty for high-sticking, sending the Warriors to the penalty kill with just three minutes remaining in a 2-2 hockey game. The Warriors penalty kill remained strong and kept the game even, requiring extra time. 

In the overtime period, Jaxsin Vaughan was assessed a two-minute penalty for holding, sending the Warriors to the power play. The Warriors couldn’t break through on the power play, and the teams remained tied. 

In the shootout, Mathieu Lajoie tallied the opening goal. Jordan Switzer held off Atte Vuori. In the second round, Rasmus Kamarainen sent home his third shootout goal of the season to give the Warriors the edge. With the game in his hands, Jordan Switzer stopped Brek Liske and the Warriors earned their third-shootout victory of the season. 

In net, Jordan Switzer made 34 saves on 36 shots, plus two saves in the shootout. Across the ice, Evan Enck made 32 saves on 34 shots. The Warriors went 1-for-4 on the power play and 3-for-3 on the penalty kill. 

The Warriors hit the road to British Coiumbia this week to take on the BC Division clubs. The team opens their road trip against the Prince George Cougars on Wednesday night. Follow along with all the action on Country 100 with James Gallo or watch for free on YouTube.

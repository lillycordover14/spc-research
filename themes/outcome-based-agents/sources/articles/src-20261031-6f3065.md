			Kathleen “Kathy” Ada Grady O’Halloran 		

			OBITUARY		

The death occurred at her home in Santa Rosa, California on October 8th of Kathleen (Kathy) Ada Grady O’Halloran. Her husband, Eamonn O’Halloran, and her son, Brian, were at her bedside when she passed peacefully.
Kathy is predeceased in death by her parents, Joan Place Grady of Middletown, Rhode Island and Raymond J Grady of Silver Springs, Maryland; by her sister-in-law, Kate Grady of Newport, Rhode Island and Joanne Sheibel of Barrington, Rhode Island.
Kathy is survived by her son Brian and her husband Eamonn, both of Santa Rosa, CA. Her sister Lisa Ann Grady (Neville) of Hollywood, Florida and her brother Dennis of Newport, Rhode Island. Her niece, Rachael Wladyka (Max) and great nieces Rose and Kathleen. Plus her cousins Beth, Maureen, Eileen and Billy from the Sheibel Family, Jay Dwyer, Sheila, Patty, KT and JulieMarie from The Sullivan Family and Kathy’s nieces and nephews in Ireland.
Kathy was born in Newport, Rhode Island and attended Parochial school at St. Lucy’s and High school in Middletown, RI. She had many interests in her formative years and developed lifelong friendships which she cherished. Her best friend Mary Ellen Nunes Helger and her family were Kathy’s second family during her teenage years. Many Memories were created in the Nunes home and time in Vermont, New Hampshire and trips across Canada in the seventies.
Kathy excelled at her studies and chose to move to Cumberland, Maryland to earn her prerequisites for her plan to train in a career in Dental Hygiene. She transferred to the Forsythe School of Dental Hygiene at Northeastern University in Boston where she graduated Suma Cum Laude in the early eighties. Working in Boston, she also travelled extensively in Europe visiting friends in France and Italy, Spain and Portugal. In the mid-eighties she decided to move to California. In 1986 she loaded up her car with two friends, Kitty Kerr and Caroline Morrow, and headed west. Recommended to try for work in North Bay, Kathy chose Santa Rosa to settle and began getting to know the area and the dental environment in which she thrived for the next 25 years.
In 1988 she met her future husband Eamonn and having married in 1994, they welcomed their son, Brian, in 1997. The picture was perfect and complete.
The arrival of illness came very gradually and with it brought great upheaval. Many years of not knowing were relieved by a diagnosis in 2013. With that in hand she picked up the pieces of what could have been a sad story and made it shine.
Through care and advocacy, Kathy’s final years were her triumph, she navigated all the confusion and uncertainty of her illness with great dignity. The anxiety and fear left her and the grace and serenity which she always possessed came to the fore. She was an active participant in her own care.
A visitation will take place on Friday, November 6th at Daniels Chapel of the Roses on Sonoma Ave, Santa Rosa, CA between 4:00pm and 7:00pm, with a rosary service at 6:00pm. Kathy’s funeral mass will take place at 11:00am at St. Joseph’s Church Cotati CA, on Saturday, November 7th.
In lieu of flowers, donations can be made to Sutter Hospice of Sonoma County or a charity of your choice.

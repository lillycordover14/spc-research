DRAFFENVILLE
 — Calloway County finished its second straight football regular season above the .500 mark
 
 Friday night with an impressive 48-6 running-clock knockout win over border rival Marshall County.
The win ends the regular season at 6-4 for the Lakers, the second year in a row they have compiled that mark. This is the first time since the late 2000s for a Calloway team to have two straight winning seasons.
Calloway also showed that it had more than learned its lesson from last year’s game with the Marshals in Murray. On that occasion, Marshall — in the same situation as Friday, playing its final game of the season with no postseason on the horizon — made a huge second-half comeback to take a late lead before Calloway earned its first winning season since 2016 with its own late comeback for a 45-40 win.
Friday night, there was none of that drama as the Lakers kept hammering the Marshals (2-8) after building a 21-0 halftime lead on an 18-yard touchdown run from quarterback Matthew Briedwell and his passes of 20 yards to Hunter Haugh and 17 yards to Jackson Wyatt.

Briedwell added a one-yard run in the third quarter that sent the score to 28-0 before Marshall quarterback Jesse Kinsall hit receiver Jasper Jones with an eight-yard strike to avert the shutout.
However, Calloway finished with power with a nine-yard screen pass to Wyatt being followed by Brayden Tupper’s two-yard run with 9:40 left in the game that triggered the running clock.
Walker Bell then took the quarterback spot and had a five-yard run that ended the scoring late in the final quarter.

We've all seen the silly electioneering being conducted by Democrats in Alaska. Can't win fairly then cheat is the official mission statement of the DNC these days. Although not as blatant, there is a great example of the same nonsense here in Utah. No one would really expect Peter Crosby to beat Blake Moore, but that doesn't stop Cache Valley Daily's own Charlie Schill trying by publishing a nonsensical article in support of Crosby. Just today we got another one that said 'Moderate Republican Group Endorses Peter Crosby.'
First of all, no true Republican - moderate or otherwise - would want to see the house go to the Democrats. It's nonsensical. Second, this group itself doesn't even call itself a group of moderate Republicans. They just say they're moderate. Adding the word Republican was Charlie's idea, and the purpose is obvious. He wants people to think that Republicans want a Democrat to represent them in Congress. It's not true. Charlie has written several articles in favor of Crosby. His intentions are obvious and a bit dumb.
Frank Pederson
Olympus Cove, UT

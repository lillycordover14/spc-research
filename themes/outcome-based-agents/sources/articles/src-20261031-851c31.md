The Kiwanis Club of La Cañada announced that its Benefit Art Auction and Artisan Marketplace will take place on Sunday, Nov. 8, from 1-5 p.m. at La Cañada Flintridge City Hall.
The community event brings together art enthusiasts, collectors, and holiday shoppers for an afternoon of shopping and bidding, all to raise funds for local youth scholarships and other community initiatives.
The marketplace will showcase a diverse selection of high-quality, handcrafted goods, including: contemporary fine art, handcrafted ceramics, vintage jewelry, artisanal wood carvings, custom succulent arrangements, apparel and La Cañada High School Students Art Show and Sale.
In addition to the artisan booths, the highlight of the event is a curated silent auction featuring collectible art. Bidders will have a rare opportunity to vie for pieces by world-renowned master Pablo Picasso and groundbreaking pop artist and original social justice pioneer Corita Kent, alongside works from other listed artists.
"We are incredibly proud to bring an event of this caliber to La Cañada Flintridge," said La Canada Kiwanis President Caroline Anderson. "Whether you are looking to acquire a piece of art history, find a unique holiday gift, or simply support our local artisans, this marketplace offers something for everyone. Best of all, a percentage of every dollar raised goes directly toward supporting the work of Kiwanis in our community."
Admission to the event is free and parking is available near City Hall. For more information about the event and sponsorship opportunities, visit 
LaCanadaKiwanis.org
 or contact event co-chair Maureen R. Siegel Sprowles at (818) 339-6639.

Woodlawn, NCHS notch inaugural 2026 wins before Halloween
 Jimmy Watson
Shreveport Times
Oct. 31, 2026, 12:34 p.m. CT
Two previously winless teams, Woodlawn and Natchitoches Central, secured their first victories of the season.
Natchitoches Central's win handed the previously undefeated Haughton Buccaneers their first loss.
Multiple teams, including North DeSoto and Calvary, maintained strong records with decisive wins.
We’re pretty sure some things happened about 
Northwest Louisiana football
 fields during the ninth week of action that no one saw coming.
For two, previously winless Woodlawn and Natchitoches Central notched their first victories of the season. Evangel quarterback Peyton Houston, who was slated to sit out this week with a shoulder injury, played and led his Eagles to a win. North DeSoto running back/safety Hayden Bell was sidelined with a minor injury as was BTW quarterback Devion Stewart – two key players possibly missing a game or two.
Here’s a look at the Friday contests:
Woodlawn 22, Bossier 6:
 The Knights (1-8) took advantage of the Bearkats (2-6) being beset with injuries at the quarterback position to get the win.
“I’m so excited for the kids,” first-year coach Lavelle Wilson Jr. said. “It’s all about them and how hard they’ve been working.”

Natchitoches Central 27, Haughton 14:
 The Chiefs (1-8) knocked the Buccaneers (8-1) from the unbeaten ranks with the win in Natchitoches. Kaden Kuykendall, Ronald Bolton, Blessed Brown and Derrick Beaudion scored for NCHS with Beaudion scoring on a 55-yard interception return.
“I’m proud for our players and coaches,” Coach Brad Laird said. “They stayed with the process, kept showing up and kept working.”
Haughton got a rushing TD by Nolan Simmons and a Taylor Weathersby to Ethan Johnson TD pass.
North DeSoto 35, BTW 20:
 Mason Smith completed 20 of 29 passes for 196 yards and a TD, while Steven Sims ran 26 times for 196 yards and 2 TDs for the Griffins (9-0) versus the Lions (6-3). Braylon Moore turned in 9 catches for 135 yards. The Griffins captured the District 1-4A title with the win.
Huntington 32, Benton 6:
 The Raiders (3-6) had an easy time with the Tigers (4-5) on the road. Jakorion Spencer scored twice on runs, while MJ Greene, Audacious Johnson and Darius Truvillion added scores.
Parkway 49, Byrd 35:
 Bryston McCoy passed for 306 yards and 3 TDs and Damien Dacaldacal rushed for 125 yards and two scores for the Panthers (5-4). Gary Burney caught 7 passes for 163 yards and two scores. For Byrd (1-8), Christian Maxie rushed 29 times for 264 yards and two scores while Collin Deere added 120 yards and two more scores.
Loyola 47, North Webster 0:
 The Flyers (6-3) won easily behind Bryce Restovich, who completed 20 of 25 passes for 232 yards and 3 TDs while JT Taylor rushed for 118 yards and a TD. Ty Walsworth returned a punt 75 yards for a score against the Knights (3-6).
Homer 68, Plain Dealing 0:
 Karson Crew returned a Lions’ fumble for a TD and Georgerick Colman added a pick-6 as the defense took shape for the Pelicans (4-5) against the Lions (1-8). Jerzyion Levingston and Antonio Casey scored twice apiece for Homer.
Lakeside 48, Red River 18:
 Malik Thorn rushed 25 times for 235 yards and 5 TDs for the Warriors (6-3) against the Bulldogs (1-8). James Roberson and Brandon Jackson also scored TDs for coach John Ware.
Haynesville 29, Cedar Creek 20:
 Moise Critton rushed for 92 yards and 2 TDs to lead the Tors (7-1) past the previously undefeated Cougars (8-1).
Ruston 49, Pineville 14:
 Sam Hartwell completed 9 of 11 passes for 201 yards and 3 TDs for the Bearcats (7-2) versus Pineville (1-9). Kohl Gray added a pair of rushing TDs for Ruston.

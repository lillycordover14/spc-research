The 
Egyptian Meteorological Authority
 (EMA) expects the weather on Saturday to be slightly hot to hot during the day across the northern parts of the country, extending to Greater Cairo and northern Upper Egypt, while remaining hot in southern Upper Egypt.

Temperatures are expected to rise on Monday, November 2, with the temperature in Greater Cairo reaching 32C during the day—an increase of up to 5C.

This rise will be temporary, with a sharp drop in temperatures set to begin by the end of this week.

The authority has warned against being misled by this rise in temperatures, noting that cool breezes are still being felt during the early morning and late night hours, with temperatures dropping across all regions.

During the night and early morning hours, the weather will grow cooler.

The EMA indicated a 30-percent chance of light rain, which may occasionally become moderate in areas along the northern coast, northern Lower Egypt, and parts of Matrouh desert.

There is also a very slight 10-percent chance of light rain in parts of the southern Lower Egypt and the Suez Canal cities, which may potentially extend to areas within Greater Cairo at intermittent intervals.

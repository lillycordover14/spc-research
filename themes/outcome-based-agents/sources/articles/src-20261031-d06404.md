WICHITA, Kan. (KAKE) — The parents of a 3-year-old Wichita girl who died after a tragic crash have donated $50,000 to the families impacted by the 
Stryker crash
 in northeast Wichita earlier this month.
Harper Ivy was in her mom's car at a gas pump at the QuikTrip in Derby in April 2023 when a car 
crashed into the pump
, igniting it. The explosion and fire killed Harper and severely burned her mother, Stephanie.
Her parents created the 
Harper Ivy Justice Foundation
, geared toward giving back to the community and local children.

“I have been truly astounded and humbled by the generosity of not only the McPherson community but also by our surrounding communities in the wake of the Stryker tragedy,” said Michelle Huddle, CEO of the McPherson County Community Foundation, in a Facebook post. “This was displayed to me again today when a generous couple who understand the loss of a child drove up from Wichita to deliver a $50,000 check to the McPherson County Community for the families who were impacted. Thank you to this sweet couple for continuing your daughter's legacy in such an amazing way.”
Saturday marks two weeks since police say a woman drove her truck into a youth soccer game in northeast Wichita, killing two and injuring 10 others. Cole Adrian, 6, and Nicholas Schafer, 35, both of McPherson, died at the scene.

Cole Adrian (left) and Nick Schafer

Family photo/GoFundMe

So far, 
no charges
 have been filed. Police have not said what led up to the crash or caused it, and have not released any updates on how the survivors are doing.

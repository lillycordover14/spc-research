POTTER COUNTY ARTISAN CENTER CLASSES AND EVENTS: NOVEMBER 2026
 

solomonswords

0 Comments
 

October 31, 2026

The Potter County Artisan Center celebrates the change in seasons with exciting holiday themed classes for adults and children.

Looking for a fun activity for your child to do on Friday, November 6 while off from school? We have just the experience!   Master Quilter, Brenda Bonczar, will be teaching a beginner sewing class for students, ages 9 and up.  This is a great opportunity for kids to learn to use a sewing machine while making a fun pillow with handles for carrying and a pocket for their favorite book (or snack!).  Great to use while traveling to visit family over the holidays or to be given as a Christmas gift.  Price of the class is $35.00. 

The Mini Young Artisan Class (Pre-school and Kindergarten) will be held on Wednesday, November 4, from 3:45-4:45. Students will practice their hand at painting a Thanksgiving painting with acrylic on canvas.  Price of the class is $15.00.  All fun, supplies and treats included. 

The Young Artisan Class will be held on Wednesday, November 11 from 3:15 – 4:30.  Students will be painting a Thanksgiving themed subject on canvas.   Price of the class is $25.00 and includes all materials. Come and celebrate with creativity, treats and fun!

Cathy Snyder will be teaching two classes on how to make a Hiker or Foraging Basket on Saturday, November 14 at 9:30 am until finished and again on November 16 and 17 from 4:30 till 7:30.   Students are welcome to bring water and snack.  Space is limited, so please register by calling the center.  Specify basket and color of reed.   

Laurie Angood will teach a Watercolor Class for ages 14 and up on Friday, November 13 from 6 – 8 pm. Students have the option of using the pre-designed painting titled ‘Thankful’ or designing their own. Artist quality transparent watercolor and ink will be used in this lesson.  Price of the class is $35.00. Appropriate for beginners and experienced artists. 

Want to arrange a fun holiday event for your group or family?  Contact the Artisan Center to inquire about scheduling a creative private class with one of our talented local artisans. Here you can learn a new skill, create something unique, and enjoy a hands-on experience.  Perfect for individuals, families, groups and celebrations. 

To register for these classes or events, please call the Potter County Artisan Center on 814-274-8165.  The Center is located at 227 N. Main Street.  The Center’s’ hours are Monday through Saturday 9:00-5:00. 

Events and classes are supported in part by the Pennsylvania Creative Industries and the Elk County Council on the Arts, state agencies funded by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.

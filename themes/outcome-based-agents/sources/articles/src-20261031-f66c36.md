FARGO — Many residents have already cast their ballots ahead of Election Day. But for those who haven't, here's where you can vote in the general election on Tuesday, Nov. 3.
In Cass County, all polling locations are open from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.

In North Dakota, voters must be at least 18 years old, a U.S. citizen and have lived in North Dakota for 30 days prior to an election.
To vote, voters must have a valid identification showing the voter's name, current residential address and date of birth. Acceptable forms of identification include a driver's license, nondriver's identification card, tribal identification or long-term care identification certificate, according to the county's website.
Those who are unable to update their current address before Election Day must also bring a document such as a lease, utility bill or bank statement showing their current address.
More information about how voters can verify their voting information and address can be found at 
    
      
vote.nd.gov.

For somebody to vote in Minnesota, they must be a U.S. citizen, at least 18 years old and a resident of Minnesota for 20 days, according to the Minnesota Secretary of State. They also cannot be currently incarcerated for a felony conviction and must not be under a court order revoking their right to vote.
Voter registration is required in Minnesota. Clay County residents can register ahead of the election online or in person at the Clay County Government Center, 3510 12th Ave. S. Voters can also register at their polling place on Election Day with proper identification. Clay County lists acceptable forms of identification at 
    
      
claycountymn.gov/2014/Register-to-Vote.

More information about voting in Minnesota, including voter registration, is available at 
    
      
sos.mn.gov/elections-voting.

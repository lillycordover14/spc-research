Shraddha Bhatt Dave Takes Over As EMA India Chief Financial Officer
1 min read
EMAINDIA
The appointment takes effect from 30 October following the company's takeover by new management and the resignation of its previous CFO
EMA India has appointed Shraddha Bhatt Dave as its Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and Key Managerial Personnel (KMP), effective 30 October 2026. The appointment follows the recent takeover of the company by new management and the resignation of the previous CFO.
The Board of Directors approved the appointment at a meeting held on Friday, 30 October 2026, based on recommendations from the Audit Committee and the Nomination and Remuneration Committee. The board noted that the company had been identifying a suitable candidate to fill the vacancy created by the former CFO's departure.
Shraddha Bhatt Dave is a Chartered Accountant with over 15 years of experience in financial governance, accounting, audit assurance and regulatory compliance within multinational environments.
Her professional experience includes managing statutory audits, Goods and Services Tax (GST) and tax compliance, as well as cross-border financial reporting under Indian and US Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) standards.
She has also worked on strengthening internal control frameworks, identifying financial irregularities and improving transparency in finance operations.
In the same board meeting, the directors accepted the resignation of Awashesh Dixit as Secretarial Auditor. Dixit, a Practising Company Secretary holding Membership No. F10860 and Certificate of Practice (COP) No. 15398, cited his inability to continue in the role. His cessation was effective 30 October 2026.
Regulatory Disclosure Details
The company disclosed the appointments and resignation under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

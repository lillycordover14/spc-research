By

				November 1, 2026			

The Beaches Artisan Market takes place today (Sunday, Nov. 1) in Kew Gardens park.

The Queen Street Market presents its November Beaches Artistan Market today.

Taking place in Kew Gardens on Sunday, Nov. 1, the market will be open from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. and is free to attend.

Along with numerous vendors offering handmade goods, vintage treasures, art and more, there will also be face painting for the kids. Area residents are invited to shop local and support local artisans at this market.

Kew Gardens is located at 2075 Queen Street East.

For more information about today’s market, please visit

Boise, Idaho (CBS2) — 
Early voting is drawing tens of thousands of voters in Ada County ahead of the 2026 midterm elections, according to new figures released by the Ada County Elections Office.
The office reported 57,581 early voters so far. It also said 58,657 absentee ballots have been issued, with 41,129 absentee ballots returned at a 70.1% rate of return. 
The early voting period took place from  Oct. 19th through Oct. 30th. All absentee ballots requested must be returned back by mail or returned to one of the Ada County drop box locations. 
Ballots must be returned by 8 p.m. on Election Day in order to be counted.

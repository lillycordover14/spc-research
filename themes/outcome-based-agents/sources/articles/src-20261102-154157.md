Anti-Acne Brand Face Reality Introduces Age Adaptive Clearing Serum
 

		Clinically tested retinoid innovation combats adult acne in aging skin.	

November 2, 2026
 

By: 
Lianna Albrizio

Associate Editor
, Happi 
 

Save

The new Age Adaptive Clearing Serum, left, can be applied after the brand’s Active Clarity Gel Cleanser and Omni Clear Mist. 

Face Reality Skincare
 knows the frustrations of battling acne and caring for aging skin.

That’s why, the brand has developed its new Age Adaptive Clearing Serum to help the 81.3 million people in midlife struggling to find the right balance in hydrating their skin while combating stress-related and hormonal acne. The brand’s first clinical-grade, multi-layered retinoid serum is clinically tested and formulated for acne-prone and aging skin. Powered by patent-pending Beta-ret (β-ret) Technology, it maintains clear skin while helping target early signs of aging in one step.

Targeting Acne at the Root

Stress, pregnancy, perimenopause and other hormonal shifts can trigger breakouts at exactly the point adults begin addressing early signs of aging, according to brand officials. While retinoids have long been a cornerstone of anti-aging skincare, Face Reality says they empathize with the “frustrating” tradeoff consumers face between managing breakouts and addressing visible signs of aging given the richness in anti-aging formulas, which can clog pores and lead to breakouts.

“Our certified skincare professionals have spent more than two decades helping clients get clear and stay clear,” said Face Reality CEO Jeremy Soine. “Age Adaptive Clearing Serum is a clinically tested innovation that lets them take a client further – addressing early signs of aging without putting the clear skin they worked so hard for at risk. We are proud to give our skincare professionals and their clients a product that serves them at every stage.”

Three-Level Serum

Age Adaptive Clearing Serum works on three levels of the skin in a single step. At the surface, alpha hydroxy acids (AHAs) are L-mandelic and lactic acids that gently exfoliate to support smoother-looking skin while staying tolerable for acne-prone and sensitized skin. In the pore Beta-ret (β-ret) Technology, salicylic acid actively maintains clear skin, helping keep pores clear of congestion and reducing the conditions that lead to future breakouts. Over time Beta-ret (β-ret) Technology, a patent-pending retinoid supports cellular renewal that helps target signs of early aging. Beta-ret (β-ret) Technology is Face Reality’s proprietary clearing and renewal complex, combining salicylic acid with a “next-generation” retinoid derivative. It is designed to be less irritating than traditional retinoids and to integrate into existing acne-prone, aging skincare routines.

Clinical Studies

In clinical testing, the serum was shown to clinically improve acne severity by 12 weeks. Ninety-seven percent of panelists saw clearer skin after 12 weeks; 94% showed clinical improvement in fine lines and wrinkles after eight weeks; and 93% saw smoother skin in the same period.

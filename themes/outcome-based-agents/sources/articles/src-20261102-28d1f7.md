Prime Minister Andy Burnham and his wife Marie-France Van Heel attended a football match between Everton and Newcastle United on Thursday evening. Spotted in the stands, the duo looked smart as they keenly watched Burnham's team, Everton, score a 3-1 victory. 
For the outing, Burnham wore a navy suit and his signature square glasses with a poppy pinned to his lapel, in memory of military service members. As for Van Heel, she is fast becoming known for her flair for style, always hitting the perfect balance of elevated and laid-back, depending on the occasion. So, it's of no surprise that her outfit for the football was pitch-perfect.
Van Heel made a wise choice for the declining temperatures, opting for a knitted jumper with a a high collar. Featuring a quarter-length zip and ribbed cuffs, which she neatly folded up, the jumper felt modern and trendy, while its cream hue lifted the overall aesthetic, making it appear elevated and sleek. It's the kind of cold weather staple you can swiftly throw on at a moment's notice and instantly look put-together. 
Robbie Jay Barratt - AMA
In addition to her chic knit, Van Heel wore an array of dazzling jewellery that gave a modern edge to the look. Twisted gold hoops, plenty of rings and and a large, chunky chain stood out against the cream jumper. Tying everything together with almond-shaped, burgundy nails and her bright blonde locks pulled back, she provided plenty of winter outfit inspiration for the months ahead.

Robin Birchall, Chief Executive Officer of Serval, commented:
 
 
"We are delighted that shareholders have voted overwhelmingly in favour of the resolutions today which allow for the completion of our recent fundraise. This positive result is in recognition of the fact that it is a transformational deal for the Company, which will allow for a large-scale drilling programme to test our highest priority targets in Namibia's Kaoko Basin, as well as advancing our projects in Botswana's Kalahari Copper Belt and the Duékoué project in Côte d'Ivoire. I would like to thank our shareholders for their support and we look forward to reporting on our progress over the coming months."
 
 
 
 
 
Admission and Total Voting Rights
 
 
Application has been made to the London Stock Exchange for the admission to trading on AIM for the 14,180,000 Conditional Placing Shares, the 2,182,000 Subscription Shares and the 2,075,448 Retail Offer Shares (together, the "
Conditional Fundraise Shares"
) ("
Admission"
). It is expected that Admission of the Conditional Fundraise Shares will become effective and that dealings will commence in the Conditional Fundraise Shares on AIM at 8.00 a.m. on 3 November 2026. The Conditional Fundraise Shares will, when issued, be credited as fully paid and rank 
pari passu
 with the Company's existing Ordinary Shares.
 
 
Following Admission, the Company will have 62,048,350 Ordinary Shares in issue, each carrying one vote, and 8,053,725 Deferred Shares, which carry no voting rights. The Company does not hold any shares in treasury. Accordingly, following Admission, the total number of voting rights in the Company will be 62,048,350 and Shareholders may use this figure as the denominator for the calculations by which they will determine if they are required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, the Company under the Financial Conduct Authority's Disclosure and Transparency Rules.

About Serval Resources
 
 
Serval Resources (AIM: SRVL) is an AIM-listed explorer focused on copper and associated future metals critical to the global energy transition and digital economy. The company is building a portfolio of exploration and development assets in the emerging copper belts of Namibia, Botswana and Côte d'Ivoire, aiming to become a leading mid-cap player by applying modern, systematic exploration techniques in underexplored but highly prospective African regions.

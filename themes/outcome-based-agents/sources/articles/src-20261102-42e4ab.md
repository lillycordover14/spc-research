CLAY COUNTY, Mo.
 —
Voters in Clay County, Missouri, are heading out to the polls in big numbers ahead of tomorrow's midterm election. 
After 11 days of advance voting, 29,545 voters have cast their ballots. 
Advertisement
That puts early voting numbers in the county up 145% compared to the 2022 midterm election. 
Only one location in Clay County is open for advance voting today. 
Voters can head to the Clay County Elections Office in Liberty, Missouri. 
Polls are open from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

OKLAHOMA CITY — 
The Journal Record
 reporter 
Sam Royka
 joined 
KFOR
‘s The Oklahoma Business Report to discuss a Tulsa engineer and burn survivor who is developing adaptive fitness technology to help people with disabilities.

During the segment, Royka shared the story of Kevon Martorell, an engineer at Eastech Flow Controls Inc., who survived severe burns and later developed a platform designed to make fitness more accessible. The software allows users to customize workouts based on their physical conditions and mobility limitations, offering a library of exercises, stretches and recipes tailored to individual needs.

Watch the full interview below to learn more about Martorell’s journey and how his experience is helping shape new opportunities for adaptive athletes.

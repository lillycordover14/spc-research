The winter holidays are just around the corner and that means it’s time to get started on those holiday gift lists! And why not enjoy a little holiday magic while you’re at it?

This December, Vinings Jubilee is introducing their first-ever Holiday Market full of amazing local goods, fun live music, holiday celebrations and more! And the best part? It’s totally FREE to enter! Keep scrolling to learn more.

Vinings Jubilee Holiday Market

Vinings Jubilee

Jubilee’s first-ever Holiday Market will bring tons of holiday cheer to the ATL this year! Market-goers can expect:

local makers & artisan crafters

live jazz performances

festive refreshments

visits with Santa

And so much more! Don’t miss out on this wonderful afternoon of holiday shopping and gathering with friends. Guests can browse for gifts before making a stop at a favorite Jubilee shop or restaurant. See ya there, Atlanta!

Okay I’ve gotta check this out! Details please!

Vinings Jubilee

When
: Saturday, December 12 from 1–4 p.m.

Where
: Vinings Jubilee Clocktower area | 4300 Paces Ferry Road, Vinings, Georgia 30339

Market admission is 
free
. All ages are welcome!

What other fun holiday happenings are coming to Vinings Jubilee?

Vinings Jubilee

The annual Christmas Tree lighting

Where

Chanukah Festival and Menorah Lighting

When

Where
: Vinings Jubilee Clocktower area | 4300 Paces Ferry Road, Vinings, Georgia 30339

hands-on Chanukah crafts, face painting, seasonal treats and refreshments.

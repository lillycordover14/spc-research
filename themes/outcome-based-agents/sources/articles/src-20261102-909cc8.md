Dynamat Taps EMA To Be Midwest Representative&nbsp;&nbsp;CES | Twice

[page text]
Dynamat has appointed Electronic Marketing Associates (EMA) as its home, architectural, and custom integration representative across Missouri, Iowa, Illinois, Nebraska and Kansas.

EMA will support Dynamat’s dealer, 
distributor
, integrator and custom installer relationships throughout the five-state territory, helping customers select sound-deadening and sound-encapsulation solutions for residential and commercial projects.

“EMA brings strong relationships and product knowledge, and a boots-on-the-ground approach we are looking for,” said Pat Johnson, National Sales Director at Dynamat. “We’re excited to have them representing Dynamat across the MIINK states and look forward to growing our presence in these important markets.”

Screenshot

Dynamat’s product lineup includes Dynamat Xtreme, Dynil, DynaCore, DynaPad, DynaBox and EnWall. The products are designed to address noise, vibration, heat, and unwanted sound in residential and commercial environments.

Based in the Midwest market, EMA has represented consumer electronics and home theater manufacturers since 1972. Led by principal Matt Selecman, the company provides product representation, dealer support, and technology guidance to specialty audio/video retailers and custom installers.

“We’re excited to work with Dynamat and their incredible team! Dynamat offers a world-class product that is proudly made right here in America,” said Matt Selecman, Principal at Electronic Marketing Associates. “We’re looking forward to bringing the Dynamat product line—and the sound encapsulation solutions they created—to integrators, dealers, and installers throughout the Midwest. Great products. A great team. Made in America. We’re excited for what’s ahead!”

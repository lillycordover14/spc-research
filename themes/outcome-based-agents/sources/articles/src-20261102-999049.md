SOUTH BEND, Ind. (WSBT) — 
The South Bend school board will meet Monday and is expected to take a vote on how to proceed on the future of the Clay High School building.
The regularly scheduled public meeting will feature a vote that could determine whether the district will fight a court order to turn over the building to a charter school.
Both the school board and South Bend Community School Corporation superintendent Mansour Eid will address the Clay High School building situation at 5:30 p.m. at the Brown Administration Building.
A court ordered last week that South Bend Schools must sell or lease Clay High to Career Academy.
That charter school had been trying to take possession of the building, citing a state law that says a verified charter school can take any vacant public school building for $1.
Career academy leadership says it wants to open Clay as its high school in the 2028-29 school year.
The Academy says it has outgrown its space, and hopes to add more athletics and fine arts to its offerings.

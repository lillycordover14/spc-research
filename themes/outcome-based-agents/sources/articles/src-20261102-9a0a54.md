Craft Buddy releases Li’l Worlds miniature model kits and Book Nooks&nbsp;&nbsp;Toy World Magazine

[page text]
The collection brings miniature model making to crafters of all ages, with projects ranging from quick builds to detailed creations.

Tiny details, cosy scenes and bookshelf displays have become one of the biggest craft trends of recent years, with Book Nooks and miniature model kits captivating hobbyists across social media. Craft Buddy is making the trend more accessible than ever with the launch of Li’l Worlds, a brand-new collection of miniature model kits and Book Nooks.

Available now, Li’l Worlds has been designed for everyone from first-time makers to experienced crafters, with projects ranging from quick builds that take just a few minutes to more detailed creations for those who enjoy spending hours on a project.

The collection includes Mini Rooms, Scene Boxes and Book Nooks, each designed to create eye-catching miniature scenes that can be displayed on shelves, desks or bookcases long after they’ve been completed.

The first collection features a mix of globally recognised licences, including Harry Potter, Hello Kitty and the fantasy artwork of Anne Stokes, alongside original Craft Buddy creations, giving collectors plenty of worlds to discover.

Gary Wadhwani, Co-Founder of Craft Buddy, commented: “We’ve seen a huge rise in interest in miniature worlds and Book Nooks, but many of the kits available can be quite complicated or time-consuming, particularly for beginners. With Li’l Worlds, we wanted to create a collection that anyone could enjoy, whether they’re discovering miniature model making for the first time or already love crafting. From quick, fun projects to more detailed builds, there’s something for everyone.

“We’re particularly excited to bring much-loved characters and licences into the collection, alongside our own original designs. We want people to enjoy the process of making something themselves and then have a beautiful miniature world they’re proud to display.”

Li’l Worlds has been created with accessibility in mind. Suitable for ages 6 through to adults, the range offers a variety of projects and price points, making it easy to discover the rewarding hobby of miniature model making.

As interest in mindful hobbies and display-worthy crafts continues to grow, Li’l Worlds combines creativity, collectability and home décor in one range, giving makers projects they can enjoy building before proudly displaying the finished result.

Li’l Worlds is available now, with RRPs ranging from £6.99 to £39.99. The collection is available at

Tee Higgins is on pace for a historic season
Higgins continues to dominate.
by
 
 
Ethan DeWitt
Nov 2, 2026, 11:30 AM UTC
Getty Images
The Cincinnati Bengals pulled off an overtime win on Sunday as they knocked off the Tennessee Titans.
Led by the offense, the team fought back from three Joe Burrow interceptions to pull off the much-needed win. Now firmly set on the right side of the playoff bubble, as 
The Athletic gives the team an 84% chance to make the playoffs
, it was Ja’Mar Chase and Tee Higgins who helped the Bengals pull off Sunday’s win.
Higgins continued his impressive season for the team as he is now on a historic pace after posting 10 catches for 155 yards and two touchdowns.
That pace? 104 catches for 1763 yards and 10 touchdowns. Would only be done for the 6th time ever in NFL history.
People talk a lot about how this Bengals squad has two No. 1 receivers in Chase and Higgins. Now with Tee healthy, we are seeing that sentiment to be true as they both continue to dominate. They have the offense to compete for a Super Bowl. Now all we can hope is that the defense finds a pulse to complement this team’s offense.

Wine bar in Essex set to close following New Year's Eve 'crescendo'&nbsp;&nbsp;Yahoo

[page text]
A 
wine bar that opened in Essex
 more than five years ago announced over the weekend that it will close following what the owner refers to as a "crescendo" on New Year's Eve.
Salt & Bubbles Wine Bar and Market 
opened in June 2021
 at the Essex Experience complex. Founder Kayla Silver honed her wine experience at the Dedalus wine shop and Honey Road restaurant in Burlington.
Along with serving what its website refers to as "ethically made wines," Salt & Bubbles put together food such as sandwiches, marinated olives, charcuterie plates and other dishes intended to complement the wines.

"It's really meant to offer two different experiences, which I think this area was really in need of," Silver told the Burlington Free Press soon after opening Salt & Bubbles. "This kind of dining experience just wasn't happening here."
Kayla Silver, owner/manager of Salt & Bubbles Wine Bar and Market, holds a burrata plate at the Essex business July 14, 2021.
Vermont Pub & Brewery, Bluebird Barbecue also closing
A 
Nov. 1 social media post
 signed "Kayla & the Salt & Bubbles Team" says the business "will cease on the bubbliest of days: New Year's Eve." The message thanked customers and staff before moving on to reasons behind the closure.
"The truth is, yes, times are hard on small businesses, but from the bottom of my heart I feel you have all supported us and then some," the post reads. "If I'm being honest, vulnerable, and getting to the heart of it, focusing on my family, my son, and myself is what sits at the forefront of my mind each day. And sadly, those priorities don't mesh with the daily grind it takes to run small businesses right now."
The past week has been a tough one on the Chittenden County food-and-drink scene. Two longtime Burlington favorites, 
Vermont Pub & Brewery
 and
 Bluebird Barbecue
, announced as October ended that they were concluding their runs.

Salt & Bubbles' social media post expressed hope that the business lives on if "someone else sees the space we've built and has a vision of their own. We welcome those folks to reach out. We believe we've created something truly special here, and we'd love to see someone carry that spirit forward, continuing to be a home for good drink, good food, and good company."

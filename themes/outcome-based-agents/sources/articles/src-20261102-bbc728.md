OXFORD, Miss. –
 In the fading October light of Vaught-Hemingway Stadium, the trenches are not a place for the timid. It is a violent, choreographed theater where games are won and lost in the brutal poetry of leverage and footwork. For Ty McCurry, a 6-foot-6, 295-pound sophomore out of Texas powerhouse Southlake Carroll, the trenches in Oxford are beginning to look an awful lot like home.

McCurry,
 
ranked as the No. 59 overall offensive tackle in the 2028 class by 247Sports, made his return to the Magnolia State this weekend to watch 
Ole Miss
 methodically dismantle Auburn 45-15. This wasn’t the Texas trench warrior’s first foray into the Grove this autumn; he was also on hand to witness the Rebels outlast the Bayou Bengals of LSU earlier in the season.

The draw for a young lineman of McCurry’s pedigree isn’t just the pageantry. It’s the schematics. He possesses a heavy-handed, aggressive point-of-attack presence combined with the nimble footwork required to execute quick sets in pass protection. He watches the game through a technician’s lens, analyzing block-sustain mechanics and inside zone concepts.

“
Getting back to Oxford to spend time with the coaches and really watch the line work was a huge priority for me. I continue to be impressed with how Coach Garrison and Coach Metcalf lead the guys. I think my style of play with my fast twitch and aggression fits really well into their scheme.
“

Ty McCurry on the Ole Miss o-line and coaches

Against Auburn, offensive line coach John Garrison and assistant coach Terrence Metcalf orchestrated a masterclass in protection. The Rebels built a clean pocket, allowing quarterback Trinidad Chambliss to operate with surgical precision without yielding a single sack. The pre-snap communication and post-snap adjustments against third-down pressure packages were exactly what a high-level recruit looks for on film.

“
I loved how aggressive the line played. They gave Trinidad a lot of time, allowed zero sacks, and were awesome on the third downs. I also loved seeing freshmen get opportunities
.”

Ty McCurry

For a prospect, the recruiting trail can be an exhausting carousel of flattering pitches and gilded promises. Yet, beneath the fast-twitch athleticism is a young man seeking something more enduring than just a depth chart slot. He is looking for a foundation, a program that builds men as effectively as it builds draft stock.

“
It’s very important to me to find one home for my college career. I want to play for the best who will push me and develop me for the next level. That’s number one. But I also want a home I will be proud to bring my future family back to. And Ole Miss checks every single box.
“

Ty McCurry

The battle for his signature is only in its nascent stages with 31 offers and counting, and a prospect with McCurry’s devastating punch and fluid hips will have his pick of college football’s cathedrals.

“
I’m very blessed to have amazing offers, and I am going to go through the entire recruiting process. But Ole Miss is a definite priority for me and it would be a privilege to play for the Rebs!
“

Ty McCurry

Until the ink dries, the suitors will keep calling. But as the autumn winds sweep through Oxford, the foundation is clearly being poured, one brick at a time, and the Texas pipeline is alive and well here in the ‘Sip.

SGMC Health Welcomes Kelsey Bree Harper, MD, OBGYN to Medical Staff 

Posted on:
 November 2nd, 2026
 

SGMC Health is pleased to welcome Kelsey Bree Harper, MD, an obstetrician-gynecologist to its medical staff. Dr. Harper will provide obstetric and gynecologic care to patients at SGMC Health while continuing to care for patients at Southern OB/GYN in Valdosta.

Dr. Harper provides comprehensive women’s healthcare through every stage of life, with clinical interests including obstetrics, pediatric and adolescent gynecology, minimally invasive robotic surgery, contraception, and family planning. 

Her approach to care focuses on creating an environment where patients feel comfortable asking questions and actively participating in decisions about their health. Through open communication and personalized care, Dr. Harper strives to help patients feel respected, informed, and confident throughout their healthcare journey.

A South Georgia native, Dr. Harper was born and raised in Sparks, Georgia. She earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Georgia before receiving her medical degree from Mercer University School of Medicine. She went on to complete her residency in Obstetrics and Gynecology at Prisma Health/University of South Carolina in Columbia, South Carolina.

“South Georgia has always been home to me, so it is incredibly meaningful to care for women and families in the community that shaped me,” said Dr. Harper. “I look forward to building lasting relationships with my patients and supporting them through every stage of life.”

With deep roots in the region, Dr. Harper is proud to care for women and families throughout Valdosta and the surrounding communities.

Outside of medicine, Dr. Harper enjoys cheering on the Georgia Bulldogs, exploring antique shops, traveling, and spending quality time with family and friends.

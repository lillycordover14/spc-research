 and her husband, UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham, watched from the stands as Everton claimed victory over Newcastle on Thursday night, with Van Heel bundling up against the cold in an elegant quarter-zip jumper. 
With her hair swept back into her now-signature soft bun, the entrepreneur added a feminine edge to her look with swathes of chunky gold jewellery, while her 
berry-red manicure 
provided a sophisticated pop of colour.

It goes without saying that a polished knit is a prized garment when it comes to dressing at this time of year, and while navy, black and burgundy tend to be the fashion set's hues of choice, Van Heel proved that optic white is an equally chic alternative – and, contrary to popular belief, needn’t be relegated to your wardrobe's depths once summer fades. Indeed, 
winter whites
 can really hold their own during the colder months (just be careful with that glass of red wine). 
A blank slate, sartorially speaking, the magic of white is that it’s incredibly easy to style and always adds a touch of elegance. While versatile enough for both evening and day wear, white really comes into its own after sunset when a little extra opulence is required; there’s a reason it’s synonymous with formal dress codes.
Moritz Scholz
//
Getty Images
But Van Heel’s 
style
 of jumper was also integral to her nonchalantly cool look. Tapping into one of this year’s biggest knitwear trends, the marketing executive opted for a quarter-zip over, say, a turtleneck or V-neck. Quarter-zip styles have soared in popularity over the last year since memorably making an appearance on Chanel’s pre-fall 2026 runway. 
Matthieu Blazy placing the quarter-zip knit front and centre in his first Métiers d'art show was no coincidence – he clearly understands its high-low appeal. Casual enough to be a wardrobe staple but with enough structure to also be considered smart, the quarter-zip pairs perfectly with everything from jeans and tailored trousers to mini skirts and dresses (stylish women have been slinging theirs over silk slips for an effortless take on eveningwear). A do-it-all knit if ever there was one.

The Brief
 
Gov. Ron DeSantis approved a death warrant for inmate Ricardo Gonzalez, setting his execution for Dec. 1 at Florida State Prison.
The 56-year-old was condemned for fatally shooting North Miami Beach Police Officer Steven Bauer during a 1992 bank robbery.
If completed, Gonzalez will mark Florida's 21st execution of the year, continuing a historic pace across the state.
 
STARKE, Fla.
 - 
A death row inmate will die by lethal injection Dec. 1 for gunning down a North Miami Beach police officer during an armed bank robbery more than three decades ago.
 
 
 
Gov. Ron DeSantis signed the death warrant Friday for 56-year-old Ricardo Gonzalez, setting the stage for what could be the state's 21st execution of the year at Florida State Prison.
 
Deadly Bank Robbery Ambush
 
 
The backstory:
 
The fatal shooting unfolded Jan. 3, 1992, as North Miami Beach Police Officer Steven Bauer walked bank tellers toward drive-thru windows at Kislak National Bank.
 
Gonzalez and three codefendants ambushed the group and robbed a cash box from a drive-thru teller.
 
Fatal Gunshot to Chest
 
What we know:
 
Detectives stopped Gonzalez at his home a little over two weeks after the bank robbery.
 
He gave both unrecorded and recorded confessions to detectives.
 
Gonzalez claimed he aimed low and believed he only struck Bauer in the leg.
 
Investigators later confirmed Gonzalez's gun delivered the deadly bullet that pierced the officer's chest.
 
Court records show Gonzalez grabbed the fallen money tray off the ground immediately after shooting Bauer.
 
A sentencing order signed by Circuit Court Judge Robert Scola, Jr. on Sept. 19, 1998, states: "Later he received $1,500 as his share of the proceeds from the robbery/murder."
 
Gonzalez was convicted of first-degree murder of a law enforcement officer, armed robbery, aggravated assault, two counts of burglary and two counts of grand theft.
 
A neuropsychologist testified Gonzalez operated under extreme mental or emotional disturbance caused by marital struggles, shifting childhood living arrangements among relatives, language barriers, impulsive behavior and brain trauma from boxing.
 
Unscheduled Execution Details
 
What we don't know:
 
Authorities have not confirmed whether Gonzalez will exhaust remaining legal appeals to halt the scheduled lethal injection before next month.
 
Officials also have not announced death warrant dates for codefendants Leonardo Franqui and Pablo San Martin, who remain among 235 inmates housed on Florida's death row as of Monday.
 
Rising Execution Counts
 
By the numbers:
 
32:
 Total prisoners put to death across six states so far this year, with Texas trailing Florida at seven executions.
21:
 Projected total executions conducted by Florida if two upcoming lethal injections scheduled for Thursday and Nov. 19 proceed ahead of Gonzalez.
19:
 Executions carried out by the Florida Department of Corrections last year, setting the previous single-year high since the U.S. Supreme Court lifted its capital punishment ban in 1976.
6:
 States that have carried out death sentences this year, including Florida, Texas, Alabama, Tennessee, Oklahoma and Arizona.
 
Lethal Injection Scheduled
 
What's next:
 
Gonzalez remains scheduled to be executed at Florida State Prison on Dec. 1.
 
Prior to his date, two other condemned inmates face execution dates set for Thursday and Nov. 19.

When it comes to top-dollar zip codes, Connecticut’s among the top, according to one list.

PropertyShark
 ranked 
the top 100 most expensive zip codes in the U.S.,
 with seven belonging to the Nutmeg state.

Taking the top spot for Connecticut and 45th overall is Greenwich’s 06870 zip code, according to PropertyShark, with an average closed sale price of $3.04 million. Three other Greenwich zips landed in the top 100 as well: 06870 at $2.83 million, 06830 at $2.56 million, and 06831 at $2.5 million.

This makes Greenwich the second most expensive community to live in across the U.S., “surpassed only by Newport Beach with six ranked zips,” according to PropertyShark.

Connecticut Ranks More Zips but Massachusetts Keeps Pricing Edge. (Photo: PropertyShark)

Darien, Norwalk and New Canaan filled out the remainder of Connecticut’s representation, ranking 60th, 65th, and 81st respectively, according to PropertyShark.

Check out these additional highlights for the Nutmeg state below, courtesy of PropertyShark:

Six of Connecticut’s seven zips in the top 100 reached record prices, led by Greenwich’s 06878 (#45), the first zip in state to surpass $3M

Old Greenwich’s 06870 (#54) and Central Greenwich’s 06830 (#71) set new record highs at $2.83M and $2.56M, respectively

Greenwich remained the second most expensive community in the U.S., with four zips in the top 100, surpassed only by Newport Beach with six ranked zips

15 states ranked zip codes in the top 100, with Connecticut just one of 11 states to reach new record prices

As for the most expensive zip code overall, the honor went to Atherton, California, according to PropertyShark, with a staggering $10.73 million. It was the only zip code in the U.S. to pass the $10 million mark.

Following a massive 6.3 billion hack on 
Binance
, 
Bitcoin
 has fallen almost 5% overnight on October 29th and is now testing the 200-Day EMA at $76,445.

Along with the 50-week EMA at 78,457.99, which has already been lost, both EMA settings have been major pivot points in the past for the king of cryptocurrency.

Simple read: 

1. Bounce here and rally at the 200-day EMA 
(in purple)
, and Bitcoin maintains its bullish posture.

2. Lose this area, however, and Bitcoin returns to a more bearish posture.

Broader level read:
 Bitcoin is still holding 
its multiyear rising logarithmic channel
 and 200-week EMA area (Bollinger Band set to 0.25 standard deviation).

Historically, since 2019, the asset has found support here 4 out of 5 times after initially breaking it to the upside.

Does that mean that the low is currently in?

No. We still have bigger concerns in the picture. First off, today is the midterm election for the United States. Bitcoin has 
historically fallen in the first 6 months after midterms
, unlike the 
S&P 500
.

Secondly, the BITBO NUPL chart is still not flashing a capitulation signal, where most Bitcoin holders are sitting in a loss and that usually signals a low is in.

Both these signals combined signal to me that it is not out of the picture for 
BTCUSD to break down from the weekly log channel
. 

Unfortunately, that does mean 
some potential turbulence
 is in the picture within a 6-month window for BTC and MSTR (MicroStrategy). 

The saving grace is that the sample size is small, but nevertheless, more downside is still the higher-probability view.

A former Clay County deputy sheriff has filed a federal lawsuit alleging retaliation by the Clay County Sheriff’s Office.
Terrance Wurdeman, a former Clay County deputy sheriff, has filed a lawsuit in U.S. District Court against Clay County, Sheriff Jeff Franklin and Chief Deputy Sheriff Kreighton Baxter.
According to the lawsuit, in June 2024, Wurdeman said at the request of a Nebraska State Patrol investigator, he reported a fellow deputy for failure to protect two children from an intoxicated driver and the sheriff’s office’s handling of the situation.
Two months later, Wurdeman said Franklin announced he would not seek re-election and was supporting Baxter to succeed him. The lawsuit said Wurdeman told Franklin people in the community were asking him to also run for sheriff and that he subsequently suffered retaliation as a result.
Wurdeman said he was the only deputy excluded from department training in September and October 2024. The lawsuit said Wurdeman was suspended without pay on Oct. 22, 2024 before being fired by phone on Oct. 28, 2024.
Wurdeman said two days later, the sheriff’s office reported to the State of Nebraska that he “left employment for reasons that could be grounds for revoking his law enforcement certification.” Since that time, he said his certification has been locked and he is unable to work as a law enforcement officer anywhere in Nebraska.
Wurdeman is requesting damages including lost wages and benefits, reinstatement as a deputy sheriff, an order directing Clay County to withdraw or correct the change of status report submitted to the Nebraska Law Enforcement Training Center, and a jury trial.

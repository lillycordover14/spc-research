Keeping Tabs: Ro Brahmand, Fashion Creator
Her collaboration with All My Love is filled with capsule wardrobe staples.
By
 
Jordan Rossman
Published Nov 3, 2026
|
11:00am
For Ro Brahmand, author of Substack 
Fixated
, your closet isn't complete without a strong rotation of elevated basics. "I think the best wardrobes are built around those everyday pieces you genuinely love and wear on repeat," she says. And lucky for us, Brahmand's collaboration with 
All My Love
 just launched, giving us the exact pieces she uses to curate the dream 
capsule wardrobe
. 
"I’ve always been someone who gets a little obsessive about the things I love, whether it’s music, fashion, interiors. or even a vintage mug I’ve found," she says. "I love that feeling of discovering something and wanting to tell everyone about it. And I couldn't stop wearing or talking about my All My Love Piece black cashmere V-neck." From there, Brahmand and the brand's founder, Georgie Dorfman, kept in touch until they finally decided to create a six-piece capsule together. "For this collaboration, we created pieces that feel simple yet special enough to stand on their own," Brahmand says. "They're versatile enough to become the starting point for something more playful."
Ahead, we caught up with Brahmand about what she's shopping for, what she's saving, and how she's been spending her days.

Salesforce Stocks Decline 3% With Cresta Voice AI Deal in Focus
Less than 1 min read
CRM
Salesforce 
CRM
 traded at $219.20, down 3.09% at 10:15 a.m. ET on November 3, as its agreement to acquire Cresta put voice AI in focus. The customer relationship management software provider aims to connect AI-powered customer conversations with Service Cloud workflows. The Next Web reported that Salesforce did not disclose the purchase price, leaving investors without a key number for assessing the deal's economics. The decline coincided with the acquisition news, although that does not establish the transaction as its sole driver.
Salesforce's announcement outlines a platform spanning voice agents, real-time support for human representatives and conversation analysis. Cresta's customers include Marriott, Comcast and Alaska Airlines, giving Salesforce an established deployment base to build on. Closing is expected in the first quarter of fiscal 2028, subject to required approvals and other conditions. The commercial test could be turning those capabilities into paid adoption across Salesforce's service business.
The GuruFocus snapshot shows a GF Score of 89 out of 100 and a price-to-earnings ratio of 19.98, with stronger readings for growth and profitability than for financial strength and GF Value. That suggests business quality deserves attention, but does not settle whether the acquisition will earn an attractive return. Salesforce needs to show that voice automation can resolve more customer problems, support retention and cover model-running costs. Integration and measurable revenue gains could matter more than the customer roster alone.

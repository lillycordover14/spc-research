Where to Vote in Austin: Find Polling Places by ZIP Code&nbsp;&nbsp;Yahoo

[page text]
Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3. Austin-area voters are choosing candidates in statewide and local races, along with ballot measures. In Travis County, voters can enter their ZIP code or address into a map to find nearby voting locations. Polls in Travis, Hays, Williamson and Bastrop counties are open from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Hays County uses vote centers, meaning registered voters there can cast their ballots at any polling location in the county, regardless of precinct. Local contests include county judges, commissioners, prosecutors, state lawmakers and school board trustees. Voters also need identification: acceptable photo identification includes a Texas driver's license, Texas election identification certificate, Texas personal identification card, Texas handgun license or United States passport. Voters unable to present an accepted photo identification document may complete a Reasonable Impediment Declaration at the polls and present an alternative form of identification. Alternatives include a utility bill, bank statement, government check or voter registration certificate.

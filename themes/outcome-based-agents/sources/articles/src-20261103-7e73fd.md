The 33-year-old signed a three-year, $38 million contract with the Mets after the 2024 season and made a successful pivot from reliever to starter after serving as a late-inning stalwart for the Yankees over parts of four seasons.

In 2025, Holmes had a 3.53 ERA with 129 strikeouts in 165 ⅔ innings. In nine starts for the Mets this year, Holmes had a 2.39 ERA with 45 strikeouts in 52 ⅔ innings before he was dealt to Chicago.

Holmes’ performance dipped with the Cubs, as Holmes recorded a 4.25 ERA with 33 strikeouts in 48 ⅔ innings in nine starts. His last Mets start came against the Yankees on May 15, when he suffered a fractured fibula on a comebacker. He debuted with the Cubs on Aug. 8.

In return for Holmes and outfielder Tyrone Taylor, the Mets received 21-year-old infielder Jefferson Rojas, who quickly became the organization’s top prospect, per MLB.com.

Rojas hit .280 with a .929 OPS, nine home runs and 29 RBIs in 29 games for Double-A Binghamton. In six games for Triple-A Syracuse, Rojas was 8-for-26 with three homers and seven RBIs.

Now the Mets have a chance to have both Rojas and Holmes, as the club will be in the market for starting pitching this winter.

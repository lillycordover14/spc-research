What do I need to bring to vote in Texas?
According to 
VoteTexas.gov
, these are acceptable forms of photo ID for voting in Texas:
Advertisement
Advertisement
Texas driver's license
Texas election ID certificate
Texas personal ID card
Texas handgun license
U.S. citizenship certificate with photo
U.S. military ID card
U.S. passport
Those who are unable to present one of the forms of ID listed above may fill out a 
Reasonable Impediment Declaration
 at the polls and present an alternative form of ID. That could be a utility bill, bank statement, government check or voter registration certificate.

November 3, 2026
 

By Jim Stefkovich, Meteorologist, Alabama Emergency Management Agency

CLANTON – Tuesday 530 am, November 3, 2026

It will be breezy across the northern half of the state from late morning through tonight, with wind gusts from 20-30 mph.  Gusts will only be 10-20 mph across the southern half.

Isolated to scattered showers will develop across much of Alabama this afternoon and continue into this evening.  A couple of thunderstorms (not severe) are possible in the far southern sections.  Then, a line of showers and thunderstorms will enter the western sections between 11 pm and 3 am, with activity continuing across much of the state into Wednesday evening.

The main threat tonight through Wednesday will be heavy rainfall and some potential flooding north of I-59 where 1-3 inches are possible, with locally higher amounts.  Lauderdale, Colbert, and Franklin counties in far northwestern Alabama will be most vulnerable to flooding in part due to the 2-5 inches of rain that fell over the weekend.

A couple of storms could produce wind gusts from 40-50 mph late tonight and again Wednesday afternoon, but no widespread severe weather will occur.

One inch or less of total rainfall will fall south of I-59.  Although most of the precipitation will end Wednesday night, a few showers may continue into Thursday in the far eastern sections of the state.

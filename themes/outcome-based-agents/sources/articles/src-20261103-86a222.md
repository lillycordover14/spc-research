Jacob Harper rejoined Morgan Lewis as a partner in its FDA & healthcare practice in Washington, the firm announced Tuesday.
Harper advises physician groups, home care, and other healthcare providers, digital and AI-driven healthcare companies, and investors on regulatory compliance, transactions, and disputes. He returns to the firm after less than a year at Elara Caring, where he was general counsel.

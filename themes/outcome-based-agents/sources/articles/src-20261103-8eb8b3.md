ChatGPT's Work and Chat Tabs Are Merging Into a 'Unified' AI Tool&nbsp;&nbsp;PCMag

[page text]
ChatGPT is getting an interface overhaul, merging its Chat and Work tabs into a single experience. 
The change, announced in an 
email
 to ChatGPT Pro subscribers, will begin testing with a small group of users soon. OpenAI says it will use any "feedback to improve the experience before bringing it to more people." 
ChatGPT currently offers separate tabs for its Chat and Work tools, with the former designed for everyday tasks and conversations, and the latter for more complex projects that require broader knowledge. OpenAI says combining them will make your experience smoother, streamlining the process of switching between modes, and giving you access to more tools from a single view. OpenAI notes how you would be able to explore an idea with ChatGPT, but then build on it, such as making a presentation or building a website, without manually switching over to the Work tab.

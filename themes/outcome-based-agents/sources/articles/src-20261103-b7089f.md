Wildwood is kicking off the holiday shopping season with its annual Holiday Artisan Fair, set for Saturday, November 7, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Visitors can browse for a one-of-a-kind gift and grab a bite to eat.

The fair will take place at the Wildwood Town Center, offering shoppers a chance to find a one-of-a-kind gift and grab a bite to eat.

Santa will also be on hand at the fair.

A Tradition With Deep Local Roots

This isn't the first time Wildwood has thrown open its town center for a holiday market. A 2024 announcement from the 
City of Wildwood
 described that year's Holiday Artisan Fair in similar terms, featuring local vendors with holiday items and food and drinks at the same Town Center location. That prior announcement also noted shoppers could meet Santa, though from noon to 2 p.m. that year, and mentioned free admission with parking available in the Public Garage or along surrounding streets — details specific to the 2024 event that the city's new post does not repeat for this year's fair.

The 2024 version of the fair also invited donations of non-perishable food, canned goods, or toiletries for Circle of Concern, or a new, unwrapped toy for Toys for Tots.

What to Expect This Weekend

The fair gives shoppers a chance to find a one-of-a-kind gift or grab a bite to eat as the holiday season gets underway. Santa's appearance adds a festive touch to the gathering.

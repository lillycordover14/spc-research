Salesforce to Enhance Customer Service with Cresta Voice Technology

Published: 
Nov 3, 2026

by 
Robert Johnson

In 
Small Business News

Salesforce has made a significant move in the customer service landscape by announcing its acquisition of Cresta, a company recognized for its innovative voice-first operating system tailored for enhancing customer experiences. For small business owners navigating the challenging terrain of customer relations, this development promises to deliver tools that can refine how they engage with clients, especially during pivotal interactions.

Cresta’s platform focuses on managing complex customer calls, leveraging voice agents that engage in natural conversations and handle various interruptions seamlessly. According to Salesforce executives, this capability is especially critical, as customers often turn to voice communication for their most pressing issues. With major brands like Marriott, Comcast, and Alaska Airlines leveraging Cresta’s technology, small businesses may soon have the chance to access tech that historically has been available only to high-profile enterprises.

In essence, Cresta’s voice agents are designed to enhance every interaction, allowing human representatives to receive real-time guidance during calls and ensuring that every conversation contributes to improved outcomes. This is particularly beneficial for small business owners who juggle multiple roles and may not always have the resources to provide extensive training or insight into customer interactions. The integration of Cresta’s technology with Salesforce’s existing Service Cloud will enable businesses to connect customer histories directly to their ongoing conversations, paving the way for better resolutions and, ultimately, higher retention and revenue.

Kishan Chetan, EVP and GM of Agentforce and Service Cloud at Salesforce, emphasized, “Voice is where service is hardest to get right, and where it matters most to customers.” This suggests that with the right tools, small businesses can elevate their customer service operations and ensure that they meet the high expectations of today’s consumers.

Cresta’s CEO, Ping Wu, further elaborated on the synergy of both companies: “Cresta’s mission is to turn every customer interaction into a competitive advantage for our customers through a single agentic operating system for customer experience.” Joining forces with Salesforce, Cresta aims to integrate real-time conversation intelligence directly into Salesforce’s comprehensive service platform. For small business owners, this may translate into actionable insights during customer interactions, allowing them to turn potential challenges into opportunities for growth.

Small business owners, however, should be mindful of potential hurdles that may arise with the introduction of such technology. Firstly, the transition to an AI-driven system can require an adjustment period, both in terms of technology adoption and employee training. Owners will need to consider how swiftly their teams can adapt to these advancements and ensure that staff are equipped to use these tools effectively. Additionally, while the integration promises streamlined customer service, the costs associated with acquiring and implementing such technology might be a concern for smaller operations with limited budgets.

The regulatory approval process may also affect when small businesses can take advantage of Cresta’s capabilities, as the transaction is projected to finalize by the first quarter of fiscal year 2028. This timeline will require ongoing assessment and strategy formulation from small business owners regarding their current customer service methods.

As Salesforce’s acquisition of Cresta unfolds, small businesses stand at the threshold of an opportunity to enhance their customer service strategies. With the ability to harness powerful voice agents and real-time support, these businesses can aim for a more engaged customer base and improved service outcomes. Exploring these innovations may well empower small business owners to redefine their approach to customer interactions and establish a competitive edge in their respective markets.

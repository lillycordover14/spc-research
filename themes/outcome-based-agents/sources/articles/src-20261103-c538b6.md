  Motoring star Richard Hammond has revealed he still regularly keeps in touch with his former Top Gear and Grand Tour co-hosts Jeremy Clarkson and James May.

  In a recent interview with The Telegraph, Mr Hammond joked: "They’re still idiots, but I’ll listen to their opinions on anything."

  The 56-year-old presenter said he would have been willing to continue filming The Grand Tour, describing the show's end as leaving a "big hole."

  Speaking on the High Performance podcast, Mr Hammond said: "Had the others said, 'Shall we carry on?', I would've carried on."

  He also left the door open for a potential future reunion, saying it is "not a definite no" to the trio appearing on screen together again.

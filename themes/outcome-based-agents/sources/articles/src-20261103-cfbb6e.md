Lewisburg, Pa. — More than 50 local artists, makers and small businesses will sell their work at the Lewisburg YMCA’s Holiday Artisan Market this month.
The market will be held on Sunday, Nov. 15, from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. at the Y, located at 120 Hardwood Drive, Lewisburg. Admission and parking are free.
Vendors will offer fiber art, jewelry, home décor, woodcrafts, bath and body products, gourmet foods, books and other handcrafted goods. New this year, the market will feature local authors, giving visitors a chance to meet regional writers and buy signed books, according to the YMCA.
“One of the things I love most about this market is that every vendor brings something unique,” said Sarah Binder, the market’s curator and owner of SB Jewelry in New Berlin.
Vendors will be set up on the first two courts inside the YMCA, and the café will be open for visitors who want lunch or a snack.
For more information, visit 
GSVYMCA.org
 or call 570-556-4191.

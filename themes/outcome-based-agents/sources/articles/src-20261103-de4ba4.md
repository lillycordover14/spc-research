Salesforce to acquire Cresta in deal reportedly valued at $2.7bn

by Laura Varley

3 Nov 2026
 

Save article

 

Image: © StockMarketVisuals/Stock.adobe.com 

The acquisition will enable Salesforce to expand into voice-powered support, as it pairs Cresta’s agents with its own agents and cloud.  

Customer relationship management platform Salesforce has announced plans to acquire Cresta, an AI-powered contact-centre software company that builds voice agents for customer service. 

While the value of the deal has yet to be confirmed, 
Dealroom reported
 it is thought to be roughly $2.7bn, citing an anonymous individual familiar with the plan. 

Salesforce’s Kishan Chetan, executive vice-president and general manager of Agentforce and Service Cloud at the company, said the voice element of Cresta’s software is a key driver of the acquisition. 

Salesforce intends to extend its digital support to phone calls by pairing Cresta’s voice agents with its own agents and cloud.  

Ping Wu, the CEO of Cresta, said: “Cresta’s mission is to turn every customer interaction into a competitive advantage for our customers through a single agentic operating system for customer experience. Through unparalleled innovation, we’ve achieved that goal with some of the world’s largest companies.

“Now, by uniting our platform with Salesforce, we will realise our full vision at scale, embedding real-time conversation intelligence and agentic experiences directly into Salesforce’s first-class customer service platform.”

The last few years have seen Salesforce take part in several high-profile acquisitions. In June of this year, the company revealed plans to acquire 
Irish customer agent unicorn Fin
 for approximately $3.6bn. Also in June, it was announced that the organisation would be buying 
Contentful
, a Berlin-founded digital experience platform, for an undisclosed amount. 

In 2025, Salesforce acquired 
Informatica
, an enterprise cloud data management business, in a deal valued at $8bn. 

The transaction related to the Cresta acquisition is expected to close in the first quarter of the 2028 fiscal year and is subject to standard closing conditions, according to Salesforce.

Nooks Secures $500 Million From General Catalyst, Launches AI Sales Assistant Node&nbsp;&nbsp;citybiz

[page text]
Nooks
, a San Francisco-based AI sales technology company, has secured a 
$500 million
 growth capital commitment from 
General Catalyst’s Customer Value Fund
 (CVF) and introduced Node, an AI-powered personal assistant designed to automate sales activities and manage opportunities throughout the revenue lifecycle.

The financing will support product development, international expansion and enterprise customer acquisition as Nooks broadens its platform from outbound sales automation to AI agents supporting prospecting, deal management, account expansion and customer retention.

The 
$500 million
 commitment comes from General Catalyst’s Customer Value Fund, which provides non-dilutive growth capital to companies with established unit economics and predictable revenue performance. Unlike a conventional venture capital equity round, the financing is structured to support growth without diluting existing shareholders. Nooks did not disclose the funding’s repayment terms or deployment schedule.

Nooks reported threefold year-over-year growth and 135% net revenue retention, indicating that revenue from existing customers increased after accounting for expansion, contraction and customer losses.

The company now serves more than 2,000 businesses, including 
Glean, Notion, Deel, Replit and Factory
. More than half of its customers use multiple Nooks products.

“AI changed how software gets built. Nooks is changing how it gets sold,” said Pranav Singhvi, partner and co-head of the Customer Value Fund at General Catalyst. “Nooks has shown a highly predictable revenue engine and strong unit economics, which is what we look for in companies with CVF.”

Founded in 2020, Nooks initially focused on AI-assisted outbound sales before expanding into sales sequencing and automated customer engagement. Its Revenue Agent Platform now combines multiple AI agents with built-in calling, email and workflow infrastructure.

The platform uses more than one billion sales interactions to develop systems that maintain account context, analyze customer communications and adapt sales strategies based on outcomes.

According to Nooks, its agents maintain continuously updated histories of customer accounts, including calls, emails, objections, changes in key contacts and buying signals. The technology is designed to reconcile conflicting information and preserve context across extended sales cycles rather than treating each interaction as an isolated task.

The platform also incorporates simulated sales conversations, historical deal analysis and feedback from completed activities to refine agent performance.

Nooks said customers typically generate three times more sales pipeline using its technology than with their previous tools, while 96% of agent-generated emails are sent without manual edits. These performance figures were reported by the company.

Its product portfolio includes 
Deal, Outbound, Expansion and Coaching Agents, with Inbound Agents
 scheduled for introduction in the first quarter of 2027.

Alongside the financing, Nooks introduced Node, an AI personal assistant designed to work continuously toward individual sales representatives’ goals.

Rather than responding only to specific user requests, Node evaluates sales opportunities, identifies potential risks and recommends or executes activities intended to advance deals.

The assistant can prioritize accounts, prepare follow-up actions and coordinate activities across sales representatives, account managers and solutions engineers. Each team member can use an individual Node assistant, with the system designed to share relevant context and successful sales practices across the team.

Nooks said early users have reported productivity improvements of up to 80%, with approximately two hours saved per day. The company did not provide details about the measurement methodology or size of the early-user group.

Node is expected to become generally available in early 2027, with early access registration currently open.

“There are 12 million people who sell for a living in the US alone and all their jobs are about to change,” said 
Dan Lee
, co-founder and CEO of Nooks. “We’re solving some of the hardest technical problems that enable sales teams to work with AI agents across the revenue lifecycle.”

Nooks plans to use the new financing to develop additional AI sales products, expand internationally and extend its technology into more revenue-generating functions.

The company is also expanding its workforce across engineering, AI research, product development, design and go-to-market operations, with more than 100 open positions.

Nooks was founded by AI researchers with experience at Stanford, Cerebras, Scale AI and Tesla Autopilot. Its investors include General Catalyst’s Customer Value Fund, Kleiner Perkins and Lachy Groom.

The financing and Node launch position Nooks to expand its role in enterprise sales operations as businesses increasingly deploy AI agents to automate customer engagement, sales execution and revenue management.

Sophia Picardo and Ryleigh Denning finished with a goal and two assists apiece as Kendall (19-1-0) clinched a spot in the NYSPHSAA Class C quarterfinal with a 4-1 win over Holley (14-6-1) on Tuesday night.
Mackenzie Cooper and Jersie Drake also found the back of the net in the win. Teagan Shaw recorded five saves for the Eagles.
Kendall will play Section VI champion Wilson (18-0-0) in the regional. That game is scheduled for a 7:30 p.m. kickoff Friday at Williamsville East High School.

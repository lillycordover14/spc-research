Republican Clay Chism defeated Democrat Mike Conner in the race for Callaway County Presiding Commissioner.
According to his campaign, Chism's priorities include fiscal responsibility, transparency in county government and strengthening economic development throughout the county.
Chism previously served as Callaway County Sheriff, but had his peace officer license revoked following accusations that he drove and worked while intoxicated.
He has since been hired by the city of Auxvasse in an administrative role and testified in March 2024 to Missouri's Administrative Hearing Commission that he was sober and that his conduct in office as sheriff was reckless.
The Callaway County Commission consists of three commissioners responsible for overseeing the county's budget, property, operations, and road and bridge funding. The presiding commissioner serves as the commission's chief elected official.

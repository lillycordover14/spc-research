ATLANTA, Ga. (Atlanta News First) — Democrat Katherine Juhan-Arnold pulled off a major statewide upset Tuesday night, defeating Republican incumbent Tyler Harper to become Georgia’s next agriculture commissioner.
The victory unseats Harper, who was first elected to the statewide post in 2022 after previously serving in the Georgia State Senate.
Full election results here
Juhan-Arnold will now take leadership of the Georgia Department of Agriculture, the oldest state department of agriculture in the nation. The agency regulates and promotes Georgia’s top economic sector, overseeing agribusiness, food safety inspections, animal health, consumer protection, and fuel pump calibration across all 159 counties.
Agriculture contributes 
approximately $91.4 billion annually
 to Georgia’s economy, according to the UGA Center for Agribusiness & Economic Development. Agriculture, forestry or related fields provide more than 381,200 jobs.

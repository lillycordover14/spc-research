Exec Believes Raiders Can Get What They Want for Maxx Crosby&nbsp;&nbsp;Raiders Beat

[page text]
The Raiders aren’t expected to trade 
Maxx Crosby
, but it sounds like they will get significant offers for their All-Pro edge rusher leading up to the November 10 trade deadline.

Since Baltimore backed out of their trade agreement with the Raiders in March, the popular opinion has been that they probably won’t would get another offer for Crosby involving two first-round picks. 

But with the Jeffery Simmons trade last week, Crosby’s trade value probably got a boost, and league insider 
Jason La Canfora
 supported that theory in a column this week. 

“[Crosby] was already traded to Baltimore in March, then the Ravens GM chickened out,” 
La Canfora wrote on Tuesday
. 

“They may be the biggest beneficiaries of the Simmons deal; the Raiders were never going to budge on what they got from Baltimore (almost), and now it might be more. Debate remains whether someone will offer two first-round picks, but this is the premier early-down run defender on the edge who never leaves the field and generates an elite 17% pressure rate despite the high-volume snap count.”

La Canfora added that a longtime NFL personnel exec told him over the weekend that the Raiders can probably “get what they want” for Crosby if they choose to trade him. 

As for “what they want” for Crosby, it has been reported in a few places that the Raiders aren’t interested in anything less than what the Ravens were offering for Crosby in March. 

Big decisions coming…

Every indication over the last few weeks has been that Crosby is not requesting a trade, and there aren’t any reasons to believe he wants to be somewhere else.

The Raiders could get a couple of premium picks for Crosby, but one of the biggest arguments against moving on from him is the fact that the defensive line is bad enough with an All-Pro edge rusher on the roster. 

By trading Crosby, John Spytek would have one more hole to fill at arguably the weakest position group on the team.

For that reason, Spytek (and the minority owner he answers to) would probably have to be blown away by a trade offer for Crosby to take it.

Raiders getting better, but aren’t there yet…

If we’ve learned anything from the first half of the season, it’s that the Raiders are capable of competing with some of the best teams in the league, but they are still a year or two away from being one of the best teams in the league. 

The expiration date on Crosby’s career might not match perfectly with the Raiders’ 2-to-3-year plan to get to the Super Bowl, but ‘The Condor’ has been the face of the organization for so long, it would be a difficult decision to move away from him now.

A Crosby trade probably won’t happen, but if the Raiders get boat raced by the 49ers on Sunday, who knows, maybe all bets would be off once again.

Chaffee Art Center hosts sixth annual Holiday Artisan Gift Show
Submitted
The Chaffee Art Center will host its sixth annual Holiday Artisan Gift Show on Nov. 6 and 7.
Friday, Nov. 6 and Saturday, Nov. 7 —RUTLAND— The Chaffee Art Center will host its sixth annual Holiday Artisan Gift Show on Friday, Nov. 6, from 4-7 p.m. and Saturday, Nov. 7, from 10 a.m.-4 p.m., offering two days of handmade gifts, local artwork and seasonal activities at 16 South Main St. in Rutland.

The free event will feature a variety of handcrafted items from artists and artisans, giving shoppers an opportunity to find one-of-a-kind holiday gifts while supporting creative businesses.

Available items include children’s books, fused glass ornaments, paintings in acrylic, watercolor, and oil, carvings, greeting cards, scarves, and other handmade creations. Organizers say the show will highlight unique, affordable gifts for the holiday season.

Visitors can also enjoy refreshments while shopping, including a hot beverage bar serving cider, cocoa, coffee and tea, along with sweet treats. Wine and massages will also be available during the event.

Families are encouraged to attend, with a free make-and-take art station where children can create their own artwork while others browse the show.

As an additional incentive, shoppers who make a purchase will receive a complimentary Ann Clark cookie cutter of their choice, limited to one per person.

The Chaffee Art Center is also seeking additional vendors for the two-day event. Organizers welcome artisans offering pottery, fiber arts, clothing, fine art, photography, jewelry, glasswork, wood products, specialty foods, cards and other handmade goods.

Booth spaces measuring 8 feet by 4 feet are available with or without tables throughout both floors of the Chaffee’s historic mansion. Additional spaces are available on the porch and lawn, with accommodations for food trucks and tents.

Interested vendors can contact the Chaffee Art Center at 802-775-0356, email 
[email protected]
 or visit the center in person.

Admission to the Holiday Artisan Gift Show is free, and the event is open to the public.

For more information, visit: 
chaffeeartcenter.org
.

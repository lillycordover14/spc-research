Police are looking for three people who entered a residence and tied up an 89-year-old woman and a 70-year-old man while they robbed their home.  
Investigators say the robbery happened on Nov. 3 just after 11 a.m. near Bainbridge Avenue and East 199 Street.  
Police say a woman and two men forcibly entered a home where an 89-year-old woman and a 70-year-old man were inside. One of the men displayed a firearm and removed the couple's cellphone, cash, and credit cards. The trio then zip-tied the victim's feet and hands before fleeing the scene.  
Police have released images of the trio; a woman and one of the men wore an Amazon vest, and the third man wore a black hooded sweatshirt.  
Anyone with information is asked to call the NYPD Crime Stoppers hotline at 1-800-577-TIPS (8477).

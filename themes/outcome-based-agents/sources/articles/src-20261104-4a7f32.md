Representatives from the American Red Cross visited the Lawrence County Emergency Management Agency (EMA) recently to discuss disaster preparedness, emergency sheltering, and more opportunities to improve the local emergency response capabilities.
Among the discussions were reviewing and updating sheltering agreements, evaluating existing plans, and looking at potential funding chances for additional community storm shelters throughout Lawrence County.
An important area of focus for EMA is expanding access to community storm shelters as they work to identify resources and funding opportunities that could help provide additional protection for local residents during severe weather.
Red Cross representatives, EMA Director Steven Carlton, and Moulton Mayor Ryan Jolley met to discuss the city’s sheltering capabilities and its role in countywide emergency planning.
Moulton plays an important role in Browns Ferry Nuclear emergency planning. The Moulton Recreation Center can serve as a reception center for local residents evacuating from communities within the designated 10-mile emergency planning zone surrounding the nuclear plant. If there were an emergency requiring evacuation, the recreation center would help support the coordinated reception and assistance of affected residents.
“These efforts are part of EMA’s ongoing work to evaluate local needs, strengthen partnerships, and pursue resources that can improve disaster preparedness throughout Lawrence County,” EMA said in a Facebook post.

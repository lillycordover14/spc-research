RENO, Nev. (KOLO) - The Reno Gem and Mineral Society is hosting its 
2026 Fall Artisan Festival
 this weekend. 
Nancy Whitney and Dorothy Pomin stopped by Morning Break to share that over thirty Society members and local vendors will offer a wide variety of their unique, beautiful handcrafted items most of which are created from stones found throughout the state. It takes place November 6-8 at the Reno Town Mall. 
There will also be art glass, local photography, wood crafting, home décor, crystals, stones and more! Every item tells a story — whether it was discovered in the Nevada landscape or transformed into art by skilled silversmiths and lapidary artists. 
It’s a great family outing - kids of all ages can learn about rocks and minerals, and have a chance to spin the Wheel of Fortune for cool prizes! Join us for a fun weekend, support local artisans, and find the perfect holiday gift!
Event Dates and Times:
Friday November 6 at 10am-5pm
Saturday November 7 at 10am-5pm
Sunday November 8 at 10am-3pm

BOISE, Idaho (CBS2) — 
Last night's general election saw a series of congressional seats flip from Republican to Democrat, and some Idaho legislative seats may see the same trend. Districts in Ada, Jerome and Bannock counties currently showing Republican incumbents trailer their challengers.
District 15
District 15 encompasses parts of west Boise between Maple Grove Rd and Eagle Rd. 
In the Senate, Republican Desi Burbank, the candidate after former Republican senator Codi Galloway announced she will not seek reelection, is behind Democrat and former Boise School District Trustee Nancy Gregory.
Gregory, as of 10:58 a.m. Wednesday morning, holds 54.52% of the vote with 20 precincts reporting. The last Democrat to be District 15's senator was Rick Just from 2022-2024.
In the House, District 15B Republican incumbent Dori Healey seeking her third term is trailing her Democratic challenger Jae Etchart.
Etchart, as of 10:58 a.m. Wednesday morning, is ahead with 53.33% of the vote with 20 precincts reporting.
District 26
District 26 contains parts of Blaine, Lincoln and Jerome counties.
House District 26A is Republican incumbent Mike Pohanka, seeking his second term, challenged by Democrat Anita Janis and Independent Bella Schiffler.
As of 10:58 a.m. Wednesday morning, Janis is ahead with 51% of the vote, followed by Pohanka with 43.86% and Schiffler with 5.14%. 33 precincts are reporting.
Where Janis pulled ahead is in Blaine County where she holds 67.47% of the vote. A total of about 7,698 votes, more than Pohanka's votes in both other counties combined. 
In House District 26B, Republican Lyle Johnstone, farmer and former Northside Soil and Water Conservation District Board member is facing Democratic candidate Kevin Marsh. Marsh also has worked as a farmer as well as a professor teaching history.
Marsh, as of 10:58 a.m. Wednesday morning is leading Johnstone with 58.99% of the vote. Following a similar trend to the 26A race, most of Marsh's votes came from Blaine County.
District 29
District 29 houses Bannock County in south east Idaho. 
House District 29A is Republican incumbent Dustin Manwaring seeking his fourth term. He is being challenged by Democrat Mary Shea, a child welfare law specialist.
As of 10:58 a.m. Wednesday morning, Shea is ahead of Manwaring with 56.37% of the vote.
House District 29B has no incumbent, the previous representative, Republican Tanya Burgoyne, lost her primary in May.
Democrat Nate Roberts, an electrician, is leading Republican Jennifer Miles, a behavioral health worker, with 55.83% of the vote as of 10:58 a.m. Wednesday morning.
All of these results are from the 
Idaho Secretary of State
 but are not yet official.

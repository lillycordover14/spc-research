5 Things to Do in Kankakee County: Artisan Faire, Hometown Cupcake Bake-Off, An Autumn Afternoon at Camp Shaw&nbsp;&nbsp;Shaw Local

[page text]
Artisan Faire (
Kankakee
):
 The 
Kankakee County
 Museum will host the annual Artisan Faire from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 7. The event will feature unique holiday gifts from local artisans. Visit the event page on Facebook for more information.
Hometown Cupcake Bake-Off (Kankakee): 
The B. Harley 
Bradley
 House will host this delicious event from 2 to 4 p.m. Sunday, Nov. 8. Sample goodies from a variety of local bakeries. Tickets are $20 per person. Visit 
wright1900.org
 for more information.
An Autumn Afternoon at Camp Shaw (
Manteno
): 
Enjoy the fall weather at Camp Shaw-Waw-Nas-See from 2 to 5 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 7. The event will have apple cider, hiking, ziplining, fall photo booth, a campfire and more. Admission is free, but zipline passes are $10. Visit 
campshaw.org
 for more information.
7th Heaven on the 7th (Bradley):
 Rock band 7th Heaven will perform at Nov. 7 at the Quality Inn in Bradley. The Silhouettes begin at 6 p.m. and 7th Heaven plays at 8 p.m. This show is a charity event by Renville Cares, and proceeds benefit Turkey for Tots, which serves local underprivileged children. Visit the event page on 
Facebook
 for more information or to purchase tickets.
Military Appreciation Night (Kankakee):
 Olivet Nazarene University’s hockey team will honor local veterans and military personnel at 7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 6 at Ice Valley Centre Ice Arena. Visit 
onutigers.com/sports/cice/2026-27/schedule
 for more information.

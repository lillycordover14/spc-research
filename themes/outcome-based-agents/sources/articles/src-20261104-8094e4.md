Holiday House brings weekly artisan market and free Santa visits to Mar Vista&nbsp;&nbsp;Yo! Venice!

[page text]
Community event space réunion partners with The Venice Fest for Sunday pop-up series.

By Sarah Lin
.

Local artisans and independent merchants will gather along Venice Boulevard each weekend through late December as neighborhood venue 
Réunion Mar Vista
 and 
The Venice Fest
 partner to host a seasonal shopping series.

Dubbed Holiday House, the curated holiday market runs every Sunday from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. through Dec. 20 at 12204 Venice Blvd., right next to the weekly 
Mar Vista Farmers Market
.

Organizers designed the eight-week activation to showcase neighborhood creatives while offering family-friendly seasonal perks. Guests can access complimentary gift-wrapping services every Sunday throughout the market’s run, with free photos with Santa scheduled during all December market dates.

Event producers are encouraging weekend attendees to combine trips with the neighboring farmers market as part of a recurring Sunday community tradition. Admission is free and open to all ages.

'American Idol' Winner Hannah Harper Praises God For 'Answered Prayers' One Year After Audition&nbsp;&nbsp;Beliefnet

[page text]
Kathy Hutchins / Shutterstock.com | Inset: @hannahharper / Instagram

One year after stepping into the “American Idol” audition room with a song inspired by motherhood, country singer Hannah Harper is looking back on the life-changing journey that followed and giving God the glory.

The Season 24 winner marked the anniversary of her audition with an emotional 
Instagram post
 on October 29, reflecting on the nerves, sacrifices and unexpected blessings that came with chasing her dreams.

“Today, I’m looking back at a year full of answered prayers I didn’t even know to pray,” Harper wrote.

The mother of three recalled standing outside the Fisher Center at Belmont University, feeling cold and nervous as she prepared to perform for the judges. Making matters worse, her then-1-year-old son had a meltdown just moments before her audition.

For Harper, who grew up singing bluegrass gospel music with her family in Southeast Missouri, performing alone before a panel of celebrity judges was unfamiliar territory.

“Up until that point, I’d only ever made music with my family, singing in churches,” she explained.

Her original plan was simple: perform her song “String Cheese,” share her testimony and return home to her family.

Instead, she earned a golden ticket to Hollywood, setting the stage for a journey that would ultimately change her life.

“If this journey has taught me anything, it’s that every hardship can become a testimony,” Harper reflected.

The singer admitted she initially worried about how she might be portrayed on national television. However, those fears gave way to gratitude as the competition provided opportunities to share her Christian faith with millions of viewers.

On May 11, Harper was crowned the winner of “American Idol” Season 24, completing a remarkable journey from singing in churches to winning one of America’s biggest singing competitions.

But she wasn’t the only one making sacrifices along the way.

Her husband, Devon Mendenhall, left his law enforcement career to support her growing music career and now serves as her tour manager.

“My entire family is on the road with me — both of my brothers, my parents, my kids,” Harper told Fox News Digital in August.

In her anniversary reflection, Harper thanked her husband, family, band and crew for helping make her dreams possible.

And the journey is far from over.

The singer teased an upcoming 2027 tour announcement, suggesting even bigger things could be ahead as she continues building her career.

Still, Harper’s message wasn’t about fame or winning a television competition. It was about faith, family and recognizing God’s hand in the unexpected turns of life.

“I can’t wait to see what God has in store!” she wrote.

For Harper, what began as a nervous audition and a song about motherhood has become a powerful reminder that sometimes God’s plans are bigger than anything we could have imagined.

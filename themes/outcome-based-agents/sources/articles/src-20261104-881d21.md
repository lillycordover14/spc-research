Harper’s Dental Hygiene Clinic provides affordable care while preparing future hygienists

Harper College News Bureau

November 4, 2026

November 4, 2026

Harper College’s 
Dental Hygienist Associate Degree Program
 equips students to become primary oral health care professionals while providing
                              much-needed dental services to the community. Under the supervision of licensed dentists
                              and dental hygienists, students provide a variety of dental services to the public
                              through programs designed to offer free or reduced-cost dental care in Harper’s state-of-the-art
                              
Dental Hygiene Clinic
.

Graduates of the limited-enrollment, 81-credit-hour program earn an Associate in Applied
                              Science Degree and are qualified to work in a variety of settings, including private
                              practices, public health clinics, hospitals and long-term care facilities, with additional
                              career opportunities available through further education.

In 2026, 100% of the graduates of Harper’s Dental Hygiene Program passed both the
                              American Dental Association’s National Board Dental Hygiene Examination (NBDHE) and
                              the American Board of Dental Examiners (ADEX) Clinical Board Examination.

Preventive dental services

Harper’s Dental Hygiene Clinic cares for nearly 1,000 patients a year, offering a
                              variety of affordable preventive dental services, including dental examinations, scaling,
                              oral cancer screenings, fluoride treatments, sealants and periodontal care.

Dental Hygiene Program Coordinator Kathi Hock said the clinic’s services are vital
                              for community members who otherwise would not receive dental care.

“Many individuals in the community may not have regular access to dental care due
                              to cost or lack of dental insurance,” she said. “The clinic helps address this need
                              by providing affordable preventive care while educating patients about oral health
                              and disease prevention.”

Hands-on clinical experience and a comprehensive curriculum prepare Harper’s Dental
                              Hygiene program students to prevent, identify and treat oral diseases.

Thomas Novakoski graduated from Harper’s Dental Hygiene Program in May. The 24-year-old
                              Crystal Lake resident said he feels ready to begin his career as a dental hygienist
                              because of the training he has received while working in Harper’s dental clinic.

“They really prepare us to be ready for what it’s going to be like in the real world,”
                              he said. “The patient comfort side is definitely the frontrunner in the program. We
                              take our time. We talk through what we’re doing, what the patient needs and educate
                              them on how to properly care for their oral health at home.”

Thomas’s goal is to start working in a clinical office where he can continue to build
                              skills and confidence as a clinician for patients.

“Long-term, I want to be a well-rounded hygienist who can provide high-quality care
                              for patients and have a good relationship with them as well, which I think is very
                              important,” he said.

In addition to preparing future dental hygienists, Harper’s Dental Hygiene Clinic
                              offers specialized programs that expand access to oral health care for community members
                              of all ages.

10-week periodontal program

For patients experiencing gum disease, Harper offers an affordable option for receiving
                              more extensive treatment through its 10-week Periodontal Program.

The program serves individuals experiencing symptoms of periodontal disease, including
                              tartar buildup, bleeding gums and loose teeth or tooth loss.

Harper dental hygiene students provide deep cleanings and other treatments under the
                              supervision of licensed dentists and dental hygienists. The program includes 10 clinic
                              visits and costs $75.

Give Kids A Smile Day

The clinic’s commitment to community care also extends to its youngest patients.

Each year, Harper’s Dental Hygiene Program provides free oral health education, preventive
                              services and dental treatment to children through Give Kids A Smile Day, an initiative
                              of the American Dental Association.

Kathi said Give Kids A Smile Day reflects Harper’s strong commitment to community
                              engagement and service.

“It allows our dental hygiene students and faculty to provide care and education to
                              children who may face barriers to accessing dental services,” she said, “and demonstrates
                              Harper’s culture of care by connecting students with meaningful service experiences
                              that benefit the community while reinforcing the importance of compassion, prevention
                              and public health.”

For students, these opportunities to serve the community are an essential part of
                              preparing for careers in dental hygiene.

“Students work with patients from diverse backgrounds and with varying oral health
                              needs,” she said. They learn how to assess patient conditions, develop dental hygiene
                              treatment plans, provide preventive services and communicate effectively with patients.
                              These experiences prepare graduates to enter the dental workforce as skilled, compassionate
                              healthcare professionals.

Students at Canton and Troy Area High Schools may have broken the world record for most soda can tabs collected in a single year as part of this year’s Kids Can’t Fight Cancer Alone Tab Wars.
As part of the yearly competition, the students collectively gathered 5,597 pounds worth of aluminum soda can tabs, which will be taken to Elysburg and recycled. The proceeds from the recycled aluminum — roughly $3,638 this year — go to the Ronald McDonald House in Danville.

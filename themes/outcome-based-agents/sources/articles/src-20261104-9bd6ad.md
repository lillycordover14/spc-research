A little more than a month into his new role, Huntingdon County’s emergency management director is getting familiar with the community, reviewing county plans and looking at ways to improve how the agency responds when emergencies arise.
Jeff Farber, of Bellwood, brings years of experience in emergency services to the position. Before joining Huntingdon County, he spent about six years as Blair County Emergency Management’s emergency management and hazardous materials planner.

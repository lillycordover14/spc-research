Over the past couple of months, proponents dressed in green across Eagle County have been advocating for local Ballot Issue 5B. It seemed that all that hard work might have paid off. 

As of 7:51 p.m., the preliminary, unofficial results showed Ballot Issue 5B was heading toward the winning vote, receiving 9,111 votes in favor of it, or 58.08% of the electorate.

Ballot Issue 5B proposes a local property tax that will begin in 2027. The additional $10.1 million tax is about $131 a year or $11 per month, for every $1 million of assessed value. The revenue generated is staying in the school district — going towards teacher attraction and retention, restoring electives that have been cut and funding mental health access for students and families within Eagle County Schools. 

In previous conversations,
 teachers and staff from across the county emphasized just how much this tax increase is needed after watching their coworkers get cut, students get less support and class sizes rise to the point of educator burnout. 

Some opposition to the ballot issue focused on the language, with some believing it was too broad for them to approve of it. The Eagle County Republican Party recommended voting against it but had expressed support for an MLO that would fund teachers’ salaries more pointedly.

The work towards promoting the ballot issue was spearheaded by the Education Foundation of Eagle County, which has been fighting for this outcome for over a year.

“Tonight, our community showed up for the people who show up for our children every day. To our educators and school staff: we see you, we value you, and we want you to stay,” said Wendy Rimel, the president and executive director of EFEC. She shared that on top of all the work being done, this was the group’s first-ever outreach that offered Spanish-speaking options to community members. 

“Thank you to every voter, volunteer, and supporter who helped make this possible. This is a powerful investment in our students, our schools, and the future of the Eagle Valley,” Rimel said.

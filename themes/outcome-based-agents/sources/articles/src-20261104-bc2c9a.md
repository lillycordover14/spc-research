Step Out Buffalo’s annual Fall Makers + Shakers: Boozy Artisan Market, aka the best shopping event of the year, is finally back for TWO 3-DAY WEEKENDS!

This year we’re hosting a total of 225 vendors across both weekends at The Powerhouse! That’s 125 vendors each weekend, starting with a 3 day weekend BEFORE Thanksgiving this year from Nov 20 – 22, followed by another 3-day stretch on Thanksgiving Weekend from Nov 27 – 29, 2026.

In addition to shopping there will also be a build-your-own Bloody Mary bar, mimosas, food trucks, booze samples and giveaways to win free stuff as you shop!

Lady Hounds notch first playoff win since 2020

Sports

 Today, 06:00 AM

KERRY BARBOZA Sports Editor

SAN ANTONIO – Boerne High was looking for its first volleyball playoff victory since 2020 when the Lady Greyhounds squared off against Seguin last Thursday at Davenport.
Only one player on the Greyhound team had ever been involved in a playoff game of any kind and that was senior Bella Pettet, who was a freshman on the 2023 team when they made the playoffs, but were bounced in the opening round by Cuero. Pettet has also been to the soccer playoffs.
BHS coach Staci Lawrence said she sensed a few nerves from her team but added that they put that aside and played well. Seguin was up 5-1 in the first game, but Boerne righted the ship and outscored the Matadors from there, 24-11 to get the 25-16 win.
The Lady Hounds took the second set, 27-25, dropped the third set, 21-25, and closed it out in Game 4, 25-20.
Boerne (30-6) faced Fox Tech (18-14) on Tuesday at Blossom Athletic Center in the second round. The Lady Buffs defeated Southside in four sets last Friday.
Lawrence said the team did a good job of settling in after falling behind in that first set.
“I think there is always a natural nervous energy when it comes to playoffs because it will be someone’s last game. Our mentality was to control what we can control and know that a game can’t be won or lost on one point,” she said. “We needed to settle in and focus on what we have been working on. We did just that to come back and win that set, 25-16.”
Lawrence said it was satisfying for everyone to get the program’s first playoff win in six years.
“Going into the playoffs we talked to the team about what they want their legacy to be. They have laid the groundwork and the foundation to put ourselves in a position to be successful during playoffs this year,” she said. “The win is a combination of the layers of bricks that have been laid the last few years and every day with these 12 athletes this season. Thursday’s win is one more highlight that we can add to this season, but this group isn’t done yet.”
Lauren Magnus finished with 41 assists, 3 aces and 2 blocks, Mallory Miles added 12 kills, Pettet tallied 14 kills, 6 aces and 9 nines, while Ava Seamans recorded 11 kills and 3 blocks and Brook Summers came up with 3 blocks and Kiley Coffland chipped in with 14 digs and 5 assists.

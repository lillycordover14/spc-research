Parloa Expands North American Agentic CX Footprint through Alliance with Deloitte Canada&nbsp;&nbsp;PR Newswire

[page text]
Modernizing customer engagement by combining Parloa's leading AI Agent Management Platform with Deloitte's deep industry experience and transformation capabilities.
NEW YORK and BERLIN
, 
Nov. 4, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- 
Parloa
, a leading AI Agent Management Platform provider, is pleased to announce an alliance and reseller agreement with Deloitte Canada to help Canadian organizations reimagine customer service through premier conversational and agentic AI solutions. Parloa enables customers to build, optimize and manage AI agents across the full customer lifecycle and all channels, with built-in capabilities to improve every conversation. The collaboration enables Deloitte Canada to bring Parloa's innovative platform to clients seeking to modernize contact centers, enhance customer experiences, and unlock new levels of operational efficiency.

As organizations across Canada face rising customer expectations, increasing service volumes, and pressure to deliver personalized experiences at lower cost, contact center leaders are looking beyond traditional automation. Together, Parloa and Deloitte help clients move from legacy customer service models to intelligent, AI-powered engagement experiences capable of handling high-volume customer interactions across voice and digital channels.
Parloa and Deloitte are structuring this collaboration to close the gaps that often stall AI programs. Pairing the Parloa platform with Deloitte's capabilities in contact center strategy, systems integration, and change management enables clients to build the governance, data foundations, and operating models that ensure AI pilots reach success in production.
"Most agentic AI projects in CX stall because the technology is forced into a tech stack it was never built for," said Chris Silver, Chief Revenue Officer, Parloa. "We built Parloa to fit each customer's unique operations: design, deploy, test, and optimize the agent around their systems, channels, and data before ever going live. Pair that with Deloitte's customer experience leaders and their ability to implement and govern at enterprise scale, and customers will successfully move past pilots into customer-ready AI."
"Customer service has become the moment of truth for brand loyalty, and AI agents are redefining what every interaction can be," said Angela Gordon, Partner, Deloitte Canada. "Our alliance with Parloa helps Canadian organizations redesign service around what AI agents and people each do best, giving customers faster, more personal resolutions and freeing teams to focus on the moments that matter most." 
Leading organizations use Parloa's AI Agent Management Platform to run AI-powered customer interactions at enterprise scale across voice and digital channels. As a result, customers enjoy more natural, personalized experiences, shorter wait times, and more consistent service, while human agents can focus on issues requiring empathy, personal judgment and appropriate oversight. 
Parloa Zones
 gives legal and security teams a way to prove data residency in Canada, and region by region, without re-architecting the platform for every new market.
The alliance positions Deloitte Canada to support clients through every phase of their transformation journey, including:
Contact center modernization roadmaps and business cases
Conversational AI and virtual agent design
Agentic AI implementation and governance
Integration with existing customer service platforms and enterprise systems
Change management and operating model transformation
Ongoing optimization and managed services
The collaboration reflects Deloitte Canada's continued investment in the Future of Service, helping organizations move beyond traditional contact center modernization toward AI-first customer engagement models that improve customer satisfaction, increase operational efficiency, and create new opportunities for growth.
About Parloa

 empowers global enterprises to build, train and manage AI agents for premier customer experiences.

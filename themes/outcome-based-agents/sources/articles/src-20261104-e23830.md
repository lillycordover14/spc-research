Ada LeAnn Wilcox

Dec 4, 1964 — Nov 4, 2026

Atoka, OK

Listen to Obituary

 
 
 

Ada LeAnn (Staggs) Wilcox, age 61, a longtime Atoka, OK resident. She was born on Friday, December 4, 1964, to Artie Roy and Clara Ann (Givens) Staggs, at Oklahoma City, OK. Ada passed away on Wednesday, November 4, 2026 at her home in Atoka, OK. She graduated from Kiowa High School with the class of 1983 and later attended trade school. Ada was a truck driver for many years and was also a waitress, deli worker, convenience store cashier and oversized load escort. She married Robert Lee Wilcox on Monday, December 5th, 2005 at Ft. Smith, AR. Ada loved to go junking and thrifting with her daughter. She also enjoyed hunting, fishing, camping with family and friends and shooting 3D targets with a bow. She bowled when she was younger and during both of her pregnancies. Ada also enjoyed crocheting, a hobby she inherited from her grandmother.
Ada is survived by her husband, Robert Lee Wilcox of the home in Atoka, OK; father, Artie Roy Staggs of Stringtown, OK; brother, Jimmy Staggs and wife Karen of Saginaw, TX; special friend, Ellen Glover of Atoka, OK; daughter, Chasity Tharp and husband Jeremy of Heber Springs, AR; son, Bryon Kennedy of Georgia; stepson, Bruce Wilcox and wife Jamie of Eldred, PA; stepdaughter, Christian Horn and husband Guy of Minneapolis, MN; grandchildren, Roy Womack of Pangbur, AR, Lily Womack of Heber Springs, AR, Trapper Kennedy of Tish, OK; step grandchildren, Mattison Wilcox, Annabella Wilcox, Ayden Andrews of Eldred, PA, Ivan Adams, Octavius Horn, Vladimir Adams; along with nieces, aunts, uncles, other relatives and dear and loved friends.
Ada was preceded in death by her mother, Clara Ann (Givens) Staggs; grandparents, Fletcher Bryan Jr and Ida Mae (Surrel) Givens, Herman Ezekiel and Bessie Juanita (Lewis) Staggs.

Ethereum Price Reclaims 200-Day EMA as Leverage Hits Five-Month High&nbsp;&nbsp;Coinpedia

[page text]
Story Highlights
Ethereum has gained roughly 11%, reclaiming its 200-day EMA band.
ETH could target $2,600 and $2,800 if bullish momentum continues.
Binance’s Ethereum leverage ratio reached 0.162, its highest level in five months.
Ethereum’s latest rally looks encouraging. But the derivatives market is flashing a warning that traders shouldn’t ignore. ETH has climbed back above its 200-day EMA band, recovering from below $2,200 in late October to approximately $2,430, marking an 11% rise.
 
Advertisement
 
 
 
 
The recovery puts higher price targets back on the table. However, rising leverage could make the next move considerably more volatile.
Ethereum Price Targets $2,600 After EMA Reclaim
With Ethereum back above its 200-day EMA, bulls have an opportunity to extend the recovery. A sustained advance could push ETH toward $2,600, with $2,800 emerging as another potential target.
Still, the $2,200 level has become crucial. If ETH fails to defend it and turns the former support into resistance, the recovery could quickly lose steam. A breakdown could expose $2,000 or even $1,800.
Binance Leverage Ratio Raises Fresh Concerns
Onchain data 
adds
 another complication. Ethereum’s Estimated Leverage Ratio on Binance has climbed to approximately 0.1862, its highest reading in five months.
The indicator measures open futures positions relative to ETH reserves held on the exchange. Its rise suggests traders are using more leverage and taking on greater derivatives exposure.
That’s not automatically bullish. The ratio doesn’t reveal whether positions are predominantly long or short, so treating it as confirmation of further upside would be premature.
Can Ethereum Hold Support Without Liquidations?
Higher leverage makes the market more sensitive to sudden price movements. If 
ETH
 moves against crowded positions, liquidations could accelerate volatility and undermine the recovery.
For now, Ethereum price has reclaimed 200-day EMA offers a constructive technical signal, but the $2,200 defense remains essential. Bulls need to protect that level while managing the risks highlighted by Binance’s rising leverage ratio.

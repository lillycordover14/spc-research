From pipeline to the full revenue stack
Nooks started with AI for outbound, then launched Sequencing earlier this year, which quickly took share from incumbents like Outreach and Salesloft. Together they gave Nooks something few AI companies have: the execution layer where revenue work actually happens and more than one billion real sales interactions flowing through it.
Today, Nooks is unifying that foundation into the Revenue Agent Platform, agents that do the work of revenue, from first touch to renewal. To teach agents to sell,
 
Nooks Research
, the company's applied AI research team, builds the models and systems that help AI understand the way people think, act and buy:
Live context that persists for months.
 Every Nooks agent carries the full, continuously updated history of an account: calls, emails, objections, champion changes and signals. It reconciles conflicting information instead of treating each task as a fresh prompt.
A learning loop that compounds.
 Nooks agents learn from outcomes, not just prompts. They are trained on real sales interactions, rehearse plays against simulated prospects before reaching real buyers, backtest strategies against similar historical deals and learn each rep's style.
Infrastructure that sees outcomes.
 Nooks agents work toward goals like pipeline created and deals advanced, not one-off tasks. Because Nooks makes the call and sends the email on its own infrastructure, they see what happens next: the reply, the meeting, the closed deal. Tools that sit on top of the CRM can't learn from outcomes they never observe.
The results show up in the work: Customers typically source 3x more pipeline with Nooks than with their previous tools; 96% of emails from Nooks agents go out without edits.
The platform includes Deal, Outbound, Expansion and Coaching Agents, with Inbound Agents arriving in Q1 2027. Each agent runs on Nooks' built-in dialing and sending infrastructure, the complex execution layer that customers rely on to connect with their buyers.
Introducing Node, the first EA for the AE
Node is the first AI personal assistant for sales. Most AI tools wait for a rep to ask for help and then complete one task. Node is goal-oriented, always on, and self-learning. A rep might be focused on hitting the quarter, breaking into a strategic account, expanding a key customer or rebuilding pipeline for next quarter.
Node works toward whatever goals matter to that seller. It decides which deals and steps will move those goals forward and either does the work or brings it to the rep, ready to approve. Node is also built for the way sales really works, as a team sport. Everyone on a deal, from the rep to the account manager to the solutions engineer, has their own Node. Those Nodes work together and learn from each other, so winning playbooks spread across the whole team automatically.
Early users are seeing up to 80% higher sales productivity, with Node saving them two hours a day that they put back into serving customers. Node will be GA in early 2027, and teams can sign up for early access today at

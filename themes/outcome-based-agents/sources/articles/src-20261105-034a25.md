Spurs' Dylan Harper: Chips in 21 points against Mavs&nbsp;&nbsp;CBS Sports

[page text]
Harper
 posted 21 points (9-15 FG, 1-2 3Pt, 2-2 FT) and five rebounds over 36 minutes during Wednesday's 112-111 win over Dallas.
It's the fourth time in eight games to begin the season that Harper has produced 20-plus points. The second-year guard is taking a big leap forward from his rookie campaign, averaging 18.6 points, 5.6 boards, 4.6 assists, 1.3 threes and 1.0 steals in 33.1 minutes while shooting 50.0 percent from the floor and 37.0 percent from beyond the arc.

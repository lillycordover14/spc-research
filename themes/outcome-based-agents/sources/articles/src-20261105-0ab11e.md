YMCA of Greater Kalamazoo to host Adaptive Field Day
PORTAGE, Mich. (WOOD) — On Saturday, the Portage branch of 
YMCA of Greater Kalamazoo
 will host its second annual 
Adaptive Field Day
.
The event runs from noon to 2 p.m. at the 
YMCA on W. Centre Avenue
 west of Oakland Drive in Portage. It's free and open to all ages.
"It's free to members and nonmembers," said Grant Hazen, association adaptive coordinator with the YMCA of Greater Kalamazoo. "All you have to do is register for the event."

During Adaptive Field Day, anyone who 
registers online prior to the event
 is invited to participate in hands-on experiences showcasing some of the YMCA's programs.
"We're going to have a bunch of trial stations set up," Hazen said. "It's kind of like a sampler opportunity."
The adaptive sports programs with YMCA of Greater Kalamazoo are designed with adjustments in mind for athletes with physical, developmental or intellectual disabilities and are available to participants of all ages.

izzio artisan bakery Names Sara Kafadar President and Chief Commercial Officer Amid 40% Retail Sales Growth&nbsp;&nbsp;citybiz

[page text]
izzio artisan bakery
, a Colorado-based, family-owned sourdough bread producer, has expanded 
Sara Kafadar
‘s role to President and Chief Commercial Officer, establishing a shared leadership structure with CEO and co-founder Etai Baron as the company accelerates its national expansion.

Kafadar, who has served as Chief Commercial Officer since 2015, will co-lead the business alongside Baron, sharing responsibility for long-term strategy, organizational leadership and corporate reputation.

The appointment formalizes their longstanding partnership as izzio reports retail sales growth exceeding 40% year over year for the second consecutive year and expands its distribution network to more than 15,000 retail locations.

“Sara has been an integral partner in building izzio into the business it is today, helping consumers rethink what they expect from their food, from the ingredients it contains to the care behind how it’s made,” Baron said.

Kafadar will continue overseeing sales, marketing, research and development, and brand strategy, while taking on broader responsibilities for revenue growth, customer relationships and operational efficiency.

She brings decades of experience in the bakery industry, including leadership positions across IAWS Foods and ARYZTA businesses such as Delice de France, Cuisine de France and La Brea Bakery.

Baron, who has served as CEO since 2014, will continue guiding the company’s overall direction, organizational development and long-term business value.

The leadership restructuring follows a period of expansion for izzio, which has increased its manufacturing capacity, introduced new products and developed additional distribution channels.

Earlier this year, the company opened a second bakery in Thornton, Colorado, increasing production capacity to support growing retail demand for its sourdough products.

izzio has also expanded its relationships with national retailers and foodservice customers while identifying opportunities to increase distribution and product assortment across the United States and Canada.

The company was included on Bain & Company’s Insurgent Brands list in both 2025 and 2026, recognizing independent consumer brands demonstrating strong performance within their categories.

“Our passion for baking real sourdough the right way sets izzio apart and clearly resonates with consumers looking for delicious, digestible, and wholesome breads made with simple, high-quality ingredients,” Kafadar said.

“I’m proud of what we’ve accomplished together, and I am excited to build on that foundation as we bring real sourdough innovation into new categories and to more tables, driving our next phase of growth.”

Product innovation is another component of izzio’s expansion strategy.

Earlier in 2026, the company introduced Organic Artisan Sourdough Sandwich Breads, extending its portfolio beyond its established artisan loaves and heat-and-serve products.

It also launched a direct-to-consumer subscription service, allowing customers throughout the contiguous United States to order its sourdough products for home delivery.

Additional products launching in November include Cranberry Orange Sourdough Batard, Super Sour Sourdough and French Sourdough.

The company also plans to introduce sourdough-based products in categories beyond traditional bread in the coming months, representing a further diversification of its portfolio.

Founded in Colorado, izzio produces naturally fermented sourdough bread using traditional baking methods, including living sourdough starters, extended fermentation and small-batch production.

Its product lineup includes sliced artisan boules and batards, heat-and-serve breads and organic sandwich loaves.

The expanded leadership arrangement comes as izzio seeks to convert strong retail sales momentum into broader distribution, additional product categories and continued growth in foodservice and direct-to-consumer channels.

With Kafadar and Baron sharing executive leadership, the company aims to maintain its product positioning while increasing manufacturing scale and reaching more consumers across North America.

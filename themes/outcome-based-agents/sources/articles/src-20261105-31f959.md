Farm Friends keeps kicking it up a notch

THE PORCH POT SEASON has begun at the Farm Friend’s

Barn in Becker to raise funds for the organization.

Author:
 Submitted Photo

Since the very beginning of the year 2015 —when an abandoned barn, from Northern Metals Recycling lot (Xcel Energy), was moved across Hwy. 10 to a plot of donated land from Bill and Margaret Cox’s family, west of the Sherburne History Center — a multitude of farming projects have been planned and completed by Farm Friends Barn (FFB) volunteers. It is an agricultural education site for Becker Primary School, kindergarten and first graders and home schoolers in the community. 
Volunteers planned and helped at every step, assisting the young farm hands as tilling, planting, watering, weeding, and harvesting took place. The Becker Food Shelf received loads of squash, potatoes, beets, carrots, and onions. The Becker Community Garden started this past summer behind the Barn. 
Other projects include adult classes like making “Barn Quilts” for hanging on the outside of barns, and Garden Club meets every Monday morning in the summer for eight weeks for ages 7-12. 
The Annual Barn Sale in April is a highlight. This is when the Barn suddenly becomes bursting full of donated items from community members, and the three-day sale ends with no items left to sell. 
The large garden work has now been completed and the scarecrows somehow turned into ghosts. 
Now, (drum roll) ….it is Porch Pot season! 
Volunteers are making all the Porch Pots the week of Nov. 9. The Sale starts Nov. 12 and 13, 1-6 p.m., and ends Saturday, Nov. 14, 9-4 p.m. Large and small pots will be available. Barn Address: 10782 27th Ave SE, Becker MN 55308. Contact 
info@ farmfriendsbarn.org 
(320) 248-2615 
After the Porch Pot Sale, the Barn magically turns into a winter wonderland of Christmas trees, lights, and lots of vintage decorations from yesteryears. Guests from Shepherd of Grace Senior Community and Woodbriar Senior Living will visit the Barn in mid December and sit a spell, sing Christmas carols, and enjoy refreshments while talking about the holiday season in the Good Ol’ Days. 
Santa’s seventh annual visit to the Barn will happen on Dec 21., 4-7 p.m. Stay tuned for more information about horse sleigh rides, bonfire, and holiday refreshments. 
Farm Friends Barn is able to “Kick it Up a Notch” because of generous sponsors and donations from community members. Every donation is appreciated. All proceeds from fundraisers go to FFB. The volunteer number is growing. Volunteers are priceless and everyone is welcome to become a volunteer. 
Jean Johnson, chair of the FFB Board, is continuously talking about ways to further the FFB Mission. 
“At Farm Friends Barn we are focused on educating youth about agricultural practices, sustainable farming, and food production system,” she said. It will be an exciting 2027 year at the Barn.

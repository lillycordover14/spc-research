BASIS Independent Bellevue
 is expanding next fall by opening a lower school campus serving kindergarten through fourth grade.
Rebecca Huang, Bellevue's associate head of school, will lead it.
The new campus will be located at 15760 NE Fourth St., across from the school's current campus. The existing campus will become the upper school for grades five to 12. 
The newly renovated, two-story lower school campus will feature 16 classrooms; a dining hall; an exercise room; and dedicated spaces for music, performing arts, visual arts, and engineering. Outdoor spaces will include a playground and covered play area.  
"Families are looking for a school that takes their children's potential seriously from the very beginning," said Toby Walker, vice president of BASIS Independent Schools, in a press release. "This expansion reflects the demand we're seeing for a BASIS Independent education in Bellevue and our commitment to investing in the community. By creating dedicated spaces for students at different stages of their development, we're building on an educational experience that combines exceptional teachers, academic challenge, and the freedom to think boldly.” 
Applications are now open for kindergarten through fourth grade for the 2027–28 school year.

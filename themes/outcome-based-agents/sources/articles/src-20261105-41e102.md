What happened

Cardano (ADA) is trading at $0.20 after a 3.89% rise over the last 24 hours on Binance spot, with volume of $20,645,574 and a daily range of $0.19 to $0.20. That keeps price close to a short-term pivot rather than in a clear breakout, which is why the current move matters: ADA has improved, but it has not yet escaped the nearby resistance cluster.

Changelly’s November 3, 2026 forecast is an institutional projection, not a named analyst call. It puts November’s estimated average ADA value at $0.176, with a projected range of $0.161 to $0.191. Taken as a reference point, that forecast sits below the current spot price and suggests the market is being asked to prove strength before the month’s implied path is confirmed.

How the chart reads

The spot chart is still in a mixed position. ADA is above its 7-day simple moving average at $0.19, but it remains below the 20-day average at $0.21 and the 50-day average at $0.23. The 200-day average is also listed at $0.21, so the broader trend structure has not yet turned decisively in ADA’s favor.

Momentum indicators do not yet confirm a strong continuation. The 14-period RSI at 39.93 is neutral and shows the token is not stretched, but it also does not show forceful upside pressure. MACD is flat at -0.0101 versus a signal line of -0.0101, with a histogram of 0.0000, which points to a market that is still deciding whether the rebound has legs. Stochastic readings of 42.93 for %K and 34.35 for %D support that same read: there is room for movement, but not a clean momentum break.

Bollinger Bands keep the setup contained. The middle band is $0.21, the upper band is $0.23, and the lower band is $0.18, with %B at 0.3373. That places ADA in the lower half of its recent band structure and shows the move has not yet expanded into a stronger trend. Daily ATR(14) is $0.01, so short-term swings can still matter relative to price.

The levels provided by the data are straightforward: immediate resistance at $0.20 and strong resistance at $0.21; immediate support at $0.19 and strong support at $0.18. In practice, that leaves ADA compressed between nearby levels, with the next directional signal likely to come from whether price can stay above $0.20 and then reclaim $0.21.

What derivatives add

The derivatives picture is constructive, but it does not fully validate the spot move. Binance Futures funding is 0.0100%, which is neutral rather than aggressively one-sided. Open interest stands at 348,896,668 contracts, with a daily open interest value of $64,966,998 and a 24-hour change of -4.38%. That combination suggests leverage has not expanded behind the latest move; if anything, positioning has eased.

The flow data lean more bullish than the open interest backdrop. The 1-hour global long/short ratio is 2.0544, with Binance global accounts at 67.3% long and 32.7% short. Top traders are more tilted still, at 2.5740 long/short with 72.0% long and 28.0% short. The 1-hour taker buy/sell ratio is 1.1982, based on buy volume of 7,233,379 versus sell volume of 6,036,644, which shows aggressive buying over that window.

Those derivatives readings should be read carefully. The Binance long/short ratios describe Binance cohorts, not retail versus institutional positioning across the whole market. Funding only shows which side pays at that moment; by itself, it does not prove durable sentiment or confirm a lasting trend.

What would change the view

If ADA can reclaim $0.21 and hold above it, the next area to watch is $0.23, where the 50-day average and the upper Bollinger Band sit close together. That would not guarantee a trend reversal, but it would show that the current bounce has begun to overcome the overhead structure.

If ADA loses $0.19, the market opens back toward $0.18, which is both strong support and the lower Bollinger Band in the supplied data. A break below that area would weaken the case that this move is only a shallow recovery inside a still-corrective structure.

For now, the cleanest reading is simple: spot price has improved, derivatives lean modestly long, and the broader chart still needs confirmation.

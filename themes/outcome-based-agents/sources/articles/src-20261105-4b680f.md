Tabs and Adyen Partner to Bring Embedded Payments Into the Entire Revenue Lifecycle&nbsp;&nbsp;Fortune

[page text]
NEW YORK, NY, November  5, 2026 (EZ Newswire) -- 
Tabs
, the AI Operating System for Revenue, today announced a partnership with Adyen, the global financial technology platform of choice for leading businesses. Adyen is the first payments partner behind Tabs Pay, Tabs’ embedded payments product, which brings payment acceptance and management into the same platform where companies manage contracts, billing, collections, and revenue recognition.
Most finance teams bill in one system and collect in another. Payments are processed in a separate dashboard, and finance teams spend hours matching payments to invoices, tracking refunds, and reconciling payouts. Tabs Pay removes that handoff by running payments on the same contract, invoice, and customer record as billing and revenue.
“Finance teams shouldn’t have to switch systems to get paid,” said Ali Hussain, CEO of Tabs. “Adyen gave us the infrastructure to build a payments experience that is secure, reliable, and designed for complex billing. Together, we’re helping our customers collect cash faster and spend less time on reconciliation.”
With Tabs Pay, customers can pay by card or ACH from the invoices merchants send in Tabs. Instant bank verification, powered by Plaid and Adyen, confirms bank accounts in seconds, and failed payments are retried automatically, with each retry linked to the original invoice. Merchants can issue refunds, manage disputes, and track payouts in Tabs, next to the invoices and customer records they belong to.
“Embedded finance is transforming how modern platforms deliver value to their users,” said Blake Breathitt, SVP, Co-Head of Global Strategy at Adyen. “By integrating Adyen’s global financial infrastructure directly into Tabs Pay, finance teams no longer have to navigate fragmented tools to invoice, collect, and reconcile. We’re excited to partner with Tabs to streamline complex billing workflows and give businesses the speed, flexibility, and security they need to scale.”
Adyen’s platform gives Tabs the infrastructure to own the merchant experience while relying on Adyen for instant bank verification powered by Plaid, tokenization, split payments, payouts, and reporting. Adyen’s hosted components also allow Tabs to deliver a seamless single-dashboard experience within the Tabs platform while keeping sensitive card data within Adyen’s PCI Level 1 environment.
Tabs Pay is available to new and existing Tabs customers. To learn more, visit the 
Tabs Pay section of the Tabs website
.

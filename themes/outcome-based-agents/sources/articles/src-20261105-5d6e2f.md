Rich People Problems 
The beleaguered Pace Gallery is in the news again, this time after its 
United Kingdom tax filings
 revealed that it lost $5.4 million last year and has a $19.2 million debt-for-equity swap in the works with its US parent company. The filings also outlined plans made in 2025 to cut costs and reduce full-time positions.
The reports come in the wake of an abrupt downsizing over the summer, when the gallery laid off 50 workers and slashed 50 artists from its roster in one fell swoop. (Multiple Pace employees told 
Hyperallergic
 that they learned of the cuts only when the 
New York Times
 broke the story in early June; one artist said the gallery demonstrated "a callous breach of trust.") Additionally, Pace closed its Hong Kong gallery last October after 12 years and is still looking for a smaller space for its London outpost, which put its 8,600-square-foot location on the rental market in June.
Ebony L. Haynes Departs Zwirner
Left to right: Rose Lord, Junette Teng, and Ebony L. Haynes (photo Maria Baranova, courtesy Marian Goodman Gallery)
Nine months after Marian Goodman's 
passing
 at age 97, her namesake gallery has appointed 
Ebony L. Haynes
 as its inaugural senior director and global head of curatorial affairs. Haynes steps into the role after six years at David Zwirner, where she founded and led 52 Walker as a space run entirely by Black staffers. Under her leadership, the Tribeca gallery exhibited work by artists including Arthur Jafa, Cauleen Smith, Pope.L, and Gordon Matta-Clark. Marian Goodman Gallery also announced 
Rose Lord
 and 
Junette Teng
, two of its longtime partners, as co-presidents.
What Else Happened?
Left to right: Artist Hank Willis Thomas, AFA CEO Pauline Forlenza, curator Estrellita B. Brodsky, and LACMA CEO Michael Govan (photo Margaret Fox, courtesy American Federation of Arts)
Estrellita B. Brodsky
, 
Michael Govan
, and 
Hank Willis Thomas
 received the annual Cultural Leadership Awards from the American Federation of Arts (AFA).
Hedwig Fijen
 stepped down as director of Manifesta after 33 years at the biennial's helm. 
Emilia van Lynden
 and 
Catherine Nichols
, appointed general director and artistic director, respectively, will jointly organize the next edition in 2028.
Ade Omotosho
, former assistant curator at the Dallas Museum of Art, was named associate curator at the Studio Museum in Harlem.
Nolan Jimbo
, former assistant curator at the Museum of Contemporary Art, Chicago, was appointed Arnhold associate curator at the Whitney Museum of American Art.
Ido Radon
 won the Betty Bowen Award, which includes a solo show at the Seattle Art Museum in 2028.
The Sundance Institute Cultural Impact Residency was awarded to documentary filmmakers 
Sara Chishti
 and 
Amin Pakparvar
; fiction writer-directors 
Gabby Beans
, 
Lydia Matata
, and 
Rippin Sindher
; and fiction film and episodic writers 
Pedro Eiras
 and 
Makisa Francis
.

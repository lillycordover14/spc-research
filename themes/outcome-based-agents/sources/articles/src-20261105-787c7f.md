Val Vinson displays her ceramics and glass art at the Warner Springs Artisan Guild's One-of-a-Kind Gift, Art & Craft Show in Julian in 2024. Anza Valley Outlook/Diane Sieker photo
The Warner Springs Artisan Guild will hold their One-of-a-Kind Gift, Art & Craft Show at the Julian Town Hall Saturday and Sunday, Nov. 21 – 22, from 10 a.m. – 5 p.m..
The annual event never fails to attract art fans, friends, and seekers of unique holiday gifts. Admission is free.
Local artists will display original paintings, photography and art, hand-made cards, one-of-a-kind glasswork, custom leather items, hand-crafted woodwork, ceramics and jewelry. Artists will be on hand to chat with guests, describe their inspiration and techniques, and offer original items for sale.
The Julian Town Hall is located at 2129 Main St. in Julian. For additional details, please visit: 
https://www.warnerspringsartisanguild.com/events1.html

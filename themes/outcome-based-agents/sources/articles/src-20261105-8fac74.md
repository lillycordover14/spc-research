McFarland senior Ada Geiger is the Leader Independent Athlete of the Week, sponsored by Bank of Deerfield.
Geiger won an eighth place medal at the WIAA Division 2 State Cross Country Championships on Saturday at The Ridges Golf Course in Wisconsin Rapids, running a time of 18 minutes, 45.2 seconds.
Geiger, who holds the Spartans’ girls school record for the fastest 5K with a time of 18:28, ran the fastest-ever time for a McFarland female at the State Championships. Geiger helped the Spartan girls place fourth as a team with 152 points.

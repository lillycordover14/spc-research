Jailer 3: Nelson got a Freehand&nbsp;&nbsp;Telugu360

[page text]
Nelson Dilipkumar carved out the franchise of Jailer which gave a huge boost for Rajinikanth. Sun Pictures, the makers have earned big money through both the films. The team announced Jailer 3 and the project will take a long time to materialize. Sun Pictures has given a freehand to Nelson on the budgets, remunerations and on the casting. Nelson has the basic idea ready and he will spend months working on the final script.

Nelson is never in a hurry and he spends ample time on the scriptwork and the pre-production work. For Jailer 2, the makers have offered him a big remuneration but there was a restriction on the budgets. But for Jailer 3, Sun Pictures have assured a freehand to Nelson. The director will soon direct Rajinikanth and Kamal Haasan in a multi-starrer and the shoot is expected to commence next year. Jailer 3 is expected to roll in 2028. Nelson is also committed to do a film for Sithara Entertainments and NTR will play the lead role in this film.

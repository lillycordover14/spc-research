ADA Member Advantage endorses small-ship luxury cruise provider

Members receive up to $1,000 in savings

 

by 
Mary Beth Versaci

November 05, 2026

 
 

 

ADA Member Advantage has endorsed Gohagan & Company as its provider of small-ship luxury cruises.

For more than 40 years, Gohagan & Company has curated all-inclusive programs for travelers interested in the culture, history and traditions of destinations. Excursions feature private or early admission to museums, specially arranged architectural site visits, lectures by faculty members or other guest speakers, and forums with local residents.

2027 cruises include Fiji & Polynesia by Sea: A South Pacific Odyssey from March 12-24 and Istanbul to Athens: Ancient Wonders in Modern Luxury from Oct. 19-29.

ADA members receive up to $1,000 in savings with a $500 early-booking discount and a $500 pay-in-full discount.

To learn more, visit the 
ADA Member Advantage website
.

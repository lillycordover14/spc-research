05/11/2026 - Light makes the world visible but at the same time transforms it. It does not only illuminate forms, but constructs them, travels over them, alters our perceptions and creates atmosphere. The 
sixth issue of Edra Magazine – Our Point of View
 is dedicated to 
light
; this editorial project explores the company, the values on which it is founded, and the creators and ideas that bring its projects to life.

"
It is thanks to light that we can appreciate what surrounds us
", says 
Monica Mazzei
, vice president of Edra. "
Immaterial and vitally necessary, it creates relationships, knowledge, and understanding. The right kind is welcoming and creates well-being
."

Light has always been a tool of expression and research for Edra. Materiality and materials—yarns, polycarbonate, crystals, mirrors and metals—absorb, reflect and multiply both natural and artificial light, changing colours and depths. Light again becomes part of the project of installations and photography, recounting a timeless and universal language of elegance and revealing the deepest qualities of collection pieces.

The sixth issue of Edra Magazine – Our Point of View

Curator Silvana Annicchiarico, journalist Giampaolo Grassi alongside author Francesco Binfaré, critic Pierluigi Masini, architects Laura Arrighi, Patrizia Piccinini and Chiara Tassano, journalist Chiara Pasqualetti Johnson and writer Jasmin Jouhar enrich the magazine with their insightful contributions, guiding the reader on a discovery of architectures and landscapes in which light takes on a different character each time.

A special section is devoted to the 
Edra Gala Evening at La Scala in Milan
, dedicated to the 
Campana brothers
. On 23 April 2026, dance, music and Edra furnishings will transform the stage into a suspended space, bringing to life a shared experience of concrete imagination.

The evening then takes on a more intimate resonance with a speech by 
Humberto Campana
: "
Edra decided to produce Vermelha just as my brother Fernando and I were about to give up
” he recalled, “
No one seemed to believe in us. Then suddenly, someone understood our vision
". This is followed by an emotional tribute to his brother Fernando, who passed away in November 2022.

Francesco Binfaré
 addresses the theme of light in conversation with 
Giampaolo Grassi
, interrogating the relationship between light and vision and the idea that what we see is a "
metaphor for reality.
" "
We need light to comprehend, which doesn't mean understanding, or knowing, but taking in, to have much
", reflects Binfaré.

Pierluigi Masini
 travels through art chasing light as Nietzsche’s ‘
dancing star
’ capable of revealing emotions and passages of history: the revelatory light of Piero della Francesca, the crisp and measured light of Johannes Vermeer, the unreal light of William Turner, the vibrant light of Giacomo Balla and, finally, the morning light of Edward Hopper.

Instead, 
Silvana Annicchiarico
 examines the relationship between light and the Edra collection, interpreting light as fundamental to experiencing materials and forms with presence, and involving photography and the construction of space. In the 
Campana brothers
’ designs, the mirror fragments light, destabilising the gaze while opening up unexpected possibilities. With 
Jacopo Foggini
, light is already inherent in the material: the polycarbonate captures and reflects light along paths that cannot be fully controlled. In the Dilly lamp, light becomes the source: the polycarbonate filament does not merely support it, but generates it.

The Places

At the 
Swarovski Kristallwelten museum
 in Wattens, crystal becomes a luminous material within a contemporary chamber of wonders, in dialogue with the Edra pieces from the Diamand Collection Cristallized™ with Swarovski®.

 light radiates from the bottles' glass and illuminates the splendour of a grand dome, reflecting the vibrant hues of Vermelha Oro.

, alongside CONI as Official Partner, continues the collaboration begun in 2016. In Milan, Cortina d'Ampezzo and Livigno furnishings contribute to creating welcoming spaces, highlighting relationships between sport, body, space, and inhabiting.

The Magazine concludes with the latest initiatives at 
Edra Palazzo Durini 
in Milan and the local area, with exhibitions, publications and the thoughts of Edra collaborators.

Edra Magazine – Our Point of View: the project

Launched in 2020 on an idea by Vice-President 
Monica Mazzei
, the Edra Magazine editorial project traces the company's history and core principles, showcases its projects, and explores the role and value of the Edra collection in defining living space.

Since issue 0 of Edra Magazine, six further issues have been published, dedicated respectively to beauty, materials, vision, time, nature, and light.

Edra Magazine is created with editorial curation by 
Laura Arrighi
 and graphic design and photography direction by 
Stefano Pasqualetti
.

The paper edition allows readers to fully appreciate the quality of the images and words. A collection to treasure on bookshelves in the home, in showrooms, hotels and museums. 

It is available at authorized retailers and online at edra.com, where digitized articles can be consulted in the Edra Magazine section in addition to the pdf format.

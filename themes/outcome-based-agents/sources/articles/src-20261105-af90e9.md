T-Bone Crash On Route 4 Near Three Notch Road Causes Traffic Delays&nbsp;&nbsp;The BayNet

[page text]
MARYLAND —
 Motorists traveling along Route 4 Thursday morning, November 5, 2026, were advised to expect traffic delays following a two-vehicle T-bone collision involving a Ford F-150 pickup truck and a blue Toyota sedan.

The crash was reported at approximately 7:58 a.m., with initial information indicating that the Toyota sedan struck the Ford F-150 in a T-bone-style collision.

Airbag deployment was reported in one of the vehicles.

Emergency medical personnel evaluated one occupant at the scene who complained of neck pain but declined further medical care and signed a refusal of treatment.

Motorists traveling through the area should expect delays as emergency crews respond and the crash scene is cleared.

Motorists can monitor traffic conditions and check for delays using 
The BayNet’s live traffic cameras
 before heading out.

The crash remains under investigation.

VADOC kept close tabs on sex offenders on Halloween, to keep kids safe&nbsp;&nbsp;Augusta Free Press

[page text]
Photo: © Daxiao Productions/Adobe Stock

I’m a fan of something the 
Virginia Department of Corrections
 did on 
Halloween Night
 to try to ensure that 
convicted sex offenders
 couldn’t mess around with kids who are out trick-or-treating.

VADOC
 engaged in efforts across the 
Commonwealth
 last week with local law enforcement, staffing community events, and working through programs called “
Trick Nor Treat
” and “
Operation Porch Light Out
.”

“Trick Nor Treat” had probationers and parolees reporting to a designated location on Halloween night. “Operation Porch Lights Out” reminded sex offenders under community supervision of their behavioral expectations on Halloween.

Other parts of this effort from VADOC

Sex offenders under supervision were instructed not to decorate their homes, hand out candy, or have any external lights on at their residence.

VADOC Probation & Parole districts
 established curfews for sex offenders on Halloween and conducted random home contacts.

Sex offenders who live in localities hosting fall festivals for children were instructed not to attend these events.

District P&P offices and the 
Sex Offender Programs and Monitoring Unit
 checked on the sexually violent offenders under their supervision, especially those with minor victims.

Results

VADOC said today in a release that it had filed 12 probation violation warrants following its Halloween night compliance checks of sex offenders.

“Thank you to every one of our Probation & Parole officers who worked tirelessly to make Halloween weekend a safe and enjoyable experience for all Virginians,” 
VADOC Director Joseph Walters
 said. “Halloween is a celebratory time for so many, and the dedication that our corrections team showed to ensure public safety is commendable. I also thank our community and law enforcement partners for their assistance throughout the weekend.”

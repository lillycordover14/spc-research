WOODBURY — Holiday shoppers looking for unique, handcrafted gifts will find plenty of inspiration at the Flanders Holiday Artisan Marketplace, opening Saturday, November 7, at the historic Van Vleck House, located at 5 Church Hill Road in Woodbury.
One of the largest artisan marketplaces in the region, this beloved annual tradition features the work of more than 70 local artisans and makers. Visitors can browse an ever-changing selection of handmade gifts, holiday décor, jewelry, pottery, fiber art, scarves, quilts, wreaths, soaps, ornaments, local honey and maple syrup, children’s items, fine art and much more.
Beautifully decorated for the holiday season, the Van Vleck House provides a warm and festive shopping experience while supporting local artists and craftspeople. Whether searching for the perfect holiday gift or a special treat for yourself, the Marketplace offers something for everyone.

“The Holiday Artisan Marketplace has become a cherished tradition for many families as they begin their holiday shopping,” said Vince LaFontan, Executive Director of Flanders Nature Center & Land Trust. “We’re proud to showcase the incredible talent of local artisans while providing visitors with a festive shopping experience that supports both local makers and Flanders’ environmental education and land conservation programs.”
The Holiday Artisan Marketplace will celebrate its Grand Opening on Saturday, November 7, and will remain open daily from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. through Wednesday, December 23. The Marketplace will be closed on Thanksgiving Day.
By shopping at the Flanders Holiday Artisan Marketplace, visitors support both local artisans and Flanders Nature Center & Land Trust’s environmental education and land conservation programs.

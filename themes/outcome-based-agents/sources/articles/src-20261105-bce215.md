Nitrosamine impurities: guidance for marketing authorisation holders

 

The European Medicines Agency (EMA) has assessed the risk of nitrosamine formation or presence during the manufacture and storage of human medicines. EMA has also provided guidance to marketing authorisation holders to avoid or minimise the presence of nitrosamine impurities.

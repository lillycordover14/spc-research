Mikey Madison in Dior at Harper's Bazaar Women of the Year Awards&nbsp;&nbsp;Red Carpet Fashion Awards

[page text]
Mikey Madison joined 
Cate Blanchett
 on the red carpet for the 
2026 Harper’s Bazaar Women of the Year Awards
 in London this evening.

Max Cisotti/Dave Benett/Getty Images for Harpers Bazaar UK

The Oscar winner wore a grey silk 
Dior
 column dress with a partial overskirt that immediately brought 1950s Hollywood glamour to mind. The fitted, strapless bodice and sculpted volume around the hips recall the couture silhouettes of that decade, particularly Christian Dior’s iconic 1947 New Look. It’s a beautiful balance of structure and femininity.

Much like 
Andrea Riseborough
 in ivory yesterday, I don’t feel this silvery-grey hue washes Mikey out. The subtle sheen of the fabric, the tonal floral pattern and the contrast with her dark hair all work in her favour. She didn’t need a bold lip colour to make the look work, either. Her soft pink makeup feels entirely in keeping with the mood.

And then there are those shoes. The colourful, floral-embellished 
Dior
 sandals were an unexpected choice. I can’t recall paying much attention to Mikey’s footwear on previous red carpets, yet these were impossible to ignore. Against the muted dress, they provide a playful contrast that I absolutely love.

Her 
Tiffany & Co.
 jewellery included a 
Sixteen Stone
 necklace, 
Bird on a Rock
 pendant and earrings, and a 
Jean Schlumberger Swirl 
ring set with a blue zircon.

Styled by Andrew Mukamal.

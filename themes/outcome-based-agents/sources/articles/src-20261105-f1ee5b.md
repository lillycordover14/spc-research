HAWLEY, Minn. — Just over a month ago, Dilworth-Glyndon-Felton walked off the volleyball court at Barnesville High School 3-0 winners over the Trojans.
Barnesville avenged that loss on Wednesday. Backed by Piper Thompson’s seven kills, the Trojans defeated the Rebels in straight sets during the Minnesota Section 8, Class 2A semifinals at Hawley High School.

ADVERTISEMENT

Set scores were 25-8, 25-20, 25-21.
Laney Shirek recorded the final kill for top-seeded Barnesville (22-8), which advanced to Saturday’s Section 8-2A championship match for the first time in four seasons.
Saturday’s title match will take place at 7 p.m. at Moorhead High School, with the Trojans set to take on No. 3 seed East Grand Forks (20-9).
The Green Wave defeated No. 2 seed Thief River Falls 3-2 (21-25, 25-18, 21-25, 25=21, 15-9) in Wednesday's second semifinal contest.
“We’re going to put our head down and keep working hard every day,” Thompson said. “There’s no shortcuts.”

Barnesville's Allison Axness sends the ball over the net during a Minnesota Section 8-2A semifinals volleyball match against Dilworth-Glyndon-Felton on Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026, at Hawley.
Chris Flynn / The Forum

Barnesville set the tone with a 9-0 scoring run to open the first set.
“It really is (a dream start),” Barnesville head coach Megan Askegaard said. “You win that serve and that captain's coin flip and you don’t expect that in a semifinal game. It gave us a good little momentum boost to start the night.”

ADVERTISEMENT

Askegaard said her team was ready to play from the get-go and the opening set showed it.
“My kids showed up to play (tonight),” Askegaard said. “I honestly have no words. It’s such a game of momentum and right from the start, it was unbelievable the way that they took those first couple of points right away and just kept rolling with it.”

Thompson is one of nine seniors on the Trojans roster and that experience showed Wednesday. Following Thompson in kills were fellow seniors Mya Stuvland with six and Allison Axness with five.
Senior Jayla Amundson led Barnesville with three aces to go with 15 digs defensively.
“When you have that many seniors, you never know how it’s going to go,” Askegaard said. “It could go one way or the other, and (tonight), it definitely went in our favor where the seniors carried us through and they want it.”
DGF, the section No. 5 seed, ended its season with a 15-14 overall record.

Dilworth-Glyndon-Felton's Ava Haas taps the ball over the net during a Minnesota Section 8-2A semifinals volleyball match against Barnesville on Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026, at Hawley. 
Chris Flynn / The Forum

Thompson said losing to the Rebels in the regular season helped fuel the Trojans’ redemption in the postseason.

ADVERTISEMENT

“I think that kind of lit a fire under us,” Thompson said. “We wanted it even more. Just because they swept us, it doesn’t mean anything. We came back and worked super hard.
“We kind of aimed for their setter a lot and came out swinging right away. Just super aggressive.”
Barnesville will now look to advance to the state tournament for the first time since 2022 and just the third time in program history.

Barnesville's Addyson Rasmussen spikes the ball during a Minnesota Section 8-2A semifinals volleyball match against Dilworth-Glyndon-Felton on Wednesday, Nov. 4, 2026, at Hawley.
Chris Flynn / The Forum

“That’s been their goal from the beginning,” Askegaard said. “That’s where they wanted to get and their saying for the year is, ‘All it takes is all you’ve got.’
“So they’re going to go out, compete on Saturday and we’ll see what happens.”
Thompson said making the state tournament is something her and her teammates have dreamed about since they were young.
“We’ve wanted it so bad since we were little,” Thompson said. “We have just one end goal, going to state. We all work super hard in and out of practice and we’re just one big family.”

The 2026 
Harper’s Bazaar
 Women of the Year Awards returned to Claridge’s this evening, celebrating the trailblazers, fashion changemakers and iconic voices shaping culture today. 
The annual event, held in partnership with The Platinum Card by American Express, honoured inspiring women across film, television, art, music, literature, sport and business. Among those in attendance were the cover stars of this month’s issue: model Stephanie Cavalli, 
The Social Reckoning'
s Mikey Madison, rising star Bella Maclean, and national treasure 
Claudia Winkleman
.
This year’s winners included Amelia Dimoldenberg, who, after leaving behind her beloved 
Chicken Shop Date 
series, is now preparing for the silver screen; Dina-Asher Smith for her record-breaking sporting inspiration; the inimitable Glenn Close and actress Cate Blanchett. “She steals a scene and never does the same show twice. Our Theatre Actress of the year is a leading lady whose presence lights up every stage,” shared 
Bazaar
’s editor-in-chief, Lydia Slater, of Blanchett’s award.
Ahead, discover all of the looks from the evening, from sculptural gowns to sharp tailoring. 
With thanks to Veuve Clicquot, Range Rover, Cloudy Bay and Paul Thomas Flowers.

Korea to Raise Drug Approval Fees by Up to Sixfold&nbsp;&nbsp;Seoul Economic Daily

The Ministry of Food and Drug Safety will raise fees for drug approvals and reviews by as much as about sixfold, saying it needs funding to hire more specialized reviewers as clinical, statistical and quality data grow more complex and advanced technologies are increasingly applied.
The ministry said on the 6th that it had issued public notice of proposed amendments to the Rules on the Safety of and Support for Advanced Biological Products and to the Regulations on Fees for Drug Approvals, which would adjust fees for product approvals, approval changes and preliminary reviews of advanced biological products, new biological drugs and data-submission drugs other than new drugs.
The steepest increase applies to data-submission drugs — products that are not new drugs but require separate data submissions for safety and efficacy reviews. The product approval fee for these will rise about 6.1-fold to 16.041 million won from 2.609 million won.
The product approval fee for new biological drugs will rise about 2.6-fold to 20.71 million won from 8.031 million won. Advanced biological products that are not new drugs will also rise about 2.6-fold, to 20.71 million won from about 8.03 million won. Advanced biological products include cell therapies, gene therapies and tissue-engineered products.
Fees for approval changes and preliminary reviews will also increase. The approval change fee for data-submission drugs will rise about threefold to 7.76 million won from 2.609 million won. Among preliminary reviews, the fee for safety and efficacy reviews will rise about 7.1-fold to 7.134 million won from 1.004 million won, while the fee for reviewing manufacturing and quality control standards will rise about 11.5-fold to 3.728 million won from 325,000 won.
Fees for customized reviews of advanced biological products will also go up. The safety and efficacy review fee will rise about 4.7-fold to 9.426 million won from 2.008 million won, and the manufacturing and quality control review fee will rise more than 12-fold to 3.728 million won from 305,000 won.
Clinical, statistical and quality data submitted with drug approval applications have grown more complex in recent years, and the increasing use of the latest technologies has raised both the difficulty of reviews and the number of specialists required. An official at the ministry said the fee overhaul would lay the groundwork for expanding approval and review staff and improve the quality of reviews for highly complex drugs.

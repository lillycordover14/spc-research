BRAZIL, Ind. (WTHI) - A former Clay County 4-H leader is facing a felony theft charge after investigators said he stole nearly $29,000 from the Horse and Pony Club.
Travis Nuckols
(Clay County Sheriff's Office)
What court documents say
According to court documents, Travis Nuckols of Brazil allegedly took more than $28,000 from the club’s bank account between September 2022 and December 2025 while he was the group’s leader.
The money was withdrawn through checks, ATM transactions and debit card purchases.
How the missing money was found
The missing funds were discovered after Nuckols stepped down and a new leader took over the account.
4-H officials and investigators said Nuckols admitted taking the money. During an interview with Indiana State Police, he said it became easy and that alcohol was a factor during that time.
Repayment and charge
Purdue Extension, which runs the 4-H program, arranged a repayment plan. Nuckols paid the full amount back in six monthly installments and made the last payment in July.
He is charged with Level 6 felony theft.

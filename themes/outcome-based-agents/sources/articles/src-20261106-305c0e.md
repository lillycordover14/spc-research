Nebraska started 5-0, now they’re 5-3.
They blew three straight fourth quarter leads against Oregon, Indiana and Washington.
The offense averaged 13.7 PPG in those games.
While the defense has allowed late touchdowns — including multiple scores in both Oregon and Indiana games — overall, they aren’t the issue.
DC Rob Aurich comes from San Diego State, and the Nebraska defense has dropped its PPG allowed to 17 from 24.
The run defense has stood out, allowing less than 80 YPC.
Meanwhile, while the Illini rank 6th in the Big Ten in rushing offense,
 it’s been inconsistent.
While they ran the ball well in the second half vs. Oregon and Maryland, they’ve struggled in first half.
It’ll be key for QB Katin Houser and his WRs to keep Nebraska honest in the pass.
If Illinois wins, they’ll improve to 6-3 and clinch a 3rd straight bowl bid, which has not happened since 1992.

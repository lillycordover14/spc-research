  Goldies Adonis made a winning show debut for the Goldie family, Townfoot, Mouswald, taking the male and supreme title at the inaugural Limousin National Calf Show at Agri Expo. Judge Thomas Illingworth, of the Glenrock herd, chose the July 2025-born bull, out of Goldies Joyeuse and by Goldies Torpedo. Torpedo topped Carlisle at 32,000gns in October 2023 and sired Goldies Vainqueur, the overall champion and 38,000gns sale-topper at Carlisle in May.

  Reserve male and reserve supreme was Grahams Anders, from Robert and Jean Graham, Boquhan Home Farm, Kippen. The November 2025-born bull is by the 26,000gns Foxhillfarm Ourbest and out of Grahams Precious, Limousin and continental inter-breed champion at the 2021 Royal Highland Showcase.

  Female champion was Seisiog Annie, reserve baby beef champion at this year’s Beef Expo, from Tecwyn Jones, Caer Gwrli, Holyhead, Anglesey. The October 2025-born heifer is out of the 20,000gns Wilodge Opulent and by the 14,000gns Seisiog Rio.

  The Goldies also took reserve female with Goldies Bella, an April-born daughter of Goldies Melody and Ampertaine Majestic, the 35,000gns Carlisle supreme champion in May 2018.

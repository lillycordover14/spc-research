MOLINE, Ill. (KWQC) - Woodrow Wilson Middle School opened a new adaptive playground Friday.
School officials say the space is designed to foster inclusive learning, recreation and social connection.
The space includes an adaptive playground, a patio and picnic tables. It will provide for varied sensory and physical needs of students across grade levels.
Tom Ferguson, building principal at Woodrow Wilson Middle School, said the project is about more than the new equipment.
Students enjoy the new playground equipment at the ribbon cutting ceremony.
(KWQC)
“It’s almost like a tear comes to your eye that these kids are having so much fun, right, they need that experience in school wherever they go, to have fun, to be included, and to be able to provide that to them is just amazing,” Ferguson said.
Ferguson said the playground is the product of multiple years of planning that included the community, school staff and parents. 
Students enjoy the new playground equipment at the ribbon cutting ceremony.

Lando Norris set the pace in opening practice at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix in McLaren’s upgraded 2026 Formula 1 car. 
The 26-year-old Briton was just 0.082 seconds faster than Mercedes’ newly-crowned F1 world champion Andrea Kimi Antonelli in the opening hour of running at Interlagos.
McLaren has brought a major upgrade for its MCL40 car to Brazil, featuring tweaks to the floor, an updated rear wing, and a new front wing concept that it plans to carry over into the 2027 season. 
Max Verstappen, who complained that the shifts in his Red Bull were “unacceptable” during FP1, ended up third-quickest, 0.236s adrift of Norris. The four-time world champion also reported steering issues with his RB22.

Lewis Hamilton was three-tenths behind Norris on a day Ferrari team principal Fred Vasseur is set to sit down with both his drivers to discuss the fallout of their costly opening-lap clash at the Mexico City Grand Prix last Sunday. 
Behind the seven-time world champion was George Russell, who was fifth-fastest. The Mercedes driver will serve a five-place grid penalty in Brazil for taking on a fresh power unit. 
 
Isack Hadjar was sixth fastest in the second Red Bull, ahead of Gabriele Mini, who impressed in the Alpine as the fastest of the six rookie drivers taking part in FP1. 
One of Brazil’s two home drivers, Gabriel Bortoleto took eighth for Audi, ahead of Franco Colapinto in the Alpine and Liam Lawson, who rounded out the top-10 for Racing Bulls.

Crews are battling a garage fire in a Crosby-area neighborhood on Friday afternoon.

SkyEye13 images show a burning garage in a Crosby-area neighborhood on Friday, Nov. 6, 2026.
Firefighters could be seen hosing down the burning garage doors.

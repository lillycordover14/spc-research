Nollywood actor, Kunle Afod, has raised concerns over the absence of emergency response services at the scene of a serious road accident along the Ibadan-Sagamu Expressway in Ogun State, calling for urgent intervention to rescue victims reportedly left helpless.

Afod, who witnessed the accident around Ishapade in Ogun State, expressed sadness and frustration over the failure of emergency responders to arrive at the scene approximately 20 minutes after the incident.

In a video message shared from the accident scene, the actor appealed to relevant authorities and emergency agencies to immediately dispatch ambulances and rescue personnel to attend to the injured victims.

According to him, several people were seen lying helplessly at the scene, while a bus and another vehicle involved in the accident were visible behind him.

He lamented that despite repeated attempts to contact emergency services, no ambulance or rescue team had arrived to provide medical assistance to the victims.

"It's a terrible accident we met on the way along the Ibadan-Sagamu Expressway right now. And it's so sad that for about 20 minutes that we've been here, there is no ambulance, no emergency services to come and help them. Nobody. We've been trying to call emergency people to come and help them," Afod said.

The actor further expressed concern that motorists and other road users were not stopping to assist in transporting the victims for urgent medical attention.

"See people lying down helplessly. You can see them, you can see the car behind me, the bus behind me now. Nobody, nobody in Ogun State to come and rescue them," he added.

Afod said attempts to reach emergency responders had continued without any apparent response, stressing that the victims urgently needed assistance.

"Till now we've been trying to call, we've been trying to make emergency calls. Nobody, nobody is trying to help them at all. Look at them, look at the bus, look at people lying down," he stated.

He questioned the situation, appealing to residents, emergency responders and government authorities to intervene before the victims' conditions deteriorated.

"What is happening in this state right now? What is happening? People should please come to their rescue right about now," the actor pleaded.

Identifying the location as Ishapade, Afod reiterated that the accident was serious and urged authorities to respond immediately.

"This accident is terrible, it is terrible. Ogun State, no ambulance to come and rescue people. People should please come to their aid right now, right now," he said.

The actor's account has drawn attention to concerns about emergency response and the availability of timely medical assistance for road accident victims along major highways.

However, there was also no immediate official statement from the Ogun State Government or relevant emergency response agencies regarding the incident at the time of this report.

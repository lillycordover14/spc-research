Ellie Shaw

The Public Affairs Council
 has elected American Express director of federal government affairs Ellie Shaw as chair of its Board of Directors for the 2026–27 term. Shaw will lead the Council’s executive committee and board as the organization advances its Horizon 2028 strategic plan and continues strengthening its role as a partner to public affairs professionals worldwide. Shaw previously served as chair-elect of the board and executive committee and chairs PAC PALS, a networking community for PAC, advocacy, consulting and political fundraising professionals. Shaw succeeds Scott Orr of Fidelity Investments, who will continue serving on the executive committee as immediate past chair. “Ellie brings a powerful combination of professional expertise, collaborative leadership and a commitment to helping others succeed,” said Public Affairs Council president and CEO Nneka Chiazor. “Her dedication to developing talent and building connections across our profession reflects the values at the heart of the Council.”

Mark Allen & Co
.
 moves into its new headquarters at 41 East 11th Street in Union Square. The space is home to the firm’s New York team and will host thought leadership events for clients and press. The open, newsroom-style office brings together the firm’s PR strategists and former journalists and sits at one of Manhattan’s busiest transit hubs. In the past year, the agency has acquired Miami-based Alban Communications, opening its first Miami office and adding expertise in U.S. Hispanic media, real estate, and health and wellness. It also launched a new brand identity and website. “It’s been a big year for us, and our new home is the next step,” said Mark Pasetsky, founder and CEO of Mark Allen & Co. “It puts us at the center of New York’s creative, media and tech worlds, with spaces to bring clients, journalists and industry leaders together.”

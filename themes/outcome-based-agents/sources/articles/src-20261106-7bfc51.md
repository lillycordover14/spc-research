The works of three members of the Hunterdon County Rug Artisans Guild have been accepted for inclusion in the 2026 “Celebration of Hand-Hooked Rugs 36,” a premier juried collection of the year’s best hand-hooked rugs sponsored by Rug Hooking Magazine.
They are:
•“Frigga,” by Dana Psoinas, of Woodbury, N.Y., in the category of Rugs Based on Original Designs. Psoinas has been rug hooking for the past 15 years. She also is the president of her local guild. Her rugs have appeared in several rug-hooking books. Frigga can transform into an owl – or any raptor – using her magical feathers.
•“Prayer Before Battle,” by Mary Passerello, of Flemington, N.J., in the category of Rugs Based on Adaptations.
Passerello started rug hooking in 2013. She has always enjoyed sewing and crafting. Her first rugs were primitives. Now, she prefers hooking more detailed rugs that challenge her with color choices and subtle shading details. She attends rug schools almost every year. Her rug is adapted from the painting “A General Before His King” by Tim Davis.
•“Late Bloomer,” by Lisa Savino, of Whitehouse Station, N.J., received an Honorable Mention. It was designed by Fritz Mitnick.
The mission of the Hunterdon County Rug Artisans Guild is to perpetuate the tradition and art of rug hooking in all its various forms. Visit 
www.hcrag.org
 for information on the guild’s programs and activities.

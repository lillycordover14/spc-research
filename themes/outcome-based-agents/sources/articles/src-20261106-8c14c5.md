AMD Sampling Versal AI Core Adaptive SoC in Enhanced Space-Grade Package for the Most Demanding Missions

                                AMD samples the Versal AI Core XQRVC1902 in an enhanced space-grade lidless organic package — built for 15-year missions and testing to MIL-PRF-38535 Class Y. Pin-compatible with VC1902, it brings adaptive AI compute to GEO, cislunar, and deep-space payloads without sacrificing reliability.                            

The AMD Versal AI Core XQRVC1902 adaptive SoC is designed to support space missions lasting up to 15 years. AMD is testing the device to the U.S. military specification MIL-PRF-38535 Class Y qualification. (Credit: AMD)

AMD today announced sampling with early access customers of the AMD Versal™ AI Core XQRVC1902 adaptive SoC in an enhanced space-grade package. Developed by AMD, the advanced space-grade organic lidless package is designed to support space missions lasting up to 15 years. AMD is currently testing the device to the U.S. military specification MIL-PRF-38535 Class Y qualification, the highest level of quality and reliability under the standard for spaceflight components.

The new package incorporates conservative design rules and an enhanced organic substrate material to mitigate thermal and mechanical stresses in space. Combined with space-grade chip capacitors with established flight heritage, it extends the advanced programmable logic and in-orbit processing capabilities of the XQR Versal AI Core device to missions requiring the highest levels of reliability assurance. These include human-rated, long-duration and other high-value missions such as geosynchronous Earth orbit, cislunar orbit, heliocentric orbit and deep space missions.

“Space missions increasingly require both advanced onboard processing and exceptional long-term reliability,” said Ken O’Neill, Aerospace and Defense lead systems architect at AMD. “The availability of XQR Versal AI Core adaptive SoC samples in AMD advanced space-grade packaging marks an important milestone toward Class Y qualification and enables customers to construct engineering models using this technology for missions demanding absolute reliability.”

The XQR Versal AI Core adaptive SoC delivers adaptable, high-performance processing for onboard payload applications with dramatic generational increases in compute density for vector-based algorithms, system logic cells, on-board SRAM and multigigabit transceivers.

The lidless design package improves thermal performance and further expands the ability of AMD to support the full spectrum of space programs, from commercial “new space” missions using commercial off-the-shelf devices to human-rated missions and the next generation of orbital, deep space and long-duration exploration. The new package is pin-compatible with existing AMD Versal AI Core VC1902 commercial, defense-grade and space-grade devices in the 2197 ball grid array package.

Availability: 
AMD Versal AI Core XQRVC1902 adaptive SoCs in the enhanced space-grade packages are described in 
AMD datasheet DS946
 and are currently sampling with early access customers. Class Y flight-qualified units are expected to be available in the second half of 2027.

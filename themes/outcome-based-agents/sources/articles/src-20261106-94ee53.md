A 34-year-old Berks County man died Friday in a two-vehicle crash that closed a portion of Route 322 in Clay Township for several hours.
Northern Lancaster County Regional 
Police said
 Dennis McDevitt died at Lancaster General Hospital. Police said the crash was being investigated.
The 6 a.m. crash and police activity closed Route 322 (West Main Street) from Clay School Road to Rock Road before it was reopened shortly before noon, the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation
 said
.
Earlier Friday, a white pickup truck with significant front-end damage was seen on Route 322, perpendicular to the direction of traffic, as police officers investigated the crash. The other vehicle, in a nearby front yard, was also badly damaged.

Clay Walker Heads Into The Holidays With New Family Christmas Album, Tour&nbsp;&nbsp;MusicRow.com

[page text]
A Clay Walker Family Christmas
,
 a new holiday collection featuring Walker, his wife 
Jessica
, and their five children, is out now.

The album features the Walkers including 
Will
 (18), 
Mary-Elizabeth
 (16),
 Eli
 (13), 
Ezra
 (8), and 
Christian
 (5) on timeless classics such as “All I Want For Christmas Is You,” “Have Yourself A Merry Little Christmas,” “Little Drummer Boy,” and more, as well as 
an original holiday single, “
All Wrapped Up in You.”

“There is nothing more special than the moments we share as family at Christmastime,” says Walker. “Music is a huge part of those memories, and I’ve dreamed about recording with my kids for years. Now that they are learned in the art, it just made perfect sense. The album will be here long after I am gone, and they will have it to play for their kids and grandkids, and the thought of that warms me to the core.”

Following the album’s release, the first-ever “A Clay Walker Family Christmas Tour” will kick off Nov. 27 in Warren, Ohio, wrapping Dec. 22 with a performance at the Grand Ole Opry in Nashville. Select shows will include a toy drive, encouraging fans to spread holiday cheer by bringing a toy for a child in need. The tour will also feature special holiday tour merchandise and appearances by Santa.

10. “Little Drummer Boy” (Mary-Elizabeth Walker, Ezra Walker)

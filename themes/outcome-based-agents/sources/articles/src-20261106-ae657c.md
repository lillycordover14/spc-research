The public is invited to the 10th Annual Little Whale Cove Artisan Showcase from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 7, at the Little Whale Cove Recreation Center.

The Little Whale Cove Recreation Center is located at 305 SW Singing Tree Lane in Depoe Bay.

Courtesy

Paintings, jewelry, photography, wearables, home decor, cards baked items and more will be available.
The Little Whale Cove Recreation Center is located at 305 SW Singing Tree Lane in Depoe Bay.

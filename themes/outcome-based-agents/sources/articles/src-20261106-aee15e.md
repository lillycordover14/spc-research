How did GAMCO perform in H1 FY27?

GAMCO reported Rs 254 crore in revenue from operations for H1 FY27, which management said was 79% above the corresponding prior-year period and represented about 84% of its full-year FY26 revenue. Total income was Rs 255 crore and total expenses were Rs 202 crore, resulting in earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, or EBITDA, of Rs 52.9 crore and an EBITDA margin of 21%.

GAMCO's H1 FY27 profit after tax, or PAT, was Rs 38.5 crore, compared with Rs 6.5 crore in H1 FY26. Management described the increase as almost sixfold and linked the reported progress to a four-vertical model comprising equity investments, real estate, logistics and hospitality, alongside its historic investing, trading and secured-lending activities.

Why was GAMCO's Q2 FY27 profit below Q1?

GAMCO's Q2 FY27 PAT was Rs 6 crore, lower than Rs 32 crore in Q1 FY27, as total expenses increased to Rs 129 crore from Rs 73 crore while revenue from operations rose to Rs 140.6 crore from Rs 114 crore. The second quarter nevertheless returned to profit from a Rs 8 crore loss in Q2 FY26, according to management.

GAMCO recorded Q2 FY27 EBITDA of Rs 12 crore and a 9% EBITDA margin, compared with EBITDA of Rs 41 crore in Q1 FY27 and an EBITDA loss of Rs 7 crore in Q2 FY26. Finance costs were Rs 6 crore in Q2 FY27, versus Rs 5 crore in both comparison quarters, which management attributed to borrowing for expansion in real estate, warehousing and hospitality.

What changed in GAMCO's balance sheet and leverage?

GAMCO's net worth was Rs 206.6 crore on September 30, 2026, up 24% from Rs 167 crore in March 2026, principally due to the half-year profit, management said. Total assets stood at Rs 557 crore, while borrowings were Rs 327 crore and other financial liabilities had declined to Rs 3 crore.

GAMCO's debt-to-equity ratio improved to about 1.6 times from 1.7 times because net worth grew faster than borrowings, according to management. The company said it funded expansion through debt and capital recycled from the sale of Visco Advisory, a warehousing asset sold for Rs 15.8 crore to a global institutional fund.

Management said the Visco Advisory sale generated a 158-times return on its investment over three years. It also said net worth had increased about 2.8 times over the last three years as its Kolkata residential projects, logistics parks and hospitality business moved from land acquisition and planning towards construction and potential revenue generation.

Which projects could expand GAMCO's revenue base?

GAMCO plans to launch 13.5 lakh square feet of proposed residential development in Kolkata within one to two years. Management expects the Alipore land parcel to generate Rs 250 crore of revenue and the Jessore Road project, held through joint venture Elika Realestate, to generate Rs 1,100 crore over five years.

In logistics, GAMCO has acquired land at Dankuni on Delhi Road with development potential of 40 lakh square feet and planned capital expenditure of about Rs 600 crore. Management is targeting rental yields of 8% to 10% and said construction had started, while revenue from the warehousing business was expected to enter its books from calendar 2028.

GAMCO also holds a 9.7% stake in the 212-acre OneWorld township in Lucknow, where 1,206 units have been completed and 1,750 units are under development. Management said it invested about Rs 19.4 crore in the stake, which has been revalued at Rs 153 crore, implying an unrealised gain of about Rs 134 crore.

When could GAMCO's hospitality business begin contributing?

GAMCO acquired a 33.33% stake in Blissara Resorts, which is developing a planned 140-key upper-upscale hotel on an 11-acre sea-facing site at Kodiyaghat in Port Blair. Management said the project had approvals including coastal-region clearance and, on the November 4, 2026 call, expected operations to begin within two to two-and-a-half years after construction starts.

GAMCO said it invested in Blissara Resorts at a post-money valuation of Rs 69 crore. Management said an international hotel operator would generally charge a base fee of 1.5% to 2.5% of gross revenue plus a share of gross operating profit, or GOP, but did not disclose final operator terms for the Port Blair property.

Management also identified hospitality opportunities in Lucknow, Himachal and Ayodhya, although these were prospective projects rather than committed transactions. It said each hotel under consideration could potentially generate at least Rs 100 crore of revenue at maturity and be valued above Rs 500 crore to Rs 600 crore, which were management expectations rather than reported outcomes.

Conclusion

GAMCO's H1 FY27 performance combined a rise in revenue to Rs 254 crore and PAT to Rs 38.5 crore with a 24% increase in net worth to Rs 206.6 crore. The second-quarter comparison also showed that profitability varied materially across the half year, with Q2 PAT of Rs 6 crore versus Rs 32 crore in Q1 as expenses and finance costs increased.

The next disclosed milestones are residential revenue from Uma Properties around March or April 2028, warehousing revenue from calendar 2028, and operations at the Port Blair hotel within two to two-and-a-half years after construction begins. Management's execution of the Rs 600 crore logistics plan, residential launches and hospitality development will determine how quickly real-asset income contributes alongside its investment activities.

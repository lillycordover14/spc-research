Celebrities at the 2026 Harper’s Bazaar Women of the Year Awards&nbsp;&nbsp;Red Carpet Fashion Awards

[page text]
Here is a roundup of the other ladies who joined 
Cate Blanchett
 and 
Mikey Madison
 the 
2026 Harper’s Bazaar Women of the Year Awards
 in London yesterday evening.

A Darker Mood

Taylor Russell 
wore a 
Dior 
paillette-covered dress that felt moodier than her usual red carpet choices, a feeling reinforced by her smoky eye makeup.

Max Cisotti/Dave Benett/Getty Images for Harpers Bazaar UK

A Rich Shade Of Wine

For a cold November evening, 
Annabelle Wallis’ 
wine-hued 
Zimmermann
 gown was a welcome dose of Fall glamour. The colour is beautiful on her, with the 
Elsa Peretti
 
Bone
 cuff in yellow gold completing the look.

Zimmermann Fall 2026

Max Cisotti/Dave Benett/Getty Images for Harpers Bazaar UK

A Few Inches Short

I wish 
Bella Maclean’s Louis Vuitton Fall 2026
  embroidered dress had been a little longer, just to conceal those black leather 
LV
 pumps. The dress itself is beautiful, but the shoes are a distraction for me. 
Bvlgari 
jewels completed her look.

Louis Vuitton Fall 2026

Max Cisotti/Dave Benett/Getty Images for Harpers Bazaar UK

More Of This, Please

Elizabeth Debicki’s
 
Wicker
 press tour passed me by, which is a shame because I wish she had served up looks like this custom velvet dress by 
Susie Cave
. The feathered neckline and hem give the dress an Old Hollywood glamour that suits her perfectly.

Max Cisotti/Dave Benett/Getty Images for Harpers Bazaar UK

Classic Gugu, With A Twist

Gugu Mbatha-Raw’s Erdem Resort 2027
 peach-toned satin and lace dress could have been another familiar slip-dress moment, but the asymmetric folds and straps gave it a different character. On the one hand, this is classic 
Gugu
, but there is a slight edge here that I appreciate. 
Manolo Blahnik 
sandals completed her look.

Erdem Resort 2027

Max Cisotti/Dave Benett/Getty Images for Harpers Bazaar UK

Red From Head To Toe

Lesley Manville 
wore a red 
Alberta Ferretti 
gown, accessorised with a matching 
Roger Vivier 
clutch. I love the lightness of the fabric, the colour coordination, right down to her pumps, and how regal she always looks.

Max Cisotti/Dave Benett/Getty Images for Harpers Bazaar UK

A Double-Take Moment

I had to do a double take when I saw 
Letitia Wright’s Prada Spring 2027
 look. The textured top immediately appealed to me, but the skirt was initially confusing. I couldn’t work out why the shape looked so awkward until I realised it was a skirt within a skirt. Usually one for tailoring, 
Letitia
 deserves credit for trying something different. Bonus points for the makeup and embellished shoes. 
Cartier
 jewels completed her look.

Prada Spring 2027

Max Cisotti/Dave Benett/Getty Images for Harpers Bazaar UK

Keeping It Simple In Navy

Rhea Norwood’s
 navy-blue 
Giorgio Armani 
gown is one of those pieces that proves you don’t need accessories to sell a look. The dress itself, and the way the blue brings out her eyes, were enough. And this is coming from someone who has spent the past two weeks celebrating statement earrings.

Gareth Cattermole/Gareth Cattermole/Getty Images

An Unexpected Colour Combination

Sam Taylor-Johnson
 wore 
Saint Laurent by Anthony Vaccarello
, and while the proportions might not work for everyone, it’s the colour combination which is working for me. You may remember that 
Natalia Dyer
 wore the same look look back in June.

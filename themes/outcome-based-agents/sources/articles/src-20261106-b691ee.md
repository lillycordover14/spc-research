Article Summary

Absolute Batman #25 sketch cover by Nick Dragotta, Jim Lee and Clay Mann sold for $18,850 on WhatNot.

Jim Lee promoted the live WhatNot and Twitch art jam, with Dragotta drawing Batman for the three-way cover.

The Absolute Batman #25 sketch cover set a new record, beating Dan Quintana’s $18,550 Absolute Batman sale.

Absolute Batman remains a powerhouse in DC’s Absolute Universe, with the series continuing to top sales charts.

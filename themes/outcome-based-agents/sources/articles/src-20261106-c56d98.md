Spencer, IA (northwestiowanow.com) —
 Spencer Rotarians remind you to stop by the book sale at the fairgrounds this weekend to shop for yourself or people on your Christmas list.

Sam Harper says it’s a win-win offering cheap books for readers, with all of the income supporting Rotary’s community projects.

And don’t forget to donate the books when you’re done.

The Spencer Rotary book sales started off with over 3 thousand titles Wednesday.

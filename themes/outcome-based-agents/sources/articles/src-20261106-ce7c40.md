12+1 London
 reaches a landmark moment with the 100th instalment of its acclaimed 
Numbers Series,
 welcoming label boss 
U Z Z V
 under his 
UNAI
 alter ego for the first time on the label.

Since its inception, the Numbers Series has been one of the defining concepts within the 
12+1 London
 catalogue: a continuous series of tracks stripped of conventional titles and identified solely by numbers. Rather than being defined by names, narratives or predetermined concepts, each release is reduced to its essential musical identity: a number, a sound and a moment in the evolution of the label.

Now, as the series reaches 
0100,
 the imprint celebrates one hundred chapters of this philosophy with a particularly significant collaboration. For this special single, label boss 
U Z Z V
 steps into the catalogue for the first time under his 
UNAI
 alias, a move that reflects the ongoing metamorphosis of his artistic identity and marks a new chapter in the evolution of his relationship with the label. Joining him on this milestone release is none other than 
Carlo Lio,
 a long-standing and influential figure within the international underground house and techno scene. Together, they deliver a track that feels perfectly aligned with the DNA of the 
Numbers Series
 while pushing its sound forward.

Built around the raw energy of Old Tech and 
Jackin House, 0100
 is a stripped-back, groove-driven production where punchy drums, infectious rhythmic patterns and carefully sculpted electronic textures come together with the unmistakable character of both artists. The result is a track that embodies what the 
Numbers Series
 has represented throughout its history: music without unnecessary labels, driven purely by rhythm, movement and production.

Reaching number 0100 is more than simply another catalogue milestone. The release also coincides with the 12th anniversary of 
12+1
 
London
 since the label was founded, making this a particularly significant moment in the history of the label and its catalogue. No titles.

No unnecessary narratives. Just numbers and the music behind them. With 0100, 
12+1 London
 celebrates both 100 chapters of the Numbers Series and 12 years of the label, as 
UNAI. (aka U Z Z V)
 and 
Carlo Lio
 deliver a powerful statement that perfectly reflects the label’s underground identity and its ongoing evolution.

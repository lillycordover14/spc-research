ROUND ROCK, Texas (KFDA) - The 2026 UIL Cross Country State Championships started Friday, November 6, at Old Settlers Park in Round Rock. 
The Canyon Lady Eagles won their third straight team title, 14th overall; the Gruver Lady Hounds claimed gold for the sixth time in program history.
Farwell’s Alexis Wade, Canyon’s Presli Pool and Perryton’s Jacob Erickson all won individual titles.

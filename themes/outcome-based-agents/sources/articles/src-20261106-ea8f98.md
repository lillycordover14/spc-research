Dr. Meg Schnoor reflects on a career shaped by education on Harper Talks

Harper College News Bureau

November 6, 2026

November 6, 2026

A suggestion from a Harper College professor helped Dr. Meg Schnoor discover a career
                              path that would shape her life. On the latest episode of Harper Talks – in which Harper
                              College alumni share their education, career and life journeys – the 1991 graduate
                              reflects on her experiences as a first-generation college student, meeting Branford
                              Marsalis while working for the Harper Box Office and how Harper prepared her for her
                              next steps, including Illinois State University.

“I just remember being very involved and being very happy here. I would have stayed
                              here for four years if it was the opportunity that you have now with the University
                              Center,” Schnoor said of her time at Harper. “I loved my time at Illinois State, but
                              I loved Harper more.

Listen to the 
full episode of Harper Talks
 as host and Associate Professor Maham Khan talks with Schnoor, a 2013 Harper Distinguished
                              Alumna. Now superintendent of the Northwest Suburban Special Education Organization
                              (NSSEO), Schnoor oversees a cooperative serving eight school districts in the northwest
                              suburbs. She also discusses the growing shortage of special education professionals,
                              opportunities for Harper students to enter the field and the lasting impact educators
                              can have on their students.

Harper Talks, a co-production between 
Harper College Radio
 and 
Harper Alumni Relations
, features Harper staff and faculty in conversation with Harper alumni about the role
                              the college played in their lives. You can listen to past episodes of Harper Talks
                              on

, WHCM and podcast streaming services, including Spotify and Apple Podcasts.

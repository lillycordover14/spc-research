Pacific Palisades Weekend Route: Coastal Skating and Artisan Markets Across Westside&nbsp;&nbsp;Palisades News

[page text]
Pacific Palisades families and coastal residents looking for weekend activities along the immediate oceanfront corridor have distinct seasonal and artistic events within a short drive down Pacific Coast Highway. In Downtown Santa Monica, the winter tradition returns with “Frost on 5th,” located at the intersection of 5th Street and Arizona Avenue. The open-air ice rink launched November 5 and remains open daily through January 18, providing scenic outdoor skating under evening coastal lights.

Continuing south past the pier, Sunday features the Venice Street Fair along 3rd Avenue from 9:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Supported by the Venice BID and CD11, the open-air festival gathers 500 regional craft makers, continuous live musical entertainment, pet-friendly strolling areas, and a kids trick-or-treat activity hunt. For music aficionados seeking an indoor performance to begin the weekend, Culver City’s Industry Cafe & Jazz presents the Dennis Mitcheltree Quartet on Friday evening at 7:00 p.m. Admission is entirely free with no cover charge.

Palisadians driving south down Ocean Avenue and Lincoln Boulevard should account for increased pedestrian activity and parking structure turnover near central shopping districts.

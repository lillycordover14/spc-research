Oklahoma business filings on record pace 

 

By : 
Journal Record Staff
//
November 6, 2026
//
 
 

 

 Listen to this article

Summary:

Oklahoma recorded 58,049 new 
business applications
 through September

Business filings rose 35% compared to the same period in 2025

High-propensity filings with planned payroll also increased 35%

Oklahomans are starting businesses at a record clip this year, according to 
U.S. Census Bureau
 data.

The state logged 58,049 new business applications from January through September, up 35% from 42,887 during the same months of 2025 and well above the 32,381 filed in 2019.
 

An application is a request to the 
Internal Revenue Service
 for an employer identification number for a new business. Not every filing becomes an operating company, and some never open.

Part of the jump came in February, when filings more than doubled to 10,034, with nearly all the extra activity between Feb. 8 and 28. No cause has been reported for the spike, which may reflect a one-time batch of filings. Even excluding February, applications were up 24% over 2025.

About 30% of this year’s applications were considered high-propensity, meaning they show signs of becoming businesses with employees, such as planned payroll. Those filings also rose 35%.
 

July and September also stood out, each up more than 50% from a year earlier.

Through September, the state had already topped its full-year total for every year since 2019.

The Census Bureau is scheduled to release October figures on Nov. 12.

The Samsung Galaxy Buds On wireless earphones, unveiled last month, are currently only available in South Korea and the 
US
. Samsung said the Buds On would come to Europe in early 2027 without getting specific. Well, we now have a more definite timeframe for the Galaxy Buds On's European debut.

Citing their sources, 
SamMobile
 claims the Samsung Galaxy Buds On will launch in Europe in the third week of 2027. We still don't have the launch date, but at least we now have a more definite launch timeframe.

The Galaxy Buds On are Samsung's first clip-on earphones. They are priced at KRW299,999 in South Korea and $229 in the US. You can 
read our Galaxy Buds On's announcement coverage here
 to learn more about them.

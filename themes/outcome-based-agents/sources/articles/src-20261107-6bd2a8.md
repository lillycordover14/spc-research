DALLAS, Tx. (WDBJ) - Virginia Tech’s defense was on the wrong end of a 51-28 defeat to SMU on Friday night in Dallas.
The Hokies (6-3) got great offensive numbers from quarterback Ethan Grunkemeyer (28-38, 291yds, TD, INT) and running back Jeffrey Overton Jr. (13 carries, 123yds, 2 TD), but the Mustangs (7-2) had their way with Virginia Tech’s defense to the tune of 569 yards on offense. QB Kevin Jennings threw for 350 yards and four touchdowns in the victory. 
The Hokies come home on November 14th to face Stanford.

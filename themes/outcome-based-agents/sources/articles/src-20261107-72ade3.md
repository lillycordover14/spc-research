Attack in Krakow/IntMin Neacsu: I convey my solidarity with Polish police officer, we are prepared to offer support

                                    Category: 
English

Date: 2026-11-07 19:28:50

Foto: 
Grigore POPESCU / AGERPRES Photo

Minister of Internal Affairs Marian Neacsu issued a message following the armed attack that took place on Saturday in Kraków, which resulted in deaths and wounded, stating that Romania stands ready to provide support, should it be requested.


'During these difficult times for Poland, our thoughts are with the bereaved families affected by the attack in Kraków, and we wish a full recovery to all those wounded. I, along with my colleagues at the Ministry of Internal Affairs, express our solidarity with the Polish police officers and first responders. We are prepared to offer support, should it be requested. Protecting human life demands vigilance, cooperation, and firm action, a responsibility that the structures of the Ministry of Internal Affairs uphold every single day,' stated Minister Marian Neacsu, according to a post on the Ministry's Facebook account.


Five people were killed and at least ten others injured in an armed attack that occurred onSaturday afternoon in the central square of Kraków, in southern Poland, Police announced.


The attacker was apprehended while attempting to flee, Police stated, adding that no other individuals are currently being sought. AGERPRES(RO - writing by: Georgiana Tanasescu; EN - writing by: Bogdan Gabaroi)

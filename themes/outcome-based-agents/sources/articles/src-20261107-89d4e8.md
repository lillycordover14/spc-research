The Tulane football team is being embarrassed by Tulsa after the first thirty minutes of play, 32-0. This is easily the worst half of football we've seen the Green Wave football team play this season. More analysis at the end of this article.
The Wave offense had to play minus two key players
 - senior wide out 
Garrett Mmahat
. The New Orleans native came out of the pregame locker room with no pads, only his game jersey. According to sources, Mmahat hurt his shoulder making that game-saving tackle against Charlotte last week.
 - junior offensive lineman 
Ryan Mickow
 was in a wrap/cast on his lower-left leg and was rolling around on a knee scooter today after his injury early against Charlotte last week.
In addition, though not listed on the injury list by Tulane, punter 
William Hudlow
 was not available for the game. We are told coach 
Will Hall
 will address that after the game.
First Half
A gorgeous, sun-shiny day at Yulman for Homecoming, 2026. Tulane won the toss and deferred to the second half
First Quarter
Tulsa opens with a quick out for a first down, then a seven-yard run and a six yard pass, a six-yard quarterback rush and Tulsa is already in Tulane territory. An off-tackle run picks up another first down. The Golden Hurricane have yet to pick up less than 6-yards on any play. Another pass gets to the Tulane 16, first and ten. Two more plays gets to the Tulane four, first and goal. An end around pass gets Tulsa into the end zone in just over five minutes.
Tulsa 7  -  Tulane 0
With 9:46 left in the 1st quarter, Tulane takes over at its 23 after a 
TJ Smith 
return. An immediate out from 
Trace Johnson
 to 
Fat Hill
 picks up ten. A short gain by 
Johnnie Daniels
, then a quick pass to Hill gets across midfield. A run by Daniels loses one. A quick out to 
Anthony Brown-Stephens
 picks up 11. Lucas picks up three, then an out pass to same picks up seven and a first down. A sweep to Daniels nets one. A blitz by Tulsa sacks Johnson, losing eight, setting up a third down and 17, the first third down faced by either team. A Daniels rush gets 12, leading to what should have been a 42-yard field goal, but a high snap to backup punter/holder 
John Allen

Cooper Helmke

Tulsa moved into Tulane territory and faced its first third down situation, coming up short at the Tulane 49. A shanked 9-yard punt flutters out of bounds at the at the Tulane 40.
But the Green Wave go three and out, setting up a 
Brooks Moore
 40-yard punt. It's his first punt as a collegian. Tulsa takes over at its own 40 after a 13-yard return. One pass play for three yards ends the 1st quarter.
End of the 1st Quarter: Tulsa 7  -  Tulane 0
Second Quarter
Tulsa opened with a 32-yard fly pattern up the sidelines to the Tulane 25. The play featured a nice fake into the line, fooling the defenders completely. Short runs and passes set up a third and two at the Tulane 18. Tulsa comes up short and goes for it on 4th down, rushing to the Wave 11. A rush right is read by 
Kevin Adams III
, who pummels the running back for a one-yard loss. A sweep left gets to the Tulane 8, setting up a 3rd and seven. A pass into the end zone falls incomplete. The Golden Hurricane fake going for it, hoping to draw Tulane offsides, but the Wave hold, forcing a 26-yard field goal by 
Owen Carey
 which is good. Carey is one of three #10s on the Tulsa roster: the starting quarterback, a defensive lineman, and the field goal kicker.
Tulsa 10  -  Tulane 0
The Wave begins on their 25 after the touchback. A 5-yard penalty on 1st down loses a down. A short run by 
Mo Turner
 then a pass that falls short of Hill on a fly pattern up the sidelines means Tulane goes three and out, leading to another punt by Moore, this one of 46-yards to the Tulsa 32.

On SI Tulane First Half Analysis
As good as Tulane played in the second half of the Charlotte game, this was the exact opposite. This was easily the worst half of football the Green Wave have played this season. 
In the first quarter, the Tulane offense was the model of predictability. In its only two drives, the Wave alternated pass-run every play. Literally. Start with a pass, follow with a run. That happened on every play in the Greenies first three drives of the game. The Tulane O has staggered and stuttered for the entire first half.
The Tulane defense looks confused on most plays, getting faked out on play-actions by the Tulsa quarterback. Most tackles by the defense have been well beyond the line of scrimmage. To give up over 300-yards in the first half is inexcusable.

DOUG JOUBERT
Doug has covered a gamut of sporting events in his fifty-plus years in the field.  He started doing sideline reporting for Louisiana Tech football games for the student radio station.  Doug was Sports Director for KNOE-AM/FM in Monroe in the mid-80s, winning numerous awards from the Louisiana Sports Writers Association for Best Sportscast and Best Play-by-Play.  High school play-by-play for teams in Monroe, Natchitoches, New Orleans, and Thibodaux, LA dot his resume.  He did college play-by-play for Northwestern State University in Natchitoches for nine years.  Then, moving to the Crescent City, Doug did television PBP of Tulane games and even filled in for legendary Tulane broadcaster, Ken Berthelot in the only game Kenny ever missed while doing the Green Wave games.

His father was an alumnus of Tulane in the 1940s, so Doug has attended Tulane football games in old Tulane Stadium, the Superdome, and Yulman.  He was one of the 86,000 plus on December 1, 1973, sitting in the North End Zone to seeTulane shutout the LSU Tigers, 14-0.  He was there when the Posse ruled Fogelman and in Turchin when the Wave made it to the World Series.  He currently is the public address voice of the Tulane baseball team.

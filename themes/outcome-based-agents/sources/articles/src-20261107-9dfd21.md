GREEN BAY, Wis. (WBAY) - Farmers’ market season may be coming to an end, but vendors will still be able to sell their items in downtown Green Bay.
The fun will be moving indoors as the temperatures get colder.
The Artisan Alley indoor market happens each Saturday from 10 a.m. until 3 p.m., starting on November 6 through December 12th inside the Bellin Building. 
Shoppers can find a variety of items and food, all made by residents around our area. 
Vendors change out each week, and small business owners say it’s a great way to support them going into the holiday season.
“You know, it gets cold here in Wisconsin, so people start staying at home. Everyone’s always looking for something to do, but it’s really nice to bring more people down here downtown on the weekends and just get to know each other. It’s really nice, as a resident myself, just to get to know other businesses. I am so impressed by how many different businesses there are out there that I just didn’t know existed,” says Ashley Gauthier, the event organizer.

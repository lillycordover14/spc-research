I often get writer’s block, which in turn saddles me with a fear of redundancy. I search for topics to write about and desperately force words into paragraphs, hoping to avoid overlap and achieve tangible results. Upon the completion of my writing by way of desperation, I then read what’s in front of me and subsequently toss it into the garbage bin.
Writing is a process, and it’s a process that must be trusted. A disingenuous idea comes off as unnatural and contrived. But when the process is allowed to run its course, the stories write themselves.

Brown, Young pace North Carolina in 109-70 drubbing of Wofford
Terrence Brown scored 21 points and reserve Jaydon Young scored 15 points and North Carolina beat Wofford 109-70
1:27

November 6, 2026, 9:35 PM

CHAPEL HILL, N.C. -- 
Terrence Brown scored 21 points, reserve Jaydon Young scored 15 points, and North Carolina beat Wofford 109-70 on Friday night.
Other Tar Heels (2-0) backups got in on the scoring action with Alex Samodurov contributing 13 points, Maximo Adams scoring 11 and grabbing 11 rebounds, and Jarin Stevenson added 10 points.
Cutter Sisk scored a game-high 27 points off the bench and fellow reserve Jyi Dawkins scored 13 for Wofford (0-2).
The Tar Heels built a 7-0 lead in the first 52 seconds and never trailed. North Carolina shot 16 of 32 — 3 of 8 from 3-point range — and 16 of 20 from the foul line in the first half.
UNC shot 52% (38 of 73) including 40% (10 of 25) from behind the arc overall.
The Tar Heels are now 5-2 all-time against Wofford, including 4-2 in Chapel Hill. However, Wofford entered Friday night's game having won two straight in Chapel Hill.
The teams last played on Dec. 15, 2019 in Chapel Hill, a 68-64 Terrier victory.

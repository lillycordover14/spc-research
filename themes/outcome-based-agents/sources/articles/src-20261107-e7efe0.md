Pasadena Adaptive Sports Festival Returns to Brookside Park Saturday&nbsp;&nbsp;Pasadena Now

[page text]
[photo credit: Triumph Foundation]
Pasadena will host its eighth annual Adaptive Sports Festival on Saturday, Nov. 7, bringing a day of free adaptive recreation and fitness activities to Brookside Park.

The festival is scheduled from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. at Brookside Park, 
360 N. Arroyo Blvd
., and is being presented by the City of Pasadena Parks, Recreation and Community Services Department in partnership with Triumph Foundation.

Activities will include wheelchair rugby and basketball, pickleball, tennis, adaptive archery, disc golf, boxing and dance. The festival will also include an interactive art workshop and a resource fair.

The city said the event is open to people of all abilities. Equipment will be provided, and trained coaches and instructors will be available to help participants try the activities. Lunch will be provided to registered attendees.

“Every individual deserves the opportunity to experience the joy, confidence and sense of belonging that sports can bring,” Parks, Recreation and Community Services Director Koko Panossian said in the city’s Oct. 15 announcement. He said the festival is designed to reduce barriers to participation and connect individuals and families with community resources.

Triumph Foundation, whose programs primarily serve people with spinal cord injuries or disorders causing paralysis and also include people with other disabilities, lists festival hours of 8:30 a.m. to 2 p.m. The City of Pasadena lists the event from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. Neither organization’s event information explains the difference in starting times.

Triumph Foundation also plans a youth camp during the festival for participants younger than 16 who use wheelchairs or have mobility impairments. The camp offers a chaperoned tour of the event and designated opportunities to try different sports.

The foundation says the first 25 participants with disabilities who arrive and register will receive an event T-shirt. Pizza lunch will be provided to adaptive athletes, while participants with special dietary restrictions are advised to bring their own lunch.

Registration information for participants and volunteers is available through Triumph Foundation. The organization can be contacted at 
info@triumph-foundation.org
.

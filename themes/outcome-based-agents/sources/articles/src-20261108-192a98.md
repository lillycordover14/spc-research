RUTLAND, Vt. (WCAX) - The Chaffee Art Center hosted its sixth annual Holiday Artisan Gift Show this weekend.
About a dozen vendors showed their handmade crafts Friday and Saturday. Shoppers and families looked at ornaments and paintings around the historic mansion.
The show is one of the most important times of the year for local artisans, organizers said.
“There’s a lot of different art and craft shows around, so, you know, we try to get in early. Get people ready to do the shopping. It’s a great way for our artist members and for artisans to showcase their work, and of course it’s their livelihood,” said Sherri Birkheimer Rooker, executive director of the 
Chaffee Art Center
.
The event is a kickoff before they begin decorating the mansion for the rest of the holidays. The center also will host other seasonal themed events, organizers said.

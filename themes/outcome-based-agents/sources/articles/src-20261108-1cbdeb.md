Article Summary

Cupertino S01E06 "Harper" puts Michael and Olivia in a race to stop an AI-twisted transplant decision before surgery.

A massive traffic jam forces the Cupertino legal fight to unfold remotely, with everyone battling the case from cars.

The preview also teases Cupertino S01E07 "YUNGWOOD," as an AI-music studio threatens a rap icon’s livelihood.

Cupertino S01E08 "Judith & Michael" raises the stakes with a murder frame-up tied to America’s surveillance machine.

CBS has handed off Sunday night to 
Robert King
 and 
Michelle King
's 
Mike Colter 
and 
Rachel Keller
-starring 
Cupertino,
 offering a reairing of the first two episodes after kicking off the night with 
S01E06: "Harper." Tonight, an AI algorithm's decision to swap around an organ donor list could prove deadly. The problem? A traffic jam has everyone fighting the legal battle from their cars. Here's a look at what's on tap tonight, followed by previews for S01E07: "YUNGWOOD" and S01E08: "Judith & Michael."

CBS's 
Cupertino
 is a David vs. Goliath legal drama set in the heart of Silicon Valley that follows a lawyer (
Mike Colter
) who is being cheated out of his stock options by his former employer, a tech start-up. Refusing to back down, he joins forces with another recently fired attorney (
Rachel Keller
) to represent those taken advantage of by the tech elite and help them fight back in a high-stakes battle against the Goliaths controlling Silicon Valley. Joining Colter and Keller are 
Ella Stiller
 (Christy Palladino) and 
Nik Dodani
 (Vikram Malhotra), with 
Busy Philipps
 (Natalia Boutella) and 
Renée Elise Goldsberry
 (Nichole E. Ferebee).

"We are interested in legal shows only in the way that they reflect what's going on in the reality of the moment," Robert shared during 
a profile
 of the upcoming CBS series. "The reality of the moment is tech is everywhere, AI is everywhere, and the Goliaths that control trillions and trillions of dollars and the economy are in one place: Silicon Valley. The difference between what the top people are making and those in the middle and the bottom, and how AI is closing down middle management, there's never been a David versus Goliath-ness of this size, where the Goliaths are so large, and the Davids are so small."

Produced by CBS Studios, the series is executive-produced by 
Robert King, Michelle King, Liz Glotzer
, and 
Sam Hoffman
.

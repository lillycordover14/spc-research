Borova Front Counteroffensive
Attribution: Map of Ukraine’s New Borova Front Counteroffensive. Map Credit: Pedro  Gomes Facebook

Ukraine is not letting up as far as its counteroffensive in the northeast is concerned. 
The full court press appears to be now on. The blanket of OPSEC is quite heavy so no confirmation from the Armed Forces of Ukraine but it looks like the counteroffensive which started out as 
Vivaldi
 has now spread out and morphed into a 135km wide front which now includes northward into Kharkiv oblast … what is being called the 
Borova Front
:

Recent reports indicate that Ukrainian forces have expanded their counteroffensive to a new front in the 
Borova
 direction, advancing along a 
135 km section
 of the frontline in the 
Kharkiv region
. According to 
AMK Mapping
 and assessments from the 
Institute for the Study of War
 (ISW), coordinated counterattacks have resulted in Ukrainian infiltrations and breakthroughs pushing at least 
5 km beyond
 the previously reported frontline locations such as 
Borovskaya Andrievka
, 
Zelenyi Hai
, and 
Vyshneve
. These gains are aimed at pushing Russian troops back from the 
Oskil River
 and relieving pressure on the town of 
Borova
 from the north.

Ukrainian forces retake village on Borova axis, OSINT sources say

Ukrainian forces have recaptured the village of Zelenyi Hai in Kharkiv region, Borova axis,

 AMK Mapping, a New Zealand-based independent OSINT project.

Strategically and operationally the Institute for the Study of War (ISW) indicates that these successes, alongside operations 
Vivaldi
 and 
Veles
, have compelled Russia to 
deprioritize offensive efforts
 toward Kupyansk and Borova, reallocating forces to other sectors while failing to achieve territorial gains in the Borova direction: 

Russian forces lose initiative across most front sectors amid record October losses — ISW

Russian forces have lost the theater-wide initiative and offensive operational capability across significant sectors of the frontline in Ukraine, the Institute for the Study of War (ISW) 
wrote
 on Nov. 1.

https://english.nv.ua/russian-war/russian-forces-cede-more-ground-than-they-take-for-second-straight-month-50655479.html

Oskil
, 
Zherebets 
and 
Siverskyi Donets

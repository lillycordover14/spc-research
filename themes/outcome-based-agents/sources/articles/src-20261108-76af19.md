Pertamina Oil Wells Exceed Production Targets by Up to Sixfold

              Heru Andriyanto            

              November 8, 2026 | 2:24 pm

Jakarta.
 Several oil wells operated by state-owned energy company Pertamina in South Sumatra and West Java have recorded production test results significantly above their initial targets, offering a potential boost to Indonesia's efforts to increase domestic oil and gas output.

Djoko Siswanto, head of upstream oil and gas regulator SKK Migas, said over the weekend that production tests at three wells in South Sumatra's Adera Field and another in West Java had delivered encouraging results.

The results come as Indonesia seeks to increase domestic crude oil production and reduce its dependence on imported energy.

At the LBK-032 well, located approximately 20 kilometers east of Prabumulih, South Sumatra, oil production during tests conducted between Sept. 30 and Nov. 7 reached 1,079.4 barrels per day.

   ADVERTISEMENT

That was nearly 5.4 times the initial target of 200 barrels per day. The well also produced an additional 0.432 million standard cubic feet of natural gas per day (MMSCFD).

Another development well, GNK-119, northwest of Prabumulih, recorded preliminary oil production of 943.5 barrels per day, well above its initial target of 200 barrels per day.

The well also delivered 4.29 MMSCFD of natural gas during the same testing period.

Meanwhile, the BNG-074 well at Adera Field produced 170 barrels of oil per day, nearly four times its target of 43 barrels per day, along with 0.548 MMSCFD of natural gas.

Djoko said the BNG-074 well had previously been idle before undergoing a workover, a procedure used to restore or improve production from an existing well.

"Before the workover, this well was idle, demonstrating that well intervention activities can deliver positive results," Djoko said in a statement.

West Java Well Also Surpasses Target

Outside South Sumatra, Pertamina's JAS-022 well at Subang Field in West Java also recorded strong results.

The well produced 370.8 barrels of oil per day during testing, approximately 6.6 times its initial target of 56 barrels per day.

It also produced 1.60 MMSCFD of natural gas.

Djoko said the results demonstrated the potential of drilling and well intervention programs to improve production from existing oil and gas fields.

"This is positive momentum toward achieving our national oil production and lifting targets," he said.

by Al Cimaglia

November 8, 2026

Breeders Crown Final winner #4 Dandy Ideal (8/5) is the morning line chalk in the field for the 11th running of the Potomac Pace Invitational at Rosecroft Raceway. The headliner is carded as Race 11 as a competitive field of 8 battle for a share of a $125,000 purse. This group includes the 2nd program chalk Womens Layer (3-1) who finished 3rd in BC Final and has drawn Post 1. Prince Hal Hanover and Coaches Corner who came up flat in the BC Final are also in the field. Bythemissal is 10-1 in the morning line, has dawn post 3, and Dexter Dunn will be at the controls. The Ron Burke trainee will be my pick and should offer a solid price.
Comments and selections below are based on a fast track.

(9:20 PM EST)

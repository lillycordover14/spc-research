Donnie Knight (third from left) was recognized for his retirement earlier this year by members of the Randolph County Commission (from left) Pam Johnson, Larry Roberts and Chris Lunsford.

The Randolph County Commission on Monday approved a measure updating the job description for the county’s Emergency Management Association director as a first step toward replacing long-time director Donnie Knight.

Knight retired from the position earlier this, and the EMA has been operating under assistant director Jan Prescott since then.

The county’s personnel policy requires that it advertise non-entry-level positions internally for two weeks before opening the job up for outside applications. The commission also agreed Monday to move forward with that process.

The job will open to current county employees for the next two weeks, and there is expected to be considerable interest during that time period.

Knight retired in August after 22 years as the county’s EMA director.

The county’s official job description for the EMA director had not been updated in decades, so the changes modernize the wording, add more detail and include things like cybersecurity that have become more recent emergency concerns.

Commission Chair Pam Johnson said Monday that the count is hopeful to hire a new director by the end of the year.

MANHATTAN, Kan. (WIBW) - Riley County police arrested a woman on Friday evening in connection with drug possession, authorities said.
The arrested woman was identified as Candice Dawn Renton, 45, of Clay Center.
Authorities said Renton was arrested around 6:20 p.m. Friday near Allen Road and Casement roads on the northeast side of Manhattan.
Riley County police arrested a 45-year-old Clay Center woman on Friday evening in connection with drug possession, authorities said.
(WIBW)
Renton was booked into the Riley County Jail in connection with two counts of possession of drug paraphernalia with intent to distribute; possession of opiate-opium-narcotic or certain stimulant; possession of a depressant; possession with intent to distribute heroin-certain stimulants-fentanyl; and possession of drug paraphernalia with intent to use. 
Renton was issued a total bond of $35,000 and remained in the Riley County Jail as of Monday morning, officials said.

Omnea gathers CPOs and CFOs from KKR, PayPal and Albertsons at One World Observatory on 19 November to explore agentic AI in finance and procurement
Omnea
 will host its Annual Summit '26 on Thursday 19 November at One World Observatory, bringing procurement and finance leaders together on the 102nd floor of One World Trade Center.

Sponsored by KPMG, one of Omnea's partners, the evening is built around one idea: procurement is becoming the heart of the business, and the teams already running agents are pulling ahead.

The venue, One World Observatory, offers uninterrupted 360-degree views across New York City.

The entrance sits on the West Plaza at the corner of West Street and Vesey Street in Lower Manhattan, which makes it easy to reach.

Some seats are still available to reserve. 
Rethinking enterprise systems
At the heart of the summit is a challenge to how large organisations run their core platforms.
Omnea's answer is an 
operating system
 that can sit at the heart of procurement: composable, connected, and agentic. Teams adopt the parts they need, connect the systems they already run, and put agents to work under their own governance, ending up with a simpler stack that gets smarter over time.
Ben Freeman, CEO and Co-Founder of Omnea
Why waiting carries a cost

For Ben Freeman, CEO and Co-Founder of Omnea, the urgency is already visible in the market: “Every quarter, the gap gets wider between procurement teams already running agents and the ones still waiting to start. Procurement has never had more influence than it does right now.

“After people, third-party spend is the biggest cost in every business, and CFOs are counting on procurement to help the whole company move faster. Teams that wait for next year’s plan will be playing catch-up.

“That’s why our Annual Summit brings together the CPOs who’ve already made the jump, the CFOs backing them, and the peers still working out where to begin. We want them comparing notes on what’s actually working.” Ben Freeman, CEO & Founder, Omnea.

Omnea’s signature customers

Just Eat

The food delivery marketplace moved from siloed spend processes to AI-powered, company-wide procurement with Omnea, replacing fragmented systems with a single AI-guided intake and orchestration process. John Butcher, Procurement & Supply Chain Director, said: "What AI is enabling me to do is have the entire company do great procurement. As a leader, it's enabling me to influence all the spend at my company."

PayPal

The fintech giant moving US$1.5tn annually across 200 markets, selected Omnea as the orchestration layer for its global payments business, with expansion planned into accounts payable, risk and sourcing.

The Adecco Group

The Zurich-based staffing and workforce solutions business with over 35,000 employees and more than US$5bn in indirect managed spend, replaced email-driven, inbox-scattered procurement intake with a single standardised AI conversational intake, cutting overall cycle times by 80% within six months, from 23.4 days to 4.6 days, and going live in 22 markets across seven languages.

Hargreaves Lansdown

The UK's largest D2C retail investment platform replaced forms, email, and its legacy procurement suite with a single procurement experience in Omnea, covering intake, approvals, supplier onboarding, and renewals.

Phil Thomas, Group Head of Procurement, said: "Omnea has completely transformed the procurement experience for our employees, approvers, and procurement team. By systemising human controls, requesters have a seamless experience, approvers get complete context, and my team no longer spends time manually gathering supplier information, chasing approvals, or telling stakeholders what is or isn't acceptable."

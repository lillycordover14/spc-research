Fort Washington, MD
 — After being eclipsed off cover by Dandy Ideal in the Breeders Crown Open Pace, Women Layer turned the tables on his rival in Sunday’s (Nov. 8) $125,000 Grade 3 Potomac Pace at Rosecroft Raceway, emerging from the pocket in the final 150 yards to post a 1:48 victory.

Yannick Gingras sealed off the pocket with Women Layer as 3-5 favorite Dandy Ideal (driven by Tim Tetrick) pushed to a clear lead through a :26 first quarter. After rating a :54.3 half, Dandy Ideal faced a first-over challenge from Prince Hal Hanover (Todd McCarthy) up the backstretch and was forced to quicken to three-quarters in 1:21.1.

Women Layer and Yannick Gingras emerged victorious off a pocket trip in the 11th edition of the Potomac Pace. Erik Jones/Room 9 Photography.

Prince Hal Hanover leveled off turning for home, enabling Gingras to wedge Women Layer to the outside with 150 yards to go. The 5-year-old 
Sweet Lou
-Loreenas Ruffian gelding shifted gears and overpowered Dandy Ideal in deep stretch to win by a half-length. Prince Hal Hanover held third, another 5-1/4-lengths behind, over Coaches Corner (Jason Bartlett), who rallied belatedly off third-over cover.

Virgil Morgan Jr. trains Women Layer, a 34-time winner with $1.21 million in career earnings. He is owned by Let It Ride Stables, Jeff Fought Racing, Larry Wills, and Jesmeral Stable. As the 3-1 second choice, Women Layer paid $8.40 to win.

Gingras took a similar path to victory in the $50,000 Pete Medhurst Legacy Pace with Railroad Station, who eclipsed early leaver Icaco Hanover (Russell Foster) at the end of a :26.2 quarter before releasing even-money choice World Of Wishes (Tetrick) to the lead with a circuit to go. The 4-year-old 
Sweet Lou
-Rodeo Rosie entire drafted behind middle splits of :53.4 and 1:21.2 before taking aim at World Of Wishes in upper stretch and powering clear late for a 2-1/4-length, 1:49.4 score. Icaco Hanover saved third, another 1-1/2 lengths back, off a ground-saving trip.

Ron Burke trains 12-time winner Railroad Station, who paid $9 to win as the 7-2 second choice, for Burke Racing Stable, Hatfield Stables, Weaver Bruscemi, and J&T Silva-Purnel & Libby.

Racing returns to Rosecroft on Thursday (Nov. 12); first post is 7:15 p.m. (EST). The 2026 Fall Meeting features live harness racing on Monday and Thursday evenings through Dec. 17.

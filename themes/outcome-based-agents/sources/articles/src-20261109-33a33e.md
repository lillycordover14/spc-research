Ema Klinec (SLO) - Modica/NordicFocus

 

 

 

Ski jumping: Ema Klinec hangs up her skis after one final victory

An important chapter in women’s 
ski jumping
 is coming to an end. On Monday, at a press conference held in Ljubljana (Slovenia),
Ema Klinec
announced her decision to retire. At the age of 28, the Slovenian is stepping away from the ski jumps at the peak of her form, just a few weeks after winning the overall title in the 2026 Summer Grand Prix.

“Today, I would like to announce that I have decided to bring my ski jumping career to an end
,
”
 she said in a 
statement
 issued by her federation, before reflecting on her fondest memories.

Ema Klinec (SLO) – Tadeusz Mieczynski/VOIGT/FIS

Trained at the Žiri club, 
Ema Klinec
 had already made a name for herself as a teenager. At just 15 years old, she won her first Summer Grand Prix competition in Courchevel (Savoie), ahead of 
Sarah Hendrickson
 and 
Sara Takanashi
. In 2016, she became Youth Olympic champion in Lillehammer (Norway).

A world title and a historic jump of 226 metres

Despite two serious knee injuries, the Slovenian has managed to establish herself as one of the world’s top female ski jumpers. In 2021, in Oberstdorf (Germany), she became the first Slovenian woman to be crowned world champion in women’s ski jumping. She went on to add three further world championship medals to her tally.

But her most memorable achievement is probably her 226-metre jump in Vikersund (Norway) in March 2023. On that day, 
Ema Klinec
 set a new women’s world record and won the first-ever women’s ski flying competition.

Ema Klinec (SLO) – Thibaut/NordicFocus

“I’m delighted that this competition showed what we were capable of on a ski-jumping hill
,
”
 she said in her farewell speech.

With 31 individual World Cup podium finishes, including two victories, she remains the second most successful Slovenian on the circuit behind 
Nika Prevc
.

Her most recent summer season will also be remembered. Having returned to her best form after a difficult spell, she won the 2026 Summer Grand Prix, capping off her campaign with victory in the overall standings.

“When I won the Summer Grand Prix in Klingenthal, I was delighted to be able to jump again the way I know how
,
”
 explained the champion.

Before thanking her family, coaches, team-mates and supporters, 
Ema Klinec
 spoke about how her sport has evolved. 
“When I started out in Žiri, I never imagined how far ski jumping would take me. Today, I’m delighted to see that young girls can dream of flying on skis right from their very first jumps.”

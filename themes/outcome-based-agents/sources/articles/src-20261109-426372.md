Now that November is in full swing and the temperature has dropped, we've officially turned our attention to a time-honoured fashion favourite: Uggs. Or, more specifically, Ugg slippers, the perfect way to stay cosy in and around your home. 
Despite these toe-warming shoes being a staple in our wardrobes for years — or, for some of us, decades — we're always on the hunt for a new pair to add to our collection. The brand comes firmly celebrity approved, with Charli XCX, Dua Lipa, Gigi Hadid among the starry names that turn to their Uggs time and time again. 
But today, it's actually Victoria Beckham's 15-year-old daughter Harper Beckham who we're taking inspiration from. Over the weekend, her older brother Cruz's girlfriend Jackie Apostel shared a series of 
photos
 with the caption, 'Post tour hibernation with a rebel era pup 🐾' 
Instagram
In the photo dump, as well as many cute pictures of their dog, was a picture of Cruz teaching his younger sister to play the guitar, and we couldn't help but notice her snug slippers on show. Harper is wearing a pair of Scuffette slippers from Ugg, which have just shot to the top of our Christmas wish list. 
The slippers are made from a suede upper with a sheepskin collar, and are available in four colours, perfect for settling into the sofa, relaxing by the fire and beating everyone you know at a game of Scrabble. Shop them below.

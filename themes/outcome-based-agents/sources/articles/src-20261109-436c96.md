InventHelp Inventor Develops Adjustable Breaker Bar (ADA-2089)
 
 
 
 
  PR Newswire 
 
 
Mon, November 9, 2026 at 7:30 AM PST
 
1 min read
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
PITTSBURGH, Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- "I wanted to create an adjustable breaker bar that allows you to apply just the right amount of torque, so a rusted or tight fastener breaks loose for removal," said an inventor, from Winter Garden, Fla., "so I invented the BREAK IT. My design helps avoid excessive straining, and it would be easy to store when not in use."
 
 
 
 
InventHelp Logo
 
 
 
The invention provides a collapsible two-piece breaker bar design. In doing so, it enables the user to select the desired length for the right amount of torque. It also eliminates the need to struggle with a large and heavy traditional cheater bar. As a result, it increases convenience. The invention features a strong and adjustable design that is easy to use, transport, and store so it is ideal for motorists, household do-it-yourselfers, contractors, tradesmen, and professional mechanics. Additionally, a prototype model and technical drawings are available upon request.
 
 
 
 
 
The original design was submitted to the National sales office of InventHelp. It is currently available for licensing or sale to manufacturers or marketers.

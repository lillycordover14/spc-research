Uggs are the soft, plump, almost cartoonish-looking boots that had the fashion world in a chokehold in the early 2000s.

Ugg boots are trending again

Firmly ensconced in the Y2K fashion movement, Uggs are back in business these days. Nicknamed the 'ugly shoe' in the early 2000s, the most popular style was the full-length version, and they were hugely popular with celebrities - from 
Sienna Miller

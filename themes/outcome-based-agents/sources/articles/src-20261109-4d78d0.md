Hannah Harper announces 2027 Behind This Guitar Tour&nbsp;&nbsp;The Music Universe

[page text]
Karissa Pulliam, Courtesy of 19 Recordings/Atlantic Outpost
The trek marks her first headlining run
Rising country singer-songwriter and 2026 
American Idol
 winner Hannah Harper announces the Behind This Guitar Tour, her first headlining run.
The 22-date tour kicks off February 4th at The Brown Theatre in Louisville, KY, and makes its way through the South, Midwest, and East Coast before wrapping up May 22nd at the Jay and Susie Gogue Performing Arts Center at Auburn University in Auburn, AL.
Presales start on Wednesday, November 11th, ahead of the general sale on Friday, November 13th at 10 am local time.
Along the way, Hannah will hit stages including The Fillmore Charlotte, Richmond’s Carpenter Theatre at Dominion Arts Center, Lancaster’s American Music Theatre and Huntsville’s Von Braun Center, plus appearances at Opp Rattlesnake Rodeo in Opp, AL, and the Smithfield Ham & Yam Festival in Smithfield, NC.

The tour announcement caps a breakout year for Hannah. She recently shared “
9 to 5 (Live From Dollywood)
,” her live tribute to Dolly Parton recorded at Dollywood in Pigeon Forge, TN, following her latest single, “
Hold Your Horses
,” and fan-favorite single, “
Married Into This Town
.” This month, she makes her Ryman Auditorium debut supporting Lauren Alaina on November 12th and returns to the Grand Ole Opry on November 13th, after recently supporting Brad Paisley on select dates.

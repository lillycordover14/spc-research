Christmas at Heritage Farm: Farm Festival & Artisan Market&nbsp;&nbsp;Travel Indiana

[page text]
Shop local artisans, enjoy live music and food trucks, and meet the Suri alpacas. Heritage Farm is home to over 50 alpacas. Feed and interact with these adorable animals and even get a photo taken with one!

The alpaca store will be open where you will find all sorts of clothing and accessories.

There will also be Christmas decoration making workshops, bonfires, tractor-drawn wagon rides, and, of course, Santa!  

Nov. 14-15, 2026
Heritage Farm
Learn more here: 
www.heritagefarmevents.com/christmas

A project to make signal improvements and bring pedestrian crossing up to Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) standards is scheduled to begin the week of November 16 in Bates, Cedar, Dallas, Newton and McDonald counties, the Missouri Department of Transportation said.
Here is where the work is being done:
Bates County
Missouri Route 52 (Ft. Scott Street) & Business Loop 49 (Orange Street) in Butler
Signal improvements
Cedar County
U.S. Route 54 & Park Street in El Dorado Springs
Signal improvements
Dallas County
U.S. Route 65 (Ash Street) & Missouri Route 32 (Dallas Street) in Buffalo
Lighting and signal improvements
Newton County
Missouri Route 43 (Main Street) & 41st Street in Joplin
Add left turn lanes on Route 43
Signal improvements
Drainage improvements
Improve pedestrian crossing to conform to ADA standards
Business Loop 49 & Waldo Hatler Memorial Drive in Neosho
Replace median islands
Signal improvements
U.S. Route 60 & Oak Ridge Drive in Neosho
Replace/add median islands
Lighting improvements
Signal improvements
McDonald County
Missouri Route 59 & McDonald County Route C (Split Log Road) in Goodman
Signal improvements
Missouri Route 59 (Marshall Street) & Business 
71/Missouri Route 76
 in Anderson
Signal improvements
Business 71 & Cedar Street in Anderson
Replace median islands
Lighting improvements
Signal improvements
Business 71 & McDonald County Route W (Jesse James Road) in Pineville
Signal improvements
Traffic Impacts:
Drivers should watch for crews and equipment working close to traffic
Drivers may encounter reduced lanes and traffic delays
Flaggers and law enforcement will direct drivers through short-term lane reductions
No signed detours
Signs and message boards will alert drivers approaching work zones
Check MoDOT's 
Traveler Information Map
 for road closings/traffic impacts
Weather and/or scheduling conflicts could alter the work schedule.

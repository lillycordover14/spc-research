EvenUp Names Vanta’s Stevie Case President of Field Operations as Contracted Revenue Surpasses $150M, Doubling Year Over Year
 
 
 
 
 
 
  Business Wire 
 
 
November 9, 2026
 
7 min read
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
One law firm's record $16 million annual agreement signals surging demand for EvenUp's deeper role in helping attorneys win more for injury victims.
 
 
SAN FRANCISCO, November 09, 2026
--(
BUSINESS WIRE
)--
EvenUp
, the AI company built for personal injury law, today announced the appointment of Stevie Case, formerly Chief Revenue Officer of Vanta, as EvenUp's first President of Field Operations. Case joins as EvenUp has surpassed $150M in recurring revenue under contract and doubled revenue since they publicly announced their October 2025 funding round.
 
 
 
 
 
That growth reflects strong demand for AI built for plaintiff law firms and EvenUp is just getting started. Thousands of personal injury firms now rely on EvenUp, including 30% of the top 100 in the United States. The platform supports 20,000 cases each week and has supported claims involving more than $20 billion in alleged damages to date. Yet EvenUp reaches just 5% of personal injury cases in the U.S. each year, leaving significant room for growth.
 
 
The depth behind the growth
 
 
Every year, an estimated 20 million Americans are injured. For many, the consequences extend far beyond the injury itself: mounting medical bills, lost income, and uncertainty about their future. EvenUp was founded to close the gap between the compensation people deserve and what they actually receive, helping more people secure justice faster.
 
 
"Many AI solutions are a mile wide and an inch deep. When someone's future depends on the details of their case, an inch deep is nowhere near enough," said Rami Karabibar, CEO and co-founder of EvenUp. "Personal injury attorneys are already stretched thin, juggling heavy caseloads, mountains of records, and clients who need their attention. They need AI that understands what happened to someone and what it has cost them. Most importantly, they need solutions that can take on the work needed to move their case forward. That's the depth we're building: to give attorneys the capacity to fight for the full value of every case and reach a resolution faster."
 
 
 
 
 
That depth is already showing up in the courtroom. Russell & Lazarus, a Newport Beach, California trial firm focused on catastrophic personal injury, used EvenUp's Companion™ from jury selection through verdict in a premises liability case involving significant injuries. The trial team of 
Marc Lazarus and Eric Wilson used Companion
 to build voir dire questions from a 700-document record, rank prospective jurors, and test the evidence against each day's trial transcript. The defense's offer rose from $250,000 before trial to $2 million during jury deliberations. Companion's citation-backed review of the daily transcripts gave the team confidence to hold out for a verdict, and the jury returned $19 million, 76 times the initial offer. The voir dire skill EvenUp built with the firm is now available to all EvenUp customers in Companion.
 
 
 
 
 
Story Continues
 
 
 
 
"During the trial, I uploaded the transcripts each day and asked the AI companion specific questions about the evidence as the jury heard it. It was like having my own personal focus group every day," said Marc Lazarus, Managing Attorney at Russell & Lazarus. "In closing arguments, we spotted inconsistencies in the defense's case and countered them with citations from the transcripts, building slides with EvenUp on the fly for rebuttal. In 25 years as a trial attorney, I've never had an experience like it, and I wish I'd had it my entire career."
 
 
 
 
 
Firms are now giving EvenUp a deeper role across the pre-litigation lifecycle, from medical records and treatment histories to demand preparation and negotiation support. Within months of introducing its expanded Pre-Litigation as a Service (PLAAS) offering in May 2026, more than a dozen firms are live, and over a third of PLAAS customers are multimillion-dollar partnerships. One firm has signed a $16 million annual agreement, the largest in EvenUp's history.
 
 
That agreement represents more than $150,000 per attorney in annual spending. General-purpose AI subscriptions typically cost hundreds to a few thousand dollars per user annually. EvenUp's agreement covers a much broader scope of technology and case operations across each firm. The breadth of reach across use cases illustrates the value firms expect when specialized AI becomes integral to how their work gets done.
 
 
Lerner and Rowe, an EvenUp customer with offices in Arizona, Nevada, New Mexico, Illinois, and Indiana, is also expanding its work with EvenUp through PLAAS.
 
 
 
 
 
"Time is the only commodity we can't create more of. The way to create more time for my best people to work on my best cases is by taking lower-value cases off their plate and getting them to someone who can do everything we want done, right, and in a timely manner," said Glen Lerner, Founding Partner of Lerner and Rowe. "We've been pleasantly surprised to finally find a company that's delivering what it promised, maybe even better than we thought it would. The initial results have been wonderful, and we're going to be committing to thousands of cases a year."
 
 
Building the organization behind the demand
 
 
Meeting that demand is why EvenUp is bringing on Stevie Case.
 
 
"When you go deep enough into an industry, you can change what a business is capable of," said Karabibar. "A commitment of this scale reflects our customer's confidence in how much more we can accomplish together. Our next challenge is building the organization to deliver on that promise for more firms. Stevie has done that at an enormous scale, and she's the operating partner we need for this next chapter."
 
 
 
 
 
Case joined Vanta in 2022 as its first Chief Revenue Officer and spent four and a half years building the revenue engine behind one of the fastest-growing companies in enterprise software. Leading sales, customer success, and revenue operations, she grew ARR 10x to more than $300 million, scaled the go-to-market team from 20 to more than 800 people, and led the company's expansion into enterprise and international markets.
 
 
At EvenUp, Case will report to Karabibar and lead sales, customer success, marketing, revenue operations, and business operations. She will focus on scaling the go-to-market teams needed to support EvenUp's rapid growth, expanding further into enterprise and large-firm accounts, building partner channels, and establishing the infrastructure to support upcoming product launches.
 
 
"I've spent my career building revenue organizations from the ground up. I look for three things: a team with product-market fit that ships relentlessly, a founder who wants a real operating partner, and a market with meaningful problems to solve," said Stevie Case, President of Field Operations at EvenUp. "EvenUp has all three. Doubling revenue in a year while landing a $16 million customer for a brand-new product is rare. This tells me the market is ready for a different way of delivering legal work. I'm here to build the organization that matches that demand, and to make sure every firm we serve sees the outcomes and return on investment that justify that trust."
 
 
 
 
 
About Stevie Case
 
 
Stevie Case began her tech career as the world's first female professional gamer, competing as "KillCreek," and went on to a 20-year career leading sales and revenue teams at startups and global brands including Visa, Twilio, and Vanta. At Twilio, she played a key role in building the enterprise business as the company grew from a private company with roughly $200 million in revenue to a public company with more than $3 billion in ARR. Most recently, as Chief Revenue Officer of Cloud 100 agentic trust platform company Vanta, she led global customer-facing functions. She is also a Founding Partner of 20SALES, a seed-stage venture fund of go-to-market leaders investing in AI and beyond.
 
 
About EvenUp

 is on a mission to close the justice gap with AI-powered technology that empowers personal injury firms to deliver higher standards of representation and fairer outcomes for millions of injury victims. EvenUp is the first proactive AI platform that streamlines workflows, automates documents, and provides actionable insights across the entire case lifecycle. EvenUp is backed by leading investors including Bessemer Venture Partners, B Capital, REV, the venture capital arm of RELX, which owns LexisNexis, Premji Invest, Lightspeed, Bain Capital Ventures (BCV), SignalFire, NFX, DCM, and more.

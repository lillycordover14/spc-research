InventHelp Inventor Develops New Dashboard Rack for Rubber Ducks (ADA-2088)
PR Newswire
PITTSBURGH, Nov. 9, 2026

PITTSBURGH
, 
Nov. 9, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- "I wanted to create a new dashboard rack that enables you to safely and securely show off your rubber duck collection," said an inventor, from Orlando, Fla., "so I invented the QUACK TRAK. My design helps prevent the ducks from sliding around or falling off while driving. It also eliminates the need for permanent modifications, and it won't block airflow from the vents."

The invention provides a secure way to display a rubber duck collection atop a vehicle dashboard. In doing so, it prevents the ducks from sliding or falling. It also offers an attractive appearance, and it eliminates the need to drill, use adhesive, or make permanent modifications. The invention features an expandible design that is easy to mount and use so it is ideal for the owners of jeep-style vehicles.
The original design was submitted to the National sales office of InventHelp. It is currently available for licensing or sale to manufacturers or marketers. For more information, write Dept. 25-ADA-2088, InventHelp, 100 Beecham Drive, Suite 110, Pittsburgh, PA 15205-9801, or call (412) 288-1300 ext. 1368.

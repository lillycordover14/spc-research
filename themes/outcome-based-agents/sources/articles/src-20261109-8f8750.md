Broncos Notch Best Finish in Program History at AVCA National Pair Championships
11/8/2026 7:24:00 PM 
 | 
Women's Beach Volleyball
Share:
HUNTSVILLE, Ala.
 – Boise State beach volleyball had its best showing at the AVCA National Pairs Championship in program history, with both tandems reaching the second round of the event.


Freshman Kate MacDonald and senior 
Addison Wolden
 finished in a tie for 23rd place, the highest finish of any Bronco duo at the national event. 
Avery Allen
 and 
Keira Demirjian
 finished tied for 31st place.


MacDonald and Wolden won in straight sets over Long Beach State in the opening round, before falling to Stanford's Charlotta Bell and Brooke Rockwell in the second round. The Bronco duo took down San Diego's Kaitlyn Bradley and Maddy White in a thrilling 18-21, 21-18, 18-16 matchup in the consolation bracket before falling in the final two matches to claim a 23rd place finish.


Allen and Demirjian also won their first matchup, taking down Stetson's Marta Carro and Julia Czurylo in three sets (16-21, 21-15, 15-6) to advance to the second round. The tandem fell to Cal Poly in straight sets, 21-15, 21-14. The duo dropped its last three matches to finish tied for 31st.


The remainder of the team competed at the Lopes Fall Classic in Phoenix, Ariz. 
Susannah Barnett
 and Kayla Ihrig led the team with three wins over the weekend, including a big-time victory over Grand Canyon on Sunday.

Quotables

"I was really proud of both pairs. They both got past their first matchups and I think it was our best showing at this event. These are 64 of the top pairs nationally and it brought a little bit of an edge out. We got some really competitive scores against some really great teams. We got a good lesson at the end of each match which is all you can ask for." – head coach 
Alex Venardos


"This finish really confirms the upwards trajectory of this program. It shows that what we've been working on in practice is paying off. There are a lot of great pairs here and we're showing ourselves how good we can be. For a young team, that is especially positive to see at this point in the season." – Venardos


"I'm really proud of how our team competed against excellent competition. We have a lot of young talent, but as the tournament went on you could watch them realize the potential they have against some of these quality opponents. It was really exciting to watch them realize they could compete at this high level." – assistant coach 
McKayla Hazard

Notables

- Kate MacDonald and 
Addison Wolden
 finished tied for 23rd place, the highest finish by a Bronco pair at the AVCA National Pairs Championship in program history.

- Both duos finished in the top half of the tournament, the first time that has occurred in program history.

- Wolden is the first Bronco to make three appearances at the national event.

- 
Susannah Barnett
 and Kayla Ihrig led the team with three wins at the Lopes Fall Classic. 

What's Next

Boise State will conclude its fall chapter in Hawaii, competing in the Kaua'i College Volleyball Invitational in Lihue, Hawaii on Nov. 20-22.

Break Below $0.9862 Would Bring $0.9380 Into View

The next major downside reference stands at $0.9862, the three-month low established in August before a recovery. That point is approximately 9.4% below the chart’s displayed $1.0880 level. A daily close beneath it would mark a breach of the earlier floor and put $0.9380 in focus.

The deeper support lies roughly 4.9% below $0.9862 and 13.8% under the displayed price. This price analysis distinguishes the confirmed crossover from a support breakdown that has not occurred.

Meanwhile, the three-month high for 
$XRP
 at $1.6999 shows the distance from the upper end of the range. A broader price analysis places recovery hurdles at the 50-day EMA of $1.3021 and the 200-day EMA of $1.3530. Those levels remain above the current trading price.

WEST JEFFERSON — Bethany United Methodist Church invites the community to celebrate creativity, craftsmanship, and the joy of shopping locally at its indoor artisan fair on Saturday, Nov. 14, from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Admission is free.
The fair will showcase a wonderful variety of handcrafted items created by talented artisans, including quilted items, jewelry, basketry, woodworking, and other handmade arts and crafts. Visitors will have the opportunity to meet the makers, discover one-of-a-kind treasures, and find thoughtful gifts just in time for the holiday season.
And for those who fondly remember the delicious homemade baked goods from Bethany’s past church bazaars, there’s more good news! These much-loved treats will be available at the artisan fair, too. With a reputation for selling out, early shopping is highly recommended.
The fair will also welcome a very special visitor — Santa Claus! Santa plans to make a quick stop at Bethany around lunchtime to spread a little Christmas cheer before heading to the West Jefferson Christmas Parade at 2 p.m. Families are encouraged to stop by and say hello while he’s there.
With a wide range of artisans participating, the event promises something for everyone — whether searching for a special Christmas gift, looking for something beautiful for the home, or simply enjoying the creativity and craftsmanship of local makers.
Held indoors at Bethany United Methodist Church in West Jefferson, the fair offers a welcoming setting to browse, shop, and enjoy a day of community fellowship, regardless of the weather.
Visitors are encouraged to bring cash, as some artisans may not be equipped to accept credit or debit cards.
Event Details
● What: Indoor Artisan Fair
● When: Saturday, Nov. 14
● Time: 9 a.m. – 3 p.m.
● Where: Bethany United Methodist Church, 495 Highway 194 South, West Jefferson, NC 28694
● Admission: Free
● Featuring: Handmade arts and crafts and homemade baked goods
● Special Guest: Santa Claus, stopping by around lunchtime before the 2 p.m. West Jefferson Christmas Parade
● Shopping Tip: Bring cash! Some vendors may not accept cards.
Come celebrate the creative spirit of the High Country, support talented artisans, enjoy a little Christmas magic, and get a head start on holiday shopping.

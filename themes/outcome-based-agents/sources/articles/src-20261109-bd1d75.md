Pennsylvania State Police, Troop P Wilkes-Barre, is searching for a missing adult male, Keenan Saniatan, 39, of Sugar Notch.

Saniatan fled the scene of a motor vehicle crash on Nov. 1 in the area of Route 11, Plymouth Twp, and was reported missing by family members several days after the crash. Anyone with information regarding the whereabouts of Saniatan is asked to contact Troop P Wilkes-Barre at 570-822-5400.

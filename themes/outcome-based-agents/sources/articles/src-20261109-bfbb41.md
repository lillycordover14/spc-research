Holiday Craft and Artisan Show Returns to Concord Nov. 7-8&nbsp;&nbsp;The Concord Insider

[page text]
The third annual Holiday Craft and Artisan Show will return to Concord Nov. 7-8, bringing together 130 exhibitors for a weekend of holiday shopping, handcrafted goods and seasonal entertainment.

Organized by The Great New England Craft & Artisan Shows, the event will take place at the Capital City Sports & Fitness Complex, 10 Garvins Falls Road, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. both days.

The show will feature a wide selection of handmade products, including jewelry, seasonal decorations, artwork, clothing, woodworking, home goods and specialty foods. Organizers emphasize that the event is dedicated exclusively to artisans, craftspeople and specialty food producers, with no direct-sales vendors or commercial businesses.

 

 

In addition to shopping, visitors can enjoy live music, craft beers and cocktails, and a variety of lunch options. A $100 door prize will also be offered during the event.

The annual show provides an opportunity for shoppers to find unique holiday gifts while supporting independent makers and small businesses throughout the region.

Admission is $7 per person and includes entry for both days. Children ages 14 and younger are admitted free. Admission will also be free for all visitors after 3 p.m. Sunday. Free parking is available at the venue, located off Exit 13 of Interstate 93.

 

 

For additional information and event updates, visit 
www.GNECraftArtisanShows.com
 or follow The Great New England Craft & Artisan Shows on Facebook and Instagram.

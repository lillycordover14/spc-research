MARSHALL COUNTY, W.Va. — 
Officials said no one was hurt and no environmental impacts have been found so far after a warehouse fire near Washington Lands.

The West Virginia Department of Environmental Protection and the state Fire Marshal assessed the area Monday morning. 
“We’re fortunate that nobody was injured during that, whether it be someone at the warehouse or one of the residents down there, none of the first responders, but also there’s no environmental impacts, as well,” Marshall County EMA Director Tom Hart said.
Washington Lands Elementary School is set to return to normal instruction tomorrow, according to the update.

Lio to perform at the Olympia on December 17, 2026

L’Olympia — visuel de communication

																											· Updated on November 9, 2026 at 08:58 a.m.
														

						Lio performs at the Olympia on December 17, 2026, at 8 p.m. The Paris date marks a new musical chapter for the singer of “Banana Split” and “Amoureux solitaires.”
					

Lio
 returns to the stage at 
the Olympia
 on 
December 17, 2026
. The singer will perform at the Paris venue as part of a tour that continues in France and Switzerland in 2027.

Having made her breakthrough in 1979 with 
Banana Split
, Lio established herself in French-language pop with songs such as 
Amoureux solitaires
 and 
Amicalement vôtre
. This Paris date is part of a career that connects the hits of her early years with new collaborations.

To be read
The best things to do this week, November 9–15, 2026, in Paris and Île-de-France

The concert comes during the 
Geoid Party in the Sky
 period, described by the venue as her twelfth studio album. For this project, Lio enlisted several songwriters and artists, including Louane, Hoshi, Corine, Marie Darrieussecq and Sofia Portanet. The writing gives a place to encounters and the passing down of knowledge between generations.

The Olympia date is presented by Play Two Live. The venue announces a seated, numbered configuration, with categories in the orchestra, mezzanine and balcony.

Blues sur Seine 2026: Festival concerts in Yvelines
Blues sur Seine returns to the Yvelines with Fatoumata Diawara, Sinclair, Fred Wesley and a showcase dedicated to emerging talent. A lineup spanning blues, funk, soul and jazz, running from November 6 to 21, 2026.
Interpol and Bloc Party in concert together at the Zénith in Paris in November 2026.
Interpol and Bloc Party will perform at the Zénith de Paris on November 16, 2026, at 7 p.m. The venue has announced that the joint concert is sold out.
Vanessa Paradis in concert at Paris' Accor Arena in November 2026
Vanessa Paradis presents her album Le retour des beaux jours at Paris Bercy on November 17, 2026, following her concerts at the Zénith de La Villette.
Almée live in Paris: a one-off evening in Paris in November 2026
Almée returns to Paris for a concert on November 19, 2026, at the ECUJE with “Les voix de la mémoire,” an evening featuring piano, electronic music, violin and surprise guests.
Asaf Avidan to give a symphonic concert at La Seine Musicale in November 2026
Asaf Avidan returns to La Seine Musicale on November 18 and 19, 2026, with the Orchestre Lamoureux, conducted by Tom Cohen. His repertoire and the album Unfurl take on a symphonic dimension for these two concerts.
Sébastien Tellier at Salle Pleyel in 2026: four concerts for Kiss The Beast
Sébastien Tellier presents Kiss The Beast at Salle Pleyel on November 16 and 17, followed by December 15 and 16, 2026, blending chanson and electronic pop.
Billy Cobham to perform at the Trianon with Time Machine in November 2026
Billy Cobham returns to Paris with Time Machine, a jazz fusion septet set to perform at the Trianon on November 22, 2026. The lineup features drums, a rhythm section and three horns.
Slift at the Trianon: Fantasia and Grandma’s Ashes opening the show
Slift returns to Paris to present Fantasia at the Trianon on November 21, 2026. The Toulouse-based psychedelic rock trio will be preceded by Grandma’s Ashes.
Tomohisa Yamashita to perform at the Casino de Paris in November 2026.
Tomohisa Yamashita brings his Dreaming tour to the Casino de Paris on November 22, 2026, at 7 p.m. The Japanese singer and actor continues his solo career after making his debut with NEWS.
Le Bataclan, one of the flagships of rock history in Paris
The Bataclan is a legendary concert venue in Paris, closely tied to the history of rock in the capital. Located in the lively 11th arrondissement, it remains an iconic fixture of the Paris music scene.
The Early Romantics: concerts on period pianos at the Château de Malmaison
In Rueil-Malmaison, Premiers Romantiques brings the historic pianos of Château de Malmaison to life. Five concerts remain to be discovered, from November 22, 2026, to June 13, 2027.

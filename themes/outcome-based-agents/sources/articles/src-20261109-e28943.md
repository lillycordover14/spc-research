ElevenLabs and SoundCloud partner to bring AI music directly to listeners&nbsp;&nbsp;RouteNote

[page text]
ElevenLabs users can now publish AI-generated music directly to SoundCloud, connecting AI music creation with a platform for discovery. Here’s what the partnership means for independent artists and labels.

AI music creation and music sharing are becoming increasingly connected. ElevenLabs has 
partnered
 with 
SoundCloud
 to let users publish AI-generated tracks directly from its ElevenMusic platform, removing the need to download and upload music separately.

Announced on November 5, the partnership brings AI-generated music closer to SoundCloud’s community of artists and listeners. It also highlights how music platforms are approaching AI differently, with opportunities for creators alongside ongoing questions about rights, transparency and the future of music discovery.

ElevenMusic users can publish AI music directly to SoundCloud

Creators can now generate music using ElevenMusic and publish it straight to SoundCloud without leaving their creative workflow. This makes SoundCloud the first external platform to support direct publishing from ElevenMusic, connecting AI music creation with an established community for sharing and discovering music.

It’s easy to get started too. Users can connect their SoundCloud account through ElevenMusic’s profile settings. Once linked, select an original track, add a description, genre and tags, then choose ‘Save to SoundCloud’ to publish it.

A bonus for SoundCloud Artist Pro subscribers

There’s also a new benefit for SoundCloud Artist Pro subscribers. Both new and existing members can claim three months of ElevenMusic Pro through SoundCloud’s Member Benefits programme.

The offer includes access to:

Up to 1,000 song creations per month

500 stem splits per month

Five custom styles or vocals per month

The companies say this is the beginning of a broader collaboration, with further opportunities to connect music creation and discovery potentially on the way.

SoundCloud’s growing relationship with AI music

The ElevenLabs partnership arrives as SoundCloud continues to expand its connections with AI-powered music tools.

In October, SoundCloud 
partnered 
with Moises, an AI-powered music production platform. This partnership allows artists to import SoundCloud tracks into Moises, work on them using its creative tools and publish them back to SoundCloud.

The ElevenMusic integration takes a slightly different approach. Rather than bringing existing tracks into a production environment, it connects AI-generated music with a platform where creators can share their work and reach listeners.

SoundCloud’s growing integration with AI tools comes at a time when the music industry remains divided over their impact.

Deezer 
has introduced 
AI music detection
 and labelling to improve transparency, while Bandcamp has 
prohibited AI-generated
 music under its platform policies. Other services are taking different approaches to identifying and managing AI-generated content.

Bandcamp puts human artists first as it clamps down on AI music

SoundCloud, meanwhile, is positioning AI as another way for artists to experiment and share music, while emphasising that its existing content policies still apply.

Tracks published directly through ElevenMusic will carry disclosures identifying their creation source, and SoundCloud says uploads must comply with its existing ownership and rights requirements.

What does this mean for independent artists and labels?

The ElevenLabs and SoundCloud partnership reflects a wider shift towards AI becoming part of the music creation and distribution process.

For independent artists and producers, tools like ElevenMusic can provide another way to experiment with ideas, explore different sounds and develop creative concepts. Direct publishing also reduces the number of steps between creating a track and sharing it with an audience.

However, making music easier to create and publish doesn’t necessarily make it easier to build a sustainable music career.

With AI-generated tracks becoming more accessible, artists still need to consider what makes their music distinctive, how they connect with listeners and how they build a recognisable identity.

Transparency and ownership remain important, too. SoundCloud’s existing policies apply to AI-generated uploads, including requirements around copyright and impersonation. Creators should make sure they understand the terms of any AI tools they use and have the necessary rights to publish their music.

AI music creation and distribution continue to evolve

The ElevenLabs and SoundCloud partnership brings AI-generated music closer to an established music-sharing platform, offering creators a more direct route from experimentation to publication.

It also raises broader questions about how streaming services will balance AI-powered creativity with artists’ rights, listener transparency and the value of human-made music.

As more platforms introduce AI tools and integrations, independent artists and labels will need to keep up with changing policies while finding new ways to connect with audiences.

Whether you’re experimenting with AI-assisted production or releasing original music, understanding your distribution options and keeping control of your creative work remain essential.

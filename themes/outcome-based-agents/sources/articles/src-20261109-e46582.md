INDIANAPOLIS — Indiana Democrats on Tuesday shattered the GOP’s 14-year supermajority in the state’s House of Representatives.
More than a dozen additional Republican incumbents appeared in danger of losing their seats as vote-counting continued late Tuesday. This includes House Speaker Todd Huston, who was trailing his opponent Democrat Lauren Cole with 99 percent reporting.

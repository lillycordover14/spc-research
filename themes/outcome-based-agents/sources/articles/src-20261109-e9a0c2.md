        Ahead of its expected IPO next week, Anthropic is hosting a private CEO forum this week in Montecito. Huang was set to be interviewed by an Anthropic executive on Monday evening, a person familiar with the event told Business Insider. He is no longer expected to attend, according to a second person familiar with the matter.
    


        Anthropic declined to comment.
    


        Huang is also no longer set to speak at the ElevenLabs Summit in NYC later this week, a spokesperson for the AI voice technology startup confirmed to Business Insider.

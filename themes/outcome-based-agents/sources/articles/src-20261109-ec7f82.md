CLAY, N.Y. — 
UPDATE:
Caughdenoy Road is now open after Monday morning's crash involving a CSX train and vehicle.
The New York State Police tells CNY Central a car was left unoccupied on tracks when the train came through. There were no injuries on-scene to the driver of the vehicle or to anyone on the train.
We are awaiting word if any citations will be issued. 
ORIGINAL:
The Clay Fire Department responded to a car vs. train crash early Monday morning. 
Caughdenoy Road at Micron was shut down because of the crash. 
The Fire Department said no injuries have been reported. 
There have been no further details released by area law enforcement as to the nature of the crash. 
This is a developing story.

LUZERNE CO. (WOLF)  — 
Pennsylvania State Police are asking for the public's help locating a 39-year-old Sugar Notch man who has been missing since a vehicle crash earlier this month.
Keenan Saniatan reportedly fled the scene of a motor vehicle crash on November 1 along Route 11 in Plymouth Township, according to state police.
Family members reported Saniatan missing several days after the crash.
Police have not released details about the circumstances of the crash, whether Saniatan was injured or why he left the scene.
Troopers from Pennsylvania State Police Troop P in Wilkes-Barre are investigating his disappearance.
Anyone with information about Saniatan's whereabouts is asked to contact state police at 570-822-5400.

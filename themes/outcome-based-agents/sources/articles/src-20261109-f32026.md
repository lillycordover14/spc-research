by
 
 
Ron Kopp Jr.
Nov 9, 2026, 7:30 PM UTC
The 
Kansas City Chiefs
’ 35-12 win over the 
New York Jets
 on Sunday only helped the team keep pace with the rest of the AFC playoff contenders leading into a big spot for the conference-leading 
Buffalo Bills

Minnesota Vikings

Jacksonville Jaguars

Baltimore Ravens

Cincinnati Bengals

Atlanta Falcons

The 
Denver Broncos

Carolina Panthers

New England Patriots

Green Bay Packers

Cleveland Browns

New Orleans Saints

Explaining the 6-2 logjam in the AFC playoff standings
The Steelers and Bengals are both 6-2, so they first have to settle the AFC North leader. Pittsburgh’s win over the Bengals slots the Steelers into fourth place.
In the Wild card, the Chiefs and Bengals are tied. The Chiefs have the best AFC record at 6-1, so they fill in at fifth. The Bengals are sixth.

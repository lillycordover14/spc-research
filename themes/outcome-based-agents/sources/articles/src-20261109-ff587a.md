Gala: No definite timeline for Osimhen return                    

                                09 November 2026 3:24 am WAT                            

                                        By 

                                            The Sun
                                        

 

Follow Us on Google

 

Okan Buruk, manager of Galatasaray, has insisted that there’s no timeline regarding
Victor Osimhen’s return to action.

The Super Eagles striker underwent doctor’s knife on his left knee at the Maslak Acıbadem Hospital, and the procedure was performed by Galatasaray doctor Yener İnce.

“There’s no definite timeline for Osimhen, only estimated ones. We need to monitor how everything progresses day by day.

“The international break has at least been a benefit for us. After that, we’ll continue to monitor and assess his progress day by day.

“Hopefully, he’ll be back as soon as possible, but what’s most important isn’t how soon he returns, but that he returns in the best possible health and as strong as possible.

“There may be situations where we won’t risk him for a week, three days or five days. That’s why we need to assess his condition day by day after the international break. These two weeks will be important for us.”

Turkish HT Spor reporter Celik Cagatay shared the latest developments on the Nigerian.

“Osimhen is expected to be sidelined for 2-3 weeks. The surgery went well, and his condition will be monitored day by day. We expect the Nigerian star to miss the Samsunspor match after the international break but return to the lineup for the Aston Villa match.”

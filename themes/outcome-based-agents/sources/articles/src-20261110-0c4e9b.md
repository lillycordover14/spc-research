CAMBRIDGE, Vt.
 —
If you normally travel via the Notch Road, you should plan on taking an alternate route tonight.
Scenic VT-108, known locally as Smugglers’ Notch or the Notch Road, is set to close on Tuesday night, Nov. 10 at 6 p.m., as road crews monitor conditions and forecasts.

The Notch Road will officially close at 6 p.m., with gates on both sides closed at that time.

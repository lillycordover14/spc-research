12+1 London marks Numbers Series 100 with UNAI. and Carlo Lio

The release also coincides with the label’s 12th anniversary.

Photo credit:

12+1 London
 has reached the 
100th installment
 of its 
Numbers Series
 with 
‘0100’
, a collaboration between 
UNAI.
 and 
Carlo Lio
. The release marks the first appearance of label founder 
U Z Z V
 under his 
UNAI.
 alias on the imprint.

Since its inception, the 
Numbers Series
 has followed a format in which releases are identified by numbers rather than conventional track titles. The concept places the focus on the music itself, with each installment documenting a different stage in the label’s development.

For the milestone release, 
UNAI.
 joins forces with 
Carlo Lio
 on a production drawing from old-school techno and jackin’ house. The track combines punchy drums, rhythmic patterns, and electronic textures in a stripped-back arrangement.

The collaboration also reflects an evolution in 
U Z Z V
’s relationship with the label, introducing another of his artistic identities into its catalog. At the same time, 
‘0100’
 marks 12 years since the founding of 
12+1 London
.

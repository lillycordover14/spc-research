NASA’s Plankton, Aerosol, Cloud, ocean Ecosystem (PACE) mission, launched in 2024, provides new insights into ocean color and its changes over time. The Ocean Color Instrument aboard PACE observes the ocean in hyperspectral color, across several hundred wavelengths of visible, near-infrared, and ultraviolet light, allowing scientists to monitor phytoplankton and other changes in ocean color. Using PACE data, researchers calculate the average color of the ocean by simplifying the measured spectrum of light into a single value known as apparent visible wavelength (AVW).
The visualizations on this page use a running 30-day average of PACE AVW data. They are updated daily as new data becomes available.

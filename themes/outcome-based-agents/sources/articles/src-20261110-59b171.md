Diann Nichols Pace

Mar 20, 1946 — Nov 10, 2026

Lexington

Diann Pace, 80, of Davidson County, passed away on Tuesday, November 10th, 2026, at Atrium Baptist Lexington Medical Center.
Diann was born on March 20th, 1946, in Gaston County, to the late William Edward Nichols and Katherine Wright Nichols.
Mrs. Pace was originally a member of First Wesleyan Church in Bessemer City, North Carolina. She enjoyed watching races, spending time reading books, and most of all, spending time with her family. Her family was an important part of her life, and she treasured the time they shared together.
Diann is preceded in death by her loving husband, Joe Murray Pace; her parents; and her sister Melissa Brock.
She is survived by her son, Todd Pace; her daughters, Joy Henderson, Angie Stiles, Chrissy Schab; and numerous grandchildren and great-grandchildren.
Condolences may be made at www.davidsonfuneralhome.net

The South Jersey homes of Phillies slugger 
Bryce Harper
 and former Sixer 
Robert Covington
 are for sale. 
Harper’s Haddonfield farmhouse is 
on the market for $4.5 million
, while Covington’s 
Cherry Hill
 home is
 listed for $2.9 million.
Situated on one-third of an acre near downtown 
Haddonfield
, Harper’s 6,200-square-foot home has five bedrooms, four full bathrooms, and two half-bathrooms. Listing photos show hardwood floors and shiplap walls, a brick fireplace, a gourmet kitchen, and a primary suite with two large walk-in closets. 
The property also has a gated-in backyard with a brick patio, a private nanny or guest suite, and a finished basement with a workout room.
The listing was 
first reported Monday
 by Philly Voice. 
Bryce Harper’s wife, Kayla Harper, is listed as the current owner in online property records, and the Phillies star has posted
 several TikToks
 that
 appear to have been shot
 in the Haddonfield home. 
The Harpers’ house was built in 2018, according to property records, and last sold for $2.1 million in 2019, the same year Harper 
signed a 13-year, $330 million deal with the Phillies.
 He is under contract with the team until 2031 and has said he wants to 
retire in Philadelphia.
Bryce and Kayla Harper, both Nevada natives, have
 four young children.
The listing agent on the Haddonfield property listing did not return requests for comment. 
A few miles away in Cherry Hill, Covington’s nearly 9,800-square-foot home is also on the market. It has six bedrooms, eight full bathrooms, and two half-bathrooms.
“It’s a Ritz-Carlton-inspired home,” said listing agent Melissa Young, of Compass Sports & Entertainment Division. “It really is drop-dead beautiful.”
Set on a little more than half an acre, the home has a grand foyer, a chef’s kitchen with heated floors, and plenty of space for entertaining. The property contains a video-gaming area, gym, and sauna, according to the listing. 
Outside, there is a saltwater pool, hot tub, fire pit, and a cabana with a full bathroom and built-in grill station, “all supported by top-tier security for complete privacy,” according to the listing. 
Covington, who last played for the Sixers 
during the 2023-2024 season
, bought the home for $3.1 million in 2023, according to online property records. Covington previously played for the team 
from 2014 to 2018
 and was 
a leader during “The Process” era.
Covington now works as
 chief basketball officer at his alma mater,
 Tennessee State University in Nashville. 
In recent years, his Cherry Hill home has “become a hub for other professional athletes,” some of whom have rented the furnished property for about $20,000 a month, Young said. 
The home can be sold fully furnished, too, Young added.
The listing points out that the property is 11 miles from the Sixers practice facility in Camden and 16 miles from the Eagles’ training complex in South Philly.
The home, located near Cherry Hill East High School, was built in 2006 by Philly-based Corso Brothers Construction. It was designed by 
Collingswood
’s OSK Designs Partners.

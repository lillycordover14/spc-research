GRAFTON, W.Va. — Top Notch Trading Post & Pawn Shop celebrates its first anniversary in Grafton, marking a year of dedicated community service and a growing customer base. The Grafton business marked this milestone Friday with a special ribbon-cutting ceremony, celebrating its first anniversary and significant investment in the local community.

Top Notch Trading Post & Pawn Shop employee Ronnie Burns cuts a ribbon, officially signifying a year of business in Grafton, as Mayor Diana Thompson, City Manager Coleman Durrett, and owners Adam Reeves and Remington Michael look on.

Staff photo by Nicki Skinner

Located next to ACE Hardware, Top Notch Trading Post & Pawn Shop provides customers a unique opportunity to buy, sell, trade, and pawn a wide variety of items. These include firearms, tools, gold, coins, silver, sports collectibles, trading cards, and other valuables.

Co-owner Adam Reeves explained that opening the Grafton business stemmed from recognizing a community need and merging two personal interests.
“I think there was a need for it socioeconomically. The last gun store that was here was Hardcore Hunters, and they went out of business. So, I saw it as an opportunity,” Reeves said. “My stepson, Remington, is a partial owner in the shop, too. He’s always wanted to be in the gun business, and I wanted to be in the pawn business, so we figured, let’s just do it.”
Despite the inherent challenges of launching a new business, Reeves expressed encouragement regarding the shop’s steady growth during its first year. With experience opening four or five businesses, he understands that building a successful operation requires time.

Mayor Diana Thompson reads a certificate recognizing Top Notch Trading Post & Pawn Shop's one year anniversary, as City Manager Coleman Durrett, employee Ronnie Burns, and owners Adam Reeves and Remington Michael prepare to cut the ribbon celebrating the milestone. 

Staff photo by Nicki Skinner

“You know, anytime you start a business, you struggle for three years. It’s that way in every business, but I’ve noticed an uptick the whole time,” he said. “There’s no straight up and down— that’s not how it works in business. But we’ve seen a steady incline this entire time, so I’m happy with the way it’s been going.”
For those new to pawn shop services, Reeves clarified that Top Notch accepts a wide variety of items. This provides customers an alternative for selling valuables or securing immediate funds through a pawn transaction.

“We have guns, gold, sports cards, any type of collectibles, silver, tools — anything that is of value that someone else wants,” Reeves said. “That’s why you come here. You don’t want it; someone else does. Or you need money, and the bank won’t loan you money today because that’s not how banks work anymore. So, if you bring us something of value, you can walk away with money in hand. No one else can provide that service locally but Top Notch.”
Top Notch Trading Post & Pawn Shop operates Monday through Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
After a successful first year, Top Notch Trading Post & Pawn Shop aims for continued growth, serving Grafton residents as a reliable local destination to buy, sell, trade, and pawn a diverse range of items.

Community members are encouraged to visit the Top Notch pawn shop at 245 Greenes Plaza, G4, Grafton, to explore its offerings. For additional information, call 304-931-9393.

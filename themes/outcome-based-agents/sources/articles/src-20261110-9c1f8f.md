HUNTINGTON CO.  (WJAC) — 
Emergency officials in Huntingdon County, including the county coroner's office and Pennsylvania State Police, are currently on scene of an alleged incident along Hare's Valley Road in Clay Township.
According to our 6 News crew on scene, members of multiple investigative agencies have responded to a farmhouse along Route 655.
As details remain limited, a state police spokesperson confirmed that he is in communication with responding troopers but does not have any information to release at this time.
Additional agencies have also not responded to WJAC requests for information at this time.

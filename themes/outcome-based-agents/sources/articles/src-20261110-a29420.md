Artisan cotton candy shaped like butterflies and Christmas trees is back at EPCOT’s China Pavilion. Prices run from $14 to $25 depending on the design.

This isn’t your average fair cotton candy – it’s more of a sculpted souvenir than a snack. Worth a look if you’re wandering through the China Pavilion and want something photogenic to carry around World Showcase.

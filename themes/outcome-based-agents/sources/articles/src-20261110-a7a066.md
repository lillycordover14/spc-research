In the U.S. midterm elections, the Democratic Party flipped at least seven Senate and 44 House seats, and regained control of both chambers of Congress with the largest midterm margin in the House popular vote for either party since 1974.

Dragon Ball Z
 was elected to serve as the attorney general of Texas; a two-time Latin Grammy-winner flipped a Texas House seat for the Democrats; and a nineteen-year-old college sophomore was elected as a clerk of a Superior Court in Arizona.

In Paraguay, the bank accounts of nearly three million people were frozen following the collapse of a digital bank favored by the country’s president, who unsuccessfully attempted to resign from his post in order to enjoy legal protections afforded to former leaders, and in Tallinn, Estonia, a delivery robot ran a red light, caused a bus to crash, and then left before the police arrived.

The New Jersey real estate market is wild, and it's even crazier if you're looking to buy a home that's currently owned by an extremely popular athlete.
Bryce Harper Is A Baseball Legend
Bryce Harper may be one of the most recognized names in baseball right now. And whether you love the Phillies or you hate the Phillies, one thing most people can agree on is that Bryce Harper is a legend in the game.
When he got signed to the Phillies in 2019, it was a huge deal. Mainly because the contract he signed, worth 330 million dollars, was one of the largest in recent history.

Bryce Harper Is Preparing To Sell His Haddonfield, NJ, Home
Now, the MLB slugger is getting ready to sell his house in Haddonfield, New Jersey, and you have the opportunity to own a piece of sports history... technically.
As I mentioned before, the housing market in New Jersey is pretty wild. Houses that are less than 1,000 square feet can sell for well over half a million dollars depending on the school district and how close the home is to the Jersey Shore.

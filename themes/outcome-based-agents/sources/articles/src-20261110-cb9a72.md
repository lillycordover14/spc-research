The Harper High School cross country teams wrapped up their season with a strong showing at the Region IV-2A meet Monday, Oct. 26, in Corpus Christi.
The Ladyhorns finished 17th as a team, while sophomore Ben Anderson represented the Longhorn boys individually and came within one place of advancing to the state meet.
Anderson finished 19th overall in the boys 5K with a time of 18:10.6.

“He missed advancing by one spot, just five seconds,” Harper head cross country coach Kenny King said.
On the girls side, King said the Ladyhorns turned in some of their best performances of the season.
“All the girls set PRs!!” King said.
Freshman Kimber Cantwell led Harper with a 79th-place finish in 14:01.4.
Freshman Adelyn Miller followed in 85th at 14:07.2, while freshman Milania Lopez finished 91st in 14:17.3 and freshman Ellie Anderson placed 99th in 14:21.7.
All four finished under 14:30.
Junior Lyliana Cantu finished 140th in 15:41.8, sophomore Sawyer Rotge was 147th in 16:01.4 and sophomore Gracie Heflin placed 154th in 17:20.
The Ladyhorns finished with 422 points in the 22-team field.
“Super proud of our Ladyhorns and Longhorns for how well they did today and all season long,” King said. “Harper cross country took another big step forward with better things coming in the future!”

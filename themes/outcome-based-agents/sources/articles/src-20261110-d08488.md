FANNIN COUNTY, Ga. — 
Fannin County emergency officials say they have received 929 damage reports and self-reports as teams continue assessing areas affected by recent flooding.
The county’s preliminary damage assessment lists 271 residential reports and 89 commercial reports across four categories. 
Officials caution that the numbers may change as information is collected and verified.
Among residential reports, 28 are classified as destroyed, 148 as major damage, 57 as minor damage and 38 as affected. 
For commercial properties, the breakdown lists two destroyed, 70 with major damage, six with minor damage and 11 affected.
The county is using FEMA definitions to classify damage:
“Affected” includes water in a crawlspace or unfinished basement, or damage to an attached building. 
“Minor” includes water in living areas up to 18 inches but below electrical outlets, or water in a finished basement. 
“Major” means water above electrical outlets or between 18 inches and the roofline. 
“Destroyed” includes water above the roofline, a home off its foundation or the complete failure of two or more structural components.
Fannin County EMA says the assessments document the damage and help advance the state and federal disaster assistance process. 
The agency says the assessments are preliminary and are not detailed structural inspections.
Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency is assisting and coordinating with local teams.

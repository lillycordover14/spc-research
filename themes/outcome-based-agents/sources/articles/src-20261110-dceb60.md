HUNTINGDON COUNTY, Pa (WJAC) — 
Emergency authorities in Huntingdon County are investigating a reported shooting at a home in Clay Township.
County coroner Paul Sharum confirmed that he, as well as Pennsylvania State Police troopers, were called to a residence along Route 655 (Hare's Valley Road) Tuesday morning.
No additional details have been provided at this time as first responders remain on scene.
Stay with us online and on-air for further updates to this developing story.
ORIGINAL | 
Emergency officials in Huntingdon County, including the county coroner's office and Pennsylvania State Police, are currently on scene of an alleged incident along Hare's Valley Road in Clay Township.
According to our 6 News crew on scene, members of multiple investigative agencies have responded to a farmhouse along Route 655.
As details remain limited, a state police spokesperson confirmed that he is in communication with responding troopers but does not have any information to release at this time.
Additional agencies have also not responded to our requests for information at this time.
Stay with 6 News online and on-air for further updates to this developing story.

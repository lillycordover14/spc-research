Rotunda Capital Partners Sells American Equipment After Sixfold Revenue Growth and 16 Acquisitions&nbsp;&nbsp;citybiz

[page text]
Rotunda Capital Partners
,
 a lower middle-market private equity firm focused on industrial businesses, has 
sold American Equipment Solutions
, a Salt Lake City-based provider of overhead crane, hoist and material handling equipment services.

The transaction concludes Rotunda’s investment in American Equipment, which began in May 2021. During the partnership, the company completed 16 add-on acquisitions, increased its revenue sixfold and expanded into a nationwide operation with more than 40 locations.

Financial terms of the transaction and the identity of the buyer were not disclosed.

Founded in 1969, American Equipment provides equipment, maintenance, repair and operational support services for industrial customers that rely on overhead cranes, hoists, rigging systems and other material handling infrastructure.

The company’s services include equipment inspections, preventive maintenance, field repairs, replacement parts, engineering, fabrication, equipment installation, system modernization and operator training.

Its operations also include Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA)-mandated inspections and maintenance, supporting customers with equipment safety and regulatory compliance requirements.

American Equipment serves thousands of customers nationwide across industrial markets where lifting and material handling equipment is critical to daily operations.

Rotunda acquired its interest in the business in May 2021 and worked with the company’s leadership to expand its geographic footprint, strengthen internal operations and develop a broader national service platform.

“Since partnering with American Equipment in May of 2021, the Company has achieved transformational growth,” said 
Ryan Aprill
, partner at Rotunda Capital Partners.

“Through 16 add-on acquisitions and significant strategic operational investments, the Company has increased topline sixfold to become a national service provider with more than 40 locations serving thousands of customers nationwide,” Aprill added.

Beyond acquisitions, Rotunda invested in expanding American Equipment’s executive leadership team, developing corporate infrastructure and implementing systems intended to support a larger and more geographically distributed business.

The firm also helped establish an internal acquisition team to identify and integrate additional businesses.

Another initiative was the development of American Equipment University, an in-house training program designed to recruit, train and develop technicians.

The program supports the company’s ability to maintain service capacity as it expands into new markets and adds customers.

“The American Equipment growth story is a great example of Rotunda’s dedication to transforming family, founder, and management-owned companies into data-driven growth platforms,” said 
Corey Whisner
, managing partner at Rotunda.

Whisner said investments in leadership, technology, acquisition capabilities and workforce development were central to the company’s expansion during Rotunda’s ownership.

American Equipment CEO 
Tom Hoyer
 will continue leading the business alongside its existing management team following the sale.

Hoyer said Rotunda’s operational resources and acquisition support helped the company scale while maintaining its focus on customer service.

“The Rotunda team provided us with the operational tools, distribution and service expertise and acquisition assistance to help the Company get to where it is today,” Hoyer said.

“With this foundational support, we believe American Equipment is well positioned for significant continued expansion,” he added.

American Equipment founder Adam Zimmerman also credited the partnership with helping the company build a larger operating platform.

“My partnership with Rotunda was exactly what I envisioned, and they lived up to everything they promised when I first met them in 2021,” Zimmerman said.

“The entire American Equipment team is thrilled to embark on this new chapter,” he added.

Dan Lipson, managing partner at Rotunda, said the company’s transformation reflected the leadership of Hoyer, Bob Creighton and the broader American Equipment management team.

The sale represents an exit from a business that Rotunda developed through a combination of acquisitions, operating investments and internal infrastructure improvements.

Founded in 2009, Rotunda Capital Partners manages approximately $1.5 billion in assets, including its most recent $735 million fund.

The firm focuses on control investments in value-added distribution, asset-light logistics, and industrial and business services companies, generally targeting businesses with enterprise values between $50 million and $400 million.

Rotunda works with founders and management teams to pursue growth through acquisitions, operational improvements and data-driven business strategies.

Lincoln International served as lead financial advisor on the American Equipment transaction, while Benesch Friedlander Coplan & Aronoff LLP provided legal counsel.

The parties did not disclose American Equipment’s current revenue, transaction valuation, purchase price or Rotunda’s investment return.

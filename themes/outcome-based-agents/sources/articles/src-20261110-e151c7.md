Resigned, but the reservation remains in Reserv+: will it protect against mobilization

                                                                                            08:00, 10 November 2026
                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                    

3 min

A man resigned from a critically important enterprise, but still sees an active reservation in Reserv+: does this status protect against mobilization if the employment relationship has already ended, who should cancel the reservation, and what to do if the information in the registry is not updated – we answer in the new section "We — for the people."

Reader’s question

"I worked at a critical infrastructure enterprise and had a reservation from mobilization. I recently resigned, but Reserv+ still shows that the reservation is valid for several more months.

The former employer says all documents were submitted, but the status in the app has not changed.

Can I legally use such a reservation while it is displayed in Reserv+? If representatives of the Territorial Recruitment Center (TRC) stop me, do they have the right to mobilize me despite the active status in the app? And who will be responsible if the information about the cancellation of the reservation was not entered into the registry on time?"

Editorial answer: status in Reserv+ does not yet guarantee the right to deferment

After resignation from the enterprise, the basis on which the employee received the reservation disappears. However, this does not mean it can be automatically considered canceled. The decisive factor is whether the reservation was terminated in the prescribed manner and what information was contained in the state registry on the relevant date.

According to Article 23 of the Law of Ukraine "On Mobilization Preparation and Mobilization," those liable for military service who are reserved in the prescribed manner and registered for special military accounting are not subject to conscription during mobilization.

The procedure for reservation and grounds for its cancellation are defined by the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine Resolution No. 76 dated January 27, 2023. One of the grounds for canceling a reservation is the dismissal of the liable person from the enterprise for which they were reserved.

Therefore, it is necessary to distinguish between two circumstances:

termination of employment;

cancellation of the reservation and updating the relevant information in the state registry.

Why Reserv+ may show an old status

Reserv+ displays information from the Unified State Register of conscripts, liable persons, and reservists "Obereg."

If information about termination of employment or cancellation of the reservation has not yet been processed, the previous status may be displayed in the app for some time.

In such a situation, it is worth checking at least two points:

whether the former employer submitted information about the dismissal to the Pension Fund of Ukraine;

whether the reservation was canceled, including by submitting the relevant application through the Diia portal, if such a procedure was applied.

The application for cancellation of the reservation through Diia is submitted by the head of the enterprise or an authorized person via the electronic cabinet of the legal entity. 
The former employee cannot independently cancel their reservation through Diia.

If the data has already been submitted but outdated information remains in the app, you can use the function "Menu" → "Correct data online" → "Outdated workplace." This is intended to clarify employment information, not to directly cancel the reservation.

Can the TRC mobilize a man if the app shows a reservation

The definite answer depends on whether the reservation was valid at the time of conscription. In particular, the current information in the state registry and documents confirming the grounds for cancellation matter.

If the reservation was properly canceled and there are no other grounds for deferment, the man may be subject to mobilization on general grounds.

At the same time, TRC representatives must check the current information about the liable person and follow the procedure established by law.

An outdated electronic document does not confirm the validity of the reservation on the date of mobilization. But a valid reservation cannot be ignored solely on the assumption that it should have been canceled.

This difference has already been the subject of court disputes.

Judicial practice: reservation in Reserv+ was valid for another ten months, but the man was mobilized

Case No. 380/666/26 was considered by the Lviv District Administrative Court on September 17, 2026.

The man had a reservation until September 16, 2026. Relevant information was contained in the electronic military registration document formed in Reserv+ on October 14, 2025.

However, on November 22, 2025, the man was mobilized. He challenged the TRC order, insisting that he had a valid deferment and was not subject to conscription.

During the court hearing, it was revealed that on November 21, 2025, one day before mobilization, the enterprise lost its critically important status. Because of this, the employee’s reservation was automatically canceled in the state registry at the request of the Ministry of Digital Transformation.

The man disputed the reliability of the registry information provided to the TRC, but the court recognized it as proper evidence. Meanwhile, the documents the plaintiff referred to were formed in October — before the cancellation of the reservation.

The court established that at the time of conscription, the deferment was no longer valid. Therefore, it denied the cancellation of the mobilization order.

Thus, even if a previously formed document from Reserv+ indicates a reservation end date many months later, this does not prove that it remained valid at the time of mobilization.

It is also worth considering that in this case, the basis for canceling the reservation was the loss of the enterprise’s critically important status, not the employee’s dismissal. At the same time, it shows the importance of the current legal status of the reservation.

What the former employee should do

The main thing is not to try to use an outdated reservation status as guaranteed protection from mobilization.

After resignation, the basis related to working at the relevant enterprise disappears. However, the existence and relevance of the cancellation must be checked by documents and registry data.

If after resignation Reserv+ still shows a reservation for the previous enterprise and the new employer cannot arrange a new one, first of all it is necessary to find out whether the previous reservation was canceled and whether employment information was updated.

A verbal explanation "we submitted everything" is not enough to be sure that the data in the state registry is current.

It is advisable for the former employer to send a written request asking to inform:

whether information about dismissal was submitted to the Pension Fund of Ukraine and when;

whether an application for cancellation of the reservation was submitted through Diia;

if submitted — the date of submission, its registration number or other identifier if available;

the result of the application processing and whether cancellation occurred;

if cancellation did not occur — what actions were taken to resolve the situation.

It is worth requesting documentary confirmation of this information if it is available to the enterprise.

At the same time, it should be taken into account that Diia does not allow canceling the reservation of an employee who is no longer registered as employed at that company through the corresponding service. Therefore, after resignation, the employer may face technical limitations.

If the usual cancellation procedure is unavailable, it is necessary to clarify the reason for the discrepancy in the registries rather than repeatedly submitting the same application.

What to do if the former employer does not respond

The employee should keep the dismissal order, documents on termination of employment, correspondence with the employer, and the current electronic military registration document.

A written request is better sent in a way that allows confirming the date of its receipt.

If employment information is not updated, Reserv+ allows using the "Correct data online" — "Outdated workplace" function.

This allows initiating clarification of personnel information but is not an independent procedure for canceling the reservation.

If necessary, you can contact Reserv+ support or the TRC to check the current reservation status.

What to inform the new employer

It is advisable to provide the new employer with documents on termination of previous employment, the dismissal date, and current military registration data.

If Reserv+ still shows the previous reservation, the new employer should be informed about this.

If the state registry continues to record an active reservation for the previous enterprise, this may hinder the arrangement of a new one. According to the standard Diia procedure, an employee who already has a reservation cannot be reserved again.

Therefore, it is important for the new employer to establish whether the previous reservation is truly active in the registry or if it is an outdated data display.

Sample request to the former employer

Please provide information regarding the reservation from conscription for military service during mobilization, arranged by your enterprise.

Due to termination of employment, please inform:

Whether information about my dismissal was submitted to the Pension Fund of Ukraine and the date of submission.

Whether the enterprise submitted an application for cancellation of my reservation through the Diia portal.

If the application was submitted — the date, number, or other identifier of the application if available, and the result of its processing.

If the reservation was not canceled — the reason and measures the enterprise can take to resolve the situation.

If possible, please provide documentary confirmation of the above information.

Currently, Reserv+ continues to display a reservation for your enterprise, although the employment relationship has ended. This creates uncertainty regarding my military registration status and the possibility of arranging a reservation at a new workplace.

Please provide a written response and inform about the measures taken.

PLYMOUTH TWP. — A missing Sugar Notch man was found dead Tuesday in the Susquehanna River in West Nanticoke, state police said.

The investigation into the disappearance and death of Keenan Saniatan remains ongoing, police said.

A day earlier, police said they were searching for Saniatan, 39, after he fled the scene of a vehicle crash on Nov. 1 on Route 11 in Plymouth Twp. near where Saniatan’s body was located in the river.

Police said days after the crash Saniatan was reported missing by family members.

State police said they were assist by the Nanticoke and Hanover Twp. fire departments in the recovery of Saniatan’s body.

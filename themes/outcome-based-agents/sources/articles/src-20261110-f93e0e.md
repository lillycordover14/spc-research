PACE, Fla. -- A new ice rink is now taking shape in Pace. The 'Panhandle Ice Pier' will be the name of the new facility.
It will include a full-size hockey rink, locker rooms, training areas, concessions, and skate rentals.
Co-owner Jennifer Burkhardt says she and her husband were inspired to create this after going to a year-round ice rink in Savannah, Georgia, for their sons' hockey game.
"They were doing a good job, but when you put them next to teams that have year-round ice, they just couldn't quite get to that skill level," Burkhardt said. 
"The skills are there, the talent is there, and the ice is not there, and he said, 'Well, what do we do to fix that?' And I said, "Build an ice rink," and he said, "Ok," Burkhardt said. 
The project has been a few years in the making. 
Rough estimates of the project are at $6 million, funded privately by the Burkhardt family.
"I think the best we can ask for is that it meets its operating costs; it will be expensive to run it year-round," Burkhardt said. "Water and electricity are expensive, and those are the two things that we need the most of, but we have a huge ice community here, despite what a lot of people think."
"We're going to do a lot of the ice sports here," she added. "Of course, ice hockey, figure skating, adult hockey, and you talk about all of that, but we also will have a lot of public skating time, birthday parties, public skating theme nights, things like that."
In addition to the Pensacola Junior Ice Flyers, the Greater Pensacola Figure Skating Club says they're looking forward to using this facility.
"We just need more ice time right now; we just have two to three days of ice time at the Bay Center," Greater Pensacola Figure Skating Club skate director Kristie Albarran said. "It's very limited, because the bay center is an event center, so when they book events, there's not really much time for ice skating, and so we are so excited that we are going to have a sheet of year-round ice."
The Burkhardts say they expect to open the rink by next summer.

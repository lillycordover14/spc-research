As requested by the Nevis City Council, municipal liquor store manager Kay Moen presented a “fix-it list” at the council’s Monday, Nov. 9 meeting.
“I do have quite a big list, but I guess the main thing that I want to say is that, structurally, our building is pretty good,” Moen said.

That said, she said something needs to be done with the building's concrete in the spring to mitigate issues with “how the water is moving,” and she anticipates that structural issues will eventually come up.
“Right now, I don't believe that's the case,” she said, “but if it's not addressed, it very well could.”
Moen brought up sagging boards that are “a tripping hazard,” and not compliant with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). 
She said the restrooms are also not ADA compliant, and lack the space to make them compliant. This means some walls will need to move, according to Moen.
“(The restrooms) are grandfathered in, but being grandfathered in isn't actually a thing when it comes to ADA compliance,” she said. “There is supposed to be a plan in place to become ADA compliant over time.”
She also said the building’s 16-camera surveillance system is nearing the end of its life and does not properly cover the basement, back room or liquor room, along with “a couple other hot spots.”
The bar top, which was replaced five to seven years ago, has sharp areas that need to be addressed, according to Moen. The bar also seems to be improperly anchored to its pedestal in areas.

Council members asked Moen to come up with a rough cost breakdown of all necessary repairs.

Zendesk launches Outcome AI Platform with outcome-based pricing&nbsp;&nbsp;Verdict

[page text]
Zendesk’s Outcome AI Platform introduces a new approach to enterprise customer and employee service. Credit: Sundry Photography/Shutterstock.com.

Zendesk has announced the launch of its Outcome AI Platform, introducing a new approach to enterprise customer and employee service that moves away from traditional seat-based pricing.

The US-based company will now provide unlimited seats and instead focus billing on outcomes, setting what it describes as a new standard for linking technology spend to measurable service results.

The Outcome AI Platform includes several features, combining AI agents, agent-to-agent collaboration, and integration tools that allow both people and AI to coordinate work across different systems.

According to Zendesk, this approach is designed to give individuals more control over their interactions with organisations and to enable businesses to address needs directly, not just respond to support tickets.

The shift comes as customer expectations around automation and assistance evolve.

In a global survey conducted by Zendesk, 52% of respondents indicated comfort with using personal AI assistants for daily tasks, while 63% said companies will need to redesign their service models to support AI-enabled interactions.

However, demand for human assistance remains high, with 84% stating that access to human support should continue to be offered.

The platform’s capabilities include the Open Service Directory (OSD), described as an open standard and public listing that enables AI assistants to locate brands’ verified support channels, regardless of whether a brand uses Zendesk.

Businesses can register these channels and determine the level of information shared and when human review is required. The directory does not access private customer data or perform service actions.

Zendesk is also introducing Zendesk Bridge, a connector designed to let organisations integrate any AI interface with its service platform while maintaining security, permissions, and human oversight.

This allows businesses to use AI tools of their choice alongside Zendesk’s systems without needing to reconstruct existing workflows.

Zendesk CEO Tom Eggemeier said: “People don’t think about which channels, systems, or departments they need to navigate to interact with an organisation. They simply want help to accomplish what they came to do.

“Outcome AI brings together the context and ability to act to help people reach the right outcome quickly, wherever their experience begins. It learns from each result and improves when it falls short. For organisations focused on loyalty and satisfaction, that’s the standard.”

Zendesk reports that with Outcome AI Platform Plans, customers can automate up to 84% of service requests, reduce resolution times, and potentially increase customer satisfaction.

The new pricing model allows customers to pay for resolved issues and completed business tasks based on an agreed Outcome Budget.

Zendesk will monitor and assess each resolved outcome to determine what qualifies under this budget.

Early adopters of its OSD include Betterment, Flex, ElevenLabs, TeamSystem, Telekom Payment, TBA Auctions, and Wireless Logic.

The platform also offers tools such as Self-Improving Zendesk, which links insights and actions across AI agents and human workflows, and Specialized Agents with domain expertise designed to handle complex cases.

These features are intended to support continuous service improvement and performance analysis for enterprise teams.

OpenAI launches business profiles on ChatGPT with engagement insights and AI agents
OpenAI's new business features include aggregated engagement metrics, product feeds with pricing and availability, and sponsored Business Agents that can serve as destinations for ChatGPT advertisements.

OpenAI launches Business Hub in ChatGPT, allows companies to manage profiles and deploy AI agents

ADVERTISEMENT
OpenAI has introduced a new set of business-focused features within ChatGPT, allowing companies to manage their profiles, update product information and understand how users discover and engage with their offerings on the platform.
The features, collectively introduced under the Businesses in ChatGPT initiative, are being rolled out in beta. The launch includes Business Hub, which allows companies to claim and manage their profiles, and Business Agents, which enable users to interact directly with businesses through AI-powered conversations.
Through Business Hub, companies can claim their presence on ChatGPT and, after completing verification, update essential business information. They can also upload product feeds containing details such as pricing and availability, allowing 
ChatGPT
 to provide users with more current information while they explore products and services.
OpenAI said businesses located in markets where ChatGPT operates can claim their profiles through the platform. Existing OpenAI Enterprise customers and advertisers can approach their OpenAI representatives for assistance, while merchants using Shopify can connect their stores through either Business Hub or the Shopify administration panel.

OpenAI introduces business insights dashboard
Business Hub will also provide companies with aggregated data on how users interact with their profiles and offerings on 
ChatGPT
. The dashboard will include metrics such as business mentions and website clicks, alongside discovery topics that highlight common themes in conversations where a company's website is referenced.
The insights are intended to help businesses understand how they appear in ChatGPT responses and identify areas where they could provide more information.
OpenAI said it would not provide businesses with access to individual user conversations or chats conducted with Business Agents. The insights will be aggregated and made available only when interactions reach specified minimum thresholds.
Business Agents to help users explore products and services
The company is also introducing Business Agents, which eligible organisations can develop, test and launch after claiming their profiles.
The feature will initially be available to eligible e-commerce and online service businesses in the United States. It will allow ChatGPT users to initiate conversations with businesses and ask questions about their products and services.
For instance, customers comparing different products can use a Business Agent to understand the differences between available options and identify features that match their requirements.
OpenAI said these conversations would be clearly identified as interactions with a business and kept separate from the user's original ChatGPT conversation. They will also remain distinct from responses generated independently by ChatGPT.
Businesses will have the option to sponsor their Business Agents and use them as destinations for ChatGPT advertisements. OpenAI said sponsored placements would carry clear labels and remain separate from regular ChatGPT responses.
OpenAI expands ChatGPT's role in business discovery
The launch marks another step in OpenAI's efforts to make ChatGPT a platform where consumers can discover businesses, evaluate products and interact with companies.
The company had previously explored enabling direct purchases within ChatGPT through an e-commerce initiative that included a checkout feature and a commission-based model. However, it discontinued that effort in March 2026.
With Business Hub and Business Agents, OpenAI is now focusing on helping companies maintain their presence on ChatGPT, provide updated information and engage with potential customers through AI-powered interactions.
The company said it plans to expand Businesses in ChatGPT and Business Agents to additional markets and business categories over time. It also intends to develop tools for organisations with more complex requirements.

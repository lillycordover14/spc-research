Keeping Tabs: Tyra Booker, Creator & AVP Of Marketing At Rare Beauty
The creative force behind Selena Gomez & Demi Lovato's viral reunion. 
By
 
Bianca Asare
Published Nov 11, 2026
|
12:32pm
In 
Keeping Tabs,
 the world's most stylish people share their search history and current open tabs—what they're searching, saving, reading, and buying for your reading (and shopping) pleasure.
Last month, one 
beauty
 campaign took the internet by storm. It stirred up nostalgia, reminded us of the power of female friendship and, most importantly, had some of us zillenials feeling like pre-teens again. We're, of course, referring to 
Rare Beauty
's latest blush campaign, which reunited Selena Gomez and Demi Lovato. "The idea for this campaign was born out of a friendship I grew up watching on YouTube in such a raw and real way that formed the early stages of my career," says 
Tyra Booker
, the creative force behind the viral campaign. 
Booker, a creative director, master marketer, and fashion creator whose outfits often end up on my moodboard, has always been a beauty girl through and through. "My first memories surrounding beauty are probably stealing my mom's 
makeup
 and learning how to put it on by watching my first YouTube video," she says. 
Ahead, Booker shares what she gets into when she's not epitomizing girlhood through a beauty campaign or coming up with her next viral idea.

Why Raiders kept Maxx Crosby after sending Kirk Cousins to Vikings ahead of trade deadline

            By 
Zac Wassink

           | 
        Last updated Nov 11, 2026 8:19 AM ET
  

    Add us as a preferred source on Google
  

Shortly after it was 
learned
 that the 
Las Vegas Raiders
 had agreed to send quarterback 
Kirk Cousins 
to the 
Minnesota Vikings
 in return for a 2027 third-round draft pick,
 some wondered whether the Raiders could also look to acquire future draft assets for star pass-rusher 
Maxx Crosby
 before the 
NFL
 trade deadline arrived on Tuesday afternoon. 
However, Crosby stayed put as the Raiders confirmed that rookie and No. 1 overall draft pick
 
Fernando Mendoza
 will serve as the team's QB1 when 4-5 Las Vegas hosts the 5-4 
Seattle Seahawks
 this coming Sunday. Later on Tuesday, Raiders general manager John Spytek was asked why he considered Crosby "a part of" the club's future.

Keeping Maxx Crosby was "
the best decision for the Raiders"

"We got a lot of calls on a lot of our players," Spytek said, per 
Sam Warren
 of The Athletic. "There’s a lot of interest in our players, but again, we’re always going to make the best decision for the Raiders going forward, and that’s what we did [Tuesday]. And we say no a lot more than we say yes."
Ever since the 
Baltimore Ravens
 backed out of sending two
 
first-round draft picks to the Raiders for Crosby due to concerns related to his surgically repaired knee this past March, there was 
never
 any real indication 
from the start of the ongoing league year through Tuesday
 that Las Vegas came close to trading the 29-year-old. Crosby made it 
clear
 he expected to stay with what's been his only NFL club since the Raiders drafted him in 2019.

A previous 
report
 indicated Crosby will remain with the Raiders through at least the start of the 2027 campaign.
"There was minimal talk" regarding a possible Maxx Crosby trade
Shortly after the trade deadline passed, NFL insider 
Jordan Schultz
 revealed that the Raiders "received calls over the last few weeks about" Crosby, but their "answer was he isn’t being moved and is staying in Las Vegas." 
Later, 
Vincent Bonsignore
 of the California Post shared that "multiple league sources indicated there was minimal talk regarding Crosby, and indicated the Raiders had something to do with that by making it clear they wanted to keep him." 
"That gave potential suitors little reason to believe a phone call would get them very far," Bonsignore explained. 

As of Wednesday morning, DraftKings Sportsbook had the Raiders as 6.5-point underdogs against the Seahawks. Nevertheless, Spytek and Raiders
 
minority owner
 
Tom Brady
 clearly didn't want to take a key defensive contributor away from first-year head coach Klint Kubiak when Las Vegas is still 
within reach
 of making the playoffs. 
"For all the attention on what a contender might be able to do with him down the stretch, the Raiders are entitled to consider what they can do with him, too," Bonsignore added about Crosby. "They have a first-year head coach and a rookie quarterback about to make his first start. There's something to be said for having a player of Crosby's caliber around while those two find their footing, especially one who wants to be here to see where it goes."
It could be interesting to learn what Crosby thinks about that decision come January.

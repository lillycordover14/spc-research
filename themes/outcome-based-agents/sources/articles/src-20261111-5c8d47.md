ElevenLabs expands Nvidia partnership to power voice agents

Wednesday, 11 November 2026, 18:07

Photo source: Variety

The AI audio company plans to use Nvidia’s Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model and Vera Rubin computing architecture for its voice-agent platform. The announcement comes as Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang is under investigation over allegations involving a Chinese tech executive.

ElevenLabs said at its New York Summit on Wednesday that it would expand its partnership with Nvidia, using the chipmaker’s Nemotron 3.5 Super VL open model to power its ElevenAgents voice-agent suite. The model helps AI agents understand workflows and human reasoning. New features unveiled for the agents include memory, routines and tools to help users design agents.

This is reported by 
Variety
.

The company is also using Nvidia’s Vera Rubin architecture to provide computing capacity for deploying its agents. ElevenLabs said this capacity would help meet demand from businesses at large scale. The companies cited voice agents used by Centralna e-Rejestracja, an appointment-making platform in Poland’s national online health system, and said they wanted to make more agents available to governments worldwide using Nvidia’s AI architecture. They are also working to bring such agents to robots.

ElevenLabs CEO Mati Staniszewski said in a statement that the expanded collaboration was necessary as “voice agents become critical infrastructure.”

“By combining our audio models and agents, with Nvidia’s LLMs, accelerated computing and security stack, we’re giving enterprises and governments the platform they need to deploy voice agents at massive scale,” Staniszewski said. “Nvidia has been a superb partner to us, and we’re excited to keep building together.”

 - Mati Staniszewski

The companies have partnered since 2022. ElevenLabs used Nvidia GPUs to power its first text-to-speech model, while Nvidia used ElevenLabs to create voiceovers for videos at company events. Nvidia became an ElevenLabs investor last year, marking the investment with a produced video posted to Staniszewski’s LinkedIn page. In September, ElevenLabs completed a $300 million employee tender offer that valued the company at $22 billion, double the $11 billion valuation it reached in February after a funding round.

The partnership expansion comes as Huang is under scrutiny over his affair with a Chinese tech executive who was allegedly recruited by China to spy on him and learn more about Nvidia’s AI technology. Huang is under investigation by Nvidia’s board of directors and the FBI, but will continue to lead the company during the inquiries.

“For decades we adapted to computers - keyboards, screens, menus. Now computers are learning to speak our language,” Huang said in a statement about the ElevenLabs partnership. “With Nvidia Nemotron reasoning inside ElevenAgents and Nvidia accelerated computing powering large-scale deployments, enterprises and governments can run voice agents securely at national scale - and make talking the fastest way to get things done.”

 - Jensen Huang

WILMINGTON, NC (WWAY) — Groups around the Cape Fear region will honor those who served on Veterans Day.

Jessie Tait, Program Director for Access of Wilmington’s Project SOAR, spoke with WWAY’s Jessica Keisling about upcoming events centered around veterans.

Tuesday:

Vets for Vets Comedy Fest

7 p.m.

Dead Crow Comedy Room

511 North 3rd Street in Wilmington

$20 cover with proceeds helping local veterans

Wednesday:

Cape Fear Community College Veterans Day Ceremony

11:11 a.m.

CFCC’s 
Union Station Auditorium (U-170)

502 North Front Street in Wilmington

The Pit Backyard BBQ is offering veterans a free meal on Veterans Day

503 Olde Waterford Way Ste 100 in Leland

Several other Veterans Day events are happening Wednesday.

Thursday:

VA Fayetteville Coastal Resource and Beneifts Fair

9 a.m. – 12 p.m.

Cumberland County VA Clinic

7300 South Raeford Road in Fayetteville

Friday:

Veterans Motorcycle Rally

11:30 a.m.

Veterans Memorial Park of America

6354 James B. White Highway South in Whiteville

Tait also spoke about the opportunities with 
Project SOAR
.

“Project SOAR is actually a division of Access of Wilmington and what we do is we provide adaptive sports for veterans and first responders,” Tait said.

While Project SOAR is mainly funded by VA grants, the program is also looking to partner with other groups and businesses in the community.

Tait said you can keep up to date with local events for veterans by following the newly launched social media pages for 
Serve and Explore on Facebook
 and 
Instagram.

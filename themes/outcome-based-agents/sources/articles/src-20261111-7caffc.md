KK Ibar Rozaje
 (Prva B) inked 21-year old 201cm forward 
Milorad Brajovic
 (college: 
Salt Lake CC
). He already started the season at 
KK Podgorica Bemax
 in the Erste Liga. Erste Liga was a little too high level for him, where in two Erste Liga games he averaged just 1.0ppg and 1.5rpg. Prva B will be definitely a better match and he can see more playing time. He also played 2 games in Erste Liga where he had only 2.5ppg and 1.5rpg.
Brajovic graduated from Salt Lake Community College in 2025 and it is his second season in pro basketball.

From "Peppa Pig" and "Monkey Baby Bon Bon" to "Ask the StoryBots" and "Cocomelon," many popular children's programs feature rapid-fire sounds and visuals.
If the fast pace of these shows stresses you out, imagine what it might be doing to your children.
Early childhood media expert 
Natalia Kucirkova
 argues that choosing the best programs for children isn't just about whether they're educational and age-appropriate. There's another important factor parents should consider: the pace of the media their children are watching.
Kucirkova is a professor at the University of Stavanger in Norway and creator of the 
Children's Slow Media Project
. She joins 
Here & Now
 to discuss what parents should look for.
This segment aired on November 11, 2026.

Guam Governor: Vincente Ada (R)
                    
 

 

November 11, 2026

by TWN Staff

 

Vincente Ada is a member of the Guam Senate, and he did not run for reelection. Before serving in the Guam Senate, he served in the U.S. Army and the U.S. Army Reserve. 

Ada’s career experience includes working as the vice president of Ada’s Mortuary, Inc., an aircraft mechanic with Airport Group, Inc., the director of the Guam Department of Youth Affairs, and a special assistant to the governor.

His humble beginnings as the son of a local businessman and former Guam senator influenced Ada to continue his family’s legacy of service.

Ada is devoted to diversifying Guam’s economy so that working Guamanians can see better wages and more opportunity. “When the next crisis comes, natural or manmade, we must be ready with industries that are not susceptible to massive shutdowns, furloughs and layoffs,” his website says.

Ada defeated Democrat Therese Terlaje in the general election on Nov. 3, 2026.

The above information was sourced from the candidate’s website and Ballotpedia.

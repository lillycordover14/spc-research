Norwich's Full Weekend Guide: The Slater Museum's Artisan Craft Show And Tellabration At Otis Library
The Slater Memorial Museum holds its fourth Artisan Craft Show Saturday, and Otis Library hosts a Tellabration storyteller.
Rich Kirby
, 
Patch Staff
Posted 
Wed, Nov  11, 2026 at 7:32 am ET
NORWICH, CT — The Slater Memorial Museum's fourth annual Artisan Craft Show fills the museum Saturday, and Otis Library hosts storyteller Susie Scheyder for a Tellabration tour of animals around the world.
This Weekend's Arts & Entertainment
 
Artisan Craft Show
, Saturday, Nov. 14, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. The Friends of Slater's fourth annual juried show of artisan vendors. 
Slater Memorial Museum
, 
108 Crescent St.
Tellabration: Animals Around the World
, Saturday, Nov. 14, 11:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Susie Scheyder uses story props to travel the seven continents and show how animals care for their young. 
Otis Library
, 
Norwich
 
Handmade gifts at the Slater and global animal tales at Otis make for a good Saturday in Norwich.

MENLO PARK, Calif., Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Skan AI, the agentic AI platform for regulated enterprises, today announced it has been named a Strong Performer in the Gartner® Peer Insights™ Voice of the Customer for Process Intelligence Platforms. Skan AI received an overall rating of 4.7 out of 5, with 64% of reviews rating Skan AI five stars and 87% of customers willing to recommend the platform, based on 22 reviews as of August 2026.
 
 
 
 
Skan AI, the agentic AI platform Fortune 50 enterprises trust for reimagining work, is a Strong Performer in Gartner Peer Insights Voice of the Customer for Process Intelligence Platforms.
 
 
 
Gartner Peer Insights Voice of the Customer positions vendors into four quadrants defined by two dimensions, User Interest and Adoption and Overall Experience, based on verified customer reviews. To us, Skan AI's position as a Strong Performer reflects high ratings on product and service experiences.
 
 
 
 
 
Reviewers consistently cited three strengths: multi-user process stitching across business and technical workflows, superior service and support, and data security and privacy.
 
 
"The most meaningful recognition comes from our customers and the value they experience firsthand. As enterprises move from understanding how work gets done to deploying AI agents at scale, process and context intelligence become critical to delivering reliable business outcomes. This recognition reinforces our commitment to helping customers succeed through innovation, trusted partnerships, and measurable business value," said Vinay Mummigatti, Chief Customer Officer at Skan AI.
 
 
In the last year, Skan AI has built and launched a complete agentic AI platform around its process capture and analytics capabilities.
 
 
As more enterprises deploy AI, they will increasingly need reliable mechanisms to identify the highest-impact AI opportunities, validate AI-driven automation, and measure the impact of AI transformation. The Skan AI platform is built for exactly that, equipping enterprises to continuously capture and analyze processes end to end while using that context to run, govern, and improve AI agents.

Venezuelan Artisan Bakery Andinos Bakery Preparing to Open in Jacksonville&nbsp;&nbsp;WhatNow

[page text]
Andinos Bakery has submitted plans to open a physical storefront (Image credit: Andinos Bakery Facebook)
 

SHARE

A new bakery is in the works along Beach Boulevard in Jacksonville. 
Andinos Bakery Corp
 has filed plans for a location at 
11757 Beach Blvd
., Suite 3, where the business is preparing to bring its Venezuelan-inspired baked goods to a physical storefront.

Andinos Bakery describes itself as a Venezuelan artisan bakery based in Jacksonville, with a focus on made-to-order products. Its business profile also uses the tagline, ‘More Than Bread, It’s Tradition’, reflecting the cultural roots behind the concept.

The Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation recorded a food-service plan-review filing for Andinos Bakery Corp on November 3, 2026. The application identifies the premises as a seating establishment, indicating plans for a customer-facing bakery rather than a production-only operation.

The new location will be at 11757 Beach Blvd., Suite 3, Jacksonville, FL 32246.

While the business has not yet shared a confirmed opening date, the filing marks an early step toward establishing its storefront in the area.

Andinos Bakery
 has directed customers to its social media and online ordering channels for updates and inquiries as it works toward the new location. More details about its offerings and the opening timeline have yet to be announced.

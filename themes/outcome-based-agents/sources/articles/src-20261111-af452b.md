Crosby Arboretum announces 50% off plant sale

Published 12:00 pm Wednesday, November 11, 2026

By 
Madison Plaisance

 

                                        The Crosby Arboretum’s quarterly plant sales are a great way to introduce the public to using native plants in the home landscape. (Photo by Pat Drackett)                                                                                

Getting your 
Trinity Audio
 player ready...

The Crosby Arboretum is kicking off another plant sale starting today, Wednesday, Nov. 11, through Saturday, Nov. 14. The sale lasts during normal hours of operation, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., with last admission at 4:00 p.m. This includes plants, trees, and azaleas, all marked at 50% off their original price.

Cresta – Contact Center AI, Conversation Automation & Real-Time Agent Guidance
#Quasa #QUA #Cresta

Cresta is an enterprise customer experience platform developed by Cresta Intelligence, Inc., a company founded in 2017. It uses AI to interpret customer conversations, automate interactions, and guide human agents during live calls and digital exchanges. Its tools include AI Agent for customer conversations, Agentic Assist for knowledge retrieval and response guidance, and Conversation Intelligence for analysis, quality management, and coaching. Deputy Agent handles operational workflows beyond the conversation. Cresta connects with existing contact center and customer management systems. Enterprise pricing is customized to deployment scope and requires contacting sales; no public self-service tier is listed. Integration requirements and performance should be assessed against each organization's workflows.

Understanding Adaptive Security as a Cybersecurity Strategy  
Adaptive security represents a proactive cybersecurity method that continuously observes users, devices, network activity, and system behavior to detect and respond to threats dynamically. It relies heavily on automation, artificial intelligence, and real-time risk assessments to prevent, identify, and mitigate attacks more effectively. By continuously adjusting protections based on emerging risks, adaptive security helps organizations close vulnerabilities, enhance defense mechanisms, and maintain smooth business operations.

2029 U.S. Adaptive Open Championship

July 9-11, 2029
CommonGround Golf Course
Home of the CGA

The USGA announced that CommonGround Golf Course in Aurora, Colo., has been selected as the host site for the 2029 U.S. Adaptive Open Championship. CommonGround previously served as the stroke-play co-host course for the 2012 U.S. Amateur and 2019 U.S. Mid-Amateur Championships.

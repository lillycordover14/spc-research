PHOENIX — As the MLB offseason continues, the Arizona Diamondbacks are reportedly keeping tabs on a Japanese standout who could bolster the middle of the lineup and address infield corner issues.

The Diamondbacks are doing their due diligence on Teruaki Sato, per @TheAthleticMLB

Owner Ken Kendrick plans to reduce the team’s payroll, but Arizona often inquires about free agents beyond their price range. https://t.co/WOwmai9CU5

— Just Baseball (@JustBB_Media) 
November 11, 2026

According to The Athletic, the D-Backs are “doing their due diligence” on third baseman Teruaki Sato. While owner Ken Kendrick has confirmed the club will reduce its payroll, the report says Arizona could still explore higher-priced free-agent options if the right opportunity arises.

Sato, a 27-year-old left-handed hitter, is coming off an 
excellent year
 in the Japan Central League with the Hanshin Tigers, batting .306 with 39 home runs, 106 RBIs and a .991 OPS in 143 games. He also posted a .383 on-base percentage and a .609 slugging percentage.

Apr 16, 2024; Phoenix, Arizona, USA; General view of the Arizona Diamondbacks logo on the field prior to the game against the Chicago Cubs at Chase Field. Mandatory Credit: Matt Kartozian-USA TODAY Sports

The infielder also played for Team Japan in the 2026 World Baseball Classic, where he hit .300 with a .417 on-base percentage, .600 slugging percentage and 1.017 OPS in five games.

With that kind of production, Sato could give Arizona another power bat in the middle of its lineup. But beyond his offensive numbers, his potential fit defensively could make him an intriguing option for the Diamondbacks.

Third baseman Nolan Arenado 
reportedly
 approached the Diamondbacks about moving away from his primary position to play first base, citing added positional flexibility and the ability to keep him in the lineup. If the Snakes take that approach, it would leave Sato squarely in line for hot corner duties while giving Arizona another established option at first base.

May 10, 2026; Phoenix, Arizona, USA; Arizona Diamondbacks third baseman Nolan Arenado in the second inning against the New York Mets at Chase Field. Mandatory Credit: Mark J. Rebilas-Imagn Images

The D-Backs have several priorities heading into the season, including improving their production against right-handed pitching and finding consistency at first base. The club struggled with a revolving door of players at the position, making it difficult to establish a reliable option throughout the season.

Sato could help address both needs. Not only would he provide another power threat in the lineup, but his defensive ability could also give Arizona some much-needed stability around the infield.

Baseball fans head to Chase Field for the Diamondbacks home opener against the Detroit Tigers on March 30, 2026. © Joe Rondone/The Republic / USA TODAY NETWORK via Imagn Images

If Arizona is serious about adding another impact bat while improving its infield flexibility, the Japanese standout could make plenty of sense as a potential addition.

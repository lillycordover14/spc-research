AI Brings Adaptive Motion Control to Earthmoving
 
 
What if excavators could do more than simply scoop and dump? Researchers have developed a reinforcement learning-based control system that enables excavators to perform more complex soil manipulation, adapting shovel movements to different material conditions. Remarkably, the same learned controller transfers between a full-size excavator and a tabletop robot without retraining. In field trials, the autonomous system constructed a 42-meter embankment in just 45 minutes, demonstrating the potential of adaptive motion control for heavy machinery.

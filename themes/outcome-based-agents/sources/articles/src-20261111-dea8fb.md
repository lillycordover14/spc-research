Farm-inspired polymer clay ornaments to be offered at Novinger craft festival

Published 9:00 am Wednesday, November 11, 2026

By Novinger Renewal, Inc.

 

                                        novinger_teenie_fb_111126.jpg                                         (Courtesy Novinger Renewal, Inc.)                                        

Getting your

 player ready...

Teenie Creations will again offer a wide variety of polymer clay ornaments, including a collection of farm-inspired ornaments, at the 40th Country Christmas Craft Festival in Novinger on Saturday, Nov. 21, according to Novinger Renewal, Inc.

The ornaments will be sold in the upper elementary hallway across from the elementary gym at Novinger School. Teenie Creations has a Facebook page with more of its offerings.

The festival will run from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Novinger School on Highway 149, one-third mile north of Highway 6. More than 75 booths of handcrafted and repurposed items will be featured. Admission is by donation, and a free drawing will be held every half-hour. A bake sale featuring homemade candy and a food stand will help raise money for Novinger Renewal.

The festival is part of Christmas in Novinger, which also includes the Holiday Bazaar at the Novinger Community Center and Shopper’s Delight at the Novinger Fire House. For information, call 660-342-6455.

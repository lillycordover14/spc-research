ChatGPT launches Businesses in beta, company profiles and Business Agent for enterprises&nbsp;&nbsp;Meteora Web

[page text]
OpenAI has launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, a set of tools that lets companies claim a profile, update their details and talk with users through a dedicated agent. For now the debut covers ecommerce and online services in the United States. It matters because ChatGPT, which according to OpenAI is used by 1.2 billion people a week, is trying to become a place where businesses are discovered and compared, much like Google.

A few terms before we get into the facts. 
Business Hub
 is the panel where a company claims its profile and sees its statistics. 
Business Agent
 is the assistant that answers users using the company's information. 
Product feed
 is a list of products with prices and availability that the company uploads itself. 
Beta
 indicates a testing phase, with features still incomplete. 
Shopify Catalog
 is the catalog from which products of Shopify stores already enter ChatGPT.

What are Business Hub and Business Agent?
According to 
OpenAI's official presentation
, Business Hub is the starting point. Here a company claims its organization and, after verification, updates the main information and adds product feeds with prices and availability.

The Business Agent comes next. Eligible organizations can create it, test it and launch it. When a business appears in a model response, the user can open the profile and, if there is an agent, start a conversation to ask follow-up questions, for example about the differences between two products.

What data do companies see?
Business Hub offers aggregated statistics. OpenAI cites the number of mentions and clicks to the website, plus discovery topics, meaning the recurring themes in conversations where the site is cited. 
TechCrunch
 adds profile views and compares the tool to Google Analytics.

On privacy, OpenAI states that it does not share with companies either users' conversations or chats with Business Agents. Data appears only when conversations reach certain thresholds.

Who can use them and how is the profile verified?
Every eligible business, located where ChatGPT is available, can claim its profile. Enterprise customers and existing advertisers go through their OpenAI contact. Shopify merchants can connect their store from Business Hub or from the admin area to simplify verification.

The verification process remains vague. Benji Shomair, OpenAI's vice president of Monetization, told journalists that there are "multiple verification methods" used for different types of businesses and markets. Business Agents, instead, are available only for ecommerce and online services in the United States.

What about ads and the failed precedent?
Companies can sponsor their own Business Agent and use it as a destination for ChatGPT Ads. OpenAI specifies that sponsored content is labeled and separated from ChatGPT's responses, just as the conversation with the agent is distinct from the chatbot's independent answers.

TechCrunch recalls that OpenAI had already tried to turn ChatGPT into a shopping hub, with commissions and a cart. The project did not take off and was withdrawn in March. We already covered the weight of ChatGPT as a search engine in 
ChatGPT accounts for 20% of Google's search volume
.

Limits to know
OpenAI does not explain the verification process in detail, nor the minimum thresholds for seeing statistics. There are no dates for arrival in Europe or Italy, only the intention to expand to more markets and categories. It is unclear how much the sponsored part will cost or how much traffic profiles will really bring. The criteria ChatGPT uses to choose which businesses to mention also remain undisclosed.

What changes for those working in digital
For now in Italy nothing changes operationally, but it is worth preparing. If a customer asks ChatGPT which supplier to choose, having correct information, updated prices and a well-organized website will matter. Those who sell online should take care of their product data, as explained in our guide to 
custom online shops
. Those already using automated assistants can compare the idea with 
low-cost chatbots for customer support
.

Our take
The idea is logical, but it shifts the balance of power. Companies will have to manage their data inside a platform owned by someone else, which decides how to show it and when to place it alongside advertising messages. It is the pattern already seen with search engines. The declared separation between answers, agents and ads is a good principle, but it will need to be verified in practice. The precedent of the withdrawn checkout advises caution and no rush to be first.

Frequently asked questions
Is Businesses in ChatGPT available in Italy?
There is no confirmation for Italy. Business Hub is in beta and can be claimed by every eligible business where ChatGPT is available. Business Agents, instead, start only in the United States.

What is a Business Agent?
It is an assistant that answers users' questions using the company's information. The conversation is labeled and kept separate from the original chat with ChatGPT.

Do companies see users' conversations?
No, according to OpenAI. Companies only receive aggregated data, such as mentions and clicks to their website, and only once certain thresholds are reached.

How is a profile verified?
The details are not public. OpenAI mentions several methods, which differ by type of business and market. Shopify stores can connect from Business Hub or from the admin area.

Can agents be sponsored?
Yes. Companies can sponsor their own Business Agent and use it as a destination for ChatGPT Ads. According to OpenAI, sponsored content is labeled and separated from the answers.

What to watch after the American debut of Businesses in ChatGPT
The beta will show whether profiles bring real customers. Useful signals will come from geographic expansion and the costs of the paid part.

Check OpenAI's announcements about opening Business Hub and Business Agent to new markets and categories.
Ask ChatGPT how it currently presents your business and note any errors or omissions.
Follow the data on mentions and clicks from early users to understand whether the channel is worth an investment.

PLYMOUTH, LUZERNE CO. (WOLF) — 
A 39-year-old Sugar Notch man who was reported missing after a crash earlier this month was found dead in the Susquehanna River on Tuesday, Pennsylvania State Police said.
Keenan Saniatan was found in the river in Plymouth Township, Luzerne County. State police said he had fled the scene of a crash near Route 11 on Nov. 1 and was reported missing by family members several days later.
The Nanticoke City and Hanover Township fire departments assisted in the search.
The investigation is ongoing. State police said additional information will be released as it becomes available.

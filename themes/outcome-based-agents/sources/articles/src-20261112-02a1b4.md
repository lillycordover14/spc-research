BRAINERD — A 22-year-old Crosby man was arrested and charged after allegedly having a sexual relationship with a 15-year-old child.
Sven Berndt Gustafson was charged Nov. 6 in Crow Wing County District Court with felony third-degree criminal sexual conduct.

Sven Berndt Gustafson.

Gustafson was granted a public defender and was released on $0 bail/bond with conditions, which require him to remain law-abiding, make all future court appointments and not use or possess any drugs or alcohol.
Gustafson is due back in Crow Wing County District Court on Nov. 17.
Criminal complaint
According to court documents, at 1:17 a.m. on Nov. 5 law enforcement was dispatched to the city of Emily for a report of a child stating their mother had pointed a gun at their chest.
When deputies arrived and made contact, the child was with Gustafson. Deputies interviewed Gustafson, the child, and the child's mother, and eventually placed the child in a foster home.

Later on Nov. 5, Crow Wing County Social Services contacted the sheriff's office to report the child was now at the community services building and the child told a social worker they lied about their mom pointing a gun at them. The child wanted to make law enforcement aware it was a false accusation.
Investigators then conducted a follow-up interview with the child. In the interview, the child stated they wanted to be with Gustafson, and when their mother told them they could not be with him, the child made up an accusation against their mother.

ADVERTISEMENT

The child reported they first met Gustafson when they were 13 and he was 19. They said in October of 2026 they started hanging out with Gustafson. The child reported that in mid-October, Gustafson had sexual relations with the child.
The child said the incident occurred in Wolford Township. In addition, the child reported Gustafson and the child would send each other nude photos. At the time of the sexual relations, the child was 15 years old and Gustafson was 22 years old.

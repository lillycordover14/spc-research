Summary
Meta shares rose 29% after the company launched a new AI product called Muse, which is designed to make agentic AI capabilities accessible to ordinary consumers.
IQVIA shares were up 39% as industry demand trends improved in drug development and the notion that the company was AI roadkill lost a bit of steam.
Universal Music Group shares declined 22% due to a disappointing earnings report.
Samsung shares declined 20% in local currency terms but only 8% in USD. The share price simply retraced a bit of the meteoric 178% rise in the first half of the year.

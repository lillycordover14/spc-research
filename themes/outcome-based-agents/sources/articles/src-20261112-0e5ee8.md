Why do shoppers love it?
It's not made for frigid temperatures, but it is designed to keep you warm and protect you from moisture when the temperatures get noticeably cooler. Made from 93% recycled polyester and elastane, the jacket has a stretchy fleece fabric that moves with your body and doesn't feel restrictive. The fabric has a water-repellent finish to keep moisture from saturating and permeating it, leaving you nice and dry. Available in two colors, the jacket has a standard but flattering fit, and it falls right along the hips on most figures.

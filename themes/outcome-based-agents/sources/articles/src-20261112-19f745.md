SOUTH BEND, Ind. (WNDU) - The Urban Artisan Market is being held Sunday, Nov. 15, at the Pottawatomie Conservatories in South Bend. The November date is new this year.
Michelle Fitzgerald, one of the organizers, said the market has been held at the conservatories for about eight years. She said organizers added a November date so people could start their holiday shopping earlier.
Market dates
The market will be held Sunday, Nov. 15, and again Sunday, Dec. 13, at the conservatories. Fitzgerald said a Small Business Saturday Market will also be held between those dates at her studio.
About 30 artists will sell handmade items at the market, Fitzgerald said.
Items for sale
Fitzgerald said Jessie, who goes by Paper Monger, will sell paper quilt art. She said the style uses thin strips of paper that are twisted and rolled into shapes. Items include earrings, wall art and cards.
Make South Bend will have a booth, Fitzgerald said. She said the items she brought were made at the studio. They include a line of South Bend-themed candles. Fitzgerald said one candle shows the river and the South Bend skyline and smells fresh. Other candles are themed for the conservatory, Erasmus Books and Bendix Woods, which she said is pine scented.
Fitzgerald said the booth will also have stained glass and fused glass ornaments and night lights. She said she made designs last year featuring the conservatory’s desert dome. They appear on a tote bag, a mug and a postcard.
Admission and what to expect
Admission to the Urban Artisan Market this year is $3. It is $2 for seniors, veterans and students. Children 14 and under are free.
Fitzgerald said the conservatory is covered in tropical plants and has a coffee vendor.
“It’s cozy,” Fitzgerald said. “It’s covered in tropical plants.”
She said shoppers can also talk with the artists and hear the story behind each item.

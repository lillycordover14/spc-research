Ryan Reynolds
 and 
Hugh Jackman
 may have officially found their way into 
Marvel Studios'
 next Avengers crossover. Both actors have received official writing credits on 
Avengers: Doomsday
, adding to the growing belief that 
Deadpool
 and 
Wolverine
 will appear in the upcoming film.
The updated 
Writers Guild of America
 (WGA) listing names Reynolds and Jackman under "Additional Literary Material." The credit appears alongside several other writers, including Stephen McFeely and Michael Waldron, who have the main writing credit.
Avengers: Doomsday continues to take shape
The listing shows that Reynolds and Jackman contributed writing material to the movie. However, this credit does not confirm that 
either actor will appear on screen
. The WGA uses this credit for writers who contribute material to a project but do not receive the main writing credit.

Still, the update adds significant weight to the duo's earlier public hints about joining the crossover. Reynolds previously appeared at San Diego Comic-Con in July wearing a gray Deadpool costume, jokingly pitching Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige for a role in the movie.
Jackman followed with his own pitch at the D23 Expo in August. He asked to join the film and help complete its already large cast, while Reynolds encouraged him from off camera. The two appearances appeared to be jokes, especially after they were told the movie had already been filmed.
Avengers: Doomsday already has a large cast. Robert Downey Jr. returns to Marvel as Doctor Doom, while Chris Evans, Chris Hemsworth, Pedro Pascal and Anthony Mackie are among the other major names. Several actors from the original Fox X-Men films are also returning, including Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen and James Marsden. Hayley Atwell, Kathryn Newton, Mabel Cadena and Alex Livinalli have also joined the cast.
Joe and Anthony Russo are directing the film, with McFeely and Waldron credited as writers. The story brings heroes from three different universes together against a major threat.

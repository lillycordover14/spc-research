Where AI ambition meets operating reality

What Leaders do differently

Leaders are the top 17% of enterprises in Genpact’s AI Maturity Index. Backed by a CEO-level mandate, they connect AI adoption with how work, technology, governance, and financial accountability operate together, building the foundations for Agentic Operations.

Redesign work across the enterprise

51% of Leaders pursue proactive, enterprise-wide operating model transformation for agentic AI, compared with 17% of other enterprises.

Build roles for human-agent work

51% create new roles specifically for human-agent operations, compared with 22% of other enterprises.

Give agents the access to act

87% provide agents with timely read and write access across the systems their workflows require, compared with 54% of other enterprises.

Govern agents across their lifecycle

47% have governance embedded across the full agent lifecycle, compared with 11% of other enterprises.

Connect AI value to the P&L

49% fully integrate AI value metrics with specific P&L lines, compared with 7% of other enterprises.

What agentic operations means in practice

Agentic operations brings people, AI agents, processes, data and technology, governance, and value capture together in one operating model. It moves enterprises beyond deploying AI for individual tasks and toward redesigning how work gets done across the enterprise.

See agentic operations at work

Redesigning accounts payable

A global automotive supplier redesigned accounts payable around people and agents, increasing straight-through processing from 27% to 71%, with approximately 98% capture accuracy.

Speeding up risk inspection reviews

A specialty insurance brokerage introduced agentic operations to inspection reviews, reducing handle time by 63% and increasing review accuracy to approximately 95%.

Where should enterprises start with agentic AI?

There is no single path to maturity. Our research points to a practical progression, based on where enterprises are today and what Leaders do differently.

 

Prove it

Start with one process with significant manual work and clear potential for value. Establish the baseline, define human and agent roles, and assign executive and financial ownership before agents go live. Define the business outcome and address data and integration gaps that could limit results.

 

Scale it

Expand across processes. Build reusable data and technology foundations, develop human-agent roles, and evolve governance into controls that work while agents run. Define value metrics and decide where freed capacity will go.

 

Run it

Move from function-by-function deployment to enterprise-wide transformation. Align business and technology around a CEO-level mandate, redesign work across functions, govern agents throughout their lifecycle, and connect AI-enabled gains to P&L outcomes and business planning.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

What is agentic operations?

Agentic operations is an operating model that brings together process intelligence, people and AI agents, data and AI, governance and security, and value capture to help turn AI progress into measurable business value.

What is autonomy in practice?

Autonomy in practice means deliberately deciding where AI agents act, where people decide or validate, and how people and agents work together to produce measurable business outcomes. It does not mean maximizing AI autonomy everywhere.

What is the autonomy dividend?

The autonomy dividend is the enterprise value captured when AI-enabled gains translate into better business performance, not simply greater productivity.

Why aren't enterprises realizing more value from AI agents?

AI deployment is advancing faster than enterprises' ability to capture its value. Genpact's research finds that 79% of respondents report AI agents live in production, yet respondents say that, on average, only 49% of AI initiatives funded in the past 24 months have delivered the business value originally expected, and just 15% fully connect AI value metrics to specific P&L lines. The challenge is increasingly operational: redesigning work, redeploying capacity, establishing ownership, and connecting AI-enabled gains to measurable business outcomes.

How can enterprises capture more value from agentic AI?

Enterprises can capture more value from agentic AI by moving from deploying individual agents to redesigning how work operates across the enterprise. Genpact's research identifies five areas to address together: redesign processes end to end, build roles for human-agent work, create a scalable data and AI foundation, govern agents throughout their lifecycle, and connect AI-enabled gains to accountable P&L outcomes.

Do AI agents replace people?

Not necessarily. Among respondents with AI agents in live production, 69% report increasing their agent population over the past year, while 92% have maintained or increased human headcount. The bigger opportunity is to redesign the work people do alongside agents and deliberately redeploy AI-enabled capacity toward higher-value work and business outcomes.

How was the Autonomy in Practice research conducted?

Genpact surveyed 2,005 senior enterprise decision makers across 17 industries and 14 primary business functions at companies with annual revenues from $1 billion to $50 billion+. The quantitative research was supplemented by in-depth interviews with enterprise and technology leaders.

Does Salesforce Look Undervalued on Earnings?
 
The P/E ratio fits Salesforce because earnings now play a bigger role in how investors judge mature software platforms that still invest heavily in new products and AI. At roughly 18.6x earnings, Salesforce trades at a clear discount to the wider Software industry average of 31.2x and an even larger gap to the peer group around 45.1x. For a reader, that means the market is currently putting a lower price on each dollar of Salesforce profit than it does on many comparable software stocks.
 
The fair value model suggests a P/E multiple that already adjusts for Salesforce's profitability profile, scale, sector and risk, and the present 18.6x level sits below that implied figure. Despite the recent Cresta acquisition headlines and the credit rating downgrade linked to higher planned leverage, the earnings multiple still prices Salesforce at a discount to both the industry and its direct peer set, which points to sentiment that has not fully followed the AI narrative.

Nebius Revenue Explodes Nearly Sixfold in Third Quarter
Less than 1 min read
NBIS
Nebius Group 
NBIS
 saw revenue surge nearly sixfold, a 571% increase in the third quarter, reaching $980.8 million and beating Wall Street expectations. The company also issued an ambitious 2027 revenue forecast of $12 billion to $13 billion, with the midpoint slightly above analyst estimates.
The results highlight the rapid expansion of Nebius' AI infrastructure business, although some revenue initially expected in 2026 is now projected to arrive in early 2027. That shift puts this year's revenue outlook near the lower end of its previous $3 billion to $3.4 billion forecast.
Nebius reported a GAAP loss of 13 cents per share for the quarter.
The company expects capital expenditures to climb to $40 billion to $45 billion in 2027, compared with approximately $27 billion in 2026. Customer prepayments and financing arrangements backed by investment-grade clients are expected to cover 75% to 85% of next year's spending.
Prepayments from third-quarter agreements already exceeded the related costs for data center construction and networking.
Chief Executive Arkady Volozh said demand remains strong and indicated the company could raise additional outside capital when financing conditions are favorable.

New Zealand Bloodstock Tabs McMahon, Hill&nbsp;&nbsp;BloodHorse

[page text]
Auction house New Zealand Bloodstock has appointed Mike McMahon and Jamie Hill as American-based sale representatives.

The bloodstock-duo are actively managing Bourbon Lane, an American-owned Thoroughbred racing partnership established in 2010, alongside major investor Dennis Foster. Following the recent appointment, NZB bloodstock sales manager Kane Jones is looking forward to them joining the team while also acknowledging Joe Miller's service in the jurisdiction.

"Firstly, I'd like to thank Joe Miller for his hard work and contribution over the past several years,' Jones said. "We wish Joe and his family all the very best going forward as he takes on more local commitments."

Bourbon Lane made its New Zealand appearance in 2019, and has purchased such graduates as Bourbonaire, Bourbon Empress, Glamour Tycoon, Super Photon, Kiwi Skyhawk, Tale of the Gypsy, and more than 55 New Zealand winners calculated up to the end of 2025.

Photo: Jetta Vaughns
Jamie Hill

"We are absolutely thrilled to welcome Mike and Jamie to the NZB team," Jones said. "They are already wonderful supporters of Karaka and the New Zealand racing industry, through their Bourbon Lane partnerships.

"With their enthusiasm and passion for New Zealand, they were the obvious choice to represent us in North America. I have no doubt they'll prove a real asset and further strengthen our international representation."

McMahon and Hill are looking forward to canvassing ahead of travelling back to New Zealand this January.

"Our first visit to Karaka was in January of 2019 where we quickly discovered the intrinsic quality and exceptional value of the New Zealand Thoroughbred," McMahon said. "Since that initial visit we have consistently acquired horses to compete for Bourbon Lane Stable.

"The enduring relationships we have cultivated with NZB, the vendors and farms, and the racing community hold immeasurable value. The caliber of the horses, the expertise of the horsemanship, and the quality of the nurseries will undoubtedly keep us, and others, coming back for years to come.

"We are honored to assume the role of U.S.-based representatives for the NZB team."

Karaka 2027 will take place Jan. 24-28 at the Karaka Sales Centre, the iconic week on the international sales calendar marking a new era as the 101st National Yearling Sale in New Zealand. The catalogs for Karaka 2027 will be available online and in letterboxes in November.

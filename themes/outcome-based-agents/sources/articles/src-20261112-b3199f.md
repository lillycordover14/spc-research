Nebius revenue jumps nearly sixfold as CEO says AI demand remains strong
1 min read
NBIS
Nebius 
NBIS
 reported a sharp surge in third-quarter revenue and forecast 2027 sales above Wall Street estimates, but its shares reversed earlier gains of 2% to fall 8% in premarket trading.
"Demand remains exceptionally strong, and we have the right mechanisms in place to capture it," said CEO Arkady Volozh.
Group revenue rose 571.3% year over year to $980.8M, beating analysts' estimates of $907.17M. The company reported a GAAP loss of 13 cents per share.
Nebius expects 2027 revenue of $12B to $13B, compared with the analyst consensus of $12.28B, and anticipates capital expenditure of $40B to $45B.
Nebius said a portion of revenue initially expected this year would now be recognized in early 2027, pushing its 2026 revenue outlook to the low end of its previously guided range of $3B to $3.4B.
"Our focus remains on building a healthy, profitable business for the long term. We will continue to invest across the business – in our capacity and infrastructure build-out, in our platform and products, and in the team that takes this to market. What this gives us is the ability to command premium prices and terms, combined with lower costs of our infrastructure as a result of designing and building it ourselves, which in turn results in higher margins, said Volozh.
The company said prepayments and financing secured against existing contracts with investment-grade customers are expected to cover 75% to 85% of its 2027 capital expenditure.
Prepayments received under third-quarter agreements covered more than 100% of the associated capital expenditure, including data center construction and networking. For 2026, the company expects capital expenditure of about $27B.
"We will remain opportunistic around raising additional external capital when it is cost-effective to do so," CEO added.

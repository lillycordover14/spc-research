Ornery and Serval reunite on ‘Axioma’

Out now via Daydreaming.

Photo credit: 
Ornery & Serval – Official

Ornery
 and  
Serval
 have reunited for 
‘Axioma’
, a five-track EP released through 
Daydreaming
.

The release brings together progressive and techno elements, balancing melodic passages with darker arrangements. The title track opens with a melodic hook and driving rhythm, appearing in both extended and instrumental versions.

‘Higher’
 continues with an energetic melodic direction, while 
‘Remembrance’
 and 
‘The Why’
 explore more introspective territory. Together, the five tracks present the latest collaboration between 
Daydreaming
 founder 
Ornery
 and 
Serval
.

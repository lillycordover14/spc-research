        Freeman Flyers notch another 50-point game en route to 9A Championship title
    

SDPB | 
                    By
                    
Jackson Dircks

        Published November 12, 2026 at 4:56 PM CST
    

Josh Jurgens

/

 SDPB
Freeman's Tayden Kerrigan (7) prepares to throw downfield in the Class 9A championships

The Freeman Flyers completed a dominant undefeated season by showcasing their offensive and defensive prowess.
Freeman defeated Bennett County 57-12 to be named Class 9A state champions. It’s the first standalone championship for Freeman in 27 years.
Bennett County’s only two losses on the season came from state champions: Wall, a team that won the 9B Championship earlier Thursday, and now Freeman.
The Freeman Flyers had a huge first half offensively, putting up six touchdowns and over 350 yards.
Flyers Coach Dustin Tschetter called it another great effort from the team.
“It’s been like that all year. We got to 50 again today, so all but the first game of the year,” Tschetter said. “Obviously we’ve got something to strive for moving forward but a dominant performance once again, and I couldn’t be prouder for these boys.”
Freeman’s day began with a 53-yard pass, which led into a 9-yard touchdown carry for Axle Schultz. Freeman quickly got the ball back after Logan Pankratz snagged an interception.
Quarterback Tayden Kerrigan added another explosive pass on the day, launching a 48-yard touchdown to Brady Sorensen.
But, after letting up two early touchdowns, the Bennett County defense responded forcing a turnover on downs on Freeman’s third drive in Bennett County territory. Though, the Bennett County offense couldn’t muster much aside from a nice one-handed catch by Rush O’Neill. Bennett County’s turnover on downs paved the way for Kerrigan to add another passing touchdown on the day, this time a 37-yard throw to Easton Tschetter.
The Flyers defense also added two sacks to its strong start and held Bennett County to -10 rush yards.
END OF FIRST QUARTER; [Flyers 22 - Warriors 0]
Freeman continued its dominance in the second quarter. Kerrigan added another touchdown pass, his third of the day, to Oliver Waltner with about five and half minutes left in the second quarter.
The Warriors were able to get on the board with 3:35 left in the half. Maddux Risse tossed a 56-yard touchdown to Rush O’Neill who ran past two Freeman defenders up the right sideline to score Bennett County’s first points of the day. The Warriors squibbed the ensuing kick, but the Flyers recovered it and got good starting field position on their own 44-yard line.
The Flyers offense took advantage of it, as Tschetter added his second touchdown of the day, punching it in with his legs and extending the lead 34-6 with less than a minute in the half.
Kerrigan’s impressive first half carried over to defense–picking off the Warriors QB and nearly returning it for a touchdown. It set up a Kerrigan run for six more points and Tschetter two-point conversion.
Bennett County was able to improve its rushing performance to close out the half, as Ryder Byerley netted 23 yards and averaged 4.6 yards per carry. For Freeman, Schultz and Tschetter both averaged over 7 yards per carry on the half.
Tschetter finished the half over 108 yards receiving. In all, Kerrigan threw for 262 yards in the first half.
END OF SECOND HALF; [Flyers 42 - Warriors 6]
The third quarter sped up as the game had a rolling clock. Bennett County’s first drive of the half began with multiple throws from M. Risse to O’Neill. The drive ate up the clock and ended with a turnover on downs.
That was Bennett County’s only drive of the quarter, pushing O’Neill’s receiving yards on the day over 100.
Axle Schultz picked up a chunk play, rushing for 36 yards and getting the Flyers offense within striking distance of a touchdown. Alaric Knittel closed the half out on a 6-yard touchdown rush. It was followed by a successful PAT by Alexis Guzeman.

END OF THIRD QUARTER; [Flyers 49 - Warriors 6]
Freeman started off the fourth quarter kicking off to Bennett County, but it was quickly punted back to Freeman with a little over 9 minutes left in the game.
Schultz continued his successful day on the ground, adding 21-yards in the first Flyers play in the quarter. Tayden Kerrigan added a rush of his own, then followed it throwing his fourth touchdown of the day to Tannen Auch.
FINAL [Flyers 57 - Warriors 12]
Hayden Risse exploded for a late-game touchdown in the fourth quarter, running 68-yards up the right sideline and notching the second and last Warriors touchdown of the day.
The Flyers finished the day with over 500 yards of total offense to the Warriors 253.
Bennett County’s O’Neill led the contest with 112 receiving yards and 8 receptions.
Freeman’s Kerrigan led passing with 273 yards and accounted for 5 touchdowns. Schultz rushed for 125 yards on 11 attempts, averaging over 11 yards per carry.
Coach Tschetter said they’ve been working at getting this win for a long time.
“These seniors, juniors I’ve been with since they were in preschool probably,” Tschetter said.
Kerrigan was named Joe Robbie MVP, though he credited his “weapons” and also pointed to winning as a standalone team.
“It’s awesome to play just as Freeman. You hear all the stories about the old Flyers teams,” Kerrigan said. “And then the community is always so supportive about the Flyers. We love playing for them.”

AI startup Serval is offering luxury vacations and referral bonuses to recruit engineers&nbsp;&nbsp;Quartz

[page text]
Serval 
is offering
 new engineering hires a paid luxury vacation worth up to $10,000, along with $30,000 referral bonuses, as the AI startup competes for technical talent.
The company 
detailed the vacation program
 in a blog post. New hires plan the trip one-on-one with a travel concierge through Serval's partner, Luxury Concierge Travel, and the first week on the job is paid, the company said. Serval's careers page says new hires can take the trip alone or bring a loved one.
The vacation applies to people who join the core engineering team or work as deployed software engineers, deployment strategists, deployment success engineers or automation engineers, according to the company.
Under the referral program, Serval pays $30,000 to an employee who refers an engineer who joins, and $30,000 to the new hire, the company said. The referral bonus comes on top of the vacation. Both perks are limited to engineering roles. The offer is open to U.S.-based applicants and is valid through Jan. 4, 2027.
Serval said it raised a $150 million Series C led by Sequoia at a $3 billion valuation. The company said it has grown its team tenfold and plans to hire more than 500 people over the next 12 months.
Serval launched a hiring push this fall aimed at bringing on 100 engineers, according to 
The Wall Street Journal
. Carlos Lezama, Serval's head of talent, told The Wall Street Journal the company needs software engineers and deployment strategists. "Anthropic and OpenAI are taking a lot of talent," Lezama said.
The company said in its blog post that it wants new engineers to start work rested and that the vacation signals its hiring approach. It said the engineers it wants have many options.

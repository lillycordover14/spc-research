Henry Clay Musician to March in Macy’s Parade

Nov
 
12
 
2026

A junior at Henry Clay High School, trumpeter Cole Kiser will be part of the 2026 Macy’s Great American Marching Band in New York City’s iconic Thanksgiving Day parade. He was chosen from a nationwide pool of young musicians who submitted written applications and audition videos. 

At Henry Clay, Cole is a three-year member of the school’s marching band, advanced wind ensemble, advanced jazz band, and steel band. Fun fact: Eleven months ago on New Year’s Day, Cole marched in the Rose Parade in Pasadena, California, as a member of the Bands of America Honor Band. 

Tune in to NBC at 8:30 a.m. Nov. 26, Thanksgiving morning, to watch for Cole and the Macy’s Great American Marching Band. Nearly 200 musicians, along with 40 color guard members and dancers, will step off from Central Park and pause to perform at Macy’s Herald Square.

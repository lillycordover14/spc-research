, and AI Leaders are tackling it with Agentic Operations
 
 
NEW YORK, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Genpact (NYSE: G), the Agentic Operations company, today released a research report, 
Autonomy in Practice: How agentic operations can turn AI progress into business value
, based on a survey of more than 2,000 enterprise executives across 17 industries. It finds that AI agents have reached production faster than enterprises have redesigned how they operate, and most are not yet capturing the autonomy dividend: the value that comes when AI-enabled gains translate into better business performance, not simply greater productivity.
 
 
 
 
Genpact, the Agentic Operations company, released a research report, Autonomy in Practice: How agentic operations can turn AI progress into business value, based on a survey of more than 2,000 enterprise executives across 17 industries,
 
 
 
Key findings from Genpact's 
Autonomy in Practice
 report:
 
 
 
79%
 of enterprises surveyed have AI agents in production, but just 
20%
 use them broadly and consistently across functions.
 
 
 
Only
 15%
 have fully tied AI investment to P&L outcomes.
 
 
 
On average, just 
49%
 of AI initiatives funded in the past 24 months have delivered the value expected.
 
 
 
Only 
9%
 have completed significant operating model transformation.
 
 
 
AI now leads or fully runs 
40%
 of decisions, more than double a year ago, yet humans still decide or audit in at least 
87%
 of cases at every decision stage.
 
 
 
Agents are scaling alongside people: 
92%
 of respondents with agents in production kept their human workforce the same size or grew it.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The findings show that autonomy in practice is not full autonomy. It is deciding deliberately where agents act, where people decide and validate, and what business outcomes the hybrid workforce produces.
 
 
AI Leaders, the 
17%
 of enterprises at the top of Genpact's AI Maturity Index, are further along in building Agentic Operations: the operating model that runs people and agents as one workforce. Genpact's research measured five building blocks critical to this model, but most enterprises have not yet connected them.
 
 
What AI Leaders do differently
 
 
Backed by a CEO-level mandate, AI Leaders are ahead on all five building blocks. They:
 
 
 
Reinvent work across the enterprise
, not function by function.
 
 
 
Build the human-agent workforce deliberately, 
creating new roles such as exception specialists and agent trainers.
 
 
 
Strengthen the data and AI foundation for repeatable scale, 
giving agents access to act across systems, not just retrieve information.
 
 
 
Govern and secure 
agents throughout their lifecycle, not just at deployment.
 
 
 
Make value capture a P&L discipline, 
tying AI-enabled gains to named owners and P&L lines.

'Holiday Edition' Artisan Night Market Returns To Fremont Friday
Organizers described the event as a "creative, community-focused evening." 
Fernando Haro
, 
Patch Staff
Posted 
Thu, Nov  12, 2026 at 8:04 am PT
FREMONT, CA — Looking for a holiday gift? The upcoming 
Age Well Center at Lake Elizabeth Artisan Night Market Holiday Edition
 may have just the perfect item. 
The artisan market, "a creative, community-focused evening" returns to Fremont on Friday, and features unique local handmade goods, inlcuding artwork and sweet treats. 
 
"Explore unique artisan displays while taking in music on a cozy winter evening beside scenic Lake Elizabeth in Fremont’s Central Park," organizers said.

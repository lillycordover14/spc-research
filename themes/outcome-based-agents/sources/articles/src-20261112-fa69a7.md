Spurs' Dylan Harper: Posts season-high four steals in win&nbsp;&nbsp;CBS Sports

[page text]
Harper
 recorded 18 points (7-18 FG, 2-4 3Pt, 2-2 FT), 10 assists, six rebounds, four steals and one block over 36 minutes in Wednesday's 124-119 win over the Bulls.
This was Harper's first career regular-season double-double, and he did it without committing a single turnover. He also set a season high in swipes and posted a plus-19 differential in the win. The 
Spurs
 have now won 10 straight, and they'll look to make it 11 in a row Friday against the Warriors.

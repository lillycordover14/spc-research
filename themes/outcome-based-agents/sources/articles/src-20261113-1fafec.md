SEGUIN — Harper High School’s football team traveled to Matador Stadium Thursday night and beat Burton 27-7 in the UIL 2A Division II bi-district playoffs, marking the Longhorns' first playoff win since 2009.
The Longhorns (10-1) overcame a slow start and a 7-0 halftime deficit, scoring 27 unanswered points in the second half while shutting out the Panthers (6-4) over the final two quarters. Harper's defense also forced two second-half turnovers to help secure the postseason win.

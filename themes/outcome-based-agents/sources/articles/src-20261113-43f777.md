CLAY COUNTY — As the Minnesota Senate District 4 race heads toward a recount, Clay County election officials are reporting 26 ballots arrived after the deadline.
The race between Democratic incumbent Rob Kupec and Republican Austin Peterson was separated by just four votes in the unofficial results.

ADVERTISEMENT

The 26 late-arriving ballots will not be counted.
The State Canvassing Board is scheduled to meet Nov. 19, after which the losing candidate can request a recount.
Kupec has said he plans to ask for a recount. 
Both Becker and Clay counties plan on doing their recounts the week of Thanksgiving.

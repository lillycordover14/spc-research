Artisan U.S. Small-Cap Growth Strategy stated the following regarding Birkenstock Holding plc (NYSE:BIRK) in its Q3 2026 investor letter:
 
 
 
"We ended our investment campaigns in Ionis, ServiceTitan and 
Birkenstock Holding plc
 (NYSE:BIRK). Birkenstock is a heritage footwear brand with a supply-constrained model designed to support high levels of full-price sell-through. Our thesis centered on the company's ability to underproduce demand, preserve pricing discipline and drive steady unit growth alongside higher average selling prices. During the quarter, however, we saw increased promotional activity and discounting, particularly among more price-sensitive consumers, counter to the thesis we initially underwrote. We exited the position given a potentially more promotional demand environment."

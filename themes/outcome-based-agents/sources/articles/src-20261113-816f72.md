Coppin State University Congratulates Alumnae Shaneka Henson ’06 and Sabrina Tapp-Harper ’96 on Historic Election Victories&nbsp;&nbsp;Coppin State University

[page text]
BALTIMORE
 — Coppin State University congratulates two distinguished alumnae, Shaneka Henson ’06 and Sabrina Tapp-Harper ’96, on their victories in Maryland's November 3, 2026, general election, marking significant milestones in their careers and reinforcing the university's longstanding commitment to preparing leaders who serve and strengthen their communities.
Shaneka Henson ’06 Makes History in Maryland Senate
Henson won election to represent Maryland's 30th Legislative District in the State Senate, becoming the first Black person elected to the Maryland Senate from Anne Arundel County.
A graduate of Coppin State University, Henson has built a career centered on public service, community advocacy and legislative leadership. She previously served in the Maryland House of Delegates before being appointed to the State Senate in January 2025. Her November 3 victory marks her first election to the chamber.
Her achievement represents another milestone in expanding representation in Maryland's legislative leadership.
Sabrina Tapp-Harper ’96 Makes History as Baltimore City Sheriff
Tapp-Harper won election as Baltimore City Sheriff, becoming the first Black woman elected to lead the office in its 181-year history.
A 1996 graduate of Coppin State University with a bachelor's degree in criminal justice, Tapp-Harper has dedicated her career to law enforcement and public service. Her election follows her victory in the Democratic primary earlier this year.
Her historic achievement reflects the importance of representation and leadership in the institutions that serve Baltimore's communities.
A Legacy of Leadership and Service
The accomplishments of Henson and Tapp-Harper reflect Coppin State University's enduring mission to develop leaders who are prepared to make meaningful contributions to society.
From the halls of the Maryland General Assembly to the leadership of Baltimore City's Sheriff's Office, Coppin alumni continue to shape public policy, advance public service and create new opportunities for future generations.
Coppin State University proudly congratulates both alumnae on their election victories and celebrates their continued commitment to serving Maryland's communities.

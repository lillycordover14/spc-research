ElevenLabs is coming for your meeting notes, dictation app, and maybe your productivity routine&nbsp;&nbsp;Digital Trends

[page text]
ElevenLabs wants to do more than turn speech into realistic voices. The voice AI company has launched an experimental app called ElevenAI, bringing meeting transcription, note-taking, voice dictation, and AI agent integration into a single workspace designed for teams.

Announced at the company’s 
11/11 Summit
 in New York on November 13, ElevenAI aims to help businesses capture conversations, retrieve information from previous meetings, and automate follow-up tasks. The app is currently in preview, with interested users required to sign up for early access rather than simply download it.

Recommended Videos

The launch puts ElevenLabs into an increasingly crowded productivity market, where tools are competing to become the place employees turn to after every meeting. But the company’s focus appears to be less about building another personal note-taking app and more about giving entire teams a shared source of conversational information.

ElevenAI wants to turn workplace conversations into searchable knowledge

ElevenAI combines features that users may recognise from services such as 
Wispr Flow
 and 
Granola
. It can take notes across meetings, transcribe conversations, and notify participants when a discussion is being recorded. Users can also search information across previous meetings and receive live assistance during conversations, making it easier to recall details without digging through old transcripts.

Wispr Flow

The app also includes a dictation feature that converts speech into cleaned-up text across supported applications. ElevenLabs says it supports more than 90 languages and can recognise industry-specific terminology in both dictation and meeting transcription.

For businesses, the more interesting addition could be its integration with ElevenAgent. Companies can use the platform to trigger follow-up actions based on meeting notes, including drafting emails. That could reduce the administrative work that often follows meetings, although the company has not detailed how broadly these automated workflows will be available during the preview.

ElevenLabs is taking aim at the enterprise productivity market

The launch reflects a wider shift in workplace AI. Meeting transcription tools are increasingly trying to become company-wide knowledge hubs, helping employees extract information from conversations and turn it into actionable tasks.

ElevenLabs, valued at $22 billion, could use ElevenAI to encourage enterprise customers to rely on its own speech models and agent platform instead of purchasing separate licences for meeting note-taking services. That is a significant ambition, particularly as established players continue to expand their own capabilities.

Unsplash

The launch also follows ElevenLabs’ release of its 
Scribe v3
 speech-to-text models, which add live transcription, custom model fine-tuning using company data, and tools for selecting language models based on specific tasks.

For now, ElevenAI remains experimental. Its early-access rollout will give teams an opportunity to try the combined workflow, but its accuracy, integrations, and usefulness at scale will determine whether it can replace existing tools or simply become another app competing for space in the workplace.

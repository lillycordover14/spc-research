Meridian, Idaho (CBS2) - A crash involving a truck and an Ada County Sheriff’s Office vehicle shut down part of Cherry Lane at the intersection of W Cherry Lane and N Ten Mile Road in Meridian.
Westbound Cherry Lane is closed, and there is a partial closure of the eastbound lanes.
Meridian Police said there were no injuries. Ada County Sheriff’s Office officials said additional updates will be provided later.
There is no estimated time for the roadway to reopen.

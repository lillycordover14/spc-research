High School/Club:

Attended Ursuline High School in London, United Kingdom... competed for Tolworth Gymnastics Club under Carla Gale-Parks... joined the Bears’ roster in January 2027.

National:

Member of the English National Squad from 2022-23 and 2026-27 and the British National Squad from 2023-26... placed second on balance beam and third on floor exercise at the British Championships... earned a seventh-place all-around finish at the 2023 British Championships... placed third on vault and fourth on both uneven bars and floor exercise at the 2023 English Championships... earned third-place finishes in both the all-around and team competitions at the 2023 European Youth Olympic Festival in Slovenia... competed at the 2023 Junior World Championships in Turkey... helped her team secure first place at the 2024 Leverkusen Cup in Germany while placing second in the all-around... earned third-place finishes in both the team competition and on balance beam at the 2025 DTB Pokal Cup in Germany.

Personal:

Daughter of Jozef Kandala and Jana Kandalova... has a sister, Ruby... hobbies include listening to music, shopping and baking... plans to study psychology and pursue a career in forensics or forensic psychology.

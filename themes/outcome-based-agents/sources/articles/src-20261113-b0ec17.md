Cetilar Ferrari Sets the Pace in Red-Flagged Paul Ricard Practice

Antonio Fuoco tops Paul Ricard practice despite lengthy FP2 red flag…

 

by
 
Finlay Ringer

November 13, 2026

Photo: Laurent Cartalade/MPS Agency

Cetilar Racing’s Antonio Fuoco set the fastest time during practice for 
the opening round of the Asian Le Mans Series at Circuit Paul Ricard.

A red flag would bring Free Practice 2 to an early end, but it didn’t stop the Ferrari factory driver heading the 46-car field across the two practice sessions.

Fuoco laid down a 1:51.114 in FP1 aboard the No. 47 Ferrari 499P. AF Corse’s Nicklas Nielsen was just 0.640 seconds adrift of the Italian in the No. 90 Ferrari, the only other Hypercar in the field.

Most of the fastest laps of the day were set during FP1 as the second practice session was halted due to an accident between the No. 62 Team Qatar by Iron Lynx Mercedes-AMG GT3 Evo and the No. 68 Car Blanche by RSL Aston Martin Vantage GT3 Evo.

Both drivers were okay, but the session was abandoned due to lengthy barrier repairs. 

LMP2 was topped by Nico Varrone in the No. 83 Oreca 07 Gibson. The AF Corse driver set a chart-topping 1:52.353.

Duqueine Team’s August Raber was the only other LMP2 driver to sneak into the 1:52 lap times, 0.512 seconds slower than Varrone.

Jack Doohan slotted his No. 50 AF Corse machine into third ahead of the No. 24 Nielsen Racing entry. 

Victor Martins set the fifth fastest time in class, topping the times in FP2 before its abrupt stoppage. 
The Frenchman is behind the wheel of the No. 11 Proton Competition Oreca this weekend
 alongside Georgios Kolovos and Charles Milesi. 

Similar to the other categories, the fastest lap of the GT3 class was set in FP1. Garage 59’s Tom Fleming led the field with a 2:02.512 in the No. 58 McLaren 720S GT3 Evo. The young British driver was the only car in the 28-strong GT3 grid to set a sub-2:03 lap. 

Genesis Magma Racing Hypercar driver Mathys Jaubert topped FP2 in the No. 57 Kessel Racing Ferrari 296 GT3 Evo, setting the second fastest time of the day in GT3.

Jaubert and Fleming were the main pace-setters, alongside GetSpeed’s No. 9 Mercedes-AMG, which finished both sessions as the second fastest car in class.

Qualifying for both of this weekend’s four-hour races is scheduled for later today at 4:05 p.m. (10:05 a.m. ET).

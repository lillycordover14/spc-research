Maxx Crosby reveals what impacted Kirk Cousins teammate relationship most&nbsp;&nbsp;New York Post

[page text]
Kirk Cousins’ time in Las Vegas was brief, but it was memorable.

Aside from 
enjoying a 3×3 from In-N-Out while celebrating a team victory
, Cousins left a lasting impact on his Las Vegas teammates — including Maxx Crosby.

3

Maxx Crosby says his favorite memory with Kirk Cousins was after they got into a fight. 
Getty Images

On Thursday, Crosby was asked about his favorite moment with Cousins and admitted it was when the two got into a 
fight during training camp.

“We were literally in the locker room laughing about it [afterward]. The whole world is making it like we hate each other, and I’m an a–hole because I went after Kirk because he’s an older guy and all this stuff,” Crosby told 
Ryan McFadden.
 “It’s on ESPN, and we’re like, bro, how does this happen? But that’s the world we live in. We both laughed it off, and it really wasn’t nothing.”

Cousins said the fight started when Crosby hit him during practice.

“Nobody threw a punch. He got pissed because I hit him, and he was screaming at me, and I’m like, ‘What the f–k?’ It really was as simple as that,” Crosby said.

As the season progressed, Crosby and Cousins’ supposed rivalry came to a close. The two were spotted hanging with one another during practice and even
 participated in TikToks for the team’s social media page.

“I’ll love Kirk Cousins till the day I’m off this planet,” Crosby said.

3

Maxx Crosby says his favorite memory with Kirk Cousins was when they got into a fight. 
Getty Images

Crosby wasn’t the only one who enjoyed having Cousins in the building. Offensive coordinator Andrew Janocko told Sam Warren of The Athletic that the 
former quarterback’s impact was
 “tremendous.”

“I don’t think you could put draft picks or any kind of price tag on that. He’s a tremendous leader, a tremendous person, a tremendous father, tremendous husband and a really, really good friend,” Janocko said. “So, I think as we continue to build, that will be an impact.”

Cousins’ successor, Fernando Mendoza, will line up under center on Sunday, and the Raiders are hopeful it will be the first of many starts.

3

Raiders players share hilarious Kirk Cousins moments. 
AP Photo/Justine Willard

On Wednesday, 
Mendoza thanked Cousins and his family
 for everything they’ve done to make his transition into the NFL easy.

“As a family, they’ve done so much for me,” Mendoza said. “They’ve essentially taken me into their household when they were here, and they welcomed me with open arms.”

Cousins played in only nine games for the Raiders (4-5), throwing for 2,340 yards, 20 touchdowns and eight interceptions.

Cousins is embarking on a homecoming with the Vikings, who are tied for second in the NFC North at 6-2.

Decagon Co-founder and CEO Jesse Zhang is scaling AI customer service agents across voice, chat and email for Chime, Duolingo, Hertz and Deutsche Telekom
Decagon
 was founded in August 2023. Little more than two years later, its AI agents had handled 80 million customer conversations and investors had valued the San Francisco company at US$4.5bn, triple its price six months earlier. 
Decagon sells what it calls concierge service at scale: AI agents that answer customers by voice, chat, email and SMS, and resolve most queries without handing over to a human. 
Chime, 
Duolingo
, Hertz and Deutsche Telekom are among the brands that have put it in front of their customers, and more than 100 enterprises signed up in 2025 alone.
The firm just introduced Missions, which are designed to power long-horizon tasks. The firm highlights that Missions coordinate the agent across systems, monitor status updates and engage multiple people as needed to move the work forward over days or weeks. 
Key facts
Founded: August 2023 in San Francisco by Jesse Zhang and Ashwin Sreenivas
Valuation: US$4.5bn after a US$250m Series D led by Coatue and Index Ventures in January 2026
Previous valuation: US$1.5bn in June 2025, tripled within six months
Revenue: about US$100m annualised in July 2026 (Sacra estimate)
Conversations: 80 million handled by late November 2025
What is Decagon?
Co-founders 
Jesse Zhang
, Decagon CEO, and 
Ashwin Sreenivas
, Decagon President, started from a simple premise: customers should get the kind of attention once reserved for a concierge, and businesses should be able to afford it. “Every customer deserves concierge treatment. And every business deserves the technology to make it possible,” says Jesse.
Decagon trains its agents on each client's help centre content, manuals and past conversations, and runs them on models from OpenAI and Anthropic. The same agent logic works across voice, chat, email and SMS, with ElevenLabs voice models behind its phone agents. 
“When we explored AI voice solutions, we saw that the most lifelike and emotionally resonant voices were powered by ElevenLabs,” Jesse said when the partnership was announced. 
Pricing is per conversation, with higher rates for complex queries and voice. The results are what sell it. Chime reached 70% chat and voice resolution and cut contact centre operating costs by more than 60%. 
Duolingo runs at an 80% deflection rate according to a blog by Microsoft, and ClassPass saw ten times the deflection it had expected at launch. “With Decagon, it's been a night-and-day difference,” Ian Riggins, Senior Operations Manager at Duolingo, has said. 
The firm's recently released Missions let the agent connect threads across multiple personas and reach each one on the most 
relevant channel
.

Growth outlook
Across its base, Decagon now reports average deflection above 80%. Roughly a third of its 2025 customers had no prior AI automation, while more than half replaced legacy IVRs, ticketing tools or CRM-based agents, putting it in direct competition with Salesforce, Zendesk and Sierra.
Growth has been fast. Coatue and Index Ventures led a US$250m Series D in January 2026 at a US$4.5bn valuation, up from US$1.5bn in June 2025. Deutsche Telekom, with more than 261 million mobile customers, signed a 
commercial pilot
 in November 2025 while its investment arm T.Capital took a strategic stake. 
Sacra estimates annualised revenue reached about US$100m by July 2026, though Decagon does not disclose revenue. In March 2026 it ran its first tender offer for more than 300 employees.
Proactive agents, launched in March 2026, predict what a customer needs and make first contact, turning service from a cost centre into something closer to account management. 
With travel brands such as Avis Budget Group and Hertz, and financial names including Block and Affirm, Decagon is betting that one agent layer can span every channel and every industry where customers expect a quick answer.

Top technology partners

OpenAI
:
 Decagon builds its customer service agents on OpenAI frontier models, trained on each client's help centre content, manuals and past conversations, so the same agent logic can answer by chat, email, SMS and voice.

Anthropic
:
 Anthropic's Claude models sit alongside OpenAI's in Decagon's stack, giving the company a choice of frontier models for reasoning through complex customer queries that it prices at a higher per-conversation rate.

ElevenLabs
:
 Supplies the voice models behind Decagon's phone agents, part of a stack that lets brands such as Chime and Hertz run the same agent across voice and digital channels with their own brand voice.

Deutsche Telekom
:
 The operator, with more than 261 million mobile customers, signed a commercial pilot with Decagon in November 2025, while its investment arm T.Capital made a strategic investment and later joined the Series D.

Salesforce
:
 Integrations with Salesforce, Zendesk and Intercom let Decagon slot into existing contact centres. More than half of its 2025 customers replaced legacy IVRs, ticketing tools or CRM-based agents with its platform.

AMES, Iowa – The North Dakota State women's cross country team finished 15th out of 35 teams at the NCAA Midwest Regional on Friday morning.

 

Jade Rypkema
 (20:46.1) placed 63rd, and 
Logan Harz
 (20:46.4) was 65th to lead the Bison women for the six-kilometer race at the Iowa State Cross Country Course.

 

Mia Hoffmann
 finished 86th in 21:00.1, 
Kylie Nelson
 took 94th in a personal-best 21:09.0, and 
Julia Link
 completed the team scoring for NDSU in 123rd place at 21:32.0.

 

The Bison finished with 430 points in the team standings for 15th place, improving two spots after taking 17th a year ago.

 

Harz's time was the second-fastest 6k of her career.

 

Iowa State won the women's team title with 72 points, and Oklahoma State was second with 91.

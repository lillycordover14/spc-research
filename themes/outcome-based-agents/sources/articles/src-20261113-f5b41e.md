Waco, Texas (KWTX)- 
 At 
Waco Adapt
, Victor Lopez helps people with disabilities build confidence and pursue healthier lives through adaptive fitness and exercise rehabilitation.
Lopez, the gym’s director of operations, was nominated for Be Remarkable by Christi Ortiz, who said he has helped her son become more confident, agile and healthy.
“We are so appreciative of your work, your sweet attitude and your contribution to our community,” Ortiz told Lopez. “And we love you and we think you’re awesome.”
Lopez said he enjoys being there for people who need encouragement, and his faith inspires him to give back.
“Most importantly, I just try to show up and be your friend,” he said. “And, try to encourage people.”
His selflessness and dedication reflect Daniel Stark Injury Lawyers’ core value of being remarkable. In recognition of his work, Lopez received a $500 check on behalf of the firm.
For Lopez, the mission is to give back to Waco’s underserved community and let people know that Waco Adapt is there for them.
“We’re here for the long run,” he said.

LangChain Raises $300 Million Series C Led by CapitalG at $3 Billion Valuation&nbsp;&nbsp;citybiz

[page text]
LangChain
, a San Francisco-based artificial intelligence infrastructure company, has raised 
$300 million
 in Series C funding at a $3 billion valuation, as businesses increase investment in developing, deploying and managing AI agents.

The financing was led by existing investor 
CapitalG
, with significant participation from new investors GIC and DFJ Growth, alongside existing backers IVP and Sapphire Ventures.

Additional investors included Salesforce Ventures, Insight Venture Partners, QuantumLight, MongoDB Ventures, Sequoia, Amplify and Benchmark.

The round brings LangChain’s total funding to $460 million.

The investment will support the expansion of LangSmith, LangChain’s platform for building, deploying, monitoring and governing AI agents in enterprise environments.

LangChain
 is positioning LangSmith as an infrastructure platform that allows companies to develop proprietary AI systems while maintaining control over their models, data, applications and operating environments.

“Agents are going to transform how companies operate and the products they deliver. The companies that control these systems will build lasting competitive advantages,” said 
Harrison Chase
, co-founder and CEO of LangChain.

“Every company should be free to choose the best models for its needs, control its data and infrastructure, and continuously improve its agents, while maintaining the governance needed to operate securely at scale.”

More than 7,000 organizations use 
LangSmith, including Nvidia, Toyota, Salesforce, Workday, Harvey, Rippling and Listen Labs
.

LangChain said customer adoption reflects growing demand for infrastructure that supports AI agents beyond initial experimentation and into large-scale production environments.

Clay uses LangSmith to monitor hundreds of millions of agent executions monthly, while Workday uses the platform to deploy and govern AI agents across its organization.

According to LangChain, Workday reduced the time required to deploy a new agent from five months to less than one day.

LangSmith’s platform is organized around three primary capabilities: agent runtime, performance improvement and governance.

Its runtime infrastructure includes LangChain, LangGraph and Deep Agents, which allow developers to build applications using different AI models and manage how information is processed.

LangSmith Agent Server and Sandboxes
 support secure deployment, while Managed Agents and Fleet provide additional services for operating production systems.

The company’s performance improvement tools include observability, evaluation, automated error detection and fine-tuning capabilities.

LangSmith Engine uses AI to identify problems, generate code and prompt modifications, and monitor production systems for performance regressions.

Its governance products provide centralized oversight of model usage, access to enterprise tools, authentication, sensitive data and AI infrastructure spending.

“The transition from experimental LLM applications to production-grade agents is one of the most significant shifts happening in enterprise software today,” said Mo Jomaa, partner at CapitalG.

“Harrison and the LangChain team have built a foundational platform for agent engineering, earning the trust of thousands of developers and global enterprises.”

The financing comes as companies increasingly develop specialized AI agents for internal operations, customer-facing products and industry-specific workflows.

Unlike applications built around a single AI model, LangChain’s model-agnostic approach allows organizations to select different models and infrastructure providers while retaining control over their agent architecture.

The company plans to introduce additional LangSmith capabilities at a product event scheduled for November 20, 2026.

LangChain
 did not disclose its revenue, profitability or the specific allocation of proceeds from the Series C financing.

Asphalt is being laid at the revamped Buenos Aires circuit, one of two ‘new’ venues on the 2027 MotoGP calendar alongside Adelaide.
After Termas de Río Hondo was dropped from this year's schedule, Argentina returns as round four next season, when the upgraded Autódromo Oscar y Juan Gálvez hosts its first Grand Prix since 1999.
“The works continue to progress,” reports ESPN presenter Martín Urruty during a visit to the circuit.
“The paving process has already begun, consisting of three different layers - the last one on which the World Championship motorcycles will ride next 11 April.

LangChain: $300 Million Series C Funding Led By CapitalG At $3 Billion Valuation To Expand AI Agent Platform

LangChain, a San Francisco-based provider of AI agent development and infrastructure technology, has raised $300 million in Series C funding at a $3 billion valuation. The round was led by existing investor CapitalG, with significant participation from new investors GIC and DFJ Growth, alongside existing investors IVP and Sapphire Ventures.

The financing brings LangChain’s total funding to $460 million and will support expansion of LangSmith, its platform for developing, deploying, monitoring, improving, and governing enterprise AI agents.

Additional new investors include Salesforce Ventures, Insight Venture Partners, QuantumLight, and MongoDB Ventures. Existing investors Sequoia, Amplify, and Benchmark also participated.

LangChain is focused on helping organizations build and operate AI agents while maintaining control over their underlying models, proprietary data, infrastructure, and security requirements.

The company believes the growing adoption of autonomous AI systems will create opportunities for businesses to develop specialized agents that reflect their unique knowledge and operating processes.

Rather than relying exclusively on general-purpose AI applications, enterprises can use LangChain’s infrastructure to create systems customized for individual business requirements.

More Than 7,000 Organizations Use LangSmith

LangChain reported that more than 7,000 organizations use LangSmith to support their AI agent development and operations.

Its customer base includes major enterprises such as Nvidia, Toyota, Salesforce, Workday, Bridgewater, and LinkedIn, alongside technology companies including Harvey, Rippling, and Listen Labs.

The platform is designed to help organizations move AI agents from experimentation into production environments.

LangChain highlighted several examples of customers deploying its infrastructure at scale.

Clay uses LangSmith to monitor hundreds of millions of agent runs each month and identify opportunities to improve agent performance.

Workday uses the platform to operate and govern AI agents across its organization.

According to LangChain, Workday reduced the time required to deploy a new agent from five months to less than one day.

These deployments illustrate how businesses are using agent infrastructure to support increasingly complex workflows while improving oversight and operational efficiency.

Agent Runtime Supports Enterprise Deployment

One of the three central components of LangSmith is Agent Runtime, which provides infrastructure for building and operating AI agents.

LangChain’s open, model-agnostic development tools include LangChain, LangGraph, and Deep Agents.

These technologies allow developers to construct agent workflows while maintaining flexibility in their selection of foundation models.

The tools also provide control over how agents manage context, interact with applications, and execute multistep tasks.

LangSmith Agent Server and Sandboxes provide deployment environments intended to support secure operation at scale.

Customers can deploy agents in cloud infrastructure or their own computing environments, depending on operational requirements.

The platform also includes LangSmith Managed Agents and Fleet, which provide managed services for building, deploying, and operating agents in production.

These offerings are intended to reduce the infrastructure complexity associated with running enterprise AI systems.

Agent Improvement Focuses On Monitoring And Performance

LangSmith’s second major component is Agent Improvement, which helps developers evaluate and improve AI agent behavior.

The platform includes observability and evaluation tools that provide visibility into agent execution and performance.

These capabilities allow organizations to analyze agent activity, identify errors, and measure output quality.

LangSmith Engine, an AI agent operating within the platform, is designed to automate portions of the improvement process.

The system can identify errors, generate code and prompt corrections, and monitor production activity for performance regressions.

LangSmith Fine-Tuning also allows organizations to use agent execution traces as training data for specialized AI models.

This approach is intended to help companies improve their agents based on operational experience.

LangChain believes continuous evaluation and refinement will become important as organizations deploy AI systems responsible for increasingly consequential business tasks.

Agent Governance Provides Centralized Oversight

The third component of LangSmith is Agent Governance.

This infrastructure provides organizations with centralized controls over how AI agents interact with models, tools, and enterprise information.

LangChain’s LLM and Tool Gateways help businesses manage model budgets, establish fallback options, and control access to external applications.

The platform also includes capabilities for protecting sensitive information and managing authentication across teams.

These controls are designed to support enterprises operating multiple agents across different business functions.

As autonomous systems become more capable, organizations need ways to manage permissions, monitor expenditures, and reduce risks associated with access to proprietary data.

LangChain believes governance will be an essential requirement for deploying agents reliably at enterprise scale.

CapitalG Leads Investment In Enterprise AI Infrastructure

CapitalG, an existing LangChain investor, led the Series C financing.

Mo Jomaa, Partner at CapitalG, highlighted the shift from experimental large language model applications toward production-grade autonomous agents.

He emphasized LangChain’s position as an infrastructure provider supporting developers and enterprises building these systems.

The participation of GIC, DFJ Growth, Salesforce Ventures, MongoDB Ventures, and other investors reflects interest in technologies supporting the broader enterprise AI ecosystem.

LangChain intends to use the financing to strengthen its platform and expand its ability to serve organizations deploying AI agents.

The company also plans to introduce additional LangSmith capabilities at a product release event scheduled for November 20, 2026.

The upcoming announcements will build on its existing infrastructure for agent deployment, improvement, and governance.

With $460 million in total funding, a $3 billion valuation, and more than 7,000 organizations using LangSmith, LangChain is positioning itself as a major infrastructure provider for enterprise AI agents.

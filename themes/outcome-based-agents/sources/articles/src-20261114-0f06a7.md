  A four-bedroom Wrexham home described as having a 'definite wow factor' has gone on the market for £360,000.

  The detached property on Ffordd Llywelyn, in the popular Little Acton area, has been extended to the front, side and rear to create around 1,580 sq ft of accommodation.

  Now on the market with Monopoly Buy Sell Rent, the house has four bedrooms, three reception rooms and two bathrooms, as well as a large driveway with space for multiple vehicles.

  Describing the property, the estate agent said: "Situated in the popular Little Acton area of Wrexham, just off Chester Road, this exceptional four bedroom detached home offers spacious, stylish accommodation with a definite wow factor."

Living room with gas fire, built-in storage and double doors to family room 
(Image: Monopoly)

  The agent says the extensions have created a home suited to modern family living, combining entertaining spaces with practical features.

  A solid oak entrance canopy sits at the front of the property, with the front door opening into a vaulted entrance area featuring large windows.

Kitchen with AEG induction hob, integrated Neff oven and grill, and pantry 
(Image: Monopoly)

  From there, the house opens into a spacious living room with a gas fire and built-in storage, while double doors lead into a separate family room with patio doors onto the garden.

  One of the main features downstairs is the open-plan kitchen and dining area.

First bedroom with full-height fitted wardrobes 
(Image: Monopoly)

  The contemporary kitchen includes an AEG induction hob, integrated Neff oven and grill, integrated AEG fridge and freezer and a pantry, while French-style doors lead out to the rear garden.

  Another reception room is currently being used as an additional living space, although the agent says it could also serve as a home office or study.

  There is also a utility area and a downstairs wet-room-style shower room with a walk-in shower, WC and wash basin.

Family bathroom with bath and overhead shower, WC and wash basin 
(Image: Monopoly)

  Upstairs are four bedrooms and a family bathroom.

  The first and second bedrooms both have full-height fitted wardrobes, while the third has built-in storage.

  The family bathroom has a three-piece suite comprising a bath with shower over, WC and wash basin.

Enclosed rear garden with paved patio, artificial lawn, lighting and outdoor tap 
(Image: Monopoly)

  Outside, the enclosed rear garden has been designed with relatively easy maintenance in mind, combining a paved patio with artificial lawn.

  Lighting, power and an outdoor tap are also provided.

  There is also a secure store suitable for bicycles.

  At the front, a tarmac driveway provides off-road parking for multiple vehicles.

  The listing says the extension benefits from high-grade insulation and slim-profile anthracite aluminium window frames, while the house has double-glazed windows throughout.

  The property is freehold and has an EPC rating of C.

  The house was added to the market on November 9 and is being advertised for £360,000 through Monopoly Buy Sell Rent.

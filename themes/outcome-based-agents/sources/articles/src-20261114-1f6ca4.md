  The Festive Artisan Fair is set to return to Black Barn Farm this December.

  The expanded fair in Salhouse will feature more than 130 businesses, including food vendors, crafters, bakers, artisans and independent traders from Norfolk and beyond.

  The fair is free to enter and runs from 10am to 8pm on Friday, December 4 and Saturday, December 5, and until 4pm on Sunday, December 6.

A previous Festive Artisan Fair 
(Image: BBF Salhouse Marketplace)

  Organisers said: "Supporting independent small businesses, crafters, bakers and artisans is at the heart of everything we do at Black Barn Farm.

  "We want this event to be about much more than Christmas shopping.

  "It’s about bringing people together, showcasing the incredible talent and creativity of independent businesses and giving them a platform to reach new customers."

  Stalls will be spread across two marquees and an additional sail tent.

  Visitors can browse handmade gifts, crafts, food and drink, and meet the people who made them.

  Entertainment will include live singers, school performances and acts from Independence Matters hubs.

  The organisers added: "Following the fantastic response last year, we’re incredibly excited to be expanding to three days and welcoming more than 130 businesses.

  "We want to create something special for Norfolk."

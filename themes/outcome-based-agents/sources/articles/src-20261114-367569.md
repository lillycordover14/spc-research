Patagonia Nano-Air Ultralight Full Zip Hoody Review

Article by Matt Kolat
Nano Air Ultralight Full Zip Hoody
 (£220)
Introduction
Being a huge Patagonia fan for years (decades even perhaps?) I was very excited when Sam, the editor of Road Trail run, reached out to me with an opportunity to review a piece from Patagonia’s newest collection. 
The piece I am having a closer look at is the Nano Air Ultralight. Patagonia officially lists it as a mid-layer but I think it can serve as an ultralight insulated jacket on those cold days when we’re working really hard, pushing uphill, where minimal insulation is all that we need.
Features
The Nano Air Ultralight is a very minimal piece of kit therefore it is not feature rich. However that is one of its strength. In my size large men’s it weighs a mere 268g / 9.4oz. 
The hood is ealistaceted and does not have a synching cable, yet it can accommodate a mountaineering helmet.
The shell and lining are made from 100% recycled polyester ripstop, offering mechanical stretch, excellent breathability and enhanced abrasion resistance. A PFAS-free durable water repellent (DWR) finish helps protect against light moisture.
The jacket has a single chest pocket which can easily fit a phone and allows for self stuffing, no need for a separate stuff sack.
The 20g weight Full Range synthetic insulation has been placed strategically where it matters, and where more breathibilty is required.  For example in the armpits, there is no insulation. 
At the bottom of the sleeves Patagonia has used a different fabric, Capeline Cool Lightweight which is extra stretchy and breathable. This provides some elastication in the cuffs which are otherwise unelasticated. 
Fit
When I think about Patagonia I normally think of very loose fitting gear, designed for comfort first and performance second. In all my Patagonia gear I weara a size large and typically I could easily also fit a medium. 
I am 6 foot / 182cm tall, broad shouldered and weigh 82kg/12.9st/180lbs. The Nano Air Ultralight in size large is very form fitting on me which I don’t mind as this is a purely performance oriented piece. If you however want this piece to fit you as most Patagonia items do I strongly suggest sizing up.
Summary
The Patagonia Nano-Air Ultralight is an exceptionally lightweight, minimalist insulated layer designed for high-output activities in cold conditions. 
Weighing just 268g in a men’s size large, it combines strategically placed 20g insulation with highly breathable, stretchy recycled polyester ripstop to provide warmth where needed without compromising ventilation. 
Its PFAS-free DWR finish offers protection from light moisture, while details such as the helmet-compatible hood, single chest pocket and breathable, Capeline Cool cuffs keep things simple and functional. 
The fit is noticeably more athletic and form-fitting than Patagonia’s typically relaxed style, making it a performance-focused piece best suited to those looking for minimal insulation during demanding endurance and other uphill efforts.

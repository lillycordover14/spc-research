Bobby Lashley speaks about Lio Rush, noting that he hopes that before he retires he gets the opportunity to work with him again.

Lashley and Rush have worked together in the past, spending a substantial amount of time in WWE paired together. The pair both share a roster right now in AEW, but have not worked together in the company so far, outside of a backstage segment together.

While 
speaking to MuscleManMalcolm in a new interview
, Bobby Lashley spoke about Lio Rush, noting he hopes to work with him again before he retires.

“He is one of my favorite people in the world. You know how talented he is? Everything he does, everything he touches is gold. He was, and I just saw him the other day. It was his birthday just the other day, wasn’t it? The 23 piece of gold, 23-year-old piece of gold. Now he’s 32. That’s what I was gonna say, man. I love Lio, and I’ve always said good things about Lio. Every time when people ask me about Loo, from Tony to before in iMPACT!, I always said, man, the dude is so talented, man. He could do anything in this business, and I hope before I do retire, I get an opportunity to work with him again.”

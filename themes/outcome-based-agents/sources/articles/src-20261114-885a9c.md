Clay, Oakleaf eliminated in first round of state football playoffs

 

		Posted
		

Saturday, 

November 14, 2026 

10:58 am

Clay Today staff

After qualifying for the FHSAA State Football Playoffs as eighth seeds, Clay and Oakleaf highs both were eliminated in the first round Friday night.
 
Clay (8-3) was bounced in the first round of the Class 4A tournament, 35-0, at Columbia, while Oakleaf (5-6) lost on the road to Mandarin, 35-7, in a Class 5A game.

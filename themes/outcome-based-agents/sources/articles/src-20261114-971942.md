PLATTSBURGH, N.Y.
 —
Plattsburgh has opened four new ADA-accessible bocce ball courts at South Acres Park, offering opportunities for people of all ages and abilities to stay active and enjoy friendly competition.  
City officials said the courts are designed to give people of all ages and abilities a chance to get outside, stay active, and enjoy some friendly competition.  
Advertisement
The project has been in development for three years and was partially funded by a $100,000 state and municipal facilities grant.  
Special Olympics athletes had the opportunity to play on the new courts and shared their hopes for future games.  
"We're hoping that the community will also get involved and come and play with us. We do have the senior citizens that live across the road on Flynn Ave. They come in and they watch, and they participate sometimes," said Hope Land, a Special Olympics New York coach.  
The city of Plattsburgh is encouraging community members to visit the new courts and celebrate their addition to the park.

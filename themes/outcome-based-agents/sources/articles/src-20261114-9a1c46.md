Garry "Clay" Childress

Mr. Garry "Clay" Childress, age 63, of Dobson, passed away Saturday, November 14, 2026, at Wake Forest Baptist Medical Center. He was born May 15, 1963, in Surry County, to the late O'Dell Childress and the late Pauline Mitchell Childress. Garry was one of 10 children. Mr. Childress was a loving father and grandfather, a skilled mechanic and body man whose work was well known in Surry and the surrounding counties. Clay was extremely loved and loved everyone he met. A best friend and father figure for too many to count. If you knew Clay, you know there's no way to sum him up in a short paragraph; he was such a wonderful man and person. Clay had so many stories to tell about his cars, and his life, and fighting when he was younger. He never stopped talking about his love for his kids and grandbabies. He was such a proud dad and "pawpaw," and there wasn't anything you could ask of him that he wouldn't give. Even when he was sick, he would be there if you needed him in a heartbeat, without question. The world feels a lot emptier without him. Left to cherish his memories are his daughter and son-in-law, Rita Childress and Robert Bailey; his daughter Chetalee “Nicole” Childress; and his son, Garry “Jason” Childress; and grandchildren; Kimala Neal, Izaiah Childress, Braison King, Shaylee King, Kayne Epperson, and Everly Bailey; his brother, Randy Childress; and many special nieces and nephews. The family will receive friends Wednesday, November 18, 2026, from 1:00-2:00 PM at Moody-Davis Funeral Service in Dobson. Funeral services will follow at 2:00 PM, in the Moody-Davis Chapel, With Rev. Terry Coe officiating. Burial will follow in the Second Chance Church Cemetery. Moody-Davis Funeral Service in Dobson is respectfully serving the Childress family.

DECATUR, Ill. (WAND) – Decatur’s Staley Museum is hosting a Holiday Artisan Market on Nov. 29.
From 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., the market will feature 26 central Illinois small businesses selling exclusively handmade or artisanal items, according to the museum.
The free event will be held at the Staley Museum, 361 N. College St.

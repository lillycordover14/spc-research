Football Rolls Past Clay, 61-27, to Advance to D-II Regional Final

Nov 13

Written by 
Brian DeBenedictis

TOLEDO, OH —
 The Central Catholic 
football
 team delivered an explosive performance in all three phases of the game Friday, powering past 
Clay
,
 61-27
, in the third round of the OHSAA Division II State Playoffs at 
Gallagher Stadium
.
With their 20th consecutive victory over the Eagles, the top-seeded Fighting Irish improve to 10-2 overall and advance to the Division II Region 6 Final.  Fourth-seeded Clay concludes its season at 9-3.
Sophomore quarterback 
Lincoln Sellers
 completed 9-of-11 passes for 193 yards and three touchdowns, connecting with junior wide receiver 
David Yharbrough
 and senior wide receivers 
Amari Eliano
 and 
Billy Williams
 for scoring strikes.
Senior running back 
Jeremiah Fuller
 fueled the ground attack, rushing for 159 yards and three touchdowns on 15 carries.  The balanced offensive effort helped the Scarlet & Gray build a 40-20 halftime advantage.
Central Catholic’s defense also contributed a pair of touchdowns.  Senior linebacker 
Antonio Elizondo
 returned an interception 22 yards for a score, while senior defensive back 
Jason Lawson, Jr.
 added a 22-yard fumble return for a touchdown.
The Fighting Irish made their presence felt on special teams as well.  Freshman defensive back 
Troy Yharbrough
 blocked a punt for a safety, and sophomore linebacker 
Ricardo Doyle Rodriguez
 blocked an extra-point attempt.
Central Catholic will face 
No. 3 seed Avon (10-2)
 in the 
OHSAA Division II Region 6 Final
 on 
Friday, November 20, at 7 p.m.
  The regional championship will be played at a neutral site, with the location to be announced by the OHSAA.

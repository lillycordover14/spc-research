CHARLESTON, W.Va. (WSAZ) - Families were able to enjoy the world of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics for the second ever ‘STEM Fest’ in Charleston’s Clay Center. 
STEM professionals and organizations around the area were also there teaching how STEM is used in the world every day. There were also lots of opportunities to talk with STEM experts from fields such as aerospace, engineering, and environmental protection. 
“There’s so many wonderful things,” said Taylor Gue, Director of Communications for the Clay Center. “Seeing kids get excited and that spark in their eye it’s really exciting to see and be able to bring that to the people in the community.” 
There were quite a few hands-on ways to learn at STEM Fest, including a straw rocket design and launch challenge.

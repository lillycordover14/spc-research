Casino RSM Cavaliers Cricket Club
Here’s a roundup of the weekend cricket matches.
Round 6 recap was a Cav-wash with both sides getting a win.
Far North Coast 1st grade Casino RSM Cavaliers returned home welcoming Terranora to the Cauldron and looking to make it 2 wins on the trot. 
Winning the toss and choosing to bat first, the Cavs got off to a solid start being 1-23 after 6 overs. 
Dylan Olive (24) and Miller Scully (52) took control of the early stage of the innings putting 63 on for the 2nd wicket. 
The Cavs were in a good spot at 3-112 at the 20 over mark but a good fight back from Terranora dismissing skipper Damien Vidler (21), Louis Siverwright (12) and Jordan Williams (16) would see the Cavs all out for 168. 
In reply the Cavs started off strong with the ball taking a wicket with the 6th ball of the first over. 
The Cavs kept the pressure on to have Terranora 4-66 at drinks before the duo of Jaydan Vidler 3-39 off 8 and old war horse Alan Nowlan 2-25 off 8 turned the game for the Cavs, in their 16 over spell the pair took combined 5-64. 
At one stage Big Al had 1/0 from 2 overs and then 2/2 from 4 overs.
The Cavs eventually dismissed Terranora for 137 handing the boys their 2nd win from the last 2 games. 
Not to be out done, Michael German took 3-20 off 6.3 and Jordan Williams set the tone early with 2-13 off his 4. 
The Cavs hit the road next week to take on Cudgen for their round 7 fixture.
FNC 2nd Grade Casino RSM Cavaliers 
After losing the toss and being sent in… 2nds, they lost their first wicket with the score at 1-28 off 4 overs. 
The wicket didn’t slow 2nds down continuing to punish any bad ball. 
Opener Matt Vrckoff (35) was joined by Connor Turner (27) putting on a nice little partnership before Matt was dismissed. 
Life member and guru Mal Hancock then came to the crease with the score 2-62. 
Mal scored a nice 15 on return for the Cavs. 
Both set batters fell but then entered man of the moment Cam Taylor Brown who hit the ball to all parts smashing an unbeaten 111 off 68 including 7x4s and 6x6s. 
Cam and Bronson McNiff (28) put on a partnership worth 110 and a last ball boundary from Alex Newton got 2nds to a massive total of 7-258 from their 40.
Then it was time to have a bowl. 
Alex Newton taking a wicket with his first ball fired the team up. 
Wickets falling at a steady rate with all bowlers bowling superbly. 
Alex Newton was the pick of the bowlers taking 4-15 off 7. 
Connor Turner also stood up with the ball taking 3-21 off 5.3. 
Young gun Hudson Scully landed ‘em and ripped ‘em to finish with the amazing figures of 2-7 off 5 including 3 maidens. 
Hudson also took what could be the catch of the season at short cover. 
Goonellabah were bowled out for 75 after 24.3 overs. 
A great team effort with big contributions and unbelievable catches giving 2nds a win by a thumping margin of 183 runs.

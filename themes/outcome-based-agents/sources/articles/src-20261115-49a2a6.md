BLOOMINGTON, Ind.
 –  First year forward 
Addy Nyemchek
 had her first career double-double while third year 
Zania Socka-Nguemen
 dropped 16 points as IU got past Milwaukee with ease, 85-47.

 

KEY MOMENTS

Nyemchek highlighted the Hoosiers (5-0) on the glass in the first, pulling down seven of her 17 boards in the first quarter as they took a 16-7 lead after the first 10 minutes.

Socka-Nguemen went a perfect 4-for-4 in the second quarter for eight points and entered double figures before the break. Indiana held a 10-point advantage over the Panthers (2-3) at the break, 33-23.

IU scored a season-high 52 points in the second half, as second year forward 
Maya Makalusky
 lit up from downtown with a pair of triples and went 4-for-5 from the field in the frame.

The game would be capped off by a strong offensive performance in the fourth quarter, as the Hoosiers shot 62.5 percent to close out the afternoon.


 

NOTABLE

Nyemchek recorded her career-first double-double, with 11 points and 17 rebounds, the most rebounds by an Indiana player since Mackenzie Holmes in 2021 (12/19/21). The Oceanport, N.J. native is the second player in the country this season with at least 11 points, 17 rebounds and five assists in a game this season (Brooklyn Fely, Southern Miss).

With 11 points, fifth year guard 
Kenzie Hare
 is now just one point away from scoring her 1000th collegiate point.

The team recorded 21 offensive rebounds, leading to 22 second-chance points.

Indiana scored 25 points off turnovers compared to Milwaukee's 2.

Socka-Nguemen led the way in scoring, recording 16 points in 21 minutes. She has now reached double figures in 9 of her last 10 games, dating back to last season.

Fourth year guard 
Malia Samuels
 dished out a career tying seven assists and set a new career-high six steals.

Indiana shot 89 percent from the free-throw line, their best mark of the season.

Five Hoosiers scored double digits: Socka-Nguemen with 16, Williams with 12, Hare with 11, Nyemchek with 11, and Makalusky with 10.

IU improves to 3-0 in the all-time series with Milwaukee.

Defending champion 
Fribourg Olympic
 (SBL) inked 28-year old American 196cm forward
Kendall Robinson
 (college: 
W.Connecticut
). He was best rebounder at 
SCMU Craiova
 in Romanian Liga Nationala. In 38 games he averaged 12.5ppg, 4.6rpg, 1.7apg, 1.1spg and 1.1bpg last season. He helped them to make it to the semifinals.
 The previous (2024-25) season he played at 
Krka Novo Mesto
 (Liga Nova KBM) in Slovenian league. Robinson also played 18 games in Slovenian Liga OTP banka where he had 7.3ppg, 3.3rpg and 1.2apg in that year. He contributed to his team winning the regular season and make it to the final. They also played in the Cup final. The other team he played shortly was 
KK Dynamic Vip Pay Beograd
 (KLS) in Serbian league.
Robinson has played also professionally in Serbia and Slovenia (LTH Castings).
Among many awards he was voted Eurobasket.com All-Slovenian League Import Player of the Year in 2024.
He graduated from Western Connecticut State University in 2023 and it will be his fourth season in pro basketball. Robinson played for other colleges like: University of the Cumberlands, Sullivan County Community College.

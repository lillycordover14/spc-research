 By: 
Noah Tylutki, Associate Director of Athletics Communications
ERIE, Pa. — 
The Akron women's basketball team won their first game of the season, as they defeated Mercyhurst, 63-50, on the road.


The Zips led the entire game and began the first quarter on a 7-0 run. The Lakers eventually cut it to one on two separate occasions, but a Macey Fegan layup put Akron ahead, 13-10, where it remained at the end of the period.


Akron maintained their advantage steadily throughout the second, growing to as many as eight two times before settling for a 26-21 lead at the half.


The Zips' advantage grew to six, 34-28, halfway to go in the third and ended on a 11-6 run to take a double-digit lead, 45-34, at the end of the third.


Akron kept their double-digit advantage throughout the fourth, growing to as many as 19, 55-36, with 5:46 remaining. A 14-4 Laker run shaved the margin to nine, 59-50, with a minute and a half left but Fegan sunk all four free throw attempts to close out the game for the Zips.

Jessie Joaquim
 led Akron with 13 points, while Fegan added 12 points. 
Tyla Heard
 chipped in eight points and a team-high seven rebounds, and 
Teniesha Clarke
 led the squad with nine points off the bench.


The Zips held the Lakers to 33.3% shooting, including 21.4% from the field. Forty-four of Akron's points came in the paint, and 26 came off turnovers.

Up Next

Akron will close out a four-game road trip at West Virginia on Tuesday, November 17 at 10:30 a.m. from Morgantown.

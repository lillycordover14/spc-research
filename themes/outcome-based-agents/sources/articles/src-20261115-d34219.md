BROOKFIELD, Wis. — Another week, another win for the Janesville Jets.
The Jets (14-5-0-0) currently hold the longest active win streak in the entire North American Hockey League with eight consecutive victories. Janesville also is one of only four teams in the entire league with at least 14 wins already on the season.
When it comes to naming ‘the hottest team in hockey’ right now, you’d be hard pressed to give the moniker to the Jets this November.
Janesville was put to the test on Saturday when for the third-straight game, the Jets took on their in-state rivals, the Wisconsin Windigo, on the road up at The Ponds of Brookfield.
The Jets swept their way past the Windigo last weekend in a home-and-home series, and now were looking to carry that momentum right into Saturday’s one-off game between the two sides.
Right from the opening puck drop, it looked as though the Jets were going to once again assert their dominance against the Windigo.
Matthew McGovern opened the game’s scoring at the 16:16 mark of the first period, putting the Jets ahead 1-0. Grant Dillard, Janesville’s leading scorer this season, then grabbed a brace a few minutes later on the power play to put Janesville ahead 2-0 less than seven minutes into the night.
Instead of rolling over, however, the Windigo responded. A power play goal for the Windigo allowed them to slice into Janesville’s lead prior to the first intermission.
The Windigo then netted yet another power play goal at the start of the second frame, leveling the score at 2-2 to erase Janesville’s early two-goal lead.
Janesville remained level headed and eventually found the back of the net at the 8:18 mark of the second period off the stick of Hans Hedlund. The Windigo once again responded just minutes later, however, bringing the teams back to equal at 3-3.
Declan Jason then capped off a high-scoring second period by notching what would end up being the game-winning goal for the Jets with just 1:10 remaining in the second, sneaking the puck past the Windigo net minder to put Janesville back in front 4-3.
The Jets withstood the Windigo in the third period, not allowing their foes to find the back of the net despite the Windigo having three power play opportunities to do so.
Matthew Dedecko scored his first goal of the season in the form of an unassisted, short-handed goal with 4:19 remaining in the game. The goal put the cherry on top of what ended up being a 5-3 Janesville victory, extending the Jets’ win streak to eight in a row.
The Jets ended up out-shooting the Windigo 35-22 in the win, however five Janesville penalties led to two goals for the Windigo offense. Janesville’s five goals were scored by five different individuals, a recurring theme for a Jets team that has showcased excellent depth at the early stages of the season.
Brandon Wright earned the start in net for Janesville. He finished with 19 saves on 22 shots. The teenager is now 4-0-0 as a starting goalie for the Jets this season.
The Jets will now hit the road for a battle between the top two teams of the NAHL Midwest Division, as Janesville will take on the Minnesota Wilderness (13-7-0-0) on November 20-21 at Northwoods Credit Union Arena in Cloquet, Minnesota.

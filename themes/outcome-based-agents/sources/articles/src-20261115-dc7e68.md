Amid the gilded shadows and stately spaces of the historic Goldenberg Mansion in Malacañang, a timeless magic awakened as Opera Belle — the premiere all-female classical vocal trio of the Philippine Opera Company featuring Daniella Silab, Nicola Lim and Sheena Bentoy — spun an afternoon of pure sonic enchantment.
THE historic Goldenberg Mansion in Malacañang.
Under the visionary mantle of The Goldenberg Concert Series, championed by First Lady Liza Araneta-Marcos, the stately venue became an opulent canvas for pure vocal sovereignty. Seamlessly gliding through a sweeping, twelve-song sonic showcase, the trio breathed a living soul into the majestic heritage space, leaving an audience of students, dignitaries, and culture epicureans profoundly enchanted.
Accompanied by the impeccable, rhythmic poetry of renowned pianist Kim Dominik Sarmiento, the performance elegantly deconstructed traditional boundaries. The evening’s carefully curated repertoire emerged as a lyrical triumph, masterfully weaving the high glamor of operatic masterpieces with the romantic nostalgia of silver-screen cinematic soundtracks, before culminating in a breathtaking homage to iconic OPM classics.

The setlist brilliantly balanced high-art refinement with surprising contemporary twists, treating guests to a diverse auditory voyage that moved seamlessly from classical heights to cinematic greats to the uplifting notes of the iconic anthem “Umagang Kay Ganda,” BINI’s infectious “Pantropiko” re-imagined with a classical flair, and a standout, fiercely passionate rendition of the beloved kundiman classic, “Pamaypay ng Maynila.” 
It stood not merely as a concert, but as a deliberate celebration of world-class Filipino talent, where heritage, modern sophistication, and ancestral musical threads were irrevocably, beautifully intertwined.

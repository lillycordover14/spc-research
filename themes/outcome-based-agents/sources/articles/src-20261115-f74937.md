COLUMBIA, Mo. – 
Gabe Whisenhunt
 won the 125-pound championship Saturday to lead the Oregon State wrestling team at the Tiger Style Invite at Hearnes Center. The Beavers placed 10 wrestlers in the top six and finished fourth as a team with 89 points.


Whisenhunt went 4-0 on the day. He pinned Morgan State's Garrett Beane in 2:52 and Little Rock's Isaiah Jones in 1:34, blanked South Dakota State's Carson Dupill, 6-0, in the semifinals, and beat South Dakota State's Brady Roark, 15-8, in the title match.

Nash Singleton
 (141) and 
Daschle Lamer
 (174) each finished second. Singleton beat Missouri's Yandel Morales, 12-9, and Easton Hilton, 3-2, before falling to Missouri's Zan Fugitt, 4-2, in the final. Lamer pinned Little Rock's Isael Perez in 1:00, won by default over Missouri's Jake Stoffel and beat Purdue's Brody Baumann, 11-5, to reach the final, where he was pinned by Missouri's Cam Steed at 6:21.

TJ McDonnell
 (184) and 
Khale McDonnell
 (HWT) each took third. 
TJ McDonnell
 beat Purdue's James Rowley, 8-7, in the third-place match after wins over South Dakota State's Brock Fettig, Little Rock's Jarrel Miller and teammate 
Cory Thomas
. 
Khale McDonnell
 beat Morgan State's Bence Veress, 11-4, for third after a 3-2 tiebreaker win over teammate 
Ricky Thomas
.

Manny Saldate
 (133) and 
Cory Thomas
 (184) each placed fifth, with Saldate winning 5-2 in sudden victory over Missouri's Zion Borge and Thomas pinning Jarrel Miller in 0:52. 
Hunter Taylor
 (125), 
Beau Priest
 (149) and 
Ricky Thomas
 (HWT) each placed sixth. 
Joel Adams
 (165) pinned Morgan State's Sincere Bailey and Joshua Greenwood, and Ayden

Garver (149) won by technical fall, 29-12, over Little Rock's Dillon Cooper.


Missouri won the team title with 163 points, followed by South Dakota State (121), Purdue (108.5), Oregon State (89), Little Rock (39.5) and Morgan State (36).


The Beavers return home to face Northern Illinois at 7 p.m. PT on Friday, Nov. 20 at Gill Coliseum.

T
his year be sure to add MAHS to your shopping list on Saturday, December 5, from 10am to 4pm, at the Kingsley-Jenter House, located at 302 E Main St. We have selected an exciting group of artisans with fabulous items that you won’t want to miss!

Vendors include: Margaret Shaw — new paintings, handwoven and sundry items; Maggie’s Primitives — candles, beeswax ornaments, fabric boxes, and more; Peter Knox — handmade sourdough crackers, Delish!; Bob Rickman — photography and cyanotypes; and Walz Family Farm — handmade beef tallow skincare products. Also selling their wares will be Manchester students from Caife Aspire; BAAM — customized bamboo and thermal products; FIZZ and Beans — coffee and bath bombs; Subzero Sweets — freeze-dried candy; Seasonal Soaps and Lotions; Flicker and Fragrance — candles; and a slew of stocking stuffers! And, finally, find some great new gift items in the MAHS Gift Shop.

The MAHS Blacksmith Shop will also be welcoming visitors from 10am to 4pm with blacksmithing demonstrations, live bluegrass music with Still Pickin’ Bluegrass Band, and hand-forged items.

The Manchester Area Historical Society is a small, friendly group with a lively sense of history. We invite you to join in our efforts to build a better local history record. “You can’t really know where you are going until you know where you have been.” —Maya Angelou

Don’t miss out on the fun!

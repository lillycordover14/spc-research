Key facts

Source: ElevenLabs
LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM — ElevenLabs has added native integrations that let enterprises run its ElevenAgents voice agents inside four contact center platforms: NiCE CXone, Genesys Cloud, Amazon Connect and Five9. Other platforms can connect over Session Initiation Protocol (SIP), so companies can automate repetitive calls without changing the systems underneath.
The agents work inside existing call flows. The contact center passes context to the agent at the start of a call, and the agent verifies the caller, completes a task such as rescheduling an appointment or filing a claim, and logs the outcome in the customer relationship management (CRM) system.
Calls go back to people with context
If a caller asks for a person, the agent hands the call back through the existing routing with full context, ElevenLabs said in a 
blog post
 on Nov. 4. The same agent also works across other channels, including chat.
ElevenLabs said customers can choose from more than 17,000 voices, or create their own brand voice, in over 90 languages. It said the agents handle interruptions, background noise, accents and complex alphanumeric information.
“Our native integration with ElevenLabs gives joint customers another way to extend their CXone environment, inside the call flows, routing, and reporting they already run,” said Jeff Comstock, president of product and technology at 
NiCE
.
How each platform connects
Genesys
 customers use a native Audio Connector integration that needs no SIP trunking. In Amazon Connect, the platform’s AI agent can bring in an ElevenLabs agent during a live contact, and control returns to Amazon Connect when the task is done.
Five9
 transfers calls over SIP through AI Agent Connect, a Five9 add-on, and receives routing and disposition data back. ElevenAgents also works with telephony providers including Twilio, Vonage, Telnyx, Plivo, Bandwidth and Exotel.
AMC Health chose ElevenLabs to prototype, build and host its AI voice agents. “Genesys is already our telephony platform, so the native integration was an important factor in our decision,” said Tom Kramer, chief technology officer at AMC Health.
What it means for contact centers
ElevenLabs is selling voice agents as an add-on to the contact center platforms enterprises already run, not as a replacement for them. It pitches lower cost per resolved call, extra capacity for call spikes and round-the-clock coverage, with people kept for complex conversations.

Sargento Teams With Country Star Hannah Harper for 5-Ton Donation of Cheese&nbsp;&nbsp;WHBL News

[page text]
10,000 pounds of Sargento Cheese has been delivered to a Tennessee food bank in a partnership with a former American Idol and current country music star.  Earlier this Monday morning, Sargento delivered pallets of real, natural cheese to the Second Harvest Food Bank of Middle Tennessee.  Harper’s connection to Wisconsin’s best-known product began with her hit release titled “String Cheese”.  Her tour of the same name was sponsored by Sargento, and she has since maintained a relationship with the Sheboygan County company.  Today’s donation came at a critical time for communities across Tennessee, where one in seven people experience food insecurity, including one in five children, according to Second Harvest Food Bank of Middle Tennessee.

Sargento’s longstanding commitment to hunger relief also includes Touchdowns for Hunger – a partnership with the Green Bay Packers – and its Double Helping for Hunger initiative with the Milwaukee Brewers.  Since its founding in 1953, Sargento has contributed millions of dollars to hunger relief, donated hundreds of thousands of pounds of food, and supported local meal programs to feed families and children in need.

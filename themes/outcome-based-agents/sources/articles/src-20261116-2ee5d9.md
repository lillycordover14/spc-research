A longtime late night television host is planning an appearance in West Virginia next year. 
Jay Leno has scheduled an evening of standup comedy at the Clay Center in Charleston on Friday, May 5, according to a news release from the venue.
Leno has spent decades entertaining live audiences and is best known for his run on NBC’s "The Tonight Show."
According to the Clay Center, member presale tickets go live at 10 a.m. on Wednesday, Nov. 18. Remaining tickets will be avalaible at 10 a.m. on Friday, Nov. 20.

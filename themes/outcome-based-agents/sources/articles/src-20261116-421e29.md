		Saints Hockey Notch Second-Straight Win at Home

               Kaleb Boyer kaleb.boyer@stignacenews.com

                  November 16, 2026            

·

 0                

Tyler Christensen cuts horizontally through the neutral zone during a crushing victory over the Findlay Force Saturday, November 7, 2026. (Kaleb Boyer/St. Ignace News)

After a rough 0-4 start, the St. Ignace Saints hockey team is riding high and finding rhythm after  downing the visiting Findlay Force 8-3 for their second straight win at Little Bear East Arena Saturday, November 7.

Fresh off their first win over Alpena on Halloween
, the Saints (1-4-0) welcomed the Force (0-5-1) to Little Bear East Arena for the first-time ever. Based out of Findlay, Ohio, the Force are a newcomer to the MIHL, and this was the first matchup between the Saints and Force in their history.

Daniel Rouillard looks over his shoulder during a crushing victory over the Findlay Force Saturday, November 7, 2026. (Kaleb Boyer/St. Ignace News)

St. Ignace put the Force on their backfoot to open the game, and the Saints had plenty of scoring chances that were turned away. Findlay forced a few turnovers in return but failed to capitalize. St. Ignace quickly regained the upper hand, and Saints forward Brent Defour had a prime scoring opportunity denied after a Findlay defender slipped and coughed up the puck.

Adam Kahpeaysewat finally rewarded the home fans and buried the opening goal from in front of the net at the 9:56 mark, 1-0 Saints. Jordan Ritchie and Jeremy Pallot-Behling set up the play and picked up an assist each. The Saints looked to add another around the 7:05 mark, but Force netminder Glen Bates froze the puck. 

Jordan Ritchie moves the puck through the neutral zone during a crushing victory over the Findlay Force Saturday, November 7, 2026. (Kaleb Boyer/St. Ignace News)

When play resumed, Findlay took the puck the other way, and Reese Boehme scored on a pass from Dalton Teeter to even the score, 1-1, at the 6:42 mark. Just over a minute later, Findlay forward Jason Wietecki shoveled a pass from Austin Nowak into the net to put the Force in front, 2-1 Force, at 5:29.

The Force nearly added another at 2;21, but Saints goalie Daniel Rouillard made a sprawling save. Findlay took the 2-1 advantage into the first intermission, and the Saints needed to regroup to erase the deficit. 

The Saints were firing on all cylinders to start the second period, and Ritchie fed a pass to Caleb Mckerchie, who rifled the puck into the net to tie the game, 2-2, just 47 seconds into the period.

When it rained, it poured for the Force, and Tristan Holzwarth put the Saints back on top, 3-2 Saints, at 14:09. Kahpeaysewat and Dominic Pelaccio assisted on the play. Moments later, Saints captain Logan Dekeyser added another goal, assisted by Ritchie and Michael Barone, at the 13:33 mark to extend the lead, 4-2 Saints. 

The rout was on. 

Daniel Rouillard tends the net during a crushing victory over the Findlay Force Saturday, November 7, 2026. (Kaleb Boyer/St. Ignace News)

Assisted by Jake Greathous, Boehme managed to score his second goal of the game for the Force at 9:57, 4-3 Saints, but the Saints stifled the visitors for the remaining 30 minutes of play. 

At 9:36, Lee Roberts was assessed a minor penalty for hooking, and Ritchie joined his teammate in the box at 8:08 for cross-checking. Findlay had a 5-on-3 powerplay, but the Saints penalty kill stood tall and killed both penalties. 

As the period was winding down, Mckerchie passed to Dekeyser, who fired the puck off the backboard behind the goalie. Tyler Christensen reacted quickly, gathered the puck, and shoved it into a wide open net at point-blank range at 5:47 to double the lead, 5-3 Saints. Dekeyser and Mckerchie earned assists.

The Force had another 5-on-3 powerplay toward the end of the period, when Roberts was caught for cross-checking at 3:14 and Ritchie earned a delay-of-game penalty at 1:07. The Saints easily killed the first penalty and then finished off the remaining penalty at the start of the third period.

In the third period, Ritchie wrist-shot the puck into the top left corner and all-but-ended Findlay’s comeback hopes, 6-3 Saints, at the 16:27 mark. Barone assisted on the play. 

Three minutes later at 13:04, Christensen scored his second goal of the game, assisted by Dekeyser and Mckerchie, to widen the gap, 7-3 Saints. 

Rory Mulready battles for the puck in the opposing end during a crushing victory over the Findlay Force Saturday, November 7, 2026. (Kaleb Boyer/St. Ignace News)

Findlay’s Eric Seferian went to the box at 8:28, and the Saints powerplay unit took the ice for the first time. They made quick work of the Force, and Lawrence Rife scored at 8:15, assisted by Gavin Darlow and Barone, 8-3 Saints. 

Force captain Matt Omiecinski went to the box at 1:43, and the Saints powerplay had another opportunity to widen the blowout. The Saints tried to feed Christensen the puck to help him complete a hattrick, but a few of his shots were turned away as the Saints won 8-3.

St. Ignace’s special team units powered the Saints to an easy victory. The powerplay went one-for-two, and the penalty kill turned away four man-advantages from the Force, including two 5-on-3 opportunities.

“We definitely did a lot better than previous games,” Pallot-Behling, owner, coach, and player of the Saints, said. “I think we’re finally starting to work as a team. We’re finally starting to know each other, understand each other, and I think that’s what made the difference on the power play.”

The Saints crowd Adam Kahpeaysewat, after he scored the opening goal during a crushing victory over the Findlay Force Saturday, November 7, 2026. (Kaleb Boyer/St. Ignace News)

Pallot-Behling said the MIHL’s expansion this year has had some impact on the team’s slow start. A few of the Saints’ players from their inaugural 2025-2026 season went to other down-state teams, since they were closer to where they lived. Pallot-Behling said he had to add a few new names to his roster this year.

“It was a whole locker room of new guys basically,” Pallot-Behling said. “Probably got 12, 13 new guys. So, we didn’t know each other. So, those first couple games were tough, especially playing against the tougher teams in the north. And I think now that we know each other, it’s making it a little bit easier.”

Caleb Wood carries the puck toward the opposing blue-line during a crushing victory over the Findlay Force Saturday, November 7, 2026. (Kaleb Boyer/St. Ignace News)

Three Stars of the Game

Jordan Ritchie, 1 G, 3 A, 4 Pts

Tyler Christensen, 2 G, 0 A, 2 Pts

Caleb Mckerchie, 1 G, 2A, 3 Pts

Honorable mentions
: Logan Dekeyser, 1 G, 2A, 3 Pts; Michael Barone 0 G, 3 A, 3 Pts; Daniel Rouillard saved 22 of 25 shots 88.0 save percentage, 3 GA.

The Saints (2-4-0) next hit the road against the Waterford Sharks (4-1-0)in Waterford, Michigan, Saturday, November 14.

        Exclusive: Canadian Country Artist Steven Lee Olsen Inks Global Distribution Deal with ADA/Warner Music Canada
    
Teaming up with the Canadian label's independent distribution arm, the multi-platinum singer-songwriter will release a new single, "Good Lord," this Friday (Nov. 20), as ADA continues to ink deals with Canadian artists and music companies.
Stefano Rebuli
Nov 16, 2026
Steven Lee Olsen

        Courtesy Photo
    
ADA and Warner Music Canada have signed Canadian singer-songwriter Steven Lee Olsen to a global distribution deal, making him the latest artist to join the label's independent distribution arm.
Over the past decade, the Canadian country singer-songwriter has been making waves in Nashville and at home, earning a handful of top 10 hits on the Billboard Canada Country airplay chart and even a No. 1 hit on the U.S. Billboard Hot Country Songs and Country Airplay charts as a songwriter on Keith Urban's 2016 track "Blue Ain't Your Color."
"I’ve spent the last year and a half locked in the studio in Nashville, making music that captures who I am and where I’m headed," Olsen says. "I’ve never been more proud of the songs or more excited about what’s ahead. To have Warner Music Canada and ADA in my corner as we bring this next chapter to life means the world to me.”
advertisement
Olsen is set to launch his new era with his upcoming single, "Good Lord," out this Friday (Nov. 20). He's gearing up for a busy year driven by the new partnership, with plans for a new LP and touring all across the country. After wrapping up a recent nationwide touring stint across Canada as part of Tyler Braden's The Days of Gold tour, the country artist will hit the road in Ontario and Western Canada in February and March, followed by a summer festival run and a return to Canada for a full headline tour in October and November 2027.
"Steven has played a huge part in the sound of modern country music as both a world-class songwriter and a recording artist,” says Eric Wong, president of Warner Music Canada, president of East West Records U.S. and EVP of Recorded Music at Warner Music Group. “We are proud to partner with Steven for this next chapter and look forward to championing ‘Good Lord’ and his vision through 2027 and beyond.”
advertisement
Olsen is no stranger to making hits on home turf. More than a decade after earning his first top 3 hit on the Canadian airwaves with "Raised by a Good Time," he has maintained a presence on the chart. He most recently cracked the top 10 with his CCMA-nominated track "Outta Yours" and "Out Here" in 2023, and "Bigger Than This Town" the following year.
ADA continues to expand its roster, solidifying itself as an all-rounder that represents an array of diverse musical genres. Since 2025, the independent distribution label has signed global distribution deals with Indigenous singer-songwriter 
Shawnee Kish
, Punjabi music company 
EYP Creations
, the Toronto-based R&B label 
Escape Records
 and fellow Canadian country singer-songwriter 
Zach McPhee
.

Be a Holiday Gift-Giving Superstar at Midtown Artisan Markets&nbsp;&nbsp;Rough Draft Atlanta

[page text]
Looking for holiday gifts that are a little more thoughtful, creative and one-of-a-kind? Skip the usual shopping routine and head to 
Midtown Green
, where Atlanta’s local makers are ready to help you become a holiday gift-giving superstar!

The 
Midtown Artisan Markets at Midtown Green
 return for two final Sundays this year: 
November 22 and December 13, from noon to 5 p.m.
 Both markets are free and open to the public, offering a festive afternoon of shopping, live music, food, family-friendly activities and seasonal surprises.

Find the perfect gift (and support local makers!)

Each market will feature 20 to 30 local artisans and small businesses offering a variety of handmade goods, art, jewelry, accessories, vintage finds, home décor, candles, bath and body products and more.

Whether you’re shopping for the person who has everything, picking up something special for a friend or treating yourself along the way, you’ll find plenty of inspiration. And the best part? Every purchase supports the talented artists, makers and entrepreneurs who help make Atlanta’s creative community so vibrant.

Forget the big-box stores and crowded malls. These markets offer a more personal way to tackle your holiday shopping list while enjoying a relaxing Sunday afternoon outdoors.

Holiday fun beyond the shopping

There’s more to discover than great gifts! Both markets will feature food and treats available for purchase, lawn games, and opportunities to connect with local arts and community organizations.

At the November 22 market
, visitors can get into the holiday spirit with a special photo booth experience where they can turn their photos into keepsake ornaments—perfect for the tree or as a personalized gift.

Alliance Theatre
 will also join the festivities to share information about its holiday production of 
A Christmas Carol
, including a special ticket giveaway. Georgia Tech Arts will be onsite showcasing its upcoming 2027 performance lineup at the Ferst Center for the Arts, with another exciting giveaway opportunity.

At the December 13 market
, the holiday cheer continues with a special giveaway of s’mores and hot chocolate, offering a sweet way to celebrate the season with family and friends.

With something different happening at each market, there’s plenty of reason to visit both!

Make holiday memories in Midtown

The Midtown Artisan Markets are about more than checking names off your gift list. They’re an opportunity to slow down during the busy holiday season, spend time with friends and family, and enjoy a festive afternoon in the heart of Atlanta.

The markets take place at 
Midtown Green
, a new four-acre public gathering space created by Midtown Alliance at 98 14th Street. Once a large vacant lot, the site has been transformed into an expansive lawn with walking paths and room to gather, relax and enjoy community programming while plans for its 
long-term future
 continue to take shape.

Located in the heart of Midtown’s Arts District, Midtown Green is also a great starting point for a full day in the neighborhood. After shopping, 
explore
 nearby cultural destinations, grab a bite at one of Midtown’s many restaurants or simply enjoy the afternoon outdoors.

So gather your shopping list, bring your favorite people and get ready to discover gifts that are as unique as the people receiving them.

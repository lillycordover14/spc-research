Harper Beckham's private Instagram photo with David proves she's the ultimate daddy's girl
David and Victoria Beckham's only daughter, 15-year-old Harper, shared the sweetest tribute to her dad on her private Instagram account.
 
 
 

Laura Sutcliffe
Fashion and Beauty News Editor
Updated: 9 hours ago
Nov 16, 2026, 12:51 PM GMT
LEAVE A COMMENT
 

Harper Beckham
 is known to have a famously close relationship 
with her dad, David
. The pair are often seen enjoying each other's company, and on Sunday, the 15-year-old shared the sweetest throwback picture of herself as a toddler, cuddling up to her dad at a basketball match and lifting his cap off his head. 
The teenager penned: "I love you so much @davidbeckham", with a series of pink hearts. Harper's account is private, but fans were able to see it as David re-shared the picture.

Harper shared the sweetest picture of herself as a child with dad, David Beckham

In the sentimental snap, Harper looked to be around two years old and wore a tiny pair of jeans and a white top.

Harper Beckham's fabulous childhood wardrobe
The youngest Beckham offspring has always been a style icon in her own right, from before she could even talk.
From a tender age, she coveted some of the most exclusive and high-end designer labels out there, including Burberry, Gucci and Chloé.

© Photo:  Getty Images
Harper wore the sweetest Marc Jacobs coat

In 2014, aged two, Harper had lunch with her family at Balthazar in New York City. Carried by her father, David, she upstaged her former footballer dad in a grey faux fur coat by Little Marc Jacobs, white tights and dainty pink ballet pumps.

© Instagram
David and Harper posed at Buckingham Palace

As she got older, Harper developed her own sense of style. In 2017, she had a 
royal tea party
 with friends at Buckingham Palace, sporting a butterfly eyelet dress by Chloé that featured delicate lace detail.

© Getty
Harper and David at the Burberry show in 2015

In 2015, little Harper joined her three brothers and parents at the
 Burberry runway show,
 where she sat next to Vogue Editor-in-Chief Anna Wintour, donning a mini trench dress by the iconic British label. With her honey-blonde hair flowing and cuddled up nervously on her footballing legend dad David's knee, it was an adorable yet iconic fashion moment.
Although designer labels are often at the forefront of her mind as a teenager, we will never forget her Percy Pig onesie from Marks & Spencer.

© David Beckham Instagram
Harper wearing a humble M&S onesie 

When David celebrated his 47th birthday in 2022, he shared a carousel of pictures of his special day, which he spent with his wonderful family. In one of the pictures, little Harper looked adorable in the high street store's Percy Pig fleece onesie, which cost £18 from M&S at the time.

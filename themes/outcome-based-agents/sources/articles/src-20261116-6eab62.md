Joyce Ann (Harper) Webster, 76, of Buckhannon, West Virginia, passed away on Wednesday, November 11, 2026.

Born on February 25, 1950, in Buckhannon, she was the daughter of the late Rezlen and Mabel Harper. Joyce was born and raised in Buckhannon, where she lived until graduating from Buckhannon-Upshur High School. Her years as an Air Force wife took her across the country, giving her the opportunity to live in several different states and experience life beyond her West Virginia roots.

While living in North Carolina, Joyce worked for the Department of Human Services. In 1987, she moved to Colorado, where she began working at the Aero Club at the United States Air Force Academy. She spent many years there and absolutely loved it. Joyce had a passion for flying and never passed up an opportunity to get up in the air.

She later transferred to the Air Force Falcons Athletic Department at the Academy, where she worked as an accountant until her retirement in 2019. Following retirement, Joyce returned to her hometown of Buckhannon, where she remained until her passing.

Joyce was giving, funny, strong-willed, and full of personality. She had a big heart, a mind of her own, and a wonderful way of making people laugh. She wasn’t afraid to speak her mind or stand her ground, but that was part of what made her so unforgettable. Joyce was truly one of a kind, and that’s exactly how her family will remember her.

Joyce adored her dogs, who were never just pets but cherished members of her family. She loved them dearly and spoiled them every chance she got. She had two lifelong dreams: becoming a Rockette and dancing with Patrick Swayze. Although she never got to fulfill those dreams here on earth, we’d like to think she’s now kicking up her heels as a Rockette and finally getting that long-awaited dance with Patrick Swayze.

Joyce is survived by her daughters, Danielle Nicole Torrez (Mary Jane Torrez) of Buckhannon, West Virginia, and Mandy Lynette Weikal (Brian Weikal) of Colorado Springs, Colorado.

Her beloved grandchildren include Raigan Nicole Webster, Ethan Daniel Jaxon Torrez, Avery Jane Ann Torrez, and Kiona Rose Smith, all of Buckhannon; Autumn Reine Weikal (fiancé Steven Smith) and LeeAnn Nicole Weikal, all of Colorado Springs; Brianna Lynn (Weikal) Marchand (Korey Marchand) of Texas; and Brooke Amber Weikal of Tennessee.

She also leaves behind her great-grandchildren, Matix Lee Snell and Lynne Faith Marchand, and her great-grandson, Dawson, expected to arrive in December 2026. Although Joyce won’t be here to welcome him into the world, her family finds comfort in believing she has already met him in Heaven and will be watching over him with love.

Joyce is also survived by her brother, Michael Lee Harper, and sister, Brenda H. Hyre, both of Buckhannon; and sister, Pauline H. Hackworth of Florida. She leaves behind many beloved nieces and nephews, great-nieces and nephews, and great-great-nieces and nephews, all of whom held a special place in her heart.

Joyce was preceded in death by her parents, Rezlen and Mabel Harper; her brothers-in-law, Richard Hyre and Richard Hackworth; and her nephews, “Lil’ Pete” Hyre and Brian Harper.

Honoring Joyce’s wishes, she will be cremated, and no formal funeral or memorial service will be held. Her ashes will be lovingly scattered alongside those of her cherished dogs in several places that held special meaning to her, keeping them together in the places she loved.

In lieu of flowers, the family kindly requests that memorial donations be made to The Friends Auxiliary at William R. Sharpe Jr. Hospital, an organization dedicated to supporting patients facing mental health challenges and helping improve their quality of life.

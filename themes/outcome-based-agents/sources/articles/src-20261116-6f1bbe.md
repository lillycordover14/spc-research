Frequently Asked Questions
What is the 3-month dapivirine vaginal ring?
It is a user-controlled vaginal ring releasing dapivirine over 3 months to reduce the risk of HIV acquisition, replaced 4 times per year instead of monthly.
How does dapivirine prevent HIV?
Dapivirine blocks the ability of HIV to replicate inside healthy cells, and the ring releases the drug locally near the cervix.
What data supported the EMA opinion?
The phase 1 IPM 054 crossover trial in 124 South African women showed the 3-month ring was well tolerated and delivered higher dapivirine levels than the monthly ring.

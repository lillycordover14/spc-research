Melbourne, Australia-based sports technology company Pelaris has announced the general availability of its adaptive AI coaching platform across web, mobile (iOS and Android), and major AI assistant environments, including ChatGPT.

According to the Pelaris team, its platform is engineered to address a key challenge for multi-sport and hybrid athletes: building a single, periodised training plan that coordinates workouts across swimming, cycling, running, triathlon, and strength training.

By sequencing inter-disciplinary workloads back from a target event date, the system helps to prevent conflicting high-intensity sessions across different sports from landing on consecutive days.

Multi-sport integration and technical capabilities

Pelaris constructs training blocks – covering base, build, specific, and taper phases – for target events ranging from introductory 10km runs to 100km ultra-marathons, full iron distance triathlons, and open-water swimming events.

Key technical and architectural features include:

Established Training Methodologies: Programs draw from 33 established sports science methodologies, including Pfitzinger, Daniels, Hansons, Lydiard, and 80/20 for running; 
Swim Smooth
 and Total Immersion for swimming; and Sweet Spot, Polarised, and Coggan frameworks for cycling.

AI Assistant Integration: Connected via the open Model Context Protocol (MCP), Pelaris functions natively inside ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, and Muse. Athletes can inspect weekly workloads, adjust upcoming workouts, or log activities within their preferred AI chat interface, with changes syncing across devices.

Two-Way Wearable Syncing: Features bidirectional integration with Garmin Connect, automatically pushing two weeks of planned sessions to the Garmin calendar and adjusting future workouts upon receiving completed activity data. The platform also integrates with Strava, Polar, Apple Health, and Health Connect, offering deduplication for activities logged across multiple sources, alongside FIT file imports for COROS, Suunto, Wahoo, and Polar.

Adaptive Recalibration: Training schedules dynamically adapt based on completed workouts, missed sessions, acute fatigue markers, and reported sleep data.

Bradley Hunt, Founder of Pelaris, said “Most endurance athletes I know aren’t only endurance athletes. They lift, they play a team sport on the weekend, and they have a job and a family. The plan has to hold all of that. Most apps track what you did. Pelaris works out what comes next: one periodised plan around the event date, a reason for every session, and a week that changes when life changes it. The idea is to stop tracking and to start navigating.”

Hunt added “Good coaching has always been priced for the few. Our goal is that anyone training for a first 10 km or a first Ironman gets a plan built on proven methods, one that keeps up with their week, at a price they can stay on for years. Tracking is free forever.”

Free sports science tools and pricing model

Alongside the core application, Pelaris has launched 20 free sports science calculators at 
pelaris.io/tools
, covering race time predictions, VO2max estimates, heart rate zone models, sweat rate and sodium fueling protocols, and HYROX station split estimations.

The platform operates on a freemium structure. Basic activity tracking and logging remain free indefinitely, with new users receiving a full periodised plan and 30 days of adaptive coaching features upon sign-up without requiring a credit card. Paid coaching subscriptions are priced at £15 / €15 / US$15 per month ,or £120 / €120 / US$120 annually.

Janji Men's AURAS Ultrafleece Half-Zip Hoodie Review Ultralight Warmth!

Article by Zack Russell
Janji Men's AURAS Ultrafleece Half-Zip Hoodie
 
($124)
Introduction
Janji releases the AURAS Ultrafleece Half-Zip as one of the lightest cold weather fleeces on the market.  
The inside is lined with their proprietary Astra fleece to keep you warm in mild to colder temperatures. The fabric is designed to pull moisture away from your body, but while also trapping air to keep you warm.  
The outside is made up of a lightweight mesh. This jacket is ideal for carrying in your pack for longer days in the mountains, due to its lightweight warmth. It folds down super small to fit in any pack and even a waistbelt.  
For as light as it is, this jacket kept me warm in 40 degree temperatures at night with a t- shirt and sub freezing temperatures with a long sleeve underneath.  
While not designed as a winter jacket, it is the perfect piece to easily throw on and off in changing temperatures that you encounter in the mountains and during long ultras.
Features
The AURAS ultrafleece top features thumbholes in the sleeves to keep hands warm.  
It has a ¼ zipper in the front. I wore the hood during some colder runs and barely noticed it on my head given how light the material is.  It also didn’t make noise like many windproof or rain jackets do, which gets annoying after a while.  
The zipper did slide down some during my runs. It is extremely lightweight and any pull on the jacket material causes the zipper to slide down when it is stressed.  
There is also a right-sided hip/rear pocket meant for packing this fleece down to fit in your running pack, pocket, or waist belt.  
Fit
I tested a size medium in the jacket.  I am 5’10 and 150lbs.  The jacket fit me pretty loosely in the shoulders and through the chest and torso. For a slimmer more  athletic fit I could have gone down to a small. 
So if you are in between sizes, I recommend going down a size.  I actually liked this looser fit when compared to other jackets I own that are slimmer in the mid section and narrow shoulders. The bottom of the jacket is not adjustable and features an open hem.  
Conclusions
I really like this jacket as an alternative to wind jackets that I typically use in the winter, although not in deep winter though.  
As I have been running before sun up and after sun down given my work, this piece was a perfect layer to throw on here in the mountains of Colorado as the day cools.  It was extremely easy to carry at 132 grams so it was barely noticeable. 
I definitely plan to use this jacket next spring and summer as the high alpine in Colorado opens from snow.  It will certainly be a staple in my running pack.  It is also a great gift for that ultrarunner who has everything as they certainly do not have a lightweight fleece like this.  Great job Janji!

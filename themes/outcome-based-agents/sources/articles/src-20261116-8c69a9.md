The Plymouth Artisan Market returned to Plymouth Community Center Nov. 14. The event was wide-ranging, with more than 70 community vendors from Plymouth, surrounding cities and beyond. Products included food, beverages, fine art, apparel, pottery, candles, wall art, woodwork, jewelry and more. Artists were spread out through the community center, taking over classrooms and various corridors in hopes of selling their goods and preparing everyone for the holiday season.

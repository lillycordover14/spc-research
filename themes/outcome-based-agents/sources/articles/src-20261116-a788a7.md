Burien firefighters find person dead at Dottie Harper Park Saturday morning, Nov. 14&nbsp;&nbsp;B-Town Blog

[page text]
A Burien fire crew found a person dead in Burien’s Dottie Harper Park on Saturday morning, Nov. 14 after stopping to investigate a strong odor, King County Fire District No. 2 said.

The crew from Aid 328 was driving past the park (map below) at about 10:45 a.m. when members reported a “horrible smell” and pulled over, Ray Desmarais, Assistant Chief and Fire Marshal told The B-Town Blog.

They found the person after entering the park and called 911 to request deputies.

 

 

“They simultaneously initiated patient care and quickly determined the person was deceased,” Desmarais said. 

The crew then stepped back to preserve any evidence and waited for deputies to arrive.

The King County Medical Examiner’s Office responded and conducted its investigation.

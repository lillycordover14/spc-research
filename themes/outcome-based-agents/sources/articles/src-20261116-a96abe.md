This past spring, 
PetaPixel
 partnered with photography t-shirt brand Harper Finch to bring you a limited-edition set of co-branded merch. It proved very popular, and we’re happy to announce that 
we’ve dropped round two
! 
Okeh, let’s talk about bokeh
!

Check Out the PetaPixel Merch Drop!

As a recap, 
Harper Finch
 is a curator of some excellent photography-inspired shirts, which is what put them on 
PetaPixel
‘s radar earlier this year. As viewers of the 
PetaPixel
 YouTube channel and 
The PetaPixel Podcast
 may have noticed, Chris Niccolls, Jordan Drake, and I have been sporting some of the brand’s designs for the past couple of months and even previewing some of the designs from this drop more recently.

This drop is headlined by bokeh! Fans of the show will most certainly recognize Chris’s phrase, “Okeh, let’s talk about bokeh,” from many of his lens reviews, and now you can wear those words on your chest in two designs! This drop also debuts a new color option: natural! All of the t-shirts in this drop are available in this new color option.

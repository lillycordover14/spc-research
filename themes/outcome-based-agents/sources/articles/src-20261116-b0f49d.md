Xtremis Awarded U.S. Army AUGUR SHIELD Contract to Advance Next-Generation Counter-UAS Capabilities

WASHINGTON
 — Xtremis today announced it has been awarded a contract supporting the U.S. Army Combat Capabilities Development Command Army Research Laboratory (DEVCOM ARL) under the AUGUR SHIELD initiative, a multi-year effort to develop advanced electromagnetic spectrum capabilities that improve the detection and mitigation of increasingly sophisticated unmanned aerial system (UAS) threats.

The AUGUR SHIELD program addresses the Department of War’s need to detect and counter rapidly evolving modified commercial systems that use frequency-agile communications to evade traditional radio frequency-based sensor systems.

As unmanned threats continue to evolve, military operators require capabilities that can continuously identify, characterize, and adapt to new RF signatures in real time rather than relying solely on pre-existing threat libraries.

Under the contract, Xtremis will develop and integrate AI-driven spectrum monitoring and multi-domain sensor capabilities that enable rapid detection, characterization, and dissemination of emerging UAS signatures. The effort combines persistent spectrum monitoring with electro-optical, infrared, radar, and acoustic sensing to improve confidence in identifying and tracking airborne threats while enabling the rapid development of targeted countermeasures.

“AUGUR SHIELD represents the next evolution of electromagnetic operations,” said Adam Jay Harrison, Chief Executive Officer of Xtremis. “Traditional counter-drone systems were designed for known threats. Today’s battlefield demands systems that can learn, adapt, and respond as quickly as adversaries innovate. We’re honored to partner with the Army Research Laboratory to help deliver that capability.”

The initiative builds upon Xtremis’ expertise in spectrum intelligence, artificial intelligence, and operational electromagnetic awareness. At the core of the effort is the company’s SpectrumOS platform, which has been deployed in support of military operations and exercises, providing persistent wideband spectrum awareness in complex operational environments.

Initial activities under the program include operational experimentation, AI model development, spectrum data collection, and integration of multi-domain sensing technologies into a unified operational framework. The architecture is designed to support future deployments across multiple Army operational environments while strengthening the Department of Defense’s ability to counter increasingly sophisticated unmanned systems.

“Warfighters need technologies that can work across multi-sensing domains to maintain overmatch beyond traditional radio-frequency communications. AUGUR SHIELD is a research program focused on providing this capability and real time decision making that reduces the cognitive and development load across multiple sensing technologies. The Army Research Laboratory leads this research program as a means to conduct proof-of-concept experiments across multiple scientific disciplines to advance sensing technologies,” said Cameron Weir ARL Electronics Warfare researcher and AUGUR SHIELD program lead.

The award reflects the Army’s continued investment in artificial intelligence and spectrum-enabled capabilities as critical components of future force modernization. By combining machine learning, software-defined radio technologies, and advanced RF sensing, AUGUR SHIELD aims to provide military operators with faster, more accurate situational awareness and a scalable approach to countering rapidly evolving aerial threats.

About Xtremis

Xtremis develops advanced spectrum intelligence technologies that enable organizations to see, understand, and act within the electromagnetic environment. The company’s AI-enabled software and hardware solutions provide real-time spectrum awareness, RF characterization, and operational decision support for defense, national security, and critical infrastructure missions.

About DEVCOM ARL

DEVCOM ARL is the Army’s sole fundamental research laboratory serving as the nexus of science between the military, academia and industry. Operating under the U.S. Army Futures and Autonomous Systems Command, ARL executes globally recognized research to accelerate delivery of war-winning, disruptive technologies for tomorrow’s Army.

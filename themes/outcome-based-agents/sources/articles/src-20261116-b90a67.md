Bringing natural, intelligent voice interactions into customer experiences and enterprise workflows
 
 
HONG KONG, Nov. 16, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Master Concept, a leading cloud and AI solutions provider in Asia, announces its partnership with ElevenLabs, an AI research and product company, to help enterprises unlock new opportunities in voice-powered AI applications. The collaboration aims to make AI interactions more natural, accessible, and actionable across customer engagement and business operations.
 
 
 
 
Master Concept Partners with ElevenLabs to Accelerate Enterprise Voice AI Adoption
 
 
 
As enterprises accelerate AI adoption, interactions are evolving beyond text-based interfaces toward more intuitive, conversational experiences. Voice AI is becoming an increasingly important interface between people and technology, enabling users to communicate naturally while AI systems understand requests, retrieve information, and perform tasks.
 
 
 
 
 
ElevenLabs offers advanced AI voice technologies, including natural-sounding speech synthesis, multilingual capabilities, and conversational AI agents. Its platform enables businesses to build responsive, voice-enabled experiences that can connect with existing knowledge bases and enterprise systems, supporting applications ranging from customer service and virtual assistants to appointment scheduling and interactive commerce.
 
 
Through this partnership, Master Concept aims to combine ElevenLabs' voice AI capabilities with its expertise in cloud infrastructure, enterprise AI, and solution integration. Together, the companies will help organizations explore practical applications of conversational AI, integrate intelligent voice interactions into existing workflows, and move from isolated AI demonstrations toward real business value.
 
 
The collaboration also reflects Master Concept's broader commitment to building a comprehensive enterprise AI ecosystem, connecting innovative AI technologies with the infrastructure, integration expertise, and business understanding required for enterprise adoption.
 
 
 
 
 
As AI continues to reshape how people interact with digital services, Master Concept and ElevenLabs look forward to helping businesses create more intuitive, responsive, and human-centered experiences powered by voice AI.

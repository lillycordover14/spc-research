El Paso County Expands ADA Access at ﻿Bear Creek Regional Dog Park&nbsp;&nbsp;El Paso County Colorado

[page text]
November 16, 2026

November 16, 2026
 — The El Paso County ADA Team is upgrading accessible parking and the pathway into Bear Creek Regional Dog Park at 1704 South 21st Street, Colorado Springs, CO. 80904.

Construction is scheduled for November 19–December 18, 2026.

During this time, on-site parking will be limited. Additional parking will be available at:

El Paso County Parks Headquarters, southeast of 21st Street and West Rio Grande Street.

Bear Creek Regional Park, directly south of the dog park at 21st Street and Argus Road.

The north side of Bear Creek Dog Park along Lower Gold Camp Road.

Entry from the dog park parking lot will temporarily move to the maintenance entrance on the south side of the dog park. Please follow posted signs to the temporary entrance.

The County thanks the public for their patience as we work to improve access to the park.

Panthers on pace to set new NFL records for most penalties and penalty yards this season

By

Michael David Smith

By

Michael David Smith

The Panthers beat the Saints on Sunday despite being assessed a whopping 17 penalties for 152 yards. If they keep it up, they’re going to end this season with more penalties and more penalty yards than any team has ever had.
Through nine games, the Panthers lead the NFL with 87 penalties for 787 yards. That puts them on pace for 164 penalties for 1,487 yards in 17 games, both of which would be new NFL records. The current NFL records were set by the 2011 Oakland Raiders, who were assessed 163 penalties for 1,358 yards.
Panthers coach Dave Canales said after Sunday’s game that he can accept some penalties that are naturally going to happen over the course of a game, but he doesn’t accept mental mistakes like jumping offside.
“Some of the penalties that are judgment calls, they’re gonna call them how they see them,” Canales said. “The things that I’m gonna focus on are the ones we have to take control of, which are offside penalties, pre-snap, delay of games, those type of procedural penalties. We’ve got to eliminate those. The competitive calls are going to happen. There’s ways to talk about the technique, but it’s really the things that we can control that we have to focus on.”
Asked if he had a problem with the officials on Sunday, who assessed the Saints just three penalties while the Panthers got 17, Canales answered, “I’m not gonna get into that.”

Canales did address the penalties with his team, however.
“We talked about the penalties. We set a new franchise record with 17, and we have to fix that. That’s not gonna be sustainable in this league, when the margins are so tight. The guys understand that,” Canales said.
What Canales will do is address what his own team can do better, perhaps improving enough over the second half of the season to avoid breaking a record that no coach wants to break.

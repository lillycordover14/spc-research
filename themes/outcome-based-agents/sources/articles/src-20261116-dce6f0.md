A new spot for craft beer, whiskey and bar bites is aiming to open soon in Clarendon.

Three Notch’d
 plans to open “this fall” below the Barnes & Noble at 2800 Clarendon Blvd, the restaurant and bar announced in a press release. It did not list a specific opening date.

The drink menu is expected to include beer and whiskey from the Charlottesville-based brewery and distillery, with options such as Virginia Light Lager, Minute Man IPA and Hatchet Head bourbon, plus canned cocktails like the vodka-based Rocket Pop.

Dining options, meanwhile, will focus on “creative pub food” like the “Nova Hot” fried chicken sandwich, the “Chesapeake Crunch” with crispy pollock and Old Bay tartar sauce, and house-made pretzel bites with beer cheese.

“The excitement is building and we are very excited to become a part of the Arlington community,” Three Notch’d President Scott Roth said.

Three Notch’d, located next to 
Xi’an Famous Foods
, also runs a 
beer club
 that provides three pints and two packs of the brewery’s beer for $20 per month.

The brewery has eight other locations, five of which are elsewhere in the commonwealth. This is its first spot in Northern Virginia.

                    EQT Ventures, the venture capital arm of EQT, is investing $10m in AI company Edra, which is developing a platform for automating tasks through integration with companies' existing systems. The Wall Street Journal reports.
The investment follows a four-week pilot project in which EQT tested Edra's technology in its internal IT support operations. 
EQT has continued using the technology to handle employee requests and plans to roll it out across more parts of the business.

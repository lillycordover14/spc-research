Filip Chytil and Linus Karlsson each scored two goals and Leevi Merilainen made 34 saves as the Vancouver Canucks snapped a seven-game losing streak with a 6-3 victory over the Vegas Golden Knights on Sunday night in Las Vegas.
Elias Pettersson recorded a goal and an assist, Brock Boeser had two assists and Drew O'Connor also scored a goal for Vancouver, which won in regulation for just the second time this season and for the first time since Oct. 3 against Calgary. It was the Canucks' first victory of any kind since they edged host Anaheim 6-5 in overtime on Oct. 27.
Mark Stone, Viktor Olofsson and Braeden Bowman scored goals and Mitch Marner had two assists for Vegas, which had an eight-game win streak over Vancouver snapped. Carter Hart stopped 21 of 26 shots.
The Canucks took a 1-0 lead at the 5:20 mark of the first period on a power- play goal by Pettersson, who scored his sixth goal when one-timed a shot from the right circle off a crossing pass by Jonathan Lekkerimaki.
Karlsson made it 2-0 midway through the period when he drove down the left wing, cut inside defenseman Shea Theodore and chipped in a shot past Hart's glove side.
Vegas closed the deficit to 2-1 at the 13:30 mark on a power-play goal by Olofsson, who slapped in a Tomas Hertl cross into an open left side of the net for his seventh goal.
Chytil, who came into the game without a goal, then scored two in a row to increase Vancouver's lead to 4-1. Chytil's second came early in the second period when the team was shorthanded on a breakaway off a Jamie Oleksiak stretch pass.
Bowman sliced the lead to 4-2 midway through the period with a one-timer from slot. Stone followed with his team-leading 11th goal on the power play at the 1:24 mark of the third period.
O'Connor then put the Canucks back up by two, 5-3, just 24 seconds later with a spinning rebound through Hart's pads.
Vegas pulled Hart for an extra attacker with three minutes remaining and Karlsson sealed the win with an empty-netter with 2:11 to go.
Stone moved into a tie for Jonathan Marchessault's franchise record for points with 417.

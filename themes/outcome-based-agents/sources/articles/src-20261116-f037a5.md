Shaffra establishes Saudi headquarters as enterprise AI workforce deployments expand

Mark Bowen
 | 
16 November, 2026

Shaffra has established its headquarters in Riyadh as it expands its Autonomous AI Teams across Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC, reporting significant operational savings from deployments in customer service, human resources and enterprise research.

Shaffra has established its headquarters in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, as the company expands its AI workforce deployments across enterprises and government organisations in the GCC.

The move builds on Shaffra’s existing operations across the region and supports its next phase of growth as Saudi Arabia increases investment in Artificial Intelligence.

The GCC-founded company develops Autonomous AI Teams designed to support customer-facing services and internal business operations, including recruitment, employee assessments, project management and research.

According to Shaffra, its AI Call Centre currently handles approximately 7,000 to 8,000 calls per day in Saudi Arabia.

The company has also deployed AI employees in human resources, with its recruitment solutions supporting an estimated 15,000 to 20,000 interviews.

In one customer deployment, Shaffra said annual employee assessment costs fell from approximately US$120,000–US$150,000 to US$30,000, while the number of employees assessed increased from 300 to 7,000.

The deployment enabled the organisation to extend assessment coverage across a larger workforce while reducing expenditure.

AI deployments deliver operational savings

Shaffra also reported that its AI Project Managers have saved between 700,000 and 800,000 working hours during 2026 to date.

In a separate enterprise research deployment, the company said AI can complete work in approximately 30 to 45 minutes that previously required more than 30 researchers working over six to seven months.

Researchers subsequently review and validate the AI-generated output, allowing them to concentrate on analysis and quality assurance while automated systems handle information gathering and processing.

Alharith Alatawi, CEO and Co-Founder of Shaffra, said: “Saudi Arabia’s push for AI capabilities has created an environment where organisations are increasingly willing to put the technology to work. Shaffra’s growth reflects that opportunity, and our priority is to make Autonomous AI Teams a practical part of how enterprises and governments operate worldwide, with AI employees adapted to local needs and designed to support people in their work. We want KSA to be a source of globally relevant AI workforce technology, shaped by the needs of the organisations and people using it.”

Supporting Saudi Arabia’s AI ambitions

The expansion comes as Saudi Arabia continues to prioritise Artificial Intelligence investment and adoption across its economy.

According to HP’s 
2026 Work Relationship Index
, 60% of Saudi workers surveyed reported using AI agents.

Meanwhile, the Kingdom’s National Strategy for Data and AI targets SAR 75 billion in domestic and foreign investment.

Shaffra aims to capitalise on this growing demand through its Enterprise AI Workforce Platform, which enables organisations to design and deploy Autonomous AI Teams across different business functions.

The platform incorporates Subconscious AI, a Saudi-developed cognitive architecture designed to help AI employees retain relevant knowledge and context across ongoing tasks while improving reasoning efficiency.

The technology is intended to support AI systems that can perform operational responsibilities while maintaining the context needed for longer-running activities.

Expanding beyond the GCC

Shaffra said its clients collectively saved two million working hours per month during 2025 through deployments of its Autonomous AI Teams.

The company’s regional expansion has also extended beyond the GCC, with the establishment of a headquarters in Azerbaijan to support operations in the Caspian region.

The move marked Shaffra’s first expansion outside the Gulf.

Backed by stc and Omantel alongside other technology investors, the company has raised more than US$10 million to support its development and growth.

Shaffra expects 2027 to be an important year for enterprise AI implementation as organisations begin to realise the operational benefits of investments and initiatives launched during 2026.

With its new Riyadh headquarters, the company aims to strengthen its presence in Saudi Arabia while developing AI workforce technology for enterprise and government customers across international markets.

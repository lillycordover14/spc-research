NEW YORK, Nov. 17, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- 
Niural
 AI
, the AI-native global workforce and payments platform, today announced that it has achieved accreditation from the Employer Services Assurance Corporation (ESAC), becoming the only AI-native payroll platform to hold the prestigious designation.
 
 
 
 
Niural logo
 
 
 
The achievement sets Niural apart from leading technology-driven competitors such as Rippling and Deel, neither of which holds ESAC accreditation. It also marks a major milestone in the evolution of payroll technology: 
a new generation of AI-native infrastructure backed by the rigorous financial controls and independent oversight historically associated with established financial institutions.
 
 
 
 
 
"The future of payroll isn't a choice between cutting-edge technology and institutional-grade financial infrastructure. The best companies must have both," said Nami Baral, CEO and Co-Founder of Niural AI. "We built Niural from the ground up to be the most technologically advanced platform in the industry, with the financial controls and operational rigor to match. Innovation without accountability isn't enough when you're responsible for people's livelihoods."
 
 
Rigorous Financial Standards and Continuous Independent Oversight
 
 
To earn accreditation, Niural underwent an extensive examination conducted by ESAC and an independent certified public accounting firm. Over several months, the review examined the company's financial records, internal controls, payroll tax obligations, employee benefits, insurance premiums, and reconciliation of funds through final disbursement.
 
 
Following the examination, Niural's accreditation was unanimously approved by ESAC's Independent Directors.
 
 
 
 
 
The Next Generation of Payroll Infrastructure
 
 
Niural's accreditation reinforces its position as a technology leader with institutional-grade financial infrastructure. The company's unified AI-native platform powers U.S. PEO and ASO services, global payroll, Employer of Record (EOR), contractor management, employee benefits, compliance, and payments.

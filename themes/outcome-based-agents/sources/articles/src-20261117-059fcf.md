Carmel’s Clay Middle School presents ‘Newsies Jr.’&nbsp;&nbsp;Current Publishing

[page text]
Clay Middle School eighth grader Charlotte Randolph is embracing her role in “Newsies Jr.”

Randolph plays Katherine Plumber, a reporter who covers the 1899 strike of New York City newspaper vendors.

“She’s a very strong and independent character and it’s just fun to bring her to life because she’s very much of a leader,” Randolph said. “It’s fun to have the sass that she has but then also takes a stand and speaks for women’s rights.”

Clay Middle School will perform “Newsies Jr.”  at  7 p.m. Nov. 19-20 and 2 and 7 p.m. Nov. 21.

Randolph enjoys the song “Watch What Happens,” her solo.

“It’s very catchy and she’s trying to show people that women can do things,” she said.

Her biggest challenge is dancing, which she admitted is not her strongest suit.

Preston Blair plays Jack Kelly, leader of the newsies.

“This is the role I wanted, so I was happy I got it,” he said. “I’m loving the role so far.”

Blair said his favorite song is his solo “Santa Fe.”

“It’s one of the reasons why I wanted this role,” Blair said. “It’s kind of the climax of the story, where many things in the story have gone wrong.”

Blair also has performed in Jr. Civic and Summer Stock shows. He also has attended Stage Room Manor camp in New York the last two years.

Eighth grader Tommy McConnell plays Crutchie.

“I feel like he was on my vocal range because when I did it in (2023 for Jr. Civic). a girl played Crutchie and I thought that was the vocal range,” McConnell said. “I just thought the role fit me.”

Eighth grader Mira Gautamplays Medda Larkin.

“She’s kind of a showgirl and kind of a mother figure to Jack and the other newsies,” Gautam said. “I like the role because I feel like there’s not only that kind of maternal nature about it, but I also get an opportunity to be really sassy and fun with it. I also love my song, ‘Just a Pretty Face,’ which has been a really fun number to put together and perform. This is probably one of my favorite roles I’ve ever played.”

Meeting Street Artisan Markets Bring Holiday Cheer to West Columbia&nbsp;&nbsp;swlexledger

[page text]
West Columbia, SC 11/17/2026 - The holiday season is coming to West Columbia as the Meeting Street Artisan Markets return for three special Holiday Edition events in December.
The markets will take place Saturday, December 5, 2026 at 12, and 19, from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at the Interactive Art Park, located at 425 Meeting Street in West Columbia.
Visitors can browse a variety of unique, handmade items created by talented local artisans. Vendor booths will feature handcrafted jewelry, original artwork, baked goods, and other one-of-a-kind treasures perfect for holiday gifts.
Guests can also enjoy festive holiday music, delicious treats, and a welcoming atmosphere throughout the events. On Saturday, December 5, families will have the opportunity to take complimentary photos with Santa and Mrs. Claus.
The Meeting Street Artisan Markets offer more than a chance to shop for the holidays. They provide an opportunity to support local artists and small businesses, enjoy the community, and make special holiday memories with family and friends.
Residents and visitors are invited to come out, shop local, and celebrate the holiday season in West Columbia.

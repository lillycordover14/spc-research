LAS VEGAS (
FOX5
) — Fremont Street Experience has launched a limited-time P!NK-themed takeover of its SlotZilla zip line ahead of the singer’s 2027 residency at Sphere.
P!NKZILLA pairs the singer’s music with visuals across the Fremont Street Experience canopy. The experience runs nightly at 5, 7 and 9 p.m. through Thursday, Nov. 19.

Sphere residency
P!NK’s nine-show residency is scheduled to open Sept. 29, 2027. Additional performances are set for Oct. 1, 2, 6, 8, 9, 27, 29 and 30, 2027.
She will be the first solo female pop artist to headline Sphere, according to the announcement.

GRANTSVILLE — The sights, sounds, and traditions of an old-fashioned Christmas will come alive as Spruce Forest Artisan Village in Grantsville welcomes visitors to its annual Christmas in the Village celebration, Friday, Dec. 4, from 4-8 p.m. and Saturday, Dec. 5, from noon to 8 p.m.
This festive two-day event invites families, friends, and visitors of all ages to experience the warmth and charm of the holiday season while celebrating the rich artistic and cultural heritage of the Appalachian Mountains.
Nestled among the historic buildings of Spruce Forest Artisan Village, visitors will have the opportunity to meet talented local artisans, explore handcrafted gifts, enjoy live holiday music, and experience traditional Christmas festivities in a picturesque village setting.

Artisans, Demonstrations, and Holiday Shopping
Throughout the weekend, visitors can explore the village's artisan studios and meet skilled craftspeople demonstrating traditional and contemporary arts. Featured artisans include blacksmiths, leatherworkers, basket weavers, watercolorists, potters, woodcarvers, glassblowers, sculptors, photographers, and other talented artists.
The Great Hall will host a Vendor and Craft Show upstairs, offering additional opportunities to shop for unique, locally made gifts just in time for Christmas.

The Holiday Trolley will also be available again this year to make visits easy and stress free. Catch the trolley at the Travel Mug in Grantsville.
A highlight of the celebration will be the Town Christmas Tree Lighting on Friday, Dec. 4, at 6:45 p.m., bringing visitors together in the heart of the village to celebrate the spirit of the season. There will be a live nativity both nights and Cookies and Cocoa with Santa. 
Live holiday music will fill the village chapel throughout both days, featuring performances by local musicians and community groups. The entertainment schedule includes traditional Christmas favorites and performances that capture the nostalgia of the holiday season.

"Christmas in the Village is a wonderful opportunity to bring families and friends together while showcasing the incredible talents of our local artisans," said Kim Folk, President of Spruce Forest. “This is a gift to our community made possible by various sponsors and a commitment from the board and artisans of Spruce Forest. The event celebrates the traditions, craftsmanship, and sense of community that make our region so special. We invite everyone to experience the magic of Christmas in this beautiful historic setting."
Celebrating Heritage and Community
Christmas in the Village is more than a holiday celebration. It is an opportunity to support local artisans, preserve traditional craftsmanship, and introduce visitors to the history and cultural heritage of the region.

The celebration is made possible through the support of community partners and sponsors, including the Maryland Heritage Areas Authority, Mountain Maryland Gateway to the West Heritage Area, Grantsville Arts & Entertainment District, Somerset Trust Company, and WVU Medicine Garrett Regional Medical Center, Penn Alps Restaurant and Whispering Pines Furniture and gift store
Visitors are encouraged to bring their families, enjoy the holiday atmosphere, and make Christmas in the Village part of their annual holiday traditions.

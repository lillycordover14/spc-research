Eliminations will not be necessary for the Grade 2 Progress Pace, with seven entrants advancing directly to the $223,960 USD final on Wednesday, Nov. 25 at Dover Downs.

The seven three-year-old pacers that were entered in the Progress Pace are listed below in alphabetical order with their trainers.

Al Papi - Ron Burke

Fragment - Petter Engblom

Highland B Best - Katie Beyer

Melillo - Ron Burke

Odds On Mr Mamba - Melanie Wrenn

Show Me Your Ace - Chris Ryder

Toast Of The Town - Noel Daley

(Standardbred Canada; photo of Odds On Mr Mamba winning the Matron Stakes at Dover Downs on Nov. 12)

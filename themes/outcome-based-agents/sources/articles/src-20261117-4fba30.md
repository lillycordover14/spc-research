BURLINGTON, Ky. — More4less Outlet, an independently owned online retailer based in Burlington, offers locally engraved gifts through The Artisan Studio alongside its selection of brand-name closeouts.

Owner Chad Miller engraves gifts in his Kentucky workshop and personally inspects, packs and ships orders. The Artisan Studio collection gives shoppers a way to browse engraved gifts from a Northern Kentucky maker online.

The wider More4less Outlet shop carries beauty and personal care products, home goods, toys and electronics, as well as small-batch Vixen Skincare. The business operates online only and does not have a walk-in retail storefront. Orders include free tracked U.S. shipping with no minimum purchase.

Explore The Artisan Studio at https://more4lessoutlet.com/collections/artisan-studio or visit the shop at https://more4lessoutlet.com/.

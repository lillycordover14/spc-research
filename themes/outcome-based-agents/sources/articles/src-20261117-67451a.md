Red Wings May Be Willing to Pass on a Larkin Trade With Minnesota

The Detroit Red Wings’ standoff with Dylan Larkin may be getting more complicated. 
NHL insider Frank Seravalli
 believes Detroit ownership has decided it will not trade the former captain to the Minnesota Wild, even if a reasonable offer is available, because it doesn’t want to give Larkin what he wants.

Larkin requested a trade in June, initially identifying Minnesota, Florida and Vegas as preferred destinations. His list has since expanded to 10 teams, but Detroit reportedly wants him to add another six to eight clubs, including Montreal, Boston, Los Angeles and Buffalo. He has resisted that request. 

It now appears the Red Wings are taking a firm stance and are not appeasing Larkin. 

Oilers’ Jake Walman Situation Raises Questions About His Future

The Edmonton Oilers continue to scratch Jake Walman, and the situation is beginning to look like something beyond a message about his recent performance. 
Some believe Edmonton
 may be waiting for the defenseman to request a trade, which would potentially make things easier given his full no-move clause.

Walman was an extra on the ice again at pregame skates for the Oilers on Tuesday as they get set to take on the Florida Panthers. 

Edmonton Oilers defenseman Jake Walman and center Jason Dickinson celebrate a goal on the Anaheim Ducks in Game 1 of the first round of the 2026 Stanley Cup Playoffs (Walter Tychnowicz-Imagn Images)

There has been no indication Edmonton has actually approached him about accepting a trade, but his lack of playing time is becoming increasingly difficult to ignore.

Head coach Mike Babcock has reportedly challenged Walman to improve his defensive play, while Edmonton has continued winning without him.

Blackhawks Have Four Players on Latest NHL Trade Board

The Chicago Blackhawks could have several players attracting attention as the trade deadline approaches. 
Daily Faceoff’s Matt Larkin
 included Teuvo Teravainen, Jordan Greenway, Ian Cole and Ryan Donato among his latest trade candidates to watch.

Teravainen, Greenway and Cole are pending unrestricted free agents, making them logical trade possibilities for a Chicago team unlikely to contend this season. Teravainen has eight points in 16 games and could interest teams looking for secondary scoring. 

Sidney Crosby’s Penguins Future Remains Entirely His Decision

Sidney Crosby’s future with the Pittsburgh Penguins remains uncertain, but a new report suggests the organization is prepared to accommodate whatever decision its captain makes.

The Athletic’s Josh Yohe reports that Crosby essentially controls the situation. He writes:

“The truth is, everything is in his hands. If Crosby wants to keep playing with the Penguins after this season, they will naturally accommodate his wishes. If he wants to retire after this season, he will retire. If he wants to be traded, Dubas will trade him.”

source – ‘What I’m hearing about the Penguins, an Erik Karlsson trade and the future’ – Josh Yohe – The Athletic – 11-16-2026

Most people Yohe has spoken with inside the organization believe Crosby will return to Pittsburgh next season. However, there is no definitive answer because Crosby himself reportedly hasn’t decided what he wants to do. For now, the Penguins appear willing to let their franchise icon determine how his career concludes.

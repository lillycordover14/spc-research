 The red circle marks the intersection of Highway 6 and County Highway 11, about  4 miles north of Crosby in Crow Wing County, that is scheduled to become a roundabout in 2028.

The county highway department will host a public informational meeting on the roundabout 4:30-5:30 p.m. Wednesday, Nov. 18, 2026, at the Crosby City Hall.

CROSBY — Another roundabout is coming to Crow Wing County at a well-traveled intersection.
In a news release Monday, Nov. 16, the Crow Wing County Highway Department announced it would move forward with the roundabout. The proposed location is 4 miles north of Crosby at the intersection of Highway 6 and County Highway 11.
The department stated its staff worked with the Minnesota Department of Transportation to develop a plan for the intersection, then decided a roundabout would be the best option.

"This project will seek to improve a variety of well documented challenges that exist at this intersection," the news release stated. "This will be accomplished by using current safety design standards, best management environmental practices and working cooperatively with utility companies to relocate service lines."
The department will host an open-house style informational meeting for the public 4:30-5:30 p.m. Wednesday at the Crosby City Hall.
The meeting will include layouts of the preliminary design, and representatives from the county will be available for comments and questions.
Construction on the project is slated for summer of 2028.

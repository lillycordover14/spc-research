Inside Dylan Harper's sophomore jump for the Spurs
Harper's sophomore ascension has unlocked a new dimension for the Spurs' offense amid their 12-game winning streak.
Ian Dardani, for NBA.com
Updated on November 17, 2026 8:50 PM
Harper ranks 2nd on the Spurs in scoring (18.3 ppg) and 3rd in both rebounds (5.5) and assists (4.7).

They haven’t lost in 25 days.
 They have won 12 straight games – their longest win streak in 11 years – and they’ve done so by an average margin of 16.3 points.

Tonight 
on NBC’s Coast 2 Coast Tuesday, the San Antonio Spurs will look to make it 13 in a row as they host the Pistons (
8 ET
).

Inside The Tear:
 San Antonio ranks 2nd in both offensive and defensive rating during its 12-game win streak, with Victor Wembanyama dominating on both ends.

But this 12-0 run
 has also been fueled by, well, running – with a sophomore guard leading the charge.

Year 2 Leap:
 After a breakout rookie postseason off the bench, Dylan Harper has stepped into the starting lineup and stepped up, ranking 2nd on the team in scoring (18.3 ppg) and 3rd in both rebounds (5.5) and assists (4.7).

And Harper’s emergence
 has unlocked a new dimension to the Spurs’ attack, helping them jump from 10th in fastbreak points last season to 1st this year at 19.9 ppg.

Up & Out:
 With Wembanyama wreaking havoc inside, the Spurs rank 5th in opponent field goal percentage (43.6%) and 1st in defensive rebounds per game (35.6)

What Does That Create?
 Transition opportunities, with Harper, Fox & Castle all getting downhill room to attack

And Harper’s Led The Way:
 The Spurs sophomore paces the team in total miles (2.3 per game) and total fastbreak points with 64, the 4th-most in the NBA

Wemby forces a tough shot, Harper grabs the board and is off and running.

Harper’s Secret Weapon?
 His strength at the rim, where he’s shooting 78.6% this season – the best mark of any guard with 5+ rim attempts per game

“He’s very, very talented,”
 said Spurs coach Mitch Johnson of Harper Monday. “When he’s in attack mode and switched on defensively, he is, already at his young age, one of the better players in this league.”

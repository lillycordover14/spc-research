MONTCLAIR, NJ — A holiday gathering that showcases local wellness, artisan vendors and the “seasonal community experience” will take place in Montclair on Sunday, Dec. 13.
The Loopwell Holiday Market will take place from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. at 80 Maple Avenue. Learn more 
here
.
 
Here’s what to expect, the Montclair-based company said:

“Celebrate the season at the Loopwell Holiday Market, a thoughtfully curated shopping experience bringing together exceptional local artisans, independent makers, and conscious brands. Discover a handpicked collection of brands and makers bringing together the best of holistic well-being, thoughtful design, and intentional living. From wellness essentials, natural skincare, and nourishing products to handcrafted home goods, distinctive accessories, and sustainable finds, explore offerings that support how we live, feel, and care for ourselves and others.”
 
According to organizers, proceeds from the Holiday Collective will directly support The Givewell Project, a Loopwell initiative that funds free community wellness events, workshops, and guest passes for local residents.

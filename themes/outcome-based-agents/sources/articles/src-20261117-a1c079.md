William Robert “Bobby” Pace, age 79, went to be with his heavenly Father on Saturday, November 14, 2026. He was the husband of Shirley White Pace and the son of the late William Jackson “Jack” Pace and Lucille Miller Pace. He was a native of Laurens County and was retired from Firmin Ford. Bob was an active member of Cross Anchor Yarborough United Methodist Church, an active member of the Laurens County NWTF Chapter, an avid golfer, and past Master of the Schroder Masonic Lodge #144 in Gray Court.
Bob was born Feb. 2, 1947 in Laurens, SC and was the eldest of two children, in addition to his wife, he is survived by his son, Chadwick “Chad” Pace and his wife, Erica Tollison of Belton, SC; his three grandchildren, Haley Hathorne of Orlando, FL; Bryna and Blaklee Norwood of Belton, SC; his sister, Karen Turner Sheehy and her husband Richard of Myrtle Beach, SC; his nieces; Carley Bryson of Myrtle Beach, SC and Kason Rosser of Ware Shoals, SC, Kayleigh Sheehy-Perry of Greenville.
A graveside service will be held at 2 p.m. Nov. 18 at Cross Anchor United Methodist Church Cemetery with Rev. Brian Williams officiating. The family will receive friends at the graveside following the service.
Condolences may be expressed to the family online at 
www.grayfunerlahome.com
Gray Funeral Home of Clinton is assisting the Pace Family with services.

PETALING JAYA (Nov 17): Crescendo Corp Bhd’s agreement to sell 40.10 acres of vacant industrial land in 
Kota Tinggi
, 
Johor
, to Pioneer Real Estate Development Sdn Bhd for RM200.88 million has become unconditional.

In a Bursa Malaysia filing on Monday (Nov 16), the group said all conditions precedent under the sale and purchase agreement (SPA) had been fulfilled, making the agreement unconditional that day. The announcement did not state that the disposal had been completed.

Its wholly-owned subsidiary, Crescendo Development Sdn Bhd, signed the SPA with Pioneer on Nov 14, 2025, according to the group’s Jan 23, 2026 circular to shareholders.
ADVERTISEMENT

The freehold parcel is within Bandar Cemerlang Industrial Park, about 5km north-west of 
Ulu Tiram
 town and 26km north of 
Johor Bahru
 city centre.

Held under title H.S.(D) 62047, PTD 50886, the land measures 162,278.90 sq m, or about 1.75 million sq ft. The sale price works out to RM115 psf.

The circular stated that the land’s express title conditions specify medium industrial use for a data centre, with construction to conform to plans approved by the relevant local authorities.

In the January circular, Crescendo estimated that the disposal would generate a net pro forma gain of RM104.36 million.

According to the January circular, RM47.39 million of the gross proceeds was earmarked for estimated development costs relating to post-completion works on the property. The remaining RM153.49 million was allocated for working capital, including income tax arising from the disposal, infrastructure works at the industrial park, repayment of borrowings and transaction expenses.

The circular identified Pioneer as a wholly-owned subsidiary of UK-incorporated Magma Holding Company Limited as at Jan 12. Its stated principal activities included real estate management, hosting infrastructure, data processing services and developing buildings for its own operation, including renting out space.

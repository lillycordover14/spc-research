ElevenLabs to Acquire Kotoba Technologies&nbsp;&nbsp;CX Today

[page text]
Kotoba Technologies is joining ElevenLabs to accelerate voice AI research and product development from Tokyo. The deal combines Kotoba’s East Asian language expertise with ElevenLabs’ research capabilities, computing resources and global business infrastructure. The acquisition is expected to close later in Q4 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and closing conditions.

Goldman Sachs increased its holdings of Ethereum exchange-traded funds to $1.22 billion at the end of the third quarter, roughly six times the previous quarter's level.

Cointelegraph reported on Nov. 17 that Goldman Sachs disclosed in its third-quarter 13F filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission that its Ethereum ETF holdings rose from $211.6 million in the second quarter to $1.22 billion.

The largest position was BlackRock’s iShares Ethereum Trust ETF. Goldman Sachs increased its holdings in the fund by 352% to 29.96 million shares, valued at $602.9 million as of the filing date. Its stake in the Grayscale Ethereum Mini Trust ETF rose 180% to 5.68 million shares, with a reported value of $144.9 million. Its holdings of BlackRock’s iShares Ethereum ETF increased 62% to 8.18 million shares, valued at $281 million. Four additional ETF positions, including the Fidelity Ethereum Fund, had a combined value of $195.4 million.

Goldman Sachs also adjusted its Bitcoin ETF positions. It reduced its holdings of BlackRock’s Bitcoin ETF by about 792,000 shares to 16.44 million shares. At the same time, it increased its Fidelity Bitcoin ETF holdings more than 50-fold, from 105,840 shares to 5.48 million shares. That stake was valued at $397.9 million.

Kamran Khosravi, a research analyst at Bitwise, told Cointelegraph that market makers such as Jane Street can shift positions among ETFs to meet client demand and hedge options exposure without changing their overall Bitcoin exposure. 13F filings, he added, do not include instruments that can offset reported holdings, including short positions, which can make routine market-making activity appear to be a large directional bet on a specific fund.

Meanwhile, Wells Fargo increased its holdings of BlackRock’s Bitcoin ETF by 103% over the same period to 49.58 million shares, worth about $2.35 billion. Its Ethereum ETF holdings also rose 125% to 22 million shares.

Soft yet durable fabrication
The Caprock Quarter-Zip is made from responsibly sourced 250 GSM 
Nuyarn
 merino wool. Unlike traditional merino wool fabric, which twists short lengths of fiber into longer yarn, Nuyarn uses a spinning technology that drafts wool fibers along a high-performance filament. This provides enhanced durability, stretch, and softness while retaining wool’s natural breathability.
During my testing, I found the quarter-zip softer and less itchy than traditional merino wool. The fabric feels loftier and plush rather than dense, which is great for layering and helps it dry faster.
“Merino has more versatility than other natural animal fibers,” Emily Walzer, editor-at-large for 
Textile Insight Magazine
, previously told 
Popular Mechanics
. “It has incredible warmth and durability, and can be spun very thin for everything from a base layer to outerwear. If I were going camping, I’d want merino. It’s just sturdier [than other natural animal fibers].”
As someone with sensitive skin, I would still wear a T-shirt under the Caprock quarter-zip, but the performance wool doesn’t scratch my arms or neck to the point where it’s too itchy to wear all day.

Made for movement
Whether you’re hiking, jogging, or sledding with your family, you’ll appreciate the freedom of movement provided by the quarter-zip’s waffle-knit construction. It provides warmth and durability without feeling heavy or restrictive, so if you break a sweat, it’s not going to make you claustrophobic.
I will definitely wear this style on my cold weather runs when I need breathable warmth. Thanks to the Nuyarn merino, it also has more stretch and shape retention than traditional merino. And for my fellow sweaty friends, wool naturally resists odor, making it a prime choice for high-output activities.
“Merino wool is a semi-hollow protein fiber that traps air, water, and odorous compounds inside, releasing them into the air over time,” Wicker previously told our editors. “Unlike synthetics, which trap water and bacteria, wool allows for the passage of air and dries quickly, making it a healthy choice for socks, underwear, and outdoor activity base layers.”
Midweight layer for all seasons
Like other wools, merino is thermoregulating, so it insulates when it’s cold and manages heat and moisture in warm conditions. Yeti’s Caprock quarter zip capitalizes on the natural fiber's benefits, making it great for days when temps are variable. So far, I’ve worn the quarter zip on cool fall days (it’s perfect for taking the bite out of the air in the evenings), and I also plan to layer it under my winter coat as temps drop this month.
Overall, I think it’s a versatile piece to keep on hand year-round, from everyday errands and camping trips to summer alpine hikes and spring transitional weather.
Final Thoughts
During testing, I found the women’s quarter-zip to run a little small. The neck opening was a tight squeeze for my head, even with it fully unzipped (I wear a size small). For a more generous fit, I would recommend sizing up. However, if you plan to layer the quarter zip under a puffer or jacket, stick to your usual size for a more form-fitting fit.
Priced at $200, Yeti’s performance apparel is definitely an investment, but with the versatility of Nuyarn’s merino wool, you may never need another midweight layer.

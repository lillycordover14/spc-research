About JASTRO

The 39th Annual Meeting of the Japanese Society for Radiation Oncology (JASTRO 2026)
 took place on November 12-14 in Chiba, Japan. This year’s theme, “Blooming Boundaries – Compassion and Innovation: Patient-Centered Radiation Therapy,” focused on the role of new technologies and scientific advances in improving cancer treatment.

The meeting brought together radiation oncologists, researchers and other healthcare professionals to share their work, discuss new developments and exchange ideas on the future of radiation therapy. Topics included adaptive radiotherapy, stereotactic radiation therapy and the use of emerging technologies in cancer care. International sessions also provided an opportunity to strengthen collaboration with the global radiation oncology community.

ElevenLabs Enters AI Note-Taking and Dictation Race With ElevenAI&nbsp;&nbsp;AI Insider

[page text]
Voice AI company ElevenLabs has released ElevenAI, an experimental app combining meeting note-taking, dictation and agent integration, joining a crowded field that includes Fireflies, Wispr Flow and Granola.

Introduced at the company’s 11/11 Summit in New York, ElevenAI is in preview and available through early-access sign-up. ElevenLabs, valued at $22 billion, is positioning it as a team-wide product rather than an individual productivity tool, giving enterprises an alternative to buying separate note-taker licences.

The app records notes across meetings, notifies participants that conversations are being recorded, and lets users query data from past meetings or get live assistance to recall key details. Companies can integrate ElevenAgent to handle follow-up actions such as drafting emails from meeting notes. A dictation feature converts speech into cleaned-up text in any app, supports more than 90 languages and recognises industry-specific terminology.

ElevenAI also serves as a showcase for the company’s own models and agent platforms, as meeting tools increasingly compete to become central knowledge hubs that automate information extraction and follow-up tasks.

Over the past week, ElevenLabs also released its v3 Scribe speech-to-text models with live transcription and custom fine-tuning, a tool for selecting the right language model by use case, and a routine creator that automates tasks from transcripts.

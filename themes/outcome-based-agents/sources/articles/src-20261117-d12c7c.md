Accreditation pairs Niural’s AI-native infrastructure with independently verified financial controls, quarterly monitoring, and annual audits

Industry-first distinction: Niural AI is the only AI-native payroll platform to achieve ESAC accreditation, setting it apart from technology-driven competitors such as Rippling and Deel.

Rigorous, ongoing financial oversight: Accreditation requires independent financial verification, quarterly monitoring, and annual audits to uphold stringent financial, ethical, and operational standards.

Advanced technology meets institutional-grade trust: Niural combines cutting-edge AI-native payroll infrastructure with independently verified financial controls and operational excellence.

Niural AI
, the AI-native global workforce and payments platform, announced that it has achieved accreditation from the Employer Services Assurance Corporation (ESAC), becoming the only AI-native payroll platform to hold the prestigious designation.

The achievement sets Niural apart from leading technology-driven competitors such as Rippling and Deel, neither of which holds ESAC accreditation. It also marks a major milestone in the evolution of payroll technology: a new generation of AI-native infrastructure backed by the rigorous financial controls and independent oversight historically associated with established financial institutions.

“The future of payroll isn’t a choice between cutting-edge technology and institutional-grade financial infrastructure. The best companies must have both,” said Nami Baral, CEO and Co-Founder of Niural AI. “We built Niural from the ground up to be the most technologically advanced platform in the industry, with the financial controls and operational rigor to match. Innovation without accountability isn’t enough when you’re responsible for people’s livelihoods.”

Rigorous Financial Standards and Continuous Independent Oversight

To earn accreditation, Niural underwent an extensive examination conducted by ESAC and an independent certified public accounting firm. Over several months, the review examined the company’s financial records, internal controls, payroll tax obligations, employee benefits, insurance premiums, and reconciliation of funds through final disbursement.

Following the examination, Niural’s accreditation was unanimously approved by ESAC’s Independent Directors.

The Next Generation of Payroll Infrastructure

Niural’s accreditation reinforces its position as a technology leader with institutional-grade financial infrastructure. The company’s unified AI-native platform powers U.S. PEO and ASO services, global payroll, Employer of Record (EOR), contractor management, employee benefits, compliance, and payments.

Together with Niural’s direct master insurance arrangements with Aetna, Kaiser Permanente, and Guardian, ESAC accreditation further demonstrates the company’s ability to combine technological innovation with the institutional credibility required to serve businesses at scale.

Niural is setting a new standard for the industry: the most advanced technology, backed by independently verified financial controls and operational excellence.

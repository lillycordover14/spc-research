Just weeks after veteran coach Clay Hendrix announced he would step down as Furman’s football coach, the Paladins have selected his successor, multiple individuals with direct knowledge told USA TODAY Sports.
Furman is finalizing a deal to hire Army offensive coordinator Cody Worley to replace Hendrix, a legendary Furman figure who coached the past nine seasons at his alma mater and spent more than 30 years at the school. Hendrix also had a successful stint as an assistant under Troy Calhoun at Air Force.
The individuals were granted anonymity because the two sides are still finalizing the deal. ESPN’s Pete Thamel first reported that Worley was Furman’s top target.

Worley, Army’s offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach, is a Furman graduate who played quarterback for the Paladins.
His coaching career also includes a lengthy stint at Kennesaw State and a brief stop at Southern Conference rival The Citadel.
Over the past seven seasons, Worley has taken on increasingly prominent roles at Army as the program has flourished under coach Jeff Monken.

Worley was promoted to offensive coordinator ahead of the 2024 season, when the Black Knights climbed into the national rankings and produced a record-setting 12-2 campaign.
Furman concludes its regular season, and the Hendrix era, Saturday at SoCon foe VMI.

                Dylan Harper finished Sunday’s win over the Jazz with 31 points (12-of-23 FGs, 5-of-5 FTs), nine rebounds, four assists, one steal and two three-pointers.
            

            
                After scoring 19 points or fewer in five straight and six of San Antonio’s first seven games of November, Harper had one of the best games of his young NBA career on Sunday. The second-year guard recorded his first 30-point game as a pro while finishing one rebound shy of a double-double. Harper has been a reliable fantasy option this season, and De’Aaron Fox (hip) not playing on Sunday raised his ceiling.

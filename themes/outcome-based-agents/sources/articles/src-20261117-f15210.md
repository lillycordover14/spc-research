Adonis Launches Proprietary Payer Forecast Report, Revealing Emerging Reimbursement Risks for 2027&nbsp;&nbsp;PR Newswire

[page text]
The inaugural report combines proprietary data and claims insights from Adonis' AI platform with independent industry research to identify three emerging payer behavior shifts putting provider revenue at risk in 2027.
NEW YORK
, 
Nov. 17, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- Adonis, a leading AI platform for healthcare revenue cycle management (RCM), today announced the launch of its Payer Forecast report, a new research initiative examining emerging payer behavior and its impact on provider reimbursement. The inaugural Q4 2026 report identifies three shifts that could put provider revenue at risk in 2027: payer policy changes that surface in claims before providers recognize what's changed, payer-specific payment patterns that remain hidden in organization-wide averages, and a growing gap between payer automation and provider operations.

The report highlights how these shifts are affecting provider reimbursement. For example, Adonis detected a 104% increase in UnitedHealthcare denials for a large orthopedic provider group, traced to a change in prior authorization requirements for outpatient physical therapy. After identifying the change, Adonis also alerted other customers in the same geographic area to the potential impact. In a separate analysis, Adonis helped a multi-specialty physician group identify more than $100,000 in potential net underpayments, with one payer accounting for 96% of the identified exposure.

These insights draw on claims-level data and resolution outcomes observed across the Adonis platform, which serves more than 10,000 providers, alongside independent industry research. This gives Adonis a granular view into how payer behavior is affecting reimbursement and helps surface patterns that may be difficult to identify through organization-wide reporting alone.

The report's findings are supported by independent industry research. A 2026 Guidehouse and Healthcare Financial Management Association (HFMA) survey of 191 provider executives found that 81% reported increased denials, 74% reported more prior authorization delays, and 73% reported more unclear denial reasons or underpayments. Independently, the Medical Group Management Association (MGMA) found that 90% of practices reported an increase in prior authorization burden over the prior 12 months, while 40% employed three or more full-time administrative staff per physician for prior authorization, denials, and quality reporting.
The report also examines how automation can help providers respond to increasingly complex payer requirements. One example highlighted in the report is ApolloMD, which partnered with Adonis to automate authorization number retrieval for Department of Veterans Affairs claims. Previously, staff had to contact the VA directly and manually enter authorization information into athenahealth before submission. With Adonis AI Agents, ApolloMD reduced related denials by 67% and saved more than 2,000 staff hours.
"Denial rates only tell half the story. They show that something went wrong, not what changed or where the dollars are concentrated," said Alison Bloom-Kiefer, Chief Product Officer at Adonis. "Across the Adonis platform, we have a unique view into how payer behavior is evolving at the claims level, often revealing patterns that aren't visible in traditional reporting. We created the Payer Forecast report to help providers spot emerging risks earlier and make smarter decisions about where to focus their teams."

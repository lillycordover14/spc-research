Starke County commissioners approve job offer for EMA director&nbsp;&nbsp;WKVI Information Center

[page text]
The Starke County Commissioners have approved a job offer for a new Emergency Management Agency director.

During their meeting Monday, November 16th, commissioners discussed the selection of Dorian Pitts, who was recommended for the position by the EMA board following a hiring process that included approximately ten interviews.

Commissioners voted to move forward with a job offer to Pitts, with a proposed start date of November 30th.

The board also discussed the use of a county vehicle for the position. Pitts will be permitted to use the vehicle while working in the county but will not initially be allowed to take it home.

Commissioners agreed to reevaluate the possibility of a take-home vehicle after six months.

Following the vote to extend the job offer to Pitts, he was directed to begin the necessary employment paperwork and pre-employment drug testing.

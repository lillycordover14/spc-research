Las Vegas Raiders star pass-rusher Maxx Crosby was excited about Fernando Mendoza's potential after the rookie quarterback made the first start of his career during the team's 21-13 loss to the Seattle Seahawks on Sunday.
"Fernando, that dude, he's a robot," Crosby said during Tuesday's episode of his podcast, 
The Rush with Maxx Crosby
 (4:59 mark). "You know what I mean? He does everything the right way. He's a great f--king kid. He's extremely talented, he's competitive and he's gonna be a great quarterback for a long time."

Mendoza became Las Vegas' starting signal-caller after the Raiders 
traded
 veteran passer Kirk Cousins to the Minnesota Vikings on Nov. 10.
While Cousins only suited up in nine games for Las Vegas, Crosby was appreciative of his tenure on the team.
"First and foremost, I'll forever love Kirk Cousins and he's good with me for life," Crosby said Tuesday (3:49 mark). "Such a good teammate, such a good dude, great quarterback playing incredible football right now. He was just the ultimate teammate, ultimate professional. He was a blessing to be around every single day. Everybody in that locker room loved him. He was such a good dude and such a great team guy. We wish him the best."
Cousins threw for 2,340 yards and 20 touchdowns compared to eight interceptions to open his 2026 campaign on the Raiders, but the 38-year-old was ultimately traded after the team fell to 4-5.
Mendoza, the No. 1 overall pick in the 2026 NFL draft, took over under center on Sunday.
The 23-year-old finished 17-of-29 for 143 yards against Seattle, getting sacked six times. He also rushed for 22 yards on six attempts, finding the end zone once on the ground.

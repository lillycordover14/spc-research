Sheridan to Install New Traffic Signals and Update ADA at Intersection of Brundage and Bighorn Ave&nbsp;&nbsp;Sheridan Media

[page text]
While appearing on Sheridan Media’s Pulse, City Councilmember Terry Weitzel and Public Works Director Hanns Mercer said in partnership with the Wyoming Department of Transportation (WYDOT), Sheridan will be installing stop lights at the intersection of Brundage and Bighorn Avenue.

Weitzel said the council and WYDOT entered into a 50/50 cost share to install the lights, with construction scheduled for next year. Each body will contribute $600,000 to the project. 

Mercer said the City initialized the conversation concerning the need for traffic control at the intersection. 

H. Mercer

Mercer said traffic delays will be minimal during the project as most of the work will be focused on the corners of the intersection and not the road itself.

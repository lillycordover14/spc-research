            ADA Accessibility Assessment Online Survey
        

                Home
            

                Posted on November 18, 2026 
            

DEADLINE: Sunday, December 6
TAKE THE SURVEY
The City of Upper Arlington has contracted with DLZ Ohio, Inc., to develop an ADA Self-Evaluation and Transition Plan of all City-owned facilities and programs, services and activities for compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). Title II of the ADA requires that City facilities, programs, services and activities be accessible and usable by individuals with disabilities. 
As part of this effort, an online survey is currently available to obtain insight from community members with direct knowledge of the challenges individuals with disabilities face going about their daily lives, to help identify issues and opportunities for improvement.
If you, a family member, or someone you know has a disability, please consider completing this survey. Please also let others who might want to share their thoughts with us know of this opportunity.
A second outreach opportunity will occur when the draft ADA Transition Plan is made available for public review and comment, which is tentatively scheduled to occur in early 2027.

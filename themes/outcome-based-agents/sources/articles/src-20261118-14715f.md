MILWAUKEE, Nov. 18, 2026 
(GLOBE NEWSWIRE)
 -- Artisan Partners has launched an ETF class for the Artisan Focus Fund and Artisan Select Equity Fund. The ETF class will hold interests in the same Fund and participate in the same underlying portfolio as the Fund’s mutual fund classes. Investors across all share classes will benefit from the same investment team, investment philosophy, portfolio construction and security selection process.

"Clients increasingly value the flexibility and convenience of ETFs without compromising on active management or investment excellence,” said Jason Gottlieb, President and Chief Executive Officer of Artisan Partners. "The introduction of ETF share classes seeks to allow us to better meet clients’ needs by expanding access to our established investment capabilities through a vehicle that aligns with their preferences.”
Artisan Partners expects to continue expanding its ETF offerings, with additional launches contemplated for the Artisan Global Equity Fund and Artisan Sustainable Emerging Markets Fund, among other potential opportunities.

"This is an exciting moment for Artisan and an important step in the continued evolution of our business,” Mr. Gottlieb added. "One of our strategic priorities is modernizing how we deliver our investment capabilities to clients, remaining vehicle agnostic and focused on providing efficient access to our differentiated, high value-added investment strategies.”

CHICAGO — Interra Realty, a Chicago-based commercial real estate investment services firm, has brokered the $7.2 million sale of 621 W. Belomont Ave., a 19-unit building in Chicago’s Lakeview neighborhood. Interra’s Joe Smazal and Mark Dykstra represented the buyer, a local private investor, and the seller, JAB Real Estate, a locally based developer, investor and operator. The property was fully occupied at the time of sale.
 
Originally constructed in 1926 as the parish house for St. Peter’s Episcopal Church, the property was fully renovated and converted to apartments in 2021. The unit mix comprises 15 one-bedrooms, three two-bedrooms and one three-bedroom floor plan. Apartments have been renovated with quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, in-unit laundry, individual HVAC and balconies or terraces in most units.

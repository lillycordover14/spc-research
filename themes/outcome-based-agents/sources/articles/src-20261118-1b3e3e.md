Mumbai, November 18:
 Kellton Tech Solutions Limited has announced the launch of KeOS, a proprietary artificial intelligence platform designed to help enterprises manage and govern AI agents operating across business functions. The platform allows organisations to monitor agent performance, track operational costs and maintain control over automated workflows. Kellton shares traded around ₹11.35 during Wednesday’s morning session, declining nearly 1.4% from the previous close. The stock moved lower despite the product announcement, although the reason for the decline has not been independently established.

Kellton Launches KeOS for Enterprise AI Agent Governance

According to the company’s November 18 regulatory disclosure, KeOS is designed to help businesses manage AI agents across finance, sales, operations and compliance functions. The platform enables enterprises to evaluate AI agents against predefined performance benchmarks before allowing them to operate in production environments. KeOS also provides tools for monitoring task completion, operational costs and agent performance across cloud and on-premises infrastructure. The company said the platform includes pre-built deployment templates, allowing customers to configure AI workflows within their existing systems. KeOS supports multiple AI models, including those from Anthropic and Microsoft Azure, while retaining compatibility with existing cloud environments and enterprise data governance policies. Kellton highlighted an implementation involving a leading US financial institution, where the company deployed KeOS to build AI agents that reduced loan application processing time by 47%. The deployment also enabled faster customer decisions while maintaining greater control over operational workflows. The launch expands Kellton’s AI-led digital transformation portfolio as businesses increasingly adopt automated agents for enterprise operations. Kellton’s latest Q2 FY27 results, announced on November 14, showed consolidated revenue from operations of approximately ₹320.13 crore, compared with ₹299.69 crore a year earlier, an increase of 6.8%. Consolidated profit after tax, as presented in the company’s earnings presentation, stood at approximately ₹23 crore, compared with ₹24.1 crore in Q2 FY26. The company’s EBITDA margin was 12.1%.

About Kellton Tech Solutions

Kellton Tech Solutions Limited is an India-based IT services and digital transformation company headquartered in Hyderabad. The company provides artificial intelligence, cloud engineering, data analytics, enterprise software and technology consulting services to businesses across international markets. Its services focus on helping organisations modernise technology infrastructure, automate business processes and improve operational efficiency.

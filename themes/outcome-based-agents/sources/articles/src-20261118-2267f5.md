Retired former Lawrence County Emergency Management Agency (EMA) Director Hillard Frost has died.
In a Facebook post, Lawrence County EMA said their thoughts and prayers are with Frost’s family during this difficult time.
“We are deeply grateful for Hillard’s many years of dedicated service to Lawrence County and our community. His commitment, leadership, and willingness to serve others made a lasting difference,” the post said. “Thank you, Hillard, for your years of service and dedication. You will be greatly missed and fondly remembered.
“Our deepest condolences to his family and loved ones.”

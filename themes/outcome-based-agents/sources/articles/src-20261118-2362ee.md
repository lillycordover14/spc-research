As expected, we all enjoyed plenty of sunshine through Wednesday afternoon alongside above average high temperatures well into the middle to upper 50s across all of Central New York.
Get set for more splendid weather to continue for a few more days before more significant changes arrive next week.
It's part of our next weather makers
:
Central New York's next weather makers
As shown above, we have more lovely weather for mid-November standards ahead! While high temperatures should continue to be even higher and well above average for this time of year, we do have one more chilly late night through early morning for late Wednesday night through early Thursday morning.
Much like early Wednesday morning, low temperatures by dawn Thursday will be wide ranging and chilly with plenty of 30s and even some middle to upper 20s for the coldest countryside.
Despite the cold start Thursday, expect a high temperature that should be even higher than Wednesday! These above average temperatures will continue. Let's see how long it lasts below.

As shown above, high temperatures will be double digits above average for the next 4 days! You will definitely want to get outside at some point to either enjoy the weather or do some outdoor work. That may include some yardwork or raking up leaves or perhaps even putting up holiday decorations!

Let's discuss some specifics
:
A weak weather maker will pass through Upstate and Central New York on Friday. Expect sunshine to mix with some clouds on Friday. The best chance for the thickest cloud cover and perhaps a sprinkle will be across the North Country and the Adirondacks.
Otherwise, I am still expecting some widespread fog and/or low clouds to develop later Friday night through Saturday morning before burning off. The rest of Saturday should be sunny and enjoyable again!
Thereafter, the forecast for later Sunday into Sunday night becomes tricky. While a new stronger cold front will still be moving through at some point later Sunday night or Monday morning, we are still tracking a coastal, possible tropical system that will be hanging out south of the Carolinas and northeast of Florida. While most computer models keep the heavier rain from this system to our southeast one reliable computer model says we may get widespread rain. My feeling is that this wetter computer "might" have this storm too close to our area. Part of the problem is that this system may become tropical or at least partly tropical. These kinds of systems are typically extremely difficult to forecast. For now, I am keeping Sunday dry with a better chance for passing rain showers Sunday night from the strong cold front and not the coastal storm. We will keep you posted. 
Expect a gusty chilly wind to develop especially Monday with falling temperatures from the upper 40s into the upper 30s by sunset. Some minor snow showers and flurries appear likely Monday night into Tuesday morning. Right now, it looks like the coldest high of the season looks possible Tuesday.

CAMBRIDGE, Vt. (
ABC22/FOX44
) – Vermont Route 108, also known as Smugglers' Notch, has officially closed for the winter season.
Scenic VT-108 closes annually. Officials say a notice will be issued in the spring upon reopening time, which is typically in May.
Vermont lawmakers also increased the fine for stuck vehicles, which now cost about $20,000 if your truck blocks traffic and needs to be extricated. This increased fine is in addition to chicanes and numerous signs on the winding pass, warning commercial vehicle drivers not to continue.

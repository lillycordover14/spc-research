Harper builds new career pathways with AI Pre-apprenticeship

Harper College News Bureau

November 18, 2026

November 18, 2026

AI is changing the way people work. Harper College is helping students explore how
                              it could shape their careers.

Building on the success of its existing 
pre-apprenticeship programs
, Harper is launching the four-session online AI Pre-apprenticeship in December. The
                              program introduces participants to AI’s workplace applications and potential career
                              pathways.

Designed for younger students exploring careers and working professionals seeking
                              new skills, the program features small class sizes and is a collaboration between
                              Harper’s Workforce Solutions office and 
Community Education
. The AI Pre-apprenticeship is supported by a grant from Illinois’ Job Training and
                              Economic Development (JTED) program.

Jessica Worst, Harper’s pre-apprenticeship and apprenticeship coordinator, said the
                              program offers an opportunity to explore AI without committing to a longer course
                              of study.

“It’s a new pipeline into what it means to be an apprentice,” Worst said. “It’s sort
                              of like a taster spoon before you’re ready to dive into a triple-scoop sundae.”

Participants will explore how AI can improve workplace productivity, communication
                              and problem-solving while practicing effective prompting techniques, evaluating AI-generated
                              information and learning about responsible AI use. The program also incorporates resume
                              development, interview preparation and guidance on using AI in job searches. Participants
                              will hear from industry speakers and have opportunities to earn a Harper digital badge
                              and an introductory generative AI credential through AWS Skills Builder.

Besa Sadiku, Harper’s manager of workforce initiatives, said the new offering builds
                              on the college’s experience with pre-apprenticeship programs in manufacturing and
                              health care.

“Pre-apprenticeships are a stepping stone into Harper’s apprenticeship programs,”
                              Sadiku said.

Seven of eight participants completed Harper’s manufacturing pre-apprenticeship last
                              spring, with three entering registered apprenticeships and four continuing their education
                              in 
manufacturing technology
 at the college. All six participants in 2025’s health care pre-apprenticeship enrolled
                              to pursue 
health careers
 credentials at Harper. The college plans to introduce a supply chain management pre-apprenticeship
                              program in 2027.

For those interested in AI, Harper also offers an 
Associate in Applied Science degree in AI and Cloud Computing
 and is working with employer partners to establish AI apprenticeship opportunities.

“This is a relatively quick-hit program of four classes along with in-depth support
                              for our students,” Worst said. “It’s manageable and gives you an understanding of
                              what the commitment is. It can confirm that you’re on the right path.”

Harper’s AI pre-apprenticeship will take place via live Webex sessions from 6 to 8:30
                              p.m. December 2, 3, 9 and 10. The program is open to individuals ages 16 and older,
                              with scholarships available to eligible participants.

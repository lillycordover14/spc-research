CORVALLIS, Ore. –
 
AC Froehlich
 tied a career high with 27 kills as Oregon State volleyball defeated San Diego State in four sets at Gill Coliseum.

 

Froehlich, named Pac-12 Offensive Player of the Week earlier in the day, has now reached 20-plus kills in three matches this season, tallying 25 against Utah State on Oct. 24 and 27 versus Washington State to open conference play. On the season, she leads the conference in kills (385) and kills per set (4.14) while ranking second for total points (426) and points per set (4.58).

Taray Tela
 came one block shy of a double-double, finishing with 10 kills and nine blocks on .389 hitting, while 
Cheyenne Green
 posted a line of 10 kills, six digs and four blocks. 
Taylor Treahy
 had seven kills and six blocks and 
Alana Marrs
 rounded out the Oregon State attack with six kills, also contributing five blocks of her own.

 

Isabel Bertelsen's
 defense played a huge role on Tuesday, totaling 23 digs, as she was one of three Beavers with double-digit digs. 
Yasemin Narlioglu
 had a season-best 15 digs in the match and 
Jackie Matias
 finished with 14 digs and 53 assists.

 

Matias has now recorded four consecutive double-doubles – 11 on the season – and her passing culminated her fifth 50-plus assist performance of the season; the Honolulu, Hawaii, native remains in control of the top spot in the Pac-12 for both assists (960) and assists per set (10.32).

 

As a team, the Beavers' 83 digs marked just the second time with 80 or more in a match this season, while their control of the net resulted in the team's second-highest block total of the season, 15 in total.

 

OSU dominated set one, which featured eight blocks and a team hitting percentage of .321. Froehlich, Marrs, Tela, Green and Treahy all totaled at least two, and Froehlich had five kills. After trading points early, OSU scored nine unanswered midway through the set, riding that momentum to a 25-11 victory.

 

It took extra points to find a winner in set two. A Narlioglu ace leveled the score at 12, but SDSU fought its way into a five-point lead later in the set until Oregon State countered with a 4-0 run of its own to bring it within a point at 21-20. Though the Aztecs had set point, back-to-back kills by Green put the Beavers back ahead by one, paving the way for a 30-28 set win.

 

Oregon State led early in set three, taking a quick 4-1 advantage. Though the Aztecs drew even at 10, OSU built a lead back out to four at 17-13, but the visitors once again tied things up at 21 and finished the set on a 4-0 run to win 25-22.

 

San Diego State jumped out to an early lead in set four as both teams went back and forth during the opening rallies. Three consecutive Beaver points midway through put the OSU advantage at 16-13, but both sides continued to battle, the score deadlocked as both teams approached 20 points.

 

Started by a Froehlich kill, Oregon State scored four straight to seal the match, winning set four 25-22 and taking the victory.

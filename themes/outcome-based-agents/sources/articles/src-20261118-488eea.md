A police pursuit ended in a crash in Clay Township just after 5 p.m. Tuesday.
The crash happened near Kenilworth and Beechwood Roads just South of the State line.
St. Joseph County Police attempted a traffic stop at around 5 p.m. 
According to police, the suspect, later identified as 31-year-old Alex Euriga, refused to stop and continued northbound. The vehicle then attempted a right turn onto Beechwood but left the road and struck a tree. 
The pursuing officer hit a patch of wet leaves and slid into the suspect's vehicle causing minor damage.

Police say Euriga kicked out his own driver side window and attempted to run, but was quickly apprehended and taken into custody.
Euriga was arrested on preliminary charges of felony resisting, possession of methamphetamine and misdemeanor resisting.
Euriga was taken to the St. Joseph County Jail.

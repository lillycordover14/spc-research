CHISHOLM—The Chisholm Area Chamber of Commerce and Minnesota Discovery Center are once again teaming up on a holiday shopping spectacular.
Now in its 16th year, the Holiday Chamber Expo & Artisan Market are set from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 21.
“This festive shopping experience brings together local businesses, talented artisans, unique gifts, and holiday cheer at the Minnesota Discovery Center and participating downtown Chisholm Chamber businesses,” Chamber Executive Director Shelly Lappi wrote in publicity for the event.
“It’s the perfect opportunity to discover new favorites, revisit familiar shops, and support the small businesses that make our community so special,” Lappi wrote. “Every local purchase makes a difference.”
Participating Locations Include: Black Bear Bakery, Casey Drug and Gifts, Cherry Greenhouse, Jim’s Sports Club, Keyboard Liquor, Lake Street Floral, Rupp Furniture, Tom & Jerry’s, Valentini’s Supper Club and Minnesota Discovery Center.
Attendees have a chance to win one of three, $100 Chamber Cash certificates. Chamber cash is a local shopping certificate created by the Chisholm Area Chamber that can be redeemed at several participating Chamber member businesses.
Attendees are encouraged to travel from location to location, exploring participating businesses and collecting stamps along the way. Once you’ve collected your stamps, turn in your completed entry to the Chisholm Area Chamber of Commerce for a chance to win.
About the Artisan Market
There are 34 vendors at the Artisan Market this year, and it filled up really quickly, Katie Toumi, MDC Catering and Events Director said on Tuesday.
The dining room at MDC will be open between 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. with a seasonal menu and a variety of hot and cold beverages.
Admission to Minnesota Discovery Center Artisan Market is a $5 donation benefitting MDC’s Discovery Fund, or a donation of a non-perishable food item (donations must be unopened and unexpired) for the Chisholm Food Shelf.
The MDC Discovery Fund supports educational programming, youth field trips, summer camps, and transportation assistance.
Parking at MDC is at its summer entrance this year due to ongoing construction at the research center. Golf cars will be running all day to transport shoppers from the admissions building over to the market.

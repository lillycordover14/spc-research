[page text]
ElevenLabs
 has launched Scribe v3 Live Medical, a version of its real-time speech-to-text model made for medical conversations. It runs inside ElevenAgents, the company's platform for AI voice agents, and is built to get drug names and medical procedures right while a call is still going.
Scribe v3 Live Medical brings medical transcription to live calls.
It helps voice agents get medications, procedures, and symptoms right, and teams can add up to 10,000 of their own terms, like provider and drug names.

The problem it goes after is the cleanup that follows a call. Care teams check what an AI agent writes down. When too many words come out wrong, correcting the note takes longer than writing it by hand. According to ElevenLabs, drug names and procedures are where most of those corrections happen.
The new model builds on Scribe v3 Live, which ElevenLabs released on November 10 together with Scribe v3. In the company's own tests on recorded speech in 102 languages, Scribe v3 Live made 43% fewer errors than the earlier real-time version. It can now tell speakers apart as they talk. Teams can also give it a list of thousands of their own terms, which it applies when it hears a likely match.
ElevenLabs already offers Scribe v2 Medical, a model tuned for medical vocabulary in recorded clinical dictation. Scribe v3 Live Medical brings that focus to live conversations, where an agent has to understand a patient while the call is happening.
ElevenAgents already handles tasks such as patient intake and appointment scheduling for healthcare teams. For these teams, ElevenLabs offers a zero retention mode that deletes audio and text as soon as a request is completed. Scribe is HIPAA-eligible once a Business Associate Agreement is signed.

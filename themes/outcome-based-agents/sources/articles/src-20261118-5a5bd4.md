Co-Founder and CEO of ElevenLabs, Mati Staniszewski, takes voice AI agents into the enterprise as ARR climbs from US$350m to US$600m in nine months of 2026
ElevenLabs
 ended 2025 with US$350m in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR).
Nine months later, its CEO says that figure has reached US$600m, and more than half now comes from enterprise customers.
The London company began in 2022 with text to speech realistic enough to fool listeners.
Four years on, it sells voice agents that answer the phone for 
Klarna's
 35 million US customers, run support inside 
Deutsche Telekom's
 network and plug into 
IBM's
 agent platform.
Investors have followed: 
Sequoia
 led a US$500m Series D in February 2026 at an US$11bn valuation, more than three times its value a year earlier.
"AI agents are becoming central to everyday work, and voice is where AI either earns trust or loses it," CEO and Co-Founder of ElevenLabs Mati Staniszewski said in a press release announcing its partnership with IBM.
Mati Staniszewski, CEO of ElevenLabs. Credit: LinkedIn

The birth of ElevenLabs
Mati Staniszewski and 
Piotr Dąbkowski
 founded ElevenLabs in 2022 as a research company, building voice models first and products on top.
“We started by building a voice that could sound human, and we did,” says Mati.
The business now runs on three lines: 
ElevenAgents
 for conversational voice agents, ElevenCreative for producing and localising audio, and ElevenAPI for developers.
Across them, it covers more than 70 languages and an excess of 17,500 voices.
“We take the models even further by optimising them for the best product experiences that we believe will redefine the benchmarks,” Mati adds.
Mati Staniszewski and Piotr Dąbkowski, Co-Founders of ElevenLabs. Credit: KPN Ventures
Partnerships and funding driving the growth
ElevenLabs’ roster of partners extend the company’s initial goals of building humanlike voices.
In June 2026, IBM combined ElevenLabs speech models with watsonx Orchestrate so banks, insurers, healthcare providers and governments can build voice agents with PCI compliance and data residency controls.
The company then moved into music generation: In September 2026 ElevenLabs signed a multi-year licensing agreement with 
Universal Music Group.
ElevenLabs in numbers
Revenue: US$600m ARR as of September 2026
Valuation: US$22bn as of September 2026
Funding: US$781m raised across five rounds by February 2026
Enterprise mix: more than 55% of ARR from enterprise customers in 2026
Team: 800 employees as of September 2026
Liquidity: US$300m employee tender offer closed in September 2026
Platform: more than 70 languages and 17,500 voices as of Eleven v4
ElevenLabs’ investor base is unusually broad. More than 30 figures from entertainment and sport, including Jamie Foxx, Eva Longoria, Matthew McConaughey and Squid Game creator Hwang Dong-hyuk, have backed the company, and 
Robinhood Ventures
 has given retail investors a route to a stake.
Santander and KPN are both investors and enterprise users, which ties the cap table directly to the customer base and the banking and telecoms markets ElevenLabs wants to win.
Yet the capital shows no sign of slowing down: following a third close of the Series D in May 2026 brought in 
BlackRock
, Wellington, 
NVIDIA
 and Deutsche Telekom, alongside a US$100m tender offer for staff, ElevenLabs’ total funding reached US$781m across five rounds by February 2026.
The team grew to 800 people in more than 50 countries, and spans offices in 14 cities, from London and New York to Tokyo and São Paulo.
ElevenLabs’ top customers
Klarna
 
launched an ElevenAgents voice assistant as first-line phone support for all 35 million of its US customers in February 2026, resolving the queries it handles up to 10 times faster, with plans to reach 114 million customers worldwide.
Revolut
 
uses ElevenAgents, ElevenLabs' platform for conversational voice agents, across use cases that include customer support, conversational commerce, internal training and inbound sales, according to the company's February 2026 Series D announcement.
Duolingo
 
uses ElevenCreative, the ElevenLabs platform for producing voiced content, to create and localise audio for its global audience of learners across many languages and markets.

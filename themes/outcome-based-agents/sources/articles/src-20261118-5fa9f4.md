Guan Chong 3Q net profit surges sixfold to RM277.7mil&nbsp;&nbsp;The Star

[page text]
Guan Chong Bhd managing director and chief executive officer Brandon Tay Hoe Lian. 

KUALA LUMPUR: 
Guan Chong Bhd
's net profit surged more than sixfold to RM277.65mil in the third quarter ended Sept 30, 2026 (3QFY26), from RM41.24mil a year earlier, driven by effective management of cocoa bean purchases and sales positions.
The world’s fourth-largest cocoa grinder’s revenue, however, fell 38.1% to RM2.31bil from RM3.73bil previously, in line with lower raw material prices.
Despite the decline in revenue, Guan Chong recorded higher year-on-year sales volumes of cocoa ingredients, which supported its earnings performance.
Finance costs declined 40.7% to RM47.5mil from RM80mil a year earlier, mainly due to lower working capital financing requirements for cocoa bean purchases as prices eased.
For the nine months ended Sept 30, 2026, net profit more than tripled to RM654.25mil from RM184.04mil, while revenue tumbled 43.5% to RM6.73bil from RM11.92bil.
The group attributed its stronger profitability to effective management of purchase and sales positions, alongside lower financing costs.
Guan Chong said the nine-month performance had put it on track for a record financial year in 2026.
Managing director and chief executive officer Brandon Tay Hoe Lian said the group expected demand for its cocoa ingredients to remain strong, supported by healthy grinding margins.
"The strong performance in 3Q26 once again reflects the resilience of global cocoa demand and GCB's ability to capture opportunities across our key markets.
"We expect this strength to continue, supported by growing demand for our cocoa ingredients and healthy grinding margins," he said in a statement.
However, Tay cautioned that the cocoa industry continued to face external risks, including potential weather disruptions from the impending El Niño, which could affect global cocoa bean production.
Meanwhile, Guan Chong's total borrowings declined 23.2% to RM2.45bil as at Sept 30, 2026, from RM3.19bil at end-2025, mainly due to lower working capital financing requirements for cocoa bean procurement as bean prices eased.
Its net gearing improved to 0.85 times from 1.35 times over the same period.
The group declared a fourth interim dividend of two sen per share for FY26, amounting to RM54.8mil. The dividend will go ex on Dec 2, 2026, with payment scheduled for Dec 18, 2026.
Including the earlier three interim dividends, Guan Chong's total dividend payout for FY26 amounts to RM205.5mil, equivalent to 7.5 sen per share.

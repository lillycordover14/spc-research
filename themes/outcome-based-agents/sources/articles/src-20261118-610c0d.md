The Pacific Maritime Heritage Center invites community members and visitors to its Holiday Artisan Market, Saturday, Dec. 5 from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. 
The Pacific Maritime Heritage Center is located at 333 SE Bay Boulevard in Newport's Bayfront.

Courtesy

Enjoy a free day at the Pacific Maritime Heritage Center! Tour the museum and enjoy our annual Holiday Artisan Market.
The market will feature eighteen local artists who have created pieces in a variety of mediums, including ceramics, paint, glass, buoys, leather and more.
Take photos with Santa with the beautiful Yaquina Bay in the background from 10 a.m. -1 p.m. and enjoy arts and crafts for all ages.
Get in the spirit and complete your holiday shopping in one spot.

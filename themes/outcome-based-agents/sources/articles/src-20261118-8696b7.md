LangChain Seattle Meetup: An Evening with Harrison Chase & Xavi Amatriain

November 18 @ 5:45 pm
 - 
8:30 pm
 

LangChain holds its first Seattle meetup, hosted by Expedia Group at its Seattle headquarters campus in the Interbay neighborhood. The evening opens with Mani Najmabadi, Expedia’s director of applied science, on the state of agents at the company, followed by LangChain co-founder and CEO Harrison Chase on building agents that continuously improve.

Chase then sits down with Xavi Amatriain, Expedia Group’s chief AI and data officer, for a conversation and audience Q&A, with networking afterward. The event is in person only and will not be streamed or recorded. Registration is subject to host approval, and the address is shared with approved guests.

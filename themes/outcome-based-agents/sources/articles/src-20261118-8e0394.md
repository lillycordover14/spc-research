FOND Regenerative Enters Salty Snacks Category with New Line of Artisan Potato Chips&nbsp;&nbsp;Nosh.com

[page text]
SAN ANTONIO 
(November 17, 2026) – FOND Regenerative, the Texas maker of nourishing pantry staples such as from-scratch bone broth and rendered animal fats, has entered the salty snacks category with the introduction of Regenerative Potato Chips. These light and crispy potato chips bring FOND Regenerative’s commitment to artisan quality, clean ingredients, and transparent sourcing to an entirely new grocery aisle. FOND Regenerative Potato Chips are now available on fondregenerative.com and at H-E-B stores in three takes on classic flavors: Simply Salted, Cultured Cream & Scallion, and Sea Salt & Vinegar.
For this launch, FOND Regenerative partnered with a small, third-generation U.S. family farm that follows sustainable practices to improve soil health, promote biodiversity, and produce better potatoes. The 75 year old farm utilizes crop rotation, cover cropping, minimal tilling, and other regenerative farming methods.
“Our new regenerative potato chips strike the right balance between craveable flavor and a clean label that reflects the FOND way and our farmers market quality standards,” said Alysa Seeland, Founder & CEO of FOND Regenerative. “We spent months perfecting the seasoning blends for Cultured Cream & Scallion and Sea Salt & Vinegar, and we have taken that same level of care into every part of the chip-making process.”
FOND Regenerative Potato Chips are made the traditional way using simple ingredients such as regeneratively grown, low sugar potatoes cooked in grass-fed beef tallow. This approach delivers a delicious and perfectly crunchy potato chip without a greasy mouthfeel or oily aftertaste. FOND Regenerative never uses junk ingredients found in conventional chip brands such as citric acid, maltodextrin, natural flavors, or artificial preservatives. FOND Regenerative Potato Chips are seed oil free and glyphosate residue free. 
The company is entering the salty snacks category at a unique inflection point. Its core business is up to historic levels year over year, including as much as 700% at Whole Foods. With an absence of a summer slowdown in July 2026, each month since has set a new record, with October being the strongest in the company’s eleven year history.
FOND Regenerative will announce additional availability for the Regenerative Potato Chips, which retail for $9.99 for a five-ounce bag, in the coming months. The company plans to introduce additional salty snacks line extensions next year.
About FOND Regenerative
FOND Regenerative is the maker of verified regenerative, artisanally crafted pantry staples, including bone broths, animal fats, demi-glace, and salty snacks. Founded by Alysa Seeland in 2015, the Texas-based brand is the first in the bone broth category to earn the Land to Market regenerative verification and Regenerative Organic Certification as part of a commitment to making nourishing goods as nature intended. FOND Regenerative products can be found at more than 4,000 major and independent retail locations nationwide, including Whole Foods Market, Sprouts, and H-E-B. Follow along @fondregenerative or shop online at fondregenerative.com.

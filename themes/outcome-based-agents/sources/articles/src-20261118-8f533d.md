Can-Fite Pivotal Phase III Psoriasis Study Under an FDA- and EMA-agreed protocol: All Patients Completed Dosing and Interim Analysis Expected Q1 2027

PRESS RELEASE GlobeNewswire 

Nov. 18, 2026, 07:00 AM

The study evaluates oral Piclidenoson and last patient in the interim-analysis cohort completes treatment  
Ramat Gan, Israel, Nov.  18, 2026  (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) --  
Can-Fite BioPharma
 Ltd. (NYSE American: CANF) (TASE: CANF), a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing a pipeline of proprietary small molecule drugs targeting oncological and inflammatory diseases, today announced completion of treatment of the first 247 patients in its pivotal Phase III study evaluating Piclidenoson for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis.
 The company announced that the last patient in the interim-analysis cohort of its pivotal Phase III psoriasis study received the final dose of study treatment. Results of the pre-specified interim analysis are expected to be read out by the Independent Data Monitoring Committee (IDMC) in Q1 2027.
Completion of dosing marks a key milestone toward the interim analysis, which will evaluate the efficacy and safety of Piclidenoson in patients with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis. The Company previously announced completion of enrolment of the first 247 patients for this analysis.
Piclidenoson is an investigational, orally administered A3 adenosine receptor agonist that targets inflammatory pathways implicated in psoriasis. Its clinical development program includes a safety database involving more than 1,500 subjects. If successfully developed and approved, Piclidenoson could offer a unique oral treatment option for patients requiring long-term management of their disease, with a favorable safety profile that supports chronically administered.
		

The randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study is being conducted under a protocol agreed with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the European Medicines Agency (EMA). Patients receive Piclidenoson 3 mg or placebo orally twice daily. The co-primary efficacy endpoints at Week 16 are the proportion of patients achieving at least a 75% improvement in the Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI 75) and the proportion achieving a static Physician’s Global Assessment (sPGA) score of 0 or 1, corresponding to clear or almost clear skin.
“Completion of dosing in the interim-analysis cohort brings us an important step closer to the anticipated results from our pivotal Phase III psoriasis study,” said Motti Farbstein, Chief Executive Officer of Can-Fite. “We believe Piclidenoson’s oral administration, selective mechanism of action, and favourable safety profile observed in clinical studies support its potential as a convenient long-term treatment option for patients with psoriasis. We look forward to reporting the interim results in Q1 2027.”
About Piclidenoson
Piclidenoson is an orally bioavailable, highly selective A3 adenosine receptor agonist with anti-inflammatory activity demonstrated in both preclinical and clinical studies. The drug has accumulated an extensive clinical safety database involving more than 1,500 subjects and is being developed as a potential chronic therapy for psoriasis and other inflammatory diseases. The drug’s mechanism of action entails inhibition of the inflammatory cytokines interleukin 17 and 23 (IL-17 and IL-23) and the induction of apoptosis of patients’ skin cell keratinocytes involved with the disease pathogenicity.
About Can-Fite BioPharma Ltd.

Can-Fite BioPharma Ltd. (NYSE American: CANF) (TASE: CANF) is an advanced clinical stage drug development Company with a platform technology that is designed to address multi-billion dollar markets in the treatment of cancer, liver, and inflammatory disease. Can-Fite’s liver drug, Namodenoson, is being evaluated in a Phase III trial for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), a Phase 2b trial for the treatment of MASH, and in a Phase 2a study in pancreatic cancer. Namodenoson has been granted Orphan Drug Designation in the U.S. and Europe and Fast Track Designation as a second line treatment for HCC by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Namodenoson has also shown proof of concept to potentially treat other cancers including colon, prostate, and melanoma.

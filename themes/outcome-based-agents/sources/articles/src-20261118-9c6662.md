James “Jim” Durand Crosby

Obituaries

 Today, 11:03 AM

 

 

 

 

 

James (“Jim”) Durand Crosby, a beloved father, grandfather, great grandfather, and transformative leader who served local governments in Oklahoma for over 60 years, passed away peacefully on November 10, 2026, at the age of 85.  He was one of Oklahoma’s most recognized municipal leaders, known for his hands-on leadership and forward-thinking approach.
Jim began his professional career in the Oklahoma City Parks and Recreation Department, eventually becoming the City Manager for Norman (1976 - 1987), Yukon (1994 – 2011, 2016 - 2021), Piedmont (2011 - 2015) and Chickasha (2024 - 2026).  He also was the Director of General Services for the City of Oklahoma City (1991-1994).  
Known for his keen sense of humor, Jim was never at a loss for a joke.  He could also famously finish just about any joke someone would tell him.  He is also remembered for his kindness, generosity, and as a mentor to everyone around him. 
Throughout his six decades of public service, Jim was committed to community engagement and economic development. His achievements are marked by visionary projects, and recognition from state and local organizations.  Some of his notable community and civic contributions include:
1. - Initiating Yukon’s Freedom Fest, a community celebration of Independence Day, held annually and one of Yukon, Oklahoma’s signature summer traditions, featuring live music, food vendors, and large fireworks displays.
2. - Organizing Yukon’s Christmas in the Park, now a signature community event featuring over 500 unique displays running nightly from the Saturday before Thanksgiving through New Year’s Eve.
3. - Developing the vision for the Chisholm Trail Park, a scenic public park known for its trails, Boot Hill overlooks, gazebos, ponds, and as a host to major community events.
4. - Establishing a commercial corridor in Yukon, which supported economic growth and brought dozens of commercial businesses to the city.
5. - Leading Yukon through its most severe financial setback with strong fiscal management and stabilizing the city’s operations.
Jim was inducted into the Oklahoma Hall of Fame for City and Town Officials in 2021 for his outstanding career in municipal leadership across Oklahoma, where he was described as “wise beyond his years” and “one of the most forwardthinking city leaders in Oklahoma.” Some of his awards for leadership include:
­­— 2018 Citizen of the Year Award — Yukon Chamber of Commerce
— 2008 Oklahoma Municipal League Gerald Wilkins Award — for outstanding performance as a city manager.
— 2003 Finalist – Oklahoma Municipal League Don Rider Award, honoring individuals who make major contributions to their community and profession
He has also served on boards including the National Recreation and Parks Association, Arts Festival of Oklahoma, INTEGRIS Canadian County Hospital, and Yukon Chamber of Commerce.  His work has left a lasting mark on Oklahoma’s municipal landscape, combining community engagement, economic development, and fiscal responsibility. 
Jim was preceded in death by his wife Pamela, his sisters Gayle and Camille, and his brother Lawrence (“Larry”).
He is survived by:
His brother Wilburn Joseph (“Bing”) Crosby; His children with Pamela, Carson and Landry Crosby; his children, grandchildren, and great grandchildren from his previous marriage to Shirley Crosby, Durand H. Crosby and Kathryn Rasmussen, their respective spouses Chele Crosby and D. Richard Rasmussen, his grandchildren Carl Wienecke and his spouse Monica, Alex Crosby and his spouse Hadley, Nicholas Crosby, Annika Rasmussen and Eleanor Rasmussen, and his great grandchildren Coco Wienecke, Felix Wienecke, Sebastian Crosby, and Scarlet Crosby; his daughter from his marriage to Virgina Brewer, Laura Pladson, Pam Crosby’s children Amy and her spouse Quincy Rinkle, and their children Sheyenne, Aiden and Gentry.
Services were held at Southern Hills Baptist Church, 8601 S Pennsylvania Ave, Oklahoma City on Friday, November 10 at 2:00 pm.

FAQs

How Much Does it Cost to Play at Soar N Bounce?

Admission is charged based on your play time, from 60-minute to 180-minute increments. They also offer all-day passes. 90 minutes is the most popular option.

Portage’s prices have not been announced yet. Other West Michigan locations range from $15-$30, depending on age and time spent at the park.

Are Waivers Required?

Yes. All Guests, including spectators, must sign a waiver.

Tip:
 Complete your waivers online before visiting to speed up check-in.

Are Jump Socks Required?

Yes. All participants must wear Soar N Bounce grip socks ($3.50/pair). 

You can purchase them at the front desk and reuse them for future visits. Spectators do not need grip socks.

What is the Weight Limit?

The weight limit for trampolines and most attractions is 250 lbs.

Do Parents or Guardians Require a Ticket?

Spectators do not require a ticket. However, any adult who participates in attractions must purchase a ticket.

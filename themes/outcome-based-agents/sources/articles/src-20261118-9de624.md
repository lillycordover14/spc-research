Looking for a new place to take in a Senators game? The newly opened Top Shelf is ready to welcome you.

Located on the 300 level between sections 328 and 302, Top Shelf is a brand-new social space designed to give fans another way to experience game day with an incredible view of the ice surface.

The wide-open space features light food options, drinks, a photobooth, and games like cornhole and Connect 4. Top Shelf is open to anyone with a ticket to the game, with no additional purchase required. The space may be made available for special ticketed experiences during select specialty games, playoff games or sold-out dates.

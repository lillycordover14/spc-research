 Kellton Tech Solutions 
 launched KeOS, a proprietary enterprise agentic AI platform designed to govern AI agents in production workflows.

The platform qualifies agents against customer-defined benchmarks before execution, tracks cost per completed task, and runs within the customer's own cloud or on-premises infrastructure. This approach aims to provide control over authorised actions and spending limits for deployed agents.

Platform capabilities and deployment

KeOS allows business teams to describe tasks in plain English, after which the system drafts an agent for review and testing. The platform includes pre-built deployment templates to facilitate setup in existing cloud ecosystems. It sets spending limits for each agent and monitors the cost per completed task, enabling teams to compare agent-assisted work costs against current processes before scaling usage.

Customers can select models suited to specific workflows, including Anthropic models and those available through Microsoft and Amazon, while retaining existing provider agreements. The platform supports deployment in Microsoft Azure or Amazon Web Services environments, or on-premises, ensuring compliance with existing data governance policies.

Operational impact and leadership view

Kellton reported that a leading US financial institution used KeOS to build AI agents that reduced loan application processing time by 
47%
. This reduction translated into faster decisions for customers and greater operational capacity while maintaining control and oversight.

Krishna Chintam, Global CEO of Kellton Tech Solutions, stated that the business case for agentic AI begins with improving process efficiency. He noted that the company built KeOS to integrate its engineering experience into the platform, allowing clients to identify where AI agents improve performance versus where human judgement remains essential.

About Kellton Tech Solutions

Kellton is an AI-led digital transformation and technology consulting company with global delivery capabilities. The firm partners with enterprises to build scalable digital platforms and accelerate intelligent automation journeys. With over 2,000 professionals across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, it delivers solutions across cloud, data, AI, and enterprise platforms.

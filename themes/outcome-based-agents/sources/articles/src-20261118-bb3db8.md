Ema announced the availability of its AI Employees for HR, IT and Finance in Microsoft Marketplace, the unified online destination for customers to buy trusted cloud solutions, AI apps and agents to meet their business needs. Ema customers can now discover and deploy trusted solutions through Microsoft Marketplace, with smooth integration and streamlined management across Microsoft Azure and other Microsoft products. Organizations can start a free trial from the listing and put Ema?s AI Employees to work through Microsoft Teams, existing applications, or voice, with each one acting within the permissions of the person using it.
Ema's AI Employees take on the work that fills HR, IT, and Finance queues: onboarding a new hire, resolving an access request, answering a benefits question. They ask for approval when a decision needs a person, and they log every action. Ema's pre-built AI Employees arrive with HR, IT and Finance domain knowledge already in place, and the trial includes more than 300 templates to start from.
Teams describe the outcome they want, add their own policies and procedures, and connect their systems through pre-built connectors. With Ema Autopilot, they build or change an AI Employee in plain language and test it against real scenarios before it goes live. Ema?s AI Employees are in production at some of the world?s largest enterprises, serving more than one million employees.
At Wipro, they handle 2.9 million queries a year for more than 240,000 associates and have cut HR response times from days to seconds.

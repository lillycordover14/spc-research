Ski film festival aims to raise $10,000 for Vermont Adaptive
Courtesy First Stop Board Barn
The third annual Quality Ski Time Film Tour Festival will occur at the Paramount Theatre on Thursday, Nov. 19.

Thursday, Nov. 19 at 7 p.m. —RUTLAND— First Stop Board Barn will host its third annual Quality Ski Time Film Tour Festival at the Paramount Theatre, 30 Center St., with a goal of raising $10,000 for Vermont Adaptive Ski and Sports.

Powered by Salomon, the event will feature ski and snowboard films, appearances by professional athletes, autograph opportunities and thousands of dollars in prize giveaways. Advance tickets are $15, plus applicable taxes and fees.

Last year’s festival attracted more than 400 attendees and raised more than $5,000 for Vermont Adaptive. Organizers hope to exceed 500 attendees this year, and additional sponsorship support will allow them to expand the event while reducing advance ticket prices.

“Last year’s turnout blew us away,” said Randy Elles, event organizer and brand director of First Stop Board Barn. “Thanks to Salomon and our other sponsors, we’ve been able to offer even more this year while bringing ticket prices down to $15. We want this to be a night everyone can be part of. We’re calling on Vermont’s ski and snowboard community to pack the Paramount, help us reach our $10,000 fundraising goal, and make this an annual tradition—the unofficial kickoff to winter.”

For Elles, a longtime member of the Killington ski community, supporting adaptive sports has a personal significance.

His brother, Maxwell Elles, suffered a spinal cord injury in a snowboarding accident in 2012.

“My brother, Maxwell Elles, a Killington Mountain School alumnus, suffered a spinal cord injury in a snowboarding accident on Ovation at Killington Resort in 2012,” Elles said. “At first, he couldn’t walk. With support from organizations like Vermont Adaptive and the High Fives Foundation, he’s made an incredible recovery. This event is a way to support other adaptive athletes like Max while inspiring the next generation of skiers and riders to see where their passion can take them.”

Net proceeds from ticket sales will benefit Vermont Adaptive, which provides inclusive sports and recreational programming for people of all abilities, regardless of their ability to pay.

The organization offers year-round activities including skiing, snowboarding, paddling, cycling, mountain biking, sailing and climbing, along with programs and retreats for veterans.

The festival will also give attendees opportunities to meet professional athletes from Salomon’s team and have posters signed. Prize giveaways will feature merchandise donated by outdoor brands including Burton, Darn Tough, Helly Hansen, Oakley, Patagonia and Smartwool.

Event sponsors include Longview Commercial Real Estate, Forerunner Ski Shop, Killington/Pico Resort, Killington Mountain School, Von Trapp Brewing, Ski Vermont, Haselkorn Inc. and Vermont Regenerative Medicine.

Organizers are seeking additional support from local businesses and community partners through sponsorships, prize donations and group attendance. Sponsorship contributions help offset event expenses and increase the amount raised for Vermont Adaptive.

Founded in 1979, First Stop Board Barn is a family-owned ski, snowboard, bike and paddle shop in Killington. The business has served recreational enthusiasts and competitive athletes for more than 47 years.

The Quality Ski Time Film Tour Festival celebrates the start of the winter sports season while supporting opportunities for people with disabilities to participate in outdoor recreation.

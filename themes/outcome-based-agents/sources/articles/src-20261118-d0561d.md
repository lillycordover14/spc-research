Sharon Horgan to headline the inaugural Harper’s Bazaar Power Salon&nbsp;&nbsp;Harper's BAZAAR

[page text]
We earn a commission for products purchased through some links in this article.
The multi-award winning Sharon Horgan will lead a line-up of fascinating women at 
Harper’s Bazaar
’s first-ever Power Salon. The intimate evening, in partnership with Natwest, will feature insightful talks and an elegant champagne reception.
Themed ‘The Power of Ambition,’ the event will celebrate the changemakers who are redefining the way we view the world and work. 
Bazaar
 editors will host two discussions with women who have created bold new visions for their lives and careers, centred on self-belief and big ideas. 
Our headline speaker is the hugely successful actress, writer, director and producer 
Sharon Horgan
, whose hit shows – including 
Catastrophe
 and 
Motherland
 – have dominated the zeitgeist. She will be in conversation with 
Bazaar's 
editor-in-chief, Lydia Slater, about her own relationship with ambition and how she has made her vision a reality in a career spanning decades. 
Dave Benett
//
Getty Images
Held on Tuesday 24 November, at the glamorous 
Hotel Café Royal
, the evening is an opportunity for our audience to be inspired, network and reflect on how they can be ambitious in redefining their own lives, while meeting likeminded women who will become part of their journey. 
Open to both 
Bazaar
 readers and 
Privé
 members, the event features a champagne and canapé reception interspersed by talks and conversations featuring inspirational female speakers and a chance to network with fellow guests. 
The evening will begin with a panel discussion dedicated to what it takes to being the change-maker of your own life, featuring insight from Natwest's MD of premier banking, Tamara van den Ban, and the CEO and founder of Vinterior, Sandrine Zhang Ferron.  
Bazaar
 has a long tradition of celebrating women’s achievements and unlocking their potential, dating right back to the magazine’s launch in 1867: its founding editor, Mary Louise Booth, was herself a suffragist. Today, that legacy continues with our ongoing 
Bazaar 
At Work 
programme, which honours the successes of women role models from all walks of life, whether entrepreneurs and business executives or activists and philanthropists.
Our past speakers at 
Bazaar
 events – who have included Nazanin Zaghari Ratcliffe, Adjoa Andoh, Nicola Fox, Daisy Edgar-Jones, Carolyn McCall, Sam McAlister, Felicity Blunt, Elizabeth Nyamayaro, Clare Waight Keller, Dina Asher-Smith, Gemma Arterton and Stephanie Shirley – showcase the breadth of talents among the women we respect and admire.
Tickets for this event are available 
here
. Access includes a champagne and canapé reception, all talks and a luxury goody bag.

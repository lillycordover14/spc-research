Agentic Orchestration for Enterprise
 

 

AI Platforms
 

 

Futurum Signal: Agentic Orchestration for Enterprise evaluates 11 vendors on their ability to coordinate, govern, and audit multi-agent work in production. Microsoft, Salesforce, and ServiceNow lead the Elite Zone as enterprises shift from deploying agents to controlling them.
Agentic orchestration for enterprise is moving from pilots into production, as organizations shift from asking whether to deploy AI agents to how to coordinate and govern dozens of them across systems. Futurum Signal: Agentic Orchestration for Enterprise provides a structured assessment of 11 vendors on their ability to run multi-agent work in documented enterprise production, maintain state across long-running tasks, enforce controls while agents act, and govern agents built by other vendors. Futurum Research projects Operations & Workflow spending on AI platforms will grow from $15.4 billion in 2025 to $92.2 billion in 2030, a 43.1% CAGR and the fastest-growing use case in its forecast.
 

A core theme in the report is that governance, not routing logic, now separates the leaders. Excellence in the Elite Zone, where Microsoft, Salesforce, and ServiceNow lead, is defined by turning large enterprise footprints into authoritative control planes that enforce runtime guardrails, manage agent identities, and capture cross-vendor agent activity at scale. The Leader Zone features AWS, Google Cloud, IBM, and UiPath, each with deep orchestration runtimes and strong standards alignment, while Databricks and LangChain in the Established Zone and OpenAI and SAP in the Aspiring Zone deliver targeted capabilities but have fewer verifiable production references and out-of-the-box governance controls. Moving forward, market leadership will depend on how quickly vendors turn protocol support for MCP and A2A into audit-ready evidence and prove that autonomous multi-agent coordination delivers measurable business returns.

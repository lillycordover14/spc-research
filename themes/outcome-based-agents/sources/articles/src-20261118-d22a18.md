The #14 Tennessee Volunteers made a statement on Tuesday night, shutting down #2 Michigan State in a dominant 61-46 win at Food City Center. With the win, the Big Orange move to 9-0 at home against AP top six teams in the Rick Barnes era. It also marks the 15th top-two win in program history.

UT held the Spartans to just 9-of-50 shooting. That marks the fewest field goals made by Michigan State in 30 years and fewest by any DI team since Jan. 2024.
While Tennessee scored first and forced misses on the Spartans’ first five field goal attempts, Michigan State quickly went on a 14-2 run to make it 14-4 early. The Vols countered with 10 of the next 14 points in the game, only trailing 18-14 with nine minutes left in the first half. The Big Orange then went on an 11-0 run to take a 27-24 lead with just over a minute to go.
Michigan State was held scoreless for over 4:30 and did not make a field goal in the final five minutes of the first half, missing six straight attempts.
UT took a 27-25 lead into the locker room and went on a 12-0 run to begin the second half, giving the team a 39-25 lead with 16:30 left on the clock. The Spartans did not make a field goal until the 15:03 mark, once again, missing their first seven of the half.
While Michigan State was able to hold Tennessee scoreless for nearly seven minutes, the Vols responded with a 10-0 run to close out the game. The final 15-point decision matched the largest lead of the night. It was also the third-largest win over an AP top-five team in program history.
Sophomore forward DeWayne Brown II and senior center Miles Rubin each had 14 points. Brown made all six of his field goal attempts, while Rubin was 6-of-8. The duo also combined for 15 rebounds. Brown’s seven boards were tied for a game and season-high, adding a game-high three blocks.
Junior guards Juke Harris and Terrence Hill Jr. each had eight points. Harris also had five rebounds and a game-best four assists.
Michigan State star and Big 10 Preseason Player of the Year Jeremy Fears was held without a field goal, with all 16 of his points coming from the charity stripe.
Tennessee improves to 3-5 in the all-time series, notching their first win over the Spartans since 1971.
The Vols will continue this homestand on Friday when the team welcomes in Coastal Carolina at 7 p.m.

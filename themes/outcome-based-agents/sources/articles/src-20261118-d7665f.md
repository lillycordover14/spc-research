Skan AI Ranked Number 155 Fastest-Growing Company in North America on the 2026 Deloitte Technology Fast 500™

Tweet

Skan AI attributes its three-year 457% revenue growth to helping enterprises build the sovereign context layer they need to operationalize agentic AI.
MENLO PARK, Calif.
, 
Nov. 18, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- Skan AI today announced that it ranked number 155 on the 
Deloitte Technology Fast 500™
, a ranking of the 500 fastest-growing tech companies in North America, now in its 32nd year. Skan AI grew 457% from 2022 to 2025.

Avinash Misra, Skan AI CEO and co-founder, credits the company's product, engineering, and go-to-market teams' efforts building the agentic AI platform that helps companies take control of their context and use it to redefine how agents and teams work together.
"When Manish Garg and I founded Skan AI, we recognized that enterprises lacked a complete understanding of their operations. Today, our platform not only continuously captures how work happens but also equips enterprises to reimagine their operations with AI agents," Misra said. "Renting intelligence is no longer enough to compete; enterprises must own their operational context, end to end. With Skan AI, our customers are continuously building the sovereign context layer they need to operationalize AI at scale."
Manish Garg, chief product officer and co-founder at Skan I, said, "The Skan AI platform is at the forefront of turning context and governance into a sovereign, unified foundation for enterprise AI. We've grown rapidly because our platform solves the biggest challenge regulated enterprises face: running AI agents that their teams can rely on, even as the work itself and its context change."

"The 2026 rankings demonstrate that established momentum and breakthrough growth can drive sustained market leadership," said Justin Yahr, Audit & Assurance partner and National Emerging Growth Company leader, Deloitte & Touche LLP. "Nearly 70% of this year's award winners returned as repeat honorees, reflecting the power of disciplined and sustained execution. More than 30% made the list for the first time, illustrating how quickly new players are shaping their respective sectors. Furthermore, private companies represented almost 80% of the list, a clear indication that the agility and strategic focus of private enterprises remain essential to scaling in competitive markets."
Skan AI previously ranked 266th as a Technology Fast 500 award winner for 2025, based on its fiscal year revenue growth from 2021 to 2024.
About the 2026 Deloitte Technology Fast 500
Now in its 32nd year, the Deloitte Technology Fast 500 provides a ranking of the fastest-growing tech companies — both public and private — in North America. Technology Fast 500 award winners are selected based on percentage fiscal year revenue growth from 2022 to 2025.
In order to be eligible for Technology Fast 500 recognition, companies must own proprietary intellectual property or proprietary technology that significantly contributes to the company's operating revenues. Companies must have base-year (fiscal year 2022) operating revenues of at least US$50,000, and current-year (fiscal year 2025) operating revenues of at least US$5 million, with a growth rate of 50% or greater. Additionally, companies must be in business for a minimum of four years and be headquartered within North America (United States and Canada).
About Skan AI 
Skan AI helps teams and AI agents in regulated industries run complex, mission-critical workflows at scale. The platform captures how work gets done, building a Context Graph of Work that grounds agents and establishing procedures that govern every action they take. Skan AI deploys with zero integrations, keeps data inside the customer's environment, is SOC 2, ISO 27001, and ISO 42001 certified, and is HIPAA compliant. Enterprises using Skan AI turn their context into governed agents running in production in weeks, not months, and agent procedures improve with every result. That's why Skan AI works with 1 in 4 Fortune 50 companies, 7 in 10 top US banks, and 3 in 5 top US insurers.

Crescendo 2026 Raises More Than $1.3 Million for UMKC Conservatory Students

The annual gala celebrated the arts while generating critical support for scholarships and student opportunities at the UMKC Conservatory

 
Crescendo 2026 brought community leaders, alumni, donors and arts supporters together at the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art to celebrate and invest in the future of the arts through the University of Missouri-Kansas City Conservatory.

The event raised more than $1.3 million to support student scholarships, academic programs and transformative educational experiences.

As the Conservatory's premier annual fundraising event, Crescendo has become a Kansas City tradition, bringing together supporters for an immersive evening that showcases the talent and creativity of Conservatory students. This year's event featured performances by dancers, actors and musicians throughout the museum's galleries and performance spaces, offering guests a unique opportunity to experience the artistic excellence that defines the Conservatory.

Led by event chair Stephanie O'Dear and honorary chairs Farhad and Lynda Azima, the annual gala highlighted the talent and promise of Conservatory students while demonstrating the Kansas City community's continued commitment to arts education. Proceeds from the event will help provide opportunities for the next generation of performers, artists and creative leaders, while supporting the continued expansion of the Olson Performing Arts Center.

Strong soybean crush demand supports local basis levels

 Read

Summary

                        Strong soybean processing demand is helping support soybean basis levels at Minnesota crush plants. NOPA reports October soybean crush reached a record 234 million bushels, up three percent from a year ago.                    

A market analyst in central Minnesota says soybean prices have been strong at local soybean processing plants.

“You’re already seeing some of the larger crush facilities start to narrow their basis, offering what is better than typical at this point. I think the demand is there,” she says. “They also know that they’ll need to keep up with this because there’s a lot of unknowns moving forward.”

U.S. soybean crush has been strong in the last month. The National Oilseed Processors Association says more than 234 million bushels of soybeans were crushed in October, up three percent from a year ago.

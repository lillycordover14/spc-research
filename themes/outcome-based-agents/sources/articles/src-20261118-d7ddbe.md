Dylan Harper Injured Slipping on Court, Latest on Spurs Star After Loss to Pistons
Adam Wells
Nov 18, 2026
In addition to suffering a 105-93 loss to the Detroit Pistons on Tuesday night, the San Antonio Spurs could be without Dylan Harper for a bit after limping off the court in the fourth quarter due to a hip injury. 
Harper slipped on the court right as Stephon Castle was passing the ball to him and appeared to be favoring his groin area, but Spurs head coach Mitch Johnson said they were still waiting for more information on an official diagnosis.  
"It just looked like he slipped obviously," Johnson 
said
. "So, hoping for the best. But don't know anything yet."

Harper was helped off the court and to the tunnel by Spurs teammates Keldon Johnson and David Jones Garcia. He went back to the locker room with the assistance of a team staffer. 
Victor Wembanyama 
called it
 a "terrible feeling" seeing a teammate go down like that, but he is hoping for the best. 
"It's a terrible feeling, terrible sight. I know as much as you guys, so I hope he's fine...But, yeah, I just hate this. I don't know how else I can say this," Wembanyama said.
Spurs guard De'Aaron Fox noted they were able to navigate things at times last season when players were out due to injuries, so they are set up to succeed again this season. 
"At certain parts of [last] season we didn't have certain guys and what made us go is we were able to kind of cover for guys when they were out," Fox said. "So, right now, it looks like we might have to do that again."
Harper finished the night with 20 points and four rebounds in 35 minutes. The 20-year-old has taken a step forward early in his second season, averaging 18.4 points, 5.4 rebounds and 4.5 assists per game. He ranks in the top three on the Spurs in all three categories. 
Tuesday's loss snapped the Spurs' 12-game winning streak after starting the season 0-2. They will look to get back on track Thursday when they host the Los Angeles Clippers.

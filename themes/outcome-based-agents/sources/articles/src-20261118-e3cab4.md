            Druid AI wins Microsoft award for healthcare agents
          

            Wed, 18th Nov 2026 (Today)
          

                SHADDA LIE CATUGAL
              

Editor

Druid AI has won Microsoft's 2026 Agents Partner of the Year Award, which recognises work on autonomous AI agents used in live business operations.

The winning submission focused on Druid AI's work with Regina Maria, a private healthcare network in Central and Eastern Europe. Six AI agents used by the healthcare provider handle about 1 million conversations a month for patients and employees.

Druid AI said the agents have been in production since 2018 and were built on Microsoft Azure and Microsoft Dynamics 365. It said they resolve 77% of patient interactions from start to finish, while the remaining cases are passed to a team of 17 advisers with the full conversation history.

The same approach is now being used in other sectors, including banking, insurance and higher education, with Druid AI citing work with Optegra's UK eye hospitals and Romania's Banca Transilvania.

Druid AI was also named Microsoft Romania Partner of the Year for the same work. That follows earlier recognition from Microsoft in 2025, when the company was identified as the Certified Software Provider for Healthcare AI in Central and Eastern Europe, the Middle East and Africa.

Microsoft says the Agents award recognises partners that move beyond prompt-based copilots to autonomous agents that can reason, plan and act across business systems to complete processes end to end. The category reflects broader efforts by technology suppliers and their customers to push AI projects from pilot programmes into day-to-day operations.

Healthcare deployment

The Regina Maria deployment is central to Druid AI's case for production use of AI agents in regulated settings. The company said patient data remains within Regina Maria's Microsoft environment and that GDPR consent is built into every conversation.

Cosmin Panaete, Business Process Director at Regina Maria, said: "In healthcare, AI can only deliver real value when it operates reliably, at scale and in full compliance with the standards and regulations that govern our industry. Since 2018, Druid has been a trusted partner in our digital transformation journey, with a consistent focus on delivering tangible business outcomes rather than just promising potential. Together, we have evolved from a single AI assistant to a network of six AI agents, helping our teams save valuable time, streamline workflows and focus more on what matters most: our patients. This is what a successful technology partnership looks like: turning innovation into measurable impact."

Druid AI said its systems have handled more than 1.2 billion automated conversations across more than 15 countries. The company positions its software as digital workers that can complete tasks across corporate systems and then pass a case to a human employee when judgment is required.

Joe Kim, President and CEO of Druid AI, said: "This award recognises AI agents that deliver real value to businesses year after year. Our customers want technologies that grow revenue, reduce costs and save time. When agents are developed, orchestrated, governed and measured from the design stage, they can achieve amazing results. This is true for general practitioners, hospital networks, banks, insurance and retail."

Production focus

The announcement comes as many companies are still trying to turn experimental AI work into systems that can be used at scale. Across the sector, suppliers have increasingly focused on tools that connect AI models to internal workflows, customer service systems and back-office processes.

For Druid AI, Regina Maria was an early enterprise customer and remains a long-running example of how the model can be applied over several years. The relationship started with a single virtual assistant before expanding to six AI agents, the company said.

Andreea Pleșea, Co-founder and COO of Druid AI, said: "Regina Maria was one of our first enterprise customers. We started in 2018 with a single virtual assistant, and today six Druid AI agents work every day for patients and for the people who care for them. We believe AI should make people's lives better, not replace them. We are very proud that technology built in Romania is being recognised by Microsoft on a global stage."

Druid AI also linked the award to its long-standing partnership with Microsoft and to wider adoption through Microsoft's commercial channels.

Irina Dochitu, Vice President of Global Partners and Alliances at Druid AI, said: "Being named Romania Partner of the Year as well makes it personal for a company with deep roots here. I want to thank the Microsoft partner team who opened doors. With Druid AI now transactable on Microsoft Marketplace, the partnership is ready for its next stage, and we intend to grow it from here."

Nicole Dezen, Chief Partner Officer and Corporate Vice President at Microsoft, said: "Congratulations to the winners and finalists of the 2026 Microsoft Partner of the Year Awards. This year's honourees are harnessing the power of Microsoft Cloud and AI to deliver transformative solutions, accelerate customer success and expand what's possible. Their ingenuity and impact inspire us, demonstrating what can be achieved when bold vision and technology come together to empower organisations around the world."

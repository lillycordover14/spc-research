BURTON — The Burton Lady Panthers used a strong first quarter on Tuesday night to defeat Sealy 43-32 at Burton High School.
Burton (2-1) picked up its second consecutive win after falling in its season opener against Anderson-Shiro earlier this year. Both wins in its current streak have come against Class 4A teams, two classifications above Burton.
The Lady Panthers started strong with a 13-2 opening quarter. Four different Lady Panthers scored in the period, including six points from guard Leilah Ellis.

Sealy edged Burton 8-7 in the second quarter, but the Lady Panthers still took a 20-10 lead into halftime. 
Burton and Sealy played competitive quarters in the second half. Both teams scored 11 points in the third quarter while Burton edged Sealy 12-11 in the fourth quarter.
Ellis finished with 13 points to lead Burton. She also tallied five rebounds, three steals and one assist. Sophomore forward Harper Hodde was close behind her teammate with 12 points, including three 3-point baskets. Hodde also tallied five rebounds, three steals and two assists.
Senior guard Valerie Hernandez earned nine points, making four shots and one of her two free throws. She also led Burton’s ball distribution with four assists and added three steals and one rebound.
Junior guard Laci Broesche earned six points, six rebounds, three steals, one assist and one of Burton’s two blocks. Sophomore guard Peyton Schubert tallied Burton’s other block and added one rebound.
Sophomore Myliah Barksdale gave Burton three points in the fourth quarter.
Burton will head to the Jerry Daniel Invitational in Martin’s Mill for its first early-season tournament of the year. The Lady Panthers will open tournament play at 1 p.m. Thursday against Dallas Sunset.

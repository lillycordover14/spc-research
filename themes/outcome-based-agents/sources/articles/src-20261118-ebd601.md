In this interview from Money20/20 USA, Ping Wu, CEO of Cresta, discusses AI-powered customer experiences, AI-agent detection, workflow automation, and the future of enterprise customer service.JD Durkin spoke with Wu about customer experience technology, artificial intelligence, contact centers, digital workforces, and operational efficiency.A significant portion of the discussion focuses on creating seamless customer journeys across voice, chat, and other communication channels.The interview also explores how businesses can leverage AI to deliver more consistent and connected customer experiences.Another major theme is the growing presence of AI agents acting on behalf of consumers and the implications for enterprise systems.The conversation further examines Cresta Sentry, a platform designed to identify whether customer interactions originate from humans or AI agents.The interview also highlights why businesses increasingly need visibility into agent-driven interactions and automated workflows.Another key topic is the emergence of digital workforces that operate alongside human employees within customer-service organizations.The discussion also explores deputy agents and how AI systems can continue executing business processes after customer interactions have concluded.Additionally, Wu discusses opportunities to automate back-office tasks and improve operational efficiency across enterprise workflows.The conversation further examines the importance of preserving customer context throughout service, support, and operational processes.Another focus is workforce augmentation. Wu believes AI should help employees become more productive while still preserving the role of human expertise.The interview also highlights the continued importance of human interaction during sensitive, emotional, and high-complexity customer scenarios.A significant theme is the feedback loop between employees and AI systems, where human expertise contributes to continual improvement of automated workflows.Looking ahead, Wu expects greater adoption of AI-powered customer experience platforms, agentic workflows, enterprise automation, and technologies designed to coordinate human and digital workers.Throughout the discussion, Wu shares perspectives on artificial intelligence, customer experience, digital labor, workflow automation, contact centers, AI agents, enterprise software, customer service, business operations, and technology innovation.Disclaimer: Informational content only.

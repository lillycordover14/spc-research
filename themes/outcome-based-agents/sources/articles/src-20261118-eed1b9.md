The C-PACE program is an innovative financing tool that enables commercial property owners to access private capital for qualifying projects that improve building performance and sustainability.

Eligible improvements may include energy efficiency upgrades, renewable energy installations, water conservation measures, and resiliency enhancements. C-PACE financing is repaid through a voluntary assessment attached to the property, allowing for long-term, competitive financing that often aligns with the useful life of the improvements.

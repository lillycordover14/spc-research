Who’s ready for the holidays in Atlanta? Uh, everyone is! Get into the festive spirit by checking out Virginia-Highland’s adorable annual Winterfest (and get a jump on that holiday shopping, too!) 

Keep scrolling to see just what you can get excited for at Winterfest!

What can Winterfest attendees expect?

 Virginia-Highland’s Winterfest has a little something for everyone to enjoy! Let us break down the weekend’s events for you.

The Jingle Jog

Virginia-Highland

When
: 
December 12th from 8:00-9:30 AM

Get a lil active this holiday season at the fun Virginia-Highland Jingle Jog! 

The 5K starts at 8:00 AM, with costumes encouraged and a long sleeve tee for every runner. The Elf Dash for runners under 12 follows at 9:00 AM.

Wanna sign up to run? Click here!

The Winterfest Gift Market

Virginia-Highland

When
: December 12th from 10:30 AM-4:30 PM

Find the perfect gift for everyone on your list at the Winterfest Gift Market! Shop 80 local artisan vendors and 20 young entrepreneurs!

From youth-made crafts to unique vintage jewelry, the Gift Market is the perfect place to shop small for the perfect one-of-a-kind gift for everyone!

But that’s not all! During the market, festival-goers can expect live music from:

30-member Atlanta Vocal Project

Christmas carolers

a women’s choir

youth performances from Ztunes Music and Tutu School Virginia Highland

And, if you’re chilly, grab a delicious drink from the Chalet-style craft cocktail and hot chocolate bar!

The Kids Corner

Virginia-Highland

When
: December 12th from 10:30 AM-4:30 PM

The kiddos will enjoy festive crafts, face painting, bounce houses, snowglobe making, and letters to Santa!

Oh, and there will also be plenty of FREE photos with Santa & Mrs. Claus!

The Virginia-Highland Tour of Homes

Virginia-Highland

When
: December 12th & 13th, multiple times

Step inside some of Virginia-Highland’s most beautiful homes, dressed up for the season. Take this self-guided tour to explore the neighborhood’s unique architecture, history, and hospitality.

Learn more & snag your tickets, here!

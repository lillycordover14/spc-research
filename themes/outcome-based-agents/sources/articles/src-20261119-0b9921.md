Koch and HappyRobot: Reimagining HR with AI Agents&nbsp;&nbsp;PR Newswire

[page text]
WICHITA, Kan.
, 
Nov. 19, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- What started as a two-day workshop in Wichita has grown into a thriving partnership exploring how AI agents can make everyday Human Resources interactions faster, simpler and more human for Koch employees, candidates and HR teams.

Koch is partnering with HappyRobot, the company putting AI agents to work across complex enterprise operations, to test how agents can support employees and candidates while giving HR professionals more time to focus on people and higher-value work.
HR teams handle a steady stream of questions about payroll, benefits and policies, while recruiters spend significant time on early-stage candidate conversations. AI agents could give employees faster answers, provide a better experience for candidates and allow HR teams to spend more time on work that requires human judgment and expertise.
"AI is transforming how we work, and that includes leveraging agents across Human Resources. We are experimenting, learning and finding new ways of working to position us for the future. By putting AI agents to work on everyday requests, we're enabling our workforce to focus on what matters most: helping employees develop, learn, continue to self-actualize and maximize their contributions," said Cara Chennault-Reid, vice president of human resources at Koch.
The journey began just a few months ago, when Koch and HappyRobot teams gathered in Wichita for a two-day workshop. The session brought together HR professionals, business leaders and the HappyRobot team to work side by side.
Rather than starting with the technology, the group started with the problems. They mapped where employees and candidates wait for answers, where HR professionals repeat the same tasks and where a faster, more consistent experience could create real value.
By the end of the second day, the teams had identified a handful of promising use cases and built two working prototypes ready for testing.
"This felt different from a typical vendor experience. Rather than just showing us their product, we worked together, challenging both their assumptions and ours while building something we could actually test. Within a couple of days, we had a working solution that was already 75% to 80% of the way toward what we were trying to accomplish," said Jeff Wendling, recruiting innovation lead at Koch.
HappyRobot's AI agents can interact across phone, email, documents and the web and connect with existing business systems. Koch teams are currently testing and exploring applications in two HR areas:
Recruiting:
 An AI agent conducts initial candidate prescreening calls, confirms key qualifications and captures information for recruiters. The goal is faster follow-up for candidates and more time for recruiters to build relationships with talent. The agent is working 24/7, allowing us to screen candidates around the clock.
HR Service Center: 
Koch and HappyRobot are exploring how AI agents can help employees navigate common HR questions, including payroll and tax navigation inquiries, and connect them with existing resources to find the information they need.
In each case, the goal is the same: faster, more consistent support for employees and candidates, with HR professionals available for the conversations where their judgment and expertise matter most.
Since the workshop, Koch and HappyRobot teams have continued working side by side, testing ideas, learning from what works and identifying new use cases to explore.
Ongoing conversations among HR experts, business leaders and HappyRobot engineers continue to surface fresh opportunities, reflecting the collaborative, experiment-and-learn mindset that drives innovation at Koch.
"What makes this partnership work is that we built it together from day one. The Koch HR professionals brought deep knowledge of the work, and HappyRobot brought the technology; and in just two days we turned ideas into working prototypes. That spirit has carried through every conversation since," said Tanner Gonzalez, director of Koch LabsÂ®.
"The Koch team rolled up their sleeves from the very start. Working side by side with their HR professionals, we learned as much as we built, and we're excited to keep building together," said Pablo Palafox, co-founder and CEO of HappyRobot.
Koch gets to test emerging technology against real business needs, while HappyRobot gets direct feedback from people using its technology in a complex operating environment.
HR is only the beginning. Teams are exploring potential applications for AI agents in customer support, logistics, sales and other service functions across Koch. The idea is simple: Pair people who understand the work with technology that can help them do it better. If it works, employees spend less time on repetitive tasks and searching for answers, and more time solving problems and creating value.

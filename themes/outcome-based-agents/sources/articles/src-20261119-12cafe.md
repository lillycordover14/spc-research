Sierra Reportedly Eyes $30B Valuation as AI Agent Race Heats Up&nbsp;&nbsp;CX Today

[page text]
Sierra, the AI customer service startup led by Bret Taylor, is in early talks to raise about $1.5 billion at a valuation near $30 billion, The Information reports. That would nearly double the $15.8 billion it was valued at in May. Sierra hasn't confirmed the round and terms could change. Investors are paying software-platform prices for AI agents that resolve customer issues, which is pressure on every incumbent's roadmap.

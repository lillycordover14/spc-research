Kestra’s Bluespring Buys $1.6 Billion Affiliate That Left Commonwealth Last Year


                      by                      Ali Hibbs                    

November 19, 2026

 

(L-R) Ronald Lomangino, William Grundig and William “Bud” James have sold their $1.6 billion Kestra-affiliated practice to Kestra subsidiary Bluespring Wealth.

Bluespring Wealth, the RIA acquisition arm of independent broker-dealer Kestra Financial, purchased a Kestra-affiliated practice with $1.6 billion in assets, according to an announcement on Thursday.

Freehold, New Jersey-based Dynasty Advisors was owned by William A. Grundig, Ronald J. Lomangino and William G. “Bud” James, according to the announcement. The team had registered with Kestra’s brokerage and advisory units in July 2025 after almost nine years with Commonwealth Financial Network. LPL Financial had 
agreed to buy Commonwealth
 four months earlier.

The Dynasty team, which totals 20 members, additionally works from offices in Clark, New Jersey, and Bucks, Chester and Montgomery counties, Pennsylvania, according to the announcement. 

Terms of the deal, which closed Nov. 16, were not disclosed. The team will remain registered under Kestra’s broker-dealer and RIA, said a company spokesperson.

Grundig and Lomangino teamed up in 2005, and James joined them in 2007, according to the announcement. Before Commonwealth, the trio was affiliated with Securities America from 2008 to 2016, records show. 

Grundig oversees operations and client relationships, while Lomangino and James lead the wealth management practice and staff development. The firm also runs an employer retirement plan and group benefits businesses.

“As we look to the future, Bluespring gives us the opportunity to continue advancing while staying true to the culture and personal approach that shaped our practice,” Lomangino said in a statement.

Dynasty is the ninth RIA Bluespring has acquired this year, representing $7.2 billion in collective assets, according to the spokesperson. Other deals include Coghill Investment Strategies, SHP Financial, 
Synthesis Wealth Planning
, 
Element Wealth Advisors
 and Whitaker-Myers Wealth Managers, according to Bluespring. Synthesis and Element were also Kestra affiliates.

Kestra Financial, which is majority owned by private equity firm Stone Point Capital, reported overseeing around $169 billion across its businesses at the end of 2025.

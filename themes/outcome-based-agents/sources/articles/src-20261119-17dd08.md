AVIANO AIR BASE, Italy — Contingency operations require more than airpower; they depend on securing resources, fostering relationships and making critical decisions under pressure. Airmen from the 31st Fighter Wing’s Contracting and Comptroller squadrons put those skills to the test during Exercise Denarii et Pactum; a two day contingency exercise at Aviano Air Base, Italy, Nov. 16-17, 2026.

The second iteration of the exercise challenged Airmen to establish and sustain a forward operating base in an austere environment, where limited resources and compressed timelines can complicate mission support.

Participants worked through simulated contracting actions, financial transactions and resource requirements while navigating challenges similar to those encountered during real-world contingency operations.

“Our standing guidance is simple: if it supports the commander’s mission priorities or protects the life, health and safety of the force, find a way to get to ‘yes’,” said U.S. Air Force Capt. Ty Modgling, 31st CONS flight commander and exercise lead.

By bringing contracting and comptroller personnel together, the exercise bolstered how funding approvals, contract execution and vendor payments depend on close coordination between the two career fields. Airmen also practiced safeguarding government funds, maintaining accountability and finding solutions when resources or established processes are limited.

“Relationships build bases,” Modgling said. “Our Airmen must be ready to form them under pressure.”

By working through realistic scenarios, Airmen strengthened their confidence, reinforced cross-functional teamwork and improved their ability to support contingency operations whenever and wherever the mission requires.

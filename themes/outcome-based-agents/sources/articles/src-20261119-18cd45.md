ATLANTA, Ga. (ATL Live) - Athens roots and Atlanta energy come together in 
Kobanova
’s red-clay sound. Founding members Wes Bacastow, Jimmy Riemer and Chris Dickson join ATL Live to share how they reunited and built momentum with nearly 30 shows in 2026, two EPs and a sold-out performance at Smith’s Olde Bar. The band is gearing up to open for fellow Athens-connected group Futurebirds at The Eastern on Black Friday. 
Kobanova also discusses plans for a sophomore album, new singles expected in spring 2027 and a New Year’s Eve surprise. Fans can look forward to soulful vocals, classic guitar riffs and the band’s next chapter.

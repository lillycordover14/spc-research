Dylan Harper could miss 2-3 weeks with left hip external rotator strain
That is much better news than a groin strain.
by
 
 
Marilyn Dubinski
Nov 19, 2026, 8:31 PM UTC
After the Spurs announced yesterday that Dylan Harper would miss today’s game against the Clippers with “hip soreness” but were still waiting for more information, they have now revealed that he suffered a left hip external rotator strain and could miss up to 2-3 weeks (although it could be less if all goes well). While any injury is not ideal news, it is certainly much less serious than a groin injury, which was the initial fear, and that timeline for return is friendlier than the 4-6 weeks social media doctors were suggesting when the injury occurred.
Harper suffered the injury in the final minute of Tuesday’s loss to the Pistons when he was running up the court and slipped, falling awkwardly into a splits and appearing to pull his groin. He stayed down for a bit before limping back to the locker room. This morning’s shootaround brought some relief ahead of the Spurs releasing the medical results, as Julian Champagnie hinted that it was not as serious as initially feared, and he was seen walking around without a limp.
Harper has been the Spurs’ second leading scorer this season after Victor Wembanyama, averaging 18.4 points on 50% shooting. He has also been the most efficient finisher at the rim among guards in the entire league, where he shoots 76.9% while averaging 5.4 rebounds and 4.5 assists per game in his first season as a starter.
Replacing his production will be a team effort. The Spurs still have De’Aaron Fox and Stephon Castle, and sliding Champagnie back into the starting line-up gives them the same unit that made the Finals last season, but bench production has been an issue so far this season. Expect Jordan McLaughlin to see some regular minutes at back-up point guard while Harper is out, and Ja’Kobi Gillespie, who is currently tearing up the G League, could be called up (down, since San Antonio is south?) from Austin.

Cruising Mist, with Tim Tetrick driving, won her fifth career race on Wednesday. PHOTO: Quenton Egan Photography

DOVER —
 Legacy Racing of Delaware’s homebred 
Cruising Mist
 cemented herself as the likely favorite in next week’s 
$110,000 Delaware Standardbred Breeders’ Fund series championship
 for 
2-year-old pacing fillies
 with her 
1:55.3 win
 in the first of two 
$20,000 second-round divisions
 on 
Wednesday, Nov. 18
, at 
Bally’s Dover
.

With 
Tim Tetrick
 again at the lines for trainer Wayne Givens, Cruising Mist methodically made her way to the top on the first turn, floating forward from post 7 and clearing to the pegs in front of Salty Beaches (driven by Allan Davis) before reaching the quarter in :29. Cruising Mist traveled uncontested through middle splits of :57.3 and 1:26.2 before shaking away from her six foes off the turn for home and evading a late inside rally from runner-up Unberdnbywhathsben (Jason Thompson) by a diminishing three-quarters of a length. Salty Beaches was a one-paced third.

Cruising Mist, a Powerful Mist-Image Cruisingaway filly with five wins and $54,668 in earnings from 10 starts, became the only two-time winner in the series. As the 7-5 favorite, she paid $4.80 to win.

In the second division, 61-1 long shot 
Sunday Suppers
 and driver 
Russell Foster
 hooked three-wide from the back of the pack turning for home and charged down the center of the track for a maiden-breaking 1:56.1 win. The U S Captain-Absolutely Best filly narrowly edged High Prophet (Corey Callahan) and Jobs Not Finished (Tetrick) in a four-horse photo finish, with first-round winner and 2-5 favorite Rynesha (Ryder Skinner) splitting foes to finish fourth, beaten just a head, after finding herself in traffic trouble on the far turn.

William Wyatt trains Sunday Suppers, who paid $124 to win, for owner Cherron Davis.

The eight fillies who qualified for the $110,000 series final, which will share top billing on the Wednesday, Nov. 25, card with the Grade 2 Progress Pace, are: Cruising Mist, Rynesha, Sunday Suppers, High Prophet, Salty Beaches, Unberdnbywhathsben, Jobs Not Finished and Beautiful Rock. There will be a $20,000 consolation for the remaining fillies.

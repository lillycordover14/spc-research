Somewhere between Halloween and New Year the gentle jingle of sleigh bells begins to build towards a glorious crescendo of festive music as we count down the sleeps to Christmas Day.
This week WA Ballet with WA Philharmonic Orchestra breaks out Tchaikovsky’s perennial favourite, The Nutcracker, at His Majesty’s Theatre from Friday until December 13, with evening and matinee shows to suit all ages.
Perth pianist James Dekleva also chimes in with a keyboard setting of the same work on Saturday evening at Government House Ballroom, part of an international tour taking in Taipei and Singapore.
The following weekend, WA Symphony Orchestra rolls out Handel’s Messiah, a choral staple written for Easter three centuries ago but now indelibly stamped on the Christmas scene.
Britain’s Chad Kelly conducts soloists Samantha Clarke, Ashlyn Tymms, Alasdair Kent and James Clayton with WASO Chorus at Winthrop Hall on November 27-28, for the climax of the orchestra’s classical season.
Next day, an Open Day at Government House features carols amid family entertainment to complement the City of Perth Christmas Lights Trail.
From December 1, Ellington Jazz Club has its own 12 days of Christmas with a dozen programs running until Christmas Eve, most from 6pm with matinees and late nights for families and night owls.
Alinta Energy’s Christmas Pageant takes over St George’s Terrace on December 5, from 7pm. Expect lights, action and music from festive marching bands, dancers, and corporate floats, with Santa and event broadcaster Channel 7.
The same weekend, OpusWA Arts Orchestra and Chorale reprise Messiah in a church setting, with performances at St Joseph’s, Subiaco, on December 4 at 7pm, and St Patrick’s, Mt Lawley, on December 6 at 3pm, directed by David Woods. They also tour to Geraldton’s Anglican Cathedral on December 12, at 7pm.

Mirabilis Collective brings a feminine touch to festivities with seasonal music written and performed by women in Christmas with Mirabilis, at UWA’s Wigmore Studio, on December 6 at 5pm.
For a family flavour, Nedlands Symphony Orchestra presents The Snowman, followed by a Christmas carol singalong at John Leckie Pavilion, Nedlands, on December 9 at 7pm.
Or catch Perth Symphony Orchestra playing the soundtrack for Christmas rom-com Love Actually, in concert at Riverside Theatre on December 9 from 7.30pm.
WASO returns to the fray on December 12 with Santa’s Symphony in the Park, a free concert at Burswood from 4pm. Jen Winley conducts the orchestra and WASO Chorus with host Genevieve McCarthy and singers Sophie Foster, Johnny Manuel, Annie Aitken and Gina Williams-Ghouse.
Two days later, West Coast Philharmonic Orchestra and conductor Sam Parry play for St George’s College Christmas in the Quad, with sopranos Emma Matthews and Lisa Harper-Brown, pianist Raymond Yong and saxophonist Erin Royer, on December 14 at 7pm.
Then the schedule gets busy.

Giovanni Consort launches a mini choral festival under guest director Alessandro Pittorino and organist Daniel van der Moezel, at St Mary’s, South Perth, on December 16, and at St Patrick’s Basilica, Fremantle, on December 17, from 7.30pm on both days. Festive favourites combine with works by local composers Perry Joyce and Lydia Gardiner in the repertoire.
Make an early start on December 17 for Morning Melodies 2026: Christmas with Gilbert and Sullivan at His Majesty’s Theatre, from 11am, featuring hits from the legendary comic operettas led by Stuart Maunder and WA Opera artists Claire Condipodero, Belinda Butler, Vin Trikeriotis and David Woods.
Then join Freeze Frame Opera under the stars for A Magical Christmas at the Quarry Amphitheatre, City Beach, with picnics from 6pm for a 7pm start, every night from December 17-20.
Singers Charis Postmus, Devon Lake, Robert Hofmann, Prudence Sanders, Caitlin Cassidy, Sam Claxton, Kohsei Gilkes, Michelle Pryor, Hattie Marshall and Penny Shaw join Tommaso Pollio (piano), Sophie Curtis (cello), Geoff Bourgault du Coudray (clarinet) and Daniel Susnjar (drums) for “music, magic, moonlight. . . and possibly a little mayhem!”

On December 18, Voyces choral ensemble returns to St Joseph’s, Subiaco, with traditional and contemporary settings of seasonal songs, starting at 7.30pm.
The same day, Perth Symphonic Chorus brings Handel’s Messiah to the heart of the city with Perth Baroque Orchestra and soloists at His Majesty’s Theatre, also from 7.30pm.
Marking 20 years of mystery and magic, WASO returns to RAC Arena on December 19 for the Christmas Spectacular, with performances at 2pm and 7pm.
Perth’s biggest free festive concert again features conductor Jen Winley with host Genevieve McCarthy and singers Sophie Foster, Johnny Manuel, Corinne Cowling, and Gina Williams-Ghouse, the WASO Chorus and Perth Broadway Chorus, plus saxophonist Matthew Styles.
That same weekend, The Ten Tenors bring A Not So Silent Night to His Majesty’s Theatre, on Saturday at 1.30pm and 7pm, with a 2pm Sunday matinee. Expect a blend of beloved traditional carols and fresh contemporary favourites.
Also that Sunday, UWA Choral Society brings its customary Christmas Celebration to Winthrop Hall, from 4pm.

Last but not least, HIP Company closes out the season at St Mary’s, South Perth, with Baroque Music for Yuletide, on December 22 from 7.30pm. The program features violinists Shaun Lee-Chen and Eliza McCracken, cellist Anton Baba, harpsichordist Stewart Smith and serene soprano Bonnie de la Hunty.
And when the turkey’s cold and the pies are all gone, Vienna Pops Orchestra and Perth Rotary celebrate New Year’s Eve, this time at His Majesty’s Theatre, with a matinee at 2pm and champagne gala from 9.30pm. See off 2026 and serenade 2027 with maestro Mark Coughlan to the timeless strains of Strauss.

On a jazzy note
Ellington’s celebration leads off with Nativity in Song, Carols with a Twist, on December 1, and Swingin’ Christmas with the Jackson Van Ballegooyen Trio the next day.
Sophie Foster and Jordan Anthony’s Christmas in the Key of Soul follows on December 6, with Bringing Back Bacharach Christmas Edition on December 8.
After that there’s Burlesque Christmas (December 12), A Very Bublé Christmas (December 17), and A Christmas Brass Party (December 18).
Saturday, December 19 features three shows: A Gospel Christmas with Providence Gospel Choir at noon; Perth Cabaret Collective Christmas Show at 6pm; and The Night Before Funkmas! from 10pm.
Jazz diva Libby Hammer presents her Kids’ Christmas Cushion Concert on December 20 from 3pm, followed by Christmas at The Ellington with Libby Hammer at 5pm, then 6pm every night until 24th.

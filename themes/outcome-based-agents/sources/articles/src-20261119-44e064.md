Harper Named to SEC Volleyball Community Service Team for Third Straight Season
 November 19, 2026 
 | 
Volleyball
Share:
ATHENS, Ga.
 – Georgia graduate middle blocker 
Tori Harper
 has been named to the 2026 Southeastern Conference Volleyball Community Service Team, the league announced Thursday. 

 

Harper is Georgia's appointee for the third consecutive season after being named to the team in 2024 and 2025. She is the third Bulldog to earn the honor three times, joining Sage Powell (2020-22) and Kathleen Gates (2009-11).

 

A native of Reno, Nevada, Harper has partnered with UGA's counseling services and the athletic department to promote mental health resources for student-athletes and has been a strong voice for student-athletes' mental health and well-being. She has volunteered at food banks and soup kitchens, helping sort donations, pack food boxes and serve meals to those in need. Harper has also volunteered at youth sports camps, leading team-building exercises that teach kids about trust, teamwork and good sportsmanship.

 

Harper was a nominee for the inaugural Allstate NACDA Fall Good Works Team in 2025. She graduated from Georgia with a degree in advertising in May 2026. In the classroom, Harper was named to the Fall SEC Academic Honor Roll in 2023, 2024 and 2025 and was a member of the SEC First-Year Academic Honor Roll in 2022-23.

 

On the court, Harper has appeared in all 25 matches with 21 starts this season. She ranks second on the team with 81 total blocks (0.94 per set) and third with 21 service aces while adding 129 kills on a .275 attack percentage. Harper was named SEC Defensive Player of the Week on Oct. 5 after recording a season-high nine blocks against Oklahoma.

The SEC sponsors Community Service Teams for all 22 league-sponsored sports. Each school selects one student-athlete per sport who has demonstrated a commitment to giving back to the community.

 

Georgia (15-10, 7-8 SEC) opens the 2026 Allstate SEC Volleyball Tournament as the No. 9 seed against No. 16 seed Ole Miss on Friday, Nov. 20, at Enmarket Arena in Savannah. First serve is set for 12 p.m. ET and will air live on SEC Network.

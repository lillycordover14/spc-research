CHICAGO — Interra Realty has brokered the $7.2 million sale of a 19-unit apartment building in Chicago’s Lakeview neighborhood. Originally constructed in 1926 as the parish house for St. Peter’s Episcopal Church, the property was fully renovated and converted into apartments in 2021. Joe Smazal and Mark Dykstra of Interra represented the buyer, a local private investor, and the seller, JAB Real Estate. The property was fully occupied at the time of sale.

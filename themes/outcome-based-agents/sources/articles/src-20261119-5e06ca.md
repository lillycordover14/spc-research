COLUMBIA, S.C. (WIS) - Beyond the Banks takes us behind the scenes at Riverbanks Zoo and Garden, where lorikeet keeper Christian shared a look at how they care for the colorful birds. We helped prepare fruit skewers with grapes, papaya, apples and bananas, plus strawberries and blackberries for extra enrichment.
Christian also introduced the zoo’s flock of lorikeets in the walk-through aviary. They explained that the birds enjoy a carefully planned diet of fruit, nectar and nutritional pellets. 
The variety lets them pick their favorites and shred foods like strawberries. Guests can also hand-feed the lorikeets nectar during a visit to the aviary.

Rothband Expands into the United States Through Partnership with Pactum Medical
 
 
 
  PR Newswire 
 
 
Thu, November 19, 2026 at 5:12 AM PST
 
2 min read
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
U.S. launch of mammo•shield™ brings the "Mind the Gap" mission to overlooked gaps in radiation protection
 
 
HASLINGDEN, England, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Rothband, the British manufacturer of radiation-protection garments, today announced its expansion into the United States through a partnership with Pactum Medical, initially introducing mammo•shield™ to U.S. healthcare professionals, supported by U.S.-based ordering, fulfillment and customer support.
 
 
 
 
 
At the center of the launch is mammo•shield™, an add-on shield developed with female surgeons to address exposure around the axilla and lateral breast. When a clinician raises an arm, a conventional apron's armhole can leave the underarm area less adequately covered. mammo•shield™ moves with the wearer to provide additional protection for this overlooked area, without replacing existing aprons.
 
 
"Our 'Mind the Gap' message is both simple and important: even when clinicians are wearing an apron, there may be areas that deserve closer attention," said Paul Dixon, Managing Director of Rothband. "mammo•shield™ was created to help address one of those gaps, and our partnership with Pactum Medical will allow us to bring that solution to healthcare teams across the United States."
 
 
"Rothband has translated a clearly identified clinical need into a practical product that can work alongside the protective equipment healthcare professionals already use," said Tom Lucas, President of Sales at Pactum Medical.
 
 
 
 
 
mammo•shield™ is now available in the United States following Rothband's FDA registrations. Rothband will showcase it at RSNA 2026, November 29 to December 3 at McCormick Place in Chicago, Booth 3841, Hall A. For demonstrations or U.S. distribution enquiries, visit rothband.com/rothband-usa or contact Tom Lucas at tlucas@pactummedical.com or 304-412-1463.
 
 
About Rothband: Rothband is a UK manufacturer of radiation-protection garments, shields and diagnostic-imaging accessories. The company traces its healthcare heritage to 1860 and manufactured its first X-ray protective cloth in 1911. Its products are manufactured in Lancashire, United Kingdom. rothband.com
 
 
About Pactum Medical: Pactum Medical is a U.S.-based medical-technology commercialization and operational partner headquartered in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, supporting manufacturers bringing new products to the U.S. healthcare market.

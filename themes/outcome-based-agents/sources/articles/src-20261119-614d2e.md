CHARLOTTE, N.C. 
– 
Zoe Best
 and 
Chari Thompson
 each recorded their second career double-doubles Wednesday evening to help Charlotte defeat Wofford, 57-51, at Halton Arena. The Niners defense came up big with a season-high 12 steals and holding Wofford to 30.6 percent shooting.


Best had a team-high 15 points and 11 rebounds for Charlotte (2-2), in addition to a season-best three steals. Thompson contributed 13 points and 10 rebounds with a pair of assists. The duo was followed by a nine-point performance from freshman 
Mariah Sain
 
in her first collegiate start.

 

HOW IT HAPPENED

Both teams traded a pair of baskets to open the game until Wofford (3-3) went on a run with 12 unanswered points for a 14-4 lead. Charlotte responded with a pair of baskets from Best, pulling within five to close out the first quarter. Charlotte pulled within a point early in the second quarter in part due to an offensive rebound and layup by Rouser. The Terriers pulled back ahead, 21-15, with back-to-back baskets before the Niners ended the half on an 8-0 run Charlotte's first lead of the game, 25-24.

 

The second half became a game of runs as Wofford opened the third quarter with a 9-0 run for a 38-29 lead. Charlotte had a response though with an 11-0 run of their own, featuring a pair of three-pointers by Best and Clark for a 40-38 Charlotte lead entering the fourth quarter. The Niners built up as large as a nine-point lead, 54-45, in the fourth quarter thanks to an 8-3 stretch to seal the victory.

 

OF NOTE

Charlotte had 12 steals as a team, its most this season.

Best recorded her third double-digit scoring game of the season and 37th of her career.

The 49ers move to 3-0 all-time against Wofford.


 

NEXT UP

Charlotte makes the short trip up to Davidson on Saturday, Nov. 21 as they look to retain possession of the 704 Cup. The game is slated for a 7 p.m. tip on ESPN+.

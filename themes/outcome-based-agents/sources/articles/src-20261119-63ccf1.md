A Chester taxi firm has raised more than £1,300 for two charities through its community fundraising scheme.
Cresta Cares, a scheme launched by
 
Cresta Cars
, sets aside 5p from every taxi journey booked through the company's app for a selected local charity or good cause.
Two organisations have benefited over the past two months, with North Wales Superkids receiving £602.30 in September and North Clwyd Animal Rescue being awarded £698.95 last month.
Founded in 1999 by the late social worker Margaret Williams MBE, 
North Wales Superkids
 supports disadvantaged children and young people across the region.
The volunteer-run charity is best known for its annual Toybox Appeal, which recently provided personalised sacks of Christmas presents to more than 2,000 referred children in a single year.
North Clwyd Animal Rescue is the second charity to benefit from the Cresta Cares scheme (Image: North Clwyd Animal Rescue)
Its work also includes pantomime trips, Santa flights and a year-round holiday project, which gives families facing crisis or hardship access to free respite breaks at a disability-adapted caravan at Hafan y Môr in Pwllheli.
The second beneficiary is independent animal welfare charity 
North Clwyd Animal Rescue
.

advertisement

Based in Trelogan, the organisation has been rescuing and rehoming abandoned and unwanted pets across North Wales since 1978. 
It relies solely on public donations and community fundraising to care for dogs, cats, small animals and horses.
The independent charity rehomed more than 850 animals in 2025 and also provides veterinary care and behavioural support to help prepare animals for life in their new homes.
A spokesperson for North Clwyd Animal Rescue said: "We would like to say a massive thank you to the wonderful team at Cresta Cars for their continued support of North Clwyd Animal Rescue.
The independent charity rehomed more than 850 animals in 2025 (Image: North Clwyd Animal Rescue)
"Support like this really does make a difference, helping us provide food, veterinary treatment, warmth, comfort and care to the dogs and cats who need us most."
Previous beneficiaries of the Cresta Cares scheme include KidsBank Chester, Rupert's Rainbow and Giddo's Gift.
Cresta Cars
 provides taxi services across Chester, Flintshire and the surrounding areas, including airport transfers, executive travel, minibuses and accessible vehicles.

advertisement

Through Cresta Cares, the company aims to ensure its day-to-day business can also benefit local charities and community organisations providing practical help and essential support to people across the region.

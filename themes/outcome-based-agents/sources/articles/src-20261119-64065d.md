Dylan Harper (Hip) Avoids Major Injury, Likely Out 2-3 Weeks
 

  By 
Luke Adams
 |
  at November 19, 2026 2:21 pm  

After undergoing additional testing on Thursday, 
Spurs
 guard 
Dylan Harper
 has been diagnosed with a left hip external rotator strain, per Jeff McDonald of The San Antonio Express-News (
Twitter link
).

It’s good news for Harper, who 
exited Tuesday’s game
 vs. Detroit late in the fourth quarter in obvious pain after slipping and falling to the floor. According to McDonald, the second-year guard could be facing a return timeline of two-to-three weeks, having avoided a more serious injury.

There were signs earlier in the day that Harper and the Spurs got good news on his injury, as 
Tom Orsborn of The San Antonio Express-News
 writes (subscription required). The former No. 2 overall pick was said to be walking without a limp at Thursday’s shootaround, and teammate 
Julian Champagnie
 hinted when he spoke to reporters that Harper wouldn’t be out for an extended period.

“Obviously, we need him, so finding out it wasn’t that serious, or as serious as it could have been, was good news,”
 Champagnie said.

Harper, 20, came off the bench as a rookie last season but has been part of San Antonio’s starting lineup through his first 15 games this fall. In those 15 outings, he averaged 18.4 points, 5.4 rebounds, and 4.5 assists in 31.7 minutes per game, with a shooting line of .505/.315/.865.

The Spurs’ new-look starting five that also includes 
Victor Wembanyama
, 
De’Aaron Fox
, 
Stephon Castle
, and 
Devin Vassell
 has been dominant so far this season, posting a defensive rating of 84.5 and a net rating of +35.0 in 125 minutes of action. That’s the best net rating of any of the 
53 five-man groups
 who have been on the floor for at least 40 minutes together since opening night.

While replacing Harper in the starting lineup with Champagnie shouldn’t have a major impact on that group, the Spurs’ depth will be tested with Harper and forward 
Tobias Harris
 both unavailable for the time being. Reserve point guard 
Jordan McLaughlin
 and second-year wing 
Carter Bryant
 are among the other candidates for increased roles while Harper recovers.

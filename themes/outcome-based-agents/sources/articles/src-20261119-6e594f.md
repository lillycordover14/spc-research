Poettker Construction’s annual clay shoot raises $30,000 for Dream on 3 charity

 //
 
November 19, 2026

 //

				Poettker Construction’s 3rd Annual Charity Clay Shoot raised $30,000 for national nonprofit Dream On 3. Photo courtesy Poettker Construction								

Poettker Construction
, a Charlotte-based general contractor, raised $30,000 with its 3rd Annual Poettker Charity Clay Shoot benefiting 
Dream On 3 Carolinas
, a charity dedicated to making sports dreams a reality for children and young adults with life-altering conditions.

“Since we first learned about the mission behind Dream On 3, Poettker has been inspired by the organization’s incredible impact inspiring kids dealing with challenging conditions by helping fulfill a sports dream,” said Keith Poettker, Chairman & CEO of Poettker Construction. “Seeing the enthusiasm of our employees, volunteers, sponsors and trade partners as they rally behind Dream On 3 makes the annual clay shoot especially meaningful. We’re grateful for everyone’s generosity and are proud to support organizations that are making a lasting difference in the lives of children and families across the region.”

Poettker sincerely thanks the host venue, Meadow Wood Farms in Waxhaw, N.C.; the 17 participating teams; and its event sponsors for making this year’s event a success. After just three years, the Annual Poettker Charity Clay Shoot has raised over $130,000 for Dream On 3.
 

“Poettker Construction has been an incredible partner in helping Dream On 3 make sports dreams come true for kids and young adults living with life-altering conditions,” said Rachel Bozarth, Community Engagement Coordinator, Carolinas for Dream On 3. “A Dream is about so much more than the experience itself. It’s about creating moments of joy, hope, and encouragement that remind our Dreamers that their possibilities extend far beyond their diagnosis. The impact of these experiences lasts long after the Dream, giving Dreamers and their families memories to hold onto and a renewed sense of what’s possible. We are so grateful to Poettker for believing in our mission and helping us create these life-changing experiences.”

About Poettker Construction: 
Established in 1980, Poettker Construction is an award-winning, family-owned business specializing in construction management, design/build, general contracting, and self-perform services with an emphasis on exceeding the client’s expectations. Poettker Construction is dedicated to providing safe, quality, sustainable, and technology solutions to clients in a diverse portfolio of markets. The company prides itself on building long-lasting relationships with their clients, business partners and the communities in which they work, with an impressive 88 percent of Poettker’s revenue stemming from repeat clients.
 
For more information, visit 
poettkerconstruction.com/southeast/.

About Dream On 3:
 Dream On 3, a national nonprofit organization, makes sports-themed dreams come true for children and young adults living with life-altering conditions, such as mental health challenges, intellectual disabilities or chronic illness. Founded in 2013, Dream On 3 programs have impacted over 44,000 individuals to date. For more information, visit 
dreamon3.org/carolinas-community/
.

Contagion Live news story
The European Medicines Agency (EMA) issued a positive scientific opinion for a 3-month dapivirine vaginal ring for HIV prevention in women and adolescent girls aged 16 years and older, according to a Population Council 
announcement
. The opinion includes women who are breastfeeding. The ring, developed by the Population Council and its affiliate IPM South Africa, requires replacement only 4 times per year instead of 12.
The opinion came through the EMA’s EU-Medicines for All procedure and provides a pathway toward regulatory applications in African countries. The monthly dapivirine ring received a positive EMA opinion in July 2020 and holds regulatory approval in 12 African countries.

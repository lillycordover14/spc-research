MacReady Artisan Bread Rises To Help&nbsp;&nbsp;Door County Pulse

[page text]
MacReady Artisan Bread in Egg Harbor is giving away bread to those in need. Submitted.

Through the month of November, MacReady Artisan Bread has been providing a free loaf of freshly baked bread to anyone in the community experiencing food insecurity. 

The Egg Harbor bakery regularly donates extra bread to Feed My People in Sturgeon Bay. 

“Our aim is to provide assistance without any conditions,” MacReady owners wrote in a social media post. “No judgement, just bread and a smile.”

MacReady Artisan Bakery is at 7838 State Hwy 42 in Egg Harbor.

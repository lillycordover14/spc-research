12th Tech Brings AI to Crescendo
 
 
 
  PR Newswire 
 
 
Thu, November 19, 2026 at 5:02 AM PST
 
2 min read
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
New AI capabilities will bring intelligent photo review and audit summaries to collateral verification
 
 
AUSTIN, Texas, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- 12th Tech today announced that it has been building the foundation for integrated artificial intelligence within its Crescendo platform and will continue introducing AI driven innovation throughout the system as a major focus of its product strategy going forward.
 
 
 
 
Powering Crescendo Hub
 
 
 
Initial capabilities will include AI assisted photo review and intelligent audit recaps and summaries, helping users review collateral information more efficiently and quickly identify the information that requires their attention.
 
 
12th Tech is developing these capabilities specifically around the workflows and challenges associated with collateral verification and wholesale finance.
 
 
 
 
 
"Artificial intelligence gives us an opportunity to make the information already captured in Crescendo more useful at the point of review," said Jeremy Walker, Director of Software Development at 12th Tech. "We are focused on applying AI in practical ways that strengthen existing workflows and help users get to the most relevant information faster."
 
 
12th Tech is pioneering practical AI for floor plan audit management, focused on solving real workflow bottlenecks, identifying risk and helping users act on important information faster. Rather than treating AI as a buzzword, the company is building integrated capabilities around the real-world challenges lenders face every day.
 
 
"For our clients, AI is about creating meaningful efficiency while improving how quickly they can understand what is happening across their portfolios," said Casey Holyk, Vice President of Business Development at 12th Tech. "These capabilities will help lenders focus their teams on what needs attention, make better use of the information already available to them and turn more of that data into actionable insight."
 
 
 
 
 
12th Tech will continue to share additional details as new integrated AI capabilities are introduced throughout Crescendo.
 
 
About 12th Tech
 
 
12th Tech exists to give wholesale lenders better visibility, stronger verification and smarter tools to manage collateral risk with greater confidence. The company believes floor plan technology should do more than document an audit. It should connect the information, workflows and verification tools lenders need to make better decisions across their portfolio.
 
 
Through Crescendo and solutions including Crescendo Hub, Crescendo Mobile, uVerify™, uReconcile™ and uFloorit™, 12th Tech provides a highly configurable technology ecosystem spanning asset onboarding and verification, physical and dealer self-auditing, reconciliation, workflow automation, risk management, reporting and portfolio intelligence. The goal is not simply to complete audits, but to help organizations manage collateral risk and portfolio operations more effectively.

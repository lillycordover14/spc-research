Putnam County Commission tabs Lawson as new prosecuting attorney&nbsp;&nbsp;WV MetroNews

[page text]
WINFIELD, W.Va. 
— There’s a new prosecutor in Putnam County.

Kevin Lawson

The Putnam County Commission has appointed Kevin Lawson to the position. He’s previously served as assistant prosecutor.

He replaces current prosecuting attorney Kris Raynes, who was elected to the open circuit judge seat in the November General Election.

Raynes won the seat vacated by former circuit judge Mark Sorsaia’s retirement. Rayne’s term begins in January.

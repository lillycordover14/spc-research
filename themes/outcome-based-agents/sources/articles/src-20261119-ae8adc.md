Spurs Lose Dylan Harper for a Few Weeks
Another star goes down just in time for the Blazers.
by
 
 
Dave Deckard
Nov 19, 2026, 8:46 PM UTC
The injury list for Portland Trail Blazers opponents keeps stacking up. The Blazers play the San Antonio Spurs next Wednesday. 
News came down today
 from Shams Charania of ESPN that Spurs guard Dylan Harper has been diagnosed with a left hip rotator strain, which has a 1-3 week return forecast. Though the injury isn’t season-ending, it will likely leave Harper out of the game versus Portland.
The second-year guard has started all 15 games this season for the 12-3 Spurs. He is averaging 18.4 points, 5.4 rebounds, and 4.5 assists in 31.7 minutes per game, shooting 50.5% from the floor and 31.5% from the three-point arc. Harper customarily starts as part of a four-guard lineup with De’Aaron Fox, Stephon Castle, and Devin Vassell alongside center Victor Wembanyama.

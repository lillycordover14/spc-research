[page text]
BINGHAMTON, N.Y. (WIVT/WBGH) - Broome County Arts Council's Artisan Gallery is offering its members 10% off this holiday season.
The gallery showcases handcrafted items made by local and regional artists.
As a thank you to its members the gallery is offering the discount from Nov. 21, until Dec. 24.
Regular shopping hours are Tuesday through Saturday, 11 a.m. until 3 p.m.
For more information visit the 
Broome County Arts Council

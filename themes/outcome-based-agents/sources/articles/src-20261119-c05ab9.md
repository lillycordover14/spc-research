(KMAland) -- Iowa State, Creighton and Graceland picked up wins over regional rivals in women's college basketball action Wednesday night.
Iowa State improved to 6-0 with a 72-57 victory at Northern Iowa, while former Dowling Catholic standout Ava Zediker scored 19 points to lead Creighton (4-1) to a 74-53 win over Omaha.
Graceland topped Peru State 72-63 in Heart of America Athletic Conference play, while Kansas City fell to UTEP and Kansas lost at No. 17 Minnesota.

The Townsend Artisan Guild is hosting a winter sale and reception at its gallery, 7719 East Lamar Alexander Parkway, Townsend. On Saturday, Nov. 21, from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., shoppers can browse through original work by more than 45 juried Gallery and Studio artists at a 10% discount. Offerings include fiber arts, stained glass, jewelry, mixed media, painting, photography, pottery and woodworking.
Between 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., artists will demonstrate fiber arts, jewelry, painting, and pottery techniques and share the inspiration behind their work.
This year, the Guild will honor longtime artist and friend, the late Lois Alexander, with a retrospective celebrating her art and the lasting mark she left on the community. Her work will be on display, and selected pieces will be offered for sale.
As an early member of the Townsend Artisan Guild, she was instrumental in writing the bylaws and other policies. She actively supported children’s events, including the Great Smoky Mountain Heritage Center Blue Ribbon Country Fair. When the gallery opened in 2015, she generously gave extra time and guidance, created many graphics and helped spread the word about the gallery. Alexander is remembered for these contributions, but also for the encouragement she offered fellow artists and the advice she shared on techniques and art sales.
Her work can still be seen at the GSM Heritage Center, in GSM National Park stores, on Women’s Basketball Hall of Fame induction programs, at Appalachian Bear Rescue, and elsewhere. She also created artwork of custom homes and custom wedding invitations for clients across the South.
Visitors to Townsend can view the recently completed ‘artistic’ bear mural.
Townsend Artisan Guild Gallery’s business operating hours are Monday, Wednesday-Saturday from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Sunday from 1:30 to 5 p.m.

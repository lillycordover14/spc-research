                Ella Langley breaks CMA Awards history; Red Clay Strays wins Vocal Group of the Year
            

Erica Thomas

				|
						11.19.26
		

Facebook

LinkedIn

Tweet

Truth

Email

Print

Alabama won big at the 60th Annual CMA Awards Wednesday evening at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena.
Hope Hull’s Ella Langley broke CMA Awards history by bringing home six awards, the most ever won in a single year. Langley took the top honor, Entertainer of the Year, as well as Single of the Year and Song of the Year for “Choosin’ Texas,” Album of the Year for “Dandelion,” Female Vocalist of the Year and Music Video of the Year for “Choosin’ Texas.”

Hope Hull's Ella Langley nominated for seven Grammy Awards
Miranda Lambert celebrated alongside Langley for her work on the Single of the Year, Album of the Year and Song of the Year.

Langley was nominated nine times across seven categories. She has consistently thanked her parents and God for her success.
Mobile’s Red Clay Strays won Vocal Group of the Year. This was the group’s second consecutive win.
Other winners included Brooks & Dunn for the 
CMA’s 2026 Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award and Stephen Wilson, Jr. took home New Artist of the Year.

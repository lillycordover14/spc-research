Sierra AI customer service startup seeks $1.5 billion fundraise at $30 billion valuation&nbsp;&nbsp;디지털투데이

[page text]
[Photo: Shutterstock]

Sierra, an AI customer service startup founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor (브렛 테일러), is discussing raising $1.5 billion at a valuation of about $30 billion, The Information reported on Nov. 18 local time.

That would be nearly double the more than $15 billion valuation in its May funding round. Lightspeed Venture Partners, Greenoaks and Benchmark are discussing an investment in Sierra. Talks are at an early stage and terms could change.

Sierra, in its third year since founding, said its annual recurring revenue topped $400 million last month. Revenue doubled in five months. The Information said the talks show investor interest remains strong in fast-growing AI application startups.

Rival Decagon is also in talks with investors about raising several hundred million dollars. Decagon's annual recurring revenue was about $100 million at the end of the second quarter, and the company expects it to reach $175 million to $200 million by year-end.

The Holland Center and Sonoran Arts League will welcome the holiday season with the sixth annual Winter Artisan Gift Market, bringing a talented group of local and regional artists together for two days of art, creativity and holiday shopping.
This juried market will feature original, handmade work including jewelry, painting, ceramics, woodworking, photography, textiles and more. Visitors can browse, meet the artists, and find gifts with a story behind them.
Part of the fun of the Winter Artisan Gift Market is not knowing exactly what you’ll find. From functional pieces for the home to wearable art and one-of-a-kind creations, the market offers plenty to discover for everyone on your holiday list and perhaps something for yourself.
Now in its sixth year, the Winter Artisan Gift Market has become a holiday tradition at The Holland Center, celebrating the creativity of our local arts community and giving artists an opportunity to share their work directly with the public.
 
The sixth annual Winter Artisan Gift Market will take place Dec. 4–5 from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. at The Holland Center, 34250 N. 60th St. in Scottsdale. Admission is free.

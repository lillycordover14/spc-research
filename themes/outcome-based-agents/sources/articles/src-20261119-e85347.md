SMECO Easements Clear Way for Three Notch Trail Phase VII -&nbsp;&nbsp;- SoMD Chronicle

[page text]
LEONARDTOWN — 
St. Mary’s County commissioners on Nov. 17 approved seven easements with the Southern Maryland Electric Cooperative that the county says are needed to build the next phase of the Three Notch Trail.

County staff told commissioners the easements for Phase VII had been approved by the county attorney’s office and carry no cost, but that they “play a key role” in moving the project forward. Commissioners approved them on a voice vote and authorized the commissioner president to sign the related documents. The action was included in the county’s 
Nov. 17 commissioner meeting rollup
.

Where the project stands

Approval of the easements is not the same as construction. According to the county’s 
Phase VII project summary
, which is undated, the project remains in pre-construction. Remaining steps include addressing design comments from state and local permitting agencies, finalizing the right of way and completing public input. The county lists a construction bid opening for 2026.

The trail is a 10-foot-wide asphalt multi-use path along the county’s railroad right of way. The county lists Phase VII’s cost at about $10.35 million:

$5.53 million from the county

$3.51 million from the Transportation Alternatives Program

$1.2 million from the Kim Lamphier Bikeways Network Program

$110,000 from Program Open Space

The county did not say Tuesday when construction would begin or how long Phase VII would take to build.

Winnipeg Jets goaltender Clay Stevenson takes a timeout during the second period of an NHL hockey game against the Pittsburgh Penguins in Pittsburgh, Monday, Oct. 5, 2026. (Gene J. Puskar/AP)

The 
Winnipeg Jets
 are juggling things around between the pipes.

The Jets placed goaltender 
Clay Stevenson
 on waivers on Friday, according to Sportsnet's 
Elliotte Friedman
.

Stevenson, the Jets' third-string netminder behind Stuart Skinner and Nico Daws, hasn't laced up for the team since a relief appearance on Nov. 1 in a 7-0 loss to the Carolina Hurricanes.

He's made five appearances (four starts) in his first year with the Jets, posting a 3.61 goals-against average with an .870 save percentage and a 1-3-0 record.

A native of Drayton Valley, Alta., Stevenson has appeared in 10 games (nine starts) during his NHL career, having previously suited up five times for the Washington Capitals.

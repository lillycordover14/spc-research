The school board for 
Dunlap School District #323
 has picked a longtime staff member and current assistant superintendent to lead the district as its next superintendent.
In a media statement, 
Dunlap
 School Board President Tom Feldman said Matthew Andrews’ deep roots in the community and exemplary service as a teacher and administrator for 20 years affirmed their decision he was the right candidate for the job.
“Dr. Andrews is a very well-respected leader in our district due to his clarity, compassion, and respect for all students and stakeholders he serves,” said Feldman.
Dunlap’s school board named Andrews superintendent at its Wednesday meeting. That followed a national search launched this summer in coordination with the Illinois Association of School Boards. As part of the search process, the district stated, leadership qualities, priorities and values that matter most to Dunlap were weighed through stakeholder feedback.
Andrews started his teaching career in 2008 as an English teacher at Dunlap High School, and later advanced to department chair. He entered administration in 2018 as an assistant director of curriculum and then became a director of curriculum. He started as an assistant superintendent in 2021.

Andrews has a doctorate in educational leadership from Western Illinois University. After earning his bachelor’s degree in secondary English from 
Bradley University
, he returned to its College of Education & Health Sciences to complete his master’s in educational administration leadership.
The new superintendent has also been lauded as an Outstanding Educator by the University of Chicago, an Administrator of the Year by the Association of Illinois School Library Educators and a “Those Who Excel” honoree by the Illinois State Board of Education.
Andrews will take over for retiring Superintendent Scott Dearman, effective July 1, 2027. Dearman became superintendent of Dunlap schools in 2018.

(
InvestigateTV
) — A new ConsumerAffairs analysis of more than 6,200 ZIP codes found the strongest buyer’s market among those studied in a Miami, Fla., neighborhood and the strongest seller’s market in a Rochester, N.Y., neighborhood.
Ella Gomes, a media relations specialist at ConsumerAffairs, said the results show how much the market can differ from place to place.
“Trends are shifting towards a buyer’s market nationally, but... the housing market is still so localized,” Gomes said. “You can have one ZIP code in a city that ranks top for buyers and one that ranks as top for sellers.”
Gomes and her team at 
ConsumerAffairs examined the housing market ZIP code by ZIP code
. They used data from April through June of this year.
In the Miami neighborhood, the supply of homes for sale was enough to last more than 15 months at the current sales pace. Nationwide, the supply is about five months.
In the Rochester neighborhood, nearly 94% of homes sold above asking price during the study period.
Gomes said the report points to more housing supply and cooling demand.
“The South and areas like Miami had a significant amount of housing added in recent years. So in some markets, there are now more properties available relative to the number of people looking to buy. And that can translate into more choice and more negotiating room for buyers,” Gomes said. “And Florida is the clearest example of that pattern in our ranking.”
Gomes said the biggest takeaway for buyers is to look beyond national numbers and even citywide averages.
“If you’re shopping in a buyer-friendly zip code, you may have more time to compare properties, negotiate on price, or ask for concessions,” Gomes said. “And in a stronger seller’s market, you may need to be prepared for homes to move quickly and for competition above asking price.”
A buyer-friendly market does not necessarily mean homes are affordable. Even with more room to negotiate, buyers should make sure the home and financing still fit their monthly budget.

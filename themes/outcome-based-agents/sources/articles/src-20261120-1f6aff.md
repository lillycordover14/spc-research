Alisha Palmowski
 got off to a flying start in her first weekend as Champion, posting the fastest time in the one-and-only Practice session around the 
Las Vegas Strip Circuit
.
Downpours ahead of Practice and limited Formula 1 running beforehand left the track slippery, with standing water throughout the 6.201km circuit. The Red Bull Racing driver seemed right at home in the rain, going quickest by over a second with a 2:24.509 ahead of Lisa Billard and Ella Lloyd.
Track running stopped three minutes into the session, with Emma Felbermayr bringing a Red Flag after aquaplaning through standing water at Turn 12 and hitting the barrier front-on. The Audi driver was able to drive back to the pits but couldn’t rejoin the session after receiving external help to get back on track.
The session resumed with 27 minutes remaining, as Rachel Robertson was the first driver to set a lap time of 2:34.992. Megan Bruce quickly took over the top spot, but was immediately beaten by Ella Stevens with a 2:33.608.
Just before the halfway mark, Palmowski went fastest on a 2:31.377, putting just over two tenths between herself and the McLaren Oxagon driver. Stevens immediately responded with a 2:30.669, becoming the first driver into the 2:30s. Alba Larsen then lowered the benchmark even further with a 2:28.947 as experienced drivers began to master the tricky track conditions.
Billard was second-quickest, 1.089s slower than Palmowski 
Running was halted shortly afterwards as Mathilda Paatz spun through the braking zone of Turn 11, with heavy contact into the barrier damaging her front left suspension after she caught standing water at the apex.
Natalia Granada was the only driver yet to set a lap time before the stoppage. Her wait was extended before the session resumed with nine minutes remaining. Before anyone could set another lap time, a Yellow Flag was shown after Payton Westcott stopped on track at Turn 13 with an electrical issue. Marshals pushed her car off track swiftly, and a green flag was shown once again.
With less than four minutes remaining, almost all drivers headed back out quickly. Larsen kept pushing, becoming the first driver into the 2:27s, with the Champion close behind. Palmowski didn’t let the Ferrari driver hold onto the top spot for long, breaking into the 2:26s on her next flying lap.
Esmee Kosterman became the next driver to get caught out by the conditions after spinning around but recovered. Wild Card driver Luna Fluxa Cross also narrowly avoided contact with the barrier.
At the chequered flag, Palmowski set her fastest time of the session with a 2:24.509. Billard had been slowly building momentum and, on her final flying lap, shot up to P2. 
Lloyd was fourth quickest ahead of Nina Gademan and Stevens. Robertson finished sixth, followed by Bruce and despite a great start to the session, Larsen had to settle with P8, followed by Granada, as Jade Jacquet rounded out the top 10.
WHAT'S NEXT?
Qualifying will get underway at 21:30 local time (UTC-8), with the fastest times setting the grid for Saturday’s Feature Race. The top eight qualifiers will be reversed to determine the grid for Friday's Reverse Grid Race.

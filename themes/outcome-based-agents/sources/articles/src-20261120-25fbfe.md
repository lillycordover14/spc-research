Nick Saban tabs three teams that have 'the right stuff' to win the national championship&nbsp;&nbsp;On3

[page text]
The 
College Football Playoff
 is quickly drawing closer and the chance for teams to make the 12-team field is rapidly diminishing. In other words, it’s starting to become more clear which teams have an actual shot to win a national championship.

Former 
Alabama
 coach and ESPN analyst 
Nick Saban
 sees things already separating a bit in terms of the real contenders and those who just have a shot to make a deep run. He listed three teams he believes have “the right stuff” to win the national championship on Friday on 
The Pat McAfee Show
.

“I think there’s three teams that have the right stuff,” he said. “And this is no disrespect to Alabama, because I think there’s probably seven to eight team that can win the championship, Alabama being one of them.

“But if you’re a betting man and you say, ‘OK, who can run the ball, who can play good defense and who has a good quarterback?’ You would say 
Georgia
 has that, 
Utah
 has that, 
Notre Dame
 has that.”

That’s three of the teams in the current Top 6 of the College Football Playoff rankings. Georgia is no stranger to winning the national championship; the program did so in 2021 and 2022. Notre Dame has also been close, reaching the national championship game following the 2024 season. Utah, on the other hand, is a bit of an outlier.

Could the Utes have the pieces to make a real run at the national title this year? Nick Saban certainly seems to think so, courtesy of quarterback 
Devon Dampier
, a strong running game and a good defense.

That’s what all three of his picks have in common. 
Gunner Stockton
 and 
CJ Carr
 are both among the nation’s best, with both 
earning Johnny Unitas Golden Arm Award finalist status
 on Friday afternoon.

The others that can potentially win a championship? They have more questions to figure out before they’re ready to hoist a trophy. That’s not to say it can’t be done. It’s just less of a sure bet for Saban.

“Everybody else maybe has a flaw somewhere along the way,” he said. “Now they might be able to overcome it, but I think those three teams have the right stuff.”

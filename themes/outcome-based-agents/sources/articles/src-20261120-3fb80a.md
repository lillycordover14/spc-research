PETALING JAYA (Nov 20): Crescendo Corp Bhd’s proposed RM200.88 million sale of industrial land in 
Kota Tinggi
, 
Johor
, has fallen through after the buyer exercised its contractual right to terminate the deal for convenience, four days after the agreement became unconditional.

In a Bursa Malaysia filing on Friday (Nov 20), Crescendo said buyer Pioneer Real Estate Development Sdn Bhd exercised that right through a termination letter dated Nov 20. The announcement did not state a specific commercial reason for Pioneer’s decision.

Under Clause 14.1.1 of the sale and purchase agreement, the earnest deposit and balance deposit will be forfeited to the seller, Crescendo’s wholly-owned subsidiary Crescendo Development Sdn Bhd, as agreed liquidated damages. The seller cannot make further claims against Pioneer for loss or damage arising from the termination.

The seller’s solicitors will release the balance deposit and interest earned on that sum to Crescendo Development.
ADVERTISEMENT

The termination announcement did not quantify the deposits to be forfeited. Under the original payment terms, the earnest deposit and balance deposit represented 1% and 4% of the purchase price respectively, totalling about RM10.04 million before interest.

Crescendo said that, apart from transaction costs already incurred and income from the forfeited deposits, including interest earned on the balance deposit, it expects the termination to have no material effect on the group’s earnings per share, net assets per share or gearing for the financial year ending Jan 31, 2027.

Crescendo said in a Nov 16 filing that all conditions precedent had been fulfilled, making the agreement unconditional that day.

Crescendo Development signed the agreement with Pioneer on Nov 14, 2025, to sell about 40.10 acres of vacant freehold industrial land in Bandar Cemerlang Industrial Park at RM115 psf, according to the original disposal announcement.

The parcel, held under title H.S.(D) 62047, PTD 50886, has an express title condition specifying medium industrial use for a data centre, with construction subject to plans approved by the relevant local authorities.

In its original disposal announcement, Crescendo estimated that completing the sale would generate a net pro forma gain of RM104.36 million.

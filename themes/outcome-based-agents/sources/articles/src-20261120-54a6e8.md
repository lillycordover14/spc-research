Ang Mo Kio Library has reopened on Level 4 of AMK Hub with more seating, gorgeous floor to ceiling shelves, a 360 immersive theatre and extended hours from 9 am. After moving to a former cineplex at AMK Hub, the library has made full use of the space and tall ceilings and transformed the space into an inviting spot for readers. 

Ang Mo Kio Library at AMK Hub

The revamped library has many cosy spaces to read and gather and designed purposefully to reflect the history, heritage and nature of Ang Mo Kio. Through nostalgic design elements, interactive experiences and welcoming reading spots, Ang Mo Kio Library will be a sanctuary for patrons of all ages. 

 

 

Highlights of AMK Library

The wow factor at the relocated Ang Mo Kio Library is definitely the double-volume space through the main library area. The floor to ceiling bookshelves highlight the verticality of the space with higher shelves displaying over 5,500 pre-loved books.

Immersive Hub

As patrons enter Ang Mo Kio Library, they will be greeted by clover shaped stone benches that pay homage to the unique HDB block 259 in a unique clover shape. A huge hill-like structure with shelves spiralling around forms the Immersive Hub. It is clearly the centrepiece of the space and its hilly structures are a nod to “green hills” of Ang Mo Kio prior to development and  reflected in the names of the area’s kampongs “Cheng Sua Lai” and “Jio Sua”.

Head to the Immersive Hub that showcases the past, present and future of Ang Mo Kio through archival footage and the assistance of AI. Exclusive to Ang Mo Kio library, patrons can watch The Heart of Ang Mo Kio: a short film tracing Ang Mo Kio’s evolution from its humble kampong origin into the thriving town it is today. 

The two interactive kiosks can be used to view a personalised video trailer of patrons using the kiosks produced using generative AI. 

 

 

Traffic Garden-inspired Children Zone

Head to the Children Zone where roads weaving between shelves await young readers. Inspired by the mini Traffic Garden at Ang Mo Kio Town Centre in the 1980s, the zone has vehicle-shaped shelving, road markings on the ground, whimsical traffic signages for little ones to navigate through the shelves. As a parent, I also loved the brightly-coloured reading nooks where parent and child can read together. 

 

 

Nature-Inspired Study & Multimedia Areas

The adult section has lotus-pad inspired curved ceiling features inspired by the former lotus pond at Ang Mo Kio Town Garden West. Patrons can also find plentiful seating and also study areas. 

Study groups can also book seven cosy study nooks perfect for collaborative study. 

Individual study stations can also be found next to the Adult Section. 

 

 

AR-Activated Singapore Collection Mural

Look out for a 18-metre long mural-style artwork that captures several familiar sights

from Ang Mo Kio’s past and present. Look closely and you would notice the spines of books forming the artwork. In addition to iconic landmarks like the former Broadway Cinema and the pair of Merlion statues located in Ang Mo Kio Avenue 1, the installation also references several lesser-known facts of the area.

For instance – nine bridges are depicted in the artwork, a reference to how, locals used to call Ang Mo Kio “Kow Tiow Kio” (“Nine Bridges” in Hokkien) in the early days, as one would have to cross nine bridges to reach Upper Thomson Road from present day Lorong Chuan.

Beyond appreciating the artwork, patrons can also engage with it using their NLB Mobile app.

By scanning a series of AR-enabled codes embedded in the artwork’s landmarks, patrons can also access recommended reading lists from the library’s Singapore Collection – spanning works across all four official languages and a wide range of genres and themes – and easily borrow the featured titles from NLB’s e-Catalogue.

 

 

Artefact Display Wall

Check out library artefacts from NLB’s collections displayed at the Artefact Display Wall next to the study nooks at the Collaboration Bridge. 

One might figure out each other’s ages especially if they looked familiar. Tools include library stamps, typewritten catalogue cards, due date slips, time and date library stamps and more. 

 

 

Extended Access

Patrons can enter the library from 9 am with self-help services available, as well as access to the Study and Multi media stations. The Study Zone will also have additional extended hours from 9 pm to 10 pm. 

Physical books that are shelved in the Study Zone will also be available for borrowing during the extended hours using the NLB Mobile App, marking the first time that patrons can borrow physical books from our libraries on public holidays.

Reopening Promotions and Activities

From 20 November 2026 to 31 January 2027, Library-goers who borrow four books or new NLB members can redeem a gift. Redemption timing is from 11 am to 9 pm daily excluding eve of Public Holidays and Public Holidays.

From 20 November 2026 to 15 March 2027, 1pm to 3pm daily, patrons can view Yip Yew Chong’s “Chinatown Wet Market, 2021”, an adaptation of the Chinatown Market scene of his 2016 “Dreams of Chinatown 牛车水昔日情怀” mural project at 30 Temple Street, at Ang Mo Kio Library.

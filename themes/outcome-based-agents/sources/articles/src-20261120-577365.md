North Clay School Board Announces 5 Year Extension for Superintendent Price
 
 

Published on November 20, 2026 10:54 am 

Last Updated on November 20, 2026 10:54 am 

 

Print
Multiple items and topics were discussed on Wednesday during the monthly North Clay School Board meeting.

The Board recognized Junior High Cross Country Runners Madi Sparks and Jazey Keller for qualifying for the State Cross Country Meet at Bloomington-Normal.

North Clay Ag Teacher and FFA sponsor Katrina Vandyke then presented to the board the plans for FFA and FFA Alumni to construct an approximately 30×50 foot pavilion for the purposes of providing a place for pork burger cookouts and other events, using funds from the Cody Blair and Cadie Weidner memorials, which the Board unanimously approved.

The Board then approved the 2026 tentative Tax Levy at 4.99%.

The Board lastly approved the hire of Dee Ann Hronec as a bus aide for the 2026-2027 school year, Marisa Perry as a paraprofessional for the 2026-2027 school year, and approved a 5 year contract extension for Superintendent Keith Price.

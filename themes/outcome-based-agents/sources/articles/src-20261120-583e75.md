What Wall Street Has Been Saying
 
 
🐂 Bullish Takeaways
 
 
 
Several firms, including Piper Sandler, JPMorgan, TD Cowen, Guggenheim and BTIG, have raised their price targets on Adaptive Biotechnologies into the upper US$20s to mid US$30s range. This supports the current blended fair value estimate near US$32.57.
 
 
 
Goldman Sachs highlights Adaptive Biotechnologies as a leader in next generation sequencing minimal residual disease testing and points to what it sees as significant remaining market penetration potential. This supports the firm's US$35 target.
 
 
 
TD Cowen and BTIG point to guideline support and large addressable markets for Adaptive Biotechnologies, as well as what they describe as volume and margin opportunities, as key parts of their positive stance heading into and following Q3 work.
 
 
 
 
🐻 Bearish Takeaways
 
 
 
Morgan Stanley maintains an Equal Weight rating with a US$27 target. This sits below the higher targets from other firms and signals a more cautious stance on valuation and execution risk.
 
 
 
Craig Hallum flags possible investor confusion around proposed 2027 CMS lab fee cuts for certain codes used by Adaptive Biotechnologies. This could create sentiment swings even though the firm currently views the direct financial impact as limited.

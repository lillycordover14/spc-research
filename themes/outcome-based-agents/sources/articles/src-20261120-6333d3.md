ADA COUNTY, Idaho (CBS2) — 
The Ada County Highway District (ACHD) completed seasonal training and equipment preparations ahead of winter weather.
Maintenance and operations crews have trained since mid-October, practicing equipment operation, safety procedures and communication. Operators also reviewed their assigned routes.
Earlier this week, crews participated in ACHD's annual "no-snow call," a simulation that lets teams prepare equipment and drive their routes before the first snowfall.
When snow or ice affects roads, ACHD crews will first focus on major thoroughfares, including arterial and collector roads, major intersections, overpasses and bridges. Priority routes also include emergency routes, railroad and school crossings, and streets with grades steeper than 6%.
ACHD will then assess conditions to determine whether crews can work on additional roadways. If conditions allow, they will then focus on school bus routes and residential streets.
ACHD plans to deploy 85 units this winter, including 41 equipped with snowplows. A total of 176 team members will support winter operations, with contractors available for major winter events.

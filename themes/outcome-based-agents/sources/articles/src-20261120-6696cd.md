Northland Adaptive Recreation reaches fundraising goal thanks to $100,000 donation

By WDIO

Northland Adaptive Recreation got some big help for their annual fundraiser, thanks to a $100,000 donation courtesy of the Kolar Auto World and Kolar family.
Every year, Northland Adaptive puts on their Northland Clay’s Classic as their only fundraiser. The money raised goes toward staffing, scholarships, and equipment, which can cost as much as $20,000 per item. They rely on community donations for all that funding.
“We don’t receive any government funding. We don’t receive insurance payments or anything like that. So, we rely on the community and this fundraiser to provide those scholarships, and allow us to purchase adaptive equipment,” said Northland Adaptive Recreation Manager Mark Hanna. “Here in the Northland, we value outdoor recreation, and recreation for people of all abilities. This fundraiser and support from Kolar and others helps make it happen.”
Next year’s Northland Clay’s Classic fundraiser is set for September.

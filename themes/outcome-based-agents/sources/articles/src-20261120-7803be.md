OGDEN — Ogden’s Holiday Electric Light Parade is for everyone, and Ogden City is taking a major step to ensure that it is viable for everyone.

On Thursday, the city announced in a press release that, for the first time ever, it will employ the use of a reservation-based ADA viewing platform on the east side of Washington Boulevard at 26th Street.

“The effort grew out of a community coalition led by Visit Ogden and includes Ogden Valley Adaptive Sports, the Trails Foundation of Northern Utah, the Utah Schools for the Deaf and the Blind, Ogden City, local business owners and community members,” the release said. “Together, the group identified opportunities to make community events more accessible and welcoming for residents and visitors.”

Taylor Hartman, director of Marketing and Communications at Visit Ogden, said he’s hoping this is only the first of many major events in the area to integrate such a viewing platform.

“The point of a community event is bringing people together,” he said in the release. “This isn’t about creating a separate experience. It’s about removing barriers so more people can enjoy the parade alongside their neighbors. We hope it becomes a model for future Ogden events.”

Courtesy Regina Esparza, Ogden City
Spectators watch the Holiday Electric Light Parade in Ogden on Saturday, Nov. 29, 2025.
The release adds accessibility comes in many shapes and forms.

“Beyond the designated viewing area, Visit Ogden and its community partners are developing additional ways for individuals with different sensory and accessibility needs to experience community events,” the release said.  “These efforts recognize that accessibility is not one-size-fits-all, with the goal of offering multiple ways for residents and visitors to experience Ogden’s traditions in ways that work best for them.”

“Ogden City is coordinating the setup of the accessible viewing area along the existing Holiday Electric Light Parade route. Visit Ogden and coalition partners are working with nearby businesses for parking arrangements and recruiting volunteers to oversee the section before and throughout the parade.”

The accessible platform proved popular enough that, according to the Visit Ogden site on Friday, the day after the announcement, reservations were totally filled.

This year’s Holiday Electric Light Parade will begin at 5:30 p.m. on Nov. 28, beginning at 22nd Street and proceeding down Washington Boulevard to 27th Street.

What Did Pope Leo XIV Hope to Convey in Latin America: The Key Messages of His Historic Trip

Pope
 | 
20/11/2026
Descargar documento
Compartir

Carolina Bellocq

Pope Leo XIV's trip to Latin America has left all kinds of messages, and this video summarizes the ones that resonated the most.
In Uruguay, he surprised everyone when he mentioned the traditional mate, which drew laughter. 
POPE LEO XIV
Mate has also arrived in Rome. Mate is a sign of familiarity, friendship and fellowship. It teaches us to share and spend time together, to strengthen the bonds that unite us as brothers and sisters. It is a sign of welcome, inclusion and shared values. 
In that country, where Catholics are a minority, he encouraged them to be brave:
POPE LEO XIV
When being just and honest comes at a price, pay it. When forgiving seems like a defeat, forgive. When everyone tells you to think of yourselves first, do not be afraid to put yourselves in the last place. 
In Argentina, he was met by a largely polarized society. And in his first speech, he spoke about exercising authority.
POPE LEO XIV
Those who exercise authority also have a duty to act prudently, to recognize the limits of the power entrusted to them, and to direct their decisions toward the good of the entire community.
Pope Francis was quoted several times. One such occasion was during the meeting with religious leaders in Córdoba, where Pope Leo XIV echoed one of his predecessors most famous sayings.
POPE LEO XIV
Dream of a Church that keeps making a mess, as your fellow Argentine Francis asked you to do!
At large gatherings, the pope addressed families in particular, calling on them to be the renewal of the country. 
POPE LEO XIV
Dear families, I encourage you to take a leading role in evangelization! The Church in Argentina needs you.
Once in Peru, his adopted homeland, he spoke clearly about the needs of the faithful.
POPE LEO XIV
Peru needs this so much. It needs a Church that plants the seeds of love, builds bridges where others put up walls, listens, heals and forgives. At the same time, one that is willing to acknowledge its own failings and ask for forgiveness with humility.
He had several words for those who are suffering, such as the ones he spoke with at the youth vigil.
POPE LEO XIV
Dear young people, when you are suffering, turn to Jesus. Don’t be afraid to speak to Him honestly, even when you struggle to find the words to pray. He knows our tears and the questions we still don’t know how to answer.
And as his trip was coming to an end, he expressed his gratitude for having felt so comfortable.
POPE LEO XIV
Thank you all very much. I’ve felt right at home here, and you’ve reminded me that the Church truly is a family. Here I was taught how to be a bishop, and now I’ve learned a little more about serving as the successor of Peter.
Nearly 40 prepared or spontaneous remarks, and dozens of quotes that sum up Pope Leo XIV’s first trip to Latin America.

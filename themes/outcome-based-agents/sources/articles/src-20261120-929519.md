KUALA LUMPUR (Nov 20): Crescendo Corporation Bhd (KL:
CRESNDO
) said on Friday the buyer of its vacant industrial land in Kota Tinggi, Johor, has called off the RM200.88 million transaction.

Pioneer Real Estate Development Sdn Bhd, a wholly owned unit of UK-incorporated Magma Holding Company, had exercised its right under the sale and purchase agreement to unilaterally terminate the deal, said Crescendo in a bourse filing.

The Johor-based property developer said it will retain a combined RM10.11 million in deposits paid by Pioneer Real Estate as agreed liquidated damages.

The agreement was signed in January through Crescendo’s unit, Crescendo Development Sdn Bhd. The freehold land measures 40.10 acres.

Besides this proposed sale, Crescendo had agreed in August 2025 to sell its 52.54 acres of land in Bandar Cemerlang Industrial Park, Kota Tinggi to MSFusion Sdn Bhd, which is linked to Singapore-based cloud computing firm Megaspeed International Pte Ltd, for RM263.21 million to build a data centre.

That sale hit a snag in August this year when Crescendo issued MSFusion a 14-day default notice to settle RM225.91 million in overdue payments, according to a report in the Nov 16-22 issue of 
The Edge Malaysia
 weekly.

MSFusion subsequently cleared the payment to Crescendo with late payment interest on Sept 9.

Behind the scenes, MSFusion has also been caught up in a legal dispute. According to a OneCredit report, Megaspeed’s unit Speedmatrix has filed lawsuits against MSFusion and its shareholders, WFCT Cayman Holdings Ltd and WFCT Global Holdings Ltd, though the court documents were not available for review.

Crescendo shares closed one sen or 0.88% higher at RM1.15, valuing the company at RM978 million. The counter has fallen over 11% in the past one year.

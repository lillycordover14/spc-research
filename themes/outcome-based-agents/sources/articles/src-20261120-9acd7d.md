While Burrow was reserved after throwing for 409 yards and four touchdowns in the win against the Steelers, there was a level of chest pounding after the Atlanta win in which he threw for three touchdowns and ran for another while throwing for 271 yards against Atlanta.
"I wanted to come out and put on a show for everybody, and show what the excitement of the NFL can be," Burrow said. "And the NFL is really all about the stars and players making big time plays. And so you saw Ja'Marr Chase, you saw me, you saw Tee Higgins, you saw a lot of great players out there doing great things, and hopefully people took notice."
The show has multiple acts.
His stats through nine games are among the best by MVP-winning quarterbacks in recent history.
Since running back Adrian Peterson won the MVP in 2012, the last 13 awards have all gone to quarterbacks who, in most cases, had smaller numbers through nine games than what Burrow has done in leading the Bengals to a 7-2 record.

SANTA CLARA, Calif. -- 
Blake Harper scored 28 points, including five in the overtime, and VCU knocked off Santa Clara 83-77 on Thursday.
Harper also contributed seven rebounds for the Rams (3-1). Lazar Djokovic scored 22 points while shooting 7 for 9 (2 for 4 from 3-point range) and 6 of 12 from the free-throw line, and added six rebounds. Nyk Lewis shot 3 of 13 from the field, including 1 for 6 from 3-point range, and went 4 for 4 from the line to finish with 11 points.
Aleksandar Gavalyugov led the Broncos (5-1) in scoring, finishing with 22 points. Chris Tadjo added 15 points and nine rebounds for Santa Clara. Elijah Mahi finished with 12 points and six rebounds. The loss broke the Broncos’ five-game winning streak.
Djokovic scored 13 points in the first half for VCU, who trailed 46-34 at the break. Harper led VCU with 17 second-half points as the teams ended regulation tied 71-71. Harper scored their five overtime points from the free-throw line.

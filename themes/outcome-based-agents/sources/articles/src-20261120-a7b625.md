Jamaica U17 head coach Wendell Downswell believes that his team’s speed and tactical discipline could prove decisive as the young Reggae Boyz begin their 2026 FIFA U17 World Cup campaign against European champions Italy today in Qatar.
 
The Boyz will face a difficult opening assignment at the Aspire Zone Pitch, with kick-off set for 10 a.m. Jamaica time.
 
Downswell, charged with leading the team throughout the age-group tournament, is confident that his players have the tools to challenge their more experienced opponents.
 
In preparation for the competition, the U17 Boyz played several friendly matches against senior teams, with their final games against Jamaica Premier League (JPL) outfit Arnett Gardens.
 
Speaking after their final warm-up match, Downswell said it was a useful exercise as they would look to use their athleticism to unsettle the European giants.
 
“We feel that more than less that against the Italians, we will have to utilise our speed as effectively as possible, by virtue of doing tactically well,” Downswell said.
 
Heading into the World Cup, Downswell said he has seen the tactical discipline he expected from his players at this stage of their preparations.
 
He also explained that their warm-up games also allowed the technical staff to manage each player’s minutes as they hope to enter the competition as healthy as possible.
 
“We have seen a number of occasions that we made the move that we expected to make from a tactical standpoint,” he explained.
 
“Overall, it was quite competitive. We were able to manage the players, give them all an almost equal opportunity of playing. We had some minor injuries, so we had to be monitoring them.”
 
 
THIRD APPEARANCE
 
 
Jamaica will be making its third appearance at the Under-17 World Cup, having previously competed in 1999 and 2011. 
 
However, they are yet to advance beyond the group stage and will be hoping to make history in Qatar.
 
The Boyz enter the competition with encouraging credentials, having topped their Concacaf qualifying group with a perfect three-win record, scoring 18 goals across their three matches.
 
Captain Jahmarie Nolan is expected to be one of Jamaica’s key contributors as he was named the best player of the qualifiers after contributing four goals and two assists.
 
Nolan also made his senior international debut with the Reggae Boyz squad, alongside teammate Jamone Lyle, who is also expected to stand out for the Boyz.
 
Italy, meanwhile, arrive as reigning European U17 champions and will provide a stern test of Jamaica’s ability to compete against elite opposition.
 
The Italians will look to their prolific marksman Diego Perillo, who has bagged 18 goals in 25 appearances, and the decisive penalty to lift the European U17 title earlier this year.
 
Following tomorrow’s encounter, Jamaica will face Uzbekistan on November 23 before closing their Group E campaign against Côte d’Ivoire on November 26.

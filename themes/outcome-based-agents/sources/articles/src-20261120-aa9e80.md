Koch And HappyRobot Test AI Agents For Human Resources

Koch is working with HappyRobot to test AI agents that could help employees and job candidates get faster answers while reducing repetitive work for HR professionals.

The collaboration began with a two-day workshop in Wichita, Kansas, that brought together HR professionals, business leaders, and HappyRobot engineers.

Rather than beginning with predetermined technology, the teams identified situations where employees or candidates regularly wait for answers and where HR staff repeatedly perform similar tasks.

The workshop produced two prototypes that were ready for testing.

Recruiting And Employee Support

One AI agent conducts preliminary candidate-screening calls, confirms qualifications, and records information for recruiters. The system operates continuously, allowing initial screening outside traditional working hours.

The companies are also exploring an HR service-center agent that helps employees find information about payroll, taxes, benefits, and company policies.

HappyRobot’s agents can communicate through phone calls, email, documents, and web interfaces while integrating with existing business systems.

Koch and HappyRobot may eventually test agents in customer support, logistics, sales, and other service functions.

Numotion Names Presidents of CRT, Adaptive Solutions Businesses&nbsp;&nbsp;HME Business

[page text]
Numotion has restructured its leadership roster following its recent 
acquisition
 by Hanger Inc.

In a Nov. 19 announcement, Numotion named John Pryles the president of its Complex Rehab Technology (CRT) business, and Bret Barczak the president of its Adaptive Solutions business.

Previously, Pryles served as executive vice president of Numotion, while Barczak was Numotion’s chief marketing officer. Both are CRT industry veterans.

Brentwood, Tennessee-based Numotion — with hundreds of CRT locations in the United States, plus dozens of locations in Canada under the Motion name — was acquired in September by Hanger, an Austin, Texas-based provider of orthotics and prosthetics devices and products.

In that announcement, Hanger said longtime CEO Mike Swinford would become CEO of the combined company.

Pryles, who has worked as an Assistive Technology Professional (ATP), will now lead Numotion’s CRT business, “bringing greater alignment across the teams that help individuals with disabilities access the equipment and services essential to their health, independence and participation in everyday life,” the announcement said. “In his new role, Pryles will oversee sales, operations, service and repair and support. His focus will be on simplifying how these functions work together, strengthening coordination and delivering a more consistent customer experience, from obtaining equipment to ongoing service and support.”

“Our customers depend on their equipment every day, and every part of our CRT organization plays a role in helping them stay mobile and independent,” said Swinford. “John shares a deep commitment to our customers and the people who serve them. With his leadership across CRT, we have an opportunity to make it easier for our teams to work together, solve problems and take care of our customers.”

Barczak takes over as president of Numotion’s Adaptive Solutions Business, which comprises online retailer SpinLife, CorLife (workers’ compensation medical equipment and services), Medical Supplies, and Numotion Speech Solutions.

“This alignment brings focused leadership to complementary businesses serving customers across retail mobility and lifestyle products, medical supplies, workers’ compensation services and communication technology, the announcement added.

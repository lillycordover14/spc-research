Sixfold Teams Up With exxis on AI Underwriting -&nbsp;&nbsp;Insurance Edge

[page text]
Some news from Sixfold and exxis for you;

Sixfold, the underwriting AI used by leading insurers globally, has partnered with exxis, the underwriting agency of the Ecclesia Group. exxis will be the first German MGA to use the Sixfold platform.

As part of the Ecclesia Group, one of Germany’s largest insurance brokers, exxis writes cyber, property, accident, and D&O business on behalf of multiple carriers across the EU.

Integrating AI underwriting into its process meant meeting the wider requirements, including EU AI Act readiness, DORA, single-tenant hosting with data kept in Europe, SOC 2 Type 2, and support for German, alongside English.

Sixfold met these requirements while keeping each carrier’s guidelines and appetite separate for each line of business, so the guidance an underwriter sees always reflects that of the carrier they are writing the risk for.

“Bringing AI into underwriting for the first time is a decision you make once, and you have to be able to defend it,” said Tim Brozio, Managing Director at exxis. “We write for several insurers within the regulatory framework, so the technology has to meet the requirements and reflect how each line and each carrier actually underwrites. Sixfold delivered on both, and our underwriters started using it on day one.”

Alex Schemelkin, Founder & CEO of Sixfold, said: “When a carrier gives an MGA authority to bind its business, it’s trusting the MGA to underwrite the way the carrier would. exxis gets capacity from several insurers, and Sixfold’s underwriting AI evaluates every submission in the context of each carrier’s appetite. That consistency is what earns exxis the confidence of its carriers and customers.”

Underwriters open a case that is already assembled, including the carrier’s appetite, the relevant sources, any gaps flagged, and a narrative drafted, so they can focus on assessing the risk rather than building the file. Each line presented a different challenge – cyber meant working through a high number of questionnaires, property required calculating loss ratios by hand, and accident involved documentation disproportionate to the premium.

“Some of our underwriters were sceptical at the start. What changed their minds was checking the output against what they already knew and finding it held up,” said Sven Grimm, Head of Underwriting at exxis.

“Using Sixfold, I can see the whole picture at once. I get to the actual risk assessment much sooner than I used to,” said Philipp Bresch, Senior Property Underwriter at exxis.

Within two months, cyber, property and accident were live on Sixfold across multiple carriers, and exxis underwriters now use it every day. They are already working with Sixfold to explore what else AI can take on in their underwriting process.

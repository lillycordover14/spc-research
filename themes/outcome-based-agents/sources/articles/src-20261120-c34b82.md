DUBLIN, Ga (WGXA) — 
Multiple fire crews responded early Thursday morning after a fire broke out at the Universal Scrap 
&
 Recyclers facility just outside of East Dublin.
According to EMA Director Bill Laird, the fire was called in at around 6:40 a.m. and was contained and isolated to prevent it from spreading by 10 a.m.

The facility, also called US1 Recycling Inc is located at 1259 Georgia-199 South.
The cause of the fire is undetermined, and workers confirmed everything was fine at the facility when they left work Wednesday. 
Laird emphasized that there is no hazard to the surrounding area, and no injuries occurred. 
Laird said a pile of recycled vehicle metal caught fire while waiting to go into the grinder. Before the metal is crushed, the company removes all hazardous materials, including tires and foam, which had already been cleared from the pile.
EMA: Multiple departments respond to scrap metal pile fire at Universal Scrap & Recyclers, November 19, 2026 (Courtesy of EMA)
Laird said there has been speculation that someone was illegally burning tires, but he confirmed no tires were burned illegally.
Several fire crews responded to the scene, including Laurens County Fire, East Dublin Fire, Dublin Fire, and then mutual aid fire crews from Bleckley, Wrightsville, Johnson and the Georgia Department of Corrections (GDC) had around seven teams helping, according to Laird.
Laird said the fire is about 80% extinguished.

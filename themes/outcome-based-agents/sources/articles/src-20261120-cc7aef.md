Daredevil zip lines nearly 570 feet while on fire&nbsp;&nbsp;upi

[page text]
Nov. 20 (UPI) --
 A professional stuntman from Poland traversed a distance of 568 feet and 6.8 inches on a zip line while his entire body was on fire.

Stanisław Madej, 43, whose credits as a stunt performer include the TV series The Witcher and the film Paradise, attempted the Guinness World Record for the 
farthest distance full body burn on a zip wire
 on Oct. 3 in Łódź, Poland.

"Personally, I believe that any contact with fire is dangerous. It is an elemental force that is often unpredictable; our experience allows us to react in advance to its erratic behavior," Madej told Guinness World Records.

"A situation that appears to be under control can very quickly escalate into a serious threat. That is why a high degree of awareness, concentration, and, above all, respect for fire are essential," he said.

Madej attempted the stunt to celebrate the 10th anniversary of the Tyrolka w Manufakturze zip line at the Manufaktura complex.

"To mark our 10th anniversary and align with my profession, I wanted to cement our presence at this location with an extraordinary feat. Looking back at it now with a cooler head, I certainly come up with some explosive ideas," he said.

The daredevil zip lined a distance of 568 feet and 6.8 inches before his support team extinguished the flames.

"I wore specialized protective clothing and was coated in a special water-based gel, which insulated my body from excessive heat. It was important to me to attempt the record with my face exposed -- without a mask, covered only in the gel -- as I believe that direct exposure to fire during such a feat elevates its significance," he said.

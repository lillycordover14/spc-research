                    Sena Yossi: First European Patient Receives CT-Guided Adaptive Radiotherapy With uRT-linac 506c

Sena Yossi,
 ORLAM Radiation Oncologist at Jean Mermoz Private Hospital, shared on 
LinkedIn:

“A European first, now a clinical reality.

ORLAM Group
 is proud to announce that the first patient in Europe has been treated on the uRT-linac 506c (United Imaging Healthcare), at our Clinique Charcot site in Sainte-Foy-lès-Lyon, Lyon area.

A 
CT-linac
 is not installed like just another accelerator. What it brings to our patients:

 diagnostic-quality CT imaging before every fraction

 
AI-driven
 auto-contouring and auto-planning

 online adaptive radiotherapy, based on the anatomy of the day

Following the 
MRIdian A3i MR-linac
 at our 
Villeurbanne site
, the 
uRT-linac 506c
 completes our care offering across our 4 centers, alongside 6 Varian Halcyon linacs, 1 Varian TrueBeam, 2 Papillon contact radiotherapy systems, 2 Varian HDR brachytherapy afterloaders and 6 photobiomodulation devices.

ORLAM is now one of the few radiotherapy groups with a 
mixed MR-guided
 and 
CT-guided
 adaptive platform, giving as many patients as possible equitable access to 
adaptive radiotherapy.

This is above all a collective achievement.

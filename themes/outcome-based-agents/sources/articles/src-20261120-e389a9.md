Serval Resources plc (SRVL), the UK-listed resources company, has received a major shareholding notification confirming that Alvar Financial Services Ltd has reduced its voting interest in the company. The TR-1 filing, submitted on 20 November 2026, discloses that Alvar Financial Services crossed a reporting threshold on 19 November 2026 following a disposal of voting rights. The London-registered firm now holds 1,700,000 shares, representing 2.739799% of voting rights, down from a previously notified position of 3.062128%. Investors monitoring significant shareholder movements at Serval Resources may note this reduction as a material change in the company's major holdings register.

Key Points

Serval Resources plc (SRVL), ISIN GB00BVRY1W08, is the issuing company subject to this notification.

Alvar Financial Services Ltd has reduced its voting stake in Serval Resources from 3.062128% to 2.739799%.

The threshold was crossed on 19 November 2026, with the notification submitted to the issuer on 20 November 2026; the holding equates to 1,700,000 direct voting rights.

Investors should watch whether further disposals by Alvar Financial Services or other major holders occur in subsequent filings.

Alvar Financial Services Drops Below Previous Notified Position in Serval Resources

According to the TR-1 standard form notification, Alvar Financial Services Ltd, a London-registered entity, has disposed of a portion of its shareholding in Serval Resources plc. The resulting position stands at 1,700,000 directly held voting rights, equating to 2.739799% of total voting rights in the issuer. This compares with a previously notified position of 3.062128%, indicating a reduction of approximately 0.322 percentage points. The disclosure confirms that all holdings are via direct voting rights attached to shares, with no financial instruments such as options or contracts for difference forming any part of the notified position.

No Third-Party Control Declared Over Alvar Financial Services' Serval Resources Position

The notification states that Alvar Financial Services Ltd is not controlled by any natural person or legal entity and does not itself control any other undertaking holding an interest in Serval Resources plc. This declaration, provided under section 9 of the TR-1 form, confirms the firm acts independently with respect to this shareholding. The filing was completed on 20 November 2026 and identifies Alvar Financial Services Limited as the place of completion. No proxy voting arrangements are disclosed, and no additional information was provided beyond the standard form disclosures required under the Financial Conduct Authority's Disclosure and Transparency Rules.

DTR5 Reporting Obligations Triggered by Serval Resources Voting Rights Movement

The notification was triggered under the FCA's Disclosure and Transparency Rules, specifically DTR5, which requires holders to notify issuers when their voting rights cross prescribed thresholds. Serval Resources plc is a UK issuer listed with ISIN GB00BVRY1W08, and all shares forming the notified position carry direct voting rights under DTR5.1. The company did not disclose any operational update, financial results, or strategic commentary alongside this regulatory filing. Investors tracking the shareholder register at Serval Resources will note this as a routine but material compliance disclosure reflecting a change in one significant holder's position.

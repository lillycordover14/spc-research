Clay Stevenson
 could return to the 
Washington Capitals
 organization this weekend.

On Friday afternoon, the 
Winnipeg Jets
 placed the 27-year-old goaltender on NHL waivers, according to 
Sportsnet’s Elliotte Friedman
.

Clay Stevenson (WIN) on waivers

— Elliotte Friedman (@FriedgeHNIC) 
November 20, 2026

The Jets originally acquired Stevenson off waivers, 
plucking him from the Capitals out of training camp on September 28
. Stevenson made five NHL appearances with Winnipeg this season, posting a 1-3-0 record, a 3.61 goals-against average, and an .870 save percentage.

If the Capitals reclaim Stevenson and are the only team to claim him off waivers, they could assign him directly to the AHL’s Hershey Bears.

Before joining the Jets, Stevenson’s lone prior NHL experience came with Washington, playing in five games over the past two seasons. In those appearances, Stevenson was 3-2-0 with a 2.60 GAA and a .906 save percentage. He has spent the majority of his pro career in the AHL with Hershey. In 37 games for the Bears last year as their number-one goalie, Stevenson went 18-13-3 with a 2.62 GAA and a .912 save percentage.

With Stevenson departing in September, the Capitals looked to address their newfound goaltending depth problem by 
trading the New Jersey Devils a draft pick for Rémi Poirier earlier this month
. The Caps then tried to sneak Poirier through waivers, but the Dallas Stars 
claimed him
 after originally losing the 25-year-old backstop to the Devils on waivers.

The Stars 
recently assigned Poirier to the AHL on a conditioning stint
, but will eventually have to address their three-goaltender roster situation. The Capitals could claim Stevenson or wait for the Stars to waive Poirier, since they already spent a draft asset to acquire him.

Both Stevenson and Poirier are signed through the 2026-27 campaign at $850k cap hits.

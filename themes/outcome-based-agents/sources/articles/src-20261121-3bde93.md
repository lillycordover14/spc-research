Three folds, and the third one is the battery

The Z-shaped body splits the jobs up: the first fold carries the DLP optical engine, the second holds the hinges and the dual 1W stereo speakers, and the third is a non-slip metal base with the 5,000mAh battery inside. Charging runs over USB-C at 24W, and Aurzen rates the pack to reach 80% in 50 minutes, so a power bank in your bag stretches the night well past one film.

Focus that keeps up with a wobbly table

A time-of-flight sensor measures the distance to the wall 30 times a second, and the ZIP uses those readings to hold focus and vertical keystone correction without you touching a menu.

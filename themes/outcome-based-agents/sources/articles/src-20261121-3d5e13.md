[page text]
Winnipeg Jets goaltender Clay Stevenson takes a timeout during the second period of an NHL hockey game against the Pittsburgh Penguins in Pittsburgh, Monday, Oct. 5, 2026. (Gene J. Puskar/AP)

Clay Stevenson
 is headed back to the U.S. capital.

The 
Washington Capitals
 claimed the goalie off waivers from the 
Winnipeg Jets
 on Saturday, it was announced Saturday.

Winnipeg acquired Stevenson via waivers when he was cut by Washington in September.

Stevenson, the Jets' third-string netminder behind Stuart Skinner and Nico Daws, hadn't laced up for the team since a relief appearance on Nov. 1 in a 7-0 loss to the Carolina Hurricanes.

He made five appearances (four starts) with the Jets, posting a 3.61 goals-against average with an .870 save percentage and a 1-3-0 record.

A native of Drayton Valley, Alta., Stevenson has appeared in 10 games (nine starts) during his NHL career, having previously suited up five times for the Capitals.

Meanwhile, the New York Rangers waived forward Luke Glendening, per Sportsnet's Elliotte Friedman.

The veteran forward was yet to hit the scoresheet in four games this season.

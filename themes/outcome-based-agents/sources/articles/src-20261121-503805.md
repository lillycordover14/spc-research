Kolhan-level journalists’ conference and felicitation ceremony held in Bistupur

Jamshedpur, Nov 21:
 Journalism is not merely the fourth pillar of democracy, but a powerful medium to guide society by delivering positive and meaningful messages through fearless, impartial, and credible reporting, said Member of Parliament Bidyut Baran Mahato on Saturday.

He was speaking as the Chief Guest at the ‘Kolhan-level Journalists’ Conference and Felicitation Ceremony-2026’, organised by the Press Club of Jamshedpur at the Michael John Auditorium in Bistupur. Highlighting how journalists risk their lives during wars and natural disasters to bring facts to light, Mahato recalled raising the issue of media welfare in the Lok Sabha and urged both central and state governments to ensure social security, including insurance and pensions, for scribes.

Addressing the gathering as Guest of Honour, Jamshedpur East MLA Purnima Sahu drew from her own journalistic background, noting that the press plays a crucial role in holding power accountable. She remarked that critical reporting should not be viewed negatively, but rather embraced as an opportunity for governance improvement, adding that a strong dialogue between the press and legislature amplifies the public’s voice.

Guest of Honour D.B. Sundar Ramam, Vice President (Corporate Services) at Tata Steel, observed that maintaining news credibility has emerged as the biggest challenge in the era of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Amid growing corporate and political ownership in media, he advised journalists to anchor their work in the ‘Three-I’ principle—Independence, Integrity, and Innovation—to preserve public trust.

The conference featured deliberations on the evolving media landscape, AI’s impact, and journalists’ welfare. During the ceremony, scribes were felicitated for journalistic excellence and long-standing service, alongside honours for meritorious children of journalists, medical professionals, and supporting organizations.

Earlier, the programme began with the national song ‘Vande Mataram’ and mantra chanting by students of Nav Jyoti Vidya Mandir, Gamharia, followed by lamp lighting and a one-minute silence in memory of departed journalists. Presided over by Press Club President Sanjeev Bhardwaj, the event saw General Secretary Vikas Srivastava deliver the welcome outline and vote of thanks, while Sahil Asthana and Sugandha Verma moderated the proceedings.

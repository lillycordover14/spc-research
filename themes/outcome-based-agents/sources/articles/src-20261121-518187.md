State Opens Facial Recognition RFI, Cites IDEMIA Dependency as Basis for Sole Source&nbsp;&nbsp;OrangeSlices AI

[page text]
The State Department is conducting market research for the facial recognition system used to verify identities and screen U.S. passport and visa applicants. The draft PWS identifies IDEMIA NSS as the incumbent and cites a proprietary IDEMIA software dependency as the primary justification for a sole-source award to IDEMIA or an authorized reseller.

State is seeking a COTS facial recognition solution supporting identification, verification and enrollment of biometric face images. The system operates against facial-image galleries containing millions of images and supports State’s passport and visa systems.

The requirement includes software licensing, operations and maintenance, technology refresh, biometric testing and tuning, image-review software, biometric repositories, cybersecurity and program management. The contractor would operate facial recognition systems at two geographically separate State computer operations centers and support integration with existing and new government applications.

The PWS also calls for API-based interfaces as State’s enterprise architecture evolves, and the contractor may be required to support additional biometric modalities, including iris. Optional enhancements include Morph Attack Detection integration.

IDEMIA currently provides licensing, maintenance, operational support and sustainment through its proprietary Multi-Biometric Search Services. State requires continued maintenance of MBSS 7.7 or higher and says its Consular Electronic Application Center remains dependent on IDEMIA’s proprietary Quality Over the Web and Quality Assessment SDK software. According to the PWS, those dependencies must remain until Consular Affairs transitions to the Consular System Modernization platform.

The contemplated firm-fixed-price requirement has a 12-month base and four one-year options, with performance anticipated to begin April 1, 2027. A Secret facility clearance is required.

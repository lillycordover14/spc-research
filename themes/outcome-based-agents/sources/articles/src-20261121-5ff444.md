Quick Read

XLE has dropped 11% in a month as crude slid 18% to $84, and its Exxon and Chevron concentration passes the pain through quickly.

Gasoline's 46-cent three-week plunge matches 2008 only in cents; the 11% drop trails 2008's 20%-plus declines and several other episodes.

Gas needs only about 11 more cents of decline by Thanksgiving to stay under AAA's $3.58 record, and the trend supports it.

The U.S. average price of regular gasoline fell 46.4 cents in the three weeks to Monday, November 16, 2026, the biggest three-week drop since 2008 on the government’s weekly retail series. The average slipped from $4.269
[
1
]
 on October 26 to $3.805 on November 16.

The slide continued after that reading. By Friday, November 20, the daily national average was $3.6898, about 59 cents, or roughly 14%, below a month earlier, and prices were lower in all 50 states.

Energy stocks moved with it. The 
Energy Select Sector SPDR ETF
 (
NYSEARCA:XLE
) stood at $60.95 in after-hours trading Friday, down 6.17% over a week and 10.58% over a month. It still holds a 39.02% gain for 2026, although falling crude now puts that gain under pressure.

What the Weekly Data Actually Shows

The latest weekly drop of 19 cents and the two-week drop of 34.8 cents are also the largest since fall 2008 on the measured weekly series.

The 2008 comparison holds in cents only. The three weeks to November 24, 2008 fell 50.8 cents, and the worst 2008 runs lost more than 80 cents and more than 20%, against 10.9% now.

Early 2015, spring 2020 and December 2022 all produced sharper percentage drops. Over four weeks, the 47.9-cent decline trails June 2026 and December 2022.

Gas also remains about 59 cents above the $3.1003 of a year ago. This is relief from an elevated level.

Thanksgiving Record Remains in Play

AAA’s Thanksgiving Day record is $3.58, set November 24, 2022. On November 19, it said the average could fall another 15 cents by the holiday and avoid a new record.

From Friday’s level, the average needs about 11 more cents of decline by Thursday, November 26. AAA framed that as possible, and the trend supports it.

Nearly 73 million people are expected to take Thanksgiving road trips. The broader forecast of 81.5 million travelers going 50 miles or more is about 121,000 fewer than last year.

How Cheaper Crude Flows Through XLE

WTI crude futures settled at $83.62 Friday, down 5.6% for the week and about 18% below $101.79 a month earlier. Producer cash flow moves with that price.

Refiners earn the spread between what they pay for crude and what they get for gasoline. RBOB gasoline futures fell to $2.481 a gallon from $3.117 a month ago, so both sides of that spread dropped together and the fund’s refining slice cannot offset its producers.

Hormuz Flows Could Keep Draining Crude’s Premium

Fuel analysts on November 16 cited lighter fall driving, cheaper winter-blend gasoline, the end of refinery maintenance and more tankers moving through the 
Strait of Hormuz
. Three of those four repeat every autumn.

Hormuz matters most for XLE, because traffic there had largely stopped earlier this year and lifted crude’s risk premium. Traffic through the Strait of Hormuz has largely been at a standstill, the government’s May outlook said, and sustained flows would keep draining that premium.

President Trump on Friday called the drop one of the largest in the history of oil. The data describes the biggest three-week decline since 2008, a far narrower claim.

What Investors Should Watch in XLE Now

The force pulling oil down is a supply route returning, which likely survives winter blend. Dividends and buybacks at the majors come out of cash flow, and boards have less of it at $83.62 oil than at $101.79.

For investors exploring energy income, the 
Alerian MLP ETF
 (
NYSEARCA:AMLP
) offers a different kind of exposure. Pipeline partnerships mostly charge fees on volumes moved, so they depend more on barrels flowing than on each barrel’s price.

The next weekly government gas reading arrives Monday, November 23. Pressure on the fund likely remains while WTI stays below the $89.53

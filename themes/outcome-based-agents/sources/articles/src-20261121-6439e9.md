Obituary

Ada F. Petersen, 73, of Minden, passed away on Saturday, November 21, 2026, at Bethany Home in Minden. Funeral services will be held at 10:30 a.m. on Tuesday, December 1, 2026, at St. Paul Lutheran Church in Minden with Rev. William Zwick officiating. The service will be streamed to the church’s YouTube page. Interment will be held following the service at White Hill Cemetery, north of Minden. Visitation with family present will be held from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. on Monday, November 30, 2026, at Craig Funeral Home in Minden. In lieu of flowers, memorials in Ada’s honor are kindly suggested to The Association for Frontotemporal Degeneration (AFTD) at theaftd.org, ARC of Buffalo County, or St. Paul Lutheran Church. Arrangements are under the care of Craig Funeral Home in Minden.
Ada Florence Petersen was born on April 28, 1953, in Minden, Nebraska to Harold and Florence (Rients) Petersen. She grew up in Upland and Minden. She attended Minden Public Schools. She married Randall “Randy” Petersen on January 17, 1970. They have two children, Brian and Jodi. They made their home on the farm north of Minden.
Ada was a hard worker and retired at the age of 70. She worked at Kearney County Health Services in the housekeeping and laundry department for over 30 years. She also worked alongside her best friend, Sheryl Morris, at Sheryl’s Daycare for a time. In addition to her jobs in town, Ada helped Randy on the farm. Ada was a member of St. Paul Lutheran Church. She bowled in the Women’s Bowling League in Minden. She also enjoyed country dancing with Randy, and they were members of the Platte Valley Dancers in Kearney. Ada loved gardening, both vegetables and flowers.
Ada was a wonderfully supportive wife, mother, and grandmother. She was Randy’s right hand through many projects and hobbies and race wife extraordinaire. She was a strong advocate for Brian and his education, joining a group of parents to bring Special Education services to Minden Public Schools. She was also a long-time volunteer and supporter of Minden Special Olympics. Ada jumped into Jodi’s activities as well, serving and volunteering for Blue Birds, Camp Fire, and 4-H. Ada never said no to a dog Jodi brought home. Ada was a wonderful grandmother and kindred spirit to Rai. She enjoyed Rai’s many stories and sometimes contributed to creating them. She agreed to be the subject of art projects despite her discomfort being the center of attention. She always offered unconditional love and acceptance.
Ada had a laugh that was loud and contagious. She was good-natured and kind, loyal and tenacious. Ada loved the holidays, particularly Halloween and Christmas. She had a knack for putting together original costumes and had a great dress up box. She made delicious Christmas candy.
Ada is survived by her children, Brian Petersen of Kearney and Jodi Petersen of Minden; grandson Rai of Minden; siblings, Margaret Griffith of Neligh, Warren (Christy) Petersen of Minden, Joy (Randy) Sinsel of Minden, and Ric (Dawn) Petersen of Boynton Beach, Florida; in-laws, Nancy Petersen of Minden, Bruce (Tammie) Petersen and Boyd Petersen, both of Minden. Ada has a host of nieces, nephews, extended family and friends.
Ada was preceded in death by her husband, Randy; parents, Harold and Florence; siblings, Linda Slade, Wilbur Petersen, and Lance Petersen; in-laws, Frank Griffith, Lee Slade, Cliff and Phyllis Petersen, Dennis Petersen, and Caryn Petersen.
Tributes and memories can be shared with the family at www.craigfunerals.com.

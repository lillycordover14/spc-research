If they traded Crosby 3 years ago, the Pens would already have 3-4 top-10 picks in their system.  If they also traded other assets, they could have had 6-7 of them and become a Cup contender in about 2029.

Now trading Crosby will only produce no more than a late 1st.  Playoff contention by 2033 would require some luck and Cup contention only a distant dream now.

And that’s the price of living in the past for 3 fruitless, wasted years.

Drake Batherson: Sidney Crosby ‘can have my house’ if he comes to Senators
Ryan Cuneo
Nov 21, 2026, 15:29 EST
Add to 
Preferred Sources
Credit: 
Charles LeClaire-Imagn Images
With the 
Pittsburgh Penguins
 currently in last place in the Eastern Conference and shaping up as sellers ahead of this season’s trade deadline, 
speculation
 has cropped up about whether captain Sidney Crosby could actually get moved to another team.

If Crosby does eventually become available, 
Ottawa Senators
 forward Drake Batherson has already started the bidding with a pretty generous offer.

“If he wants to come, the door will always be open for him. He could have my house,” Batherson told Bruce Garrioch of the Ottawa Citizen on Saturday. “Obviously, I don’t know what Sid’s going to do. We’ll see.”

Crosby, 39, has 
six goals and eight assists for 14 points
 in 21 games this season. The Cole Harbour, Nova Scotia native is in his 22nd season with the Penguins, and is the franchise’s all-time leader in points (1,775), assists (1,115), and games played (1,441). He has won three Stanley Cups in Pittsburgh, along with two Hart Trophies, two Maurice “Rocket” Richard Trophies, and two Conn Smythe Trophies, along with many other accolades.

Batherson, who skates with Crosby in the summer near Halifax, Nova Scotia, would obviously welcome the future Hall of Famer on the Senators, but also acknowledged how strange it would be to see Crosby wearing the jersey of a team that is not the Penguins.

“It’d be weird,” he said. “I wasn’t alive and watching hockey when Gretzky got traded, but I’m sure it’d be very similar to that. Him and (Alex Ovechkin), you kind of see them in their colours and can’t really picture them on any other team, but I don’t know what will happen.”

Crosby is in the final season of a 
two-year, $17.4 million contract
 with Pittsburgh.

Center Evgeni Malkin, Crosby’s longtime running mate with the Penguins, is also a pending unrestricted free agent and could be another trade candidate.

Tis the season for community! Local school, Eagle Rock, is throwing “Winterfest,” an artisan sale geared towards raising money for graduating students on December 4th and 5th.
Eagle Rock School and Professional Development Center are hosting a “Winterfest” sale for their grad fund. The grad fund is a donation to their graduating students to assist them with future educational opportunities such as college, technology, and housing. Eagle Rock School is a local Estes Park school with a focus on autonomy, leadership and community. The school provides learning opportunities to students who find it hard to thrive in a normal, public school environment. ERS encourages students to become leaders within their community and live with others in peaceful harmony.

This year, students at Eagle Rock are creating homemade gifts to sell at the Recreational Center of Estes. Winterfest is an opportunity for students and staff in a single class to learn about budgeting, marketing, branding, etc; it is also a fun chance for community members to purchase homemade products for your home, family, and friends. Winterfest will have lots of goods, including ceramics, essential oils, body butters, honey, and beeswax.
For more information about “Winterfest” or Eagle Rock, go to 
eaglerockschool.org
. Flyers will be posted around town, on our social media, and in the Facebook Jibber Jabber group. We hope to see you there supporting our students.

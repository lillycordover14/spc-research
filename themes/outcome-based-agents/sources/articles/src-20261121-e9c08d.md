Hawks notch road win at Xavier, 83-65
Isaia Howard had a career high 24 points with Trevin Jirak adding 21 in a big road victory for the Hawks at Xavier
by
 
 
Harrison Starr
Nov 21, 2026, 3:06 AM UTC
After a week where Iowa lost two in a row and saw the football team get jumped in the CFP standings, it felt like a bit of a nadir in the sports landscape. That changed tonight as the Hawkeyes (3-2) were boosted past Xavier (5-1) in a dominant road victory, 83-65.
It started off shaky enough, with Mike Nwoko making three straight 3s for the Musketeers and an and-1 layup for their first 11 points of the game. After Ben McCollum cycled centers - first Andrew McKeever and then Cam Manyawu - the offense was put on jet fuel behind Trevin Jirak who had 14 first half points on 5/5 shooting (including 4 threes). Jirak’s 3rd three inside of 90 seconds gave the Hawks a lead they would never relinquish. He finished with 21 on 8/10 shooting
The man of the second half was Isaia Howard, who used a desperation three at the end of the first half to set his night on fire (5 of 6 from deep). He finished with an insane stat line of 24 points (a career high), 8 boards, 5 assists, and 3 steals. Oh yeah, and no turnovers (Iowa had just 4 on the game). Jirak’s extended minutes provided a lot of spacing for the Hawks and Howard was able to take advantage with 3 of 4 shooting inside throughout the second half.
Hawks wall off the rim
Both teams were on fire from three throughout the first half with Iowa going 9/13 while the Musketeers started 7/10. While Xavier was starting hot, Iowa did well to make sure that contested threes were the best shots they could get as the Hawks continually defended the rim in excellent fashion. They had 3 blocks and allowed 5/9 on layups/dunks in the first half. Perhaps even more impressive is how they forced Xavier into a ton of jumpers the rest of the way, with only 1 layup attempt in the second half…which came with less than 2 minutes remaining.
A huge reason for that is Iowa’s backend communication. Cooper Koch has picked up where he left off as a defender and is regularly in the paint, making opponents question whether it’s worth taking it to the rim. When they do, his verticality has been superb. Throughout the game Iowa was able to make that area of the game tough, and when the three stopped falling for them, it really shut Xavier’s water off.
Iowa sets school record with 29 assists
On the flip side, Iowa did not settle for 3s after starting the game incredibly hot. In fact, they used that spacing to drive and kick and drive some more. After going 6/15 from 2 in the first half, they made 13/18 in the second half. Just beautiful basketball.
Kael Combs led the way in the category with 7 assists in a mostly ho-hum game for him. Ty’reek Coleman joined Howard with 5, despite playing just 13 minutes in the game. In fact, every guy who saw the floor, except for Jaidyn Coon & Peyton McCollum, had an assist.
It’s going to be really interesting to see if this can continue against like competition. If it can, it will be okay if Iowa never has a constant leading scorer given Howard was the 6th guy in 6 games to lead Iowa in scoring.

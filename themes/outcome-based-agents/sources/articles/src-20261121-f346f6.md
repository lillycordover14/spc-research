 November 20, 2026

ATLANTA, Ga. – 
Georgia (6-0) scored the game's first dozen points, pushed that margin to 19 points late in the first half and withstood several second-half rally attempts en route to an 88-82 victory over archrival Georgia Tech (5-1) at McCamish Pavilion on Friday night. 

 

FIVE FAST FACTS:

• Georgia has now won nine of the last 11 meetings in the series, including four consecutive victories.

• 
Kanon Catchings
 led a quintet of Bulldogs in double figures with 19 points, followed by 
Kareem Stagg
 with 17, 
Blue Cain
 and Marcus "Smurf" Millender with 15 each and freshman 
Mading Kuany
 with 10.

• Georgia opened the game with a 12-0 run over the opening 3:56 and eventually pushed the margin to 38-19 with just under a minute left in the half. The Yellow Jackets ended the period on a 5-0 spurt and methodically trimmed the margin to 56-53 with 10:47 left, but the Bulldogs never allow Tech closer.

• With the Bulldogs up 83-78, Cain nailed a 26-foot 3-pointer to beat the shot clock and push the margin back to three possessions with 44 seconds remaining.

• After scoring 17 minutes in 44 minutes of action during Georgia's first five games, Kuany scored 10 points in a 5:03 segment in the first half. Kuany also has two blocks and an emphatic dunk during that span.

 

UP NEXT:

The Bulldogs will remain on the road for their next two games, trekking to Disney's Wide World of Sports to face Cincinnati and UCF in the ESPN Events Invitational on next Thursday and Friday, respectively.

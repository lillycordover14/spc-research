Mr. A. P. On Pace to Impress Again in Native Diver&nbsp;&nbsp;BloodHorse

[page text]
The disappointing return and subsequent retirement of 2025 champion 2-year-old male 
Ted Noffey
🔥
 
 
 put another nail in the sketchy legacy of the field for the 2025 Breeders' Cup Juvenile (G1). With only six starters, it was the smallest gathering in the history of the race (tied with six in 2026), but that did not mean fairy tales could not come true as the young hopefuls matured into their 3-year-old campaigns.

Then one by one they fell by the wayside until, one year later, the dubious title of last man standing goes to Juvenile runner-up 
Mr. A. P.
 
, who will carry the colors of Dave and Holly Wilson into the $100,000 Native Diver Stakes (G3) Nov. 21 at 
Del Mar
.

The other five from the 2025 Juvenile have just one win in a cumulative 14 starts, and only fourth-place 
Litmus Test
🔥
 
, winner of the Los Alamitos Futurity (G2) one start after the Juvenile, is currently in training.

Mr. A. P., on the other hand, has made good on the promise of his Breeders' Cup performance, in which the son of 
American Pharoah
 
 was beaten only a length by Ted Noffey at odds of 20-1. Mr. A. P. emerged from that effort with a bone chip in his right knee that required surgery. He returned to the races in July, beginning a steady, late-season campaign after a patient recovery under the supervision of his trainer Vladimir Cerin.

The highlight has been his thoroughly professional victory in the 1-mile Tokyo City Cup Stakes Oct. 24 at 
Santa Anita Park
, in which he defeated 
Lyell's Song
 
 by 1 3/4 lengths. Lyell's Song was making his stakes debut that day for the Mark Glatt barn and returns for a rematch, along with Sunland Park Stakes winner 
Vodka Vodka
 
, who finished last after an eventful trip.

An eclectic group of eight was entered for the Native Diver, named for the Hall of Fame streak of near-black fire whose free-wheeling style racked up 34 stakes in California during the mid-1960s. The total included three versions of the San Diego Handicap at Del Mar, where Native Diver's remains can be found beneath a marker in the infield after they were transferred from their original resting place at Hollywood Park.

The swift 
Pony Express
 
, under Armando Ayuso, will likely try to do a Native Diver imitation from his inside post, with Lyell's Song in close pursuit. It has been nearly 13 months since Pony Express last raced, although he has been working steadily for John Sadler on Santa Anita's Tapeta training track since early September.

"He's a very nice horse we've had to stop and start on a few times," Sadler said. "This has been the race we've aimed for to get him started again, and hopefully to give us some options on what to do come the new year."

The rest of the field includes 
Midway Lane
 
, who makes his stakes debut for Sadler after winning a starter allowance event at Santa Anita; Santa Anita Handicap (G1) winner 
British Isles
 
, badly beaten in three starts since; and 
Bullard
 
, a 
Gun Runner
 
 
 colt who has hit the board six times in 10 starts without winning since a score in the 2024 Bob Hope Stakes (G3) at Del Mar.

Mr. A. P.'s stiffest opposition could come from fellow 3-year-old 
Zihnal
 
, impressive winner of the West Virginia Derby (G3) in August for Augustin Stables and trainer Jonathan Thomas. The gelded son of 
Quality Road
 
 
 lost a close one to 
Silent Tactic
 
 in the Oklahoma Derby (G3) at 
Remington Park
 last time out.

Photo: Coady Media/Renee Torbit
Zihnal wins the West Virginia Derby at Mountaineer Casino Racetrack

"He's a big, beautiful horse—a typical Quality Road," Thomas said. "Most everybody asks who he is when they see him. In hindsight, you always want to regret gelding a horse like him. But these days, there's a very algorithmic formula for the stud farms to take any interest, and one of them is being a good 2-year-old."

Zihnal did not race until April, then passed his maiden test in June at 
Horseshoe Indianapolis
.

"Truth be told, at 2 he was kind of a coarse, heavy horse, and in the wrong areas," Thomas added. "Gelding him really cleaned him up."

As for Mr. A. P., he was a $150,000 claim out of his first race in July 2025 at Del Mar. Three starts later, after a maiden win at a mile, he nearly pulled off the Breeders' Cup Juvenile upset.

The Wilsons and Cerin began making headlines in 2000 with the victory of Early Pioneer
 
in the Hollywood Gold Cup (G1). At odds of 24-1, he was the longest price in the field. (Winning jockey Victor Espinoza dyed his hair orange before the race to match the Wilsons' silks.) Graded stakes winners
 
Designed for Luck
 
and 
Briecat
 
 have followed.

Cerin, who has trained since 1982, has been at the forefront of employing a variety recuperation therapies, including the hyperbaric chamber, to enhance the healing and recovery process in a sidelined racehorse such as Mr. A. P.

"I'm lucky enough to have owners who let me do what needs to be done," Cerin said. "I never even turned him out so we could work with all the modalities at my disposal. He's a little bit bigger than he was at 2, but not a whole lot, and he still glides over the racetrack."

Cerin initially brought Mr. A. P. back to the races on turf with a third, beaten a neck for second money, behind the dominant 
Proletariat
 
 in the Oceanside Handicap at Del Mar. After winning an allowance optional claimer on grass, his people were inspired to roll the dice with a trip to 
Kentucky Downs
 for the Gun Runner Stakes, a listed event worth more than $1.7 million. Mr. A. P. finished ninth of 14 after what can be generously described as a disorienting trip that ended with a futile late run down the middle of the worn course.

After that, Cerin shifted his horse back to the main track for a fourth-place finish in the Goodwood Stakes (G1) against older horses.

"It took that one race in the Goodwood to get back, then I think he put it all together in the Tokyo City," Cerin said. "He's slowly maturing, and this being a little tougher race than his last one, he'll need to improve a little more. All said, I'm very excited to see him run."

Francesca Ada Lizama captured double gold at the 2026 IBJJF Atlanta Fall International Open, earning first place in both the Adult Brown Belt Medium-Heavyweight Division and the Adult Brown Belt Absolute Division.
Lizama delivered a dominant performance, finishing both of her matches by submission with zero points scored against her, according to a press release from Purebred Jiu Jitsu Guam.
Representing Guam, Lizama continues to build an impressive 2026 competition season on the international Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu circuit.

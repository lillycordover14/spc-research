Celtics' Ron Harper: Season-high 13 minutes Saturday&nbsp;&nbsp;CBS Sports

[page text]
Harper produced three points (1-4 FG, 1-4 3Pt) and one assist across 13 minutes during Saturday's 97-96 loss to Sacramento.
With Jayson Tatum (rest) and Derrick White (shoulder) unavailable in the front end of this back-to-back set, Harper logged a season-high 13 minutes. However, he struggled with efficiency and is now averaging 2.6 points on 36.4 percent shooting in 7.5 minutes per tilt across eight regular-season appearances.

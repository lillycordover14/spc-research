[page text]
By 
Johnathan L. Wright

/

Las Vegas Review-Journal

					November 22, 2026 - 12:25 am				

 

T
he word curated has become clichéd, loosely applied to many things that are not curated at all, merely chosen. But at Akin Cooperative, the word fits.

The downtown Las Vegas shop showcases artisan goods, artwork and other items, along with craft beer, natural wines and pantry picks. The collection reflects true curatorial intelligence, ranging across about a dozen categories — including accessories, stationery, ceramics, clothing and jewelry — and several dozen brands. The selections are compelling and often surprising.

The collection changes seasonally, so you might find vintage sunglasses and shift dresses in floral prints designed by artists, coconut citrus body scrub and handmade prosecco glasses fashioned from recycled glass, small leather goods and turquoise jewelry, and groovy stickers and pins.

The cooperative also offers workshops, tastings, a weekend Neighborhood Market DTLV, and a Weekend Pick Up with baked goods, wine and more. Many of the goods showcased at the cooperative are made in Vegas. 
1325 S. Commerce St.,
 
akincooperative.com.

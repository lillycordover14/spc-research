Beaumont to change ZIP Services in December&nbsp;&nbsp;Beaumont Examiner |

[page text]
The City of Beaumont is announcing changes to BMT ZIP bus service that will take effect on Monday, December 7, 2026. The changes are designed to improve connections for riders, provide better access to businesses and services and make the transit system more efficient.
The changes include adjustments to two bus routes:
Route #9 North 11th/Dowlen:
 The route will be extended to Delaware Street and Dowlen Road, providing riders with improved access to shopping, businesses and other destinations.
Route #1 Magnolia/Pine:
 The Magnolia and Pine routes will be combined into one route, extending service along Pine Street.
The changes will also provide more opportunities for riders to connect between routes, helping make travel across Beaumont more convenient.
In addition to the route adjustments, Saturday operating hours for regular bus service will change to 
7 a.m. to 6 p.m. 
Weekday operating hours will remain unchanged.
Saturday hours for Special Transit Services (STS), which provide transportation for eligible riders with disabilities, will remain unchanged.
These updates are part of the City's ongoing efforts to improve public transportation while making the best use of available resources.
Residents are encouraged to review the updated routes and plan ahead before the changes take effect on December 7.
For more information, visit B
eaumontZip.com/NewRoutes
 or call the BMT ZIP office at 
409-835-7895
.

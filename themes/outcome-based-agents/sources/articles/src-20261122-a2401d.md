CHARLOTTE – The Center Point Lady Pirates broke into the win column for the first and second times this basketball season when they competed in the Charlotte Trojanette Classic held Nov. 19-21.
Both wins were over McMullen County, which entered the tourney 3-1 compared to the Lady Pirates being 0-3. The opening-day victory was 25-22, and on Saturday the Lady Pirates won 24-20 to end the tournament 2-3.
In Saturday's game, Center Point outscored the Cowgirls 13-6 in the fourth quarter to rally from behind.

Center Point, 2-6 overall after the tourney, had Christina Reyes and Kalyani Ritter named to the All-Tournament Team for their five-game performances, while head coach Brent Beadle recognized several more Lady Pirates for their efforts as well.
vs. McMullen County No. 2
In Saturday's closing contest against McMullen County, Reyes scored 13 points, made 10 steals and grabbed five rebounds. Reyes had 22 total steals in five games for an average of just over four per contest.
Ritter had seven points in win number two over the Cowgirls in addition to 11 rebounds and three blocked shots. Ritter averaged almost six boards per game at Charlotte.
Cobee Beckerson pitched in the four remaining points for the Lady Pirates, pulled in nine rebounds and blocked five shots.
Other games

Center Point began the tournament with its three-point win over the same McMullen County squad when Ritter put in 10 points to lead the way.
In between wins over McMullen County, the Lady Pirates lost to host Charlotte 61-40, Navarro 43-18 and Utopia 49-39.
Beckerson scored 21 points and had nine rebounds against Charlotte. Ritter had 13 points, and Reyes added four.
The Lady Pirates were down by nine at halftime against Navarro, but the Panthers went on a 13-2 run in the third period. Ritter's 10 points were best for Center Point, and Reyes had the other eight.
Center Point had its rematch with Utopia since the teams met one week prior at Center Point, where the Lady Buffaloes took a nine-point win. In the tourney faceoff, Reyes scored 16 points, Beckerson had 10 and Ritter seven.
Besides Center Point's "Big 3," Beadle singled out Ashley Macklin and McKaila Heinen for performances that showed up when Macklin finished with 20 boards and 11 steals, while Heinen had 10 steals and nine rebounds as well. Seven of Heinen's thefts came against Utopia.
"I'm proud of the way our girls battled. Our shooting percentage doubled, our turnover rate dropped by 20 percent, and we got some really good playing time for some of our rotational girls. Outside of Christina, Cobee and Kaylani, we got some really solid performances from Ashley and McKaila. Both girls contributed tremendously to our defensive cause, holding other teams under 30 percent shooting against us and forcing a higher turnover rate."
Center Point's pre-Thanksgiving games consisted of a home-court tilt with Nueces Canyon on Monday and traveling to Leakey on Tuesday.

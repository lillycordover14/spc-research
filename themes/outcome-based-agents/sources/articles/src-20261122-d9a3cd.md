DU records nine podium finishes Saturday to remain atop the team standings
Share:
Lynchburg, Va. – 
The Duquesne swimming and diving team continued its strong showing Saturday at the TYR '85 Invite, earning nine podium finishes to maintain its lead in the team standings through the second day of competition at Liberty University.


The Dukes remain atop the women's team standings with 1,783.5 points, with host Liberty (1,563) sitting in second place. UNC Asheville (906), Marshall (880) and UNC Pembroke (699) round out the top five.


Freshman 
Lainey Sheets
 led the way for Duquesne, capturing the 100-yard butterfly with a time of 53.91. Sheets also added a runner-up finish in the 100-yard backstroke, touching the wall in 54.66, while junior 
Sierra Snow
 followed in third with a time of 55.81. Sophomore 
Norah Kotnik
 added a fourth-place finish in 55.91.


Duquesne also enjoyed a strong showing in the 100-yard butterfly, placing three swimmers among the top five. Freshman 
Maya Freeman
 finished fourth in 55.07, while junior 
Rachel Howard
 added a fifth-place finish in 55.58. Sophomore 
Kira Schrecongost
 (57.51), senior 
Julia Sobun
 (58.26) and freshman 
Addi Ebol
 (58.67) also competed in the event.


In the 400-yard individual medley, junior 
Ashley Freel
 led the Dukes with a runner-up finish in 4:22.20. Freshman 
Caitlin Goff
 followed closely behind in third at 4:23.99, while sophomore 
Emily Kyre
 placed sixth in 4:30.74. Freshman 
Kathryn Cinson
 (4:44.35) and sophomore 
Anna Klotz
 (4:36.59) also scored points for Duquesne.


Junior 
Jess Burns
 paced the Dukes in the 100-yard breaststroke, securing second place with a time of 1:02.10. Junior 
Surrena Luna
 added a fourth-place finish in 1:04.00, while Ebol touched the wall in 1:05.08 to finish fifth. Goff (1:05.73) and Schrecongost (1:06.37) also earned points in the event.


In the 200-yard freestyle, Snow secured a runner-up finish with a time of 1:50.01, while junior 
Kaitlyn Connors
 added a sixth-place finish in 1:52.33. Duquesne opened the evening session with a pair of strong performances in the 200-yard medley relay. The A squad of Freel, Burns, Howard and sophomore 
Lucy Backus
 finished second in 1:41.30, while the B team of Kotnik, Luna, Sheets and Connors placed fourth in 1:44.05.


The Dukes also turned in a pair of top-four finishes in the 800-yard freestyle relay. The A squad of Sheets, junior 
Caitlin Horning
, Connors and Freel finished second in 7:28.44, while the B team of Backus, Kotnik, Durbin and Donovan placed fourth in 7:46.23.


On the diving boards, 
Brianne Dempsey
 paced Duquesne in the three-meter competition, placing fourth with a score of 257.10. Sophomore Gracy Hedger followed in sixth with 241.60 points, while sophomore 
Elly Huron
 finished 10th (230.15) and senior 
Erin Castagnero
 placed 13th (221.80). Freshman 
Emily Setlock
 rounded out the Duquesne contingent with a score of 204.25.

UP NEXT

Duquesne concludes competition at the invite on Sunday, Nov. 23 as the prelims will begin at 10:00 a.m. with the finals set for 5:00 p.m.

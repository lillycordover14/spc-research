Marilyn M. Harper

Marilyn M. Harper

WARREN — Marilyn M. Harper, 95, passed away peacefully, Thursday, November 19, 2026, at Windward Gardens in Camden. Born in Rockland, March 31, 1931, she was the daughter of Charles (Bernie) and Gertrude Fitzgerald.
 
December 18, 1951, she married the love of her life, Clifford E. Harper, in Rockland after meeting on a blind date. While Clifford served in the Navy, they lived in San Diego and Coronado, California. Additionally, they lived a short time in Moorestown, New Jersey, before moving back home to Maine.
 
Marilyn worked at many local businesses, including Port Clyde Packing Company, Van Baalen, Sylvania and Mammoth Mart before becoming a homemaker and raising her four children. She also was a member of the Rockland Emblem Club.
 
Some of Marilyn’s favorite things were painting ceramics, hooking rugs, doing crossword puzzles and beach combing for shells and sea glass.
 
Marilyn is predeceased by her parents and her sister Yvonne. She is survived by her children, Debbie and Dale Schafer of Kansas, Brian and Lynda Harper of Warren, Jeff and Vicky Harper of Thomaston and Betty and Larry Oakes, Jr. of Spruce Head; her sister Rosemary Perdiue of Cary, North Carolina; eight grandchildren and many great-grandchildren.
 
A private family service will be held to celebrate Marilyn’s life.
 
The family would like to thank the staff of Windward Gardens for their compassionate care of Marilyn for the last three years. Additionally, the family thanks Constellation Hospice for bringing comfort to Marily during her end of life.
 
To share a memory or story with Marilyn’s family, please visit their Book of Memories at www.bchfh.com. Arrangements are in the care of Burpee, Carpenter & Hutchins Funeral Home, Rockland.

Kiryat Ata tabs Dakarai Tucker 

 - November 22, 2026

POSTED BY:

Follow us on

 

Urban Latti Kiryat Ata
 (Winner League) inked 32-year old American 196cm small forward 
Dakarai Tucker
 (college: 
Utah
, agency: 
SLN
). He played most recently at 
Gotham City Bockers
 (The Basketball Tournament) in the U.S. In two TBT games he just recently played he averaged 13.0ppg, 4.0rpg and 2.0apg last season.
In 2021 (five years after his graduation) he was drafted by Iowa Wolves (G-League) in first round (17th overall).
He graduated from University of Utah in 2016 and it will be his eleventh (!!!) season in pro basketball.
Tucker is a very experienced player. He has quite traveled the world as his pro career brought him to nine different countries on three different continents (Europe, Latin America and of course North America). Tucker has played professionally in Germany (EWE Baskets), Hungary (Szolnok), Uruguay (Welcome), Israel (Hapoel Holon, Ramat Gan, Ashkelon, Maccabi Raanana and Hapoel Haifa), Dominican Republic (Sameji), NBA G League (Rio Grande Valley Vipers), France (La Vendeenne), NBA Summer League (Phoenix Suns) and The Basketball Tournament (LA Ignite). Tucker's team made it to the German Final Four in 2026.
Among many awards he was voted to Eurobasket.com All-Hungarian League Honorable Mention back in 2020.

STATELINE, Nev. — In a season of firsts for the New Mexico Goatheads, the team checked off another franchise milestone.

This weekend, the Goatheads swept the Tahoe Knight Monsters, their first sweep in franchise history, albeit in a two-game series. New Mexico won handily 7-1 on Friday, then came out with a 2-1 win on Saturday.
This series gave the Goatheads a chance to close the gap. While New Mexico entered the series 14th in the Western Conference standings, Tahoe was dead last at 15th. After this weekend's series, the Knight Monsters (4-10-1) remained at the bottom, while New Mexico (5-7-4) moved up to 10th. The wins extended the Goatheads' winning streak to three games, another franchise-high. 
After setting a franchise record with six goals in their previous game against the Kalamazoo Wings, the Goatheads carried that momentum into Tahoe and broke the record again with seven on Friday. New Mexico got off to a quick start with three first-period goals, then matched that in the second before getting their seventh and final in the third. Tahoe scored to break the streak of seven unanswered Goathead goals, but it was window dressing for what would become a New Mexico rout.

Lynden Breen scored twice, and Cameron Buhl recorded a goal and two assists. Grant Ahcan, Chris Hedden, and Maksim Barbashev each added multi-point performances, while Francesco Arcuri, reassigned to New Mexico on Nov. 17, scored a shorthanded goal. Newest New Mexico acquisition Alexander Dameski, who signed with the team on Nov. 18, also posted his first assist as a Goathead.
Saturday saw fewer goals, but the same result, even though Tahoe had 12 more shots on goal than New Mexico. Kylor Wall opened the scoring for the Goatheads with a first-period goal. Then, team captain Derek Daschke scored in the second to put New Mexico up two goals. Tahoe did respond with a goal nine minutes later, but the Goatheads held on to the lead to secure the sweep.
While Friday was all about goals, the series was highlighted by New Mexico’s play in the net. Goalie Nikita Novoselov turned away 27 of 28 shots to secure Friday's win. Then, Isaiah Saville matched Novoselov, allowing just one goal and stopping 35 of 36 shots. This was a welcome sight for New Mexico, which had been near the top of goals allowed in the ECHL.
After a strong road trip that saw the Goatheads go 3-2, New Mexico returns to the Rio Rancho Events Center this week for some home cooking. The Goatheads will play a three-game series against the Florida Everblades over Thanksgiving week, beginning Wednesday, Nov. 25, and continuing Nov. 27-28. 
This will certainly be a step up for New Mexico. After facing one of the bottom feeders in Tahoe, the Goatheads will face the ECHL's best team in the Everblades. Florida (12-2) has won four of the last five Kelly Cups, the trophy awarded to the ECHL playoff champion.

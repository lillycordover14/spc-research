ODOT completes chip sealing, highway upgrades and ADA projects across Eastern Oregon

By 
Garrett Christensen
 on Monday, November 23rd, 2026 in 
Northeastern Oregon News
 
Northeastern Oregon Top Stories
 

 

 

 

EASTERN OREGON 
– With winter on the way, highway projects across Eastern Oregon are winding down. Despite the current funding issues, the Oregon Department of Transportation (ODOT) reports that several major projects were completed across the region, ranging ADA updates to local streets to major highway resurfacing, while others will resume work in the spring. 

For ADA upgrades, ODOT reports that ADA compliant curb ramps were completed in North Powder along highway 237, Halfway along OR 413 and OR 86-S, and Richland along OR 86 for a final cost of $5.4 million dollars. Meanwhile in Vale, the intersection of OR-451 and U.S. 20 saw new pedestrian sidewalk and railroad crossing upgrades at a cost of $1.1 million dollars. Other ADA and pedestrian infrastructure projects were also completed in Umatilla, Hermiston and Pendleton. 

For Chip Sealing, ODOT reports over 151 miles of highway saw surface repairs including:

Grant, Baker and Malheur counties – 40 miles of U.S. 26.

Malheur County – 51 miles of U.S. 95.

Union County – 9 miles of OR 203 and 10 miles of OR 237.

Umatilla County – 16 miles of OR 11.

Baker County – 25 miles of OR 7.

For bridge and highway safety, around seven miles of outdated guardrail were replaced along OR 86 west of Richland between mileposts 34 and 71 at a cost of $4.5 million dollars. New lights were installed along I-84 on Cabbage Hills Westbound (mileposts 220 to 223) and in Lad Canyon both east and westbound (mileposts 270.5 to 272) for a cost of $657,000 dollars. Lastly, ODOT painted new striping along portions of I-84 in Union, Baker, and Umatilla counties from Meacham to Huntington for a cost of $1.8 million dollars.

Alongside the completed projects, ODOT made progress and several others that are expected to resume this spring. Locally, this includes crossovers built on I-84 on the North Spruce Street Bridges in La Grande to prepare for future construction, paving work and highway upgrades on I-84 between Huntington and Ontario, as well as ADA upgrades in La Grande, Haines, and Sumpter.

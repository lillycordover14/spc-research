USDC Supply on Injective Rises Sixfold to $71.5 Million as Circle Winds Down Noble

 
                                            bsc.news
                                        

                                                                2 h
                                    

The supply of 
$USDC
 issued on @injective ($INJ) has reached $71.5 million, up from about $11 million a week ago. The sixfold increase comes as @circle winds down 
$USDC
 on Noble, the chain that issued the stablecoin for the Cosmos ecosystem. Cosmos chains and dYdX are switching to 
$USDC
 issued on Injective instead.

Circle Sets Timeline for Noble Exit

According to Injective's published timeline, new minting of 
$USDC
 to Noble through Circle Mint stopped on October 13, 2026. Legacy CCTP burn limits begin stepping down toward zero from October 31, and Circle Mint and CCTP access for Noble ends on January 13, 2027.

The shutdown affects a sizeable pool of liquidity. CryptoSlate reported that roughly $92 million of 
$USDC
 is on a deadline, and that exchanges, decentralized finance protocols and more than 50 Cosmos-linked chains must shift liquidity and integrations away from Noble. Cosmos Labs has coordinated a replacement route through Injective, where Circle already issues native 
$USDC
, the outlet said.

Injective said it is working with Circle, Cosmos Labs, dYdX and ecosystem partners on the transition from Noble 
$USDC
 to native 
$USDC
 issued by Circle on Injective. Users can migrate through the Skip:Go routing tool, which opened a path from Noble 
$USDC
 (
$USDC
.n) to Injective 
$USDC
 (
$USDC
.inj) on September 11, according to Live Bitcoin News.

dYdX Moves Its 
$USDC
 Backing to Injective

dYdX is also changing its set-up. A v9.9 chain upgrade introduces a Canonical 
$USDC
 module that gradually replaces Noble-issued 
$USDC
 with Injective-issued 
$USDC
 as the backing asset, according to TokenPost. Under the plan, deposits are supported only through Injective once migration begins, and deposits from Noble are rejected and returned.

Existing balances are not affected. Longbridge, citing ChainCatcher, reported that dYdX said users do not need to act on 
$USDC
 already deposited on-chain, and that open positions and margin remain unaffected. Withdrawals are issued as Injective 
$USDC
, so users need an Injective address to receive them.

The jump in supply on Injective reflects these migrations. Whether the new 
$USDC
 balance is sustained after the Noble deadline passes will depend on continued usage across Cosmos chains and applications.

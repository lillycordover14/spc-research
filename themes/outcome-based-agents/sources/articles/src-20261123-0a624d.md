JACKSONVILLE, FLA.: For Veterans living with back injuries, limited mobility, or other physical challenges, something as routine as preparing a golf ball for a shot can become an obstacle to enjoying the game. At American Lake Veterans Golf Course in Lakewood, Washington, 
Power Tee
‘s automated teeing technology is helping remove that obstacle, giving Veterans a more comfortable, convenient, and independent way to practice golf.

Set against the picturesque backdrop of the Pacific Northwest, 
American Lake Veterans Golf Course
 is an 18-hole Jack Nicklaus Signature Course featuring tree-lined fairways, bentgrass turf, and accessible design elements tailored to wounded and disabled Veterans.

 

How Power Tee Helps Golfers Overcome Physical Challenges

For Dan, a retired U.S. Army Veteran who broke his back while serving in Iraq in 2009, even some of the simplest parts of practicing golf can be challenging. Known as “Mongoose” during his nearly 22 years in the Army, much of it with the 1st Special Forces Group, Dan has four screws and two plates in his back.

Located near Joint Base Lewis-McChord, American Lake has provided Dan with a place to enjoy golf alongside other Veterans, regardless of their physical abilities.

All 10 bays at its 
covered driving range
 feature Power Tee automated teeing systems, providing a more comfortable and consistent practice experience.

“What I love about this course is that they have Power Tee, so I don’t have to bend down to put the ball on the tee,” Dan said.

Instead of manually placing the ball on the tee each time, which takes time away from practice, Power Tee automatically tees the ball at the golferâs preferred tee height. Thus, Power Tee allows players to stay in position, working on their swing. That helps build consistency, rhythm, and muscle memory so golfers can get the best out of your game.

For Veterans with back injuries or limited mobility, this seemingly simple convenience can make a meaningful difference in their ability to practice more comfortably and independently.

“Many of our golfers are disabled or older Veterans, and teeing up a ball at the driving range can be extremely difficult,” said Sam Williams, General Manager of American Lake Veterans Golf Course. “Power Tee makes it much easier for these golfers to practice and enjoy the game with greater independence. Partnerships like this help us remove barriers and deliver on our mission of making golf accessible to Veterans.”

A Golf Course Built Around Veterans

American Lake Veterans Golf Course is dedicated to providing Veterans with affordable, accessible golf. Its commitment to adaptive golf extends throughout the facility, including accessible course features, specialized mobility-impaired golf carts, and a Rehabilitation and Learning Center.

Equally impressive is the community behind it. More than 200 volunteers, many of whom are Veterans themselves, help operate and maintain the facility. Supported by donors, sponsors, and community partners, American Lake offers more than a place to play. It provides recreation, camaraderie, and a welcoming environment where Veterans can enjoy the game together.

For Dan, the people are an important part of what makes American Lake special.

“The people here are phenomenal,” he said, reflecting on the volunteers and the experience of playing at a course designed for those who have been injured or wounded during their service.

A Partnership with a Greater Purpose

Power Tee and American Lake Veterans Golf Course recently 
renewed their partnership
, ensuring that automated teeing technology remains available across all 10 covered driving range bays.

For Power Tee, whose technology is installed in 19 countries worldwide, American Lake highlights an important application beyond traditional measures of revenue growth and operational efficiency. Here, the value is reflected in the experience of Veterans who can practice more comfortably and independently.

“The work that ALVGC does for our nation’s veterans is extraordinary, and we are honored that Power Tee can play a small role in making the game more accessible and enjoyable for the men and women who have served our country,” said Martin Wyeth, Founder of Power Tee.

The continued partnership demonstrates how innovative technology, accessible facilities, and community support can work together to make golf more inclusive.

At American Lake Veterans Golf Course, Power Tee is helping remove everyday barriers, giving Veterans more opportunities to practice, connect, and enjoy the game they love.

About American Lake Veterans Golf Course

American Lake Veterans Golf Course provides a first-class, affordable golf experience for Veterans and their families. Founded in 1956, the course was created to provide Veterans with a place for recreation and relaxation and has evolved into a nationally recognized Jack Nicklaus Signature Course dedicated to accessible and adaptive golf.

The course is managed, operated and maintained by more than 200 volunteers, many of whom are Veterans. The Friends of American Lake Veterans Golf Course, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, has contributed more than $14 million toward improvements and capital projects at the facility.

Gardaí following 'definite line of enquiry' on fatal scrambler bike collision in Ballina
 

 

 


 

 

Gardaí investigating a fatal hit and run road traffic collision at Mercy Park, Mc Dermott Street, Ballina, Co. Mayo on Friday 20th November 2026, continue to appeal for witnesses.
 A post mortem on the deceased, who has been identified locally as
 John Queenan of St Patricks Estate, Ballina
, has taken place at Mayo University Hospital.
Investigating Gardaí are following a definite line of enquiry.
A male juvenile has attended voluntarily at a Garda Station, in the presence of a parent, and has been questioned in relation to this incident.
Gardai will in the first instance prepare a file for the National Juvenile Office as required under the Children Act 2001.
Gardaí continue to appeal to any road users or local residents who may have camera footage (including dash-cam and doorbell cameras) of a scrambler motorcycle in Ballina, Co. Mayo on the evening of Friday 20th November 2026 are asked to make this footage available to investigating Gardaí.
 In addition, any road users who were travelling in the vicinity of Mercy Park (locally known as Parkside), Mc Dermott Street, Ballina, Co. Mayo between 9:00pm and 9:30pm on Friday 20th November 2026 are asked to make this footage available to investigating Gardaí.
 Anyone with any information is asked to contact Ballina Garda Station on (096) 20560, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any Garda Station.

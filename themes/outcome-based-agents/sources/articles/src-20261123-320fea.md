Bella Hadid
 is shedding layers - both literally and emotionally - in her 
new Harper's Bazaar cover story. 
The runway star goes fully naked for the front of the glossy's December 2026/January 2027 issue, wearing not a stitch of clothing on her statuesque physique save for fluffy heels. 
At 30, Hadid is one of the world's most famous models who has been in the industry along with her sister Gigi for years. 
The star peeled back the curtain to her glittery life in a candid interview with Harper's Bazaar's editor-in-chief Samira Nasr, where she lifted the lid on the health challenges she has battled both publicly and privately, as well as the modeling industry's obsession with weight.  
Hadid was the vision of confidence as she opened
 the 2026 
Victoria's Secret
 Fashion Show in floral wings but only moments earlier suffered a health scare that left her struggling to breathe. 
'I have POTS [postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome], and I have a lot of PMDD [premenstrual dysphoric disorder], endometriosis, plus my Lyme and things that affect my body very deeply,' she explained. 'Right before I walked out on the runway for my opening look, I went to go stretch and my whole clavicle completely … I don’t even know what it was. My lungs almost collapsed, and I couldn’t breathe, and then it was like, “Five, four …”

It's an example of how Hadid has been coming to terms with her body's limitations in light of her health challenges. 
'So my body goes through these different motions of feeling really good and then all of a sudden crashing on me at the worst moments. I need to realize maybe my body can’t handle all that I have going on. 
'Because I know how to walk. One thing I can do is I can strut. But it’s a lot of pressure; we all have our own insecurities. Sometimes my body just can’t connect with my brain.

'My brain wants to do it, and my body is like, “No, we can’t do that today.” And I’m like, “Okay, great. So let’s figure out another way.” But I think people don’t realize how much I struggle with the fatigue and the body pain, and so I just try my best.' 
Hadid has remained one of the world's top models despite her chronic health challenges. 
In an industry obsessed with image, that can pose a challenge for someone not always feeling their best. 
The beauty is no stranger to the fashion industry's fixation with appearance and has also faced scrutiny over her weight.  
When Hadid was just 17, she faced scrutiny over her own figure from an unidentified stylist who told her she was 'too big' to wear Saint Laurent. 
'When I was 17, going to my first go-see with a stylist I will not name, I was basically told I was too big to wear Saint Laurent and needed to lose a couple of pounds,' she said. 'And that’s when I was just a baby coming out of high school. Twelve years later, now I’m the face of Saint Laurent. I’m the face of Prada, which is incredible and insane.

'I want to do things that are fulfilling for me in my soul: having a greenhouse that I can garden in, having my glassblowing and ceramic studio, and having lots of children and being a mother.

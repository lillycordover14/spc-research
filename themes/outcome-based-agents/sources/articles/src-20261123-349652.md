November 23, 2026

WORCESTER, MASS. — MassDevelopment, the state’s finance agency and land bank, has provided a $1.8 million loan to Bluestone Residential for the renovation of a former industrial building at 98 Beacon St. in Worcester. The four-story building, developed circa 1900, which has been vacant for the past 10 years, will be converted into 58 market-rate apartments. Civco Development is a partner on the project.
 
Financing also includes a $7.5 million first mortgage loan from Clinton Savings Bank. The Massachusetts Historical Commission provided an allocation of approximately $2.6 million in state historic rehabilitation tax credits for the project, with MassDevelopment’s $1.8 million loan serving as a bridge until the project is completed and the tax credits are sold and monetized. Construction is underway, and the project is expected to be complete within two years.

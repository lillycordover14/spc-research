        STL celebrates 145% sales surge in Q2 with EBITDA up sixfold year on year
        
 
 

        Sterlite Technologies has posted a record turnover of INR 25.3 billion (USD 272.4 million) for its second fiscal quarter to 30 September. Sales soared 145 percent year-on-year on strong demand for STL's AI-grade fibre-optic cables and other optical connectivity equipment. Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation surged sixfold to INR 8.5 billion, while STL's order book at 30 September reached INR 352.7 billion.

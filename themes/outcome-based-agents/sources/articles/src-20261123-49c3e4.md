Harper Elementary celebrates family involvement through classroom reading

Published 1:58 pm Monday, November 23, 2026

By 
Staff reports

 

                                        READY FOR A STORY: Students sit on the carpet as they wait for the story to begin. (Submitted Photo)

THOMASVILLE — Harper Elementary School recently welcomed parents and family members into its classrooms to share the joy of reading with students, creating meaningful connections between home and school.

Family members took time out of their busy schedules to visit classrooms and read stories to students, bringing excitement and smiles to the school day.

The visits provided students with an opportunity to enjoy stories read by some of the most important people in their lives while reinforcing the importance of literacy and education.

School officials said family participation in classroom activities sends a powerful message to children about the value of reading, education and lifelong learning.

Harper Elementary expressed its appreciation to everyone who participated, recognizing the time, enthusiasm and support families showed through the special activity.

The school emphasizes that family involvement plays an important role in student success and thanked its families for partnering with educators to create meaningful learning experiences.

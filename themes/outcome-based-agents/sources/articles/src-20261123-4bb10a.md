BURLINGTON, Vt. (WCAX) - Another resort has joined the growing list of Vermont ski areas delaying their opening days: Sugarbush.
Sugarbush had hoped to open on Black Friday or Saturday, but said Thursday that despite snowmaking efforts, they don’t have enough terrain covered to open. They did not set a new target date.
And Sugarbush is not alone; Smugglers’ Notch, Bromley Mountain Ski Resort, Stratton Mountain and Bolton Valley have also all postponed their opening days.
Smugglers’, Bromley and Bolton all now hope to open next weekend. Stratton has not yet set a date.

Jay Peak will kick things off Friday with its annual Day for the Devoted, with the slopes reserved for season passholders, employees and some resort guests. Starting Saturday, the mountain will be open to the public.
Okemo will open Saturday, too, along with the Stowe Mountain Resort.
Stowe had initially hoped to open this past Saturday, but warm weather changed those plans. Now, the resort plans to open Saturday and Sunday. They will close after that to keep making snow and then reopen on Friday, Dec. 4.

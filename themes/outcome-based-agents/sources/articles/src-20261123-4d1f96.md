GARDEN CITY
 – The 200th all-time meeting with Garden City turned out to be a signature victory for the No. 23-ranked Hutchinson Community College men’s basketball team.

Sophomore 
Chudier Dorar
 scored a career-high 29 points and the Blue Dragons earned their first KJCCC victory of the season after a 96-85 victory over the 14th-ranked Garden City Broncbusters on Saturday at Conestoga Arena.

The Blue Dragons (7-1, 1-1 KJCCC) earned their first victory over a ranked opponent of the season. Hutchinson will play its third-straight KJCCC road game to start the conference season at 7:30 p.m. on Tuesday at Cowley in Arkansas City. The Busters (7-1, 1-1 KJCCC) suffered their first loss of the season.

Hutchinson snapped a three-game losing streak to Garden City.

Trailing tied at 81 with 4:29 to play, a Dorar dunk sparked a 15-4 run to close the game.

Dorar was 12 of 17 shooting overall and was 5 of 7 from the free-throw line. Dorar also added nine rebounds and four of Hutchinson’s 11 blocked shots.

Evan Williams posted his first double-double as a Blue Dragon with season highs of 16 points and 10 rebounds.

Mykel Jenkins
 finished with 13 points and five assists. 
Kordell Williams
 had 10 points, seven rebounds and six assists. 
Micah Young
 added 10 points and five rebounds.

The Blue Dragons shot 52.2 percent (36 of 69), hitting 4 of 11 from 3-point range and 20 of 23 from the free-throw line. The Blue Dragons out-rebounded Garden City 41-25 and had 24 assists on 36 made baskets.

Held 22 points under its season-scoring average, Garden City shot 39.3 percent overall (33 of 84), hitting 11 of 33 from 3-point range and 8 of 10 from the free-throw line. Makkiah Sanders led the Busters with 16 points. Mangok Gach and Kayden Gray had 15 points each.

The game featured 18 lead changes and 14 ties.

The Dragons’ largest deficit was only five points, which happened once in each half. The Blue Dragons trailed 30-28 and went on a 9-0 run, which was capped by a Williams layup to give Hutchinson a 37-30 lead with 6:15 to play in the first half. Garden City then answered with an 8-1 run to tie the game at 38.

Tied at 43, Jackson scored after grabbing an offensive rebound with 2:12 to go. Jackson’s free throws with 1:54 to go gave the Dragons a 47-43 lead. Young hit a pair of free throws with 36 seconds to play for a 51-48 lead, but Garden City’s Gach scored with 16 seconds to play to give the Dragons a 51-50 lead at halftime.

Garden City built a 68-63 lead with 10:21 to play in the second half. The Blue Dragons scored seven unanswered points and took a 70-68 lead on a Jackson field goal with 8:05 to play.

From that point, the Dragons had three more three-point leads, but Garden City tied the game in each instance with a 3-pointer.

Tied at 81, Dorar’s dunk with 4:07 left sparked the game-clinching 15-4 Blue Dragon run. Dorar had two more dunks and Williams added a bucket in the paint to build an 89-81 lead with 2:39 to go. The Blue Dragons held Garden City to 2 of 7 shooting and forcing five turnovers in the final 4:07.

GAME NOTES
 – The Blue Dragons are now 1-1 against ranked opponents this season. … The Blue Dragons are now 146-54 all-time against Garden City. … This was all-time win No. 1,963. … Now with 94 career blocked shots, 
Chudier Dorar
 moves into 11th on the Blue Dragon career blocked shots list. … This was Hutchinson’s fifth game to shoot 50 percent or better this season. … The Dragons reached the 90-point mark for the sixth time this season.

Saviynt Launches EU Identity Security Offering on French S3NS's Trusted Cloud
 
 Cloud
 
 23.11.2026 
 
 12:20 pm 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Saviynt
, the leader in identity security for AI, today announced the availability of its cloud-native Identity Security solution in the European Union on PREMI3NS, S3NS’s Trusted Cloud offering qualified by the French National Cybersecurity Agency (ANSSI). This deployment brings Saviynt’s identity security platform to a locally operated, trusted cloud environment tailored for European government agencies, critical infrastructure providers, and regulated enterprises. Thales, as a trusted partner, will operate and support the SaaS service with dedicated EU-based personnel.
Across Europe, digital sovereignty is becoming a vital consideration as governments and enterprises seek greater control over critical data, technology, and infrastructure, while strengthening resilience and reducing strategic dependencies.
Saviynt’s new EU offering is designed to address these stringent requirements by deploying its modern identity security capabilities on S3NS's SecNumCloud-qualified infrastructure, with operational support provided by Thales as a sovereign trusted third party. Operated and supported exclusively by EU residents employed by S3NS, the solution runs on PREMI3NS in an environment separated from Saviynt’s global commercial cloud, ensuring local control over data, infrastructure, and operations. The solution delivers Saviynt’s modern, cloud-native, and AI-enabled identity security capabilities for organisations operating in highly sensitive environments.
“Saviynt does not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach to sovereignty. Our EU Sovereign Identity Security Cloud complements our data residency and private cloud offerings, extending our portfolio with a modern, cloud-native, AI-enabled SaaS identity security solution. It is designed to help government agencies, regulated enterprises, and critical infrastructure providers protect their critical assets and systems while meeting stringent regulatory and sovereignty requirements,” said Niraj Gopal, Vice President of Product Management, Saviynt.
“European organisations should not have to choose between sovereignty and innovation,” said Simon Gooch, Field CIO, Saviynt. “With this offering, we are extending our cloud-native identity security platform to organisations that require complete sovereignty over their identity environments. By combining local control with operational independence, we are helping European organisations protect critical access, strengthen resilience and modernise identity security without compromising their sovereignty requirements.”
“By hosting Saviynt’s identity security platform on S3NS’s SecNumCloud-qualified offering, PREMI3NS, we provide a trusted environment where organisations can modernise their security capabilities while maintaining operational control over their most sensitive data and critical operations,” said Estelle Samwells, Chief Sales Officer at S3NS.
"European sovereignty requirements extend beyond where data is hosted to include how cloud services are operated. Through this major partnership, Thales’s expertise in secure European operations and regulatory environments complements Saviynt’s identity security platform, helping government agencies and regulated enterprises strengthen security while maintaining greater operational control. This partnership also reinforces our vision of digital trust, where identity serves as a foundation for securing critical systems, data and emerging AI-powered environments," said Christophe Bianco, VP, Thales Cybersecurity Services.
By operating independently from Saviynt’s global commercial cloud, organisations can maintain critical identity security operations while reducing reliance on external infrastructure and services.
Organisations interested in learning more should contact their local Saviynt sales representative.

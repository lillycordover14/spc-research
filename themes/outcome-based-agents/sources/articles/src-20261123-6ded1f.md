ElevenLabs Buys Japanese Voice AI Startup for USD 100M&nbsp;&nbsp;Slator

[page text]
ElevenLabs
 has 
agreed
 to acquire Kotoba Technologies, a developer of Japanese-focused speech models, in a deal expected to close before the end of the year. ElevenLabs is acquiring the startup for more than USD 100m, 
according to Japanese media reports
.

Kotoba was founded in May 2023 and has its headquarters in San Francisco, with research and development and most of its staff based in Tokyo. Its 15 employees will join ElevenLabs. 
Kotoba said
 this is ElevenLabs’ first acquisition in Japan.

Kotoba has developed the Koto voice model, which supports speech-to-speech translation, in addition to speech recognition and speech synthesis, with Japanese, Korean, and Chinese as particular strengths. Since August 7, 2026, Koto has handled Japanese speech recognition and synthesis in 
Salesforce’s Agentforce Voice
, which runs autonomous customer conversations by phone and inside websites and apps. Salesforce Ventures has invested in both Kotoba and ElevenLabs.

Nikkei Asia linked the acquisition to faster overseas distribution of Japanese content, including anime. Kotoba had already named media localization among the use cases driving demand for direct access to its models, which puts ElevenLabs closer to work currently handled by dubbing providers serving Japanese rights holders.

For enterprise buyers, ElevenLabs said it will expand its local sales team and invest further in Japanese infrastructure over the coming months, targeting Japanese companies and multinationals operating in Japan.

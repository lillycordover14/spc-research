A Kansas City, Missouri, man has been charged with murder after another man was shot and killed outside an apartment complex in Clay County on Sunday morning. 
Charles Johnson is charged with second-degree murder and armed criminal action. 
Advertisement
Court documents say Kansas City police responded to a disturbance in the 8400 block of North Boon Avenue shortly after midnight on Sunday. 
Police officers on scene found a man lying in the grass with a gunshot wound. He later died from those injuries at the hospital. 
A woman told police she was inside her apartment with the victim when Johnson allegedly entered and pointed a gun at her. 
Johnson is accused of firing his gun and striking the victim. She later told police she heard three gunshots after the victim climbed through a window and began running. 
She told police she saw the defendant leave the scene in a light grey Infinity with a "Long Live King Tutt" custom sticker. 
A $1 million bond has been set.

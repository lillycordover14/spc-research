MURRAY COUNTY, Okla. (KOKH)  — 
An Ada man has died after hitting a deer on an Oklahoma highway.
Shortly before 5 a.m. on Nov. 17, emergency crews were called to a crash along Hwy 1 in Murray County.
Investigators say 66-year-old James Johnson was driving along Hwy 1 when his vehicle hit a deer in the road. 
As a result of the impact, Johnson's vehicle left the roadway to the right and he overcorrected, which caused it to roll.
Troopers with the Oklahoma Highway Patrol say Johnson was rushed to OU Medical Center but later died from his injuries.

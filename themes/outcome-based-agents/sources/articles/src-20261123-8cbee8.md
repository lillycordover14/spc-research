This past September, we reported on the perhaps unsurprising but still sad news that PvPvE extraction shooter 
Synduality: Echo of Ada
 would be 
going offline next year
 and transitioning instead into a single-player offline title. And while that technically means there won’t be any major news out of the game after its online sunset, there are still some 
questions being answered by the devs
 about how it will change mechanically when it does so.

Generally speaking, the game’s story will progress as normal in the offline version, while being defeated in the game will not allow players to gather up their dropped items. The FAQ did further confirm that various items offered during limited-time events and certain materials will be woven into offline game rewards, and alternate ways to earn cash shop cosmetics will be added.

Other points in the FAQ of note include confirmation of balance adjustments that will be made to gameplay overall in order to account for the shift to full-on PvE, word that there will be no follow-up balance or bugfixing patches once the offline game is released, and a reminder that those who do want to start over will lose any progress earned from the online version, while progression and content for starting over hasn’t been tied down yet. 
Check the FAQ
 for some more details.

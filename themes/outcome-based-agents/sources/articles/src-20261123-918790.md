November is Winter Weather Preparedness Month, and the Grundy County Emergency Management Agency is reminding residents to prepare their vehicles before winter weather arrives.
Officials encourage motorists to winterize their vehicles, make sure they are in good working condition and carry an emergency travel safety kit. Recommended supplies include blankets, flashlights, batteries, a first aid kit, bottled water, nonperishable food, jumper cables, extra clothing, an ice scraper, a small shovel, rope, sand or kitty litter and a cellphone charger.

Motorists should also keep their gas tanks full and monitor the latest weather forecasts, warnings and advisories through NOAA Weather Radio, local radio stations or television. The Grundy County EMA reminds drivers to remain alert to changing road conditions and avoid unnecessary travel during hazardous winter weather.

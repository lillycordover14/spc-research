Cresta has just been acquired by Salesforce for $2.7 billion. In France, only one specialist offers something similar to Cresta: integrating AI into customer journeys, notably with voice agents, while committing to results. That company is comete.ai. Will Frédéric Donati and his partners, all veterans of BPO and digital customer experience, manage to build comete.ai's value as successfully? Either way, what they've managed to transform at ELM Leblanc is worth a closer look.
A company that helps another 
simplify its processes and customer journeys
, while improving the experience for both customers and employees, has real value. Salesforce paid roughly 50 times Cresta's revenue. 
comete.ai
's future value could therefore prove significant if its growth curve holds.
Recently, at a boiler manufacturer, the approach worked well. Jacky Dufier, head of after-sales service at Bosch and ELM Leblanc, confirms it. One very strong argument wins over prospects: comete.ai commits to measurable results at the end of its engagements. Its co-founder, Frédéric Donati, describes the business in simple terms: "an orchestrator of BPO solutions that improve customer journeys by blending AI technology and human intervention."
Jacky Dufier
Context and challenges
For ELM Leblanc (Bosch Group), winter activity peaks tied to boiler maintenance and after-sales service are a major challenge: absorbing large call volumes, limiting wait times, and delivering a quality customer experience while keeping operating costs under control.
comete.ai rethought the operating setup to simplify access to appointments, smooth the customer journey, and improve the quality of care. Through technology and artificial intelligence, this approach combines customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and cost control.
The overall approach
comete.ai supported ELM Leblanc at every stage of the project, from the strategic audit to custom technical integration and the rollout of a hybrid setup combining artificial intelligence and human expertise.
The teams analyzed customer journeys, assessed quality indicators, and audited the IT architecture, telephony, and CRM to identify areas for optimization. This led to the design and integration of a set of complementary solutions: an AI voice bot, process automation, automatic CRM and calendar entry, satisfaction surveys, and real-time multichannel information.
Beyond the technical deployment, comete.ai handled the integration of existing APIs, AI training, testing, and production rollout. Ongoing support also adapts the setup to changes in processes and the technical environment, while ensuring coordinated management of human and digital operations.
comete.ai's founders © Edouard Jacquinet
A smart, high-performing, and secure solution
A fluid, autonomous voice bot: comete.ai designed an automated voice journey that handles requests end to end: customer identification (contract number or name, address, email, phone number), verification of contractual entitlements, real-time booking on technicians' calendars via API, and instant appointment confirmation by text message.
A robust, secure architecture: the system runs on a secure technical and AI infrastructure, including a dual LLM setup to ensure high availability, service continuity, and secure exchanges.
A hybrid organization combining AI, technology, and human expertise: business rules automatically route requests to the most appropriate solution. AI bots and digital processes handle recurring requests, while expert advisors step in for complex situations, complaints, and specific needs.
Everything is managed from a real-time dashboard that gives a consolidated view of both human and technological performance.
The Bot Academy
's expertise: a true pillar of conversational performance, the Bot Academy turns journeys originally designed for human agents into scenarios adapted to automated interactions.
Its teams work on how questions are phrased, the consistency of answers, and the optimization of the identification and customer-reference collection steps, to make exchanges as smooth and natural as possible. They also provide ongoing coaching of the bots through prompt improvements, continuous analysis of conversations, and precise adjustments to scenarios. This continuous improvement process strengthens the understanding of requests and the relevance of answers, for an ever more effective voice experience.
Key impacts and results
The setup deployed for ELM Leblanc combines accessibility, autonomy, and customer satisfaction:
100% answer rate: no call goes unanswered.
Up to 80% of requests handled autonomously by the voice bot, with the remaining 20% transferred to ELM advisors for appropriate handling.
Over 90% customer satisfaction (CSAT): an experience built on immediate handling, available 24/7, and tailored to each customer's needs.
Jacky Dufier, head of after-sales service at Bosch ELM Leblanc, is a happy man. He is due to retire soon, and he is at ease: "I've known Frédéric Donati since he ran Konecta in France, formerly Comdata. He's an engineer, but also someone who will sit down with a customer service rep, put on a headset, and listen in on a call. So his recommendations are very practical. And his team has vastly improved how the AI voice agents work since they went live. To the point that when a caller now reaches our contact center, he starts the conversation by saying, 'I was just on the line with Ms. X a few moments ago.' The voice has come so far, and the response times and personalization are such, that callers are convinced they're talking to a human being. It's mind-blowing!"

Bella Hadid opened up about her health and diagnoses in the new issue

“I have POTS [postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome], and I have a lot of PMDD [premenstrual dysphoric disorder], endometriosis, plus my Lyme and things that affect my body very deeply,” 
Bella 
explained. 

She then revealed what happened on the Victoria’s Secret runway: “Right before I walked out on the [Victoria’s Secret] runway for my opening look, I went to go stretch and my whole clavicle completely … I don’t even know what it was. My lungs almost collapsed, and I couldn’t breathe, and then it was like, ‘Five, four …’ So my body goes through these different motions of feeling really good and then all of a sudden crashing on me at the worst moments.” 

She continued, “I need to realize maybe my body can’t handle all that I have going on. Because I know how to walk. One thing I can do is I can strut. But it’s a lot of pressure; we all have our own insecurities. Sometimes my body just can’t connect with my brain. My brain wants to do it, and my body is like, ‘No, we can’t do that today.’ And I’m like, ‘Okay, great. So let’s figure out another way.’ But I think people don’t realize how much I struggle with the fatigue and the body pain, and so I just try my best.”

2

She also called out a stylist

“When I was 17, going to my first go-see with a stylist I will not name, I was basically told I was too big to wear Saint Laurent and needed to lose a couple of pounds. And that’s when I was just a baby coming out of high school. Twelve years later, now I’m the face of Saint Laurent. I’m the face of Prada, which is incredible and insane,” she shared. 

She continued, “Times have changed so much, and the people that have come into the industry to make it a better place for us as models are so important. Not that that comment ever impacted me. I went through enough struggles with my body before that, so by the time she said that, I was like, ‘Okay, girl, I’m actually coming out of something that I dealt with very heavily when it came to my body. And now I look at food in a different way, and I love my body, and that’s okay if you think that I don’t look the way that you think I should look.’”

This week, 
CALICO
 is the New Music Monday highlight!

CALICO is a four-piece folk, Americana and indie roots rock band with members based in both Victoria and on the mainland.

The band is comprised of Tony Cecchetti on rhythm guitar and lead vocals, Chris Couto on drums and vocals, Will Lloyd and JeanSe Le Doujet. 

Cecchetti referred to their genesis as a band as a pandemic-fuelled project, as they came together in 2020 as a way to navigate the feelings of isolation. 

After 15 years of songwriting, he and Couto decided to put together a band—even if it would begin in a virtual jam space. 

“When the world shut down, we did Zoom calls and decided this is the time to co-create and this is the time—when we have some time—to take all these songs I’ve written and see what we can do with them,” Cecchetti told Victoria Buzz. 

“It was virtual to start—so virtual that we had been a band together for two years before we actually played our first live show.”

They released their first album 
Under a Hidden Sun 
in the summer of 2021 and have since released three more albums, all the while navigating living in different cities along BC’s coast. 

Their latest record, 
Desolation Sound,
 which was released on November 3rd, has gotten them a significant amount of attention in the short time it’s been out. 

It earned CALICO a Western Canadian Music Award nomination for Roots Artist of the Year. They were nominated alongside several heavy hitters in the Canadian roots scene. 

“It’s by far the biggest award yet, and we were nominated alongside the likes of William Prince and Boy Golden and other Juno-nominated artists who are well on their way,” said Cecchetti. 

“That is what has really jump-started the project.”

Despite also being newcomers to the Western Canadian Music Awards, fellow Vancouver Island-based act The Southern Residents ended up winning the Roots Artist of the Year award for 2026. 

“We didn’t win, but it was a win just to be nominated,” Cecchetti continued. “It puts us in a different conversation now.”

On the new album, 
Desolation Sound
, Cecchetti says that the songs are all connected by themes of connection to the natural world, love and grief. 

He noted that the title track of the album came from a friend’s experience losing their father and going on a long fishing trip while grieving his loss. 

“He said it was the first time since his dad had passed that he felt this incredible closeness in proximity to him,” Cecchetti explained. 

“It shaped this vision of the body of work, it shaped the vision for the song itself and its all talking about kind of the connection between these two worlds—the world that we walk and live on and the spirit world.”

He said this idea is what binds the album together, though each song has its own meaning and theme. 

Highlight track – “Blue Rose”

“Blue Rose” was the second single to be released off of 
Desolation Sound.

Cecchetti said this one holds a special place in his heart because it feels a bit different sonically and was written about his own experiences growing up. 

“It’s a song about hope,” he explained. 

“I grew up in California and was raised by two women, I have two moms, and so a lot of the verses in that are like, ‘oh my mothers, did you read the news today, did you walk along the side streets and back again.’”

He noted that his experiences, those of his mothers’ and seeing the state of the United States today inspired him to write the song. 

“The idea behind ‘Blue Rose’ is that there is hope and there always is hope, it’s just sometimes we got to look for it and sometimes it’s there and we don’t even know it,” he continued. 

Cecchetti says that the two singles, “Desolation Sound” and “Blue Rose” are two really good indicators of what the new album has to offer in how dynamic their sound is.

Currently, CALICO doesn’t have any confirmed upcoming shows, but Cecchetti says they are working on lining up some festival performances for the coming summer and hopes to play in Victoria soon. 

Aside from festivals, he says the band is tentatively planning to do a west coast tour of sorts in the new year. 

Additionally, CALICO will be performing live on CFUV’s Basement Sessions show in the coming months, though the exact date is still to be determined.

CFUV
 is a non-profit campus and community radio station that plays a ton of local music of all kinds across Vancouver Island. If you like to support local music they are an amazing resource with a plethora of new local tunes in their arsenal. 

“[I want to thank CFUV] for creating a space for us as artists to share our art, the meaning of our art and getting to share that with wider audiences,” said Cecchetti. 

“No matter how many or who listens to the music, it’s something that gives artists a place to share and sometimes that’s the hardest thing to find.”

[page text]
Key Takeaways:
All 3 KT-621 doses beat placebo in the phase 2b BROADEN2 trial, and the mid dose produced the largest gains.
Lio views KT-621's efficacy as broadly comparable to injectable IL-4/IL-13 biologics, though cross-trial comparisons cannot establish superiority.
He attributes the mid-dose advantage partly to small sample sizes and the drug's catalyst-like mechanism.

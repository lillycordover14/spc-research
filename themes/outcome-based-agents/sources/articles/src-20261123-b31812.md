KANSAS CITY, Mo.
 —
The North Kansas City Mistletowne Market is underway, featuring 24 artisan booths and marking the start of the holiday season.
Lucy Erb, owner of Luna's Fairy Emporium, shared how she prepares for the event.
Advertisement
"I usually take some notes even in January with what went well the year before," Erb said. "As soon as I start seeing it in the stores, which is like July, then I start being like, what should I make?"
Erb expressed her excitement about the festive atmosphere.
"I love being able to be part of such a festive Christmas festival," she said.
Heather Garrett, co-director of the event, explained the variety of participants.
"Some of these artists are from right here in North Kansas City, others come in from surrounding areas," Garrett said.
One of the returning vendors, Kate Taylor of Matchbox Kansas City, creates matchbook displays.
"I think it's a good combination of, you know, Thanksgiving and Christmas vibes, so everybody seems to be having a great time," Taylor said.
The two-day event is seen as the official start of the holiday season in North Kansas City.
"Because next up is our Mayor's Christmas tree lighting," Garrett said.

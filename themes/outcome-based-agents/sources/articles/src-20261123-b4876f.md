Senadores, Criollos, Leones notch victories in LBPRC&nbsp;&nbsp;San Juan Daily Star

[page text]
San Juan starter Ryder Ryan earned his first win of the season on Saturday night after pitching five and one-third innings, allowing two runs on four hits and striking out three in a 7-3 win over Santurce at Hiram Bithorn Stadium.
By THE STAR STAFF
The Senadores of San Juan, Criollos of Caguas and Leones of Ponce were winners on Saturday night in a trio of Roberto Clemente Professional Baseball League (LBPRC by its initials in Spanish) games.
At Hiram Bithorn Stadium in Hato Rey, the Senadores defeated the Cangrejeros of Santurce, 7-3.
The Senadores (4-9) benefited from a strong outing by starting pitcher Ryder Ryan. The right-hander earned his first win of the season, bringing his record to 1-1, after pitching five and one-third innings and allowing two runs. He also gave up four hits, issued two walks and recorded three strikeouts.
Ryan was supported by a potent offensive performance from Ramón Rodríguez (3-for-5) and Randal Díaz (2-for-3).
A three-run home run by Matt McDermott extended the Senadores’ lead to 6-0 in the sixth inning. It was the first hit of the season for the San Juan leadoff man, coming in his fifth game.
After an RBI single by Yariel González made it 7-0, Brian Navarreto hit a two-run homer in the bottom half of the inning to put the Cangrejeros (7-8) on the board. An RBI double by Santurce’s Brian Rey in the seventh completed the scoring.
The loss went to Santurce starter Eduardo Rivera (0-1), who allowed two runs over three and one-third innings, during which he issued four walks and recorded three strikeouts.
Meanwhile, the Criollos defeated the Gigantes of Carolina 6-5 at Estadio Yldefonso Solá Morales Stadium in Caguas.
With the win, Caguas (8-5) snapped a three-game losing streak to reclaim second place in the league standings. Carolina (7-6) dropped to third place.
A total of four home runs were hit in the game, two by each team.
Leading 4-3, Caguas loaded the bases against relief pitcher Gabriel Cotto in the fifth inning, before he was replaced on the mound by Matt Givin. The right-hander began by issuing a walk to José Miranda, which brought in the Criollos’ fifth run. Vimael Machín then singled to right field, extending the Caguas lead to 6-3.
A second home run by Adrián Rodríguez, with Miguel Pabón on base, brought the Gigantes within 6-5 in the sixth inning for what would be the final tally.
Left-hander Jorge Benítez (2-0) earned the win after pitching a scoreless inning in relief. José Espada recorded his third save of the season. Right-hander Kaito Ushino (1-2) was charged with the loss, having allowed five runs over four innings. 
In Mayagüez, José Torres and Colton Becker each hit a home run to lead the Leones to an 8-2 victory over the Indios at their home field, Francisco “Paquito” Montaner Stadium.
It was the third-straight win for first-place Ponce (9-5). Fifth-place Mayagüez dropped to 6-8.
The win went to Ponce left-hander Jorhan Laboy (2-0), who allowed two runs in one inning of relief work. Starter Ashton Goudeau (0-1) was tagged with the loss, as three of the Leones’ runs were charged to him during his four innings.

Bella Hadid Just Wants to Settle Down in a Small Town With “a Man That I Love” and Have “Lots of Children”&nbsp;&nbsp;Harper's BAZAAR

[page text]
Karim Sadli
2 min read
Bella Hadid may be the face of Saint Laurent and Prada, and she may own every high-profile runway she’s on, but her long-term life goal doesn’t involve spotlights or mega fame. In her new 
cover story for 
Harper’s Bazaar
, the supermodel opens up like never before about what she sees for her future.
“From an external space, success is about how known you are, how much you make, how many times you can be on TV, how many covers you’re on, how many fashion shows you do. And that is really important in our industry, especially when you’re building,” she notes. “But for me, I am different.”
Karim Sadli
While she feels endlessly lucky for her place in the fashion industry, and admits that having 
opened the Victoria’s Secret fashion show
 last month was a dream come true, she yearns for an easy life surrounded by family and nature.
“Success, for me, can be living in peace, in a small town, with a man that I love, with my child, having my animals,” Hadid says.
Karim Sadli
A cowgirl at heart, the Orebella founder has down-to-earth goals that do not necessarily involve modeling, but do involve having a small local business, leaning into hobbies that bring her peace, and becoming a wife and mother.
“I’ve always wanted to start a farm-to-table bakery, with flowers, and everything comes from the community. I want to do things that are fulfilling for me in my soul: having a greenhouse that I can garden in, having my glassblowing and ceramic studio, and having lots of children and being a mother,” she says. “It wasn’t a dream that I’ve always had, but it’s something that now is so intense for me. My intuition is screaming so loudly at me about it.”
We love a happy Bella.

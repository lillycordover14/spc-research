Doppio Zero, Ranked No. 20 Among World's Artisan Pizza Chains, Opens In Livermore
The Southern Italian restaurant chain specializing in Neapolitan, wood-fired pizza celebrated a grand opening in Blacksmith Square. 
Michael Wittner
, 
Patch Staff
Posted 
Mon, Nov  23, 2026 at 11:46 am PT
CA Restaurants
LIVERMORE, CA — Doppio Zero, a restaurant specializing in Southern Italian cuisine and Neapolitan pizza, celebrated the grand opening of its new Livermore location at 23 South Livermore Avenue last Tuesday. 
“Get ready for authentic Neapolitan pizzas, delicious Southern Italian cuisine, and a little taste of Italy right in your neighborhood,” Doppio Zero said in an Instagram post. The grand opening featured a ribbon cutting followed by live music, pizza, pasta, and wine samples. 
 
Doppio Zero was founded by two Italian-born restaurateurs and first opened in Mountain View in 2014, according to 
50toppizza.it
, a site that awarded it 20th place in its list of the 50 Top World Artisan Pizza Chains for 2025. The name “Doppio Zero” is Italian “double zero,” a reference to the 00 Flour that it uses to make its pizza dough and fresh pasta. The restaurant has been certified for making “Vera Pizza Napoletana”, made from the most refined 00 Flour, and cooked in a wood-fired dome operating at 900°F.

PONTOTOC COUNTY, Okla. (KXII) - A pursuit that went through Ada Monday morning ended with a crash. 
According to the Pontotoc County Sheriff’s Office, the pursuit, which was initiated by Chickasaw Lighthorse Police, made its way through town and then out to Highway 1, towards Roff. 
According to The Chickasaw Lighthorse Police Department, the pursuit started after a traffic stop. Police said the driver, Luke Tatum, 45, drove off after officers found out Tatum had outstanding warrants.
During the pursuit, police said Tatum’s vehicle struck another one near Eighth and Beard Street. 
A Lighthorse officer used a tactical vehicle intervention mauver to stop Tatum, which led to the crash near Highway 1 and County Road 1580. Tatum was arrested and taken to a local hospital to be evaluated. 
Charges will be presented to the Office of Tribal Justice Administration. 
The officer who crashed was hurt. No word on what led to the pursuit.

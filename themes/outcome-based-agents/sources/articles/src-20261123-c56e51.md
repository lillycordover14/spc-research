Hostinger Managed apps: How to set up and manage n8n&nbsp;&nbsp;Hostinger

[page text]
n8n in Hostinger Managed apps runs n8n, a workflow automation platform with a visual node-based editor, on its own dedicated microVM managed by Hostinger. You choose between n8n and n8n with AI Assistant during setup and connect Hostinger AI Router. After setup, you open n8n and create your owner account to start building workflows.
Before you start

You need a Hostinger Managed apps plan. See 
Hostinger Managed apps: What it is and how to get started
 for plans and how to add an app.

If your n8n app is listed under 
AI agents
 → 
n8n
, follow 
Hostinger Managed n8n: Overview and setup
 instead.

How to set up n8n in Hostinger Managed apps
Step 1 – Start the setup

In the Hostinger dashboard, go to 
Managed apps
 in the left sidebar.

Click 
Set up
 next to a plan with the status 
Waiting for setup
, and select 
n8n
. To buy a new plan for n8n, click 
Get new app
 instead. After you pay, the n8n setup starts right away.

Step 2 – Choose n8n or n8n with AI Assistant
Click 
Select
 under the n8n version you want:

n8n
: the workflow automation platform with a visual node-based interface.

n8n with AI Assistant
: n8n with an AI assistant that builds and debugs workflows for you.

Step 3 – Set up Hostinger AI Router
Select the 
AI Router API key
 that powers n8n, or click 
New key
 to create one. Hostinger AI Router uses your Hostinger credits, and you can change the key later.
Click 
Continue
, or 
Skip for now
 to set it up later. n8n setup starts and takes a few minutes. You can leave the page, and the setup will keep going.
Step 4 – Create your n8n owner account

When setup finishes, click 
Open app
.

On the 
Set up owner account
 page, enter your email, first name, last name, and a password with at least 8 characters, one number, and one capital letter.

Click 
Next
.

The n8n 
Overview
 page opens, where you can create and manage workflows, credentials, and executions.
How to manage n8n in Hostinger Managed apps
Go to 
Managed apps
 and click 
Manage
 next to your n8n app to open its 
Overview
 page. It shows the usage tiles, 
IP address
, 
SSH access
, 
n8n version
, plan, and expiration date. You log in to n8n with the owner account you created, so the Overview page shows no n8n login details.
To connect with SSH or the Web console, or to stop, restart, or delete n8n, see 
Hostinger Managed apps: What it is and how to get started
.
n8n environment variables in Hostinger Managed apps
Hostinger creates the n8n environment variables during setup, including 
N8N_ENCRYPTION_KEY
, which n8n uses to encrypt your saved credentials, 
N8N_POSTGRES_PASSWORD
 for the n8n database, 
N8N_PUBLIC_HOST
 for your app’s address, and 
AI_ROUTER_API_KEY
 and 
AI_ROUTER_BASE_URL
 for Hostinger AI Router. To view, add, or edit variables, select 
Environment
 in the left menu. See 
Hostinger Managed apps: What it is and how to get started
 for the steps.

Changing 
N8N_ENCRYPTION_KEY
 can make your saved n8n credentials unreadable, and changing 
N8N_POSTGRES_PASSWORD
 can disconnect n8n from its database.

How to change the Hostinger AI Router key for n8n in Hostinger Managed apps
n8n in Hostinger Managed apps uses Hostinger AI Router as its AI provider.

On your n8n app’s page, select 
AI provider
 in the left menu.

Select a different 
AI Router API key
, or click 
New key
.

Click 
Save changes
.

Click 
Top up
 to add Hostinger credits. See 
What is Hostinger AI Router and how to get started
 for details.
Your n8n app is now set up in Hostinger Managed apps. Log in with your owner account to build workflows, and return to the Hostinger dashboard to check usage or change the AI Router key.

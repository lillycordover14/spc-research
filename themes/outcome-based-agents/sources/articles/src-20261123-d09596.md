Dover, DE
 — A pair of Ron Burke-trained millionaires — Al Papi and Melillo — have been made the morning-line favorites in Wednesday’s (Nov. 25) $223,960 Grade 2 Progress Pace, for 3-year-old male pacers, at Bally’s Dover.

Al Papi counts the Adios among his wins this season. Chris Gooden photo.

Al Papi, who set the pace in the Grade 2 Hap Hansen Matron on Nov. 12 before Odds On Mr Mamba overpowered him late for a 1:49.3 victory, has been installed the 2-1 morning-line favorite from the pole. The 
Papi Rob Hanover
-Baron Remy colt has won six of 19 races this season and will retain regular driver Yannick Gingras. His stablemate Melillo will start from post 5 as the 5-2 second choice, with Todd McCarthy taking over the driving duties of the 
Sweet Lou
-Rosemary Rose colt from Ronnie Wrenn Jr. after a fourth-place effort in the Matron.

Odds On Mr Mamba and driver Dexter Dunn will start from post 2 as the 7-2 third choice following their win in the Matron. The 
Odds On Equuleus
-Honky Tonk Woman gelding is one of two other millionaires in the field, along with Fragment (Jason Bartlett, post 3, 9-2).

The complete Progress Pace field, with drivers, trainers and morning-line odds, is as follows:

$223,960 Grade 2 Progress Pace – 3-Year-Old Colts and Geldings

Al Papi (Yannick Gingras, Ron Burke) 2-1

Odds On Mr Mamba (Dexter Dunn, Melanie Wrenn) 7-2

Fragment (Jason Bartlett, Petter Engblom) 9-2

Highland B Best (Troy Beyer, Katie Beyer) 6-1

Melillo (Todd McCarthy, Ron Burke) 5-2

Show Me Your Ace (Patrick Ryder, Chris Ryder) 10-1

Toast Of The Town (Tim Tetrick, Noel Daley) 8-1

The Progress is carded as the 10th of 15 races on the Wednesday program at Bally’s Dover. The program also features the $110,000 Ramona Hubbard Delaware Standardbred Breeders’ Fund series final for 2-year-old pacing fillies, carded as race 6. First post is 4:30 p.m. (EST).

Iconic retailer partners with Zip to capture untapped consumer demand and drive incremental revenue ahead of the holiday shopping season.
 
 
NEW YORK, November 23, 2026
--(
BUSINESS WIRE
)--Zip Co Limited (ASX: ZIP), the digital financial services company offering innovative, people-centered products, today announced a strategic partnership with Lord & Taylor as the retailer returns to market. By adding Zip alongside its existing payment options, Lord & Taylor aims to close gaps in its payment offerings, reach untapped customer segments, and unlock new revenue opportunities ahead of the holiday shopping season.
 
 
 
 
 
"Lord & Taylor has always been about meeting our customers where they are. As we write the next chapter of this iconic brand, giving shoppers more ways to pay is part of making luxury feel accessible and welcoming again. Zip helps us reach a new generation of customers just in time for the holidays," said Sina Yenel, Chief Strategy & Brand Officer at Lord & Taylor.
 
 
The partnership reflects a critical shift in retail strategy: having flexible payments isn't enough if your payment stack isn't reaching every potential eligible customer. By integrating Zip, Lord & Taylor is expanding beyond its existing payment solutions to engage shoppers who prefer Zip, capture incremental demand, and turn missed opportunities into sales.
 
 
"Having flexible payment options isn't enough if you're still missing potential customers," said Peter Volynsky, Chief Commercial Officer at Zip US. "By adding Zip, retailers like Lord & Taylor can reach more shoppers, capture incremental demand, and turn more opportunities into sales. That's how a smarter payment strategy drives sustainable growth."
 
 
 
 
 
As Black Friday approaches, the partnership positions Lord & Taylor to maximize its holiday sales potential while building a stronger foundation for long-term customer acquisition. The message is clear that the right payment strategy doesn't just give customers more ways to pay, it gives retailers more customers to reach.
 
 
With more than 32,600 US merchant partners and 4.7 million active US customers, Zip connects everyday Americans to flexible payment options wherever they need them most.¹ The Lord & Taylor partnership further expands Zip's presence in the retail space as demand grows for flexible payment solutions.
 
 
¹Zip's Q1 FY27 results released to the ASX

BEND, Ore. -- More than a decade after a climbing accident left him paralyzed from the waist down, a Bend man is working to overcome another obstacle: replacing the expensive and now stolen adaptive hand cycle that helped him reclaim an active outdoor life.
Josh Hancock has 
started a GoFundMe
 to help him buy a new hand cycle. He said his old cycle was stolen out of his garage.
Insurance is covering $11,000, but he needs another $
15,739
 to pay for the rest and GoFundMe fees. As of Monday, Nov. 23, he had raised nearly $7,000.
Hancock was paralyzed in a 2014 ice climbing accident in Washington state. In his GoFundMe post, he said a hand cycle brought him back to the outdoor adventures he loves. He's even been featured in the film "Adapted," which follows three Bend athletes with disabilities as they pursue extraordinary expeditions.

By November 20, the S&P 500 had slipped about 11.6% from its 52-week high, and chip stocks took the hardest hit, with the PHLX Semiconductor Index down roughly 36% from its own peak. Yet the profits underneath held up. S&P 500 earnings grew by about 30% in the third quarter, and the large majority of companies beat estimates.
 
 
 
 
 
The result is a valuation reset. More than a third of the roughly 700 stocks in our hedge fund database worth over $15 billion now trade below 15 times forward earnings. When prices fall while earnings estimates keep rising, the stocks hit hardest can end up trading at single-digit or low-teens multiples, even with sales still climbing fast.
 
 
Why Brookfield Made Our List
 
 
Our screen looked for stocks in our hedge fund database worth more than $25 billion, trading below 15 times forward earnings and expected to grow revenue by at least 15% next fiscal year. After excluding the names from our earlier cheap-stocks list, we kept the 10 largest and ranked them by the number of hedge funds holding them in the third quarter. Brookfield qualified with an $86.87 billion market cap, a forward P/E of 10.98 and forward revenue growth of 18.36%, and 48 hedge funds held it at the end of the quarter.
 
 
Brookfield Corporation 
- A Dirt-Cheap Stock for 2027
 
 
Brookfield owns a global alternative asset manager, a fast-growing insurance business and a portfolio of real assets, all in one stock. It trades at about 11 times forward earnings, and the shares closed at $38.94 on November 19, closer to their 52-week low of $35.61 than their high of $49.57.
 
 
 
 
 
The business keeps compounding. In its third-quarter report on November 12, distributable earnings before realizations rose 12% to $1.50 billion, or 14% per share to $0.64, and total distributable earnings climbed to $0.73 per share from $0.63. Brookfield raised $25 billion of new capital in the quarter, its Wealth Solutions business grew distributable earnings 22% to $511 million, and it has bought back $889 million of its shares so far this year.

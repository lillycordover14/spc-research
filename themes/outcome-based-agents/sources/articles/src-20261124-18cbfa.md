Section1 of the D Line extension, which connects the Metro station at Wilshire and Western Boulevards to the new Metro stop at Wilshire and La Cienega Boulevards, may have opened in May, but it is already a regular part of commuters' lives, and the roam-around worlds of out-and-about fun-seekers.

Chalk up the D Line's instant popularity to several strongly held emotions, such as the fact that a person can now move from Downtown Los Angeles to Beverly Hills in a matter of minutes, something that still astounds.

But there are other reasons fans have chosen to "Ride the D" so wholeheartedly: A host of intriguing pop-ups and events have graced the stations at Wilshire Boulevard and La Brea Avenue, as well as Wilshire Boulevard and Fairfax Avenue.

The station-adjacent events are free, too, with roller-skating meet-ups and music-centered celebrations adding verve to both locations.

Now a year-ending yuletide gathering is headed for Wilshire and Fairfax, and it is focusing on local artists and imaginative SoCalers who create splendid things: It's the 
Made in LA Holiday Market
, a happening headed to the Miracle Mile station on 
Saturday, Nov. 28.

The gifty and/or gift-yourself finds run the gamut, from jewelry to jerky, and "free samples" are promised. Skincare, goodies for a stocking, and wearables will also fill out the browse-around market, which will have around 30 or so vendors.

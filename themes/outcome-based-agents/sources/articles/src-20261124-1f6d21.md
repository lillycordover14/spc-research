Couchbase Signs Strategic Collaboration Agreement with AWS to Expand Delivery of the AI Data Plane for Production AI Agents&nbsp;&nbsp;PR Newswire

[page text]
Collaboration aims to help enterprises move AI agents from pilot to production on AWS with a single, governed data foundation that lowers cost and speeds response times
SAN JOSE, Calif.
, 
Nov. 24, 2026
 /PRNewswire/ -- 
Couchbase
, the Operational Data Platform for AIÂ®, today announced it has signed a strategic collaboration agreement (SCA) with Amazon Web Services (AWS) to help enterprises move AI agents from experimentation into dependable production. Through the agreement, Couchbase will expand the availability of the Couchbase AI Data Plane on AWS, so customers can build agents that are faster, more cost-efficient, and easier to govern as they scale.

Enterprises are moving quickly from AI pilots to production, but many agent initiatives stall when they meet real-world requirements for cost, performance, and governance. Most teams spread agent context across separate systems for memory, vectors, operational data, and analytics. Each added system brings its own licensing, security model, and sync logic, and every hop between them adds latency that end users feel and cost that the business absorbs. As organizations grow from a handful of agents to many, that fragmentation becomes the gap between a promising pilot and a service the business can rely on.
The collaboration is designed to close that gap. By combining the breadth of foundation models available through Amazon Bedrock with Couchbase's unified operational data platform, customers have access to one governed foundation for the memory, context, and real-time data their agents depend on. This means teams can deploy with confidence, keep costs predictable as usage grows, and adopt new models without re-architecting.
"Enterprises have proven that AI agents can work. The challenge now is running them in production at a cost and speed the business can sustain," said Tim Finley, SVP of AI at Couchbase. "Our customers want the freedom to use the best model for each job without rebuilding their data layer every time something changes. Together with AWS, we're giving them a trusted foundation that keeps agents grounded in what's true right now, so their teams spend less time stitching systems together and more time delivering AI their customers and employees can count on."
"Customers are moving beyond experimenting with generative AI to running agents that support core parts of their business, and they need a data foundation that can keep pace," said Mark Relph, Managing Director, Data & AI Partner GTM, AWS. "Our collaboration with Couchbase brings the model choice and serverless simplicity of Amazon Bedrock together with a unified, governed operational data layer, helping customers deliver more responsive agents while managing costs as they scale."
At the center of the collaboration is the Couchbase AI Data Plane on AWS, which serves key-value, SQL, full-text search, vector search, and real-time columnar analytics against a single copy of JSON data. Agents retrieve operational and historical context in one call, so their answers reflect current business data. Because Couchbase runs in the cloud, in private data centers, or at the edge, customers get a consistent data layer wherever their agents run.
Couchbase's native Amazon Bedrock integration gives customers serverless, pay-per-
token
 access to hundreds of foundation models over private networking, with no GPU infrastructure to manage. Couchbase adds semantic caching alongside that access, answering repeat and similar questions without calling the model again to reduce 
token
 spend and speed up responses for high-volume workloads.
Couchbase's managed Agent Memory persists agent profiles, conversation history, and vectorized facts across sessions, so agents pick up where they left off. In Couchbase testing on the LoCoMo benchmark, Agent Memory reduced 
token
 usage by 33 to 45 percent compared with leading alternatives. A Couchbase-managed MCP server gives agents and tools a governed, open-standard path to Couchbase data with nothing to deploy or operate. Both are validated with Strands Agents, LangGraph, CrewAI, and LlamaIndex, so engineering teams can switch or combine orchestration frameworks without rebuilding their memory layer.
"Aptos runs store and commerce operations for some of the best-known brands in retail, and we've moved those customers onto Couchbase Capella on AWS," said John Carney, Vice President, Head of Architecture, Aptos. "Working with the Couchbase team, we've optimized our clusters to bring costs down while every brand stays healthy in production. As we bring in new AI functionality, Couchbase's AI Data Plane will let us build on the platform we already run instead of adding another system."

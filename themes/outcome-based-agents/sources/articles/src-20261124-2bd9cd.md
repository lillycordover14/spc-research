Las Vegas Raiders
 star Maxx Crosby and members of the team’s defensive line took time away from stuffing gaps to stuff cars with Thanksgiving meals Tuesday.

Crosby, his teammates and members of The Hope Movement distributed 300 Thanksgiving meals to Southern Nevadans in need at M Resort in Henderson. Cars lined up throughout the resort’s west parking lot to receive turkeys and side dishes from Raiders players and volunteers.

“It’s incredible. We have a bunch of people here for the same reason, giving back to the community and handing out meals and turkeys for Thanksgiving,” Crosby said. “It means a lot. It’s something I love doing, bringing my teammates out here. I think we got the whole D-line out here. It means a lot to see everybody’s faces and their reactions, helping them out for Thanksgiving.”

Some fans posed for selfies with Crosby from inside their vehicles. Others hopped out for quick photos or collected autographs from Raiders players while receiving their food.

It was a family affair for Crosby, as his wife, Rachel, daughter Ella and other family members helped with the giveaway. Crosby said he wouldn’t be able to do the work he does through his namesake charity without their support.

“Without the support, I wouldn’t be able to do all these amazing things for others,” Crosby said. “Obviously, Rach and having Ella here means the world to me. My agent, he’s always here. It’s a family thing for sure. We have a big vision for the things we want to do and bring to life. I wouldn’t be able to do it by myself.”

The Raiders’ defensive line participated in the holiday giveaway one week after the team’s offensive line held its own 
Thanksgiving meal distribution
 at Allegiant Stadium. The dueling events have created a healthy competition among the position groups.

“Everything’s a competition,” Crosby said. “The more people we can have, the better. But it’s incredible. The turnout is even bigger and better than last year. It’s wrapped around the whole casino.

“There are a lot of people in need, and it’s something we love doing.”

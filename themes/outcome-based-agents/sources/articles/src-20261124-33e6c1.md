 took exception to that, and responded to Robinson’s claim during
 Tuesday’s episode

“I mean that’s a casual a– take. I’ll start with that,” Crosby said.

“I mean, it’s so funny… You got to think about it like this: When you talk about the NFL, it’s a business, right? Who are your highest paid people?… It’s pass rusher. Then it’s receiver, then it’s offensive tackle. So, that ain’t a coincidence. There’s a reason. Yeah. Number two, edge rusher,” he added.  

3

Las Vegas Raiders defensive end Maxx Crosby (98) reacts after sacking San Francisco 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy on Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, in Santa Clara, Calif. 
AP Photo/Justine Willard

“
If it was easy to play the position 
and get one sack a game, why doesn’t everyone do it? Why is it—why out of a thousand snaps, getting 10 sacks is great? Think about how hard it is to get a sack if you got to play a thousand snaps to get double digits,” Crosby continued.

Advertisement

“You got to be not only big, athletic, you got to be strong, you got to be twitched up, you got to be just as bendy as you are powerful. You got to be able to defend the run versus multiple people all the time.”

“Running back, you get handed the ball and you go play football. You know what I mean? You run and you catch,” Crosby added. “When you’re talking about hard to play, running back has easily got to be the easiest position to play. It’s not even a question! They don’t have to think about nothing.” 

“There’s a reason why they pay the [offensive] linemen and not the running backs.”

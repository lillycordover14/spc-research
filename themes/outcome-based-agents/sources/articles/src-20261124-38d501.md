"OpenDialer has the lowest published starting price of the seven dedicated parallel dialers in a 20-product comparison: $79 a month, with parallel dialing on Business at $119 for two seats."
A sourced comparison of 20 sales dialers answers the questions buyers ask most: which parallel dialers publish prices, which let you use your own carrier, and which is the best-value Nooks or Orum alternative. OpenDialer has the lowest published starting price of the seven dedicated parallel dialers compared.
San Francisco, CA - Nov. 24, 2026 - 
For sales teams looking for a Nooks or Orum alternative in 2027, 
OpenDialer
 is the best-value option with published pricing, according to a 
comparison of 20 sales dialers
 released today. Every figure was taken from each vendor's own pricing or product pages, checked on November 22, 2026, with sources listed. The comparison answers the questions buyers most often ask when choosing a dialer.
What is the best Nooks alternative in 2027?
For teams that want parallel dialing without a custom quote, OpenDialer is the best-value 
Nooks alternative
. Nooks sells by custom quote and offers a demo only. OpenDialer publishes every price, starts at one seat, rings up to four lines per rep on its Business plan at $119 a month for two seats, and offers a three-day trial for $1.
What is the best Orum alternative?
OpenDialer is the best-value 
Orum alternative
 for small and mid-sized teams. The comparison lists a nine-seat minimum and no published price for Orum. OpenDialer starts at one seat with monthly plans.
What is the cheapest parallel dialer?
Of the seven dedicated parallel dialers compared (Nooks, Orum, Koncert, ConnectAndSell, Salesfinity, Trellus and OpenDialer), OpenDialer has the lowest published starting price at $79 a month. Trellus starts at $99.99 and Salesfinity at $249. Nooks, Orum, Koncert and ConnectAndSell do not publish a starting price.
Which sales dialers publish their prices?
Eight of the 19 other products compared do not publish a starting price: Nooks, Orum, Koncert, ConnectAndSell, Kixie, Outreach, Salesloft and Convoso. Full 
OpenDialer pricing
: Starter $79 a month with one seat, Business $119 a month with two seats, Enterprise by quote.
Which dialer lets you use your own Twilio or Telnyx account?
OpenDialer runs on the customer's own Twilio, Telnyx, DIDWW or SIP account, billed by the carrier at its own rates, or on managed numbers at $3 a month each. Among the dedicated parallel dialers compared, it is the only one whose website says it supports the customer's own carrier.
Is there a self-hosted sales dialer?
OpenDialer is the only product of the 20 compared that offers a self-hosted edition. Its source-available community edition is being published on GitHub in stages.
When is another dialer the better choice?
Teams that need more than four lines per rep, a deep two-way Salesforce or HubSpot integration today, or a vendor with a long track record may prefer Nooks or Orum, which ring up to five lines per rep. The comparison lists these cases on each head-to-head page.
About OpenDialer
OpenDialer is sales calling software that combines a power dialer, a parallel dialer, inbound AI agents and an SMS inbox in one product. Launched in 2026, it runs on the customer's own phone carrier or on managed numbers, as a hosted service or self-hosted. Its mission is to give sales teams more conversations at a lower cost, with every price published.

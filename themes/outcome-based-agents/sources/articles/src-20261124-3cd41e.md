Moulton, Ala. (
WAFF
) - Lawrence County Emergency Management Agency received nearly 10-thousand-dollars in grant funding to supply all seven senior centers in the county with automated external defibrillators (AED). 
The grant through NARCOG paid for eight devices, as well as wall mounts and signage. 
Each AED cabinet will also include Narcan. 
Firefighters with Moulton and Caddo will train volunteers at the senior centers on how to use the devices in an emergency. 
Robbi Ligon, Director of Aging and Public Transportation for the Lawrence County commission says an estimated 80 to 100 people visit the county’s senior centers each day. 
Lawrence County EMA Director Steven Carlton said the goal is to make sure the facilities are prepared. 
“When I started, I went around to the different county departments trying to figure out what EMA can do to help build the preparedness levels, and out of that, we discovered, due to the heightened risk of the populations that are utilizing the senior centers, we thought this would be a great use of applying for a grant through NARCOG, that way if there ever is a medical emergency, they do have the resources to attempt to save a life,” Carlton said. 
Leaders hope the equipment is never needed but said having it on-site could make a difference when seconds count. 
The EMA will take the remaining AED before the county commission to determine where it will be placed. 
Carlton said he will recommend the judicial administrative offices as the location because it is a high-traffic area in the county.

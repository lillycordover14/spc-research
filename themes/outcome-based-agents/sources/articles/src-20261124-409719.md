iLerna Lleida
 (Liga Endesa) inked 31-year old Serbian ex-international 206cm power forward 
Dragan Apic
. He played most recently at 
Soles
 in Mexican LNBP. In 36 games he just recently played he averaged 8.8ppg and 3.4rpg last season. He helped them to win the LNBP title. The lasthe played at 
Breogan Lugo
.
Apic was also a member of the Serbian international program for some years. He played for Serbian Senior National Team between 2020 and 2022 and previously for the Under 18 National Team back in 2013.
Apic is a very experienced player. He has played also in France (Le Mans), Montenegro (Buducnost), Poland (Zastal), Russia (BC Pari Nizhny Novgorod and Lokomotiv), Spain (Burgos) and Serbia (Crvena zvezda, Vrsac and KK FMP SoccerBet Beograd).
Among many awards he was voted Eurobasket.com All-Polish League Center of the Year back in 2022.

ERBIL (Kurdistan24) –
 Kurdistan Region President Nechirvan Barzani has expressed "surprise and deep concern" over a statement by the spokesperson for the Commander-in-Chief of the Iraqi Armed Forces, rejecting the use of military rhetoric in addressing the Kurdistan Region and calling for outstanding disputes to be resolved through constitutional means and dialogue.

In a statement issued on Tuesday, Barzani said, "We heard the statement by the spokesperson for the Commander-in-Chief of the Iraqi Armed Forces with surprise and deep concern, particularly at a time when dialogue between the Kurdistan Region and the federal government is ongoing to resolve outstanding issues."

"We categorically reject any form of addressing the Kurdistan Region in military rhetoric. Experience has proven that this approach does not resolve any problems and serves neither the interests of Iraq nor those of any party," he added.

Barzani emphasized that the Kurdistan Region has been a key partner in building the new Iraq and drafting its Constitution, reaffirming its commitment to protecting national resources and enforcing the law.

He stressed that relations between Erbil and Baghdad, as well as efforts to resolve outstanding disputes, must be guided by the Iraqi Constitution, respect for the Kurdistan Region's constitutional status and rights, and the principles of partnership and mutual understanding.

The Kurdistan Region President also highlighted the importance of unity and solidarity within Kurdistan, urging all Iraqi political forces and parties to act with wisdom and a sense of national responsibility.

"The strength and stability of the Kurdistan Region are the strength and stability of Iraq, and respect for the Constitution and federalism is the guarantee of our shared future," Barzani concluded.

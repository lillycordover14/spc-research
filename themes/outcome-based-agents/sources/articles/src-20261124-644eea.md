Horses, cats, dogs, and kites soar in the imaginations of local authors who will read their children’s books at a free Small Business Saturday event at Artisan Alley boutique in Newfane.
The event at 2714 Main St. runs 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. and includes a pop culture trivia contest to win a $100 store gift card. The store is stocked with the creations of local craftspeople, from handmade wooden music boxes to knitwear, candles, pottery, and a wide variety of jewelry styles.

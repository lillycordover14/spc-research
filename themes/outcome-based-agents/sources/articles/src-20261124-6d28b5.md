Calgary’s WCD Partners With Glean to Bring Paper Archives Into AI Search&nbsp;&nbsp;Calgary.Tech

[page text]
Calgary-based WCD has 
partnered with enterprise AI company Glean
 to help Canadian organizations search decades of paper records. For a procurement manager, that could mean asking which supplier contracts renew next quarter and getting an answer even if the signed agreements have spent years in storage.

The agreement builds on WCD’s 
Intelligent Scanning service
, launched November 18. WCD scans paper files or works from existing digital copies, extracts details such as supplier names and renewal dates, and has staff verify them against the original records. Glean can then use those details to answer questions, while respecting the access permissions on each source document.

“Now a customer can go from a room full of records to AI-ready with one partner and stay that way as new documents arrive,” said Karen Brookman, WCD’s president and CEO.

As a Glean Services & Solutions Partner, WCD also handles what arrives after the initial scan. When a supplier sends a revised agreement, for example, WCD’s intake service can classify it and route it to the company’s contract system, keeping the new terms available for future searches.

WCD says it can process up to three million images a month. Its scanning operations already have a foothold in Alberta’s energy sector, where it has 
partnered with Calgary-based StackDX
 to digitize land files and exploration records.

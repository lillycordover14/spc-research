ARDMORE, Okla. (KTEN) — A man wanted in connection to a fatal shooting in Ardmore earlier this month has surrendered to authorities.
Police linked Tyqwan Kanye McGee to a shooting on November 8 in the 400 block of Park Street Southeast.

Ardmore police spokesperson Sgt. Matt Dunn told KTEN that McGee — accompanied by a family member — turned himself in to Lighthorse police in Ada around midnight Monday after more than two weeks at large.
McGee, 22, is facing a first-degree murder charge after 37-year-old Darin Lee was shot and killed.

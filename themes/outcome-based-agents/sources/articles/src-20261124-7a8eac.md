Being a country music star comes with plenty of perks. You get to play big shows, meet fans and make memories that last a lifetime. But sometimes, those fans ask for something a little out of the ordinary.
Jason Kempin, Getty Images
Jason Kempin, Getty Images
The 
Red Clay Strays
 have signed their fair share of autographs over the years. But when I asked them about the craziest things they've ever been asked to sign during an interview on 
Taste of Country Nights
, their answers did not disappoint.
The Red Clay Strays Once Signed a Fan's Prosthetic Leg
When I asked if anyone had ever been asked to sign something really strange, the band brought up a prosthetic leg.
"Oh, yeah, we've done a couple of them," one member says.
One of those experiences really stood out because the leg didn't have any other autographs on it. The Red Clay Strays were the first ones to sign it.
Photo by 
Vladyslav Tobolenko
 on 
Unsplash
Runner with prosthetic leg racing on asphalt path
"Those things are expensive and then we took it to the green room and they didn't get it back for like probably 30-45 minutes till it got signed."
They also had plenty of questions. They wondered whose leg it was, what the owner was doing while they had their leg backstage and whether the owner could sell it for enough money to buy two more and even whether a prosthetic leg would float in water.
"That's a good question," one of them joked.
The Red Clay Strays Have Signed Some Pretty Unusual Body Parts
The prosthetic leg wasn't the only unusual autograph story. The guys also talked about signing an elderly woman's chest, a man's chest and their own sister's forehead.
Lead singer, Brandon Coleman said he had signed an 80-year-old woman's chest before, which led to a funny exchange about what you call that part of the body at that age.
Cindy Ord, Getty Images
Cindy Ord, Getty Images
"You just got to call it a chest after 80 years old," Coleman joked.
One of the members of the band said "I signed my own sister's forehead one time."
Another member of the band said he's signed a male fan's chest before.
"I've signed a man's t-t before in the past," he admits.
Another member shared that he once signed his own sister's forehead.
Between foreheads, chests and prosthetic legs, the Red Clay Strays have clearly seen just about everything when it comes to fan autograph requests.

Country Artists Who Are Unrecognizable Without Their Signature Looks
While country artists are most known for their music and the lyrics they write, there are a few who you could pick out of a lineup based on their silhouette. In other words, there are several artist who are known for their sense of style just as much as they are known for their songs.
Take for instance, Lainey Wilson's bell bottoms. Her vintage western look is not only becoming on her, but it's also 
by design
. Her one-of-a-kind style was born out of a need to stand out from the crowd and now it's become a part of who she is.
For others artists, their signature look may be a ball cap or sunglasses. These items are so engrained with an artist that it's almost uncomfortable to see them without them. But we went there anyway.

ARDMORE, Okla. (KXII) – A 22-year-old man wanted in an Ardmore 
murder
 earlier this month has been arrested, police confirmed Tuesday morning.
Tyqwan Kan’ye McGee, 22, is in police custody after turning himself in to Chickasaw Lighthorse Police in Ada, Ardmore Police told KXII 12.
McGee was wanted for first-degree murder 
in the death of
 37-year-old Darin Lee.
Ardmore Police Sgt. Matthew Dunn said the department’s new tip line and the evidence, such as shell casings, led investigators to McGee as their suspect.
“The tip line and the evidence, really all of it put together, is what all pointed in the same direction,” Dunn said.
Officers were called to the Chickasaw Hills neighborhood on Park Street SE in the early morning hours of November 8. They found Lee dead. He had been shot several times.
Police said there had been a large gathering outside. They believe Lee and McGee were arguing.
“Now, what they were specifically arguing about, we’re still investigating, but we do know they were in a disturbance and had been for a little bit of time that night,” Dunn said.

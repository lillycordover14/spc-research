Harper Ensemble Theatre Company to hold auditions for ‘Water by the Spoonful’

Harper College News Bureau

November 24, 2026

November 24, 2026

The Harper Ensemble Theatre Company is inviting students to audition for its spring
                              production of 
Water by the Spoonful
 by Quiara Alegría Hudes. The play centers on Elliot Ortiz, a Puerto Rican veteran
                              of the Iraq War who struggles to readjust to civilian life in his hometown of Philadelphia.
                              Performances will run April 23-25 and April 30-May 2, 2027, in the Harper College
                              Drama Lab Black Box Theatre, 1200 W. Algonquin Road, Palatine.

Auditions will take place 1-6 p.m. Friday, December 4, and 11:30 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday,
                              December 5, in the Drama Lab (Building L, Room L109). Student performers of all ethnicities,
                              gender identities, physical abilities and ages are encouraged to audition. Those auditioning
                              should be prepared to participate in table reads and discussions about the play.

Reserve an 
audition spot
. For more information, contact director Ilknür Ozgür at 
oi16084@harpercollege.edu

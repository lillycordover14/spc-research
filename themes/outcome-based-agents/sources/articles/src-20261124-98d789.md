Hot Toys Gives Loki New Artisan Edition Figure for Avengers: Doomsday
Hot Toys reveals its exclusive 1/6-scale Loki Artisan Edition from Avengers: Doomsday, featuring wool-material hair implants, a TVA-inspired costume and signature TVA gear.
Published 
Tue, 24 Nov 2026 11:22:09 -0600
 
by 
Tyler Roberts
 | 
Comments
Loki has come a long way since audiences first met him as the mischievous God of Mischief in 
Thor
 and again in 
The Avengers
. Over the course of nearly 22 films, audiences watched Loki evolve from a villain into a hero, only to see him fall at the hands of Thanos in 
Avengers: Infinity War
. However, 
Avengers: Endgame
 opened the door for another version of Loki to escape with the Tesseract, creating an alternate timeline that would eventually lead him to the TVA and a mission to stop multiversal threats. His journey took a surprising turn in the Disney+ series 
Loki
, where he embraced a much bigger purpose as the God of Stories. 
Avengers: Doomsday
 is now bringing him back into the spotlight, and Hot Toys is joining the excitement with a brand-new limited-edition Artisan Edition 1/6-scale figure.

Loki has been faithfully recreated in his TVA-inspired outfit, complete with a tailored coat and an impressive Tom Hiddleston head sculpt. He also features the signature rolling-eyeballs function and wool-like hair implants. For accessories, Hot Toys includes a TemPad, a computer, a Reset Charge, an identification card, and a translucent Timedoor backdrop. Limited to just 3,000 pieces, this exclusive release will be a must-have for fans looking to take their Doomsday collection to new levels. Pre-orders are not live just yet, but fans can keep an eye on 
Sideshow Collectibles
 to RSVP.

Avengers: Doomsday – Loki (Artisan Edition) Exclusive

"This figure features a finely crafted head sculpt with separate rolling eyeballs, capturing Tom Hiddleston's iconic portrayal as Loki with unmatched realism. As an Artisan release, the head sculpt comes meticulously implanted with wool-material hair, recreating his signature black wavy hair with depth and natural flow. Crafted based on his appearance in Avengers: Doomsday, he is dressed in a tailored TVA-issued costume, including a double-breasted coat, dress shirt, tie, pants, belt with a TVA logo buckle, socks, and shoes."

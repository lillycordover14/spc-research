Supervisor Lesko will meet PORA officials to find ADA transportation solutions

							Sun City West resident Marie Boyd and her daughter Diane Henry attended the Sun City West Foundation Find Your Ride Resource class.						

							(Independent Newsmedia/Jennifer Jimenez)						

Maricopa County officials are working toward a solution for Valley Metro’s RideChoice program challenge that could leave disabled Sun Cities residents with one less transportation option.
Maricopa County District 4 Supervisor Debbie Lesko stated, “My office will be reaching out to PORA to schedule a meeting between PORA, top county staff and my office to discuss the RideChoice issue and work toward a solution.”
The Property Owners and Residents Association Board Director Cindy Putman said the board is supporting efforts to return RideChoice access for elderly and disabled residents.

Obituary

William Sidney “Rudder” Harper Jr. age 61, of Lewisburg, TN passed away November 24, 2026, in Lewisburg, TN. Rudder was born September 7, 1965, to William Sidney Harper Sr. and Betty Jean Bennett Harper. Rudder was a member of The Lord’s House of Prayer. He was preceded in death by his sister Deborah Harper and his brother Greg Harper.
Rudder loved his family dearly. He loved spending time with them and being active with his grandchildren. Rudder was known to make you laugh from his humorous jokes or his funny stories he would share. One of the many passions he shared was driving that light blue number 41 at Duck River Raceway. He also found great joy with his motorcycles. His heart was big for his community and friends. Rudder loved his kids, grandchildren, and additional kids. He loved giving and always helping others. He would be seen often helping others before wanting any payment for his work. His kind heart, generous spirit, and loving personality will be missed for many generations to come.
Rudder is survived by his daughter Kayla Harper of Lewisburg, TN; 2 grandchildren, Wade Jamison and Sidney Harper of Lewisburg, TN; parents, William Sidney Harper Sr. and Betty Jean Bennett Harper; a sister, Teresa Harper Smithson of Peytonsville, TN; brothers, Roger (Lori) Harper of Rally Hill Community, Travis “Hog” (Debbie) Harper of Kingfield, TN; several nieces and nephews, great nieces and nephews, as well as many friends who will remember him.
A visitation with the Harper Family will be Sunday, November 29, 2026, at 4PM from 8PM at Lawrence Funeral Home. The Funeral Service will be Monday, November 30, 2026, at 11AM from the Chapel of Lawrence Funeral Home with burial to follow in Williamson Memorial Gardens in Franklin, TN.
Services are in care of Lawrence Funeral Home 203 S. Horton Pkwy Chapel Hill, TN. 37034. 931-364-2233 www.lawrencefuneral.net

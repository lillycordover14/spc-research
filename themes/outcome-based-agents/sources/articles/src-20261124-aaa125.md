The Lawrence County Emergency Management Agency (EMA) received up to a $10,000 grant from North Central Alabama Regional Council of Governments (NARCOG) in which they have used the funds to purchase eight automated external defibrillators (AEDs) for each county senior center.
“One of my goals as coming into the Emergency Management Director was to build up our county’s preparedness capabilities to where we could handle any type of emergency that the county has,” said Lawrence County EMA Director Steven Carlton. “One of the first things I did was look at the county’s departments to see what I could do. The senior centers have higher risk elderly populations.”
Carlton said Caddo-Midway Fire & Rescue District Chief Administrator Darwin Clark and State Rep. Ernie Yarbrough got him in touch with NARCOG.
 
Robbi Ligon is the director of Lawrence County Aging and Transportation. She said the seven free senior centers offer a hot meal every Monday through Thursday at each center and to the homebound. That amounts to just over 1,200 meals in a week. Ligon said the centers are available for rent.
“The AEDs will be greatly beneficial to the seniors,” she said. “It’s something that is needed because mostly the elderly come. Some are in better health than others. Now, it’s there if anything happens. At least they have the chance to help somebody.”
Ligon said they have never had AEDs in the centers before.
“There have been times when we had to call an ambulance – to several of the centers actually,” she said. “So, this will be a great thing.”
With putting an AED in all seven senior centers there is still one left over. Carlton said he is still undecided as to where to install it.
“That’s going to be a discussion I have with the new county commission,” he said. “My thought right now is I’m trying to look at the highest populated and heaviest traveled county building. Right now, I’m leaning more towards the administrative and judicial center. I think it’s the most populated due to the courts, probate, tags, voting office, things that go on in that building.”

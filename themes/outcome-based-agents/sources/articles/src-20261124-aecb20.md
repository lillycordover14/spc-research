As part of the annual meeting of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation, a trilateral summit took place between Donald Trump, Xi Jinping, and Vladimir Putin; the White House issued a statement denying that Trump had fallen asleep during a speech by Xi; and Trump responded to questions regarding a series of technical malfunctions that had hit the United States’ National Security Agency and a major bank, saying, “It’s fine.”

Northeast ZIP Codes 19111 and 19152 Among Philly’s Lowest for 311 Closure Rates&nbsp;&nbsp;Northeast Times

[page text]
An abandoned car overgrown with vegetation. Photo: Yannis Papadopulos, 
Pexels

Two Northeast Philadelphia ZIP codes, 19111 and 19152, are among the ZIP codes with the lowest closure rates for 311 quality-of-life requests this year, an 
NBC10 Investigators analysis
 found. Residents in some West Philadelphia ZIP codes, the analysis added, wait longer on a typical request to see a case closed.

NBC10 reviewed requests filed from Jan. 1 to Oct. 19, 2026. The city’s Managing Director’s Office cautioned against concluding from the data alone that the gaps result from race, income or where someone lives.

What the Analysis Covered

The NBC10 Investigators focused on quality-of-life complaints, including abandoned vehicles, vacant lots, street trees and illegal dumping. They also counted rubbish and recycling collection, short dumping, sanitation violations, street problems, and interior and exterior property maintenance. Abandoned vehicles, recycling collection and rubbish collection were the most common.

Five ZIP codes produced some of the highest request volumes: parts of Southwest and West Philadelphia, Tioga/Nicetown and Juniata Park. NBC10 reported that residents there are predominantly Black or Hispanic. Median household incomes in those areas fall below the city average.

Closure rates were high across the city. In every ZIP code, more than two-thirds of the requests analyzed had been closed. Rates reached as high as 91%. Rittenhouse, Graduate Hospital, Queen Village, Fishtown and Overbrook were among the areas with the highest shares closed, each at 84% or better.

Typical Closure Times Differ by ZIP Code

How long a typical request stayed open varied more sharply. NBC10 found the slowest closures in three West Philadelphia ZIP codes. Center City and nearby neighborhoods were among the fastest.

Linda Medley, who lives in 19143, showed NBC10 an empty lot in her neighborhood. “There’s that empty lot there that people use that to dump trash bags,” Medley said. She also reported a tree that is buckling a sidewalk.

“I think they used to care more about this neighborhood,” Medley said.

Lowest Closure Rates Include Two Northeast ZIP Codes

Closure rate and closure speed are separate measures. NBC10 listed 19120, 19111, 19153, 19152 and 19144 among the ZIP codes with the lowest closure rates. Both 19111 and 19152 are in Northeast Philadelphia.

NBC10 gave detailed figures only for 19144, which includes parts of Germantown. That ZIP code generated nearly 3,000 requests in the period. Roughly 32% were still open. Rubbish and abandoned vehicles were the leading complaints there.

NBC10 drew its data from the city’s 311 records. The Northeast Times has also reported on 
311 response times in poorer ZIP codes
, using a different measure of waiting, and on 
illegal dumping reports in Northeast ZIP codes
.

City Cites Complexity of Cases

In a statement, the city said the findings do not point to unequal treatment. “The findings do not suggest unequal intent or unequal commitment to service,” the city said.

As relayed in NBC10’s report, the city’s explanation centers on the requests themselves. Some underserved communities report more complicated problems, such as abandoned vehicles, vacant lots and property issues. Those cases can require extra steps, including finding the property owner. They can take longer to resolve.

Daniel Ramos, Philly 311’s community engagement manager, pointed to outreach through neighborhood groups. “There’s a lot of community groups out there that work with us to try to be proactive,” Ramos said.

A Closed Request Is Not Always a Fixed Problem

NBC10 also reported that a closed 311 request does not necessarily mean the problem is gone. Tony Kenney said he has submitted multiple requests over several years about a buckled road in Germantown.

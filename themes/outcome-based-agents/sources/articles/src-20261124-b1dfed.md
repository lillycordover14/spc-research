Ravens Swindled Themselves with Botched Maxx Crosby Deal&nbsp;&nbsp;Raiders Beat

[page text]
The Raiders might not have any notable rivals outside of their own division and the 49ers, but they came close to adding one in the offseason.

With the Ravens’ decision to back out of the Maxx Crosby trade in March, there haven’t been many Raider fans wishing good fortune on the Ravens in the eight months since. 

No one wanted to see Lamar Jackson to go down with an injury, but the thought of Trey Hendrickson failing in Baltimore?

That’s a scenario every Raider fan can get on board with. 

And as fate would have it, the Hendrickson deal has already turned into a disaster for Baltimore. 

Pro Football Focus unimpressed with Hendrickson 

Among Ravens with at least 100 defensive snaps on the season, Hendrickson is the 12th-ranked player (
Pro Football Focus
) on the Baltimore defense this year. 

That’s embarrassing production from a player who signed a 
four-year, $112 million contract
 in the same league year. 

Hendrickson’s PFF season grade (59.9) comes in well behind Maxx Crosby (86.1), who has been, by far, the Raiders’ best defensive player through the first 11 weeks of the season. 

And for what it’s worth, Crosby is also 2 years and 8 months younger than Hendrickson.

The Ravens might not have known how Crosby’s knee was going to hold up, but they did know Hendrickson was going to turn 32 during the season. 

Now the Ravens have an aging $2 million player at a $100+ million cost. 

La Canfora says Hendrickson is already a bust

League insider 
Jason La Canfora
 has been on a mission against Ravens GM Eric DeCosta since long before the Crosby deal fell through, and La Canfora wrote another scathing rebuke of DeCosta and the Ravens this week. 

“[Hendrickson] is already a bust,” La Canfora wrote in a Monday column. 

“These Ravens will ‘compete’ to be possibly the worst defense in franchise history in 2026, and won’t be contending for anything in 2027, either, with a rebuild imminent. This is a sunk cost, already. (Oh and the Bengals defense and pass rush is markedly better without him and hold-ins, as evidenced when they crushed the Ravens). Literally nothing about how Hendrickson was billed has showed up.”

Like most Raider fans, La Canfora has been rooting against the Hendrickson signing since the day it was announced and it definitely seems like the Raiders are going to get the last laugh on the Crosby trade that never happened. 

The good news for Crosby is he was never going to win a championship in Baltimore.

And, in fact, Baltimore was never the place Crosby preferred to be traded in the first place.

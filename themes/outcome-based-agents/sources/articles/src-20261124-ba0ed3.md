  [Anchor] 
  

 At the parliamentary confirmation hearing for Justice Minister nominee Lee Yong-cheol held at the National Assembly today (November 24), questioning focused on whether charges against President Lee Jae-myung would be dropped. Nominee Lee stated that he would not unreasonably exercise his supervisory authority, but he did not give a definitive promise that he would not do so. Addressing criticism over a "reward appointment," he drew a line, saying that while it is true he has a long-standing friendship with President Lee, he is not a close aide. 
  

 Reporter Jang Min-seong reports. 
  

 [Reporter] 
  

 From the very beginning, the People Power Party pressed Nominee Lee on whether he intended to direct prosecutors to drop the charges in cases involving President Lee Jae-myung. 
  

 [Joo Jin-woo / People Power Party Lawmaker: "Is there a possibility of dropping the charges or not? As Minister of Justice, do you intend to direct the withdrawal of the indictment or not?"] 
  

 [Lee Yong-cheol / Justice Minister Nominee: "I have no intention of exercising unreasonable supervisory authority that would be difficult for the public to accept, but I do not think it is appropriate to answer a hypothetical question based on such preconceived assumptions."] 
  

 When asked whether the remaining charges would stand even if an upcoming special counsel investigation reveals some problems with the investigation and indictment in the president's cases, Nominee Lee responded as follows: 
  

 [Lee Yong-cheol / Justice Minister Nominee: "I believe that would depend on various circumstances."] 
  

 As the opposition persistently questioned the nominee regarding the withdrawal of the indictment, the Democratic Party pushed back. 
  

 [Seo Young-kyo / Chair of the Legislation and Judiciary Committee (Democratic Party): "Then are you saying we should just leave alone the prosecutors who concealed and fabricated evidence?"] 
  

 Heated exchanges also took place over claims that the nomination was an attempt to take care of figures from the so-called "Seongnam faction." 
  

 [Kwak Kyu-taek / People Power Party Lawmaker: "Based on past experience, it seems (President Lee) knows that 'if the nominee is given a post, he certainly repays the favor to the person who appointed him.'"] 
  

 [Kim Ki-pyo / Democratic Party Lawmaker: "For (President Lee) to rise to the presidency, it is inevitable that there were many exceptionally capable people around him and close to him."] 
  

 Nominee Lee Yong-cheol responded as follows: 
  

 [Lee Yong-cheol / Justice Minister Nominee: "It is true that I have maintained a long-standing friendship with the President, but I am not one of his close aides."] 
  

 He also repeatedly denied gambling allegations raised by Yoo Dong-gyu, a key figure in the Daejang-dong development project. 
  

 [Yoon Sang-hyun / People Power Party Lawmaker: "(Yoo Dong-gyu) claimed that you ate chicken soup and played poker several times, 3 to 4 times, yet you keep lying saying that's not true..."] 
  

 [Lee Yong-cheol / Justice Minister Nominee: "I cannot eat meat."] 
  

 Nominee Lee also denied allegations that he was implicated in the Daejang-dong development corruption scandal and the Seongnam FC corporate sponsorship case, stating that they are untrue.

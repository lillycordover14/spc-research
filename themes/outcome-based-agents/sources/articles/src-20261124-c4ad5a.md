Sidney Crosby’s agent: “His goal is to be a Penguin forever”
by
 
 
Julia Stumbaugh
Nov 24, 2026, 1:00 PM UTC
SAN JOSE, CALIFORNIA - NOVEMBER 11: Sidney Crosby #87 of the Pittsburgh Penguins participates in warmups at SAP Center on November 11, 2026 in San Jose, California. (Photo by Bryan Kennedy/NHLI)
 
NHLI via Getty Images
Pat Brisson seemingly offered a vote of confidence toward Sidney Crosby’s desire to stick around in Pittsburgh, at least for now, despite Kyle Dubas’ 
consideration
 of trading veterans for future assets.
When asked on a Monday appearance on Sportsnet’s 
Real Kyper & Bourne 
show about Crosby potentially being a Penguin for life, Brisson said, “I think that’s his goal. He’s been very clear with that.
“I think, at the same time, Kyle came out last week and made it clear that he’s going to trade for futures, and I’m sure as the process goes he’s going to evaluate.”
Brisson concluded, “Sidney’s evaluating, watching, but his goal is to be a Penguin forever. That’s always been his goal, and that’s why he’s in no rush right now to jump to conclusions on November 23rd.“
Brisson’s appearance on the show came four days after he responded to a Dubas 
press conference
 regarding the future of the Penguins by saying Crosby “has been in Pittsburgh for over 20 years and he isn’t in any rush to make a decision.”
”Kyle will do what he feels is best for the organization in the meantime,” Brisson concluded in his original statement, per TSN’s 
Pierre LeBrun
.
The Penguins lost their fourth straight game and fell to 2-8-1 in their last 11 games with Sunday’s shutout by the Dallas Stars.
Sportsnet’s Elliotte Friedman 
said
 on Sunday’s edition of his 
32 Thought
s podcast he felt Dubas “wants to get a jump on the market,” and that he believes the Ottawa Senators have “interest” in both Bryan Rust and Rickard Rakell.
How the Penguins play over the coming weeks and how Dubas handles that reported trade interest could ultimately play a role in what Crosby’s perspective looks like when it comes time to make a decision regarding his expiring contract.

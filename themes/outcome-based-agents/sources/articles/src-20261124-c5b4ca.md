Bella Hadid is getting vulnerable in more ways than one.

The model, 30, scored the 
cover of Harper’s Bazaar’s Art Issue
, posing in Prada, Alaïa, Saint Laurent and more. But one of the most memorable images is Hadid in her birthday suit.

That is to say, she shows some skin without any clothes at all — save for a pair of dazzling Chopard pearl earrings and shearling Bottega Veneta heels.

In the interview, she was laid bare as well, opening up about her health struggles, being told she needed to lose weight when she first started in the modeling industry as a teenager and the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show.

While the star has now walked in five shows for the lingerie brand, she candidly shared, “I don’t think I’ve been ready until this year, to be honest.”

In years prior, she was burning the candle at both ends “pulling a Bella, and Belinda comes out,” referring to her alter ego, though she “felt really strong” for the 2026 spectacular — which 
she opened, wearing a green and floral look
.

Hadid has had her fair share of health struggles over the past few years, and she finally decided that she “had to make a decision for [herself] to have boundaries with [her] own health.”

After the last year’s Victoria’s Secret show, 
fans were concerned
, commenting that she seemed to “struggle” as she walked the runway in a silver fringed bra and enormous wings.

Though she remains slightly more covered in the inside pages, she still wears looks that hug her frame.

“To not be able to know yourself in an industry that tells you how to be every single day is really hard,” she said. 
courtesy of Harper’s BAZAAR/Karim Sadli

It was only a month after she was 
hospitalized due to her chronic Lyme disease
 and Hadid 
replied to fans’ concerns
, saying that the wings weighed 50 pounds.

Speaking with Harper’s Bazaar, she shared, “for 10 years … I didn’t know what my favorite color was, what I liked to eat. I stopped listening to music. To not be able to know yourself in an industry that tells you how to be every single day is really hard.”

In addition to Lyme, Hadid reveals that she also has POTS (postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome) and PMDD (premenstrual dysphoric disorder) — and “goes through these different motions of feeling really good and then all of a sudden crashing.” She even encountered a similar issue right before this year’s show, but said she pulled it together before hitting the runway.

When asked whether she had a little bit of Belinda still inside her, Hadid replied, “Fortunately and unfortunately, I think it was Bella this year.”

						Cox Automotive Forecast: November New-Vehicle Sales Pace Steadies as Fewer Selling Days Pull Volume Lower					

						Nov. 24, 2026
						

							2 min Read						

ATLANTA, Nov. 24, 2026
 – November new-vehicle sales volume is expected to fall well below October and last year’s level, largely because the month has fewer selling days. After adjusting for the calendar and seasonal patterns, the seasonally adjusted annual rate, or SAAR, is expected to finish near 15.8 million, up from 15.5 million in October and matching November 2025.

According to Cox Automotive’s forecast, sales volume is expected to reach 1.20 million, down 10.3% from October and 7.3% from November 2025. November has 23 selling days, five fewer than October and two fewer than last November. On a per-selling-day basis, the daily sales rate is tracking about 1% above November 2025.

“Gas prices have been trending lower through November, giving consumers at least one reason to feel more optimistic,” said 
Jonathan Gregory
, senior director of Economic and Industry Insights at Cox Automotive. “With Black Friday weekend upon us, I expect auto dealers will try to turn some of that optimism into sales before the month closes.” 

Affordability remains a constraint, Gregory added. “Lower gas prices don’t shrink car payments, and payments are a significant barrier for many households. With auto loan rates rising and recent stock market volatility, we forecast new-vehicle sales to hold steady through the remainder of the year.”

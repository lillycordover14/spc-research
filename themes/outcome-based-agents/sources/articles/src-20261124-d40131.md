InComm Gift Card Shop on TikTok; Lord & Taylor Adds Zip Payment Option

Social-media users scrolling on TikTok have a new option to buy and send digital gift cards with the launch of The Gift Card Shop from InComm Payments on its e-commerce site, TikTok Shop.

The new shop means TikTok users don’t have to leave TikTok to make digital card purchases. Multi-merchant gift cards, such as those from Zift, and brand-specific ones are available on the site. InComm, in its 2026 Holiday Report, found that 69% of gift recipients consider digital gift cards just as thoughtful as physical ones and other kinds of gifts.

In addition to gift cards, 
InComm
 also has been developing its offers platform. In September, Sam’s Club began using InComm to create personalized digital offers for its customers.

In related news, online department store Lord & Taylor is adding 
Zip Co Ltd.
 as a buy now, pay later option. Lord & Taylor has been an e-commerce retailer only since 2021.

Lord & Taylor says the addition is meant to close gaps in its payments options, reach new customer segments, and boost revenue.

In October, Zip expanded its partnership with Best Buy Co. Inc. with its BNPL payment also available in Best Buy physical stores. Then, Zip said it had more than 32,600 U.S. merchant partners and 4.7 million active U.S. customers.

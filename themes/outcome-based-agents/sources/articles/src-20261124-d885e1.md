Cardano
 (
CRYPTO:ADA
) stands at 14.4 cents, with a potential drop of 30.6% to 10 cents or a climb of 38.9% to 20 cents. Cardano has fallen sharply, down 29.4% over the past month, and is now 95.3% below its all-time high of $3.09, reached in September 2021.

The last time ADA closed above 20 cents was on October 28, but it hasn’t touched 10 cents since November 2020. So which is the Cardano price more likely to reach first?

Cardano Last Hit 20 Cents in November 2026 and 10 Cents in November 2020

ADA last approached the 20-cent mark on November 4, briefly touching an intraday high of 20.2 cents but closing the day at 19.7 cents. The last daily close above 20 cents was on October 28, at 20.6 cents. Since then, selling pressure has increased, pushing ADA down to an intraday low of 13.3 cents on November 21—the lowest price seen since December 2020, when it reached 12.9 cents.

Back in November 2020, ADA opened at 9.3 cents, dipped to 8.8 cents, and closed the month at 16.3 cents. This increase was fueled by 
the Shelley upgrade
, which allowed holders to stake ADA, thus securing the network and earning rewards.

ADA Trades Below Its Moving Averages as Bitcoin Slips Under $60,000

In 2026, ADA has shown some recovery, bouncing back from the 13-cent range earlier this year. It reached 13.8 cents in June, stuttered at 19.98 cents in July, and climbed to 25.9 cents in August. Despite the recent challenges, as we discussed in 
whether Cardano is dead
, the initial low isn’t always the final downfall.

ADA is trading below its moving averages. It’s under the 20-day moving average of 16 cents, and its 50-day and 200-day averages both hover around 20 cents. Trading below these averages indicates a downward trend, and any attempts to rally toward 20 cents will likely meet resistance there, where sellers typically wait.

Bitcoin
 (
CRYPTO:BTC
) isn’t providing any support either. On November 24, it trades at $59,508—down 4.2% over the last 24 hours and 25.3% over the past month. Bitcoin’s drop below the $60,000 mark has erased prior gains in the crypto market. In contrast, ADA has taken an even bigger hit, losing 29.4% over the same month.

Cardano Voters Are Pushing Back on a 25 Million ADA Treasury Request

The Cardano Foundation, a nonprofit organization supporting the network, submitted a proposal on November 16 requesting around 25 million ADA (approximately $3.6 million) from the community treasury to fund activities in 2027. This request requires a vote by delegated representatives (DReps) who represent ADA holders. Early voting results indicate stronger opposition to this request, with voting ongoing until December 20.

If the proposal is rejected, the requested ADA—less than 0.1% of the total circulating supply—will remain in the treasury and off the market. The opposition demonstrates the community’s concerns about spending while the price continues to decline.

In September, ADA had risen to 26.5 cents following the Van Rossum hard fork, a network upgrade introduced on July 18, but it has since lost those gains. The upcoming Dijkstra upgrade aims to complete the first phase of coding by the end of the fourth quarter, as stated on Cardano.org.

Is the Cardano Price More Likely to Hit 10 Cents or 20 Cents First?

ADA is more likely to reach 10 cents before 20 cents. It’s trading below all three moving averages, and Bitcoin is under $60,000, while the 30.6% drop to 10 cents is shorter than the 38.9% climb to 20 cents. The 13.3-cent low from November 21 is the level to watch. A close below it would mark ADA’s lowest price since December 2020, with 10 cents the next target below.

ADA could still reach 20 cents first. For that to happen, ADA would need to close above its 20-day average of 16 cents, and Bitcoin would have to rise back above $68,540. If both conditions are met before the treasury proposal voting ends on December 20, 20 cents could become the more realistic target, and our outlooks on 
whether Cardano will recover in 2027
 and 
getting back to $1 by 2028
 could improve.

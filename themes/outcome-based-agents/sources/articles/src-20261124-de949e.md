Demolition bids opened for former EMA building

        Commissioners also approve IT contract termination agreement
    

 

The former Carroll County Emergency Management Agency building at 11 E. Main St. in Carrollton is slated for demolition. County commissioners opened three bids for the project Nov. 23, ranging from $24,500 to $122,000, and tabled the bids until Nov. 30 for further review.

AD

Carroll County commissioners opened three bids for demolition of the former Emergency Management Agency building at 11 E. Main St. in Carrollton during their Nov. 23 meeting, with proposals ranging from $24,500 to $122,000.
The bids include demolition and post-demolition work on the property. Commissioners tabled the proposals until Nov. 30 to allow for further review before selecting a contractor.

AD

Clerk Melissa N. Schaar conducted the bid opening for Package A of the county's Building Demolition and Site Revitalization program.
Martin Construction & Demo LLC of Big Prairie submitted the lowest bid at $24,500, including $20,000 for demolition and $4,500 for post-demolition work.
J Green Excavating LLC of Mechanicstown submitted a bid of $89,960, including $75,960 for demolition and $14,000 for post-demolition work.
ProQuality Demolition of Youngstown submitted the highest bid at $122,000, including $100,000 for demolition and $22,000 for post-demolition work.
Officials explained that the county had established a list of prequalified contractors for demolition projects, allowing companies working in both Carroll and Holmes counties to submit proposals.
The former EMA building is the first property being considered, with three additional properties expected to be included in a future bid package.

AD

Commissioners thanked the contractors for their interest and agreed to delay awarding the contract until the bids could be reviewed.
In another matter, commissioners approved signing a mutual consent contract termination agreement with Ruts Consulting Group LLC and authorized the final payment, bringing the county closer to concluding its IT services contract with the company.
The action follows the commissioners' Nov. 13 special meeting, when the board voted to pursue a $13,000 closeout rather than continue into the third year of the agreement.
In other business, commissioners:
— APPROVED bills totaling $109,103.86, along with bills without prior authorization.
— APPROVED a $42,000 contract with Verdantas for an update to the county's comprehensive plan, with funding coming from economic development funds.
— APPROVED an $8,400 transfer of funds to cover a shortage in the county law library's 2026 budget.
— RECEIVED Dog Warden Merle Long's report for Nov. 15-21, showing two dogs impounded, two adopted, none redeemed or euthanized and no citations issued. Dog licenses for 2027 are now on sale.
— APPROVED seasonal 25% weight reductions on all county roads from Dec. 1, 2026, through May 1, 2027, as recommended by County Engineer Brian J. Wise.
— ANNOUNCED that the commissioners' office will be closed Dec. 9-11 while commissioners and staff attend the County Commissioners Association of Ohio winter conference in Columbus. There will be no commissioners meeting Dec. 10.
— MOVED the Dec. 14 commissioners meeting to 1:30 p.m. because of the conference.
— REPORTED that challenge coins have shipped, with a tentative emergency services appreciation ceremony planned for Dec. 17.

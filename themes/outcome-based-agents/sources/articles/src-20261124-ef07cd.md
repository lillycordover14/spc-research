Adelaide man Adonis Georgiou faces jail over cocaine trafficking network

24 / 11 / 2026
 

Â·

 
The Greek Herald

A 21-year-old man has asked a South Australian court to spare him jail after pleading guilty to trafficking hundreds of grams of cocaine through an undercover police operation.

Adonis Walter Georgiou
 appeared in the District Court earlier this month for sentencing submissions after admitting to multiple counts of cocaine trafficking between October 2025 and May 2026.

Prosecutors told the court Georgiou sold 611g of cocaine over six months, including 279g of pure cocaine valued at up to $199,600.

One of the more unusual details involved a secret compartment behind the steering wheel of his car, which prosecutors said Georgiou used to conceal drugs during a meeting with an undercover police officer.

Georgiou also pleaded guilty to money laundering involving $4,500.

His lawyer, Michael Woods, argued that his client deserved consideration for home detention, pointing to his age, rehabilitation efforts and lack of previous criminal convictions.

The court heard Georgiou was working at a tobacco shop alongside co-accused Luke Marrollo, where the pair were also involved in selling illegal vapes.

Mr Woods said Marrollo had initially established contact with the undercover officer and introduced the possibility of selling drugs. Georgiou became involved through their existing friendship.

He also argued the so-called James Bond-style compartment was more a sign of bravado and immaturity than sophisticated criminal planning.

Prosecutors, however, pushed for an immediate prison sentence.

The wider police operation resulted in the seizure of 1.8kg of cocaine, more than $30,000 in cash, cryptocurrency, a firearm, illegal tobacco products and illicit vapes.

Marrollo is yet to enter pleas to multiple drug trafficking charges and is due to face court later this month.

Georgiou is expected to be sentenced in January 2027.

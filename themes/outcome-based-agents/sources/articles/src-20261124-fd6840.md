Weather forecast - file


	CAIRO - 24 November 2026: The Egyptian Meteorological Authority (EMA) has announced that the latest satellite imagery indicates a continued, slight decline in temperatures on Tuesday, affecting the northern part of the country, including Greater Cairo and northern Upper Egypt.


	 


	 

Temperatures in Greater Cairo


	 


	The Meteorological Authority noted that the daytime high in Cairo will drop to 24°C, while nighttime temperatures will fall to 15°C.

ADA, Okla. (KXII) - An Ada couple has died after a house fire. 
It happened earlier this month at a home on County Road 3530.
The home was fully engulfed when firefighters arrived, and Ronnie Cheek, 76, and Judy Cheek, 69, died at the scene.
The fire is believed to have started in one of the bedrooms.
The state fire marshal’s office is investigating the cause of the blaze.

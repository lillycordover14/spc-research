(KMAland) -- East Atchison, Mound City, Platte Valley, Nodaway Valley and Albany picked up victories on Tuesday night in KMAland Missouri boys basketball action.
Carter Oswald and Josh Schlueter combined for 50 points in East Atchison's win over Plattsburg, while Moses Wieland scored 27 points to lead Albany past Northeast Nodaway.
Jax Cowden poured in 23 points for Nodaway Valley, Grady Gockel scored 20 to pace Platte Valley and Cole White led Mound City with 16 points, while North Nodaway suffered a loss to North Harrison.

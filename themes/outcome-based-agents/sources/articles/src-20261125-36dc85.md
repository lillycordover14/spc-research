Global artificial intelligence (AI) audio company ElevenLabs said on the 25th that it participated as a technology partner in The Collaboration 2026, an AI short film project.
The Collaboration 2026 is an AI short film project co-hosted by Creative Mango and the 52nd Seoul Independent Film Festival. Seven Korean film directors — Ahn Sang-hoon, Lee Myung-se, Jang Cheol-soo, Shin Yeon-shick, Park Shin-woo, Cho Ba-reun and Kim Da-min — collaborated with nine AI directors and global AI technology companies to produce seven short films.
ElevenLabs provided AI audio production tools for "The Signal," a short film produced by director Ahn Sang-hoon, whose films include "Empire of Lust," and AI director Park Hyun-shin.
The seven short films produced through the project will be unveiled on the 27th at CGV Apgujeong in Seoul. Alongside the screenings, the directors will discuss their production processes and AI technology companies will deliver keynote speeches. Each film is scheduled to be screened once more during the 52nd Seoul Independent Film Festival.
Hong Sang-won, who heads ElevenLabs' go-to-market (GTM) strategy for Korea and Japan, said, "We are pleased to participate as a production partner in a project where Korean filmmakers and AI creators are experimenting with a new cinematic language. We will continue to provide AI audio production tools for various projects so creators can bring their ideas to life without technical constraints."

KUALA LUMPUR (Nov 25): Kuala Lumpur Kepong Bhd (KL:
KLK
) posted its highest quarterly net profit in more than four years, driven by improved operating performance and lower losses from its investment in UK-listed Synthomer plc.

Net profit for the fourth quarter ended Sept 30, 2026 (4QFY2026) surged nearly sixfold to RM549.58 million from RM95.96 million a year earlier — KLK's highest quarterly earnings since 3QFY2022 when it reported RM558.3 million.

Quarterly revenue rose 19.7% to RM7.54 billion from RM6.30 billion, the plantation group's bourse filing on Wednesday showed.

KLK declared a final dividend of 40 sen per share, payable on Feb 16, 2027, bringing its full-year dividend to 60 sen per share, unchanged from the previous financial year.

The plantation segment recorded a 30.9% increase in quarterly profit to RM819.4 million, supported by higher crude palm oil (CPO) and palm kernel selling prices, improved sales volumes and lower production costs.

The group also achieved record plantation yields in FY2026, reflecting improved productivity, operational efficiency and the rehabilitation of previously acquired estates.

Meanwhile, the manufacturing segment returned to profitability, mainly driven by a recovery in its oleochemical business, despite wider losses from its refinery operations.

KLK also recognised its maiden profit contribution of RM30 million from MP Evans Group plc in the fourth quarter and expects to recognise a full year's contribution in FY2027.

On a quarter-on-quarter basis, KLK swung back into the black from a net loss of RM1.34 billion in 3QFY2026, while revenue rose 6.9% from RM7.05 billion.

Despite the strong fourth-quarter performance, KLK posted a net loss of RM118.39 million for the full year against a net profit of RM817.28 million in the previous year, even as revenue grew 9.9% to RM27.50 billion.

The loss was largely due to the RM1.62 billion impairment recognised in the third quarter on its investment in loss-making specialty chemicals associate Synthomer.

Excluding the impairment and its share of losses from Synthomer, KLK said its full-year net profit would have risen 66.3% to RM1.67 billion.

“The impairment of our investment in Synthomer provides greater clarity on the group’s earnings outlook going forward,” KLK chief executive officer Lee Jia Zhang said in a separate statement.

Looking ahead, the group expects CPO prices to remain supported by sustained biodiesel demand, particularly from Indonesia's B50 mandate, and a seasonal decline in production, with potential upside arising from El Niño conditions in early 2027.

While KLK expects its oleochemical business to sustain its recovery, it cautioned that its refinery operations could continue to face challenges in FY2027 amid excess capacity and weak refining margins.

“Moving ahead, we will continue to strengthen our upstream operations, improve operational efficiency and reposition our downstream business towards higher value-added markets. We are assuredly confident of delivering a good operational performance in FY2027,” Lee added.

Shares of KLK closed up 26 sen or 1.25% at RM21.06 on Wednesday, valuing the group at RM23.5 billion.

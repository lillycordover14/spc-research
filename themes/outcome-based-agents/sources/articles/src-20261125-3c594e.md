San Antonio Spurs guard Dylan Harper is currently out with an left hip strain, and his absence could expose a De'Aaron Fox flaw. Fox has taken a backseat offensively this season despite playing more with the ball in his hands. 

As a result, he's currently averaging just 12.4 points, the second lowest scoring season of his career. That is cause for concern with him struggling to get to the basket and his 3-point shot not falling at a reliable enough clip to compensate. 

In fact, he's connecting on a career-low 26.5% from three. Nevertheless, San Antonio will need to
 rely on Fox more without Harper
, and he hasn't proven that he can help carry the scoring load this season. 

Dylan Harper's injury may expose De'Aaron Fox's offensive regression

Fox's play has flown under the radar, with San Antonio getting off to a dominant start. But, in many ways, the problems that plagued him in the NBA Finals have returned this season. He isn't nearly explosive as he once was and isn't able to gain the same separation that he did when playing with the Sacramento Kings. 

As a result, opposing guards can stay in front of him, and he hasn't been able to finish at the same level as he did at his peak. To his credit, he has been terrific as a passer and has helped San Antonio's offense by limiting turnovers, but that's not nearly enough. 

They are paying him nearly $50 million this season, and him averaging 12.4 points and 5.8 assists isn't going to cut it. If Harper misses three weeks, then San Antonio will need Fox to actually look to score, and that's something that he hasn't been asked to do thus far.

The Spurs need more from De'Aaron Fox in Harper's absence

Fox is currently taking fewer shots than Devin Vassell and only slightly more than Julian Champagnie. That should tell you all that you need to know about his aggressiveness this season. It's one thing to be selfless, but come on, we need more from Fox.

It was one thing when he was dishing out eight assists per game earlier this season, but he isn't even leading the team in assists any more. He's not standing out as either a scorer or a playmaker. 

Yes, he still has a positive impact on the offense, especially with him being a low turnover option. Even so, right now he is more of a role player than a star. It's hard to believe that he was averaging 25 points prior to joining the team less than two years ago.

De'Aaron Fox must rediscover his scoring in Harper's absence 

He likely won't get back to that level, but the Spurs still need for him to look to score. That starts with him being more aggressive, and Fox isn't going to score by taking just 8.5 shots per game. 

Even if he isn't the same player physically, he can still find ways to score. Whether it be by taking more floaters, mid-range jumpers, or threes, if he can't consistently finish at the rim. 

Fox might sacrifice some efficiency, but he isn't going to rediscover his scoring ability by taking only a handful of shots per game, especially in Harper's absence. Harper's absence gives Fox the opportunity to reestablish himself, but it's up to him to take full advantage.

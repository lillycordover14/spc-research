PolyAI – Enterprise Conversational AI, Voice Automation & Agent Development
November 25, 2026
Read the article 
PolyAI is an enterprise conversational AI platform developed by PolyAI, a company founded in 2017 by Cambridge researchers. It builds agents for customer service calls, with capabilities covering questions, bookings, payments and call routing. Its proprietary models support conversations, while integrations connect agents to business systems. Dialog Studio provides tools for building, testing and deploying agents, and a Python development kit supports teams working in code. The platform also supports chat and messaging, conversation oversight and performance monitoring. Platform voice usage is priced per minute, including maintenance and ongoing support; rates require discussion with sales. Separate model API access is available. Current pricing is listed on the official website.

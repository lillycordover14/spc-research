Lorikeet – AI Customer Support Platform, Workflow Automation & Multichannel Agents
November 25, 2026
Read the article 
Lorikeet is an AI customer support platform developed by the company founded by Steve Hind and Jamie Hall. It serves businesses with complex support processes, including fintech, healthcare and insurance teams. Its agents use company documentation and policies, retrieve customer information and perform actions through connected business systems. Supported channels include chat, email, voice, SMS and WhatsApp. Teams can test workflows using historical tickets and simulated scenarios, configure guardrails and transfer conversations to people. Coach provides setup assistance and quality scoring. Start costs US$2,100 monthly and Scale US$5,100 monthly, both paid annually, plus usage charges. Signature has custom pricing. Teams should confirm the full deployment quote and applicable usage rates.

CORE STRENGTHS
Actions through connected business systems
Support across five communication channels
Configurable permissions and operational guardrails
Historical ticket replay before deployment
Quality scoring through Coach

IDEAL FOR
Support teams in fintech, healthcare and insurance that need agents to execute complex workflows within defined policies.

HIGHLIGHTS
Zendesk and Salesforce integrations
HubSpot and Front integrations
Voice, chat, email, SMS and WhatsApp
Start, Scale and Signature plans
Thirty-day free trial listed

POTENTIAL CONSIDERATIONS
Annual payment for Start and Scale
Usage charges supplement plan fees
Voice pricing requires consultation
Workflows need configuration and testing
Confirm data residency and contractual requirements

Peek inside Maine’s Book Nooks&nbsp;&nbsp;The Portland Press Herald

[page text]
Reading shapes our lives, and no more so than when we keep a personal library. Bookshelves become an anchor in our homes: a collection of knowledge, memories and beloved stories that we share with family and friends. 

Over the past few months, we’ve invited readers to share their Book Nooks, and the responses have shown us just how personal these spaces can be. Some are filled with family memories and treasured collections. Others are just comfy places to settle in with a good book.

We’re wrapping up 2026 with a collection of submissions from readers. Take a look at how your neighbors have made room for reading in their homes, and start thinking about sharing your own.

In 2027, Book Nook will become a monthly feature!
 Each month, we’ll share a reader’s favorite reading space and randomly select one entrant to receive an exclusive Vic the News Goat tote bag.

Want to participate? Send a photo of your Book Nook and a short description of what makes it special to 
[email protected]
. We can’t wait to see where you read.

We made a pretty sweet little library for the grandkids by repurposing the closet under the stairs with lots of books, plus art and lights. Much cozier than Harry Potter’s space!

— Beth Goodwin

We are excited to show you our living room Book Nook. Our friend Jean custom-built the bookcase for our new little home in Scarborough, my friend Anita reupholstered the “found” chair, and we added some of our favorite things to the groups of books.

Sadly, we had to give away many of our books when we downsized to this house but still kept our favorites to read and read again in this little library corner.

— Cheryl and Hank Lawson

My Book Nook covers an entire wall in our study. My husband and I are both retired teachers, so books have been the cornerstones of our lives. Our “nook” is a home library with various mementos perched here and there.

— Dinah Johnson

This is my favorite space in the house. Morning sunshine, a cup of coffee, a recliner, wood stove and all my favorite books.

— Donna L. Stevens

Bella the cat takes the warm chair when I go for a snack.

— Joseph Phillips

This is a photo of the Book Nook at my camp on Little Sebago Lake in Gray. During the summer season, I spend many hours in this corner reading, writing and listening to the loons.

— Marilyn Seguin

Here’s a photo of my Book Nook, located next to the fireplace and taken early on a weekend morning, when I’m getting ready to settle in with a good book and a cup of something warm, while Magnus, our corgi, has yet to stir.

The children’s books on the cushion are neatly arranged and awaiting the arrival of a grandchild who likes the cozy nook when reading, her back against one wall and toes on the other, waiting for her “Bestefar” to bring a warm cup of cocoa.

— Mark Terison

My home library wall makes this room a favorite. The Cape-style house was built in 1860 and feels extra homey and perfect for an escape into books of all kinds, especially historical fiction, which is my favorite.

— Ms. Brooks Sharp

As a retired teacher and librarian, I am still an avid reader of children’s books. I have a very difficult time parting with my books. Now I’ve organized them in my basement for my young granddaughters, who, thankfully, love it when I read to them!

I’ve also included a picture of the wall beside my bookshelves displaying some of the many craft projects I’ve made with the toddlers and preschoolers after reading books to them when I was the Story Lady at North Gorham Public Library.

— Paula Boyce

This Book Nook serves as a wall divider in the South Portland home of two artists! Measuring over 13 feet wide by 10 feet tall, it has “Indian clubs” along the top, a turn-of-the-20th-century piece of exercise equipment.

— Peter Thibault and Frances Hamilton

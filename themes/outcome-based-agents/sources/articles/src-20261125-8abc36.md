Mobile gaming, consoles like the Nintendo Switch and Switch 2, and handheld PCs are all extremely popular. People love to game on the go and take premium gaming experiences with them everywhere, whether that’s a hotel room or a friend’s house.
 
The ZIP Pro 
lets you set up a 1080p gaming experience anywhere
, as long as you have a suitable wall to project against. The projector’s Z-fold shape doubles as an adjustable stand, letting you fine-tune the position of the 60 to 80-inch image.

Credit: Aurzen

 Best of all, if you own a Nintendo Switch console, you don’t even have to bring your dock. The ZIP Pro acts as a dock and will activate the console’s TV mode with just one cable. In fact, any device that supports USB-C DisplayPort Alt Mode will work, whether that’s a handheld PC, gaming laptop, or smartphone.
 
Using the projector’s dedicated Game Mode, response time is cut down to the 20 to 40ms range, so it’s perfect for impromptu Street Fighter II tournaments.
 
The built-in battery offers up to 2.5 hours of runtime in ECO mode, and with built-in Dual 2W Stereo Speakers (another thing you don’t need to transport).
 

                        Project patterns and plans wherever you need them
               

Credit: Aurzen

 The great thing about projectors is they can project onto anything. Using the ZIP Pro’s unique capabilities, you could, for example, project sewing patterns onto your work surface.
 
The Time-of-Flight autofocus keeps fine pattern outlines sharp, and four-point keystone correction helps align the projected image with your workspace. You can cast directly to the projector as you work using USB-C, AirPlay, Miracast, or Google Cast.

Cherno More Ticha Varna
 (NBL) inked 28-year old American 204cm center 
Chevez Goodwin
 (college: 
USC
). He already started the season at 
NHSZ-Szolnoki Olajbanyasz
 in Hungarian A Division. In 9 games he averaged 12.9ppg and 9.9rpg. He also played 7 games in FIBA Europe Cup where he had 9.3ppg and 8.3rpg.
Goodwin is a very experienced player. He has quite traveled the world as his pro career brought him to six different countries on three different continents (Europe, Asia and of course North America). Goodwin has played professionally in Germany (Seawolves), Greece (Aris and Kolossos), Spain (Lleida), Taiwan (DEA) and The Basketball Tournament (Aftershocks and WoCo Showtime).
Among many awards he won The Basketball Tournament championship title in 2025. He was voted to Eurobasket.com All-German Bundesliga Honorable Mention in 2024.
Goodwin graduated from University of Southern California in 2022 and it is his fifth season in pro basketball. He played for other colleges like: Wofford College, College of Charleston.

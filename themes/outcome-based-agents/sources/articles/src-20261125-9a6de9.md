Bitcoin hovers near $59,000, on pace for its worst month since June 2022
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Ines Ferré   
 
·
 Senior Business Reporter
 
 
Wed, November 25, 2026 at 12:43 PM CST
 
1 min read
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
BTC-USD
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Bitcoin (
BTC-USD
) trimmed losses on Wednesday, hovering near $59,500 per token amid a broader market sell-off, as the AI trade continued to come under pressure.
 
 
The trimmed steeper declines as President Trump announced tariffs of up to 20% on 
China and other trading partners.
 That move "would likely reinforce the Fed's hawkish posture and further pressure liquidity conditions," said Sean Farrell, head of digital assets at Funstrat.
 
 
 
 
 
"For now, the signal favors maintaining [bitcoin] exposure, but I would prefer to see broader confirmation from rates and credit before deploying additional cash," he added.
 
 
Bitcoin is down more than 20% 
so far in November
, putting it on pace for its worst monthly performance since June 2022, according to Yahoo Finance data.
 
 
 
The world's largest cryptocurrency is facing several headwinds. Spot bitcoin exchange-traded funds (ETFs) 
have seen sustained outflows
 throughout most of November, signaling weak institutional demand.
 
 
Bitcoin miners are facing pressure as investors pulled back from riskier assets and instead allocated capital toward this year's big IPOs. Mining companies have responded by selling their bitcoin holdings 
at a faster pace.
 
 
Analysts have also highlighted the impact of elevated interest rates, which have made investors less willing to take on riskier assets. Polymarket bettors place a 94% chance on the Federal Reserve 
raising interest rates at its December meeting.

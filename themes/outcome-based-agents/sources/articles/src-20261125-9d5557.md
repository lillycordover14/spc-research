Credit: Aurzen

Fabric artists know the pains of preparation before even getting to the sewing part of the project. Printing PDF patterns and dealing with a slew of papers can be cumbersome, but with the ZIP Pro, you can project digital sewing patterns directly onto your work surface.

The 1080p DLP image helps keep your pattern lines and markings legible, while ToF auto-focus and four-point keystone correction help you maintain clarity and proper alignment from front, side, or ceiling projection angles. With both wired and wireless connection options, you can get straight into your creative flow without fumbling through a complex setup.

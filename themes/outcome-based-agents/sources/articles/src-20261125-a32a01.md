Christmas in Cashmere brings Santa, live music and an artisan market downtown Dec. 5

 

										This year, Christmas in Cashmere brings Santa, the CHS band, a pony, live music and much more, beginning at 10 a.m. on Dec. 5.									

										CASHMERE CHAMBER OF COMMERCE									

		Posted
		

Tuesday, 

November 24, 2026 

5:01 pm

By ANDREW SIMPSON

Ward Media Staff Reporter

CASHMERE — Santa Claus will ride into downtown Cashmere on a firetruck Saturday, Dec. 5, as Christmas in Cashmere fills Cottage Avenue with live music, artisan vendors and free activities for kids.

The celebration runs from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Cottage Avenue will be closed from the bank to the fire station, according to the Cashmere Chamber of Commerce.

The Cashmere High School Band will play from 10 a.m. to noon, and a Christmas pony will be available for photos during those same hours. Santa is due downtown at 10:30 a.m. and will stay until shortly before noon, when he heads to Martin’s Market for photos from noon to 2 p.m. He’ll return downtown from 2 to 3 p.m.

Welter Brothers Music will play downtown from noon to 2 p.m., and Christmas llamas will be available for photos during that time. Children can also decorate cookies, make ornaments and play games for free. Participating local businesses will serve free hot chocolate.

The artisan market will run from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. at all locations, with vendors along Cottage Avenue and at Brick and Rafters and the Urban Buildings. Food trucks will also be on hand. For more information, call the Cashmere Chamber of Commerce at 509-782-7404.

For families who want to catch the firetruck, the high school band will already be playing when Santa arrives. And for kids who come downtown after lunch, there’s still time for a photo with a llama and a chance to catch Santa on his second trip down Cottage Avenue.

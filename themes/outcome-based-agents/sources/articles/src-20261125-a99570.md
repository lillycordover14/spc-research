LAS VEGAS (
FOX5
) — Las Vegas Raiders players Maxx Crosby and Ashton Jeanty hosted Thanksgiving events Tuesday for families.
Crosby hosts drive-thru pop-up food pantry
Crosby partnered with a local nonprofit to host a free Thanksgiving drive-thru pop-up food pantry at the M Resort. Crosby, his family and members of the Raiders’ defensive line helped hand out meals to families facing food insecurity.
Crosby said he hopes to keep helping the community in this way.
“It’s all about what you give to the world,” said Crosby. “It’s about taking the time and helping others.”
The event was in partnership with Sysco Foods and Three Square. Each family received a whole frozen turkey, shelf-stable Thanksgiving sides and fresh produce for their holiday dinner.
“It’s something I take a lot of pride in,” he said.

Jeanty serves meals with SafeNest
Raiders running back Ashton Jeanty and his Running to the Future foundation also served Thanksgiving meals Tuesday to families in need. 
The service was in partnership with SafeNest, a local nonprofit dedicated to helping survivors of domestic violence.
“You’re important. You matter,” said Liz Ortenburger, CEO of SafeNest, describing the message a star’s involvement sends to children. “It’s building what we call these little bricks of hope for our kids and our survivors.”
Jeanty helped serve the meals, took photos with fans and had activities for kids. SafeNest said Jeanty’s star power helps make events like this a little brighter.
More than 150 people in SafeNest’s programs received a holiday meal.

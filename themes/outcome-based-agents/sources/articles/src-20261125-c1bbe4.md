	CUMBERLAND COUNTY EMA RELEASES THANKSGIVING SAFETY GUIDELINES FOR LOCAL RESIDENTS

Shea James
 
0
 Like Post

 

 

As residents prepare to gather with family and friends for Thanksgiving, the Crossville/Cumberland County Emergency Management Agency (EMA) has issued a set of holiday safety reminders focused on cooking, home security, and travel.

To prevent kitchen fires—a common hazard during the holiday—officials urge residents never to leave stovetop food unattended and to keep flammable items, children, and pets away from hot appliances. For those deep-frying turkeys, the EMA emphasizes keeping fryers outdoors and away from structures, ensuring the bird is completely thawed, and avoiding overfilling the pot with oil. Having a working fire extinguisher nearby is also recommended.

For residents traveling or hosting guests, the agency advises locking all doors and windows, keeping exterior lights on, and storing gifts and valuables out of view from windows. Officials also caution against posting travel plans on social media until returning home and encourage residents to report any suspicious activity to local law enforcement.

Finally, the EMA reminds holiday motorists to plan ahead by checking weather and road conditions, allowing extra travel time, and getting adequate rest before driving. Drivers and passengers are urged to wear seatbelts, properly secure children in car seats, and avoid distracted or impaired driving.

The agency concluded its message by wishing the community a safe and enjoyable holiday.

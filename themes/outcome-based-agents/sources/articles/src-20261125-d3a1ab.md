NEWBURYPORT — The fourth annual Art & Soul Holiday Artisan Show takes place Saturday, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., at the Newburyport Brewing Co., 4 New Pasture Road, Newburyport.

The festive afternoon includes shopping, socializing, and maybe even a little beer drinking. Over a dozen local artisans, including jewelers, potters, weavers, photographers, fine artists and more will be there to help attendees tackle their holiday shopping.

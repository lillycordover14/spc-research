ElevenLabs to support AI short film production at The Collaboration 2026&nbsp;&nbsp;디지털투데이

[page text]
A scene from 'The Signal.'

Global AI audio company ElevenLabs will participate as a technology partner in the AI short drama film project 'The Collaboration 2026,' which will be held on Nov. 27.

In the project, ElevenLabs provided AI audio production tools for the short film 'The Signal' by director Ahn Sang-hoon (안상훈) and the AI director team led by Park Hyun-shin (박현신).

'The Collaboration 2026' is co-hosted by Creative Mango and the 52nd Seoul Independent Film Festival. It will present seven short films created through collaboration among seven Korean film directors including Ahn Sang-hoon, Lee Myung-se, Jang Cheol-soo, Shin Yeon-shick, Park Shin-woo, Cho Ba-reun and Kim Da-min, nine AI directors including Park Hyun-shin, Lee Ban-seok, Hong Jin-wook, Park I-do, Kang Seung-pyo, Bae Jae-hyung, Hwang Se-jun, Jang Hoon and Kim Dong-chan, and global AI technology companies.

ElevenLabs joined as a production partner for Ahn, who directed films including 'Age of Innocence,' and Park's AI director team. It provided audio production tools to Ahn and Park.

ElevenLabs' Japan and Korea GTM lead Sang-won Hong (홍상원) said, "We are very pleased to join as an official production support partner in a meaningful project where Korean film directors and AI creators experiment with a new cinematic grammar." He added, "We will continue to support AI audio tools for various projects so that creators can turn imaginative ideas into reality without technical constraints, bringing a new wave to the domestic video, audio and film markets."

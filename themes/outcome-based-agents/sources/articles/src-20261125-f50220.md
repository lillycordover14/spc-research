Ocean Pines hosts pre-holiday farmers and artisan market

November 24, 2026

Julia Green
, 

 

OCEAN PINES, Md.- Ocean Pines is helping residents get all their Thanksgiving ingredients in one spot, all while supporting local businesses.

The Ocean Pines Farmers and Artisan Market hosted their pre-holiday market. The event brought together local vendors, selling everything needed for the fall holiday.

Shawn Cross is the co-manager for the market, and explained the convenience of the event.

“Right before Thanksgiving, it gives the local people in Ocean Pines a chance to get back up with their local vendors, their bakers, their produce vendors, to come out, get their last minute shopping items that they’re going to need for their Thanksgiving Day feast.” Said Cross.

From fresh produce and homemade spice mixes, to dog treats and decor, the Tuesday market offered plenty of last minute essentials, that shoppers sure are “thankful” for.

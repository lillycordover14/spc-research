NILES - Patricia M. Clay, 63, died on Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, at Hospice House.

She was born on March 22, 1963, in Muncie, Indiana, a daughter of William Clay and Addie Mae Fife Clay.

Survivors include her son, Patches Hanshaw of Niles; daughters, Priscilla Miller, Thelma Hanshaw and Angie Hanshaw, all of Niles; brothers, Rodney, William, George, Roger, and Gary Clay; sister, Mary Prinkey; her 10 grandchildren; and three great-grandchildren.

She was preceded in death by her sister, Laurie Carroll; two brothers; and her lifetime partner, Michael Zapp.

A celebration of life will take place at a later date.

Arrangements by Holloway-Williams Funeral Home.

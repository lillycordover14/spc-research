MEMPHIS, Mo. (WGEM) - The Scotland County R-1 School District will receive more than $200,000 to complete its ADA Sidewalk Improvement Project.
Superintendent Ryan Bergeson said discussions about public accessibility have been a main point of discussion during his time with the district.
“It’s been something in our long-range plan for a long time,” Bergeson said. “I think clear back to when I started here, we talked about access to the public and ADA sidewalks. If you’ve ever been to the softball games before we got the project done, there was no sidewalk to the backstop, so you’re on uneven ground. If you have any mobility issues at all, it really caused problems for people to get down there.”
Bergeson also said that in smaller communities, the school can serve as a common place for gatherings and events.
Bergeson said the district made a specific request in the awarded grant when the opportunity became available.
“It’s something we wanted to mirror at the softball field, as well,” Bergeson said. “If you look at our long-range planning, we’ve always talked about ADA accessibility and connected sidewalks, and it’s just something that’s always been on there. When the TAP project became available, we wrote a grant specifically for that and we were lucky enough to be awarded the grant and get it completed.”
Contractors were given 90 days to complete the work. Crews completed 13,000 square feet of sidewalk in less than four months.

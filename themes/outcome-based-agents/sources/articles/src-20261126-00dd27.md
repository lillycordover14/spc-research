Brandy Turpin of the Ada Area Chamber of Commerce reads the number of the winning ticket in the $10,000 Santa Shops Here giveaway in 2023. The event is underway in its sixth year this year and the drawing is set for Dec. 16.

Holiday shopping is in full swing after the Thanksgiving and Black Friday holiday, but a community contest will see one lucky local spender win $10,000.
The Ada Area Chamber of Commerce kicked off its annual Santa Shops Here event, which takes course over several weeks in an effort to encourage shopping local during the holidays. The promotion kicked off Friday, Nov. 6 in its sixth year and will culminate Wednesday, Dec. 16 when one lucky winner goes home $10,000 richer.

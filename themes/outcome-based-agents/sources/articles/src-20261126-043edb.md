LAFAYETTE, La. (Acadiana News First) - Lafayette restaurant owner Ema Haq, who was born in Bangladesh, opens his doors every Thanksgiving to people who may not have a place to go.
Haq is the owner of Ema’s Cafe and Bailey’s Tandoori Grill. He started the Thanksgiving meals in 1993.
“I think I’m very blessed,” Haq explained. “I think if God gave us, I think we have an obligation to help out the people who are not as fortunate as we are.”
He described the menu for the meals.
“The turkey, rice dressing, corn maque choux. I’m going to make green beans, sweet potato yams, bread,” he said.
Haq came to Louisiana in the 1980s for college. He credited his parents and a priest in Bangladesh with helping him get there.
“Father Pichero said, ‘Ema, have you ever heard about Louisiana?’ I said, ‘No.’ ‘I know New Orleans,’ I said. Look, it’s very much like Bangladesh. You know, people eat spicy food, hot, and humid,” he recalled.
At 19, Haq made his way to what was then the University of Southwestern Louisiana. He studied mechanical engineering, earned his degree, and worked for a while as a mechanical engineer in the oil industry.
He noted how he did as a student.
“I think I could have done better, but obviously, I paid for my college, went to school. I think I was decent,” he added.
Haq became a U.S. citizen in 1997. He described that day.
“That was special. You know, went to Shreveport. Really a great feeling to be an American,” he said.
He later entered the food industry through his restaurants, offshore catering, and food services, and when people called, he answered.
“This country gave me a lot of opportunity, and then until I die, I’ll continue to do so,” he stated.
Haq has served the Acadiana area for decades. He and his volunteers prepare nearly 2,000 meals on Thanksgiving Day. He now calls Acadiana home.
He expressed why he continues to share his meals.
“As a person, you know, I mean, God, I’m so blessed. And we need to share our blessings. I think it’s, if I don’t do it, I don’t think I’m going to feel good about myself,” he explained.
Thank you, Ema.

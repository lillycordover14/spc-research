When 
the NCAA Transfer Portal 
opens each season, most of the attention is on where some of college football's best Division I talents are headed. 
And understandably so. After all, most of those players end up being program-changers; some even get the opportunity to take their talents to the next level of the sport eventually. 
However, that doesn't mean programs, especially ones like Ohio State, can overlook players trying to make the jump from the lower levels of the college football scene, especially Division II. 
On Wednesday, Nov. 25, news broke that the Division II leader in passing yards, Augustana signal caller Gunnar Hensley, would be entering the Transfer Portal.

Hensley Could Be a Big Ten Quarterback
Former Ferris State quarterback Trinidad Chambliss made the jump from DII to Power Four back in the 2025 season before getting an injury break and going on to have a really strong career at the highest level.
From what Hensley has shown at the Division II level, like Chambliss, he makes an intriguing case to receive legitimate looks from colleges all across the Division I landscape. 
Whether the Buckeyes end up having any interest in pursuing Hensley remains to be seen, but regardless, his eventual destination will be worth monitoring, particularly if he lands at another Big Ten program.
Hensley will want to play, and there are certainly a number of schools in the conference that will be looking to re-up the quarterback position ahead of the 2027 season. 
Illinois's Katin Houser is in his final season of eligibility, while Indiana, with Josh Hoover and Grant Wilson set to graduate, and USC, which will be without Jayden Maiava and Sam Huard, are both expected to be on the lookout for their next signal caller in the transfer portal. 
USC has always loved a good passing quarterback, and someone like Hensley, who threw for such a high volume of yards this past year, could end up making sense. 
Penn State is another interesting option, especially considering head coach Matt Campbell could be searching for an experienced transfer portal addition, too. Hensley already has three years of on-field experience under his belt.

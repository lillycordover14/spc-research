Raiders DE Maxx Crosby Makes Feelings Clear About Aaron Rodgers Amid Controversy

Raiders defensive end Maxx Crosby heaped praise on Aaron Rodgers after the Steelers released the veteran quarterback, but not every fan shared his view.

November 26, 2026 | 8:56 AM EST
 

Las Vegas Raiders defensive end Maxx Crosby did not hold back his praise for Aaron Rodgers after the Pittsburgh Steelers released the veteran quarterback this week. Crosby called Rodgers one of the “greatest quarterbacks ever playing this game” and said he sees him as a Hall of Famer.

Not everyone watching was in such a generous mood. Some fans applauded Crosby, while others took shots at Rodgers. A few critics argued that the way his time in Pittsburgh ended fits a pattern of messy exits.

The release came just days after the Steelers lost 19-16 to the Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday, Nov. 22. That leaves a question hanging over one of the league’s biggest names: Was that the last game of his career?

Maxx Crosby Heaps Praise on Aaron Rodgers

Crosby was asked about the Steelers cutting Rodgers in a video of his media session posted Wednesday on X. At first, he was not sure about the timing of the move. “Oh they cut him today?” Crosby said, before he took a moment to react to the news itself. “That’s crazy,” he added.

The Raiders pass rusher made clear he did not know the details behind the decision. Crosby said he did not know what happened or transpired. He guessed that there was more to the story than had come out so far, and he expected more information to surface. “People are gonna be digging for sure,” he said.

Crosby then turned to Rodgers’ career, and he was clear about where he ranks him. He said that “if that’s his final ride,” it is “probably not the most ideal” way to go out. He called Rodgers “somebody that changed the game, one of the most innovative I would say passers and players to play the game.”

“So I got a ton of respect for him,” Crosby said, adding that he wished Rodgers the best. He also compared the situation to what happened to Chris Paul, saying it felt like it happened overnight. Crosby added that “you don’t wanna see anybody go out like this.” He wrapped up by saying he had a “lotta respect for him.”

Plenty of fans liked what they heard from Crosby. “How can you not love Crosby!” one fan wrote on X.

Fans Take Aim at Rodgers’ Exit

The mood was much sharper among fans who were less impressed with Rodgers himself. One fan said Rodgers’ Hall of Fame moment could be awkward because of how his time with teams has ended. “He will be the first player to go into the HOF without a team to represent. He has burned every bridge with every team he was ever a part of. Maybe he can go in with a Cal U hat,” the fan wrote.

Rodgers’ last outing for Pittsburgh was a close loss. In the 19-16 defeat in Philadelphia, he completed 30 of 44 passes for 227 yards and one touchdown. The Steelers actually outgained the Eagles, 255 total yards to 214, but they could not turn that edge into a win.

The full story behind the release has not come out. Crosby himself said he expected there was more to it than what fans had seen so far.

For now, the big question is what comes next for Rodgers. Crosby framed it as a possible ending, not a certain one, saying “if that’s his final ride.” If it is, Rodgers will leave the game with a strong backer in the Raiders’ locker room, and a split among fans over how his career is wrapping up.

Acqua S.Bernardo Cantu
 (Serie A) inked 24-year old American 204cm power forward 
Kendal Coleman

CBU
). He already started the season at 
Promitheas

In 8 GBL games he recorded 11.3ppg and 6.6rpg. Coleman also played 7 games in FIBA Europe Cup where he averaged 11.6ppg, 8.0rpg and 1.3apg. And finally he recorded 3 games in Basketball Champions League where he had 12.7ppg and 5.3rpg. His team made it to the Greek final in 2026.
He was voted to Southland Conference All-Defensive Team back in 2022 at his college time.
Coleman graduated from California Baptist University in 2025 and it is his second season in pro basketball. He played for other colleges like: Louisiana State University, Northwestern State University.

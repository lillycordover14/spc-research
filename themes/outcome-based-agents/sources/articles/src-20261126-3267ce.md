THIS NOVEMBER, it is totally fine to retreat to your reading nooks all day and get lost in a world of words, adventures, and ideas. As the country commemorates National Book Month, OGAWA Philippines is supporting readers in building a habit of reading and learning. After all, the best reading experiences happen in a space that lets you fully bask in the pleasure of turning every page. 

“Reading is more than just a pastime; it is a great way to nurture our mental well-being. OGAWA is here to support bookworms and those just getting into the habit of reading. OGAWA wants to make those reading sessions worthwhile and more meaningful by helping them create a space where reading and relaxation go hand-in-hand,” said Shey Agustin, marketing executive of OGAWA Philippines.

Books and reading have become increasingly part of Filipinos’ lives. They frequent bookstores and book fairs to pick up the latest YA novels, fantasy books, history books, and classics. They add these books to their growing library, tucked in a warm, well-lit corner of their home. This is a bookworm’s safe haven—a space where every open book is a door to new wonders and possibilities. 

The shelves filled with books are the most important fixture of this corner. For a cozy, immersive reading experience, a nook needs a comfortable chair that turns the reading ritual into a retreat that soothes the mind and body.

OGAWA’s My Sofa Luxe Plus is the ideal addition to everyone’s reading corner. It is packed with features that will delight every bibliophile. As for one, its compact build makes it easy to integrate into reading nooks, big or small. Its Zero Wall feature is a space-saving design, allowing it to recline to about 12 cm from the wall.

More than something to sit on, the My Sofa Luxe Plus is packed with features that will delight every bibliophile. Whether they’re devouring a long novel, a book of poems, or a collection of short stories, this massage chair provides an ergonomic escape that keeps them comfortable and focused. Turning every page and opening a new chapter becomes an opportunity to nurture our imaginations and relax our bodies. 

My Sofa Luxe Plus’ zero gravity technology alleviates spinal pressure by creating a feeling of weightlessness, reducing pressure on the back. Moreover, the massage chair provides heated calf massages that ease tight tissues and calm the nervous system. Beyond its massage features, the My Sofa Luxe Plus also includes Bluetooth speakers for literature lovers who want to add a soundtrack to the stories unfolding before their eyes. 

The My Sofa Luxe Plus is ideal for every bookworm. Whether they’re trying to get through a reading slump, building a habit, or catching up on their to-be-read pile, OGAWA’s My Sofa Luxe Plus is the perfect chair to curl up, get comfy, and recharge with their favorite read.

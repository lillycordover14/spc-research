City Kickboxing feels like it is firing on all cylinders right now. Unfortunately, one of those cylinders is drama-powered.
The New Zealand gym has two great light heavyweights in UFC champ Carlos Ulberg and contender Navajo Stirling. Dan Hooker is back in the win column. And lightweight Tom Nolan just beat Renato Moicano in the first main event of his career.
But not everything is golden. 
There’s pressure
 for Ulberg and Stirling to fight. Before this last win, Hooker looked like his career 
(and life)
 was going off the rails. and CKB’s original star Israel Adesanya 
left the gym
 to do his own thing. Most recently, Ulberg responded to whispers that he and Adesanya don’t like each other because Ulberg dated one of Adesanya’s ex-girlfriends.
“False accusations, I don’t move like that,” Ulberg wrote on Instagram. “Someone tag Israel, that’s his move.”

In a new interview with Ariel Helwani, Dan Hooker suggested everyone shut the f--k up.
“Days of our f--king lives,” Hooker joked. “Brother, I have my own problems. It’s a lot of drama and I don’t care about f--king any of it. I don’t give a s--t about any of it. He didn’t write it on a note and hand it to me, he wrote it on social media. I don’t know what they’re up to, everyone just needs to shush.”
“Shush, shush,” he reiterated. “At the end of the day it’s none of my business, they’re all grown ups.”
“I like what Eugene Bareman said,” Hooker concluded. “All of this is family business and none of this needs to be put on social media, none of this needs to be spoken about in public, we all have each others numbers. If you have a problem, I’m on Team ‘This is family business and this should be taken care of behind closed doors,’ None of this needs to be done in the public eye.”

City Kickboxing has the advantage of being hella far away from the rest of the world, so you think their drama wouldn’t carry all the way to North America so easily. But all it takes is some loose lips around the gym or some twitchy Twitter fingers to reveal what’s really going on … or just enough to get UFC fans picking up on the drama and spreading it everywhere.
We’ll see if everyone takes Hooker’s advice and just shushes moving forward, because the spotlight is only getting brighter on the camp as a whole.

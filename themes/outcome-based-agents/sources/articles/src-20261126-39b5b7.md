KUALA LUMPUR (Nov 26): Mega Fortris Bhd's (KL:
MEGAFB
) first-quarter net profit jumped more than sixfold, supported by recovering customer demand and improved profit margins.

Net profit for the quarter ended Sept 30, 2026 (1QFY2027) surged to RM7.11 million from RM1.12 million a year earlier, while revenue grew 39.2% to RM52.44 million from RM37.68 million, its bourse filing showed.

This marked the security seal manufacturer's highest quarterly revenue since the quarter ended December 2024.

Gross profit margin improved to 48.4% from 42.5% a year earlier as revenue growth outpaced the increase in production costs.

Its manufacturing segment returned to profitability, posting a pre-tax profit of RM8.26 million against a loss of RM322,000 a year earlier. The Asia-Pacific region accounted for half of the group's revenue.

On a quarter-on-quarter basis, net profit more than doubled from RM3.5 million, while revenue increased 24.5%.

Despite the improved performance, Mega Fortris said ongoing geopolitical tensions continued to drive up raw material and shipping costs, while disruptions to shipping routes in the Middle East resulted in delivery delays.

Looking ahead, the group remains cautiously optimistic about FY2027, with plans to strengthen sales and improve operational efficiency.

Its UK manufacturing facility is now operational and progressing to its second phase, which is expected to shorten delivery times, reduce logistics costs and expand its international reach.

The group is also looking to develop its premium card business.

The improved earnings come after a volatile period for Mega Fortris' shares, which have triggered several intraday short-selling suspensions since late October.

The counter lost more than RM1 billion in market value over two trading days in October, prompting Bursa Malaysia to issue an unusual market activity query.

In response, Mega Fortris disclosed that its major shareholders had obtained a court order seeking documents relating to certain share transactions in March, although it could not establish a link between the court order and the sell-off.

Shares of Mega Fortris closed up three sen or 1.52% at RM2.01 on Thursday, valuing the group at RM1.7 billion. Year to date, the counter is up 130%.

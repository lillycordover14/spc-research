ElevenLabs Targets $33 Billion Valuation in Early 2027 Funding Round                    

By

PYMNTS
 

 | 

November 25, 2026

 | 

Listen to Article

Listen

ElevenLabs
, a builder of artificial intelligence (AI) voice models and agents, plans to launch a funding round process in the first three months of 2027 at a target valuation of $33 billion, Bloomberg 
reported
 Wednesday (Nov. 25), citing unnamed sources.

 

That figure would be up from the $22 billion valuation ElevenLabs achieved in September after an employee tender offer, according to the report.

ElevenLabs said in September that its annual recurring revenue (ARR) had reached $600 million. One of the unnamed sources told Bloomberg that the firm’s ARR is now $700 million, per the report.

ElevenLabs declined Bloomberg’s invitation to comment on the claims, per the report.

CEO 
Mati Staniszewski
 has said that ElevenLabs may start preparing for an initial public offering in 2027, according to the report.

The Financial Times reported in September that the share sale announced that month doubled ElevenLabs’ valuation and made the startup one of the 10 most valuable 
AI labs
 outside China.

About six months earlier, in February, ElevenLabs was valued at $11 billion when it raised $500 million from a group of Silicon Valley venture capital firms.

Staniszewski said in the September report, “We are competing for talent with the biggest AI labs. We need to stay competitive in that market, too. So of course, the secondary offers help with that.”

Bloomberg reported Nov. 3 that Staniszewski said ElevenLabs could begin preparing to 
go public
 in 2028.

Speaking at a Bloomberg Tech event in London, Staniszewski said, per the report, “We’d love to IPO, build a public company, build that step in a longer journey. IPO, of course, is not an end in itself. It’s a step in the journey.”

PYMNTS reported in August that ElevenLabs offers a licensing platform called Iconic Marketplace that connects brands with the rights holders of history’s most recognizable 
voices
.

With Iconic Marketplace, a brand, studio or publisher browses the catalog and submits a project request describing the format, audience and intended use. ElevenLabs then connects that request to the rights holder, whose approval and signed terms are required before any audio is generated. Approved uses span film and documentary narration, advertising, games, audiobooks, podcasts and educational content.

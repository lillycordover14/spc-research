MAXX CROSBY RESPECTS AARON RODGERS

The Aaron Rodgers saga in Pittsburgh is over. The Steelers 
have released the veteran quarterback
 after initially benching him for their Week 12 game against the Denver Broncos. Rodgers is one of the best quarterbacks of all time, and he was playing alright with the Steelers, but it sounds like his relationship with head coach Mike McCarthy soured.

Las Vegas Raiders edge rusher Maxx Crosby showed Rodgers a lot of respect when asked about the quarterback’s release. 
Vegas Sports Today on Twitter shared
 a video of Crosby finding out that the Steelers let Rodgers go. He was nothing but complimentary of Rodgers, saying that it’s unfortunate if this is how his career ends.

Raiders DE Maxx Crosby reacts to Aaron Rodgers getting cut by the Steelers:

“Aaron Rodgers is one of the greatest QBs to ever play this game. If that’s his final ride, that’s not his ideal way of going out. He’s a first-ballot Hall of Famer. He’s somebody who changed the game…

BRONCOS ADD DL

Despite all the drama, the Steelers still have a game to play this week. They’re facing the Denver Broncos on a short week. Thankfully, the game is being played in Pittsburgh, but it still figures to be an uphill battle for the Steelers. The Broncos are one of the AFC’s best teams.

The Broncos 
also announced on Twitter that
 they’ve added defensive lineman Khalen Saunders to their practice squad. Due to injuries to other players, Saunders could be elevated for this game. He entered the NFL in 2019 as a third-round pick by the Kansas City Chiefs, and he’s bounced around the league since then. We’ll see if he plays against the Steelers.

BRYCE YOUNG REACTS TO AARON RODGERS NEWS

The NFL world was shocked to see the Steelers bench Aaron Rodgers for Mason Rudolph late on Tuesday of this week. Things only got worse for Pittsburgh from there. Rodgers went on to bash Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy, leading the team to release him.

Carolina Panthers quarterback Bryce Young reacted to the news, 
as shared by Steve Reed on Twitter.
 Young said that he feels for Rodgers in this situation. That goes to show the respect Rodgers has, even from younger quarterbacks across the league. The Steelers will play the Panthers later this season, but Rodgers won’t be a part of that game.

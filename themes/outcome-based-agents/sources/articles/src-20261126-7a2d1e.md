HMT plans to augment production amid demand spike for its watches

Updated 
 - November 26, 2026 06:53 pm IST - Bengaluru
 The Hindu Bureau

Amid revival efforts, the renewed interest in Hindustan Machine Tools (HMT) watches has triggered a massive demand for over 1 lakh watches a month, according to the public sector unit’s (PSU) demand survey.
However, the PSU is currently able to assemble only 10,000 watches a month, amid a shortage of working capital. A senior HMT official said the rising demand has prompted the company to increase its production capacity to 13,000 units. “Meanwhile, a new production unit is being planned to increase capacity to 25,000 units. The goal is to meet at least half of the monthly demand by mid-2027,” the official told 
The Hindu
.
New designs
The HMT design team is also working on three new designs, according to the source. However, these are yet to be completed and finalised. “The designs are an outcome of the planning undertaken based on opinions collected from customers. We are trying to understand the pulse of the people and their expectations, based on which our internal team is working,” the official said.
HMT Kannadiga
The development comes amid brisk sales of the new variants of HMT Kannadiga, which have exceeded the PSU’s expectations. Between November 2 and 3, HMT sold more than 3,000 units.
“A few thousand more are currently in production and will be released soon. However, we may have to stop production after a point, as this was a limited edition variant,” the official explained. Union Minister for Heavy Industries and Steel H.D. Kumaraswamy unveiled the watches on November 1.
N. Ramesh Kumar, chairman and managing director of HMT, said the watch features a sunburst dial and genuine Italian leather straps. “If you look at premium watches with a sunburst dial, they would cost anywhere between ₹10,000 and ₹16,000. We have brought out a watch of comparable quality at ₹3,600,” Mr. Ramesh said.
Demand for old designs
Meanwhile, older brands such as the Sona and the Stellar series are also witnessing brisk sales.
Among watch enthusiasts, the HMT Roman had created a buzz. However, a source close to the development said the variant was unlikely to be restocked soon, as its case had been specially designed and sourced from manufacturers elsewhere. Doing it again will take some time.
Meanwhile, variants such as Gandabherunda, Vijay, and Kohinoor are being manufactured in large numbers and restocked frequently. Despite this, the watches sell out within days amid mounting demand.

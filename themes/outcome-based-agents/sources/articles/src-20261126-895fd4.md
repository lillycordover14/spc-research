BMTC invites tenders for 200 mini buses, to augment Metro feeder services&nbsp;&nbsp;Public TV English

[page text]
BENGALURU: In a boost for city commute, the Bengaluru Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC) has invited tenders for the purchase of 200 mini buses. Of these, 100 mini buses will be of 7 metre length and the remaining 100 buses of 11 metre length.

The BMTC has asked for design of buses, seating arrangement and design of door system in the tender document. All 200 buses will non-AC CNG-diesel buses and will be conductor-less. The buses should run in narrow roads, densely populated layouts also and hence 7-metre long buses were chosen. The buses will also have digital ticket vending machines installed.

The tender will be finalised after the design is approved. The buses will be used as Metro feeder services for passengers from Metro stations to improve last mile connectivity.

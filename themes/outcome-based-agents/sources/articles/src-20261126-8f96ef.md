Notre Dame keeping tabs on rising 2028 QB Nash Davis&nbsp;&nbsp;On3

[page text]
After a turbulent summer with quarterback recruiting in the 2028 class, Notre Dame is evaluating all options and does not have any offers out to uncommitted prospects at the position yet.

Position coach 
Gino Guidugli
 leaves no stone unturned in quarterback recruiting and is showing interest in one of the more intriguing passers in the cycle.

Birmingham (Ala.) Spain Park’s 
Nash Davis
, who is unranked on Rivals but has a three-star ranking by 247 Sports, visited Notre Dame for the SMU game on Nov. 21. Guidugli opened up a line of communication with Davis in the weeks leading up to the trip, which the 6-5, 220-pound signal-caller loved.

“I had a great time,” Davis told Blue & Gold. “The overall experience was great, and I really enjoyed getting to see the football facilities as well as the campus.

“The stadium atmosphere was crazy. It was definitely more than I expected. The fans are so bought into the program.”

Meeting with Guidugli and others was another top highlight of Davis’ trip.

“The staff was incredible,” he added. “I got to speak with Coach Gino a good bit along with others and they were all great to me. They showed that they were very interested in me, and they want to come see me again in the spring.”

Davis would be over the moon if he adds the Irish to his offer list that currently includes Wake Forest, Tulane, Jacksonville State, Samford, Murray State, UAB and USF.

“I loved Coach Gino,” Davis continued. “He seems like a great guy as well and a great coach. You can tell that all of the guys in that room enjoy being around him and want to learn from him.”

In the 10 games with stats recorded on his MaxPreps page, Davis completed 126 of 206 passes for 1,643 yards with 21 touchdowns and 5 interceptions.

“I’m a bigger quarterback who can make any throw on the field with a big arm, and I can use my legs when I need to. My IQ for the game has grown, and I’m working to grow it more every day.”

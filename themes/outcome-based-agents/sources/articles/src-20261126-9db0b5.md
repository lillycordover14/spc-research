Kharge asks Imran Masood to zip it on SP alliance

Thu, 26 November 2026

Share:

17:12

Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge/ANI Photo
 

															Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge on Thursday asked the party's outspoken Uttar Pradesh working president Imran Masood to focus on collective work rather than making statements.
Kharge's apparent reprimand came as Masood has been quite vocal amid the Congress' seat-sharing talks with its alliance partner Samajwadi Party for the Uttar Pradesh elections.
The two parties are yet to finalise a deal. 
The Congress is said to have handed a list of about 130 seats to the SP against Akhilesh Yadav's party's offer of 26.
Addressing the Samvidhan Raksha Sammelan here, Kharge said Masood always speaks with passion, be it in Parliament or in television debates, but the need of the hour is working collectively.
"Imran Masood ji puts forward his views with great enthusiasm. I also hear him regularly on television, where he speaks quite forcefully. But more than statements, we have to work on the ground," the Congress president said.
He said the party would progress only if its leaders and cadres worked together.
"The more we work collectively, the further we will move forward. When Imran Masood ji is in Parliament, he always fights for the party, trying to put forward his views and get them accepted," Kharge said.

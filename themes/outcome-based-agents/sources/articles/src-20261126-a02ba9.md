CLAY COUNTY, W.Va. (WCHS) — 
One of West Virginia’s brightest stars visited Clay County to help distribute holiday meals for those in need.
Actress and philanthropist 
Jennifer Garner took to social media
 Wednesday to share her volunteer work with 
Save the Children
 and 
the Mountaineer Foodbank
.
A distribution center was established to provide families in remote areas access to food ahead of Thanksgiving.
The food bank spokesperson said the distribution point typically serves 280 families, but expected to assist upwards of 350 ahead of the holiday.
"The Mountaineer Food Bank works tirelessly every single day to fight hunger across all of West Virginia. Lucky me to get to see their work in action and volunteer with Save the Children who is striving to do more together to help fight food insecurity in West Virginia,” Garner wrote. “Happy Thanksgiving to the team working hard behind the scenes, the incredible volunteers and families I met that day, and to you and yours!”
Food distribution sites may be found by using
 the food bank's online search feature
, calling 304-803-7721 or emailing 
findfood@mountaineerfoodbank.org
. 
Last year, 
Garner visited Clay County Schools 
to help package meals for a food delivery program, provide literacy lessons and participate in a roundtable to discuss school programming.
The Charleston native rose to fame with her lead role in the Alias television series back in the early 2000s and has since starred in several feature films including “Daredevil,” “13 Going on 30” and “Dallas Buyers Club.”

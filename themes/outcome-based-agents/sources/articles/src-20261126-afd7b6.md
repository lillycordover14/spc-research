Work is underway to link Clay and Dickinson counties with bike trail

 by 
Radio Iowa Contributor
 | 
Nov 26, 2026

State officials estimate there are over 2,500 miles of paved bicycle trails, and construction has begun on the second phase of a recreational trail that will connect trails in two northwest Iowa counties.

Erin Reed of the Dickinson County Trails Board says so far crews are making good progress.

“They’ve really been getting after it and installing a lot of the drainage pipes and doing the dirt work this fall,” Reed said. “They’re going to get as far as they can as long as the weather holds and then button up the site for the winter and then come back in the spring and finish with the paving.”

This latest project covers a two-mile stretch in Dickinson County.

“And then we’re following up with a bid letting this spring to do that last mile next summer as well,” Reed said. “We’re looking at late summer, early fall to be connected to Clay County.”

The current two-mile project is financed with $930,000 worth of grants from two state agencies and the Okoboji Foundation. Another $537,000 in grants have been lined up for the final mile that will connect to the Clay County line.

There are currently about 50 miles of paved trails in Dickinson County for biking, running and roller blading as well as cross-country skiing and snowmobiling in the depths of winter.

(By Ed Funston, KILR, Estherville)

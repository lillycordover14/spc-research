Holly Jolly Farmers & Artisan Christmas Market and Santaland return to Streets of Indian Lake 
	

                    By Chris Gregory                

on 

                    November 26, 2026                

ABBEY NUTTER
Hendersonville Standard
 

This Christmas tree marks the center of The Streets of Indian Lake, site of the Holly Jolly Farmers & Artisan Christmas Market. ABBEY NUTTER

The third annual Holly Jolly Farmers & Artisan Christmas Market took over the plaza in The Streets of Indian Lake last weekend.

“It’s really a launch to the holiday season, period, which includes Thanksgiving,” Cheryl Puryear said.

Saturday’s event is an extension of the Street’s of Indian Lake’s Santaland event programming, which has been providing visits and photos with Santa Claus inside for 18 years. The next Holly Jolly Farmers & Artisan Christmas Market will be Dec. 12.

“We use whatever space is available in the Streets, so we’ve had larger spaces and we’ve had vendors set up in those larger spaces, but then we ended up with two years with a small space,” Puryear said. “I’d been tinkering with moving it to the plaza anyway, so I just moved it to the plaza, so this is the third year of the Christmas market on the plaza.”

The Holly Jolly Farmers & Artisan Christmas Market is also considered an extension of the Hendersonville Farmers & Artisan Market. This year, 55 vendors filled the plaza, some of which were returning vendors from the Hendersonville Farmers & Artisan Market, some of which were returning vendors from previous years of the Holly Jolly Farmers & Artisan Christmas Market, and 13-14 of which were new vendors to the market.

“Everybody absolutely expects it,” Puryear said. “People reach out to me all the time asking, ‘When is this, when is that?’ Before they make their plans they want to know when we’re doing this. It’s always an excellent response.”

Santaland will continue at the Streets of Indian Lake through December at 300 Indian Lake Blvd., Unit C220. Hours will vary throughout the holiday season and can be viewed online at 
www.visitsantaland.com
. While walk-ins are welcome, appointments for photos with Santa Claus can also be made on the website.

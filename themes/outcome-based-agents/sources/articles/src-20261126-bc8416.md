GENESEO — Get ready to skip the retail outlets and buy holiday gifts from regional artists and craftspeople.
Come shop completely local at the “Live & Local Holiday Market” presented by the Genesee Valley Council on the Arts. The three-day shopping event is scheduled from Dec. 10 to 12 at the Big Tree Inn, 46 Main St., Geneseo. Hours are 11 a.m. until 7 p.m. each day.

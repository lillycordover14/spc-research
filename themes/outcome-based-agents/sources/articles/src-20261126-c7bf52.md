EDRA JANICE GAINES BEACH&nbsp;&nbsp;NEMOnews Media Group

[page text]
Edra Janice Gaines Beach, 87, of Shelbyville, Missouri, passed away Wednesday afternoon, November 18, 2026, at the Knox County Nursing Home surrounded by family.

She was born on January 17, 1939, near Hurdland, Missouri, the daughter of Albert D. and Hettie Mae Long McGlothlin.

Edra attended Novelty High School.

At the age of sixteen, she married Waldo Graydon Gaines on July 2, 1955, in Shelbina, Missouri. To this union, two girls and twin boys were born. He preceded her in death on September 23, 1991.

On February 14, 1993, in Leonard, Missouri, Edra married Weldon Leroy Beach, and he preceded her in death on June 21, 2019.

Waldo and she farmed just outside of Novelty, Missouri for more than 35 years, and Weldon and she farmed just north of Leonard, for more than 26 years.

In 1965, Edra joined the Elim Chapter Order of Eastern Star in Bethel, Missouri and is currently a member of the LaBelle Chapter #316. She later received her Fifty-Year Pin in 2015. Edra was a member of the Baring Country Club, where she served as a Board Member. Edra was an active member of the Locust Hill Community Church. She was an avid fan of the North Shelby Raiders.

Edra enjoyed the work of the Eastern Star and held many stations. She enjoyed owning a cabin at the Baring Lake and spent many years there. Most of all, she loved spending time with her children, grandchildren, and great grandchildren.

Edra is survived by her son, Brent and Kim Gaines of Shelbyville, Missouri; two daughters, Walla and Eric DeRosear of Baring, Missouri, and Lora and Bobby Pfanner of Monroe City, Missouri; grandchildren, Katie Simmons, Scott and La’Costa Gaines, Graydon and Ashley Gaines, and Danielle Sanders; great grandchildren, Jagger Gaines, Daxon Gaines, Lucy Sanders, and Mackinlee and Brynlee Gaines along with a host of friends.

As with all second marriages, Edra gained her extended family with Weldon’s family: Stella Bertoni, Harold and Jan Beach, and Sheryl and Carl Dodd, along with eleven step grandchildren, 14 step great grandchildren, and a step great-great grandchild.

She was preceded in death by her parents, Albert and Hettie McGlothlin; two husbands, Waldo Gaines and Weldon Beach; a brother, Alvin McGlothlin in 1957; a son, Trent Gaines on November 23, 2012; and her grandson, Joel Eric DeRosear on August 28, 2007; one step grandchild and 2 step great grandchildren.

Visitation, Order of the Eastern Star service, and Funeral Services were Friday, November 20, 2026, at the Doss Funeral Home in Edina, Missouri.

Interment was in the Leonard Cemetery, in Leonard, Missouri.

Reese Blaine officiated and music was provided by Jamee Beach. Casket Bearers were Scott Gaines, Graydon Gaines, John Morton, Matt Beach, Chris Beach, Brad Dodd, Glenn Dodd, and Will Dodd.

An expression of sympathy in memory of Edra may be left to the Leonard Cemetery. A memorial may be left at or mailed to the Doss Funeral Home 208 N. 4th Street, Edina, Missouri 63537.

Jennifer Garner helps Mountaineer Food Bank at Clay County distribution&nbsp;&nbsp;WV MetroNews

[page text]
CLAY, W.Va. 
— Actress Jennifer Garner spending part of her latest trip back home to West Virginia helping Mountaineer Food Bank distribute food to Clay County residents.

Jennifer Garner showing one of the items given away in a recent Mountaineer Food Bank distribution in Clay County. (Facebook)

Garner, who spent most of her childhood in Charleston and graduated from George Washington High School, made a 
Facebook video describing
 the distribution that took place last weekend in the parking lot of Clay County High School.

“Welcome to Mountaineer Food Bank distribution right here in Clay County, West Virginia, where there is no grocery store,” Garner said in the video. “There is no access to fresh food.”

Garner then went down the distribution line and described what was being given away in the drive-thru event.

“Per family, two bags of apples, two bags of carrots and a beautiful bag of potatoes,” Garner said as she went down the line. The distribution also included ham, blackberries, spaghetti, sauce, cans of corn and more.

The video included a quick interview by Garner that said 350 families were expected to be helped through the distribution.

The video ended with Garner putting the food in cars and trucks as residents drove through the line and a group shot of all who helped.

They all yelled, “Happy Thanksgiving!”

Garner included a message with her Wednesday Facebook post:

“Mountaineer Food Bank works tirelessly every single day to fight hunger across all of West Virginia. Lucky me to get to see their work in action and volunteer with Save the Children US who is striving to do more together to help fight food insecurity in WV. Happy Thanksgiving to the team working hard behind the scenes, the incredible volunteers and families I met that day, and to you and yours!”

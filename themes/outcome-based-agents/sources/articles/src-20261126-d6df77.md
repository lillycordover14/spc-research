ADA is 95.2% below its all-time high of $3.09, achieved in September 2021. Over the past 30 days, the cryptocurrency has dropped 26.6%, and it has fallen 65.1% over the past year, leaving many recent investors at a loss.

Price fluctuations have been dramatic this year. ADA closed as high as 42.3 cents on January 13 but later fell to its lowest close of 14.3 cents on November 20. Additionally, ADA supply has increased by about 2.9% annually.

So what’s a realistic Cardano price prediction for the end of 2027?

Claude AI also anticipates that ADA might close 2026 around 12 cents, and the model sets ADA’s 2027 trading range between 9 cents and 34 cents. The model assigns only a 3% chance of ADA reaching $1 in 2027, because the market value would need to soar to nearly $38 billion.

As ADA supply increases, reaching higher price levels becomes increasingly challenging. Around 37.6 billion ADA are in circulation out of a maximum of 45 billion. When ADA closed at $1.08 on January 2, 2018, its market value was approximately $27.9 billion. Now, for ADA to reach $1, its market value would need to rise to about $37.6 billion.

This dilution also complicates the more optimistic price scenarios. For instance, ADA’s price of 42.3 cents in January had a market value of $15.6 billion. If it reaches 45 cents again, it would exceed its previous peak market cap based on the increased coin supply.

Bitcoin Has to Turn, and Fed Rate Cuts Alone Haven’t Lifted ADA

For ADA to reach the predicted 22-cent price, Bitcoin’s performance is critical. Claude’s base scenario assumes the bear market will bottom out, allowing ADA to recover before the next Bitcoin halving, the upcoming reduction in the rate of new Bitcoin creation. On November 26, 
Bitcoin
 (
CRYPTO:BTC
) is trading around $60,725, down 23.7% over the past month and 51.8% below its all-time high of $126,080.

Typically, altcoins like ADA rebound strongly once Bitcoin begins to rise. For instance, during Bitcoin’s surge to an all-time high after the 2024 U.S. election, ADA started November 2024 at 34 cents and closed at $1.08.

The more optimistic price scenario for ADA also hinges on potential interest rate cuts by 
the Federal Reserve
. The Fed’s target rate, which determines what banks charge each other for overnight loans, returned to 4% in September.

In December 2025, the rate was cut to 3.75%, but that did not lead to a notable increase in ADA’s price, which fell from 41 cents to 33 cents by the end of that month. Thus, any future rate cuts in 2027 could facilitate a recovery, but they would likely need to coincide with rising Bitcoin prices.

Cardano’s Peras Upgrade Is Due in the Second Quarter of 2027

Anticipation is building around Cardano’s upcoming Peras upgrade, expected in the second quarter of 2027. This upgrade follows the Dijkstra phase, which is slated for release in the fourth quarter of 2026. The Peras upgrade aims to enhance transaction speed and confirmation times, potentially benefiting applications that rely on quick transactions.

Upgrades have historically boosted ADA’s price, although the impact has diminished over time. The all-time high in 2021 followed the announcement of the Alonzo hard fork, which introduced smart contracts. After the Van Rossum hard fork on July 18, ADA reached 26.5 cents in September before falling about 44% since then. Despite the recent downturns, 
our review of every 90% drop
 found that all five earlier drops resulted in gains within 12 months.

Where Is the Cardano Price Headed by the End of 2027?

We believe ADA could settle between 12 cents and 25 cents by the end of 2027. Claude’s base scenario of 22 cents is within reach, although the model’s 50% probability seems ambitious. First, ADA must remain above its low of 13.3 cents from November 21, and Bitcoin must stop hitting new lows. ADA also needs to move above its 200-day moving average, near 20 cents, 34% above its price, before Peras arrives.

The December 31 close will be the first key checkpoint. If ADA finishes 2026 above 12 cents, then Claude’s outlook could stay on track. Should ADA end any month in 2027 above 20 cents, the 22-cent mark could be more attainable, as our 
2027 recovery analysis
 and 
look at $1 by 2028
 explain. Conversely, if ADA closes a month below 13.3 cents, Claude’s bearish scenario of 8 cents may become more likely.

ElevenLabs Targets $33 Billion Valuation as It Lines up Next Raise
Kalbir Talwar
Published 
Nov 26, 2026
 
 
 
Share:

Summary: 

ElevenLabs is in early talks about a new funding round that would value the company at $33 billion, according to people familiar.

Annual recurring revenue is now $700 million, up from $600 million reported in September.

Founded in 2022, CEO Mati Staniszewski has indicated the company could begin laying the groundwork for an IPO in 2028.

Funding plans and timing

ElevenLabs is exploring a fresh raise pegged to a $33 
billion valuation
, say people who asked not to be named because the information is private. The aim is to begin the process during the first quarter of 2027, although some investors are pressing for an earlier start, and discussions are still tentative. ElevenLabs declined to comment.

The company's last mark was $22 billion in September after an employee 
tender offer
. It often sets headline valuations at a multiple of 11 - a wink to its name - which would put it toward the high end versus many peers.

Business metrics and products

Annual recurring revenue - a run rate that extrapolates yearly sales - is $700 million, according to one of the people. Back in September, ElevenLabs said ARR had reached $600 million. The company, formed in London in 2022 by Polish entrepreneurs Mati Staniszewski and Piotr Dabkowski, builds 
voice and audio models
 for realistic speech used in games, audiobooks and interactive AI agents.

It also rolled out a detector that determines if the sound in a live stream originates from a human or is produced by AI.

Peer and market context

Sticking with the 11 themed valuation convention would set ElevenLabs apart, despite the broad range in how AI firms value themselves and report ARR. A February blog post noted that London-based Synthesia carried a $4 billion valuation in January, with roughly $140 million in ARR. In September, French model maker Mistral AI was pegged at €21 billion ($23 billion), and it reached $400 million in ARR in early 2026.

Fresh risk appetite is in the air. Anthropic's record $107.3 billion IPO delivered large gains for early backers, helping fuel expectations for sizable payoffs from top AI names. According to PitchBook, AI startups pulled in over $500 billion during the first nine months of 2026, surpassing the $267 billion total for all of the prior year.

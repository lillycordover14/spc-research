Carolina, Santurce & Mayagüez notch wins in LBPRC&nbsp;&nbsp;San Juan Daily Star

[page text]
By THE STAR STAFF
The Gigantes of Carolina, Cangrejeros of Santurce and Indios of Mayagüez all earned victories on Tuesday night in Roberto Clemente Professional Baseball League (LBPRC by its initials in Spanish) play.
At Francisco “Paquito” Montaner Stadium, Edwin Díaz hit a home run and, alongside Javier Vaz, drove in five runs to lead the Gigantes to an 8-2 victory over the Leones of Ponce.
With the win, Carolina (9-6) moved into a three-way tie for first place alongside Ponce and Caguas.
Vaz went 3-for-4 with a triple, a walk, two RBIs and three runs scored. Meanwhile, Díaz went 3-for-5 with three RBIs and two runs scored.
Right-hander Alexander Castro earned his second win of the season after holding the Leones’ bats in check during his one inning of relief. Harold Cortijo (2-1) took the loss for Ponce (9-6), allowing one run in his lone inning of work.
In Caguas, Brian Navarreto homered twice, and Roy Morales added a solo home run, helping the Cangrejeros post an 8-2 victory over the Criollos at Yldefonso Solá Morales Stadium.
Santurce (8-9) snapped a four-game losing streak.
Navarreto went 3-for-5 with four RBIs and two runs scored, and Morales went 3-for-4 with two RBIs and two runs scored.
Left-hander Brian Morán (1-0) earned the win for Santurce, pitching three scoreless innings in relief, allowing just one hit and striking out three batters. Right-hander José de León (0-1) took the loss for Caguas (9-6), surrendering seven runs over four innings of work. He fanned three and allowed all three home runs.
At Hiram Bithorn Stadium in Hato Rey, a double by Rubén Castro combined with a single by Matthew Lugo gave the Indios of Mayagüez a 7-6 come-from-behind win over the Senadores of San Juan.
Mayagüez (7-8) remained in fifth place in the standings, while the Senadores (4-11) sit in sixth place.
With San Juan leading 6-5 in the top of the seventh, Shawn Ross and Castro hit consecutive singles, followed by a single from Lugo, which put the Indios back in the lead, 7-6. Rafael Kelly (1-0) earned the win for Mayagüez after pitching one and one-third scoreless innings, while Yariel Rodríguez recorded his first save of the season. San Juan’s Ralph Garza Jr. (0-1) was charged with the loss, as the Indios’ final two runs were pinned on him during his one-third of an inning of work.

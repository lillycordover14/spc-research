ElevenLabs Seeks $33 Billion Valuation in Early 2027 Funding Round
Add preferred source
ElevenLabs has begun preliminary talks to raise funding at a $33 billion valuation, with management planning to launch the process in the first quarter of 2027 even as some investors push for a faster timeline. The target is 50% above the $22 billion valuation set in a September employee tender offer and triple the $11 billion mark from a $500 million Series D round in February 2026. Annual recurring revenue has grown to $700 million from $600 million in September. The company, founded in 2022 by CEO Mati Staniszewski and Piotr Dabkowski, sells AI voice and audio models for audiobooks, gaming and enterprise support, and faces regulatory scrutiny over voice cloning in the EU and US. Staniszewski has said IPO preparations could begin as early as 2028.

ElevenLabs, the London-based developer of AI voice and audio models, has opened preliminary discussions about a new financing round that would value the company at $33 billion, according to people familiar with the matter. Management intends to formally kick off the fundraising in the first quarter of 2027, though some prospective backers are pressing for an earlier start.

The target represents a 50% increase over the $22 billion valuation the company secured in September through an employee tender offer. ElevenLabs raised $500 million in a Series D round at an $11 billion valuation in February 2026, meaning the proposed price would triple that mark within roughly a year.

Revenue has scaled quickly alongside the valuations. Annual recurring revenue, which annualizes sales from a shorter period, has climbed to $700 million from $600 million in September, the people said. ElevenLabs declined to comment.

At $33 billion, ElevenLabs would carry a steep premium to other European AI developers. Video AI company Synthesia reached a $4 billion valuation in January on about $140 million in ARR, while French model developer Mistral AI was valued at €21 billion (approximately $23.1 billion) in September after passing $400 million in ARR earlier in the year. Revenue multiples vary widely across the sector, but the gap underscores how aggressively investors have priced category leaders.

Appetite for late-stage private AI assets has been strengthened by Anthropic's record $107.3 billion initial public offering, which delivered large returns to early venture investors and demonstrated public-market demand for high-growth AI infrastructure. Institutions are increasingly competing for allocations in leading startups ahead of potential listings.

Founded in 2022 by CEO Mati Staniszewski and Piotr Dabkowski, ElevenLabs has built its position on realistic synthetic speech deployed in audiobooks, gaming and enterprise customer support. Its commercial lineup spans ElevenAgents for conversational workflows, ElevenCreative for multilingual content and dubbing in more than 90 languages, and ElevenAPI for low-latency developer integrations. The company has also introduced real-time detection software that distinguishes human speech from AI-generated audio in live streams.

Staniszewski has previously said the company could begin preparing for its own IPO as early as 2028. The voice-cloning technology at the core of the business is also drawing regulatory scrutiny in major markets, including the European Union and the United States.

For investors, the talks signal that private capital remains willing to pay up for AI companies with visible revenue growth, even as public markets have grown more selective about AI spending. A successful round at the target price would make ElevenLabs one of Europe's most valuable private AI firms and set a benchmark for other voice and audio startups seeking late-stage capital.

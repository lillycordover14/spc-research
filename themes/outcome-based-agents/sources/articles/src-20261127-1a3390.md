ElevenLabs
, a company developing AI voice models and agents, is in early discussions for a funding round that could value it at $33 billion, according to a 
Bloomberg
 report. The London-based company reportedly plans to begin the fundraising process in the first quarter of 2027, though discussions are still preliminary. 
Some investors are pushing for the process to begin sooner, the report said.
The company's annual recurring revenue (ARR), an estimate of annual sales based on a shorter period, has reached $700 million, according to one person cited in the report. The company had reported an ARR of $600 million in September.
The company was last valued at 
$22 billion in September
, following a $300 million employee tender offer led by Wellington Management and T. Rowe Price. This was double its $11 billion valuation in February, when it raised $500 million in a Series D funding round led by Sequoia Capital. A proposed $33 billion valuation would be 50% higher than its September valuation. 
ElevenLabs develops 
AI voice and audio models
 that generate synthetic voices for applications such as gaming, audiobooks and interactive AI agents. 
In a November interview with 
Bloomberg
, he said the company may need to change how users identify AI-generated content as the technology becomes more widespread.
“Today you assume that you’re interacting with a human unless it’s proven to be AI,” Polish entrepreneur and ElevenLabs Co-founder Mati Staniszewski told 
Bloomberg
. “In the future I think it will be the opposite.”
The company has expanded its product range beyond text-to-speech tools into 
conversational AI agents
 that can handle customer interactions and business workflows. In September, it launched Eleven v4, an updated text-to-speech model, followed by Eleven Realtime in October, a speech-to-speech model for building voice agents. In November, it introduced Scribe v3 and Scribe v3 Live, its speech-to-text models for recorded and real-time audio.
In September, ElevenLabs said enterprise customers accounted for 55% of its revenue. It also said its technology was being used in the daily operations of five of the world's 10 largest technology companies, five of the 10 largest insurers and four of the 10 largest telecom companies.
Expanding its geographic reach through acquisitions, it announced plans to acquire Japanese voice AI company Kotoba, which develops speech models for text-to-speech, speech-to-text and simultaneous interpretation, in November. The acquisition is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to regulatory approvals and other closing conditions.
ElevenLabs was founded in London in 2022 by Staniszewski and Piotr Dabkowski. CEO Staniszewski has previously said the company could begin preparing for an IPO in 2028.

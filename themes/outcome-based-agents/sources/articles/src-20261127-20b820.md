Update (1:20 p.m. CT): 
Austin Reaves has been made available
 for the Spurs-Lakers game Friday night, Mike Trudell.
The injury reports are out for tonight's mega clash in the Emirates NBA Cup between the Spurs and the Lakers. A game already carrying plenty of excitement gets added hype because De'Aaron Fox, and even Dylan Harper might be suiting up and Austin Reaves is back.
Victor Wembanyama and Luka Doncic are both playing, which means the first clash between the two MVP front-runners is officially on at Frost Bank Center. Right now, it's a two-horse race in the eyes of most. Doncic is averaging 36.6 points and 6.6 rebounds on 46% shooting. Wembanyama is at 30.7 points, 12.5 rebounds, and 3.8 blocks on 54.3%. 
Both have been making their cases, and tonight, they get to do it against each other. And they might just 
get to do it at full strength
 or at least as close to it as they've been this season, as San Antonio continues to wait on Tobias Harris' debut.
Fox and Harper put the Spurs close to full strength
Spurs injury report
Maliq Brown - Out (Two-Way)
De'Aaron Fox - Questionable (Right Shin Contusion)
Dylan Harper - Questionable (Left Hip External Rotator Strain)
Tobias Harris - Out (Left Calf Strain)
Jayden Quaintance - Out (Right Knee Surgery)
Lakers injury report
Quentin Grimes - Available (Right Finger Splint)
Austin Reaves - Available (Left Heel Contusion / Bone Bruise)
The headline of the report is that the Spurs could get De'Aaron Fox 
and 
Harper back at the same time. Fox has missed two games after the Jeremiah Fears trip, and Harper has been out four. Shams Charania initially reported Dylan's hip strain at 1-3 weeks, and then we heard 2-3 weeks elsewhere. It looks like Shams was closer to being correct. 
That would be a huge boost for a team that just watched its shooting fall apart in Portland. Fox and Harper give the Spurs the guard-advantage that keeps their offense organized, and keeps the pressure on defenses all game long. The three-guard group was a big reason for San Antonio's hot start, so it would be tremendous to get it back. 
Shout out to being young, because young people recover faster. 
Los Angeles had its own question mark. Reaves was questionable with a left heel bruise after missing the last five games, but JJ Redick has sounded like a coach who expected his number two back. "
We're optimistic for the rest of this week going forward
," Redick said after Reaves played in a "stay ready" game. He'll be suiting up, giving the Lakers their full rotation around Doncic.
Wembanyama has the team to beat Doncic
Wembanyama used to have trouble beating Luka's teams. Whether Vic was in the lineup or not, the Spurs lost every game to the Mavericks while Doncic still resided in Texas and didn't beat the Slovenian powerhouse until last season. With a real team around him, Wemby finally beat Doncic's Lakers and did it twice, including a 40-point night from the Alien in 26 minutes in February.
Now, Wembanyama is in year four, and he's arrived. He's putting up 
50-point and 40-point games
, pulling down ridiculous rebounding numbers, and maintaining his freakish defense. There's about to be fireworks on the East Side of San Antonio. I expect 
Wembanyama to go off
. I expect both players to put their stamp on the game. 
And we can't forget about the Cup stakes. The Spurs lost to the Trail Blazers on Wednesday, which left four teams tied at 2-1 in the group, and LA is one of them. A win moves San Antonio to 3-1 and keeps them in the knockout-round mix. A loss drops the Spurs to 2-2 and leaves them on the outside looking in. 
So, the Spurs need this one. Wembanyama already 
confirmed that it was a big game
, so the energy will be high. If everyone suits up, the biggest winners will be the viewers. We're the ones who get to watch greatness collide. It doesn't get much better than that.

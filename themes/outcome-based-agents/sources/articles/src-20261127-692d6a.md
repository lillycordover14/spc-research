YOUR VIEW: A ‘definite no’ on Plymouth data center&nbsp;&nbsp;Plymouth Independent

[page text]
I am a definite NO on a data center in Plymouth.
Most people are aware of the environmental 
consequences of data centers
 and the enormous demands they place on electricity and water supplies—demands that can drive up utility costs for every homeowner.
But there is another risk that deserves attention: those promised tax revenues may not be nearly as dependable as we are being led to believe.
Companies are racing to build data centers in anticipation of enormous future AI revenues. Yet few are making profits today, and there is no guarantee that many of these companies will survive in the long run. As competition intensifies, weaker players will inevitably fall by the wayside. Some will surely fail go bankrupt.
So, the question is simple: Are we willing to gamble Plymouth’s future on the assumption that this particular data center will be among the winners? Or could we end up with a massive complex of empty buildings, no meaningful tax revenue, and a legacy of environmental damage and higher utility costs?
– 
Robert Antall

Syracuse women’s basketball: Orange notch victories over Southern and Wisconsin
Two wins in two days for ‘Cuse.
by
 
 
Dominic Chiappone
Nov 27, 2026, 2:00 PM UTC
The Syracuse Orange women’s basketball team (6-1) is currently spending the holiday week down south and, so far, will bring two wins back to campus.
Syracuse picked up two wins over the past few days in the Discover Puerto Rico Shootout. First, the Orange squeaked by in a defensive battle Wednesday against Southern, then secured a 13-point win on Thanksgiving versus Wisconsin.
Beginning with the Southern game, the Orange relied on its top player to put the ball in the basket. Uche Izoje finished with a game-high 23 points on 10/14 shooting. Izoje also tallied 6 rebounds and 3 blocks. Izoje and Sarah Rambus (season-high 13 points on 6/12 shooting and career-high 8 rebounds) were the only Syracuse players to finish in double figures against the Jaguars.
Syracuse’s defense was the other key to its 62-47 win. The Orange held the Jaguars to 14 points or less in all four quarters. That allowed Syracuse to slowly build a lead as the game progressed and keep it en route to its first victory of the Puerto Rico Shootout.
After Southern came arguably the most important opponent of the round-robin tournament: the Wisconsin Badgers.
And the Orange came out feasting in that one.
Syracuse topped Wisconsin 75-62 on Thursday in its first game of the season against a Power Five opponent.
Izoje had another strong day. She finished this one with a game-high 26 points on 13/24 shooting, 16 rebounds, and 2 blocks in 36 minutes. This time, however, she had some help offensively.
Emely Rodriguez ended with a career-high 22 points, going 8/13 overall and 4/5 from three. Sarah Rambus (15 points) and Tyi Skinner (10 points) also reached double figures. The four of them accounted for 73 of the Orange’s 75 points against the Badgers.
Coming off a 12-point loss to Harvard, Syracuse certainly had plenty to play for in Puerto Rico and needed a bounce back. So far, it’s risen to the occasion in two games.
It will now look to leave Puerto Rico with a perfect 3-0 record. Syracuse will conclude the tournament Friday morning against Auburn (7-1) in a rematch of last year’s ACC/SEC Challenge. Like the Orange, the Tigers are also 2-0 in the Discover Puerto Rico Shootout. They beat Wisconsin by 16 and Southern by two.

'Clay in the Potter's Hands': Archbishop Weisenburger's Homily (First Sunday of Advent)

Archbishop Edward J. Weisenburger

Nov 27, 2026

Voices

En

In this homily for Nov. 29, 2026, the First Sunday of Advent, Archbishop Weisenburger reminds us never to despair when we look at ourselves or the world around us. God has not given up on us. Like clay in the hands of the Potter, we are a work in progress, still capable of becoming something beautiful under the touch of the Master artist.
Readings for Sunday, Nov. 29 (
First Sunday of Advent
)
Isaiah 63:16b-17, 19b; 64:2-7
Psalm 80:2-3, 15-16, 18-19
1 Corinthians 1:3-9
Mark 13:33-37

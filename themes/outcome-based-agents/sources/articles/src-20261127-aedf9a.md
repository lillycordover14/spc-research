            US pork exports to Dominican Republic stay above record pace
        

September volume up 3% but value falls 5% to $21 million

 27 November 2026
                

                    1 minute read
                                

                            By: 
 Global Ag Media
 

North America

 

September pork exports to the Dominican Republic increased 3% from a year ago to 7,417 tonnes, though value declined 5% to $21 million, 
according to the US Meat Export Federation
 (USMEF). 
January to September shipments to the Dominican Republic remained well above last year's record pace, increasing 20% in volume (79,840 tonnes) and 19% in value ($233.3 million).

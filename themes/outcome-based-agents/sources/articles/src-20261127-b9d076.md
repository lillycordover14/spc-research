Jason Crosby to Take His Cosmic Charlie Brown Christmas on the Road This December
RS
By 
Relix Staff
6:00 AM EST on November 27, 2026
Add Relix to Google
Jason Crosby
 will bring his Cosmic Charlie Brown Christmas show on tour in December.The fiddler/keyboardist, who has been working closely with Jackson Browne and Terrapin Family Band as of late, has been performing a set of music blending works by Vince Guaraldi and the Grateful Dead at Mill Valley, CAs Sweetwater Music Hall for several years and this holiday season, the 61
st
 anniversary of 
A Charlie Brown Christmas
, Crosby will bring the special show to several different markets. Those gigs include Newtown, CT’s Edmond Town Hall (12/4), Woodstock, NY’s Bearsville Theater (12/5), Brooklyn, NY’s Brooklyn Bowl (12/6), Patchogue, NY’s 89 North Music Venue (12/7), Santa Cruz, CA’s Kuumbwa Jazz (12/11) and Menlo Park, CA’s The Guild Theatre (12/13). The celebration will then loop back to Mill Valley’s Sweetwater Music Hall on December 20, with shows at both 1 and 7 pm. The Brooklyn Bowl show will also be a kids-friendly 12pm performance presented with The Rock & Roll Playhouse.
ALO’s Dan Lebowitz, Steve Adams and Ezra Lipp will back Crosby on the West Coast, Kevin Reilly and Dave Diamond will perform with Crosby in Patchogue, and Reilly and John Morgan Kimock will appear with the multi-instrumentalist in Connecticut, Brooklyn and Woodstick. Crosby also surprised fans with an impromptu Cosmic Charlie Brown Christmas spot in the lobby of Bearsville Theater last year.

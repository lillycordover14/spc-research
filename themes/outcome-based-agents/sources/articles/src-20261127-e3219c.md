Testudo provides AI agent insurance to AI-native law firm Crosby&nbsp;&nbsp;Reinsurance News

[page text]
Testudo, the specialist artificial intelligence (AI) liability insurer and Lloyd’s coverholder, has insured the agents of Crosby, a New York-based AI-native law firm.

Operating as a hybrid law firm, Crosby combines licensed attorneys alongside AI agents. The firm focuses on commercial contract matters, combining human legal expertise with AI to complete thorough contract reviews within hours.

Additionally, Crosby has paralegal agents, always-on assistants that answer immediately, to help routine requests move faster.

Testudo, considered the biggest specialist insurer of standalone AI liability insurance for US companies, according to the firm, has issued policy protection for Crosby backed by Lloyd’s of London.

This collaboration is designed to complement and strengthen the law firm’s existing risk management framework and provide clients, investors and regulators confidence in responsible AI deployment.

“AI native companies like Crosby represent exactly the kind of business we built Testudo to support,” said Testudo CEO and Co-founder George Lewin-Smith. “AI is already changing how professional services are delivered, and innovative businesses need risk transfer solutions that evolve alongside them.

“We went through a rigorous underwriting process with the world-class Crosby team and came away impressed by both their approach to the technology and the risk management surrounding it. They are thinking proactively about how institutions can trust AI-enabled legal services and how insurance forms part of that framework.”

Adding: “As AI agents become capable of undertaking more sophisticated work, new liabilities will emerge alongside the opportunities. Our job is to understand and affirmatively insure those exposures with the full faith of the A+ rated Lloyd’s of London balance sheet. We look forward to partnering with Crosby as they innovate on the cutting edge of technology, legal AI and regulations.”

Testudo launched its standalone AI Liability insurance at the beginning of this year, backed by Lloyd’s of London capacity with support from multiple syndicates including Apollo, QBE and Hiscox.

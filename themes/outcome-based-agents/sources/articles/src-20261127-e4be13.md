Spurs receive major Dylan Harper injury update ahead of Lakers game

2026-11-27 06:21

Spurs guard Dylan Harper is officially listed as questionable for Friday's NBA Cup showdown against the Lakers, potentially returning earlier than expected from a hip injury. Lakers' Austin Reaves is also questionable for the game.

The 20-year-old has been sidelined since November 17, when he suffered a left hip external rotator strain during a game against the Detroit Pistons.
 
Harper initially faced a recovery timeline of one to three weeks, with reports suggesting he could miss approximately two weeks.
 
While his final availability remains uncertain, Friday's game comes just 10 days after the injury.
 
Harper has averaged 18.4 points, 5.4 rebounds, and 4.5 assists in 15 games while shooting 50.5% from the field.
 
Credit Imagn Images - Scanpix
 
Austin Reaves also questionable for Lakers
 
Meanwhile, the Lakers could also receive a big boost. 
Austin Reaves
 is listed as questionable for Friday's matchup due to a left heel contusion.
 
The 28-year-old guard has missed Los Angeles' last five games after suffering the injury against the Milwaukee Bucks on November 11.
 
However, Reaves participated in Wednesday's practice, raising hopes that he could return against San Antonio.
 
He is averaging 24.7 points, 4.7 assists, and 3.3 rebounds this season.
 
Friday's NBA Cup matchup between the Western Conference-leading Lakers (15-3) and the Spurs (15-4) is scheduled for 9:30 p.m. ET (8:30 p.m. local time) at Frost Bank Center in San Antonio.

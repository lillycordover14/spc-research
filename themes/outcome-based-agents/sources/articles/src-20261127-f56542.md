Sierra Doubles Valuation to $30B With $1.75B Series F as Enterprise AI Agent Demand Surges&nbsp;&nbsp;AI Insider

[page text]
Sierra
, the enterprise customer experience company co-founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor, has secured $1.75 billion in a Series F round that doubles its valuation to $30 billion. The deal comes less than six months after a previous round 
valued it at $15 billion
.

Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners led the round, joined by existing investors including Benchmark, Sequoia, GV and Tiger Global.

Sierra said it serves nearly half of Fortune 50 companies, including BNY, HSBC, Wells Fargo and JPMorganChase, and that its outcome-based pricing helped double run-rate revenue to 
$400 million in five months
. The company is 
building a platform for creating agents
 and developing 
agent protocol definitions
 with Meta, Stripe and Walmart. The new capital will fund further agent development and expanded sales teams.

Customer experience AI startups are commanding soaring valuations, with ElevenLabs at $22 billion, 
Wonderful
 at $5 billion and 
Decagon
 at $4.5 billion.

Co-founder Clay Bavor has argued that most Sierra customers are reassigning contact center staff rather than replacing them.

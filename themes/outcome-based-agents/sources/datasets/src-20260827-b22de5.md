Many customer service representatives work in call centers.
Customer service representatives held about 2.7 million jobs in 2025. Some of the largest employers of customer service representatives were as follows: Retail trade 15%; Insurance carriers and related activities 12; Business support services 8; Professional, scientific, and technical services 7; Wholesale trade 6.
The median hourly wage for customer service representatives was $21.53 in May 2025. The lowest 10 percent earned less than $15.27, and the highest 10 percent earned more than $30.57.
Median hourly wages May 2025 by industry: Wholesale trade $23.41; Insurance carriers and related activities 22.47; Professional, scientific, and technical services 21.77; Retail trade 17.96; Business support services 17.68.
Employment of customer service representatives is projected to decline 5 percent from 2025 to 2035. Despite declining employment, about 289,500 openings for customer service representatives are projected each year, on average, over the decade.
There is expected to be less demand for customer service representatives, especially in retail trade, as their tasks continue to be automated. Self-service systems, social media, and mobile applications enable customers to do simple tasks without interacting with a representative. Advancements in technology will gradually allow these automated systems to do even more tasks.

Edison Scientific is a San Francisco-based company building AI systems for scientific R&D, with an emphasis on biopharma research workflows and autonomous scientific analysis. The company describes its flagship product, Kosmos, as an "AI Scientist" for R&D teams that can move from hypothesis generation to registration-supporting work much faster than traditional workflows, targeting biopharma teams and frontier researchers. [1] Edison launched as a commercial spinout from FutureHouse in 2025, with FutureHouse continuing as a nonprofit research organization focused on foundational biology while Edison commercializes AI scientist technology. [2]
Edison's core platform is Kosmos, which reads literature, analyzes data, generates hypotheses, branches investigations, and executes scientific workflows autonomously. [1] The company says Kosmos uses structured world models to aggregate information across many agent trajectories, and an individual run can involve reading about 1,500 papers and running tens of thousands of lines of analysis code. [3] Edison also offers Edison Analysis, a scientific data-analysis agent that iteratively updates Jupyter notebooks and can use Python, R, Bash, and external data access; Edison states that this system underpins Kosmos. [4]
Kosmos is positioned for the full drug-development lifecycle, from target identification through clinical development and regulatory filing support. [1] Edison highlights applications including literature synthesis, experimental and clinical data analysis, hypothesis generation, molecular design, toxicology, clinical trials, and regulatory-documentation reasoning. [5] The company also emphasizes enterprise features such as integration with ELNs, assay data, biomarker data, cloud storage, unstructured notes, SOC 2 compliant infrastructure, and auditable workflows. [5]
Edison is a software platform company rather than a therapeutics developer, so it does not appear to have a conventional drug pipeline. Its development roadmap is product-led: improving Kosmos for enterprise biopharma R&D, keeping core research agents available through Edison Playground, and expanding access as capacity permits. [5] The company has reported Kosmos-enabled scientific demonstrations across neuroscience, materials science, statistical genetics, Alzheimer-related biology, and metabolic-disease mechanisms, but these are platform validation examples rather than wholly owned clinical assets. [3]
In May 2026, Edison announced a partnership with Incyte Corporation to embed Kosmos across Incyte's discovery and development lifecycle. [5] The initial deployment is focused on target discovery, target validation, and translational biology, with the goal of synthesizing experimental, clinical, and biomarker data and supporting R&D decision-making. [5] Edison also says Kosmos is trusted by leading biopharma organizations and academic institutions, though its public website does not list all customers in text form. [1]

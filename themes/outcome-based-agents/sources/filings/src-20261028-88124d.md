SUMMARY
ServiceNow, Inc. (NOW -26.95%) reported third-quarter performance that exceeded guidance across primary financial and operational metrics. Management emphasized the company's transition into a governance and security layer for enterprise artificial intelligence through its Super Intelligence Control Tower. The company reported the adoption of a hybrid business model that integrates traditional subscription revenue with consumption-based monetization to drive long-term value. Management indicated that it is strategically investing in deployment expertise for large customers to accelerate the time to value for artificial intelligence solutions, which resulted in a minor accounting shift from subscription to professional services revenue. Operational efficiencies driven by the internal use of its own platform contributed to a significant raise in the full-year free cash flow margin target.
- The company announced an expanded partnership with OpenAI to develop security and risk solutions powered by cyber models and integrated into the ServiceNow Control Tower.
- Management launched Flow, an AI-native conversational service desk that deploys instantly outside the main platform to serve teams requiring rapid speed and simplicity.
- ServiceNow announced the Control Tower Alliance, an ecosystem including founding partners Anthropic, AWS, Cisco, NVIDIA, and Okta to support secure AI adoption at scale.
- CFO Mastantuono reported that the internal use of artificial intelligence has enabled the company to grow quota-bearing headcount at double-digit rates while slightly declining total headcount.
- The company reported that Nestle is deploying agentic marketing operations across 60 countries with the potential to reduce marketing campaign coordination by 25%.
- Management noted that Germany's professional soccer league consolidated fan services from seven channels into a single platform, resolving 89% of inquiries at first contact.
- CEO McDermott stated, "ServiceNow always closes strong. We have no intention of delivering anything less than the best year in our history."
INDUSTRY GLOSSARY
- ACV: Annual Contract Value, representing the annualized revenue from a customer contract.
- Action Fabric: A technology layer that enables AI agents to execute actions and connect to third-party systems through secure workflows.
- Agentic AI: Artificial intelligence systems capable of autonomous reasoning and performing complex tasks rather than simply responding to text prompts.
- AIOps: Artificial Intelligence for IT Operations, the application of machine learning to automate and enhance IT management processes.
- cRPO: Current Remaining Performance Obligations, representing the contract revenue expected to be recognized within the next 12 months.
- EmployeeWorks: An AI-integrated front-door platform for employees to manage tasks across IT, HR, finance, and procurement.
- ITOM: IT Operations Management, the area of management responsible for an organization's IT infrastructure and components.
- ITSM: IT Service Management, the processes and policies for managing and supporting information technology services.
- Now Platform: The core cloud-based software platform used for enterprise-wide workflow automation.
- SICT: Super Intelligence Control Tower, a solution used to discover, govern, and secure AI agents and activities across an organization.
- T4C: Terms for Convenience, a contractual provision that allows for termination without cause, which can impact how certain backlog is reported in financial metrics.

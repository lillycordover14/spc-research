AI agents are going live in the enterprise. In 2026, money, hiring, and M&A will flow to the layers that make agents actually work.
Below, we identify 5 high-momentum markets that tell the story of where agents are going live, what’s breaking in production, and where the dollars go next.
- Multimodal AI agents (voice, text, image, video) win the customer service land grab
- Voice AI deployments go high-touch as vendors embed engineers on-site
- Continuous red teaming becomes standard for enterprise AI agent deployments
- AI agent observability & evaluation tooling becomes an M&A battleground
- World models power the next generation of physical AI agents
How we built this report:
We used CB Insights’ Market Index — which aggregates company data and signals across 1,600+ tech markets — to filter on high-momentum AI agent markets based on average Mosaic startup health potential scores (>510). Using our Q4’25 enterprise survey, we identified leading areas of adoption and top implementation blockers to surface key enabling markets. Then, we applied CBI datasets including hiring momentum & insights, private company revenue, and M&A probability to predict where each market is headed next.
#1 Multimodal AI agents (voice, text, image, video) win the customer service land grab
In 2026, the customer service AI land grab will be won on multimodal ground. Voice is a key proving ground, with high costs, low tolerance for latency, and little room for handoffs. Agents that can nail voice and seamlessly extend to text, images, and documents will define the future of enterprise support.
The stakes are high. Customer service is the #1 area of AI agent adoption in our Q4’25 enterprise survey. 115+ companies operate in the market (putting it in the top 10 of all CBI-tracked markets) and 6 private companies are generating $100M+ in revenue.
While the majority of private market revenue leaders were founded before the genAI wave, AI-native entrants are gaining ground fast. Companies like Sierra (founded in 2023) and Crescendo (founded in 2024) have already broken into the top 10 by revenue generation.

Incumbents are also making moves, either launching AI solutions or snapping up startups. Overall, 54% of companies in the market were founded since 2023.
Hiring data backs this up. Rising entrants Decagon (top 3% in hiring among all private companies) and Giga (top 6%) are both investing in engineering capacity to deliver real-time voice capabilities. Revenue leader PolyAI CEO Nikola Mrkšić explained to us:
Voice-first isn’t about audio. It’s about latency tolerance, interruption handling, and turn-taking logic baked into the architecture. Text agents adding voice bolt on a modality; they don’t redesign for it…In 2026, enterprises running millions of calls will choose players who’ve solved barge-in and silence detection at scale.
Beyond solving voice, winners will unify channels and modes. The leading agents will move seamlessly between voice and text while also interpreting richer inputs like documents, images, and video. Sierra and Crescendo already emphasize multichannel and multimodal positioning, signaling that the next phase of competition will shift from channel coverage to multimodal performance.
#2 Voice AI deployments go high-touch as vendors embed engineers on-site
Voice AI development platforms are shifting focus from developer-led models to high-touch enterprise deployments.

Accenture Pulse of Change. July 26, 2026
82% of C-suite leaders are increasing AI investment
55% are confident their agentic AI initiatives will deliver board-reportable business outcomes within the next year
23% report widespread, sustained business value from AI, down from 32% earlier this year
49% of companies are piloting or deploying AI agents
7 in 10 C-suite leaders say the impact of agentic AI has been greater than expected on employee productivity
81% of employees feel AI tools have increased their overall productivity at work
52% would continue to invest in AI even if a bubble burst, yet only 10% believe a significant bubble exists
78% of leaders expect employee roles to change in 12 months; 57% of employees have indicated their roles have already changed
Methodology: Accenture conducted two complementary global surveys between April and June 2026. The first surveyed 3,000 C-suite leaders from the world's largest organizations (annual revenues greater than $500 million) across 19 industries and 20 countries. The second surveyed 3,000 employees (non-C-suite) from organizations of the same scale, also across 19 industries and 20 countries.

McKinsey, The state of AI in 2026: On the road to ROI, 2026-08-25. [agent notes, not verbatim; key lines quoted]
Survey: online, fielded May 4 to June 8, 2026, n=1,719 in 97 nations; 36% at orgs with >$1B revenue; weighted by national GDP.
"Forty percent of respondents from large organizations (those with annual revenues of more than $1 billion) report scaling AI agents, up from 27 percent last year." Smaller orgs flat at 22%.
About two in ten scaling software coding agents (31% at larger enterprises). 32% decided against buying at least one software product or feature because they could build it with agentic coding tools.
About 20% say AI operating costs (incl. tokens) constrained AI use. 37% attribute some EBIT impact (flat). High performers about 6% (flat). 80% report individual productivity gains.
44% report AI scaling across the enterprise (vs 38%). 39% expect AI-related headcount declines next year (vs 32%); 14% report actual declines last year.
High performers: nearly three-quarters fundamentally redesigned workflows (vs 55% last year; one-quarter of others).

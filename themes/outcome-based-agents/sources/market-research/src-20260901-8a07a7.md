Agentic AI at Scale (IDC, Matt Eastwood, September 2026 event deck). Excerpts.

Production is the norm now — but rarely cross-functional. 95.3% of enterprises now run at least one production agent workflow — averaging ~11 concurrent workflows. Only 3.4% report zero. ~1 in 3 organizations has implemented a genuinely cross-functional, multiagent solution to date. <50% of proofs of concept convert to production. If one agent step succeeds most of the time, a chain of three dependent agents succeeds three quarters of the time. ~90% success x3 = ~73% end-to-end success. It's execution maturity, not ambition, that's the binding constraint. Source: Future Enterprise Resiliency & Spending Survey, Wave 4, IDC, July 2026.

Non-human identities now outnumber human identities in the average enterprise (45:1).

Spend is fragmented, not concentrated: Business process automation 60.7%, Customer-facing agents 56.9%, Decision-support agents 56.0%, Coding agents 53.6%. No single agent type dominates budget. Source: Future Enterprise Resiliency & Spending Survey, Wave 4, IDC, July 2026.

Gartner Forecasts Worldwide IT Spending to Grow 15% in 2027, Totaling $7.35 Trillion
ORLANDO, Fla., October 21, 2026
With AI Investment Accelerating Across the Technology Stack, IT Spending Will Cross the $7 Trillion Mark for the First Time
Worldwide IT spending is expected to reach $7.35 trillion in 2027, up 15% from 2026, according to the latest forecast by Gartner, Inc.
"Collectively, the market forces are reinforcing spend around AI infrastructure, AI-enabled cloud platforms and advanced software ecosystems, as these are the most attractive opportunities in global IT spending," said John-David Lovelock, Distinguished VP Analyst at Gartner.
The next $1 trillion dollars in IT spending growth is being shaped by two interconnected AI domains: AI infrastructure and AI software. Spending on AI-optimized servers, AI-optimized infrastructure as a service (IaaS) and AI software platforms continues to strengthen as organizations seek to industrialize AI deployment. At the same time, hardware markets are experiencing growing pressure from memory-related inflation (see Table 1).
Table 1. Worldwide IT Spending Forecast (Billions of U.S. Dollars)
| | 2026 Spending | 2026 Growth (%) | 2027 Spending | 2027 Growth (%) |
| Data Center Systems | 879 | 73.8 | 1,366 | 55.5 |
| Devices | 847 | 7.2 | 916 | 8.1 |
| Software | 1,464 | 15.2 | 1,679 | 14.6 |
| Services | 1,557 | 4.4 | 1,633 | 4.9 |
| Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) | 292 | 31.3 | 367 | 25.7 |
| Communications Services | 1,352 | 4.1 | 1,391 | 2.9 |
| Overall IT | 6,391 | 14.5 | 7,352 | 15.0 |
Source: Gartner (October 2026)
Over the past 12 months, demand from data center systems has driven memory prices to more than double, with cost increases cascading throughout storage, server, networking, PC and device supply chains.
Gartner's IT spending forecast methodology relies heavily on rigorous analysis of the sales by over a thousand vendors across the entire range of IT products and services.

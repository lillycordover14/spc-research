summary: Sierra co-founder and CEO Bret Taylor, also OpenAI board chair, tells John Collison that Sierra charges a pre-negotiated rate when the AI agent resolves a customer service case with no human intervention, and escalations to a person are free; for sales it would be a commission. He argues outcome pricing differs from usage pricing because token use is not reliably correlated with value, and compares the shift to ad platforms moving from impressions to CPC. He says customers automate anywhere between 70-90% of cases, and that average handle time for the remaining human cases goes up because they are more complex. He frames the atomic unit of AI productivity as a process, not a person, and says large companies adopt slowly because they ship their org charts. He calls the model disruptive because most legacy software companies are not equipped to sell this way.

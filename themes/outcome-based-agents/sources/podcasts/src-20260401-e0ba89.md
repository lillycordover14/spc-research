summary: Duvo founder and CEO Tomas Cupr, who built a pan-European grocery e-commerce group (captioned 'Rohik') to about $1.5 billion in revenue since 2014 and is still its group CEO, says he started Duvo after automation projects on his own retailer's legacy systems and supplier portals without APIs kept coming back as 6 to 18 month efforts. Duvo first maps processes with Duvo Clarity, voice agents that interview specialists and overlay recordings of their work, mapping hundreds of people in under a week and recommending the top processes to automate, replacing work he says companies otherwise buy from McKinsey or the Big Four. Examples include an agent that phones suppliers right after a missed fulfillment-center delivery window, a step the SOP required but mapping showed nobody did, and should-cost analysis on every supplier price increase, which he guarantees 95% of retailers do not do. He argues agents sit on top of existing systems because no sane enterprise will replace its ERP, that
  clean data is not a prerequisite, and that people become agent operators rather than being replaced. Change management gets easier because agents follow new instructions immediately, while consultants will still be needed to steer hybrid human and agent organizations.

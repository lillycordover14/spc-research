summary: Rohan Murty, founder of Workfabric, argues that despite agent hype the core truth is that agents only succeed in a few areas such as code generation and perhaps some legal work, and that most functions have no successful agents producing ROI. His reason is that models generalize well but do not understand the local context of the organization they serve. Workfabric's platform, Context Fabric, captures context from how people interact with their devices using edge models, then assembles it in the cloud on GPUs, for example combining what delivery, support and legal teams learn about a customer into proactive pitches for sales. He says the future of traditional IT services, excluding BPO, shifts from writing code for microprocessors to shipping context to models, and that when intelligence is democratized an organization's context is what differentiates it. He expects AI to disrupt some human labor and intermediaries such as analyst firms.

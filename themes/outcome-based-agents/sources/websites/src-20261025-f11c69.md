3. Fees, Credits, and Billing
3.1 Fees. Customer will pay the fees stated in the applicable plan, Order Form, and Pricing Definitions. 3.2 Billable Resolutions â the positive-signal gate. A "Billable Resolution" occurs only when a conversation or task handled by the Services is confirmed resolved by a positive signal under the four-tier signal framework published in the Pricing Definitions: Tier 1 (explicit confirmation by the end user), Tier 2 (system-verified outcome evidenced in a Connected System), or Tier 3 (corroborated silence, billable only when all three published conditions hold, including at least one enumerated corroborator). Tier 0 signals never bill, including: uncorroborated silence, greeting-only exchanges, abandoned conversations, negative CSAT, conversations in which a human agent of Customer sent a message, test/sandbox/evaluation traffic, conversations where the agent could not help, and misuse of the agent as a general-purpose assistant. Ambiguity always resolves to unbilled. Routing or escalating a conversation to a human is free and is not a Billable Resolution.
3.3 Resolution classes. Each Billable Resolution is assigned exactly one class per the discriminators published in the Pricing Definitions (knowledge, read, write_simple, write_financial, orchestrated, compliance_gated (not available or sold until Tied holds the applicable compliance instruments), and long_horizon_case (enterprise; billed as a case, not a resolution)). Classes are derived mechanically from the action audit trail over successfully completed calls; the highest class reached bills once; failed writes neither upgrade a class nor disqualify a resolution; modifiers (proactive, voice, save, upsell) are itemized separately and priced only when enabled per contract. For enterprise customers, class definitions and discriminators may be modified by Order Form.
3.4 Void windows and the guarantee. A billed resolution may be voided by either of two triggers, in each case credited automatically as its own invoice line with the reason attached, with no claim, ticket, or dispute required: (a) reopen void â the same end user recontacts about the same issue within 30 days of the Billable Resolution, per the matching criteria in the Pricing Definitions; and (b) negative-feedback void â the end user leaves negative feedback on that resolution, retroactively and with no time limit. A void credits the orchestration fee (including any modifier uplifts) for that resolution; LLM passthrough costs reflect tokens actually consumed and are not credited (and in BYOK mode never appear on a Tied invoice).
3.5 Guarantee integrity. The negative-feedback void applies to genuine end-user feedback on the resolution concerned. It does not apply to feedback that is fraudulent, induced, automated, or generated by Customer or its personnel for the purpose of obtaining credits, and Tied may reverse credits obtained through such feedback. Nothing in this Section limits the one-click dispute mechanism on each resolution receipt.

4. LLM Costs â Inference Passthrough and BYOK
4.1 Two paths, every tier. For AI-model inference, Customer chooses either: (a) "BYOK" â Customer supplies its own model-provider keys, pays that provider directly under its own agreement, and Tied charges only orchestration fees; or (b) "Itemized Passthrough" â Tied contracts with model providers and bills Customer for inference at cost as described in Section 4.2.
4.2 What "at cost" means â the precise representation. Under Itemized Passthrough, Tied meters inference using token counts priced against Tied's effective-dated price table, which records each provider's published rate and the date range it applies to; resolutions are priced against the entry in effect when they ran, so mid-period provider price changes do not retroactively reprice earlier resolutions. At the end of each month, Tied reconciles the price table against actual provider invoices, and any difference â in either direction â is allocated back to customers pro-rata by tokens consumed, appearing as an explicit reconciliation line on the next invoice. Tied's commitment is that, after monthly reconciliation, Customer pays no more for inference than Tied pays its providers for Customer's usage â Tied takes no margin on tokens. Provider rate decreases benefit Customer in the same period they benefit Tied. Each receipt itemizes the model(s) used, tokens consumed, provider rate, and inference amount, separately from orchestration fees.

OUR MISSION
Where bold thinkers become builders, and the future of work is created one breakthrough at a time.
What drives our mission
One ema
We collaborate to achieve unified goals of making our customers and Ema successful.
Everyone  builds
We hire builders and creators. We hire people who can write software, create original content, and build new processes.
AI first 
We’re an AI First company. This means we try to make maximal use of AI in everything we do internally in the company and also the products we build for our customers.
Hire best talent
We hire fewer people with a hunger and growth mindset and augment the human workforce with AI Employees. Every one of our hires must be a 10X-er. 
No Wait, No Boundaries
We move with urgency. We do not believe in the traditional structure of fixed “ownership” in the enterprise. If something helps the company go ahead and do it irrespective of your role.

Your Journey
Joining Ema
At Ema, excellence goes beyond expertise. Our interview process looks at not just what you can do, but how you think, collaborate, and create impact. Week 1 carries that forward: you'll experience our culture firsthand and ship your Week 1 Starter Project - a real first step into building the future of work with AI.
1 Week
Understanding the Ways of Working at Ema
By the end of your first month, you are no longer finding your feet - you are contributing. You have completed your core certifications, mastered our tools, and built the relationships that will define how you work here. You have a clear view of working@ema, managing@ema, culture@ema and your priorities, your teammates, and the customers or workstreams you are beginning to own. You have posted your first Snippets, run your first real work through Claude Cowork, and understand how Ema operates day-to-day. Most importantly, you have started making a measurable impact - not just learning, but doing.
1 Month
Creating Impact Across Teams
At three months, you are no longer ramping - you are owning. You have moved from learning the system to shaping it. You are driving your core workstream independently, contributing meaningfully across teams, and bringing ideas and energy to the problems that matter most. You understand our customers, our product, and our culture well enough to operate without guardrails. The people around you have noticed.
3 Months
Lead with Ownership
Six months in, you are a core part of what makes Ema work. You bring judgment, not just execution. You identify problems before they are raised, propose solutions before they are asked for, and take ownership of outcomes - not just tasks. You have helped shape something: a product, a process, a pipeline, a team. The extraordinary thing we are building here has your fingerprints on it.
6 Months
Life at Ema
A small team with a hunger for hard problems and a habit of celebrating the person, not just the launch.

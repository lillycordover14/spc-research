# About Sycamore

Sycamore builds the operating system for enterprise AI.

Forge turns hard business processes into software that runs them. Guard brings every AI system the organization builds or buys under accountable control.

Together they form one operating system for enterprise AI: a runtime where agents do durable work, and a control plane where people set policy, grant authority, and inspect the evidence.

Your team brings the problem and the judgment. Forge maps the work and carries an approved plan into production. Guard keeps the owner, approval, identity, runtime policy, observed behavior, and evidence together as the system runs.

The team includes researchers from Stanford and Cornell and engineers from Meta, Google, Atlassian, and other leading technology companies. Sycamore is headquartered in Palo Alto, California.

## Founder

Sri Viswanath, CEO & Founder
Sri has spent over two decades building enterprise platforms at global scale, from Sun Microsystems and VMware to CTO roles at Groupon and Atlassian, where he led its cloud transformation. He founded Sycamore to build the operating system for enterprise AI: software that can take on durable work, with accountable control built into the runtime.

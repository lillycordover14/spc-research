Operating scale
85,000 products, 1,700 suppliers, 14 warehouses
We built a $2 billion retailer and saw the same pattern everywhere: the process crossed SAP, supplier portals, spreadsheets, and approval threads.
Credit notes went missing between systems. Order details sat in messages until someone rekeyed them. Whenever we were ready to automate a process, the answer was the same: ask the person who runs it. They had usually built it over years, and most of it lived only in their head.
When we asked people why they were not doing their best work, most said they had no headspace. Duvo cleans up the processes around your people, so there is less mess to work around and more room for the work that makes money. Agents take on the agreed steps. The people who know the work run those agents and build the next process.

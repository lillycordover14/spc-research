ContextFabric · The platform beneath the simulation. The context backbone powering your digital twins.
Every digital twin in the simulation runs on ContextFabric. Our proprietary context platform senses how your people actually work across the apps they already use, then delivers the precise context every twin needs at runtime.

No integrations required. ContextFabric works across mainframes, legacy systems, cloud, and AI applications, without requiring any integrations.
One context layer. Every agent. ContextFabric plugs directly into your AI stack through a single MCP server, providing a unified context layer for every agent.

Zero-Setup Discovery
Edra reads your tickets, logs, and messages to reverse engineer how your business actually runs and organizes it in a library of executable knowledge. You get instant visibility without any interviews or workshops.
Transparent AI Automation
Edra deploys auditable agents that follow your specific processes to work directly inside your existing systems. Every rule is traceable, every decision is auditable, so you know exactly why your agents do what they do.
Unlike black-box solutions, Edra produces human-readable playbooks. Review, edit, and own every rule that powers your automation - then deploy agents directly in your existing platforms.
Continuous Learning
Edra detects inconsistencies, process changes, and knowledge gaps, updating your knowledge library automatically as your company evolves. So your AI agents always run on knowledge that is up to date.

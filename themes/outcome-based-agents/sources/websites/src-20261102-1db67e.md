Frequently asked questions
Will this work with my Pharmacy Management System (PMS)?
Yes! Our agents are vendor-agnostic and work directly in your PMS interface, reading the screen the way a technician would, so no APIs or system replacements are required.
Is it HIPAA-compliant?
Absolutely. Our bots operate entirely within your infrastructure with zero data retention, ensuring full HIPAA compliance and patient data privacy.
How long does it take to set up?
Community pharmacies on TJM Data Entry Bot are typically live in about 4 days. Custom deployments for other pharmacies are typically live within 4 weeks, with minimal disruption to your existing workflows.
Can the bots handle system updates or changes?
Yes! Our AI agents adapt to UI changes automatically, and we offer ongoing support to ensure bots continue to operate smoothly.
What happens if there's an error or exception?
Our bots flag exceptions or incomplete data for your staff to review, ensuring accuracy while saving significant manual effort.
Do I need IT expertise to manage this?
No. Our team handles the setup and provides ongoing support, so you don't need additional IT resources to run or maintain the bots.

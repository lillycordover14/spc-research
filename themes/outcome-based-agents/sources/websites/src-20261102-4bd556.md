A rare moment to join
AI is changing how people and organizations work. That puts orchestration, and n8n, at the heart of what comes next.
The product
n8n is the orchestration layer between AI and business systems. You’ll work on a product that engineers actually enjoy using. It’s flexible enough for complex workflows and accessible enough for everyone else. 
The community
Our community is our inspiration, our growth engine, and one of our greatest strengths. Builders don’t just use n8n. They shape the product, share what they create, and many go on to join the team. 
The people
Ask anyone why they joined n8n and they’ll probably mention the people first. Titles matter less than ideas. The focus is on solving problems, not climbing ladders. Everyone has a direct line to leadership and the space to do meaningful work.
The ambition
We believe n8n will become fundamental to how people and organizations work with AI. That’s the scale of what we’re building, and we want people who are excited by the ambition of getting there.

Our aim is to reduce the cost of healthcare
 
 
The US spends more on healthcare than any comparable country, and lives shorter lives for it. The burden of healthcare administration is a big factor towards this.
We exist to fix that, starting on the payer side of the system by turning frontier model intelligence into trustworthy work performed in a regulated, high-stakes environment, and by building transformational programs that remove the structural and human constraints holding health plans back.
Your work will have an impact on something that affects two of the most personal things someone possesses: their health and their finances.

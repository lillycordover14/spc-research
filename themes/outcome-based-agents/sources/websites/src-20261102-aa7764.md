Platform Capabilities
Identify the Opportunity
Find where the work actually lives before you automate it. Notch researches where volume, delays and rework build up across your team, surfaces the bottlenecks, and shows which use case pays off first.
Build your AI Agent Workforce
Turn the workflows your team repeats every day, like intake, servicing and renewals, into agents that run end to end inside your systems. No clean SOPs needed. We map your process and hand building over as agents stabilize.
Run Tests and Evals
Test every agent or workflow against your real scenarios before go-live. Simulations stress whole workflows at volume, regression tests protect what already works, and shadow mode lets your team give feedback before deployment.
Deploy with Control
Go live in gated steps, widening only after you see results. Anything that needs your team's judgment goes straight to them with full context. Notch can run inside your own cloud, under your security controls.
Govern Every Action
Every agent works inside rules you set and can prove. A live LLM judge blocks anything not grounded in your approved content. Hard limits cap what agents can do, regional rules apply automatically, and every action is auditable.
Analyze Every Case
Configurable live dashboards show volume, turnaround, escalations and cost across every agent and desk. Drill into any conversation or task, before or after go-live, to see what happened and how to improve it.

About Notch
Regulated enterprises run on people who make high-stakes decisions every day. Too much of their time goes to everything around the decision: chasing documents, switching between systems, and repeating the same steps. Our mission is to give those teams that time back, so they can move faster, serve customers better, and focus on the work only they can do.
We partner with forward-thinking insurers and financial institutions to make that real today. Notch gives their teams an AI workspace for the work in front of them and a workforce for the work that repeats, with the control and accountability regulated industries require. The result is faster operations, better customer experiences, and teams with more room to grow.

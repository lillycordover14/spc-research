About Magentic
We're building the AI workforce for the physical world.
Supply chains are the hidden engines of our world: complex, fragile, and too often overlooked until they break. We've lived that grind first-hand, losing days inside ERP systems, chasing price shocks, regulatory twists, and sustainability targets with little more than grit and spreadsheets. We started Magentic when the world ran out of the medications that kept families alive. That crisis drove us to build technology that stays ahead, not just keeps up.
Our founders
Our founders
Magentic was founded by Robin Van Aeken and Odhran O'Donoghue. Odhran comes from the bleeding edge of AI research at OpenAI, and Robin understands the world of procurement from leading supply chain projects at McKinsey and Company. They launched Magentic in July 2025 to build more resilient supply chains by delivering advanced AI to the teams navigating their complex reality.

How do you handle complex, multi-factor decisions beyond simple rules?
Critical enterprise business processes are defined by nuance and complexity, not simple 'yes/no' rules. This is the challenge that causes most automation projects to fail and where generic, non-specialized tools fall short.
The Otera platform was architected differently from the ground up to solve this exact problem, delivering true, governed decision-making through its purpose-built

A Team of Specialists, Not a Single Generalist
Instead of relying on a single, monolithic AI, the platform operates like a coordinated digital workforce comprised of specialists all working together on a single task, ensuring every step is handled with expert precision. This architecture is fundamentally different from trying to force a generic AI into a complex business process.
The platform's specialized agents were designed specifically for challenges like these:

1. The Proof Agent: For Embedded Complexity
This agent was built to tackle rules buried in dense, unstructured documents like contracts, regulations, and policies. It reads and understands them, then automatically translates that complexity into self-enforcing, deterministic rulesets. It turns static documents into

 This replaces the slow, error-prone process of having humans manually interpret complex policies or navigate through proxy systems, creating a single, reliable source of truth that is always compliant.

back where it belongs

How It's Different:
 This replaces rigid, code-heavy rules engines that require long IT cycles for any change, giving the business the agility to adapt to market shifts in minutes, not months.

Frequently asked
 questions
Do you sponsor visas?
We aim to hire the best talent globally. While we generally do not sponsor visas, we review individual cases where possible and assess options depending on the role and location.
What hiring/contract options do you offer?
We’re a global team with a flexible approach to how we work together.
We maintain legal entities in Austria, the United Kingdom, and the United Arab Emirates, which allows us to employ talent directly in these regions.
In other locations, we offer alternative engagement options depending on what works best:
Direct Employment
 via our local entities (where available) + Germany.
Employer of Record (EOR):
 Full-time employment through our partner Deel
B2B Contracting:
 Independent contractor agreements for self-employed professionals
Employment Of Record Partner:
 We partner with Deel as our Employer of Record provider. Through Deel, we are able to hire internationally while ensuring compliance with local labor laws, payroll, and benefits administration.
What does the hiring process look like?
We aim to keep our hiring process transparent, efficient, and respectful of your time. While steps may vary slightly by role, you can typically expect:
‍
1.Application Review
Our team reviews your application and experience to assess alignment with the role.
2.Introductory/ Screening Call
A short conversation with our HR team to get to know you, your background, and your expectations.
3. Role-Specific Interview(s) with Hiring Manager/ Team Colleagues
You’ll meet with the hiring Manager to dive deeper into your skills, experience, and approach to work.
4. Case Study or Task (if applicable)
For some roles, we may ask you to complete a task to understand your practical approach better.
5. Final Conversation with Senior Leadership
A final discussion to align on expectations, answer any remaining questions, and ensure mutual fit.
6. Offer
If everything aligns, we’ll move forward with an offer and contract preparations
How can I prepare myself for an interview with Otera?
Familiarize yourself with our company values, mission, and the specific role you are applying for. Be prepared to discuss your experience and how it aligns with the position.

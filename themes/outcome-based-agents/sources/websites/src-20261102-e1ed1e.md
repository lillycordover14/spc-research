What we’re all about
Kindness and creativity run deep at Clay. We care about bringing out the brightest parts of one another and building world class products.
OPERATING PRINCIPLES
Negative maintenance
Go beyond your job to make life easier for others. Kill ambiguity before it slows others down, solve problems upstream, and proactively improve systems.
Non-attached action
Be intentional & adaptable without being trapped in ego. Move quickly, but don’t hold on tightly. Be thoughtful, but don’t overthink. Take action, but don't be attached to any result.
How we operate follows from those two principles. We act based on our best judgement and keep each other in the loop. We "make it work," then "make it great." We have quiet egos and prioritize the success of our team.

Our story
Clay began in Brooklyn in 2017 with the idea that more people should have access to the power of programming. We built a spreadsheet that let anyone connect information and automate work without writing code.
We noticed salespeople, growth teams, and small agencies began using Clay to find and reach customers. We focused on helping them and learning from what they were building.
Today, our goal is to give every company the tools to build their own revenue system, guided by the belief we started with: give creative people powerful building blocks and let them shape those blocks around their ideas.

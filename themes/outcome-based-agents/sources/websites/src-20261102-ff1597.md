What does a typical interview process look like?
Our interview process is designed to be thorough yet efficient. It typically consists of four stages, though this can vary depending on the role's seniority or specific requirements:
Initial Screening:
 A brief conversation with our talent acquisition team to get to know you and your background.
Hiring Manager Interview:
 A deeper discussion about your experience and how it aligns with the role and our company culture.
Technical Task:
 A practical exercise to assess your skills and problem-solving abilities.
Final Interview:
 A concluding conversation with key stakeholders to finalize the process.
We aim to keep our process as lean as possible while ensuring we find the right fit for both you and our team.
Can I work remotely?

Can you sponsor visas?

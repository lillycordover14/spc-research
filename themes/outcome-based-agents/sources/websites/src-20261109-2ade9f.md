Our Principles
Eyes on the prize
We win as a team. We focus our time and energy on the work that creates the most value for Relevance, and we're willing to step outside our usual remit when it helps the team achieve something bigger.
Work on the factory, not in it
We use AI agents, systems and automation to do more than simply execute. By automating repeatable work, we create more time for the strategic, creative and human work where we can have the greatest impact.
Be truth-seeking
We pursue truth relentlessly, even when uncomfortable. We use evidence, challenge assumptions, surface problems early and change our minds when the facts change, always with empathy and respect.
Know your customer by name
Great work starts with deeply understanding who you're solving for. Whether our customer is external or someone inside Relevance, we get close to their goals, challenges and context before deciding what to build or how to help.
Let's find a way
We believe ambitious problems can be solved. When things get difficult, our instinct is to explore what's possible, find the next step and keep moving, balancing optimism with clear thinking about risks and constraints.
Momentum matters
We turn ideas into action quickly. We make deliberate decisions, create clear ownership and keep things moving, because the faster we execute and learn, the faster we improve. Speed never means lowering the quality bar or cutting corners.

Working with Duvo
What is Duvo?
Duvo is the Enterprise Execution OS: it maps how a process really runs from interviews with the people who do the work, then runs it with AI agents in the systems you already use. It was built by the team that ran a $2 billion retailer, and raised a $32 million Series A on 14 October 2026, led by Balderton Capital.

Which systems does Duvo work with?
Duvo works inside the systems your team already uses, including SAP, Coupa and Outlook. The first process map needs no system access or integration.
Platform integrations
Is our data used to train AI models?
No. Duvo does not train its own or third-party models on customer data. It holds SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 and supports GDPR requirements.
Trust Center
How is Duvo different from process mining?
Process mining reads system logs. Duvo Clarity interviews the people who do the work, so it captures the emails, spreadsheets and judgement calls a log never records. Interviews take around 10 minutes and run in 177+ languages.

Yes, in two ways: a free two-week Enterprise trial on one of your processes, guided by Duvo, or the self-serve Free plan (2 Clarity interviews and 100 agent runs, lifetime).

The mission
What do you mean, the GDP of an employee?
At Duvo, we see every employee as a unit of profit, not a unit of cost. A nation’s economy is the sum of its companies, and a company’s success is the sum of its employees. So we start with the employee.
Our mission
Isn’t AI going to replace jobs?
That’s the belief when people are seen as a cost: fewer of them becomes the efficiency play. We build AI that cleans up the mess around people so their work becomes more valuable. More value from the people you have, not the same value from fewer of them.

Before Duvo, we ran a $2 billion retailer
That experience showed us how much critical work still depended on spreadsheets, inboxes and the memory of experienced operators.
Backed by
The real process never lived in one system
Operating scale
85,000 products, 1,700 suppliers, 14 warehouses
We built a $2 billion retailer and saw the same pattern everywhere: the process crossed SAP, supplier portals, spreadsheets, and approval threads.
Credit notes went missing between systems. Order details sat in messages until someone rekeyed them. Whenever we were ready to automate a process, the answer was the same: ask the person who runs it. They had usually built it over years, and most of it lived only in their head.
When we asked people why they were not doing their best work, most said they had no headspace. Duvo cleans up the processes around your people, so there is less mess to work around and more room for the work that makes money. Agents take on the agreed steps. The people who know the work run those agents and build the next process.

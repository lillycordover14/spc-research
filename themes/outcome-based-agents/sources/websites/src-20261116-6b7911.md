Our Values
We work hard, encourage strong perspectives, and are maniacally focused on reducing patient delays across the U.S. healthcare system.

Clarity
Everything we do depends on clarity of thought, speech, and writing. Clear communication sets a strong foundation.
Problem Obsessed
We live and die by creating value for our customers and solving one of healthcare’s biggest problems. We don’t trust the smiling face or quiet Slack channel. A commercial nose is one that tracks only the scent of business ROI—or the stench of rabid failure to deliver real value. Picking up the scent of failure is even more important than the former.
Confrontation

Courage

About us
Things we believe
Solving the pre-visit patient processing problem will massively reduce patient delays and denials across the U.S. healthcare system. And the problem is completely solvable.
Number 1
We believe no referral should ever fall through the cracks.
Every patient referral is an opportunity to serve—and an obligation to follow up. We've built a platform that ensures nothing falls into the black hole of the U.S. healthcare system, so practices can grow efficiently, patients get better care, and referrers get true visibility into the status of their patients.
Number 2
We believe healthcare technology companies need to meet providers where they're at—faxing isn't the problem.
We believe this so strongly, we hired professional actors to make the point for us. Providers are drowning in piles of messy documents, ever-changing payer guidelines, and 45 minute phone tree wait times. We meet providers where they're at, integrating with receiving providers' EHRs and letting referrers fax away (yep, our models even handle checkboxes and messy handwriting).
Number 3
We believe in "AI" transparency
We're tired of software companies hand-waving the term “AI” to mask their shortcomings. We built our own machine learning models and provide transparency into how they're trained, how they benchmark and what they do. It's not just about 'automating' it's about making sure every patient is taken care of like your best teammate was on the case.
– The Tennr Team
